#title
Abad Alfau and the Chattering Skull

#summary
A human skull embedded in a church wall terrified an entire village by moving and chattering at night until a brave young sergeant climbed up with his sword and smashed it, revealing that mice were animating the remains.

#details
Outside the historic Iglesia y Convento de los Padres Dominicos in the Colonial Zone, a human skull sat inside a small niche in a brick wall, accompanied by an ominous inscription warning passersby that they would one day share its fate. For a long time, locals ignored it, until one evening when the lamp-lighters had finished their rounds and the skull began to nod, grin, and make unsettling sounds. Night after night, the terrifying phenomenon caused the townsfolk—and even brave military guards—to flee in terror, refusing to walk down the street after dark. Tired of the cowardice of his peers, 19-year-old battalion sergeant Antonio Abad Alfau took matters into his own hands. Armed with a ladder and his trusty sword, he confronted the wall while his trembling comrades watched from a distance. Alfau struck the skull, shattering it to the ground, only to discover a nest of mice scurrying out of the shards that had been moving the remains. 

#location
The Church and Convent of Dominican Fathers stands at the corner of Calle Padre Billini and Calle Hostos in the Colonial Zone of Santo Domingo.

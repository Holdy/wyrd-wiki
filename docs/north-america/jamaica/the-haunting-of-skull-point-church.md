#title
The Haunting of Skull Point Church

#summary
The restless spirit of a brutally executed slave and other duppies haunt an old abandoned church and the surrounding roadways of Skull Point. Passing motorists frequently warn travelers of spirits suddenly darting out from the ruins.

#details
Long ago in Mile Gully, a Christian slave named James Knight from the Lyndhurst Estate was beheaded and his skull mounted on a pole as a warning. Denied a proper Christian burial, his violent demise caused his restless spirit to roam the community alongside other malevolent duppies. When a journalist later investigated the crumbling ruins of an old local church rumored to be haunted, a passing motorist shouted a warning about duppies running out onto the road. Upon entering the silent ruins, the reporter heard eerie, maniacal squeals echoing from within, cementing the location's terrifying ghostly reputation.

#location
The ruins of an old abandoned church situated at Skull Point in the district of Mile Gully, Jamaica.

#title
The Awakening at Oberlin High

#summary
During a morning devotion led by a teacher warning that God was displeased, multiple students at a Jamaican high school suddenly collapsed, spoke in strange voices, and exhibited signs of demonic possession that required fervent spiritual deliverance.

#details
At Oberlin High School in St. Andrew, Jamaica, a morning school devotion took a dramatic turn when a teacher announced she had a message from God warning that He was not pleased with the institution. As the teacher finished praying and students responded, several attendees—predominantly girls—began screaming, speaking in unnatural and weird voices, and dropping unconscious. Witnesses described the chaotic scene as resembling a demonic invasion where students were overpowered by unseen entities, acting wildly and unable to control their physical bodies. Local evangelists and church members were subsequently brought in to perform deliverance rituals, using prayer, water, and olive oil to cast out the spirits that had supposedly taken residence within the afflicted students.

#location
Oberlin High School is located in the rural hill district of St. Andrew, near Lawrence Tavern in Jamaica.

The John Craig Light

Summary

Fishermen and residents off Lamèque Island and Miscou Island have long reported seeing a fiery phantom ship engulfed in flames as an omen of tragedy or disaster.

Details

For over two centuries, residents and fishermen around Lamèque Island and Miscou Island in the Baie des Chaleurs have reported sightings of an eerie, fully rigged phantom ship engulfed in red-orange flames. Local tradition ties this apparition, often called the "John Craig Light," to a tragic shipwreck that occurred over 200 years ago involving a vessel named the John Craig. Regarded by local Acadian fishermen as both a spectral warning light and a harbinger of approaching storms or death, the blazing ship is said to silently bob upon the dark waters before vanishing into the night.

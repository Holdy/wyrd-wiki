The Crosses of Sugarloaf Mountain

Summary

Two sisters tragically fell to their deaths while hiking Sugarloaf Mountain in 1924, prompting enduring local legends and memorial crosses painted on the cliffs.

Details

On November 9, 1924, two sisters, Dorval McLean and Lottie Ramsay, hiked up Sugarloaf Mountain in Campbellton before a planned family dinner. When they failed to return, an overnight search party discovered both women had fallen from the high cliffs to their deaths. The tragic event stunned the community and became a lasting part of local folklore, inspiring various speculations about the cause of the fall and leading local veterans to paint two large red crosses on the rock face to commemorate the sisters.

#title
The Phantom Burning Barn and Pirate of Hartland Island

#summary
Residents near Hartland frequently witnessed a massive barn ablaze on the river island below the town, only to find it completely unharmed the next morning while guarding a pirate's hidden treasure.

#details
According to a historical newspaper clipping from the *Hartland Advertiser* preserved in a scrapbook kept by historian William Ganong, area residents along the Saint John River often saw a large barn engulfed in flames on the island below Hartland, New Brunswick. Yet, when daylight broke, the barn stood completely untouched and intact. It was widely believed by locals that whenever this phantom fire appeared, pirates were on the island counting their gold. Those brave or foolish enough to approach the island for a closer look were struck by a sudden, paralyzing fear upon encountering a ragged, sunken-cheeked pirate specter who would place his shrunken, icy hand upon intruders seeking the hidden booty.

#location
An island in the Saint John River located immediately downstream from Hartland, New Brunswick.

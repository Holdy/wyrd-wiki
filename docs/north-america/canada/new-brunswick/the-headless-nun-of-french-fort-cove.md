#title
The Headless Nun of French Fort Cove

#summary
An 18th-century French nun was brutally decapitated while traveling through the woods, and her headless ghost has haunted the area ever since, eternally searching for her missing head.

#details
Dating back to the mid-18th century during the colonial conflicts in the region, the legend centers on Sister Marie Inconnue, a French nun who served local settlements. According to lore, she was beheaded—either by sailors attempting to force her to reveal the location of hidden treasure or by a deranged trapper wandering the forest. Her head was never recovered, and her spectral, headless form has been reported roaming the wooded trails of the cove for over two centuries, striking fear into travelers and locals.

#location
French Fort Cove in Nordin, now part of the City of Miramichi, New Brunswick.

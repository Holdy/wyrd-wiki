The Residence Ghost

Summary

A friendly spectral woman believed to be original owner Mae Boyce haunts a historic Victorian mansion on Waterloo Row, where she has historically tidied rooms, tucked in university students, and directed residents to a hidden kitchen safe.

Details

Built in 1913 by Mae and Walter Boyce, Boyce House was later converted into a student residence for the University of New Brunswick in the 1960s. Over the decades, multiple student residents and staff experienced encounters with a benevolent grey lady who roamed the halls, played the piano to empty rooms, and occasionally cleaned apartments or tucked sleeping students into bed. In one famous early encounter, a residence Don commanded the ghost, who directed him to uncover a hidden safe behind wall paneling in the kitchen. Though the safe was ultimately empty, the gentle haunting became a celebrated piece of local folklore.

The Ghost of the Condemned Cell

Summary

The historic Charlotte County Gaol in St. Andrews is haunted by the spirit of Tom Hutchings, an executed inmate whose terrifying presence causes visitors to experience a choking sensation.

Details

In 1944, Royal Air Force sergeant Tom Hutchings was convicted of murder and became the last person to be hanged at the Charlotte County Gaol in St. Andrews. Because the gallows were improperly built, his execution was a prolonged and agonizing ordeal. Ever since, his small, dark stone cell has been a hotspot for paranormal activity. During an investigation by paranormal researchers, visitors inside the cell reported feeling an intense, deathly cold wrap tightly around their throats in simulation of hanging, accompanied by unexplained electromagnetic spikes. Tour guides note that this choking sensation is frequently experienced by visitors who dare enter the cell.

The Dungarvon Whooper

Summary

A young lumber camp cook was murdered for his money along a remote river, and his restless ghost haunted the forest with terrifying screams until a priest laid the spirit to rest.

Details

In the 19th century at a logging camp along the Dungarvon River, a young Irish cook named Ryan was murdered by his boss for the savings he carried in his money belt. After hastily burying the cook in the frozen forest, the remaining lumberjacks were kept awake all night by unearthly, piercing whoops and screams echoing through the trees, forcing the terrified crew to abandon the camp. The haunting continued for years until a Catholic priest visited the gravesite to offer prayers and perform an exorcism, finally bringing peace to the troubled woods.

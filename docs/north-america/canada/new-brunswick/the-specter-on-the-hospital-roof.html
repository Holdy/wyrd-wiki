    The Specter on the Hospital Roof

    Summary

    Night-shift nurses at Oromocto Hospital frequently report seeing a ghostly man sitting on the roof's "H" sign and disembodied children tickling patients' feet in the wards.

    Details

    Staff and visitors at the Oromocto Hospital have shared numerous accounts of paranormal activity over the years. Night-shift nurses have encountered mysterious figures wandering the halls after dark, most notably a phantom man believed to be the ghost of an individual who tragically ended his life by jumping through the open stairwell before building modifications closed off the gap. Witnesses have also described seeing his apparition perched along the edge of the building directly above the rooftop "H" sign. In the patient wards, individuals frequently complain about unseen children tickling their feet during the night, forcing them to kick out in distress.

The Haunting of Piccadilly Road

Summary

A remote property along Piccadilly Road near Sussex became the site of terrifying ghostly encounters, ranging from moving dolls and phantom footsteps to a spectral hand reaching from a closet.

Details

Family members visiting a house along Piccadilly Road recounted a series of unnerving and aggressive paranormal events over the years. Visitors and residents reported seeing dark figures of men walking through the hallways, rocking chairs moving entirely on their own, and dolls' heads visibly turning to stare at them. In one particularly violent incident, a young boy was grabbed by the throat by a ghostly hand reaching out from a closet in plain sight of other family members. Additionally, witnesses frequently observed unexplained, glowing green and blue lights moving through the dense woods on the mountain behind the property where no vehicles could travel.

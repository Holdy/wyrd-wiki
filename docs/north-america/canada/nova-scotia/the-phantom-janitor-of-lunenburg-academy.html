The Phantom Janitor of Lunenburg Academy

Summary

Perched atop Gallows Hill next to a centuries-old cemetery, the 19th-century Lunenburg Academy is famously haunted by a beloved former school custodian whose face was captured in spirit photographs decades after his death.

Details

Dominating the Lunenburg skyline from its hilltop perch, the sprawling Victorian-era Lunenburg Academy has long been a focal point for local ghost lore. Generations of students whispered tales of a heavy, oppressive atmosphere in the basement—locally nicknamed "The Dungeon"—while paranormal investigators later captured the clear apparition of a face peering from one of the school's windows. When circulated among town residents, locals immediately recognized the face from a 1960 school yearbook as Sidney Kernickle, a long-serving former school janitor. Directly across the street sits Hillcrest Cemetery, established in 1761, where local legend also tells of the restless spirit of a 14-year-old girl named Sophia, buried behind a wrought-iron fence after reportedly dying of a broken heart.

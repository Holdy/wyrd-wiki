The Phantom of Parker Road

Summary

Residents and travellers passing along a rural country lane in Berwick have reported encountering unsettling spectral phenomena after dark.

Details

Documented by regional folklorist Vernon Oickle, the rural stretch known as Parker Road in Berwick, Nova Scotia, is famously associated with haunting local legends. Local accounts gathered from area residents and travellers speak of a mysterious phantom figure that haunts the roadway. Those passing through after dark have reported sudden chills, an oppressive atmosphere of being watched, and fleeting apparitions crossing the lane, cementing the country road's spooky reputation in local folklore.

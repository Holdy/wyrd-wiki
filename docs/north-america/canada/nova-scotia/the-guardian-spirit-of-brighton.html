The Guardian Spirit of Brighton

Summary

Occupants of a historic house in Brighton, Digby County, believe they are haunted by the ghost of a woman named Edith, who reportedly died waiting for her sailor husband and allegedly intervened to save them from a dangerous electrical fire.

Details

In October 2018, family members moved into a home in Brighton, Digby County, Nova Scotia, and were informed by the real estate agent that the house came with a resident ghost named Edith. Local lore and former residents recounted that Edith had passed away in the house while waiting for her husband to return from sea. Shortly after moving in, the current residents heard violent banging on the walls late at night, which coincided with a burning smell and electrical issues. Upon checking, they discovered severely damaged, sparking wiring in the conduit that would have burned the house down had the power not been shut off. They attribute their survival to Edith's spectral warning. Later, residents and neighbors frequently witnessed mysterious glowing lights moving around the windows at 4:30 in the morning, which the current occupant channeled into a vivid poem about Edith seeking reunion with her husband.

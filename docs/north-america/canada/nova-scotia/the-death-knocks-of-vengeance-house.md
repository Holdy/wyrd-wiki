#title
The Death Knocks of Vengeance House

#summary
A historic Yarmouth tavern plagued by mysterious tapping sounds supposedly predicted the exact day a doubting neighbor would die.

#details
Located on the southern end of Main Street in Yarmouth, Richans Tavern—locally known as "Vengeance House" after Captain John Richan’s ship—was the site of a bizarre haunting in the late 19th century. Whenever the captain's daughter Lydia had a friend stay over, persistent tapping and knocking sounds echoed from within the walls. A pious local neighbor, convinced it was mere trickery, mocked the presence and demanded to know how many days he had left to live. Three loud knocks echoed in response. Exactly three days later, the skeptic dropped dead. The mysterious knocking continued for a time until a snake slithered into a room where one of the girls was staying, after which the ghostly rappings ceased entirely.

#location
Richans Tavern (Vengeance House), located on Main Street in Yarmouth, Nova Scotia.

The Oppressive Quiet of Oak Grove Cemetery

Summary

Established in 1817, Kentville's historic Oak Grove Cemetery possesses an unnatural, heavy atmosphere and is tied to folklore surrounding its unmarked graves. Visitors have long noted an eerie quiet and oppressive feeling when walking its hilled grounds.

Details

Tucked away on a rolling, hilled landscape along the Evangeline Trail in Kentville, Oak Grove Cemetery has served as a burial ground since the late 18th century. Surrounded by moss-covered trees and gothic scenery, visitors have long reported an unnatural, oppressive quiet that seems to muffle all outside traffic and sound. The cemetery contains numerous unmarked graves of impoverished townspeople and historical figures, such as long-serving Kentville police chief Rupert Davis, fueling local ghost stories and folklore about the lingering spirits of the forgotten dead.

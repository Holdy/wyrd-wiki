#title
The Blue Nun and the Red Priest of Gilmora Hall

#summary
Gilmora Hall at St. Francis Xavier University is said to be haunted by the tragic spirits of a nun and a priest who took their own lives after an illicit romance in the late nineteenth century.

#details
According to longstanding campus lore dating back to the late 1800s, a young nun working at Mount St. Bernard College entered into a forbidden affair with a Catholic priest. Overwhelmed by guilt after becoming pregnant, the young nun took her own life, prompting the heartbroken priest to follow suit. Ever since, students and staff residing in Gilmora Hall have reported paranormal disturbances including doors slamming on their own, objects moving unassisted, mysterious footsteps echoing through the corridors at night, and the drifting apparition of a woman in a glowing blue habit.

#location
Gilmora Hall on the campus of St. Francis Xavier University in Antigonish, Nova Scotia.

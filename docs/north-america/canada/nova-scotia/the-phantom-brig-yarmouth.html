The Phantom Brig Yarmouth

Summary

For six decades following its disappearance at sea, a spectral 200-tonne brig named the Yarmouth was regularly witnessed returning to its home port.

Details

Originally built in Yarmouth in 1811 under the command of Captain Randall McDonald, the brig sailed to Antigua with a cargo of salt, fish, and lumber. After a single letter confirmed its safe arrival in the Caribbean, the ship and its entire crew vanished without a trace. Exactly one year later, the ship was reportedly spotted entering Yarmouth harbour. According to local accounts, men who rowed out toward the vessel claimed to hear Captain McDonald shouting hoarsely for them to keep off before the phantom ship vanished entirely into thin air. The ghostly vessel faithfully returned to its home harbour year after year for sixty consecutive years, gradually appearing fainter until it was last seen in 1872.

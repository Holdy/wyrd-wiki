The Seminary House Ghost

Summary

Seminary House at Acadia University in Wolfville is haunted by the spirit of a 19th-century female student who tragically took her life.

Details

Opened in 1878 as the Acadia Ladies' Seminary before later being integrated into Acadia University, Seminary House is the site of one of the university's most enduring campus legends. According to local lore, a young female student in the late 19th century discovered she was pregnant out of wedlock and, fearing the social stigma of the era, hanged herself in "The Well"—a large open space with a banister on the second floor beneath the building's skylight. Over the decades, students and residents have reported persistent paranormal activity, including lights turning on and off by themselves, doors opening and closing, footsteps echoing along the back stairwell, and sightings of a polite, demure blonde female apparition quietly wandering the halls. Some believe her spirit is Constance Hagan, affectionately nicknamed "Connie" by modern residents.

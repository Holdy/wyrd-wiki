The Redcoat Apparitions of Smith's Cove

Summary

In the winter of 1940, a young girl at Smith's Cove on Oak Island witnessed spectral eighteenth-century British soldiers sitting silently on the rotting timbers of the old wharf.

Details

In 1940, five-year-old Peggy Adams—whose father served as the island's caretaker—ran home in tears from Smith's Cove on Oak Island after encountering three unusual men. One man had dark skin, while the other two wore 18th-century British red military jackets and trousers with broad yellow stripes, sitting upon the old wharf timbers. When her father hurried to the cove to investigate, he found no men and no footprints in the fresh snow. Decades later, when Peggy visited the Citadel Museum in Halifax, she immediately recognized the historical military uniforms on display as the exact attire of the spectral figures she had encountered in her childhood.

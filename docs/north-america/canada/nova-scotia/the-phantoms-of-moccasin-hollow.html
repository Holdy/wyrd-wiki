The Phantoms of Moccasin Hollow

Summary

Believed to be the site of a historic 1747 ambush, Moccasin Hollow is said to be haunted by the spirits of fallen soldiers. Residents historically reported strange lights and phantom sounds of combat echoing through the woods after dark.

Details

Moccasin Hollow, located west of Kentville, was the site of a bloody skirmish in 1747 when French and Indigenous forces ambushed British troops. Over time, local folklore and regional historians recorded that the hollow became notorious for paranormal activity. On certain nights, residents reported seeing mysterious lights flickering among the trees and hearing the distinct sounds of fighting and cries of battle, prompting neighborhood children and locals to strictly avoid the area after nightfall.

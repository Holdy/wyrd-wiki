The Apparitions of Montague Row

Summary

Staff and visitors at the Admiral Digby Museum have reported chilling paranormal encounters, including unexplained footsteps, mysteriously locked doors, and shadowy figures roaming the historic building.

Details

Housed within a nineteenth-century building on Montague Row in Digby, the Admiral Digby Museum has accumulated a reputation for strange, unexplainable phenomena over the years. Museum staff members have recounted walking toward the basement only to find heavy doors unexpectedly locked from the inside when no one else was downstairs. Additional reports include phantom footsteps echoing through empty exhibition halls, sudden cold spots, and shadowy apparitions glimpsed out of the corner of the eye. While local speculation occasionally connects the activity to former local figures and businessmen, no definitive mundane explanation has ever been found for the persistent disturbances.

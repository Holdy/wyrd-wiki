The Ghost of Cooper's Inn

Summary

The historic Cooper's Inn on Dock Street in Shelburne is famously haunted by a phantom woman known as Mrs. Crowell, alongside unexplained thumping noises and phantom footsteps on the stairs.

Details

Built in 1785 during the influx of Loyalists, Cooper's Inn has long been associated with paranormal activity. When the property's owner washed dishes in the kitchen, she frequently felt an invisible presence tickling the back of her neck—once so intensely that she gasped and dropped a wine glass. Winter tenants and caretakers also reported being awakened by violent thumping and bangs echoing through the building at night, with no logical source. On one occasion, a caretaker looked into a bedroom overlooking Dock Street and saw the clear apparition of a woman in a high-collared dress with a towel over her arm staring out the window. When described to a neighbor, the figure was immediately recognized as "Mrs. Crowell," a phantom reported by locals years prior. Guests staying at the inn also regularly report hearing heavy, hurried footsteps running up and down empty staircases during the night.

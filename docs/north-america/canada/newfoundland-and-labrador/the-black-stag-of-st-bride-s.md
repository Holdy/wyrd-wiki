#title
The Black Stag of St. Bride's

#summary
A mysterious black caribou stag encountered on a winter road near St. Bride's is said to bring a curse of unexplained death and terror to those who cross its path.

#details
In 1804, a hardworking young man named Thomas Conway was traveling along the road between St. Bride's and Point Lance in Placentia Bay to deliver provisions. Along the way, he encountered an impressive black caribou stag standing in a clearing. Though he shot at it three times at close range, the animal remained entirely uninjured, gazing at him with an unsettling, supernatural intelligence that filled him with dread. Later that same day, after returning from Point Lance in the dark, Thomas's empty sled and exhausted pony returned to St. Bride's, and Thomas was found dead on his sled with no marks or signs of struggle. Sometime later, his wife Emily and infant son—along with their traveling companions—were found dead in the snow near the same clearing after fleeing in terror from an unseen presence, cementing the legend of the cursed black stag.

#location
A wooded road and clearing between St. Bride's and Point Lance along Placentia Bay, Newfoundland and Labrador.

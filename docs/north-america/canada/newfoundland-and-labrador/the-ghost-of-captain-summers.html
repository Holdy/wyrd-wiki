    The Ghost of Captain Summers

    Summary

    Local oral tradition tells of the specter of Captain Summers, a seafaring figure whose ghost was said to haunt a prominent rock along the shoreline. Generations of residents passed down the tale of his lingering spirit frequenting this coastal landmark.

    Details

    In an oral history interview recorded for the Baccalieu Trail Heritage Corporation, local resident Jack Barrett shared regional folklore concerning Captain Summers. According to the account, the captain's spirit was believed to haunt a specific rock situated along the coastal waters of Bishop's Cove. The legend maintained a strong presence in local memory as part of the community's traditional ghost stories.

The Phantom Smoker of West Street

Summary

Late-night visitors walking along Corner Brook's historic West Street have reported encounters with a lingering ghost who appears outside downtown halls asking for a cigarette.

Details

Local legend holds that the downtown core of Corner Brook is traversed by various spectral figures. Among the most popular urban tales told during historical night walks is that of a phantom apparition standing outside prominent community and commercial halls on West Street, who approaches passersby to bum a cigarette before dissolving into thin air.

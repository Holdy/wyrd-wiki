The Forerunner of Outer Cove

Summary

A young Outer Cove planter planning to work on a steamship was warned against the voyage when the ghostly, dripping wet apparition of his drowned brother appeared at his bedside.

Details

In the winter of 1918, Jimmy Carroll, a young planter from Outer Cove, was thrilled to have signed on as a deckhand for the S.S. Beverley, scheduled to transport codfish between Harbour Grace and Gibraltar. Despite older local fishermen warning him that the vessel was unseaworthy for the North Atlantic, Jimmy struggled to sleep. Around dawn, the apparition of his older brother Watty—who had been lost at sea years earlier during the seal fishery—appeared at the foot of his bed, dripping wet in his oilskins with water pooling on the floor. Interpreting this terrifying encounter as a forerunner warning of disaster, Jimmy fled to St. John's and cancelled his contract. His caution saved his life, as the S.S. Beverley was subsequently lost at sea with all hands on January 21st.

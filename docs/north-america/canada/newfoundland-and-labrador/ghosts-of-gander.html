    Ghosts of Gander

    Summary

    A secluded memorial park hidden among the trees near Gander Lake marks the tragic site where 256 American soldiers and aircrew perished.

    Details

    Situated just south of the Trans-Canada Highway in Gander, Newfoundland and Labrador, a gravel road leads downhill toward Gander Lake to a quiet, wooded park. This solemn site commemorates the disaster of December 12, 1985, when an Arrow Air charter flight carrying members of the U.S. Army's 101st Airborne Division crashed shortly after takeoff on a winter morning, resulting in the loss of all 256 people on board as they returned home for the holidays. Visitors to the tranquil grove frequently report a profound, haunting atmosphere and deep emotional weight, surrounded by trees and a monument depicting a U.S. soldier flanked by two children honoring the peacekeeping mission.

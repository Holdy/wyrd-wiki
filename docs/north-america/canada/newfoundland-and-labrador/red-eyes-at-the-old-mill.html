    Red Eyes at the Old Mill

    Summary

    An abandoned pulp and paper mill ruin in Glovertown is said to be haunted by a malevolent spirit known as "Red Eyes," whose glowing gaze tracks anyone foolish enough to enter after dark.

    Details

    Construction on a massive pulp and paper mill began in Glovertown in 1921, but funding eventually fell through, leaving behind a hulking concrete shell on the banks of the Terra Nova River. Over the decades, the derelict structure became a popular after-hours hangout for local teenagers and a breeding ground for eerie folklore. According to local legend, a construction worker died during the mill's hurried or unsafe building—with various accounts claiming he fell through a tower, was pushed from the roof, or became entombed in the wet concrete when the foundation walls were poured. To this day, people whisper warnings about "Red Eyes," a restless spirit whose glowing red eyes haunt the upper towers and cast an ominous presence over anyone lingering near the plant after sunset.

    The Phantom Rifleman of Second Pond

    Summary

    Walking home from Avondale late one summer night in 1960, two young men encountered a silent, rifle-carrying figure that moved effortlessly along the water's edge without disturbing the pond's surface before vanishing without a trace.

    Details

    Around midnight circa 1960, Patrick Whelan and his friend Harold were walking home to Chapel's Cove after accompanying their girlfriends home in Avondale. Under a bright full moon shining over Hickey's Mountain, as they passed by the shores of Second Pond in Lakeview, Whelan spotted a strange figure moving outside the water's edge bushes. The silhouette appeared to be a man carrying a rifle supported on his shoulder and wearing a salt-and-pepper cap. Despite the young men shouting "Hey, hey!", the figure gave no response and continued moving silently without disturbing the deep muddy bottom or the water of the pond. When Whelan and Harold threw crushed roadside stones at the apparition and ran fifty feet ahead, they looked back to find that the figure had completely vanished into thin air.

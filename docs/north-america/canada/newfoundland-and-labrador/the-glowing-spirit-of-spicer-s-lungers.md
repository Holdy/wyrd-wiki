#title
The Glowing Spirit of Spicer's Lungers

#summary
While traveling home with a sack of flour, a local man and his goats witnessed a mysterious ball of fire that crossed the road before vanishing.

#details
One evening, a man named Mr. Ryan was returning home to New Perlican carrying a sack of flour for his mother. Stopping at Spicer's Lungers, located between Turk's Cove and New Perlican, to let his goats have a drink, he noticed the animals becoming restless. Out of nowhere, a small light resembling a ball of fire approached them. As the fiery sphere drew closer, it shrank in size, crossed the road, and abruptly disappeared out of sight. The terrified goats shook with fear and bolted all the way home. Local folklore suggests this glowing orb is a spirit that watches over the community, though whether it is benevolent or malevolent remains a mystery.

#location
Spicer's Lungers, an area on the road between Turk's Cove and New Perlican, Newfoundland and Labrador.

The Moving Mop of Long Point Lighthouse

Summary

An old tar mop kept at the base of the lighthouse tower persistently rotates and reverses its orientation overnight without any human intervention. Incoming light keepers were repeatedly warned about the object's mysterious, autonomous movements.

Details

At the base of the tower in the historic Long Point Lighthouse, an old tar mop with a round, brush-like head was kept resting on a bracket supporting the staircase. Although never used for tarring roofs, the mop gained a reputation among the light keepers for having a mind of its own. When new keepers arrived, they were cautioned that the heavy mop frequently changed position all by itself, turning to face the opposite direction over the course of a day or two without anyone touching it.

The Girl on Maddox Cove Road

Summary

Drivers traveling along Maddox Cove Road have reported encountering a mysterious young woman who entices them to her aid only to leave them paralyzed with fear.

Details

Passed down through local folklore in the coastal community of Maddox Cove, this chilling roadside legend features a phantom young woman who appears along the dark, winding asphalt. She attempts to flag down passing motorists, enticing unsuspecting drivers to pull over and offer her assistance. However, once the drivers stop and engage with her, an overwhelming and unnatural terror grips them, rendering them completely paralyzed with fear as the ghostly figure vanishes into the coastal fog.

The Phantom Hitchhiker and Guardian of Black Bank Road

Summary

Motorists and travelers along the road through Black Bank near St. George's have repeatedly encountered eerie phenomena, including a mysterious spectral man who vanishes into trees and an unseen entity that hitches a ride onto passing vehicles.

Details

Travelers passing through Black Bank along the road leading toward St. George's have long shared chilling encounters with local spirits. Campers and visitors near the beach and turn-around area have reported seeing a tall man leaning out from behind a tree, only to completely vanish when approached. Others driving or riding bikes along the old road have felt a sudden, heavy weight land on their back or the rear pegs, mimicking a hitchhiker, which then vanishes as they cross the Gut Bridge or reach Mattis Point. Additionally, motorists have reported mysterious projectiles hitting their car roof accompanied by the sound of scratching nails along the isolated stretch.

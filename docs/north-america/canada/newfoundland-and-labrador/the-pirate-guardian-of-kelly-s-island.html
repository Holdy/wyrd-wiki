The Pirate Guardian of Kelly's Island

Summary

Kelly’s Island in southeastern Conception Bay is said to be haunted by the ghost of a murdered pirate guarding his buried treasure.

Details

According to local legend in the Conception Bay South and Kelligrews area, Kelly’s Island—situated just off the coast in southeastern Conception Bay—was once the domain of a pirate named Captain Kelly, who allegedly sailed alongside Peter Easton. As the story goes, Kelly was murdered by his own crewmates, who buried his body atop a massive hidden cache of stolen treasure. Ever since, the pirate’s restless spirit has remained on the island to serve as a ghostly guardian. Campers and visitors to the uninhabited island over the decades have reported eerie encounters, unexplained noises, and shadowy figures pacing the shorelines after dark.

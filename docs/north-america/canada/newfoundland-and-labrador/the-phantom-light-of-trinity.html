The Phantom Light of Trinity

Summary

Beginning around 1916, a brilliant and unexplained ghost light regularly appeared over the water near Trinity narrows, sparking fears of wartime sabotage before becoming a legendary nautical phantasm.

Details

Around 1916, residents and mariners in Trinity began noticing a mysterious, highly brilliant light shining over the water just a few miles off Trinity narrows between 9:00 p.m. and 11:00 p.m. with astonishing regularity. The glow was so convincing that the Fort Point lighthouse keeper once rowed across the harbour in great haste expecting the arrival of the SS Prospero, only to discover no ship in sight. During the First World War, frightened locals feared the phenomenon might be a German submarine attempting to tamper with the undersea telegraph cable. Later, fishermen reported trying to row toward the light on the dark fishing grounds, only for it to vanish entirely as they drew close. While some pragmatists theorized it was the headlight from a newly extended railway line, the true nature of the eerie phantom light was never fully explained.

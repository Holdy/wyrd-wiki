#title
The Ghostly House on Bayview Street

#summary
A home on Bayview Street is said to be haunted by a former owner who continues to do household chores and open doors for pets. 

#details
Local lore in Stephenville Crossing tells of a notoriously haunted house on Bayview Street. Residents and visitors have frequently experienced flickering lights, doors opening and closing on their own, and disembodied voices echoing through empty rooms. Among the most famous occurrences, a resident once left stained nursing scrubs out to soak, only to return home and find them washed, dried, and neatly hung up in the bathroom with every stain completely vanished. Pets have also been seen reacting to closed doors that mysteriously unlatch themselves, and locals attribute these uncanny phenomena to the lingering spirit of a former owner, often recalled as Mrs. Downey or Murphy.

#location
A house situated on Bayview Street in Stephenville Crossing, Newfoundland and Labrador.

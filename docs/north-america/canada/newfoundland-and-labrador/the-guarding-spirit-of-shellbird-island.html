The Guarding Spirit of Shellbird Island

Summary

Shellbird Island in the Bay of Islands is haunted by the restless ghost of a murdered pirate buried alongside a hidden chest of stolen treasure.

Details

Regional legend holds that during a flight from French warships, pirates buried chests of plunder on Shellbird Island in the Bay of Islands. To ensure the loot remained undisturbed, a pirate first mate executed a companion and interred his corpse with the gold so his spirit would serve as an eternal guardian. Treasure hunters and locals have long maintained that the murdered pirate's ghost still haunts the island to protect the remaining hidden treasure.

The Coffin House Post of Mockbeggar

Summary

In the Mockbeggar neighbourhood of Bonavista, a house was built over unmarked graves where a wooden support post eventually sank into the mud and rested directly on top of a coffin.

Details

Collected from Newfoundland elder Wilson Hayward, local legend holds that human caskets were once found scattered or shallowly buried across the ground in the Mockbeggar area of Bonavista. According to the account, one particular house in the neighborhood was constructed in such a way that its support post slowly sank down through the mud over time until it struck and rested squarely upon an underground coffin. This chilling encounter with the dead aligns with Mockbeggar's long-standing reputation as a haunted locality, reinforced by decades of accidental discoveries of well-preserved 17th-century remains and unrecorded burials during construction work.

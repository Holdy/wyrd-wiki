Paddy Mahony's Gold

Summary

A hermit searching for buried treasure on a lonely island encounters a terrifying supernatural guardian.

Details

In days gone by, a man named Paddy Mahony lived in a solitary hut at Horse's Head on Little Colinet Island. When it was mysteriously revealed to him that a great treasure was buried nearby, Paddy eagerly set out with a shovel to unearth the gold. However, upon digging deep into the earth and reaching the bottom of his pit, he discovered that the hidden wealth was fiercely protected by a supernatural guardian, cutting his quest for riches short.

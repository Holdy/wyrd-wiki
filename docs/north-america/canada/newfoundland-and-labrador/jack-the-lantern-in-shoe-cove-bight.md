#title
Jack the Lantern in Shoe Cove Bight

#summary
Local fishermen rowing home from La Scie at night were stalked by a mysterious, shape-shifting Will o' the Wisp known as Jack the Lantern.

#details
In the waters and coastal settlements around La Scie and the nearby community of Shoe Cove Bight, locals long whispered tales of Jack the Lantern—a supernatural light that appeared in the dark to mystify observers or lead travelers astray. While older residents warned youngsters of the phantom, the local young men were thoroughly skeptical. One night in September, after spending the evening in La Scie, a group of young men set off in a small punt to row home to Shoe Cove Bight. As they ventured out onto the water, a dim, flashlight-like glow emerged and began to pace their boat, stubbornly hovering just out of reach of their paddles despite their best efforts. When the boat finally reached the bight and headed toward the mooring area, the light withdrew, swelling and transforming into a massive, brilliantly illuminated shape resembling a full-rigged schooner lit up from stem to stern before fading into the night.

#location
Shoe Cove Bight, located approximately ten kilometers southwest of Cape St. John near La Scie, Newfoundland and Labrador.

#title
The Phantom Laughter and Waters of Julie's Harbour

#summary
Visitors and cabin owners in a secluded Newfoundland bay frequently report the eerie sound of children laughing in the woods and mysterious nocturnal disturbances down by the shore.

#details
Visitors camping or staying in cabins within the remote, resettled community of Julie’s Harbour near Triton, Newfoundland, have long reported inexplicable paranormal events. A local man staying alone in his cabin was driven away into the night by the loud, persistent sound of children laughing in the empty surrounding woods—an experience echoed by a group of kayakers who also had to abandon their camp halfway through the night due to children calling from the trees. Additionally, a camper who pulled his boat safely ashore woke up to a continuous overnight ruckus and found his boat mysteriously half-filled with water by morning.

#location
Julie’s Harbour and its surrounding pond and beach area in Badger Bay, near Triton, Newfoundland and Labrador.

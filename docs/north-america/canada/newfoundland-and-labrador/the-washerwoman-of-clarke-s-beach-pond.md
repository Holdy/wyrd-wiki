#title
The Washerwoman of Clarke's Beach Pond

#summary
A tragic drowning at a local pond gave rise to ongoing sightings of a ghostly woman with glowing red eyes washing clothes on the adjacent hillside.

#details
Years ago, a local woman kneeled by the edge of Clarke's Beach Pond to wash her clothes by hand. Losing her balance, she tumbled into the deep water and drowned because she did not know how to swim. According to local legend, anyone standing on the metal bridge overlooking the pond can sometimes spot her apparition on the hillside, relentlessly continuing her laundry with glowing red eyes.

#location
Clarke's Beach Pond, near the metal bridge and adjacent hill in Clarke's Beach, Newfoundland and Labrador.

#title
The Midsummer Echoes of Epaves Bay

#summary
On the night of June 23rd each year, mysterious sounds of foreign speech and invisible longboats beaching themselves echo along the grassy shores of L'Anse aux Meadows. Local tradition holds that the restless spirits of ancient Norse explorers return to retrace their historic landings.

#details
Local oral tradition maintains that the historical legacy of North America's earliest European settlement leaves an auditory trace every summer. Specifically around June 23rd, residents and visitors near the coastal waters of L'Anse aux Meadows have reported hearing the distinct sound of wooden hulls scraping against gravel, oars striking water, and men conversing in an ancient, unintelligible tongue. When investigators or locals look toward the water or approach the shoreline, no boats or travelers are visible, suggesting an ethereal reenactment of the Norse arrival from a thousand years ago.

#location
This annual haunting is reported along the coastal terrace and Epaves Bay shoreline surrounding the L'Anse aux Meadows National Historic Site on the northern tip of Newfoundland.

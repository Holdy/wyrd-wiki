The Mystery Lady and Moving Mop of Long Point Lighthouse

Summary

Long Point Lighthouse is famously associated with an autonomously moving tar mop and the spectral apparition of a lady in white who allegedly rescued a light keeper from a fatal fall.

Details

Constructed in 1875, the Long Point Lighthouse in Twillingate has accumulated distinct supernatural folklore over the decades. One prominent local curiosity involves an old tar mop kept near the bottom of the tower's staircase that inexplicably reverses its position overnight with no human intervention. A darker, earlier legend from the early 1900s tells of a light keeper who slipped while working on the clockwork weights and plunged twelve metres down the center of the tower toward the unforgiving brick floor. Just before impact, he was miraculously caught in mid-air by a woman dressed entirely in white, who vanished into thin air the moment he turned to thank her.

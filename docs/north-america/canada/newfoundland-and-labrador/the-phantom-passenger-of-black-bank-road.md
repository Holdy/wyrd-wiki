#title
The Phantom Passenger of Black Bank Road

#summary
Cyclists traveling the old road between Barachois Brook and Stephenville Crossing frequently report feeling an unseen entity hitch a ride on their bike.

#details
Along the old road running past the former campground area at Black Bank, between Barachois Brook and Stephenville Crossing, travelers navigating the route late at night have reported a chilling supernatural encounter. A bicyclist pedaling through the stretch felt a sudden, heavy weight settle onto his rear bike pegs, as if an invisible passenger had hopped on to hitch a ride. The oppressive weight remained until he reached the end of the old road near the Mattis Point turnoff, where the invisible hitchhiker abruptly vanished. Other travelers in the area have similarly reported objects striking their vehicle roofs and scratching sounds echoing along the lonely stretch of coastal road.

#location
The old road passing the former campground at Black Bank between Barachois Brook and Stephenville Crossing, Newfoundland and Labrador.

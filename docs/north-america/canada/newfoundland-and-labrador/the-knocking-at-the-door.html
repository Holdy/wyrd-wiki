    The Knocking at the Door

    Summary

    In 1949, a miner named John Mullowney died in an underground accident at the Oriental mine in Buchans, while at his family's home topside, mysterious and unexplained knocks sounded at the door before anyone received word of the tragedy.

    Details

    In 1949, a miner by the name of John Mullowney perished in an underground mining accident at the Oriental mine in Buchans, Newfoundland. While the fatal accident occurred deep beneath the earth, no one topside or at his family home yet knew of the disaster. Suddenly, three sharp knocks sounded at the door of the Mullowney family residence. When someone answered the door, no one was standing on the step. Shortly after this eerie phenomenon, a mine worker arrived at the house to formally inform Mrs. Mullowney that her husband had been killed in the mine. In local folklore, such phantom knockings are regarded as death tokens or premonitions foreshadowing a person's passing.

The Ghost Ship of Trepassey Bay

Summary

Following its tragic sinking in 1881, the vessel Jane Hunter became a phantom ship said to haunt the waters of Trepassey Bay, repeatedly appearing as a ghostly silhouette sailing into the harbour at midnight.

Details

In 1881, the ship Jane Hunter met its end when it sank in the treacherous waters of Trepassey Bay on the southern shore of Newfoundland. Following the disaster, local fishermen and residents began reporting eerie sightings of the vessel drifting in and out of Trepassey Harbour at midnight. The spectral encounters continued until a local priest finally blessed the wreck site, though according to local legend, ghostly apparitions of the ship still return to glide into the mouth of the harbour during violent storms.

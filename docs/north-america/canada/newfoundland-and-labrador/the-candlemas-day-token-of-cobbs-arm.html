    The Candlemas Day Token of Cobbs Arm

    Summary

    During a harsh blizzard on Candlemas Day, a stubborn man perished in a freezing bog while walking home with his lantern, inspiring decades of sightings of his wandering phantom light. Years later in 1963, a couple parked in a car between Summerford and Pikes Arm had a terrifying encounter with this very apparition when a glowing light approached their vehicle before vanishing into thin air.

    Details

    In the community of Cobbs Arm, local residents gathered for a Candlemas Day celebration when a fierce blizzard suddenly struck. While everyone else opted to stay safely inside the warm lodge, one contrary soul insisted on walking home despite zero visibility. Carrying a lit lantern, he stepped out into the howling storm and disappeared. The next morning, a search party discovered his frozen body out on the bog, his fingers still tightly clenched around the lantern. Ever since, locals have reported seeing a mysterious wandering light over the bog in early February. In 1963, a young man driving from Summerford to Pikes Arm parked his 1950s Volkswagen on a quiet road alongside the bog to spend time with his girlfriend. As they sat talking, a glowing light emerged from the bog, drifted straight toward the car, and shone directly through the windows. When the young man stepped outside to see if someone needed help, the light darted around to the back of the car and completely vanished into thin air, leaving no one in sight.

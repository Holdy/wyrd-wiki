The Haunting of the Carbonear Laneway House

Summary

New homeowners in Carbonear experience unexplained apparitions, phantom footsteps, and eerie warnings in a historic turquoise house tucked away on a downtown hill.

Details

After purchasing a two-storey turquoise house sight unseen in downtown Carbonear, homeowners Chris Brennan and Larry Mullett quickly sensed an unsettling atmosphere, discovering crow feathers tucked into ceilings and an old root cellar resembling a tomb in the backyard. Strange phenomena soon followed, including a white, person-shaped apparition moving across a pitch-black upstairs guest bedroom window while the owners were outside in the yard. Guests and friends who later stayed at the home also reported hearing phantom footsteps walking down the stairs and entering the kitchen when no one was there, alongside red eyes staring from windows at night. Adding to the mystery, a dark poem and note addressed to them was discovered in their locked shed attic, reading, "There still remains under my invisible tree, a gift for you!"

#title
The Dark Force of Stephenville Crossing

#summary
A residential home in Stephenville Crossing is reputed to be inhabited by an aggressive dark force that hurls objects and appears in empty windows.

#details
According to longstanding local legends in Stephenville Crossing, a particular house in the town is plagued by a malevolent poltergeist presence. Neighbors and acquaintances have reported that objects are violently thrown from shelves by unseen hands when the house is entirely empty. Witnesses walking past the property at night have also caught sight of a dark, shadowy apparition peering out from the windows of the unoccupied building.

#location
A residential property in Stephenville Crossing, Newfoundland and Labrador.

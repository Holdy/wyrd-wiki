#title
The Ghosts of Goat Cove

#summary
Local folklore warns that supernatural spirits haunt the area around Goat Cove near Beachy Cove School, where travelers after midnight have reported eerie presences and encounters.

#details
According to oral history recorded from long-time resident Ruth Bugden, the area around Goat Cove near Beachy Cove School was widely considered haunted by local tradition, which held that no one should attempt to pass through the cove at midnight. Bugden also recounted an experience her mother had while staying at a dwelling known as Granny Talker's house, where unseen hands abruptly reached out toward her throat in the dark before an old woman reassured her that the spirit was not meant for her.

#location
Goat Cove, located near Beachy Cove School in Portugal Cove–St. Philip's, Newfoundland and Labrador.

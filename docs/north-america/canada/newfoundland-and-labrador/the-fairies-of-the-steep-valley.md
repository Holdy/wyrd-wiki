#title
The Fairies of the Steep Valley

#summary
Children who wandered into a steep valley in Upper Island Cove vanished without a trace, believed to have been taken by the fairies.

#details
Residents of Upper Island Cove historically forbade children from walking into the steep valley leading from the barren land because, many years ago, children walked down into the gorge and were never seen again. Elders maintained that the children were taken by the fairies and still live with them down in the valley.

#location
The steep valley leading from the barren lands in Upper Island Cove, Newfoundland and Labrador.

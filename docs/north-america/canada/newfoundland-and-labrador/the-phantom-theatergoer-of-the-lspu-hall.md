#title
The Phantom Theatergoer of the LSPU Hall

#summary
Patrons and staff at a historic downtown St. John's arts venue have frequently reported sightings of a deceased stagehand who continues to attend performances from the auditorium seats.

#details
The LSPU Hall, located at 3 Victoria Street in St. John's, has a long history as a gathering place and community theater housed in a late 19th-century timber-framed building. Over the decades, staff and visitors have reported unexplained noises, phantom footsteps, and dark shadowy figures roaming the halls. Its most famous spectral resident is Fred Gamberg, a 24-year-old local punk rock musician and hall worker who tragically drowned in July 1995. Following his death, theatergoers reported seeing a young man thoroughly enjoying performances from the audience, only for him to vanish completely into thin air when the house lights came up.

#location
The LSPU Hall is located at 3 Victoria Street in St. John's, Newfoundland and Labrador.

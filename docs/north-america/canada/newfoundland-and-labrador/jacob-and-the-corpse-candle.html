    Jacob and the Corpse Candle

    Summary

    A father in St. Lawrence witnesses a glowing, discarnate flame drift from his home to the local cemetery, foreshadowing the tragic illness and death of his young son.

    Details

    In the coastal town of St. Lawrence on the Burin Peninsula, a grieving widower named Tom McGrath was struggling to raise his twelve-year-old son, Jacob, following the death of Tom's own father. One early morning, Tom heard mysterious footsteps in his late father's locked bedroom upstairs. Investigating, he discovered no one inside, but moments later watched in astonishment as a steady, golden flame with no wick or candle hovered down the stairs, passed through the front door, and drifted across the path toward the village churchyard cemetery before fading away. Days later, Jacob fell violently ill with a high fever. As the boy lay dying, his fever broke just long enough for him to whisper that his grandfather was there to light the way, right as the room filled with a warm golden glow before he passed away.

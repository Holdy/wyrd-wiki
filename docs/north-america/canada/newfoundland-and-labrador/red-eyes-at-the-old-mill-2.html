Red Eyes at the Old Mill

Summary

An abandoned pulp and paper mill on the Terra Nova River is haunted by "Red Eyes," the malevolent spirit of a construction worker said to be entombed in the concrete. Visitors after dark report an oppressive sense of dread and glowing eyes watching from the shadows.

Details

Over a century ago, construction on a grand pulp and paper mill by the Terra Nova Sulphite Company halted when funding collapsed, leaving behind a hulking concrete shell on the banks of the Terra Nova River. According to local legend, a worker died during construction after falling into wet concrete, sealing his body permanently inside the structure. Locals and teenagers visiting the ruins report that his spirit never left, with glowing red eyes frequently seen peering from the darkness and an overwhelming feeling of being watched.

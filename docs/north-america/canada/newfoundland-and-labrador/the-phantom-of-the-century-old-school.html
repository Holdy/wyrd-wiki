The Phantom of the Century-Old School

Summary

Locals report that a century-old school in Bryant's Cove built over a burial ground exhibits supernatural phenomena, including self-activating interior lights and apparitions appearing inside closets and windows.

Details

According to local accounts, an old school building in Bryant's Cove that has stood for over a century is said to be built on an indigenous burial ground. Residents and passersby have noted that on certain nights, the interior lights of the building turn on by themselves even when entirely empty. Years ago, while a student was inside the school, he ventured down to the basement and opened a closet door, only to find a strange man standing inside, terrifying him so badly that he never returned to the school. In another incident, a group of people standing outside the building at night looked through a window and spotted a figure staring down at them from inside.

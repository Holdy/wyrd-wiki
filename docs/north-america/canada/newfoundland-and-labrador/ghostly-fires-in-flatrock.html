    Ghostly Fires in Flatrock

    Summary

    In December 1954, a family in Flatrock, Newfoundland, experienced a terrifying series of spontaneous, unexplained fires that consumed household objects without any source of ignition.

    Details

    In late 1954, The Evening Telegram and Western Star reported on bizarre paranormal events occurring at the home of Mr. and Mrs. Mike Parsons and their 19-year-old daughter, Josephine, in the community of Flatrock. Over a two-week period, everyday items throughout the residence repeatedly burst into flames out of nowhere. The eerie phenomenon started when Mrs. Parsons discovered a burning dictionary in the wood box, despite adjacent dry sticks remaining completely untouched. Soon after, a sack of sugar in the front room caught fire, which remarkably went out the moment Mr. Mike Parsons touched it with his hands. Subsequent blazes destroyed a box of religious books and a young grand-daughter's doll, scorching furniture and bedroom flooring. The RCMP and local fire department investigated the property but could find no evidence of arson or rational ignition sources, leading contemporary columnists to compare the baffling occurrences to poltergeist activity.

    The Ghost of Main Dam

    Summary

    During the construction of the Main Dam near Howley, a worker named Sam tragically fell into the wet concrete and became permanently entombed within the structure, where his ghost is said to still linger.

    Details

    In the 1920s, thousands of workers labored to construct the massive Main Dam at Grand Lake to supply hydroelectric power to the booming pulp and paper mill in Corner Brook. According to local legend recorded by folklorist Dale Jarvis, a worker named Sam suffered a fatal accident during the concrete pouring process, plunging into the freshly laid mix before it could be halted or cleared. Unable to be retrieved without compromising the dam's structural integrity, his remains were left sealed within the concrete. Over the decades, construction workers and visitors to the remote dam have reported eerie nocturnal sounds and sightings of a ghostly figure haunting the historic structure.

#title
The Headless Spectre of Queen's Road

#summary
In 1745, a St. John's resident returning home late by carriage was forced to walk the final stretch down a lane off Queen's Road, where he encountered a towering headless phantom.

#details
Around 1745, a man named Samuel Pettyham rented a small house located at the end of a lane branching off Queen's Road in St. John's. After spending a late evening visiting a friend in the west end, his host offered to drive him home in a carriage. As the horse drew near the laneway, it suddenly stopped in its tracks and stubbornly refused to go any further. Opting to walk the remaining distance, Pettyham stepped out, but after walking only twenty yards, a spectral figure stepped into the moonlight. Terrified, Pettyham saw a very tall man missing his head. He fled in absolute horror back up to Queen's Road, bursting into a boarding house and vowing never to spend another night in his house.

#location
A laneway off Queen's Road in downtown St. John's, Newfoundland and Labrador.

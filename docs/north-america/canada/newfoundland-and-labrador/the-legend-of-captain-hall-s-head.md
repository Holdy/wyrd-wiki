#title
The Legend of Captain Hall's Head

#summary
Hall's Bay, upon which Springdale is situated, derives its name from an 18th-century sea captain whose decapitated remains and those of his crew were famously left on pikes along the coast.

#details
According to local historical lore surrounding Hall's Bay—the body of water adjacent to Springdale—an ill-fated ship captain named Hall and his crew established operations in the region in the 18th century. Following fatal conflicts with the local Beothuk people, Captain Hall and his men were killed and decapitated. Local tradition and regional lore recount that the heads of Captain Hall and his crewmen were subsequently found displayed on top of wooden pikes stuck directly into the ground along the coast of the bay.

#location
The coastal shores of Hall's Bay adjacent to Springdale, Newfoundland and Labrador.

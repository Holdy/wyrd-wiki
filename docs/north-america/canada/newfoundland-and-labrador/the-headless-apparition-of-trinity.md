#title
The Headless Apparition of Trinity

#summary
Residents of Trinity frequently reported encountering a headless man roaming the local streets.

#details
In William White's 1925 compilation of local folklore and ghost accounts from Trinity, several traditional phantoms were noted as part of the town's oral history. Among these was a spectral figure described as a headless man who was routinely sighted by multiple townspeople near the home of Paddy Murphy.

#location
Near Paddy Murphy's historic home in the town of Trinity, Newfoundland and Labrador.

    The Ghost of the James House

    Summary

    New owners of a historic 19th-century home on Riverhead Road in Brigus experienced an unexplained paranormal encounter with a family member's spirit on the second floor. Despite modern renovations, the otherworldly presence of a former occupant continues to linger in the upper rooms.

    Details

    Originally built in the early 1800s and later moved to its current site on Riverhead Road by farmer Stephen James, the simple two-story dwelling remained in the James family for generations, eventually being passed down to Fredrick James. Years after the property was purchased by new owners who carried out interior and exterior renovations, they experienced an unexpected run-in with a ghost on the second floor. According to local historical documentation, the apparition is believed to be a former member of the James family.

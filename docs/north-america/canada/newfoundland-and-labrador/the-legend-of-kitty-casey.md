#title
The Legend of Kitty Casey

#summary
The spirit of Kitty Casey, a tragic nineteenth-century woman who took her own life, is said to roam the coastal landscape of Sandy Cove without a head.

#details
In September 1862, while her husband Patrick Casey stood trial in St. John's, Kitty Casey tragically took her own life at her home in Bird Island Cove (now Elliston). Because suicide victims at the time were denied burial in consecrated ground, Kitty was buried not in a church cemetery, but in a quiet droke near Sandy Cove on land that was once part of Robert Tilley's farm. Over the generations, a chilling local legend developed around her final resting place, telling of the headless phantom of Kitty Casey wandering the foggy shores and paths of Sandy Cove after dark.

#location
Sandy Cove in Elliston, Newfoundland and Labrador.

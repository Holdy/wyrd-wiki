#title
The Webber of Gull Pond

#summary
A terrifying woodland creature with webbed hands and feet is said to stalk the marshes and camps of Stephenville, hunting anyone who wanders out after dark. Generations of children and campers have been warned of its shadowy presence lurking near the water.

#details
Rooted in the local folklore of Stephenville, Newfoundland—particularly around Harmon Air Force Base, Gull Pond, and Camp Ashanti—the legend of The Webber describes a half-human, feral monster born with severe webbing between its fingers and toes. According to various iterations of the tale, the creature originated from a tragic wartime plane crash or a secluded upbringing, leaving it isolated, insane, and deeply resentful of human company. Locals and summer camp counselors long shared tales of the Webber creeping up from the lake waters at night, leaving wet webbed footprints on cabin decks, scratching at windows with long claws, and preying on unwary campers who strayed too far into the woods after sunset.

#location
The legend centers around Gull Pond and Camp Ashanti in Stephenville, Newfoundland and Labrador.

#title
The Ghost Ship of High Street

#summary
A gruff local resident frequently witnessed a fully rigged phantom ship sailing up the middle of High Street in Trinity.

#details
In a historical account compiled by local historian William White, residents recalled numerous local ghost stories from Trinity, Newfoundland. Among the most enduring was the tale of a man nicknamed "Crusty Harry," who was known for his gruff manner. He repeatedly claimed to witness a full-rigged sailing ship making its way up High Street, passing right by the dwelling house of the late Wm. Kelson.

#location
High Street, near the historic Wm. Kelson property in the town of Trinity, Newfoundland and Labrador.

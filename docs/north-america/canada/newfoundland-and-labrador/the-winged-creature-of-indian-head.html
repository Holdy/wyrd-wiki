The Winged Creature of Indian Head

Summary

A hunter encountering rustling outside his cabin near Indian Head came face to face with a towering, winged humanoid entity with glowing red eyes.

Details

While staying at his hunting shack in the isolated Indian Head area near St. George's, a local hunter heard suspicious rustling outside his cabin late one evening. Expecting to find a moose, he stepped outside to investigate, only to be confronted by a tall, black humanoid creature equipped with wings and piercing red eyes. Before the hunter could react, the terrifying entity let out a piercing screech and launched itself into the night sky, flying away into the darkness.

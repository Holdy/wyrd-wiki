    The Ghost of Kitty Casey

    Summary

    Following her tragic death in 1862 while her husband was imprisoned, the spirit of Kitty Casey has long been rumored to roam the foggy shores and drokes of Sandy Cove.

    Details

    In September 1862, Patrick "Paddy" Casey was tried and convicted in St. John's. While he was away, a messenger arrived from Bird Island Cove (now Elliston) with the devastating news that his wife, Kitty Casey, had tragically taken her own life. Because those who died by suicide were barred from burial in consecrated ground at the time, Kitty was buried secretly in a secluded droke near Sandy Cove, close to Casey Gulch. Over the generations, local folklore preserved her sorrowful end, and residents began reporting sightings of a mournful female spirit—sometimes described as headless—wandering the foggy landscape of Sandy Cove in search of peace and forgiveness.

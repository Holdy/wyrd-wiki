#title
The Phantom Light of Trinity Narrows

#summary
Beginning in 1916, a brilliant and unexplained light regularly appeared off the narrows of Trinity Harbour, fooling the local lighthouse keeper into believing an unscheduled steamship had arrived.

#details
Documented extensively by local historian William White, a mysterious and blindingly bright light began appearing a few miles outside the narrows of Trinity Harbour around 1916. The apparition occurred with astonishing regularity between 9:00 p.m. and 11:00 p.m. On one occasion, the Fort Point Lighthouse keeper was so convinced that the glow belonged to the arriving steamship *S.S. Prospero* that he frantically rowed across the harbour to the public wharf, only to find empty water. During World War I, the recurring light caused widespread excitement and fear among residents who worried it might be a German submarine tampering with the Atlantic telegraph cables, while a local fisherman later reported the light vanishing right as he drew within a few hundred yards of it.

#location
Fort Point Lighthouse and the public wharf near the narrows of Trinity Harbour, Trinity, Newfoundland and Labrador.

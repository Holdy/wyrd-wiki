The Weather Lights of Salvage Bay

Summary

Ghostly glowing lights are said to drift into Salvage Bay on the Eastport Peninsula just ahead of severe storms, unnerving local residents. These unexplained nocturnal phenomena share roots with traditional Jacky Lantern legends used to warn people away from dangerous bogs and waters.

Details

In the coastal communities around the Eastport Peninsula, residents long spoke of mysterious "weather lights"—strange, luminous glows that moved across the water and marshes. Often appearing before inclement weather, these ghostly lights were viewed with suspicion and unease. Folklore connected them to Jacky Lanterns or Will-o'-the-wisps, eerie phantoms said to lead travelers astray into treacherous bogs and icy waters.

The Haunting of Hell Hill Pond

Summary

Motorists and travelers passing along the Southern Shore highway near Hell Hill Pond frequently report encountering ghostly apparitions, mysterious figures on the road, and unexplained disturbances tied to a tragic 1932 drowning.

Details

On August 7, 1932, a group of young women from Witless Bay and St. Joseph's went swimming at Hell Hill Pond. Tragically, three of the young women—Veronica Daley, Esther Mullowney, and Mary Norris—stepped into deep water and drowned. Decades later, a white wooden cross remains maintained on the embankment by local residents, and local folklore holds that the area is haunted. Drivers and locals report eerie sightings such as a mysterious man in a long black coat walking behind cars, silent figures standing by the roadside, car radios malfunctioning, and sudden unexplained noises or projectiles near the pond and LaManche Road.

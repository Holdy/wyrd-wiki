#title
The Phantom Pirate Ship of Petty Harbour

#summary
Three men digging for treasure near Petty Harbour encountered a spectral pirate ship and a rowing dory, resulting in a terrifying fatality and disappearance.

#details
According to folklore records from Memorial University's archives, three men were once digging for hidden treasure near Petty Harbour. While working in their trench, they spotted the eerie light of a pirate ship on the horizon. To their horror, a dory was unloaded from the phantom vessel and began rowing directly toward the shore. Two of the treasure hunters panicked and fled from the trench. When they later summoned the courage to return, they discovered their third companion dead in the dirt and the trench completely filled in. Shortly after, the two surviving men disappeared from their homes and were never seen again.

#location
The coastal area and historical treasure-digging site located near Petty Harbour, Newfoundland and Labrador.

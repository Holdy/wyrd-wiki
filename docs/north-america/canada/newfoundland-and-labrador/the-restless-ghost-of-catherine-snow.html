The Restless Ghost of Catherine Snow

Summary

Following her controversial execution for murder in 1834, the ghost of Catherine Snow has been sighted for nearly two centuries haunting the grounds of former courthouse sites and cemeteries in St. John's.

Details

Catherine Snow was convicted and hanged outside the old courthouse on Duckworth Street in St. John's on July 21, 1834, for the murder of her husband, maintaining her innocence to her dying breath. Ever since her execution—which many citizens believed was a grave miscarriage of justice—her phantom has been spotted walking the grounds and hallways of successive courthouse buildings as well as her burial site. Witnesses have reported seeing her drifting silently down corridors, accompanied by phantom footsteps and elevators moving independently. Though she was symbolically exonerated in a modern mock trial in 2012, reports of her spectral presence persist around the downtown area.

    The Brass Button Man

    Summary

    A malevolent phantom known as the Brass Button Man stalks the foggy coastal paths of Burnt Islands, demanding brass buttons from anyone he encounters under threat of abduction. Those unable to satisfy his strange request are snatched away in his dory, never to be seen again.

    Details

    According to local folklore along the southwest coast of Newfoundland, a strange and menacing spirit roams the outport community of Burnt Islands on foggy nights. Known as the Brass Button Man, the figure stealthily approaches unsuspecting wanderers, taps them on the shoulder, and asks for brass buttons. If the person has a brass button to hand over, they are spared and permitted to leave safely. However, if they have none, the spirit seizes them and carries them off in his dory, disappearing into the fog with his victim forever.

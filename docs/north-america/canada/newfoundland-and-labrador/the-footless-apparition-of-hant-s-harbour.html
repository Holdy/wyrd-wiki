    The Footless Apparition of Hant's Harbour

    Summary

    A young girl in Hant's Harbour awoke one night to find a footless male specter standing in her bedroom after her family moved into a historically older home. Alongside the apparition, the household experienced poltergeist phenomena, including objects flying off tables and cupboards opening and closing by themselves.

    Details

    According to local folklore compiled from generational accounts, a family moved into a house in the coastal community of Hant's Harbour and soon found themselves sharing the home with unseen and restless energy. One night, the mother—who was then a child living in the home—woke up to see a strange man standing in her bedroom who remarkably had no feet. Local lore attributed the haunting to the deceased former owner of the house, who allegedly resented the renovations and interior changes the family had made. The supernatural activity was not limited to visual encounters; family members reported startling poltergeist occurrences, such as items flying off tables and cupboards swinging open and shut of their own accord.

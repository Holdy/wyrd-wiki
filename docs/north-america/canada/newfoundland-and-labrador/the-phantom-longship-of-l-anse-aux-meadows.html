The Phantom Longship of L'Anse aux Meadows

Summary

On select summer nights along the shores of L'Anse aux Meadows, witnesses have reported encountering a spectral Viking longship complete with fur-clad warriors and battle horns. This ghostly vessel has appeared to stranded fishermen and nighttime trespassers alike before vanishing into mist.

Details

According to local folklore rooted in the historic Norse settlement site, a phantom Viking longboat periodically haunts the waters off L'Anse aux Meadows. In one prominent account, a lone fisherman delayed his return ashore when his boat's motor inexplicably died as bad weather rolled in. Hearing the sound of paddles, he looked out to see a longship with red and white striped sails and armed men brandishing swords bearing down on him, only for a battle horn to sound and the vessel to vanish into thin air, instantly allowing his engine to restart. In another occurrence, two young men breaking into an outbuilding to steal whiskey looked out toward the bay and witnessed the same menacing longship, complete with shouting warriors shaking their sabres, before it dissolved into a cloud of mist.

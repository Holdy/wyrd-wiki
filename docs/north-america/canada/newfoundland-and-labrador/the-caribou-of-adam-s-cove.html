The Caribou of Adam's Cove

Summary

A bed-ridden man suffered under a witch's curse for eight years until the witch, transformed into a caribou, dramatically crashed through his house.

Details

In the community of Adam's Cove, a resident named John Murray was famously bed-ridden for eight years due to what locals recognized as a powerful witch's spell. The strange ordeal came to an abrupt end one day when the witch, taking the physical shape of a wild caribou, charged through the village and ran directly through Murray's house. The destructive passage broke the supernatural bond of the curse, releasing Murray from his long confinement.

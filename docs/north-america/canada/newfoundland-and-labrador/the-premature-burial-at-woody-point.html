The Premature Burial at Woody Point

Summary

During the funeral service of a teenage girl from Woody Point who supposedly died of epilepsy, horrifying sounds from inside her coffin revealed she had been buried alive.

Details

In the village of Woody Point, a 16-year-old girl passed away following a series of severe epileptic convulsions and was laid to rest. Shortly after she was buried, mourners and undertakers gathered for the funeral service heard a distinct, muffled knocking coming from deep within the coffin. Panic-stricken, the undertakers frantically shoveled away the earth and forced the casket open. Inside, they discovered the young girl turned face down with her eyes wide open in sheer terror, indicating she had tragically regained consciousness after burial.

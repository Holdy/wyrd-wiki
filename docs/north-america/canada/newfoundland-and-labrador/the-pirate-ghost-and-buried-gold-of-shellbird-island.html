    The Pirate Ghost and Buried Gold of Shellbird Island

    Summary

    Legend holds that pirate treasure buried near Shellbird Island and Hughes Brook in the Humber River is protected by the vengeful ghost of a murdered crewman.

    Details

    According to local folklore in Western Newfoundland, the notorious 17th-century pirate Peter Easton retreated up the Humber River near Hughes Brook to evade a French warship and hid his plunder. He dispatched his crew to bury chests of gold on Shellbird Island. To guard the secret, the first mate fatally shot his companion so that the man's ghost would eternally watch over the treasure, before the mate himself drowned in the river's strong current. Generations of locals and treasure hunters have whispered that anyone disturbing the island or seeking the gold near the rock formation known as the Man in the Mountain will face the spectral guardian and the cold, dead hands of a vengeful pirate ghost.

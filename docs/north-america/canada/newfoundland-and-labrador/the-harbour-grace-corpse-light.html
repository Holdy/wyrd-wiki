The Harbour Grace Corpse Light

Summary

A glowing corpse light that typically portended tragedy instead guided a lost minister and his wife to safety during a blizzard outside Harbour Grace.

Details

At the beginning of the twentieth century, Reverend Canon Noel and his wife traveled by horse and sleigh to visit a blacksmith several miles away. Due to delays, they had to make the return journey during a fierce blizzard, and their horse soon became bogged down in high snow, leaving them completely lost. As they struggled in the freezing storm, a bright, disembodied light appeared, moving around them. Believing it to be rescuers, they shouted for help, but the light passed silently by with no person carrying it. Following the eerie glow, the frozen couple was led to a stone fence and subsequently to a house where they found shelter for the night. Local residents were unsurprised, noting that while corpse lights in Harbour Grace usually appeared before terrible tragedies, this one chose to act as a protector.

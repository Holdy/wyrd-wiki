#title
The Elves of Roche's Meadow

#summary
Two children walking near a countryside road in Outer Cove at dusk encountered three small, red-and-black-clad figures dancing on a stone wall.

#details
According to local oral history collected in the area, a young girl and her brother were walking home around dusk along Barnes Road in Outer Cove near Roche's Meadow. As they approached, they spotted three peculiar little figures about three feet tall dancing and hopping along a stone wall, dressed in red and black attire. Fearing the otherworldly nature of the encounter, the children made the sign of the cross, upon which the mysterious dancing figures instantly vanished.

#location
Roche's Meadow on Barnes Road, Outer Cove, Newfoundland and Labrador.

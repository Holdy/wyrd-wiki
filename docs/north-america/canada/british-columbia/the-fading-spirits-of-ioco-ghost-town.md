#title
The Fading Spirits of Ioco Ghost Town

#summary
The historic company town of Ioco in Port Moody is remembered through local ghost walks and folklore recounting its boomtown past and the atmospheric remnants of abandoned worker housing.

#details
Established in the early 20th century as a company town for workers at the local Imperial Oil refinery, Ioco grew into a tight-knit community complete with dozens of houses, churches, grocery stores, and recreational facilities. As the industry modernized and the residential area faded away, it earned a reputation as a local ghost town. Today, historical events and guided ghost walks explore the eerie, atmospheric history and preserved remnants of the old townsite across the Burrard Inlet.

#location
The historic Ioco townsite is situated in the northern section of Port Moody, British Columbia, along the Burrard Inlet.

    The Phantom Girl of Zuckerberg Island Bridge

    Summary

    A teenager visiting Zuckerberg Island in Castlegar repeatedly encounters a sorrowful young girl who silently follows him across the high suspension bridge before inexplicably vanishing into thin air.

    Details

    In the summer, a fourteen-year-old boy named Lloyd Langill visited Zuckerberg Island in Castlegar, British Columbia, with his brother and friends. As twilight fell, Lloyd noticed a young girl sitting alone on a bench across an open field, watching their group. When he briefly looked away and turned back, she had vanished entirely, and none of his companions had seen her. Later, as the group headed toward the large suspension bridge to leave the island, the girl reappeared and began silently following them, refusing to answer calls. As she quickened her pace and caught up, the group panicked and ran across the bridge. Lloyd, lagging behind as the youngest, felt someone reaching out directly behind him. Reaching the end of the bridge, he looked back and found no one there, despite the bridge taking several minutes to cross and the girl never passing him. As they drove away, he looked back one last time to see her standing on the bridge with an expression of profound sadness. He later learned of local talk that a young girl had previously committed suicide off the suspension bridge.

#title
The Lady of O'Keefe Mansion

#summary
Staff and paranormal investigators at the historic O'Keefe Ranch north of Vernon have reported feeling a lingering female presence, seeing a mysterious woman watch from an upstairs window, and capturing disembodied screams in the attic.

#details
Founded in 1867, the Historic O'Keefe Ranch is regarded as one of the most haunted historic sites in the region. Staff members working alone in the stately O'Keefe Mansion frequently report a strong sense of a woman's presence watching them. Observers outside have occasionally spotted a lady peering out from an upstairs window. Furthermore, paranormal investigation groups utilizing audio equipment in the mansion's attic have recorded unexplainable disembodied voices and a woman's scream echoing through the empty space.

#location
The Historic O'Keefe Ranch is situated at 9385 Highway 97 North just north of Vernon, British Columbia.

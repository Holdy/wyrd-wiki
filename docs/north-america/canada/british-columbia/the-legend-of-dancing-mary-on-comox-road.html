The Legend of Dancing Mary on Comox Road

Summary

A spectral woman known as Dancing Mary has haunted Comox Hill and Comox Road near Courtenay for over a century, appearing within a shimmering blue mist.

Details

Local folklore tells the tragic story of a young K'ómoks First Nation woman—named Mary by early settlers—who was betrayed and murdered. For generations, her ghost has haunted Comox Hill and the surrounding road, appearing as a glowing blue orb or a swirling fog that sways in a spectral dance. One of the most famous historic encounters occurred in 1914, when a soldier cycling down Comox Road rode straight through the ghostly light and experienced an intense, unforgettable otherworldly cold.

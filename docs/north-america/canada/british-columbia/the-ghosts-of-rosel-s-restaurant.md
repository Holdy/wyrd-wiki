#title
The Ghosts of Rosel's Restaurant

#summary
Operating out of a historic building at 1624 Seventh Avenue in downtown Prince George, Rosel's Restaurant was plagued for years by crooked photographs, phantom footfalls, and a chilling encounter with a cold apparition.

#details
During the 1980s and 1990s, Wilf and Rosel Vogt operated Rosel's Restaurant at 1624 Seventh Avenue in downtown Prince George. Unbeknownst to them at first, the building was haunted. According to local lore shared by neighbors, the ghost was a former manservant who had hanged himself in a closet after being separated from his family; ironically, family photographs hung precisely on the wall where that closet had once been removed. Every morning, the photographs were found completely askew or sideways. Staff and owners frequently heard loud footfalls upstairs and mysterious noises like an unplugged vintage radio playing or phones ringing when the building was locked. The most terrifying encounter occurred when Rosel, while closing up, felt a freezing-cold entity brush past her, physically turning her sideways in the dark hallway. Despite these strange and recurring events, the owners felt the spirit meant no harm and eventually built an attached dwelling where they lived undisturbed by the hauntings.

#location
The former Rosel's Restaurant is located at 1624 Seventh Avenue in downtown Prince George, British Columbia.

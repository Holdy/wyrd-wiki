#title
The Phantom Gramophone and Ringing Doorbell

#summary
Staff and visitors at a historic Pitt Meadows building frequently experience unexplained phenomena, including a vintage gramophone playing on its own and a back door bell ringing with no one there.

#details
Documented in the museum's ghost logs, staff members and visitors have experienced numerous strange paranormal events. In September 2007, a staff member and a visitor listened to the museum's antique gramophone play after replacing the needle, then walked into another room after flipping the needle upright to stop it; moments later, both clearly heard a bar of music play again from the machine, which was confirmed to have its needle resting upright. On other occasions, including during committee meetings, multiple attendees with no prior knowledge of the building's legends independently heard the back door bell tinging continuously when no visitors or members were entering. Other logs note doors opening on their own, objects shifting across desks, and phantom footsteps climbing the stairs when the building is empty.

#location
The key location is the Pitt Meadows Museum General Store Site situated at 12294 Harris Road in Pitt Meadows, British Columbia.

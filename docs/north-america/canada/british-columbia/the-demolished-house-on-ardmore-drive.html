The Demolished House on Ardmore Drive

Summary

A once-grand residence on Ardmore Drive gained a reputation for intense paranormal activity before it was ultimately demolished because no one could be persuaded to live there.

Details

Local folklore and ghost-lore chroniclers on the Saanich Peninsula recount the tale of a particular residential property on Ardmore Drive that was plagued by persistent, inexplicable disturbances. The haunting was so pervasive and unsettling that successive occupants fled, and prospective buyers refused to move in, leading local authorities and owners to eventually tear the building down entirely.

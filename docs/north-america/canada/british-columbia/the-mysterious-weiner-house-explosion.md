#title
The Mysterious Weiner House Explosion

#summary
Homesteader Nils Weiner mysteriously vanished in 1944 when a massive nighttime explosion completely obliterated his Sproat Lake cabin, leaving behind a legacy of local mystery and unrecovered remains.

#details
Nils Weiner and his wife Anna arrived in the Alberni Valley around 1913, homesteading near Sproat Lake Landing. Following Anna's sudden and tragic death at age 42, Nils became an isolated and troubled figure. In the early hours of September 23, 1944, residents around the lake were jolted awake by a tremendous explosion that rattled windows for miles. The Weiner house was completely obliterated, and all outbuildings burned to the ground. Despite the devastation, Nils's body was never found, fueling decades of enduring local speculation over whether the event was a tragic accident, foul play, or an intentional disappearance.

#location
The former Weiner homestead site near Sproat Lake Landing in Port Alberni, British Columbia.

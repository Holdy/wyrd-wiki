#title
The Serpent of Cowichan Lake

#summary
Settlers and local residents have reported decades of eerie encounters with Stin-Qua, a giant serpent-like lake monster inhabiting the deep waters of Cowichan Lake.

#details
For over a century, witnesses across Cowichan Lake and adjacent waterways have reported sightings of an enigmatic aquatic creature known as Stin-Qua. Early accounts date back to 1885, with frequent reports emerging during warm, dry summers when low water levels allegedly force the creature toward the surface. In September 1928, hunters named Jarvis and Hill were crossing Bear Lake near Cowichan Lake when they spotted an eight-foot tapering neck and serpent-like head rising out of the water before it vanished with a serpentine motion. Months later, Riverside Inn proprietor Norman Thomas and his passengers encountered what appeared to be floating logs that suddenly bowed in the middle and submerged with a massive commotion, revealing a snake-like entity roughly thirty feet long. In 1930, Youbou resident Charlie "Cougar Charlie" Caldwell also reported seeing the whitish serpent while fishing on the lake. While no physical evidence has ever been recovered, local folklore and Indigenous oral history maintain that a massive creature lurks in the lake's deepest depths.

#location
Cowichan Lake on Vancouver Island, British Columbia, Canada.

The Haunting of Canada's Oldest Operating Movie Theatre

Summary

Operating continuously since 1913, the Patricia Theatre in Powell River's Townsite is haunted by a strict former dentist and active poltergeist phenomena.

Details

Housed in a Spanish revival building designed by architect Henry Holdsby Simmonds, the Patricia Theatre has accumulated numerous ghostly legends, especially following major renovations in the late 1990s and early 2000s. Workers experienced severe paranormal activity, including self-opening curtains, moving supply carts, inexplicable cold spots, and tools mysteriously shifting. A contractor named Dan Kressel was pushed down a flight of concrete stairs by an unseen force and quit on the spot. The building's most famous spirit is Dr. Samuel Paul Marlatt, a stern dentist who practiced on the upper floor from 1928 to 1956 and is frequently seen as a man in a white medical smock. Conversely, long-time owner Myron McCleod is also reported to peacefully appear sitting at an old-fashioned desk before fading away.

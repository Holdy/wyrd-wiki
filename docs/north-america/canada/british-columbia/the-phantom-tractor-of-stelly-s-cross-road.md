#title
The Phantom Tractor of Stelly’s Cross Road

#summary
Motorists passing along Stelly’s Cross Road at night have reported seeing a ghostly farmer operating a phantom tractor to tend his fields in the dead of night.

#details
Local legend on the Saanich Peninsula tells of a supernatural agricultural apparition that appears in the fields near Stelly’s Cross Road. Witnesses report seeing a glowing or shadowy farmer diligently working his land aboard a vintage tractor during the darkest hours of the night, only for the tractor and operator to vanish completely the moment someone looks away.

#location
The agricultural fields are situated along Stelly’s Cross Road in Central Saanich, British Columbia.

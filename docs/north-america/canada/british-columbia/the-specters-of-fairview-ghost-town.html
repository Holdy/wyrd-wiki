The Specters of Fairview Ghost Town

Summary

The historic gold-mining boomtown of Fairview, located just west of Oliver, is said to be haunted by the restless spirits of miners and pioneers who perished during its rough-and-tumble era.

Details

Established in the 1890s during the South Okanagan gold rush, Fairview grew into a bustling settlement before fading into an abandoned ghost town by the 1920s. Its brief history was marked by sudden fires, gold heists, and violent deaths. Today, nighttime tours through the old townsite and the historic Fairview Cemetery regularly report strange phenomena, inexplicable shadows, and the lingering presence of the town's former residents.

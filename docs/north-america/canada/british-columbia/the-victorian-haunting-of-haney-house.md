#title
The Victorian Haunting of Haney House

#summary
Built in 1883, the historic Haney House has become the center of local ghost lore, with visitors and paranormal investigators reporting unexplained energies and ghostly encounters.

#details
The Victorian-era Haney House, which was inhabited by the Haney family for decades and witnessed multiple family members pass away within its walls, is frequently investigated by professional paranormal teams. Organizers and investigators note that the house retains an eerie, authentic atmosphere filled with original family artifacts, where people frequently sense unseen presences, cold spots, and unexplained anomalies after dark.

#location
Haney House is located at 11612 224th Street in Maple Ridge, British Columbia.

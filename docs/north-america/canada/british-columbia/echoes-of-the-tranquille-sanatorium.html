Echoes of the Tranquille Sanatorium

Summary

Built in 1907 as a tuberculosis hospital, this sprawling facility is widely considered one of Canada's most haunted locations due to lingering disembodied moans and ghostly apparitions.

Details

Originally established as a treatment center for tuberculosis sufferers before later housing individuals with mental disabilities, the Tranquille Sanatorium near Kamloops witnessed countless deaths over its decades of operation. Witnesses and visitors to the historic grounds have reported intense paranormal activity, including mysterious orbs drifting through empty hallways, eerie disembodied groans, and shadowy figures wandering the property. The subterranean tunnels beneath the complex—historically used to transport supplies and bodies away from the view of recovering patients—are particularly noted for carrying the heavy residual energy of the past.

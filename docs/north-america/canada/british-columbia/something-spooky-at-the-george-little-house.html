Something Spooky at the George Little House

Summary

Staff and paranormal investigators at the historic George Little House in Terrace experience unexplained heavy presences, moving rocking chairs, and electronic voice phenomena associated with the home's original owners.

Details

Built in 1914 and later relocated to Kalum Street, the George Little House—former residence of city founder George Little and his wife Clara—has long been reputed to harbor restless spirits. House manager Debbie Letawski and other staff reported an oppressive, draining heaviness in the upstairs office rooms, as well as rocking chairs moving on their own. During an investigation by regional paranormal teams, investigators captured photographic anomalies, including a shadowed figure in an empty room, and recorded electronic voice phenomena (EVPs) using a spirit box that specifically answered with the name "Clara," believed to be Clara Little, who passed away in the home.

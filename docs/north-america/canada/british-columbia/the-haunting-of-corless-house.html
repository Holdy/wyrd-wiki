The Haunting of Corless House

Summary

Corless House, built in 1917 and formerly owned by a pioneer undertaker, is reportedly haunted by the spirits of a tall man, a woman, and two young girls who roam the stairs and halls.

Details

Corless House at 1276 Fourth Ave in downtown Prince George was built in 1917 and served as the home of Richard (Dick) Corless, a pioneer businessman and undertaker who operated during the 1918 influenza pandemic. Today, the building houses Sisters Rock'n Gems. Co-owner Sue Bloomingdale and various customers have reported numerous supernatural occurrences, including heavy footfalls coming from the second floor when no one is upstairs, lights turning on and off by themselves, and objects moving without explanation. Witnesses have seen a tall, thin man walking confidently through the store—believed to be Dick Corless—alongside a woman, as well as two young girls in long dresses playing on the stairs. During the 2023 downtown Prince George explosion, despite neighboring buildings suffering severe window and frame damage, Corless House sustained zero damage, which staff attribute to the protective presence of its resident spirits.

The Spectral Apparitions of DeHart House

Summary

The historic 1914 DeHart House in Kelowna is said to be haunted by a phantom man in a grey suit and the spirit of a young soldier appearing on a bedroom bed.

Details

Constructed in 1914 on Bowes Street by the DeHart family and later owned by Premier W.A.C. Bennett, DeHart House (also known as Brookside Manor) has accumulated numerous ghost stories over the decades. Subsequent residents and family members reported seeing an elderly man dressed in a grey suit wandering the halls. Additionally, during the Second World War, a maid witnessed a young man sitting on a bed in the house right around the exact time that one of the DeHart sons was killed in battle overseas.

#title
The Welcoming Spirit of Sandwick Manor

#summary
Built in 1911, the historic Sandwick Manor in Courtenay is haunted by the gentle apparition of its first resident, Anna Duncan.

#details
Stately Sandwick Manor, recognized as the oldest residential structure east of the Courtenay River, was constructed in 1911 by Eric Duncan. Following the death of his first wife, Anna Duncan, in 1927, her benevolent spirit began manifesting within the Edwardian home. Over the decades, "Aunt Anna" has been witnessed by various visitors and family members—including comforting a frightened 12-year-old girl in 1938 and appearing to another young girl in 1998. Adults have also reported seeing a hazy glowing form and experiencing quirky phenomena such as light bulbs being unscrewed.

#location
Sandwick Manor, located east of the Courtenay River in Courtenay, British Columbia.

#title
The Spectral Resident of Room 10

#summary
Female guests staying in Room 10 of the historic European Bunkhouse at the North Pacific Cannery frequently experience their bed covers being mysteriously pulled off during the night.

#details
Operating for over a century, the North Pacific Cannery has accumulated local legends of paranormal activity and ghostly encounters. Among the most famous accounts is that of a spirit woman who haunts the European Bunkhouse, specifically Room 10. According to local lore and investigations by regional paranormal teams, female visitors sleeping in this room have reported feeling an unseen presence that tugs at their bedsheets. Additionally, investigators recording audio in the hallways captured an ethereal voice responding with the name "Amanda," while historic typewriters in the building's common room have been witnessed depressing their keys independently.

#location
The North Pacific Cannery National Historic Site is located in Port Edward, just outside Prince Rupert, British Columbia.

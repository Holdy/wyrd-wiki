The Ghosts of the Coldwater Hotel

Summary

The historic Coldwater Hotel in Merritt, famous for its golden dome and ties to train robber Billy Miner, is said to be haunted by unseen presences and former guests.

Details

Constructed in 1910, the Coldwater Hotel is one of Merritt's most prominent historical landmarks, instantly recognizable by its flag-topped gold dome. Beyond its history of hosting famous figures like the legendary train robber Billy Miner—who famously hid stolen securities in the dome—the hotel carries a long-standing reputation for haunting encounters. In the late 1960s, a transient man was found dead in one of the rooms, adding to the building's somber history. Guests and visitors exploring the hotel after midnight have reported hearing disembodied growls ordering them to leave, feeling unexplainable chills, and witnessing ghostly apparitions.

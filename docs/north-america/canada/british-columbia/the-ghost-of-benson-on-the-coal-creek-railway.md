#title
The Ghost of Benson on the Coal Creek Railway

#summary
Following a tragic murder-suicide sparked by a workplace dispute in the early days of Fernie, a miner named Benson was said to haunt the railway tracks where he ended his life.

#details
In the early frontier days of Fernie, a miner named Benson grew enraged when his fireboss moved him out of spite. In a fit of sullen anger, Benson shot at his fireboss at the mine offices and then fled to the nearby railway tracks in Coal Creek, where he fatally shot himself. Tragically, the superintendent had already ordered Benson's reinstatement and the fireboss had only survived with minor injuries. For decades afterward, residents and passersby reported supernatural encounters along that specific section of the tracks, including cold hands pulling people backward and dark, shadowy figures.

#location
The historic railway tracks at Coal Creek, just outside Fernie, British Columbia.

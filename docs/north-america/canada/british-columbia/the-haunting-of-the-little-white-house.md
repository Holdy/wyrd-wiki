#title
The Haunting of the Little White House

#summary
A 1930s teenager named Hazel, who tragically died in the building after ingesting poison, is said to haunt a historic boutique in Fort Langley, frequently locking doors and moving objects.

#details
In the 1930s, a 15-year-old girl named Hazel lived in the house that is now known as the Little White House in Fort Langley. After becoming pregnant, she tragically ingested rat poison and was found dead on the living room sofa. Decades later, after the building became a boutique and tea room, owners and staff began experiencing unexplained paranormal activity. Security footage has captured doors mysteriously swinging open with no draft or wind present, staff have been locked inside rooms by solid, lockless doors requiring a locksmith, and invisible presences have left indentations on furniture. The spirit, affectionately called "Hazel" or the "lady in white," is believed to be quite peaceful, though she tends to play cheeky pranks whenever renovations or new staff arrive.

#location
Little White House & Co is located at 9090 Glover Road in Fort Langley, British Columbia.

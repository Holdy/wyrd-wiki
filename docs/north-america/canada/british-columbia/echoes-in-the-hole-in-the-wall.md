#title
Echoes in the Hole in the Wall

#summary
Phantom train noises and unexplained mechanical sounds echo through the abandoned, collapsed Kwinitsa railway tunnel along Highway 16 outside Prince Rupert.

#details
Located approximately 72 kilometers outside of Prince Rupert along Highway 16 between Terrace and Prince Rupert, the Kwinitsa Tunnel—often referred to locally as "The Hole in the Wall"—is an abandoned historical railway tunnel marked by a major collapse. Over the years, travelers and investigators exploring the dark, blocked ruins have reported an oppressive atmosphere and strange auditory phenomena. Visitors have documented phantom train sounds rumbling through the passage, echoing the history of railway construction and accidents along the Skeena River corridor.

#location
The abandoned Kwinitsa Tunnel is situated along Highway 16 between Terrace and Prince Rupert, British Columbia.

The Apparition on Discovery Pier

Summary

A stoic newcomer and other local witnesses reported seeing the ghostly apparition of a dead girl lingering on the waterfront pier at night.

Details

For years, rumours circulated among teenagers and residents in Campbell River about a ghostly girl haunting the local waterfront pier. These stories were given chilling credibility when a newly hired immigrant worker returned from his lunch break in sheer terror. Having recently arrived in Canada with significant language barriers and no prior knowledge of local folklore, the quiet, reserved worker pointed frantically toward the pier, stammering that he had encountered the ghost of a dead girl.

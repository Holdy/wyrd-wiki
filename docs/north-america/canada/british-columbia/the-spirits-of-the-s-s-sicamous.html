The Spirits of the S.S. Sicamous

Summary

The historic paddlewheeler S.S. Sicamous is rumored to be haunted by former crew members and local journalists whose personal artifacts were brought aboard.

Details

The S.S. Sicamous, a historic paddlewheeler moored on the shores of Okanagan Lake, has long been a focal point for ghostly legends in Penticton. According to local historians and archivists, strange phenomena began occurring after the personal desk, photographs, and belongings of former Penticton Herald writer and photographer Ed Aldredge were transferred to the ship. Staff members subsequently reported hearing unexplained footsteps, slamming doors, and faint voices echoing through the vessel. Historians often attribute these noises to Aldredge's spirit interacting with the ghost of Captain Joseph B. Weeks, the ship's former captain. Additionally, the vessel is believed to host the spirit of RCMP Constable Geoffrey Aston, who was tragically shot on a sister vessel in 1912.

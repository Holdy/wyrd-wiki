The Apparition Behind a Gibsons Home

Summary

A teenager looking out his bedroom window at 5:00 a.m. in Gibsons witnessed a tall, mist-like apparition shaped like a woman with a veil walking slowly across the backyard.

Details

Late one night in 2005, a 14-year-old resident named Mike was staying up late watching television in his upstairs bedroom in Gibsons. Around 5:00 a.m., he peered through the window blinds to check the dawn light and spotted a lone figure moving across his family's backyard patio. Described as a translucent white mist in the shape of a person—resembling a woman with a veil over her head and hands raised upward as if in prayer—the entity moved very slowly from right to left beneath his window toward the kitchen deck before vanishing out of sight. Shaken by the encounter, he woke his father, who dismissed it as a dream, but Mike remained certain of what he witnessed, noting that a neighbor had recently passed away undiscovered in their home nearby around the same time.

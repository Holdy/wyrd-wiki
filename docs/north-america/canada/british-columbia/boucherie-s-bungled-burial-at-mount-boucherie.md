#title
Boucherie’s Bungled Burial at Mount Boucherie

#summary
The volcanic slopes and former ranch lands of Mount Boucherie in West Kelowna are steeped in folklore surrounding early pioneer burials and restless spirits.

#details
Named after early French-Canadian rancher Isidore Boucherie, Mount Boucherie is a prominent geographic landmark in West Kelowna with a history dating back to the earliest agricultural settlements of the Okanagan Valley. According to local historical folklore, the harsh realities of pioneer life led to hasty and unconventional burials across the ranch lands. Visitors and heritage guides recounting the area's paranormal history report hearing disembodied noises, unexplained cold spots, and eerie atmospheric disturbances along the lower slopes and old ranch trails, attributed to spirits unsettled by the rough handling of early deaths in the remote settlement.

#location
The slopes of Mount Boucherie and the surrounding historical ranch lands in West Kelowna, British Columbia.

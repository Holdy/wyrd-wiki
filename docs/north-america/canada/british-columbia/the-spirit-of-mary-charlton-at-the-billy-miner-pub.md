#title
The Spirit of Mary Charlton at the Billy Miner Pub

#summary
The historic building housing a local pub is said to be haunted by the spirit of Mary Charlton, who once ran businesses in the area alongside her husband.

#details
Local historical research into the Billy Miner Pub revealed that the property is associated with the ghost of Mary Charlton. Rather than acting as a malevolent entity, local folklore and paranormal researchers suggest that Mary’s spirit remains tethered to the location simply because she deeply loved the place and her life there during the pioneering days of the community.

#location
The Billy Miner Pub is located at 22330 Lougheed Highway in Maple Ridge, British Columbia.

    The White Lady of Thetis Cove

    Summary

    A mournful young woman in a flowing white dress haunts the rocky shore of Thetis Cove, endlessly gazing out to sea for a partner who never returned from a voyage. Local folklore and historical research tie her apparition to the historic Four Mile Pub and Portage Park area in View Royal.

    Details

    Local lore and paranormal accounts on Vancouver Island describe a spectral young woman with long, flowing hair and a wispy white gown who appears on the rocky shoreline of Thetis Cove and Portage Park. According to tradition, she is the spirit of a 19th-century woman—identified in local historical research as Margaret Montgomery, a visitor to the nearby historic Four Mile House—whose husband or fiancé perished at sea while she awaited his return. Staring vacantly across the water past Richards Island, the phantom reportedly does not interact with witnesses and fades into nothingness when approached, leaving behind an overwhelming sense of profound sorrow and loneliness. To honor the enduring legend, the nearby Four Mile Pub features a stained-glass window depicting the White Lady and serves a specialty drink named in her honor.

The Wandering Nuns of St. Ann's Academy

Summary

St. Ann's Academy is reported to be haunted by the spirits of nuns wandering the convent grounds and the ghost of famous Canadian painter Emily Carr.

Details

Operating as a 19th-century Catholic school and convent before closing in 1974, the historic site has since generated numerous ghost stories after the early graves on the property were relocated. Staff and visitors have reported seeing phantom nuns roaming the gardens and halls, as well as sightings of renowned artist Emily Carr.

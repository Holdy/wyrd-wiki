#title
The Haunting of Keeping Road

#summary
Keeping Road in Abbotsford is widely regarded as one of Canada's most haunted rural stretches, notorious for legends of a hanging apparition, phantom demon dogs, and inexplicable vehicle disturbances.

#details
Located near the base of Sumas Mountain, Keeping Road has accumulated a dark and prolific local lore over the years. Numerous motorists and ghost hunters have reported eerie phenomena along the deserted stretch after dark. Among the most enduring legends are a spectral little girl who emerges from the darkness to remove keys from the hoods of parked cars, and a phantom man hanging from a bank of trees who appears when drivers flash their headlights. Other terrifying encounters include sightings of a woman in white walking the road, a giant "demon dog" with glowing green eyes that chases vehicles, and mysterious audio distortions, vehicle malfunctions, and doors opening by themselves.

#location
Keeping Road, located near Sumas Mountain in Abbotsford, British Columbia.

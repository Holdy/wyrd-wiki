The Whispers and Shadows of Minnekhada Lodge

Summary

Caretakers and visitors exploring a historic 1930s timber lodge nestled in the woods have reported encountering shadowy figures on the stairs, disembodied humming, and unexplained whiffs of roses.

Details

Built in the 1930s by business mogul and future B.C. Lieutenant Governor Eric Hamber, Minnekhada Lodge stands in a remote park area with a history that includes a devastating 1929 farm fire. Over the decades, staff, caretakers, and paranormal investigators have reported lingering phenomena within its timber walls. A caretaker's relative once fled in terror after spotting a dark shadow figure standing atop the staircase when the building was entirely locked and empty. Later ghost-hunting groups conducting evening investigations reported hearing a woman softly humming a few bars of a tune, alongside sudden, inexplicable scents of roses filling rooms devoid of any flowers.

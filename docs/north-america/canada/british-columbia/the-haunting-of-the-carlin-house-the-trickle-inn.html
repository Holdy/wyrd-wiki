The Haunting of the Carlin House (The Trickle Inn)

Summary

The historic Carlin House along the Trans-Canada Highway is said to be haunted by a family grandmother and other lingering paranormal energies.

Details

Built in stages starting in the late 19th century and purchased by James Carlin in 1904, the grand white and green house served as a family home and later as an inn. Throughout its history as a residence and a boarding house, owners, visitors, and professional mediums reported feeling distinct paranormal presences. In particular, mediums identified one of the persistent entities as a grandmother from the original Carlin family who once inhabited the sprawling home.

The Spirits of Overlynn Mansion

Summary

The historic Overlynn Mansion in North Burnaby is reportedly haunted by phantom noises and apparitions stemming from its decades as a Catholic girls' boarding school.

Details

Constructed in 1912 as an opulent model estate in Vancouver Heights, Overlynn later operated for 40 years as Seton Academy, a boarding and day school run by the Sisters of Charity of Halifax. Paranormal investigators exploring the grand mansion have captured audio of a man coughing in empty rooms and doors slamming shut. Witnesses have also reported encountering a swinging arm and a little girl in a white dress who abruptly appears and vanishes, particularly near the heavy-locked attic where students once slept.

#title
The Spectres of Irving House

#summary
Built in 1865, Irving House in New Westminster is famously haunted by riverboat captain William Irving, who died in the home in 1872 and whose presence is marked by mysterious footsteps, pipe tobacco scents, and phantom indentations on his deathbed.

#details
Captain William Irving, known as "The King of the River," had Irving House built in 1865 in the Gothic Revival style using redwood shipped from California. After his death in the home in 1872, staff, visitors, and family members reported continuous paranormal occurrences. Betty Miller, mother of a former curator, felt the ghost of William Irving brush past her on the stairs whispering "I must make haste" on his birthday. Over the decades, caretakers and visitors have reported white feathers drifting down the stairs, cold spots, the distinct scent of pipe tobacco, and an unexplained body-sized indentation appearing on the antique mattress in John's room where the captain passed away.

#location
Irving House is located at 302 Royal Avenue in New Westminster, British Columbia.

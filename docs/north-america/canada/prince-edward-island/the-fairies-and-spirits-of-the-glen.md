#title
The Fairies and Spirits of the Glen

#summary
The winding forest passage known as the Glen near Souris is steeped in generations of folklore concerning woodland fairies, phantom encounters, and uncanny local legends.

#details
Located just northeast of Souris, the scenic forest road and farming hamlet known locally as "the Glen" (Glencorradale) has long held a reputation in Island folklore as a place where the veil between the ordinary world and the supernatural thins. For generations, local children and travelers shared accounts of watching for mischievous fairies among the massive old-growth elms. The area's eerie reputation was further reinforced by figures such as Nellie MacPhee, a lifelong resident of the Glen renowned for her reputed fortunetelling abilities, cementing the wooded road as a site of lingering mystery.

#location
Glen Road (Route 303) in the Glen / Glencorradale area northeast of Souris, Prince Edward Island.

#upstream-source
Frank Harold MacArthur, Legends of Prince Edward Island (1966).

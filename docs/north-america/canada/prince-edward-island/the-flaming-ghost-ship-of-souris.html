The Flaming Ghost Ship of Souris

Summary

A mysterious, three-masted schooner engulfed in roaring flames has been sighted for over two centuries cruising the Northumberland Strait off the coast of Souris.

Details

For more than 200 years, mariners and coastal residents looking out over the Northumberland Strait near Souris and Wood Islands have reported harrowing sightings of a phantom sailing vessel wrapped in fire. Witnesses consistently describe a full-rigged schooner whose sails, rigging, and hull burn brightly without ever being consumed. Whenever local fishermen or onlookers attempt to row out and rescue any supposed crew members, the spectral ship abruptly fades into the mist or sinks beneath the dark waves.

The Last-Runner Black Dog of Alberton

Summary

When an old house in Alberton was moved from the site where a mistreated boy had mysteriously disappeared, a phantom black dog suddenly bolted out from beneath the structure.

Details

According to local oral history and traditional folklore from the Alberton area, a severe resident named Mr. Brian had an adopted boy who vanished under suspicious circumstances, with neighbors strongly suspecting foul play and abuse. Years later, when the house standing on the property was hoisted onto wooden runners to be relocated, a mysterious black dog bolted out from the exact spot where the building had rested. According to local belief, the apparition served as a supernatural "last-runner" tied to the spirit of the mistreated and vanished boy, matching regional legends of haunting manifestations and unexplained lights shining across nearby fields.

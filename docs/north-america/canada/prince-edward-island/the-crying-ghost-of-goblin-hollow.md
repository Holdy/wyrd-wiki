#title
The Crying Ghost of Goblin Hollow

#summary
Following the tragic 1859 murder of Annie Beaton, a wooded ravine along the Montague River became haunted by an eerie silence and the sounds of a woman wailing.

#details
On May 12, 1859, 41-year-old Annie Beaton left a neighbor's home to walk the short mile back to her farm along Queens Road. She cut through a ravine near the Montague River, where she was brutally assaulted and murdered. Following her tragic death, the site was renamed Goblin Hollow. Ever since, visitors entering the hollow report experiencing an unnerving, sudden silence accompanied by the haunting sounds of a woman crying, screaming, and wailing in the dark.

#location
Goblin Hollow is located along the Montague River near Queens Road in Montague, Three Rivers, Prince Edward Island.

    The Poltergeist of Gallant's Clover Farm

    Summary

    Security cameras at a historic grocery store in Rustico captured unexplained paranormal activity, showing items sliding and flying off shelves in the middle of the night.

    Details

    Gallant's Clover Farm, a family-run store operating on Prince Edward Island's North Shore for decades, became the center of local ghost lore when owner Keith Gallant experienced bizarre disturbances. After locking up for the night, Gallant noticed on consecutive mornings that merchandise was consistently thrown or knocked onto the floor. When he checked the overnight security camera footage, he witnessed boxes of candy bars sliding off the counter and bread flying onto the floor at 3:00 a.m. with absolutely no one else in the building. While locals joked that former patrons and old characters from the store's long history had come back to browse the aisles, Gallant maintained that the baffling security footage was entirely genuine.

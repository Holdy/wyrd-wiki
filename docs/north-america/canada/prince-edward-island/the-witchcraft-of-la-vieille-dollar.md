#title
The Witchcraft of La Vieille Dollar

#summary
An Acadian woman settling in Tignish in the 1830s with a reputation for sorcery allegedly placed a fatal curse on a neighbour's baby, perishing herself the moment the infant's shirt was burned in an oven to break the spell.

#details
Marguerite Dollar, widely known as "Old Dollar," settled in Tignish with her family in the 1830s, carrying a dark family reputation for witchcraft inherited from her father in New Brunswick. Local folklore held that Marguerite could transform into animals and cast malevolent curses. When a young local man rejected a marriage proposal from her daughter, the man's newborn baby suddenly fell gravely ill with a mysterious sickness. Suspecting witchcraft, the desperate family placed the baby's shirt inside an oven to break the curse. As the cloth burned, Marguerite's husband rushed to their home to announce that she was suddenly dying with her skin covered in painful blisters, expiring just as the shirt was consumed by the fire.

#location
The historic settlement area of Tignish, Prince Edward Island, where Marguerite Dollar and her family resided in the 1830s.

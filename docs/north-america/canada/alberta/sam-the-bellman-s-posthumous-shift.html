Sam the Bellman's Posthumous Shift

Summary

A beloved head bellman who worked at the Banff Springs Hotel in the 1960s and 70s swore to return after death, and his helpful ghost is frequently encountered assisting guests and operating elevators.

Details

Sam McAuley served as the head bellman at the Fairmont Banff Springs Hotel during the 1960s and 1970s before passing away in 1975. Having loved his work and the hotel deeply, Sam reportedly vowed to return to his post post-mortem. Over the decades, staff and guests have reported encounters with an elderly Scottish bellman in an antiquated uniform matching Sam's description. Sightings include him unlocking doors for confused guests, haunting his former mezzanine office (now converted into a guest room), and causing elevator doors to open and close mysteriously on various upper floors.

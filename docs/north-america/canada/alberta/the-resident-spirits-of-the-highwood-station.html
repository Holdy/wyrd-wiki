The Resident Spirits of the Highwood Station

Summary

Staff at the historic Museum of the Highwood railway station frequently encounter mysterious phenomena, including self-locking doors, disembodied footsteps, and lights turning on by themselves.

Details

Housed in an old Canadian Pacific Railway station where the bodies of victims of train accidents were once temporarily stored on the north end, the museum has long been considered a paranormal hotspot. Employees have reported doors locking and unlocking on their own, inanimate objects shifting overnight, and shadows walking past hallways. Paranormal investigators detecting abnormal energy fields have noted the presence of lingering spirits within the historic building, affectionately referenced by some staff as a resident ghost named George.

#title
The Crying Ghost of the Bowman Building

#summary
Employees and visitors in Lethbridge's historic Bowman Building have repeatedly heard the distressing sound of a young girl crying from the women's restroom, though no one is ever found inside.

#details
Local ghost lore holds that a young Chinese girl wearing traditional clothing entered the ladies' washroom of the Bowman Building, where she was tragically mistaken for a boy and beaten. Her spirit is said to remain trapped within the building, continuously weeping and crying out from the women's restroom. Whenever concerned staff or visitors investigate the noises to offer help, they find the room completely empty.

#location
The historic Bowman Building located in downtown Lethbridge, Alberta.

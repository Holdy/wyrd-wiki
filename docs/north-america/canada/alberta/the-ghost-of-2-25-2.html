The Ghost of 2-25

Summary

Oilfield workers at an isolated river valley battery near Wainwright reported eerie encounters, including blood-curdling screams and mysteriously rearranged tools, rooted in a dark 1930s homestead legend.

Details

In the Giltedge river valley field situated between Vermilion and Wainwright, a remote satellite oilfield facility known as the 2-25 battery became infamous among workers for unexplained paranormal occurrences. Local lore claimed that in the 1930s, a homesteader in the valley suffered a mental breakdown and murdered his entire family. Decades later, operators working the night shift reported chilling experiences. One worker on a solo call at 1:00 AM heard a close, blood-curdling woman's scream right in his ear, prompting him to flee the site immediately. Other workers frequently returned to find locked heavy doors wide open despite no wind in the valley. In another instance, a technician walked to his truck for a socket set, stepped inside a building for two minutes, and walked back out to find the entire socket set neatly arranged on the ground while its plastic case remained untouched on the tailgate. The phenomena were so unsettling that several employees refused to perform call-outs at the battery alone.

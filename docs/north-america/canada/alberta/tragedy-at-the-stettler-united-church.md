#title
Tragedy at the Stettler United Church

#summary
The Stettler United Church is a poignant focal point of local ghost and history walks, tied to the lingering memories of devout parishioners Ken and Betty Welty who were brutally murdered in 1975.

#details
During Stettler's annual Historic Ghost Walk, participants gather near the downtown park and stop at the historic Stettler United Church to reflect on the dark and tragic events that have marked the region's past. Among the prominent historical tragedies recounted on the tour is the 1975 murder of Ken and Betty Welty, beloved members of the church congregation who were killed in their home north of town. Local guides share how the community's heavy history and the memory of such violent crimes continue to cast a shadow over Stettler's enduring institutions and places of worship.

#location
The Stettler United Church is located at 4804 49 Street in Stettler, Alberta.

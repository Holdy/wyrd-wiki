#title
Racing Specters at the Edmonton International Raceway

#summary
Beyond the roar of engines, paranormal investigators have detected lingering spectral energy and unexplained phenomena at the Edmonton International Raceway in Wetaskiwin.

#details
While cataloging paranormal hotspots throughout the region, researchers investigated the Edmonton International Raceway just outside Wetaskiwin. Investigators documented anomalous audio recordings, shadow disturbances, and unexplained energy spikes, pointing to residual spiritual activity connected to the grounds and the local racing community.

#location
The Edmonton International Raceway is located in the County of Wetaskiwin, Alberta.

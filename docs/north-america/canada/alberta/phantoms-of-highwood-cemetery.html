Phantoms of Highwood Cemetery

Summary

Visitors to Highwood Cemetery have frequently captured inexplicable white lights and glowing orbs hovering over headstones in photographs.

Details

Serving as the final resting place for generations of High River residents, Highwood Cemetery has long been a focal point for ghostly encounters and paranormal interest. Annual haunted history tours and visitors wandering the grounds have consistently reported an eerie atmosphere, with inexplicable white lights and luminous orbs manifesting above people's heads in photographs taken among the graves.

The Phantom of Centennial Park

Summary

The ghost of a teenage girl is said to haunt Centennial Park every year on October 13, leaving investigators with unexplained encounters of whistling and glowing eyes.

Details

Local urban legend maintains that the spirit of a teenage girl who was assaulted in Centennial Park during the 1970s returns to the grounds annually on October 13. Ghost investigators exploring the park on this anniversary have reported strange paranormal phenomena, including picking up mysterious whistling sounds on parabolic microphones and encountering unsettling glowing eyes in the dark.

The Lady in White at Al's News Basement

Summary

Employees working in the basement of Al's News in downtown Grande Prairie have reported seeing a spectral woman dressed in a long white gown.

Details

Housed in one of the oldest buildings in downtown Grande Prairie, Al's News has long been associated with paranormal activity in its basement. Multiple past employees have witnessed the figure of a lady wearing a long white dress lurking downstairs. Paranormal investigators have set up equipment in the basement, and staff frequently experience unexplained occurrences such as items falling off shelves and floorboards creaking when the store is empty.

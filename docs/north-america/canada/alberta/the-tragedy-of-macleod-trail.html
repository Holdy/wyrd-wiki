The Tragedy of Macleod Trail

Summary

In 1912, High River witnessed its first bloody domestic murder on Macleod Trail, an event preserved in local dark history and folklore.

Details

Following weeks of intense domestic disputes and disagreements regarding property and estate rights, Robina Bertrand sought police assistance against her husband, Caliste Bertrand. In broad daylight on Macleod Trail just outside her home, an enraged Caliste shot Robina point-blank in the face, resulting in High River's first recorded murder. Local history and haunting tours continue to recount the sordid event as part of the town's darker historical lore.

The Execution of Marie Courtorielle

Summary

In 1886 near the mouth of the Lesser Slave River, a Cree woman convinced she was transforming into a windigo spirit was killed and cremated by her family.

Details

In 1886, near the mouth of the Lesser Slave River, Marie Courtorielle became convinced she was turning into a wihtikow (windigo), a legendary cannibalistic spirit. After her husband Michel and son Cecil spent 20 days trying unsuccessfully to cure her, she escaped into the wilderness. Believing she posed an imminent threat of violence and cannibalism, they tracked her down, killed her, burned her body, and buried the remains before willingly surrendering to the authorities for investigation.

#title
The Spirits at the Stettler Hotel

#summary
Following multiple devastating fires and its grim use as a temporary mortuary during the 1959 Cook family murders, the 1948 Stettler Hotel has become the center of local ghost lore involving spectral children and unexplained phenomena.

#details
The Stettler Hotel stands on Main Street in downtown Stettler on the exact grounds where two earlier hotels burned down in 1908 and 1942. Following the horrific mass murder of the Cook family in June 1959, local funeral facilities were completely overwhelmed, prompting authorities to temporarily store some of the victims' bodies in the hotel's walk-in cooler before preparation. In the decades since, guests, staff, and paranormal investigators have reported anomalous activity throughout the building, including unexplained electronic disturbances, glowing orbs in the basement, and sightings or sounds of phantom children.

#location
The Stettler Hotel is located at 5006 50th Street in downtown Stettler, Alberta.

#title
The Grey Lady of Dunvegan Bridge

#summary
In November 1960, two men driving across the newly completed Dunvegan Bridge encountered a hooded female apparition walking through a snowstorm who vanished, leaving behind no footprints.

#details
In November 1960, shortly after the completion of the Dunvegan suspension bridge south of Fairview, Alberta, two men driving through the snowy valley encountered a woman leaning into the swirling snow with her head bent beneath a hooded cloak, her bare feet exposed to the cold. When they pulled over and one man walked back following their fresh tire tracks to check on her, the bridge was completely empty except for his own footprints. Subsequent visits by the witnesses and their friends led to another sighting higher up a hill where she appeared to be standing knee-high in the snow picking berries. Over time, witnesses variously described her as wearing a cloak or a nun's habit, cementing her legend as the Grey Lady of Dunvegan.

#location
Dunvegan Suspension Bridge crossing the Peace River on Highway 2, approximately 20 minutes south of Fairview, Alberta.

The Phantom Nun of the Old Castor Hospital

Summary

Staff and visitors at the historic 1911 hospital in Castor report chilling encounters, including the apparition of a nun on the stairs, unexplained cold spots, and poltergeist activity on the untouched third floor.

Details

Built in 1911 by the French-speaking Daughters of Wisdom, Our Lady of the Rosary Hospital served as a regional medical hub, convent, and boarding school. The third floor, where the nuns once slept in small wooden cubicles, has remained largely untouched over the decades. Today, staff and visitors frequently report eerie occurrences: motion sensors triggering with no one present, clocks mysteriously turning backward, phantom names being called down hallways, items vanishing from lower floors only to reappear on the third floor, and the unsettling sensation of bedsheets being violently yanked away from readers at night. Most notably, witnesses have reported seeing the spectral apparition of a nun walking the stairwells.

The Apparition of the Red Brick School

Summary

Staff and visitors at the historic 1925 Red Brick School in Stony Plain have frequently reported encountering a ghostly woman in a 1950s print dress, alongside unexplained poltergeist activity and phantom shoves.

Details

Built in 1925 and later saved from demolition to become the anchor of the town's heritage site, the Red Brick School has accumulated numerous firsthand accounts of paranormal activity. Staff working late at night often experience a sudden, overwhelming feeling that they have overstayed their welcome and must leave immediately. Museum managers have reported seeing out of the corner of their eyes an apparition of a woman dressed in a 1950s-era print dress standing near office doorways. During evening tours and exhibition setups, paranormal phenomena have escalated: computers and audio equipment have started running on their own, unexplainable knocking echoes through empty rooms, and staff members have reported feeling sudden, physical shoves while on ladders.

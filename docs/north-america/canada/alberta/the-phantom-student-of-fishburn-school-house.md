#title
The Phantom Student of Fishburn School House

#summary
Apparitions of a child and unexplained childlike voices have been encountered inside the historic Fishburn School House.

#details
Built in 1897 as the first rural one-room schoolhouse in the area, the Fishburn School served as a community and educational hub before closing in the 1960s. In 2022, a summer student closing up the museum buildings opened the door to the schoolhouse to find a young boy in strange clothing sitting very still at a desk staring at the blackboard; when the door opened wider, the boy jumped up, ran to the window, and vanished while giggling. Later, during a 2025 ghost-hunting event, an archivist and her friends felt an overwhelming presence inside the schoolhouse, while spirit box recordings captured childish voices angrily telling them to get out.

#location
The Fishburn School House at the Kootenai Brown Historical Park at 1037 Bev McLachlin Drive in Pincher Creek, Alberta.

The Spirits of Oppertshauser House

Summary

Visitors and staff touring the historic Oppertshauser House in Stony Plain have reported inexplicable basement noises and lingering presences tied to the tragic early history of the property.

Details

Situated on the heritage grounds in Stony Plain, the Oppertshauser House is one of the area's prominent historic structures. Local lore and tour accounts associate the building with tragic chapters from the region's pioneering days, including sickness and quarantine periods. During evening ghost tours, visitors and guides have repeatedly heard unexplained knocking, heavy footsteps, and mysterious sounds echoing from the cellar and basement levels when the building is otherwise empty. Researchers and paranormal investigators note that the house remains an active energetic hotspot with frequent auditory anomalies.

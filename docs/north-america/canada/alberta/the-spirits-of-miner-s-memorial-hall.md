#title
The Spirits of Miner's Memorial Hall

#summary
Serving as a makeshift morgue during Drumheller's dangerous coal-mining era, Miner's Memorial Hall in downtown Drumheller remains a focal point for ghostly encounters and unexplained sounds.

#details
During the peak of Drumheller's coal-mining boom in the early 20th century, Miner's Memorial Hall played a sombre role as a makeshift morgue for miners who lost their lives in the treacherous local underground mines. Over the decades, locals and visitors traversing downtown Drumheller have reported unexplained phantom noises, cold drafts, and shadowy figures lingering within and around the hall. As one of the anchor points on Drumheller's historic downtown ghost walks, the building carries the sorrow and memories of the miners who never returned home.

#location
Miner's Memorial Hall in downtown Drumheller, Alberta.

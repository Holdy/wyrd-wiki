#title
The Specters of the Alberta Hospital for the Insane

#summary
The historic asylum in Ponoka, plagued by a dark history of harsh psychiatric treatments and forced sterilizations, is widely reputed by locals to be haunted by the troubled spirits of past patients.

#details
Opened in 1912 on the rural outskirts of Ponoka as Alberta's first mental health institution, the hospital housed hundreds of patients under difficult and often grim conditions. Over the decades, generations of local residents and former staff members have passed down chilling accounts of paranormal activity on the grounds, attributing eerie noises, disembodied cries, and unexplained apparitions to the profound suffering and tragic experiences of those who lived and died within its walls.

#location
The historic grounds of the former Alberta Hospital for the Insane (now the Centennial Centre for Mental Health & Brain Injury) located on the southeast outskirts of Ponoka, Alberta.

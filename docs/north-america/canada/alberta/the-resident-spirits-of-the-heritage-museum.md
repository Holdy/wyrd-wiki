#title
The Resident Spirits of the Heritage Museum

#summary
Paranormal investigations at the historic Wetaskiwin and District Heritage Museum have revealed over a dozen lingering spirits who continue to interact with staff and visitors.

#details
Located in the historic Star Store building in downtown Wetaskiwin, the museum has been the focus of extensive research by local paranormal investigators. Researchers and staff have documented thirteen distinct entities inhabiting the building. These spirits—representing early residents and builders of the community—frequently make their presence known through electronic voice phenomena, temperature fluctuations, and friendly interactions during public ghost tours.

#location
The Wetaskiwin District Heritage Museum Centre is located at 5035 50 Avenue in downtown Wetaskiwin, Alberta.

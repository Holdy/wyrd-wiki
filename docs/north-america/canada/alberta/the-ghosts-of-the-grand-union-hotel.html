The Ghosts of the Grand Union Hotel

Summary

For decades, staff, guests, and owners of Athabasca's historic Grand Union Hotel reported eerie occurrences, including doors slamming on their own, keys vanishing from locks, and strange noises echoing through the underground Buffalo Room.

Details

Standing at the busy intersection of Highways 2 and 55 in Athabasca, the Grand Union Hotel was a century-old social landmark before its destruction by a major fire in 2024. Over its long history, generations of visitors and employees shared accounts of supernatural phenomena within its walls. Most notably, strange noises and unexplained occurrences were frequently reported late at night in the building's basement, known as the Buffalo Room. Staff and patrons also told tales of a mischievous spirit that inhabited the old hotel, prone to rearranging items, slamming doors shut, and mysteriously slipping keys right out of their locks.

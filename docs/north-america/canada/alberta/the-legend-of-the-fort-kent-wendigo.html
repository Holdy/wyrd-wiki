The Legend of the Fort Kent Wendigo

Summary

Local folklore in the rural area surrounding Bonnyville tells of a tall, dark, glowing-eyed creature that haunts the local woods, terrifying residents and ranchers with eerie wails and aggressive encounters.

Details

Dating back decades in local oral tradition within the Bonnyville region, the legend of the Wendigo in Fort Kent describes a terrifying cryptid haunting the woods. Area ranchers and locals have reported hearing unearthly moans, howls, and wails echoing from the dense forest at night.

One prominent local account tells of a rancher who armed himself with a rifle to protect his horses from an attacker outside his home, only to be confronted by a massive, dark, shambling shape with glowing red eyes. When the rancher fired a shot at the entity, it unconcernedly retreated back into the timberline. Other versions of the regional lore tie the creature's presence to local tragedies and isolation in rural northern Alberta.

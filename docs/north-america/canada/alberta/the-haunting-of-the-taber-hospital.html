The Haunting of the Taber Hospital

Summary

Following its closure, the abandoned Taber Hospital and its adjacent nurses' residence became the site of spectral lights, phantom sounds, and a lingering spirit named Maynard who reportedly followed staff to the new facility.

Details

When the original medical facility in Taber closed its doors, urban explorers and former staff began reporting eerie paranormal activity. Nurses staying in the adjacent residence frequently watched an unexplained light turn on and off in the empty attic. Staff also reported a lingering presence they named "Maynard," who supposedly transferred with them when the hospital moved to its new location. Additionally, long-term care residents famously spoke of visits from a spectral figure on nights when a patient would unexpectedly pass away.

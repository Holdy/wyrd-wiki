#title
The Haunting of Buck Hill

#summary
During the Great Depression, a heartbroken father lost his young daughter in the woods near a lonely Ottawa Valley hill and now haunts the roadway. Generations of visitors test their nerve by performing a nighttime ritual to summon his spirit.

#details
According to local folklore stemming from the Great Depression, a man and his young daughter lived at the bottom of Buck Hill near Round Lake. When the family dog slipped out one day, the little girl ran after it into the dense surrounding woods and became permanently lost despite her father's desperate searches. Following his passing, locals believe the grieving father's spirit remained bound to the area, endlessly searching for his child. Today, visitors drive to the isolated road at night, turn their headlights on and off three times, and shout "Daddy, Daddy, Daddy!" into the darkness to evoke phantom lights, revving engine sounds, and other paranormal phenomena.

#location
Buck Hill Road is located near Round Lake within the township of Killaloe-Hagarty-Richards in the Ottawa Valley, Ontario.

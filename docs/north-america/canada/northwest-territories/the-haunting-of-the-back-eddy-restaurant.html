    The Haunting of the Back Eddy Restaurant

    Summary

    The former owner and visitors of Hay River's Back Eddy restaurant experienced numerous unexplained supernatural occurrences, including phantom crashes, floating shadows, mysterious indoor clouds, and a ghost resembling the "Michelin Man."

    Details

    For years, Linda Duford, the former owner of the Back Eddy restaurant in Hay River, experienced a series of unnerving and inexplicable events within the building. Her encounters began shortly after her family purchased the establishment in 2000, when a loud crash behind the swinging doors knocked her to the ground with fright. In 2004, Duford and her parents witnessed a spectral figure pass through the dining area, believed to be the spirit of a former owner who had worked in the building for half a century prior to his death. Another recurring apparition was a bulky figure dubbed the "Michelin Man," wearing a large puffy jacket echoing a man from Fort Resolution who had recently passed away. This entity seemingly manifested near the entrance and caused light bulbs to strobe erratically over female patrons who were relatives of the deceased man. Other baffling phenomena documented at the restaurant included the shadow of a tall cowboy floating above the floor with no visible feet, and anomalous patches of "clouds" visible in the mirrors when the dining room itself was clear.

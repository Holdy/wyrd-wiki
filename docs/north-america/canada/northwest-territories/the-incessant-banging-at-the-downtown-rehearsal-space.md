#title
The Incessant Banging at the Downtown Rehearsal Space

#summary
Artists using a mentorship rehearsal space in downtown Yellowknife reported a recurring phantom visitor that relentlessly pounded on the emergency exit door every evening.

#details
During late-night practice and composing sessions at the Northern Arts and Cultural Centre's mentorship rehearsal space located inside the downtown mall, musicians and composers like Carmen Braden and Greyson Gritt frequently heard aggressive, minutes-long banging on the fire exit door starting around 8:15 p.m. Despite the alarming and relentless pounding that sounded like someone sitting directly outside the door, opening it was strictly discouraged by previous occupants due to rumors that the space was haunted. The eerie acoustic ambience of the decaying, cavernous dead mall heightened the sense that unseen presences were lingering just out of sight.

#location
The Northern Arts and Cultural Centre mentorship rehearsal space located inside the downtown mall in Yellowknife, Northwest Territories.

#title
The Ghost Bride of Milner Ridge

#summary
A driver on PR 214 picks up a silent young woman in a wedding gown, only to watch her mysteriously vanish from the back seat while driving through Milner Ridge.

#details
Decades ago on an October night, a motorist driving along Milner Ridge Road (PR 214) between Lac du Bonnet and Seddon's Corner encountered a lone woman walking in the pitch-black cold. Dressed entirely in a white wedding gown, she appeared stranded and needed help. The driver pulled over and offered her a ride; the woman silently got into the back seat without speaking a word, her hair hiding her face. As the car traveled along the dark highway, the driver repeatedly asked her questions and received no response, noticing only a blank stare in the rearview mirror. Deciding to take her to the RCMP office in Beausejour for safety, the driver looked back again to announce the plan—only to find the back seat completely empty. Local legend attributes the ghostly apparition to a tragic bride who perished on her wedding day in pioneer times and wanders the highway seeking her lost fiancé.

#location
PR 214 (Milner Ridge Road) between Lac du Bonnet and Seddon's Corner, Manitoba.

#title
The Specters of Daly House

#summary
Built in 1882 for Brandon's first mayor, the historic Daly House Museum is reputed to be haunted by a pacing former resident and the spirits of children playing on the stairs.

#details
Constructed in 1882 for Thomas Mayne Daly, who served as Brandon's first mayor, Daly House has accumulated numerous ghostly legends over the decades. Paranormal investigators and visitors have reported feeling the presence of a worried man with a high forehead and mustache pacing in the den, as well as hearing the sounds of young boys running up and down the stairs. During investigations by local paranormal groups like Shadows of Manitoba and television production teams, observers have noted anomalous phenomena such as floating blue orbs in the guest room and unexplained sounds throughout the Victorian-era mansion.

#location
Daly House Museum, located at 122 18th Street in Brandon, Manitoba.

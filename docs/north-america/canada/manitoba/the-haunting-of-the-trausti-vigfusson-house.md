#title
The Haunting of the Trausti Vigfússon House

#summary
The historic Trausti Vigfússon house at the Arborg & District Multicultural Heritage Village is said to be haunted by a small elderly woman and a partial phantom climbing the stairs.

#details
Originally built in 1898 and later relocated to the Arborg & District Multicultural Heritage Village along the Icelandic River, the historic log house carries a reputation for unexplained paranormal activity. Local residents and preservationists have reported encountering spectral figures within the building, most notably a small old woman who inexplicably appears and vanishes at the top of the stairs, as well as an unusual apparition described as "half a man" climbing the steps.

#location
The Trausti Vigfússon house at the Arborg & District Multicultural Heritage Village on the southeast end of Arborg, Manitoba.

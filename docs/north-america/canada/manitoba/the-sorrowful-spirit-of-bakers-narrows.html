The Sorrowful Spirit of Bakers Narrows

Summary

Bakers Narrows is haunted by the apparition of a grief-stricken woman who tragically ended her life along the water's rocky banks.

Details

Situated north of Flin Flon amid the rugged and scenic waterways of northern Manitoba, Bakers Narrows is known not only for its natural beauty but also for a dark local legend. Oral tradition maintains that a deeply heartbroken woman, overwhelmed by grief and despair, took her own life at the water's edge. In the years following, campers and travelers navigating the narrows have reported hearing inexplicable crying or sighs carried on the wind, along with fleeting sightings of a sorrowful female figure standing near the rocky shoreline at dusk.

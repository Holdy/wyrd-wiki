The Haunted Wartime House of Minnedosa

Summary

A family residing in a cramped wartime house in Minnedosa experienced relentless nocturnal terrors, including phantom soldiers sliding across curtains, disembodied apparitions, and poltergeist activity.

Details

Growing up in a small wartime house in Minnedosa, Manitoba, residents endured persistent and frightening paranormal phenomena. Bedtime frequently brought terrifying encounters, such as phantom soldiers wearing contour hats sliding across window curtains, a gray decapitated head appearing on the old television screen, and a mysterious wind-tunnel roar echoing in a solitary bedroom. Additional activity included doors unlatching on their own, echoing laughter from above, and phantom paper-thin frogs manifesting on the rug while a translucent child hefted a ghostly brick.

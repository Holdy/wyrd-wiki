#title
The Spirits of Mallard Lodge

#summary
The historic Mallard Lodge on the shores of Lake Manitoba near Portage la Prairie is said to be haunted by a friendly caretaker and the tragic spirit of a worker sealed into its foundation.

#details
Situated at the Delta Marsh near Portage la Prairie, Mallard Lodge was constructed in 1932 as part of a research and environmental facility. According to local lore and accounts from former staff and students, the lodge experiences frequent supernatural phenomena. A benevolent spirit known as Murray—believed to be a former caretaker—causes doors and windows to open and close, lights to flicker, and chains to rattle. More ominously, legend states that during the lodge's construction, a worker accidentally fell into the foundation and was sealed inside. Visitors have reported terrifying encounters with a hooded skeleton form haunting the building's structure.

#location
Mallard Lodge at Delta Marsh, near Portage la Prairie, Manitoba.

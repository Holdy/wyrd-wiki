#title
The Phantom Cigar Smoke of the Enterprise Bulletin Building

#summary
The former Enterprise Bulletin newspaper building in Jordan Village is haunted by phantom footsteps and the distinct, unidentifiable scent of cigar smoke lingering in empty rooms.

#details
Long before modern commerce settled into the historic commercial blocks of Jordan Village, local print culture played a vital role in community life. The former Enterprise Bulletin newspaper building has long been the subject of local ghost lore, with multiple witnesses reporting eerie after-hours phenomena. Staff and visitors have frequently noted the unmistakable, heavy aroma of cigar smoke drifting through completely vacant rooms, alongside rhythmic footsteps pacing across the creaking wooden floorboards upstairs—phenomena locals attribute to the ghost of a former editor or publisher who spent decades working late nights in the press office.

#location
The former Enterprise Bulletin newspaper building is situated on Main Street in Jordan Village, Town of Lincoln, Ontario.

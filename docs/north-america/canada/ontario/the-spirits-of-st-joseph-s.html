    The Spirits of St. Joseph's

    Summary

    The crumbling ruins of the former St. Joseph's School for Girls are regarded as a haunted site where the sorrowful spirits of children who passed away within the institution linger. Survivors and visitors report an enduring presence of restless energy remaining on the abandoned grounds.

    Details

    Operating from 1913 to 1962, St. Joseph's School for Girls in Spanish, Ontario, was part of the largest residential school complex in the province. Decades after its closure and abandonment, the decaying ruins off Garnier Road remain a profound focal point of grief and remembrance. Former students have recounted that the spirits of the children who lived, suffered, and died there remain trapped at the site, needing release. Today, visitors and survivors continue to visit the sacred ruins, leaving behind memorials, shoes, and toys in acknowledgement of the heavy, lingering atmosphere.

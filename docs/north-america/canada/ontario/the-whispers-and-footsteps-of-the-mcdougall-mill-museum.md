#title
The Whispers and Footsteps of the McDougall Mill Museum

#summary
Visitors and paranormal investigators at the historic stone mill in Renfrew have frequently reported unexplained footsteps on the upper floor, the sound of children playing, and the apparition of a mysterious woman.

#details
Built in the 19th century along the Bonnechere River in Renfrew, Ontario, the McDougall Mill Museum preserves local pioneer history and heritage. Over the years, caretakers, visitors, and members of paranormal research groups have documented various eerie phenomena inside the building. People touring the museum have reported hearing heavy footsteps echoing on the empty wooden floorboards upstairs, as well as the faint, playful sounds of children when no children are present. During formal investigations by groups like the Canadian Haunting and Paranormal Society, investigators have glimpsed the shadowy figure of a woman and heard sudden, unexplained loud bangs near their recording equipment.

#location
McDougall Mill Museum, located in O'Brien Park along the Bonnechere River in Renfrew, Ontario.

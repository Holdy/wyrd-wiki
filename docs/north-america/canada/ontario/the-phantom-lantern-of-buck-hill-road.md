#title
The Phantom Lantern of Buck Hill Road

#summary
Travelers who drive up a lonely dirt road near Round Lake in Renfrew County and perform a nighttime ritual often report seeing a mysterious glowing lantern light and hearing ghostly cries.

#details
Local legend holds that during the Depression, a logging family lived near the base of Buck Hill. When their young daughter chased the family dog into a severe snowstorm and vanished, her grieving father searched for her endlessly with a lantern until his death. To this day, motorists visiting Buck Hill Road at night who flash their headlights three times and call out into the darkness report seeing a baseball-sized orb of amber, green, or white light swaying among the trees, mimicking someone walking with a lantern. Visitors have also reported mysterious handprints appearing on their car windows and hearing disembodied screams echoing through the forest.

#location
Buck Hill Road, located near Round Lake Centre in the Township of Killaloe-Hagarty-Richards, Renfrew County, Ontario.

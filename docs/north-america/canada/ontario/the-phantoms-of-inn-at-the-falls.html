The Phantoms of Inn at the Falls

Summary

Overlooking the rocky waterfalls of Bracebridge, the historic Inn at the Falls is reputedly haunted by former residents including Judge William Mahaffy, a tragic pregnant woman known as Mrs. Kirk, and a mischievous entity named Bob. Guests and staff frequently report unexplained cigar smoke, disembodied footsteps, and shadowy apparitions pacing the halls.

Details

Originally built in the 1870s and later owned by district judge William C. Mahaffy until his death in 1911, the building now operates as a boutique inn and has accumulated numerous ghost legends. Apparitions of Judge Mahaffy are said to wander the lower lounge accompanied by the phantom scent of cigar smoke. Another frequent specter is Mrs. Kirk, a pregnant woman who tragically fell down the stairs in the 1930s; her grieving figure is seen gazing out second-floor windows and her soft crying echoes down the hallways. Additionally, staff report a mischievous kitchen spirit named Bob who throws pots and pans, alongside a spectral child and a Lady of the Stairs.

#title
The Spectral Hostess of Emma's Back Porch

#summary
The historic 19th-century building in Burlington that once housed the famous Estaminet and Emma's Back Porch restaurants is said to be haunted by its former owner and mysterious phantom children who manifest through chilling basement noises and apparitions.

#details
Originally built in the 1800s and later run by Emma and George Byrens starting in 1919, the establishment hosted numerous dignitaries, politicians, and celebrities. Following its decades as a beloved restaurant, staff and managers reported intense supernatural activity, particularly in the basement—affectionately dubbed "The Dungeon"—where dishwashers encountered a silent little boy sitting on the floor whose form could be passed right through. Employees also frequently heard disembodied sounds of clinking glasses, heavy pounding on the walls, and ghostly figures along the historic staircases.

#location
2084 Old Lakeshore Road, Burlington, Ontario.

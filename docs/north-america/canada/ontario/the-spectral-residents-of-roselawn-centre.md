#title
The Spectral Residents of Roselawn Centre

#summary
Built in 1860, the historic Roselawn Centre in Port Colborne is reported to be haunted by spectral figures wandering its staircases and peering from its upper windows.

#details
Dating back to the 1860s, the grand Second Empire-style Roselawn Centre stands as a prominent historic landmark on Fielden Avenue. Former employees, visitors, and paranormal investigators have consistently recounted chilling encounters with unseen entities and spectral figures. Apparitions are said to manifest on the building's staircases and gaze out from the lonely window at the very top of the three-storey structure, cementing its reputation as one of the region's most active haunted locations.

#location
The Roselawn Centre is located on Fielden Avenue in Port Colborne, Ontario.

The 1830s Time Capsule of Ireland House

Summary

Burlington's oldest surviving home, built in the 1830s by English immigrant Joseph Ireland, serves as a hotbed of paranormal activity and ghostly encounters tied to nearly 150 years of family history.

Details

Completed in 1837 by farmer Joseph Ireland along the Guelph Line, Ireland House remained occupied by generations of the same family until 1985, leaving behind an untouched time capsule of Victorian furniture, diaries, and personal artifacts—including a traditional human hair wreath. Operating today as a historic museum, the house is frequently investigated by paranormal groups. Visitors and staff report sudden shifts in atmosphere, unexplained cold spots, and distinct presences echoing the daily lives of the Ireland family who lived and loved there for over a century.

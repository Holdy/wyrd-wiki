#title
The Legend of Maria Baldwin's Cursed Grave

#summary
At one of St. Thomas's oldest churchyards, the gravestone of a young mother turned pitch black, sparking enduring local legends that she was a witch whose tomb caught fire on Halloween.

#details
Located in the historic cemetery of the Old St. Thomas Church in St. Thomas, Ontario, the grave of Maria Baldwin has inspired eerie local folklore for decades. Maria tragically died in 1863 at the age of 22 from complications during childbirth, and her infant child passed away nine months later to be buried beside her. Beginning in the 1970s, her gravestone inexplicably turned a stark, pitch black. This discoloration prompted widespread local rumors that Maria was a witch, that her tomb would spontaneously catch fire on Halloween night, and that anyone who dared cross the four corner posts and touch her stone would be cursed. In truth, historians explained that the stone darkened because it was made of a highly porous material that reacted over time to acid rain and soot from nearby railways.

#location
The cemetery at the Old St. Thomas Church, located near the ravine in St. Thomas, Ontario.

The Forgotten Graves of A.R. Goudie

Summary

The lawn outside the A.R. Goudie facility serves as an unmarked 19th-century graveyard where over 260 unclaimed residents were buried, leaving an unsettling atmosphere for nighttime visitors.

Details

Local historical records and accounts indicate that the field in front of what is now the A.R. Goudie Eventide Home contains an unmarked cemetery where more than 260 individuals were buried between 1869 and 1907. These people had lived in a 19th-century house of refuge providing shelter for the homeless and destitute, and those who passed away unclaimed by family were interred directly on the front lawn. Visitors walking past the site after dark report an eerie, oppressive sensation of being watched and an overwhelming curiosity about the forgotten souls resting beneath the grass.

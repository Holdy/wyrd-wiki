#title
The Man in the Balcony

#summary
Staff and patrons at the historic Avon Theatre have repeatedly reported seeing a glowing, solitary figure seated in the empty balcony who vanishes when approached.

#details
Opened in 1901 as a vaudeville house called Theatre Albert before becoming the Avon Theatre in the 1940s, the building is said to harbor lingering spirits from its theatrical past. Witnesses have observed a pale face glowing in the darkness of the empty upper balcony, offering a blank stare before slowly dissolving into nothingness when staff walk up to investigate. 

#location
The Avon Theatre is located at 99 Downie Street in downtown Stratford, Ontario.

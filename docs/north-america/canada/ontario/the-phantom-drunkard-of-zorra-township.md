#title
The Phantom Drunkard of Zorra Township

#summary
For twenty years after breaking his neck in a drunken wagon accident, the ghost of Robert Gourley haunted a rural side road near Woodstock, lamenting his thirst in Gaelic.

#details
Robert Gourley and his brothers were notorious for hard drinking, though they made a weekly habit of driving eight kilometers to attend church services in nearby Embro. One Sunday afternoon in the late summer, after finishing church, the brothers began drinking heavily on their return journey. When the wagon hit a bump on a dusty Zorra Township path, Robert was thrown from his perch and slammed headfirst into a tree root, breaking his neck. He was found the next day still clutching his broken whisky bottle. For two decades, travellers along that wooded side road reported encountering a stout, shadowy figure with glowing red eyes who groaned and called out for "deoch"—the Gaelic word for drink—until a church elder finally confronted the spirit and heard its last confession.

#location
A rural side road in Zorra Township near Embro, just outside Woodstock, Ontario.

The Invisible Patrons of Annandale Golf and Curling Club

Summary

Staff at the historic Annandale Golf and Curling Club in Ajax have frequently reported doors slamming, objects moving on their own, and unexplained ghostly apparitions.

Details

Originally opening in 1902 as the Duffin's Creek Golf and Country Club before expanding to include a curling arena in 1963, the club has accumulated a reputation for paranormal activity over the decades. Current and former staff members have reported unexplainable occurrences such as sudden door slams, objects shifting unassisted, and the distinct presence of unseen patrons lingering throughout the clubhouse and surrounding property.

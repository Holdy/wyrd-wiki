#title
The Vanishing Hitchhiker of Waterloo Region

#summary
Motorists driving along roads in the Waterloo Region have reported picking up an elderly, bearded hitchhiker who inexplicably vanishes from the back seat while the vehicle is in motion.

#details
Dating back to documented accounts published in the 1930s, local motorists have shared encounters with a mysterious elderly man sporting a patriarchal beard. Drivers picking him up along regional roads note that he briefly converses—mentioning economic times and forecasting when the Great Depression would end—before abruptly disappearing from the locked rear seat while the car travels at highway speeds. The tale has become a persistent piece of roadside folklore across the Waterloo area.

#location
Roadways throughout the Waterloo Region, Ontario.

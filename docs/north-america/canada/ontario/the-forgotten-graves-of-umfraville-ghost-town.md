#title
The Forgotten Graves of Umfraville Ghost Town

#summary
The overgrown pioneer cemetery of the abandoned 19th-century logging and farming village of Umfraville is said to be haunted by the spirits of early Irish settlers and infants who perished in the harsh wilderness.

#details
Umfraville was once a bustling Irish settlement established along the Old Hastings Road north of Bancroft in the 1860s, boasting hundreds of residents, mills, and churches before economic decline turned it into a ghost town. Today, the village has been entirely reclaimed by the forest, save for stone foundations, cedar fences, and its remote pioneer cemetery located along Umfraville Road. Hidden deep in dense bush, the cemetery contains weathered headstones—including that of Bridgeta Cavanaugh—and numerous unmarked graves of infants and pioneers who succumbed to harsh frontier conditions. Visitors exploring the ruins and the overgrown graveyard have reported an oppressive, eerie atmosphere and unexplained ghostly phenomena tied to the forgotten hardships of the early settlers.

#location
Umfraville Road, north of Ormsby in the Bancroft area, Ontario.

The Ghost of the Albion Hotel

Summary

The historic Albion Hotel in downtown Guelph is said to be haunted by the spirit of a woman linked to American gangster Al Capone who died tragically on the premises.

Details

Built in 1856, the Albion Hotel holds the second liquor licence in Ontario and has long been a focal point for local folklore. According to local ghost walks and historical accounts, infamous American gangster Al Capone frequented the hotel during the Prohibition era, utilizing underground tunnels to visit his mistress. Legend holds that a woman associated with the Capone era met her end within the building, and her restless spirit has since been witnessed peering from upstairs windows or lingering in the historic rooms.

#title
The Ghosts of Hotel Iroquois

#summary
Children growing up in the 1960s at the Hotel Iroquois experienced terrifying encounters with spectral phenomena, bone-chilling sounds, and eerie spirits lurking in the building's basement and ice rooms.

#details
During the 1960s, Kim Price and her sister Alexa Wollan lived at the Hotel Iroquois in Iroquois Falls while their father managed the property for the Abitibi Paper Company. The children frequently had to brave the hotel's terrifying boiler room, where the furnace banged mysteriously amidst deep darkness, as well as the creepy, creaking abandoned ice room. Price maintained a firm conviction that spirits and ghosts haunted the hotel's dark basement before the structure was eventually demolished in 1977.

#location
The former site of Hotel Iroquois, situated right outside the gate of the Abitibi paper mill in Iroquois Falls, Ontario.

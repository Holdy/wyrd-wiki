Spirits of the Peel County Jail

Summary

Staff working inside the historic Peel County Jail building have reported hearing unexplained footsteps and seeing shadowy figures lurking in the corners of empty rooms.

Details

The Peel Art Gallery Museum and Archives (PAMA) complex in downtown Brampton incorporates the historical Peel County Courthouse and jail. Dating back to the 19th century—with several executions having taken place on the grounds—the building has developed a reputation for paranormal activity. Museum staff and night-shift workers have frequently reported hearing mysterious knocks, echoing footsteps in empty corridors, and catching shadowy figures darting at the edge of their vision.

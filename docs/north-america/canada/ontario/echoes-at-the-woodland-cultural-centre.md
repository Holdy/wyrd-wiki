#title
Echoes at the Woodland Cultural Centre

#summary
The former residential school grounds in Brantford are marked by reports of translucent figures crossing the property followed by distant, muffled voices.

#details
Operating today as a cultural center, the building was formerly a residential school carrying a tragic history of mistreatment and abuse. Local residents and visitors have frequently reported seeing translucent human figures crossing the grounds, which quickly vanish only to be followed by the sound of distant, muffled voices echoing across the property.

#location
The Woodland Cultural Centre is located at 184 Mohawk Street in Brantford, Ontario.

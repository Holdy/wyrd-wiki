The Spirits of the Grey Goat Inn

Summary

Main Street's historic Grey Goat Inn is home to two distinct spirits: a benign woman dressed in black on the second floor and a menacing presence in the basement.

Details

Housed in a heritage building that once served as a private residence and later a funeral parlor in its basement, the Grey Goat Inn on Main Street is reputed to be haunted by two different entities. On the second floor, staff and patrons frequently encounter "Georgette," a benign spirit in a long black dress who hangs around an office and resembles a woman from an old portrait once displayed in the home. In sharp contrast, the basement—utilizing the footprint of the former funeral home—harbors a negative and unsettling presence, with staff reporting invisible fingers grasping at their ankles as they walk the stairs.

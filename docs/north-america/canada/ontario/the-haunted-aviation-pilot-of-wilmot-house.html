The Haunted Aviation Pilot of Wilmot House

Summary

The former home of decorated military pilot and mayor Charles Eardley Wilmot is said to be haunted by his restless spirit following his tragic decapitation accident.

Details

The Wilmot House was the residence of S/L Charles Eardley Wilmot, A.F.C., a skilled World War military pilot and former mayor of Belleville. On October 15, 1941, Wilmot died a violent and tragic death when he was struck by an airplane propeller on a tarmac. Local folklore maintains that his restless spirit continues to roam his former residence on Dundas Street West.

The Haunting of Oak Manor

Summary

The historic Oak Manor farmhouse at Yonge Street and Stouffville Sideroad is said to be haunted by the spirits of former residents Edith Legge and her son Homer, whose ghostly presence manifests as phantom footsteps, flying objects, and figures vanishing into walls.

Details

Standing at the northeast corner of Yonge Street and Stouffville Sideroad in the Oak Ridges area of Richmond Hill, the old farmhouse known as Oak Manor has a long-standing reputation for paranormal activity. In earlier decades, the building served as an ice cream parlor operated by Edith Legge and her son Homer. According to local lore and accounts compiled by researchers, the spirits of the Legge family never left. Visitors, business owners, and workers over the years have reported inexplicable phenomena, including pictures flying off walls, the sound of jingling keys, heavy phantom footsteps echoing on upper storeys, and a man seen running across the third-floor loft before vanishing directly into the solid wall. Contractors and electricians working in the building have also fled in terror after hearing violent stomping overhead and seeing a mysterious man staring down at them from a third-storey window when no one else was inside.

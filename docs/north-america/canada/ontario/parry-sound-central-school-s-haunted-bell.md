#title
Parry Sound Central School’s Haunted Bell

#summary
When the historic bell from the demolished Parry Sound Central School was moved to the Museum on Tower Hill, it allegedly brought along two ghostly child spirits who frequently cause mischief within the building.

#details
Following the demolition of the Parry Sound Central School in 1966, its old school bell spent years changing hands before being donated to the Museum on Tower Hill in 2001. According to staff and curators, the bell was accompanied by the spirits of two children who once attended the school. Over the years, workers have reported unexplained disturbances such as drawers and cupboards left wide open, paintings turned on their sides, floorboards creaking in empty rooms after closing, and the phantom sound of shattering glass with no resulting damage. 

#location
The Museum on Tower Hill, located on Tower Hill Road in Parry Sound, Ontario.

The Trio of Spirits at MacKechnie House

Summary

Built in 1843, the historic MacKechnie House in Cobourg is home to three distinct spirits, including a phantom bagpiper and a grandmotherly ghost who tucks guests into bed.

Details

Constructed in the mid-19th century, MacKechnie House has long held a reputation as one of Cobourg's most haunted residences. According to local lore, the home is inhabited by a trio of harmless ghosts. The first is a bagpipe player whose music is frequently heard echoing through the building even though he is rarely sighted. The second is an elderly woman residing in the Rose Room, where she is said to affectionately tuck sleeping guests into bed at night and temporarily rearrange room items. The third is a little girl named Elizabeth, who reportedly died of pneumonia on the property and spends her afterlife playing hide-and-seek and watching visitors from the staircase.

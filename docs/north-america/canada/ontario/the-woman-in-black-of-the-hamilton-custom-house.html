The Woman in Black of the Hamilton Custom House

Summary

The historic Hamilton Custom House is said to be haunted by a grieving spirit who arrived on an immigrant ship and tragically died before she could reunite with her loved one.

Details

Located near Bayfront Park, the Hamilton Custom House is a striking 19th-century landmark that harbors one of the city's most famous ghost legends. According to local lore, a young woman arrived in Hamilton on an immigrant ship and passed away shortly after stepping onto the docks. Her body was reportedly buried on the grounds where the Custom House was later built. Ever since, stories have persisted of a "Woman in Black" roaming the building and grounds, endlessly waiting for her lost partner to arrive.

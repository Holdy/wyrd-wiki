The Traynor Avenue Poltergeist

Summary

Residents of a Kitchener townhouse complex experienced intense paranormal disturbances in the 1980s, including self-activating plumbing, flying objects, and unseen entities.

Details

During the 1980s, tenants living in units 18 and 25 of a townhouse complex at 300 Traynor Avenue reported a series of baffling and frightening occurrences. Witnesses described bath taps suddenly turning on full blast, tea towels flying off racks in closed rooms, footsteps echoing on stairways, and the phantom sound of cats lapping milk. More disturbingly, residents reported sudden waves of fear and physical sensations of being held by invisible arms. While some tenants fled the complex due to the activity, others grew accustomed to the unseen presence and affectionately considered it a "good neighbor."

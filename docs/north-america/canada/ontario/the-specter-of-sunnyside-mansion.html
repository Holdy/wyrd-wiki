The Specter of Sunnyside Mansion

Summary

Sunnyside Mansion, the historic 22-room estate built by oil baron John Henry Fairbank, carries a long-standing local reputation as a classic haunted house defined by its imposing Victorian architecture and decaying grandeur.

Details

Constructed in 1890 by pioneer oilman John Henry Fairbank, Sunnyside Mansion is a massive 22-room estate that has long captured the imagination of Petrolia residents. Over decades of changing fortunes and gradual disrepair, the mansion acquired a strong reputation in local legend as a haunted landmark, characterized by its weathering woodwork and imposing presence. Its classic spooky ambiance eventually attracted filmmakers to use it as a haunting movie set, cementing its status as the town's most legendary historical structure.

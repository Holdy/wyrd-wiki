#title
The Phantom of the Grand Theatre

#summary
Following his sudden disappearance in December 1919 after selling his massive theatre empire, millionaire Ambrose Small became the legendary ghost said to haunt London's Grand Theatre.

#details
On December 2, 1919, Canadian theatre magnate Ambrose Small sold all his theatre holdings—including the Grand Opera House in London, Ontario—for a record-breaking sum. Hours later, he deposited a massive cheque, met with his lawyer and wife, and vanished without a trace, never to be seen alive again. His mysterious disappearance spawned enduring urban legends and local folklore, including dark rumors that he was murdered and his body cremated in the furnace of the Grand Theatre itself. Over the decades, actors, staff, and visitors to the historic theatre have reported unexplained cold spots, strange footsteps, and shadowy sightings attributed to the ghost of Ambrose Small roaming the halls and backstage areas of the building.

#location
The Grand Theatre is located at 471 Richmond Street in London, Ontario.

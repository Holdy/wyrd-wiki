#title
The Restless Spirits of the Blue Church

#summary
The historic Blue Church and its surrounding burying ground near Prescott are reputed to be haunted by early religious pioneers and the spirit of a murdered parish clerk.

#details
Located just west of Prescott along Ontario Route 2, the Blue Church rests on hallowed ground established in 1790. It serves as the final resting place of Barbara Heck, widely known as the founder of Methodism in Upper Canada, who was buried there in 1804. Generations of locals and visitors walking through the antique cemetery have reported eerie phenomena, including sudden cold spots, whispered voices, and shadowy figures pacing among the headstones. Among the enduring legends of the churchyard is the ghost of George Stelz, a former parish clerk whose brutal murder in 1897 is said to tether his restless spirit to the sacred grounds, where people occasionally report catching sight of an unsettled figure lingering near the old graves.

#location
The Blue Church is located at 1512 Ontario Route 2, approximately 4 km west of Prescott, Ontario.

The Specter of the Sanderson Centre

Summary

A historic downtown theatre experiences violent poltergeist activity and the recurring apparition of a former manager.

Details

Opened in 1919, the Sanderson Centre remained free of paranormal claims until 2008, when a lone stagehand working in the lighting booth heard a sudden table crash next door and witnessed a vacant chair hurl across the room. Subsequent investigations by the Brantford Paranormal Society recorded further anomalies, most notably when an investigator spotted an elderly man sitting in a chair who vanished upon approach. The theatre manager later identified the apparition as the former manager who regularly sat in that exact spot with his wife to watch performances.

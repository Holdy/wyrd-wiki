#title
The Haunting of Cedar Street

#summary
A subdivision home in Shelburne, Ontario became the site of intense paranormal activity, including unexplainable appliance failures, falling shelves, and phantom noises. Local investigators and a spiritual medium were called in after the homeowner's young son reported terrifying encounters with spirits in his bedroom.

#details
After moving into a newly purchased house on Cedar Street in Shelburne, Ontario, homeowner Michelle and her family quickly realized something was profoundly wrong with the residence. Unexplained events began almost immediately, including bedroom doors swinging open on their own, mysterious kitchen noises, and rapid electrical burnouts that saw eighteen pot lights fail in a short span. Major household appliances—such as the furnace and hot water tank—continually malfunctioned despite professional replacements and repairs. Physical anomalies also occurred, culminating in a securely anchored wooden wall shelf suddenly ripping out of the wall and striking Michelle's daughter. Most distressingly, Michelle's five-year-old son began waking up terrified, stating that spirits lived under his bed and refusing to sleep in his room. Seeking answers, the family invited the local paranormal investigation group Team Spirit—led by medium Jen Abra and investigator Gerry Rooney—to examine the house, where investigators captured anomalous technological readings and thermal fluctuations.

#location
A residential home located on Cedar Street in Shelburne, Ontario.

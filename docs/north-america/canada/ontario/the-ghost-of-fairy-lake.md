#title
The Ghost of Fairy Lake

#summary
A pioneer father hunting during a harsh winter tragically mistook his daughter—who was wearing a bearskin pelt to keep warm—for a bear and shot her, leaving her spirit to wander the fields by Fairy Lake.

#details
In the late 1800s, a pioneer family lived on a meager farm on a property adjoining Fairy Lake in Huntsville. After the mother passed away, the young daughter took over household chores to help her grieving father. During a brutal winter when food grew dangerously scarce, the father went out into the snow to hunt for fresh meat. When he failed to return by late afternoon, the worried daughter pulled a bearskin pelt over her shoulders to ward off the bitter cold and ventured out into the deep snow to find him. Returning empty-handed and desperate with hunger, the father spotted a dark shape moving through the trees, took aim, and fired. Upon rushing to his kill, he was devastated to discover he had fatally shot his own daughter. For generations afterward, area residents reported seeing a spectral figure clad in animal hide wandering the field where the tragedy occurred.

#location
A property adjoining Fairy Lake in Huntsville, Ontario.

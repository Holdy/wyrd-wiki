The Eternal Gratitude of Whitehern Mansion

Summary

Staff members at Whitehern Mansion in downtown Hamilton have reported feeling watched and encountering disembodied legs, believed to be the grateful spirits of the historic McQuesten family.

Details

Whitehern Mansion, tucked away behind Hamilton City Hall, was the grand home of the influential McQuesten family starting in 1852. Following the financial ruin and tragic suicide of Isaac McQuesten in his study in 1888, his family's legacy was saved and the property was ultimately preserved as a museum. Over the years, workers and museum staff maintaining the historic estate have reported unexplainable phenomena. In one notable account, a worker named Jennifer turned around while arranging velvet ropes near an antique seat and saw a pair of disembodied legs standing behind her before they vanished, leaving behind an overwhelming sense of calm and protective gratitude from the spirits who still care for the home.

    The Haunting of the Bayview Hotel

    Summary

    The historic Bayview Hotel in Bruce Mines is said to be haunted by phantom footsteps, mysterious sounds from the attic, and the inexplicable scent of lilacs.

    Details

    Dating back to the 19th century, the Bayview Hotel has long been associated with local ghost stories and paranormal encounters reported by residents and visitors. Local legend suggests that a mentally disabled child was hidden away in the building's attic during the 1800s, and people have frequently reported hearing the distressing sound of an infant crying from above at night. Other strange occurrences documented at the property include doors and cupboards opening and closing by themselves, household objects vanishing and turning up in odd places later, and heavy footsteps pacing through the hallways. Furthermore, occupants and visitors have noted an overwhelming, sweet smell of lilacs drifting through the halls, a scent some local accounts connect to the presence of the 10th Marquess of Queensbury.

#title
The Singing Estate of Glenhyrst

#summary
The former Cockshutt estate in Brantford, now an art gallery, is frequently visited by ghostly singing, self-playing pianos, and spectral activity.

#details
Glenhyrst Art Gallery, originally the estate of prominent local resident Edmund Cockshutt, has long been regarded by staff and regional ghost hunters as an active site of paranormal phenomena. Staff and visitors have frequently reported hearing the estate's piano playing by itself, doors opening and closing autonomously, and translucent figures crossing the surrounding gardens and grounds. Paranormal investigators documented an eerie encounter when they heard a woman singing inside the historic building even when no audio equipment or live sources were present.

#location
Glenhyrst Art Gallery is located at 20 Ava Road in Brantford, Ontario.

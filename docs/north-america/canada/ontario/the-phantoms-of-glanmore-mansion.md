#title
The Phantoms of Glanmore Mansion

#summary
Built in 1883, Glanmore National Historic Site is haunted by the apparition of a British redcoat soldier, a self-playing piano, and the ghostly presence of original owner Hattie Phillips.

#details
Glanmore House, a striking Second Empire style Victorian mansion built in 1883 by John and Hattie Phillips, has long been associated with paranormal activity in Belleville. Family members and visitors have reported several distinct encounters over the decades, including a British redcoat soldier standing at attention and smiling by a bedside, the drawing room piano playing autonomously, heavy doors swinging open without wind, and sightings of a tiny woman in a grey silk dress with keys at her waist identified as original owner Hattie Phillips.

#location
Glanmore National Historic Site is located at 257 Bridge Street East in Belleville, Ontario.

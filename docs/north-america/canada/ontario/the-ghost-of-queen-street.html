The Ghost of Queen Street

Summary

In August 1939, unexplained violent pounding and phantom lights on an attic door at a Queen Street home in Picton baffled residents, neighbors, and police.

Details

In August 1939, 86-year-old Mrs. James Ackerman, along with her niece and nephew, were awakened at midnight by violent pounding on the attic door of her home on Queen Street in Picton. Despite the nephew nailing the door shut and sticking a pair of scissors into the hinges, the loud thumping intensified and the heavy door burst open. Local police officers, including Constable John Armstrong, investigated the home and witnessed the inexplicable phenomena firsthand, leaving the mystery officially unresolved.

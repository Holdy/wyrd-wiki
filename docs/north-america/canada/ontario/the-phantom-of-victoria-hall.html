The Phantom of Victoria Hall

Summary

Originally built in 1858 as an opulent residence and later converted into a hospital, Victoria Hall on Drummond Street is haunted by the spectral presence of a frail, white-haired woman and associated with an early cursed family legacy.

Details

Judge John Glass Malloch constructed a magnificent 17-room stone mansion named Victoria Hall in Perth in 1858. During its construction, a bitter dispute arose between the judge and a tardy building supplier over unpaid materials, leading the frustrated supplier to curse the judge and his family for as long as they lived in the home. Following premature deaths among his family members and the judge's own passing in 1873, the vacant mansion gained a spooky reputation as the "Haunted House of Perth" due to locals reporting figures wandering its halls at night. In 1923, the building was converted into the Great War Memorial Hospital. Decades later, during the 1970s, evening kitchen and support staff regularly encountered the apparition of a small, slender woman with snow-white hair and a faded blue robe standing outside what nurses later confirmed had been empty utility storage space for years.

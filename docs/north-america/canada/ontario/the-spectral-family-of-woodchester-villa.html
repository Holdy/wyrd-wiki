The Spectral Family of Woodchester Villa

Summary

Built in 1882 by wool mill owner Henry James Bird, the octagonal Woodchester Villa is said to be haunted by a spectral woman and two ghostly children who perished before moving to Bracebridge. Visitors and staff have reported hearing soft cries, footsteps, and a woman's singing echoing through the historic hilltop home.

Details

Henry James Bird built Woodchester Villa, an innovative three-storey octagonal home in Bracebridge, following the tragic death of his first wife Sarah Jane and their two young children from tuberculosis. Today, the villa is widely regarded as one of the most haunted sites in the region. Reports include the soft cries of a baby, the rapid footsteps of little feet on the upper levels, and cold drafts near the basement stairs where visitors can sometimes see their own breath in summer. Many believe these lingering spirits are Sarah Jane and the children following Henry north to the home he built.

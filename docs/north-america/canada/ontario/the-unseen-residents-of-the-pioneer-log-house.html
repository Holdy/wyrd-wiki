The Unseen Residents of the Pioneer Log House

Summary

Staff and paranormal investigators at the Pembroke museum have repeatedly encountered moving objects, disembodied footsteps, and mysterious noises within its historic pioneer structures.

Details

Located in Pembroke within Renfrew County, the Champlain Trail Museum and Pioneer Village features historical buildings reflecting the early settlement era. Multiple paranormal investigations conducted by the Canadian Haunting and Paranormal Society have documented persistent eerie occurrences. Staff and visitors have reported hearing distinct footsteps walking across the upper floor of the pioneer log home when the building is empty, watching toys shift position on their own in the children's exhibit room, and hearing dishes clinking in the dining area.

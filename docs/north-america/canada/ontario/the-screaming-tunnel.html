The Screaming Tunnel

Summary

A limestone drainage tunnel on the outskirts of Niagara Falls is haunted by the ghost of a young girl who died in a fire, and striking a match inside supposedly summons her terrifying scream.

Details

Built in the early 20th century as a limestone drainage culvert beneath a railway line, the Screaming Tunnel has become one of the region's most famous ghost legends. According to local folklore preserved in community archives, a young girl fleeing a burning barn with her clothes and hair ablaze stumbled into the tunnel and perished from her burns. Another variation of the legend claims she was driven into the tunnel and set on fire by an enraged father. Tradition dictates that if a person enters the passage at midnight and strikes a wooden match, an unseen force will extinguish it amidst a blood-curdling shriek. Alternatively, oral history suggests the name originated from a local woman who frequently screamed in the tunnel during domestic disputes.

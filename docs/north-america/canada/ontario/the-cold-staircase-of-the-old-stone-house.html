The Cold Staircase of the Old Stone House

Summary

Visitors and staff at the historic Ermatinger Old Stone House have reported experiencing unexplainable cold spots and phantom noises tied to a tragic historical accident.

Details

According to local lore, a small child fell down the steep wooden stairs of the historic home and later died from the injuries. Years later, staff and visitors frequently report sudden, unnatural drops in temperature at the base of the staircase. Additionally, staff working in the summer kitchen have heard doors squeaking open and shut when no one else is inside the building.

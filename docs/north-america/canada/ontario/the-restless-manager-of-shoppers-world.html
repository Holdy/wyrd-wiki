The Restless Manager of Shoppers World

Summary

Following a tragedy in the office of a former shopping centre movie theatre, overnight staff frequently experienced doors opening on their own and objects moving mysteriously.

Details

Local tradition maintains that a movie theatre once situated on the Shoppers World property in Brampton became haunted after its manager reportedly died by suicide in the administrative office. When retail businesses later occupied the footprint of the former theatre, employees working overnight shifts reported unsettling phenomena, including heavy doors swinging open unassisted, furniture shifting positions, and an oppressive, watchful presence in the back rooms.

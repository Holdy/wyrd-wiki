#title
The Ghost of Murdoff's Tavern

#summary
The ghost of a mid-19th-century tavern keeper is said to haunt the farmlands along Deseronto Road, endlessly pacing the fields in search of gold he buried and forgot during times of wartime panic.

#details
During the mid-19th century, Robert Russell Murdoff operated a bustling tavern situated on the riverbank along Deseronto Road, catering to log drivers and river traders. Deeply distrustful of banks and gripped by fears surrounding the American Civil War and the Fenian Raids, Murdoff amassed and buried his savings in gold coins somewhere on the property. He ultimately forgot the exact hiding place, spending his later years frantically pacing the fields. Local folklore holds that his restless ghost continues to roam the property at night, eternally searching for his lost treasure.

#location
The former tavern site and haunted farmlands are located on the historic Daly Farm property along Deseronto Road in Richmond Township, near the Napanee River.

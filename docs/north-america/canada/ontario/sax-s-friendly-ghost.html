Sax's Friendly Ghost

Summary

Uncle Eddie, a former resident who passed away in 1969, haunts his family's restaurant in Keswick with harmless pranks, disembodied footsteps, and moving objects.

Details

Sax's Fish and Chips Restaurant (later operating as the Church St. Diner) at 207 Church St. in Keswick has been a local landmark operated by the Saxton family since the 1950s, with the original building dating back to 1906. The most notable spirit haunting the premises is Uncle Eddie, the brother-in-law of the owner's mother, who lived in the building for the final ten years of his life until his death in 1969. In his later years, Eddie spent much of his time smoking at a small table and helping with chores. On the night he died, six-year-old Carolynne woke up and found his room bathed in daylight while Eddie sat in his chair, telling her not to worry before he passed away in his sleep. Ever since, staff and visitors have frequently encountered his presence: cleaning personnel have seen him slouched in his chair smoking, toilets flush and doors slam by themselves, kitchen burners and ovens turn on unexpectedly, and personal items like keys and toilet paper teleport or fly across rooms.

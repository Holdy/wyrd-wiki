#title
The Ghost of Captain Colin Swayze

#summary
The resident ghost of a Canadian militia officer killed during the War of 1812 haunts a historic inn in Niagara-on-the-Lake as long as the British flag flies overhead.

#details
During the American invasion in May 1813, Captain Colin Swayze delayed his retreat to rendezvous with his sweetheart. Surprised by American soldiers searching the inn, he hid in a wine barrel in the cellar, where he received a fatal wound from a bayonet. His ghost is said to wander the halls of the inn at night, and modern security cameras have even captured figures resembling an 1812 militia officer walking through the premises. Local lore maintains that his spirit will remain harmless as long as the British flag continues to fly over the doorway.

#location
The Olde Angel Inn at 224 Regent Street in Niagara-on-the-Lake, Ontario.

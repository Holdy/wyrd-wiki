The Haunting of Blinkbonnie

Summary

Built over 170 years ago by town co-founder Charles MacDonald, the historic Blinkbonnie estate is said to be haunted by its former patriarch and lingering family spirits.

Details

Blinkbonnie, an iconic heritage landmark, was originally inhabited by prominent businessman and Gananoque co-founder Charles MacDonald. Following generations of family history and subsequent ownership changes, family members such as Charles MacDonald II remained deeply attached to the property until their deaths. Over the years, psychics, paranormal investigators, and visitors have reported inexplicable phenomena within the house, including slamming doors, unexplained footsteps, and water faucets turning on by themselves, suggesting the original occupants never truly left their ancestral home.

The Ghostly Apparition of Lily Herchemer at the Hochelaga Inn

Summary

Guests and staff at the historic Hochelaga Inn report frequent encounters with the phantom of Lily Herchemer, a young woman who perished in a tragic fire in the 19th century.

Details

Built in 1879 by John McIntyre and his wife Harriet (a relative of Sir John A. Macdonald), the Victorian-era building now operating as the Hochelaga Inn holds a long-standing local legend regarding a woman named Lily Herchemer. According to local folklore, Lily fell deeply in love with a sailor against her parents' strict wishes. To signal him when it was safe to visit, she would hang a lit lantern in her bedroom window. One night, she fell asleep with the lantern burning, and a sudden gust of wind knocked it over, igniting the room and engulfing her in flames. Over the years, guests and innkeepers have reported eerie phenomena throughout the inn, including a ghostly woman in a black dress sitting by the front windows or rustling past hallways, and visitors waking abruptly in the night to sensations of dread or chilling laughter at the foot of their beds.

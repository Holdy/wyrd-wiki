The Hidden Spirits of Old L'Amable Road

Summary

Deep along the historic Old L'Amable Road near Bancroft lies a hidden, overgrown pioneer graveyard where visitors have experienced chilling supernatural encounters.

Details

L'Amable was a thriving lumber and farming community established in the mid-19th century along the colonization roads near Bancroft. While much of the old settlement has faded or been replaced by modern cottages, remnants of the 1800s remain along Old L'Amable Road. Tucked away in the surrounding woods is the hidden and heavily overgrown St. George's Cemetery. Descendants of early settlers and local explorers who have ventured into the graveyard report a profoundly unsettling, creepy atmosphere, sudden drops in temperature, and the lingering sense of unseen watchers among the ancient headstones.

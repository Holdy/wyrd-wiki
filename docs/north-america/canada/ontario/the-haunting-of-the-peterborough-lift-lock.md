#title
The Haunting of the Peterborough Lift Lock

#summary
The historic Peterborough Lift Lock on the Trent-Severn Waterway is associated with the restless spirits of workers, including an unrecovered construction worker from 1903 and a former employee named Arthur.

#details
The Peterborough Lift Lock, once the world’s tallest hydraulic boat lift, has accumulated several tragic legends and ghost stories over more than a century of operation. According to local lore, during the lift's construction in 1903, a worker fell to his death and his body could not be retrieved, leading workers to reportedly build around him within the structure. Another prominent spirit associated with the site is Arthur, a former worker who passed away from a heart attack; employees today customarily greet him by calling out "Hi, Art!" upon arriving for work. Visitors and paranormal investigators have also reported unexplained phenomena around the stairwells, such as phantom footsteps echoing on the concrete steps when no living person is present.

#location
The Peterborough Lift Lock on the Trent-Severn Waterway in Peterborough, Ontario.

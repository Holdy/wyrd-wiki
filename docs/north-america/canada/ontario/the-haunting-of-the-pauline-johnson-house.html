The Haunting of the Pauline Johnson House

Summary

The historic childhood home of renowned Canadian author Pauline Johnson is said to be haunted by aggressive unseen forces and a vanishing apparition.

Details

Built in 1856, the house where poet Pauline Johnson spent her childhood has long been a site of strange occurrences investigated by the Brantford Paranormal Society. During their visits, an investigator was physically kicked down a flight of stairs by an unseen force. Paranormal teams also documented unexplainable foul smells and a persistent apparition near the top of the stairs that repeatedly vanished into the attic whenever approached.

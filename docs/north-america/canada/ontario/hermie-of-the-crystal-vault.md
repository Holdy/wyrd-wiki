#title
Hermie of the Crystal Vault

#summary
A historic 1906 bank building turned boutique at 7 Prospect Avenue in Cobalt is haunted by a mischievous miner spirit named Hermie who rearranges items and drops books.

#details
Originally established as a bank in 1906 and later serving as an inn and mining office, 7 Prospect Avenue became active with paranormal phenomena after modern renovations stripped away old ceiling tiles. Shop owners and visitors frequently encounter "Hermie," a spirit believed to be a fallen miner. Encounters include crystals and books being inexplicably thrown to the floor—including a local ghost history book left open overnight—and hockey team photographs capturing dozens of glowing orbs hovering near the old bank vault stairs.

#location
The Crystal Vault is located at 7 Prospect Avenue in Cobalt, Ontario.

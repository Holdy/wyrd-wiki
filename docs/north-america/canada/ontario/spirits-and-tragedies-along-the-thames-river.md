#title
Spirits and Tragedies Along the Thames River

#summary
Riverside folklore in St. Marys preserves dark historical accounts of fatal quarry accidents, a mysterious mill fire, and a disgraced nineteenth-century doctor.

#details
Local history and ghost lore tied to the early years of St. Marys recount tragic events that allegedly left lingering spirits along the water. Guided walking tours and local legends highlight several chilling occurrences, including fatal accidents in the town's deep limestone quarries, a devastating and mysterious fire at an old local mill, and the unsettling tale of a disgraced nineteenth-century medical doctor whose spirit is said to haunt the river path.

#location
The tour route begins at the flagpole along the river path at The Falls in St. Marys, Ontario.

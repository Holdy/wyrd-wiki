#title
The Restless Spirits of Hill House

#summary
Hill House within Muskoka Heritage Place is believed to be haunted by its 19th-century builder, Robert Norton Hill, and his wife, Caroline.

#details
As part of the recreated pioneer village at Muskoka Heritage Place, the two-storey frame Hill House carries a rich and storied history. It was originally built by Robert Norton Hill, a minister who moved to the region in 1867 and established the local post office before passing away in 1895. Local historian and author Andrew Hind identified Hill House as the most spiritually active building in the pioneer village during historical ghost tours. Visitors and staff have reported encountering the enduring presence of both Robert and Caroline Hill, who continue to watch over the historic home they built in the 19th century.

#location
Hill House at Muskoka Heritage Place located at 88 Brunel Road in Gravenhurst, Ontario.

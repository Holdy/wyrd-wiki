The Resident Ghosts of the Princess Avenue Playhouse

Summary

Performers and visitors at a historic St. Thomas theatre have reported numerous paranormal phenomena, including the weeping ghost of a young girl named Maisie and a mischievous spirit named Mort who returns lost items when cussed out.

Details

Originally built as a church before becoming the Princess Avenue Playhouse (home of the Elgin Theatre Guild), the building at 40 Princess Avenue in St. Thomas is famously haunted by multiple spirits. Among them is a 5- or 6-year-old blonde girl named Maisie, whose crying for help has startled cast members into conducting thorough late-night searches of the building. Another famous entity is a prankster spirit known as Mort (identified by mediums as John), who frequently hides crew members' personal possessions during rehearsals and shows. Crew members discovered that if they stand center stage and loudly hurl profanities at Mort, their missing items promptly reappear where they were left. Other reported apparitions include an unhappy tower ghost of a former church secretary, a shadowy male figure sitting quietly in the balcony, and a mysterious "Philanthropist" who leaves the box office float consistently over by a single nickel every night.

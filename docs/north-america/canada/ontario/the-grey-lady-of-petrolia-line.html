The Grey Lady of Petrolia Line

Summary

A grand Victorian house in Petrolia is said to be haunted by a benevolent spirit known as the Grey Lady, alongside a restless musical presence and an oppressive energy in the basement.

Details

Local folklore and paranormal accounts recount that a prominent Victorian house along Petrolia Line is home to several lingering spirits. Most notable is the "Grey Lady," an apparition frequently spotted by visitors and residents at the top of the staircase. Witnesses have also reported hearing piano music played by an unseen pianist in the home's music room, while investigators have documented a heavy, forceful presence lurking in the basement.

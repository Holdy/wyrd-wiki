The Phantom Manager of the Sanderson Centre

Summary

A historic theatre in downtown Brantford is haunted by anomalous noises, flying furniture, and the lingering apparition of its former manager.

Details

Opened in 1919 in downtown Brantford, the Sanderson Centre for the Performing Arts became the site of startling paranormal activity. A stagehand working alone in the lighting booth heard a table crash in an adjacent room and watched a vacant chair get thrown across the room, leaving him severely shaken. During subsequent investigations by local paranormal researchers, a team member spotted an elderly man sitting quietly in a chair. When approached, the figure vanished into thin air. Upon showing a description and photograph to the theatre manager, the investigators learned the apparition matched the former manager, who historically sat in that exact spot with his wife to watch performances.

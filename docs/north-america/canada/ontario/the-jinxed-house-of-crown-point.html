    The Jinxed House of Crown Point

    Summary

    Built in 1865 by Lieutenant John Grierson at Crown Point near Arnprior, this historic stone house became infamous for a century-old curse after a superstitious innkeeper allegedly refused shelter to a freezing traveler, leading to generations of tragic accidents and reports of paranormal activity.

    Details

    Constructed along the Ottawa River at Crown Point near Arnprior, the Grierson House served various roles over the decades, including as an inn, a medical clinic, and an Oddfellows hall. According to local folklore, its dark reputation began in the 1870s when an innkeeper turned away a stranded traveler during a severe winter storm, believing him to be the devil; the traveler subsequently froze to death outside. Over subsequent decades, occupants experienced severe hardships and unsettling occurrences. In 1967, family members fell through melting river ice and the homeowner suffered a fatal heart attack. In 1975, the Bender family moved in and reported mysterious rashes, missing property, and a hidden hollow stone in the fireplace that fueled superstitious rumors. Tragically, their three-year-old daughter Natasha wandered out into the snow and drowned after falling through the ice on the Ottawa River. Later tenants and visitors continued to report disembodied footsteps, rattling doors, and an oppressive, sorrowful atmosphere clinging to the old stone walls.

    The Spectral Lady of Hillary House

    Summary

    Hillary House in Aurora, Ontario, is a historic Victorian Gothic estate where visitors and paranormal investigators have reported sightings of a mysterious woman lingering near the second-floor windows.

    Details

    Built in 1862 on Yonge Street, Hillary House stands as one of Canada's best-preserved examples of Gothic Revival architecture. For over a century, the residence served as both a family home and a working medical practice for several generations of local doctors. Over the years, community rumors and investigations by paranormal teams such as The Paranormal Seekers have highlighted the property's ghostly reputation. The most prominent recurring phenomenon reported at the estate is the sighting of an unidentified woman looking out from one of the home's second-floor windows.

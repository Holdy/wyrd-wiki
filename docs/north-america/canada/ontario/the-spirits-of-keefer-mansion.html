The Spirits of Keefer Mansion

Summary

Built in the late 19th century and later serving as a hospital and maternity ward, Thorold's historic Keefer Mansion is reportedly haunted by the lingering spirits of former patients, crying infants, and shadowy figures.

Details

Constructed in 1885 by the Keefer family, this grand heritage mansion in Thorold remained in their possession only briefly before financial ruin forced its sale. Over the decades, the building underwent dramatic changes in purpose, serving at various times as a hospital, a maternity ward, and a chronic care facility. In the years since, visitors, staff, and paranormal investigators have reported hearing the distressed cries of unseen babies, witnessing phantom children playing in the upper halls, and experiencing heavy doors slamming shut on their own in the dead of night.

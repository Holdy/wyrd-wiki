#title
The Haunting of the Kenora Armouries

#summary
The historic Kenora Armouries building, which previously operated as a courthouse and jail, is tied to dark judicial history and persistent hauntings.

#details
Local paranormal investigations and archival research in Kenora highlight the historic Kenora Armouries building—which stands on a site previously serving as a courthouse and jail—as a focal point for spooky activity. Historical records from the Lake of the Woods Museum document that criminals were tried and hanged at the site during the frontier era. Investigators exploring the building report an oppressive atmosphere, disembodied footsteps, and whispered echoes reminiscent of its penal past. 

#location
The Kenora Armouries, Kenora, Ontario.

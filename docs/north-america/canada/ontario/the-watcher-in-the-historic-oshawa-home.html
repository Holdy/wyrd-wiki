The Watcher in the Historic Oshawa Home

Summary

A family moving into a turn-of-the-century house near Parkwood Estate in Oshawa found themselves stalked by an unseen presence that moved their belongings, plunged rooms into freezing temperatures, and slammed doors.

Details

After moving from Boston into a large turn-of-the-century home not far from Colonel Sam McLaughlin’s historic Parkwood Estate in Oshawa, a homeowner experienced a series of unsettling paranormal events while renovating alone. His watch repeatedly vanished from his nightstand, only to turn up later in distant rooms he had never entered. He also encountered sudden, localized temperature drops, doors that slammed shut by themselves in empty hallways with freezing doorknobs, and the distinct, chilling sensation of being watched by someone gauging his reactions.

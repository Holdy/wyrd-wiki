#title
The Haunting of Guelph's First Jail and Gallows

#summary
The site of Guelph's original county jail, which hosted at least nine executions between 1847 and 1919, is plagued by reports of shadowy figures and unexplained paranormal activity.

#details
Constructed in 1839 on Woolwich Street, the old county gaol witnessed numerous public and private executions, including those of convicted murderers whose final moments went tragically awry. Tour guides and courthouse staff working near the preserved limestone walls have frequently reported an eerie sense of being watched, shadows moving down empty corridors, and unexplained spikes on electromagnetic field detectors. Spirits such as inmates who died within the prison walls—including those who maintained their innocence—are said to still walk the exercise yard and surrounding administrative grounds.

#location
The historic old Guelph jail site is situated behind the County of Wellington Courthouse and Administrative Centre at 74 Woolwich Street in Guelph, Ontario.

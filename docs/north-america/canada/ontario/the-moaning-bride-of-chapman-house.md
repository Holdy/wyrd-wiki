#title
The Moaning Bride of Chapman House

#summary
A luxurious 1923 residence on Main Street West is said to be haunted by the tragic spirit of a young bride who was permanently injured by a falling bathtub just before moving into her dream home.

#details
Built in 1923 by lumber merchant Cyril Chapman for his young bride Mary Brock, the three-story brick home featured imported hardwoods, New York wainscoting, and a lion-footed bathtub. Days before occupancy, workers hoisting the heavy bathtub through an upper-window pulley system dropped it; the tub struck Mary, paralyzing her for the rest of her short life. For decades, local residents reported hearing eerie moaning sounds, the buzzing of deer flies, flickering candles, and sightings of a figure in a wedding gown near the upper windows on moonlit nights. Although later building investigations attributed the moaning and buzzing to plumbing vent stacks and roof flashing responding to wind, modern owners still report security alarms inexplicably tripping on moonlit nights.

#location
Chapman House on Main Street West, North Bay, Ontario.

#title
The Jilted Lady of Room 217

#summary
A heartbroken woman who was abandoned by her lover in the early 20th century continues to haunt Room 217 at the historic Briars Resort in Jackson's Point.

#details
Originally built as a homestead in 1840 by Captain William Bourchier, The Briars Resort and Spa in Jackson's Point is renowned for its rich history and active paranormal phenomena. Most famously, Room 217 and the manor's watchtower are said to be occupied by the ghost of a woman who was jilted by her fiancé during a stay in the early 1900s. Guests and staff have reported numerous unsettling occurrences in and around the room, including handprints appearing out of nowhere on freshly made beds, heavy curtains billowing even when there is no breeze, and the spectral apparition of a woman in a white dress with long dark hair who vanishes into thin air while leaving behind the distinct, sweet fragrance of rosewater.

#location
The Briars Resort and Spa at 55 Hedge Road, Jackson's Point, Ontario.

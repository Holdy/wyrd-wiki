The Knocking Spirit of the Old Grimsby Jail

Summary

A historic downtown Grimsby building that formerly served as a local jail and fire hall is reportedly haunted by an unseen spirit that knocks three times.

Details

Located at 15 Main Street East in downtown Grimsby, this historic building once operated as a municipal jail and fire hall before later housing community businesses. According to local accounts and regional reports, the building is inhabited by an unseen presence, suspected to be a former police officer. Staff and visitors have repeatedly heard distinct, heavy knocks echoing three times throughout the rooms, and witnesses have reported seeing spectral figures moving across the interior space.

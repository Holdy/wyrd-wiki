The Phantom Lantern of the Minto Pond

Summary

Local folklore in Minto Township tells of a family who tragically drowned when their horse and buggy fell through pond ice, leaving a ghostly lantern light to haunt the frozen waters.

Details

According to longstanding local legend in the community, a family perished during the winter when their horse and buggy broke through the ice of a rural pond popular with local youth. Ever since the tragedy, residents claimed that the flickering light of the family's lantern could be seen moving across the frozen surface on winter nights. Years later, local teenagers including future mayor George Bridge attempted to stage a practical joke by securing a flashlight in a nearby tree to mimic the ghostly lantern and scare visitors. However, the prank took an eerie turn when the flashlight appeared to detach from the tree branch and float freely up and down in mid-air, remaining defyingly unexplained to this day.

#title
The Devil on Old Morley's Farm

#summary
In mid-nineteenth-century Goderich Township, a pioneer farmer was plagued for years by mysterious disasters, mutilated livestock, and sightings of a malevolent entity lurking near his Maitland River property.

#details
Henry Morley, a pioneer settler living alone near the Disney mill on the Maitland River in Goderich Township, suffered an extraordinary series of misfortunes during the mid-nineteenth century. Local newspapers like the *Huron Signal* documented how Morley’s farm was supposedly targeted by unearthly and malicious forces. Over the years, his log cabin was burned down, large sums of money were stolen, and his livestock—including dozens of sheep, cows, and chickens—were repeatedly poisoned, drowned, driven over precipices, or mysteriously maimed. Morley was also reportedly waylaid and attacked on his own land in broad daylight. While swamp gas and human malice likely played a role in what later commentators viewed as local deviltry, nineteenth-century lore firmly cast the farm as being haunted by a visible devil before Morley eventually sold the property and prospered elsewhere.

#location
Old Morley's farm site at the edge of a swamp near the Disney mill on the Maitland River in Goderich Township, Ontario.

#title
The Hauntings of Salem United Church Cemetery

#summary
Salem Cemetery in Ajax is said to be haunted by its 19th-century caretaker, William Brown, alongside phantom barking and restless spirits connected to its historic grounds.

#details
Established in 1831 and featuring a red brick church erected in 1849, Salem Cemetery is a quiet rural graveyard steeped in local ghost lore. According to accounts, William Brown, who was appointed as the cemetery caretaker in 1831, continues to wander the grounds alongside his faithful hound, with visitors and investigators reporting unlocatable barking and the lingering energy of a man. Inside the small chapel, paranormal investigations have captured electronic voice phenomena (EVPs), sudden battery drains on equipment, and the presence of a lone parishioner who reportedly continued to worship in his pew long after the congregation dissolved in 1890.

#location
Salem Cemetery is located on Salem Road at Highway 7 in North Ajax, Ontario.

Shadows of Rolph Street and the Station Arts Centre

Summary

Historic public and cultural buildings in downtown Tillsonburg, including Rolph Street Public School and the Station Arts Centre, are legendary locations where educators and residents have reported eerie paranormal encounters.

Details

During historical research and local ghost walk initiatives across Tillsonburg, teachers and staff from institutions like Rolph Street Public School have recounted unsettling, unexplained encounters within the aging school halls. Nearby cultural landmarks downtown, such as the Station Arts Centre on Bridge Street West, have similarly been tied to local ghost lore, whispers of secret societies, and unexplained nighttime occurrences that anchor the town's guided Eerie Encounters tours.

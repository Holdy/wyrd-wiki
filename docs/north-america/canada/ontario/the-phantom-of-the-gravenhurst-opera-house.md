#title
The Phantom of the Gravenhurst Opera House

#summary
The historic Gravenhurst Opera House is said to be haunted by Ben, a former lighting technician who tragically fell to his death from the catwalk.

#details
Opened in 1901 to serve as both a town hall and an opera house, the Gravenhurst Opera House has stood as a cultural cornerstone of the community for over a century. Over the decades, local lore has linked the historic building to a resident phantom named Ben. According to the legend, Ben was a dedicated lighting operator who either accidentally slipped or fell from the overhead catwalk above the stage. Since his untimely death, staff and visitors have reported inexplicable cold spots, sudden chills, doors slamming shut unprompted, and fleeting sightings of a translucent figure roaming the backstage area.

#location
The Gravenhurst Opera House located at 290 Muskoka Road South in Gravenhurst, Ontario.

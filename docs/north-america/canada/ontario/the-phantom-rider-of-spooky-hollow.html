The Phantom Rider of Spooky Hollow

Summary

A dramatic wooded ravine in Norfolk County is said to be haunted by the ghost of a traveling salesman murdered at a long-gone hotel who searches on horseback for his lost head.

Details

Spooky Hollow is a dramatic 1.2-kilometre forested gorge and stretch of road leading toward Normandale from Highway 24 in Norfolk County. Local legend traces its ominous reputation back to a hotel fire over a century ago. According to folklore, a traveling salesman was murdered and dismembered at the hotel, and his headless ghost can still be seen riding a spectral horse through the hollow at night. During the Great Depression, the Cornell family raised ten children in the hollow's sole house and recalled that while superstitious visitors would lock their cars in terror as they passed through, the family lived there peacefully, though the home also held memories of family members passing away in the front room.

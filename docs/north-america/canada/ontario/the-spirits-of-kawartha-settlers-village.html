The Spirits of Kawartha Settlers' Village

Summary

Kawartha Settlers' Village in Bobcaygeon comprises historic relocated buildings—such as the Duggan, Kimble, and Wray houses—where visitors and investigators have reported disembodied voices, shifting furnishings, and oppressive heaviness linked to past pioneer hardships.

Details

Established to preserve the pioneer history of the region, Kawartha Settlers' Village features over twenty historic structures collected from across the Kawartha Lakes area. Within buildings like the 1850s Duggan House and the Kimble House, visitors and paranormal investigators have experienced sudden sensations of suffocation, unexplained floor vibrations, and shifting shadows. Electronic Voice Phenomena (EVP) captured across the village have recorded whispered voices and replies from unseen entities, particularly inside the old General Store, which bears the lingering atmospheric weight of historic local tragedies and pioneers' struggles.

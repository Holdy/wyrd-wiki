The Specter of Hill House

Summary

Hill House at Muskoka Heritage Place in Huntsville is haunted by the spirit of its former resident, Mrs. Hill, where visitors and staff report unexplained footsteps, disturbed beds, and thrown objects.

Details

Hill House, a historically furnished home located within Muskoka Heritage Place in Huntsville, appears quaint and peaceful on the exterior, but it hides a restless paranormal presence inside. Staff and visitors have repeatedly heard heavy footsteps pacing the upper floor when no one else is in the building and found beds bearing the clear impression of someone having sat on them. In one dramatic encounter, two witnesses were chased from the house after an antique hairbrush was hurled across a room by unseen hands. On rare occasions, the stubborn spirit of Mrs. Hill—the strong-willed former resident—can be glimpsed peering out from a second-floor window.

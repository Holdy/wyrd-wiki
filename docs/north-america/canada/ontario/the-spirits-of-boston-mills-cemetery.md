#title
The Spirits of Boston Mills Cemetery

#summary
The historic Boston Mills Cemetery and its adjacent 1888 one-room schoolhouse in Caledon are said to be haunted by the apparition of an old priest, floating orbs, and the ghosts of children playing among the headstones.

#details
Settled in the early 1800s along the Credit River, the Boston Mills area originated as a non-denominational burial ground after a local pioneer named David Williams was struck and killed by a falling tree in 1823, reportedly buried in a shroud made from the tree's bark. Decades later, an 1888 stone one-room schoolhouse was built nearby, which later served as a winter mortuary when the school board amalgamated. Over the years, locals, hikers, and paranormal researchers have reported numerous strange occurrences on the grounds. Visitors have witnessed floating orbs of light, the spectral figure of an old priest wandering solemnly among the tombstones, and the lingering presence of children playing gleefully around the graves—believed to be the echoes of pupils who once attended school alongside the hallowed grounds.

#location
Boston Mills Cemetery is located at the intersection of Boston Mills Road and Chinguacousy Road in Caledon, Ontario.

The Dragging Ghost of Mackenzie Hall

Summary

Staff and visitors at Windsor's historic Mackenzie Hall have long reported a mysterious "Dragging Ghost" that scrapes heavy ladders across the floor and leaves unexplained paranormal traces.

Details

Built between 1855 and 1856 by Alexander Mackenzie (who later became Canada's second Prime Minister), Mackenzie Hall served as the Essex County courthouse, jail, and site of executions until 1963. Beyond its grim history of public and shielded hangings, staff frequently experience unexplained phenomena. A custodian working alone at night reported hearing a heavy ladder scrape across the floor three distinct times, only to find the room empty. Other accounts include a young boy witnessing a floating apparition staring intently at the portrait of Judge Stewart (nicknamed the "bloody judge"), unexplained handprints appearing on windows, and electronic voice recorders capturing mysterious moans.

The Death Carriage

Summary

A spectral carriage pulled by fiery-eyed black horses haunts the historic streets and parks of Guatemala, collecting the souls of the dying and dooming anyone who witnesses its passage. The curse ensures that those who encounter it must live in perpetual dread of its phantom return.

Details

As night falls across the old city streets and colonial parks of Guatemala, a terrifying dread settles over anyone unfortunate enough to remain outside past the witching hour. According to local lore, a spectral vehicle known as the Death Carriage races through the darkness, pulled by black horses with blazing fiery eyes and driven by a figure cloaked entirely in black. Its purpose is to sweep through neighborhoods and collect the souls of those whose time has come. One evening, after a long day working in the fields, a traveler named Mario was hurrying home when he heard the ominous clatter of wheels and hooves fast approaching. Overcome by a sudden, paralyzing terror, he fled and hid behind the trees of a local park, spending the chilling night in concealment. Dismissing his fright the next morning as foolishness, he later recounted the event to a friend, who warned him of the fatal rumors surrounding the phantom carriage. Driven by curiosity, the two men returned to the park after dark to wait. Sure enough, the terrifying black carriage emerged from the fog, rolling silently above the ground towards them. The driver stared intently at the pair with a gaze so dreadful that both men collapsed unconscious. Ever since that fateful night, the survivors remain cursed to hide in terror whenever the phantom wheels echo through the nocturnal streets.

#title
The Exorcism at Iglesia Visión Celestial

#summary
Convinced that a young woman had been overtaken by demons, a local preacher and his followers locked her inside a remote jungle church and subjected her to a fatal exorcism by fire.

#details
In February 2017, in the isolated jungle hamlet of El Cortezal near Rosita, Nicaragua, 25-year-old Vilma Trujillo began experiencing hallucinations and erratic behavior, leading her family and a local preacher named Juan Gregorio Rocha to believe she was possessed by the devil. She was brought to the Iglesia Visión Celestial, a wooden church situated on a secluded hillside, where she was kept captive for nearly a week without adequate food or water. On the sixth night, after a congregant claimed a divine revelation that demons must be purged with fire, the group tied Vilma to a guava tree beside a burning pyre. She suffered catastrophic burns across eighty percent of her body before her relatives rescued her, though she tragically succumbed to her injuries days later in a hospital in Managua.

#location
The Iglesia Visión Celestial (Celestial Vision Church) located in the remote jungle settlement of El Cortezal, near the town of Rosita in the North Caribbean Coast Autonomous Region of Nicaragua.

#title
The Duende Curse of Santiago Church

#summary
According to local legend, the historic Santiago Church in León was thwarted and left in ruins due to a powerful curse cast by mischievous duendes. Today, only its weathered bell tower and remnants stand as a testament to the failed construction.

#details
In the city of León, Nicaragua, construction on the Santiago Church faced inexplicable and continuous setbacks. Builders and townspeople soon attributed these failures to local folklore involving *duendes*—mythical, mischievous spirits or fairies. According to legend, the duendes placed a curse upon the sacred site, sabotaging the efforts to complete the church and ensuring that the structure would never be successfully finished. Over time, the construction was abandoned, leaving behind the striking open-air ruins and standing bell tower that locals and travelers visit today.

#location
The ruins of Santiago Church are located in León, Nicaragua, situated one and a half blocks away from San Juan Bautista church.

Conrad the Duppy

Summary

A Bajan woman living in Paynes Bay is possessed by a malevolent spirit named Conrad after her seaman husband unknowingly brings home a cursed package.

Details

According to Bajan folklore, a woman named Doris residing in Paynes Bay, Saint James, fell victim to spirit possession when her seaman husband unwittingly brought home a supernatural package intended for him. An entity known as Conrad took over Doris, exerting complete control over her voice and actions. Conrad would loudly demand specific meals through her and forced her to consume white rum against her wishes. After terrorizing her and stripping away her normalcy and sanity, Conrad was finally cast out and vanquished inside a church located in the parish of Saint Michael.

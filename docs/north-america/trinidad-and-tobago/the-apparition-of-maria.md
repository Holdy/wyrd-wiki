#title
The Apparition of Maria

#summary
A heartbroken young woman who died after falling from a hill while rushing to warn her lover of her father's plot now haunts the highway as a phantom runner. Her apparition is frequently seen darting across the tarmac at night.

#details
In 1909, a young Spanish woman named Maria fell in love with an Indian labourer on the Forres Park Sugar Estate, much to the fury of her father. After learning that her father and his trusted workers plotted to kill her lover, a desperate Maria fled her home to warn him. While crossing the path, she was bitten by a poisonous snake and, weakened by the venom, fell to her death from the adjacent hill. Overcome with grief, her father erected a memorial statue of the Virgin Mary on the estate's highest hill. To this day, Maria's spirit is said to haunt the area, appearing as an apparition running frantically across the highway at night to reach her doomed lover.

#location
The haunting takes place along the Solomon Hochoy Highway near the Claxton Bay turnoff and the former Forres Park Sugar Estate in Trinidad and Tobago.

#title
The Curse of the Caura Water Dam

#summary
When the village and church of Caura were forcibly demolished to build a major water reservoir, a displaced Catholic priest placed a curse upon the project. Consequently, the dam project collapsed amidst scandal and corruption, leaving the valley transformed into a recreational river site.

#details
In the mid-20th century, the historic rural settlement of Caura in northern Trinidad was acquired by authorities, and its vibrant village and church were entirely demolished to make way for a massive water dam intended to supply North Trinidad. Outraged and heartbroken by the destruction of the community and its sacred spaces, the village's Catholic priest cast a curse upon the ambitious construction project. Plagued by escalating costs, political scandal, and administrative corruption, the dam project was abruptly abandoned just a year into construction. Today, the cursed site has evolved into the popular Caura River Valley, where visitors lime along the riverbanks while the remnants of the failed dam project remain a lingering local legend.

#location
The Caura River Valley and former dam site are located in the Northern Range of Trinidad, north of El Dorado and Tacarigua.

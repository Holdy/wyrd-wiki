The Phantom Couple of Upper Blackwell Road

Summary

A phantom couple frequently appears along Upper Blackwell Road trying to flag down passing vehicles, only to vanish into thin air the moment a car slows down. Additionally, the nearby Black Tram Bridge is associated with chilling legends of phantom cars and supernatural pursuits.

Details

Upper Blackwell Road, a 3.6-mile stretch leading to the small town of Blackwell, Missouri, has long held a reputation for local hauntings and eerie occurrences. Among its most famous spectral inhabitants is a ghostly couple who stands by the roadside trying to flag down passing cars. Drivers who attempt to slow down or offer assistance find that the figures disappear completely before contact can be made. Further along the route at the Black Tram Bridge, local lore warns of similar supernatural phenomena, including reports of phantom vehicles that chase motorists who cross the bridge at night.

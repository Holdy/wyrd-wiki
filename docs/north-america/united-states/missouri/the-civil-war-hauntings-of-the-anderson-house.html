The Civil War Hauntings of the Anderson House

Summary

The historic Anderson House in Lexington, Missouri, which served as a makeshift hospital during the Civil War Battle of the Hemp Bales, is haunted by the lingering spirits of fallen soldiers and poltergeist activity.

Details

Built in the mid-19th century, the stately Anderson House became the focal point of the 1861 Civil War Battle of the Hemp Bales, where combatants utilized stacked hemp bales for cover. The house traded hands multiple times and operated as a blood-soaked field hospital, leaving permanent bloodstains and structural battle scars. Decades later, tour guides and visitors frequently report seeing phantom Confederate and Union soldiers roaming the pristine grounds—which have never been farmed since the battle—as well as hearing unexplained sounds, experiencing physical pushes on the stairs, and witnessing heavy wooden locking bars lifted and thrown across rooms.

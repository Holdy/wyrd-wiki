#title
The Legend of Momo the Missouri Monster

#summary
During the summer of 1972, multiple residents of Louisiana, Missouri, reported terrifying encounters with a massive, foul-smelling, hairy bipedal creature roaming near Marzolf Hill. The sightings sparked a massive local panic and an armed search party before concluding with mysterious, unexplainable footprints discovered at a nearby farm.

#details
In July 1972, a wave of bizarre encounters shook the small town of Louisiana, Missouri. The phenomenon began when eight-year-old Terry Harrison and his brother saw a six-to-seven-foot-tall, black, hairy creature standing near the edge of their yard at the base of Marzolf Hill. The beast was described as having hair covering its face and carrying what appeared to be a dead dog under its arm, while giving off an overpowering, nauseating odor. Over the following days, local resident Edgar Harrison heard heavy clanging and animal-like growls near the water reservoir atop Marzolf Hill, prompting large groups of armed townspeople to investigate. Sightings continued around the area, including one by local resident Ellis Minor, who spotted the coal-black creature standing directly in the middle of Great River Road. The panic eventually drew national media attention and a multi-day search party led by local police and state conservation officers. The flap finally concluded in early August after unexplained, massive footprints appeared abruptly in the middle of a garden at the Suddarth farm northwest of town without any matching tracks leading to or from the site.

#title
The Lumber Baron of Rockcliffe Mansion

#summary
Rockcliffe Mansion in Hannibal, Missouri, remains haunted by its eccentric creator, lumber baron John Cruickshanks, who died staring out at the Mississippi River.

#details
Constructed at the turn of the 20th century by wealthy lumber baron John Cruickshanks overlooking the Mississippi River, Rockcliffe Mansion was an architectural marvel filled with opulent mahogany wood and expensive wallpaper. After Cruickshanks passed away in his bedroom facing the river, his grief-stricken family abandoned the mansion, leaving the original furniture and belongings untouched for decades. When the mansion was eventually restored, caretakers and visitors reported regular paranormal disturbances, including the sound of front doors opening and closing, unseen footsteps walking up staircases, and a ghostly figure resembling Cruickshanks enjoying a drink in the bar. Furthermore, modern visitors note that the mattress on the bed where he died continually sinks on one side as if someone is sleeping on it.

#location
Rockcliffe Mansion is located in Hannibal, Missouri.

#title
The Legend of Rosie at the Plains Hotel

#summary
Following a tragic shooting in the early 1900s involving a jealous newlywed bride, her unfaithful groom, and a mysterious woman, the historic Plains Hotel has remained haunted by their restless spirits.

#details
Opened in 1911 in downtown Cheyenne, Wyoming, the Plains Hotel quickly became a premier destination for travelers on the Western frontier. Alongside its reputation for luxury and architecture, a enduring local legend took root concerning a newlywed bride named Rosie. According to local lore, Rosie and her husband were staying at the hotel on their honeymoon when she discovered him in the lounge with another woman. After following them upstairs, Rosie allegedly shot both her husband and his companion in a fit of rage before returning to her room to take her own life. Ever since, staff and guests have reported persistent paranormal phenomena throughout the building, including disembodied crying and laughter echoing from Room 444, the apparition of Rosie wandering the fourth-floor corridors in a blue dress, and sightings of the other woman and the groom on different floors of the hotel.

#location
The Plains Hotel is located at 1600 Capitol Avenue in downtown Cheyenne, Wyoming.

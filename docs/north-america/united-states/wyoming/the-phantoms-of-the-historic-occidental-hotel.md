#title
The Phantoms of the Historic Occidental Hotel

#summary
Guests and staff at the historic Occidental Hotel in Buffalo, Wyoming, frequently report disembodied footsteps, objects moving on their own, and phantom visitors climbing into bed with sleeping guests.

#details
Operating since the late 19th century in Buffalo, Wyoming, the Occidental Hotel has accumulated a rich reputation for paranormal phenomena. Alongside the famous ghostly child named Emily who tragically died of cholera in the early 1900s, employees and guests have experienced numerous unexplained occurrences. Waitresses and desk clerks report hearing piano keys play by themselves in an empty lobby, wine glasses shattering without being touched, and bottles popping off shelves. In the hotel's guest suites, visitors have reported smelling distinct perfumes and feeling invisible presences slide into bed between them in the middle of the night.

#location
The Historic Occidental Hotel is located on Main Street in Buffalo, Wyoming.

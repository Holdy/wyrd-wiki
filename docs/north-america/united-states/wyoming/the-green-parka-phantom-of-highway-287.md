#title
The Green Parka Phantom of Highway 287

#summary
A woman driving late at night near Jeffrey City repeatedly encounters a mysterious man in a heavy coat who seemingly teleports alongside the highway before stepping directly into the path of her car. Local legend indicates this apparition has haunted the route since the era of stagecoaches.

#details
While driving home from Denver late at night, a woman passing through Jeffrey City noticed a dark, hunched figure walking through the sagebrush toward the road. Approximately half a mile down the highway, she spotted the same man again, now closer and clearly wearing a large green parka. After traveling another half mile, she reached over to wake her passenger to share driving duties; as her friend stirred, the identical man materialized right at the edge of the road, stepping across the white line beside their moving vehicle. Terrified, they accelerated away and later learned that encounters with this hitchhiking phantom have been reported along the route since stagecoach days.

#location
Highway 287 just outside Jeffrey City, Wyoming, along the historic route over South Pass.

The Ghost of Emily's Bridge

Summary

A jilted lover is said to have taken her life inside a 19th-century covered bridge in Stowe, Vermont, where her angry spirit now haunts visitors.

Details

In the mid-19th century, a young woman named Emily was scheduled to meet her fiancé at the Gold Brook Bridge in Stowe to elope. When her lover failed to show up, the heartbroken Emily allegedly hanged herself from the rafters of the wooden structure. In the decades that followed, the site—commonly known as Emily's Bridge—became the center of local ghostly lore. Visitors and superstitious travelers have reported hearing strange, disembodied voices echoing inside the dark tunnel, feeling sudden unnatural cold spots, and even finding mysterious, claw-like gouges scored into the sides of cars parked near or driven through the bridge.

#title
The Ghost of the Old Stagecoach Inn

#summary
Built in 1826 as a private residence and later operating as an inn in Waterbury, the historic building is said to be haunted by former residents whose lingering presence manifests as flickering lights, self-activating room fixtures, and odd nocturnal noises.

#details
The Old Stagecoach Inn, located at 19 N. Main St. in Waterbury, Vermont, dates back to 1826 when it was constructed as a private home. Over the generations, the building transitioned into an inn, but former residents and guests have reportedly left an enduring imprint. Guests and staff have recounted numerous inexplicable occurrences over the years, such as rocking chairs moving on their own, cabinet doors swinging open, window shades shifting, and lights flickering or turning on spontaneously in empty rooms. While modern innkeepers remain skeptical, visitors continue to report feeling an unseen presence and experiencing unexplained paranormal phenomena throughout the historic building.

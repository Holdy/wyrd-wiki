The Ghostly Guests of the Equinox Hotel

Summary

Standing since 1769 in Manchester, Vermont, the historic Equinox Hotel is said to be haunted by former guests and staff, including First Lady Mary Todd Lincoln and a former owner who met a tragic end on the property.

Details

Operating in various forms since Marsh Tavern opened in 1769, the Equinox Hotel in Manchester has hosted numerous U.S. presidents and notable figures. Among its most famous spectral residents is First Lady Mary Todd Lincoln, who stayed at the hotel in the summer of 1864 with her sons. She loved her stay so much that guests and staff claim her spirit—along with the plaintive sounds of her young son Tad—still lingers in the historic halls. Additionally, the hotel's long-time owner George Orvis, who tragically drowned in the nearby Equinox Pond after hitting his head in 1917, is believed by staff to haunt the building and grounds he cherished in life.

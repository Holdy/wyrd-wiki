The Legend of Emily's Bridge

Summary

A young woman heartbroken after her lover failed to meet her for their elopement is said to have hanged herself from a historic covered bridge in Stowe, where her restless spirit now haunts travelers and vehicles.

Details

Built in 1844, the Gold Brook Covered Bridge in Stowe Hollow spans Gold Brook and is widely known as Emily's Bridge. According to local folklore, a young woman named Emily arranged to meet her beloved at the bridge to elope against her family's wishes. When he failed to show up, a distraught Emily allegedly hanged herself from the bridge's rafters. Over the decades, visitors and paranormal investigators have reported various eerie occurrences at the site, including physical touches, scratches appearing on cars driving across at night, unexplained equipment failures, cold spots, and sounds of whispering or crying echoing through the wooden interior.

The Dog-Loving Ghost of Brantview House

Summary

Brantview House, a historic mansion on the campus of St. Johnsbury Academy in Vermont, is haunted by a polite, dog-loving phantom who interacts with household pets and startles residents.

Details

Once a grand private residence and now part of St. Johnsbury Academy, Brantview House carries a longstanding local reputation for paranormal activity. On one notable evening recounted in Vermont folklore, the house mother was reading in the parlor while her Afghan hound rested at her feet. Suddenly, the dog began snarling as its fur was ruffled by an invisible hand. Recognizing the presence, the dog quickly relaxed and watched as an unseen visitor walked across the room and politely closed the door behind them before departing.

The Green Man of the Forest

Summary

A tall, moss-covered green humanoid is said to protect the Southern Ohio woods and has been glimpsed darting between trees.

Details

Deep in the forests of Southern Ohio, there are tales of a creature known as the Green Man, described as a tall, green-skinned humanoid covered in moss and leaves who protects the woods and scares off trespassers. Hikers and hunters have reported strange noises, sudden movements, and glimpses of a green figure darting between trees.

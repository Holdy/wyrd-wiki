#title
The Haunting of La Fonda Hotel

#summary
The historic La Fonda Hotel in Santa Fe is haunted by several restless spirits, most notably a distraught salesman who leapt into a well after losing his company's funds.

#details
Standing at the termination of the historic Santa Fe Trail, the site of the La Fonda Hotel has hosted travelers and inns since the early 1600s. Over the centuries, the location witnessed numerous violent episodes, including lynchings and the 1867 fatal shooting of Territorial Supreme Court Chief Justice John P. Slough in the lobby. Over a century ago, a traveling salesman who lost all of his company's money in a card game became so distraught that he jumped to his death down a deep well located just outside the gambling hall. Today, the hotel's La Plazuela dining room sits directly over that old well. Guests and staff have frequently reported seeing a ghostly figure walk to the center of the room, re-enact the fatal jump, and vanish into the floorboards. Other reported apparitions include a tall man in a long black coat who wanders the upper hallways and disappears into stairwells.

#location
La Fonda Hotel is located at 100 E. San Francisco Street in the historic downtown district of Santa Fe, New Mexico.

The Mad Trucker of the Devil's Highway

Summary

Drivers traveling late at night along a remote desert highway in New Mexico have reported terrifying encounters with a relentless, speeding semi-truck piloted by a deranged phantom driver.

Details

Spanning the barren stretches of what was once designated as U.S. Route 666 (now U.S. Route 491) in New Mexico, motorists often find themselves driving alone in the dark when a massive semi-truck suddenly appears. Moving at breakneck speeds well over 100 miles per hour, the truck straddles the center of the asphalt, barreling directly toward oncoming vehicles. According to persistent local legends, the phantom vehicle is driven by a homicidally insane trucker—sometimes described as wreathed in flames or driven by malevolent intent—who forces terrified drivers off the road before vanishing into the night.

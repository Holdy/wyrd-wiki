The Spectral Mistress of the Staab House

Summary

The historic Staab Mansion at La Posada Hotel in Santa Fe is said to be haunted by Julia Staab, who fell into a profound depression following the death of her newborn child and lived as a recluse until her death in 1896.

Details

Abraham and Julia Staab built a magnificent three-story French Second Empire mansion in 1882 following Abraham's accumulation of wealth supplying the U.S. Army during the Civil War. Their lives took a tragic turn when their seventh child died shortly after birth, plunging Julia into a lifelong grief that resulted in her remaining isolated on the upper floor until she passed away at age 52. Ever since, staff and guests have reported sightings of Julia's translucent figure near fireplaces and grand rooms, along with poltergeist activity such as flying drinking glasses, self-activating fireplaces, and waitstaff being pushed from underneath their serving trays.

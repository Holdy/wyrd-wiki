#title
Sightings of the Ohio Grassman
#summary
Witnesses over decades have reported sightings of a large, hairy humanoid known as the Ohio Grassman roaming forests, accompanied by strange footprints and eerie howls.
#details
The Ohio Grassman, a creature similar to Bigfoot, is described as a large, hairy humanoid roaming the forests. Sightings date back decades, with witnesses reporting strange footprints and eerie howls.

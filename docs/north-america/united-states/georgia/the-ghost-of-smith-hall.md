#title
The Ghost of Smith Hall

#summary
Mortally wounded during the Civil War, a young soldier named John J. Griffen, Jr. died while seeking refuge at LaGrange College, and his restless spirit continues to haunt the building's halls.

#details
During the Civil War, John J. Griffen, Jr. suffered a battle wound near Newnan, Georgia. Deciding to bypass an overcrowded military hospital, he traveled to LaGrange to seek medical care and shelter from his sister, Indiana, who was a student at the academy. Despite her nursing, his injuries proved fatal, and he passed away in the building's guest room. In the years since, students and college officials have reported frequent paranormal phenomena inside Smith Hall, including sudden freezing temperatures, being tripped on staircases, and hearing a soft voice echoing through the corridors, calling out the name "Indiana."

#location
Smith Hall stands on the campus of LaGrange College in LaGrange, Georgia.

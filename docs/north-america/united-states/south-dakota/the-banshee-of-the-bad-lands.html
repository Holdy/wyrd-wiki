The Banshee of the Bad Lands

Summary

Travelers passing Watchdog Butte after dark are haunted by a weeping, phantom woman who vanishes with a chilling shriek. She is frequently accompanied by a ghostly skeleton that lures unsuspecting listeners away with unearthly fiddle music.

Details

In the rugged, labyrinthine expanse of the South Dakota Badlands, near the geographic feature known as Watchdog Butte, terrifying nocturnal apparitions have long been reported. By moonlight, a mournful female banshee is frequently seen standing on a nearby hilltop, her hair blowing and arms tossing in desperate gestures. Whenever travelers, cowboys, or hunters pass the haunted butte at night, the surrounding rocks flash with phosphorescent light and the specter sweeps toward them as if trying to speak. If anyone asks what she wants, she throws her arms aloft and vanishes with a blood-curdling shriek that echoes across the gullies. According to local lore, this weeping spirit is sometimes joined by a glowing, fleshless skeleton that wanders through the ash and clay searching for music. If provided with a violin, the skeleton plays haunting melodies through the night, but anyone foolish enough to follow the spectral musician is led into inescapable rocky pitfalls where their soul is charmed away.

#title
The Shadow of Spook Road

#summary
Drivers venturing down a lonely, tree-canopied road at night frequently report being followed by a dark, ghostly figure and spotting apparitions hanging from the trees. According to local lore, the haunted stretch is also tied to occult activity and shifting geography.

#details
Spook Road, officially known as 264th Street, is a narrow, three-mile stretch of dirt road winding through heavily wooded areas south of Brandon, South Dakota. For decades, nighttime drivers have treated traveling the serpentine road as a chilling rite of passage. Legend holds that a dark spirit or shadowy human figure can be seen following motorists as they drive west from the local graveyard. Other terrifying accounts describe ghostly figures hanging from the ancient cottonwood trees overhead. Additionally, drivers report eerie anomalies such as bridges and intersections appearing to change count depending on the direction of travel.

#location
264th Street (commonly known as Spook Road), located just south of Brandon in Splitrock Township, South Dakota.

#title
The Haunting of the Rookery

#summary
The oldest standing residence on Fort Leavenworth is said to be haunted by multiple spectral entities, most notably a violent, long-haired woman who claws at visitors.

#details
Built in 1832 on Summer Place, the duplex known as "The Rookery" has been continuously occupied since its construction and is reputed to be one of the most haunted houses in Kansas. Residents over the decades have reported encounters with a variety of ghosts, including an older man in a nightshirt who disturbs sleeping guests, a chattering older woman, a crying child, and a menacing long-haired woman who rushes forward to scratch and claw at anyone entering her space.

#location
The Rookery duplex at 12 and 14 Summer Place on Fort Leavenworth, Kansas.

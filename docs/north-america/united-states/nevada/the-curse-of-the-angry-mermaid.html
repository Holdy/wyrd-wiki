The Curse of the Angry Mermaid

Summary

An enraged mermaid places an ancient curse on a tribe after being unjustly banished from their village. The hex brings generations of conflict, violent storms, and unending misfortune to the region.

Details

A Paiute warrior fell in love with a mermaid and brought her back to his village to marry her, but the tribe demanded he immediately return the strange creature to the deep waters. Enraged by her sudden banishment, the mermaid placed a curse upon the people. This ancient hex is said to have fueled the bloodshed of the Pyramid Lake War and continues to manifest today through sudden turbulence, eerie water baby legends, mysterious drownings, and lingering bad luck for those connected to the lake.

The Lady in Red

Summary

A glamorous woman known as the Lady in Red was murdered on the fifth floor of the Mizpah Hotel in the 1920s by a jealous lover or husband. Her spirit continues to roam the historic corridors, occasionally leaving pearls on guests' pillows.

Details

Opened in 1907 during Tonopah's silver mining boom, the Mizpah Hotel is widely celebrated as one of America's most haunted hotels. Its most infamous resident spirit is the Lady in Red, who operated as a charismatic prostitute or courtesan in the hotel during the 1920s. According to legend, a wealthy patron discovered she had numerous other clients and, in a fit of jealous rage, stabbed and killed her on the fifth floor between rooms 502 and 504. Another variation of the tale claims her husband discovered her infidelity after missing a train and beat her to death in the hotel room. To this day, guests staying on the fifth floor report seeing a spectral woman in a red dress wandering the hallways and the elevator shaft. Astonished visitors have occasionally discovered unexplained pearls left behind on their pillows or nightstands after reporting encounters with her.

The Millionaires' Club Haunting

Summary

Once an elite social sanctuary for Comstock Lode millionaires, the historic Washoe Club in Virginia City now harbors restless spirits, including a mysterious Lady in Blue and a thieving old prospector. Its dark history includes storing winter corpses in its back crypt and tragic nineteenth-century deaths.

Details

Founded in 1875 on B Street and later relocated to C Street after a devastating fire, the Washoe Club originally served as an opulent, exclusive retreat for mining barons, intellectuals, and political dignitaries during the height of Nevada's silver bonanza. Following the decline of the Comstock mines, the club closed in 1897, but its upper floors remained largely untouched for over a century. Because the frozen winter ground in Virginia City prevented gravediggers from burying the dead, the building's back crypt was famously used as a holding area for corpses, cementing its grim reputation. Today, visitors and paranormal investigators frequent the saloon and museum, reporting chilling encounters with the "Lady in Blue"—who manifests at the top of the club's famous unsupported spiral staircase—alongside a phantom prospector who steals unattended drinks, swinging doors, and mysteriously tipping barstools.

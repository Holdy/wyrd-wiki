The Hauntingly Impeccable Spirits of Mackay Mansion

Summary

Built during the Comstock Lode mining boom, the historic Mackay Mansion is haunted by multiple restless spirits, including a tidiness-obsessed former maid named Harriet and the apparition of a young girl.

Details

Constructed in 1859 in Virginia City, Nevada, the Mackay Mansion served as the headquarters and home for mining barons like John Mackay. Today, the grand Victorian estate is famous for its active paranormal presence. Among its most notable spectral residents is Harriet, a former maid whose devotion to cleanliness is so enduring that her ghostly presence is said to intensify whenever a room is left in disorder. Other reported spirits include giggling children on the third floor, shadowy figures, and the apparition of John Mackay himself. Notably, actor Johnny Depp reported waking up during a 1995 filming stay to see the ghostly figure of a young girl in white standing at the foot of his bed.

The Specter of Room 109 at the Goldfield Hotel

Summary

Visitors and paranormal investigators report encountering the sorrowful apparition of a woman named Elizabeth, who was allegedly chained to a radiator in Room 109 during the town's mining boom. Alongside her, other restless spirits—including prankster children and a knife-wielding entity known as "The Stabber"—continue to haunt the decaying hotel.

Details

Built in 1908 during Nevada’s gold rush, the luxury Goldfield Hotel eventually fell into disuse as the mines dried up and the town became nearly deserted. According to local lore, a woman named Elizabeth was locked and chained in Room 109 by wealthy mining magnate George Wingfield after she became pregnant with his child, meeting a tragic end either during childbirth or by murder. Today, guests and paranormal researchers report that Room 109 is intensely cold and resists photographic equipment. Other paranormal phenomena reported throughout the four-story brick building include phantom cigar smoke attributed to Wingfield himself, unseen prankster children near the lobby staircase, and a menacing entity called "The Stabber" who brandishes a kitchen knife in the grand dining room before disappearing.

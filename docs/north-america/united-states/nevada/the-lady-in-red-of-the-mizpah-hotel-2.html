The Lady in Red of the Mizpah Hotel

Summary

Guests staying on the upper floors of the historic Mizpah Hotel frequently encounter a ghostly woman in red who whispers into men's ears and leaves behind pearls. Alongside her gentle presence, phantom children running through the halls and miners lingering in the basement haunt the landmark.

Details

Opened in 1907 in the silver mining hub of Tonopah, Nevada, the Mizpah Hotel was designed as a luxurious haven for prospectors and travelers. However, its history hides a tragic tale concerning a resident woman dubbed the "Lady in Red"—often said to have been a kept mistress or working woman who met a violent, fatal end when she was strangled by a jealous lover, scattering pearls across the floor. Ever since, visitors on the fifth floor report uncanny encounters, including soft whispers in the antique elevator and small pearls discovered mysteriously beneath pillows. Additionally, guests and hotel staff frequently hear the sound of phantom children running down the third-floor corridors and sense miners wandering the subterranean spaces near the old silver mines.

The Spirits of the Squirrel Cage Jail

Summary

A historic rotary jail in Council Bluffs is said to be haunted by the spirits of former jailers, prisoners, and a mournful little girl.

Details

Built in 1885 and operating until 1969, the unique three-story revolving cell structure in Council Bluffs has long been reported to host strange paranormal phenomena. Staff and visitors have reported disembodied footsteps, doors opening on their own, cold spots, and the apparition of a mournful little girl locked inside empty cells. Some believe the hauntings stem from the building's site on an old church morgue, or from former caretakers and inmates who left behind a lingering presence.

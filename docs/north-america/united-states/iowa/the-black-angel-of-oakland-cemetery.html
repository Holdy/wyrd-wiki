The Black Angel of Oakland Cemetery

Summary

An imposing bronze statue in Iowa City turned mysteriously black after its installation, giving rise to persistent folklore that anyone who touches, kisses, or vandalizes it is doomed by a fatal curse. Over the years, numerous local legends claimed that crossing the monument brings about sudden death or supernatural retribution.

Details

Commissioned in 1913 by Teresa Feldevert to honor her late son and husband, a towering bronze angel statue shocked its creator and townspeople by turning a deep, eerie black shortly after arriving at Oakland Cemetery. As oxidation transformed its appearance, dark rumors took root that the monument carried a malignant curse. Over the decades, local lore warned that kissing the statue would stop a person's heart, touching it on Halloween would bring death within seven years, and any attempt to vandalize or deface the figure would result in violent, mysterious retribution.

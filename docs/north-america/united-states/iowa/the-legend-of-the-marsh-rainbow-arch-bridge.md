#title
The Legend of the Marsh Rainbow Arch Bridge

#summary
A historic arched bridge south of Lake City is said to be haunted by an invisible entity with an insatiable appetite for chocolate.

#details
Spanning the North Raccoon River, the Marsh Rainbow Arch Bridge dates back to 1914 and features a distinctive arched profile. According to local folklore, the bridge is visited by a spirit that loves chocolate; local legend holds that if an unopened bar of chocolate is left in the middle of the bridge at midnight, the chocolate will vanish within five minutes while leaving the outer wrapper completely intact.

#location
The Marsh Rainbow Arch Bridge spans the North Raccoon River just south of Lake City, Iowa.

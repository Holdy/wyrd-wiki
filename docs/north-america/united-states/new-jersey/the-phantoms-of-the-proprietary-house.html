The Phantoms of the Proprietary House

Summary

Visitors on haunted history tours of the colonial Proprietary House in Perth Amboy encounter the restless specters of Revolutionary War figures and former residents.

Details

Standing at 149 Kearny Avenue in Perth Amboy, the Proprietary House is a historic colonial governor's mansion dating back to the pre-Revolutionary era. Over the centuries, the grand brick building has gained a reputation for intense paranormal activity, with tour guides and visitors reporting encounters with the scary specters of Governor and Mrs. Franklin, local celebrity William Dunlap, a frightful ghost of a Revolutionary War soldier, and old Matt Bruen. Specific focal points of the hauntings include the colonial brick wine cellar—rumored to have been used as a dungeon during the Revolution—the haunted staircase where a bloody murder allegedly took place, and dining room corners where eccentric former occupants still linger.

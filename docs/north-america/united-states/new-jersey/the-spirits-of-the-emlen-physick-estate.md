#title
The Spirits of the Emlen Physick Estate
#summary
Cape May's iconic 1879 Victorian mansion is haunted by the lingering presence of its original occupants, including unexplained footsteps, cold spots, and sightings of a woman in white.
#details
The Emlen Physick Estate is an 18-room Victorian mansion built in 1879 by architect Frank Furness for Dr. Emlen Physick, who lived there with his mother Frances and aunt Emilie until his death in 1916. After falling into disrepair and nearly facing demolition in the 1970s, the estate was preserved and turned into a museum, but visitors, staff, and paranormal investigators quickly noted that the house felt remarkably inhabited. The most persistent legend involves a "Woman in White"—believed to be Dr. Physick's mother, Frances—who is frequently spotted looking out upper-floor windows or drifting down hallways. Other reported phenomena include disembodied footsteps pacing empty staircases, the murmur of indistinct voices, sudden temperature drops, and an intense feeling of being watched in vacant rooms.
#location
The Emlen Physick Estate, located at 1048 Washington Street in Cape May, New Jersey.

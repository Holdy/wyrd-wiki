The Ghost Boy of Clinton Road

Summary

Motorists who stop at the bridge on Clinton Road and throw coins into the creek are said to have their coins tossed back by the spirit of a young boy who died there.

Details

Winding through the isolated wilderness of West Milford, New Jersey, Clinton Road is heavily laden with local folklore, of which the legend of the ghost boy is the most famous. According to accounts passed down among locals and late-night visitors, a young boy tragically died either by drowning or being struck by a car near the bridge at Dead Man's Curve. It is believed that his spirit lingers beneath the bridge in the dark, tea-colored waters of the stream. Drivers who stop their vehicles on the bridge and toss coins into the creek report hearing a splash or seeing ripples, followed by the coins being mysteriously thrown back or hurled against their car windows.

The Ghost Boy of Dead Man's Curve

Summary

Travelers who stop at midnight on Clinton Road and throw coins into the water beneath the bridge will have them returned by the spirit of a deceased young boy.

Details

According to local legend, a young boy tragically died near the bridge on Clinton Road. Visitors who park their vehicles at the bridge located at Dead Man's Curve and toss coins like pennies or quarters into the stream below can experience the phenomenon firsthand; shortly after tossing the money, they hear a splash or have coins thrown back at them or against their car by the unseen phantom child.

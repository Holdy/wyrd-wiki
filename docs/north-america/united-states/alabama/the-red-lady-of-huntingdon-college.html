The Red Lady of Huntingdon College

Summary

An isolated student who was sent away to college against her will tragically died in her dorm room, leaving behind a lingering spirit that still haunts the historic campus hall.

Details

Martha, a young woman from New York, was unwillingly sent by her parents to Huntingdon College in Montgomery, where she struggled deeply with loneliness and isolation. She became well known on campus for her distinct fondness for the color red, frequently wearing a red robe and decorating her living quarters with red accents. Eventually, the unhappy student was found dead in her fourth-floor dormitory room in Pratt Hall, dressed in her red robe and lying upon a red carpet. Ever since, students and staff have reported eerie encounters and sightings of the "Red Lady" wandering the upper floors of Pratt Hall, tethered forever to the site of her tragic youth.

#title
The Bell Witch Haunting

#summary
Between 1817 and 1821, a farming family in Middle Tennessee was subjected to relentless physical and verbal harassment by an invisible, shapeshifting entity.

#details
Starting in 1817 on the Bell family farm near the Red River in Adams, Tennessee, patriarch John Bell Sr. encountered a strange animal that vanished into thin air. This sighting was followed by eerie noises inside the family home, including knocking, gnawing sounds, and dragging chains. The unseen entity—later dubbed "Kate" or the Bell Witch—became increasingly aggressive, pulling hair, slapping, and pinching family members, particularly John Bell and his daughter Betsy. The spirit eventually learned to speak fluently, holding conversations, singing hymns, and famously reciting two different sermons word-for-word from miles away at the exact same time. The torment culminated in the mysterious poisoning and death of John Bell in December 1820, after which the entity gradually quieted and vanished.

#location
The historic Bell family farm and cave property located along the Red River in Adams, Robertson County, Tennessee.

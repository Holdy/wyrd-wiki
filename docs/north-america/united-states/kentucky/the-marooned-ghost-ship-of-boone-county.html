The Marooned Ghost Ship of Boone County

Summary

An abandoned 120-year-old steam-powered vessel with a history spanning luxury, war, and sightseeing now rusts away in a secluded creek in Kentucky.

Details

Built in 1902 as the luxury yacht Celt, the 175-foot steam-powered ship was repurposed by the US Navy during World War I as the USS Sachem to sink German U-boats, and again in World War II as the USS Phenakite. After the wars, it served as a sightseeing vessel in New York City before being abandoned in the 1970s. In 1985, a Cincinnati man named Robert "Butch" Miller purchased the massive boat, repaired it, and navigated it to a small waterway off the Ohio River in Boone County, Kentucky, where it remains today in a dilapidated and perilous state.

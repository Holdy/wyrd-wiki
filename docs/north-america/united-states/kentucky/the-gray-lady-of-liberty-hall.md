#title
The Gray Lady of Liberty Hall

#summary
Following her unexpected death in 1817 and subsequent reburial in an unmarked grave, Margaretta Varick's spirit began appearing as a gray-veiled apparition. She is frequently sighted wandering her family's historic estate in Frankfort.

#details
In the summer of 1817, Margaretta Varick traveled from New York to visit her niece at Liberty Hall. Just three days into her visit, she fell ill and died at the age of 73. Unable to afford the cost of returning her body to New York or placing a gravestone, the family buried her on the property before later moving her remains to an unmarked plot in the Frankfort Cemetery. 

Decades later, in the 1880s, family member Mary Mason "Mame" Scott woke up to see a tall figure veiled in gray standing at the end of her bed in the exact room where Aunt Varick had passed away. Since then, generations of residents, museum staff, and visitors have reported encountering the "Gray Lady." Known as a kind and playful spirit, she is seen descending the stairs, looking out the Palladian window, rocking chairs, and occasionally misplacing guests' keys and belongings.

#location
Liberty Hall Historic Site is situated at 202 Wilkinson Street in Frankfort, Kentucky.

#title
The Lady of the Stairs

#summary
A young woman known as "Miss G" reportedly paces the stone steps of an Old Louisville church at dusk, eternally waiting for a fiancé who never returned from World War I.

#details
In 1918, a prominent young woman from Bardstown known as "Miss G" fell in love with a dashing soldier. When her parents tried to arrange a marriage with a wealthier, older man, they sent her to live with relatives in Old Louisville. Her fiancé, having survived the war, re-enlisted and was stationed at nearby Camp Zachary Taylor so they could secretly meet outside an ice cream parlor and plan their elopement. However, before they could run away together, the young soldier contracted the Spanish Flu and was quarantined, passing away days later without ever notifying her. Miss G, waiting on the stone steps of the church through the chilly nights with a small suitcase, also contracted the flu and died shortly after. Locals say her heartbroken specter continues to pace the steps in the evening, waiting for her soldier to arrive.

#location
The First Church of Christ Scientist at the corner of Third Street and Ormsby Avenue in Old Louisville, Louisville, Kentucky.

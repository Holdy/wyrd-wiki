#title
The Friendly Ghost of Old Richardsville Road Bridge

#summary
Motorists traversing a historic bridge at night report that the spirit of a tragic young woman will physically push neutral vehicles across to safety. According to local legend, she died in the waters below after becoming pregnant and falling into despair.

#details
Local folklore surrounding the bridge tells of a young woman who, facing an unplanned pregnancy and overwhelming shame, either flung herself into the creek or accidentally drove off the structure into the water below. Generations of residents and curious night-time visitors have shared accounts of encountering her benevolent spirit. The most famous ritual associated with the site involves drivers stopping on the bridge at night, putting their car in neutral, and feeling the phantom push their vehicle all the way across to prevent them from meeting the same tragic fate. Other variations of the legend claim that leaving a single rose on the windshield during a storm will reveal her hand as she takes it.

#location
The Old Richardsville Road Bridge is a historic bowstring truss bridge located on Old Richardsville Road on the northern outskirts of Bowling Green, Kentucky.

#title
The Curse of Frozen Creek

#summary
Frozen Creek and the grounds of the former Frozen Inn in Breathitt County are bound by a grim legacy of lawlessness and a deadly 1939 flood. The valley remains spiritually tethered to its tragic past, manifesting as phantom cries, glowing orbs, and restless apparitions along the water.

#details
Nestled in the Appalachian hollers of Breathitt County, Kentucky, Frozen Creek holds a notorious reputation rooted in violence and natural disaster. The historic site of the Frozen Inn once operated as a lawless roadhouse where outlaws and bootleggers engaged in deadly gunfights, spilling blood that soaked the local soil. This atmospheric dread was amplified exponentially by a devastating flood in 1939 that swept through the valley, abruptly ending the lives of nearly forty individuals. Local folklore maintains that these accumulated tragedies formed an enduring curse over the watershed. Today, residents and travelers report eerie phenomena throughout the area, including phantom footsteps, mysterious glowing orbs, and sorrowful cries echoing near the creek banks and the old Frozen Creek School grounds.

#location
Frozen Creek flows through Breathitt County in eastern Kentucky, passing the historic site of the former Frozen Inn along its banks.

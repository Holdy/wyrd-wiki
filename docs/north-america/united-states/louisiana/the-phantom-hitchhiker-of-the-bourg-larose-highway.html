The Phantom Hitchhiker of the Bourg-Larose Highway

Summary

Motorists traveling a lonely Louisiana highway late at night frequently encounter a young woman in white thumbing for a ride who mysteriously vanishes before reaching her destination. This eerie apparition is a deeply ingrained piece of regional folklore in Lafourche Parish.

Details

For decades, drivers traversing the Bourg-Larose Highway between the communities of Bourg and Larose in Lafourche Parish, Louisiana, have reported seeing a young woman standing by the roadside at night, signaling for a hitch. Some compassionate or frightened motorists pull over to let her into their vehicles. Once inside, she either remains completely silent or gives vague directions before abruptly disappearing into thin air while the car is still moving, leaving behind a cold seat or the faint scent of flowers. Other sightings describe her standing near the bayou or vanishing into the shadows of the cypress trees, rooted in regional Cajun and Creole folklore regarding restless spirits.

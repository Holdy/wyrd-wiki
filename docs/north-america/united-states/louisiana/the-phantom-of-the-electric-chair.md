#title
The Phantom of the Electric Chair

#summary
Following her historic 1942 execution in the courthouse basement, the restless spirit of Toni Jo Henry is said to haunt the halls with inexplicable cold spots, flickering lights, and phantom noises. As Louisiana's only executed female inmate, her lingering presence remains a fixture of local lore.

#details
Toni Jo Henry stood trial three times before being sentenced to death by Louisiana's electric chair in November 1942—becoming the first and only woman executed in the state's electric chair, which was housed in the Calcasieu Parish Courthouse in Lake Charles. Ever since her execution, staff and visitors have reported eerie phenomena throughout the building, including sudden cold spots, fluttering lights, mysterious noises, and even the faint scent of burning hair. Local historians and courthouse employees take these unexplained occurrences as the undeniable mark of an infamous outlaw refusing to be silenced.

#location
The Historic Calcasieu Courthouse is located at 320 Division Street in downtown Lake Charles, Louisiana.

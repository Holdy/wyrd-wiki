#title
The Wandering Sailor of St. Louis Cemetery No. 1

#summary
A 19th-century sailor whose family tomb was sold while he was at sea wanders the labyrinth of New Orleans' oldest cemetery searching for his final resting place.

#details
Henry Vignes was a nomadic 19th-century sailor who entrusted his boarding house owner with his papers and tomb deeds before departing on a voyage. Upon returning to New Orleans, he discovered she had sold his family tomb; shortly thereafter, he fell ill and died, being buried in an unmarked pauper's grave. Visitors to St. Louis Cemetery No. 1 have reported encountering his tall, blue-eyed apparition as he roams the above-ground crypts asking if anyone knows where the Vignes tomb is located.

#location
St. Louis Cemetery No. 1 is located at Basin Street and St. Louis Street in New Orleans, Louisiana.

The Apparitions of the Vieux Carré Stage

Summary

Behind the curtains of a historic New Orleans theater, staff frequently encounter spectral figures, including a phantom patron in a captain's hat and a shadowy colonial-era apparition. These lingering manifestations are believed to be the residual energy of artists and patrons who never truly left the spotlight.

Details

Operating for over a century in the French Quarter, Le Petit Théâtre de Vieux Carré is steeped in theatrical superstition and ghostly encounters. Late-night stage crews and technical directors have reported seeing unearthly figures vanish into thin air, such as a man wearing a captain's hat sitting quietly in the upper balcony before disappearing. Other encounters include a spectral apparition in colonial-era clothing rushing directly through staff members backstage, and a mysterious woman in white frequently seen gazing plaintively out of the courtyard windows.

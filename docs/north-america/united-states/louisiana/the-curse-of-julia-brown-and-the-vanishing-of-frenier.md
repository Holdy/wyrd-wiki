#title
The Curse of Julia Brown and the Vanishing of Frenier

#summary
Before passing away, a local healer named Julia Brown placed a fatal curse on her village out of resentment for feeling unappreciated. Shortly after her funeral, a devastating 1915 hurricane struck, wiping the settlement of Frenier entirely off the map.

#details
Julia Brown was a well-known healer and midwife who lived in the small timber and rail village of Frenier, situated along the western rim of Lake Pontchartrain in Louisiana. Feeling ignored and unappreciated by her neighbors, she composed a chilling song declaring that when she died, she would take the entire town down with her. In September 1915, Julia passed away, and as mourners gathered at her funeral under heavy skies, a massive hurricane roared ashore. The catastrophic storm surge and fierce winds obliterated Frenier and neighboring Ruddock, submerging the settlements into the Manchac Swamp and leaving behind only scattered pilings and the raised grounds of Frenier Cemetery.

#location
Frenier Cemetery is located on a small, elevated rise of land within the Manchac Swamp along the western rim of Lake Pontchartrain, near LaPlace, Louisiana.

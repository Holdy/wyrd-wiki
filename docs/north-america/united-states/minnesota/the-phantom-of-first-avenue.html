The Phantom of First Avenue

Summary

Housed in a former Greyhound bus depot, this legendary Minneapolis music venue is said to be haunted by a distraught woman who died by suicide and a mischievous spirit named Flippy.

Details

Before transforming into Minneapolis's premier concert destination, the building at 701 First Avenue North served as a Greyhound bus station. Local folklore maintains that a heartbroken woman waiting for her husband to return from World War II learned he had died in combat, leading her to hang herself in the women's restroom. Over the years, patrons and employees have reported seeing her spirit lingering near the stalls, or dancing in the main room wearing an army jacket—only to reveal she has no legs. Other reported phenomena include disembodied moans in recording equipment, hair being pulled by unseen hands, and a playful poltergeist nicknamed "Flippy" who rearranges bar stools.

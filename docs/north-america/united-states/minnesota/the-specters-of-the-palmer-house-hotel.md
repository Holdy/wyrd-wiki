#title
The Specters of the Palmer House Hotel

#summary
Built on the ashes of a 1900 fire, the historic Palmer House Hotel in Sauk Centre has earned a reputation as a bustling hub of paranormal activity where guests and staff report phantom footsteps, shadowy apparitions, and disembodied children's laughter.

#details
Rising in 1901 after a devastating fire destroyed the original Sauk Centre House, the Palmer House Hotel quickly became a cornerstone of community life and even employed Nobel Prize-winning author Sinclair Lewis as a night clerk in his youth. Over the decades, however, the grand three-story brick building accumulated rich local lore regarding its otherworldly inhabitants. Guests and paranormal investigators in areas such as Room 11 and the subterranean basement frequently report eerie occurrences, including moving furniture, plunging temperatures, shadow figures standing at the foot of beds, and spectral children playing in the corridors in the dead of night.

#location
The Palmer House Hotel is located at the corner of Sinclair Lewis Avenue and Main Street in Sauk Centre, Minnesota.

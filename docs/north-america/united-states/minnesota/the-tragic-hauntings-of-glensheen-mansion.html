The Tragic Hauntings of Glensheen Mansion

Summary

This sprawling 20,000-square-foot English-style estate on Lake Superior is shadowed by a dark history of greed, murder, and ghostly encounters.

Details

Built between 1905 and 1908 by iron ore millionaire Chester Congdon, Duluth's magnificent Glensheen Mansion became the site of a notorious double murder on June 27, 1977, when elderly heiress Elisabeth Congdon and her night nurse, Velma Pietila, were brutally killed in a botched inheritance plot. Following the tragedy, staff and visitors have reported numerous chilling experiences throughout the 39-room estate, including sudden cold spots in the bedrooms where the murders occurred, flickering lights, mysterious objects moving on their own, and wispy, shadowy figures gliding through the grand hallways.

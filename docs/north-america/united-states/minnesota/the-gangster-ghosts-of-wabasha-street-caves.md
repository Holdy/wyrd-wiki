#title
The Gangster Ghosts of Wabasha Street Caves

#summary
Carved out of soft sandstone cliffs along the Mississippi River, these subterranean St. Paul caves hide a bloody history of mobster executions and restless spirits.

#details
Originally mined for silica sand and later used as mushroom farms and Prohibition-era speakeasies like the Castle Royal, the Wabasha Street Caves on St. Paul’s West Side were reportedly frequented by notorious gangsters such as John Dillinger and Ma Barker. According to local legend, a card game in the caves' Fireside Room turned deadly when four mobsters were ambushed and executed with a submachine gun, with their bodies supposedly spirited away by corrupt police or rival gang members. Today, visitors and staff report bullet holes still visible in the stone fireplace, along with sightings of phantom dancing couples and a forlorn woman roaming the underground chambers.

#location
The Wabasha Street Caves are located at 215 Wabasha Street South in St. Paul, Minnesota.

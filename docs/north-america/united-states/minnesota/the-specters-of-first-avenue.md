#title
The Specters of First Avenue

#summary
The legendary music venue and former Minneapolis bus depot is haunted by the spirit of a heartbroken woman who tragically took her life in the building.

#details
According to local lore circulating since the 1980s, a woman waiting for her husband to return from World War II learned of his death in combat and hanged herself in the women's restroom of what was then a Greyhound bus depot. Staff, disc jockeys, and concertgoers have reported eerie phenomena ever since, including disembodied moans in headphones, record player arms bouncing inexplicably across vinyl records, and sightings of a legless woman dancing or a weeping figure in the stalls.

#location
First Avenue is located at 701 North First Avenue in downtown Minneapolis, Minnesota.

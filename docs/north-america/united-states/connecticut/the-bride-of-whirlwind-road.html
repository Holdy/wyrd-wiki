The Bride of Whirlwind Road

Summary

A jilted bride who drowned herself in the 1930s haunts Whirlwind Road, appearing soaked on the roadside before vanishing into thin air.

Details

In the 1930s, a young woman was devastated after being left at the altar by her fiancé. Overcome by heartbreak, she ended her life by drowning. Her spirit now haunts Whirlwind Road in Wallingford, Connecticut, where drivers frequently spot her standing in the dark, entirely soaked and appearing in distress. Those who slow down or look away find that she disappears instantly, leaving behind an eerie presence that has startled motorists and local police alike.

#title
The White Lady of Union Cemetery

#summary
Motorists passing a historic Easton cemetery frequently report encountering a spectral woman in a Victorian nightgown who vanishes upon impact, while paranormal investigators claim the site is stalked by otherworldly entities.

#details
Union Cemetery in Easton is recognized as one of Connecticut’s oldest and most famously haunted burial grounds, with grave markers dating back to the 1760s. For decades, drivers along the nearby roadways have shared chilling accounts of seeing a "White Lady"—a figure dressed in a white Victorian nightgown and veil—wandering the roadside or stepping directly into the path of oncoming cars, only to vanish without a trace upon impact. Famous paranormal investigators Ed and Lorraine Warren documented numerous claims regarding the cemetery in their 1992 book *Graveyard*, asserting that the spirit's solid manifestations were amplified by electrical interference from nearby power transformers and recounting sightings of mysterious glowing red eyes in the surrounding woods.

#location
Union Cemetery is located at the junction of Route 59 and Route 136 in Easton, Connecticut.

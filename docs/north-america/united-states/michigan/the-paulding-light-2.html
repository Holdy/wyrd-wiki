The Paulding Light

Summary

A mysterious glowing orb that appears nightly in a remote valley in Michigan's Upper Peninsula has inspired decades of folklore, most famously attributed to the ghost of a railway brakeman holding a warning lantern.

Details

Since the 1960s, visitors have gathered in a remote viewing area off Robbins Pond Road near Paulding, Michigan, to witness unexplained, shimmering orbs of light dancing across a distant valley. While scientific investigations have attributed the phenomenon to refracted automobile headlights on US Highway 45, local folklore offers several supernatural explanations. The most enduring legend claims the light is the phantom lantern of a railroad brakeman who tragically died trying to prevent a train collision, while other tales attribute the glowing orb to a slain mail courier or a spectral dancer.

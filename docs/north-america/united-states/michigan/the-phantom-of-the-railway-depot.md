#title
The Phantom of the Railway Depot

#summary
Housed in an 1886 railway depot in Ann Arbor, Michigan, the Gandy Dancer restaurant is reportedly haunted by lingering spirits from its past as a transit hub and temporary holding morgue for fallen soldiers, with staff experiencing flying glassware and shadowy figures.

#details
Originally constructed in 1886 as the Michigan Central Railroad Depot, the grand building was a bustling center of travel. According to historical accounts shared by long-time employees, the depot was also used during World War I to temporarily hold the bodies of fallen soldiers being shipped home to their families for burial. Decades later, converted into a restaurant named after railroad section hands, diners and staff have encountered bizarre, unexplainable phenomena. Lights have reportedly turned upside down, glasses have flown off shelves without physical cause, and witnesses have seen a well-dressed gentlemanly apparition wandering the historic dining rooms.

#location
The Gandy Dancer restaurant is located at 401 Depot St, Ann Arbor, MI 48104, near the edge of downtown Ann Arbor.

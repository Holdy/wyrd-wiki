The Red Rug of Agnes Felt

Summary

Agnes Felt, who passed away just six weeks after moving into her luxurious summer estate in Holland, Michigan, is said to still haunt the halls, leaving behind eerie phenomena such as a mysteriously relocated rug in her former bedroom.

Details

Built between 1925 and 1928 by self-made millionaire and inventor Dorr E. Felt as a summer retreat for his wife Agnes, the magnificent estate on the dunes of Lake Michigan was meant to be a place of leisure. However, Agnes fell ill and died just six weeks after the family moved in. Over the decades, as the mansion changed hands—serving as a prep school, a convent, and later a state prison—it fell into abandonment before restoration efforts began. During early restoration work, staff laid a red rug in Agnes's former bedroom, only to return the next day to find it rolled up and pushed neatly into a corner. When straightened out, the rug was found rolled up in the corner again later that same day. Since then, staff and visitors have reported inexplicable sounds, cold spots, and fleeting apparitions.

#title
The Spirits of Battle Alley

#summary
The historic Holly Hotel in Michigan is reputed to be haunted by several permanent spectral residents, including its original innkeeper and a playful young girl.

#details
Built on Battle Alley in the early 1890s, the Holly Hotel survived two major fires and has long been regarded as one of Michigan's most active haunted locations. Paranormal investigators and guests have frequently reported encountering Mr. Hirst, the original hotelier, who manifests through the scent of cigar smoke, baritone laughter, and occasional top-hatted apparitions. Other noted spirits include Nora Kane, a gracious hostess who enjoys playing the piano and leaves behind the scent of flowery perfume, as well as an anonymous young girl who tinkers with kitchen utensils and meat cleavers.

#location
The Holly Hotel is located at 110 Battle Alley in Holly, Michigan.

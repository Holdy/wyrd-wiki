The Gray Man of Pawleys Island

Summary

A spectral figure in a gray coat wanders the beaches and sand dunes of coastal South Carolina to warn residents of impending hurricanes. Those who heed his sudden appearance and evacuate are rewarded with minimal property damage.

Details

According to local coastal lore dating back to the 19th century, the Gray Man is believed to be the spirit of a young man named Beauregard who tragically sank into quicksand and died while rushing on horseback to visit his fiancée. Over the decades, visitors walking the shoreline during hurricane season have reported encountering a silent figure of medium build dressed entirely in gray, who abruptly vanishes into the sea mist and leaves behind no footprints other than the observer's. Sightings of the phantom have frequently preceded devastating storms, most notably Hurricane Hazel, with homeowners who heeded the warning returning to find their properties miraculously spared amid widespread devastation.

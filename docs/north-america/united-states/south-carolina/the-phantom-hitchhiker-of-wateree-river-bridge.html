The Phantom Hitchhiker of Wateree River Bridge

Summary

Since the 1930s, a young woman has been seen hitchhiking on a bridge over the Wateree River, engaging drivers in conversation before vanishing just before reaching the end of her journey.

Details

Dating back to the 1930s when the bridge was newly built, motorists traveling westbound across the Wateree River Swamp Bridge have frequently encountered a young female hitchhiker. Looking to catch a ride to Columbia to check on her ill mother, the woman appears completely solid and engages in normal, pleasant conversation with her driver. However, right before the vehicle reaches the end of the bridge, she inexplicably disappears from the passenger seat, doomed to repeat her journey over and over again through the decades.

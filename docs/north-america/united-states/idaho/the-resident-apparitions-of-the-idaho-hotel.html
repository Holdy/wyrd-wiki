The Resident Apparitions of the Idaho Hotel

Summary

Built during the 1860s mining boom and relocated to Silver City, Idaho, the historic Idaho Hotel is said to be haunted by a woman in white known as Screaming Alice, a Victorian gentleman, and children playing in its upper hallways.

Details

Originally constructed in Ruby City in 1863 before being dismantled and reassembled piece by piece in Silver City in 1866, the Idaho Hotel has served as the social and emotional heart of the Owyhee Mountain mining district for over a century. According to local lore and visitor accounts, the weathered wooden building is haunted by several resident spirits. Among them is "Screaming Alice," an apparition in a white dress frequently associated with Room 27 and the adjoining south saloon, where a former owner tragically took his own life.

#title
The Resilient Spirits of the Idaho Hotel

#summary
Nestled in the historic ghost town of Silver City, the 19th-century Idaho Hotel is said to host lingering spirits from the Owyhee County mining boom.

#details
Originally established in 1863 in the mining camp of Ruby City, the hotel's three-story wing was physically dismantled, loaded onto sleds pulled by oxen, and hauled across the snowy Owyhee Mountains in 1866 to its new home in Silver City. After operating through decades of local booms, brawls, and celebrations, the hotel closed in 1942 before being lovingly restored starting in 1972. Guests and visitors staying in the historic building have reported uncanny phenomena, including the sound of children playing in empty hallways, phantom footsteps creaking across old floorboards, and the persistent presence of 19th-century travelers who never checked out.

#location
The Idaho Hotel located in the historic ghost town of Silver City in Owyhee County, Idaho.

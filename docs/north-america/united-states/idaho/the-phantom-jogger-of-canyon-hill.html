The Phantom Jogger of Canyon Hill

Summary

A legless female apparition haunts the perimeter of an Idaho cemetery, appearing after dark to jog alongside vehicles and tap on car windows.

Details

Local lore in Caldwell, Idaho, tells of a woman who met a tragic end after being struck by a vehicle while jogging near Canyon Hill Cemetery. Her restless spirit now traverses the grounds and roadway as a legless specter. Drivers who park between two specific trees facing the cemetery at night frequently report sudden drops in temperature, an oppressive sense of surveillance, and the chilling experience of the phantom runner tapping directly on their car windows before fading into the darkness.

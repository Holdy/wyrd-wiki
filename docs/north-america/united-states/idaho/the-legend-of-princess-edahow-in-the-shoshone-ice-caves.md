#title
The Legend of Princess Edahow in the Shoshone Ice Caves

#summary
Visitors and staff at the Shoshone Ice Caves in southern Idaho have long reported hearing mysterious voices and heavy footsteps echoing through the subterranean lava tube, which local legend attributes to Princess Edahow, whose remains were supposedly buried within the cave's ancient ice.

#details
Formed thousands of years ago within a volcanic lava field in southern Idaho, the Shoshone Ice Caves maintain a freezing year-round temperature that preserves solid ice floors deep underground. According to local folklore, centuries before the caves were rediscovered in the 1880s by a young boy searching for a lost goat, a revered Shoshone princess named Edahow died and was laid to rest within the cave's massive ice formation. Legend holds that her spirit remains trapped in her icy coffin, whispering to visitors and occasionally manifesting as shadow figures behind the ice walls in hopes of being set free.

#location
The Shoshone Ice Caves are located off State Highway 75 near Shoshone, Idaho.

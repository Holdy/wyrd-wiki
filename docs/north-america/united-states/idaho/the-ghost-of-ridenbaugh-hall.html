The Ghost of Ridenbaugh Hall

Summary

Built in 1902 as a girls' dormitory on the University of Idaho campus, Ridenbaugh Hall is said to be haunted by a female student who tragically took her own life.

Details

Originally constructed in 1902 as a women's dormitory on the University of Idaho campus in Moscow, Ridenbaugh Hall now houses music practice rooms and art galleries. Legend maintains that room 225 is haunted by the spirit of a young woman who allegedly hung herself there. Over the decades, students and staff have reported unexplained temperature drops, phantom music and singing when the building is entirely empty, and shadowy figures roaming the halls. In 1992, renowned paranormal investigators Ed and Lorraine Warren visited the building during a campus tour, with Lorraine reporting the distinct essence of a young female entity dressed in 1930s fashion with her hair pulled back in a bun.

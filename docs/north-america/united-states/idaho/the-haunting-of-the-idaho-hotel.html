The Haunting of the Idaho Hotel

Summary

Ghostly legends and disembodied screams haunt this historic 19th-century hotel in the remote Owyhee Mountain ghost town of Silver City.

Details

Established during the gold and silver rushes of the 1860s, the Idaho Hotel in Silver City has borne witness to frontier violence, including the fatal 1868 shoot-out between J. Marion Moore and Samuel Lockhart right outside its front doors. Decades later, guests and staff have reported numerous eerie paranormal phenomena throughout the creaking wooden structure. Most famously, a spectral presence known as "Screaming Alice"—said to be a woman who tragically fell to her death from the third floor—is frequently heard screaming in the dead of night, while disembodied footsteps and unexplained cold drafts echo through the long-closed corridors.

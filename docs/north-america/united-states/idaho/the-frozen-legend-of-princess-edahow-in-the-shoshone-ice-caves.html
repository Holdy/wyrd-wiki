The Frozen Legend of Princess Edahow in the Shoshone Ice Caves

Summary

A natural subterranean ice tube in southern Idaho is said to be haunted by the whispering spirit of a Shoshone princess buried within its ancient ice walls.

Details

Discovered by accident in the 1880s by a young boy searching for a lost goat, the Shoshone Ice Caves are a 1,000-foot-long lava tube maintaining freezing temperatures year-round. According to local legend, centuries ago a beloved Shoshone princess named Edahow died, and her tribe buried her remains inside the sacred ice of the cave so she would rest forever. Visitors and staff exploring the subterranean walkways have reported hearing unexplained voices whispering from the dark recesses, shadowy figures moving behind walls of ice, and heavy footsteps echoing when no one else is around.

#title
Kay's Cross

#summary
Looming in a remote hollow in Davis County, an imposing twenty-foot stone and mortar cross is surrounded by dark folklore of murderous polygamists and supernatural hauntings.

#details
Constructed in the mid-twentieth century, Kay's Cross in Kaysville, Utah, became the subject of intense local legend. According to popular myth, the cross was built by a deranged polygamist who murdered six of his wives and buried them around its base, or encased his seventh wife's heart within the stone. In reality, the land was once inhabited by Elden Kingston and his polygamous followers, who built the cross at the behest of cult leader Krishna Venta. Over the years, the site attracted rumors of occult rituals, animal sacrifices, and the ghost of the alleged seventh wife glowing under a full moon, before the structure was heavily damaged by a mysterious explosion in 1992.

#location
Kay's Cross is located in a secluded hollow off US-89 in Kaysville, Davis County, Utah.

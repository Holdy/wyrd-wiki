#title
The Phantoms of Big Cottonwood Canyon

#summary
Motorists traveling along Big Cottonwood Canyon Road after dark frequently encounter a distressed, dripping-wet woman standing by the roadside who vanishes when approached. Drivers have also reported picking up mysterious hitchhikers who disappear mid-ride.

#details
Stretching roughly 15 miles from the canyon's mouth, Big Cottonwood Canyon Road is reputed to be one of the most haunted drives in Utah. After sunset, travelers have reported seeing a young woman standing distressed and drenched by the asphalt, only for her to completely vanish when motorists pull over to help. Other drivers have encountered a lone male hitchhiker near the canyon entrance who seemingly fades into thin air, leaving behind only the echoing sound of clinking carabiners. Another frequent apparition involves a hitchhiker and his dog who climb into cars before inexplicably vanishing from the rearview mirror as the vehicle approaches the canyon floor.

#location
Big Cottonwood Canyon Road begins at the mouth of the canyon in Salt Lake County, Utah.

The Haunting of the Ben Lomond Hotel

Summary

The historic Ben Lomond Hotel in Ogden is said to be haunted by a tragic bride who drowned in her bathtub and her grief-stricken son who took his own life in the adjacent room.

Details

Constructed in 1927 along Washington Boulevard in downtown Ogden, the grand Ben Lomond Hotel gained a dark reputation through local folklore surrounding its upper floors. According to legend, a newlywed bride drowned in the bathtub of Room 1102 just days after her wedding. Guests and visitors over the decades have reported unexplained sounds of running water and unearthly chills when the room is empty. Furthermore, lore states that her grief-stricken son took his life in the adjacent Room 1101, where visitors frequently describe sudden cold spots, oppressive sensations, and sightings of a somber male figure.

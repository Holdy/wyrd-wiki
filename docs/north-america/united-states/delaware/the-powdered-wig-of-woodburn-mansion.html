The Powdered Wig of Woodburn Mansion

Summary

The official Delaware governor's residence in Dover, known as Woodburn, has been haunted since the early 19th century by the ghost of its original builder, Charles Hillyard III.

Details

Built in the late 18th century, Woodburn has served as the home for Delaware's governors and carries a long-standing tradition of spectral visits. The haunting dates back to around 1815, when a guest reported encountering a phantom man dressed in a powdered wig, knee britches, and a ruffled shirt, identified as the home's builder, Charles Hillyard III. Over the subsequent two centuries, various visitors and residents—including a governor's wife who reportedly left out wine for him—have continued to experience his spectral presence in the historic halls.

#title
The Headless Soldier of Cooch’s Bridge

#summary
Cooch’s Bridge in Newark, Delaware, the site of a fierce Revolutionary War battle, is said to be haunted by a soldier who lost his head to a cannonball.

#details
On September 3, 1777, Cooch’s Bridge was the site of the only major Revolutionary War battle fought on Delaware soil. According to local folklore, the violent clash left behind residual energy and restless spirits, most notably a headless soldier who was decapitated during the cannon fire. Passersby and locals have shared tales of encountering the phantom soldier wandering the historic bridge and surrounding area after dark.

#location
Cooch’s Bridge crossing in Newark, Delaware.

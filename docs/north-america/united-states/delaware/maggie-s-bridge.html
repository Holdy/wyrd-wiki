Maggie's Bridge

Summary

The headless ghost of a young woman named Maggie haunts a secluded bridge and surrounding woodlands in Seaford, Delaware. Visitors frequently report shadowy figures and unexplained orbs in this eerie area.

Details

In the late 19th century, a young woman named Maggie Bloxom suffered a tragic accident resulting in her decapitation on a small bridge in Seaford. Ever since, her restless spirit has been said to linger around the crossing, which is now widely known as Maggie's Bridge. Paranormal investigators and locals frequently report shadowy figures moving through the adjacent woods, and photographs taken at the site often capture unexplained orbs in the darkness.

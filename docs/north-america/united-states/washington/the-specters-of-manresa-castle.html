The Specters of Manresa Castle

Summary

Dating back to the late 19th century, this historic hilltop hotel in Port Townsend is said to be haunted by a Jesuit priest who hanged himself in the attic and a heartbroken woman who leapt from the tower.

Details

Originally built in 1892 as a private residence for businessman Charles Eisenbeis and later operated as a Jesuit seminary, Manresa Castle has accumulated numerous ghost stories over the decades. Guests and staff have reported inexplicable cold spots, mysterious whispers echoing through empty corridors, and doors creaking open on their own. Among the most enduring local legends are the spirit of a Jesuit priest said to hang out in the attic and a woman mourning her lost husband who haunts Room 306 after leaping from the tower.

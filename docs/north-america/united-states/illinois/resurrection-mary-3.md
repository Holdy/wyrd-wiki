#title
Resurrection Mary

#summary
A young woman in a white ball gown is frequently reported hitchhiking along Archer Avenue before mysteriously vanishing near Resurrection Cemetery.

#details
According to local legend, a young woman named Mary attended a dance at a ballroom in the Chicago area during the late 1920s or early 1930s. After leaving, she tragically died in a vehicular accident. Over the decades, numerous drivers traveling along Archer Avenue have reported picking up a young, blonde female hitchhiker wearing a white dress. As the vehicle approaches Resurrection Cemetery in Justice, Illinois, the passenger inexplicably vanishes from the backseat. Sightings and eerie encounters associated with Resurrection Mary have persisted for generations, becoming one of Illinois's most enduring urban legends.

#location
Resurrection Cemetery is located at 7201 S. Archer Avenue in Justice, Illinois.

The Phantoms of the Rialto Square Theatre

Summary

Known as the "Jewel of Joliet," this opulent 1926 movie palace is said to be haunted by a glamorous actress and other spectral figures who refuse to leave the stage. Patrons and staff frequently report icy drafts, unexplained footsteps, and ghostly apparitions lingering in the historic auditorium.

Details

Opened on May 24, 1926, the Rialto Square Theatre in Joliet was built as an ornate vaudeville and movie palace reflecting European Renaissance and Baroque architecture. Although saved from demolition in the late 1970s and fully restored, the grand building retained more than just its architectural splendor. Staff, patrons, and workmen have frequently reported seeing a nameless, pretty young woman in her twenties—believed to be a former actress—floating through the theater. Additional reports describe a pair of spirits in the balcony who allegedly fell to their deaths, along with sudden icy coldness, strange noises, and invisible poking fingers.

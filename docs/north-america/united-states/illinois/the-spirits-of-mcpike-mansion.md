#title
The Spirits of McPike Mansion

#summary
Built in 1869 by prominent resident Henry Guest McPike, this grand Victorian estate in Alton became famously haunted by roughly fifteen paranormal entities after falling into abandonment. Visitors and owners frequently experience inexplicable sounds, lights, and spectral figures throughout the decaying property.

#details
The McPike Mansion was constructed in Alton, Illinois, in 1869 by horticulturist and entrepreneur Henry Guest McPike. After McPike's death in 1910 and subsequent abandonment, the building deteriorated into disrepair. In 1994, Sharyn and George Luedke purchased the property to restore it, but quickly encountered persistent paranormal activity. Owners and guests regularly report strange orbs of light, loud inexplicable bangs, mists, and human-like silhouettes appearing in windows. Today, the owners conduct historical and paranormal tours of the grounds and cellar to share these mysterious encounters with the public.

#location
The McPike Mansion is situated on its historic estate property in Alton, Illinois.

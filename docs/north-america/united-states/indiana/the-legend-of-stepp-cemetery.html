The Legend of Stepp Cemetery

Summary

Situated deep within the Morgan-Monroe State Forest, Stepp Cemetery is said to be haunted by a ghostly woman in black who mourns over an infant's grave, surrounded by decades of chilling Hoosier folklore.

Details

Dating back to at least the mid-nineteenth century, Stepp Cemetery in Indiana's Morgan-Monroe State Forest gained a dark reputation beginning around the turn of the 20th century, when a fringe religious sect known as the Crabbites reportedly held unusual gatherings on the grounds. As the cemetery became increasingly remote following its incorporation into the state forest in 1929 and popular among local teenagers in the mid-20th century, the folklore rapidly expanded. Local legends describe a ghostly woman in black who wanders the grounds and visits the grave of an infant, alongside tales of a mysterious tree stump known as the "Warlock's chair" associated with local curses.

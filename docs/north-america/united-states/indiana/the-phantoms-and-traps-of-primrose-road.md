#title
The Phantoms and Traps of Primrose Road

#summary
Generations of motorists visiting a remote rural road northwest of South Bend have shared local legends of vanishing farmhouses, ghostly warnings, and unexplained vehicle malfunctions.

#details
Northwest of South Bend, Primrose Road has long been the subject of local ghost lore. Generations of curious locals and teenagers have driven down the remote stretch at night, passing near the historic Portage Prairie Dunkard Cemetery. Popular legend holds that driving along the unlit road at specific speeds causes cars to mysteriously stall or tires to go flat, while phantom farmhouses and apparitions emerge from the adjacent cornfields to warn travelers away.

#location
Primrose Road is situated northwest of South Bend in St. Joseph County, Indiana.

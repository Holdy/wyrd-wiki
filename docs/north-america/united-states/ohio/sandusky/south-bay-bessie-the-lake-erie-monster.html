South Bay Bessie, the Lake Erie Monster

Summary

A snake-like or sturgeon-like lake monster measuring 20 to 40 feet in length has been reported in Lake Erie since 1793.

Details

South Bay Bessie is a lake monster measuring 20 to 40 feet in length, described as dark or gray, and appearing either snake-like or like a sturgeon with arms. The first recorded sighting of the creature occurred off the shores of Sandusky in 1793, with later sightings reported near Toledo and the Marblehead peninsula.

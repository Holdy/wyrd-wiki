#title
Orphan Ghosts at Fairmount Children's Home
#summary
Fairmount Children's Home is haunted by the ghosts of orphans buried in the basement, alongside cold spots and electrical sparks in treatment rooms.
#details
According to locals, Fairmount Children's Home is haunted by the ghosts of orphans who were buried in the basement. Investigators have reported seeing sparks in some of the rooms where electroshock therapy was administered, and cold spots have been felt throughout the home.

#title
Glowing Tombstones at Mount Eaton Road Cemetery
#summary
Hidden at the end of a driveway, Mount Eaton Road Cemetery features tombstones that glow different colors at night.
#details
According to locals, there is an old cemetery hidden at the end of a long driveway on Mt. Eaton Road, where investigators say tombstones glow different colors at night and some have been chased out by vengeful spirits.

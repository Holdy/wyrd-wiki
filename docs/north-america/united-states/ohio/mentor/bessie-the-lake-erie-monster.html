    Bessie the Lake Erie Monster

    Summary

    A snake-like lake monster known as Bessie or South Bay Bessie has been reported in Lake Erie by witnesses since 1793.

    Details

    Known as Bessie or South Bay Bessie, this lake monster has intrigued northeastern Ohio and Michigan for centuries. The earliest recorded sighting dates to 1793 when a sloop captain reported a creature over sixteen feet long moving through the water near Sandusky. Following a wave of sightings in 1817, reports have continued, describing a snake-like creature 30 to 40 feet long, at least a foot thick, and grayish in color.

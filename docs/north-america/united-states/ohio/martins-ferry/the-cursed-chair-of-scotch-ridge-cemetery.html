    The Cursed Chair of Scotch Ridge Cemetery

    Summary

    A cemetery monument carved to look like a wooden chair is said to carry a fatal curse that kills anyone who sits on it within 24 hours, days, or weeks.

    Details

    A grave monument in Scotch Ridge Cemetery erected for Thomas Mitchell, who died in 1876, resembles a wooden chair with a cloak draped over the back and a scroll bearing the biblical inscription "Thou shalt be missed, because thy seat will be empty." According to local legend, anyone who sits on the chair will die within 24 hours, or failing that, within 24 days or 24 weeks. Local accounts linked to the curse include a farmer's wife who sat on the chair and was subsequently found hanging in her barn the next day, a Vietnam soldier who sat in it and was killed in battle 24 weeks later, and two young people who died in a car accident after visiting the site.

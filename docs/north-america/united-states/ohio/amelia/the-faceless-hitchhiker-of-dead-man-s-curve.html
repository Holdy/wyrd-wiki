The Faceless Hitchhiker of Dead Man's Curve

Summary

The spirit of a faceless hitchhiker haunts the accident intersection at Dead Man's Curve and has caused countless crashes.

Details

The spirit of a faceless hitchhiker has been seen at the intersection of the fatal accident at Dead Man's Curve and has caused countless accidents, with a crash survivor reporting seeing the faceless man at least five different times.

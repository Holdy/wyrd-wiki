    The Hanging Man of Carpenter Road

    Summary

    Carpenter Road is haunted by the ghost of a man who hanged himself from a tree in the 1970s.

    Details

    Carpenter Road is said to be haunted by the ghost of a man who hung himself from a tree in the 1970s, and his ghost is sometimes seen walking down the road moaning.

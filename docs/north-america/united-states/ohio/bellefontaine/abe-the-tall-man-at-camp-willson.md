#title
Abe the Tall Man at Camp Willson
#summary
A tall man in a tall hat nicknamed "Abe" wanders the trails at Camp Willson at night.
#details
Countless ghosts have been seen at Camp Willson over the years, the most famous of which is a tall man with a tall hat named "Abe" by locals, who is most often seen wandering the trails at night.

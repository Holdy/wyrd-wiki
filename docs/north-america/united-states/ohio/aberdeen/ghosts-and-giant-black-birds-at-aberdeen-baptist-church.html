    Ghosts and Giant Black Birds at Aberdeen Baptist Church

    Summary

    Aberdeen Baptist Church is haunted by the ghosts of three children, eerie screams, and giant black birds that vanish into darkness.

    Details

    Investigators report feeling a strange presence at night, and screams are heard coming from deep within the Aberdeen Baptist Church. Additionally, giant black birds have been seen flying out of the windows before quickly disappearing into the darkness.

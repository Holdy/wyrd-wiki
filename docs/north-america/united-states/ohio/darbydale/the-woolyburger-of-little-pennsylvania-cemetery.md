#title
The Woolyburger of Little Pennsylvania Cemetery
#summary
A Bigfoot-like cryptid known as the Woolyburger is said to stalk the woods near a historic Darbydale cemetery.
#details
Little Pennsylvania Cemetery, also known colloquially as Woolyburger Cemetery, features plots dating back to the 1800s and is the center of numerous urban legends including occult activity and shadowy figures. In addition to these tales of shadowy figures and a burned-down haunted house, some claim that a Bigfoot-like creature called the Woolyburger stalks the woods surrounding the cemetery.

    Mudhouse Mansion Hauntings

    Summary

    Mudhouse Mansion in Lancaster was plagued by ghost stories, including tales that it was the original location of Bloody Mary and haunted by a child-killing woman.

    Details

    Before its demolition in September 2015, Mudhouse Mansion in Lancaster generated chilling urban legends. The most popular stories claimed that the home was the original location of the iconic Bloody Mary legend and that it was haunted by a woman who murdered her children.

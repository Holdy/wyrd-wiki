#title
Train Wreck Victims at Ashtabula River
#summary
The ghosts of passengers killed in the horrific 1876 Ashtabula train disaster return to the crash site on the anniversary of their death.
#details
On December 29, 1876, the Pacific Express crashed as a bridge collapsed over the Ashtabula River due to heavy snowfall, plunging cars into the river where passengers drowned or burned to death. 92 of the 159 passengers died, and locals report their ghosts still return to the site of the crash on the anniversary of their death.

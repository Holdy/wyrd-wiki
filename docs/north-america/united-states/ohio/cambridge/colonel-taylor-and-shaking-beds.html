Colonel Taylor and Shaking Beds

Summary

The Colonel Taylor Inn is haunted by Colonel Taylor and his wife, featuring cold spots and beds that shake at night.

Details

The Colonel Taylor Bed & Breakfast is said by employees and guests to be haunted by Colonel Taylor and his wife. Cold spots are felt throughout the building, and the beds in certain rooms sometimes shake at night, while the colonel appears to be watching over the property and is usually friendly.

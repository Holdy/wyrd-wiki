The Woman in White at Northwood Cemetery

Summary

Investigators at Northwood Cemetery have reported seeing the apparition of a woman in a white dress on summer nights.

Details

Investigators have reported seeing the apparition of a woman in a white dress on summer nights at Northwood Cemetery, believed to have passed away during the 1920s.

    Starved Children at Gore Orphanage

    Summary

    Gore Orphanage is haunted by the ghosts of a young boy and girl who starved to death, and its TVs turn on by themselves to cartoons.

    Details

    Televisions in the orphanage turn on by themselves, always to cartoons or kids shows. It is believed to be haunted by the ghosts of a young boy and girl who starved to death there.

#title
The Ghostly Soldier of Circleville Courthouse

#summary
A ghostly soldier has been spotted haunting the upper floors of the Circleville Courthouse.

#details
The current Circleville Courthouse was built in 1845 and was later updated in 1888. Sometime after the remodeling, a ghostly soldier began appearing in the building, and has been seen on its upper floors.

#title
The Man in the Blue Jacket on East Franklin Street
#summary
Witnesses have seen a mysterious man in a blue jacket near the bathroom of an East Franklin Street house, where glasses have also mysteriously shattered.
#details
A house on East Franklin Street is the site of multiple paranormal occurrences, including glasses that have mysteriously shattered. Witnesses have specifically reported seeing a man wearing a blue jacket near the home's bathroom, only to find no one there when the house is thoroughly searched.

The Circleville Letters

#details
Starting in the late 1970s, residents of Circleville, including a local school superintendent, began receiving anonymous threatening letters filled with accusations and warnings. The mystery escalated with attempts to uncover the sender, leading to arrests and ongoing suspicion in an unsolved case.

#title
The Beer Cooler Spirit at Jenkins Sunoco
#summary
The Sunoco station in Circleville is haunted by the owner's husband who died of a brain aneurysm in the beer cooler.
#details
The Sunoco station on the west end of Circleville is believed to be haunted by the ghost of the current owner's husband, who died of a brain aneurysm in the beer cooler, and late shift employees report feeling a presence watching them and seeing a figure moving around in the cooler.

#title
Chief J.M. Baer at the Franklin Street Firehouse
#summary
The former Franklin Street Firehouse was haunted by Chief J.M. Baer, who suffered a fatal heart attack inside the building.
#details
After dying from a heart attack at the Franklin Street Firehouse, Chief J.M. Baer reportedly continued to haunt the station. Footsteps and shadowy figures could occasionally be observed in the building before it was eventually demolished.

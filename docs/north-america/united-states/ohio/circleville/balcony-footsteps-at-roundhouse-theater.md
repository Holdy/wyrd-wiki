#title
Balcony Footsteps at Roundhouse Theater
#summary
The Roundhouse Theater is marked by mysterious sights and sounds, including balcony footsteps and a dog terrified of an unseen presence.
#details
The Roundhouse Theater is the site of numerous reported ghostly sights and sounds. Witnesses have frequently heard mysterious footsteps echoing in the balcony, and one dog was once so terrified by an unseen presence in the building that it had to be removed.

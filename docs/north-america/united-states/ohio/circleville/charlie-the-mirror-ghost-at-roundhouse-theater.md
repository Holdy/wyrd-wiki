#title
Charlie the Mirror Ghost at Roundhouse Theater
#summary
A ghost named Charlie haunts the Roundhouse Theater by peering out of mirrors directly into the eyes of onlookers.
#details
Among the ghosts residing at the Roundhouse Theater is an entity named Charlie. Charlie is said to manifest by peering out of the building's mirrors directly into the eyes of anyone looking into them.

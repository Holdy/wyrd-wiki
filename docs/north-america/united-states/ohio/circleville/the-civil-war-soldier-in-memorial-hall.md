#title
The Civil War Soldier in Memorial Hall

#summary
A Civil War soldier has been seen appearing in the mirrors of Circleville Memorial Hall.

#details
Built in the 1890s as a memorial to those who served in the Civil War, Circleville Memorial Hall has served as a public library and an armory, and is now home to a local theatrical group. Within the building, a Civil War soldier has been seen in the mirrors.

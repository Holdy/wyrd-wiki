#title
The Circleville Letter Writer
#summary
Hundreds of Circleville residents received threatening anonymous letters beginning in 1976, leading to a fatal crash, a booby-trapped pistol, and a controversial criminal conviction.
#details
Anonymous letters started arriving in Circleville mailboxes around 1976, initially targeting school bus driver Mary Gillispie before expanding to hundreds of residents, businesses, and government offices with threatening secrets. In August 1977, Mary's husband Ron died when his truck crashed while he went to confront the writer, though it was ruled an accident. Years later, Mary discovered a booby-trapped pistol rigged to a sign on her route, and her brother-in-law Paul Freshour was convicted of attempted murder, continuing to receive letters in prison until his release in 1994, after which the letters stopped and the case was eventually closed without identifying the writer.

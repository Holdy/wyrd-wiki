Charlie at the Roundtown Players Theater

Summary

The Roundtown Players Theater is haunted by Charlie, whose reflection appears in mirrors alongside footsteps on the balcony.

Details

The Roundtown Players Theater is believed to be haunted by a ghost named Charlie, whose spirit has been seen reflected in mirrors and whose disembodied footsteps are heard on the balcony.

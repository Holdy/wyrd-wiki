#title
The Haunted Pickaway Township Elementary School
#summary
A century-old school is haunted by the ghosts of individuals who died on the property, including the apparition of a child.
#details
Pickaway Township Elementary is a 100-year-old school allegedly haunted by the ghosts of those who died on the property. Custodians and teachers working at the school have reported witnessing many strange activities and have specifically seen the ghost of a child.

#title
The Ghost of the Samuel Moore House

#summary
An old home that served as a station on the Underground Railroad is said to be haunted by the ghost of an escaping slave.

#details
The Samuel Moore House, which was part of the Underground Railroad during the time of slavery, is said to be haunted by the ghost of an escaping slave remaining within the old home.

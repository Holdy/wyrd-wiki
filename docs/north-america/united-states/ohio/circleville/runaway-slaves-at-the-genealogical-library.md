#title
Runaway Slaves at the Genealogical Library
#summary
An old house functioning as a genealogical library is said to be haunted by the runaway slaves who once hid in its basement.
#details
An old house that now serves as a genealogical library is reportedly haunted. According to local lore, the runaway slaves who once sought refuge and hid in the basement of the structure still haunt the place.

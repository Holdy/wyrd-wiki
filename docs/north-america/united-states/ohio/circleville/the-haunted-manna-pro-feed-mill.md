#title
The Haunted Manna Pro Feed Mill
#summary
The Manna Pro Feed Mill is reported to be extremely haunted, featuring shadowy figures, self-starting machinery, and rattling man-lifts.
#details
Four known deaths have occurred at the Manna Pro Feed Mill, and employees report that the facility is extremely haunted. Night guards doing their rounds have seen shadowy figures, machinery frequently starts operating on its own, and the man-lifts rattle as if someone is stepping on or off them when nobody is there.

#title
The Beer Cooler Ghost at the West Main Street Sunoco
#summary
A West Main Street Sunoco is haunted by an employee who died in the beer cooler, where items move independently and workers feel watched.
#details
The Sunoco station on West Main Street is supposedly haunted by a worker who died from a brain aneurysm while inside the beer cooler. Night shift employees have reported experiencing the unsettling feeling of being watched and witnessing objects moving on their own inside the cooler.

#title
The Civil War Veteran of Memorial Hall
#summary
Memorial Hall is haunted by a Civil War veteran in a blue uniform who makes toilets flush by himself and appears in mirrors.
#details
Memorial Hall is said to be haunted by a ghost wearing a blue uniform, believed to be a Civil War veteran who may have helped construct the building in the 1890s. Phenomena reported in the hall include toilets flushing by themselves and sightings of the soldier's reflection in mirrors.

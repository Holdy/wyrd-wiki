#title
Imogene Remus in Black at Eden Park
#summary
Eden Park is haunted by the ghost of Imogene Remus wearing a black dress, matching her 1927 murder by her bootlegger husband.
#details
Eden Park is believed to be haunted by the ghost of a woman wearing a black dress who has been seen near the gazebo. A 1927 newspaper article tells of a famous lawyer and bootlegger from Cincinnati who shot his wife, Immogene Remus, in the park while wearing a black silk dress on their way to court to get a divorce.

#title
White Lights and Red Eyes on Holcomb Road
#summary
Holcomb Road is haunted by white lights and red eyes following a historic school bus crash, alongside signs of occult worship.
#details
According to local legend, a bus full of children on a school field trip crashed into a tree on Holcomb Road. Drivers report seeing strange white lights and glowing red eyes in the woods, and there is evidence of occult worship including strange etchings on stones and human-shaped sticks hanging from trees.

The Old Poorhouse Shed at Wood County Museum

Summary

The Wood County Museum, formerly a poorhouse and hospital, has unexplained noises coming from its psychiatric shed.

Details

The Wood County Historic Museum once was used as a hospital, then later as a nursing home, and strange unexplained noises have been heard coming from the shed where mentally ill patients were once housed.

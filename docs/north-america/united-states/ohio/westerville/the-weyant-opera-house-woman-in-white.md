#title
The Weyant Opera House Woman in White
#summary
A ghostly woman in white is seen at a Westerville building that was once the site of a fatal 1800s opera house fire.
#details
In the 1800s, a fire broke out on the third floor of the Weyant Opera House during a performance when a performer knocked over a lamp, killing two people. In the time since, people have reported seeing a ghostly woman in white at the building, which is now an Old Bag of Nails location. Some believe this apparition is an unidentified third victim of the historical fire.

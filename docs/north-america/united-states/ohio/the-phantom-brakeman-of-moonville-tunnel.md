#title
The Phantom Brakeman of Moonville Tunnel

#summary
Built in 1856 to transport coal through southeastern Ohio, the abandoned Moonville Tunnel is famously haunted by the ghost of a railroad worker and a mysterious lantern-wielding light.

#details
Located in the dense woods of Vinton County, the Moonville Tunnel was part of the Marietta and Cincinnati Railroad, serving the mining community of Moonville and the nearby Hope Furnace. According to local legend and historical records, a brakeman was fatally injured on the tracks on March 31, 1859, after being crushed by an oncoming train. Over the following decades, several other tragic fatalities occurred along the rails near the tunnel. Visitors and locals have long reported seeing a hovering orb of light or a ghostly figure carrying a lantern walking through the dark brick archway where the railway once ran.

#location
Moonville Tunnel is located along the multi-use trail in Zaleski State Forest in Vinton County, Ohio.

The Insane Foreman at Lock No. 4

Summary

Lock No. 4 is haunted by an insane foreman and his workers who allegedly died in a sulfuric acid massacre when the canal was completed.

Details

When the canal was completed in 1857, an angry foreman learned the project was ending, went insane, and killed himself and many of the men working on the lock with sulfuric acid. He and the disfigured ghosts of his fellow workers have been spotted many times by investigators.

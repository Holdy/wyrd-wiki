#title
Paranormal Activity at Warehouse On The Canal
#summary
Warehouse On The Canal hosts apparitions, self-operating lights, footsteps, and malfunctioning electronics.
#details
The Warehouse On The Canal is said to be host to a wide array of paranormal occurrences, where apparitions have been seen, lights turn on and off by themselves, disembodied footsteps are heard, and electronics such as radios sometimes change stations or malfunction.

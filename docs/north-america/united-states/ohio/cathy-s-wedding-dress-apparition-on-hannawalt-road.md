#title
Cathy's Wedding Dress Apparition on Hannawalt Road
#summary
A bride named Cathy, killed in a buggy wreck on her way to her wedding in the 1800s, haunts Hannawalt Road in her wedding dress.
#details
In the 1800s, a woman named Cathy was traveling to her wedding when her horses were spooked, causing a buggy wreck that killed her—an accident widely blamed on her groom-to-be. Her ghost is frequently seen walking down a hill in her wedding dress near the Deer Creek dam just off Hannawalt Road. People who witness the apparition have reported experiencing bizarre occurrences until they visit the grave of Cathy Smith at a nearby cemetery.

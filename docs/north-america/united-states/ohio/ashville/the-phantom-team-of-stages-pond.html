The Phantom Team of Stages Pond

Summary

The phantom screams and stomping hooves of a farmer's wagon team, which sank into quicksand-like muck during an 1800s storm, can still be heard near the pond before bad weather.

Details

One muggy August day in the 1800s, a farmer living across Ward Road was taking in hay when a storm blew across the fields. Lightning and thunder startled his wagon team, causing them to bolt down the road and straight into the marshy, quicksand-like muck around Stage's Pond. The horses sank so deeply they could not be retrieved, and today the screams of the horses and their stomping hooves can still be heard near the pond area prior to a storm.

#title
The Grabbing Bar Owner

#summary
Visitors sitting down in a local bar report feeling the phantom shoulder-grab of the establishment's late former owner.

#details
When the former owner of a local bar was alive, he had a habit of grabbing people's shoulders as a gesture to say thank you. After his death, patrons sitting down inside the venue report feeling someone grab their shoulder as a way of saying hello.

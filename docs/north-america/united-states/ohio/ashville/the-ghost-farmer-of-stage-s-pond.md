#title
The Ghost Farmer of Stage's Pond

#summary
A farmer who drowned with his horses in a marsh during a violent storm over a century ago is said to haunt Stage's Pond, where his phantom wagon and screams are still heard.

#details
During a sudden and violent thunderstorm, a farmer driving a horse-drawn cart attempted to find shelter. He missed a turn and his cart flipped over, plunging him and his horses into the marsh near the pond where they perished. Over a hundred years later, employees at the preserve still hear the sounds of the farmer's wagon and the screaming horses rushing down the road. Staff have also reported hearing a knock and seeing the lodge door open on its own immediately after hearing the horses' screams, only to find no one there.

#title
The Phantom Horse and Buggy of Stage’s Pond
#summary
The phantom sounds of a horse and buggy that sank into quicksand in the 1800s can still be heard around Stage’s Pond.
#details
In the 1800s, a horse and buggy were pulled into Stage's Pond in an area surrounded by quicksand. To this day, the ghostly sounds of the horse and buggy can still be heard near the pond.

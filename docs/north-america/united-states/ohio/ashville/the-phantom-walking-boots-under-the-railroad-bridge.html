The Phantom Walking Boots Under the Railroad Bridge

Summary

A man walking under a railroad bridge at night was accompanied by a mysterious pair of boots walking beside him, linked to an 1890s train accident.

Details

In the mid-1950s, a child walking home alone at night had to pass underneath a railroad bridge when a pair of boots suddenly started walking right beside him. The phantom boots kept pace with him until he cleared the bridge, terrifying him. Years later, he learned that a man had been hit by a train on that same railroad bridge in the 1890s, an impact that knocked his boots off.

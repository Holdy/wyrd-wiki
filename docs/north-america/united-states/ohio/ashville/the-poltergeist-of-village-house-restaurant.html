The Poltergeist of Village House Restaurant

Summary

The Village House Restaurant is supposedly haunted by its former owner, who pulls pranks like floating objects and rearranging furniture.

Details

The Village House Restaurant is reported to be haunted by the ghost of its former owner. This spirit is said to engage in playful and mischievous pranks, such as making objects float in the air and rearranging furniture on its own.

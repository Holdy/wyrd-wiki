#title
The Murdered Girl of the Bar

#summary
Patrons of a local bar report seeing the ghost of a young woman who was allegedly murdered in or near the establishment.

#details
Visitors to a local bar have shared a story about a woman in her mid-twenties who was murdered either inside the building or nearby. According to accounts, some people claim to have seen her apparition inside the bar.

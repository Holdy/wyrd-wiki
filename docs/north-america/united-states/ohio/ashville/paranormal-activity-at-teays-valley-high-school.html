Paranormal Activity at Teays Valley High School

Summary

Teays Valley High School is reported to be haunted by ghostly footsteps, self-operating doors, falling or hanging balcony figures, and floating orbs.

Details

Several locations within Teays Valley High School are said to experience paranormal activity. Ghostly footsteps have been heard echoing in the main gymnasium, and a large, heavy door near the band room that is normally locked opens and closes by itself. The balcony is widely avoided by students due to figures reportedly hanging from it or falling over the edge, and orbs regularly appear in photographs taken throughout the school.

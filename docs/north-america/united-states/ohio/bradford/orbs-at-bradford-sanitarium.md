Orbs at Bradford Sanitarium
#summary
Investigators at the ruins of the 1920s Bradford Sanitarium have reported seeing orbs at night.
#details
The Bradford Sanitarium was built in the 1920s and burned down in 1935, and investigators have reported seeing orbs at night.

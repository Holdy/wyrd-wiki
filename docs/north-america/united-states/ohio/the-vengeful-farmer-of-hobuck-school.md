#title
The Vengeful Farmer of Hobuck School
#summary
An abandoned school on Dennis Road was haunted by the ghost of a farmer who threw objects at trespassers after trying to stop satan worship.
#details
The abandoned Hobuck School on Dennis Road was once haunted by a local farmer who had boarded up the building in an attempt to prevent children from using it to worship Satan. The farmer's ghost was said to throw objects at trespassers before the schoolhouse was eventually demolished between 2006 and 2009.

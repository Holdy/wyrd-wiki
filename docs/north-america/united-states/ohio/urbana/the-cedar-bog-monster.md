#title
The Cedar Bog Monster
#summary
An ape-like cryptid was spotted around the opening of the Cedar Bog Nature Preserve in Urbana.
#details
An ape-like creature known as the Cedar Bog Monster was spotted around the opening of the Cedar Bog Nature Preserve in 1942. Because the nature preserve has remained virtually undisturbed for 10,000 years, the wetland provides a fitting home for the creature.

    Custodian Clark Danbury at Avon Lake High School

    Summary

    Avon Lake High School is haunted by a former custodian, featuring unexplained lights, mysterious banging, and an auditorium apparition.

    Details

    Avon Lake High School is said to be haunted by the ghost of one of its former custodians, Clark Danbury, who passed away in the early 1900s. Unexplained lights are seen by students, mysterious banging is heard throughout the school, and an apparition was seen in the auditorium by several people.

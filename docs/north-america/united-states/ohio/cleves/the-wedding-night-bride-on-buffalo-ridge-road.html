The Wedding Night Bride on Buffalo Ridge Road

Summary

Buffalo Ridge Road is haunted by a woman killed on her wedding night who walks near the road in a white dress.

Details

Buffalo Ridge Road is said to be haunted by the ghost of a woman who was killed on her wedding night, and she is seen walking near the road in a white dress.

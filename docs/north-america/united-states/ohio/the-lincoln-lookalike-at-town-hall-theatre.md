#title
The Lincoln Lookalike at Town Hall Theatre
#summary
The Town Hall Theatre is haunted by a tall man resembling Abraham Lincoln, basement cold spots, and an upstairs bedroom spirit.
#details
The Town Hall Theatre is haunted by the ghost of a tall man who looks much like Abraham Lincoln, though his identity cannot be verified. Cold spots are felt in the basement area, and his spirit is seen often in the upstairs bedroom.

#title
The Phantom Runner of Deadman’s Run

#summary
A man killed during a flood occasionally appears running across the bridge over Deadman’s Run in Bellville.

#details
Deadman's Run is a small creek in Bellville named after a man who appears from time to time on the bridge that spans it, running from one side to the other. It is believed the man worked for a local judge but was killed during a flood.

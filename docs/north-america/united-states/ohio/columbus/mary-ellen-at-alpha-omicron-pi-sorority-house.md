#title
Mary-Ellen at Alpha Omicron Pi Sorority House
#summary
Alpha Omicron Pi sorority house is haunted by Mary-Ellen, who broke her neck jumping into a lake, causing moving objects and doors.
#details
The sorority house is believed to be haunted by the ghost of a former student named Mary-Ellen. According to legend, Mary-Ellen and some other students were drinking one night and decided to jump into the lake; Mary-Ellen broke her neck and later died in the house. Objects move by themselves, and doors open and close when nobody is around.

#title
The Charles H. Hayden Mausoleum Knocking
#summary
Knocking on the chained door of a wealthy banker's mausoleum in Greenlawn Cemetery is said to be answered from within.
#details
Charles H. Hayden, a wealthy early 20th-century Central Ohio banker, and several family members are interred inside a large chained mausoleum at Greenlawn Cemetery. Legend has it that if a visitor knocks on the heavy door, spirits inside the structure can be heard knocking back in reply.

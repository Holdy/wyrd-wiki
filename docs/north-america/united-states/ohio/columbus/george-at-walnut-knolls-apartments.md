#title
George at Walnut Knolls Apartments
#summary
Originally a mental institution, Walnut Knolls Apartments are haunted by a ghost named George who throws objects and causes attic noises.
#details
Walnut Knolls apartments are believed by residents to be haunted. A ghost named George is known to throw objects at residents, and footsteps are heard coming from the attic, which is closed off. The apartments were originally built as a mental institution in the 1940s and 1950s, and many deaths occurred on the property.

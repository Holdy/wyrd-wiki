#title
Pomerene Hall's Unseen Footsteps
#summary
Pomerene Hall is haunted by phantom footsteps and doors that open by themselves.
#details
Inside Pomerene Hall on the Ohio State University campus, people have reported hearing footsteps when no one else is around and witnessing locked doors open by themselves. This haunting is believed to be connected to a former OSU professor, Dr. Frederick Clark, who took his own life in the early 1900s following a failed mining investment.

Civil War Spirits at Fort Hayes School

Summary

Fort Hayes School features Civil War soldiers, nurses, screams, and suicides in the Shot Tower built on a Civil War camp site.

Details

Fort Hayes School campus is said to be haunted by many restless spirits, built on the site of an old Civil War era camp. Disembodied voices and screams are heard, and the spirits of Civil War soldiers and nurses are seen. The "Shot Tower" was used to manufacture ammunition during the Civil War where workers died or committed suicide; visitors report feeling a strange presence on the top floor, and students in newer buildings report footsteps, slamming lockers, and a watching presence.

#title
Tom Bateman's Office Routine
#summary
Retired clerk Tom Bateman continues to follow his precise daily work schedule in the Ohio Statehouse after death, causing office lights to flicker along his former route.
#details
For decades, Tom Bateman served as a clerk in the Ohio Statehouse until his retirement in the early 1970s. Following his death in the early 1980s, his ghost continued to show up for work, maintaining a tight schedule by taking the same route to and from his office in the senate chamber and leaving by 5 p.m. People inside the building have reported lights flickering in a pattern matching his exact route out of the office.

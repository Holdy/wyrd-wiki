The Drowned Student at Mirror Lake

Summary

Mirror Lake is haunted by the ghost of a drowned Ohio State University student, causing visitors on the shore to feel a strange chill.

Details

The lake itself is said to be haunted by the ghost of an Ohio State University student who drowned there, and visitors on the shore have reported feeling a strange chill.

#title
The Lady of the Lake
#summary
A phantom woman in outdated clothing is seen skating along Mirror Lake on cold winter nights.
#details
On cold, wintry nights on the Ohio State University campus, some have reported seeing a woman dressed in outdated clothing skating along Mirror Lake. This spirit is believed by some to be the wife of a former OSU professor who took his own life in the early 1900s, and who lived until the 1920s having vowed to haunt the campus after she passed away.

#title
The Murder-Suicide Bridge on Wallahalla Road
#summary
Wallahalla Road Bridge is haunted by the reflections of a murdered wife and daughter and the hanging apparition of the murderer.
#details
According to local legend, a man murdered his wife and daughter in a house on Wallahalla Road, threw their bodies over the nearby bridge, and then hung himself. It is said that if you drive over the bridge at night, you can see the reflection of his wife and daughter floating in the river, and the apparition of the man is sometimes seen hanging from the bridge.

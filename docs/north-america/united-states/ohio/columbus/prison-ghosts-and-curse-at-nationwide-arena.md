#title
Prison Ghosts and Curse at Nationwide Arena
#summary
Built over the old Ohio State Penitentiary, Nationwide Arena is believed to be cursed due to freak accidents causing deaths.
#details
Nationwide Arena was built over the old Ohio State Penitentiary, where many prisoners were hanged and others forced to undergo electroshock therapy. Since the arena was built, there have been countless freak accidents resulting in even more deaths, leading locals to believe it is cursed.

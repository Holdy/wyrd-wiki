#title
Running Airplanes at the Neil Armstrong Museum
#summary
Security guards at the Neil Armstrong Museum see apparitions and hear museum airplanes running or starting up after hours.
#details
Security guards have reported seeing strange apparitions after hours at the Neil Armstrong Museum, and sometimes the airplanes in the museum can be heard running or starting up.

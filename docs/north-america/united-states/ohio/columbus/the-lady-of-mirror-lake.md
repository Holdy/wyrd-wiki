#title
The Lady of Mirror Lake
#summary
A woman in pink is said to skate or float across Mirror Lake on cold campus nights, tied to the tragic suicide of a former professor's husband.
#details
At Ohio State University, campus tradition tells of a woman in pink who is said to skate or float across Mirror Lake on cold nights. Campus lore links her to a professor's wife who vowed never to leave the area after her husband died by suicide near where Pomerene Hall now stands, with staff in Pomerene Hall subsequently reporting unexplained footsteps and open locked doors.

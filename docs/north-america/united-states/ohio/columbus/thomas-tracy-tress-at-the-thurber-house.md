#title
Thomas Tracy Tress at the Thurber House
#summary
A man in a white collar is seen in the fireplace mirror of the Thurber House, accompanied by unexplained footsteps and self-opening doors.
#details
Before author James Thurber lived in the Columbus house, a previous homeowner named Thomas Tracy Tress died there in the early 1900s following a firearm accident. Since then, a man in a white collar has been seen in the fireplace mirror, and occupants have reported hearing nighttime footsteps, doors opening on their own, and a clock opening by itself.

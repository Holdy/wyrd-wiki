#title
Alpha Delta Pi Sorority House Ghost
#summary
Alpha Delta Pi sorority house at Ohio State University is haunted by a student who died of an illness, appearing in mirrors and hallways.
#details
According to students at Ohio State University, the Alpha Delta Pi sorority is haunted by the ghost of a former student who died of an illness while living in the house. Residents have reported seeing the girl's reflection in windows and mirrors, sometimes even walking down the hallways, and strange disembodied noises are heard throughout.

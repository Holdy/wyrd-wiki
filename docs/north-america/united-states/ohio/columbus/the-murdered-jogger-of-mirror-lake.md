#title
The Murdered Jogger of Mirror Lake
#summary
Mirror Lake is haunted by the apparition of a murdered jogger who constantly looks over his shoulder as if being followed.
#details
The northwest shore of Mirror Lake is said to be haunted by the apparition of a jogger who was murdered there, and those who have seen him say that he is always looking over his shoulder as if being followed.

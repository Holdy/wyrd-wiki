#title
The Lady in Gray at Camp Chase Cemetery
#summary
Camp Chase Confederate Cemetery is haunted by "The Lady In Gray," who walks through the cemetery leaving flowers on graves.
#details
Camp Chase Confederate Cemetery is said to be haunted by the ghost of a woman known as "The Lady In Gray," who is often seen walking through the cemetery leaving flowers on the graves of fallen soldiers.

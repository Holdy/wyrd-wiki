#title
Drowned Children at Berliner Bike Path
#summary
The Berliner Bike Path is haunted by disembodied children's voices, flashing lights, and a shadowy figure following six drownings.
#details
In 2001, six children were drowned in the river near the Berliner Bike Path. Locals walking on the path at night report feeling as if someone is following them, disembodied children's voices are heard, and reports include strange flashing lights in the woods and the apparition of a tall, shadowy figure.

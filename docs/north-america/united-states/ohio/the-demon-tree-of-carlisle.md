#title
The Demon Tree of Carlisle
#summary
A tall dead tree in Carlisle biking trails appears to grow, emits disembodied voices, and chases people away.
#details
Among the wooded biking trails stands a tall dead tree known as The Demon Tree; although dead for a long time, locals claim it is growing, strange disembodied voices are heard, and a presence is felt that sometimes chases people away.

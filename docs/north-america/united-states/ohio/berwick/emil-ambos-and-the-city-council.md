#title
Emil Ambos and the City Council
#summary
A medium reported that local tycoon Emil Ambos' ghost called Columbus City Council members "short-skates" from the afterlife.
#details
In life, Emil Ambos was a confectionery tycoon whose land was intended to be donated to Columbus as a public park after his death. When City Council brought in a medium to help decide whether to accept the donation, the medium reported that Ambos' ghost viewed city lawmakers from the afterlife and insulted them as "a bunch of short-skates." Consequently, the city rejected the donation, and the land was sold in 1899 to become the Berwick neighborhood.

The Woman in Gray of Camp Chase Cemetery

Summary

Camp Chase Confederate Cemetery in Columbus, Ohio, where over 2,000 Confederate prisoners died during the Civil War, is haunted by a sorrowful apparition known as the Woman in Gray who mourns over specific graves.

Details

Serving as a prison camp during the Civil War, Camp Chase saw thousands of deaths due to disease and malnutrition. While thousands of soldiers rest within the cemetery grounds, its most famous specter is not a soldier, but a young woman dressed in a Civil War-era gray traveling suit. Witnesses have reported seeing her with her head bowed in grief, walking among the headstones, passing effortlessly through locked iron gates, and pausing to weep over the grave of the Unknown Soldier and the grave of Private Benjamin F. Allen. Fresh flowers have frequently been found left at these tombstones following her reported appearances, and reenactors have even recorded hearing mournful sobbing in the empty night air.

#title
The Legend of Helltown
#summary
The village of Boston emptied out following a 1974 federal land buyout, sparking dark urban legends about satanic cults, mutant creatures, and dead-end roads.
#details
After the federal government created the Cuyahoga Valley National Recreation Area in December 1974 and bought up local properties, the village of Boston in Summit County became known locally as Helltown. As homes sat boarded up, dark rumors filled the vacuum, alleging a satanic cult meeting in the church basement, a mutant snake in the woods, a chemical spill, and a road that dead-ended at the edge of the world.

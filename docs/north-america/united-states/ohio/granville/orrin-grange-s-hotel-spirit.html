Orrin Grange's Hotel Spirit

Summary

The original builder of the Buxton Inn is one of the earliest ghosts reported at the historic hotel.

Details

Orrin Grange, the original builder of the Historic Buxton Inn, is also said to haunt the property. Though his spirit is far less often seen today, he was recorded as one of the earliest ghosts encountered at the hotel.

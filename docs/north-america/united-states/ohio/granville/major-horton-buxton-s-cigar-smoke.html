Major Horton Buxton's Cigar Smoke

Summary

The spirit of Major Horton Buxton makes his presence known at the historic Buxton Inn through the scent of his cigar.

Details

The Historic Buxton Inn, open since the 1810s, is haunted by several former owners and associates, including Major Horton Buxton, for whom the inn was named. His presence is reportedly encountered by people on the property who smell the distinct scent of his cigar smoke.

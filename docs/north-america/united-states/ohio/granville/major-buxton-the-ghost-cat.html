Major Buxton the Ghost Cat

Summary

A friendly feline spirit named Major Buxton curls up on guest beds and plays on the first floor of the Buxton Inn.

Details

Alongside human spirits at the Historic Buxton Inn, guests have encountered a friendly animal ghost. Named after Major Buxton, this feline spirit has been reported to jump onto beds to curl up with guests or play on the first floor.

#title
The Lady in Blue at the Buxton Inn
#summary
The ghost of a long-time owner known as the Lady in Blue haunts the historic Buxton Inn.
#details
The Historic Buxton Inn is inhabited by multiple spirits, including the Lady in Blue. This apparition is believed to be the ghost of Ethel Houston Bounell, a former owner who ran the inn for nearly thirty years until her death in 1960.

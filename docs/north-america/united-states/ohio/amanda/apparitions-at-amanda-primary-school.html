    Apparitions at Amanda Primary School

    Summary

    The apparitions of children have been seen near the old condemned schoolhouse and woods next to Amanda Elementary School.

    Details

    Many who have attended Amanda Elementary School have reported paranormal occurrences, including the apparitions of children seen in the old condemned schoolhouse next to the elementary school and a strange mist in the woods nearby.

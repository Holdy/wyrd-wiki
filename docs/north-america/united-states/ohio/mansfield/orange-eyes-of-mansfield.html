Orange Eyes of Mansfield

Summary

A sasquatch-like creature with huge glowing orange eyes allegedly lived under a Mansfield cemetery before relocating to the woods near Mill Lake.

Details

Orange Eyes is a large sasquatch-like creature said to have originally inhabited the area under Riverside Cemetery in Mansfield until construction disrupted its home. It subsequently took up residence in the woods near Mill Lake, and some theorists suggest it may be an extraterrestrial visitor following local UFO sightings.

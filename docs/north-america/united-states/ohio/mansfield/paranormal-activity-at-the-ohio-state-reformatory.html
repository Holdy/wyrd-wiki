Paranormal Activity at the Ohio State Reformatory

Summary

The imposing Ohio State Reformatory in Mansfield is renowned for its architectural beauty and widespread reports of paranormal activity and ghostly sightings.

Details

The Ohio State Reformatory in Mansfield, known for its imposing and haunting architecture, has served as a backdrop for numerous films and built a strong reputation for paranormal activity. Visitors and staff have frequently reported eerie occurrences, strange noises, and ghostly apparitions within its cell blocks and corridors.

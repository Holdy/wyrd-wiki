#title
The Apparitions of Oak Hill Cottage

#summary
Oak Hill Cottage is haunted by the former residents who all died inside the historic building.

#details
Built in 1847, Oak Hill Cottage was home to the Robinsons and later the Johanne and Frances families until 1864. All of them died in the house and are said to still wander its rooms and corridors, creating a dark atmosphere that leads some visitors to refuse to enter.

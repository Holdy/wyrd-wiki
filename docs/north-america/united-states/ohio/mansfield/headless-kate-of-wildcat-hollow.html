Headless Kate of Wildcat Hollow

Summary

Headless Kate haunts Wildcat Hollow after meeting a tragic end at the hands of her murderous butcher husband.

Details

Poor Kate was a girl who married a butcher with jealous and murderous tendencies, and she met her end in the lonely and spooky Wildcat Hollow.

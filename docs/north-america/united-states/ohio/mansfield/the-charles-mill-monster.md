#title
The Charles Mill Monster
#summary
A huge, armless humanoid creature with glowing green eyes has been reported near Charles Mill Lake since 1959.
#details
The Charles Mill Monster is a huge, armless humanoid entity with glowing green eyes known to leave tracks near the shore. Reports of the creature patrolling the waters and surrounding areas of Charles Mill Lake have occurred since 1959.

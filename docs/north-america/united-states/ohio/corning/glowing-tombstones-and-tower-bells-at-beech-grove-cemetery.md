Glowing Tombstones and Tower Bells at Beech Grove Cemetery
#summary
Beech Grove Cemetery features glowing tombstones at night and mysterious ringing bells in a tower with no bells.

Investigators have reported seeing glowing tombstones at night and hearing bells ringing in the tower even though there are no longer bells there at Beech Grove Cemetery.

Klan Executions in Weaver Cave

Summary

Disembodied screams of African Americans executed by Ku Klux Klan members are heard in Weaver Cave.

Details

According to locals, a group of Ku Klux Klan members once executed African Americans in Weaver Cave, and their disembodied screams have been heard by many.

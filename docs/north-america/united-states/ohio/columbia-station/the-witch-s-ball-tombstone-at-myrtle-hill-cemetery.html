The Witch's Ball Tombstone at Myrtle Hill Cemetery

Summary

Myrtle Hill Cemetery features a tombstone for those accused of witchcraft, near which a strange mist is often seen.

Details

There is a tombstone in the Myrtle Hill Cemetery where those accused of witchcraft were buried, and a strange mist is often seen near the grave.

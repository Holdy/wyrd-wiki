#title
The Ghost of Moonville Tunnel
#summary
The abandoned coal mining town of Moonville features a historic tunnel believed to be haunted by a ghostly figure who died tragically beneath its tracks.
#details
Moonville, an abandoned coal mining town, is famous for the haunted Moonville Tunnel. Surrounded by local legends, the historic tunnel is said to be home to a ghostly figure who met a tragic end beneath the railway tracks running through it.

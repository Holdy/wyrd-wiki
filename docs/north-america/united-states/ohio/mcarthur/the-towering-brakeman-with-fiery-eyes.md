#title
The Towering Brakeman with Fiery Eyes

#summary
An eight-foot-tall brakeman ghost with glowing eyes is said to wander the path where the old railroad tracks once ran.

#details
Folklore surrounding the dangerous railway operations of Moonville includes the apparition of an eight-foot-tall brakeman. This towering phantom is said to wander the old path, distinguished by eyes that shine like bulbs of fire.

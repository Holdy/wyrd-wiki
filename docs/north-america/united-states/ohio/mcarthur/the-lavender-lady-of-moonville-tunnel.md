#title
The Lavender Lady of Moonville Tunnel

#summary
An older woman killed by a passing train is said to haunt the area near Moonville Tunnel, her presence marked by the scent of lavender.

#details
An older woman was struck by an oncoming train with such tremendous force that her body was thrown clear of Moonville Tunnel. Because she was known to use lavender on her skin, people walking near the tunnel still report catching the scent of lavender where her spirit is believed to haunt.

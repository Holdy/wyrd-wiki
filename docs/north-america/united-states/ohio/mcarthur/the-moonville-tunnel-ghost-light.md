#title
The Moonville Tunnel Ghost Light
#summary
Visitors to an abandoned train tunnel in Vinton County can see a mysterious ghostly light linked to historical railway fatalities.
#details
In the ghost town of Moonville, visitors to an abandoned train tunnel can see a ghostly light. Mid-19th-century accounts attribute this light either to an engineer killed in a collision between two trains inside the tunnel, or to the lantern of a brakeman who fell asleep at the depot and was struck and killed by a train when he woke up and stumbled onto the tracks.

The Lantern-Waving Engineer

Summary

The ghost of a train engineer is said to appear near the old railway site, seen waving a glowing lantern in the dark.

Details

Among the many spirits tied to the deadly railway history of Moonville, the ghost of a train engineer has been reported by locals. This apparition is seen holding and waving a lantern as he haunts the former railroad area.

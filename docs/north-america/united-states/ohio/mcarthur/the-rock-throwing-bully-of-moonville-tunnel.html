The Rock-Throwing Bully of Moonville Tunnel

Summary

The spirit of a murdered town bully known as Baldie is said to still throw rocks at visitors passing through Moonville Tunnel.

Details

When he was alive, a local resident known as Baldie acted as the town bully, picking fights and throwing stones at passersby until he was eventually murdered. To this day, his ghost is said to linger in Moonville Tunnel and throw rocks at people who walk beneath it.

#title
Shadowy Figures and Guardian Spirits at Serpent Mound
#summary
Visitors to Serpent Mound report eerie phenomena such as sudden chills and shadowy figures, accompanied by folklore of guardian spirits.
#details
Visitors to the 1,300-foot effigy mound often describe an eerie feeling along with occasional unexplained phenomena, such as sudden chills or shadowy figures. Local folklore also tells of ancient spirits guarding the site and warnings against disturbing the earth.

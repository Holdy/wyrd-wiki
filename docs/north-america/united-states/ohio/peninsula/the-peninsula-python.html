    The Peninsula Python

    Summary

    In the summer of 1944, residents of Peninsula reported sightings of a nearly 20-foot-long python leaving giant tracks.

    Details

    In the summer of 1944, residents of Peninsula reported sightings of a nearly 20-foot-long python. The giant snake allegedly raided henhouses, trampled cornfields, and left truck-tire-sized tracks in its wake, though it was never captured.

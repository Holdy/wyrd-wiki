#title
The Lab Coat Professor at Capital University
#summary
The Capital University science building is haunted by a former professor in a lab coat who cleans classrooms and moves microscopes.
#details
The Capital University science building is said to be haunted by the ghost of one of its former professors, who has been seen in several classrooms wearing a lab coat and cleaning up after students, while microscopes and other objects move around by themselves.

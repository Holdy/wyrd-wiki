#title
Phenomena at Hell's Church Ruins
#summary
An empty field where a church burned down is known as Hell's Church, featuring chilling screams, glowing orbs, and a barren dirt patch.
#details
A chimney stands by itself in an empty field where a church allegedly burned down after being struck by lightning or during a blood ritual. Known as "Hell's Church," chilling screams are heard at night, a phantom vehicle has been spotted, glowing orbs appear in the field, and a barren patch of dirt near the chimney refuses to grow grass where the altar allegedly stood.

#title
The Bleeding Tree of Bloody Mary

#summary
A woman burned at the stake for witchcraft haunts Lucas Cemetery, where her carved tree allegedly bleeds at night.

#details
In Lucas Cemetery lies the ghost of Bloody Mary, who was burned at the stake in the 1800s on suspicion of witchcraft. The tree that stands where she was buried has her name carved into its bark, and local legend states that visiting the cemetery at night causes the tree to bleed and brings a drop in temperature.

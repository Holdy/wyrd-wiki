John the Student Protector at Brooklyn Middle School

Summary

Brooklyn Middle School is haunted by a bullied student who died in a crash and now protects other bullied students.

Details

Brooklyn Middle School is said to be haunted by the ghost of a former student named John, a shy student often picked on by classmates who died in a car accident shortly after graduation. His spirit has been seen by many students and is said to stick up for those who are being bullied.

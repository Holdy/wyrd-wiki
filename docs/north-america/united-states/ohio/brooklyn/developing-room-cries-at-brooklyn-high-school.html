Developing Room Cries at Brooklyn High School

Summary

Photography students at Brooklyn High School hear strange sounds and a crying child inside the developing room.

Details

Students who take photography classes at Brooklyn High School have reported hearing strange sounds inside the developing room, as if someone is there with them, and a young child has also been heard crying.

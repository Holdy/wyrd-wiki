#title
Clarence the Auditorium Spirit at Brooklyn Middle School
#summary
The auditorium at Brooklyn Middle School is haunted by Clarence, a spirit who causes trouble if not asked for permission before productions.
#details
The school auditorium is said to be haunted by a ghost named Clarence, and students say that if they do not ask for her permission to use the auditorium before a production, something always goes wrong.

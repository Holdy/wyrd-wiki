    The Crosswick Monster

    Summary

    In 1882, two boys were attacked by a vicious 12- to 14-foot-long lizard near Crosswick.

    Details

    In 1882, two boys were attacked by a vicious, 12- to 14-foot-long lizard. Village men hunted the creature across the area, but townspeople were unable to track it down, and it was never seen again.

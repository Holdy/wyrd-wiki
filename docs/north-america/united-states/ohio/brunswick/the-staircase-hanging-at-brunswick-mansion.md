#title
The Staircase Hanging at Brunswick Mansion
#summary
Brunswick Mansion was widely believed to be haunted by a man who hanged himself from the spiral staircase before its demolition.
#details
This mansion was torn down in 2005 but was widely believed to be haunted by the ghost of a man who once hung himself from the spiral staircase.

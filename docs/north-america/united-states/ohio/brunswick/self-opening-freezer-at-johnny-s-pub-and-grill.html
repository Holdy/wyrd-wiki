Self-Opening Freezer at Johnny's Pub and Grill

Summary

Employees at Johnny's Pub and Grill have reported heavy freezer doors opening and closing and dishes moving by themselves.

Details

Employees have reported seeing the heavy freezer door open and close by itself, and objects like dishes move by themselves at Johnny's Pub and Grill.

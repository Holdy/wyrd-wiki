#title
The Melon Heads of Kirtland
#summary
Humanoid creatures with enormous heads known as Melon Heads wander the woods near Kirtland after escaping a mad doctor's lair.
#details
The Melon Heads are humanoid beings with enormous heads who wander the woods near Kirtland. According to local legend, they escaped or were brought from the lair of a mad doctor or scientist, and sightings have primarily been reported along Wisner Road.

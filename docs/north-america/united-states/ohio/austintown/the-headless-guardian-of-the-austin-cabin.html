    The Headless Guardian of the Austin Cabin

    Summary

    A headless apparition is seen guarding the 18th-century Austin Log Cabin Museum.

    Details

    Locals report seeing a headless apparition who appears to be guarding the Austin Log Cabin Museum, which was built by John Austin in the late 1700s.

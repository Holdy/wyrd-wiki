#title
The Grassman of Salt Fork State Park
#summary
A 7-foot-tall, human-looking ape-like creature known as the Grassman is said to roam the wilderness and trails of Salt Fork State Park.
#details
The Grassman, frequently referred to as Ohio's Eastern Bigfoot, is a 7-foot-tall ape-like creature described as being more human-looking than its Bigfoot cousin. It is known to reside in the wilderness and along the hiking trails of Salt Fork State Park.

Salt Fork State Park, Guernsey County, Ohio

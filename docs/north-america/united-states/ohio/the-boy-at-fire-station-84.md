#title
The Boy at Fire Station 84
#summary
Fire Station No. 84 is haunted by a young boy whose spirit is seen by firefighters while doors move and items disappear.
#details
Fire Station No. 84 is said to be haunted by the ghost of a young boy whose spirit has been seen many times by firefighters, where doors open and close by themselves and objects go missing and reappear in strange places.

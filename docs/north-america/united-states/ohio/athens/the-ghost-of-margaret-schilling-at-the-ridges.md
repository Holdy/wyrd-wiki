#title
The Ghost of Margaret Schilling at The Ridges
#summary
A patient who went missing at the Athens Lunatic Asylum in 1978 was found weeks later, leaving a permanent body stain on the floor of a closed-off ward.
#details
Opening in 1874 on a hill overlooking Athens, the Athens Lunatic Asylum (later renamed the Ridges) became the center of a famous legend involving patient Margaret Schilling. After going missing in December 1978, she was found weeks later in an abandoned, closed-off section of the hospital where her body had left a lasting stain on the floor that remains visible to this day.

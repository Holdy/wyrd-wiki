Stroud the Buffalo at Stroud's Run

Summary

Stroud's Run is haunted by the ghost of a giant buffalo whose skull was buried with coins after British soldiers killed him.

Details

British soldiers killed a wild buffalo named Stroud for food, leading townspeople to storm their camp and leave no survivors. A soldier allegedly buried the British soldiers' money inside Stroud's skull, and the skull was never found while the ghost of a giant buffalo is seen drinking from the stream at night.

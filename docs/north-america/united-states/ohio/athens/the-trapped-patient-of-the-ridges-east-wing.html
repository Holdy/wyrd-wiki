The Trapped Patient of the Ridges East Wing

Summary

The spirit of a patient who died under mysterious circumstances remains trapped in the east wing of the former asylum, where cries and flickering lights are reported.

Details

A patient who died under mysterious circumstances is said to have a spirit that remains trapped in the building. People claim to hear cries and see flickering lights in the building's east wing.

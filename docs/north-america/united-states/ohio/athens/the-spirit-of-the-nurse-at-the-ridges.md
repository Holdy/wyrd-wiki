#title
The Spirit of the Nurse at the Ridges
#summary
The ghost of a nurse who cared for patients during the asylum's operation is frequently sighted wandering the building's halls and windows at night.
#details
According to legend, a nurse who cared for patients when the Ridges asylum was operational now appears to those wandering the grounds at night. Witnesses describe her as a faint figure in a white uniform, sometimes seen near old hospital windows or in the hallways.

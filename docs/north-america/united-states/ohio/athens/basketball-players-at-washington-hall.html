Basketball Players at Washington Hall

Summary

Washington Hall is rumored to be haunted by the spirits of female basketball players killed in a car crash.

Details

Washington Hall is rumored to be haunted by the spirits of a group of female basketball players who were killed in a car crash while at an away game, and some residents have heard the girls playing basketball at night or talking.

#title
Haunting and Possession at Wilson Hall
#summary
Wilson Hall at Ohio University is haunted by apparitions, voices, and objects moving, with a legend of a student suicide from demonic possession.
#details
Residents of Wilson Hall report seeing apparitions, hearing voices, and objects moving by themselves, while cold spots are felt throughout the building. Some students insist that a girl once committed suicide in Wilson Hall after being possessed by a demonic force, built over an ancient Native American burial site.

    Kohler Hall Hauntings at Baldwin Wallace University

    Summary

    Kohler Hall features basement voices and footsteps, echoing its past as a Civil War hospital, mental institution, and orphanage.

    Details

    Residents at Kohler Hall have reported hearing strange voices and footsteps when nobody else is around, especially in the basement area where an old tunnel leads to the campus chapel. Kohler Hall was built in the mid-1800s and served as a Civil War hospital, underground railroad station, mental health institution, orphanage, and mortuary.

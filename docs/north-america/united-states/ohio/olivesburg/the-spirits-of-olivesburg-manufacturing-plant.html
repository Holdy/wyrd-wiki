    The Spirits of Olivesburg Manufacturing Plant

    Summary

    Five workers killed in a tragic molten metal accident are said to speak to visitors about their final moments on certain nights.

    Details

    During the 1940s at Olivesburg’s Quarry and Manufacturing Plant, the chain of a large iron kettle broke, covering five workers in molten metal. All five slowly died on the floor of the plant, immersed in iron and screaming in pain, and locals believe that visiting on certain nights causes the workers to speak about their final moments.

    The Dogman of Defiance

    Summary

    A humanoid dog-like creature allegedly attacked two railway workers in Defiance in the early morning hours of 1972.

    Details

    The Dogman of Defiance, also called the Werewolf of Defiance, is a humanoid dog-like creature that made its presence known by attacking two railway workers in 1972. While recent sightings are rare, the creature is said to occasionally dart into the woods near Independence Dam State Park.

#title
Phantom Boulders and Headlights at The Narrows
#summary
On Route 7 known as "The Narrows," drivers report unnatural cold spots, phantom headlights in mirrors, and falling phantom boulders.
#details
On a stretch of road known as "the narrows," many drivers have reported feeling unnatural cold spots, seeing phantom headlights in their mirror, and even seeing phantom boulders fall onto the road.

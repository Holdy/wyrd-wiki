The Buried Servant at TKE Fraternity House

Summary

TKE Fraternity House is haunted by the ghost of a former female servant who was buried in a hidden grave behind the house.

Details

TKE Fraternity House is rumored to be haunted by the ghost of a young woman who is believed to have been a servant at the house when it was still a residence and was buried in a hidden grave behind the house. Her ghost has been seen in many areas inside the house and outside on the property.

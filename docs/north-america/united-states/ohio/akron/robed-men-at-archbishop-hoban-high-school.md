#title
Robed Men at Archbishop Hoban High School
#summary
A group of robed men is seen walking the hallways near the old dormitories during the feast of St. Joseph.
#details
The admissions office, art rooms, and tutoring center at Archbishop Hoban High School are said to be haunted by a group of robed men who are seen walking the hallways near the old dormitories during the feast of St. Joseph.

#title
The Violent Spirit at the Campus Sorority House
#summary
Another sorority house on the University of Akron campus experiences moving furniture, opening attic doors, and thrown objects.
#details
There is another sorority house on the University of Akron campus that is said to be haunted by a spirit known for being violent and mischievous. Footsteps and furniture moving can be heard from the first floor when nobody is home, doors in the attic open and close by themselves without warning, and residents have reported having objects such as plates and dishes thrown at them.

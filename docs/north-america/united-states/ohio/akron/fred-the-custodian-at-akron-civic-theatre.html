Fred the Custodian at Akron Civic Theatre

Summary

The Akron Civic Theatre is haunted by a lifelong custodian named Fred who continues his duties and protects the building from thieves.

Details

According to employees, a lifelong custodian named Fred died one night while cleaning after hours at the Akron Civic Theatre. He now continues performing his custodial duties in the afterlife and is known for protecting the theater by attacking thieves and vandals.

Mr. Litchfield's Haunting at Litchfield Community Middle School

Summary

Doors open and close, lockers slam, and footsteps and laughter echo through Litchfield Community Middle School at night.

Details

Litchfield Community Middle School is said to be haunted by the ghost of Mr. Litchfield, after whom the school is named. At night, doors open and close by themselves, and lockers slam open or shut unexpectedly, while footsteps and laughter are heard throughout the school even during the daytime.

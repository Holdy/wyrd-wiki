#title
The Boiler Room Suicide at Sigma Nu Sorority House
#summary
Sigma Nu Sorority House is haunted by the ghost of a former student who hanged herself in the boiler room.
#details
Sigma Nu Sorority House is said to be haunted by the ghost of a former student who hung herself in the boiler room. To this day, most residents will not go into the boiler room alone.

#title
The Lady in White at Rose Hill Burial Park
#summary
A lady in white is seen in the forest behind Rose Hill Burial Park, accompanied by a strange mist covering tombstones at night.
#details
A lady in white is seen in the forest near the back of the cemetery at Rose Hill Burial Park, and a strange mist is sometimes seen covering the tombstones at night.

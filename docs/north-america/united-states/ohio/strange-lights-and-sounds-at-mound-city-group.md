#title
Strange Lights and Sounds at Mound City Group
#summary
Locals report experiencing unexplained strange lights and sounds at night around the Mound City Group earthworks.
#details
At the Mound City Group site near Chillicothe, which contains dozens of conical and geometric mounds where archaeologists have uncovered artifacts and human remains, locals report strange lights and sounds around the area at night.

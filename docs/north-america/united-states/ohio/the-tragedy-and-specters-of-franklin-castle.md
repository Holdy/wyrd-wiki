#title
The Tragedy and Specters of Franklin Castle

#summary
Built by German immigrant banker Hannes Tiedemann in the late 19th century, Cleveland's ornate Franklin Castle is dogged by rumors of family curses, secret passages, and ghostly apparitions including a crying girl and a woman in black.

#details
Constructed between 1881 and 1883 for banker Hannes Tiedemann, the imposing Victorian-style mansion on Franklin Boulevard became associated with tragedy after multiple family members, including four of Tiedemann's children and his mother, died within a short span of years. Over subsequent decades, successive occupants and visitors reported paranormal activity ranging from flickering lights and mysterious slamming doors to the sounds of a sad, crying girl and sightings of a spectral woman dressed in black, alleged to be a former maid.

#location
Franklin Castle is located at 4308 Franklin Boulevard in Cleveland, Ohio.

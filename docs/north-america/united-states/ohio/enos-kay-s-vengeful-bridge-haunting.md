#title
Enos Kay's Vengeful Bridge Haunting
#summary
The ghost of a heartbroken man is said to violently shake the cars of unsuspecting couples crossing Timmons Bridge.
#details
In the 1800s, Enos Kay took his own life on Timmons Bridge after his fiancée eloped with a stranger she met at a church picnic. Before his death, he vowed to haunt foolish lovers until Judgment Day. Drivers have reported that when couples cross the bridge, their cars shake violently, windows are clawed or scratched, and the sinister face of a young man stares in at them, while solo drivers and arguing couples are left alone.

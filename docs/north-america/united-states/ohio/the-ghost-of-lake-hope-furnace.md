#title
The Ghost of Lake Hope Furnace
#summary
A watchman who guarded the historic iron furnace during the 19th century is said to still walk the grounds with his lantern on stormy nights.
#details
At Lake Hope State Park, the ruins of an iron furnace that operated from 1854 to 1874 are the setting for a ghostly tale. According to legend, a watchman who once guarded the furnace died during a storm and never stopped making his rounds, appearing with his lantern when storms roll in.

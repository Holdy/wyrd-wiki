The Ghost Wife at Squire's Castle

Summary

Squire's Castle is haunted by the squire's wife, who died there and is seen wandering amidst cold spots.

Details

The squire's wife is said to haunt the castle where she died, with cold spots felt throughout the building and her spirit seen on many occasions wandering around the castle.

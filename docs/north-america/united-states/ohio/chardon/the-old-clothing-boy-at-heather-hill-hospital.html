The Old-Clothing Boy at Heather Hill Hospital

Summary

Heather Hill Hospital is haunted by a young boy in old-fashioned clothing who runs through the hospital and knocks on walls.

Details

Heather Hill Hospital is reported by employees to be haunted by the ghost of a young boy dressed in very old, out-of-date clothing, who has been seen by workers on the late shift and heard running around the hospital or knocking on walls.

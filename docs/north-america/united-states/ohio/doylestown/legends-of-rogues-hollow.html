    Legends of Rogues’ Hollow

    Summary

    The historic coal mining village of Rogues’ Hollow is known for eerie tales including a crying baby at night, ghost trains, and a headless horseman.

    Details

    Rogues’ Hollow, often called Ohio’s Sleepy Hollow, was a notorious coal mining village frequented by outlaws and gangsters. Over time, the area accumulated chilling local legends, including the sounds of a crying baby at night, phantom ghost trains, and Ohio's own headless horseman.

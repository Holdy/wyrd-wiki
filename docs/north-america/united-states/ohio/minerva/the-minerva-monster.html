    The Minerva Monster

    Summary

    A 7-foot-tall furry creature was spotted by residents in the late 1970s around Minerva.

    Details

    The Minerva Monster is a 7-foot-tall furry creature that was first sighted in the late 1970s. Although a few additional sightings have occurred in neighboring counties, the creature is most closely associated with Minerva.

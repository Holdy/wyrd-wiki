    Barn Fire Children on Zeck Road

    Summary

    The ghosts of two children killed in a 1960s barn fire on Zeck Road are seen and heard near the property.

    Details

    There is an old barn on Zech Road that burnt down sometime during the 1960s, killing two young children whose ghosts have been seen and heard in the area near the barn.

#title
Haunted History of Paul Peters Farm Cemetery
#summary
Paul Peters Farm Cemetery is known for electronic failures, cold spots, high EMF readings, nighttime screams, and a history of hangings.
#details
Located just south of the Franklin and Pickaway county border, Paul Peters Farm Cemetery (also known as the old Cholera Cemetery) features destroyed or buried gravestones hidden from the road. Visitors report that electronic equipment inexplicably fails on the premises, cold spots are felt, and electromagnetic field readings spike at the front of the cemetery. According to lore, hangings once took place from a large tree in the cemetery where bodies were buried, and nocturnal screams have been heard echoing from the grounds.

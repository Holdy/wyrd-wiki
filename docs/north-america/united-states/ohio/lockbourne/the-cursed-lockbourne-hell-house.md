#title
The Cursed Lockbourne Hell House
#summary
A house near Paul Peters Farm Cemetery was known as a hell house plagued by moaning shadows, flickering lights, and a cycle of fatal tragedies.
#details
Once located down the driveway from Paul Peters Farm Cemetery, the Lockbourne Hell House was haunted by shadowy figures that moaned and caused kitchen lights to flicker. The property earned a reputation for bringing extreme bad luck: the original brick farmhouse was destroyed by a tornado that killed its elderly resident, two subsequent families lost their husbands or fathers in violent car accidents, and the final family lost their head-of-household when the home burned to the ground. Today, only the foundation remains.

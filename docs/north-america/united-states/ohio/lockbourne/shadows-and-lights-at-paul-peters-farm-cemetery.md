#title
Shadows and Lights at Paul Peters Farm Cemetery

#summary
An old cholera cemetery is said to be haunted by a stalking shadow in the woodlot, bobbing red lights, and nighttime screams.

#details
The Paul Peters Farm Cemetery, also known as the Harrison Township Cholera Cemetery, was used during cholera epidemics along the old canal. Today, a shadow has been seen stalking the woodlot behind the old cemetery, red lights bob around the area, and screams have been heard there at night.

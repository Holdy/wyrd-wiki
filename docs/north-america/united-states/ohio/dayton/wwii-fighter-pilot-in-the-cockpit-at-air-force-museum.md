#title
WWII Fighter Pilot in the Cockpit at Air Force Museum
#summary
Visitors at the Dayton Air Force Museum report seeing the ghost of an old World War II fighter pilot in a plane's cockpit.
#details
Many visitors have reported seeing the ghost of an old World War II fighter pilot in the cockpit of one of the old fighter planes at the Dayton Air Force Museum.

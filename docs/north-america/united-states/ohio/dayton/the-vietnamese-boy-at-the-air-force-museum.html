The Vietnamese Boy at the Air Force Museum

Summary

The ghost of a young Vietnamese boy killed by a displayed helicopter walks the hallways of the Air Force Museum at night.

Details

At night, the ghost of a young Vietnamese boy is seen walking the hallways at the Dayton Air Force Museum, believed to have been killed by one of the Vietnam-era helicopters now on display.

#title
The Witch’s Tower Lightning Tragedy and Haunting
#summary
A stone lookout tower struck by lightning in 1967, killing a teenager, is now said to be haunted by the ghosts of a girl and boy.
#details
In 1967, a lightning strike killed a teenager named Peggy Harmeson while she sought shelter from a storm inside a stone lookout tower in a Dayton park. Since the tragedy, the tower has gained a dark reputation, with visitors reporting sightings of a ghostly girl and boy whenever lightning flashes in the sky.

#title
The Murder and Haunting of Billy Fee

#summary
Following a brutal honeymoon murder on the Ohio River, the spirit of Billy Fee allegedly haunts the waters near Lawrenceburg with nightly reenactments and a spectral face etched in stone.

#details
After a Cincinnati wedding, 17-year-old Eva Dickinson and 19-year-old John Keys embarked on a honeymoon down the Ohio River on a shanty boat accompanied by Bert Rusk and Billy Fee. While fishing in a skiff on the Kentucky side of the river, screams and gunshots rang out, and only three men returned. Days later, Billy Fee's naked body was recovered from the waters with gunshot wounds and a slashed neck. The surviving trio blamed each other, and no one was ever brought to justice. Today, Billy Fee is said to haunt the waters near Lawrenceburg, where ghostly reenactments of the crime occur nightly with shrieks and gunshots, and his spectral face is reportedly etched into a rock on the Kentucky side of the river.

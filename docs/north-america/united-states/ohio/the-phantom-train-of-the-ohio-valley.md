#title
The Phantom Train of the Ohio Valley
#summary
Ghostly train whistles and spectral locomotives are reported moving through abandoned tracks in the Ohio Valley on foggy nights.
#details
Linked to tragic train accidents or lost souls of railroad workers, phantom trains are said to appear on foggy nights, passing silently through areas where no trains run anymore. People describe hearing the sound of a distant whistle, the rumble of wheels, and seeing a glowing train moving through the darkness.

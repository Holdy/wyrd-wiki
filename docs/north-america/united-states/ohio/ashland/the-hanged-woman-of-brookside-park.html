    The Hanged Woman of Brookside Park

    Summary

    Brookside Park is rumored to be haunted by the ghost of a young woman who was hanged in the woods by the pond.

    Details

    Brookside Park is rumored to be haunted by the ghost of a young woman who was hanged in the woods by the pond, and people have claimed to see her ghost there at night.

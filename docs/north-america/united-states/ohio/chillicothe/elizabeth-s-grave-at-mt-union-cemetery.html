Elizabeth's Grave at Mt. Union Cemetery

Summary

Chillicothe is haunted by a ghost named Elizabeth who hanged herself from a tree, with her tombstone in the back and grave in the front.

Details

According to locals, Chillicothe is haunted by a ghost named Elizabeth who hung herself from a tree in the cemetery. Her tombstone is located in the back of the cemetery, but her actual grave site is in the front.

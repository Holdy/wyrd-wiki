#title
Apparitions and Noises at the Ross County Courthouse
#summary
The 19th-century Ross County Courthouse is reported to be haunted by former prisoners and tragic victims whose footsteps, voices, and apparitions are seen in the hallways.
#details
Built in the 19th century, the Ross County Courthouse is said to be haunted by former prisoners and victims of tragic events. Visitors and staff report hearing footsteps and voices, and seeing apparitions in the hallways.

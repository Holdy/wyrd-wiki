The Spirits and History of the Majestic Theatre

Summary

A historic theatre in Chillicothe that served as a makeshift morgue during the 1918 flu pandemic is reportedly haunted by soldiers and other spirits.

Details

During the 1918 flu pandemic, the Majestic Theatre in Chillicothe was used as an overflow mortuary when nearby Camp Sherman's camp mortuary ran out of space, with bodies stored in dressing rooms and embalmed on stage, leading to the alley behind being dubbed Blood Alley. Following this dark chapter, ghost stories emerged at the theatre, with visitors reporting encounters with Civil War or Army soldiers, a little girl, and a keys-jingling janitor.

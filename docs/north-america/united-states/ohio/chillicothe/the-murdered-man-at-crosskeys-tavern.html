The Murdered Man at Crosskeys Tavern

Summary

Crosskeys Tavern is haunted by the ghost of a man murdered in the alleyway who throws objects and glasses.

Details

Cross Keys Tavern is reportedly haunted by the ghost of a man who was murdered in the alleyway, and objects and glasses are sometimes thrown across the room by him.

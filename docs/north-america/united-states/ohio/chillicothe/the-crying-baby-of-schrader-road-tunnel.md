#title
The Crying Baby of Schrader Road Tunnel
#summary
Schrader Road Tunnel is haunted by a woman and baby hit by a train, with the baby's cries heard near the bridge at night.
#details
The tunnel on Schrader Road is said to be haunted by the ghost of a woman and her baby who were hit by a train while crossing the bridge. Her spirit has been seen by locals many times, and a baby is heard crying near the bridge at night.
#location
Schrader Road Tunnel, Chillicothe, Ohio

#title
The Loveland Frogman
#summary
A four-foot-tall humanoid frog has been spotted hopping along roadsides and near the Little Miami River since the 1950s.
#details
The Loveland Frogman is a cryptid described as a humanoid frog standing about 4 feet tall. The first reported encounter happened in 1955 when a businessman claimed to see three frog-like creatures standing upright, and a later sighting in 1972 involved a police officer reporting a similar creature on the side of the road that disappeared suddenly.

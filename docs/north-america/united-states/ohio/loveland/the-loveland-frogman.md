#title
The Loveland Frogman
#summary
A four-foot-tall bipedal frog-creature has been sighted in marshes along the Little Miami River near Loveland.
#details
Also known as the Loveland Frog, this bipedal frog-creature stands four feet tall and has been spotted in the marshes and along the riverbanks near Loveland since 1955. The creature is considered peaceful with no known attacks, and sightings have frequently occurred around Lake Isabella in Hamilton County.

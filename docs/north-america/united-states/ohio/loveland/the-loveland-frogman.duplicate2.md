#title
The Loveland Frogman
#summary
A four-foot tall, upright-walking amphibian cryptid has been sighted multiple times by citizens and police officers along the Little Miami River in Loveland.
#details
Ohio's most famous cryptid is a four-foot amphibian creature that supposedly walks upright along the Little Miami River in Loveland. A businessman reported seeing such creatures near a bridge in 1955, and the legend gained renewed attention in 1972 when two Loveland police officers reported separate encounters within weeks of each other, with one officer even firing a shot at the creature.

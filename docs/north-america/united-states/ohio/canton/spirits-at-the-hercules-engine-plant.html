    Spirits at the Hercules Engine Plant

    Summary

    Workers at the historic Hercules Engine Plant report cold spots, touches from spirits, apparitions, voices, and nighttime orbs.

    Details

    Workers have reported feeling cold spots throughout the building, being touched by spirits, seeing frequent apparitions, hearing disembodied voices, and seeing orbs at night when nobody is around at the Hercules Engine Plant, which served as a U.S. Army training facility during WWI and WWII.

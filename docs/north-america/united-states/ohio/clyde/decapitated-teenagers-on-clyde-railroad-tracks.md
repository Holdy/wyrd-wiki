#title
Decapitated Teenagers on Clyde Railroad Tracks
#summary
Railroad tracks in Clyde are haunted by four teenagers who were hit by a train and whose heads were never found.
#details
The railroad tracks on the outskirts of Clyde are said to be haunted by the ghosts of four teenagers who were hit by a train, who were decapitated in the crash and whose heads were never found.

#title
The Legend of Gore Orphanage
#summary
Local folklore claims an orphanage on Gore Orphanage Road burned down with children inside, though historical records show no such fire occurred there.
#details
According to local lore in Lorain County, an orphanage on Gore Orphanage Road near Vermilion caught fire, killing the children inside, and their cries can still be heard at night. Historical research has untangled this myth, revealing that while a real orphanage existed nearby with poor conditions, and a local mansion was burned by vandals in 1923, the deadly children's fire actually occurred in Collinwood in 1908, with no evidence that any child ever died at Gore Orphanage.

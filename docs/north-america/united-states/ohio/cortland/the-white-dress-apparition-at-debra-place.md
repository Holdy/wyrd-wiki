#title
The White Dress Apparition at Debra Place
#summary
Debra Place Apartments tenants are awakened by presences, moving objects, and a woman in a long white dress.
#details
Guests report being awakened in the middle of the night and feeling as if there is a presence in the room at Debra Place Apartments. Objects are seen moving by themselves, and the apparition of a woman in a long white dress is seen.

The Tall Hat Man at Pauni Apartments

Summary

Pauni Apartments, built over a Native American burial site, feature cold spots, slamming doors, floating objects, and a man in a tall hat.

Details

It is rumored that Pauni Apartments are built over an ancient Native American burial site. Cold spots are felt throughout, doors slam shut by themselves, and objects are seen moving or floating. There have also been reports of a man who walks the halls wearing a tall hat.

#title
1800s Ballgoers and Whispers at Cincinnati Music Hall
#summary
Built over a graveyard, Cincinnati Music Hall features angry whispers, a singing woman's voice, and a phantom 1800s ballroom gathering.
#details
The Cincinnati Music Hall was built over an old graveyard, and during 1980s renovations, graves were uncovered. Angry whispers and disembodied laughter are heard at night, a woman's voice sings throughout the hall, and a custodian once came downstairs to find a ballroom full of people dressed in 1800s attire having a gathering.

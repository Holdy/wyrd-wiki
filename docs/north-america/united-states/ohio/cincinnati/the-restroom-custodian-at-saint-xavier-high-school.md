#title
The Restroom Custodian at Saint Xavier High School
#summary
Saint Xavier High School is haunted by a former custodian who hanged himself in the bathroom stalls.
#details
St. Xavier High School is believed to be haunted by the ghost of a former custodian who hung himself in the bathroom stalls and has been seen many times in the hallways or the men's bathrooms.

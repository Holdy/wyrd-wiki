#title
Subway Construction Ghosts in Cincinnati
#summary
The abandoned Cincinnati downtown subway tunnels and platforms are haunted by the spirits of those who died during construction.
#details
The Cincinnati subway began construction in 1920 but was stopped in 1924 due to budget problems and discontinued during the Great Depression. The tunnels and platforms are said by locals to be haunted by the spirits of those who died during the construction of the subway.

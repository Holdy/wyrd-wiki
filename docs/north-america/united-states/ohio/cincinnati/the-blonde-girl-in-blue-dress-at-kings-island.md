#title
The Blonde Girl in Blue Dress at Kings Island
#summary
A young blonde girl in a blue dress plays hide and seek with workers after hours at Kings Island.
#details
Kings Island Amusement Park is believed to be haunted by several spirits, the most infamous of which is a young girl with blonde hair who is often seen wearing a blue dress and is known for frightening workers by playing hide and seek with them after hours.

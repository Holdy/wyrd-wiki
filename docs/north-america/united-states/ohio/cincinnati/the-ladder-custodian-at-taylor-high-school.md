#title
The Ladder Custodian at Taylor High School
#summary
Taylor High School is haunted by a custodian who died of a heart attack on a ladder, causing doors to open and toilets to flush.
#details
Taylor High School is believed to be haunted by the ghost of one of its former custodians who died of a heart attack one night while working on a ladder, and ever since, students and teachers report doors opening and closing by themselves and toilets flushing on their own.
#location
Taylor Public High School, Cincinnati, Ohio

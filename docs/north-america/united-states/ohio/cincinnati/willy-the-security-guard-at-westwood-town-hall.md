#title
Willy the Security Guard at Westwood Town Hall
#summary
Westwood Town Hall is haunted by security guard Willy, who committed suicide by hanging in the premises' apartment.
#details
The Westwood Town Hall is said to be haunted by the ghost of a former security guard named Willy, who according to local legend committed suicide by hanging himself in the apartment on the premises after being fired.

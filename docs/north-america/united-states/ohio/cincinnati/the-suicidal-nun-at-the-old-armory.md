#title
The Suicidal Nun at the Old Armory
#summary
The Cincinnati Job Corps Center is haunted by footsteps, water faucets turning on, and a nun who committed suicide in the convent.
#details
Students at the Cincinnati Job Corps Center report seeing apparitions and hearing footsteps in the hallways on the 2nd and 3rd floors, and water faucets turn on by themselves. The most commonly sighted spirit is that of a nun who committed suicide in the building when it was still a convent.

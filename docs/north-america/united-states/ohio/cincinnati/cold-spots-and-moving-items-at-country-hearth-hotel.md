#title
Cold Spots and Moving Items at Country Hearth Hotel
#summary
The Country Hearth Hotel features cold spots, self-opening curtains, pounding on walls, and moved personal items.
#details
Cold spots are felt throughout the hotel even when the heat is on, curtains in the rooms open and close by themselves, pounding is heard on the walls, and guests have reported having personal items moved or misplaced.

#title
The Drowned Foreign Exchange Student at Western Hills High School
#summary
Western Hills High School pool is haunted by a foreign exchange student who drowned while an invisible force held him down.
#details
According to local legend, a foreign exchange student was drowned in the pool one afternoon at Western Hills High School, and the custodian who witnessed the event claims to have seen something invisible holding the boy down under water.

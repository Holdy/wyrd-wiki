#title
Phantom Dogs and Watching Statue at Spring Grove
#summary
Spring Grove Cemetery features two phantom dogs running on the castle porch and a bronze statue that watches visitors.
#details
Visitors claim that when sitting on the porch of the castle in front of Spring Grove Cemetery, two phantom dogs can be seen running in the grass, and the bronze statue in the cemetery watches visitors.

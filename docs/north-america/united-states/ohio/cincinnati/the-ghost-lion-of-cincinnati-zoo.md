#title
The Ghost Lion of Cincinnati Zoo
#summary
Visitors at the Cincinnati Zoo report feeling followed and hearing soft footsteps from a ghost lion.
#details
The Cincinnati Zoo & Botanical Garden is said to be haunted by the ghost of a lion who was once held there, with guests reporting feeling as if they are being followed and hearing the sound of soft footsteps when nothing is there.

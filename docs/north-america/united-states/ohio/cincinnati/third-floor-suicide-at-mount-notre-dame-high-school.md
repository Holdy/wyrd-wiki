#title
Third Floor Suicide at Mount Notre Dame High School
#summary
The 3rd floor of Mount Notre Dame High School features self-opening lockers and lights turning on by themselves.
#details
The 3rd floor of Mt. Notre Dame High School is said to be haunted by the ghost of a female student who committed suicide in the early 1940s. At night, the lights in the school turn on by themselves, and occasionally all the lockers on the 3rd floor are found wide open.
#location
Mt. Notre Dame Catholic High School, Cincinnati, Ohio

#title
The Short Woman in Oak Hills Auditorium
#summary
The Oak Hills High School auditorium is haunted by a short woman seen in the back seats or catwalk.
#details
The Oak Hills High School auditorium is said to be haunted by the ghost of a short woman who is known for turning lights on and off and opening and closing doors without warning, and has been seen seated in the back of the auditorium or on the catwalk above the stage.
#location
Oak Hills Public High School, Cincinnati, Ohio

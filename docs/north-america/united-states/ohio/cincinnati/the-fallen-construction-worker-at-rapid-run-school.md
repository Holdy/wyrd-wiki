#title
The Fallen Construction Worker at Rapid Run School
#summary
Rapid Run Middle School auditorium is haunted by a construction worker who fell from a ladder to his death.
#details
The Rapid Run Middle School auditorium is said to be haunted by the ghost of a worker who was killed during its construction after falling from a ladder to his death. Lights sometimes turn on and off by themselves, and chairs go missing or move by themselves.

Rapid Run Middle School, Cincinnati, Ohio

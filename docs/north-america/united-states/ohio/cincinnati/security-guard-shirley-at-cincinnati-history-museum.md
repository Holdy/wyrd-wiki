#title
Security Guard Shirley at Cincinnati History Museum
#summary
The Cincinnati History Museum is haunted by a security guard shot by burglars, with self-locking doors and footsteps.
#details
The Cincinnati History Museum is said to be haunted by the ghost of one of its former security guards, named Shirley, who was shot and killed by burglars on the 4th floor. Doors are heard closing and locking by themselves, and footsteps are heard after hours when nobody is around.
#location
Cincinnati History Museum At Union Terminal, Cincinnati, Ohio

#title
Charlie the Mall Presence at Western Woods Mall
#summary
Security guards at the Western Woods Mall experience cold spots and an unseen presence named Charlie.
#details
Cold spots are felt in some areas of the mall, and a presence can be felt that has been named "Charlie" by the security guards who have felt his presence at the Western Woods Mall.

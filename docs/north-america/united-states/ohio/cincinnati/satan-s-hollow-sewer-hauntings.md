#title
Satan's Hollow Sewer Hauntings
#summary
Satan's Hollow sewer system features satanic worship, animal sacrifices, screams, apparitions, and the demon YG ("The Shadow Man").
#details
Satan's Hollow is an old sewer system in the suburbs of Cincinnati where satanic worship is allegedly practiced. Screams are heard coming from the sewers at night, animal sacrifices are performed at an altar deep within, and many apparitions have been seen. It is also rumored that the demon YG, also known as "The Shadow Man," has been located in the sewers.

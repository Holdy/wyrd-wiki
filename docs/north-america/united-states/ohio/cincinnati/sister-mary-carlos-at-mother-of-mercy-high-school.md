#title
Sister Mary Carlos at Mother Of Mercy High School
#summary
Sister Mary Carlos turns on lights and moves objects in the auditorium at Mother Of Mercy High School.
#details
The Mother of Mercy High School auditorium is believed to be haunted by the spirit of Sister Mary Carlos, who has been known to turn lights on and off and move objects around in the theater. It is a tradition to invite her to every performance, otherwise something goes wrong.

Mother Of Mercy Catholic High School, Cincinnati, Ohio

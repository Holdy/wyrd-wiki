Rollercoaster Derailment Screams at Puritas Springs Park

Summary

Puritas Springs Park is haunted by victims of a rollercoaster derailment, with terrifying screams heard on the hill at night.

Details

Puritas Springs Park is said to be haunted by the ghosts of a number of men, women, and children who were killed during a rollercoaster derailment. At night, horrible screams are heard on the hill, and nearby apartment residents frequently report hearing strange disembodied noises.

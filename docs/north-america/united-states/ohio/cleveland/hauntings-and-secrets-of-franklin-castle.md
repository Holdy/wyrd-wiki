#title
Hauntings and Secrets of Franklin Castle
#summary
Cleveland's historic Franklin Castle is wrapped in ghost stories and persistent rumors of hidden chambers and Prohibition-era passageways.
#details
Built by Hannes Tiedemann in the early 1880s, Cleveland's imposing Franklin Castle is widely considered Ohio's most haunted house. The historic structure is surrounded by persistent rumors of secret rooms and Prohibition-era passageways, alongside numerous ghost stories and reported paranormal encounters.

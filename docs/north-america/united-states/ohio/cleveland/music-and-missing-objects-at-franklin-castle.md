#title
Music and Missing Objects at Franklin Castle
#summary
Franklin Castle features cold spots, misplaced items, and the sound of music coming from the 3rd floor ballroom.
#details
Investigators at Franklin Castle have reported feeling cold spots throughout the castle, families have reported objects going missing or showing up in strange places, and the sound of music is heard coming from the 3rd floor ballroom.

#title
Glowing Lights and Screams at Davron Cemetery
#summary
Closed in the late 1800s, Davron Cemetery features strange glowing lights at night and disembodied screams and howls.
#details
Strange glowing lights are seen in Davron Cemetery at night, and disembodied screams and howls are heard, after the cemetery was closed in the late 1800s due to poor maintenance.

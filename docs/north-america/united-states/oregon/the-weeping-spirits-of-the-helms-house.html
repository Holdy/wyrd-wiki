The Weeping Spirits of the Helms House

Summary

The historic Helms House in Jacksonville is reportedly haunted by the grief-stricken spirits of a mother and her young daughter who died during a 19th-century epidemic.

Details

Built in 1878 next to an earlier log cabin, the Italianate-style house belonged to German immigrants Herman and Augusta von Helms, a family that suffered immense tragedy, including the loss of three daughters to a typhoid epidemic and another to murder. Their youngest daughter, Minnie, died at less than two years old and was buried on the property. According to local lore, the grieving spirit of Augusta or the young child can be heard crying at night, while phantom apparitions wander the staircases and hallways before abruptly vanishing.

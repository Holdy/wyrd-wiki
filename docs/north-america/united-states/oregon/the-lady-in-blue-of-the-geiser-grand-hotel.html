The Lady in Blue of the Geiser Grand Hotel

Summary

The opulent Geiser Grand Hotel in Baker City is famous for phantom parties and its lingering resident ghost known as the Lady in Blue.

Details

Opened in 1889 during the Eastern Oregon gold rush, the grand hotel was meticulously restored in the 1990s after decades of closure. Almost immediately upon reopening, staff and guests reported doors closing on their own, dining chairs shifting, and mysterious late-night party sounds echoing through empty hallways. The hotel's most legendary spirit is the "Lady in Blue"—believed to be Birdie Geiser, wife of former owner Albert Geiser—who wears a 19th-century Gibson Girl dress and haunts her regular quarters in Room 302 or pinches patrons in the bar.

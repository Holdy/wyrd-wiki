#title
The Friendly Phantoms of the Geiser Grand Hotel

#summary
Baker City's historic Geiser Grand Hotel is said to be haunted by a headless chef, a mysterious Lady in Blue, and the spirit of former owner Maybelle Geiser who still inspects guests' jewelry in Room 302. Built during the gold rush era, the luxury hotel has welcomed countless spirits back to life alongside its living guests following a massive 1990s restoration.

#details
Opened in 1889 as the Hotel Warshauer and later renamed by Swiss immigrant Albert Geiser in 1900, the Geiser Grand Hotel became the crown jewel of Baker City during the regional gold rush. After falling into severe disrepair and closing in 1968, the building was rescued and restored in the 1990s—a process that seemingly awakened its dormant ghosts. Among the reported apparitions is a headless chef who was crushed by a dumbwaiter and reacts angrily by throwing pots and slamming doors, as well as the elegant "Lady in Blue" who haunts the staircases. In Room 302, guests report encounters with Maybelle Geiser, the former owner who lived in the room and is known to playfully inspect visitors' jewelry and knock on walls.

#location
The Geiser Grand Hotel is situated at 1996 Main Street in downtown Baker City, Oregon.

The Royal Hauntings of ʻIolani Palace

Summary

The historic ʻIolani Palace in downtown Honolulu is said to be haunted by Queen Liliʻuokalani and other former residents who wander the grand halls and sacred grounds.

Details

As the former royal residence of the Hawaiian monarchy, ʻIolani Palace holds deep historical significance and enduring ghost stories. Security guards and visitors have repeatedly reported seeing the ghostly figure of Queen Liliʻuokalani walking the palace grounds around 5:30 in the morning or peering out from the second-floor bedroom where she was imprisoned following the 1893 overthrow. Witnesses have also reported hearing a piano mysteriously playing in the Blue Room, disembodied Hawaiian chanting in the upper chambers, and restless spirits residing among the ancient banyan trees planted on the palace grounds by Queen Kapiʻolani.

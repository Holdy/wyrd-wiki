The Mothman of Point Pleasant

Summary

A large winged creature with glowing red eyes was seen near Point Pleasant in the 1960s and famously linked to the Silver Bridge collapse.

Details

First seen in the 1960s near Point Pleasant just across the Ohio River, the Mothman is described as a large, winged creature with glowing red eyes. Its sightings are famously linked to the tragic collapse of the Silver Bridge in 1967 which killed 46 people, with some believing the creature served as an omen or warning of the disaster.

#title
The Mothman and the Silver Bridge Collapse

#summary
Point Pleasant was plagued by sightings of a red-eyed, winged entity for a year before the tragic 1967 collapse of the Silver Bridge into the Ohio River.

#details
In mid-December 1967, the Silver Bridge in Point Pleasant collapsed during afternoon rush hour, plunging 64 people into the icy Ohio River and resulting in 46 deaths. For a year leading up to this disaster, the town was haunted by accounts of a red-eyed, winged entity known as Mothman. These sightings strangely diminished after the fateful day of the collapse.

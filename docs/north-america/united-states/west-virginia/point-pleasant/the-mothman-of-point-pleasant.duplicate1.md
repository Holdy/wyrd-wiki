#title
The Mothman of Point Pleasant
#summary
A winged cryptid known as Mothman was sighted taking to the skies over Point Pleasant prior to a tragic bridge collapse.
#details
In 1966, a cryptid described as a radioactive bird or interdimensional chaos demon took to the skies over Point Pleasant, West Virginia. These sightings set off a chain of events culminating in the tragic collapse of the Silver Bridge, which connected Point Pleasant to Gallipolis, Ohio.

#title
Little Lily of Ward R

#summary
Visitors and investigators touring the former asylum in Weston frequently encounter the spirit of a young girl named Lily who passed away within the building in the 19th century.

#details
The Trans-Allegheny Lunatic Asylum is said to be haunted by a young girl named Lily, who allegedly lived and died within the hospital's walls in the 1800s. According to local lore, she was either a young patient or born to a woman who arrived at the asylum during the Civil War. Her spirit is believed to haunt the fourth floor in Ward R, where visitors often leave toys like rubber balls that mysteriously move across the floor. Audio recordings from her former room have captured ghostly EVPs resembling a child's voice whispering. Beyond Lily, security guards and staff have reported sightings of a gray female figure peering from the open kitchen windows and watching those walking the corridors at night.

#location
The Trans-Allegheny Lunatic Asylum is located at 71 Asylum Drive in Weston, West Virginia.

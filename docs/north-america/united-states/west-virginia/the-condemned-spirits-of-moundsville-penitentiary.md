#title
The Condemned Spirits of Moundsville Penitentiary

#summary
The Gothic stone architecture of the West Virginia Penitentiary houses decades of violent history and restless spirits, including executed inmates and shadowy entities pacing the cellblocks.

#details
Operating for over a century before closing in 1995, the West Virginia State Penitentiary witnessed numerous executions, riots, and homicides, earning a reputation as one of the most haunted prisons in America. Legend attributes some of the unrest to its proximity to the ancient Grave Creek Mound. Among the most reported hauntings is the ghost of Orville Adkins, an inmate whose 1938 hanging at the North Wagon Gate was notoriously botched when the trap door opened prematurely, causing him to fall twenty feet before being hanged a second time. Visitors and staff have reported his heavy footsteps echoing along the stone walkway, alongside sightings of the "Shadow Man," phantom voices pleading for help from solitary confinement, and paranormal activity in the infamous basement recreation room known as the Sugar Shack.

#location
The West Virginia State Penitentiary is located at 818 Jefferson Avenue in Moundsville, West Virginia.

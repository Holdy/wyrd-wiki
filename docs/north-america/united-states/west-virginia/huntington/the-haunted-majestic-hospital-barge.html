    The Haunted Majestic Hospital Barge

    Summary

    A former World War II military hospital barge anchored in Huntington, West Virginia, is famously haunted by restless souls who died on board.

    Details

    Known as "the ship of death," the Haunted Majestic is a two-story military hospital barge assembled from two barges that handled casualties from the Battle of Normandy, listing 3,000 injured patients with over 300 men dying on board. Faced with an overwhelming flood of patients, doctors and nurses had to make harrowing triage decisions. Paranormal investigations have revealed many restless souls still aboard who do not realize they are dead and remain trapped on the vessel.

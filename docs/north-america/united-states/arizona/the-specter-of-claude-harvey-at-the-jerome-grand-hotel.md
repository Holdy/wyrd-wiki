#title
The Specter of Claude Harvey at the Jerome Grand Hotel

#summary
A former hospital maintenance worker whose mysterious death was ruled an accident is said to still haunt the elevator shaft and basement of the historic Jerome Grand Hotel.

#details
Built in 1927 as the United Verde Hospital on Cleopatra Hill, the five-story structure served the bustling mining community of Jerome until closing in 1950. In 1935, maintenance worker Claude "Scotty" Harvey was found crushed to death beneath the hospital's elevator. Though officially ruled an accident, many locals suspected foul play because the elevator was found in perfect working order. Ever since his death—and continuing after the building was restored and reopened as the Jerome Grand Hotel in 1996—staff and guests have reported eerie sounds, phantom elevator activity, and sightings of a shadowy, angry figure in the basement and stairwells.

#location
The Jerome Grand Hotel located at 200 Hill Street in Jerome, Arizona.

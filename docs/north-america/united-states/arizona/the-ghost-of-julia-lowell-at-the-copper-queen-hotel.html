The Ghost of Julia Lowell at the Copper Queen Hotel

Summary

Built in 1902 during Bisbee’s copper boom, the historic Copper Queen Hotel is haunted by the spirit of Julia Lowell, a former resident who took her own life in the building.

Details

Completed in 1902 by the Phelps Dodge Mining Company, the Copper Queen Hotel in Bisbee, Arizona, is the state's oldest continuously running hotel. According to local legend, the hotel's most famous spectral resident is Julia Lowell, a woman who worked as a prostitute and frequented the hotel. After falling deeply in love with a client who did not return her affections, she tragically took her own life at the property. Today, guests—particularly male visitors staying in Room 315 (known as the Julia Lowell room)—frequently report feeling invisible hands playing with their feet or hearing whispering voices in the dark.

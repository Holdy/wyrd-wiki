The Phantom Maintenance Worker of the Jerome Grand Hotel

Summary

Once operating as the United Verde Hospital, the Jerome Grand Hotel is haunted by the restless spirit of a maintenance worker who died in the elevator shaft.

Details

Perched high upon Cleopatra Hill in Jerome, Arizona, the Jerome Grand Hotel originally opened in 1927 as the United Verde Hospital before closing in 1950 and reopening as a hotel in 1996. Among its many reported ghosts—including a bearded miner and a small child—the most famous is Claude "Scotty" Harvey, a hospital maintenance worker whose body was found pinned under the elevator in 1935 under mysterious circumstances. Though officially ruled an accident, many suspected foul play. Ever since his death, unexplained phenomena have plagued the elevator and basement areas, including self-moving elevator cars, phantom lights in the shaft, and an angry shadowy presence.

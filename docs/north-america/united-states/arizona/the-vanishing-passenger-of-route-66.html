The Vanishing Passenger of Route 66

Summary

A truck driver on an isolated Arizona highway picks up a young woman in a white dress who silently disappears from the locked cab of his truck mid-journey.

Details

Late one winter night, a long-haul truck driver traveling along the Arizona stretch of Route 66 between Seligman and Kingman noticed a young woman standing alone by the roadside wearing a thin white dress despite the freezing cold. Concerned for her safety miles from any town, he pulled over and offered her a ride. The woman silently climbed into the cab and maintained a distant, quiet demeanor as the truck continued down the highway, offering only brief, monosyllabic answers when spoken to. A few miles later, when the driver glanced back over to the passenger seat, she had completely vanished without a sound or any movement of the cab door. When he later described the mysterious encounter to locals in a nearby town, they noted that she strongly resembled a young woman who had tragically died in a car accident on that exact stretch of highway years prior.

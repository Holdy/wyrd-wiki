The Phantom Bellboy of the Hotel Monte Vista

Summary

Opened on New Year's Day in 1927 along Route 66, the historic Hotel Monte Vista in Flagstaff is famously haunted by a phantom bellboy who knocks on guest doors in the middle of the night.

Details

Built entirely through public municipal bonds and opening in 1927, the Hotel Monte Vista in Flagstaff, Arizona, hosted numerous notable figures over the decades, including John Wayne. Over the years, the hotel accumulated a reputation for heavy paranormal activity, attributed to bootlegging days, wild brawls, and tragic deaths. The most enduring ghost story centers on Room 210, known as the Zane Grey room, where guests are frequently awakened by a phantom bellboy knocking on the door to announce room service, only to reveal an empty hallway upon opening. Additionally, local lore notes that dogs placed in this room routinely exhibit extreme fear and agitation.

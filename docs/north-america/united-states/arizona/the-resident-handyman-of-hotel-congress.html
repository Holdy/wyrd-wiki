The Resident Handyman of Hotel Congress

Summary

A long-term resident handyman who lived at Tucson's Hotel Congress for over three decades reportedly continues to play practical jokes from beyond the grave.

Details

Operating in downtown Tucson since 1919, Hotel Congress is renowned both for its 1934 connection to outlaw John Dillinger and its enduring resident ghosts. Among the most famous is Vince Szuda, a maintenance worker who lived in Room 220 for 36 years and was known in life for collecting and repurposing butter knives as screwdrivers. Following his death in 2001, hotel staff and guests have repeatedly reported finding butter knives mysteriously laid out in plain sight in his former room, along with other playful paranormal disturbances throughout the historic building.

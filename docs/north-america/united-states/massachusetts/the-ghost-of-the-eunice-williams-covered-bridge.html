The Ghost of the Eunice Williams Covered Bridge

Summary

The ghost of Eunice Williams, a young mother killed during the tragic 1704 Deerfield Massacre, is said to haunt the covered bridge spanning the Green River where she breathed her last.

Details

On February 29, 1704, during Queen Anne's War, combined French and Native forces attacked the frontier settlement of Deerfield, Massachusetts, killing dozens of residents and taking over a hundred captives on a grueling winter march to Canada. Among the captured was Eunice Williams, wife of the town's minister, who had given birth just a day prior. Weakened and unable to keep up with the column of prisoners, Eunice collapsed near the Green River and was struck down. Over the centuries, locals and travelers have reported encountering her sorrowful spirit haunting the riverbanks and the covered bridge that now bears her name, endlessly searching for her family.

The Red-Headed Hitchhiker of Route 44

Summary

A malicious red-haired phantom haunts a dark stretch of road in Massachusetts. The spirit terrorizes unsuspecting motorists before vanishing into thin air.

Details

After mocking local ghost tales over dinner, a disbelieving couple decides to drive home along Route 44 late at night. Suddenly, a bushy-bearded man with red hair, a plaid shirt, and blue jeans appears running alongside their car, leering through the passenger window. When the terrified driver speeds up, the phantom instantly reappears inside the back seat, tormenting them with maniacal laughter. As the panicked wife begins reciting the rosary, the entity grimaces at the sacred words and vanishes without a trace.

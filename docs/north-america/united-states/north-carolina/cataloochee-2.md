#title
Cataloochee

#summary
A mountain community of almost 200 buildings by 1900, emptied in the mid-1930s when the national park was created. Most buildings were burned or pulled down; a few houses, a school, a chapel and a church still stand.

#location
Cataloochee Valley, Great Smoky Mountains NP, Haywood County

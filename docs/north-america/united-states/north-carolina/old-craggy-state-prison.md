#title
Old Craggy State Prison

#summary
A prison from 1924 to 1989 with unheated dorms, basement solitary cells and repeated mass escapes. It stands derelict, and locals tell of phantom footsteps and a woman in white.

#location
Craggy, Woodfin, Buncombe County

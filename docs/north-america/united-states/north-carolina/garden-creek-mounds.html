    Garden Creek Mounds

    Summary

    Four Native American mounds, from Middle Woodland (200-600 CE) to a Pisgah-phase platform mound built over two earth-lodge council houses; burials were found beside Mound 1. Appalachian State researchers report possible solar and lunar alignments in its enclosures.

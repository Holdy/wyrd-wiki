#title
Joara and Fort San Juan (Berry Site)

#summary
The largest Mississippian town in North Carolina, visited by de Soto in 1540. In 1567 the Spanish built Fort San Juan here, the first European settlement in NC; within about 18 months the locals killed the garrison and burned it. Archaeologists found the burned fort in 2013.

#location
NW of Morganton, Burke County

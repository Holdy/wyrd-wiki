#title
Tsuwatelda: the town inside Pilot Mountain

#summary
Mooney's story of Kanasta: spirit people, the Nunnehi, led a whole settlement through a door in the cliff into a hidden town inside the mountain, where they are said to live still. The Cherokee origin story of Kanati and Selu is also set at Pilot Knob.

#location
Pilot Mountain near Brevard, Transylvania County

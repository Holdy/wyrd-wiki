Rich Mountain meteorite (fell 1903)

Summary

Witnesses heard a tornado-like rumble and blasts and saw a fireball 'the size of a flour barrel'; a man in a field saw a stone come down through a tree 40 feet away. A Smithsonian paper (1907) records the fall.

#title
The Legend of Blackbird Hill

#summary
The ghost of a young woman murdered by her husband in 1849 is said to haunt a hilltop overlooking the Missouri River, where her chilling screams echo every October 17.

#details
In 1849, a young woman living atop Blackbird Hill unexpectedly reunited with her former fiancé whom she thought had died abroad years earlier. When her husband returned home and discovered she wished to leave him, he attacked her with a hunting knife and leaped with her in his arms off the cliff into the Missouri River. Today, the path where the murder occurred remains barren, and witnesses report hearing the woman's terrifying screams of agony on the anniversary of her death every October 17.

#location
Blackbird Hill is located eight miles north of Decatur, Nebraska, overlooking the Missouri River on the Omaha Indian Reservation.

#title
The Haunting of Seven Sisters Road

#summary
Restless spirits of seven sisters allegedly murdered by their brother haunt a winding road southeast of Nebraska City, where motorists experience unexplained car trouble and hear phantom screams.

#details
According to local folklore in Otoe County, a local man flew into a rage and hanged his seven sisters from trees atop seven distinct hills along a meandering country road. Though no official historical records substantiate the murders and the hanging trees were later cut down, the road is said to be heavily haunted. Drivers frequently report their cars stalling, headlights dimming, windows rolling down on their own, and hearing terrifying screams and muffled whispers in the darkness.

#location
Seven Sisters Road (now known as L Street) is located about five miles southeast of Nebraska City, Nebraska.

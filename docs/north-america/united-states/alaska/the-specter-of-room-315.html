The Specter of Room 315

Summary

Juneau's historic Alaskan Hotel harbors a notorious reputation for hauntings centered around Room 315, where eerie phenomena culminated in a dramatic incident involving a visiting Navy sailor.

Details

Opened in 1913, the Alaskan Hotel in downtown Juneau is widely believed by staff and visitors to be haunted, with the epicenter of its ghostly lore located in Room 315 on the third floor. In May 2007, a U.S. Navy sailor aboard a docked missile cruiser specifically requested to stay in the hotel's famously "haunted room." Later that night, police and hotel staff were summoned after glass shattered and the sailor crashed through a third-story window, falling between adjacent buildings before miraculously surviving his injuries. Responding officers discovered the walls of Room 315 covered in blood, cementing the room's chilling reputation among employees and guests.

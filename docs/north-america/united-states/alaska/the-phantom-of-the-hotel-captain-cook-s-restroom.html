The Phantom of the Hotel Captain Cook's Restroom

Summary

Following a tragic suicide in 1972, the spirit of a young woman took up residence in the women's restroom of Tower 2 at the Hotel Captain Cook in Anchorage, becoming particularly possessive of the final toilet stall.

Details

Constructed by former Alaska governor Walter J. Hickel, the Hotel Captain Cook opened its first tower in 1965 and expanded with the addition of Tower 2 in 1972. According to local legend, following the grand opening festivities of the second tower, a distressed young woman tragically took her own life inside the public women's restroom. Over the decades, visitors and hotel staff have reported unsettling paranormal occurrences in the room, including sudden feelings of intense unease, fainting spells, and more aggressive manifestations such as stall walls shaking violently or invisible hands grabbing users by the ankles. To appease the spirit and mitigate disturbances, hotel management ultimately ordered the final stall on the left to be permanently bolted shut.

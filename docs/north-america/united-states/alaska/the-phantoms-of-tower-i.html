The Phantoms of Tower I

Summary

Guests and staff at an iconic downtown Anchorage hotel frequently report disembodied voices, vanishing colonial-era apparitions, and mysterious noises echoing from locked rooms.

Details

Opened in 1965 by Walter J. Hickel to aid in the post-earthquake recovery of Anchorage, the Hotel Captain Cook became a major landmark of the city. Over the decades, staff and visitors have reported numerous supernatural occurrences, particularly within Tower I. Accounts include a colonial-era gentleman in antique attire standing in the corridors before vanishing, service phones ringing from completely empty rooms between 3:00 AM and 4:00 AM, and faint waltz music and dancing footsteps echoing through locked, empty ballrooms.

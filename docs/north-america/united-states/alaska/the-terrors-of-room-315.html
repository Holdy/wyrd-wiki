The Terrors of Room 315

Summary

A historic Juneau hotel features a notorious third-floor room where a visiting Navy sailor experienced a terrifying, bloody encounter and leaped through a window to escape.

Details

Opened in 1913, the Alaskan Hotel in downtown Juneau has long harbored a reputation for being haunted. On May 19, 2007, a U.S. Navy sailor from the docked USS Bunker Hill specifically requested to stay in "the haunted room," Room 315. Late that night, police and hotel guests heard screaming, followed by the sound of glass shattering as the sailor plunged through the window onto the floor below. When police broke into the room, they found the walls covered in blood and signs of an unexplained, violent disturbance.

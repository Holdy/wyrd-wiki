#title
The Terror of Room 315

#summary
A Navy sailor who requested to stay in a notoriously haunted hotel room became so overwhelmed by a terrifying paranormal presence that he smashed through a window and plunged three stories to the ground. Despite suffering severe injuries and leaving behind blood-splattered walls in the locked room, he miraculously survived.

#details
In May 2007, a U.S. Navy sailor visiting Juneau, Alaska, specifically asked for the haunted room at the historic Alaskan Hotel and was assigned Room 315. Late that evening, police and bystanders were alerted by frantic screaming and shattering glass coming from the locked room. When officers broke the door down, they found the walls entirely covered in blood in a chilling, inexplicable scene. Meanwhile, the sailor had crashed through the room's window, falling three stories down between narrow alley buildings and shattering another window on his way down. Miraculously surviving the catastrophic fall and severe injuries, the sailor was medevacked out of the city while the harrowing events inside Room 315 remained an unsolved mystery.

#location
Room 315 is situated on the third floor of the Alaskan Hotel located at 167 S. Franklin Street in downtown Juneau, Alaska.

#title
The Lady in White of Chatham Manor

#summary
A young woman separated from her forbidden lover by her father and George Washington returns as a glowing apparition on the anniversary of her death.

#details
Built in 1771 in Fredericksburg, Virginia, Chatham Manor was the site where a wealthy young Englishwoman was sent by her father to separate her from a man he deemed beneath her social class. Her lover followed her to Virginia, and the two planned to elope. However, a servant overheard their plans and reported them to General George Washington, who intercepted the young woman at her bedroom window instead of her lover. Sent back to England where she was forced into an arranged marriage, she vowed to return. Legend states that her ghost, known as the Lady in White, paces the path along the Rappahannock River every seven years on June 21, dressed in a colonial-style glowing gown.

#location
Chatham Manor is located at 120 Chatham Lane in Fredericksburg, Virginia.

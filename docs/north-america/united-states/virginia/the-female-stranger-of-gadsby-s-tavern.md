#title
The Female Stranger of Gadsby's Tavern

#summary
In 1816, a mysterious veiled woman died of an illness at Alexandria's City Hotel after arriving from the Caribbean with a secretive companion, and her ghost continues to haunt the historic building.

#details
In October 1816, a mysterious couple arrived by ship from the Caribbean in Alexandria, Virginia, renting a room at the City Hotel (now known as Gadsby’s Tavern). The woman was desperately ill and wore a black veil, while her male companion refused to reveal their identities to local doctors or nurses, making everyone swear oaths of secrecy. Three weeks later, on October 14, 1816, the woman died. Her companion arranged for her burial at St. Paul’s Cemetery under the poignant moniker "The Female Stranger" before abruptly leaving town without paying outstanding debts. Ever since, staff and visitors have reported paranormal phenomena throughout Gadsby’s Tavern, including full-bodied apparitions, phantom crying from Room 8, objects moving on their own, and lanterns mysteriously heating up as if just blown out.

#location
Gadsby's Tavern is located at 134 North Royal Street in Alexandria, Virginia.

#title
The Phantom Hitchhiker of Highway 365

#summary
A motorist picks up a soaked young woman hitchhiking on a lonely stretch of highway. Upon reaching her purported home, the driver learns she died years prior and finds the passenger seat empty.

#details
On stormy nights along Arkansas Highway 365 between Woodson and Redfield, drivers frequently report encountering a distressed young woman standing by the roadside seeking a ride. In the classic retelling, a driver named Josh picks up the wet, quiet hitchhiker and drives her to the address she provides in Redfield. When he knocks on the door and asks the homeowner if her daughter needs assistance, the shocked resident reveals that her daughter died in a car crash exactly four years prior. Rushing back to his vehicle, the driver finds the passenger seat completely empty. Other variations of the legend describe the hitchhiker vanishing mid-ride, or motorists finding her coat hanging upon her headstone.

#location
Arkansas Highway 365 between the communities of Woodson and Redfield, Arkansas.

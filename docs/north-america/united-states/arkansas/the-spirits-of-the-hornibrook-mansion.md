#title
The Spirits of the Hornibrook Mansion

#summary
Constructed in Little Rock in 1888, the historic Queen Anne Victorian mansion—now operating as a bed and breakfast—is said to be haunted by its original builder, who died tragically shortly after its completion.

#details
The Hornibrook House, completed in 1888 as an elaborate Queen Anne Victorian residence in Little Rock, was built for businessman James H. Hornibrook. Tragically, just two years after the mansion's completion, Hornibrook suffered a fatal stroke at the front gate after hosting an evening gathering at his saloon. Over subsequent decades, as the grand home passed through various owners and functions—including a women's college, rooming house, and nursing home—occupants and visitors reported unexplained occurrences, including flickering lights, strange sounds, and the persistent presence of a well-dressed gentlemanly spirit believed to be Hornibrook himself.

#location
The Hornibrook House (The Empress of Little Rock) located at 2120 Louisiana Street in Little Rock, Arkansas.

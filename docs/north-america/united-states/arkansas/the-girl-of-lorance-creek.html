The Girl of Lorance Creek

Summary

Following oil drilling near a historic Saline County cemetery in 1920, workers reported hearing the screams and cries of a young girl who was murdered and buried near the creek during the Civil War.

Details

Lorance Creek in Saline County is associated with the legend of an unnamed girl who was murdered by being pushed into the creek and buried at Cockman Cemetery on December 24, 1863. In 1920, when oil workers began drilling near the creek and cemetery, they reported hearing a woman's scream and crying out "Why?", followed by sightings of her walking in a white burial dress toward the creek before vanishing.

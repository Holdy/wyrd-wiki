The Phantom Teenager of Hotel Meade

Summary

Visitors and children at the historic Hotel Meade in the abandoned mining town of Bannack frequently report sightings of a spectral teenage girl wearing a blue dress who drowned in a nearby creek long ago.

Details

Established during the 1862 gold rush, Bannack eventually became a deserted ghost town, with its former 1875 courthouse transformed into the Hotel Meade in 1890. Over the decades, visitors have reported cold spots and eerie cries of children inside the building, but the most enduring apparition is that of Dorothy Dunn, a teenager who drowned in a dredge pond along Grasshopper Creek. Witnesses and children have seen her ghost moving through the second-floor hallways or peering out of an upstairs window in her long blue dress, sometimes attempting to mouth silent words to visitors.

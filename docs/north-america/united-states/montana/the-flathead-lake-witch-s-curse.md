#title
The Flathead Lake Witch's Curse

#summary
A reclusive woman accused of witchcraft in the 1800s placed a dark curse on the region surrounding her secluded cabin near Flathead Lake before vanishing. Local folklore holds that her malevolent magic lingers, causing unexplained accidents, fires, and mysterious fogs over the water.

#details
During the 1800s, a mysterious woman who lived alone in a secluded cabin near the shores of Flathead Lake in Montana practiced herbalism, brewing potions from local plants. After a series of unexplained tragedies struck the surrounding community, including mysterious fires and unusual accidents, superstitious locals blamed the reclusive woman. When she abruptly disappeared without a trace, rumors quickly spread that she had cursed the area before leaving. Ever since, inhabitants have associated the strange, lingering fogs and ongoing misfortunes of the region with her dark magic.

#location
A secluded cabin site near the shores of Flathead Lake, Montana.

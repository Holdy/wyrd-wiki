The Haunting of Room 320 and the Cruise Room Postman

Summary

Room 320 of Denver's Oxford Hotel is said to be haunted by a woman who committed a tragic murder-suicide there in 1898, while the hotel's adjacent Cruise Room bar is visited by a phantom postal worker.

Details

In 1898, a woman named Florence Montague shot and killed her lover before taking her own life in Room 320 of the Oxford Hotel in downtown Denver. Ever since, single male guests staying in the room have reported feeling invisible hands pulling their arms and having their bedsheets violently ripped away. Downstairs in the hotel's Art Deco Cruise Room bar—a former Prohibition-era speakeasy—bartenders and patrons frequently report seeing a ghostly postal worker in a vintage uniform. He orders a beer, mutters about delivering Christmas gifts to the children, and leaves behind a bottle that inexplicably remains completely full.

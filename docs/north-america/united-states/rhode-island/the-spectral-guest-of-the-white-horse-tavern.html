The Spectral Guest of the White Horse Tavern

Summary

A colonial-era overnight guest who died mysteriously at America's oldest operating tavern is said to still wander its dining rooms and hallways.

Details

Built in 1652 in Newport, Rhode Island, the White Horse Tavern holds a long and storied history as the oldest operating tavern in the United States. In the 1720s, an overnight guest died in his sleep of unknown causes, sparking quarantine fears. Today, staff and guests report seeing the apparition of a man dressed in shabby colonial attire, frequently spotted near the fireplaces in the main dining room or upstairs. Other unexplained phenomena include a mysterious entity that taps people on the shoulder, the sound of heavy footsteps in empty rooms, and the soft crying of a little girl near the second-floor restrooms.

#title
The Spirits of Preston Castle

#summary
Built in the 19th century as a juvenile reform school in Ione, California, Preston Castle is defined by a dark history and the restless ghost of its murdered head housekeeper.

#details
Originally opened in 1894 as the Preston School of Industry, the grand Romanesque Revival building in Ione saw decades of institutional hardship, disease outbreaks, and violent episodes. The facility's darkest chapter occurred on February 23, 1950, when head housekeeper Anna Corbin was brutally bludgeoned to death inside the building in a murder that remains officially unsolved. Ghost hunters, staff, and visitors have long reported sightings of Anna Corbin roaming the decaying halls, alongside other spectral phenomena throughout the massive structure.

#location
Preston Castle is situated in Ione, Amador County, Northern California.

The Restless Spirits of the Whaley House

Summary

Built on the site of a former execution ground in San Diego, this historic home became a site of repeated family tragedy and lingering supernatural presences.

Details

Commissioned in 1857 by Thomas Whaley in Old Town San Diego, the Greek Revival house was constructed on land that had previously served as the town gallows, where a notorious thief known as "Yankee Jim" was hanged. The Whaley family endured numerous hardships and tragic deaths while living in the home, which also briefly functioned as a general store and county courthouse. Today, visitors and docents report numerous ghostly encounters, ranging from heavy footsteps and disembodied voices to the phantom apparition of Yankee Jim and members of the Whaley family.

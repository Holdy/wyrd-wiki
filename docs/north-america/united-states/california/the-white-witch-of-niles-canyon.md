#title
The White Witch of Niles Canyon

#summary
Drivers navigating a winding mountain highway frequently report encounters with a spectral woman in white who vanishes without a trace or intervenes to prevent deadly accidents.

#details
For decades, motorists driving along Niles Canyon Road have reported encountering a ghostly young woman dressed in white along the dark, winding asphalt. Local folklore depicts her as a phantom hitchhiker or wandering spirit who appears suddenly in the roadway or in the back seats of passing cars, only to vanish into thin air as the vehicle approaches bridges or dangerous curves. In one notable encounter, a late-night driver speeding along the ravine felt an invisible hand grasp his shoulder and force him to brake just before a treacherous turn, saving him from a catastrophic crash.

#location
Niles Canyon Road is a seven-mile stretch of State Route 84 running alongside Alameda Creek between Fremont and Sunol, California.

The Curse of Griffith Park

Summary

Cheated out of her rightful inheritance by political deceit, a grief-stricken young woman cursed her family's ranch to bring endless ruin, blight, and ghostly terror to all future owners.

Details

In 1863, Don Antonio Feliz fell gravely ill and was manipulated on his deathbed into deeding his vast Rancho Los Feliz estate away from his beloved niece, Petranilla. Upon discovering the betrayal immediately after her uncle's death, Petranilla confronted the new owner and placed a sweeping curse upon the land and its usurpers, wishing upon them financial ruin, violent deaths, blighted fields, and devastating floods before dropping dead herself. True to her dying words, successive owners faced unrelenting misfortunes—from murdered lawyers and dying livestock to withered oaks and destructive floods—culminating in Colonel Griffith J. Griffith donating the blighted land to the city. Even after becoming a public park, the ghosts of Antonio and Petranilla allegedly haunted the grounds, famously appearing to crash and scatter a celebratory banquet at the historic Feliz Adobe.

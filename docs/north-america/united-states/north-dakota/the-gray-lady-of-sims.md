#title
The Gray Lady of Sims

#summary
For over a century, the ghost of a former pastor's wife known as the "Gray Lady" has been reported haunting the historic Scandinavian Evangelical Lutheran Church and parsonage in the ghost town of Sims, North Dakota.

#details
Bertha Dordal moved to Sims, North Dakota, in 1916 with her husband, Reverend Lars Dordal, and their three young children, hoping the dry western climate might help cure her tuberculosis. Tragically, her health declined, and she passed away in 1917 at just 36 years old. Following her death, parishioners and residents began reporting unusual phenomena inside the town's newly built Scandinavian Evangelical Lutheran Church and parsonage. Witnesses heard phantom organ music and unexplained footsteps, while others reported seeing a gray-toned apparition walking upstairs. Affectionately dubbed the "Gray Lady" due to the pallor common to tuberculosis sufferers, Bertha's spirit is remembered as a comforting and benevolent presence who would adjust windows, tidy cupboards, and lay blankets over cold visitors during chilly nights. 

#location
The Scandinavian Evangelical Lutheran Church and parsonage are located in the historic ghost town of Sims, North Dakota, situated approximately 47 miles southwest of Bismarck.

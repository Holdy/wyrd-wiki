#title
The Haunting of Chateau de Mores

#summary
Built in 1883 as a summer hunting lodge, the historic Chateau de Mores in Medora is said to be haunted by the apparition and lingering presence of Medora de Mores.

#details
Constructed in 1883 by French diplomat and entrepreneur Marquis de Mores for his wife Medora, the 26-room hunting lodge and summer home overlooks the Badlands of Medora, North Dakota. Though Medora died while traveling and never returned to live permanently in the home, visitors, tour guides, and locals have long reported paranormal occurrences within its walls. Witnesses frequently report seeing the apparition of an elegant woman moving through the rooms, the indentation of a body on Medora's side of the bed, inexplicable cold spots, mysterious laughter, and lights turning on by themselves when the building is entirely empty.

#location
Chateau de Mores is located atop a bluff overlooking the town of Medora and Theodore Roosevelt National Park in western North Dakota.

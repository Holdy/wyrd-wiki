The Possession of Remedios

Summary

In 1682, the parish priest of Remedios documented that a local woman named Leonarda was possessed by Lucifer, who spoke through her during formal ecclesiastical proceedings. The resulting panic prompted unsuccessful attempts by church authorities to relocate the entire town.

Details

In 1682, Father José González de la Cruz, the rector priest and inquisitorial commissioner of San Juan de los Remedios del Cayo, asserted that the town was besieged by demonic forces. Most notably, a Cuban-born slave woman named Leonarda was claimed to be physically possessed by Lucifer, the Prince of Darkness, alongside several other local individuals. On September 4, 1682, formal minutes were recorded before a notary public inside the parish church, documenting that the entity spoke directly through Leonarda and confessed that demonic possession had occurred due to human sins. Convinced that the town sat upon a gateway to Hell, Father José attempted to force the populace to abandon their settlement and relocate to Hato del Copey, though many skeptical residents resisted and remained behind.

The Ghost of Hatchet Bay

Summary

Following her tragic murder by villagers, a young mother's spirit haunts a roadway in Eleuthera. She routinely flags down passing drivers for a ride before mysteriously vanishing between two speed bumps.

Details

In the early 1960s in Rock Sound, Eleuthera, a hardworking young woman became pregnant by a married man, sparking a severe local scandal. Enraged villagers dragged her to an old silk cotton tree near a road curve and stoned her to death. Ever since, drivers passing by late at night have reported seeing a barefoot woman in old-fashioned clothes standing under the tree, often cradling a baby. When motorists stop and offer her a ride, she accepts silently and then vanishes without a trace between the first and second speed bumps before reaching the settlement.

The White Lady of Malindi Street

Summary

While walking along Malindi Street at night, Abbas Darjani encountered a spectral white lady. The terrifying meeting left him profoundly shaken, leading to a severe illness that ultimately caused him to lose his eyesight.

Details

Abbas Darjani encountered a spectral white lady while walking along Malindi Street at night. The sudden appearance of the ghostly figure terrified him so deeply that he fell seriously ill soon after. His condition deteriorated over time, culminating in complete blindness.

    Exorcism at the Lasys Zinphirin Church

    Summary

    During a Sunday service at a clandestine village church in Congo, a frail woman underwent a grueling exorcism to expel sickness-inflicting entities, while a young priest simultaneously fell into a violent, uncontrollable trance.

    Details

    At the Lasys Zinphirin church—a precarious wooden structure with a corrugated steel roof in a rural Congolese village—Pastor Nicolas led a Sunday congregation through fervent worship and offerings. Midway through the service, an ailing, elderly woman was brought in from the backyard to undergo deliverance from sickness and evil forces. Amid rhythmic drumming, singing, and chanting from the villagers, Pastor Nicolas aggressively whipped, splashed, and pressed wooden sticks against the distressed woman to expel the spirits afflicting her body. As the ritual reached a crescendo, the congregants and nuns entered a frenzied, trance-like state. A young priest participating in the service became entirely overwhelmed, falling into a violent, spastic trance from which he was unable to recover for hours, sitting outside on a truck tire rim and shouting wildly into the afternoon.

#title
The Lady of Malagwane Mountain

#summary
A phantom female hitchhiker haunts a mountain pass at night, tricking compassionate drivers into giving her a ride. Once picked up, she forces her victim to drive in circles around the city until sunrise or fuel exhaustion.

#details
Motorists driving down Malagwane mountain after dark frequently encounter a well-dressed, respectable-looking woman standing by the roadside seeking a lift. Moved by pity, unsuspecting drivers stop to pick her up. However, her benevolent appearance masks a supernatural trap; the ghostly woman directs the vehicle on an endless journey, leading them in circles around the city until dawn breaks or the car runs completely out of fuel. Local folklore holds that this phantom specifically targets and preys upon married men.

#location
The winding Malagwane mountain pass along the main roadway leading through the Ezulwini Valley in Eswatini.

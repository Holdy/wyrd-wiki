Ostrich

Summary

A young schoolgirl in Addis Ababa is haunted by the spectral body of a dead man lying perpetually in the street in front of the National Palace, visible only to her. Years later, long after leaving Ethiopia, the lingering memory of the specter compels her to return and see if he is still there.

Details

Set against the turbulent backdrop of revolutionary Ethiopia, a young girl commuting to school notices a chilling apparition every single day: a dead man lying motionlessly in the road directly in front of the National Palace. Although the street is busy, no one else seems to see or acknowledge the corpse, making it an intensely lonely and haunting fixture of her daily routine. The apparition serves as a powerful symbol of the silent casualties and unresolved trauma of the era. Even after she grows up and moves abroad, the memory of the spectral man lingers persistently, ultimately drawing her back to Addis Ababa to confront the past and check if he is still lying in the street.

#title
The Immovable Sword of Okomfo Anokye

#summary
The legendary priest Okomfo Anokye drove a mystical sword into the earth over three centuries ago, placing a binding decree upon the land that the entire Ashanti nation would collapse if the weapon were ever successfully pulled out. Despite countless efforts by monarchs, international dignitaries, and legendary athletes, the blade remains completely unmovable to this day.

#details
Over three hundred years ago, during the founding of the mighty Ashanti Empire, the revered high priest and statesman Okomfo Anokye performed great spiritual feats to unify the various clans. At a sacred location where the legendary Golden Stool of Asanteman descended from the heavens, Anokye drove his personal sword deeply into the ground. He placed a powerful curse and decree upon the weapon: it was declared completely immovable by mortal hands, and it was prophesied that if anyone ever succeeded in pulling the sword out, the entire Ashanti state would instantly collapse and perish. 

Generations have passed, and the blade has stood as a test of the curse. Numerous individuals—including famous strongmen, visiting world leaders, and even boxing legend Muhammad Ali during a 1994 visit—have attempted to wrench the blade from the earth, but none have ever budged it even a fraction of an inch. Today, the legendary sword remains firmly wedged into the stone and soil as a permanent anchor protecting the legacy and fate of the kingdom.

#location
The sword and its sacred site are located on the grounds of the Komfo Anokye Teaching Hospital in Kumasi, Ghana.

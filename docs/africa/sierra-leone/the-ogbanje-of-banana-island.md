#title
The Ogbanje of Banana Island

#summary
During a turbulent boat journey to Banana Island, a young Sierra Leonean man suffered violent physical seizures as spirit entities attempted to take over his body and drag him back to the spirit world.

#details
Amadu, a young man residing in Freetown, Sierra Leone, became the center of a terrifying occult ordeal after attempting to break his covenant as an ogbanje—a spirit entity inhabiting a human host. When he refused to leave his human family and return to the spirit realm, his spirit siblings initiated vicious metaphysical attacks to sever his connection to his physical body. While crossing the waters toward Banana Island, Amadu suddenly began shaking uncontrollably, losing consciousness, and crawling under benches as unseen forces violently wrenched his body. Upon docking at Banana Island, terrified locals initially blocked their entry before allowing Amadu to be carried into a local hut for urgent healing rituals. Over subsequent months of intense seizures, hallucinations, and dangerous compulsions—such as being pulled toward drowning in the ocean—traditional healers, mullahs, and priests were consulted to combat the possessing entities and help him survive.

#location

The Vanishing Hitchhiker of Joshua Mqabuko Nkomo Street

Summary

Motorists in Bulawayo who pick up a strikingly beautiful female hitchhiker at a city intersection find that she mysteriously vanishes from their cars before reaching Athlone Cemetery.

Details

Late-night motorists in Bulawayo, Zimbabwe, have reported picking up a beautiful young woman wearing a long dress and a hoodie at the intersection of Joshua Mqabuko Nkomo Street and Connaught Avenue. After requesting a ride toward Campbell Avenue near Athlone Cemetery, the mysterious passenger engages in casual conversation before suddenly vanishing into thin air while the vehicle is still in motion. Local lore suggests the apparition is the restless spirit of a female sex worker who was murdered by a client and buried in the nearby cemetery, returning to haunt and terrify unsuspecting male drivers.

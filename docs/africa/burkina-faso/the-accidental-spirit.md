#title
The Accidental Spirit

#summary
During a traditional voodoo ceremony in Burkina Faso, a visiting traveler is brushed by a spirit-possessed dancer and inadvertently becomes invaded by the entity himself. This unexpected possession necessitates an emergency exorcism on-site using an ancient lion femur.

#details
While observing an Egun Gun voodoo ceremony in the village of Cove (KoVay), Burkina Faso, travel writer James Michael Dorsey watched Bobo tribe dancers become possessed by ancestral spirits. When one of the spinning dancers surged forward and his billowing robes washed over the author, the spiritual entity crossed over and entered the writer's body. Recognizing the severe danger of an outsider hosting a spirit, the head Mambo and a secondary witch doctor intervened. Using a lion femur encrusted with cowry shells as a ritual wand, the exorcist chanted and drew the spirit out of the author's body and into the bone, ultimately saving him.

#location
The central square of the village of Cove (KoVay) in Burkina Faso.

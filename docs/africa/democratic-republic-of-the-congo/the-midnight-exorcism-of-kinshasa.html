    The Midnight Exorcism of Kinshasa

    Summary

    During a midnight deliverance service in Kinshasa, a prophet performs intense exorcisms on individuals believed to be possessed by malicious spirits. The ritual unfolds amidst widespread cultural fears of demonic possession and witchcraft.

    Details

    Inside a small concrete church in Kinshasa, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Prophet Pierre Pinda Buana leads a nocturnal deliverance service packed with believers seeking healing and liberation from evil spirits. Amidst chants, flickering lights, and fervent prayers, a woman collapses onto the floor, howling and writhing as the pastor confronts a demon allegedly attacking her body. Later, several children accused of being possessed sorcerers are brought forward for deliverance, revealing the pervasive local belief in spirit possession that drives thousands of vulnerable individuals into churches for exorcisms.

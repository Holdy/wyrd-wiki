#title
The Lake That Travelled With the Undesirable Twins

#summary
When a newborn twin is cast into a village lake to appease a father's superstition, the offended lake curses the settlement and abandons its basin. The resulting deprivation plunges generations of inhabitants into permanent agricultural ruin and famine unless appeased by annual sacrifices.

#details
A long-married couple who struggled with infertility finally conceived, but the husband strictly feared and detested non-identical twins, ordering his wife to destroy any if they appeared. When she secretly spared one twin by hiding it in the ceiling, her husband discovered the child and ordered his other son to throw it into the local village lake as an undesirable object. Instead of drowning, the infant was gently received by the parting waters, which overnight became filled with wrath. A booming voice cursed the inhabitants for treating the life-giving water as a dumping ground for unwanted kin, causing the lake to completely pack up and leave the village alongside the rescued boy while casting a blighting famine upon the land.

#location
The lakebed and surrounding village landscape tied to the ancient traditions of the region in Cameroon.

#upstream-source
Ndi-deng, recorded by Divine Che Neba in *Our Mythical Childhood Survey*.

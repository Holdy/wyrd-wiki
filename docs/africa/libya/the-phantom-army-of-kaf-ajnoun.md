#title
The Phantom Army of Kaf Ajnoun

#summary
A local resident witnessed an entire ghostly military division marching in formation around the base of Kaf Ajnoun before vanishing completely into the desert night. The apparition is one of many supernatural legends associated with the remote mountain.

#details
In the Fezzan region of southern Libya, the isolated sandstone massif of Kaf Ajnoun—frequently referred to as the Mountain of Ghosts or Idinen—has long held a reputation among local Tuareg nomads as a supernatural domain inhabited by jinn and spirits. Among the various encounters reported near the peak, a local man swore by Allah that he watched an entire phantom army division march in formation around the base of the mountain one night. The ghostly soldiers marched silently along the desert tracks flanking the mountain before disappearing entirely into thin air right before his eyes.

#location
The events take place at the base of Kaf Ajnoun (Idinen), a prominent desert mountain located approximately 25 kilometers north of Ghat in southern Libya.

    The White Woman of Flic en Flac

    Summary

    While packing up their campsite after a night of paranormal disturbances, a family and their tourist guests encountered a ghostly woman in white standing by a lane. She later vanished and reappeared directly beside their car door before they fled.

    Details

    In 1971, a family camping on the wild coast of Flic en Flac with a South African couple experienced a terrifying night marked by mysterious wailing sounds, falling objects, and a woman in a long white dress walking from the sea into the bushes. As they frantically packed up their gear to escape, they spotted the same white-robed woman standing by a nearby lane staring at them. When the driver attempted to move the car away, the apparition suddenly vanished and reappeared right next to the driver's door before disappearing again into the darkness as they sped off.

    The Curse of Simbi Nyaima

    Summary

    A wealthy village in Kenya was struck by a catastrophic curse after its residents cruelly turned away a mysterious old woman seeking shelter, causing the entire settlement to be swallowed overnight into a new lake. Only a single kind-hearted woman and her children escaped the deluge by heeding the stranger's warning to flee to the hills.

    Details

    Long ago, the bustling village of Simbi was consumed by endless feasting, drinking, and celebration under the rule of Chief Okumu. One evening, a frail, hunched old woman arrived on the outskirts of the settlement, begging for food and a place to sleep. The wealthy villagers and the chief mocked her, driving her away into the cold night. Only a humble woman named Awino showed compassion, taking the stranger into her home and feeding her. Before dawn, the old woman revealed that a terrible curse was about to befall the village for its greed and cruelty, advising Awino to flee with her children into the nearby hills. As morning broke, a ferocious torrential storm and earthquake struck Simbi, tearing huts apart and sinking the entire village into the earth. When the storm cleared, the settlement had vanished entirely, replaced by a vast, mystical body of water known today as Lake Simbi Nyaima.

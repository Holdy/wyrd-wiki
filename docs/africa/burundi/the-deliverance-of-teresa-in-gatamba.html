    The Deliverance of Teresa in Gatamba

    Summary

    A Burundian woman tormented by violent spiritual voices and severe sleep-walking was miraculously delivered from demonic possession during a village evangelistic gathering. For years, Teresa had sought help from various sources, only to find ultimate freedom when intercessors prayed for her at a public rally in Karuzi Province.

    Details

    Teresa had suffered under the severe torment of internal voices that commanded her to act violently and abusively toward others, alongside chronic sleep-walking and sleep-talking. Having visited multiple churches and traditional practitioners without relief, she attended a large evangelistic gathering in the village of Gatamba. During the service, as the speaker issued a call for commitment, Teresa hurled both of her shoes from the crowd in a dramatic manifestation of the possessing entity. She was immediately led behind the stage, where a team of intercessors prayed over her. Following this prayer, she was set free from the demon, completely losing the compulsion for violence and permanently ending her sleep-walking afflictions.

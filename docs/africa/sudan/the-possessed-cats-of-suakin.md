#title
The Possessed Cats of Suakin

#summary
The feral cats roaming the crumbling coral ruins of Suakin are believed by locals to be physical vessels possessed by water-dwelling jinn. These mysterious felines inhabit the historic Red Sea port town as living manifestations of supernatural entities.

#details
Along the Red Sea coast of Sudan sits the ancient, partially abandoned port town of Suakin, a place deeply intertwined with local legends of the supernatural. Among the crumbling 15th-to-20th-century coral-block ruins of the historic island, a distinct population of feral cats roams freely. According to traditional folklore, these animals are not ordinary felines; they are considered "demonic" cats that have been taken over and possessed by the spirits of jinn dwelling within the adjacent Red Sea waters. Locals regard these cats with cautious reverence, viewing their presence and unusual behaviors as proof of the enduring bond between the historic port and the unseen jinn realm.

#location
The historic island ruins of Suakin located on the Red Sea coast of eastern Sudan.

#title
The Midnight Passengers of Savai'i

#summary
A police officer driving his pickup truck through a deserted rainforest road in Samoa picks up two elderly women dressed in Sunday best, only to discover they have mysteriously vanished from the passenger seat upon reaching the edge of town.

#details
In the 1960s or 1970s, police officer Lipine was driving his battered two-seater pickup truck alone at night through a dark rainforest road on the island of Savai'i. Rounding a bend, he was startled to see two large, older women dressed in matching Sunday best walking along the empty road. Lipine stopped and offered them a ride, and the women climbed into the crowded bench seat, filling the cabin with their loud chatter and laughter. However, when Lipine reached the outskirts of town and turned to let them out, the passenger seat was entirely empty.

#location
The rainforest road on the southern edge of the island of Savai’i, Samoa.

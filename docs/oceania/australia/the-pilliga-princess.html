The Pilliga Princess

Summary

An eccentric woman who famously wandered the Newell Highway in New South Wales was tragically struck and killed by a truck in 1993. Since her death, motorists and truck drivers have frequently reported seeing her ghostly apparition pushing her shopping trolley along the highway after dark.

Details

For many years, an eccentric woman known as the "Pilliga Princess" was a well-known figure along the Newell Highway between Narrabri and Coonabarabran in New South Wales, frequently hitchhiking and pushing her shopping trolley. On a dark night in 1993, her life came to a tragic end when she was struck and killed by a truck while wandering across the road. Ever since that fateful night, drivers traveling along the highway continue to encounter her spectral form, faithfully pushing her shopping trolley through the dark Australian bushland.

#title
The Spirit Possession at Nacavanadi Church

#summary
During a prayer service in an island village, a congregant became possessed by a spirit that falsely accused a local homeowner of practicing witchcraft. Prompted by the entity's claims, the minister and villagers destroyed the targeted house, leading to a landmark court ruling against the church.

#details
In April 2007, a religious prayer session was held at the Nacavanadi Methodist Church on Gau Island, Fiji. During the service, a member of the congregation became possessed by an entity. Speaking through the possessed individual, the spirit declared that a villager named Ravuama Vonu practiced witchcraft and kept a worshipped skull inside his nearby house. Driven by the spirit's claims, the local Methodist minister, church steward, and members of the congregation broke into the home, smashing the concrete floor and destroying personal property in search of the alleged relics. The homeowners subsequently took legal action against the church officials, and the court found the minister and the Methodist Church vicariously liable for the destruction, ordering financial compensation.

#location
The Nacavanadi Methodist Church and the adjacent village home located in Nacavanadi Village on Gau Island, Fiji.

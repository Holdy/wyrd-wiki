#title
The Possessed Girl of Abaiang

#summary
A thirteen-year-old girl in the Gilbert Islands became possessed by a malevolent ancestral spirit, exhibiting an unnaturally swollen abdomen and superhuman strength, until a missionary successfully performed an exorcism at the village church.

#details
During the 1930s in the Gilbert Islands (now Kiribati), traditional beliefs regarding involuntary spirit possession were documented by local clergy. Father Ernest Sabatier recorded a striking account of a thirteen-year-old girl who fell victim to a hostile ancestral spirit (*anti*). The entity invaded her body, lodging within her hard, swollen abdomen and causing violent convulsions. Despite her young age, the possessed girl displayed terrifying, unnatural strength during the ordeal. When the missionary intervened at the mission church, he engaged in a dramatic spiritual struggle, performing an exorcism to cast out the possessing entity and free the girl from the torment.

#location
The Roman Catholic mission church and compound on the atoll of Abaiang in Kiribati.

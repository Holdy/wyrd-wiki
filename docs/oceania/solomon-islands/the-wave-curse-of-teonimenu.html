The Wave Curse of Teonimenu

Summary

After his wife eloped with another man to the island of Teonimenu, a betrayed husband purchased a devastating wave curse that unleashed successive waves until the entire island was submerged. Survivors scattered across neighboring islands, passing down the catastrophic tale through generations.

Details

When a woman named Sauwete'au eloped from Ali'ite Island with another man named Kaliita'alu and settled on the island of Teonimenu, her aggrieved husband Roraimenu vowed vengeance. He travelled and used shell money to purchase a powerful wave curse consisting of eight destructive waves. Returning to Ali'ite Island, Roraimenu climbed a high hill in the interior to watch as the magical waves battered Teonimenu one by one. The eighth and final wave completely overwhelmed and sank the island into the sea, drowning its inhabitants. While most perished, a few survivors managed to float on debris and driftwood, eventually washing ashore on nearby islands such as Ulawa, Malaita, and Makira, where oral traditions continue to preserve the memory of the disaster.

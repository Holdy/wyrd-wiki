#title
The Hat Man

#summary
A tall shadow figure in a wide-brimmed hat and often a long coat, the Hat Man appears in bedrooms at night, usually to people who have woken and cannot move.

#details
Shadow people are dark human-shaped figures seen at the edge of vision or at the foot of the bed, and the Hat Man is one of them. He is recognised by his outline. He has no visible face, and he usually stands still in a doorway or a corner of the room and watches.

He does not usually speak or touch the people he appears to. Witnesses most often report a strong feeling of dread.

He appears most often during sleep paralysis, when a person is awake but unable to move.

Accounts of the Hat Man became widespread around the turn of the twenty-first century, through late-night radio and internet forums. Witnesses who had not heard of him have described the same figure.

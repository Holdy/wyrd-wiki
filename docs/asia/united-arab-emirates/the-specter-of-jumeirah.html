The Specter of Jumeirah

Summary

Occupants of a residential villa in Dubai experienced terrifying nocturnal visitations by a malevolent entity that pinned them down, shook furniture, and locked doors.

Details

Deep in the heart of Jumeirah 1 in Dubai, a seven-bedroom residential villa became the center of relentless paranormal activity experienced by a group of occupants over the course of a year. The disturbances frequently manifested at night. One resident, Lora, woke up at 3:00 AM to an intense pressure crushing her entire body, as though an invisible entity was lying directly on top of her. Unable to move anything except her fingers and toes, she witnessed a dark shadow cast over the curtains above her. A few nights later, groaning sounds emanated from her roommate's bed, where a similar dark shadow was reflected on the wall. Other flatmates reported terrifying incidents, including a bed shaking incessantly during quiet hours while nothing else moved, a secretary being locked inside a bathroom by unseen forces for two hours, and socks inexplicably peeling off feet repeatedly throughout the night. Local folklore attributes these phenomena to malicious spirits or jinns inhabiting the older properties built upon historical areas of the district.

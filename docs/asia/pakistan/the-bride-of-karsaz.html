The Bride of Karsaz

Summary

A ghostly bride in a red dress haunts a major Karachi roadway where she tragically died on her wedding night. Drivers traveling late at night encounter her pleading for help or attempting to stop passing vehicles.

Details

According to local folklore, a young woman was traveling in a car on Karsaz Road in Karachi on her wedding night when a tragic accident killed her, her groom, and their driver. Ever since, her restless spirit, known locally as the Bride of Karsaz or Karsaz ki Churail, has haunted the asphalt. She is most frequently reported after midnight, appearing as a figure in a traditional red bridal lehenga. Some witnesses claim she flags down cars or asks for a lift, while others describe her appearance turning terrifying or bloody upon closer inspection. Local lore warns drivers not to stop for her or meet her gaze, as encountering the spectral bride can lead to disorientation or disaster on the road.

#title
The Exorcism and Curing of Nakasang's Possessed Hosts

#summary
In the riverside village of Nakasang in southern Laos, individuals believed to be possessed by ravenous *phi pop* spirits are banished from their homes to undergo rigorous biannual exorcism and water-cleansing rituals. These ceremonies serve to purge malevolent entities and restore community harmony.

#details
Rooted in ethnic Lao folklore, individuals accused of hosting malevolent, organ-devouring *phi pop* spirits are exiled from their home communities and sent to Nakasang Village in southern Laos for cultural healing and spiritual treatment. Upon arriving, the afflicted must formally acknowledge their possession and endure a mandatory three-year rehabilitation program. During biannual ceremonies at the village's sacred *ta ho* spirit house, spirit mediums channel ancestral entities while sanctified herbal water is poured through the floorboards onto the crouching hosts below. Following this, the possessed individuals must dash through the muddy *nong cheut* pond and swim downstream in the Mekong River to completely expel the possessing spirit.

#location
Nakasang Village, Khong District, Champasak Province, southern Laos, specifically centered around the village's *ta ho* spirit house and *nong cheut* ritual pond adjacent to the Mekong River.

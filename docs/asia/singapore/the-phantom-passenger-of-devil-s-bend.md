#title
The Phantom Passenger of Devil's Bend

#summary
Taxi drivers navigating the winding curves of Old Upper Thomson Road at night have reported picking up a mysterious woman in white who pays with banknotes that turn into hell notes.

#details
Old Upper Thomson Road, once utilized as a racing circuit for the Singapore Grand Prix, contains a notorious sharp V-turn known as Devil's Bend that has claimed numerous lives over the decades. Local taxi drivers working the night shift have shared chilling encounters of picking up a lone female passenger dressed in white along this dark stretch of road. After completing the journey and dropping her off, the drivers discover that the regular cash she handed them for payment has mysteriously transformed into traditional joss paper used for the deceased.

#location
Devil's Bend is a sharp V-turn located along Old Upper Thomson Road in Singapore.

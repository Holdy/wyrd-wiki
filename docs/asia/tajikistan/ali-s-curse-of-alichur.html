    Ali's Curse of Alichur

    Summary

    The remote high-altitude village of Alichur in Tajikistan derives its name from a permanent curse uttered by the Prophet Muhammad's son-in-law, Ali, who was tormented by the region's biting cold and relentless winds during his travels.

    Details

    According to local folklore, when the prophet's son-in-law Ali journeyed through the freezing expanses of the Pamir Mountains, he was so overwhelmed by the exceptionally harsh climate and penetrating winds that he pronounced a curse upon the land. Ever since that ancient journey, the settlement and surrounding valley have borne the name Alichur, meaning "Ali's curse," cementing the region's enduring reputation for bitter weather and forbidding isolation.

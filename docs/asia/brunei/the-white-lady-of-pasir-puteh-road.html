The White Lady of Pasir Puteh Road

Summary

Driving late at night along the old road to Belait, a family encountered a mysterious white lady standing in their path who vanished after they drove straight through her.

Details

Late one night, an aunt, uncle, and their family were traveling back to Seria along the old road to Belait before the modern coastal highway was built. As they passed through the Pasir Puteh area, they noticed someone waving by the roadside. As their vehicle drew closer, a ghostly white lady suddenly stepped directly into the middle of the road. Unable to stop in time, the car drove right through the spectral figure, but when they looked back behind them, the figure had completely disappeared.

#title
The Petrification of Nakhoda Manis

#summary
A wealthy merchant who rejected his poor mother upon returning home was struck by a devastating curse that capsized his ship and turned it into stone. Today, that permanent wreckage remains as an isolated rock outcrop in the river.

#details
Nakhoda Manis grew up in poverty in Kampong Ayer before leaving to seek his fortune abroad. Years later, he returned as a wealthy trader commanding a magnificent ship, accompanied by his high-born wife. Hearing of his arrival, his aging mother, Dang Ambon, paddled out in a small boat to welcome him with open arms. Ashamed of her humble and impoverished appearance in front of his aristocratic wife, Nakhoda Manis cruelly rejected her and ordered his crew to drive her away. Heartbroken by this betrayal, the mother called out a bitter curse, bringing down a fierce tempest that smashed into the vessel and capsized it. When the storm cleared, the ship and its arrogant captain were transformed into a permanent stone formation.

#location
Jong Batu is a rocky outcropping situated in the Brunei River, located to the east of Istana Nurul Iman in the Brunei-Muara District.

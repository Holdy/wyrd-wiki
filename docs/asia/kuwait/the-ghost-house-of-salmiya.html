    The Ghost House of Salmiya

    Summary

    A long-abandoned villa in Kuwait became the center of persistent urban legends and rumors of a curse after tragic circumstances left it vacant. Generations of locals claimed that anyone who attempted to sleep inside would mysteriously wake up outside on the highway.

    Details

    Built in the 1960s by Saleh Al-Ibrahim, the villa gained notoriety across Kuwait as the "Ghost House" after family tragedy struck before it could be inhabited. Over the decades, chilling folklore surrounded the property, with locals and adventurous teenagers sharing tales of strange noises, shadowy figures, and malevolent jinn. The most famous legend claimed that any trespassers daring to spend the night inside would find themselves inexplicably tossed out or waking up on the nearby highway by morning. Even during the Iraqi invasion and later redevelopment phases, rumors of a lingering curse persisted among workers and visitors until the site was ultimately replaced by a hotel and mosque.

The Architect's Curse of Zhongar Dzong

Summary

The master architect of Zhongar Dzong placed a dying curse on the fortress after being betrayed, condemning the stronghold to inevitable ruin and spiritual torment.

Details

According to eastern Bhutanese folklore, the magnificent fortress of Zhongar Dzong was constructed under the direction of a master architect and carpenter named Zowo Balip (also known as Bala) from Paro. Impressed by his peerless craftsmanship, the local authorities or rulers feared that he might build an even greater structure for a rival, or they grew envious of his fame. In a cruel act of betrayal, they decided to have him put to death. As Zowo Balip lay dying in agony within the unfinished complex, he uttered a ferocious curse against the fortress: he vowed that the dzong would never see lasting peace, that it would ultimately be destroyed, and that his spirit would return as a malevolent entity known as Zhongar Neypo to plague the land and bring misfortune to those who crossed its path. True to his dying words, the grand fortress has suffered repeated disasters, fires, and structural collapses over the centuries, eventually being left completely abandoned in ruins. To this day, locals regard the site with deep apprehension and avoid the crumbling compound after dark to escape the lingering wrath of the architect's curse.

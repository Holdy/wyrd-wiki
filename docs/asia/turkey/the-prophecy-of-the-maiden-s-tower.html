The Prophecy of the Maiden's Tower

Summary

An anxious ruler constructed a fortress in the middle of the Bosphorus to protect his daughter from a deadly prophecy, but a venomous snake hidden within a basket of fruit ultimately fulfilled the curse.

Details

According to longstanding Istanbul folklore, a Byzantine ruler was devastated when an oracle prophesied that his beloved daughter was cursed to die from a venomous snakebite before her eighteenth birthday. Desperate to outwit fate, the king ordered a fortified tower to be built upon a rocky islet in the middle of the Bosphorus strait, believing the surrounding waters would keep all snakes at bay. The princess grew up in total isolation inside the stone tower, safely cut off from the mainland. However, on her eighteenth birthday, a celebratory basket of fruit sent from the city was delivered to her quarters. A deadly serpent had coiled itself unseen within the fruit, striking and killing the princess just as the oracle had foretold.

The Curse of Three-Year Hill

Summary

A farmer falls on a named hill in South Korea that is believed to curse anyone who trips there with only three years left to live. However, he learns to outsmart the curse by falling and rolling down the hill repeatedly to extend his lifespan.

Details

Farmer Yoon travels over Three-Year Hill to trade his harvest at a seaside market, always careful because of the local legend that anyone who falls on the hill is cursed to have only three years left to live. When he accidentally trips over a loose pebble, he panics and returns home, where his despair and worry make him gravely ill. A mysterious young boy visits him and points out the clever loophole: if one fall gives you three years, falling multiple times will extend your life proportionally. Enlightened, Farmer Yoon rushes back to the hill and rolls down it repeatedly, breaking the psychological despair of the curse and living a long, joyful life.

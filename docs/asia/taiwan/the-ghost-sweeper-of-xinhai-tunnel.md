#title
The Ghost Sweeper of Xinhai Tunnel

#summary
Drivers passing through Taipei's Xinhai Tunnel late at night frequently encounter a ghostly old woman sweeping the roadway who abruptly vanishes when approached.

#details
Built directly upon a former hillside cemetery, the Xinhai Tunnel in Taipei is notorious for nocturnal apparitions. Late-night drivers have reported encountering an old woman calmly sweeping the roadway in the middle of the tunnel. Despite motorists honking their horns for her to move, she continues sweeping without acknowledging them until vehicles come within a few feet, at which moment she vanishes into thin air.

#location
The Xinhai Tunnel is located on Xinhai Road in Taipei, Taiwan, connecting central Taipei to the Muzha area of Wenshan District.

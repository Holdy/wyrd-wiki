#title
The Curse of the Sanzhi UFO Houses

#summary
An ambitious futuristic coastal resort in Taiwan became plagued by rumors of a dragon curse and fatal accidents after construction mysteriously collapsed and left the pod-shaped buildings abandoned. The eerie ruins eventually gained global notoriety as a haunted, cursed ghost city before being demolished.

#details
Beginning around 1978, developers in Taiwan set out to build an innovative holiday resort featuring futuristic, spaceship-shaped pod houses on the grey coastal flats of Sanzhi, near Tamsui. Designed on concrete pillars to offer seaside views, the complex aimed to attract city dwellers looking for a sci-fi getaway. However, financial ruin, economic shocks, and soaring costs caused construction to grind to a halt around 1980, leaving the eccentric capsules unfinished and abandoned. As the salt air and typhoons weathered the uncompleted structures, dark folklore took root around the site. Rumors spread that the project was doomed by the curse of a stone dragon statue that had been moved or destroyed during initial land clearance, disturbing the local feng shui. Other macabre tales claimed that a series of fatal accidents and deaths among construction workers had cursed the ground, trapping vengeful spirits within the hollow pods. The site became widely known as one of Asia's most famous cursed ruins until it was finally demolished between 2008 and 2010.

#location
The Sanzhi UFO Houses were located along the coastal flats in Sanzhi District, New Taipei City, Taiwan.

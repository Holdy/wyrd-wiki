The Ditchdigger of Old Ghost Road

Summary

Motorists traveling along a secluded Georgia road at night frequently encounter a ghostly figure digging a ditch who angrily approaches vehicles before vanishing.

Details

Drivers venturing after dark down Robertson Road, commonly known as Old Ghost Road, in Brooklet, Georgia, often notice a strange, faint orange light glowing in the distance. As cars draw closer, the light illuminates a phantom man diligently digging a ditch in the roadway. If observers watch him for too long, the aggressive spirit turns and charges toward the vehicle before dissolving completely into thin air.

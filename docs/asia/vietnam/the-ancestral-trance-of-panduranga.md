#title
The Ancestral Trance of Panduranga

#summary
During an annual spring ritual at a family home in Panduranga, a designated young medium is overtaken and possessed by ancestral spirits to judge and guide living descendants.

#details
In the ancestral homelands of Panduranga, modern descendants of the ancient Cham civilization preserve their living heritage through sacred spring rituals held shortly after the Cham New Year. During this ceremony, when the boundary between the living and the dead is believed to grow thin, a young female family member chosen as the household's designated spirit medium kneels before a candlelit altar. As traditional *baranưng* and *ginăng* drums beat a hypnotic rhythm accompanied by piercing *saranai* music, the medium enters a deep trance and is completely possessed by ancestral entities. Brandishing blue hand fans, she leaps and spins in an animated dance, summoning family members forward to prostrate themselves, receive spiritual judgment, and pay proper homage to their lineage.

#location
The ceremony takes place at a family home located just outside the city of Phan Rang-Tháp Chàm in Ninh Thuận Province, Vietnam.

The Curse of the Sleeping Vishnu

Summary

Following a prophetic vision in the seventeenth century, King Pratap Malla was warned that any ruling monarch of Nepal visiting the Budhanilkantha Temple would meet an untimely death. Fearing this royal curse, generations of Nepalese kings avoided entering the sacred site.

Details

In the seventeenth century, King Pratap Malla experienced a terrifying prophetic dream and vision warning him that a divine curse had been placed upon the royal lineage. According to the vision, if any reigning King of Nepal were to set foot inside or visit the sacred Budhanilkantha Temple—home to the monumental reclining statue of Lord Vishnu floating in a pool—he would suffer a sudden and premature death. Gripped by this supernatural decree, Pratap Malla and all subsequent monarchs of Nepal strictly avoided visiting the temple grounds, sending representatives or worshiping at alternative shrines instead to evade the fatal hex.

The Woman in Red on the Thimi Highway

Summary

Two motorcyclists riding home late at night encounter a towering, pale-skinned woman in a red sari standing motionless in the middle of the highway. As they attempt to steer past her, her arms unnaturally stretch out toward them before she vanishes into thin air.

Details

Late one night, a driver from Bhaktapur and his friend were returning home from a party on separate motorcycles. Riding ahead on a dark, empty stretch of the highway around Thimi, the lead driver noticed what looked like a tall red pillar standing directly in the middle of the road. Upon drawing closer, they realized the pillar was actually an exceptionally tall woman wearing a red sari with golden embroidery, featuring loose black hair, an expressionless face, and impossibly white skin. As the motorcyclists tried to ride around her on either side, the woman extended her arms and made a beckoning gesture. Her hands began stretching unnaturally toward them at terrifying speed. Narrowly avoiding her grasping hands by inches, the two men sped away without looking back, leaving the road completely empty behind them. Both men fell ill from the shock and trauma of the encounter, which has also been witnessed by other nighttime travelers passing through the area.

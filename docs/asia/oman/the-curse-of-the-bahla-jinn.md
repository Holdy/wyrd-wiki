#title
The Curse of the Bahla Jinn

#summary
Bahla Fort is bound by a persistent curse where any attempts to renovate or rebuild its ancient mud-brick structures mysteriously collapse overnight due to a haunting jinn. 

#details
According to Omani folklore, the medieval Bahla Fort is plagued by a dark curse tied to its supernatural origins and mystical history. Believed by locals to be haunted or initially conjured by powerful jinns and sorcery, a malevolent spirit guards the citadel. For generations, whenever builders, craftsmen, or government authorities attempt to restore, repair, or add to the decaying fortifications, the curse manifests itself. Newly completed walls and restored rooms mysteriously crumble or collapse into ruin overnight, frustrating human efforts to tame the ancient stronghold.

#location
Bahla Fort is a prominent historic fortification located in the heart of the oasis town of Bahla in the Ad Dakhiliyah Governorate of Oman.

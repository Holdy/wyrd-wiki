#title
Curse of Turin Cave

#summary
Queen Oh Pyae Thin inscribed a potent curse upon the Turin Cave in Bagan to damn anyone who desecrates her sacred offerings to endless torment and starvation.

#details
During the historic Bagan era, Queen Oh Pyae Thin established a sacred shrine at Turin Cave and sealed it with a fearsome stone inscription curse. She decreed that anyone who maliciously destroys or interferes with her pious deeds would be cast into the depths of the Avici hell, rendered unable to ever witness the Buddhas of the universe, and condemned to perpetual, agonizing starvation and thirst where they wish to eat and drink but can never do so.

#location
Turin Cave is situated in the ancient archaeological temple zone of Bagan, Mandalay Region, Myanmar.

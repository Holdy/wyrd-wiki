The Curse of King Ue-Lue

Summary

A grieving and vengeful serpent king cursed a mortal ruler to transform into stone and remain bound to a mountain cave for eternity after a bitter deception.

Details

In the ancient kingdom of Rattappa Nakhon, Prince Fahoong fell in love with a mysterious woman named Nakkarintrani, who eventually revealed herself to be a Naka serpent in human form. When her true nature was exposed to the royal court, King Ue-Lue banished her in anger, prompting her father—the Naka King—to wage war, submerge the kingdom beneath the waters of what is now Khong Long Lake, and place a fierce curse upon King Ue-Lue. The monarch was transformed into a giant stone serpent imprisoned forever in the mountains, where his scaled rock formation can still be seen today at Naka Cave.

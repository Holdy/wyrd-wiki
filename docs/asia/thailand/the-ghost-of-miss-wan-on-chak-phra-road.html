The Ghost of Miss Wan on Chak Phra Road

Summary

A narrow road in Bangkok's Thonburi district is haunted by the spirit of a pregnant woman who was murdered by her husband. Her terrifying apparition frequently manifests to motorists near the Wat Taling Chan temple.

Details

Dating back to the late 1970s, a grim urban legend surrounds Chak Phra Road in the Thonburi district of Bangkok, which runs alongside the Khlong Chak Phra canal. According to the tale, a local woman named Miss Wan—referred to as Phi Yai Wan—was brutally murdered by her husband while she was pregnant. Her restless, vengeful spirit now haunts the roadway in various disturbing ways. In particular, her ghost is frequently encountered by passersby directly in front of the Wat Taling Chan temple that the road passes through. Fearing paranormal encounters with her spirit, many local taxi and tuk-tuk drivers strictly avoid driving down Chak Phra Road after sunset.

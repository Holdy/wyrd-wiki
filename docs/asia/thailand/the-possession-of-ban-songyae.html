The Possession of Ban Songyae

Summary

In 1908, multiple families in the northeastern Thai village of Ban Songyae fell victim to an outbreak of spirit possession by the cannibalistic Pop entity. The afflicted villagers were ultimately delivered when two Catholic priests intervened with religious rites at the village church.

Details

In 1908, panic gripped the remote northeastern village of Ban Songyae in Yasothon province when members of five local families were overcome by a malevolent, visceral-eating entity known in Isan folklore as a Pop. The possessed individuals displayed alarming and erratic behavior, plunging the community into terror. Desperate for deliverance, the villagers appealed to the local clergy. Two Catholic priests, Father Desaval and Father Ambrosio, intervened and performed solemn prayers, blessings, and religious rites centered around the settlement's wooden church, successfully subduing the entity and restoring peace to the community.

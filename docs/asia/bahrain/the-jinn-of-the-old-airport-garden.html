    The Jinn of the Old Airport Garden

    Summary

    During a family picnic at a public garden in Bahrain, a young boy disturbed a hidden spirit and became possessed by an angry Jinn. The entity spoke through the child in a mature voice before releasing him unharmed at dawn.

    Details

    While visiting the old airport garden in Bahrain, a young boy spent his time playing near the trees and plants. Shortly after returning home, he fell ill and was put to bed by his parents. Just before the Fajar prayer, the boy woke up, began aggressively banging on his parents' bedroom door, and spoke in an unfamiliar, mature tone. The voice revealed that the boy had disturbed a Jinn at the garden, resulting in his possession. As soon as the Fajar call to prayer echoed from the mosque, the entity demanded the door be opened, issued a strict warning never to return to the park, and left the child completely unharmed.

#title
The Jinn Possession at Makunudhoo School

#summary
Students at a remote Maldivian island school were struck by mysterious faintings and violent possessions attributed to a jinn residing in a schoolyard tree.

#details
A decade ago at Makunudhoo School in the Maldives, an alarming series of events began when several students suddenly fainted without explanation. Over the following month, the episodes escalated as some children were seized by a strange, violent force, thrashing with such inhuman strength that groups of four or five men struggled to restrain them. Whispers quickly spread across the island regarding jinn possession and supernatural attacks, with locals pointing blame at a large rain tree (*reethigas*) growing on the school grounds that was believed to be infested with spirits. As school sessions were suspended, both modern psychiatrists and traditional exorcists (*fanditha verin*) were brought to the remote island, and the controversial rain tree was unceremoniously chopped down. While psychiatrists diagnosed nineteen of the children with mass hysteria born of shared terror, four students were considered genuinely afflicted by the spirits, requiring traditional exorcists to trap the jinn in a bottle and sink it into the ocean.

#location
Makunudhoo School, situated on the island of Makunudhoo in the Haa Dhaalu Atoll of the Maldives.

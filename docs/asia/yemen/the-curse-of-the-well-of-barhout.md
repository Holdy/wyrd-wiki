#title
The Curse of the Well of Barhout

#summary
Formed as a supernatural prison for jinn and evil spirits, a massive sinkhole in the eastern desert of Yemen is regarded as a cursed abyss that brings misfortune and disaster to anyone who approaches its edge.

#details
Deep within the barren terrain of Yemen's Al-Mahra province lies a colossal natural sinkhole known as the Well of Barhout. According to regional folklore and tradition, the chasm was created as a cursed prison designed to trap malevolent jinn and demons. Locals believe that the site is one of the most reviled places on Earth, frequently emitting foul odors and bringing severe misfortune to those who venture too near its gaping edge. For centuries, people avoided the area due to warnings of supernatural dangers and mysterious sounds emanating from its depths, until modern explorers finally descended into its dark interior.

#location
The Well of Barhout is located in the eastern desert of Al-Mahra province in Yemen.

The Vanishing Passengers of the Cemetery Road

Summary

A taxi driver in Suriname who picked up two women near a motion-picture house discovered they had vanished from his vehicle as he drove past the cemetery.

Details

Late one night at eleven o'clock, a taxi driver in Paramaribo was parked near a motion-picture house when two young women, dressed in pink as if for church, hired his cab. They agreed on a fare of one guilder fifty cents and directed him down a road that led past the local cemetery. However, upon passing the cemetery, the driver turned around to ask for their exact directions, only to find the back seat completely empty. The terrifying encounter left the driver severely shaken and sick with fever for months afterward.

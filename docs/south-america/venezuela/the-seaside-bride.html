The Seaside Bride

Summary

A spectral bride haunts a mountain highway, waiting on the roadside or manifesting in the back seat of lonely vehicles to cause fatal accidents.

Details

Drivers traveling at night along the old Caracas-La Guaira road are warned never to pick up lone female hitchhikers. Those who ignore the warning may suddenly feel an unseen gaze from the back seat, only to discover a terrifying apparition if they look in the rearview mirror, causing them to crash. Others have spotted her walking slowly along the roadside dressed in a bridal gown, featuring a gruesome twisted torso where her face looks backward while her feet face forward, re-enacting the tragic agony of her wedding day death.

The Possession of José in San Pedro Cave

Summary

In February 1991, a young boy named José became possessed by a malevolent entity after exploring a forbidden cave on Cerro San Pedro in Oruro, Bolivia, leading to a harrowing, multi-hour exorcism.

Details

In February 1991, a robust and cheerful boy named José, living in the San Pedro neighborhood of Oruro, Bolivia, decided to explore a natural fissure inside a cave located on top of Cerro San Pedro, a site locally associated with dark rituals. Within three months of his visit, José's appearance and behavior drastically deteriorated; he became emaciated, deeply aggressive, and began shouting in strange, unrecognizable languages while emitting deep, guttural laughs. Recognizing the signs of severe possession, local pastors intervened. Accompanied by his uncle and witnessed by journalist Humberto Cabezas from the newspaper La Patria, a grueling exorcism session lasting over twenty hours was conducted. During the ordeal, the entity inside the boy resisted fiercely, proclaiming that the child and his family belonged to it. Through continuous prayer, singing, and scripture readings, the entity was eventually cast out, allowing José to make a full recovery.

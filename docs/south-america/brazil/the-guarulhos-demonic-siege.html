The Guarulhos Demonic Siege

Summary

A young mother and her family in Brazil became the center of a terrifying multi-year demonic haunting involving aggressive spirit entities that slashed furniture, sparked fires, and forced family members into trances. Researchers from the Brazilian Institute of Psychobiophysical Research extensively documented the harrowing events as an apparent supernatural siege.

Details

Beginning in April 1973 in Guarulhos, São Paulo, 21-year-old Noêmia and her extended family experienced bizarre and violent phenomena in their home. Furniture, mattresses, and folded blankets were repeatedly slashed with deep parallel cuts resembling claw marks, while mysterious stones rained down onto their roof. Spontaneous fires broke out across the house, and family members—including Noêmia, her father-in-law Pedro, and her young children—were periodically overtaken by violent trances, spasms, and visions of a red-furred beast with long black claws and a fiery visage. Investigators from the Brazilian Institute of Psychobiophysical Research (IBPP), led by Hernani Guimarães Andrade, studied the case for over a decade, ruling out natural causes and noting that religious scriptures and prayers specifically provoked the entities. Believing they had been cursed through witchcraft involving umuluns or Exú spirits summoned by a spurned ex-lover, the family endured years of torment until Noêmia finally stood her ground against the entity during a terrifying nocturnal confrontation.

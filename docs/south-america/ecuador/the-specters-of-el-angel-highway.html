The Specters of El Ángel Highway

Summary

While parked on a road near El Ángel in Carchi, a woman and her companions witnessed three robed figures carrying scythes and a trident float past them towards the local cemetery before vanishing into the dawn mist.

Details

In 2021 during the COVID-19 pandemic, Sintia Robles was traveling by car with family and friends from Carchi to Pichincha. At around 2:30 in the morning, she stopped the vehicle near the city of El Ángel to adjust the air conditioning. While waiting, she noticed three figures approaching down the road. As they drew closer, she realized they were dressed in dark robes resembling Holy Week penitents. The two figures on the outer edges carried scythes, while the middle figure carried a trident. Chillingly, the specters were not walking on the ground but floating, and as they passed, a thick mist enveloped them. All five occupants of the vehicle shared the terrifying vision as the entities drifted toward the El Ángel cemetery before disappearing completely into the dawn fog.

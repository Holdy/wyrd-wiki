The Incomplete Basilica Curse

Summary

A persistent urban legend claims that if the massive Basilica del Voto Nacional in Quito is ever fully finished, it will trigger the end of the world. Because of this ominous, delayed curse, construction is kept permanently incomplete.

Details

Dominating the historic skyline of Quito, the Basílica del Voto Nacional is the largest neo-Gothic basilica in the Americas. According to widespread local folklore, the structure carries a fearful supernatural penalty linked to its completion. It is said that the day the final stone is laid and the building is declared fully finished will mark the end of the world. To avert this apocalyptic doom, construction has been intentionally prolonged and left incomplete through perpetual minor repairs and unfinalized details, ensuring the fateful milestone is never reached.

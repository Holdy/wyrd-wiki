#title
The Curse of the Cliffside Church at Vražji Kamen

#summary
A forbidden wedding at a remote 14th-century cliffside church in southern Serbia ended in fatal tragedy, establishing a lasting curse that prevents couples from ever marrying there again. Local folklore holds that any wedding party attempting to celebrate at the site is doomed to disaster.

#details
Perched precariously atop the jagged rock formation known as Vražji Kamen (Devil's Rock) in the Pčinja district of Serbia stands the medieval Virgin Mary’s Church, built between 1350 and 1380. According to local legend, a young couple from nearby Donje Trnica fell in love despite their parents' staunch disapproval and decided to defy them by holding their wedding at this remote mountaintop church. Following the ceremony, as the wedding party made a commotion with music and noise, the groom's horse was startled and plunged into the chasm below. Overcome with grief, the bride immediately dismounted and leaped after him. Ever since this tragic event, the church and its surrounding grounds have been considered cursed, and weddings are strictly avoided there to this day.

#location
The 14th-century Virgin Mary's Church sits atop the towering Vražji Kamen monolith in the Pčinja district of southeastern Serbia, near Vranje.

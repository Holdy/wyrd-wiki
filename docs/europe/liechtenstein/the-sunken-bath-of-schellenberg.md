#title
The Sunken Bath of Schellenberg

#summary
An arrogant innkeeper refuses to observe the start of Lent with proper penance and instead mocks the sacred season, bringing a devastating cataclysm upon his spa house and all who remained inside.

#details
Long ago, between Nofels and Ruggell near the foot of the Schellenberg, a popular spa operated with abundant sulfurous waters, a lavish kitchen, and a lively cellar. While it brought comfort to many, the innkeeper fostered an atmosphere of hedonistic indulgence, ignoring moral limits and turning a deaf ear to warnings with scorn. On Shrove Tuesday, the revelry grew exceptionally wild, continuing long into the night with drinking, dancing, and music well past the midnight transition into Ash Wednesday. When a few prudent guests warned against desecrating the holy start of Lent with such debauchery, the arrogant innkeeper mocked them, declaring he would rather go to hell with his merry guests than sing penitential hymns. Several guests wisely fled the building. Scarcely fifty paces away, they felt a violent tremor of the earth and turned around to witness the entire spa building sink straight into the ground. Only a chimney cap briefly remained visible before it vanished, leaving behind a deep pool of water where the sinful celebration had taken place.

#location
The site is located in the lowlands between Ruggell, Liechtenstein, and Nofels at the foot of the Schellenberg.

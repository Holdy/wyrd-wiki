#title
The Woman in Black of Pinzgauer Bundesstrasse

#summary
Since 1981, motorists driving along an Austrian highway have reported encountering a mysterious hitchhiking woman in black who issues a chilling warning before vanishing. Believed to be the spirit of a tragic traffic victim, she forewarns drivers that they would have crashed had they not stopped for her.

#details
Motorists traveling along the Pinzgauer Bundesstrasse in the Salzburg region of Austria have frequently reported seeing a young woman dressed entirely in black standing by the roadside, attempting to flag down rides. According to recurring accounts dating back to 1981, once a driver picks her up and she is inside the vehicle, she turns to them and says, "If you hadn’t stopped and picked me up, you would have had an accident," immediately after which she vanishes into thin air. Local lore associates her with the ghost of a 23-year-old waitress from St. Veit who lost her life in a horrific road crash nearby in 1980, when her car ran off the road and straight into the path of a speeding train on the parallel tracks.

#location
The Pinzgauer Bundesstrasse is a federal highway running through the Pinzgau and Pongau regions in the Salzburg region of Austria.

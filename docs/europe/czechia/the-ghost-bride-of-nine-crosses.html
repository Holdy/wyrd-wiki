The Ghost Bride of Nine Crosses

Summary

Motorists driving along a notorious highway section are frequently startled by the apparition of a phantom bride in a white shroud. This haunting stems from a tragic 16th-century wedding massacre at a historic crossroads.

Details

In 1540, a wedding party traveling from a church in Velké Bíteš to the village of Lesní Hluboké was ambushed at a remote crossroads by a jealous Hungarian merchant and his accomplice. The ambush went horribly wrong when gunfire killed seven people, including the bride, the groom, and the bride's father; the attackers also perished in the ensuing chaos. Nine victims were buried at the spot, which became marked by wooden crosses. Today, the white-shrouded ghost of the slain bride haunts the D1 motorway near Exit 168, appearing to drivers and causing them to swerve or lose control.

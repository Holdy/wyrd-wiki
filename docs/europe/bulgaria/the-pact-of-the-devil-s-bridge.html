    The Pact of the Devil's Bridge

    Summary

    A master builder struck a fatal bargain with the devil to conquer a curse that destroyed every bridge at a treacherous river crossing, sealing his wife's fate into the stone. Today, the enduring structure still mirrors the face of the devil in its reflection.

    Details

    For generations, any attempt to bridge the fast-flowing Arda River at a narrow gorge was thwarted when the violent waters repeatedly washed away every structure, leading locals to believe the crossing was cursed. Desperate to conquer the site, a local master builder named Dimitar accepted a dark bargain offered by the devil to ensure his bridge would withstand the torrents. In exchange for architectural guidance, the devil demanded that Dimitar wall up his wife's shadow into the masonry, which meant she would die shortly after the construction was completed. Dimitar fulfilled the pact in forty days, finishing the magnificent stone bridge, but true to the curse's delayed cost, his young wife fell gravely ill and passed away soon after.

    St Ursula's skulls

    Summary

    The church of St Ursula in Cologne once had small boxes of skulls crowding its walls above the arches and below the clerestory windows. In later years they were hidden behind curtains, but the sacristy still looked like a charnel house.

    Details

    Writing about the veneration of relics, Sabine Baring-Gould described St Ursula's in Cologne, where only a few years earlier the space above the arches and below the clerestory windows was crowded with small boxes containing skulls. The church had since put them behind curtains, perhaps a little ashamed of the display, but its sacristy still had the appearance of a charnel house.

#title
The Terror of Heol Fanog

#summary
A family residing in an isolated Welsh farmhouse suffered seven years of severe supernatural torment, including mysterious animal fatalities, suspected possession, and numerous exorcisms.

#details
In 1989, Bill and Liz Rich moved into Heol Fanog, a converted stone farmhouse situated in the remote Brecon Beacons, hoping for a peaceful rural life. Soon after arriving, they and Bill's son Laurence were subjected to escalating terrifying phenomena: heavy phantom footsteps that abruptly halted on the stairs, radical temperature drops, and the overpowering stench of sulphur. The household also experienced massive, unexplained electrical surges and the sudden, mysterious deaths of their animals. Most alarming were the physical injuries inflicted on family members and an apparent demonic possession that caused a dramatic alteration in Laurence's personality. Desperate for help, Liz invited a local priest to perform an exorcism. Although the haunting paused briefly, it returned with redoubled force, resulting in a succession of multiple exorcisms over seven years before the family finally escaped the ordeal.

#location
Heol Fanog is a remote farmhouse located in the rural uplands of the Brecon Beacons in Powys, Wales.

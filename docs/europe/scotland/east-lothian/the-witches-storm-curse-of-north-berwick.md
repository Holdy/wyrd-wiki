#title
The Witches' Storm Curse of North Berwick

#summary
In 1590, a coven of alleged witches gathered at a local church in East Lothian to conjure a deadly storm to drown King James VI, sparking one of Scotland's most notorious witch panics.

#details
As King James VI of Scotland sailed back from Denmark with his new bride, Anne of Denmark, their fleet was battered by violent, inexplicable tempests that nearly capsized their ships. Convinced that treasonous forces were at work, the deeply paranoid king initiated a massive investigation that unveiled a terrifying tale of witchcraft. Under extreme torture, accused individuals confessed that a coven had gathered inside the old church on All Hallows' Eve to raise the devil. They claimed to have dug up corpses for gruesome relics, drowned a cat with human organs in the sea, and chanted powerful curses designed to summon a crushing tempest to sink the royal fleet.

#location
St Andrew's Auld Kirk Green stands on the coast in the town of North Berwick, East Lothian.

#title
The Phantom Bell of Kirkmaiden

#summary
When the bell and pulpit of the old Kirkmaiden church were unlawfully removed and transported across the bay, a sudden storm sank the ship. Ever since, the sunken bell tolling from the watery depths serves as a delayed and fatal omen predicting the death of a McCulloch.

#details
Before the parish of Kirkmaiden merged with Glasserton, a decision was made to relocate the church's bell and pulpit across Luce Bay to a newly built church in the Rhins. Despite it being a calm and clear day when the vessel set out, a sudden and violent storm blew up, causing the ship to flounder and sink beneath the waves. The bell was lost to the sea, but it did not fall silent forever; now and again, its ghostly toll can be heard echoing up from the dark, watery depths of the bay, acting as a grim warning that portends the approaching death of a member of the McCulloch family.

#location
Luce Bay, off the coast of Wigtownshire, Scotland.

The Ghostly Parade of Kinmount Straight

Summary

Drivers on the A75 in Dumfriesshire have reported seeing ghostly figures, including groups of people pulling handcarts or carrying bundles.

Details

The A75 road in Dumfriesshire is notorious for its supernatural sightings. Drivers have reported encountering ghostly figures, particularly on the Kinmount Straight section. These apparitions often appear as groups of dejected and bedraggled individuals pulling handcarts or carrying bundles, reminiscent of medieval camp followers. One driver was so traumatized by witnessing this "parade" that he quit his job altogether.

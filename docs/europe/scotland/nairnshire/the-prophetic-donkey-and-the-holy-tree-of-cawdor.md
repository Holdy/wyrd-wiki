#title
The Prophetic Donkey and the Holy Tree of Cawdor

#summary
Guided by a dream, the Thane of Cawdor loaded a chest of gold onto a donkey and vowed that his family's fortress would stand forever wherever the animal chose to rest. The donkey laid down beneath a sacred tree, ensuring prosperity and survival for generations as long as the foundation remained anchored to it.

#details
Seeking a more secure stronghold than his previous residence, the Thane of Cawdor received divine instructions in a dream. Following this vision, he loaded a coffer of gold onto the back of a donkey and set the animal loose to roam the Nairnshire district for an entire day. He decreed that wherever the donkey finally laid down to rest at nightfall, there his new castle must be built to secure eternal prosperity for his lineage. The donkey wandered until it stopped and lay down beneath a tree. The castle tower was subsequently constructed directly around this tree, which generations later was protected within the building's stone vaults and survived centuries of turbulent Highland history.

#location
Cawdor Castle is located near the village of Cawdor, southwest of Nairn in Nairnshire, Scotland.

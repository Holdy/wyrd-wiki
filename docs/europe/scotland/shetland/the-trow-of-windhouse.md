#title
The Trow of Windhouse

#summary
An annual supernatural visitation struck a remote Shetland estate every Christmas Eve, claiming the life of a family member each Christmas morning until a shipwrecked sailor stayed behind to break the deadly cycle.

#details
The proprietor of the grand Windhouse estate died, leaving his son and large family to inherit the big house. However, a terrifying curse or supernatural visitation plagued the property: every single Christmas Eve between midnight and one o'clock, a great noise and a violent whirlwind would shake the building. Inevitably, every Christmas morning would dawn to find a member of the household—whether a daughter, a son, or a serving maid—dead. 

Desperate to escape the annual tragedy, the family packed their belongings and prepared to abandon the house before the next Christmas. Just as they were leaving, a shipwrecked sailor arrived seeking shelter. Undaunted by the grim tale of the property, the sailor insisted on staying behind to face the mysterious trow (troll or fairy). When midnight arrived and the violent manifestation began, the sailor confronted the creature with a great axe. He pursued the trow and hurled the weapon at it, striking the entity before the creature fled, with the heavy axe flying all the way to the head of the Mid Yell voe. The next morning, the household was spared its usual death, and the sailor later revealed that he had struck the creature, with the axe head recovered covered in blubber.

#location
Windhouse is a ruined estate located on a hillside in Mid Yell on the island of Yell in the Shetland Islands, Scotland.

#upstream-source
Collected from oral tradition by fieldworkers (including Calum Iain Maclean), as told by narrator Brucie Henderson who heard it from Lawrence Williamson.

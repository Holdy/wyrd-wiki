#title
The Wet Jacket

#summary
A bus driver picks up a mysterious hitchhiker on a dark road between Dollar and Tillicoultry.

#details
Joanne Dowd tells the tale of a bus driver who, one stormy night, picked up a hitchhiker on the road between Dollar and Tillicoultry. The hitchhiker was dressed in a wet jacket and asked to be taken to Tait's Tomb. Despite the driver's attempts to dissuade him from going out in such terrible weather, the hitchhiker insisted. When they arrived at the tomb, the hitchhiker got off the bus and disappeared into the night. The next day, the driver returned to the spot and found a wet jacket hanging on the tomb, with no sign of its owner.

#location
Tait's Tomb is located on the road between Dollar and Tillicoultry in Clackmannanshire.

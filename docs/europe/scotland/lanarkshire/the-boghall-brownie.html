The Boghall Brownie

Summary

After being mistakenly offered warm blankets during a harsh winter, a loyal farm brownie curses the garments and abandons the homestead forever, leading to its permanent decline in prosperity.

Details

At Boghall Farm, a diligent brownie worked tirelessly every night, cleaning and tending to the property while the household slept, asking for nothing more than a bowl of porridge and fresh milk. One exceptionally bitter winter, the well-meaning mistress of the farm placed warm blankets on his straw bed in the barn out of concern for his comfort. Offended by this gift—as providing clothing or bedding banishes brownies—the spirit recited a rhyming verse leaving his curse upon the blankets and departed instantaneously. Following his departure, the farm never recovered its former happiness or fortune.

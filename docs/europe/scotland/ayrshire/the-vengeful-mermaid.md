#title
The Vengeful Mermaid

#summary
A mermaid curses a family after their destruction of her favorite rock, leading to the extinction of the family line.

#details
Knockdolian Castle, located on the Ayrshire coast, was once home to a family whose heir was born amidst great joy. However, the newborn's cries disturbed by a mermaid singing from a large black rock near the shore. The Lady of the house ordered the rock destroyed, hoping to end the disturbances. Enraged, the mermaid cursed the family, vowing that there would never be another heir to Knockdolian. Tragedy struck as subsequent children were stillborn or died shortly after birth, and eventually, the Lord and Lady perished, leaving no heirs. The castle fell into ruins, standing as a testament to the mermaid's vengeful curse.

#location
Knockdolian Castle is located on the Ayrshire coast, south of Girvan.

The Ghost of Hugh Ross

Summary

The ghost of a wealthy landowner is said to haunt his former room, throwing legal papers around in anger.

Details

Hugh Ross was a wealthy man who had made his fortune through the use of slaves in the West Indies. He purchased many estates in his native county but was not popular due to his wealth and progressive nature. Before his death, he was engaged in a bitter lawsuit and died amid his legal papers. After his death, no one would venture near his room for a time because bundles of legal papers were being tossed about the room by an invisible hand. Many credible witnesses declared that the papers were thrown about as if by an angry man, furious at having mislaid something. It was later discovered that an important deed on which Ross relied could not be found.

    MacLeod's Tears

    Summary

    An elegantly dressed gentleman who was secretly the Devil promised to restore the ruined fortune of Ardvreck Castle in exchange for the hand of the MacLeod chief's daughter in marriage, leading to a fateful wedding celebration and her everlasting grief. Whenever a thunderstorm rolls over the loch, her ghostly white figure is seen weeping by the stony beach and the MacLeod burial vault drips with perpetual tears, a drop of which brings death to anyone it strikes.

    Details

    In the days of the MacLeods of Assynt, a mysterious, elegantly dressed gentleman arrived at Ardvreck Castle and offered to make the crumbling fortress grander than Dunvegan in exchange for the MacLeod chief's soul. When the chief refused, the stranger instead proposed marriage to the chief's beautiful young daughter. The marriage took place, but as the couple sailed away into the dark on a stormy night, a violent tempest struck and they vanished forever. The chief received none of the promised fortune and his lineage eventually expired. Ever since, a white-clad female figure has haunted the shores of Loch Assynt, weeping on stormy nights, while at the ancient burial vault in Inchnadamph, a mysterious, perpetual drip known as 'MacLeod's Tears' falls from the stone—and legend holds that anyone upon whom a drop falls will soon meet their death.

The Ghostly Woman of Newhouse

Summary

A woman dressed in black with a large hat is seen by two girls and later by their cousins on the same stretch of road near Newhouse, Dounby.

Details

Johina Leith, nee Kirkness, recalled an eerie encounter when she was about nine years old. She and her sister Mary were playing near their family home in Newhouse, Dounby. Suddenly, they saw a woman dressed in black with a large hat adorned with flowers. The woman crossed the road into a field and vanished as abruptly as she had appeared. Years later, Johina's cousins, Charlotte Clouston and Maggie Kirkness, had a similar experience on the same stretch of road. Maggie realized she had forgotten her gloves and ran back to the house. When she came out, she saw a figure ahead and called out, but there was no reply, and the figure vanished. Decades later, this story was shared with a school friend of Johina's daughter Stephanie, whose grandfather had a similar encounter while driving along the same road.

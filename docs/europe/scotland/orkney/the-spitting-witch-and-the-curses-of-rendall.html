The Spitting Witch and the Curses of Rendall

Summary

An elderly Orkney woman accused of witchcraft inflicts madness upon a doubting farmer and a laird's horse before facing her execution with a final prophetic promise.

Details

Living at the isolated cottage of Rigga on the shores of Wass Wick in Rendall, Jonet Rendall cultivated a reputation as a cunning woman who consulted on personal troubles to earn her keep. When a local farmer named Andrew accused her of stealing the profit of his cow's milk, Jonet warned he would regret it, resulting in Andrew falling violently mad three days later until Jonet arrived to cure him with a triple spit over her shoulder. Later, when the wealthy laird Gilbert Sandie refused her silver, his prize horse was struck with madness ("wood"). Eventually arrested and brought to trial in 1629, Jonet met her fate at Gallow Ha in Kirkwall with serene calm, leaving the laird with a parting prophecy that her little friend from beyond would soon visit him too.

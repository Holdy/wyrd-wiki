The Victorian Ghost of Annan Road

Summary

A spectral old lady in Victorian attire haunts a road near Annan, seen by multiple witnesses.

Details

The A75, known as Scotland's Ghost Road, is infamous for its eerie encounters. One such apparition is the Victorian Ghost of Annan Road, a spectral old lady dressed in Victorian attire who has been sighted by numerous witnesses. This ghostly figure adds to the road's chilling reputation, where history and mystery intertwine.

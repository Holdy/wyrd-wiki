The Exorcism at the Swiss Guardhouse

Summary

During Pope Benedict XVI's pontificate, an elderly cardinal performed an emergency exorcism on a possessed young woman inside the guardhouse of the Swiss Guards at Vatican City.

Details

A young Italian woman attended a papal Mass held by Pope Benedict XVI in St. Peter's Square. During the gathering, she was suddenly overtaken by a demonic presence, manifesting visible signs of possession and causing great distress among her companions. As they sought assistance near the colonnade, Cardinal Ivan Dias happened to approach. Recognizing the spiritual emergency, he asked for a private space to pray for her soul, and the young guards offered the nearby guardhouse of the Swiss Guards. Inside the secure guardhouse, Cardinal Dias conducted intense prayers of deliverance over the course of twenty minutes, after which the young woman was seemingly freed from the entity and calmly departed.

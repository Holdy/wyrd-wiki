#title
The Ghost Girl on the Arrabassada Road

#summary
A ghostly young woman in a white dress is frequently picked up by drivers on a treacherous mountain pass before vanishing into thin air once the danger has passed.

#details
Drivers navigating the winding Arrabassada Road in Barcelona have long reported encountering a young female hitchhiker wearing a white dress near the sharp bend known as *Revolt de la Paella*. After being welcomed into a passing car, she grows noticeably anxious as the vehicle nears the dangerous curve, urgently warning the driver to slow down to avoid a crash. Once the bend is safely negotiated, she inexplicably vanishes from the moving vehicle. According to local legend, she is the spirit of a tragic traffic accident victim who spends her afterlife trying to prevent others from sharing her fatal fate.

#location
The haunting takes place at the *Revolt de la Paella* sharp bend on the Carretera de l'Arrabassada (BP-1417) connecting Barcelona and Sant Cugat del Vallès in Spain.

The Curse of Ghost Alley

Summary

After confessing on her deathbed to murdering her sister out of jealousy over a sailor, a woman was cursed by her husband for all eternity. Her tormented spirit now haunts the narrow Amsterdam alley where the crime took place.

Details

In 18th-century Amsterdam, two sisters named Helena and Dina lived with their father, a tanner on the lane known as Spooksteeg. Both sisters fell in love with the same handsome sailor, but when Dina announced her impending marriage to him, a jealous Helena pushed her down the stairs into the tannery cellar, resulting in her death. Helena subsequently married the sailor in Dina's place. Years later, on her deathbed in 1753, a guilt-ridden Helena finally confessed to the murder. Upon hearing the truth, her husband cursed her soul for all eternity, preventing her spirit from ever finding rest. To this day, ghostly sightings and harrowing screams are reported in the narrow alleyway.

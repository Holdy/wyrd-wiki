#title
The Jealous Sister of Spooksteeg

#summary
Consumed by jealousy, a woman murdered her sister to steal her lover and now eternally haunts the narrow Amsterdam alleyway where her crime took place.

#details
In 18th-century Amsterdam, a tanner named Schaap lived with his two daughters, Helena and Dina. When a visiting sailor named Wouter fell in love with the kind-hearted Dina instead of the passionate Helena, jealousy consumed Helena. She pushed Dina down the cellar stairs and ultimately beat her to death, making it look like an accident. Wouter subsequently married Helena, unaware of the truth, until Helena confessed on her deathbed in 1753 and was abandoned in disgust by her husband. Ever since her death, people walking through Spooksteeg—an alleyway just off the Zeedijk—have reported encountering her sorrowful, lingering spirit.

#location
Spooksteeg is a historic alleyway located in the center of Amsterdam, Netherlands, connecting the Zeedijk to the Warmoesstraat.

The Possessed Wolf of Auvinyà

Summary

After a village leader kills a monstrous wolf terrorizing an Andorran valley, he becomes gravely ill and possessed by the vengeful spirit of a dead warlord that had inhabited the beast.

Details

In ancient times, the southern border pass of Andorra was guarded by a mysterious figure known as the White Lady. Years later, a ruthless warlord who had long terrorized the region vanished upstream along the Valira river. Shortly after his disappearance, a ferocious, unnatural wolf began slaughtering livestock across the area. A prominent local leader organized a hunt, cornering and killing the beast, which uttered strange, unintelligible words with its dying breath. Days later, the leader fell gravely ill, suffering from terrifying nighttime deliriums until the White Lady appeared to him. She revealed that the body of the wolf had been taken over and animated by the soul of the missing warlord. Though the harrowing affliction ultimately claimed the leader's life, he died knowing his sacrifice had freed the valleys from the entity.

The Phantom of Via Bagnera

Summary

The restless spirit of Italy's first serial killer is said to haunt a narrow Milanese street where he once committed his gruesome murders. Visitors walking down this historic alleyway can experience sudden, chilling drops in temperature as his phantom presence paces the pavement.

Details

During the 19th century, Antonio Boggia—historically recognized as Italy's first serial killer, known as the "Monster of Milan"—operated out of a home and basement situated on the narrow street of Via Bagnera, where he brutally murdered his victims. Following his arrest and subsequent execution, his dark deeds left a permanent mark on the alleyway. Today, local lore maintains that Boggia's restless spirit continues to roam the pavement, making the street feel unnaturally and inexplicably cold to anyone passing through where his crimes took place.

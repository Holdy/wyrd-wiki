The Demonic Fall of St. Olaf's Builder

Summary

During the construction of St. Olaf's Church in st, a mysterious master builder plunged to his death from the spire, after which a snake and a toad crawled from his mouth as proof of his diabolical possession.

Details

In the 16th century, the city of Tallinn sought to build a massive spire on St. Olaf's Church, but the project became plagued by tragedy as seven successive master builders fell to their deaths, leading locals to believe the site was cursed. Eventually, a mysterious stranger named Olav stepped forward and promised to complete the towering structure for a thousand pieces of gold. Olav successfully finished the perilous work, but as he hammered the final nail into the spire and prepared to attach the golden rooster, he slipped and plummeted to the ground. When his body struck the earth before the horrified crowd, a snake and a toad crawled out of his mouth, revealing that he had been possessed and driven by dark, diabolical forces.

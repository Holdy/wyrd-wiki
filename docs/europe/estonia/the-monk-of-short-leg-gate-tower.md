#title
The Monk of Short-Leg Gate Tower

#summary
A violent phantom monk, believed to be a 13th-century executioner's apprentice, haunts a medieval gate tower and street in Tallinn. He frequently pulls pranks, hides items, and physically interacts with the living.

#details
In 1233, a young novice named Justinius—who had previously worked as an executioner's apprentice—was killed before he could repent for his past sins. Ever since, his dark, sometimes bloodied apparition has been witnessed roaming the medieval lane of Lühike jalg and the Short-Leg Gate Tower. Known for his mischievous and aggressive behavior, the spirit has been reported breaking items, hiding objects, and even pulling toes or kicking the ribs of unsuspecting residents and visitors in the area.

#location
The story takes place at the Short-Leg Gate Tower on Lühike jalg street in the Old Town of Tallinn, Estonia.

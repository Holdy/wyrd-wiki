#title
The Phantom Signal of Sofia Perovskaya

#summary
The ghostly apparition of the 19th-century revolutionary Sofia Perovskaya haunts a St. Petersburg bridge on the anniversary of Tsar Alexander II's assassination. Anyone unfortunate enough to witness her waving her white handkerchief is doomed to fall into the canal and perish.

#details
Sofia Perovskaya was a key member of the Narodnaya Volya revolutionary group that orchestrated the 1881 assassination of Emperor Alexander II, during which she signaled the attackers by waving a white handkerchief. Following her subsequent trial and execution by hanging, local folklore emerged that her spirit returns annually on the night of March 1st. She is said to appear on the bridge near the Church of the Savior on the Blood, performing her fatal signal once more, which lures unlucky witnesses to their watery deaths in the canal below.

#location
The bridge adjacent to the Church of the Savior on the Blood spanning the Griboyedov Canal in St. Petersburg, Russia.

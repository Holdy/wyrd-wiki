    Weeping icon of Máriapócs

    Summary

    A village icon wept for weeks in 1696 and was taken by the Emperor to St Stephen's Cathedral, Vienna, where it still hangs. The copy left behind at Máriapócs is said to have wept in 1715 and 1905. Hungary's main pilgrimage site.

#title
The Cursed Stretch of the A3 Highway

#summary
A notorious segment of the A3 motorway in Croatia is plagued by ghostly phenomena and a devastating number of fatal crashes stemming from disturbed graves. Drivers navigating this road at night frequently experience eerie hallucinations and supernatural encounters.

#details
The section of the A3 motorway running between Staro Petrovo Selo and Nova Gradiška in Croatia has gained a dark reputation as one of the most haunted stretches of highway in Europe. According to local folklore, the road's curse originated when construction workers used stone and materials from old tombstones and graves during its paving, combined with youths mocking the resting places at the time. This desecration is believed to have angered dark forces and trapped restless spirits, resulting in a staggering frequency of unexplained accidents, hallucinations among drivers, and ghostly apparitions. Over the years, the deadly stretch has claimed dozens of lives, including several prominent figures who perished in eerily similar circumstances just meters apart on the exact same section of the road.

#location
The haunted stretch of the A3 motorway is located in Croatia between the towns of Staro Petrovo Selo and Nova Gradiška.

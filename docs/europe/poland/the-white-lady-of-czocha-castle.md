#title
The White Lady of Czocha Castle

#summary
After betraying her family to enemy forces in the 15th century, a woman named Gertrude was cursed by her brother to wander the halls of Czocha Castle for eternity, barred even from the gates of hell.

#details
In the 15th century, Czocha Castle was home to a brother and sister named Gertrude. Following a bitter dispute between the siblings, Gertrude sought ruthless revenge by conspiring with the invading Hussites, betraying her family and attempting to help the enemy conquer the stronghold. Her plot ultimately failed, and her furious brother ordered her execution. Before she was beheaded, he placed a powerful curse upon her soul, condemning her to roam the castle grounds forever. Because of this hex, Gertrude cannot even enter the gates of hell, leaving her spirit endlessly trapped within the fortress. Visitors and staff at the historic castle have reported encountering her apparition—known as the White Lady—wandering the corridors and rooms of the estate.

#location
Czocha Castle is located in the village of Sucha, near Leśniańskie Lake in southwestern Poland.

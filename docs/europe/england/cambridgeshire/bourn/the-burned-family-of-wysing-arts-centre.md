#title
The Burned Family of Wysing Arts Centre
#summary
A former homeowner haunts the Wysing Arts Centre following a historic fire that killed an entire family.
#details
Once a private home before becoming an art museum, the Wysing Arts Centre is said to be inhabited by one of its former homeowners. Legend notes that the original house burned down and killed the entire family.
#location

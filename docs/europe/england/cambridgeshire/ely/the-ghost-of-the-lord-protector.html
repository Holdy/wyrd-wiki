    The Ghost of the Lord Protector

    Summary

    The ghost of Oliver Cromwell is said to haunt his old bedroom in Ely as well as Ghost Hill near Murrow.

    Details

    Oliver Cromwell, the former Lord Protector of England, is said to have a restless spirit that lingers in the Fens, perhaps because the final resting place of his body remains a mystery. His ghost is reported to appear in his old bedroom at Ely, and he has also been spotted at Ghost Hill near Murrow, a hamlet close to Wisbech.

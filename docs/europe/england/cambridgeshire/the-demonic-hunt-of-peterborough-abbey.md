#title
The Demonic Hunt of Peterborough Abbey
#summary
In 1127, witnesses across the country saw and heard black huntsmen riding black horses and accompanied by jet-black hounds with saucer-like eyes.
#details
According to the 1127 Peterborough Chronicle, a version of the Anglo-Saxon Chronicle, many people saw and heard a supernatural hunting party immediately following Abbot Henry of Poitou's arrival at Peterborough Abbey. The huntsmen were described as black, huge, and hideous, riding black horses and he-goats, and accompanied by jet-black hounds with glowing eyes like saucers.

    The Glowing-Eyed Hound of Wisbech

    Summary

    During the 1980s, a woman and her son were paced by a massive black dog with glowing yellow eyes in Wisbech.

    Details

    In the 1980s, a woman walking with her young son encountered a large black dog featuring glowing yellow eyes in Wisbech. The spectral beast kept pace beside them before disappearing without a trace.

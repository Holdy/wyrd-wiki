#title
The Spectral Hound of Littleport

#summary
Black Shuck is a legendary hellhound said to haunt the Fenland waterways, with origins linked to protecting a local girl and a tragic carriage accident.

#details
Black Shuck, a legendary black hellhound said to have inspired The Hound of the Baskervilles, is spotted around Littleport on the Great Ouse near Ely. One local legend says the beast haunts the town after being slain while protecting a local girl from a lustful friar, while another tale places the spectral dog along the A10 where its owner drove his carriage into a bog and drowned in the 1800s. The legend gained further attention when archaeologists discovered the remains of a seven-foot dog in the grounds of Leiston Abbey in Suffolk.

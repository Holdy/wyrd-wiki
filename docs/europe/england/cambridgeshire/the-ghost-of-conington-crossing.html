The Ghost of Conington Crossing

Summary

Conington Crossing in Cambridgeshire is believed to be haunted by the ghost of a colonel and his dog, who died in a train accident in the 1940s. The crossing has been the site of numerous accidents and paranormal activity.

Details

The tale of Conington Crossing near Peterborough is dubbed by natives as 'Cambridgeshire's crossing of death'. According to legend, the crossing is haunted by the ghost of a colonel and his dog who died after an accident in the 1940s. The Colonel was heading home with his friend Mr Percival, when tragedy struck. The colonel's friend got out of the car to open the gates but while crossing, the 4pm train to London struck his Chrysler and he and his dog died instantly.

Since then, people have reported lots of paranormal activity at the crossing, with most people attributing it to one of the aforementioned calamities. Voices have been heard - sometimes yelling or crying and some people have reported seeing a phantom car trying to use the crossing before disappearing into thin air. According to the site, there have also been reports of the gates opening and closing seemingly by themselves.

    The Lantern Man of Wicken Fen

    Summary

    The Lantern Man is a mysterious spectral light in the Fens that lures unwary travelers off safe paths and into dangerous bogs.

    Details

    The Lantern Man is a chilling figure of Fenland folklore linked with Wicken Fen, appearing as a mysterious spectral light that drifts across the marshland. While science suggests the phenomenon may be caused by pockets of burning phosphorous gas, folklore paints a darker picture of the light intentionally luring unwary travelers from the safety of paths into dangerous bogs and waters.

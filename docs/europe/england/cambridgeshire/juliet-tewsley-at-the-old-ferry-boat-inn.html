Juliet Tewsley at the Old Ferry Boat Inn

Summary

Juliet Tewsley, who hanged herself after a broken heart in 1050, returns to the Old Ferry Boat Inn every March 17th.

Details

A woman named Juliet Tewsley hanged herself on March 17th, 1050, after suffering from a badly broken heart. According to legend, her spirit returns to the Old Ferry Boat Inn every year on March 17th on the eve of her suicide.

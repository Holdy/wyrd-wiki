    The London Stone

    Summary

    An ancient limestone relic embedded in Cannon Street holds the fate of the capital, dictating that as long as it remains safe and undisturbed, London will prosper. Removing or damaging this legendary artifact is believed to bring total disaster upon the city.

    Details

    Believed by some to be a Roman relic or a sacred foundation marker, the London Stone has occupied a place of deep-seated mystery and folklore in the capital for centuries. According to legend, the destiny of London is inextricably bound to the physical safety of the stone. If it were ever to be destroyed or removed from its protective niche, a catastrophic curse would fall, spelling the ruin and downfall of the entire city. Because of this enduring superstition, the stone is treated with immense reverence and caution whenever construction takes place around it.

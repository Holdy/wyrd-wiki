    The Black Lady of Bradley Wood

    Summary

    A solemn-looking woman known as "The Black Lady" is seen and sometimes captured on camera at Bradley Wood.

    Details

    Bradley Wood is reputed to be haunted by a solemn-looking woman referred to as "The Black Lady," who is reported to occasionally show up on camera.

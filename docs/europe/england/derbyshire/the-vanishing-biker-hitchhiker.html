The Vanishing Biker Hitchhiker

Summary

A courting couple on a motorcycle offered a lift to a female motorcyclist near Hathersage, only to discover she vanished during the journey and had died days earlier.

Details

One winter's evening, a young couple riding a motorcycle with a sidecar near the Fox House inn close to Hathersage stopped to give a lift to a young woman dressed in motorcycling leathers and a crash helmet who was thumbing for a ride. She gave an address in Sheffield and rode pillion as they continued their journey. Upon reaching the city boundary, the driver looked back and found she had completely vanished without a trace. Troubled, they visited the address she had provided, where a grieving mother tearfully explained that her daughter had recently been killed in a motorcycling accident on that very stretch of road and buried just days prior.

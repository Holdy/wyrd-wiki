#title
Sally in the Woods

#summary
The road near Bath, known as Sally in the Wood, is believed to be haunted by the ghost of a murdered girl named Sally. The area is notorious for eerie occurrences and an unsettling atmosphere.

#details
Sally in the Wood is a rural lane located just outside Bath, stretching between Bathford and Farleigh Wick. It is widely regarded as one of the most haunted locations in the world. The road's eerie reputation stems from tales of a murdered young girl whose spirit is said to haunt the area, causing crashes and other supernatural events.

One of the most chilling aspects of this legend is that birds in the surrounding trees are reportedly unable to sing, adding to the unsettling atmosphere. Some people refuse to travel along the road at night due to these ghostly tales. Various theories exist about the origins of Sally's name, including stories of a murdered girl, a spooky crash involving a young couple, and connections to the English Civil War.

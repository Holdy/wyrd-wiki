The Permanently Damp Floor Stain at Husbands Bosworth Hall

Summary

A permanent, damp floor stain remains in Husbands Bosworth Hall, supposedly left by spilled communion wine or blood during a priest's escape from Cromwell's men.

Details

Elsewhere in Husbands Bosworth Hall, a stain on the floor remains permanently damp. It is allegedly there as a result of communion wine, or blood, being spilled when a priest escaped Cromwell's men.

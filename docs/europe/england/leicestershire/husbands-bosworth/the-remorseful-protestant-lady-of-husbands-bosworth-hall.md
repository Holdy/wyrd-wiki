#title
The Remorseful Protestant Lady of Husbands Bosworth Hall
#summary
The ghost of a Protestant lady prowls Husbands Bosworth Hall, her loud footsteps echoing as she suffers remorse for refusing a Catholic priest access to a dying servant.
#details
The ghost of a Protestant lady prowls the hall at Husbands Bosworth in Leicestershire, unable to settle. Her footsteps can be heard loud and clear as she wanders, suffering from remorse for not allowing a Catholic priest to attend a dying servant.

    Freda

    Summary

    Freda's skull is kept behind the bar of Ye Olde White Harte in Hull. If it is moved, its former owner is said to haunt the building until it is put back.

    Details

    Behind the bar of Ye Olde White Harte in Hull sits a skull known as Freda. The pub's tradition is that it must stay where it is: if it is moved, Freda returns to haunt the building until her skull is back in its place.

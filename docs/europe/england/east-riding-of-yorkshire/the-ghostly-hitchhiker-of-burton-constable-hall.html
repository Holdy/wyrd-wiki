The Ghostly Hitchhiker of Burton Constable Hall

Summary

A driver experiences a supernatural presence while driving to Burton Constable Hall, East Yorkshire's most haunted house.

Details

On a misty autumn evening, a woman drove through the isolated country roads towards Burton Constable Hall. She felt an eerie presence in her car, as if she was not alone. As she approached the hall, the feeling intensified, and she heard breathing and movement on the back seat. This was not the first time such an incident had occurred at Burton Constable Hall, known for its supernatural visitors.

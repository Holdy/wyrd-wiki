The Screaming Skull of Burton Agnes Hall

Summary

When a young woman's dying wish to have her skull kept in her newly built family manor was ignored, a violent curse of supernatural disturbances plagued the house until her remains were brought inside.

Details

In the 17th century, Anne Griffith, the youngest daughter of the family building Burton Agnes Hall, was brutally attacked and robbed by a highwayman while visiting friends in nearby Harpham. Gravely injured, she was brought back home where she tragically died from her wounds. Before passing away, she made a specific dying request: that her skull be removed after her death and kept inside the newly completed hall so she could forever witness its completion. Horrified by this morbid wish, her family ignored her and buried her intact in the local churchyard. Immediately, a terrifying curse fell upon the manor; relentless poltergeist activity—including slamming doors, shattering furniture, and mournful groans echoing through the corridors—made sleep impossible for the household. Realising their mistake, the family exhumed her body and retrieved her skull into the hall, causing the haunting to cease instantly. Thereafter, whenever anyone attempted to remove or banish the skull, the curse would violently reawaken with shaking walls and bolting horses until the skull was safely returned to the building.

#title
Bran's head

#summary
The dying Bran the Blessed ordered his followers to cut off his head and, after years of feasting with it, bury it on the White Hill in London facing France. So long as it lay undisturbed Britain would be safe from invasion, until King Arthur dug it up, choosing to hold the island by his own prowess.

#details
Wounded in the foot by a poisoned arrow in Ireland, Bran the Blessed told the seven survivors of his party to cut off his head and take it back to Wales. At Harlech the head would talk with them over dinner as entertainingly as ever, and at Gwales they would feast with it for as long as they left shut a door that looked towards Cornwall. Once that door was opened, they were to carry the head to London and bury it on the White Hill, where the Tower now stands, with its face towards France. So long as it lay there undisturbed, Britain would have nothing to fear from foreign invasion. In a fit of vainglory, King Arthur dug it up, declaring that he would hold the island by his own prowess alone.

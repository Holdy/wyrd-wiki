Tunnel Phantoms of Fort Amherst

Summary

Fort Amherst features heavy fog in photos, shadows, disembodied voices, child handprints, and a ghost soldier.

Details

It is common to capture heavy fog in photographs at Fort Amherst, where visitors have seen shadows, full-bodied apparitions, and heard strange voices. Those walking through the tunnel occasionally wind up with a child's handprint on them, and a ghost soldier is frequently seen by regulars.

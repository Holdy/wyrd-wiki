#title
Lady Blanche de Warenne's Fatal Fall at Rochester Castle
#summary
Lady Blanche de Warenne's accidental death during a 1264 siege at Rochester Castle relives through recurring falls and footsteps.
#details
Lady Blanche de Warenne was killed during a 1264 siege when an arrow intended for her husband bounced off his breastplate and struck her instead, causing her to fall from the keep. Some witnesses claim to see her fatal fall repeating itself, while others hear disembodied footsteps within the castle.

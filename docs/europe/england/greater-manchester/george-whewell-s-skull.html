    George Whewell's skull

    Summary

    An old brown skull behind the bar of the Pack Horse in Affetside is said to be that of George Whowell, the local headsman who executed James Stanley, 7th Earl of Derby. Rumour has it that the skull cannot be moved, or it will scream.

    Details

    The Pack Horse at Affetside was built in 1443, and an old brown skull sits behind its bar. It is said to belong to George Whowell, who during the English Civil War saw his family murdered in the Bolton Massacre by Royalist soldiers led by James Stanley, 7th Earl of Derby. Stanley was later captured by Cromwell's soldiers and sentenced to death for supporting Charles II, and the local headsman who executed him was George Whowell. How Whowell's skull came to be at the pub is unknown, though he may have been connected to the Butterworths, a local farming family. Rumour has it that the skull cannot be moved, or it will scream.

#title
The Screaming Skull of Wardley Hall

#summary
A centuries-old skull housed within a medieval manor brings fierce, destructive storms and supernatural torment if ever it is removed or buried elsewhere. Generations of occupants learned that the relic refuses to stay away from the hall.

#details
Wardley Hall, a medieval manor house in Greater Manchester, became known for centuries as the 'House of the Skull' due to a grisly relic kept within its walls—traditionally believed to be the skull of the martyred Catholic priest Father Ambrose Barlow. According to local folklore, the skull possesses a powerful and unyielding will. Whenever previous inhabitants attempted to permanently bury it, throw it into the moat, or banish it from the building, the action triggered terrifying, raging storms, howling noises, and unceasing distress across the estate. The paranormal turmoil would only cease when the skull was safely returned to its niche inside the hall, proving that it could never be successfully moved or separated from its home without bringing a curse upon the place.

#location
Wardley Hall is located in Wardley, Worsley, within the City of Salford, Greater Manchester.

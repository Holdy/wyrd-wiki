The Devil's Highway

Summary

The A666 in Bolton, known as 'The Devil’s Highway,' is reputed to be haunted by shadowy figures and has a high accident rate.

Details

The A666 in Bolton, dubbed "The Devil’s Highway" due to its ominous number 666, is notorious for sightings of ghostly apparitions. Drivers have reported seeing shadowy shapes and figures that mysteriously appear and vanish. One notable account involves a hunchbacked figure limping along the road at night. Another story tells of a taxi driver who saw the ghost of an elderly woman with long hair sitting in the back of his cab.

The road also has an unusually high accident rate, particularly between Egerton and Darwen, leading some to speculate about supernatural influences. However, improvements such as reduced speed limits and better safety measures have significantly decreased accidents, suggesting that the high accident rate may be due to human error rather than paranormal activity.

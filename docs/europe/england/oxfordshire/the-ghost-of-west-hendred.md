#title
The Ghost of West Hendred

#summary
Two sisters driving on the A417 near West Hendred encounter a ghostly figure who vanishes before impact.

#details
In late 1964, Margaret Prior and Marcia Colling-Hill were driving at night on the A417 near West Hendred when a figure suddenly darted out in front of their car. They slammed on the brakes but felt no impact. When they looked around, there was no sign of the figure. Both sisters described the apparition as a man wearing an overcoat and cap, leading them to believe it was supernatural. The incident occurred near the site where an elderly man had reportedly been killed three years prior, raising questions about whether they witnessed a replay of that tragic event.

#location
The A417 road near West Hendred, Oxfordshire

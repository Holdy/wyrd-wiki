Betty's Haunting on Betty Haunt Lane

Summary

An innkeeper's daughter murdered by smugglers 200 years ago is said to manifest as a spectral figure walking along Betty Haunt Lane in Newport.

Details

Local legend states that Betty, an innkeeper's daughter, was killed by smugglers in the 18th century near the old Newport inn. Her ghost is reportedly seen walking the lane at dusk, wearing a 19th-century dress, with a distressed expression. The lane's name directly references her tragic death, with residents claiming to hear faint sobbing on foggy evenings.

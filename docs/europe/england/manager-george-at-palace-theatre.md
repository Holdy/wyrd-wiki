#title
Manager George at Palace Theatre
#summary
Former manager George, who hanged himself over financial ruin, haunts the Palace Theatre with tobacco smells and shoulder touches.
#details
Former theater manager George hanged himself after discovering he was in dire financial straits. He is reported at the Palace Theatre, accompanied by the smell of tobacco, the sensation of someone touching your shoulder, and occasional sightings of a woman in white.

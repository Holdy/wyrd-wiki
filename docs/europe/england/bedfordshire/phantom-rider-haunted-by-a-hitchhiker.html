    Phantom Rider: Haunted by a Hitchhiker

    Summary

    A young man in Bedfordshire picked up a hitchhiker on Station Road, Stanbridge, who vanished from his car. The encounter left him terrified and convinced he had encountered a ghost.

    Details

    In October 1979, Roy Fulton, a 26-year-old carpet fitter from Dunstable, was driving home late at night after a pub darts match in Leighton Buzzard. As he traveled along Station Road on the outskirts of Stanbridge, he noticed a young man standing by the roadside, thumbing for a ride. Fulton decided to offer him a lift. The hitchhiker, dressed casually in dark trousers and a jumper with a white-collared shirt, got into the car but remained silent throughout the journey.

    Fulton attempted to engage the hitchhiker in conversation by offering him a cigarette. When he turned to hand over the packet, he discovered that the passenger had vanished without a trace. Panicked, Fulton drove to his local pub, The Glider in Lowther Road, Dunstable, where he recounted his terrifying experience to the landlord and other patrons. He was haunted by the thought that the eerie pale-faced youth might follow him home.

    Fulton's story gained attention when he was interviewed by writer Michael Goss and later featured in the television documentary series Arthur C. Clarke’s World of Strange Powers. His account remained consistent, detailing how one night in October 1979, he took a ghost for a ride.

    The Coombe Hill Sea Serpent

    Summary

    A monstrous sea serpent terrorized the residents of Coombe Hill until a brave local man named Tom Smith won its trust and slew it. In gratitude, the village rewarded him with limitless free beer.

    Details

    Long ago, a massive sea monster—resembling a knucker dragon—swam up the River Severn and made its lair on the riverbank at Coombe Hill. Initially content to prey on sheep and poultry, the creature soon grew bolder, terrorizing the local community by devouring children and milkmaids. Fearing for their lives, many villagers fled the area. A local lad named Tom Smith devised a daring plan to stop the beast. He began bringing offerings of food, placing them near the monster's resting place and watching it eat. Over time, the serpent grew accustomed to Tom and learned to trust him entirely, eventually allowing him to feed it by hand. On one such occasion, while offering the creature a large marrowbone, Tom swiftly drew an axe and brought it down on the monster's head, killing it instantly. As a reward for ridding the area of the beast, Tom was granted free ale for life at the local village pub.

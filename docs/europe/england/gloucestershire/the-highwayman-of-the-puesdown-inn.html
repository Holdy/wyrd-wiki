    The Highwayman of the Puesdown Inn

    Summary

    The historic Puesdown Inn in Compton Abdale is said to be haunted by a highwayman known as "The Duke," who staggered to the inn after being shot nearby and hammered on the door before dying on the threshold.

    Details

    Dating back to the 13th century, the ancient Puesdown Inn stands in an isolated position on the top of the Cotswolds along the A40 road in Compton Abdale. Historically known as a remote coaching inn frequented by highwaymen, the building became the center of local ghost lore. According to legend, a highwayman nicknamed "The Duke" was shot during a robbery nearby. Fatally wounded, he managed to make his way to the inn, hammering desperately on the door three times before collapsing and dying just as he crossed the threshold. Following his death, visitors and staff over the centuries have reported unexplained nocturnal phenomena, including ghostly footsteps walking across the lounge and upstairs rooms, a silent horseman riding away into the night, and the chilling sound of three heavy knocks echoing against the door.

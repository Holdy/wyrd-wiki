#title
The Giant of Oxenton Hill and the Tibblestone

#summary
A furious giant attempting to hurl a massive boulder toward the River Severn slipped on the slopes of Oxenton Hill, causing the stone to tumble down and land permanently at the Teddington Hands crossroads.

#details
According to local Gloucestershire legend, a giant standing near the slopes of Oxenton Hill and Dixton Hill once decided to hurl a massive stone across the countryside towards the Severn at Tewkesbury. As he heaved the heavy boulder, his foot slipped on the hillside, leaving a permanent mark in the earth. Thrown off balance, the giant failed to reach his target, and the great stone plummeted to the ground far short of the river, coming to rest at the road junction known as Teddington Hands. The stone, known historically as the Tibblestone, remains there to this day.

#location
The Tibblestone is located at the Teddington Hands crossroads (Grid Reference SO 96333384) just north of Oxenton Hill in Gloucestershire.

    The Broad Stone of Tidenham

    Summary

    The Wibdon Broad Stone is a towering megalith said to have been hurled across the countryside from Tidenham Chase during a legendary stone-throwing contest between Jack O'Kent and the Devil.

    Details

    Standing over eight feet tall in the parish of Tidenham, the Wibdon Broad Stone is an ancient standing stone steeped in regional folklore. According to local legend, the monumental rock was thrown all the way from Tidenham Chase by the Devil during a contest of strength and prowess against the legendary folk hero Jack O'Kent. The Devil let fly with the massive boulder, which sailed across the valley and embedded itself firmly in the earth near Wibdon, overlooking the Severn levels. The stone has remained standing ever since as a physical monument to the supernatural feat, joining a cycle of tales where Jack O'Kent and the adversary compete across the borderlands of the Wye Valley.

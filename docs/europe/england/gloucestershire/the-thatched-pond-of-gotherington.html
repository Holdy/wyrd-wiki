    The Thatched Pond of Gotherington

    Summary

    According to traditional Gloucestershire folklore, the villagers of Gotherington once attempted to protect their waterfowl by building a thatched roof over the village pond so the ducks could swim in the dry.

    Details

    In regional folklore, Gotherington is featured among the communities associated with "local taunts" and simpleton tales, much like the legendary Wise Men of Gotham. According to this humorous tradition, the naive townsfolk of Gotherington worried about their ducks experiencing harsh weather, prompting them to construct a traditional thatched roof directly over the village pond. In their earnest foolishness, they believed this architectural marvel would allow the ducks to continue swimming while remaining completely dry.

#title
The Phantom Cortege of Cleeve Hill

#summary
While driving down from Cleeve Hill, a motorist witnessed a peculiar Victorian funeral procession traversing a field before it abruptly and impossibly vanished.

#details
A teacher driving down from Cleeve Hill in Gloucestershire noticed an unusual funeral procession making its way across a field adjacent to the road rather than using the proper thoroughfare. The cortege was accompanied by men dressed in black who appeared tipsy and were laughing loudly. Intrigued by the strange sight, she turned her car around to get a closer look, but by the time she returned, the entire procession had completely disappeared in an impossibly brief span of time. Local folklore notes that Victorian funeral parties sometimes took unconventional routes across fields to prevent restless spirits from finding their way back home, and mourners occasionally imbibed heavily, suggesting she had encountered a spectral time-slip overlooked by nearby St Peter's Church.

#location
The road descending from Cleeve Hill near Cheltenham, Gloucestershire, passing near St Peter's Church.

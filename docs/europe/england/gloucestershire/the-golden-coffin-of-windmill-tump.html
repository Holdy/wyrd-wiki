    The Golden Coffin of Windmill Tump

    Summary

    Local folklore surrounding the Windmill Tump burial mound near Rodmarton long maintained that the ancient prehistoric structure concealed a hidden coffin made of solid gold.

    Details

    For centuries, rural communities across the Cotswolds attributed wondrous origins to the prehistoric earthworks dotting their landscape. Rather than recognizing them as ancient Neolithic burial sites, local tradition often recast these stone monuments and barrows as the resting places of kings, giants, or immense wealth. In Rodmarton parish, the long barrow known as Windmill Tump became the subject of persistent local legend, which claimed that deep within its stone chambers lay a legendary gold coffin. Although archaeologists eventually excavated the site and uncovered ancient human remains rather than any glittering treasure, the belief in the golden relic persisted as a cherished piece of local lore.

    The Heart and Charger of Coberley Church

    Summary

    Following his death in the Crusades, the heart of medieval knight Sir Giles de Berkeley was returned to his ancestral Gloucestershire village for burial, while his faithful warhorse Lombard was laid to rest in the churchyard.

    Details

    Sir Giles de Berkeley, a 13th-century knight and landholder based in Coberley, fought in the Crusades. Although his body was buried at St Giles Priory in Little Malvern, Worcestershire, his heart was brought back to his family home. Inside St Giles' Church in Coberley, a 13th-century wall monument depicts Sir Giles holding a large heart over his shield. Local tradition and an inscribed plaque directly beneath the monument record that his cherished battle charger, Lombard, was buried in the churchyard alongside his master's memorial.

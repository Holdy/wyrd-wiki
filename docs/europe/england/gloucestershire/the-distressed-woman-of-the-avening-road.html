The Distressed Woman of the Avening Road

Summary

A motorist driving late at night near Minchinhampton Golf Club encountered a distressed woman attempting to flag him down on the road between Avening and Minchinhampton, only for her to vanish into thin air as he stopped to help.

Details

Local folklore long held that the road between Avening and Minchinhampton was haunted by the earthbound spirit of someone who had died by suicide. In November 2013, this local legend gained chilling credibility when a motorist experienced a strange encounter along that very stretch. Driving home from Malmesbury at around 1:00 AM on a clear, cold winter night under ambient moonlight, the driver approached the area near Minchinhampton Golf Club. Suddenly, he spotted a slim, middle-aged woman wearing only a dress—completely unsuited for the freezing weather—standing by the roadside and trying desperately to flag him down while looking profoundly distressed. Reacting immediately, he pulled his car over and rolled down his window to ask if she needed assistance. The moment he stopped, the woman vanished completely, leaving behind an empty road and a profound impression of the supernatural.

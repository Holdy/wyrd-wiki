    The Ghostly Boy of The Tudor Arms

    Summary

    A bitter eleven-year-old labourer from the Victorian era is reported to haunt The Tudor Arms in Slimbridge, appearing so angry that staff have refused to return to work after encountering him.

    Details

    Documented in local ghost lore, the canal-side pub known as The Tudor Arms in Slimbridge is said to be haunted by a young boy. Approximately eleven years of age, the apparition is seen wearing worn and dirty Victorian attire—complete with a hat, waistcoat, shirt, trousers, and hobnail boots resembling a young Victorian labourer. The landlord and staff have reported multiple sightings of the youth, whose fiercely bitter and angry expression has left workers so deeply shaken that some have refused to return to their jobs.

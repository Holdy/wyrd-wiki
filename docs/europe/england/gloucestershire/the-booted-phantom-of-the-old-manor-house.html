    The Booted Phantom of the Old Manor House

    Summary

    The Old Manor House in Hinton-on-the-Green was plagued by the heavy, invisible footsteps of a booted entity that caused such terror the owners were forced to demolish part of the building. The unseen presence regularly walked through the rooms and ascended the stairs, terrifying all who encountered it.

    Details

    According to late 19th-century accounts reported by the local clergyman, the Old Manor House in Hinton-on-the-Green was subjected to a persistent and deeply unsettling haunting. An invisible presence, characterized by the distinct sound of heavy boots, would enter the building, cross the hall, climb the stairs, and move through the upper levels. The phenomena became so unbearable and frightening to inhabitants that a portion of the historic structure ultimately had to be pulled down to deal with the disturbance.

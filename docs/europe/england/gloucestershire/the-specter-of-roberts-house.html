    The Specter of Roberts House

    Summary

    A 17th-century Cotswold manor house in Siddington is haunted by the tragic legacy of religious persecution, where residents and visitors have long reported chilling paranormal encounters.

    Details

    Located in Siddington near Cirencester, Roberts House is a historic 17th-century property originally purchased in 1618 by John Roberts, one of the founding figures of the Quaker movement. Because Roberts and his followers challenged the religious and political establishment of the era, they faced intense persecution. During a violent incident in July 1670, armed objectors stormed the house, forcing the Quakers upstairs and violently pushing them down the stairs, resulting in the fatal injury of a respected midwife named Elizabeth Hewlings. Over the centuries, subsequent residents and visitors have reported unexplained phenomena, phantom sounds, cold drafts, and an unsettling presence lingering in the upper rooms where the historical violence took place.

#title
The Bishop's Curse at Blockley Church

#summary
When a visiting bishop discovered a local priest neglecting his duties, his sudden curse struck the man dead before divine mercy brought him back to life as a reformed character.

#details
Around 1060 or 1070, St. Wulfstan, the Bishop of Worcester, journeyed to the settlement of Blockley to celebrate Easter Mass. Upon inspecting the local parish, he found the resident priest to be indolent, disrespectful, and utterly neglectful of his sacred responsibilities. Filled with righteous wrath, the bishop rebuked and cursed the priest. Instantly struck down by the force of the curse, the unfortunate man collapsed and appeared dead. Overcome with sudden remorse, Wulfstan knelt and prayed fervently for divine forgiveness, whereupon the priest miraculously revived, instantly transformed into a deeply pious and devoted servant of the church.

#location
St Peter & St Paul's Church, situated on a prominent knoll in the village of Blockley, Gloucestershire.

The Underground Chambers of Torbarrow Hill

Summary

Two gravel diggers near Cirencester discovered hidden underground chambers beneath a prehistoric mound guarded by a hostile automaton, only for the subterranean complex to collapse upon their return.

Details

According to an account published in 1685, two workmen digging for gravel near Cirencester sank four yards deep into Torbarrow Hill and discovered an entrance leading into the belly of the earth. Exploring inside, they found a series of rooms and tunnels containing furniture and carpets that crumbled to dust upon touch, along with numerous urns filled with ashes and ancient Roman coins. In the deepest chamber, they encountered the life-sized image of a man in armor holding a truncheon, illuminated by a burning glass lamp. When the men approached, the figure struck out, smashing the lamp and extinguishing the light amid hollow groans before the hill caved in and buried the entire subterranean discovery.

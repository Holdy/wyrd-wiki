#title
The Cooper's Hill Cheese-Rolling

#summary
An annual folk tradition in Brockworth, Gloucestershire, where competitors race down the steep slopes of Cooper's Hill in pursuit of a rolling wheel of Double Gloucester cheese.

#details
Every Spring Bank Holiday, participants and spectators gather at Cooper's Hill in Brockworth, Gloucestershire, for the world-famous Cheese-Rolling and Wake. With written records dating back to 1836 and roots believed to extend centuries earlier into pagan fertility rites or common land rights, the event is one of Britain's most enduring and eccentric traditional customs. A Master of Ceremonies initiates the race from the top of the hill by releasing a 7-to-9-pound wheel of Double Gloucester cheese wrapped in a wooden casing, giving it a one-second head start. Competitors then hurtle themselves down the 200-yard, 50-percent gradient slope, tumbling and sprinting after the fast-moving dairy. Though catching the cheese is rare due to its immense speed, the first person across the finish line at the bottom wins the cheese wheel as their prize.

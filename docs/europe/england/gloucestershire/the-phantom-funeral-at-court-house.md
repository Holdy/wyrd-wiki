#title
The Phantom Funeral at Court House

#summary
In May 1757, members of the Yate family residing at Court House witnessed a spectral funeral procession making its way up the avenue. Exactly one year later, the last male heir of the family passed away.

#details
On May 24, 1757, residents of Court House in Arlingham belonging to the Yate family observed a dark and eerie premonition. A ghostly funeral procession was seen making its way up the avenue toward the house. Exactly one year to the day after this phantom manifestation, the last male heir of the Yate family died, leading to the ancient estate passing out of the family's hands.

#location
Court House is situated in the village of Arlingham, Gloucestershire.

#title
The Phantom Landlady of Coaley

#summary
Regular patrons of a village pub in Coaley have repeatedly reported encounters with the spectral figure of a former landlady. Her ghostly apparition continues to frequent the historic building long after her passing.

#details
At the Fox and Hounds pub in the Gloucestershire village of Coaley, staff and regular customers have noted unexplained paranormal occurrences over the years. Most notably, multiple witnesses have reported seeing the ghostly apparition of a former landlady roaming the interior rooms. Though she appears as a familiar figure for brief moments, she vanishes into thin air when approached, leaving patrons startled by her lingering presence in the establishment she once managed.

#location
The Fox and Hounds pub, located in the village of Coaley, Gloucestershire.

The Spectres of Freezinghill Lane

Summary

Freezinghill Lane in Cold Ashton is marked by a distinctive line of beech trees known as the Caterpillar and is frequently haunted by ghostly apparitions of seventeenth-century soldiers from the English Civil War.

Details

Freezinghill Lane in the village of Cold Ashton, South Gloucestershire, sits atop one of the highest points between Bristol and Bath and features a landmark line of over fifty beech trees known locally as the Caterpillar. The road and its surroundings bear the bloody history of the English Civil War, specifically the 1643 Battle of Lansdown, where Sir William Waller's Parliamentary forces fought Sir Ralph Hopton's Royalists from dawn until dusk. Over the centuries, local residents, walkers, and travellers have reported eerie, unexplained sightings of seventeenth-century figures roaming the landscape under the cover of night or mist.

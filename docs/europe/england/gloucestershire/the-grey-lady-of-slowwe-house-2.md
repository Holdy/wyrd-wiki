#title
The Grey Lady of Slowwe House

#summary
While renovating the historic Gloucestershire property in the 1970s, a local family repeatedly encountered a phantom woman dressed in a long grey dress and bonnet who vanished into thin air.

#details
During the 1970s, the Lacey family moved into the rear wing of Slowwe House on Silver Street in Arlingham to undertake renovations on the neglected building. Almost immediately, they experienced unexplained phenomena, including lights turning on by themselves, windows operating without assistance, and wall hangings repeatedly falling down. Mrs Lacey subsequently caught sight of the apparition—a woman wearing a long grey dress and grey bonnet—walking across the courtyard before disappearing. The spectral figure was also encountered inside the house, notably on the landing, in the kitchen where she vanished right in front of Mrs Lacey, and in the guest bedroom where the family's young daughters awoke to find her standing silently.

#location
Slowwe House is a Grade II listed building located on Silver Street in the village of Arlingham, Gloucestershire.

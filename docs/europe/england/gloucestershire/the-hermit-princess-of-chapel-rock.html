The Hermit Princess of Chapel Rock

Summary

According to local legend, a 4th-century Welsh princess who retired as a hermit to a tidal islet off Beachley Point was brutally murdered by sea raiders in her cell.

Details

In the 4th or 5th century, a princess from Gwynedd named Tecla abandoned her father's royal court to seek a solitary religious life as an anchoress. She settled upon Chapel Rock, a tiny, isolated tidal island in the Severn Estuary just off Beachley Point. There, she lived a life of devotion, but her peaceful retreat ended tragically when sea pirates or raiders attacked the islet and murdered her in her cell. Centuries later, the ruins of a 13th-century chapel—and an earlier dark-age sanctuary—still mark the site, where a holy well was once celebrated for its healing properties and where visitors faced treacherous tidal waters to remember the martyred princess.

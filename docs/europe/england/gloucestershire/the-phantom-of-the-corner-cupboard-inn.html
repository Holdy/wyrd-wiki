    The Phantom of the Corner Cupboard Inn

    Summary

    A historic Gloucestershire inn is haunted by the spirit of a young girl who was thrown to her death by a 19th-century landlord after rejecting his advances.

    Details

    Originally built around 1550 as a farmhouse using stone from the dissolved Winchcombe Abbey, the building later became an inn. Local legend states that during the late 19th century, a young girl working at the establishment refused the landlord's advances, prompting him to throw her down the back steps to her death. Ever since, her ghost—a child of roughly 12 or 13 wearing a blue dress—has haunted the premises. Witnesses have reported hearing phantom running footsteps and seeing an empty rocking chair move on its own upstairs. Additionally, staff members recall that if a barmaid wears a blue dress while having a drink in the bar, the unseen spirit will deliberately tip the glass into her lap.

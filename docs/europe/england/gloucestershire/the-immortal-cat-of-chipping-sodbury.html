    The Immortal Cat of Chipping Sodbury

    Summary

    An alchemist's tomcat accidentally drank an experimental potion granting eternal life, leaving it to prowl the market town's streets indefinitely.

    Details

    Long ago in Chipping Sodbury, an apothecary spent his nights attempting to brew an elixir of everlasting life. When he finally succeeded, he foolishly left a saucer of the potion out to cool. The landlady's inquisitive tomcat sneaked into the apothecary's quarters while he slept and lapped up the entire draught. Having consumed the elixir, the cat became immortal rather than dying, and locals maintain that the exact same feline can still be spotted prowling around the marketplace and street corners at dusk.

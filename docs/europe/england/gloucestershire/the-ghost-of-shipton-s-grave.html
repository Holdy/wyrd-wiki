The Ghost of Shipton's Grave

Summary

An old coaching track passing through Barton End leads to Shipton's Grave at a remote crossroads, where the spirit of a locally executed man is said to haunt travelers.

Details

In earlier centuries, stagecoaches traveling between Bristol and Minchinhampton would turn off the A46 at Tiltups End to follow the old track passing Barton End toward Shipton's Grave, before descending through Hazel Wood toward the Weighbridge Inn. The crossroads and grave are named after a local man who was executed for stealing sheep to provide for his starving family. Over generations, locals and travelers navigating this isolated track high above Nailsworth have reported eerie encounters and sightings associated with the ghost of Shipton at the crossroads.

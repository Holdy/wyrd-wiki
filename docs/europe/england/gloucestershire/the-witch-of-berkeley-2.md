#title
The Witch of Berkeley

#summary
When a notoriously wicked woman in Berkeley died after a lifetime of practicing dark arts, her children tried in vain to protect her corpse from demonic forces using deer skin, heavy iron chains, and church vigils, only for a horde of demons to smash open her tomb and drag her away on a barbed horse.

#details
In 11th-century Berkeley, a wealthy woman known for her indulgence in witchcraft and debauchery received a dreadful omen when her pet jackdaw spoke with human speech, warning her that her time was short. Shortly thereafter, word arrived that her son and his entire family had perished. Knowing her own death was near, she summoned her surviving children—a monk and a nun—and confessed her alliance with dark powers. Terrified that demons would seize her body and soul, she begged her children to protect her corpse by sewing it into the hide of a deer, sealing it within a stone sarcophagus bound by three massive iron chains, and having priests chant psalms over her for three nights before burial. Despite their diligent efforts, demonic forces violently battered the church doors on the first two nights. On the third night, a towering, terrifying demon shattered the heavy doors, snapped the iron chains as if they were flax, and dragged the woman from her coffin out of the church, placing her upon a fierce black horse bristling with iron barbs before vanishing into the night amid her desperate cries for mercy.

#location
St Mary's Church in the town of Berkeley, Gloucestershire, is the historic site where the woman's body was entombed and ultimately snatched away by demons.

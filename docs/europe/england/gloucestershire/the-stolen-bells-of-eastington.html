    The Stolen Bells of Eastington

    Summary

    An old local legend maintains that the village church of Eastington once possessed a peal of six bells which were stolen by stealthy night-raiders from neighbouring Stonehouse to hang in their own tower.

    Details

    For centuries, historical records and church warden entries noted that St Michael's Church in Eastington held only a single bell, famously known locally as 'Mournful Minnie'. However, an old and persistent local legend claimed that Eastington originally boasted a magnificent peal of six church bells. According to the tradition, churchmen from neighbouring Stonehouse coveted the bells and, under the cover of darkness, raided the Eastington church tower, stole the entire peal, and rehung them in their own parish tower at St Cyr's Church.

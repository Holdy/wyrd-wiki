    The Woman in Black of Frith Lane

    Summary

    A resident walking home along Frith Lane in Wickwar was repeatedly terrorized by spectral encounters with a pale-faced woman dressed in black inside his home.

    Details

    While walking home late at night from the local social club to his house on Frith Lane in Wickwar, a man felt an intense and unsettling sensation of being watched. Upon arriving home, he put his coat in the cupboard and looked toward the stairs, where he was startled to see a woman dressed entirely in black staring down at him. After fixing him with an evil look, she walked further onto the upper landing. Believing at first that his wife was playing a game, he followed her upstairs, only to discover his wife entering through the front door from outside. Two weeks later, at three in the morning, he was awakened by a piercing screeching scream. Upon investigating the house, he looked down from the top of the stairs and once more encountered the spectral figure, her painfully pale face staring directly back up at him.

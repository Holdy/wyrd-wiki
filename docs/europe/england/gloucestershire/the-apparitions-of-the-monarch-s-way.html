The Apparitions of the Monarch's Way

Summary

Walkers along the Monarch's Way footpath near Broadwell have long reported sighting an elderly woman in period dress carrying an umbrella who vanishes when approached, alongside motorists encountering a phantom Cavalier soldier.

Details

Just outside the village of Broadwell, along the narrow wooded section of the Monarch's Way footpath leading towards Stow-on-the-Wold, two distinct ghostly encounters are locally famous. Dating back to just before the outbreak of World War II, walkers frequently spotted an elderly woman dressed in old-fashioned clothing and holding an open umbrella. Always seen from behind and moving at a surprisingly brisk pace, her umbrella would frequently catch on hedges and briars, frustrating anyone attempting to pass her on the narrow track. Whenever anyone spoke to her or tried to get ahead, she would instantly vanish into thin air without ever revealing her face. In the same vicinity, motorists driving up the hill from Broadwell toward Stow-on-the-Wold at night have reported encountering a second apparition: a phantom Cavalier soldier from the 17th century English Civil War who steps directly into the path of oncoming vehicles before disappearing without a trace.

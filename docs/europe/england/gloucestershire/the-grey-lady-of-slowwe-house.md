#title
The Grey Lady of Slowwe House

#summary
During renovations in the 1970s, residents of Slowwe House repeatedly encountered the spectral apparition of a woman in a grey dress and bonnet. The phantom was witnessed moving across the courtyard, wandering the upper landing, and standing silently in the guest bedroom.

#details
Slowwe House on Silver Street in Arlingham is an ancient property with a history stretching back to the Domesday Book, having once served as a hunting lodge for Berkeley Castle. In the 1970s, the Lacey family moved into the rear wing of the tumbledown building to undertake extensive renovations. Soon after beginning work, strange phenomena occurred: lights were repeatedly switched on after being turned off, windows opened and closed on their own, and hung pictures continually fell off the walls. Mrs Lacey eventually witnessed a ghostly figure of a woman wearing a long grey dress and a grey bonnet walking across the courtyard before vanishing into thin air. The entity, dubbed the Grey Lady, was subsequently seen by family members and guests in the kitchen, along the upstairs landing, and standing motionless in the guest bedroom.

#location
Slowwe House is located on Silver Street in the village of Arlingham, Gloucestershire.

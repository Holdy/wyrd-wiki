    The Glowing Monk of Leonard Stanley

    Summary

    A tall, pale green glowing hooded monk has been witnessed gliding from the church gates past the local village pub by residents and clergy over the decades.

    Details

    More than sixty years ago, local resident Peggy Davies was just ten years old when she and a group of friends experienced an unforgettable encounter outside the White Hart pub in Leonard Stanley. As they looked up, a remarkably tall figure with his face concealed under a large hood glided down the path from the church gate, glowing a pale green color. While some of the younger children were frightened, Peggy was captivated and never forgot the apparition. Over the years, other locals and clergy members, including former vicar Canon E.S. Saleby, have reported similar encounters with the hooded specter haunting St Swithun’s Parish Church and its surrounding grounds.

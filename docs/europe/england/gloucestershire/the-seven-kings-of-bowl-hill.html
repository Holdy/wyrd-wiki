    The Seven Kings of Bowl Hill

    Summary

    Arthurian legend research and earth mystery investigations suggest that the legendary burial place of seven ancient kings or chieftains lies hidden on the hillside above the hamlet of Little London.

    Details

    In 1987, the Gloucestershire Earth Mysteries group launched an investigation into a mysterious circular depression on the steep slopes of Bowl Hill near Stroud. Drawing upon interpretations of writings from The Four Ancient Books of Wales and Geoffrey of Monmouth’s History of the Kings of Britain, local Arthurian researchers proposed that a traditional legend placing the burial site of seven kings, chieftains, or warriors on a hillside between London and Amberley pointed directly to this landscape. Because the hamlet of Little London sits immediately beneath Bowl Hill and Amberley lies a short distance to the south, investigators deduced that the hillside held the forgotten royal tombs. Compounding the mystery, the circular ring on the hill shares an intriguing structural resemblance to the central octagonal motif of the famed Roman Orpheus mosaic discovered in the valley below at Woodchester.

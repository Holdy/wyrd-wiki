#title
The Ghosts and Apparitions of Woodmancote

#summary
The historic Gloucestershire village of Woodmancote is home to several enduring legends, including spectral figures re-enacting an ancient battle along Butts Lane, a hooded monk walking through autumn mists, and phantom horses galloping in Bushcombe Lane.

#details
Local tradition in the Cotswold parish of Woodmancote records several distinct paranormal occurrences across its ancient lanes and historic properties. Parts of Butts Lane are reputed to be the site of a forgotten battleground where ghostly figures still clash in perpetual re-enactment. Close to the former location of 'The Woodlands' on Butts Lane, witnesses have reported encountering a silent, hooded monk moving through the autumn mists. Other eerie manifestations include the phantom sound of galloping horses echoing along Bushcombe Lane, as well as a mysterious, unexplained face that has been witnessed peering from a window at 'Washpool' on Stockwell Lane.

#location
Butts Lane in the village of Woodmancote, Gloucestershire, marks the historic area where ghostly figures are said to re-enact an ancient battle.

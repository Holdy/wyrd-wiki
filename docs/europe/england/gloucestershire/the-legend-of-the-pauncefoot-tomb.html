    The Legend of the Pauncefoot Tomb

    Summary

    St Mary's Church in Hasfield preserves the historic tomb of the Pauncefoot family, which is tied to a local legend of profound fidelity and sacrifice where a noble lady surrendered her right hand as ransom for her captured husband.

    Details

    Local tradition in the Gloucestershire village of Hasfield associates the historic Pauncefoot family monument with a dramatic tale of love and devotion. When a member of the Pauncefoot family was captured and held by enemies who demanded a severe price for his release, his devoted wife chose ultimate self-sacrifice to secure his freedom. According to the legend, she agreed to surrender her right hand to the captors as the ransom demanded for her husband's return. Today, the 16th-century chest tomb of Dorothy Pauncefoot within St Mary's Church stands as a lasting memorial connected to this striking tale of medieval loyalty and endurance.

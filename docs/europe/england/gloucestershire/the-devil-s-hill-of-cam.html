The Devil's Hill of Cam

Summary

Cam Peak was supposedly created when the Devil grew disheartened by a cobbler's deception and dumped his wheelbarrow full of earth on the spot.

Details

Local tradition maintains that the Devil once sought to dam the Severn Estuary out of spite. Haunting the countryside with a wheelbarrow laden with soil, he encountered a shoemaker near Cam and inquired how far he still had to travel to reach the river. The resourceful cobbler pointed to a huge pile of worn-out shoes, lying that he had worn them all out just walking to and from the Severn. Believing the river was impossibly far away, the weary Devil gave up, tipped his barrow, and left the mound of earth that is known today as Cam Peak.

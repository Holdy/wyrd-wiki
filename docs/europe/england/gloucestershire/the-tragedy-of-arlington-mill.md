#title
The Tragedy of Arlington Mill

#summary
Forced into a marriage with an older miller in Arlington, young Mary fell in love with his eldest son, leading to a jealous murder and her own freezing death on a winter's night.

#details
In times past, a young woman named Mary was married off by her ambitious parents to the wealthy older widower who ran Arlington Mill. While her husband was frequently away on business, Mary grew desperately unhappy and fell in love with the miller's eldest son. One bitter winter night, the miller returned home unexpectedly to find the two lovers together. Consumed by rage, he hurled his eldest son from the high window of the mill to his death in the millpond below, and cast Mary out into the freezing night, locking her outside. Mary perished in the snow by the waterside before morning. Ever since, her tormented spirit—known as the Grey Lady—has haunted the footpath beside Rack Isle, while ghostly faces are occasionally glimpsed peering from the mill's windows.

#location
Arlington Mill stands alongside the River Coln in the village of Arlington, near Bibury in Gloucestershire.

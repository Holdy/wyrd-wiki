The Cheltenham Ghost

Summary

Beginning in 1882, multiple members of the Despard family and their household staff repeatedly witnessed the apparition of a veiled woman in black within their home on Pittville Circus Road.

Details

In April 1882, Captain Despard and his family moved into a house known as 'Donore' (formerly 'Garden Reach') on Pittville Circus Road in Cheltenham. Soon after, in June, the captain's daughter Rosina (writing under the pseudonym 'Rose Morton') became the first to encounter a mysterious figure: a tall woman dressed in soft black woolen material with her face partially hidden in a handkerchief and a widow's cuff visible on her wrists. Over the next few years, seventeen different people—including family members and domestic servants—saw the apparition, often noting how lifelike and physical it appeared before vanishing. The haunting became one of the earliest and most thoroughly investigated cases by the Society for Psychical Research, documented extensively by Frederic Myers and Rosina Despard, and remains one of Britain's most celebrated ghost accounts.

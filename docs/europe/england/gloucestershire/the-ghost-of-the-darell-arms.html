    The Ghost of the Darell Arms

    Summary

    For years, landlords and residents of the Darell Arms at Framilode Passage experienced unexplained phenomena, culminating in an alarming physical encounter when a landlord's shirt was violently torn from his back by an unseen force.

    Details

    Built on the site of the ancient Passage House Inn near the historic Severn crossing at Lower Framilode, the Darell Arms was long rumored to be haunted by a capricious phantom. While previous licensees, including Mrs. Alec Honey, reported objects mysteriously shifting or falling from kitchen shelves, later landlord Alec Pratt remained skeptical. However, Pratt's skepticism vanished entirely one day while standing in the kitchen with his wife. As she washed dishes at the sink, Pratt felt his shirt suddenly ripped from his back as though a cat had leaped and clawed at him. He spun around instantly, but no one was there, leaving customers in the bar mystified by his shouts. Although subsequent kitchen renovations eventually put an end to the disturbances, the haunting remained a legendary piece of the inn's history until it closed and became a private residence.

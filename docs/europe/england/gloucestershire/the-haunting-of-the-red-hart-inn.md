#title
The Haunting of the Red Hart Inn

#summary
Staff and residents at the historic Red Hart Inn in Awre have reported encountering unexplainable ghostly figures and unseen presences within the building.

#details
Situated in the village of Awre, the historic Red Hart Inn—which originally operated as a farmhouse before becoming an inn—has long been associated with local accounts of the paranormal. Former managers and residents have shared unsettling experiences of unexplained phenomena on the premises, including one manager who witnessed a mysterious apparition on a Sunday evening while preparing to open the bar. Subsequent owners reported that their Staffordshire bull terrier would repeatedly react with bristling fur and fixed attention, tracking an invisible entity as it walked across the upstairs private lounge. Local tradition also connects the property's ghostly reputation to a former farmer who was allegedly murdered there before the building was converted into a public house.

#location
The Red Hart Inn, located in the village of Awre, Gloucestershire.

    The Foolish Tower of Ebrington

    Summary

    Convinced their church tower was too short and could be moved to higher ground, the villagers of Ebrington attempted to push it up the hill after stacking their coats on the ground. While they strained against the stone, a prankster stole their garments, leading the villagers to believe they had successfully shoved the massive tower right on top of their coats.

    Details

    According to local tradition, the people of Ebrington—historically nicknamed the "Yubberton Yawnies" by their neighbors—long envied the taller church tower in neighboring Chipping Campden. Seeking a remedy to elevate their status, the villagers devised a plan. Believing that the structure would stand taller if positioned further up the slope, a group of townsfolk fortified themselves at the local pub before marching to St Edburgha's Church on a warm summer's evening.

    Arriving at the churchyard, they stripped off their jackets and piled them into a heap on the ground. They then positioned themselves around the ancient stone tower and began heaving and pushing with all their might, attempting to slide the building uphill. While they exerted themselves in the dark, a mischievous passerby stole the pile of coats. When the exhausted villagers finally stopped to rest, they discovered their jackets were missing and became thoroughly convinced that they had pushed the heavy stone tower completely over their garments.

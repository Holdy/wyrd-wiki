    The Restless Sexton of St Nicholas's

    Summary

    In the 1940s, an anxious church sexton who took a Bible home to protect it from a leaking roof died before returning it, leading his wispy apparition to haunt his son until the book was brought back to St Nicholas's Church.

    Details

    During the 1940s, St Nicholas’s Church in Lower Oddington suffered from a severely leaking roof. Concerned that dampness and water damage would ruin the church's holy books, the elderly sexton removed the parish Bible from the building for safekeeping at his home. However, before the next scheduled service, he unexpectedly passed away and was laid to rest in the churchyard. Soon after his burial, his son began encountering the wispy apparition of his father drifting by the church. Realizing that the old man was restless and remembering the unreturned Bible, the son took the book back to the church, whereupon the haunting ceased and the ghost faded away.

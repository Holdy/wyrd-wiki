    The Laying of Miss Nicholls' Ghost

    Summary

    Following her tragic suicide, the restless spirit of a woman named Miss Nicholls haunted her former home in Upton St Leonards until twelve ghost-layers successfully banished it beneath the hearthstone at midnight.

    Details

    In years past, a local resident known as Miss Nicholls tragically took her own life by hanging herself in her home. Thereafter, her ghost was reported to haunt the building, creating a lingering paranormal presence that captured the attention of the local community. To combat the haunting, a contingent of twelve ghost-layers was brought in. At the stroke of midnight, they performed rituals that successfully subdued and laid the spirit to rest, trapping the ghost beneath the house's hearthstone.

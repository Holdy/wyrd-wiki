    The Misty Apparition of The Frogmill Inn

    Summary

    Patrons and staff at a historic 16th-century Cotswolds inn have reported encountering an indistinct misty shape that mysteriously materializes and dissolves in the bar area. This spectral presence forms out of thin air before rapidly dispersing.

    Details

    Nestled in the Cotswolds near Andoversford, The Frogmill Inn is a historic 16th-century coaching inn steeped in local legend. Alongside its rustic charm and crackling winter log fires, the establishment is reputed to harbor a resident spirit. Witnesses in the bar area have described observing an ill-defined, semi-transparent misty shape that slowly coalesces into view before vanishing just as suddenly.

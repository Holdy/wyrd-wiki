#title
The Phantom Walker of Broadwell's Monarch's Way

#summary
At twilight, walkers on the Monarch's Way footpath between Stow-on-the-Wold and Broadwell frequently encounter an elderly woman in period dress carrying an umbrella who mysteriously vanishes when approached.

#details
Local legend holds that twilight along the narrow section of the Monarch's Way footpath leading into Broadwell brings a strange and spectral encounter. Ramblers and villagers walking the historic route have reported seeing an elderly woman dressed in dated period attire, walking at a surprisingly brisk pace ahead of them. Always viewed from behind, the figure moves steadily along the confined track. However, whenever walkers quicken their step to pass her and call out a greeting, the woman abruptly disappears into thin air, leaving no trace behind.

#location
The Monarch's Way footpath connecting Stow-on-the-Wold to the village of Broadwell in Gloucestershire.

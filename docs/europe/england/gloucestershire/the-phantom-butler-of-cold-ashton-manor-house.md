#title
The Phantom Butler of Cold Ashton Manor House

#summary
During the 1930s, a lost motorist named Lady Winifred Pennoyer stopped at Cold Ashton Manor House for directions and tipped a butler half a crown, only to later discover the building was completely empty and locked, with her coin resting on the doorstep.

#details
In the 1930s, Lady Winifred Pennoyer became lost in the dark and sought help at Cold Ashton Manor House, recognizable by its wrought iron gates flanked by stone pineapples. After ringing the bell, a man she believed to be a butler answered the door, provided clear directions, and received half a crown as a token of appreciation. Upon arriving at her destination and recounting the encounter, her host recognized the property and drove her back, where they found the gates chained shut and the building entirely abandoned. To their astonishment, the half-a-crown coin was found sitting waiting on the doorstep. Several other motorists have since reported similar experiences of stopping at the manor to ask for directions from the phantom butler.

#location
Cold Ashton Manor House stands in the village of Cold Ashton, South Gloucestershire, positioned near Bath and Bristol.

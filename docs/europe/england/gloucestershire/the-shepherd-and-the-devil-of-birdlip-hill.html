The Shepherd and the Devil of Birdlip Hill

Summary

A resourceful shepherd walking up Birdlip Hill outwits the Devil by tricking him into drinking a foul-tasting sheep medicine disguised as spiced ale. Consequently, the enraged Devil roars in agony and flees the area permanently.

Details

A shepherd walking up Birdlip Hill after fetching medicine for a sick ewe knew the Devil was lying in wait to catch travelers on the road. Knowing that horses refused to travel that route due to the fiend's presence, the shepherd went on foot, carrying a drinking horn filled with ale and his parcel of pungent sheep tonic. As he neared the turn by Black Dog's Lane, he took a sip of ale, poured the rest of the boiled sheep medicine into the horn, and continued onward. The Devil promptly sprang out and demanded the ale. Handing over the horn while taking to his heels, the shepherd watched as the Devil eagerly gulped down the concoction. The vile sheep tonic caused the Devil to let out a roar heard all the way in Cheltenham, and he has never dared go near Birdlip Hill since.

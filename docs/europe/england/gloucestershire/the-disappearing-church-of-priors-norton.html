    The Disappearing Church of Priors Norton

    Summary

    St Mary's Church in Priors Norton is locally renowned as the "disappearing church" because its unique topographical positioning creates an optical illusion that causes the building to vanish from sight as travelers pass along the A38. Built just beyond the brow of a hill, this historic landmark has long amazed passersby who watch the structure seemingly sink into the earth.

    Details

    Standing sentinel over the village of Priors Norton in Gloucestershire, St Mary’s Parish Church features medieval architecture with a nave dating back to the 14th century, a 13th-century chancel, and a 15th-century tower. Over generations, travelers journeying north along the A38 between Gloucester and Tewkesbury noted a strange optical phenomenon caused by the church's exact placement just beyond the crest of a local hill. As motorists and pedestrians approach and pass the site, the church appears to gradually sink out of view, earning it the enduring local legend and claim to fame as the "disappearing church."

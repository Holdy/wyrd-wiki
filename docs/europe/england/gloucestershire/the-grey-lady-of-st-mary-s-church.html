The Grey Lady of St Mary's Church

Summary

St Mary's Church and the nearby Grange in Bitton are reputed to be haunted by a spectral Grey Lady who materializes near the choir vestry and vanishes toward the historic rectory.

Details

The village church of St Mary in Bitton, Gloucestershire, alongside the neighbouring house known as the Grange (historically the prebendal manor house and rectory), has long been associated with the apparition of a Grey Lady. According to parish records, the phantom figure appears near the choir vestry within the church before drifting off toward the Grange. Documented encounters include a sighting in the early 1900s by the grandmother of a churchwarden, as well as further sightings reported within the Grange during the Second World War.

    The Legend of the Ninety-Nine Yew Trees

    Summary

    Local folklore dictates that exactly ninety-nine yew trees grow in the churchyard of St Mary's Church in Painswick, and any attempt by anyone to plant a hundredth tree will be destroyed by the Devil.

    Details

    For generations, visitors and parishioners have counted the meticulously trimmed yew trees surrounding St Mary's Church in Painswick and invariably found ninety-nine. According to the traditional legend, the Devil intervenes to prevent a hundredth tree from ever taking root or surviving. Despite this persistent superstition, the church boldly planted a hundredth yew tree on the north side of the church near the bus stop in the year 2000 to commemorate the millennium, which has since continued to thrive without demonic interference.

    The Spectre of Ashchurch Tower

    Summary

    A mournful woman in trailing white garments haunts the north-west tower of Ashchurch, serving as a sinister omen of death for anyone who witnesses her apparition.

    Details

    Mr. Cortram investigates the unsettling haunting centered around the north-west tower overlooking the churchyard in Ashchurch. The apparition takes the form of a woman draped in long white robes who silently sweeps around the corners of the stonework. According to local belief, catching a glimpse of her ghostly garments portends an impending tragedy or death within the family. Following the investigation, efforts are made to appease the restless spirit.

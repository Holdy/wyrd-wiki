    The Ghostly Messenger of Shaw Green Lane

    Summary

    A medieval messenger riding to the Battle of Tewkesbury in 1471 was killed by an archer in Prestbury, and his phantom horseman continues to haunt the lane where road workers later discovered his skeletal remains pierced by an arrowhead.

    Details

    During the Wars of the Roses in 1471, a royal messenger was dispatched on horseback toward Edward IV's camp at Tewkesbury. As he rode through the village of Prestbury, he was struck down by an archer. According to local tradition, the phantom of a white horse and its rider can still be seen and heard galloping along Shaw Green Lane on spring mornings. Decades later, during road construction work in the area, workmen unearthed a human skeleton with an ancient arrowhead lodged precisely between the ribs, seeming to confirm the tragic legend.

#title
The Screams Beneath Chedworth Roman Villa

#summary
Local residents report hearing the haunting, phantom screams of young boys echoing from beneath the ancient hypocaust flooring of Chedworth Roman Villa at night. According to local lore, these sounds recall children who were trapped and burned alive while stoking the Roman heating systems.

#details
According to accounts shared by locals living near the ancient archaeological site of Chedworth Roman Villa, chilling sounds can frequently be heard in the dark. Legend holds that in Roman times, young boys were sent crawling beneath the stone floorboards to tend the hypocaust heating fires, and some were tragically trapped and burned alive. The haunting memory of their fate lingers in the form of phantom screams heard echoing from the ruins, with local residents refusing to approach or enter the villa after nightfall.

#location
Chedworth Roman Villa is located in a sheltered valley within the Cotswolds in Gloucestershire, situated just outside the village of Chedworth and overlooking the River Coln.

#title
The Ghostly Hitchhiker of Hulbert Road

#summary
A spectral hitchhiker is said to haunt a stretch of road in Waterlooville, Hampshire. Drivers have reported picking up a young woman who vanishes before reaching her destination.

#details
Hulbert Road in Waterlooville is known for its ghostly hitchhiker. Several drivers have reported picking up a young lady from this street, only to have her vanish before they reach their destination. Other versions of the story involve terrified drivers seeing the girl standing in the lane in front of ongoing traffic. Another tale tells of workmen stopping to pick up a young woman caught on the side of the road in the pouring rain but deciding against it after noticing she was bone dry and something wasn't right. It is believed that this spectral hitchhiker is the ghost of a young woman killed in a hit-and-run accident while walking home from Leigh Park in 1976.

    The Roaring Bull of Bagbury

    Summary

    Following the death of a notoriously cruel squire who transformed into a ferocious devil-bull, a team of clergymen trap the beast beneath the stone steps of Hyssington Church.

    Details

    A notoriously cruel and wicked squire lived at Bagbury Farm near Churchstoke, and upon his death, his restless spirit manifested as a massive, hellfire-breathing black bull that terrorized the countryside. The creature roamed the roads by night, roaring so violently that roof tiles flew off buildings and travelers were driven into a panic. Eventually, a group of clergymen pursued the devil-bull into Hyssington Church to exorcise it. During a fierce struggle, the bull's mighty roar cracked the church walls from top to bottom. Through relentless prayer and the resourcefulness of a blind parson whose candle remained lit inside his boot, the clergymen forced the beast to shrink until it was small enough to be trapped and imprisoned beneath the church steps.

#title
The Black Bull of Wem

#summary
A group of rowdy drinkers in a Wem pub conjure the Devil in the form of a terrifying black bull, which is eventually banished by a clever clergyman using an everlasting candle.

#details
Around the start of the 18th century, a group of young men seeking excitement in a pub on New Street in Wem used a witchcraft recipe to draw a chalk circle on the quarry-tiled floor and summon the Devil. Their spell succeeded all too well, filling the room with smoke and conjuring a massive, snorting black bull with glowing horns. When local vicars attempted to banish the beast, the bull blew out their candles, leaving them helpless in the dark. Desperate for assistance, the lads travelled to Battlefield Church and enlisted the help of the Reverend Leonard Hotchkiss. Armed with an unbreakable "ever-burn" candle guaranteed for a hundred hours, Rev. Hotchkiss returned to the New Street pub, successfully recited the counter-incantation, and banished the demonic bull back into the earth through the cracking floor.

#location
The main events of the story took place inside a public house situated on New Street in the town of Wem, Shropshire.

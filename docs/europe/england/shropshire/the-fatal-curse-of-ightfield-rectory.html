    The Fatal Curse of Ightfield Rectory

    Summary

    A dismissed garden boy is followed by a persistent demonic shadow after his former employer curses him, leading to his mysterious death three days later.

    Details

    In the village of Ightfield, a young garden boy was sacked by the local rector for being cheeky. Later that day, the boy ran into the rector again while searching for work; the rector sniggered and cruelly told him he would never find employment again before driving off. Immediately following this encounter, a strange, sinister shadow appeared on the road and doggedly refused to leave the boy's side. The boy fell violently ill with unceasing chills, telling his doctor that the shadow creature had accompanied him ever since his conversation with the rector. Despite medical attention, the boy died three days later, leaving the local folk convinced that the rector had secret dealings with the Devil.

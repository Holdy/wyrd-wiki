#title
Wild Edric in the Shropshire Hills
#summary
The rebel Edric is said to live in the Shropshire mine tunnels, making knocking noises to guide miners and riding out to foretell war.
#details
Edric, a Shropshire man who survived the Norman Conquest and fought William as a rebel, was apparently still alive in the nineteenth century, living in the mines under the Shropshire hills. His noise was a knocking sound that told miners where the best lodes were, and he would ride out to foretell war, having been seen riding with his wife, Lady Godda, just before the outbreak of the Crimean War.

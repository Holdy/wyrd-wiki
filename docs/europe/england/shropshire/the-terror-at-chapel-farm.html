    The Terror at Chapel Farm

    Summary

    The Devil drove a congregation from their place of worship near the River Clun through a relentless campaign of terrifying nocturnal noises, leading to the building's eventual conversion into a farm.

    Details

    Near the River Clun in Clunbury, a building known as Chapel Farm marks the site of an ancient place of worship. According to local folklore, the Devil targeted the location in a vicious campaign of terror, violently disrupting sermons and tormenting the village with loud, unearthly noises throughout the night. Overwhelmed by fear and unable to endure the demonic persecution, the congregation ultimately fled and abandoned the church, allowing the property to eventually be transformed into a farm.

    The Frogs of Causeway Bank

    Summary

    A picturesque spring along the ancient Roman road between Ruckley and Acton Burnell serves as a haunting ground for the Devil and his imps disguised as frogs. The largest frog, representing Satan himself, remains hidden at the bottom of the rocky basin.

    Details

    Situated by the side of the Roman road between Ruckley and Acton Burnell, halfway down Causeway Bank, a beautiful spring known as Frog Well drips into a deep rocky basin surrounded by the trees of Causeway Wood. Though its waters have long been reputed to possess healing properties for sore eyes, local folklore warns that the spring is frequented by the Devil and his demonic attendants. Three frogs are invariably spotted together here; the smaller ones act as his imps, while the largest frog, representing Satan himself, stays at the bottom of the pool and rarely shows itself.

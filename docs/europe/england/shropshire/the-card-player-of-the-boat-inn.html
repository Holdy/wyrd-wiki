    The Card Player of the Boat Inn

    Summary

    During a card game at a local pub, patrons discover that their mysterious and charismatic opponent is the Devil himself after catching a glimpse of his cloven hooves beneath the table.

    Details

    On a cold winter night at the Boat Inn in Jackfield, within the parish of Broseley, local men gathered by the fire to drink and play cards. A well-dressed stranger with an eccentric appearance entered the crowded pub, charmed the barmaids, and joined the gaming with promises of vast riches. Winning hand after hand, the stranger boasted of his skill until a gruff local man stepped forward to challenge him. Frustrated by the competitive match, the stranger grew clumsy and scattered the cards across the floor. When the challenger reached down to pick them up, he looked beneath the table and saw a pair of great black cloven hooves. Crying out in terror that they were playing with the Devil, a violent gust of wind instantly tore through the tavern, shattering glasses and sweeping the laughing fiend out into the night.

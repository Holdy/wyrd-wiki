    The Mermaid of Child's Ercall

    Summary

    Two agricultural workers walking to work early one morning encountered a freshwater mermaid in a parish pool—initially fearing she was sent by Old Nick—who offered them hidden gold before vanishing when one of them swore in astonishment.

    Details

    Early one morning, two farm labourers in Child's Ercall were walking to work when they reached a pool in a field near the River Meese. Squatting upon the surface of the water was a mermaid. Terrified, the men initially believed she was a demonic entity sent to drag them straight off to Old Nick himself. However, when she spoke to them in a voice of exquisite sweetness, their fear vanished and they fell instantly in love with her. The mermaid told them of a vast treasure of gold hidden at the bottom of the pool and invited them to wade in and take it. Wading in until the water reached their chins, the men watched as she dived down and brought up a lump of gold nearly as large as a man's head. But just as one of the men reached out to grab it and swore in amazement, the mermaid gave a sharp scream, snatched the gold back, and plunged beneath the water, never to be seen again.

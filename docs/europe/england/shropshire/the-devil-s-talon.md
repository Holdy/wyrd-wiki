#title
The Devil's Talon

#summary
A mysterious stranger who acquired ungodly wealth abroad hosted a lavish Christmas Eve party at Minsterley Hall, only to be violently claimed by the Devil himself. The host was found dead with a pitch-black talon embedded in his eye after the estate was struck by a terrifying, fiery entity.

#details
During the Georgian period, the abandoned Minsterley Hall was purchased by a wealthy stranger whose origins were unknown and whose fortune seemed to multiply unnaturally. On Christmas Eve, he hosted a magnificent and extravagant party for the county's elite. As the festivities peaked, a terrifying, disembodied voice echoed through the property's tree-lined avenue shouting "YOU ARE MINE," followed by a fiery red entity that seemingly engulfed the hall in flames. When the terrified guests returned after the commotion subsided, they found the interior devastated and the host brutally slain beneath an overturned table, marked by deep gashes and a sharp, pitch-black talon embedded in his eye.

#location
Minsterley Hall is a historic timber-framed house located in the village of Minsterley, Shropshire, UK.

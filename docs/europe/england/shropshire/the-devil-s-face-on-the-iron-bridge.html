    The Devil's Face on the Iron Bridge

    Summary

    Old Nick is said to have cast his own silhouette into the ironwork of the historic bridge to watch over the people of Madeley Wood.

    Details

    Local folklore surrounding the iconic Iron Bridge in Shropshire suggests that the mysterious facial silhouettes visible near the apex of the structure were cast by the Devil, or 'Owd Nick' himself. According to legend, Old Nick placed his image into the bridge's design to keep a watchful eye over the good folk of Madeley Wood. While alternative theories attribute the hidden face to Abraham Darby III or designer Thomas Farnolls Pritchard, the devilish legend remains a popular tale among local river men and residents.

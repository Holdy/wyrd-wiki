    The Midnight Roll-Call of Chirbury

    Summary

    Two men who drunkenly perform a forbidden midnight Halloween ritual at St Michael's Church hear a chilling supernatural voice reading out a roll-call of parishioners destined to die within the year. True to the prophecy, their friend whose name was called out passes away just a week later.

    Details

    In the village of Chirbury, local folklore warns that visiting St Michael's Churchyard on All Hallows Eve brings terrible danger. On Halloween in 1788, two skeptical men scoffed at the warnings and decided to test the legend by walking twelve times counter-clockwise around the church before waiting until midnight. As the clock struck twelve, a raspy, unearthly voice emerged from the mist, reading out a lengthy roll-call of villagers who would perish before the next Halloween. Among the names called out was that of a close friend. Shaken, the two men rushed to his house to warn him, but he mocked their superstition and turned them away. Exactly one week later, he was dead.

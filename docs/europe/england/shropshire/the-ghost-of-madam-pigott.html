The Ghost of Madam Pigott

Summary

The ghost of Madam Piggott is said to haunt a stretch of road near Chetwynd Park, Shropshire. She is often seen as a woman in white carrying a baby.

Details

Madam Piggott was the wife of a local noble who died in childbirth along with her baby. Her spirit is said to haunt the area around the A41 between Newport and Market Drayton. Many drivers and walkers have claimed to see her, often described as a woman in white carrying a baby. The ghost has been reported to jump onto horsemen's backs or appear in the passenger seats of cars. One notable sighting involved a figure gliding along the road that disappeared at high speed when approached by car headlights.

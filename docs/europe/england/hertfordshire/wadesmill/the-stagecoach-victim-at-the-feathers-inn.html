    The Stagecoach Victim at The Feathers Inn

    Summary

    The ghost of a little girl who was run over by a stagecoach is sighted at The Feathers Inn.

    Details

    The Feathers Inn is haunted by the spirit of a little girl who tragically lost her life after being run over by a stagecoach.

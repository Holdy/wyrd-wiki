Stampede Screams at Bethnal Green Tube Station

Summary

The screams, crying, and sobbing of victims from a tragic 1943 stampede still echo at Bethnal Green Tube Station.

Details

On March 1st, 1943, an anti-aircraft battery sound was mistaken for a Luftwaffe attack, leading fleeing Londoners into Bethnal Green Tube Station where a deadly stampede resulted in 173 deaths. The screams, crying, and sobbing of the victims are still heard at the station.

Ghostly Barking Dogs at Sutton House

Summary

Unexplained sounds of barking dogs echo through Sutton House despite no live animals being present.

Details

Alongside sightings of the lady in blue and white, dogs are frequently heard barking inside Sutton House despite no live animals being inside the building.

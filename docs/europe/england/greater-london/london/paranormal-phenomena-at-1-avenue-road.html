Paranormal Phenomena at 1 Avenue Road

Summary

Residents at 1 Avenue Road report flickering lights, loud footsteps, disembodied noises, and mysterious chanting.

Details

This former gothic mansion, recently refurbished into a block of apartments, still experiences paranormal activity. Residents report strange flickering lights moving around upper floors, loud footsteps, disembodied noises, and chanting heard in parts of the building.

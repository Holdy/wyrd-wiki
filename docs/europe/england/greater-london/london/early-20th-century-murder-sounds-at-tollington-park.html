Early 20th Century Murder Sounds at Tollington Park

Summary

The sounds of someone being chased and choking to death from an early 20th-century murder echo through Tollington Park.

Details

The sounds of a historic murder from the beginning of the 20th century have been heard at Tollington Park, generally described as the sounds of someone being chased followed by choking to death.

#title
The Blue and White Lady of Sutton House
#summary
A woman in a white or blue dress, believed to be John Machell the younger's wife, haunts Sutton House.
#details
A woman—thought to be the wife of John Machell the younger—has been seen at Sutton House wearing a white or sometimes blue dress. Guests have been awakened in the Exhibition Room by her standing right beside them.

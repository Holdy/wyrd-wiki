#title
The Hornsey Coal Ghost at 8 Ferrestone Road
#summary
A violent poltergeist dubbed the 'Hornsey Coal Ghost' threw objects and caused banging noises at 8 Ferrestone Road.
#details
This old terrace house is reportedly haunted by a violent poltergeist that forced a family to leave after throwing objects around and making mysterious banging noises. Dubbed the 'Hornsey Coal Ghost', it is believed to be one of the people buried in the garden, which was formerly a coal merchant site.

The Bow Bells Washroom Spirit

Summary

A washroom spirit at The Bow Bells flushes toilets while in use, plays with doors, and oozes from the ground as fog.

Details

A spirit occupies the washroom at The Bow Bells, where it flushes toilets while they are in use, plays with doors, and has manifested as fog that oozes up from the ground.

#title
The Faceless Woman of Becontree Tube Station
#summary
Workers at Becontree tube station report feeling watched and seeing a faceless blonde woman in white following a 1958 tragedy.
#details
Workers at Becontree tube station frequently feel as if they are being followed or watched. One worker reported seeing a blonde woman in a white dress who had no face, featuring a blank area instead—a phenomenon possibly linked to an accident that claimed ten lives in 1958.

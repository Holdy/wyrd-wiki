#title
The Ghostly Hitchhiker of Blue Bell Hill

#summary
The story revolves around a phantom hitchhiker seen on the A229 near Maidstone, Kent. This ghost is often described as a young woman in white who appears to be hit by vehicles and then vanishes.

#details
The tale begins with a tragic car crash in 1965 involving three young women on Blue Bell Hill. Since then, numerous sightings have been reported of a young woman in white, sometimes described as a would-be bride, stepping onto the road and seemingly being hit by vehicles before disappearing when drivers stop to help.

Another frequently reported apparition is a female phantom hitchhiker who vanishes from the back seat after being given a lift. In some accounts, the driver, upon contacting the address given by this mysterious woman, is informed that she had died years earlier.

#location
The key location in this story is Blue Bell Hill on the A229 near Maidstone, Kent.

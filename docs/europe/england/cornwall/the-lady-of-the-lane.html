The Lady of the Lane

Summary

The Lady of the Lane is a ghostly apparition said to haunt the narrow roads between West Looe and Talland Bay in Cornwall. She appears as a woman dressed in white, with a sorrowful expression and no eyes.

Details

The story tells of a man walking down a dark lane near Talland Bay one summer evening. He hears sobbing and sees a woman in white nightclothes standing barefoot in the middle of the lane. As he approaches, he realizes she is floating above the ground. When she turns to face him, he sees that she has no eyes, just empty sockets. Terrified, the man flees, never looking back.

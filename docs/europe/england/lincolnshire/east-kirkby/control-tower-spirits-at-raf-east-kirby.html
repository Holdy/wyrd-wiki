    Control Tower Spirits at RAF East Kirby

    Summary

    The control tower at RAF East Kirby experiences footsteps and a strong feeling of not being alone from accident victims.

    Details

    The control tower at RAF East Kirby exhibits the highest concentration of paranormal activity, where people report hearing footsteps and feeling an unnerving presence. Numerous accidents took place at the site, which may explain the haunting entities.

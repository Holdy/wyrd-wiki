    Warbleton Priory skulls

    Summary

    Two skulls are hidden in the walls of the ruined Warbleton Priory: one of a murdered prior and one of the man who killed him. Mayhem is said to follow any attempt to remove them, though one once stayed silent while a blue tit nested inside it.

    Details

    Warbleton Priory, now a ruin on a modern farm near Rushlake Green, is said to hide not one screaming skull but two somewhere within its walls. One belonged to a prior who was murdered, the other to his murderer, and the usual mayhem is said to follow any attempt to take them from the Priory. Before they were bricked up, the skulls were for some reason placed in the branches of an apple tree, where a blue tit built its nest inside one of them, using the eye sockets as doorways. The skull apparently approved and remained silent.

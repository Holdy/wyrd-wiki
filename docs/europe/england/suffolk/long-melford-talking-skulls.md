#title
Long Melford talking skulls

#summary
Local legend tells of a pair of skulls that once sat in Long Melford churchyard. After dark, they were said to chatter to one another.

#details
According to a Long Melford legend, two skulls once rested in the village churchyard, and once night fell they would talk to each other. The tale does not say whose skulls they were or what became of them.

#location
Holy Trinity churchyard, Long Melford, Suffolk, England

The Jester of the Rectory Gates

Summary

The spectral figure of a jester haunts the rectory gates in Great Livermere near a grove of trees that unnerves local animals.

Details

Local resident Beryl Dyson reported sightings of the ghostly figure of a jester near the rectory gates in Great Livermere, which may be the same apparition witnessed by M.R. James during the 19th century. Dogs in the village exhibit noticeable fear when passing a particular clump of trees growing close to this haunted spot.

#title
The Phantom Woman in Red

#summary
A ghostly woman dressed in red haunts the roads of Great Livermere by stepping directly into the path of oncoming traffic.

#details
According to local folklore compiled in Great Livermere, a phantom woman dressed in red frequently startles drivers by stepping suddenly into the path of oncoming traffic before mysteriously disappearing into thin air.

#title
The Terrifying Face at the Garden Gate

#summary
During his childhood at the rectory in Great Livermere, M.R. James experienced haunting nightmares and reportedly encountered a malevolent pink face staring through a garden gate.

#details
As a child living at the rectory in Great Livermere, author M.R. James experienced dreadful nightmares involving creeping movements outside, footsteps on the stairs, and a hand on his bedroom door. When he went out to investigate the garden, he saw a terrifying, pink face with large open eyes looking at him through a hole in the gate. As he fled back to the house, he witnessed a draped figure shambling away among the trees.

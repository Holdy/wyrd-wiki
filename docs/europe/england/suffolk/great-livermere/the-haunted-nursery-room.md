#title
The Haunted Nursery Room

#summary
Guests sleeping in a former nursery room at Great Livermere Rectory are subjected to horrific nightmares that drive them away.

#details
After a room previously used as a nursery in the Great Livermere Rectory was converted into a guest bedroom, visitors who slept there experienced terrifying dreams and nightmares. The hauntings were severe enough that guests refused to ever sleep in that room again.

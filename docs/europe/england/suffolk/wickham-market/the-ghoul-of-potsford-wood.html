    The Ghoul of Potsford Wood

    Summary

    A hooded skeletal ghoul and a moaning black figure have been reported near the site of an old gibbet in Potsford Wood.

    Details

    Potsford Wood once held a gibbet where the corpse of executed criminal Jonah Snell hung until 1740. In the 1980s, a lorry driver reading the plaque felt a tap on his shoulder and turned to face a hooded, robed skeleton, while in 1997 a stranded couple reported being harassed by a moaning black figure in the woods.

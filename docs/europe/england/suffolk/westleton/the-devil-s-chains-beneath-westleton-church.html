    The Devil's Chains Beneath Westleton Church

    Summary

    Visitors who perform a specific ritual at St Peter's Church in Westleton can hear the Devil's chains rattling beneath the building.

    Details

    St Peter's Church is suspected to house the devil in a small grating by the priest's door. According to local lore, if a person places a handkerchief in the grate, uses the 14th-century 'witch's stone' gravestone as a base, and runs seven times anti-clockwise around the church without looking back, the handkerchief disappears and the sound of the Devil's chains rattling can be heard beneath the church.

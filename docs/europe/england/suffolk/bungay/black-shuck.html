Black Shuck

Summary

A legendary demon dog with burning red eyes prowls East Anglia, famously terrorising churches in Bungay and Blythburgh during a 1577 storm.

Details

Black Shuck is a massive black dog said to stalk the countryside of Norfolk and Suffolk, with sightings reported in Reedham, Coltishall, Potter Heigham, and the ruins of Burgh Castle. On 4 August 1577, during a severe storm, the beast burst into St Mary’s Church in Bungay, killing a man and a boy before fleeing into the night. Simultaneously, lightning struck Holy Trinity Church in Blythburgh, collapsing the steeple and revealing Shuck, who left burnt claw marks known as the devil's fingerprints on the north door. Though often seen as a hellhound bringing doom, Shuck is also viewed by some as a protector of lost travelers or a bearer of warnings, and the legend is celebrated annually in Bungay.

The Secret Tunnel of Bungay Castle

Summary

Legend tells of a mysterious underground tunnel built beneath Bungay Castle by command of Oliver Cromwell leading to Mettingham Castle.

Details

Beneath the imposing ruins of Bungay Castle, local legend holds that a secret tunnel leads all the way to Mettingham Castle. The tunnel was supposedly dug on the orders of Oliver Cromwell, although no such passage has ever been discovered.

The Demonic Hound of Holy Trinity Church

Summary

The monstrous black dog known as Black Shuck violently burst into a Suffolk church during a thunderstorm, killing two people and causing the steeple to collapse.

Details

During a violent thunderstorm, the demonic, shaggy-furred hound known as Black Shuck burst through the doors of Holy Trinity Church and ran up the aisle, killing two people and causing the church steeple to collapse. As the hell-hound made its exit through the north door, it left scorch marks that can still be seen today.

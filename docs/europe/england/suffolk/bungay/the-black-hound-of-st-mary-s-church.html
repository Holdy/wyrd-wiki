The Black Hound of St. Mary's Church

Summary

During a storm in 1577, a massive black dog burst into a church in Bungay and instantly snapped the necks of two kneeling parishioners.

Details

On August 4, 1577, parishioners gathered at St. Mary's Church in Bungay during a fierce storm. A large black dog ran swiftly through the church, passing between two people kneeling in prayer and twisting their necks backward so they died instantly. The creature also attacked another man, tearing his flesh before fleeing.

Black Shuck's Coastal Terror

Summary

The legendary demonic black dog known as Black Shuck roams the coastline, leaving a trail of destruction and death behind him.

Details

The Black Shuck is a famous phantom dog in folklore that varies in size from a large dog to the scale of a calf or horse, described as a true manifestation of fear with saucer-like eyes or breath like fire. According to legend, on August 4, a large black dog burst through the doors of St Mary's Church in Bungay during a clap of thunder, killing a man and boy and collapsing the steeple, before moving on to Blythburgh Church to maul more people. He is said to scour dark lanes and lonely footpaths of the coast, howling loudly while his footfalls make no sound.

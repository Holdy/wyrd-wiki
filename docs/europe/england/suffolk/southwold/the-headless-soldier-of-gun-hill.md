#title
The Headless Soldier of Gun Hill

#summary
A headless soldier is said to haunt Gun Hill in Southwold, the site of a fatal cannon misfire in 1842.

#details
In 1842, a gunner named James Martin was killed during a cannon-firing celebration when a misfired cannon went off as he looked down the barrel. Today, on quiet nights, a headless soldier can still be seen lingering on Gun Hill.

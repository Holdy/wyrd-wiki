#title
The Rendlesham Forest UFO Incident

#summary
US servicemen witnessed a glowing metallic UFO descend into Rendlesham Forest in December 1980 in an event dubbed 'Britain's Roswell'.

#details
In December 1980, patrolling US servicemen from RAF Woodbridge saw bright lights descend into Rendlesham Forest and witnessed a glowing, metallic object with coloured lights. When approached, the object zoomed off, sending nearby farm animals into a frenzy, while subsequent investigations revealed triangular ground impressions, broken branches, burn marks, and above-average radiation readings.

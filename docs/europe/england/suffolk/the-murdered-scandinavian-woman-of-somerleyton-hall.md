#title
The Murdered Scandinavian Woman of Somerleyton Hall
#summary
A brutally murdered Scandinavian woman is said to be responsible for strange sounds and poltergeist activity at Somerleyton Hall.
#details
Somerleyton Hall is home to reports of strange sounds and poltergeist-like activity attributed to a Scandinavian woman who was brutally murdered. Room 7 is noted as being particularly active.

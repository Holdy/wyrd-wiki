    The Wild Man of Orford

    Summary

    A wild, merman-like creature was captured off the coast of Orford Ness and imprisoned in Orford Castle in the 12th century.

    Details

    In 1167, fishermen trapped a naked, bearded "wild man" with rough beast-like skin and horse-like hooves in their nets off Orford Ness. Taken to Orford Castle and interrogated, he made only unintelligible noises and was fed raw fish. When placed in a fenced area in the sea to test his behavior, he escaped underwater and was never seen again, though a small stone carving of him remains at St Bartholomew's Church font.

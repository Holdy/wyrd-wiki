#title
The Sneering Man of Corton

#summary
The story tells of a terrifying encounter with a "sneering" ghost that stalks the coast road between Great Yarmouth and Lowestoft. Drivers have reported seeing this figure and even driving through it.

#details
In 1974, a man driving home from Great Yarmouth to Lowestoft along the old A12 near Corton Long Lane had a chilling experience. His headlights illuminated a figure standing in the road, which he described as having a horrible sneering face with a "flesh and blood" appearance. Despite braking hard, his car went straight through the figure. The man was so unsettled by this encounter that he avoided driving on that stretch of road whenever possible.

#location
The key location is Corton Long Lane near Great Yarmouth in Suffolk.

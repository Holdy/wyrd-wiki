#title
Ghostly Monks of Bury St Edmunds Abbey

#summary
Ghostly monks are said to roam the ruins of the Abbey of Bury St Edmunds on quiet, moonlit nights.

#details
It is said that if visitors are in the Abbey Gardens on a quiet, dark night under a full moon, they can observe ghostly monks going about their daily business just as they did before the Dissolution of the Monasteries.

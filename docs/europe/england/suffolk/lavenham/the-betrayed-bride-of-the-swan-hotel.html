The Betrayed Bride of The Swan Hotel

Summary

A bride who took her own life after her groom left the altar haunts Room 15 of The Swan Hotel.

Details

After a groom left the altar ahead of a planned marriage out of wedlock, the distraught bride took her own life. She is most commonly seen in Room 15, which is the location where she hanged herself.

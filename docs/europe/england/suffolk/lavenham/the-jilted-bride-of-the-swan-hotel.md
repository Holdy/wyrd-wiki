#title
The Jilted Bride of The Swan Hotel

#summary
A jilted housekeeper who hanged herself after being left at the altar haunts The Swan Hotel in Lavenham.

#details
In the 19th century, a housekeeper at The Swan Hotel became pregnant, and though the father promised marriage, he abandoned her at the altar. Distraught, she hanged herself in the hotel's lodgings, and her ghost is frequently sighted by guests woken in the middle of the night.

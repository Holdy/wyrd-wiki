The Shug Monkey of Rendlesham Woods

Summary

A shape-shifting fantastical beast known as the Shug Monkey haunts Rendlesham Woods.

Details

Described as part muscular bear and part enormous ape, the Shug Monkey is a fantastical beast said to inhabit Rendlesham Woods. One witness in the 1960s reported watching in horror as the creature shimmered, transformed its shape into a winged gargoyle, and took flight into the darkness.

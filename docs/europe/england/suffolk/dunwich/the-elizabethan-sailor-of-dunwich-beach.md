#title
The Elizabethan Sailor of Dunwich Beach

#summary
A forlorn Elizabethan sailor's ghost roams Dunwich beach searching for his lost love before boarding a spectral ship.

#details
The melancholy ghost of a brightly-dressed Elizabethan sailor is said to haunt Dunwich beach, eternally looking for his lost love. Once finished searching, he is reportedly seen boarding a spectral ship.

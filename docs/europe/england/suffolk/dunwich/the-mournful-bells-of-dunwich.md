#title
The Mournful Bells of Dunwich

#summary
The ghostly sound of church bells can be heard emanating from beneath the waves off the coast of Dunwich, Britain's lost medieval city.

#details
Dunwich was once one of the largest and most prosperous ports in the country until a series of devastating storm surges between 1286 and 1362 swallowed the town beneath the North Sea. Today, visitors standing on the clifftop can listen out for the mournful sound of church bells ringing from beneath the water.

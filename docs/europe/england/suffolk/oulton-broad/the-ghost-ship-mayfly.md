#title
The Ghost Ship Mayfly
#summary
A phosphorescent white-sailed wherry steered by a skeleton relives a bloody maritime robbery and murder every June.
#details
On the 24th of June, the *Mayfly*, a white-sailed wherry etched in phosphorescence, sails into Oulton Broad with a skeleton at her tiller, trying to make port. Her skipper, the wherryman "Blood" Stephenson, attempted to steal £400,000 being carried in Miss Millicent's trunk from Lowestoft to Great Yarmouth. Once out at sea, a fight broke out between Blood, Miss Millicent, and the mate Bert, resulting in the deaths of Miss Millicent and Blood while Bert escaped in the dinghy. Ever since, only the ghostly wherry has been seen returning.

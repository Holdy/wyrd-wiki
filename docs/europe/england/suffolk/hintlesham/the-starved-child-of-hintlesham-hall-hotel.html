    The Starved Child of Hintlesham Hall Hotel

    Summary

    A young girl starved to death by her stepmother in the 18th century haunts Hintlesham Hall Hotel.

    Details

    Hintlesham Hall Hotel near Ipswich is said to be haunted by the spirit of a young girl who was starved to death by her cruel stepmother when the family owned the building during the 18th century. Her ghostly form continues to roam the hotel grounds and rooms to this day.

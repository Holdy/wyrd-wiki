The Victorian Woman of Somerleyton Hall

Summary

The apparition of a Victorian woman is commonly spotted on the grounds of Somerleyton Hall.

Details

In addition to poltergeist activity, the apparition of a Victorian woman is a common sight across the grounds of Somerleyton Hall, with Room 7 experiencing the most paranormal activity.

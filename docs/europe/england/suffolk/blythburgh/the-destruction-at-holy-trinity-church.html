The Destruction at Holy Trinity Church

Summary

On the same day as the Bungay attack in 1577, a black hound burst into a Blythburgh church with a thunderclap, collapsing the steeple and killing two men.

Details

On August 4, 1577, twelve miles away from Bungay, a great black dog burst through the doors of Holy Trinity Church in Blythburgh amid a mighty clap of thunder that caused the church steeple to collapse. Two men were killed with their necks broken as the beast raced past. Scorch marks attributed to the creature's claws can still be seen on the church's north door today.

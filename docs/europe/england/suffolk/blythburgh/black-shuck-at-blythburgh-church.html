Black Shuck at Blythburgh Church

Summary

A savage hellhound with blood-red eyes burst into Blythburgh's church during a 1577 storm, leaving scorch marks known as the 'Devil's Claws' on the doors.

Details

On August 4, 1577, a terrible storm struck Blythburgh while villagers sheltered inside Holy Trinity Church. The oak doors suddenly burst open, and a savage black dog with blood-red eyes bounded into the building, terrifying the congregation and wreaking havoc. Scorch marks known as the 'Devil's Claws' can still be seen on the church's front doors today.

    The Friendly Ghosts of The Bildeston Crown

    Summary

    The Bildeston Crown is said to be haunted by four friendly ghosts, including a grey lady, two children, and an old man.

    Details

    The Bildeston Crown is claimed to be home to four friendly spirits: a grey lady who waves to guests from an upstairs window, two children who attempt to hold guests' hands, and a ghostly old man who sits quietly in the corner of the bar.

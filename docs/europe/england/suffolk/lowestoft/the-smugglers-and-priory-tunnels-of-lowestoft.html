    The Smugglers' and Priory Tunnels of Lowestoft

    Summary

    Lowestoft is crisscrossed by secret underground tunnels once used for smuggling contraband and connecting the town's high street to the parish church.

    Details

    The town of Lowestoft features a network of secret subterranean passages. One route reportedly links the High Street to the parish church and is likely connected to the mythical priory of St Bartholomew, while another served as a smugglers' tunnel running from the town centre out to the cliffs to bring contraband into old houses and crypts.

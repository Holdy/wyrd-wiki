    Richard Evered at The Bull Hotel

    Summary

    Richard Evered, who was stabbed to death during a 1640s political argument, haunts room 4 of The Bull Hotel.

    Details

    In the 1640s, a political argument at The Bull Hotel grew so heated that Richard Evered was stabbed to death. Ever since his death, his spirit has been reported residing in room 4.

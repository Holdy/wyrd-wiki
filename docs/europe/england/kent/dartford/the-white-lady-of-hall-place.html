The White Lady of Hall Place

Summary

The White Lady of Tower at Hall Place witnessed her husband's murder, died of grief, and is seen near the tower.

Details

The White Lady of Tower is said to have witnessed her husband's murder and died of grief shortly afterward. She is seen near the tower at Hall Place, sometimes looking out the window.

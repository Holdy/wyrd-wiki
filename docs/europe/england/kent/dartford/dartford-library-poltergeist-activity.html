Dartford Library Poltergeist Activity

Summary

Dartford Library experiences falling books, grabbed employees, clothing tugs, and a constant sense of being watched.

Details

Books are said to fall off their shelves at Dartford Library, while employees are grabbed or have their clothing tugged, accompanied by a persistent feeling that someone is always watching.

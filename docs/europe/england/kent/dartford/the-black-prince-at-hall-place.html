The Black Prince at Hall Place

Summary

A figure known as the Black Prince appears at Hall Place and is considered a bad omen.

Details

In addition to the White Lady, a man known as the Black Prince also appears at Hall Place and is regarded as a bad omen, accompanied by various shadow phenomena.

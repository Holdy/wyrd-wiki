The Crying Children of Reculver Towers

Summary

The sounds of crying emanating from Reculver Towers are explained by the skeletons of children uncovered at the site.

Details

Uncovered skeletons of children at Reculver Towers and Roman Fort are believed to explain the disembodied sounds of crying that emanate from the location.

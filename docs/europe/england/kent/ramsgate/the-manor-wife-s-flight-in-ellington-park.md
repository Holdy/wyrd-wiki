#title
The Manor Wife's Flight in Ellington Park
#summary
The wife of a former manor owner who stabbed her husband to death is seen running away in Ellington Park.
#details
The site of Ellington Park was once a manor where the wife of the owner stabbed her husband to death. Legend states that her ghost is still seen running away from someone on the grounds.

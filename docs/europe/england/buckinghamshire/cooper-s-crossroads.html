    Cooper's Crossroads

    Summary

    A highwayman named Cooper is said to haunt the junction of a bridleway near his burial site in Buckinghamshire, continuing his spectral presence with the treasure he amassed during his lifetime.

    Details

    Cooper, a notorious 18th-century highwayman who robbed travelers along Buckinghamshire's roads, was buried at a crossroads marked by two stones. Local legend claims he remains restless, his ghostly form appearing near his grave with the treasure he buried alongside him. Witnesses report seeing a figure at the junction of the bridleway, particularly at night, though the exact nature of his haunting remains tied to his earthly exploits.

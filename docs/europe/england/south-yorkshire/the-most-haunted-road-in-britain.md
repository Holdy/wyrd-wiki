#title
The Most Haunted Road in Britain

#summary
In September 1987, two security guards driving along Stocksbridge Bypass in South Yorkshire encountered a group of young children dancing and singing by the roadside who vanished into thin air. Later, they saw a hooded figure dressed in monk-like garb that also disappeared.

#details
It was late one night in September 1987 when David Goldthorpe and Steven Brooks, two security guards, were driving along Stocksbridge Bypass in South Yorkshire. The road was desolate, with no housing nearby, making their encounter all the more unsettling. They came across a group of young children dancing and singing by the side of the road. Shocked, they stopped the car, but the children had vanished without a trace. The mud beneath their feet showed no sign of any footprints apart from theirs.

Later that night, as they continued along the bypass, they saw a hooded figure standing on Pea Royd Bridge. The figure was dressed in monk-like garb, and when they directed their headlights at it, the beam seemed to pass through the body. As they moved closer, the figure vanished. The two guards, described as down-to-earth South Yorkshire lads, fled the scene in terror.

The incident left a lasting impact on both men, who refused to work at the site again and left the industry shortly afterward. Their boss was bewildered by their reaction, noting that such experienced night security men were not easily spooked. The story gained media attention, with local newspapers reporting on the ghost sightings and police officers feeling a presence in the area.

#location
The key location of this story is Pea Royd Bridge on Stocksbridge Bypass in South Yorkshire.

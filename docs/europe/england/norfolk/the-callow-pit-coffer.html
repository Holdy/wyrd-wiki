The Callow Pit Coffer

Summary

A sunken iron chest filled with treasure lies hidden in a tree-shrouded pond at a remote crossroads, jealously guarded by supernatural forces. Attempts to haul the coffer ashore are thwarted when a single spoken word breaks the required silence, causing the chains to snap and the treasure to vanish forever into the depths.

Details

At a lonely crossroads where the parishes of Cantley, Southwood, and Moulton meet lies Callow Pit, a deep and murky pond shrouded by ancient trees. According to local folklore, a heavy iron coffer containing immense treasure—believed to be hidden by outlaws or smugglers—rested at the bottom of the pit. Determined to claim the wealth, local villagers gathered with a team of white oxen and heavy iron chains to drag the chest from the water. They successfully hooked the coffer and began hauling it toward the surface. However, magical tradition dictated absolute silence during the retrieval; the moment the chest broke near the surface, someone spoke out in excitement. Instantly, the spell was broken, the chains snapped with a deafening crack, and the coffer plunged back down into the dark abyss, never to be seen again.

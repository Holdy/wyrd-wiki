The Ghost Donkey of Honing

Summary

A phantom white donkey that breathes billows of sulphuric smoke from its nostrils is said to haunt the road leading to the parish church in Honing.

Details

In the quiet North Norfolk village of Honing, local legend speaks of an unusual and unsettling apparition that startles travelers after dark. A ghostly white donkey is reported to roam the lonely road leading towards St Peter and St Paul's Church. According to regional folklore accounts, witnesses have encountered the spectral beast standing silently or wandering the lane, exhaling thick, choking clouds of smoke and fire from its nostrils into the night air.

    The Golden Cradle of Pilgrim's Meadow

    Summary

    According to local folklore in East Harling, a priceless golden cradle remains hidden at the bottom of a deep pit once located within Pilgrim's Meadow.

    Details

    In the Norfolk village of East Harling, traditional folklore preserves the legend of a mysterious hidden treasure. Within a field historically known as Pilgrim's Meadow, there was once a deep pit. Local tradition maintains that a solid golden cradle lies buried at the very bottom of this pit. Although the pit has since been filled in and leveled over the years, the tale of the golden cradle has endured as part of the landscape legends of Eastern England.

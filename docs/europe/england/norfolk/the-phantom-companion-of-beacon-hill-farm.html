The Phantom Companion of Beacon Hill Farm

Summary

During the pre-First World War era, a woman walking near Beacon Hill Farm House encountered a spectral black dog that vanished into thin air upon being touched. When she reached down to pat the creature, her hand passed straight through its body.

Details

In the years leading up to the First World War, a woman was walking home near Beacon Hill Farm House in Terrington St. Clement when she noticed a black dog walking quietly by her side. Mistaking the phantom hound for a living animal, she bent down to pet it, only to discover that her hand passed completely through the apparition.

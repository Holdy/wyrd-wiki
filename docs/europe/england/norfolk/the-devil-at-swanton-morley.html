The Devil at Swanton Morley

Summary

According to local folklore, the Devil can be summoned at All Saints Church in Swanton Morley by running around the building at midnight and whistling through the crypt grille or keyhole.

Details

Recorded by folklorist Leslie Valentine Grinsell after speaking with villager Charles Lewton-Braine, the legend dictates that anyone wishing to see the Devil must run around All Saints Church at the stroke of midnight. Once the circumambulation is complete, the person must whistle through the keyhole of the door or through the grille peering into the crypt beneath the chancel, at which point Satan will appear.

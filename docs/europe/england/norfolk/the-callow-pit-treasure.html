The Callow Pit Treasure

Summary

Two local men attempting to salvage a sunken treasure chest from a deep Norfolk pit are thwarted when a mysterious black hand rises from the water to reclaim it.

Details

Legend long maintained that a heavy treasure chest was hidden in the murky depths of Callow Pit, guarded by a headless horseman and supernatural forces. Ignoring the danger, two local men laid ladders across the water and probed the depths with long poles until they struck a hard, iron-bound box. As they successfully hoisted the chest to the surface, one of the men arrogantly boasted that not even the Devil could take the prize from them. Instantly, a thick cloud of sulphurous vapor enveloped them, and a spectral black hand and arm emerged from the water to grab the box. Despite a fierce struggle, the infernal grip dragged the chest back down into the depths, leaving behind only the iron ring attached to their lifting pole, which was later fixed as a door handle on Southwood Church.

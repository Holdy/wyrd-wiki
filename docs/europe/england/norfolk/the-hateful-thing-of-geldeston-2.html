The Hateful Thing of Geldeston

Summary

A woman walking back to Geldeston along the market path with her daughter and her companion encounters a terrifying, shapeshifting beast. The entity trails them before finally disappearing up the sandy lane leading to the churchyard.

Details

While returning to Geldeston from Gillingham along the market path at night, a mother and her daughter—who possessed the gift of second sight from being born during chime hours—encountered a mysterious entity. Initially perceived as a dog, the creature grew larger and shifted shapes as they walked, becoming visible to the mother the moment her daughter touched her. Although the daughter's companion could only hear its heavy thumping footsteps, the two women watched the black, shapeshifting beast pace ahead of them for half a mile until it turned into the sandy lane leading up to St Michael's churchyard.

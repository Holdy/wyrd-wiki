The Phantom Wolf of the She-Wolf

Summary

Queen Isabella, renowned as the She-Wolf of France, haunts the upper chambers of Castle Rising accompanied by the spectral apparition of her pet wolf. Paranormal investigators and visitors have captured photographic evidence of the phantom canine alongside reports of ghostly howls and rustling medieval garments.

Details

Following decades spent residing at Castle Rising in Norfolk, Queen Isabella's spirit is said to remain tied to the fortress where she lived out her later years. During a night-time paranormal investigation in the castle's White Room, researchers captured a full-spectrum photograph depicting the shadowy figure of a medieval queen accompanied by a distinct dog or wolf-shaped entity at her feet. Witnesses have also reported hearing the swishing sound of heavy dresses sweeping across stone staircases and phantom animal howls echoing through the ancient stone keep in the dead of night.

    The Phantom Hound of the Salthouse Road

    Summary

    While bicycling along the road between Salthouse and Kelling on a moonlit night, a local man encountered the terrifying phantom dog known as Black Shuck as it passed straight through a closed gate.

    Details

    On a clear night lit by a full moon, a resident named Tom Stirling was pushing his bicycle along the road leading from Salthouse toward Kelling. Hearing the ominous, distinct sound of rattling chains growing closer behind him, he initially assumed a horse had strayed from the nearby marshes and stepped aside to let it pass. Instead, a massive, shaggy black dog—the legendary hellhound Black Shuck—trotted past him and vanished straight through a closed five-barred gate on the opposite side of the road. When Stirling walked over to investigate, he found the gate still firmly locked and closed. The following day, residents in Kelling confirmed to him that the apparition was none other than Old Shuck, a spectral beast frequently seen roaming the North Norfolk roads.

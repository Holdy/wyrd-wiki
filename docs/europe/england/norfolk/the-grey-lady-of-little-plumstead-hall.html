    The Grey Lady of Little Plumstead Hall

    Summary

    The heartbroken ghost of a former matron, known as the Grey Lady, is said to haunt the grounds of Old Hall in Little Plumstead, wandering near the old stables and duck pond while sobbing.

    Details

    Local legend holds that the Grey Lady was once a matron at the estate when Plumstead Hall served as a hospital. According to the tale, she suffered a tragic fate after her engagement to the estate owner's son was called off, leading to her death by drowning in despair. Finding no rest in death, her weeping, semi-transparent apparition has reportedly been witnessed by staff, visitors, and late-night travelers drifting through the foggy grounds near the stables and duck pond.

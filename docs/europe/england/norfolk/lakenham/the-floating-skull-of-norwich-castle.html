The Floating Skull of Norwich Castle

Summary

A floating skull is among the paranormal phenomena reported at Norwich Castle, a former site of imprisonment and execution.

Details

Because Norwich Castle used to be a place for imprisonment and execution, it is occupied by a number of ghosts, including reported sightings of a floating skull.

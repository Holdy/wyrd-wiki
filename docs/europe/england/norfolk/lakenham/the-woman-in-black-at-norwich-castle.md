#title
The Woman in Black at Norwich Castle

#summary
A woman dressed entirely in black has been recorded haunting Norwich Castle since at least 1820.

#details
Occupying the former prison and execution grounds of Norwich Castle, a ghost of a woman dressed in all black has been recorded since at least 1820 and still appears to this day.

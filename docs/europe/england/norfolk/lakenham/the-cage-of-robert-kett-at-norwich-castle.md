#title
The Cage of Robert Kett at Norwich Castle

#summary
The decomposing remains of rebel Robert Kett are said to haunt Norwich Castle, appearing as a ghost hanging from the castle in a cage.

#details
Among the spirits occupying the former prison and execution site of Norwich Castle are the decomposing remains of rebel Robert Kett, whose ghost apparently hangs from the castle in a cage.

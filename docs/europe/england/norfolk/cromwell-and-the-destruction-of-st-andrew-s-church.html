    Cromwell and the Destruction of St Andrew's Church

    Summary

    According to local folklore in Rocklands, Oliver Cromwell had his men position a cannon at How Furze in neighboring Great Ellingham to bombard and destroy St Andrew's Church in Rockland All Saints.

    Details

    In the Norfolk parish encompassing Rockland All Saints, St Andrew's Church eventually fell into ruin, but local tradition attributes its destruction to a dramatic military assault during the English Civil War. As the story is told, Oliver Cromwell had his troops position a cannon on high ground at How Furze—west of Great Ellingham Hall—and aimed it squarely at Rockland to demolish the church. Local belief in the legend was long reinforced by the discovery of an old cannonball found lying precisely along the direct line of fire between the cannon emplacement and the ruined church.

    The Phantom Newfoundland of Roudham Heath

    Summary

    A motorist driving across Roudham Heath late at night encountered a gigantic, supernatural Newfoundland dog crossing the ancient Peddar's Way. Upon returning home, his mother immediately recognized the apparition as Old Shuck.

    Details

    In the spring of 1962, a service member on leave was driving back from London along the A11 across Roudham Heath around half past midnight. As he approached the intersection where the Roman road Peddar's Way crosses the A11, a massive Newfoundland dog suddenly emerged from the left along the path directly into his line of travel. Expecting a collision, he braced for impact, but there was no crash; instead, the dog simply reappeared on the right side continuing its journey. Unfamiliar with local folklore at the time, he arrived home looking visibly shaken and pale, prompting his mother to declare that he had witnessed Old Shuck. Convinced the entity was benign and possessed two normal eyes rather than a single Cyclopean one, the witness later developed a heightened awareness of other-worldly phenomena.

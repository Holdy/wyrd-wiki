#title
The Reading Ghost of Felbrigg Hall
#summary
A resident who died trying to save books from a fire can still be seen sitting in the library reading.
#details
When a fire broke out at Felbrigg Hall in 1809, William Windham III died trying to rescue precious books from the library. His ghost is sometimes spotted sitting in the library and reading books he did not read during his life.

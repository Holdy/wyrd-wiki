William Windham III, the Phantom Bibliophile of Felbrigg Hall

Summary

William Windham III, who died after trying to rescue books from a fire, is said to return to Felbrigg Hall's library to read.

Details

Felbrigg Hall is a 17th-century country house haunted by the ghost of William Windham III, known as the phantom bibliophile. William reportedly risked his life around 200 years ago trying to rescue books from a fire, succumbing to his injuries weeks later. Legend says he still visits his extensive library to read unread books, and placing an exact combination of books on the library chair will cause his ghost to appear.

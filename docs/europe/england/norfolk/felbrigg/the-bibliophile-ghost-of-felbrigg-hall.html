The Bibliophile Ghost of Felbrigg Hall

Summary

The ghost of bibliophile William Windham III can be seen poring over books in the Gothic library of Felbrigg Hall.

Details

William Windham III, a bibliophile who died in 1810 after rescuing books from a fire in a friend's library, haunts Felbrigg Hall. His spectre can be seen in the Hall's Gothic library poring over his books, not far from where he was buried in the family vaults at Felbrigg Church.

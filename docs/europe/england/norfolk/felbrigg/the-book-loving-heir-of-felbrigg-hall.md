#title
The Book-Loving Heir of Felbrigg Hall
#summary
William Windham, who died trying to rescue books from a fire, haunts Felbrigg Hall library where he sits and reads.
#details
After inheriting Felbrigg Hall in 1749 and building a library, William Windham died in 1809 from injuries sustained while trying to rescue precious books from a burning London house. His passion for books continues in death, and people frequently report seeing him sitting in his library reading.

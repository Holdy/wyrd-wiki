The Phantom Bibliophile of Felbrigg Hall

Summary

The ghost of William Windham III visits the library at Felbrigg Hall where he died trying to rescue his precious book collection from a fire.

Details

The phantom bibliophile William Windham III is believed to visit the library where he perished trying to save his precious volumes during a fire. According to lore, his ghost will only manifest when an exact combination of books is placed upon the library chair.

    The Headless Ghost of Anne Boleyn at Blickling Hall

    Summary

    On the anniversary of her execution, the headless ghost of Anne Boleyn is said to return to Blickling Hall in a coach driven by a headless horseman.

    Details

    Anne Boleyn was beheaded in 1536 on the orders of her husband Henry VIII after she failed to provide an heir to the throne. On the anniversary of her execution, her headless ghost reportedly returns to the residence in a coach drawn by a headless horseman, with Anne carrying her own head on her lap.

#title
The Rattling Chains of Acle Bridge
#summary
Chains can be heard rattling on Acle Bridge at a certain time of year where a man was allegedly hanged in chains.
#details
Legend has it that a man was hanged in chains from Acle Bridge. At a certain time of the year, people are said to be able to hear the chilling sound of those chains rattling against the bridge.

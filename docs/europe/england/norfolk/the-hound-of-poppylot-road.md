#title
The Hound of Poppylot Road

#summary
A cyclist traveling along Poppylot Road at midnight in the 1930s encountered a massive black hound with glowing green eyes that was dragging a heavy rattling chain.

#details
In the 1930s, a witness was cycling along Poppylot Road between Feltwell and Southery at approximately midnight on a clear, moonlit night. Two green eyes suddenly appeared in the beam of their front bicycle light, revealing a large black dog running directly towards them in the center of the road. The oversized creature was dragging a heavy, rattling chain behind it, but maintained its course and completely ignored the cyclist as they passed by.

#location
Poppylot Road situated between Feltwell and Southery in Norfolk.

The Shrieking Pits of Aylmerton

Summary

A weeping, white-clad phantom of a woman wanders among ancient woodland pits near Aylmerton, endlessly wailing in search of her murdered baby.

Details

In the wooded slopes close to Gresham Cross in Aylmerton, five shallow pits—originally medieval iron ore workings—are known locally as the Shrieking Pits. According to local folklore, these depressions are haunted by the spectral figure of a tall, willowy woman dressed in white. The legend tells that her jealous husband murdered their infant child, convinced the baby was not his, and buried the infant in a pit west of Aylmerton church before turning on and killing his wife. Ever since, her ghost wanders between the pits, wringing her hands and uttering piercing, heartbreaking cries as she searches eternally for her lost child.

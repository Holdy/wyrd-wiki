#title
The Burial of the Fiddler

#summary
A musician and his cart are said to lie interred beneath a tree-shrouded earthwork known as Fiddler's Hill.

#details
Local legend maintains that a fiddler and his cart are buried beneath Fiddler's Hill, a mysterious tree-covered earthwork on the parish boundary. Whether regarded as an ancient burial mound, a boundary bank, or former archery butts, the site carries the dark folklore of the musician and his transport being buried together.

#location
Fiddler's Hill is located at grid reference TG22262005 on the Stratton Strawless side of the boundary with Hainford.

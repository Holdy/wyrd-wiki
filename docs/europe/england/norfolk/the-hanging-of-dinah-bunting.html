The Hanging of Dinah Bunting

Summary

According to local Norfolk folklore, Dinah's Lane in Spooner Row derives its name from a woman named Dinah Bunting who was hanged from a tree for theft.

Details

Local folklore in the Norfolk village of Spooner Row tells the grim tale of Dinah Bunting, a local woman after whom Dinah's Lane (historically also known as Wick Lane or Bunting Lane) is traditionally believed to be named. Situated near the medieval moated site of Wick Hall, the country lane is associated with oral traditions recorded by local historians and the Wymondham Heritage Society, which state that Dinah was condemned and hanged from a tree alongside the lane for the crime of stealing.

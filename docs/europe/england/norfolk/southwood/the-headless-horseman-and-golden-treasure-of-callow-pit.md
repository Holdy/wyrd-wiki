#title
The Headless Horseman and Golden Treasure of Callow Pit
#summary
Callow Pit in Southwood is said to conceal sunken gold protected by a headless horseman and a supernatural black hand that rises through sulphurous smoke.
#details
Callow Pit, a gloomy pond on the boundary of Moulton St Mary and Cantley in Southwood, served as a hiding place for smugglers and features a history of unease with locals. Patrolled by the ghost of a headless horseman, the pond allegedly holds a large quantity of sunken gold. One legend recounts that when foolish men tried to pull the treasure chest to the surface, a thick cloud of sulphurous smoke enveloped them as a black hand and arm rose through the water to reclaim it.

The Devil's Chest

Summary

As treasure seekers almost pulled a chest of gold from the water, someone spoke, prompting a black hand to emerge.

Details

Treasure hunters managed to get a chest of gold almost completely out of the water in a joint effort. However, someone spoke a word and broke the silence, causing a terrifying black hand to rise up and thwart the recovery.

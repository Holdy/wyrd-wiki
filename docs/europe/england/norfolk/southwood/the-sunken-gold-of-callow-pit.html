The Sunken Gold of Callow Pit

Summary

Two men attempted to pull an iron chest of gold from Callow Pit, but a supernatural hand emerged from the water and wrestled it away after one of them boasted.

Details

An iron chest full of gold supposedly lay at the bottom of Callow Pit between Southwood and Moulton parishes. When water levels dropped in 1855, two men laid ladders across the pit as a platform, hooked a staff through the chest ring, and hoisted it out. When one man boasted that the Devil himself could not take it from them, a cloud of sulphurous smoke surrounded them, and a hand and arm thrust out of the water to wrestle the chest back into the depths. The men only managed to keep the iron ring from the lid, which was attached to a church door.

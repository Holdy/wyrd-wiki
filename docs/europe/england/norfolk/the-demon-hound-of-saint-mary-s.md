#title
The Demon Hound of Saint Mary's

#summary
The abandoned ruins of an eleventh-century church in Fulmodeston are haunted by Black Shuck, a terrifying demon hound said to roam near the site. Local lore also speaks of an unburied coffin beneath the rubble whose occupant wanders the derelict grounds.

#details
Standing at the end of a dirt track five miles east of Fakenham, the ivy-wreathed ruins of Saint Mary's Church in Fulmodeston have been abandoned since 1882. Over the centuries, the hollowed-out medieval husk has gathered chilling local legends, including reports of Black Shuck—the legendary demon hound of East Anglia—being sighted near the site. Alongside sightings of the spectral black dog, tales persist of an unburied coffin remaining beneath the rubble, with its restless occupant said to haunt the decaying nave and chancel.

#location
The ruins of Saint Mary's Church are located at the end of a dirt track in the village of Fulmodeston, Norfolk, UK.

#title
The Spectral Squash Players of RAF Bircham Newton
#summary
Three squash fanatics keep their promise to meet on the court after death at RAF Bircham Newton.
#details
A squash ball is regularly heard as three squash fanatics keep their promise to meet on the courts after death at RAF Bircham Newton, and occasionally one of them appears in uniform to watch others play.

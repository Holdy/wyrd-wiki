#title
The Wandering Airmen of RAF Bircham Newton
#summary
The ghosts of airmen who died at RAF Bircham Newton wander the rooms touching sleeping women and pushing men into mattresses.
#details
Dead airmen who never returned from the 1940s wander the rooms of RAF Bircham Newton looking for sleeping women whom they gently touch, while young men are often pushed firmly into their mattresses.

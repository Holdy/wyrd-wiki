Spectral Squash Games at Bircham Newton

Summary

Students at a former RAF base heard the sounds of a boisterous squash game coming from locked, dark courts where US Airmen once agreed to play before a fatal mission.

Details

At the old RAF base in Bircham Newton, returning students heard a lively squash game inside the locked and darkened courts at night. Legend says US Airmen had arranged to play there after a bombing mission from which they never physically returned, suggesting their ghosts finally played their match.

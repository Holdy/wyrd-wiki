The Fiddling Ghost of Binham Priory

Summary

A local fiddler who vanished when priory tunnels collapsed can still be heard playing violin music from underground.

Details

Local fiddler Jimmy Griggs took his dog to check out the Binham Priory tunnels after they collapsed during a storm. The tunnels completely collapsed, and though the dog escaped, Griggs was never seen again; violin music can still be heard coming from the tunnels even though he did not take his violin inside.

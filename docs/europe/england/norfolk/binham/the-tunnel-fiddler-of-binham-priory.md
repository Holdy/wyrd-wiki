#title
The Tunnel Fiddler of Binham Priory
#summary
A fiddler tracing the footsteps of a ghostly monk into the ruins of Binham Priory vanished into the tunnels, leaving only his terrified and shaking dog to emerge.
#details
A fiddler entered a tunnel under the Benedictine ruins of Binham Priory to trace the footsteps of a ghostly monk, potentially the 'mad monk' buried in chains. As he played his fiddle to track his movement, the music abruptly stopped, and only the dog emerged from the tunnel terrified and shaking.

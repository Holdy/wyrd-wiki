#title
The Disappearing Fiddler of Fiddler's Hill
#summary
A musician who walked into a subterranean tunnel playing his violin vanished entirely, leaving only a terrified dog.
#details
A fiddler attempted to trace an underground tunnel from Binham to Little Walsingham by walking through it and playing his music while people tracked him from above. Accompanied by a dog, his music stopped at Fiddler's Hill, where the dog reappeared shaking with fear while the fiddler was never seen again.

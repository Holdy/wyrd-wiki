The Ghost Fiddler of Binham Priory

Summary

A local fiddler named Jimmy Griggs entered the tunnel near Binham Priory playing his fiddle and was never seen again, his music still heard at midnight.

Details

After a section of the tunnel near Binham Priory caved in, a local fiddler named Jimmy Griggs ventured inside with his dog Trap, playing his fiddle as he walked until the music abruptly stopped. Later that night, a great storm caused the tunnel entrance to collapse completely, and Jimmy was never seen again, though his mysterious music is sometimes heard playing at midnight.

The Black Monk of Binham Priory

Summary

A black monk is reported to haunt the grounds at night, emerging from an underground tunnel linking the priory to Walsingham.

Details

A black monk reportedly haunts the grounds of Binham Priory at night, emerging from an underground tunnel that links the priory to the shrine at Walsingham three miles away.

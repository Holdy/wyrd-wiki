The Nanny at the Ship Hotel

Summary

The Ship Hotel in Brancaster is said to be haunted by the ghost of a young Lord Nelson’s nanny.

Details

The Ship Hotel in Brancaster bears a spectral reputation, reportedly haunted by the nanny who cared for the young Lord Nelson during his early years.

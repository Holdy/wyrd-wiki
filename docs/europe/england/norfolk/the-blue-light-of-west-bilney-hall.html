The Blue Light of West Bilney Hall

Summary

A gamekeeper patrolling the grounds of West Bilney Hall late at night was startled by a mysterious and intensely bright blue light flying close to his face.

Details

In 1897, a gamekeeper working at West Bilney Hall near King's Lynn was out late at night performing his duties, specifically stopping up fox-earths. During his nocturnal patrol, he encountered a strange and exceptionally bright blue light that suddenly darted through the dark and flew close to his face, leaving him deeply startled.

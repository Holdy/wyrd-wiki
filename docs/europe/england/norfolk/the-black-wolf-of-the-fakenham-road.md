#title
The Black Wolf of the Fakenham Road

#summary
A driver traveling between Fakenham and Norwich encountered a large black wolf that locked eyes with him while feeding on carrion.

#details
While driving along the road connecting Fakenham and Norwich, a motorist encountered a strange black wolf—potentially related to Black Shuck. The creature slowly and deliberately looked up from the carrion it was eating, locked its gaze onto the driver's eyes, and then calmly returned to its kill.

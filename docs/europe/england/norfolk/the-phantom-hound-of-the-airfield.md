#title
The Phantom Hound of the Airfield

#summary
During the early months of the Second World War, an airman on duty near Methwold encountered a colossal supernatural hound lurking amongst the trees. The terrifying apparition was recognized by servicemen as a massive, otherworldly beast.

#details
In the winter of 1939 to 1940, shortly after the establishment of the military airfield just south of Methwold, wartime operations were accompanied by eerie local encounters. An airman walking near the base perimeter spotted a gigantic phantom dog among the surrounding trees. Struck by its unnatural appearance and immense scale, he described the apparition as a huge supernatural beast before it vanished into the wintry gloom.

#location
The encounter took place in the wooded perimeter surrounding the former Methwold Airfield, situated just south of the village of Methwold in Norfolk.

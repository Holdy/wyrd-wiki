#title
The Curse of the Stockton Stone
#summary
Moving a glacial megalith by the A146 in the 1930s supposedly triggered a fatal curse that brought sudden death to one of the workmen involved.
#details
The Stockton Stone is an oblong sandstone glacial megalith on the west side of the A146 road that is said to guard a curse of misfortune or death for anyone who moves it. When road-straightening work in the 1930s shifted the stone diagonally eastwards by about 14 feet, locals viewed the disturbance with misgiving, which seemed confirmed when one of the workmen involved suddenly collapsed and died.

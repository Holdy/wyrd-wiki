The White Lady of Scole Inn

Summary

The historic Scole Inn is haunted by the ghost of Emma, a young woman who was murdered by her jealous husband in the 1750s.

Details

Dating back to 1655, the Scole Inn was built as a grand coaching inn on the Norfolk and Suffolk border and has accumulated numerous legends over the centuries. Among its most famous spectral residents is Emma, often referred to as the White Lady of Scole. According to local lore, Emma was staying at the inn during the 1750s when she was murdered by her husband in a fit of jealous rage. Visitors and staff have reported sightings of her tearful yet friendly apparition. The inn's folklore also includes accounts of a highwayman who allegedly rode his horse up the building's grand staircase to escape capture.

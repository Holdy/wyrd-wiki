Mother Chergrave and the Graveyard Love Charm

Summary

Mother Chergrave was a 19th-century self-professed witch and charmer in southeast Norfolk who sold graveyard love charms and attempted to pass demonic familiars to her daughter. Her story illustrates the persistence of traditional witchcraft beliefs into the Victorian era.

Details

Operating in the Loddon and Chedgrave area until her death in the early 1870s, Mother Chergrave made a living as a service magician and charmer, notably selling a verse charm that promised to reveal the name of a future husband at the cost of sacrificing the user's final year of earth-life. She had a single daughter, Mara, whom she declared was bound to Satan. On her deathbed, Mother Chergrave handed Mara a box containing bat-winged imps, instructing her to let them bite her breasts to become an even greater witch. However, immediately after her mother's death, Mara threw the unopened box onto the fire and subsequently married.

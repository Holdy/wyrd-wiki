    The Secret Passage of Besthorpe Hall

    Summary

    A subterranean passage connecting Besthorpe Hall to All Saints Church was allegedly used by the Catholic Harbord family during 16th-century religious persecutions.

    Details

    Local legend maintains that an underground tunnel runs across the fields between Besthorpe Hall and All Saints Church. During the 16th century, when Catholics in England faced severe repressions and legal prohibitions for practicing their faith, this hidden subterranean passage was reportedly utilized by the resident Harbord family to move secretly between their manor house and the church without detection.

#title
The Grey Lady of the Maids Head Hotel

#summary
A ghostly woman dressed in grey takes sedate walks through the 13th-century Maids Head Hotel, accompanied by the strong scent of lavender.

#details
Visitors and guests at the historic 13th-century Maids Head Hotel in Norwich report the presence of a woman in grey. Her gentle walks through the atmospheric rooms and corridors are often heralded by a powerful fragrance of lavender before she is even seen.

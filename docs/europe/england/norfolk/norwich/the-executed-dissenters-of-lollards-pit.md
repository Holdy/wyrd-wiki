#title
The Executed Dissenters of Lollards Pit
#summary
The screams of religious dissidents who were burned to death at a former chalk pit during the 15th and 16th centuries can still be heard near Riverside. Witches have also been witnessed crossing the nearby bridge.
#details
Lollards Pit, located at the site of the Lollards pub on Riverside, served as a chalk pit and a burial ground during the 15th and 16th centuries where individuals were burned to death for differing religious beliefs, including approximately 50 people during the reign of Queen Mary. Prisoners were forced to walk the streets and across Bishopgate Bridge to their deaths while crowds watched. Today, the screams of those burned people are reportedly still heard, and witches are said to be seen crossing the bridge.

#title
The Buried King of Norwich Castle

#summary
Legend holds that the hill beneath Norwich Castle conceals the grave of an ancient king buried alongside a vast hoard of silver and gold.

#details
Local folklore suggests that the very hill upon which Norwich Castle proudly stands conceals the secret burial site of an ancient king, who rests alongside his treasure hoard of silver and gold.

#title
The Wild German Boy of the Wild Man Pub
#summary
A local legend claimed that the Wild Man pub was haunted by a wild German boy who died in a cellar fire after running across the bar area.
#details
Claims surrounding the pub alleged that the building formerly served as an asylum and was haunted by a wild German boy who was seen running across the bar area before disappearing, with barrels heard moving in the cellar where he supposedly died in a fire. However, historical research notes that the historical figure Peter the Wild Boy actually survived until age 73 elsewhere, debunking the local pub folklore.

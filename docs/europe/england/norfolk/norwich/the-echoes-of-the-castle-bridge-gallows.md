#title
The Echoes of the Castle Bridge Gallows
#summary
Paranormal investigators conducting psychometry on the old castle bridge at Castle Mall experienced oppressive physical sensations and visions connected to past executions.
#details
During a charity public investigation by Npeg at Castle Mall—which is built upon former castle grounds—investigators accessed the surviving castle bridge and performed psychometry. They picked up sensory impressions of black flies, jeering crowds, skeletal hands glowing in green light, fuzzy heads, and heavy, wobbly legs, believed to be residual psychic energy from the hangings that historically took place on the bridge.

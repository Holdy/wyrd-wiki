#title
The Mournful Mayor in the Maids Head Courtyard
#summary
A former mayor has been witnessed walking around the courtyard of the Maids Head Hotel while shaking his head in distress.
#details
In addition to the resident maid, a phantom former mayor is frequently seen pacing the courtyard of the Maids Head Hotel with a troubled expression, continually shaking his head.

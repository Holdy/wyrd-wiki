#title
The Highwayman and Tumbling Bottles of the Coachmakers Arms
#summary
The Coachmakers Arms pub is haunted by falling bottles and glasses, a woman in black descending the stairs, and a phantom highwayman standing at the end of the bar.
#details
Dating back to at least 1802, the Coachmakers Arms experiences unexplained phenomena where bottles and glasses routinely fall from the shelves. Additionally, witnesses have reported seeing a woman in black walking down the stairs and a shadowy figure resembling a highwayman standing motionless at the end of the bar.

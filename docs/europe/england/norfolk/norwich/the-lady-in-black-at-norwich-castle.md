#title
The Lady in Black at Norwich Castle

#summary
Once a prison and execution site, Norwich Castle is haunted by the floating rotten corpse of rebel leader Robert Kett and an apparition known as the Lady in Black.

#details
Having served as a prison that witnessed public hangings and executions, Norwich Castle is haunted by multiple spirits. Among them is rebel leader Robert Kett, whose rotten corpse is often seen floating outside the castle at night. However, the most frequently sighted apparition is the Lady in Black, who has been seen wandering and floating through the air around the castle grounds.

#title
The Gildencroft Bogey

#summary
In the 1880s at Gildencroft on the outskirts of Norwich, a large, hairy creature with glowing tea-saucer eyes, sharp teeth, and noxious breath was spotted lurking in the shadows.

#details
In the 1880s, on the outskirts of Norwich where knights once practiced their jousting at Gildencroft, a strange creature was spotted lurking in the shadows. A witness described the beast as big and hairy, with eyes glowing in the dark as big as tea-saucers, big sharp teeth, and a most noxious breath. The threat of this bogeyman was long remembered by local people as a warning for children to behave.

#title
The Golden Mound of King Gurgunt
#summary
King Gurgunt, who allegedly built a Roman-era castle on the site, is said to sit atop the Norwich Castle mound guarding a table of silver and gold.
#details
The oldest legend associated with Norwich Castle concerns King Gurgunt, who allegedly constructed an early castle during Roman times. Upon his death, the castle mound was built for him, and folklore holds that his spirit still sits on the mound holding a sword next to a table piled high with silver and gold.

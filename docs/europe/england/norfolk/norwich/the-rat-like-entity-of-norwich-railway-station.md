#title
The Rat-Like Entity of Norwich Railway Station
#summary
Norwich Railway Station is haunted by an unusual entity that takes the form of a rat-like creature with bad breath and large teeth.
#details
Unlike typical human ghosts reported elsewhere in the city, an entity at Norwich Railway Station uniquely forms into a rat-like creature characterized by exceptionally bad breath and large teeth.

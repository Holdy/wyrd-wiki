#title
The Vanishing Woman in Black at Norwich Castle
#summary
A woman in a black Victorian dress—first sighted by terrified prisoners in 1820—wanders the art exhibition area and floats around the grounds of Norwich Castle.
#details
The most frequently sighted ghost at Norwich Castle is a woman wearing a black dress and Victorian-style clothing. First documented in 1820 by several prisoners who were terrified by the encounter, she is seen wandering the museum's art exhibition area—which formerly served as the gaol—where she turns corners and vanishes entirely. The same female figure has also been observed floating around the castle grounds.

#title
The Grey Maid of the Maids Head Hotel
#summary
A 17th-century maid wearing grey haunts the Maids Head Hotel, rattling keys and leaving a lingering scent of stale lavender.
#details
Known as the Lady in Grey or the Grey Maid, a middle-aged woman wearing a 17th-century maid's uniform haunts the Maids Head Hotel, rattling keys and leaving behind a lingering smell of stale lavender.

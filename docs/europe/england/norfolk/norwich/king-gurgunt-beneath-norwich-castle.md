#title
King Gurgunt beneath Norwich Castle

#summary
King Gurgunt, the semi-mythical founder of Norwich, is said to lie buried beneath the mound of Norwich Castle, waiting to rise again if Britain faces dire peril.

#details
According to local myth, King Gurgunt, Norwich’s semi-mythical founder, is buried beneath the mound of Norwich Castle. The legend states that he lies sleeping there, ready to rise again should Britain ever face dire peril.

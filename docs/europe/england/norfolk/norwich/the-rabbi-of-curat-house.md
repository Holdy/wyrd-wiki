#title
The Rabbi of Curat House
#summary
Following a building extension, the ghost of a former rabbi named Mr. Isaacs—believed to have killed his wife—has been seen in Curat House.
#details
Since a building extension was completed at Curat House in the city centre, the ghost of a former rabbi, Mr. Isaacs, has been witnessed. Local lore suggests he may have murdered his wife during his lifetime.

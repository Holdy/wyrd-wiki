#title
The Vanishing Wanderer of Rosary Cemetery
#summary
A visitor photographing ruined gravestones in Rosary Cemetery on a late November afternoon saw someone walking towards them, only for the person to vanish without a trace and without appearing in any photos.
#details
On a Saturday afternoon in late November, a visitor taking photographs of old ruined gravestones in Rosary Road Cemetery saw a figure wandering between the stones and walking straight toward them. Standing up to speak to the person, the visitor found nobody there, and the individual could not have left the open area unobserved. Upon returning home, the visitor checked the photographs, but no figure—real or spiritual—had been captured on film.

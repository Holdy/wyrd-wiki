#title
The Hanging Landlord of the Reindeer Pub
#summary
Staff at the Reindeer Pub have encountered cold breezes, rolling paper driven by invisible wind, white mist behind the bar, and phantom laughter, linked to a former landlord who hanged himself.
#details
Workers resting after a shift at the Reindeer Pub on Dereham Road observed a cold breeze, a screwed-up piece of paper rolling across the bar as if blown by wind, and a white misty shape walking behind the bar at the exact same pace. When the pub was closed, staff often heard the voices of elderly men talking and laughing around the table near the bar. Later historical research initiated by a skeptical visitor revealed that a previous landlord had hanged himself behind the bar from the trapdoor leading to the cellar.

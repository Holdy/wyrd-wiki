#title
The Disemboweler of Bishopgate
#summary
The spirit of William Sewart, who confessed to murdering and dismembering his wife in 1842, is seen carrying a bucket or a decapitated head in the Bishopgate area. His wife's head was never recovered after he scattered her body parts across Norwich.
#details
In 1842, body parts making up a whole body up to the neck were discovered across Norwich over five nights, following an unhappy marriage between William and Martha Sewart. Seventeen years later, in 1859, William turned himself in to an east end police station and confessed that Martha had hit her head during an argument. Fearing exposure because he was known to beat her, he cut her into pieces, boiled her on the stove, and disposed of her parts around Norwich, though he never revealed the location of her head. The spirit of William Sewart has since been seen in the Bishopgate area carrying either a bucket or a decapitated head.

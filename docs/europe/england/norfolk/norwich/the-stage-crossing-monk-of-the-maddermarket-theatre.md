#title
The Stage-Crossing Monk of the Maddermarket Theatre
#summary
A friendly monk ghost originating from old chapel confession boxes haunts the Maddermarket Theatre, where he manipulates props, saves actors from hazards, and offers comforting hugs.
#details
Built on a site containing former Roman Catholic chapel confession boxes, the Maddermarket Theatre is haunted by a monk. A director during rehearsals once saw the monk walk from an empty confession box across the stage, and he has been seen and felt repeatedly in the alleyway, building, and courtyard. Credited with opening doors, hiding wigs, and moving costumes, the monk is also credited with benevolent acts: he once saved a young actress from falling lights and hugged an actor who forgot his lines.

#title
The Executed Wife-Murderer of Botolph Street
#summary
The ghost of Robert Watts is seen outside his house on Botolph Street after he was executed there in 1701 for murdering his wife.
#details
In 1701, Robert Watts murdered his wife and was subsequently executed outside his home. Ever since, his ghostly figure has been reported on Botolph Street.

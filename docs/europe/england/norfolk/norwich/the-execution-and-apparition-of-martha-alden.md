#title
The Execution and Apparition of Martha Alden
#summary
After murdering her husband in Attleborough in 1807 and being executed before a rowdy crowd, Martha Alden's ghost was seen haunting Norwich Castle Hill.
#details
On July 18, 1807, Martha Alden murdered her husband in Attleborough by decapitating him with a billhook, hiding his body in a garden ditch before moving it to a common pond. Arrested and claiming she acted as a battered wife defending herself against an abusive alcoholic, she was hanged on July 31, 1807, before a rowdy crowd on a hurdle as a mark of shame, after which her body was dissected. Though the enraged Attleborough crowd burned down her former cottage, a ghostly figure identified as Martha appeared on Norwich Castle hill just days later.

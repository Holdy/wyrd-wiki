#title
King Gurgunt's Slumber Under Norwich Castle

#summary
King Gurgunt, the mythical founder of Norwich, is said to sleep fully armed beneath Norwich Castle alongside his vast treasures.

#details
Norwich is said to have been founded by the mythical King Gurgunt, who is buried deep beneath the hill where Norwich Castle stands. During Queen Elizabeth I's royal progress through Norwich in 1578, it was alleged that he tried to step forward from the crowd to speak to her, but a sudden rain shower forced the Queen to run for shelter. To this day, the legend claims he remains sleeping under the hill, fully armed and ready for battle, surrounded by scattered gold and silver treasures.

#title
The Monastic Chants of Dragon Hall
#summary
Visitors and investigators at Dragon Hall, located near a former Augustine Friary, can hear residual chanting and feel the residential energy of medieval monks in the lower cellars.
#details
Dragon Hall, a medieval former trading hall situated near a former Augustine Friary, retains residual residential energy from monks. Investigators have noted that ethereal chanting can be distinctly heard echoing in the lower cellars.

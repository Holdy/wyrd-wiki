#title
The Silent Watchers in Cathedral Close Doorways
#summary
A teenager cycling through Cathedral Close late at night frequently witnessed silent human shapes standing in the doorways of houses near the cloister.
#details
While cycling home late at night after finishing work in the city, a teenager regularly observed shadowy human shapes standing motionless in the doorways of houses near the cloisters, appearing as ordinary people waiting on their doorsteps.

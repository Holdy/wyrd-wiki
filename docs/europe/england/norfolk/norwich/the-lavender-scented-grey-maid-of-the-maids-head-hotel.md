#title
The Lavender-Scented Grey Maid of the Maids Head Hotel
#summary
A former maid dressed in grey, accompanied by the distinct scent of lavender, haunts the halls of the Maids Head Hotel.
#details
Staff and guests at the Maids Head Hotel report encounters with a former maid wearing grey period clothing who wanders the premises, leaving a lingering trail of lavender scent in her wake.

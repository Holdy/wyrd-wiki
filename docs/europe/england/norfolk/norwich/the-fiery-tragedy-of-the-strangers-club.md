#title
The Fiery Tragedy of the Strangers Club
#summary
Phantom footsteps are heard upstairs at the Strangers Club, commemorating a historical 1507 fire where a father perished after lowering his family from a window.
#details
The Strangers Club on Elm Hill, rebuilt by Mayor Augustine Steward in the 1400s and once home to the Paston family, was the site of a devastating fire in 1507 where a family was trapped upstairs. The husband managed to lower his wife and children to safety from the window before perishing in the flames, and phantom footsteps are still heard upstairs.

#title
The Vanishing Woman Walking to Pulls Ferry
#summary
An eyewitness walking near Cathedral Close watched an elderly woman walking ahead of him vanish entirely into thin air.
#details
An account from a resident's father recalls walking home early one evening from town towards Pulls Ferry. An elderly woman was walking in front of him, but before his eyes, she simply faded and disappeared completely.

#title
The Indescribable Prison Haunting at Norwich Castle

#summary
Early nineteenth-century prisoners held at Norwich Castle were terrified nearly to death by an unknown, "indescribable" entity.

#details
In the early part of the nineteenth century, when Norwich Castle still served as a prison, inmates were reported to have been "scared half to death" by an entity that was officially recorded as being "indescribable."

#title
The Lavender-Scented Spirit of The Maids Head Hotel
#summary
A former woman whose presence is detected by the smell of lavender is one of the most commonly reported spirits at The Maids Head Hotel.
#details
The Maids Head Hotel is known for reports of two primary spirits. One of these is a woman whose presence is frequently detected by the distinct smell of lavender.

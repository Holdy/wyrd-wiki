#title
The Courtyard Mayor of The Maids Head Hotel
#summary
An elderly man thought to be a former mayor of the city haunts the courtyard of The Maids Head Hotel.
#details
Alongside the lavender-scented spirit, the ghost of an elderly man is frequently reported in the courtyard of The Maids Head Hotel and is believed to be the spirit of a former mayor of the city.
#location
The Maids Head Hotel, Norwich, Norfolk, England

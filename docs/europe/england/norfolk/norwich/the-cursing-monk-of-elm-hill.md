#title
The Cursing Monk of Elm Hill
#summary
A 19th-century monk who cursed his opponents with eternal damnation is reportedly seen walking along Elm Hill holding a bible and cursing passersby.
#details
In the 1800s, Father Ignatius founded a monastery on Elm Hill and cursed anyone who opposed him with eternal damnation before locals forced him out of the city. He is said to still walk along the hill clutching a bible and cursing those who pass by.
#location
Elm Hill, Norwich, Norfolk, England

#title
The Headless Horseman of Cow Tower

#summary
A spectral headless horseman is said to appear near Cow Tower at dusk every Christmas Eve as church bells toll midnight.

#details
By the riverside near Cow Tower, a Christmas Eve legend tells of a headless horseman. Locals gather at dusk hoping to catch a glimpse of the spectral rider, who is said to appear precisely as church bells toll midnight.

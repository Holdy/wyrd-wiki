#title
The Swinging Cage of Robert Kett
#summary
The ghost of rebel leader Robert Kett is reported to dangle from the top of Norwich Castle in a rotting, swinging cage.
#details
Following his rebellion, Robert Kett's ghost is reportedly seen hanging from the top of Norwich Castle, trapped inside a swinging cage where his body appears to rot.

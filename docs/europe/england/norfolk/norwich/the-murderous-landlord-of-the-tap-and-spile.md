#title
The Murderous Landlord of the Tap and Spile
#summary
Poltergeist activity and phantom footsteps at the Tap and Spile pub on Oak Street are attributed to a former landlord who murdered a prostitute.
#details
At the Tap and Spile on Oak Street, a former landlord committed a murder by killing a prostitute. Following his crimes, ongoing poltergeist disturbances and unexplained footsteps inside the establishment are attributed to his lingering presence.

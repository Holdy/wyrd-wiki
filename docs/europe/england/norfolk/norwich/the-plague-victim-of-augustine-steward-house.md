#title
The Plague Victim of Augustine Steward House
#summary
The ghost of a plague victim's daughter who resorted to cannibalism before choking to death is seen at Augustine Steward House, St George’s Church, and a local pub.
#details
During an outbreak of the plague, an entire household in Augustine Steward House perished, leading officials to board up the house and paint a red cross on the door. Four weeks later, gravediggers opened the house to collect bodies and discovered that one of the daughters had tried to survive by attempting to eat members of her family, leaving bite marks before she tragically choked to death. Her ghost is subsequently seen at Augustine Steward House, Tombland Alley, St George’s Church, and the Edith Cavell pub.

#title
The Haunting and Murder of Lord Sheffield at the Adam and Eve Pub
#summary
Staff at Norwich's oldest operating pub have experienced poltergeist activity, phantom touches, and a ghostly hand holding a head, believed to be connected to Lord Sheffield who was murdered nearby in 1549.
#details
Operating since at least 1249, the Adam and Eve pub has been known for hauntings since 1549, with staff reporting hair-pulling, shoulder taps, temperature drops, self-ringing bells, footsteps, and dancing tankards. In 2006, a man sitting in the car park saw a ghostly hand holding a head. The haunting is linked to Lord Sheffield, who commanded 500 troops during Robert Kett's rebellion, surrendered at Bishopgate, and was struck with a cleaver by a butcher named Foulkes before dying at the pub.

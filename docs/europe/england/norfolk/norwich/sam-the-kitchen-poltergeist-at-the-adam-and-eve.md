#title
Sam the Kitchen Poltergeist at the Adam and Eve
#summary
A kitchen worker at the Adam and Eve pub experienced various unexplained phenomena attributed to a friendly resident ghost known as Sam.
#details
A former kitchen worker who spent four years at the pub noted that ash trays would fall from the bar on Sunday afternoons when the pub was closed and empty. Customer toilets had a strange feeling with fixtures turning on and off by themselves, and objects moved independently in the cellar. Staff were aware of the entity, whom they called 'Sam', and viewed him as an accepted part of the staff without fear.

#title
The Plagued House of Tombland Alley

#summary
A young girl who tragically survived being quarantined during a plague outbreak by resorting to cannibalism now haunts the streets of Norwich as a grey ghost.

#details
During the 1578 plague in Norwich, homes of infected individuals were boarded up by bailiffs. When one such house was unsealed forty days later, the bodies of a man, woman, and young girl were discovered inside. Examination revealed that the parents bore human bite marks, indicating the surviving young girl had choked on their flesh out of starvation before she could escape. Today, a young woman in grey is said to haunt the area of Tombland and Tombland Alley behind Augustine Steward House.

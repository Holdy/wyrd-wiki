#title
The Victorian Murder Victim of Magdalen Street
#summary
A Victorian murder victim named Sarah haunts a former pub on Magdalen Street, where staff have reported footsteps, cold spots, and shadowy figures. Witnesses believe she still thinks she is alive.
#details
During the 1980s, staff at the shop—formerly the Key Merchants Arms—heard footsteps in empty areas, felt drafts and cold spots, and saw a shadowy figure on the staircase alongside poltergeist activity. Historical accounts indicate Sarah, a Victorian woman, was drinking when a man attempted to take her upstairs and killed her when she struggled; the murderer was subsequently executed at Norwich Jail. During a séance, information came through suggesting Sarah still believes she is alive, which is thought to explain the ongoing paranormal phenomena.

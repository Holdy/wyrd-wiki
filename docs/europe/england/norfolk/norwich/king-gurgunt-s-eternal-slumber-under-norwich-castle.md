#title
King Gurgunt’s Eternal Slumber Under Norwich Castle
#summary
Legend says King Gurgunt founded Norwich and now sleeps eternally beneath the castle mound with his treasures, ready to wake and save Britain if needed.
#details
King Gurgunt was so fond of Norwich that he built the castle and established the city around it. According to legend, upon taking his last breath, he seated himself at a grand table deep underneath the man-made castle mound accompanied by his silver, gold, jewels, and treasures, falling into an eternal sleep. Like King Arthur, he is said to be ready to rise from his slumber to save Britain if she ever falls into peril.

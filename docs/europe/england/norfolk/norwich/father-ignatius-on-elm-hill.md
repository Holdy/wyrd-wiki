#title
Father Ignatius on Elm Hill

#summary
The twilight spirit of a curse-spreading holy man named Father Ignatius walks Norwich's Elm Hill carrying a large black Bible.

#details
Father Ignatius was a holy man who placed fatal curses on locals while alive, eventually forcing enraged citizens to drive him out of Norwich. His ghost now wanders Elm Hill in the twilight holding a large black Bible, continuing to harass and curse the unwary.

#title
The Bell-Ringing Lord of the Adam and Eve Pub
#summary
A nobleman stabbed to death at a Norwich pub still drinks there and rings the pub bell when it is empty.
#details
Lord Sheffield was stabbed to death at the Adam & Eve pub in 1549, and his ghost still drinks there, occasionally giving the pub bell a jingle when the establishment is empty.
#location
The Adam and Eve, Bishopsgate, Norwich, Norfolk, England

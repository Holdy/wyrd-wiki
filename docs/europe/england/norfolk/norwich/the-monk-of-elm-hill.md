#title
The Monk of Elm Hill

#summary
The ghost of a monk named Father Ignatius is said to walk the cobblestones and crooked timbers of Elm Hill after dark.

#details
Along Elm Hill, known for its crooked timbers and cobbles, the ghost of a monk named Father Ignatius is said to walk after dark. Visitors have reported hearing footsteps or feeling the brush of chill air where his ghost is said to pass.

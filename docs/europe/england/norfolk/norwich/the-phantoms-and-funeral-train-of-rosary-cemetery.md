#title
The Phantoms and Funeral Train of Rosary Cemetery
#summary
Visitors to Rosary Cemetery have encountered wandering spirits near gravestones, a black cat walking through a solid wall, and a phantom horse-drawn funeral train.
#details
A visitor returning to Rosary Cemetery as a teenager frequently saw figures standing beside gravestones or wandering in the newer section, while also feeling drawn to the grave of someone named Rowen in the older section. Near the grave of men who died in a train crash, a black cat was witnessed walking straight through a solid stone wall. Furthermore, a phantom funeral train—complete with a horse-drawn carriage and mourners walking behind—materialized directly in front of the church and moved down the path. A child visiting years later also reported hearing phantom horse neighing at the exact same spot.

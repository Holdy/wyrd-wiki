#title
The Entangled Lives of Christopher and Alice Burroway

#summary
Alice abandoned her newborn on Norwich Cathedral steps, only to marry him years later as an adult without realizing he was her son.

#details
Alice, the daughter of a local landowner, became pregnant by her father and abandoned her newborn baby son on the steps of Norwich Cathedral, marked by a large mole under his left shoulder blade. Years later, after inheriting her father's estate, Alice fell in love with and married a steward named Christopher Burroway, discovering on their wedding night that he had an identical blemish in the exact same spot. This unusual family tragedy is commemorated on Christopher's gravestone at Martham Church, which describes Alice as his sister, mistress, mother, and wife.

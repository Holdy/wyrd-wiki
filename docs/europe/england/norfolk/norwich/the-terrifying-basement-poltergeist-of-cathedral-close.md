#title
The Terrifying Basement Poltergeist of Cathedral Close
#summary
Builders and accountants working in a Cathedral Close building encountered violent poltergeist activity, prompting a priest who investigated the basement to flee in terror.
#details
When builders broke up an old stone staircase in a basement in Cathedral Close, items began moving upstairs, tools slid across floors, cupboard doors opened and shut, paperclips rose into the air, and a wall clock moved independently. Later, when a firm of accountants occupied the building, cleaners and staff reported severe poltergeist activity including thrown staplers, moving chairs, and hidden objects, with the basement being considered so terrifying that staff refused to enter. A priest asked to investigate the basement ran back upstairs after a couple of minutes with a look of absolute terror, refusing ever to discuss what happened down there.

#title
The Unseen Thrower of Elm Hill
#summary
People walking along Elm Hill have heard unexplained footsteps sounding like objects being thrown, glimpsed faces in the old monastery, and felt an intense sense of being watched.
#details
Personal accounts from Elm Hill describe footsteps echoing on the street that sound as though something is being thrown, alongside faces sighted inside the old monastery, strange noises, and a persistent feeling of being watched.

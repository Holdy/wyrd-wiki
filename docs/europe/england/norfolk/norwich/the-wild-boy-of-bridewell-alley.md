#title
The Wild Boy of Bridewell Alley

#summary
A feral boy discovered in Hanover was later locked in Norwich's Bridewell Gaol after wandering the streets as a vagrant.

#details
During the 18th century, a mysterious 12-year-old feral boy discovered in the forests of Hertswold, Hanover, became a nationwide sensation and was briefly kept by King George I as 'Peter'. After disappearing from Hertfordshire decades later, a sturdy vagrant identified as Peter was arrested in Norwich and locked in Bridewell Gaol, where he survived a giant fire in Bridewell Alley. He was eventually recognised, returned home, and commemorated locally with a pub named the Wildman on Bedford Street and a nearby plaque.

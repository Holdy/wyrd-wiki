#title
Father Ignatius of Elm Hill
#summary
A strict 19th-century priest who cursed those who did not pray hard enough still roams Elm Hill.
#details
Father Ignatius lived in 1864 and cursed people who did not pray hard enough. Though eventually driven out of the city by frustrated locals, his ghost roams the area, continuing to haunt and curse people.

#title
The Headless Coach of Blickling Hall
#summary
On the anniversary of her execution, the headless ghost of Anne Boleyn rides to Blickling Hall in a coach driven by a headless horseman.
#details
Every year on May 19th—the anniversary of Anne Boleyn's execution—the ghost of her decapitated body rides up to her childhood home of Blickling Hall in a coach drawn by a headless horseman, disappearing into thin air upon arrival.

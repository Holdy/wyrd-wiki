#title
The Executed Priest of Tombland
#summary
The ghost of Father Tunstall has been sighted in Tombland after he was executed in the 17th century for his religious beliefs.
#details
Father Tunstall suffered a gruesome execution—being hanged, drawn, and quartered during the 17th century due to his religious beliefs. Following his death, his ghost has been seen in Tombland.

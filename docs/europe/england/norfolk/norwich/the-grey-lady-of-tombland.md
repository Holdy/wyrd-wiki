#title
The Grey Lady of Tombland

#summary
A ghost known as the Grey Lady has reportedly wandered Tombland since the plague year of 1578, either mourning lost loved ones or warning of misfortune.

#details
In Tombland, just steps from the cathedral, a ghost known as the Grey Lady is said to have wandered since the plague year of 1578. According to different versions of the tale, she either mourns lost loved ones or acts as an omen warning of upcoming misfortune.

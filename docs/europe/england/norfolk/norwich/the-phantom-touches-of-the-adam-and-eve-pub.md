#title
The Phantom Touches of the Adam and Eve Pub

#summary
Norwich's 13th-century Adam and Eve pub is plagued by unexplained temperature shifts, self-ringing bells, and phantom touches attributed to the ghost of Lord Sheffield.

#details
The Adam and Eve pub in Norwich, dating back to the 13th century, is reputed to be the region's most haunted pub, partly due to the ghost of Lord Sheffield, who was killed during Kett's Rebellion in 1549. Phenomena reported by visitors include sudden hot and cold spots, the last-call bell ringing on its own, footsteps with no visible source, and unseen hands touching shoulders or running fingers through hair.

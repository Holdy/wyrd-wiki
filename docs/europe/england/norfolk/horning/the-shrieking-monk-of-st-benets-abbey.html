The Shrieking Monk of St Benets Abbey

Summary

A traitorous monk who betrayed his abbey to the Normans was brutally executed and now haunts the ruins with his agonizing screams.

Details

Following the Norman Conquest, a bailiff monk named Essric betrayed his brethren at St Benets Abbey by letting William the Conqueror's soldiers inside in exchange for being made Abbot. Although the Normans appointed him as promised, they despised traitors, dressing Essric in full regalia before nailing him to the abbey doors and skinning him alive. On May 25th each year, his agonizing screams can still be heard, followed by the sight of his form writhing in agony from the former bell tower.

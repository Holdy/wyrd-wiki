#title
Brother Pacificus and His Dog

#summary
A monk who survived the slaughter of his brethren during the Dissolution of the Monasteries is seen paddling toward St Benet's Abbey with his dog.

#details
During the Dissolution of the Monasteries, Brother Pacificus returned to St Benet's Abbey after working on a screen at St. Helen's in Ranworth to discover all his fellow monks had been savagely slaughtered. He lived out his remaining days at the abbey as the sole survivor. Following his death, his wispy ghost is seen paddling toward the abbey in a boat with his faithful dog standing at the bow, disappearing along with the boat and dog upon reaching the shore.

#title
The Traitorous Monk of St Benet's Abbey

#summary
A bailiff monk named Essric who betrayed his monastery to the Normans is said to reenact his brutal execution on the abbey doors every May 25.

#details
Shortly after the Norman conquest, Essric, the bailiff monk of St Benet's, opened the abbey gates to invading forces in hopes of becoming abbot. Although the Normans made him abbot, their hatred of traitors led them to seize him during prayer, nail him to the abbey doors, and slowly skin him alive. On the anniversary of his execution on May 25, his bloody and battered apparition is reportedly seen writhing and screaming in pain on the abbey doors.

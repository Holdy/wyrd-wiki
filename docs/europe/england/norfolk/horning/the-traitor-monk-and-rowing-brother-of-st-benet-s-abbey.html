The Traitor Monk and Rowing Brother of St Benet's Abbey

Summary

St Benet's Abbey is haunted by the screams of a hanged traitor monk every May 25th, as well as a monk named Brother Pacificus rowing with a dog.

Details

According to legend, William the Conqueror bribed a monk to open the abbey gates in 1066, after which the traitor was dressed in full abbot regalia and hanged from the gate; his screams are reportedly heard late at night every May 25th. Additionally, another resident ghost known as Brother Pacificus has been seen quietly rowing across the river at the abbey in a small boat accompanied by a dog.

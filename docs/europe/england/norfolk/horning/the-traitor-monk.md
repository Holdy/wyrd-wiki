#title
The Traitor Monk
#summary
A monk who betrayed his abbey to the Normans to secure the position of abbot was promptly hanged by them.
#details
A treacherous monk sold his abbey to the Normans so that he could become abbot. The Normans kept their word by giving him the position, but immediately afterwards, they hanged him.

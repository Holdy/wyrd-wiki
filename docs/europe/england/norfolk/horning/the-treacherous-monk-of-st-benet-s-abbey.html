The Treacherous Monk of St Benet’s Abbey

Summary

A monk who betrayed St Benet's Abbey to the invading Normans in exchange for being made abbot was instead hanged from the tower, and his death is replayed by a phantom scene at dusk.

Details

When Norman knights arrived at St Benet’s Abbey, a monk named Brother Veritas slipped out to open a door for them on the promise that he would be made abbot. After seizing the abbey, the Normans prepared a ceremony, slipped a noose around Veritas's neck as he bowed, and hanged him from the tower as punishment. At dusk, when mist rolls in, witnesses may see the abbey reappear in its former glory and watch the dreadful scene replay, concluding with Veritas dangling and screaming.

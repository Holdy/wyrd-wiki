St Walstan's Spring

Summary

A humble farmhand who became a saint left behind a miraculous spring along the route of his funeral cart.

Details

St Walstan died as a simple farmhand and became a saint. Pilgrims still visit the spring that miraculously sprang forth where his funeral cart paused or passed.

    The Treasure and Battleground of Western Close

    Summary

    Local folklore in Feltwell maintains that the meadow known as Western Close was once an ancient battleground and conceals a hoard of buried treasure beneath a solitary tree within its medieval moat.

    Details

    For generations, local tradition in the Norfolk parish of Feltwell has centered on Western Close—a meadow containing the remains of a medieval moated manor site locally known as "The Roundabout." According to long-standing legend, the meadow was the site of a historic battle, with villagers variously attributing the conflict to Boudicca fighting the Roman invaders, a skirmish between Roundheads and Royalists during the English Civil War, or an attack by Hereward the Wake against the Normans. Furthermore, a persistent folklore tradition handed down through generations claims that a vast hoard of hidden treasure lies buried directly beneath the solitary large tree growing within the center of the moat.

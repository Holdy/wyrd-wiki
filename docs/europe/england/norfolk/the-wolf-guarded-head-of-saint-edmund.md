#title
The Wolf-Guarded Head of Saint Edmund

#summary
After King Edmund was martyred and decapitated by Vikings, his head was miraculously guarded by a wolf in the forest until it was reunited with his body.

#details
In 869, King Edmund was captured by Vikings and executed after refusing to renounce his Christian faith. The Vikings decapitated him and threw his head into Thetford Forest. When the Saxons searched for it, guided by a mysterious voice, they found a wolf guarding the head, which miraculously reattached itself when placed back onto his corpse.

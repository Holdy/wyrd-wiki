#title
The Ghostly Lantern of Thurne Church Tower

#summary
A phantom lantern shines from Thurne Church tower to summon monastic help whenever a family in the isolated marshes falls sick.

#details
When a mother or child falls sick in any of the isolated marsh cottages around Thurne, a ghostly lantern is said to shine out at night from the top of Thurne Church tower in an attempt to summon the help of the monks of St. Benet's Abbey.

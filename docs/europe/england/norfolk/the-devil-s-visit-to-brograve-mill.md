#title
The Devil's Visit to Brograve Mill

#summary
After losing a wager against the devil, a hard-drinking landowner locked himself inside a remote drainage mill where his spirit and phantom rider continue to haunt the surrounding wetlands.

#details
In 1771, Sir Berney Brograve made a reckless wager that he could mow a beanfield faster than the devil. Having lost the bet, he fled and locked himself inside Brograve Mill overnight while the devil pounded on the heavy wooden door with cloven hooves. By morning, the door was left pitted with hoof prints and tilted westward. Today, Brograve's ghost and his flame-snorting horse haunt the Brograve Level between Waxham and Horsey.

#location
Brograve Mill stands derelict on its own along the Waxham Cut near Horsey in Norfolk.

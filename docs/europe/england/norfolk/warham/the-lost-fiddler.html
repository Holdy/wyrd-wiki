The Lost Fiddler

Summary

A village followed a fiddler's music underground until it stopped at a mound, and he never resurfaced.

Details

A fiddler played music that bewitched an entire village into following him underground. When they reached a mound, the music abruptly stopped, and the fiddler never came back up.

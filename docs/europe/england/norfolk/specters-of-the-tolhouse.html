Specters of the Tolhouse

Summary

Tolhouse Gaol in Great Yarmouth is plagued by the tormented spirits of those imprisoned and executed during Matthew Hopkins' 1645 witch trials. Visitors and staff frequently report physical encounters with unseen hands, extreme temperature drops, and bloodcurdling screams echoing through the historic building.

Details

Originally constructed in the 12th century, Tolhouse Gaol served as Great Yarmouth's primary penal institution for centuries. Its darkest chapter occurred in 1645 when the notorious witch-finder general Matthew Hopkins arrived to conduct witch trials, leading to the imprisonment and hanging execution of five accused individuals in the dismal holding area known as "the hold." Today, centuries of suffering, torture, and death have left a heavy supernatural imprint on the stone building. Witnesses inside report full-bodied apparitions pacing the corridors, sudden and freezing temperature drops, and being physically grabbed, pushed, or touched by unseen entities, accompanied by the disembodied echoes of terrified screams.

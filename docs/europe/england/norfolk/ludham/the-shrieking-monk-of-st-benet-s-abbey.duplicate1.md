#title
The Shrieking Monk of St Benet's Abbey

#summary
A treacherous monk who betrayed his abbey was executed by invaders, and his ghost now swings and shrieks from an archway in the ruins.

#details
Essric, a monk who betrayed St Benet's Abbey to its enemies in exchange for the title of Abbot, was immediately sentenced to death by the conquerors as an example to all informers once they seized the church. In the remaining archway of the abbey ruins on the bank of the River Bure, the ghost of Essric is said to swing and shriek out to this day on cold autumn nights when the ground is hard.

#location
St Benet's Abbey, Ludham, Norfolk, England

The Betrayal and Execution of Monk Essric

Summary

A monk who betrayed St Benet's Abbey to Norman invaders was brutally executed and now haunts the marshes with his midnight screams.

Details

The monk Essric opened the doors of St Benet’s Abbey to the Norman invaders on the promise that they would make him the abbot. They fulfilled the promise for just a few hours; once proclaimed abbot, he was nailed to the gatehouse door and skinned alive. His screams echo across the marshes at midnight on the 25th of May every year.

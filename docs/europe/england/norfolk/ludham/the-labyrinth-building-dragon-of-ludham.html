The Labyrinth-Building Dragon of Ludham

Summary

A dragon that dug a subterranean labyrinth beneath Ludham terrorised residents before being blocked inside and flying off to attack St Benet's Abbey.

Details

According to legend, Ludham was once plagued by an uninvited guest: a dragon that excavated a network of tunnels beneath the village. The creature emerged at night through an entrance located between the Carpenter's Arms and the parish church. One warm day, after it dragged its 15-foot-long body out to sun itself, a brave resident blocked up the hole. Enraged, the dragon flew away, battered the walls of St Benet's Abbey, and slinked into a tunnel beneath the abbey's gatehouse.

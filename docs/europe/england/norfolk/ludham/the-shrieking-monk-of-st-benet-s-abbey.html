The Shrieking Monk of St Benet's Abbey

Summary

A traitorous monk who betrayed his abbey to the Normans was promptly hanged, and his screaming ghost is witnessed re-enacted annually.

Details

In 1067, during a Norman siege of St Benet’s Abbey, the doorkeeper betrayed the monastery to the invaders in exchange for being made Abbot. After the ensuing massacre, the Norman commander deemed him a traitor and had him hanged from the bell tower. On the anniversary of his execution on May 25th, witnesses have seen the abbey temporarily restored to its former glory and watched the screaming monk's execution replay.

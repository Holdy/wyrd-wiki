#title
Black Shuck

#summary
Black Shuck is a terrifying shaggy black hound with fiery eyes that haunts coastal paths, fields, and churches across East Anglia.

#details
Dating back to the 16th century—associated with devastating summer storms in Bungay and Blythburgh—Black Shuck is a supernatural shaggy black dog with one fiery eye, saucer-like eyes, and hot breath like fire, though he can also disguise himself as a cat or appear headless. He is said to guard buried treasure at the ruined St Benet's abbey, has been seen at Coltishall Bridge and High Street where two RAF officers watched him vanish into thin air in the 1960s, roams Caister-on-Sea looking for a master, and drives boys out to sea at Lowestoft.

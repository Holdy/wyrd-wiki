Tom Hickathrift

Summary

A giant of enormous strength used a cart axle and wheel to defeat a fen giant, and his grave is marked in a churchyard.

Details

Tom Hickathrift, a giant-killer of enormous strength, is said to have beaten a giant using only a cart axle and wheel. His grave is pointed out at the churchyard in Tilney All Saints.

The Halvergate Shuck Encounter

Summary

In November 2000, a motorist driving past the marshes toward Halvergate during a storm was accompanied alongside his vehicle by a massive wolfhound-like spectral dog with burning red eyes.

Details

While driving home from Great Yarmouth during a severe storm in November 2000, a motorist passed the Vauxhall Caravan site and headed onto the open road crossing the marshes toward Halvergate. Glancing to his right, he was astonished to see an enormous, wolfhound-sized hound with glowing red eyes running alongside his car. Recognizing the legendary apparition, he felt exhilarated rather than afraid as the beast kept pace for about half a mile before turning back across the marshes. Years later, another driver encountered the same spectral hound on the nearby Acle Straight at two in the morning, where it fell in beside his van and accompanied him along the road.

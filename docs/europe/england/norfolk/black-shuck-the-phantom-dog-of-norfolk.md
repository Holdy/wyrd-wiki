#title
Black Shuck, the Phantom Dog of Norfolk
#summary
Black Shuck is a legendary phantom black dog haunting East Anglian coastal roads and graveyards, whose gaze prophesies death by the end of the year.
#details
Black Shuck is a folkloric devil dog that haunts the East of England, particularly coastal roads, graveyards, crossroads, woodlands, and marshes. Described as a large black dog seen with or without a head, possessing one or two glowing eyes, and floating on mist, local lore warns that looking into the beast's eyes will bring about the death of the witness or someone in their household before the year ends.

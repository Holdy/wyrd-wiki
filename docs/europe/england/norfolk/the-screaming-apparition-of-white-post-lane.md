#title
The Screaming Apparition of White Post Lane

#summary
In Hanworth, Norfolk, the phantom of a distressed woman named Hannah Whitepost is said to haunt White Post Lane, where she repeatedly crosses the road and lets out three terrifying shrieks.

#details
Though frequently linked in popular lore to the neighboring town of North Walsham, traditional accounts and local archives place the haunting of Hannah Whitepost along White Post Lane and the adjacent common land in Hanworth, locally known as "Hannah's Common." According to historical reports, the apparition is that of a distressed woman who startles travelers by crossing the road three times in the exact same direction, culminating in three blood-curdling shrieks that echo into the night.

#location
White Post Lane in Hanworth, Norfolk.

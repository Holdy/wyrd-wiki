The Drowned Child of Cuckoo Lodge

Summary

A young girl from a family living at Cuckoo Lodge tragically fell into a deep garden well and drowned, and her ghost continues to haunt the cottage.

Details

In the mid-1800s, Cuckoo Lodge was home to a family where a young girl fell into the garden's deep well and drowned in the dark waters below. The ghost of this girl is said to still haunt the cottage.

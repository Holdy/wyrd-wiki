#title
The Glowing-Eyed Creatures of Hethersett

#summary
A man walking on the outskirts of Hethersett was knocked to the ground by a gust of wind before spotting a calf-sized creature with bicycle-lamp eyes.

#details
In 1920, writer Walter Rye recorded an encounter on the outskirts of Hethersett where a man was suddenly knocked off his feet by a gust of wind. Upon recovering, he saw a calf-sized creature with abnormally large, glowing eyes like bicycle lamps slinking away. Locals familiar with these creatures, known as the Hethersett Faines, made a point to give them plenty of room when passing.

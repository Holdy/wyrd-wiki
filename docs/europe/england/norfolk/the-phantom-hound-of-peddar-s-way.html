The Phantom Hound of Peddar's Way

Summary

An 18-year-old scout on a night hike along Peddar's Way was suddenly confronted by a massive black spectral hound that leaped toward him before mysteriously vanishing. Exactly one year later, the witness suffered a near-fatal accident, fulfilling the local omen associated with sighting the beast.

Details

On Sunday, 23 October 1977, an 18-year-old member of the 14th Wootton Venture Scout Unit was participating in a night hike heading south along Peddar's Way in Great Massingham. At around 5:30 in the morning, while walking about a quarter of a mile past a landmark known as Shepherd's Bush, he spotted what he initially took to be a sheepdog running rapidly toward him down a slight rise in the road. As the distance closed, the creature leaped directly at him. Described as a huge black dog comparable in size to a St. Bernard, it featured a shaggy black coat, a wide mouth with prominent teeth, and wide eyes. The scout braced for impact and stumbled to his knees, but the anticipated physical thrust never materialized, and when he looked up, the animal had completely vanished. Later that day, his mother told him about the legend of Black Shuck and the folklore that seeing the apparition portends death within the year. True to the superstition, exactly one year later on 23 October 1978, the witness was struck as a pedestrian in a severe road traffic accident that left him in critical condition.

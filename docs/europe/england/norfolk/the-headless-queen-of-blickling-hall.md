#title
The Headless Queen of Blickling Hall

#summary
On the anniversary of her execution, the ghost of Queen Anne Boleyn arrives at her former home in a headless horse-drawn carriage. Carrying her severed head, she wanders the ancient corridors until dawn.

#details
Every year on May 19th, the anniversary of her execution in 1536, the spirit of Anne Boleyn returns to Blickling Hall. She is said to arrive in a carriage driven by a headless coachman and pulled by four headless horses. Stepping out of the coach dressed all in white, the headless apparition carries her own severed head on her lap. She then glides silently through the ancient rooms, staircases, and countless corridors of the estate until daybreak. 

#location
Blickling Hall is a historic stately home and estate located in the village of Blickling, just north of Aylsham in Norfolk, United Kingdom (NR11 6NF).

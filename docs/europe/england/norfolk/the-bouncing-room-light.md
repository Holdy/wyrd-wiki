#title
The Bouncing Room Light

#summary
A Norfolk couple woke up to find an unexplained light bouncing around their bedroom without any storms in the area.

#details
In September, Marisa and her husband woke up to see a light bouncing around their room. There had been no thunder or storms in the area, and unlike lightning, the light did not illuminate the entire room in a flash.

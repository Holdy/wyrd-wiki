    Wolferton Hall

    Summary

    While investigating the dark history of an ancestral Norfolk estate, a doctoral researcher awakens vengeful occult forces tied to the property.

    Details

    Hugh Terne, a Ph.D. student researching the Throgmorton family, travels to Wolferton Hall to examine family archives and documents. He uncovers evidence suggesting that a 15th-century lawyer, Sir Charles Throgmorton, used a forged deed to steal the hall from its rightful owner, Thomas Shackleton. As Terne delves deeper into Shackleton's history of necromancy and black arts within the building, his research unearths terrifying supernatural manifestations that leave him with far more than academic notes.

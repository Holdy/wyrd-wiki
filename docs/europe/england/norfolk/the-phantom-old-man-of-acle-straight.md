#title
The Phantom Old Man of Acle Straight

#summary
An old man spirit suddenly appears in front of driving cars on Acle Straight and vanishes when drivers check on him.

#details
Drivers on Acle Straight have experienced an apparition of an old man who suddenly appears directly in front of their moving cars. When startled drivers pull over and get out to see if the man is okay, they find no one there.

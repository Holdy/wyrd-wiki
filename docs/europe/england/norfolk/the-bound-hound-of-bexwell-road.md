#title
The Bound Hound of Bexwell Road

#summary
In 1870, the Bexwell parish clerk encountered a phantom black dog that vanished through a hedge with rattling chains and a scent of sulfur. Although the local rector insisted it was merely his own escaped hound, he could offer no explanation for the supernatural stench.

#details
Around 1870, the parish clerk of Bexwell was traveling along the road between Downham Market and Bexwell when he had a harrowing encounter with a spectral black dog. The creature abruptly vanished through a hedge accompanied by the heavy rattling of chains and an overwhelming smell of sulfur. When the incident came to light, the Rector of Bexwell stepped forward to rationalise the sighting, claiming the phantom was actually his own large black dog which had managed to break its chain. However, the rector remained entirely at a loss to explain where the distinct smell of sulfur had originated.

#location
The encounter took place along the A1122 road running between Downham Market and Bexwell in Norfolk.

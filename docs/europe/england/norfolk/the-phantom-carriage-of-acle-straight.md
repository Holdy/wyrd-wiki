#title
The Phantom Carriage of Acle Straight

#summary
A ghostly carriage drawn by horses is reported to charge into oncoming traffic on Acle Straight before vanishing.

#details
Unsuspecting motorists travelling along Acle Straight near the junction with Halvergate Road have encountered a phantom carriage drawn by ghostly horses. The spectral vehicle is known to head directly into oncoming traffic before vanishing entirely just before a collision occurs.

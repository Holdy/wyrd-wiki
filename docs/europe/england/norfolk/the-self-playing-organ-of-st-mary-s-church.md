#title
The Self-Playing Organ of St. Mary’s Church
#summary
A Victorian pipe organ at St. Mary’s Church in Wiggenhall famously plays loud music by itself, frightening visitors and workmen.
#details
According to a 1986 account published in Norfolk Fair, the Victorian pipe organ at St. Mary’s Church would play loud music all by itself with no performer present. This unexplainable spectacle terrified visitors and caused a group of workmen to flee the church in a panic when the organ burst into song.

#title
The Viking Ship of Death

#summary
South Walsham Broad is said to be haunted by a spectral Viking longship carrying a dead chief, with both the vessel and its chieftain set ablaze before sinking into the water. This fiery apparition serves as a chilling reminder of the region's ancient Scandinavian history.

#details
According to traditional accounts, the waters of South Walsham Broad are occasionally visited by a macabre phantom sight: a Viking warship bearing a deceased chief, both entirely engulfed in roaring flames before plunging beneath the surface of the lake. 

#location
South Walsham Broad is a picturesque inland lake located in the parish of South Walsham within the Norfolk Broads, England.

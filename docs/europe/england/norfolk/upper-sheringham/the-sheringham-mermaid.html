The Sheringham Mermaid

Summary

A mermaid was drawn from the sea to All Saints Church in Upper Sheringham by heavenly singing, where she stayed forever and was immortalised in wood.

Details

Inside the 900-year-old All Saints Church in Upper Sheringham, the bench end of the pew closest to the north door features a wooden carving of a mermaid. Legend has it that this siren of the sea was drawn to the church from more than a mile away by the sound of heavenly singing. Despite the encumbrance of her tail, she dragged herself laboriously from the beach to the churchyard and stayed forever.

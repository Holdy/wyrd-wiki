#title
The Stolen Saint
#summary
Monks crept into Dereham at night to steal a saint's body, leaving water to gush up from the empty grave.
#details
Monks covertly crept in during the night to steal the body of a saint from Dereham. When the body was removed, water mysteriously welled up out of the empty grave.

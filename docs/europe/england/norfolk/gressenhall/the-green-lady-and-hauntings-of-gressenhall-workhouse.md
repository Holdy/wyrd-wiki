#title
The Green Lady and Hauntings of Gressenhall Workhouse

#summary
Former workhouse Gressenhall is haunted by a Green Lady, phantom footsteps, children laughing, and poltergeist activity like self-moving objects and submerged clocks.

#details
Dating back to 1777 and later serving as a harsh workhouse following the Poor Law of 1834, Gressenhall has a long history of paranormal reports. Staff have documented lights turning on, boxes moving, windows opening, children laughing, and an overwhelming feeling of being watched. A wall clock was once found submerged in a sink overnight after having crossed a room by itself, and sightings of a Green Lady are accompanied by echoing footsteps and self-opening doors.

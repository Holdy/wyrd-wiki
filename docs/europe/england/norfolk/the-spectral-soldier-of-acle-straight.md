#title
The Spectral Soldier of Acle Straight

#summary
A uniformed soldier's ghost is seen marching silently down the middle of Acle Straight, completely ignoring passing cars.

#details
Motorists driving along Acle Straight have reported seeing a spectral soldier wearing a uniform silently marching down the exact middle of the road. The apparition remains entirely oblivious to the cars driving past him.

    The Phantoms of Deopham Green Airfield

    Summary

    Deopham Green Airfield, the historic World War II base of the USAAF 452nd Bombardment Group, remains haunted by the enduring echoes and atmospheric presence of B-17 Flying Fortresses.

    Details

    During World War II, Deopham Green (designated USAAF Station 142) was home to the 452nd Bombardment Group (Heavy) of the United States Eighth Air Force, which flew B-17 Flying Fortresses on strategic bombing campaigns. Today, while the buildings have largely vanished and parts of the old runways have reverted to farmland and country roads, the site is remembered as one of Norfolk's ghost airfields. The haunting legacy of the base evokes the memory of the aircraft and airmen, with locals and visitors imagining the heavy bombers once again thundering across the expansive Norfolk skies.

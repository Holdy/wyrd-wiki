#title
The Unseen Hound of Cley Hill

#summary
Walking home from Cley along the A149 at midnight, a woman was stalked by an invisible, chain-rattling spectral hound that followed her up Cley Hill before turning down Back Lane.

#details
Around midnight during the summer of 1968, a woman walking home from Cley to Blakeney along the A149 road heard the light dragging and tinkling of a chain near the bottom of Cley Hill. Although she could see nothing, the unseen presence closely followed her up the hill. Upon reaching the lit crossroads at the top, she waited to observe whatever was making the noise, but nothing materialized. She then heard the phantom entity pass away down Back Lane toward the marsh banks, recognizing the classic signs of Black Shuck and fleeing home down the High Street.

#location
The encounter took place along the A149 road leading from Cley next the Sea up Cley Hill toward the crossroads and Back Lane.

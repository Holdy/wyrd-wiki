#title
The Blood-Stained Child of Cromer Churchyard

#summary
While crossing the ruined chancel of the town's parish church at night, a laborer was confronted by a small white-clad figure that grew to his height before revealing a bleeding, slit throat and vanishing into thin air. 

#details
In the late 19th century, a workman hired to clear away rubbish from the ruined chancel of St Peter and St Paul’s Church in Cromer took a shortcut across the old churchyard path after dark. As he walked through the eerie ruins, a small child-like figure dressed in white suddenly rose from the ground directly in front of him. To his horror, the figure grew taller until its face was level with his own, at which point a deep gash opened across its throat. A torrent of blood poured down its white clothing before the apparition vanished in a flash, leaving behind only a faint sighing sound.

#location
The churchyard of St Peter and St Paul’s Church, situated on Church Street in the coastal town of Cromer, Norfolk.

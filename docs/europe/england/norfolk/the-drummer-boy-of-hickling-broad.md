#title
The Drummer Boy of Hickling Broad

#summary
During the bitter winter of 1815, a young drummer boy tragically plunged through the ice and drowned while skating across Hickling Broad to meet his secret lover. Ever since, his ghostly form and the rhythmic beating of his drum have haunted the frozen waters.

#details
In the winter of 1815, shortly before the Battle of Waterloo, a poor young drummer boy home on leave fell in love with a local girl whose wealthy family disapproved of the romance. Undeterred, the star-crossed lovers arranged secret nocturnal meetings at a hut in Swim Coots on the edge of Hickling Broad. When freezing temperatures locked the Broad in thick ice, the drummer boy donned his bright yellow and scarlet uniform and skated across the glittering expanse, beating a tattoo on his kettle drum to announce his arrival to his sweetheart. On a freezing February night, after hearing the drumbeats suddenly cease, the waiting girl rushed to the edge of the ice only to find silence. He had ventured across thin ice near the channel and broken through. His lifeless body in its distinctive uniform was recovered days later after the thaw. Local lore maintains that on cold winter nights, the ghostly sound of skating blades and the muffled roll of a drum still echo across Hickling Broad.

#location
Hickling Broad, a large expanse of water in the Norfolk Broads near the village of Hickling, Norfolk.

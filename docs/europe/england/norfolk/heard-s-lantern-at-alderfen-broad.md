#title
Heard's Lantern at Alderfen Broad

#summary
The ghost of a criminal waving a lantern on misty evenings was eventually trapped in a bottle by three men reading the Bible.

#details
Alderfen Broad was once said to be haunted by the ghost of a criminal named Heard, who waved his lantern on misty evenings. His haunting finally ended when three men trapped his ghost in a bottle by reading the Bible aloud.

#title
The Devil's Footprint in King's Lynn
#summary
A priest in King's Lynn cornered the Devil in a street and banished him with prayers and holy water, causing the Devil to stamp a footprint in rage.
#details
King's Lynn is home to Devil's Alley, where legend states that a priest spotted and cornered the Devil in the street. The priest banished him back to the netherworld using prayers and holy water, which infuriated the Devil and caused him to stamp his foot with such force that it left an imprint on the ground. While the alley remains, the footprint has since disappeared due to modern roadworks resurfacing the cobbles.

#title
The Apparition at the Exorcist's House

#summary
Two visitors to an empty 17th-century cottage in King's Lynn were startled to see an elderly woman sitting by the fire, who was later recognized as the home's recently deceased former resident.

#details
Mr. and Mrs. Buckley previously resided in a 17th-century cottage located close to the churchyard of St Nicholas’ Chapel in King’s Lynn. Following Mr. Buckley's death in 1976, Mrs. Buckley sold the property and moved to America. When the new owner left the cottage unoccupied for several years, his two stepdaughters visited the property and spotted an old lady sitting in a rocking chair by the fire. After they described the woman to their stepfather, he recognized her as Mrs. Buckley, who had recently passed away.

#title
The Witch-Deterring Cat Remains of Duke’s Head Hotel
#summary
Hidden cat remains discovered in the ceiling of Room 10 at the Duke’s Head Hotel reflect a 17th-century practice to ward off witches and evil spirits.
#details
In 2011, cat remains were discovered concealed in the ceiling of Room 10 at the Duke’s Head Hotel. Local historians believe these remains were intentionally placed there during the 17th century as a popular protective method to deter evil spirits and witches from entering the property.
#location
Duke’s Head Hotel, King’s Lynn, Norfolk, England

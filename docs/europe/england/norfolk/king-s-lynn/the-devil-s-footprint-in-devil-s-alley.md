#title
The Devil’s Footprint in Devil’s Alley
#summary
Local folktale holds that the Devil arrived by ship to steal souls in King’s Lynn before being cornered and banished by a priest in a cobbled alleyway.
#details
According to local legend, the Devil came to King’s Lynn by ship to steal the souls of townsfolk. A priest discovered him and cornered him in a narrow, cobbled alleyway now known as Devil’s Alley. The priest banished the Devil using prayers and holy water, making the Devil so furious that he stomped his foot into the cobbles and left a hoofprint before fleeing back to his ship.

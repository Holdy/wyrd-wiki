The Residents of True’s Yard Fisherfolk Museum

Summary

True’s Yard Fisherfolk Museum is believed to be home to over 37 resident ghosts, including dark figures in empty windows and a poltergeist named Henry.

Details

Visitors and staff at the heritage site have experienced numerous unexplainable phenomena, such as dark figures staring out of empty windows in the 18th-century cottages. Additionally, a poltergeist nicknamed Henry is reported by staff to knock paintings off walls and throw staplers across the room where fisherfolk once lived.

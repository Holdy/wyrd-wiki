#title
The Exorcist’s House on Chapel Lane
#summary
Chapel Lane's medieval exorcist's house is tied to a 16th-century exorcism that ended in a woman's execution and later housed a broadcaster with occult collections.
#details
Down Chapel Lane stands the former home of a medieval exorcist. The last exorcism performed there in the 16th century allegedly led to the tragic death of a woman accused of witchcraft, who was subsequently burned at the stake on Tuesday Market Place. In later years, broadcaster Frank Buckley—who claimed to be a wizard—lived in the house and maintained a large collection of occult objects there.

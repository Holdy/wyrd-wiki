#title
The Murdered Bride of the Tudor Rose Hotel
#summary
A short woman wearing a wedding dress haunts the Tudor Rose Hotel, said to be the spirit of a bride murdered by her husband on her wedding day.
#details
Visitors to the 16th-century Tudor Rose Hotel have reported seeing a short woman in a wedding dress roaming the historic building. According to legend, she is a bride who was murdered by her new husband on their wedding day, causing her spirit to remain at the hotel indefinitely. Paranormal occurrences at the site also include phantom footsteps, moving objects, closing doors, and mysterious shadows.
#location
Tudor Rose Hotel, King’s Lynn, Norfolk, England

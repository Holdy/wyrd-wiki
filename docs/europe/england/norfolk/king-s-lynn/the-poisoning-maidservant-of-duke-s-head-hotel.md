#title
The Poisoning Maidservant of Duke’s Head Hotel
#summary
The Duke’s Head Hotel is haunted by the ghost of a maidservant who was executed for poisoning her mistress.
#details
Built in 1683 on the site of an ancient inn called the Gryffin, the hotel is said to be haunted by a maidservant. According to reports, the maid was found guilty of poisoning her mistress and sentenced to be boiled alive on the Tuesday Market Place.
#location
Duke’s Head Hotel, King’s Lynn, Norfolk, England

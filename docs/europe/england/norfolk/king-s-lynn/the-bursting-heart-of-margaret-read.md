#title
The Bursting Heart of Margaret Read
#summary
A woman executed for witchcraft in 1590 left a permanent mark when her heart burst onto a nearby building.
#details
Margaret Read was burned at the stake as a witch on the Tuesday Market Place, and the intense heat caused her heart to burst from her body and hit a building. Although the building has changed, the spot is still marked by a carved heart in a diamond on the brickwork above a window.

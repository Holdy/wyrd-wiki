#title
The Devil's Hoof-print in Devils Alley
#summary
A narrow thoroughfare in King's Lynn preserves the impression of the Devil's foot after a confrontation with a priest.
#details
In a passageway named Devils Alley, a hoof-print of the Devil is said to be embedded in the ground where he stomped his foot after being driven away by a local priest.

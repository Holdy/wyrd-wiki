#title
The Skull of Criminal Eugene Aram
#summary
The Stories of Lynn Museum exhibits the skull of schoolteacher and convicted criminal Eugene Aram alongside a fragment from his victim's skull.
#details
Moving to King’s Lynn in 1758 after local shoemaker Daniel Clark disappeared in Yorkshire, schoolteacher Eugene Aram became a primary suspect. After a skeleton believed to be Clark was uncovered in Yorkshire, constables arrested Aram in King’s Lynn. He was tried, found guilty, and hanged in York in August 1759. Decades later, in 1993, Aram's skull was returned to King’s Lynn and gifted to the museum, where it is displayed next to a skull fragment believed to belong to Daniel Clark.

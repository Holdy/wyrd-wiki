The Manic Laughter of Castle Rising Castle

Summary

The upper floors of Castle Rising Castle ring out with the manic laughter and screams of Queen Isabella, who went mad with grief.

Details

Queen Isabella went mad with loneliness and grief after a troubled life and failed romance, and now her manic laughter and screams can be heard ringing out from the upper floors of Castle Rising Castle, accompanied by tapping and strange mists.

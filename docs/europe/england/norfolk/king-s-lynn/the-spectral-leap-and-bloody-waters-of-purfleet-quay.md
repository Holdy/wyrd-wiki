#title
The Spectral Leap and Bloody Waters of Purfleet Quay
#summary
Purfleet Quay is haunted by a phantom that throws itself into the water, alongside ghostly sounds of fighting soldiers and blood-red waters.
#details
At Purfleet Quay, the medieval anchorage overlooked by the Custom House, witnesses have reported seeing a ghost wander around before screaming and flinging itself into the waters below. Ghostly screams of fighting soldiers have also been heard in the area, and the water is said to run red with blood.

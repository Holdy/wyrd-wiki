The Grey Lady and Christmas Snow Cleaner of the Tudor Rose Hotel

Summary

Witnesses at the Tudor Rose Hotel have reported a Grey Lady in the restaurant and a strange figure clearing snow in the yard at Christmas.

Details

In addition to other phenomena at the Tudor Rose Hotel, witnesses have reported seeing a Grey Lady who makes herself visible in the hotel restaurant. Furthermore, a strange figure has been observed clearing snow in the yard during Christmastime.

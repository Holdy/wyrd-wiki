#title
The Witch’s Heart of Tuesday Market Place
#summary
Following the 1590 execution of Margaret Read for witchcraft, her heart allegedly burst from her flames and smashed into a nearby building.
#details
In the 16th and 17th centuries, the Tuesday Market Place was the site of public executions for alleged witches. When Margaret Read was sentenced to death by burning in 1590, witnesses reported that as her body burned, her heart burst from her chest and smashed into the building opposite. Today, a 16th-century building still stands at numbers 15 and 16 in the Tuesday Market Place, featuring a heart carved into the red brick above a window to mark the spot where her heart landed.

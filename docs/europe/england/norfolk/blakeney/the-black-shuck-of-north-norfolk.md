#title
The Black Shuck of North Norfolk

#summary
A terrifying phantom hound with glaring eyes haunts the salt marshes, coastal paths, and lanes of North Norfolk, often appearing before a terrible event.

#details
The legendary Black Shuck is a huge shaggy beast widely spotted on North Norfolk's salt marshes and coastal paths. Depending on the version of the legend, it has glaring red or green eyes. It is also reported to haunt Little Lane in Blakeney alongside a spooky coach and horses. Sometimes appearing as a headless monster or floating on a swirl of mist, almost all sightings of the phantom hound precede a terrible event.

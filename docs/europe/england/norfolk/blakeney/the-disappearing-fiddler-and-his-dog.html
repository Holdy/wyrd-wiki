The Disappearing Fiddler and His Dog

Summary

A musician and his little dog vanished forever after exploring a mysterious underground smuggling tunnel while playing his fiddle.

Details

A fiddler and his pooch were enticed into a mysterious tunnel underneath Blakeney. The musician went to explore the underground passage while playing his fiddle, and neither he nor his dog were ever seen again. Their disappearance is commemorated on Blakeney's village sign.

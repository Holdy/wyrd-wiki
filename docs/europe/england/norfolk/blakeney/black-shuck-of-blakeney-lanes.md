#title
Black Shuck of Blakeney Lanes
#summary
The ghostly dog Black Shuck prowls the lanes of Blakeney to the sound of jangling chains, howling for his master who drowned in a shipwreck.
#details
The huge ghostly dog known as Black Shuck prowls Back Lane in Blakeney, floating above the ground to the sound of jangling chains, and also haunts Little Lane. The legend originates from early 1709 during a terrible storm when a cargo ship was wrecked at Salthouse, drowning the crew; Black Shuck washed ashore dead next to his master and has haunted the area ever since, howling for him.

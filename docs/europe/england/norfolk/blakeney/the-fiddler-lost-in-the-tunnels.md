#title
The Fiddler Lost in the Tunnels

#summary
A fiddler who explored the tunnels under Blakeney Guildhall while playing his music suddenly stopped and disappeared forever.

#details
A series of tunnels run under Blakeney Guildhall. A fiddler once offered to explore these tunnels while playing his fiddle so villagers could follow his progress, but the music suddenly stopped and he was never seen again.

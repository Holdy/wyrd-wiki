#title
The Hyter Sprites of Blakeney Tunnels
#summary
Spidery, fairy-like creatures known as hyter sprites are said to inhabit the tunnels beneath Blakeney and kidnap lost children.
#details
Legend states that the secret tunnels beneath the village of Blakeney are home to 'hyter sprites'—creatures that resemble a cross between fairies and spiders—which are said to kidnap naughty children who get lost on the salt marshes.

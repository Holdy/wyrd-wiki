The Hytersprites of Blakeney Tunnels

Summary

A network of tunnels beneath the village of Blakeney is said to be home to spider-like creatures known as hytersprites.

Details

According to legend, an array of creatures inhabits a network of underground tunnels beneath the village of Blakeney, among them being spider-like entities referred to as hytersprites.

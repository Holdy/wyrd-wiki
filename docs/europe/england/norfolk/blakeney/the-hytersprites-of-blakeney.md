#title
The Hytersprites of Blakeney

#summary
Shy, green-eyed creatures that can transform into birds kidnap misbehaving children on the salt marshes, but return them when shown kindness.

#details
Hytersprites are sand-coloured, green-eyed creatures native to Blakeney that possess the ability to transform into Sand Martins, a commonly seen small bird. These very shy beings are known to kidnap badly behaved children who stray onto the salt marshes. However, they are grateful for human kindness and have been known to safely return lost children to their homes.

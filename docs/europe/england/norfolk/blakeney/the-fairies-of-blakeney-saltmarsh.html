The Fairies of Blakeney Saltmarsh

Summary

Fairies on the saltmarsh in Blakeney are said to kidnap badly behaved children who stray, though they return lost children to their families.

Details

Legend states that fairies inhabit the saltmarsh in Blakeney and will kidnap badly behaved children who stray onto the marsh, though they ultimately return any lost children safely to their families.

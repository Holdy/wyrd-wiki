Black Shuck

Summary

For centuries, the phantom dog Black Shuck has patrolled the coastal paths of Norfolk as a spectral portent of death.

Details

Black Shuck is a spectral hellhound that has patrolled the coastal paths of Norfolk for centuries, serving as a terrifying portent of death.

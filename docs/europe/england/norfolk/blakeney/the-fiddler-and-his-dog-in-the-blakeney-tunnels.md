#title
The Fiddler and His Dog in the Blakeney Tunnels
#summary
A fiddler and his dog entered a smuggler passage leading from Blakeney Guildhall playing music to chart his progress, but the music suddenly stopped and they were never seen again.
#details
A fiddler volunteered to explore a smuggler passageway leading from Blakeney Guildhall on Mariner's Hill. He went into the tunnel with his dog and played his fiddle so villagers could listen and chart his progress. Suddenly, the music stopped, and neither the fiddler nor his dog were ever seen again.

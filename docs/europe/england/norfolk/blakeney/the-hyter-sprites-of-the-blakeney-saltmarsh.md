#title
The Hyter Sprites of the Blakeney Saltmarsh
#summary
Long-limbed fairy sprites inhabit the smuggler tunnels of Blakeney and are said to kidnap badly behaved children who stray onto the saltmarsh.
#details
The long-limbed fairy sprites known as hyter sprites live in the network of smuggler tunnels beneath Blakeney. According to legend, they kidnap badly behaved village children who stray onto the saltmarsh, serving as a warning to others to beware of incoming tides.

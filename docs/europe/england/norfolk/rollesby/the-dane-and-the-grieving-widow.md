#title
The Dane and the Grieving Widow
#summary
The ghosts of a slain Saxon lord, his killer, and the lord's grieving wife famously haunt Rollesby on the second Monday of every month.
#details
Rollesby is haunted by two distinct ghosts who appear at midnight on the second Monday of every month. One is a Dane whose face is covered in blood from the Saxon lord he killed, and he is followed closely by the ghost of the Saxon lord's wife, who wrings her hands and begs for her husband's life.

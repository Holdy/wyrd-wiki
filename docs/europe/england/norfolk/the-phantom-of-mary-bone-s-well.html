The Phantom of Mary Bone's Well

Summary

A woman was tragically drowned in an ancient well by a corrupt priest near the historic priory, leaving her ghostly spirit bound to the waters.

Details

Deep in the quiet fields of West Norfolk near the River Tat, Mary Bone's Well marks the site of a dark historical tragedy. According to local folklore, a woman associated with the nearby Augustinian St. Mary's Priory was murdered when a local priest drowned her in the well. The priory itself was historically plagued by scandal and lax discipline among its clergy, fostering an environment where such dark deeds could occur. To this day, the phantom of a White Lady is said to haunt the well, forever linked to the violent crime that tainted the priory's primary water supply.

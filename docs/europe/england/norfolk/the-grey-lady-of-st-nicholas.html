The Grey Lady of St. Nicholas

Summary

A spectral Grey Lady is frequently sighted gliding through the churchyard and grounds of old Brandiston Hall, accompanied by sudden drops in temperature and unexplained noises.

Details

Residents and visitors to Brandiston have long reported eerie paranormal encounters centered around the historic Church of St. Nicholas and the surrounding area of old Brandiston Hall. Most notably, a phantom figure known as the "Grey Lady"—believed to be the spirit of a former resident or nun—is seen in the late evening and early morning hours gliding silently through the churchyard. These apparitions are frequently accompanied by an abrupt, unnatural chill and disembodied voices. In addition to the spectral sightings, older cottages in the village have experienced poltergeist phenomena, including doors opening and closing violently, heavy objects moving on their own, and unsettling knocking sounds echoing through the walls.

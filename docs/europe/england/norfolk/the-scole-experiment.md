#title
The Scole Experiment

#summary
During the 1990s, the cellar of a 17th-century farmhouse in Scole became the site of controversial seances and alleged paranormal phenomena.

#details
Starting in 1993, the Scole Experiment Group—comprising local couples and mediums—began conducting regular seances in the cellar of Street Farmhouse in Scole. Operating in complete darkness, the group reported extraordinary phenomena, including mysterious spirit voices recorded on tape, floating lights captured on film, and physical objects moving autonomously. These sessions drew international renown and led to similar gatherings across Europe and America. However, after five years of investigation, the group abruptly halted the experiments, claiming they had encountered interference from an anomalous group of time-space travelers whose motives were potentially dangerous.

#location
The cellar of Street Farmhouse is located in the village of Scole, Norfolk.

The Spectral Dog of Buxton Church

Summary

A young woman walking near Buxton church encountered a phantom black dog that vanished instantly, moments before her brother died in Liverpool. Years later, the village schoolmaster recounted tales of Black Shuck sightings in Buxton.

Details

While walking home toward Lamas with a female friend after parting near Buxton church, a young woman watched the church clock strike 4:00 pm. Instantly, a large black dog appeared at her side. Feeling no fear, she reached down to pat the animal and said hello, but before she could finish her sentence, the dog vanished into thin air. Four days later, she received a letter informing her that her brother had passed away in Liverpool at the exact date and time of the sighting. In the 1920s, a local schoolmaster recounted to his pupils that Black Shuck had been seen in Buxton.

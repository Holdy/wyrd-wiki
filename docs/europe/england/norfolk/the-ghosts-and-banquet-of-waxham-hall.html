The Ghosts and Banquet of Waxham Hall

Summary

Waxham Hall is reputed to be haunted by the restless spirits of the Brograve family, whose 18th-century owner famously hosted a midnight New Year's Eve banquet for the ghosts of his ancestors.

Details

According to local tradition, the historic Waxham Hall in Norfolk is haunted by the unshriven spirits of the Brograve family, whose male descendants frequently met violent or tragic ends. In the late 18th century, the eccentric landowner Sir Berney Brograve—known locally as 'Owd Sir Barney'—became the center of dark folklore. Legend recounts that on New Year's Eve, Sir Berney prepared a grand banquet within the hall, laying covers and filling glasses for six ghostly ancestor guests. He solemnly drank toasts to each of the spectral figures until midnight struck and they vanished. Beyond the ghostly dinner, the hall became notorious for its haunted attic chamber, purported bloodstains, and sightings of Sir Berney himself driving a phantom coach along the local causeway.

The Secret Tunnel of Stalham

Summary

According to local legend, a secret underground passage once linked St Mary's Church and the medieval Maids Head Inn in Stalham. The passage was supposedly built during the construction of the church to connect the parish place of worship with the workers' lodgings.

Details

Local folklore in the Norfolk market town of Stalham maintains that a concealed subterranean tunnel connects St Mary's Church with the former Maids Head Inn across the High Street. The inn itself is said to have originated as a medieval building erected to house the stonemasons and labourers constructing the church. Over generations, stories persisted among residents that the passage allowed clandestine movement between the tavern and the holy site. Although the Maids Head Inn has since closed and its building converted into retail shops, the legend of the underground tunnel endures as part of the town's historical mythology.

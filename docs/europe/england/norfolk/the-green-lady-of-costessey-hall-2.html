The Green Lady of Costessey Hall

Summary

A spectral woman dressed in green guards the ivy-clad ruins and belfry of a historic Norfolk estate, acting as an echo of its tumultuous past. Witnesses report her lingering presence around the remaining structures of the once-grand Gothic hall.

Details

Costessey Hall, a magnificent Gothic mansion built by the Jerningham family, met its demise after being requisitioned for military training during World War One, leaving behind only scattered ruins and a belfry. Amidst this architectural loss, local lore speaks of the Green Lady, a restless female spirit who watches over the grounds. Believed by some to embody the protective spirit of Catholic residents who once sheltered persecuted priests in secret attic chapels and priest holes, the apparition is said to haunt the surviving structures, including the ill-fated Thornbury Tower before its collapse. To this day, locals recount encounters with this elusive guardian of the estate.

#title
The Jilted Countess of Oxburgh Hall

#summary
The ghost of a jilted Italian countess who reportedly drowned in the moat is said to roam Oxburgh Hall's north bedroom and staircase.

#details
A countess in Italy became a jilted lover and is thought to have jumped out of the north bedroom window of Oxburgh Hall, drowning in the moat below. Visitors to the hall have seen a woman in a Tudor dress walking through the grounds and have also felt a drop in temperature, even though the hall did not have costume volunteers on the day of those reports.

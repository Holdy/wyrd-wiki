The Jilted Countess of Oxburgh Hall

Summary

Oxburgh Hall is haunted by the Countess of Italy, a jilted lover who fell from the North Bedroom window and drowned in the moat.

Details

The 15th-century moated country house is said to be haunted by the Countess of Italy, a jilted lover who allegedly fell to her death from the North Bedroom window into the moat below. Visitors to the property have reported sightings of a woman in Tudor dress walking the grounds when no costumed volunteers were present, alongside sudden, unexplained drops in environmental temperature.

The Builder's Bargain

Summary

A poor man who sold his soul to the devil in exchange for funds to build the local church outwitted the demon by dying just in time to be buried within consecrated ground.

Details

According to local folklore, a man struck a bargain with the Devil, trading his soul for great riches, part of which he used to finance the construction of the original church at Burgh St Peter. When the Devil eventually returned to collect his payment, he arrived to find that the man had died just hours earlier and had already been buried in the sacred churchyard, safely out of the demon's reach. Frustrated by this trick, a skeleton representing the cheated soul-seller—or the Devil himself disguised as an old man—is said to haunt the church porch or churchyard on the night of May 2nd, clutching the parchment contract. Additionally, traditional lore holds that the Devil spends one night a year sitting on the Devil's Stile near the former rectory.

#title
The Spectral Apparition of Rainthorpe Hall

#summary
On the anniversary of her mysterious Elizabethan death, the tragic noblewoman Amy Robsart is said to return as a specter, appearing alongside a gentleman in the gardens of Rainthorpe Hall.

#details
Amy Robsart, the ill-fated wife of Robert Dudley (the Earl of Leicester) whose suspicious death in 1560 at Cumnor Place has baffled historians for centuries, is woven into local Norfolk folklore. According to tradition, after her former residences were demolished, her wandering spirit travelled back to Rainthorpe Hall in Tasburgh. Every year on the anniversary of her passing, the ghostly figure of Amy is said to materialize in the historic hall's gardens, sitting quietly alongside a mysterious gentleman companion—sparking ongoing local debate over whether the spirit beside her is her husband or her half-brother.

#location
Rainthorpe Hall, situated in the parish of Tasburgh, south of Norwich in Norfolk, England.

The Apparition of Mary Barley

Summary

An elderly woman dressed in dark clothing and a shawl is occasionally seen wandering at night in Short Beck. She is believed to be the spirit of Mary Barley, who passed away in Feltwell in 1875.

Details

Mary Barley died in Feltwell in 1875 at the age of 67. Since her passing, she has been reported appearing at night in the Borough—the triangular patch of land situated between the left and right forks in Short Beck—near the house where she once lived. Local folklore suggests she continues to walk the area because of a hidden or buried hoard of money she cannot leave behind.

    The Hidden Treasure of Gold Hill

    Summary

    According to local Fenland folklore, a portion of King John's legendary lost treasure was hidden on a small marshland mound at Gold Hill in Welney parish.

    Details

    The tiny hamlet of Gold Hill, situated beside the B1411 just inside Welney parish next to the Hundred Foot Washes, was once no more than a low mound rising slightly above the surrounding marshland where early Fenmen lived in rough huts. Local tradition maintains that this isolated mound served as the hiding place for a portion of King John's royal treasure, which was allegedly stolen from him at Wisbech Castle long before the remainder of his baggage train famously met its watery end in the Wash.

The Ghostly Resident of Hainford Hall

Summary

Hainford Hall is reputed to be haunted by the spirit of a young girl who tragically died within one of the manor's rooms.

Details

During a visit to the derelict and abandoned Hainford Hall—which was surrounded by a motor salvage yard—an explorer was informed by the property manager that the manor house was haunted. The manager recounted the tragic story of a young girl who had died in one of the rooms, noting that an old kettle still sat on the cooker in that very room.

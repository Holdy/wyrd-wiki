    The Skipping Block of Barnham Broom

    Summary

    At a historic crossroads on the parish boundary between Barnham Broom and Kimberley once stood a remarkable stone monolith featuring carved mounting steps known to locals as the Skipping Block. Although the stone has since vanished, its memory endured in local folklore as an ancient mark-stone.

    Details

    At the crossroads historically situated on the boundary between Barnham Broom and Kimberley—now mapped as Skipping Block Corner—there once rested a large, unusual stone. According to local tradition, the rock featured steps carved into its surface, serving both as a parish boundary marker and a mounting block for travelers traversing the old parish lines. Over generations, the stone became a focal point of local folklore regarding ancient mark-stones in East Anglia, before eventually disappearing from its roadside position.

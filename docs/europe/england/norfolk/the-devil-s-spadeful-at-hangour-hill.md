#title
The Devil's Spadeful at Hangour Hill

#summary
The Devil created Hangour Hill by shaking excess earth from his spade after digging the ancient Bichamditch. Beneath this barrow, a mysterious pair of silver gates is said to be hidden.

#details
According to local tradition, the Devil took up a spade and carved out the linear earthwork known as the Bichamditch, or Devil's Ditch, in Beachamwell. Upon finishing his excavation, he shook the leftover earth from his spade, which formed the prominent round barrow known as Hangour Hill. Folklore adds that deep beneath this earthen mound, a hidden pair of silver gates remains buried out of sight.

#location
Hangour Hill is a round barrow located at grid reference TF752087 in Beachamwell, Norfolk.

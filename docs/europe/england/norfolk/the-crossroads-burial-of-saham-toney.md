#title
The Crossroads Burial of Saham Toney

#summary
Following an act of ostracism after he poured wine and beer into the river, a local man took his life around 1790 and was interred at a village crossroads.

#details
Around 1790, an unnamed resident in Saham Toney began pouring wine and beer into the local river. This eccentric behavior outraged the villagers, who ostracized him by sending him to "Coventry." Distressed by the community's rejection, the man died by suicide and was consequently buried at the crossroads in accordance with post-medieval custom for self-murderers.

#location
The crossroads where Bell Lane, Ovington Road, Cley Lane, and Chequers Lane meet in Saham Toney, Norfolk (Grid Reference TF907019).

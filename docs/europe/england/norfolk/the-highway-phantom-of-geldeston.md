#title
The Highway Phantom of Geldeston

#summary
A terrifying spectral black dog as large as a horse once stalked lone travellers along the road from Geldeston to Bungay, growling fiercely and dragging victims by their clothing.

#details
At Geldeston in Norfolk, a monstrous black hound was historically known to prowl the highway leading toward Bungay. Described as being as large as a horse with fiery eyes and foaming jaws, the spectral beast would target pedestrians travelling alone at night. It stalked its victims closely, growling menacingly whenever anyone attempted to turn back, and occasionally seizing terrified travellers by their clothes to drag them along the road.

#location
The highway leading from Geldeston towards Bungay in Norfolk.

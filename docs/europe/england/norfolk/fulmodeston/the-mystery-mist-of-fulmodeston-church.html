The Mystery Mist of Fulmodeston Church

Summary

Walkers exploring the abandoned ruins of St Mary at Fulmodeston encountered a strange white mist, the smell of sulphur, mysterious voices, and a spectral figure.

Details

In August 2005, a group of walkers visiting the ivy-covered remains of the abandoned St Mary at Fulmodeston church decided to explore the ruins. However, they were met by a strange white mist and an overpowering smell of sulphur. As they hurried away from the site, mysterious voices were heard and a spectral figure was seen walking on a floor that had long since crumbled away.

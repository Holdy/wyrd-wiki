#title
The Long Coastguardsman

#summary
Betrayed by a double-crossing informant and brutally murdered by smugglers on the Norfolk coast, a coastguardsman's spirit is condemned to endlessly patrol his final post.

#details
In the late 18th century, a coastguardsman tasked with policing the treacherous Norfolk coastline believed he had turned a local informer to help catch smugglers red-handed. Alerted that a contraband ship was due at night, he sent a boy for military backup while waiting at the cliff edge. However, the boy betrayed him, leading the smugglers directly to his position instead of the troops. Overwhelmed and hacked to pieces by the gang, his remains were hurled over the cliff into the sea. Bound to the site of his violent death, his towering apparition has haunted the lonely coastline for centuries—with encounters recorded near Ostend, close to Walcott—where he strides purposefully through the darkness and screams into the wind.

#location
The coastal cliff edge and beach area at Ostend, near Walcott, Norfolk.

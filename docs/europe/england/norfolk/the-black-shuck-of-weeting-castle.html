The Black Shuck of Weeting Castle

Summary

Visitors walking around the moat of Weeting Castle have reported encountering the infamous Black Shuck, a noiseless giant black hound with a single fiery eye.

Details

While Weeting Castle experienced a largely peaceful history free from sieges or conflict, its 12th-century ruins are occasionally disturbed by ghostly phenomena. In addition to reports of strange mists and disembodied voices, a couple walking around the castle's moat had a chilling encounter with the legendary Black Shuck. Known across Norfolk as a silent black dog as large as a calf with a single fiery eye in the middle of its head, encountering this spectral beast is traditionally believed to portend death before the year's end.

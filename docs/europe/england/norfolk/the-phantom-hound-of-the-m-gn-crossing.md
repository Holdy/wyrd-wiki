#title
The Phantom Hound of the M&GN Crossing

#summary
A spectral dog is said to haunt a former railway crossing in Hempton at midnight on the anniversary of its death after being struck by a steam train. The haunting local legend was preserved through oral family tradition.

#details
According to local memory passed down by a resident whose grandmother moved to the area in 1930, a ghostly dog haunts the old Midland and Great Northern (M&GN) railway crossing at midnight on the anniversary of its death. The hound was reportedly struck and killed by a passing steam train while heading home toward the Grove, a rambling house situated near the railway station. As children grew up hearing the tale, crossing the railway line at night remained an eerie and unsettling experience.

#location
The former M&GN railway crossing in Hempton, located near the old railway station and the Grove house (grid reference TF917292).

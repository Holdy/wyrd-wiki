#title
The Vanishing Visitor of Oxburgh Hall
#summary
A visiting Spanish or Italian woman mysteriously disappeared while heavily pregnant at Oxburgh Hall during the 16th century.
#details
During the 16th century, a Spanish or Italian woman visiting Oxburgh Hall disappeared while heavily pregnant. Her ghost has since been reported at the property, though no historical records document any tragic consequences from her visit.

#title
The Old Man of Hopton

#summary
A motorist driving along the Hopton Bypass encountered a strange, stocky figure with oversized boots plodding across the dual carriageway.

#details
In 1980, British Transport Police officer PC Frank Colby and his wife were driving along the Hopton Bypass toward Lowestoft when they reached the southern section of the road. There, PC Colby spotted what he initially believed to be a man crossing the dual carriageway. The apparition was about 5'6" tall, stocky in build, and wore a calf-length shapeless garment. Its head was hunched into its shoulders with spiky hair, and it wore enormous boots that it seemed to lift heavily as it plodded along. Earlier recorded accounts of this jaywalking phantom date back to the 1950s, with various drivers reporting similar spine-chilling encounters with the figure on the highway.

#location
The southern section of the Hopton Bypass (A47) in Hopton-on-Sea, Norfolk.

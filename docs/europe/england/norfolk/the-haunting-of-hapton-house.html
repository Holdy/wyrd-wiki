The Haunting of Hapton House

Summary

While working upstairs alone in Hapton House, a local cleaner experienced phantom footsteps, doors repeatedly slamming and opening on their own, and flowers moving across shelves.

Details

During her years working across various homes in the village, Joan Sculfor spent time cleaning at Hapton House. While working alone upstairs in the building, she heard unexplained footsteps pacing along the passageway outside. When she checked, nobody was there. Immediately afterward, a door slammed shut by itself, then creaked open, repeating the cycle despite the complete absence of any breeze or draughts. During her time there, she also witnessed flowers mysteriously shifting positions on the shelves.

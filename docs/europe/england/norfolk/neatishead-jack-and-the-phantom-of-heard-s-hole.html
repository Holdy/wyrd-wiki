Neatishead Jack and the Phantom of Heard's Hole

Summary

A malevolent will-o'-the-wisp spirit known as Neatishead Jack terrorized travelers near Alderfen Broad until three men successfully bound his ghost with the distraction of a pair of pigeons.

Details

On the Neatishead side of Alderfen Broad, a watery spot known as Heard's Hole was said to be haunted by the spirit of a criminal named Heard who had drowned there after committing numerous crimes. Manifesting as a glowing Jack-o'-Lantern light, the malevolent spirit frequently tormented locals by smashing lanterns, knocking horse riders off their mounts, and following people home. Seeking to banish the nuisance, three men attempted to lay the ghost by reading passages of Scripture aloud. However, the cunning spirit stayed constantly one verse ahead of them, rendering their prayers useless. Their stalemate was only broken when a local boy brought a couple of pigeons and laid them down before the spirit; distracted by the birds, the ghost lost his verse, allowing the men to successfully bind his spirit.

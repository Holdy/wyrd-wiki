#title
The Happisburgh Torso
#summary
Farmers walking home near the coast have reported seeing a legless, hunchbacked figure coming from Cart Gap with its severed head trailing down its back.
#details
Farmers walking home late are said to have seen a legless figure coming from the direction of Cart Gap. Initially thought to be hunchbacked, witnesses realized the hump was actually its half-severed head hanging down its back.

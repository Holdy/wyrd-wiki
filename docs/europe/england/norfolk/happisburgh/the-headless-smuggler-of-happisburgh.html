The Headless Smuggler of Happisburgh

Summary

A headless and legless apparition carrying a sack is said to haunt the village before storms, disappearing into the well where a murdered smuggler's body was hidden.

Details

Legend tells of a murdered smuggler whose mutilated body was hidden in a village well. Villagers later reported seeing a headless and legless apparition carrying a sack through the streets before it disappeared into the well. The ghost was said to reappear before storms, becoming a lasting part of local folklore.

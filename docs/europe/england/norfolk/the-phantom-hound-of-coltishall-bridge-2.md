#title
The Phantom Hound of Coltishall Bridge

#summary
Black Shuck, the legendary ghostly black hound of East Anglia, is frequently sighted as a silent, massive beast near Coltishall Bridge and vanishing into thin air on Coltishall High Street.

#details
Black Shuck, the terrifying spectral hound of East Anglia, is said to haunt the upper reaches of the River Bure at Coltishall Bridge, appearing variously as a headless beast or a silent creature as big as a calf. Sightings of the phantom dog persisted well into the 20th century, notably when two RAF officers in the 1960s were stunned to encounter a large black dog on the cobbles of Coltishall High Street, only for the apparition to completely vanish into thin air.

#location
Coltishall Bridge spans the River Bure in the Norfolk village of Coltishall.

    The Phantom Players of the Squash Courts

    Summary

    The spirits of three aircrew members who died in a World War II plane crash are said to haunt the 1918 squash courts where they frequently played in life. Visitors and documentary crews have repeatedly heard the phantom sounds of a bouncing ball and doors moving on their own.

    Details

    During World War II, the military installations around Docking and RAF Bircham Newton saw intense activity and numerous aircraft losses. Among the tragedies was a crash involving a Lockheed Hudson bomber, resulting in the deaths of three crew members. In life, these men were passionate players of the base's 1918 squash courts. Following their deaths, their spirits reportedly returned to the courts. Numerous witnesses—including a Japanese television documentary crew filming on location—have reported hearing the distinct sounds of a ball striking string and walls, as well as interior doors mysteriously opening and closing.

Fulmodeston's Unwelcoming Mist

Summary

In August 2005, a group of walkers exploring the ruined St Mary's Church in Fulmodeston were confronted by a sulphurous white mist, phantom voices, and a ghostly figure walking on non-existent upper floors.

Details

In August 2005, a group of walkers stumbled upon the atmospheric, ivy-covered ruins of St Mary's Church in Fulmodeston, which had been abandoned in the 1880s. As they navigated the brambles to explore the site, they were suddenly met by an unnatural white mist and an overpowering smell of sulphur. Terrified by the hostile atmosphere, the group attempted to beat a hasty exit, during which they heard disembodied voices and witnessed a spectral figure walking upon a church floor that had long since crumbled away.

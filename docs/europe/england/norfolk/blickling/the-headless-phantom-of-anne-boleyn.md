#title
The Headless Phantom of Anne Boleyn
#summary
On the anniversary of her execution, the apparition of Anne Boleyn is said to ride through Blickling Estate carrying her head.
#details
According to local legend, the ghost of Anne Boleyn haunts Blickling Estate on the anniversary of her execution, appearing as a phantom riding through the grounds while carrying her severed head.

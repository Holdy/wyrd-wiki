#title
The Headless Coach
#summary
A phantom procession featuring a headless queen, headless horses, and a headless coachman drives up to Blickling once a year.
#details
On the anniversary of her execution, Anne Boleyn is said to ride up to Blickling in a phantom coach. The macabre procession consists of a headless queen, headless horses, and a headless coachman.

Sir Thomas Boleyn's Twelve Bridge Penance

Summary

Condemned for failing to save his children, the ghost of Sir Thomas Boleyn races a spectral coach across twelve local bridges before cockcrow.

Details

For not saving Anne and George Boleyn from execution, Sir Thomas Boleyn is condemned to cross twelve local bridges before cockcrow. His phantom is seen racing a spectral coach in a straight line from Blickling through Aylsham, Burgh, Buxton, Coltishall, Meyton, Oxnead, and Wroxham to fulfill his ghostly penance.

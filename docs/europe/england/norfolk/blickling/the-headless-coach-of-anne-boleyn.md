#title
The Headless Coach of Anne Boleyn

#summary
On the anniversary of her death, Anne Boleyn's headless ghost arrives at Blickling Hall in a carriage drawn by headless horses and a headless coachman.

#details
On the anniversary of her execution, Anne Boleyn's ghost makes a dramatic appearance at Blickling Hall. She arrives seated in a ghostly carriage drawn by headless horses and driven by a headless coachman. She is also headless, holding her severed, dripping head in her lap. Upon reaching the door, the coach and driver vanish, leaving the headless Queen to glide alone into the hall to roam the corridors and rooms until daybreak.

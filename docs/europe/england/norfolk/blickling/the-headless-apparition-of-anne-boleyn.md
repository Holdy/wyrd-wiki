#title
The Headless Apparition of Anne Boleyn

#summary
Anne Boleyn's headless ghost arrives annually at her birthplace in a coach drawn by headless horses while carrying her severed head.

#details
Anne Boleyn, who was born at Blickling Hall, is said to haunt the estate. Each year on May 19th, her headless ghost appears arriving in a coach drawn by headless horses, carrying her own severed head.

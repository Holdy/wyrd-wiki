The Dying Groans of Sir Henry Hobart

Summary

The dying groans of Sir Henry Hobart echo down the centuries through Blickling Hall following his fatal 1698 duel.

Details

Sir Henry Hobart died of his wounds inside the house following a duel in 1698. His dying groans are reported to still echo down the centuries from time to time, chilling the blood of everyone who hears them.

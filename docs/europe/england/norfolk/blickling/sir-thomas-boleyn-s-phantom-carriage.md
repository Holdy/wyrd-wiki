#title
Sir Thomas Boleyn's Phantom Carriage
#summary
Anne Boleyn's father is doomed to drive a carriage and four headless horses across twelve local bridges once a year.
#details
Sir Thomas Boleyn is condemned to spend one night every year on May 19th driving a coach and four headless horses around twelve bridges in the area. He carries his head under his arm while flames emanate from his mouth.

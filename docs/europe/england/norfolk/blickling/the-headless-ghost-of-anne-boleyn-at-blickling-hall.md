#title
The Headless Ghost of Anne Boleyn at Blickling Hall
#summary
The ghost of executed queen Anne Boleyn returns to her Norfolk birthplace at Blickling Hall each year carrying her head.
#details
Anne Boleyn, Henry VIII's second wife who was beheaded at the Tower of London in 1536, is said to return to her Norfolk birthplace at Blickling Hall every year on May 19th, riding through the grounds while carrying her head.

#title
The Headless Apparition of Anne Boleyn
#summary
Anne Boleyn's ghost returns to Blickling Hall in a headless coach and glides through the rooms.
#details
Executed by Henry VIII on May 19th, Anne Boleyn's ghost is seen every year on that date when a headless coachman drives up to the house with Anne sitting inside, headless while nursing her poor head. She has also been seen gliding through doors and appearing regularly in the South drawing-room.

Blickling Hall, Blickling, Norfolk, England

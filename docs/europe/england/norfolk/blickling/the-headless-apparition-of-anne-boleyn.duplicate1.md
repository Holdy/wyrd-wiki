#title
The Headless Apparition of Anne Boleyn

#summary
Annually on the anniversary of her execution, a headless Anne Boleyn is said to arrive at Blickling Hall in a carriage drawn by spectral horses before gliding through the corridors.

#details
Anne Boleyn, the second wife of Henry VIII who was beheaded in 1536, is said to return to her birthplace, Blickling Hall. Each year on May 19th, she arrives in a carriage driven by a headless horseman and drawn by four spectral horses, clutching her severed, blood-dripping head. Once at the hall, her spectre glides through the corridors from dusk until sunrise.

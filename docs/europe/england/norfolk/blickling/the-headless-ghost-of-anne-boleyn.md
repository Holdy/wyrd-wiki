#title
The Headless Ghost of Anne Boleyn
#summary
The executed Queen of England is said to wander the drawing room of her childhood home carrying her severed head.
#details
Anne Boleyn, who spent her early years at Blickling Hall, is said to haunt the building's Drawing Room while carrying her head in her hand.

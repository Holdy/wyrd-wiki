#title
The Dragged Spirit of Lord Rochford

#summary
On the anniversary of his sister's execution, the headless ghost of Lord Rochford is dragged across the countryside by four headless horses.

#details
Appearing on the same night as Anne Boleyn's ghostly return, her brother Lord Rochford manifests as a headless spirit. Unlike his sister, he does not have the comfort of a carriage and is instead dragged across the surrounding countryside by four headless horses.

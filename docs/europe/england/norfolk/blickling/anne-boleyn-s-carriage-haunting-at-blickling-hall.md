#title
Anne Boleyn's Carriage Haunting at Blickling Hall
#summary
Anne Boleyn's ghost returns to Blickling Hall at midnight on her execution anniversary in a coach driven by a headless horseman.
#details
On the anniversary of her execution, King Henry VIII's second wife Anne Boleyn is said to haunt her ancestral home of Blickling Hall. Once the clock strikes midnight, her ghost appears in a coach drawn by a headless horseman, carrying her own severed head on her lap.

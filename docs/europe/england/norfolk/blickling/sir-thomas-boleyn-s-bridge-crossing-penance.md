#title
Sir Thomas Boleyn's Bridge-Crossing Penance

#summary
As a thousand-year penance, Sir Thomas Boleyn must attempt to drive his spectral coach and horses over twelve bridges between Wroxham and Blickling while carrying his head under his arm.

#details
Once a year for a thousand years following his death in 1539, Sir Thomas Boleyn must perform a dreadful penance. He attempts to drive his spectral coach and horses over twelve bridges that lie between Wroxham and Blickling, a nearly impossible task made even harder because he must steer while carrying his own head under his arm.

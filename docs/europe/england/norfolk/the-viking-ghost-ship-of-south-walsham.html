The Viking Ghost Ship of South Walsham

Summary

South Walsham Broad is said to be haunted by a phantom Viking ship bearing a dead chief. Both the vessel and its chieftain are seen ablaze before sinking beneath the water.

Details

According to folklore, the waters of South Walsham Broad are visited by a macabre apparition from the past: a burning Viking ship. A dead chief rests aboard the vessel as it is set on fire and ceremoniously sinks into the depths, re-enacting a fiery Norse funeral ritual.

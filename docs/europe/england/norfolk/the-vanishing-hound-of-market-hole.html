The Vanishing Hound of Market Hole

Summary

A young cyclist returning home late at night nearly collided with a massive spectral dog that suddenly materialized on a country road before vanishing into thin air.

Details

Mrs. Sophia Wilson, a native of Hempnall, recounted an unsettling encounter experienced by her 24-year-old son. As he was cycling home from Norwich along the stretch of road known as Market Hole, he suddenly spotted a large black dog about to cross directly in front of his bicycle. Expecting a violent crash, he braced for impact, but the creature abruptly vanished. Stopping to inspect the dark road, he found nothing at all, returning home pale and deeply shaken by the paranormal encounter.

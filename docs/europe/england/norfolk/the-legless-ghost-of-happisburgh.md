#title
The Legless Ghost of Happisburgh

#summary
Farmers walking near Cart Gap encounter a terrifying legless apparition carrying a sack, which eventually vanishes into a well on Whimpwell Street.

#details
While heading home near Cart Gap in Happisburgh, several farmers are horrified to encounter a lurching figure of a legless sailor carrying a filthy sack, with his head dangling from his neck by a ribbon of flesh. The spectral entity is subsequently tracked to a grassy triangle where Whimpwell Street meets Whimpwell Green and Coronation Road, where it vanishes into a well. Upon investigating the well, locals discover a sack containing sailor's boots with legs inside, as well as a torso and skull.

#location
The key location where the ghost vanishes into the well is at the grassy intersection where Whimpwell Street meets Whimpwell Green and Coronation Road in Happisburgh.

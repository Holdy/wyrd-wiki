#title
The Malicious Lantern Men of the Marshes
#summary
The malevolent marsh lights known as Lantern Men were said to lure travelers into bogs, smash lanterns, and attack anyone who showed them disrespect.
#details
The Lantern Men, or marsh gas lights, were regarded in local folklore as entirely malevolent entities bent on luring people into bogs and ponds. They despised lanterns, with one 1870s account describing a Lantern Man becoming engrossed in kicking an abandoned lantern while a frightened man fled. They also demanded strict respect; one Lantern Man knocked a man from his horse for calling him "Will o' the Wisp," while another followed an old man home who jeered at him.

#title
Mary Bone's Well

#summary
An ancient well in East Rudham is said to be named after a local woman who was brutally drowned in its waters by a medieval priest from the nearby priory.

#details
Located a short distance south-east of the ruins of the Augustinian St. Mary's Priory between Coxford and Broomsthorpe in East Rudham, the ancient Mary Bone's Well carries a grim local legend. According to village tradition, the well gets its moniker from a local woman named Mary Bone, who met a tragic end when she was drowned in its depths by a corrupt priest hailing from the nearby priory. The historic site continues to be marked on Ordnance Survey maps.

#location
Mary Bone's Well, situated south-east of St. Mary's Priory ruins between Coxford and Broomsthorpe in East Rudham, Norfolk (Grid Reference TF847287).

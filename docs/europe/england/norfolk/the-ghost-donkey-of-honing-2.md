#title
The Ghost Donkey of Honing

#summary
A translucent white donkey exhaling sulphurous smoke haunts the grounds of St Peter and St Paul church in Honing. The spectral animal terrifies onlookers by galloping at high speed and phasing straight through solid masonry.

#details
A witness near the sacred church of St Peter and St Paul in Honing was stunned to encounter a phantom white donkey. The creature, with sulphurous smoke pouring from its flared nostrils, galloped past at a frantic pace. To the witness's utter amazement, the beast was entirely transparent, allowing them to see right through its body to the hedge beyond before it shockingly passed straight through a church wall.

#location
The key location is St Peter and St Paul church in the village of Honing, Norfolk.

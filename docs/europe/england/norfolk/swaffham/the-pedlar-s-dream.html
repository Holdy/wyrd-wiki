The Pedlar's Dream

Summary

A pedlar traveled to London because of a dream, only to be told by a stranger to dig in his own garden where he found treasure.

Details

A pedlar walked all the way to London after having a dream about finding fortune there. In London, a stranger told him the dream meant he should go straight home and dig under his own tree, where he found treasure.

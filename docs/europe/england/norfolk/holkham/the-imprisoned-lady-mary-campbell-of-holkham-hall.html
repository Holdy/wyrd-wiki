    The Imprisoned Lady Mary Campbell of Holkham Hall

    Summary

    Lady Mary Campbell haunts Holkham Hall after being locked up for a year for shunning her new husband on her wedding night.

    Details

    Lady Mary Campbell, daughter of the Duke and Duchess of Argyll, was locked up at Holkham Hall for a full year after shunning her new husband on her wedding day. This dark period in her life led to her ghost returning to haunt the hall, with witnesses reporting her walking around her old room in old-fashioned clothes—prompting her mother to have the room exorcised. Her story also inspired Lady Glenconner's novel "A Haunting of Holkham".

#title
The Fiery Phantom Coach of Potter Heigham

#summary
On the anniversary of a cursed wedding, a fiery coach carrying a kidnapped bride plunges off Potter Heigham bridge.

#details
In the 18th century, Lady Carew sought the help of a local witch to secure a wealthy husband for her daughter Evelyn, promising whatever payment the witch later demanded in return. After Evelyn married Sir Godfrey Haslitt, a skeleton driving a coach drawn by four black horses interrupted the wedding reception and kidnapped the bride. The ensuing chase headed toward Potter Heigham, where the coach burst into flames and plunged into the water with Evelyn still inside, fulfilling the witch's demand. Every year on May 31st, witnesses report hearing hooves and wheel screeches before a fiery coach appears over the bridge and plunges into the water.

#title
The Drummer Boy of the River Thurne
#summary
A young drummer boy drowned in the frozen River Thurne in 1815 while visiting his secret girlfriend, and his ghost is now said to haunt the waterways.
#details
In early 1815, a drummer boy and the daughter of an influential local man maintained a secret relationship. He would skate across the frozen River Thurne to visit her at Hickling Broad, playing his drum as he went. One night in February, the ice cracked, and he fell into the freezing river and drowned. His girlfriend realized what had happened when his phantom approached her and vanished. According to local tradition, his ghost continues to skate and drum on the river, with some accounts suggesting he warns people when the ice is too thin.

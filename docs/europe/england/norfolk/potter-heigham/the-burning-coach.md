#title
The Burning Coach
#summary
Every year on May 31, a flaming coach resulting from a witch's love potion crashes off a bridge.
#details
A witch's love potion came at a severe price, and now on May 31st every year, a flaming coach still crashes off the bridge at Potter Heigham.

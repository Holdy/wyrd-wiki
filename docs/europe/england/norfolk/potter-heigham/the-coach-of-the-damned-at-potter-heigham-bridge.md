#title
The Coach of the Damned at Potter Heigham Bridge

#summary
A woman who used witchcraft to win a man's heart was dragged away by a skeletal figure into a flaming carriage that plunged off Potter Heigham bridge.

#details
Lady Carew used a love potion obtained from a witch to win Sir Godfrey Haslitt, agreeing to give the witch whatever she wanted in return. When the pair married at the altar, the witch—appearing as a skeletal figure—claimed her reward, dragging Lady Carew from the church and throwing her into a flaming carriage drawn by black horses. The coach raced away and plunged from Potter Heigham bridge into the water below. On the night of May 31, a flaming carriage pulled by diabolical horses is reportedly seen racing over the bridge before vanishing.

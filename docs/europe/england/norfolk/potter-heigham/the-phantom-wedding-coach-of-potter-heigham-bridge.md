#title
The Phantom Wedding Coach of Potter Heigham Bridge

#summary
A skeletal driver races a doomed coach across Potter Heigham bridge at midnight on May 31st, reenacting the abduction of a bride on her wedding night.

#details
Over 200 years ago, Lady Carew used a witch's potion to make Sir Godfrey Haslitt fall in love with her daughter, Lady Evelyn. After a lavish spring wedding in Norwich, a skeleton appeared at midnight on their wedding day, dragged Lady Evelyn away into the dark in a coach drawn by four black horses, and the coach burst into flames and crashed into the waters below as her mother watched. It is said that standing still and quiet on Potter Heigham bridge at midnight on May 31st allows witnesses to hear the terrifying clatter of the coach racing to its doom.

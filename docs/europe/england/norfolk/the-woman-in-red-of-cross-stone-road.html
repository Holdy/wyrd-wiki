The Woman in Red of Cross Stone Road

Summary

A phantom woman dressed in scarlet is said to appear and sit upon the post-medieval boundary stone at the road junction near Chedgrave. Locals and travelers traversing the rural lanes have passed down the legend of this spectral figure for generations.

Details

Along the narrow, winding country route leading from Chedgrave toward Hardley lies Cross Stone Road. At this specific road junction stands an old boundary stone or the remnants of a historic cross. According to local legend, on certain nights, the ghostly apparition of a woman in red can be seen sitting quietly upon the stone.

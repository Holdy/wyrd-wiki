#title
The White Figure of Cranworth Ash

#summary
A mysterious tall white figure was long reported to emerge from an old ash tree in Cranworth, walking along the road toward Letton Hall before vanishing at midnight.

#details
According to local tradition recorded in the late nineteenth century, a phantom white specter was said to haunt Cranworth. The ghostly figure would materialize from a long-vanished ash tree, pace up and down the road leading toward Letton Hall, and finally disappear into thin air exactly at midnight.

#location
The road leading from Cranworth toward Letton Hall in Norfolk.

#title
The Black Hound of Blickling Hall

#summary
Following his death, the corpse of an unpopular Blickling baronet was repeatedly rejected by the consecrated earth before transforming into a terrifying spectral black dog that plagued the estate. The beast was eventually subdued by a London wizard and imprisoned within the south-east turret of Blickling Hall.

#details
After Sir Henry Hobart died from wounds sustained in a 1698 duel, his burial at Blickling church was interrupted when the earth repeatedly cast out his corpse, forcing villagers to sink it into the estate lake with heavy stones. A few days later, a gamekeeper caught a monstrous eel from the lake and brought it home, where it suddenly transformed into a giant black dog possessing the dead man's eyes and unleashed a dark curse across the entire Blickling estate. A famous wizard summoned from London eventually tamed the beast with seven whistles and led it into the south-east turret of Blickling Hall, where it was sealed behind a bricked-up door—only for sightings of the phantom hound to resume after the room was reopened in the 1860s.

#location
Blickling Hall is a historic Jacobean stately home situated in the village of Blickling, just north of Aylsham in Norfolk, UK.

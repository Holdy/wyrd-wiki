    The Lady of Wellingham Hall

    Summary

    Wellingham Hall is haunted by the apparition of a former lady of the manor, whose spectral sightings near the old nursery are accompanied by unexplained cold spots and the distinct scent of lavender.

    Details

    For generations, inhabitants and staff of the historic Grade II listed Wellingham Hall have reported various supernatural occurrences within its older wings. Witnesses have frequently sighted the apparition of a woman in white—believed to be a former lady of the manor—particularly near the former nursery. These ghostly encounters are often preceded by inexplicable drops in temperature and an overwhelming, sudden fragrance of lavender, alongside disembodied voices and objects moving independently.

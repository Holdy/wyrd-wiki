    The Mist on the Coast Road

    Summary

    Unexplained misty apparitions have been witnessed materializing on the main road between Burnham Deepdale and Brancaster during otherwise clear nights. Travellers navigating this coastal stretch have reported unsettling encounters with drifting vaporous forms.

    Details

    Motorists and travellers journeying along the main road connecting Burnham Deepdale to Brancaster have reported ghostly encounters on perfectly clear nights. Without warning, unnatural patches of mist appear on the road, taking on indistinct, spectral shapes that startle those passing by before dissipating into the night air.

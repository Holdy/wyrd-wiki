#title
The Phantom Hound of Swanton Morley

#summary
In the winter of 1939, a young soldier cycling past All Saints Church in Swanton Morley was unexpectedly accompanied by a large, silent black dog along a country lane.

#details
During a dark night in late November 1939, a young territorial soldier was cycling back to his military unit after visiting his parents. Passing All Saints Church in Swanton Morley on his way towards Dereham, he became disoriented at a road fork and turned down a narrow green lane. When the moonlight broke through the clouds, he noticed a large black dog running right beside him. The hound's tongue was lolling out and its flanks heaved as it panted, yet it made absolute silence and emanated a surprising sense of companionship. As heavy clouds blocked the moonlight once more, the soldier emerged onto the tarmac road to Dereham, and his phantom companion had completely vanished.

#location
The encounter took place along a green lane branching off near All Saints Church in Swanton Morley on the route towards Dereham.

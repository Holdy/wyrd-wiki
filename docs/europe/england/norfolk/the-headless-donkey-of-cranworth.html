The Headless Donkey of Cranworth

Summary

A terrifying apparition of a towering, headless donkey and phantom coaches pulled by invisible horses haunt the rural parish of Cranworth.

Details

Late at night in the parish of Cranworth, a farmer's wife was startled by approaching footsteps in the dark and turned to find a monstrous donkey standing on its hind legs, prancing and towering above her without a head. Alongside this bizarre entity, locals have reported chilling encounters with phantom coaches driven by headless or invisible horses traversing local routes like Shipdam Road.

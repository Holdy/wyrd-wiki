The Phantoms of RAF West Raynham

Summary

The abandoned buildings of a World War II air station are plagued by multiple restless spirits, including a downed Polish pilot and a tragic mechanic. Their lingering presences continue to haunt the officer's mess, chapel, and armoury.

Details

RAF West Raynham, an active military air base during World War II, accumulated immense tragedy that seemingly left a ghostly imprint on its grounds. Among the most frequently reported apparitions is a Polish pilot who was shot down during the war, whose spirit is said to haunt the officer's mess and walk toward the kitchen. Other haunting manifestations include the apparition of a mechanic hanging from the rafters in the armoury, an angry high-ranking airman haunting the base chapel, poltergeist activity in the control tower, and eerie sounds of sobbing and screaming echoing from the Station Medical Centre.

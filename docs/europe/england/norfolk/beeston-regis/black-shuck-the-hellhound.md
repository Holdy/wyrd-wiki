#title
Black Shuck the Hellhound

#summary
A terrifying black hellhound with burning red eyes is said to have emerged from the marshes around Beeston Regis and Beeston Bump to terrorize locals and livestock.

#details
In 1901, author Arthur Conan Doyle heard the local legend of Black Shuck while staying at Cromer Hall on the Norfolk coast. The legend describes a black hellhound possessing burning red eyes that rose from the marshes near Beeston Regis and Beeston Bump, terrorising local people and livestock, which ultimately inspired the Hound of the Baskervilles.

#title
The Magical Fairy Cow of South Lopham
#summary
A magical cow reportedly appeared in South Lopham during times of drought to offer free milk, leaving a hoofprint in sandstone before vanishing when hardship ended.
#details
During periods of great hardship and drought, a magical fairy cow would appear in South Lopham to freely offer her milk to all who needed it. Once the drought ended and wellbeing was restored, the beast would stamp down on a slab of sandstone, burning the imprint of her hoof into the stone, and then vanish.

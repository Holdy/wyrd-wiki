The Phantom Dog of Church Lane

Summary

A witness walking along Church Lane in Hardley at dawn encountered a large, shaggy grey dog that vanished mysteriously into the barley fields.

Details

At around half past four on a midsummer morning, a person walking back from a friend's house near the church in Hardley encountered a large, charcoal-grey dog with matted, shaggy hair. The creature—roughly the size of an elongated Rottweiler—darted rapidly across Church Lane between tall grain fields and vanished into the barley without a trace. Though dismissed by acquaintances as a ghost story, the witness was profoundly shaken and noted that within a year of the encounter, their younger sister developed cancer.

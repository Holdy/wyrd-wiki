#title
The Poltergeist of Sandringham House
#summary
Sandringham House experiences disturbing paranormal activity including sudden cold blasts, flying objects, moving clocks, and phantom footsteps.
#details
Sandringham House has been the site of disturbing paranormal reports, including sudden blasts of cold air, clock hands moving freely, books flying off shelves, Christmas cards soaring across rooms, and blankets pulled off beds. Phantom footsteps, wheezing sounds, and lights operating by themselves are also frequently witnessed.

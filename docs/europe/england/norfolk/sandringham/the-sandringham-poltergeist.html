The Sandringham Poltergeist

Summary

The Queen's Christmas home experiences inexplicable paranormal activity, including books flying off shelves and blankets being pulled off beds.

Details

Books fly off shelves, clock hands suddenly turn, blankets are pulled off beds, and sudden blasts of cold air are felt at the Sandringham Estate.

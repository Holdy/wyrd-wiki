The Christmas Poltergeist of Sandringham House

Summary

A mischievous poltergeist at Sandringham House sends blasts of cold air, knocks items around, and switches lights on starting every Christmas Eve.

Details

Every Christmas Eve, poltergeist activity begins at Sandringham House and continues for several weeks. The mischievous spectre sends blasts of cold air through rooms, knocks books off shelves, pulls blankets off beds, sends Christmas cards flying, switches lights on and off, and can be heard wheezing and walking around the house.

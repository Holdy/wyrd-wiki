Poltergeist Disturbances at Sandringham House

Summary

Sandringham House experiences extensive poltergeist activity, including flying books, pulled blankets, and mysterious wheezing noises.

Details

Poltergeist activity is frequently reported at Sandringham House, featuring phenomena such as books flying off shelves, blankets being pulled, footsteps, cold blasts of air, lights turning on and off, and an unexplained wheezing noise.

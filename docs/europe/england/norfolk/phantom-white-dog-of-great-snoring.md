#title
Phantom White Dog of Great Snoring

#summary
A phantom white hound terrorised travellers and cyclists on a rural road south of Great Snoring just before the Second World War.

#details
In the late 1930s, a farmer driving along a rural road south of Great Snoring was shocked when his car drove straight through a spectral white dog. Terrified by the encounter, he stopped his vehicle and fled on foot. Numerous local cyclists reported similar chilling sightings of the phantom hound prowling the same stretch of road during the pre-war period.

#location
A rural road situated immediately south of the village of Great Snoring in Norfolk.

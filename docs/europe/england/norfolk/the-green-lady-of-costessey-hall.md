#title
The Green Lady of Costessey Hall

#summary
Legend holds that the ruins of Costessey Hall are haunted by a benevolent Green Lady who guarded the historic estate and its secret Catholic heritage. According to local tradition, her spirit was bound to the hall's Thornbury Tower, casting a curse on anyone who attempted to fully demolish it.

#details
Costessey Hall was a magnificent Gothic mansion built by the Jerningham family, who were staunch Catholics that concealed priests and maintained secret places of worship during periods of religious persecution. After being used for military training during the First World War, the hall fell into disrepair and was largely torn down, leaving behind ivy-clad ruins and towers. Local residents and former estate servants long recounted sightings of the Green Lady, a protective spirit whose presence was tied to the history of the house. When demolition contractors attempted to blast away Thornbury Tower in the 1950s, the structure stubbornly resisted destruction, eventually sinking into the ground before partially collapsing during a storm in the 1960s, fulfilling the dark local tradition that the spirit would haunt those who dismantled her domain.

#location
The remaining ivy-clad ruins of Costessey Hall and the former site of Thornbury Tower stand near the 18th fairway of Costessey Park Golf Course in Costessey, Norfolk.

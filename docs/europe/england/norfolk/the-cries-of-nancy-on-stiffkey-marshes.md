#title
The Cries of Nancy on Stiffkey Marshes

#summary
The ghost of a young girl named Nancy is said to roam the mud flats and scream on foggy nights after getting lost while cockling.

#details
A young girl named Nancy got lost while cockling as the tide started turning and thick fog descended on the marshes. Although villagers searched for her on their boats and heard her cries, Nancy could not be found, and her body was discovered the next day to be buried in a local churchyard. Since then, there have been many reports of her ghost roaming the mud flats as well as her screams being heard, particularly on a foggy night.

#title
The Farmer and the Hooded Spectre

#summary
A furious Norfolk farmer was continually terrorized by a hooded grey ghost that leapt from behind a glacial boulder to spook his horses at sunset. To lay the spirit to rest, he requested that the very boulder be placed atop his grave upon his death.

#details
In Beeston Regis, a farmer named James Reynolds frequently drove his horse and cart along a pathway near the ruins of Beeston Priory. At sunset, a hooded grey ghost would repeatedly leap out from behind one of two large glacial erratic boulders guarding the gateway to frighten the horses and attempt to grab their reins. Unfazed by the supernatural harassment, Reynolds vowed to stop the haunting. Upon his death in 1941, he arranged for the boulder to be hauled and placed directly over his final resting place at All Saints' Church, believing that burying the ghost's hiding place under stone would permanently trap and quiet the spirit.

#location
James Reynolds' grave is located in the churchyard of All Saints' Church in Beeston Regis, Norfolk.

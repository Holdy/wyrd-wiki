The Ghostly Whistler of Weybourne

Summary

The whistles of smuggler John Smythe can be heard during a full moon in Weybourne to alert his fellow tax evaders.

Details

The whistles of smuggler John Smythe can apparently be heard echoing when the moon is full, which are believed to serve as signals to attract the attention of his fellow tax evaders.

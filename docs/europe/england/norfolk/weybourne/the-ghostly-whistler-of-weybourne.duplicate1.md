#title
The Ghostly Whistler of Weybourne
#summary
A ghostly whistler roams Weybourne at full moon, playing tunes intended to attract smugglers.
#details
In Weybourne, at the full moon, a ghostly whistler is said to appear, playing tunes that were historically intended to attract other smugglers along the coast.

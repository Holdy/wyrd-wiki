Devil's Mere

Summary

Langmere on East Wretham Heath was long feared as the domain of the Devil, where driving livestock onto its temporary island brought certain misfortune.

Details

Langmere is a small fluctuating lake located on East Wretham Heath within the Breckland region of Norfolk. During periods of low water, a narrow isthmus forms, connecting the shore to a small island at the center of the mere. According to local tradition, any shepherd or cattleman who dared drive his animals onto this island would suffer severe misfortune and ill luck. The waters themselves were believed to belong entirely to the Devil, and local people strictly avoided fishing or hunting wildfowl there out of fear of supernatural retribution.

The Wandering Airman of Hethel

Summary

Following his death from combat wounds at a nearby medical facility, the spirit of a USAAF bomber crew member continually walks the nocturnal path back to his wartime airfield. Local residents report encountering his phantom figure roaming between the hospital grounds and the old base.

Details

During the Second World War, a young crew member serving aboard a USAAF Liberator heavy bomber stationed at Hethel airfield suffered severe, life-threatening wounds during a combat mission. Evacuated from the base, he was transferred for emergency medical care to the military hospital at Morley. Despite medical intervention, the airman succumbed to his injuries and passed away. In the years following the war, local folklore and resident accounts maintain that his restless spirit never truly left his post; at night, the phantom airman is said to walk the lonely rural route between the hospital site and his former station at Hethel.

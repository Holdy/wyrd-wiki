The White Hound of Great Snoring

Summary

In the years preceding the Second World War, a ghostly white dog was frequently seen darting across the road connecting Great Snoring and Little Snoring, culminating in an encounter where an unsuspecting motorist drove straight through its spectral form.

Details

During the late 1930s leading up to the outbreak of World War II, locals and travelers frequently reported encounters with a mysterious white dog along the roadway linking the Norfolk villages of Great and Little Snoring. The phantom hound would suddenly appear, racing across the path of moving vehicles. On one particularly astonishing occasion, a motorist driving along the route drove their car directly through the ghostly animal, passing through its incorporeal body entirely unharmed.

#title
The Treasure of the Lily Pond Well

#summary
A hidden treasure chest was recovered from an old well near Hoe Hall, and its iron handle was subsequently attached to the village church door.

#details
In a field south of Hoe Hall, close to a square pond known as the Lily Pond, an ancient well once stood. According to local tradition, a heavy treasure chest was discovered and hauled up from the depths of this well. While the contents of the chest vanished into history, local legend holds that its iron handle was salvaged and fitted onto the door of the parish church as a lasting reminder of the discovery.

#location
The Lily Pond and well site are located in a field south of Hoe Hall in the parish of Hoe, Norfolk (approximately at grid reference TF999162).

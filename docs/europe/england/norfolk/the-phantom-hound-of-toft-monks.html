    The Phantom Hound of Toft Monks

    Summary

    While walking through the rural lanes of Toft Monks, a traveller is warned by a local woodman about the presence of Black Shuck, a spectral demon dog as large as a calf. The phantom hound is said to roam the lonely paths and marshes of the Norfolk countryside without making a sound.

    Details

    During his travels across East Anglia, author William A. Dutt recorded an encounter in the parish of Toft Monks where he met a local woodman who pointed out the haunting grounds of Old Shuck. The creature, a terrifying demonic hound described as being as big as a fair-sized calf, was said to prowl the lonely tracks and field footpaths of the area. Unlike living animals, this spectral beast moves entirely silently across the landscape, adding to the dread of anyone unfortunate enough to cross its path.

Barton's Lady of the Lake

Summary

The ghost of a young woman accidentally killed by her father's crossbow appears floating on Barton Broad every August 4th.

Details

During the Crusades, a knight returned from the Holy Land and failed to recognize his grown daughter, eventually falling in love with her. When she refused his hand and ran to her noble lover, the enraged knight fired a crossbow arrow intended for the lover, but it struck and killed his daughter instead. Her spirit, known as Lady Edythe, is said to appear floating on the lake every year on August 4th as a blessing to anyone who sees her face.

    The Legend of Thieves' Pit

    Summary

    Three hollows beside Peddar's Way include a depression named after a historic burglary at Illington Hall where thieves left their horses with reversed shoes. This clever deception was intended to confuse anyone tracking their escape route across the heath.

    Details

    According to local tradition, three conspicuous hollows or pits lay beside Peddar's Way on Roudham Heath, not far from the hamlet of Illington. One of these depressions became known as Thieves' Pit. The name originated from a daring burglary at Illington Hall, during which the thieves attempted to evade capture by reversing the shoes on their horses' hooves. By altering the footwear of their mounts, any hoofprints left behind would falsely indicate the horses were traveling in the opposite direction when they gathered at this spot.

The Guardian Hound of Bawburgh

Summary

While cycling home late at night, a young man encountered a colossal spectral hound sitting by a village signpost before an unlit vehicle seemingly crashed into it and vanished. The supernatural encounter was later believed by the witness to have miraculously saved his life.

Details

Late one moonlit night, Mr. E. Ramsey of Bawburgh was cycling home from a darts match in Norwich. As he approached his home village, he spotted the largest hound he had ever seen sitting by the village signpost, its eyes shining like glowing coals. Though nervous, he cycled past the motionless beast, but soon heard heavy paws beating the road behind him. The giant dog sprinted past him so closely that he could smell its rank odor, before abruptly stopping and standing aggressively in the middle of the road near a spinney. Terrified, Ramsey dismounted and sought cover behind his bicycle. At that exact moment, an unlit vehicle roared out of the spinney, careered erratically, and appeared to smash straight into the hound, sending Ramsey tumbling into the hedge. When he picked himself up, the dog was still standing unharmed before turning and vanishing completely into thin air. Ramsey later associated the appearance of the ghostly hound—which he believed saved him from being struck by the runaway vehicle—with the tragic death of his wife two years later.

    The Treasure-Seeker of Creake Abbey

    Summary

    Following the devastating plague that wiped out the monastic community at Creake Abbey in the early 16th century, treasure-seekers and necromancers attempted to invoke spirits among the ruins to unearth hidden wealth.

    Details

    Creake Abbey, founded in the 13th century as an Augustinian priory, suffered a catastrophic blow in 1506 when a severe outbreak of sweating sickness killed all the resident monks, leaving only the dying abbot. In the decades that followed, local rumors spread that vast monastic treasures had been buried and left behind within the grounds. In 1528, a renegade monk named William Stapleton traveled to the abbey alongside two necromantic assistants. Using forbidden rituals and invoking spirits among the stone foundations, they attempted to force the supernatural entities to reveal the location of the hidden hoard, though their magical endeavors ultimately yielded nothing.

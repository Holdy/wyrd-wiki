Murder in the Well

Summary

Mary Bone's Well, situated between Coxford and Broomsthorpe, is said to be named after a local woman who was drowned in its waters by a priest from the neighboring priory.

Details

Marked on Ordnance Survey maps near the remains of the Augustinian St. Mary's Priory, Mary Bone's Well carries a grim local legend. According to tradition, the ancient well derives its eerie name from a woman named Mary Bone, who met a violent end when she was drowned in the well's depths by a corrupt priest from the nearby priory.

#title
The Child in White

#summary
A man in a Norfolk home encountered a mysterious child in white in the early hours of the morning.

#details
In the early hours of the morning, Marisa's husband saw what he believed was their three-year-old son, describing him as a child in white. He said the boy's name and reached out to touch him, but felt nothing; when he turned on the lights there was still nothing, and their actual son was found sound asleep in his bed wearing a blue shirt.

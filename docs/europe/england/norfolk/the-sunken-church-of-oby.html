    The Sunken Church of Oby

    Summary

    The medieval parish church in the tiny Norfolk hamlet of Oby mysteriously vanished after allegedly sinking into the ground. Although historical records once placed the church by the roadside, local folklore maintains that the entire building sank beneath the earth.

    Details

    In the Norfolk Broads parish of Ashby with Oby, local tradition long held that the ancient parish church had completely vanished because it sank into the ground. While an 1829 history of Norfolk noted that the church stood beside the road leading from Thurne to Yarmouth, by the time folklorist W.B. Gerish investigated the area prior to the First World War, locals recounted that the building had disappeared entirely into the earth.

#title
Joseph Bexfield's Fatal Marsh Light

#summary
A man named Joseph Bexfield drowned in a marsh after being led astray by a mysterious Jack o'Lantern.

#details
In 1809, a man named Joseph Bexfield drowned in Thurlton Marsh after being led astray by a Jack o'Lantern—a strange ethereal light that appears in the marsh after dark. His ghost is said to still appear each August, trying to find his way home.

#title
The Drowned Wherryman and the Marsh Lights
#summary
After ignoring warnings about malicious marsh lights, a wherryman drowned in 1809 and was subsequently seen as a shadowy figure wandering the misty marsh.
#details
In 1809, a 38-year-old wherryman named Joseph Bexfield tied up his boat at Thurlton Staithe and went drinking at the White Horse Inn. When preparing to return home across the marsh at night, his friends warned him against the dangerous Lantern Men, but he scoffed and headed out anyway. His body eventually washed up between Reedham and Breydon. Afterward, locals reported seeing a shadowy figure wandering the marsh on misty nights before disappearing into the river.

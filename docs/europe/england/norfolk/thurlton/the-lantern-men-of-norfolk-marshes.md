#title
The Lantern Men of Norfolk Marshes
#summary
Glowing ghost lights known as Lantern Men hover over Norfolk marshlands, drawing whistling victims to a watery death in reed beds and mud.
#details
The "death fires" that hover above marshland and fens are feared evil spirits said to try to draw victims to their deaths in reed beds and mud. Particularly drawn to people who whistle, the only way to escape them is to lie face-down with your mouth in the mud. They have been witnessed across various sites including Thurlton, Bawburgh, Alder Carr, Wicken Fen, Irstead, Horning, Gimingham, and Southrepps.

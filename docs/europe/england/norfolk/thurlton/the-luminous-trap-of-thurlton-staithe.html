The Luminous Trap of Thurlton Staithe

Summary

Ignoring the warnings of fellow sailors, a man named Joseph Bexfield ventured into the misty marshes after seeing strange lights and was never seen alive again.

Details

In August 1809, while drinking at The White Horse Inn by Thurlton Staithe, a sailor named Joseph Bexfield remembered a parcel he had left on a wherry. Despite warnings from his companions, he stepped out into the dark marshes amidst swirling mists and strange lights. Bexfield was lured to a watery grave by the eerie phenomenon known as Lantern Man, and his ghost is occasionally reported wandering the area today.

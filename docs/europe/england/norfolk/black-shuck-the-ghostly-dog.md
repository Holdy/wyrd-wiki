#title
Black Shuck the Ghostly Dog

#summary
Black Shuck is a legendary ghostly black hound said to roam the countryside and coastline of East Anglia.

#details
Black Shuck is an infamous ghostly black dog that roams the coastline and countryside of East Anglia. Numerous accounts of the beast exist over the years, including an encounter by 20th-century author Christopher Marlowe, who rented a cottage off the A149 to track the creature down and was chased through the night by an indefinable animal with a great black body and ferocious eyes. The legend also famously inspired Arthur Conan Doyle's visit to Cromer Hall prior to writing The Hound of the Baskervilles.

The Phantom Spectres of Cromer Pier

Summary

Actors at the Pavilion Theatre on Cromer Pier have reported strange spectres and a man in a tall black hat appearing on stage with them.

Details

The Pavilion Theatre on Cromer Pier is said to be haunted, with actors reporting strange spectres appearing on stage next to them during performances, including a man wearing a tall black hat.

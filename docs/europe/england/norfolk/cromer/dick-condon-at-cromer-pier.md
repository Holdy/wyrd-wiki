#title
Dick Condon at Cromer Pier
#summary
Performers at Cromer Pier's Pavillion Theatre have claimed to see the stage ghost of Irish impresario Dick Condon.
#details
The theater at the end of Cromer Pier, known as The Pavillion Theatre, is said to be haunted by Irish impresario Dick Condon, whose spirit performers have claimed to witness on stage during live shows.

#title
The Phantom Child of Cromer Church

#summary
A man in 1889 reported seeing a child-like figure in white rise from the ground at Cromer Church, revealing a bloody gash across its throat before vanishing with a sigh.

#details
In a tale recorded in 1889 by author Walter Rye regarding the days when Cromer Church was in ruins and its churchyard was common land, a man experienced a terrifying encounter. A child-like figure dressed in white rose from the ground within arm's length of him. Horrifyingly, a gash appeared across its throat, and blood poured over the white dress before the apparition vanished with a lingering sigh.

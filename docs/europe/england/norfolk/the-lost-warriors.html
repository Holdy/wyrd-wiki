The Lost Warriors

Summary

Ancient warriors haunt the Hockwold Fens, their loud battle cries echoing across the fenland where Roman-era hoards were later buried and discovered.

Details

Long before archaeological excavations uncovered ancient Roman pewter hoards in the East Anglian Fens, the fenlands around Hockwold were reputed to be haunted by the spirits of ancient warriors. The phantom sounds of fierce battle cries and fighting can be heard ringing loud across the watery landscape, believed by locals to be the lingering echoes of ancient British tribesmen clashing with Roman invaders.

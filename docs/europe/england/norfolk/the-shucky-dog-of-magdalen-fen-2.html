The Shucky Dog of Magdalen Fen

Summary

Two villagers checking on an isolated fen resident during a harsh winter discovered him dead of sheer terror after finding solitary paw prints leading right up to his cottage door.

Details

During a severe winter around 1865, two men walked out to the remote cottage of Abe Mindham in Magdalen Fen to check on his welfare. Upon arriving in the heavy snow, they noticed a single set of dog paw prints leading directly to his front door, with no prints leading back away from it. Entering the dwelling, they found Mindham's body lying on the floor, cold and rigid, with his eyes frozen in an expression of absolute horror. Drawing upon local folklore of the Shucky Dog—a massive black hound said to gallop across the fen fields on moonless nights dragging a broken chain—they concluded that Mindham had opened his door to the spectral beast and died instantly of fright.

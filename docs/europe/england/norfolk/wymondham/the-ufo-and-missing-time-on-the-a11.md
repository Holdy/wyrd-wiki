#title
The UFO and Missing Time on the A11

#summary
A driver on the A11 spotted a silent, spherical UFO that changed color and corresponded with hours of missing time.

#details
While driving on the A11 near Wymondham, Sam saw a shining object in the night sky that initially looked like aeroplane headlights, but grew to the size of an orange, turned red, and hovered still with no sound. The unidentified object disappeared near Hethersett, and Sam subsequently realized that the journey from Brighton had taken five hours instead of the usual three.

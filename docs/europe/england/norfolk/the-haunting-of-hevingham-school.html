    The Haunting of Hevingham School

    Summary

    An enigmatic Edwardian photograph of a young girl at a Norfolk school appears to animate, plunging a newcomer into terrifying poltergeist activity and haunting dreams. The spectral phenomenon unearths tragic echoes of wartime trauma and dark village secrets.

    Details

    Judith relocates to a remote rural community and becomes embroiled in a chilling haunting originating from Hevingham School. Pupils had long claimed that an old photograph depicting a girl in Edwardian clothing seemed to move and was responsible for disruptive poltergeist phenomena. As Judith investigates, the eerie image begins invading her dreams, intertwining with dark secrets, wartime tragedy, and past abuse, leading to escalating supernatural terror.

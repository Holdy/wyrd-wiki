The Luminous Barn Owl of West Bilney Hall

Summary

In 1897, a gamekeeper working late at night near West Bilney Hall encountered a mysterious glowing blue light that flew close to his face. After tracking and shooting at the light on a subsequent night, he discovered it was a barn owl emitting an unnatural luminescence.

Details

In 1897, a gamekeeper was out late at night at West Bilney Hall, near King's Lynn, stopping up fox-earths. During his work, he was startled when a very bright blue light flew close to his face, appearing several times throughout the night. Intrigued and unsettled, he returned on subsequent nights armed with a gun to uncover the source of the illumination. Eventually, he spotted the light skimming low over the ground, waited until it came within a few yards, and fired his weapon into the darkness. Upon inspecting the spot, he was astonished to find a dead barn owl, realizing that the strange, glowing blue light had been emanating directly from the bird itself.

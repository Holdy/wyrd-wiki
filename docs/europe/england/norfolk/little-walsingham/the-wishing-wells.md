#title
The Wishing Wells
#summary
Wells in Little Walsingham that once cured headaches later began granting wishes, provided the wishers never told anyone.
#details
The water at the wells in Little Walsingham was once renowned for curing headaches. In later lore, the wells shifted to granting wishes, under the strict condition that the wisher never revealed their wish to anyone.

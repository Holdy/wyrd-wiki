#title
The Phantom Hound of Cooke's Lane

#summary
A late-night cyclist returning home along the road between Thurton and Bergh Apton was terrified when a demonic black hound suddenly leaped from a roadside pond and vanished directly into a solid wall.

#details
Late at night, a cyclist riding along Cooke's Lane—the road connecting Thurton and Bergh Apton—was returning home from a darts match when he was suddenly confronted by a massive black dog. Emerging from the pond opposite Washingford House, the beast rushed toward him with snarling teeth and a shaggy coat, level with his bicycle's crossbar. Expecting to be knocked down and bitten, the terrified cyclist watched in disbelief as the creature vanished straight through a stone wall on the right-hand side of the road, leaving him shaken and pedalling frantically home.

#location

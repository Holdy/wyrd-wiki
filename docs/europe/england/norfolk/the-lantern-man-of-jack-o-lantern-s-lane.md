#title
The Lantern Man of Jack o' Lantern's Lane

#summary
A malevolent ghostly light known as the Lantern Man haunts the remote lane connecting Gimingham and Southrepps, terrorising unwary travellers. Anyone who crosses his path risks having their lights smashed or being relentlessly pursued to their homes.

#details
For generations, locals have avoided Jack o' Lantern's Lane—the path connecting Gimingham and Southrepps—after dark due to the presence of the Lantern Man. Appearing as a glowing, dancing blue-white flame that hovers above the ground like a candle in a lantern, this restless spirit is notoriously aggressive. Tradition warns that the entity will actively attack travellers, smashing lanterns to pieces, knocking horsemen from their mounts, and even following mockers home to peer menacingly through their windows.

#location
Jack o' Lantern's Lane is the path connecting the villages of Gimingham and Southrepps in Norfolk, UK.

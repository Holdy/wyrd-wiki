The Mistletoe Bride

Summary

During a Christmas wedding celebration at Brockdish Hall, a young bride playfully hides in an old oak chest during a game of hide-and-seek, accidentally trapping herself inside. Years later, her skeletal remains are discovered in her wedding dress, and the tragic tale became a famous English ghost legend.

Details

The legend tells of a festive winter wedding at Brockdish Hall where the young bride challenged her new husband and guests to a game of hide-and-seek. Looking for a clever and secluded hiding spot, she slipped away to a dusty attic and climbed inside an ancient oak chest. Tragically, the heavy lid closed and locked shut with a spring mechanism, sealing her fate in a living tomb. Despite extensive searches by her distraught groom and family over the following days, weeks, and years, she could not be found. Long after grief had aged the household, the old chest was finally opened, revealing her skeleton still wearing her bridal wreath and gown.

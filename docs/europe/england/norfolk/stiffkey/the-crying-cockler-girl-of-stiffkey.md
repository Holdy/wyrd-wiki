#title
The Crying Cockler Girl of Stiffkey
#summary
The ghost of a young girl named Nancy can be heard screaming for help on the mud flats of Stiffkey after she was trapped by the tide.
#details
A poor young girl cockler named Nancy was working at the water's edge in Stiffkey when the tide turned and a thick fog rolled in. Despite villagers hearing her cries and searching in vain, her body was found the next day and she was buried in the village churchyard. Ever since, particularly on foggy nights, her shadowy figure and desperate screams for help can be heard on the mud flats.

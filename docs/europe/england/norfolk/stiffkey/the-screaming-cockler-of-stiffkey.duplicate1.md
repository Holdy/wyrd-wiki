#title
The Screaming Cockler of Stiffkey

#summary
A cockler named Nancy drowned in the mudflats after lingering too long in a thick fog, and her terrified screams now warn others away.

#details
In the 18th century, a woman named Nancy stayed behind on the salt flats near Stiffkey to harvest cockles despite warnings about an incoming tide and thick fog. She became trapped and drowned in the rising waters. Since her death, people walking near the salt flats on cold, foggy mornings report hearing her terrified screams warning them of danger.

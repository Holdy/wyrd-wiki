#title
The Screaming Cockler of Stiffkey Salt Marshes
#summary
A young girl who got lost in a thick fog and tide while cockling still roams the salt marshes screaming for rescue.
#details
A young girl named Nancy got lost while cockling when the tide came in and thick fog closed around her; despite her cries, she could not be rescued and her body was found the next day. Her ghost and screams can still be heard as she roams the marshes for eternity, especially on foggy nights.
#location
Stiffkey Salt Marshes, Blakeney National Nature Reserve, Norfolk, England

Nancy the Screaming Cockle Picker of Stiffkey

Summary

Caught by a rising tide and sea fret in the 19th century, a cockle picker named Nancy perished in the mud, and her screams and ghostly form still haunt the saltmarshes.

Details

In the early 19th century, a cockle picker named Nancy worked late on the Blacknock mudflat to fill her bag, failing to notice the rising tide, creeping night, and rolling sea fret. Disorientated by the thick mist, she became trapped in the cloying mud and drowned despite her desperate screams. When frets roll in off the North Sea, Nancy's screams can still be heard, and a ghostly apparition with seaweed in her hair and water pouring from her mouth has been reported on the marshes.

The Screaming Cockler of Stiffkey

Summary

A young girl named Nancy, who drowned while cockling in thick fog, is seen and heard screaming while roaming the mud flats of Stiffkey.

Details

A young girl named Nancy was lost while cockling after the tide turned and thick fog closed in. Although villagers searched the water by boat and heard her cries, she could not be found, and her body was recovered the next day. Her ghost has since been frequently reported, with her screams heard while she roams the mud flats on foggy nights.

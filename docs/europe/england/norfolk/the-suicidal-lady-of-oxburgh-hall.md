#title
The Suicidal Lady of Oxburgh Hall
#summary
A 16th-century lady who threw herself from a bedroom window and drowned in the moat is said to haunt the north bedroom and staircase.
#details
A 16th-century woman haunts the north bedroom and staircase of Oxburgh Hall. According to the legend, she threw herself from the bedroom window and drowned in the moat below.

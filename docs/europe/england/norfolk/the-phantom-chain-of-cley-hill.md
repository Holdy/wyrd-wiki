#title
The Phantom Chain of Cley Hill

#summary
A woman walking home along the A149 road in Cley next the Sea late at night was stalked by the invisible, chain-rattling spectral hellhound known as Black Shuck.

#details
In the summer of around 1968, a woman walking home from Cley to Blakeney along the A149 road reached the bottom of Cley Hill around midnight. In the stillness of the dark, she began to hear the chilling, light dragging and tinkling sound of a chain. Although she could see nothing, the unseen presence closely followed her step-for-step as she climbed the hill. Arriving at the street-lit crossroads at the top near Back Lane, she stopped and waited for it to pass, but saw only empty space before hearing the rattling chain turn down Back Lane toward the marsh banks. Realising she had encountered Black Shuck—the demonic black dog of East Anglian folklore—she fled in terror down the High Street.

#location
The encounter took place along the A149 road at the foot of Cley Hill and the crossroads leading to Back Lane in Cley next the Sea, Norfolk.

    The Phantom Hound of Foulden Lane

    Summary

    A young boy cycling down a village lane in Foulden was pursued by a terrifying large black dog that abruptly vanished upon reaching a closed gate.

    Details

    In 1997 or 1998, a six- or seven-year-old boy was staying at his grandparents' house in the Norfolk village of Foulden. While cycling up and down a lane running behind the property—which bordered an open field on the opposite side—he turned back toward the house and found himself pursued by a massive black dog. Experiencing pure terror, he pedaled hard toward the property, only to discover that the gate, which had previously been open, was now shut. He crashed directly into the gate, at which moment the phantom hound vanished entirely right before his eyes, leaving an impression that would haunt him for the rest of his life.

#title
The Headless Carriage of Queen Anne Boleyn

#summary
On the anniversary of her execution, a headless carriage carrying the ghost of Queen Anne Boleyn travels through Norfolk to Blickling Hall.

#details
Every year on May 19th, the anniversary of her execution, a carriage driven by a headless man with a flaming mouth crosses twelve local bridges and stops at the drive of Blickling Hall. From the carriage steps the ghost of Queen Anne Boleyn, carrying her own dripping head under her arm to look sadly upon her former home before fading into the night.

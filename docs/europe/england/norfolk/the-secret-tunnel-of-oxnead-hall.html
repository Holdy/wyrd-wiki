    The Secret Tunnel of Oxnead Hall

    Summary

    Local folklore maintains that a subterranean passageway links the historic Oxnead Hall to the village parish church.

    Details

    According to local tradition and accounts gathered from residents, a secret underground tunnel connects the historic site of Oxnead Hall to the nearby parish church. Such subterranean passages are a common feature in local legends across Norfolk estates, linked to the era when the grand mansion was inhabited by the wealthy Paston family before falling into ruin in the eighteenth century.

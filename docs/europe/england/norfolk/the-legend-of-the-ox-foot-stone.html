The Legend of the Ox-Foot Stone

Summary

A weathered sandstone slab in South Lopham bears a mysterious indentation said to be the hoofprint left by a magical fairy cow that vanished after a period of village hardship. In an alternative local variant, an ordinary cow kicked the rock in agony after being milked with a sieve by a drunken villager.

Details

The Ox-Foot Stone is an oblong slab of weathered sandstone situated in South Lopham. According to traditional folklore, during a severe period of dearth, a magical fairy cow would regularly appear in the village to provide free milk to the inhabitants. Once the drought and hardship ended, the creature stamped its hoof onto the stone and vanished. Another version of the legend claims the stone was kicked by a village cow when a drunken local attempted to milk her using a sieve, causing the distressed animal to lash out and leave a permanent hoofprint on the rock.

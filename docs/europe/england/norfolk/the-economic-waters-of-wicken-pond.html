    The Economic Waters of Wicken Pond

    Summary

    In the Norfolk parish of Tattersett, the water level of Wicken Pond is traditionally believed to rise and fall in mysterious synchronization with the market price of corn. This curious folk belief intertwines local agrarian economics with the natural world.

    Details

    According to local folklore associated with Wicken Pond in Tattersett, the water body possesses a strange and supernatural connection to the agricultural economy. Tradition holds that whenever the market price of corn fluctuates, the water levels within the pond rise and fall in exact tandem with those economic changes, creating a mystical bond between the wealth of local farmers and the depth of the pond.

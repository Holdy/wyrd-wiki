#title
Black Shuck the Phantom Dog
#summary
A colossal black hound with a single glowing red eye roams the stormy coastline, portending death for anyone who spots him.
#details
Black Shuck is a massive dog as black as night with one great big red eye who roams the coast on stormy nights, howling with the wind. Anyone who catches sight of him is doomed to die within the year, a legend allegedly used to frighten the militia away from smugglers.

The Phantom of the Rampant Horse

Summary

Passersby and residents have reported seeing a mysterious dark figure peering from the upper windows of the former Rampant Horse public house in Gayton. Occupants have also experienced unexplained cold spots and missing items within the historic building.

Details

The former Rampant Horse public house in Gayton, which has been converted into private residential units, is reputed to be haunted by a shadowy apparition. People passing by on the road have frequently spotted a dark figure watching from the upstairs windows, even during periods when the building was empty. Inside, residents note that the bedroom situated above the old kitchen is noticeably colder than the rest of the dwelling and difficult to heat, while personal belongings occasionally vanish without explanation.

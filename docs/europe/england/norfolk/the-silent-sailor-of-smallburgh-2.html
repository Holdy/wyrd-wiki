The Silent Sailor of Smallburgh

Summary

Passers-by near Smallburgh have reported seeing a spectral sailor standing motionless and gazing out at the canal. He is believed to be a mariner who met his tragic fate in the canal waters and now eternally watches over them.

Details

Along the North Walsham & Dilham Canal near Smallburgh, witnesses have encountered the phantom figure of a sailor. The silent specter stands perfectly still by the water's edge, staring across the canal. According to local lore, he is the spirit of a seafarer who died in the canal waters and remains bound to the location, forever watching over the waterway.

The Ghost of Queen Isabella at Castle Rising Castle

Summary

The upper rooms of the medieval Castle Rising Castle are said to be haunted by Queen Isabella, whose final years were marked by violent mental decline. Visitors and local villagers have reported hearing her hysterical laughter and chilling shrieks echoing from the empty top floors during the early morning hours.

Details

According to local legend, Queen Isabella—infamously known as the "She-Wolf of France" for her role in the murder of her husband, Edward II—spent her later years residing at Castle Rising. Although historical records indicate she lived in regal comfort, popular tradition maintains that her mind deteriorated into violent dementia towards the end of her life. To this day, visitors touring the castle have been startled by the sound of hysterical cackling emanating from the top floor. Furthermore, residents in the surrounding village are occasionally disturbed in the dead of night by ghostly shrieks and maniacal laughter echoing across the countryside from the ancient keep.

The Phantom of Shrieking Woman Grove

Summary

The ghostly apparition of a wailing woman haunts a wooded footpath in Brooke, where her piercing cries echo through the trees. Local folklore and historical heritage records link the haunting to a violent crime committed at the site centuries ago.

Details

Within the Norfolk village of Brooke, remnants of an ancient forest stretch toward Bergh Apton along a shaded track and wooded area known as Shrieking Woman Grove. Along this footpath and between the trees, witnesses have reported seeing a ghostly female figure. Her appearance is invariably heralded or accompanied by terrifying, heartbreaking shrieks that pierce the woodland silence. Although the specifics of her mortal life have been lost to time, regional heritage archives note that a brutal and fearful crime once took place in this exact area, binding the restless spirit to the grove.

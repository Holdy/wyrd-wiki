The Phantoms of The George Hotel

Summary

Ghost hunters investigating a historic Norfolk coaching inn discover multiple active spirits, including the infamous Green Lady and an unexpected mischievous presence in the dry store room. Guests and staff report chilling encounters ranging from unnerving room disturbances to physical contact.

Details

During an overnight paranormal investigation at The George Hotel in Swaffham, investigators explored the 16th-century coaching inn using scientific equipment and spiritual mediums. While investigating the hotel's dry store room—formerly linked to an old bakery—mediums encountered an assertive energy that interacted with their hair and clothing. Later, staff confirmed that a young waiter had experienced an unseen force attempting to undo his trousers in the exact same room. When shown an old sepia photograph of former bakery staff, the mediums identified the spirit. Elsewhere in the hotel, the elusive Green Lady manifested to a guest in Room 204, another guest was pinned to their bed in Room 206, and spirits behind the bar frequently sent glasses flying off the shelves.

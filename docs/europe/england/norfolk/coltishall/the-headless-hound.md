#title
The Headless Hound
#summary
While many Norfolk lanes are haunted by black dogs, a specific bridge is haunted by one entirely missing its head.
#details
While plenty of Norfolk lanes are known for sightings of a black dog, this particular bridge is uniquely haunted by a hound with no head.

#title
The Sinister Church Door of Burgh St Peter
#summary
Visitors to St Mary's Church at Burgh St Peter have experienced a powerful sense of evil near the porch, where a ghost is said to linger.
#details
When visiting the church locally known as the "wedding cake" church, a visitor felt an overwhelming sense of evil and dread approaching the door and could go no further, while a ghost is reportedly associated with the church door.

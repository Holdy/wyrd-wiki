#title
The Phantom of the Lily Pit

#summary
A deep pond along the Gorleston to Beccles road is haunted by a midnight phantom pony and trap, tied to local legends of tragic drownings and coaching accidents.

#details
Close to the side of the main Gorleston to Beccles road (now the A143) lies a deep pond known as the Lily Pit. At midnight, a phantom pony and trap are said to thunder along the road and plunge directly into the water. Three distinct legends attempt to explain the haunting: that a mail-coach missed the road and vanished into the pit forever; that a farm-hand's beloved drowned in the pool after an elopement, leading the young man to hang himself from a nearby tree; or that the pit was the ancient well of a chieftain named 'Old Canoba'. These dark tales are anchored by grim real-world events, such as an 1888 incident where a local man named James Keable lost his way in the fog and drowned in the pit, and the 1892 discovery of an ancient British burial skeleton resting on paved flints at the site.

#location
The Lily Pit pond, situated just west of the former Otter's Farm alongside the Gorleston to Beccles road (A143) at grid reference TG518049 in Gorleston-on-Sea.

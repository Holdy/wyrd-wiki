The Hethel Thorn

Summary

England's oldest hawthorn tree, which may have sheltered rebels, is alternatively said to have grown from a holy man's staff.

Details

Hethel Thorn is recognized as England's oldest hawthorn. Legend suggests it may have sheltered rebels in the past, while other accounts claim the ancient tree grew directly from a holy man's staff.

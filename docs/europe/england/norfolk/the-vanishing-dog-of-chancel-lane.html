    The Vanishing Dog of Chancel Lane

    Summary

    A witness walking past Garveston church in broad daylight watched a dark, shaggy dog trot across an open field before it abruptly vanished into thin air.

    Details

    While walking up Chancel Lane, a narrow footpath passing Garveston church during autumn, a man spotted a large, dark dog about the size of a Labrador with a round face trotting through an open field to his left. As he briefly glanced away at another pedestrian approaching in the lane, he turned back to look at the dog only to find it had completely disappeared from the open field, which offered no cover or hiding place whatsoever.

#title
The Madness of the She-Wolf

#summary
The upper rooms of Castle Rising are haunted by the crazed spirit of Queen Isabella, who is said to pace the corridors and fill the night air with manic laughter and shrieks. Visitors and locals have reported hearing her terrifying outbursts and the rustle of her dress in the historic keep where she spent her final years.

#details
Following the execution of her lover Roger Mortimer and the assumption of power by her son Edward III, Queen Isabella lived at Castle Rising for decades until her death in 1358. Legend holds that her final years were plagued by violent dementia and mental instability. To this day, ghostly phenomena plague the 12th-century keep, particularly the upper floors and the White Room. Locals and visitors have reported hearing bloodcurdling shrieks, hysterical cackling echoing across the surrounding countryside in the early morning hours, and the phantom sound of a dress brushing against the floors.

#location
Castle Rising Castle is located off Lynn Road (PE31 6AH) in the village of Castle Rising, just northeast of King's Lynn in Norfolk.

#title
The Devil's Nightcap of Croxton

#summary
On damp autumn evenings, a mysterious circlet of mist known as the Devil's Nightcap forms over the Devil's Punch Bowl in Croxton. Local folklore attributes this haunting atmospheric phenomenon to the devil's presence in the landscape.

#details
In south-west Norfolk, traditional folklore holds that the devil was once very busy shaping and roaming the countryside. Near the village of Croxton lies the Devil's Punch Bowl, a striking circular depression in the Breckland terrain. On damp autumn evenings, a distinct circlet of mist frequently appears hovering above this hollow, earning the local moniker the Devil's Nightcap as a testament to the area's enduring devil legends.

#location
The Devil's Punch Bowl is a circular depression located just north of the village of Croxton in Norfolk.

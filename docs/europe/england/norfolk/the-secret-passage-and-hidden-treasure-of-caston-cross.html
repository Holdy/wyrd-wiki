    The Secret Passage and Hidden Treasure of Caston Cross

    Summary

    According to local legend, a secret underground passage connects Caston's Holy Cross Church to the village green cross, beneath which a cache of Catholic treasure remains hidden.

    Details

    Local folklore in the Norfolk village of Caston holds that a subterranean passage runs from the Holy Cross Church to either the 15th-century cross-base on the village green or to Church Farm, which once served as a monastic refectory for pilgrims traveling the route to Walsingham. While no physical traces of the tunnel have ever been discovered, an enduring local tale insists that a secret chamber beneath the village cross conceals a stash of "Catholic treasure" left behind from turbulent historical times.

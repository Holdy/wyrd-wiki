The Pig-Faced Monk of West Dereham

Summary

A secret underground tunnel was supposedly built between St. Andrew's church and the abbey in West Dereham to hide a monk with a pig-like face from public view, but his spiteful ghost still haunts the area today.

Details

Local folklore in West Dereham tells of a subterranean passage constructed between St. Andrew's church and the nearby 12th-century abbey. The tunnel was allegedly built specifically for a monk whose head and face so closely resembled a pig that he was deemed too hideous to walk openly through the village streets. However, the monk proved ungrateful for this considerate arrangement, and his vengeful spirit now haunts West Dereham out of spite.

The Lost Tunnel of Elsing Hall

Summary

According to local legend, a hidden subterranean passage connects Elsing Hall to St. Mary's church. A dog sent to explore the tunnel vanished into the darkness, its collar bell fading into eternal silence.

Details

Set in the Norfolk village of Elsing, local tradition maintains that a secret underground passage measuring nearly a mile long once linked the 15th-century moated manor house of Elsing Hall to St. Mary's church. Years ago, when an opening to the tunnel was uncovered, a dog with a small bell tied around its neck was sent inside to investigate the depths. As the animal ventured further underground, the chiming of the bell slowly grew fainter until it stopped altogether, and the dog was never seen again. Following the disappearance, the entrance to the passageway was sealed shut and its true location remains lost to this day.

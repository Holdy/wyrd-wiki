#title
The Phantoms of Flordon Station House

#summary
Following the closure of Flordon Railway Station, the occupant of the station house experienced distressing phantom cries and a gentle ghostly touch while alone at night.

#details
In the early 1970s, after the railway station in Flordon had closed down, the old station house was home to a young family. One night, while her husband was away working a night shift, the mother put her two young children to bed and sat down downstairs. She was suddenly startled by the sound of a young child crying. Rushing upstairs, she found both children sound asleep. After returning downstairs, the crying happened a second time, and another inspection revealed the children still peacefully sleeping. Later, as she lay awake in bed before falling asleep, she felt a small, disembodied hand gently brush against her cheek.

#location
The former Flordon Railway Station House situated along the disused railway line in Flordon, Norfolk.

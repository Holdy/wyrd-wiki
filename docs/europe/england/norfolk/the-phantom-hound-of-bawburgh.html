The Phantom Hound of Bawburgh

Summary

While cycling home late at night, a young man encountered a colossal spectral hound with glowing eyes that mysteriously vanished after an unlit vehicle seemingly crashed through it. The witness later believed the ghostly beast had saved him from a fatal accident.

Details

Late one moonlit night, Mr. E. Ramsey was cycling home to Bawburgh after attending a darts match in Norwich. As he approached his home village, he spotted an enormous hound sitting by the roadside signpost, its eyes shining like coals of fire. Though nervous, he passed safely, but half a mile further down the road, he heard the heavy beating of paws approaching from behind. The massive beast overtook him so closely that he could smell its rank odor before stopping aggressively in the middle of the road ahead. Just as Ramsey dismounted in terror behind his bicycle, an unlit vehicle careered out of a nearby spinney and seemed to crash straight through the hound. Ramsey fell into the hedge in shock as the vehicle sped past. When he picked himself up, he was astonished to see the dog completely unharmed; it then turned and vanished into thin air. Although folklore dictated that seeing the ghostly hound foreshadowed death—an omen he later associated with his wife's passing two years later—Ramsey came to believe the apparition had saved his life by occupying the exact spot where the speeding vehicle would have struck him.

The Witchcraft of Mrs Reeve

Summary

In 1878, a farmyard worker in Lakes End believed he had been cursed with a sudden infestation of lice by a local woman reputed to be a witch. Upon threatening to retaliate against her, the affliction mysteriously vanished just as the woman learned of his words.

Details

In 1878, a farmyard yardman named Bob Crawford crossed paths with an elderly local woman named Mrs. Reeve, who was widely credited in Lakes End with witchcraft powers after a family dispute. Shortly after speaking out about Mrs. Reeve's daughter, Crawford returned home one night heavily infested with lice. The following day at work, he warned a fellow worker nicknamed Twister to keep his distance, declaring that if he could not rid himself of the parasites by the end of the day, he would "shake hell over that old gal." That evening, Twister visited his mother's house and happened to mention Crawford's condition and his threat, unaware that Mrs. Reeve was present in the room. Upon hearing what Crawford intended to do, Mrs. Reeve was immediately taken ill and rushed home; concurrently, when Crawford arrived at his own house, he discovered his head and body were entirely clear of the lice.

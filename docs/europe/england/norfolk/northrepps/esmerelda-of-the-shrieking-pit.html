Esmerelda of the Shrieking Pit

Summary

A young woman named Esmerelda is said to have thrown herself into a medieval pit after a doomed romance, and her shrieks can still be heard on certain nights.

Details

Local folklore tells of a young woman called Esmerelda who died in the pit following a doomed romance. According to the legend, her screams echoed across the countryside and can still be heard on certain nights, which gave the eerie site its name. The pit itself is believed to have medieval origins linked to iron ore workings.

The Old Man of the Hopton Bypass

Summary

A police constable and his wife encountered a stocky old man in a calf-length garment crossing the Hopton Bypass in 1980.

Details

In 1980, British Transport Police officer PC Frank Colby was driving along the Hopton Bypass toward Lowestoft with his wife. As the car reached the southern section of the bypass, PC Colby saw what he believed to be a man crossing the dual carriageway. The figure was 5'6" tall, stocky, and wore a calf-length shapeless garment, a hunched head with spiky hair, trousers, and huge boots that he lifted up as he plodded along.

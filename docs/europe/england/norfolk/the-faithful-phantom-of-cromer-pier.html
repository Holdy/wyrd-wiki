The Faithful Phantom of Cromer Pier

Summary

A ghostly black dog haunts the shoreline near Cromer Pier, eternally gazing out to sea and vanishing into thin air when approached.

Details

Along the coast in Cromer, local folklore tells of a spectral black dog that haunts the beach near the town's famous pier. The loyal phantom is often seen sitting where the waves break on the sand, staring out toward the open water near the site of the swallowed medieval town of Shipden, waiting faithfully for an owner who never returned from the sea. Whenever curious onlookers or visitors attempt to approach the hound, it abruptly dissolves into thin air. Furthermore, local tradition associates the area with the legendary Black Shuck, whose ghostly tracks are said to run through Mill Lane and into the grounds of nearby Cromer Hall.

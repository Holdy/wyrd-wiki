The Phantom Bishop's Barge

Summary

A ghostly barge carrying twenty-eight rowers regularly appears near Brundall on specific summer and autumn dates.

Details

The bishop's barge, manned by twenty-eight rowers, passes Brundall on the 24th of June and the 18th of September every year.

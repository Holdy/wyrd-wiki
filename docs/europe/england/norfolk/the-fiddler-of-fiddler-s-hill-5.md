#title
The Fiddler of Fiddler's Hill

#summary
A daring musician who ventured into a legendary underground tunnel connecting Blakeney Guildhall to Binham Priory vanished without a trace when his music abruptly ceased beneath a roadside mound. Locals whispered that the Devil himself had snatched him away, forever naming the site Fiddler's Hill.

#details
Local folklore long whispered of a mysterious subterranean tunnel running from Blakeney Guildhall all the way to Binham Priory, rumored to be haunted by a spectral, black-robed monk. To solve the mystery of the tunnels, a brave fiddler volunteered to walk underground while playing his instrument so townsfolk could track his progress above ground. However, when his music suddenly stopped underneath a Neolithic burial mound at a crossroads, searchers found no trace of him. Rumors quickly spread that the Devil had taken him, and the mound was subsequently named Fiddler's Hill in memory of his uncanny disappearance.

#location
Fiddler's Hill is an archaeological mound located at a crossroads on the North Norfolk coast between Blakeney and Binham Priory.

#title
Summoning the Devil at Swanton Morley Church
#summary
Satan can supposedly be summoned at All Saints church in Swanton Morley by running around the building at midnight and whistling through the keyhole or crypt grille.
#details
Local legend states that the Dark Lord will appear if a person runs round All Saints church at midnight and then whistles through the keyhole. A variation of the ritual involves circling the church at the witching hour and instead whistling through the grille that looks into the crypt under the chancel.

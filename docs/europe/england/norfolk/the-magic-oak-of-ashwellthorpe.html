    The Magic Oak of Ashwellthorpe

    Summary

    During a Christmas gathering at Ashwellthorpe Hall, a mysterious visitor conjured a massive oak tree from a single acorn inside the great hall, which was subsequently dragged away by a pair of magical goslings.

    Details

    In the late sixteenth century, Sir Thomas Knyvett welcomed a mysterious Londoner and his companions into Ashwellthorpe Hall during the festive season. Wanting to show his gratitude, the visitor pulled out a magic acorn and planted it on the floor of the Grand Hall, causing an enormous oak tree to sprout instantly and fill the space with branches. As this blocked the upcoming Christmas supper, Sir Thomas ordered the tree removed. When his men found the logs too heavy to budge, a pair of miraculous goslings appeared and dragged the timber outside, where the branches vanished without a trace. According to local lore, this marvel accounted for why geese were traditionally kept on the hall's moat for generations.

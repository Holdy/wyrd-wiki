#title
The Silent Sailor of Smallburgh

#summary
Passers-by near Smallburgh have reported seeing a spectral sailor standing motionless and gazing out over the canal where he met his fate.

#details
Along the stretches of the waterway near Smallburgh, travelers and locals have occasionally encountered a ghostly apparition known as the silent sailor. The spectral figure stands completely still by the water's edge, persistently gazing out across the canal. According to local belief, the phantom is the spirit of a mariner who tragically lost his life in the canal waters and now remains bound to the site, eternally watching over the waterway.

#location
The North Walsham & Dilham Canal where it passes near the village of Smallburgh in Norfolk, England.

Sounds of Anglo-Saxon and Roman Battles

Summary

Each year on April 27th, the sounds of clashing swords and screaming men echo around Burgh Castle to mark past battles between Anglo-Saxon and Roman armies.

Details

On the 27th of April each year, ghostly sounds of past battles between Anglo-Saxon and Roman soldiers—specifically clashing swords and screaming men—can be heard around the fort.

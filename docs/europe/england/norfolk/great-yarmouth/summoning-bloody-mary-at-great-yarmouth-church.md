#title
Summoning Bloody Mary at Great Yarmouth Church
#summary
Running around St George’s churchyard in Great Yarmouth three times and shouting ‘Bloody Queen Mary!’ was once believed to make Mary Tudor’s face appear at a window.
#details
An 18th-century churchyard legend claimed that if a person ran around St George’s church three times without stopping and shouted "Bloody Queen Mary!", the face of Mary Tudor would appear at the nearest window. Daredevils were reportedly still attempting this dare up until the late 1800s.

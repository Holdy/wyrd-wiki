The Ghost Thrown From the Fort Wall

#summary
A ghostly apparition of an unknown person wrapped in a white flag is reported being thrown over the south wall of Burgh Castle.

A reported ghostly sighting at Burgh Castle features an unknown person wrapped in a white flag being thrown over the south wall. Although the specific backstory for this individual has been lost to time, historical context points out that this was a common form of punishment for people found guilty of wrongdoing back then.

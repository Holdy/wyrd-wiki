#title
The Knee-High Legionnaires of Burgh Castle

#summary
Ghostly apparitions of Roman legionnaires are said to walk a narrow lane near Burgh Castle on moonlit nights, appearing only from the knees up.

#details
At the 2,000-year-old Roman fort of Burgh Castle, quiet moonlit nights on the narrow lane close to the church of St Peter and St Paul bring forth ghostly apparitions of Roman legionnaires. These figures are visible only from the knees up because the road level has risen significantly since Roman times.

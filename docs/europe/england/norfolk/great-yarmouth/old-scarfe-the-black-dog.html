Old Scarfe the Black Dog

Summary

A 13th-century mercenary knight haunts Burgh Castle and Great Yarmouth as a ghostly black dog named Old Scarfe after being sent back by the devil.

Details

A ghostly black dog known as Old Scarfe is rumored to haunt Burgh Castle and the surrounding area of Great Yarmouth. He is believed to be the apparition of Baron Rudolph Scarfe, a 13th-century mercenary knight who fled to the village of Burgh Castle after being excommunicated from Germany for wicked and depraved actions. After continuing his evil acts in life, the devil sent him back to earth upon his death in the form of a black dog to cause further havoc in the surrounding areas.

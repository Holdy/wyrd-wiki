The Whispering Phantoms of St George's Theatre

Summary

Spirits are heard whispering and seen gliding over staircases at St George's Theatre, where CCTV has captured ghostly silhouettes.

Details

Staff and visitors at St George's Theatre hear spirits whispering and see ghostly figures gliding over staircases and watching rehearsals, while CCTV footage has captured two silhouettes walking through corridors and doors opening by themselves.

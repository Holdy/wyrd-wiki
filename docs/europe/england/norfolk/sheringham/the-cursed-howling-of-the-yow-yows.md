#title
The Cursed Howling of the Yow Yows

#summary
Drowned sailors whose boat was ignored by local fishermen seeking to salvage cargo cursed Sheringham, and their eerie cries can still be heard moving across the sea.

#details
Local folklore states that sailors drowned on a stormy night because townsfolk refused to help them—intending instead to salvage the boat's cargo for profit—and cursed Sheringham before perishing. Known as the Yow Yows, the eerie howling and cries of these doomed mariners can be heard echoing from the sea, mysteriously shifting locations when fishermen investigate the source.

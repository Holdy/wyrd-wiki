The Sheringham Yow Yows

Summary

Severe coastal squalls and sudden strong storms in Sheringham, known as Yow Yows, are linked to the ignored cries of a drowned ship's captain.

Details

In Sheringham, severe squalls and sudden, violent coastal storms are known as Yow Yows. According to folklore, these storms are linked to the captain of an old, drowned ship whose cries for help were turned away by local fishermen. Cries heard at sea off the coast of Sheringham serve as a warning of danger and a sign to return to land before the Yow Yows strike.

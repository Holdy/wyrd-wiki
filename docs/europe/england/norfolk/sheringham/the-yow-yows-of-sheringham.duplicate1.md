#title
The Yow Yows of Sheringham
#summary
The ghostly cries of doomed sailors known as Yow Yows echo during severe weather off the coast of Sheringham.
#details
During brief but violent squalls off the coast at Sheringham, ghostly noises known as Yow Yows can be heard. These cries are said to be made by drowning sailors whose distress was ignored by local fishermen seeking to salvage cargo, and the sounds act as an attempt to lure the living into a watery grave.

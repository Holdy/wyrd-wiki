The Yow Yows of Sheringham

Summary

Ghostly cries of doomed mariners who drowned in a storm are heard off the coast of Sheringham, hoping to lure the living into a watery grave.

Details

Ghostly noises known as the Yow Yows echo off the coast of Sheringham before a storm. Believed to be the calls of perished mariners who drowned after no one attempted to save them, these cries reportedly attempt to lure the living into a watery grave.

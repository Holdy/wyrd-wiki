The Ghostly Whistler of Weybourne Beach

Summary

A smuggler left behind by his shipmates drowned trying to reach them and can still be heard whistling across the beach.

Details

John Smythe was a smuggler whose shipmates abandoned him ashore at Weybourne and sailed away without him. He tried to wade out to reach them but drowned when he could not beat the tides, and to this day his whistling can be heard as he tries to get their attention.

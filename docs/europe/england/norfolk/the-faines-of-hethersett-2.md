#title
The Faines of Hethersett

#summary
Calf-sized spectral creatures with glowing saucer-eyes and phantom hounds have historically haunted rural lanes and open fields in Hethersett.

#details
Antiquary Walter Rye recorded accounts of "The Faines"—mysterious, calf-sized beasts with glowing saucer-eyes that were said to frequent Hethersett, noting that one creature kept to Mill Road while another haunted Gravel Pit Lane. Years later, local witnesses reported encounters with a giant, silent, rough-coated phantom dog that shadowed individuals along Henstead Road and across the Hethersett Memorial Playing Field before abruptly disappearing into thin air.

#location
Hethersett Memorial Playing Field is situated in the village of Hethersett, accessible via Recreation Road and Canns Lane in South Norfolk.

#title
The Ancient Battle Cries of Hockwold Fen
#summary
The Hockwold Fens are said to be haunted by ancient warriors whose loud battle cries echo across the fenland.
#details
Long before pewter items dating from Roman times were discovered in the 1960s, the Hockwold Fens were said to be haunted by ancient warriors. Their loud battle cries could be heard sounding across the fenland, possibly belonging to British warriors or the Iceni fighting Roman invaders.

#title
The Sinking Bells of Hell Hole in Tunstall
#summary
A bubbling pool near Tunstall churchyard is said to conceal church bells dragged to hell by the Devil, whose muffled peals can still be heard on quiet nights.
#details
Near the ruins of St Peter and St Paul’s church at Tunstall is a boggy pool of water known as "hell hole" that bubbles in the summertime. The bubbles are said to be sinking church bells taken to hell by the Devil after he snatched them away while the parson and churchwardens argued over ownership. On quiet nights, the muffled peal of bells can supposedly be heard across the marshes as Satan rings them in his underworld.

#title
The Devil and the Tunstall Church Bells

#summary
After a dispute over church bells following a fire, the Devil stole them and buried them near a bubbling pool known as Hell Hole, where muffled bells can still be heard.

#details
When a fire ravaged a church in Tunstall, an unholy argument broke out between the parson and churchwardens over who should claim the valuable, unscathed bells. Seeing an opportunity to settle the dispute, the Devil stepped into the smoking timbers and carried the bells away. As he scrabbled through the earth toward his underworld lair with the stolen loot, he created a boggy, bubbling pool of water known as Hell Hole, where a muffled peal of bells can still be heard on dark, quiet nights.

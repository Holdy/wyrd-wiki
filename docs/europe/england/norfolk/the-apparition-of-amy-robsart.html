The Apparition of Amy Robsart

Summary

The tragic Elizabethan noblewoman Amy Robsart is said to haunt the grounds of Rainthorpe Hall every September. On the anniversary of her death, her apparition appears in the gardens alongside a gentleman.

Details

Amy Robsart, whose mysterious death at the foot of a staircase in 1560 sparked historic scandal, is said to return to Rainthorpe Hall due to her family's close ties to the estate. According to local lore, every year on the evening of September 8th, her ghostly figure is spotted meeting a mysterious gentleman in the pleasure gardens of the Elizabethan manor.

    The Ghostly Patron of the Ferry House

    Summary

    A phantom man and his loyal dog are said to haunt a historic riverside inn, mysteriously making any half-pints of beer left out overnight disappear.

    Details

    The Ferry House Inn in Surlingham, established in 1725, carries a longstanding tradition regarding a spectral patron and his faithful dog. Rather than terrifying visitors, this harmless ghostly duo is believed to roam the premises at night and tidy up any half-finished glasses of beer left behind, ensuring that no drink goes to waste even in the afterlife.

#title
The Buried Witch and Oak Tree of East Somerton Church
#summary
A witch buried alive at St Mary’s church in East Somerton allegedly had her wooden leg grow into a massive oak tree that destroyed the building.
#details
Amidst a thicket of trees at East Somerton stand the ruins of St Mary’s church. According to local folklore, a witch was buried alive in the nave by villagers, whereupon her wooden leg grew into a massive oak tree that shattered the church and still stands today. Legend says that walking around the tree three times and calling her name will release the witch's spirit.

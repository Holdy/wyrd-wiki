The Witch of East Somerton Church

Summary

A witch buried alive beneath St Mary's Church in East Somerton allegedly had her wooden leg grow into an oak tree that destroyed the building.

Details

At the 15th-century ruins of St Mary's Church, legend tells of a witch who was buried alive in the nave by villagers. Her wooden leg supposedly grew into a massive oak tree that eventually destroyed the church. The oak tree still remains in the center of the ruined church, and visitors are warned that walking around the tree three times will release her spirit.

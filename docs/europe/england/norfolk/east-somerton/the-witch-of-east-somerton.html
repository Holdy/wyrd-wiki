The Witch of East Somerton

Summary

An oak tree grew from the wooden leg of a witch buried alive in the foundations of St Mary's Church. Walking around the tree three times is said to release her spirit.

Details

Hidden amongst a thicket of trees, the ruins of 15th-century St Mary's Church in East Somerton hold a piece of local folklore. According to the legend, a witch with a wooden leg was caught by angry villagers and buried alive in the church's foundations. An oak tree subsequently grew from her wooden leg and ruined the church building. It is said that walking around the oak tree three times will release her spirit.

#title
The Witch's Leg of East Somerton

#summary
A woman accused of witchcraft was buried alive at a Norfolk church, willing her wooden leg to sprout into an oak tree that would destroy the building.

#details
During the height of the British witch trials, a woman accused of being a witch was put on trial and buried alive at St Mary's Church in East Somerton. According to legend, she willed her wooden leg to sprout into an oak tree that would bring the church crashing down above it. Today, the ruins are tucked away in a mysterious woodland area, and visitors who circle the tree three times at twilight and gently whisper her name can reportedly speak to the witch beneath the earth.

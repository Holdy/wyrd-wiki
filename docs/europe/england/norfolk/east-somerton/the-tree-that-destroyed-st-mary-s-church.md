#title
The Tree That Destroyed St Mary's Church

#summary
A suspected witch with a wooden leg was buried alive beneath a ruined Norfolk church, where she enchanted her prosthesis to grow into a massive oak tree that destroyed the building above.

#details
During the height of the English witch trials, a woman suspected of witchcraft and possessing a wooden leg was buried alive beneath St Mary's Church. She allegedly enchanted her wooden leg to grow into a massive oak tree that eventually reduced the church to a roofless ruin. Legend holds that walking around this central tree three times will release the witch's spirit.

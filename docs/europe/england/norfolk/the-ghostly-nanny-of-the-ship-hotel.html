The Ghostly Nanny of The Ship Hotel

Summary

The historic Ship Hotel in Brancaster is reputed to be haunted by the ghost of Lord Nelson's nanny, whose spectral presence lingers in the ancient coastal inn.

Details

Situated in the historic coastal village of Brancaster—close to the Norfolk roots of Horatio Nelson—The Ship Hotel is steeped in local folklore. According to traditional accounts shared among villagers and visitors, the ancient building is haunted by the ghost of the young naval hero's nanny, who once lived and worked in the area. Patrons and staff of the old inn have occasionally reported uncanny phenomena, inexplicable cold spots, and fleeting shadowy figures moving through the corridors, attributed by tradition to the spirit continuing to watch over the coastal landmark.

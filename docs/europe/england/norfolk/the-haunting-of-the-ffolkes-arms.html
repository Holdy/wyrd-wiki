The Haunting of the Ffolkes Arms

Summary

The historic 300-year-old coaching inn in Hillington is famously haunted by the spirit of Agnes, a young nanny who plunged to her death from an attic bedroom. Her restless apparition and chilling paranormal phenomena continue to be reported by guests and staff.

Details

Standing on Lynn Road in Hillington, the Ffolkes Arms is a venerable 300-year-old coaching inn steeped in local history and folklore. According to traditional accounts handed down through generations of staff and travelers, the inn is haunted by the ghost of a young nursemaid or nanny named Agnes. In the 19th century, Agnes tragically fell or leapt from her quarters in the attic rooms, reportedly becoming impaled upon spikes below. Ever since, her spectral presence has supposedly lingered within the historic building. Visitors and staff have reported encountering eerie anomalies, including chilling drops in temperature in the back rooms regardless of the weather, and fleeting sightings of a heavy, shadowy black shape filling doorways.

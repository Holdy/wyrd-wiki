The Sudden Appearance of Saham Toney Mere

Summary

According to local tradition, the circular mere in the center of Saham Toney miraculously materialized overnight.

Details

Local folklore records that the shallow freshwater mere in Saham Toney was said to have appeared overnight. The village sign famously depicts the mere alongside historical figures, reflecting local tales that monks from Castle Acre Priory were granted rights to fish in its waters twice a year, and early lords of the manor such as Sir Roger Toni were closely tied to the location.

The Fate of Colonel Thomas Sidney

Summary

A cruel colonel who won a New Year's Eve horse race by maliciously shooting a rival's horse was dragged away by the devil into Ranworth Broad.

Details

Colonel Thomas Sidney of Ranworth Hall was a violent and cruel man. During a New Year’s Eve horse race, he shot the horse directly in front of him, causing the rider to fall and break his neck, which allowed Sidney to win. That night, at the celebration dinner, the devil came for the colonel, slung him over his stallion, and plunged beneath the waters of Ranworth Broad. Every New Year’s Eve, the ghost of Colonel Thomas Sidney can be seen hanging over a galloping black stallion ridden by the devil as it plunges into the Broad.

#title
The Rowing Monk of Ranworth Broad
#summary
A monk who survived the slaughter of his brethren during the Dissolution of the Monasteries now haunts Ranworth Broad, rowing across the water with a ghostly dog in his boat.
#details
In the 1530s, Brother Pacificus, a monk at St Benets Abbey, worked on repairing a rood screen at St Helen’s Church in Ranworth, rowing across the Broad each morning with his dog. One evening, he returned to find Henry VIII’s soldiers had slaughtered his fellow monks. After his death and burial at St Helen's, locals began reporting sightings of his sorrowful figure rowing across the Broad to the church early in the morning, accompanied by a ghostly dog standing in the bow.

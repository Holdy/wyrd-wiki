#title
The Rowboat Journey of Monk Pacifus
#summary
The sole surviving monk of an abbey massacre rows his boat between Ranworth and St Benet's Abbey with his dog, searching for his murdered brothers.
#details
The monk Pacifus rows his little boat with his dog by his side between Ranworth and St Benet’s Abbey, searching for his fellow monks who were rumored to have been murdered by King Henry VIII’s men. Pacifus was the only monk to escape the slaughter because he was away mending the rood screen at St Helen’s church when the attackers arrived.

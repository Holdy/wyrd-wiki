The Huntsman's Ride at Old Hall

Summary

A cruel and vicious huntsman met a terrifying end when a hooded stranger carried him away on horseback after he shot a rival's horse.

Details

Colonel Thomas Sydney of Old Hall was a hard-drinking, vicious huntsman known for his unkindness. On December 31, 1770, during a hunting race, he deliberately shot his neighbour's horse upon realizing he was losing, causing the rider to be thrown and break his neck. That evening, as Sydney boasted about the incident at a hunt ball, a hooded stranger appeared at Old Hall, threw the Colonel across a saddle, and galloped off into the darkness. Sydney was never seen alive again, and he is now occasionally sighted slung across the devil's saddle as it gallops over the marshes.

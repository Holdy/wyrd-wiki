The Phantom Drummer of Hickling Broad

Summary

The rhythmic beat of a kettle drum echoes across Hickling Broad on winter nights, sounded by a poor drummer boy who drowned beneath the frozen ice.

Details

In the early 19th century, a poor drummer boy fell in love with a rich man's daughter and secretly married her. They met each night in a hut on the edge of Hickling Broad, and during the winter freeze, he would skate across the ice while beating his kettle drum to signal his approach. One night, the ice gave way, plunging him to a chilling death beneath the frozen water. On winter nights, the rhythmic tattoo of his ghostly drum can still be heard across the broad as he continues his eternal search for the girl.

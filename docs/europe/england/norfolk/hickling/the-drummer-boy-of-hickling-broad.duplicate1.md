#title
The Drummer Boy of Hickling Broad
#summary
A secret-marrying drummer boy who fell through thin ice on the frozen broad is heard drumming in the winter.
#details
A 19th-century drummer boy and a rich man's daughter secretly married. While crossing the frozen Broad at night to meet his beloved while rhythmically drumming, he fell through thin ice and drowned. Locals report hearing the same rhythmic drumming in winter as he searches for her.

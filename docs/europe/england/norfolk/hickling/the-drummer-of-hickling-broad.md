#title
The Drummer of Hickling Broad

#summary
A young drummer boy who drowned in Hickling Broad after falling through ice in 1815 is seen shivering on the shore and heard beating his drum.

#details
In 1815, a young drummer boy visiting Potter Heigham fell deeply in love with a local girl, but her father rejected his marriage proposal. Continuing to see her in secret, the young man was skating on Hickling Broad on a warm February morning while beating his drum when the ice gave way and he drowned. Eyewitnesses have since reported seeing his shivering apparition on the lake's shore and hearing the sound of a drum carried on the breeze.

#title
The Drummer Boy of Hickling Broad

#summary
A young soldier who drowned while skating across Hickling Broad to see his secret lover is said to haunt the icy waters every February.

#details
During the winter of 1815, a drummer boy from Potter Heigham fell in love with a local girl whose father rejected his marriage proposal. The couple met in secret at Swim Coots on the edge of Hickling Broad, with the boy skating across the water while playing a rhythmic tattoo on his kettle drum to announce his arrival. One evening, the ice gave way, plunging him to his death into the icy waters. Every February, his shivering ghost can be seen skating across the Broad while the rhythmic beat of his drum echoes through the mist.

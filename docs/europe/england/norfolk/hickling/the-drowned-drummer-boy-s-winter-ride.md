#title
The Drowned Drummer Boy's Winter Ride
#summary
A drummer boy visiting home before the Battle of Waterloo drowned under the frozen ice of Hickling Broad while skating to see his sweetheart.
#details
A drummer boy home on leave in the winter before the Battle of Waterloo had fallen in love with a local girl, and the two met every evening in a small hut on the edge of Hickling Broad. During a particularly cold year when the river was frozen, he skated up the river playing his drum to reach her, but he fell through the ice and drowned. His sweetheart swore she saw him coming up the river that night and caught his hand, but he vanished just before his body was recovered the next day. On frosty winter nights, the beat of his drum and the swish of his skates can still be heard heading up river to see his love.

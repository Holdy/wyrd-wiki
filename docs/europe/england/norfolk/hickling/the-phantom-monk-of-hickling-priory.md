#title
The Phantom Monk of Hickling Priory

#summary
A ghostly monk haunts the ruined Augustinian priory at Hickling and vanishes down a secret tunnel leading to a marsh drain.

#details
Following a plague that wiped out most of the community at Hickling Priory, the ruins became haunted. Sightings have been reported of a monk who roams the site before disappearing down an underground tunnel that stretches half a mile away to a marsh drain.

#title
Sir Berney Brograve’s Wager with the Devil at Brograve Mill
#summary
After losing a mowing contest against the Devil, a 1771 mill owner slammed his door, leaving it forever scarred with hoof-prints and permanently leaning westward.
#details
Brograve Mill is a ruined windmill near Waxham and Horsey whose owner in 1771, Sir Berney Brograve, foolishly wagered his soul that he could out-mow the Devil. Lucifer won easily, but when he came to collect, Sir Berney escaped into the mill and slammed the door. In a fit of rage, the Devil pounded on the door with his cloven hooves; the next morning, the door was found pitted with hoof-prints, and the entire mill leaned westward where Satan had tried to blow it down.

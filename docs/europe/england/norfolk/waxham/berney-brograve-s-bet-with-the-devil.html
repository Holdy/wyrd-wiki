Berney Brograve's Bet with the Devil

Summary

After losing a mowing race against the devil, Sir Berney Brograve locked himself in a mill as the devil pounded on the door, and his ghost now haunts the local area.

Details

In 1771, Sir Berney Brograve made a bet that he could mow a beanfield quicker than the devil, wagering his soul. After losing, he hid and locked himself inside Brograve Mill, where the devil pounded on the door all night with cloven hooves. By morning, the door was pitted with hoof prints and tilting gently westward. Brograve's ghost now haunts the local area, riding across Brograve Level on a horse snorting flame from its nostrils.

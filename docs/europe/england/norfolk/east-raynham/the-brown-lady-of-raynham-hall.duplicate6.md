#title
The Brown Lady of Raynham Hall

#summary
Lady Townshend, who was locked in a room by her husband after rumors of infidelity, is said to haunt Raynham Hall wearing an old brown dress.

#details
A long time ago, a woman named Lady Townshend was locked in a room within Raynham Hall after her husband Charles Townshend heard she was cheating on him and denied her permission to leave. Nearly 100 years after her death, the first sighting of her ghost occurred in 1835, and for hundreds of years she has apparently appeared in an old brown dress.

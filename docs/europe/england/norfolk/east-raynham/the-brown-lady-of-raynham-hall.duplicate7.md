#title
The Brown Lady of Raynham Hall
#summary
Lady Dorothy Walpole, known as 'The Brown Lady', has been seen wearing a brown dress throughout Raynham Hall since the 1830s.
#details
Raynham Hall is the location where one of the most famous ghost photographs was ever taken. The ghost of Lady Dorothy Walpole, referred to as 'The Brown Lady', has been seen throughout the building wearing a brown dress in sightings dating back to the 1830s.

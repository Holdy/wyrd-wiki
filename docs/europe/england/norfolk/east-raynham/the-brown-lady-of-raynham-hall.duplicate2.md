#title
The Brown Lady of Raynham Hall

#summary
The ghost of Dorothy Walpole, known as the Brown Lady, haunts Raynham Hall with an illuminated face and dark hollows instead of eyes.

#details
Dorothy Walpole was imprisoned in her quarters at Raynham Hall by her husband Lord Charles Townshend after an affair with Lord Wharton was discovered. She died there in 1726 after wasting away or suffering a mysterious tumble down the grand staircase where she broke her neck. Her ghost has become a classic haunting, recognized by her strangely illuminated face and dark hollows for eyes. Novelist Captain Maryatt famously shot at her with a pistol—the bullet passing straight through the phantom and embedding in a door—and a Country Life photographer captured a baffling photograph of her in 1936.

#title
The Brown Lady of Raynham Hall
#summary
Lady Dorothy Walpole, who was locked up by her cruel husband, haunts Raynham Hall wearing a brown dress.
#details
Lady Dorothy Walpole, who lived from 1686 to 1726 and was locked up by her cruel husband, haunts Raynham Hall near Fakenham dressed in a brown gown. She is also said to haunt Houghton Hall and Sandringham House.

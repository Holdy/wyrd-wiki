The Brown Lady

Summary

A ghostly figure in brown captured on film at Raynham Hall remains one of Britain's most famous and debated ghosts.

Details

At Raynham Hall, a photographer pointed a camera up the stairs and captured an apparition known as the Brown Lady, making her one of the best-known ghosts in Britain and a subject of ongoing debate.

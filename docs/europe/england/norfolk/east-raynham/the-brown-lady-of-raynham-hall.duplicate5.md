#title
The Brown Lady of Raynham Hall
#summary
The ghost of Dorothy Walpole, known as the Brown Lady, haunts Raynham Hall with hollow eye sockets and an eerie glow.
#details
Dorothy Walpole, who died in 1726 after being locked away by her husband following an illicit affair, haunts Raynham Hall as the Brown Lady. Described as having dark hollows instead of eyes and an eerie glow, she was once shot by novelist Captain Maryatt—though the bullet passed straight through her—and was captured on camera by a Country Life photographer in 1936.

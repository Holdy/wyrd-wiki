The Brown Lady of Raynham Hall

Summary

Lady Dorothy Walpole is said to haunt the stairs of Raynham Hall as an ethereal, vaporous figure trapped by her husband after her alleged adultery.

Details

Captured in a famous 1936 photograph by a Country Life magazine photographer, the ghost of Lady Dorothy Walpole manifests as a vapour form assuming a woman's appearance. As punishment for discovered adultery, she was locked in her rooms during her life and now wanders the hall, confined to the staircase.

#title
The Brown Lady of Raynham Hall
#summary
A spectral woman wearing a brown dress and lacking eyes haunts the corridors of a historic country house.
#details
Supposed to be Dorothy Walpole, who married the second Viscount Townshend, a ghost known as the Brown Lady walks the staircases and corridors of Raynham Hall with empty eye sockets. A visitor named Captain Marryat once fired a pistol at her, but the bullet passed straight through and lodged in a door.

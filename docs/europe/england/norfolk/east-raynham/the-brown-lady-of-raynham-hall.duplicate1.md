#title
The Brown Lady of Raynham Hall
#summary
A woman locked in a room by her husband before dying of smallpox in 1726 still haunts Raynham Hall in a brown dress.
#details
Charles Walpole discovered his wife was having an affair and locked her in a room at Raynham Hall. She died of smallpox there in 1726 and is known as the Brown Lady because she still appears wearing an old brown dress.
#location
Raynham Hall, East Raynham, Fakenham, Norfolk, England

    The Lost Chapel of Postwick Lodge

    Summary

    Local tradition maintains that the historic 16th-century Postwick Lodge was built upon the foundations of a vanished medieval place of worship known as St. Ethelred's Chapel. Although no visible ecclesiastical ruins remain, villagers long preserved the legend that the sacred site lies buried beneath the grounds.

    Details

    Postwick Lodge is a complex historic building featuring late medieval and 16th-century flint and brick architecture situated in the Norfolk parish of Postwick. According to enduring local folklore, the estate occupies the site of the legendary St. Ethelred's Chapel, an ancient ecclesiastical building that disappeared centuries ago. While early parish records and Domesday references acknowledge early religious foundations in the area, the exact whereabouts of the lost chapel have been the subject of persistent local tradition, with generations of residents pointing to the lands surrounding Postwick Lodge as its true location.

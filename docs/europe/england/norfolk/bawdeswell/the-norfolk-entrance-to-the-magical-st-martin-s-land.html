The Norfolk entrance to the magical St Martin's Land

Summary

A hole found in a field on the road between Bawdeswell and Swanton Morley serves as a potential portal to the Otherworld and Fairyland.

Details

On the road between Bawdeswell and Swanton Morley, in a field next to the thoroughfare, a hole was found that serves as a potential portal to The Otherworld in a legend delighting those who believe in the little people and Fairyland.

The Cursed Foundations of Kirkescrofte

Summary

Following a vow of gratitude to St. Faith, medieval nobles attempted to build a monastery at Kirkescrofte in Horsford, only for the masonry to collapse nightly until the project was relocated.

Details

After returning from a pilgrimage to Rome, Norman nobleman Robert Fitzwalter and his wife Sybil de Cheney believed they were miraculously delivered from a gang of robbers by the intervention of St. Faith. In gratitude, they vowed to erect a monastery dedicated to the saint on a plot of land in Horsford known as Kirkescrofte. Construction commenced, but every wall and pillar raised during daylight hours mysteriously tumbled into ruin overnight. Interpreting this nightly destruction as a sign that divine will opposed the site, the couple abandoned Kirkescrofte and successfully re-established the monastery further away at Horsham St. Faith.

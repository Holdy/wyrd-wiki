#title
The Lost Warriors of Hockwold Fens

#summary
The marshlands of Hockwold cum Wilton are said to echo with the spectral battle cries of ancient warriors from bygone conflicts. Locals long reported hearing the terrifying sounds of clashing weapons and war shouts rolling across the fenland.

#details
Long before archaeological excavations uncovered Roman-era pewter hoards buried in the fenland soils of Hockwold cum Wilton, local tradition held that the area was haunted by the spirits of ancient fighters. On dark nights across the fens, witnesses reported hearing loud, disembodied battle cries and the metallic clash of weapons ringing out over the marshes. Folklore associated these phantom sounds with the ancient Britons or Iceni warriors who once fought against Roman invaders in the East Anglian fens.

#location
The fenlands surrounding the village of Hockwold cum Wilton in southwest Norfolk.

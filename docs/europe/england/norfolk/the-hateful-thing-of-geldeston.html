The Hateful Thing of Geldeston

Summary

While walking home along a lane at night, a woman and her daughter encounter a terrifying, shapeshifting apparition known as the Hateful Thing, which trails them until it finally heads toward the local churchyard.

Details

As recorded in 19th-century East Anglian folklore, a local woman named Mrs. S set out for an evening walk from Gillingham back to Geldeston with her daughter and future son-in-law. As they crossed a stile into the road, the daughter—who had been born during the chime hours and possessed the sight to perceive hidden things—shouted in terror that a dog was blocking their way. Although neither Mrs. S nor the young man could initially see anything, the daughter watched in horror as the entity expanded in size until it was larger than a horse. When the daughter grabbed hold of her mother's arm, Mrs. S was suddenly granted the sight to witness the beast: a black, shapeless, ever-shifting entity that accompanied them down the road. The trio kept pace with the thumping apparition until it reached the sandy lane leading toward St. Michael's churchyard, where it finally disappeared.

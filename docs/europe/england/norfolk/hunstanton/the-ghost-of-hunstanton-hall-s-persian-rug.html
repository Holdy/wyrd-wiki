The Ghost of Hunstanton Hall's Persian Rug

Summary

After her prized Persian rug was cut up and distributed following her death in 1768, Armine Styleman's ghost flew into a rage and haunted Hunstanton Hall until the rug was restored.

Details

In the 1700s, Hunstanton Hall owner Armine Styleman loved a particular Persian rug so much that on her deathbed in 1768, she declared she would return from the grave if anything happened to it. After she died, the rug was cut into several pieces and given to nearby villages. Armine's spirit burst into a furious rage and haunted the hall until the rug was eventually restored, though many still believe she wanders the area.

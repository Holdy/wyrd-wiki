#title
The Cursed Persian Rug of Hunstanton Hall
#summary
Hunstanton Hall is haunted by Dame Armine Le Strange after her prized Persian rug was cut up by a new mistress.
#details
In the 1700s, Dame Armine Le Strange made her gambler son Nicholas promise never to harm her prized Persian rug, gifted from the Shah of Persia, threatening to haunt the hall if he broke the vow. Nicholas kept it locked in an attic box until he died. Nearly a century later, a new American mistress named Emmeline Le Strange discovered the box and had the rug cut into tiny pieces to distribute to the poor. Immediately afterward, Armine's ghost—known as the "Grey Lady"—appeared at the window in fury, forcing Emmeline to recover all the pieces and sew the carpet back together.

    When Marnie Was There

    Summary

    A lonely orphan girl sent to the Norfolk coast for her health forms a secret bond with a mysterious girl living in a derelict marsh house, eventually discovering her ghostly connection to her own family history.

    Details

    Sent away from her foster home to recover from an illness, young Anna arrives in the coastal village of Burnham Overy Staithe. While wandering across the vast, lonely salt marshes, she discovers the isolated Marsh House—a building known locally for its blue windows—where she meets a golden-haired girl named Marnie. The two form an intense friendship, sharing secrets and midnight adventures against the backdrop of the creeks and mudflats. However, Marnie frequently disappears under mysterious, ethereal circumstances, and the house itself shifts between being abandoned and inhabited. Years later, details emerge revealing that Marnie was the spirit of Anna's grandmother, whose lingering presence reached across time to comfort and heal her orphaned granddaughter.

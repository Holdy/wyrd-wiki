#title
The Magical Healing Properties of the Southery Stone
#summary
Discovered after an explosive 1642 Halloween thunderstorm, a mysterious boulder in Southery gained a reputation for magical powers including healing joint pain and predicting rain.
#details
A thunderstorm over Southery on Halloween in 1642 uncovered a hole filled with fire and a mysterious stone with magical properties. Locals claimed women with joint problems could be cured by sitting on the stone naked at midnight, farmers said the stone would sweat before rain, villagers spat on it for good luck, and an 80-year-old man credited drinking its morning dew with his continued ability to father children. The stone is currently missing.

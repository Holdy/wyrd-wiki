    The Dragon of Ludham

    Summary

    A fearsome dragon that terrorized the village of Ludham through a network of subterranean tunnels was thwarted when a local resident blocked its lair, causing the enraged beast to flee to St Benet's Abbey.

    Details

    According to local folklore, in ancient times a dragon held the village of Ludham in a reign of terror, carving out a hidden labyrinth of tunnels beneath the settlement between the churchyard and the main street. One day, the creature crawled out into the open street to bask in the warm sun and stretch its wings. Seizing the opportunity, a quick-witted and brave local man crept up behind the monster and rolled a heavy boulder directly across the mouth of its subterranean tunnel. Trapped outside its lair and realizing it had been outsmarted, the enraged dragon took flight, descending upon nearby St Benet's Abbey where it smashed the stone walls before vanishing forever.

#title
The Demonic Demolition of Kirkescrofte

#summary
Twelfth-century monastic building work in Horsford was repeatedly destroyed overnight by devils until the project was relocated.

#details
After believing they were miraculously saved from robbers by St. Faith while returning from a pilgrimage to Rome, Robert Fitzwalter and his wife Sybil de Cheney vowed to erect a monastery in Horsford. They commenced building their first foundation on a plot of land known as Kirkescrofte. However, supernatural interference thwarted their efforts; every wall and structure erected by workmen during the day was mysteriously thrown down and destroyed by devils during the night. Concluding that the site was cursed and that neither God nor the saint wished for the monastery to stand there, the founders abandoned Kirkescrofte and successfully relocated the priory to Horsham St. Faith.

#location
The historic site of Kirkescrofte in Horsford, Norfolk.

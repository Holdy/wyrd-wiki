    The Demonic Curse of Margaret Wade

    Summary

    In 1611, a Barton Bendish woman was brought before the Norfolk Quarter Sessions for publicly wishing that the devil would carry away a local man.

    Details

    In 1611, community tensions in the village of Barton Bendish boiled over when Margaret Wade engaged in a bitter dispute with a local resident named Mr. Lynghoke. During the confrontation, she unleashed a torrent of abuse, labeling him a "Rascall, Jack, knave" alongside other severe imprecations. Most damningly, she publicly prayed that "the devile myght fetch him awaye," invoking demonic forces against him. Her aggressive verbal malediction led to formal articles of misbehaviour being filed against her at the Norfolk Quarter Sessions, mirroring the terrifying accusations of malediction and cursing that frequently accompanied early modern witchcraft trials.

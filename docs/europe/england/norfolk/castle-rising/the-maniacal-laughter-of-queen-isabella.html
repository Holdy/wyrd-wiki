The Maniacal Laughter of Queen Isabella

Summary

The raucous cackle and maniacal laughter of Queen Isabella echo from the upper levels of Castle Rising Castle centuries after her death.

Details

In 1330, King Edward III sent his mother, Queen Isabella, to live in unofficial imprisonment at Castle Rising Castle for her part in the murder of her husband, Edward II. After her mind gave way and she descended into insanity, she lived out her final days as a gibbering wreck. Over six hundred years after her death, her raucous cackle and maniacal ghostly laughter are still heard echoing from a room on the upper levels of the castle, loud enough to chill anyone who hears them.

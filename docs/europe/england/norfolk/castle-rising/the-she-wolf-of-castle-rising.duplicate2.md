#title
The She Wolf of Castle Rising
#summary
Isabella the Fair, the wife of Edward II, haunts the ruins of Castle Rising and is said to turn into a wolf.
#details
Isabella the Fair, wife of Edward II, was sent to Castle Rising in honorable confinement after her brief reign as regent and suffered episodes of madness. The ruins are now home to sightings of her ghost, and she is known to turn into a wolf, earning the moniker the She Wolf of Castle Rising.

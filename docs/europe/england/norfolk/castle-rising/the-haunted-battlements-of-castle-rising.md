#title
The Haunted Battlements of Castle Rising
#summary
Queen Isabella is said to haunt Castle Rising, her screams echoing from the battlements after going mad from loneliness during her imprisonment.
#details
According to fourteenth-century chronicler Froissart, Edward III imprisoned Queen Isabella at Castle Rising after the execution of her lover, Mortimer. It is said that she went mad from loneliness and that her screams could be heard as she wandered the battlements, lamenting her fate.

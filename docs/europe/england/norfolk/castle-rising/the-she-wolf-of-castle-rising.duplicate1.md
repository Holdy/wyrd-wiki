#title
The She Wolf of Castle Rising
#summary
Isabella the Fair, the wife of Edward II, is reported to haunt Castle Rising after suffering bouts of madness, sometimes appearing as a wolf.
#details
Ghostly sightings have been reported at the ruins of Castle Rising, including encounters with the wife of Edward II, Isabella the Fair, who is said to transform into a wolf and reportedly suffered bouts of madness while living there.

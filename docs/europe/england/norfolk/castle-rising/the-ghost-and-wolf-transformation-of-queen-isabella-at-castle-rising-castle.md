#title
The Ghost and Wolf Transformation of Queen Isabella at Castle Rising Castle
#summary
The upper floor of Castle Rising Castle is haunted by Queen Isabella, who allegedly screams, laughs hysterically, and transforms into a wolf.
#details
Queen Isabella, known as the "She-Wolf of France" and wife/alleged murderer of Edward II, resided at the 12th-century Castle Rising Castle from 1330 until her death in 1358, suffering from bouts of madness in her final years. Local residents report hearing hysterical laughter and ghostly screams from the castle in the early hours, while visitors have heard the swishing sound of her dress and even reported sightings of the Queen transforming herself into a wolf.

#title
The She-Wolf of Castle Rising
#summary
Queen Isabella, known as the She-Wolf of France, is said to haunt Castle Rising with cackling laughter and the ability to transform into a wolf.
#details
Castle Rising, built during the 12th century, served as the home of Queen Isabella between 1330 and 1358. Married to and supposedly involved in the death of King Edward II, her spirit is said to wander portions of the keep today. Visitors report hearing her cackling laughter around the grounds, and some believe she has the ability to transform into a wolf to roam the area.

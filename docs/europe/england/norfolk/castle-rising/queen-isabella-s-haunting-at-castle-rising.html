Queen Isabella's Haunting at Castle Rising

Summary

Former French royalty Queen Isabella, who went mad at Castle Rising, is linked to sounds of screaming, maniacal laughter, and mist.

Details

After the death of King Edward II, former French royalty Queen Isabella lived the rest of her life at Castle Rising and reportedly went mad. People at the castle have since heard screaming and maniacal laughter, along with sightings of apparitions and mist.

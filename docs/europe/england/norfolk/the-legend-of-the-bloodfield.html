    The Legend of the Bloodfield

    Summary

    Local folklore in the parish of Beetley holds that a field north-west of East Bilney church was the site of an English Civil War battle, where historical military gear has been unearthed.

    Details

    In the parish of Beetley, within a meadow near East Bilney church known locally as the "Bloodfield," local tradition maintains that a battle or skirmish took place during the English Civil War. Over generations, agricultural work and metal detecting in the area have reportedly yielded post-medieval military artifacts, including sword hilts, spurs, and horse-bits. Although formal historical archives contain no official record of a Civil War engagement at this location, the evocative name and the discovery of period military equipment have preserved the tale in local folklore.

    Pigg's Grave

    Summary

    A remote crossroads in north Norfolk marks the resting place of a legendary highwayman named Pigg, who was reportedly hanged and buried at the boundary of three parishes. Depending on the version of the local tradition, he was either executed for his highway robbery or became a victim of lawlessness himself.

    Details

    At the intersection where the Aylsham to Fakenham road meets the Holt to East Dereham road lies Pigg's Grave, a junction where the parishes of Melton Constable, Briningham, and Swanton Novers meet. According to local folklore, Pigg operated as a highwayman in the surrounding countryside before being captured and executed by hanging directly at the crossroads, where outlaws were traditionally interred. Alternate iterations of the legend propose that Pigg was actually the victim rather than the perpetrator, with his killer subsequently meeting justice at nearby Gallowhill Lane. True to age-old English custom regarding isolated meeting points, the site is also historically associated with the unceremonious burial of suicides.

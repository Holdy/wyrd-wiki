    The Perfumed Ghost of Hangings Hill

    Summary

    The scent of perfume drifts along a roadway near a church, remaining as the sole manifestation of an executed maid.

    Details

    After a maid was hanged for stealing from Thorpe Hall, her haunting manifests not as a visual sighting but as a fragrant smell. Her perfume wafts along the road below the church on Hangings Hill.

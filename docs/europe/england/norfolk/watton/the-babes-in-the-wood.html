The Babes in the Wood

Summary

Two children from a famous ballad were left by their wicked uncle to die in Wayland Wood, where the tree they lay under was later struck by lightning.

Details

Wayland Wood is said to be the location where the children in the ballad were left to die by their wicked uncle. Tradition holds that the tree under which they were said to have lain was struck by lightning in 1879.

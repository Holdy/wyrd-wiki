#title
The Dark Origins of Babes in the Wood near Watton
#summary
The classic fairy tale Babes in the Wood is said to stem from a real 1500s event near Watton where an uncle allegedly murdered his young nephew to inherit Griston Hall.
#details
In the 1500s near Watton, Griston Hall owner Edmund de Grey had a young grandson named Thomas whose father died when he was seven. Thomas's uncle, Robert de Grey, stood to inherit Griston and its land if young Thomas died before marrying and having a family. When Thomas went missing at age 11 and Robert claimed the wealth, dark rumors flew that Robert had murdered the boy and hidden his body in the forest, inspiring the Babes in the Wood tale.

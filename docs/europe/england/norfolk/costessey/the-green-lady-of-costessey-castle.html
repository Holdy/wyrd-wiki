The Green Lady of Costessey Castle

Summary

The ruins of Costessey Castle are haunted by the Green Lady, an echo from the era when Catholicism was illegal.

Details

The ruins of Costessey Castle are haunted by the Green Lady, a phantom rumored to date back to the days when Catholicism was illegal in England and Catholics in Costessey reportedly wore green to identify themselves.

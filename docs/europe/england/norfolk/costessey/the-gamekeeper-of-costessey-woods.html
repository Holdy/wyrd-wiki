The Gamekeeper of Costessey Woods

Summary

A smiling 1920s gamekeeper with a rifle patrols the woods of Costessey before vanishing into thin air.

Details

Dressed in hunting clothes with a rifle slung over his shoulder, the ghost of a 1920s gamekeeper smiles pleasantly at those he encounters in the woods before mysteriously vanishing.

#title
The Ghostly Figure of Haddiscoe Dam

#summary
A driver and his colleagues captured video footage of a mysterious, dark-clad figure walking along the treacherous and unpaved Haddiscoe Dam late at night. Local residents later confirmed that the accident-prone road has a long-standing reputation for being haunted.

#details
While driving home from work late at night along the twisty and unpaved Haddiscoe Dam, 26-year-old Aden Hudson and his two colleagues were entirely unaware of any pedestrians on the road. However, upon reviewing video footage of the foggy drive home, they discovered a chilling, shadowy figure dressed completely in black strolling along the roadside. Given the dangerous nature of the road and the absence of any pavements, seeing someone walking there at that hour was unheard of. Upon sharing the footage online, local residents informed him that the accident-prone route is widely regarded as a haunted road where ghostly apparitions are frequently reported.

#location
Haddiscoe Dam is a specific road located in Haddiscoe, Norfolk, UK.

#title
The Phantom Hound of Clip Street Lane

#summary
A grandmother encountered the legendary Black Shuck just outside the gate of her bungalow on a green lane in Bale. This eerie sighting of the spectral hound was passed down through family recollections across generations.

#details
A resident's grandmother experienced a chilling encounter with Black Shuck, the demonic phantom dog of East Anglian folklore, only a few yards from the gate of her bungalow. The sighting took place on Clip Street Lane in Bale. Although the direct witnesses passed away, the story remained vivid within the family after being recounted from early childhood by an aunt who remembered the event.

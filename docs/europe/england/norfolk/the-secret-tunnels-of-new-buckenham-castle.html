    The Secret Tunnels of New Buckenham Castle

    Summary

    Local legend maintains that a network of subterranean passageways stretches across the Norfolk countryside from the ruins of New Buckenham Castle to several distant historic estates and religious sites.

    Details

    According to local tradition, the Norman remains of New Buckenham Castle are connected to the wider landscape by three secret underground tunnels. Folklore dictates that these hidden passages lead from the castle ruins over many miles: one eastward toward the now-demolished Elizabethan mansion of Boyland Hall at Morningthorpe, another heading south-west to Kenninghall Place, and a third running north toward the scant remains of the priory that once stood on the site of Old Buckenham Castle.

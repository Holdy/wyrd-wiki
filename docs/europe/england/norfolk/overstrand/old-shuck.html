Old Shuck

Summary

A massive black dog with a single burning eye pads along coast roads at night, and meeting him is said to warn of an approaching death.

Details

Nineteenth-century accounts set the great black dog of East Anglia on the coast road between Beeston and Overstrand. Described as the size of a calf with a single fiery eye, meeting him was said to warn of a death before the year was out.

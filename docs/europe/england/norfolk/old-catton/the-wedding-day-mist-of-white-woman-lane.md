#title
The Wedding Day Mist of White Woman Lane
#summary
A family traveling by pony and trap along White Woman Lane halted as a drifting mist crossed the path, commemorating a bride killed on her wedding day.
#details
While traveling down White Woman Lane in Old Catton via pony and trap, a family watched their pony abruptly come to a halt as a drifting mist emerged from a field on the left, crossed the track, and vanished, after which the pony resumed its journey. Local folklore attributes the ghost to a girl named Gemma who fell in love with a poor coachman; on their wedding day as he left with his belongings, she ran after him wearing white and tragically slipped under the wheels of his coach.

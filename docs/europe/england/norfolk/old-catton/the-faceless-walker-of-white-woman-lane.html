The Faceless Walker of White Woman Lane

Summary

Two teenage girls cycling down White Woman Lane encountered a woman walking ahead of them whose face was entirely replaced by darkness.

Details

An anonymous personal account describes two teenage girls cycling down White Woman Lane when they saw a woman wearing a head scarf walking ahead in a field, initially presuming she was walking a dog. As they approached and looked back at her, they discovered that where her face should have been, there was nothing but complete darkness, prompting them to cycle home in record time.

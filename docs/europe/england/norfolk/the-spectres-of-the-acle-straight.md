#title
The Spectres of the Acle Straight

#summary
Motorists travelling along the Acle Straight have reported harrowing encounters with a phantom pedestrian near the Halvergate turn-off who vanishes as vehicles pass right through him.

#details
A driver heading towards Acle watched in horror as a middle-aged man stepped out from the right side of the road into the path of their car near the Halvergate turn-off. With no time to brake, the vehicle passed straight through the figure, who turned and looked directly at the driver before disappearing into thin air. Other motorists across this nine-mile stretch have similarly reported unexplained apparitions, phantom horse-drawn carts, and sudden, unprompted impulses to slam on their brakes.

#location
The Acle Straight (A47) near the Halvergate turn-off and the former Stracey Arms between Acle and Great Yarmouth.

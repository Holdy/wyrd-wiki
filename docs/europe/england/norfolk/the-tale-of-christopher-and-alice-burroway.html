    The Tale of Christopher and Alice Burroway

    Summary

    A local woman who abandoned her newborn on the steps of Norwich Cathedral later married her grown son without knowing his identity until their wedding night.

    Details

    Alice, the daughter of a local tradesman, gave birth and abandoned her newborn baby boy on the steps of Norwich Cathedral. The healthy infant was distinguished by a large mole under his left shoulder blade. Years later, after Alice inherited her father's estate, she fell in love with a steward named Christopher Burroway, and the two were wed. On their wedding night, as they undressed, Alice discovered an identical blemish beneath Christopher's left shoulder blade in the exact same spot, revealing that her husband was her long-lost son.

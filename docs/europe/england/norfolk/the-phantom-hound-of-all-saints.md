#title
The Phantom Hound of All Saints

#summary
In 1913, a late-night cyclist narrowly avoided crashing into a spectral dog near a ruined church tower in Gillingham before the creature vanished into the graveyard.

#details
One night in 1913, a cyclist was pedalling quickly along a road in Gillingham when a ghostly dog suddenly materialized directly in front of him near the ruined tower of All Saints Church (grid reference TM411923). Expecting to be thrown from his bicycle by the impact, the cyclist braced for a collision. At the last possible second, however, the spectral hound darted away and fled into the adjacent, still-used graveyard.

#location
The encounter occurred near the ruined tower of All Saints Church (grid reference TM411923) in the parish of Gillingham, Norfolk.

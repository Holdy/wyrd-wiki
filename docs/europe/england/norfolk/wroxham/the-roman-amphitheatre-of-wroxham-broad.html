The Roman Amphitheatre of Wroxham Broad

Summary

On Julius Caesar's birthday, July 21st, Wroxham Broad is said to transform into a Roman amphitheatre.

Details

According to a book on local tales, Wroxham Broad was a Roman amphitheatre during the time of Caesar. On Caesar's birthday, July 21st, the location supposedly manifests into the shape and form of the amphitheatre once again.

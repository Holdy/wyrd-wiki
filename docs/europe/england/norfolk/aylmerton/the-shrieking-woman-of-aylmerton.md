#title
The Shrieking Woman of Aylmerton
#summary
A ghostly woman wanders near St John's Church at night, shrieking in despair as she searches iron working hollows for her murdered baby.
#details
Between 850 and 1100, iron workings between Aylmerton and West Runton Heath left depressions in the fields. Shrieks ring out in the dark night near St John's Church, uttered by a ghostly woman searching among the hollows for the body of her baby. Legend has it that her husband murdered his wife and buried the child in one of the pits, and she now wanders and looks into each hollow, shrieking with despair when it is empty because she does not know which one contains her child.

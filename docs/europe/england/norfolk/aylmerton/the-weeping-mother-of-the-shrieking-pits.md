#title
The Weeping Mother of the Shrieking Pits
#summary
Iron working pits near Aylmerton are haunted by a distraught woman searching for her infant after her jealous husband threw the baby to its death.
#details
At some point in the last 500 years, a woman gave birth to a child near Aylmerton. Believing another man fathered the child, her husband flew into a jealous rage, took the baby, and threw it into one of the local iron working pits to die. Driven insane by the cruel act, the mother wanders from pit to pit, screaming and wringing her hands in a desperate search for her infant.

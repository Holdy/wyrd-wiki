The Library Ghost of William Windham III

Summary

Staff at Felbrigg Hall have reported seeing the ghost of former resident William Windham III sitting in the library.

Details

William Windham III inherited Felbrigg Hall in 1749 and was obsessed with books. When a fire broke out in a friend's London library, he risked his life to rescue precious volumes, sustaining injuries that led to his death a few weeks later. Staff say they have since seen his ghost sitting at a library table or in a chair.

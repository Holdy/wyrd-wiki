#title
The Shrieking Pits
#summary
A spectral figure in white wanders between pits on the heath, emitting mournful shrieks.
#details
A mysterious figure dressed in white moves from pit to pit across the heath in Aylmerton, shrieking constantly for something it lost, with nobody knowing the true nature of its sorrow.

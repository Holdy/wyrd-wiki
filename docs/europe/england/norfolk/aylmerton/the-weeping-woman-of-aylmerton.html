The Weeping Woman of Aylmerton

Summary

Five shallow pits in Aylmerton are haunted by a ghostly woman in white who endlessly weeps and wails while searching for her murdered baby.

Details

Located three miles from Cromer near Gresham Cross, five shallow pits in Aylmerton are known locally as the Shrieking Pits. Folklore states that the pits are haunted by a ghostly figure in white who weeps and wails as she walks between the depressions in the ground. She is endlessly searching for her infant child, whom her husband killed and buried in a pit west of the church after becoming convinced the baby wasn't his, before returning to kill his wife as well.

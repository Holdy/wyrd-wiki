The Pin and the Church Ghost

Summary

In 1940s Hilgay, children believed that sticking a pin into the door of All Saints church and running around the building three times would summon a ghost.

Details

During the 1940s, a persistent local superstition among children in the Norfolk village of Hilgay centered around a ritualistic challenge at All Saints church. It was widely believed that if an individual pushed a pin into the wooden church door and then ran completely around the building three times, a ghostly apparition would appear.

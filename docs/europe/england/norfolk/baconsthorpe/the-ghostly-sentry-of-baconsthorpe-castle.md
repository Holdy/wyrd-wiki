#title
The Ghostly Sentry of Baconsthorpe Castle

#summary
Visitors walking through Baconsthorpe Castle are often startled by stones splashing into the moat, thrown by a ghostly medieval soldier stationed on the walls.

#details
Visitors exploring the shattered remains of Baconsthorpe Castle often experience a sudden break in the silence when stones break the still waters of the moat as if thrown from a height. Confused by the ripples, visitors turn to look and catch sight of a ghostly sentry or medieval soldier standing on the castle walls, throwing stones as if to pass the time.

    The Healing Mineral Spring of East Winch

    Summary

    A woodland spring in East Winch was historically famed for its potent medicinal waters, which local tradition held could cure rheumatism and rival the most celebrated spas.

    Details

    Recorded in the early 19th century, several natural springs were noted within the parish of East Winch. Among them, one particular source was said to be strongly impregnated with sulphate of iron, granting it remarkable healing properties. Locals and visitors regarded the water's medicinal virtues highly, particularly for its perceived ability to relieve rheumatism. This legendary spring was located in a copse north-west of the village, historically known as Mineral Plantation.

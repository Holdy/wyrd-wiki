#title
The Tunstead-Worstead Church Tunnel

#summary
Local legend maintains that a subterranean tunnel connects the medieval churches of St Mary in Tunstead and St Mary in Worstead, starting from a mysterious vaulted chamber behind the altar.

#details
According to local folklore, a secret underground passage runs between St Mary's Church in Tunstead and St Mary's Church in Worstead. The hidden tunnel is said to originate at the Tunstead church in a vaulted chamber situated beneath an unusual and unexplained platform behind the altar. Elder residents have traditionally recalled tales of the passage being opened up in the past, only to be quickly sealed back up again due to the presence of foul, stale "bad air."

#location
St Mary's Church in Tunstead, Norfolk.

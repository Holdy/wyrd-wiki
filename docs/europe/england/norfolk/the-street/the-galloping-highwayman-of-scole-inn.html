The Galloping Highwayman of Scole Inn

Summary

A ghostly highwayman can be heard riding his horse around the historic Scole Inn.

Details

Visitors to the Grade I listed Scole Inn can hear the sound of galloping ghost horses and might encounter a ghostly highwayman riding about the grounds.

Emma of Scole Inn

Summary

A woman murdered by her husband centuries ago remains at the Scole Inn and is willing to talk about her death.

Details

A woman named Emma, who was murdered by her husband a couple of hundred years ago, continues to haunt the Scole Inn and is reportedly up for chatting about her demise.

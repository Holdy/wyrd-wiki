#title
The Phantom Drummer Boy of Hickling Broad

#summary
On cold February evenings, the ghost of an 1815 drummer boy who drowned while skating across Hickling Broad to meet his forbidden lover can still be heard beating his drum across the ice.

#details
In the winter of 1815, shortly before the Battle of Waterloo, a poor drummer boy from Potter Heigham fell in love with the daughter of a wealthy family. Forbidden by her father to marry, the star-crossed lovers arranged to meet in secret every night at a small hut at Swim Coots on the edge of Hickling Broad. When a severe winter freeze set in, the drummer boy would skate across the frozen water wearing his bright yellow and scarlet coat, beating a drum tattoo to signal his approach. One freezing evening, the ice cracked beneath him, and he plunged into the icy depths and drowned. Since that night, on misty winter evenings, people have witnessed his spectral figure gliding across the broad and heard the eerie sound of his drum and skates.

#location
Swim Coots is located on the marshy edge of Hickling Broad within the Norfolk Broads, UK.

#title
The Silver Chalice of the River Wensum
#summary
Two men recovered a silver chalice from the River Wensum, but it magically leaped back into the water the moment one of them shouted aloud.
#details
In a story from the start of the 19th century near Lyng, two men managed to pull a silver chalice out of the River Wensum. However, as soon as one of them cried aloud in excitement, the chalice instantly jumped out of their hands and fell back into the water, escaping their grasp.

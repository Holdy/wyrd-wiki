The Bleeding Stone of Lyng

Summary

A glacial erratic in Lyng shaped like a giant toad is said to bleed when pricked with a pin on certain nights.

Details

A glacial erratic in Lyng that resembles a giant toad is linked to legends of a martyred saint, a ruined nunnery, and ritual sacrifices. According to the folklore, this strange stone is said to bleed if it is pricked with a pin on certain nights.

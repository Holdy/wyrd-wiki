The Drowned Convent Bell of Lyng

Summary

The sound of a drowned bell from a deserted convent can be heard ringing at night from under the water where it was thrown near Lyng.

Details

Local legend surrounding the Lyng area holds that the bell of a deserted convent was thrown into nearby water. At night, the sound of the drowned bell can still be heard ringing from under the water.

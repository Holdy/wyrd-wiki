    The Sunken Church of Oby

    Summary

    Local tradition holds that the medieval parish church of Oby did not merely fall into ruin, but instead mysteriously sank completely beneath the marshy ground.

    Details

    While 19th-century county histories noted that the parish church of Oby stood beside the road leading from Thurne to Yarmouth, by the early 20th century the structure had completely disappeared. Local folklore maintained that the medieval building had literally sunk down into the earth of the Norfolk fens. Although Oby's church was historically abandoned and lost during the late 16th century, the legend of its subterranean descent persisted among local residents as a tale of a sunken sanctuary.

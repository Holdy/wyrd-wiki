#title
The Phantom Rabbit of Thetford Warren Lodge

#summary
A huge ghostly white rabbit with flaming red eyes is said to guard the doorway of Thetford Warren Lodge as an omen of death.

#details
Harking back to the building's warrening history, local legend at Thetford Warren Lodge states that a large, ghostly white rabbit with flaming red eyes guards the doorway. Sighting this creature is said to serve as an omen of death to anyone who lays eyes on it.

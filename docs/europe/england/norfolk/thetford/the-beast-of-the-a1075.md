#title
The Beast of the A1075

#summary
Numerous witnesses have spotted a Bigfoot-like cryptid creature foraging in the forest along a stretch of the A1075 between Thetford and Wretham.

#details
Along a stretch of the road between Thetford and Wretham, a strange creature resembling descriptions of Bigfoot has been spotted by numerous witnesses. This yeti-like beast is believed to forage in the forest, camouflaging itself in deep thickets and being disturbed only on rare occasions.

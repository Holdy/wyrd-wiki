#title
The Window Apparition of Thetford Warren Lodge

#summary
Apparitions, including an eerie face and a man in blue and white with gaping black holes for eyes and mouth, have been seen peering from the upper floor windows of Thetford Warren Lodge.

#details
Despite having no floors remaining, Thetford Warren Lodge has been the site of multiple window apparitions. An eerie face has been reported looking out from the first-floor window of the building. Additionally, in 2011, a man was seen peering from a second-floor window wearing blue and white clothing and boasting gaping black holes where his eyes and mouth should have been.

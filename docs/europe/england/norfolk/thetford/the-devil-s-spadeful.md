#title
The Devil's Spadeful
#summary
A hill was formed when the Devil cleaned his spade, and six silver bells are said to be buried underneath it.
#details
The Devil stopped to clean his spade at Thetford, leaving behind a mound of earth known as the Devil's Spadeful. Local legend maintains that six silver bells are hidden beneath the hill.

#title
The Ghostly Monks of Thetford Priory

#summary
Ghostly monks, chanting, and a clinking of keys have been reported by visitors among the ruins of the 12th-century Thetford Priory.

#details
Built in the 12th century as a burial site for earls and dukes, Thetford Priory is haunted by ghostly monks seen walking through its ruins, accompanied by reports of chanting being heard. In 1992, a group of teenagers heard the clinking of keys before watching a monk run past them, and another sighting describes a cloaked figure descending a set of stairs that is not actually there.

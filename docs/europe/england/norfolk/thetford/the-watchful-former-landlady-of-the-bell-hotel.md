#title
The Watchful Former Landlady of The Bell Hotel

#summary
A former landlady named Elizabeth Radcliffe is frequently seen on the ground floor of Thetford's Bell Hotel, watching over her past home amid various unexplained phenomena.

#details
Elizabeth Radcliffe, a onetime landlady of the 15th-century Bell Hotel in Thetford, continues to be sighted on the ground floor as she watches over her former residence. Her apparition is accompanied by a host of unexplained happenings throughout the building, including sudden atmospheric chills, feelings of being watched, and heavy, unseen footsteps.

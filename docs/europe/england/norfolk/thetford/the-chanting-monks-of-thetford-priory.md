#title
The Chanting Monks of Thetford Priory
#summary
Ghostly monks are heard chanting and singing in Latin after their tombs were disturbed.
#details
Following the removal of several tombs from the Priory of Our Lady of Thetford in 1520, ghostly hooded monks have been reported chanting and singing in Latin in accounts dating back to 1930.
#location
Thetford Priory, Water Lane, Thetford, Norfolk, England

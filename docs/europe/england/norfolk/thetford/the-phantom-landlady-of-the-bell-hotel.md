#title
The Phantom Landlady of The Bell Hotel
#summary
The ghost of an 18th-century landlady who died after a quarrel haunts room 29 and performs ghostly housekeeping duties.
#details
Betty Radcliffe was either the hotel owner or landlady in the 18th century when a quarrel with her lover in room 29 ended her life. Guests have seen her standing at the window of room 29 or felt her presence, and at night, keys clink and skirts swish as she conducts ghostly housekeeping.
#location
The Bell Hotel, Thetford, Norfolk, England

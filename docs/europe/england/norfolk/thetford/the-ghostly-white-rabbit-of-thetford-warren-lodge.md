#title
The Ghostly White Rabbit of Thetford Warren Lodge
#summary
A white ghostly rabbit with red glowing eyes is said to haunt the doorway of Thetford Warren Lodge as an omen of death.
#details
Built in the 1400s to protect gamekeepers and hunting parties against poachers, Thetford Warren Lodge is said to be home to a white ghostly rabbit that can be seen in the doorway. Possessing red glowing eyes, it is considered an omen of death to anyone who lays eyes on it.

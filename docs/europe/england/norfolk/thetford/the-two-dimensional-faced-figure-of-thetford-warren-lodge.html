The Two-Dimensional Faced Figure of Thetford Warren Lodge

Summary

A figure with a strange, two-dimensional face has been seen wandering near Thetford Warren Lodge, terrifying witnesses near the former leper hospital site.

Details

Rooted in the history of the nearby leper hospital close to Thetford Warren Lodge, a strange figure with a flat, two-dimensional face has been reported wandering the area. The entity is known for gibbering horribly and terrifying anyone who witnesses it.

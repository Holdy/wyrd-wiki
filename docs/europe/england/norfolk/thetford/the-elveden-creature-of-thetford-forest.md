#title
The Elveden Creature of Thetford Forest

#summary
A large, long-haired beast with a shaggy coat has been frequently sighted running on four legs or standing upright on the A1075 in Thetford Forest.

#details
Operating as Norfolk's equivalent to Bigfoot, the Elveden Creature is a large, long-haired beast with a grey or jet-black shaggy coat that inhabits Thetford Forest. Numerous sightings have occurred along the A1075 between Thetford and Wretham, where witnesses have seen the creature running on all fours across the road or standing on its hind legs like a human.

The Window Spectre of Thetford Warren Lodge

Summary

An eerie face has been reported looking out from the first-floor window of Thetford Warren Lodge, even though the building no longer has any floors.

Details

At Thetford Warren Lodge, an eerie face has been reported looking out from the building's first-floor window. This chilling sight occurs despite the fact that the historic lodge no longer has any floors remaining inside.

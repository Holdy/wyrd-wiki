#title
The Hooded Monk of The Bell Inn
#summary
A hooded monk is frequently seen by guests in the main suite bedroom of The Bell Inn.
#details
Guests staying at The Bell Inn have reported seeing a hooded monk in the main bedroom suite, alongside sensations of being watched, smelling perfume, or experiencing sudden drops in temperature.

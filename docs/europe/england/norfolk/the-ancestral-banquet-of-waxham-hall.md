#title
The Ancestral Banquet of Waxham Hall

#summary
On New Year's Eve, an eccentric Norfolk baronet hosted a ghostly banquet for the shades of his ancestors in his ancestral home. As midnight struck, the phantom guests vanished, leaving him alone in the freezing hall.

#details
Sir Berney Brograve, a famously eccentric landowner residing at Waxham Hall, decided to host a macabre gathering on New Year's Eve. He laid six places at his dining table and filled glasses for the ghostly shades of his ancestors who had died violent deaths. Throughout the evening, toasts were solemnly drunk to the departed spirits. Exactly as the clock struck midnight, the phantoms vanished into thin air, leaving Sir Berney alone in the cold and desolate hall.

#location
Waxham Hall is a historic manor house located in the village of Waxham, Norfolk, situated near the east coast north-east of Stalham.

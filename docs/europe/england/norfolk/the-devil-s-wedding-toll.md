#title
The Devil's Wedding Toll

#summary
On her wedding night in Bastwick, a young bride is abducted by the devil in a fiery, skeleton-driven coach as payment for a magical love potion.

#details
Lady Carew enlisted a local witch to help her daughter, Evelyn, win the hand of the wealthy bachelor Sir Godfrey Haslitt of Bastwick. The witch provided a potent love potion in exchange for a solemn vow that whatever she later demanded would be granted. Following Sir Godfrey and Evelyn's wedding on May 31, 1741, and their return to Bastwick for the reception, a skeleton-driven coach thundered up to the hall doors at midnight. The devil leapt from the vehicle, seized the bride, and sped off toward Potter Heigham. Though pursued by wedding guests, the coach burst into flames and plunged into the river at Potter Heigham bridge, claiming Lady Evelyn as the witch's ultimate price.

#location
Potter Heigham bridge carries the A149 road across the River Thurne in Norfolk.

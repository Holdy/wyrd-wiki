#title
The Lady in Brown of Raynham Hall
#summary
Raynham Hall is haunted by an apparition of a lady in a brown brocade dress, believed to be Lady Dorothy Walpole.
#details
One of the most well-known hauntings features the apparition of a woman wearing a brown brocade dress at Raynham Hall. According to legend, the spirit is Lady Dorothy Walpole, who was locked away inside the hall by a cruel husband.
#location
Raynham Hall, Fakenham, Norfolk, England

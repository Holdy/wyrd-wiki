    The Midnight Ride of Anne Boleyn's Ghost

    Summary

    Anne Boleyn's headless ghost is said to ride a track between Blickling and Whitwell Hall at midnight on Christmas Eve.

    Details

    Residents living near an old estate boundary recounted that Anne Boleyn's headless ghost could be seen riding the track from Blickling to Whitwell Hall at midnight on Christmas Eve, leading locals to avoid Bar Lane after dark.

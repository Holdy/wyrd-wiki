    The Mistletoe Bride of Terrington St Clement

    Summary

    According to local legend, the ghost of a young bride who accidentally locked herself inside a wedding chest during a Christmas game haunts the village church, emerging once a year to join in the Christmas carols.

    Details

    Terrington St Clement traces its earliest Saxon origins to Hay Green before settling on the higher ground surrounding St Clement’s Church. The parish is home to a chilling 17th-century legend of a "Mistletoe Bride." During her wedding feast, the young bride playfully decided to hide in a heavy wooden chest during a game of hide-and-seek, challenging her new husband to find her. Tragically, the heavy lid engaged a hidden spring lock, trapping her inside. Though guests initially believed she had fled, her fate remained unknown until years later when her skeleton was discovered inside the chest, still wearing her wedding dress and clutching a sprig of mistletoe. Local lore holds that her spectral form returns every year on the Sunday before Christmas to join the choir in the church.

The Whispers of Winston Hall

Summary

Winston Hall in Gillingham is reputed to be haunted by unseen spirits whose rustling silk garments, heavy footsteps, and disembodied calls echo through the ancient farmhouse at night.

Details

Dating back to the reign of Queen Elizabeth I, the historic farmhouse of Winston Hall has long been associated with local legend and tradition, including the tale that Richard Cromwell once resided there in retirement. Although the spirits residing within its walls are never seen, they are frequently heard by those inside. Occupants and visitors have reported hearing the distinct rustling sound of a stiff silk dress sweeping along the corridor at the back of the house. At night, heavy footsteps pace the rooms, and a phantom voice calls out "are you there?" from a little-used exterior door, though opening it reveals no one standing outside.

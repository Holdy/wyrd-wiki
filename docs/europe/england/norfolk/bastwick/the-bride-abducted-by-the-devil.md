#title
The Bride Abducted by the Devil
#summary
On her wedding night in 1741, a bride whose marriage was arranged via a witch's love potion was snatched away by the devil in a skeleton-driven coach that ultimately plunged into the water at Potter Heigham Bridge.
#details
In 1741, Lady Evelyn married Sir Godfrey Haslitt, a match made possible by a love potion her mother, Lady Carew, obtained from a witch. On her wedding night, a coach driven by a skeleton thundered up to the hall door at Bastwick, and the devil leapt out, grabbed the bride, and whisked her away. The coach was chased by Sir Godfrey and his guests until it burst into flames as it crossed Potter Heigham bridge, falling without a trace into the water below.

The Upright Gambler of Rodney Street

Summary

An eccentric wealthy contractor allegedly made a fatal pact with the devil and arranged to be buried sitting upright in a pyramid tomb to prevent Satan from ever claiming his soul.

Details

William Mackenzie was a successful 19th-century civil engineering contractor who amassed a vast fortune. According to local folklore, he was also an avid and reckless gambler who entered into a high-stakes poker game with the devil, ultimately losing his soul. Fearing that the devil would come to claim his payment once he was laid horizontally beneath the earth, Mackenzie arranged a clever loophole. Upon his death, he was interred inside his tomb in an upright, seated position at a table clutching a winning hand of cards. Because he was never traditionally buried flat in the ground, the devil could not take him, leaving his restless ghost to wander the surrounding streets at night.

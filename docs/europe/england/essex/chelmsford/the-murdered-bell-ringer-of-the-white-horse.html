The Murdered Bell Ringer of the White Horse

Summary

The screaming of a bell ringer murdered on the stairwell is heard at the White Horse pub.

Details

A bell ringer was allegedly murdered on the stairwell of the White Horse pub. Visitors occasionally hear his screams echoing from the exact spot where he died.

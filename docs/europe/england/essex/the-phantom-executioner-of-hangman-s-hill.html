The Phantom Executioner of Hangman's Hill

Summary

A ghostly executioner is said to drag unsuspecting people up the hill using his spectral noose.

Details

Hangman’s Hill in Epping Forest, Essex, is a place shrouded in mystery and legend. The hill is known for an eerie phenomenon where vehicles parked at the bottom with their handbrake released appear to roll uphill. Local lore suggests that this occurs because the spirits of those executed on the hill are pulling the cars up to join them in the afterlife. Another tale involves a phantom executioner who drags unsuspecting people up the hill with his ghostly noose. Despite these chilling stories, the truth behind the rolling cars is an optical illusion caused by the topography and surrounding trees.

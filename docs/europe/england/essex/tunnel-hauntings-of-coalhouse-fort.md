#title
Tunnel Hauntings of Coalhouse Fort
#summary
Coalhouse Fort's tunnels feature shadows, full-bodied apparitions, photographic mist, and physical attacks.
#details
Dating back to ancient times, Coalhouse Fort is said to retain the presence of those who oversaw it. In the tunnels, people report shadows, full-bodied apparitions, and mist appearing in photographs, with some visitors even being grabbed or having objects thrown at them.
#location
Coalhouse Fort, East Tilbury, Essex, England

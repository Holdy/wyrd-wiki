The Drowned Wife of The White Hart

Summary

A woman who drowned after falling through a frozen river while searching for her husband haunts The White Hart.

Details

A woman crossing a frozen river reportedly fell through the ice and drowned while looking for her husband. Her spirit is said to haunt The White Hart as she continues her search.

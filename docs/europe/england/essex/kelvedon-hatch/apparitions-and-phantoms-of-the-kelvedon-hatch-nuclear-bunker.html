    Apparitions and Phantoms of The Kelvedon Hatch Nuclear Bunker

    Summary

    The Kelvedon Hatch Nuclear Bunker is haunted by former personnel, a tall elderly woman, and a dark sick-bay force over an ancient burial site.

    Details

    The Kelvedon Hatch Nuclear Bunker features apparitions of former bunker and RAF personnel, a tall elderly woman, and a dark force in the sick bay. Stories suggest the bunker was built over an ancient Bronze Age burial site, and another tale claims a worker was buried alive in concrete.

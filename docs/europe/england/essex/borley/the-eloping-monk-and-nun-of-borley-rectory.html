    The Eloping Monk and Nun of Borley Rectory

    Summary

    Borley Rectory is haunted by photographic apparitions and moving objects, tied to a monk and nun who tried to elope and were killed.

    Details

    Borley Rectory is reputed to be one of the most haunted locations in the United Kingdom, featuring photographic apparitions, self-moving objects, and out-of-place figures across the grounds. Local legend holds that a monk and nun tried to elope here before being caught and killed.

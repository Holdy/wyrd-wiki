Lord Marney at Layer Marney Tower

Summary

Former owner Lord Marney haunts Layer Marney Tower, seen traversing the spiral staircase and near his former bedroom.

Details

Former home owner Lord Marney is said to be displeased with how his home turned out, choosing to remain behind to haunt Layer Marney Tower. He is frequently seen moving up and down the spiral staircase or near his former bedroom.

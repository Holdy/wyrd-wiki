    Sarah's Affair at The Anchor

    Summary

    Flying objects and the scent of perfume at The Anchor are attributed to Sarah, murdered after a marital affair.

    Details

    Objects flying around and the sudden scent of perfume are reported at The Anchor in Canewdon. This activity is attributed to a ghost named Sarah, who was murdered after being caught in a marital affair.

Little Girl Alice at Brook Red Lion Hotel

Summary

A little girl named Alice who was murdered in 1638 haunts rooms 5, 6, and 10 of the Brook Red Lion Hotel.

Details

A little girl named Alice was murdered at the Brook Red Lion Hotel and has remained there since her death in 1638. She is usually seen in rooms 5, 6, and 10, and some believe she also manifests as a woman.

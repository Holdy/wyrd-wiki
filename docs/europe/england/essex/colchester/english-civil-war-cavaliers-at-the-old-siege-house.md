#title
English Civil War Cavaliers at The Old Siege House
#summary
English Civil War soldiers and Cavaliers make The Old Siege House their home, occasionally vanishing before stunned guests.
#details
Soldiers from the English Civil War are said to still inhabit The Old Siege House Bar and Brasserie. Cavaliers have vanished before stunned guests, and certain areas give off an overwhelmingly negative energy that staff try to avoid.

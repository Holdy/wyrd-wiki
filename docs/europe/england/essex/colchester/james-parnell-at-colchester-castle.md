#title
James Parnell at Colchester Castle
#summary
Imprisoned man James Parnell, who suffered a fatal fall after climbing a rope for food, haunts Colchester Castle.
#details
A man named James Parnell was imprisoned at Colchester Castle until his death. Part of his punishment required climbing a rope to obtain his food, and he never recovered after suffering a fall. His spirit has been reported throughout the building ever since.

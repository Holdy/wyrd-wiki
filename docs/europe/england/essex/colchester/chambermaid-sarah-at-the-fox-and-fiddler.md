#title
Chambermaid Sarah at The Fox and Fiddler
#summary
A short, blonde chambermaid named Sarah allegedly walled up by Matthew Hopkins haunts The Fox and Fiddler.
#details
A chambermaid named Sarah is said to have been walled up inside The Fox and Fiddler by the Witchfinder General, Matthew Hopkins. Described as short, blonde, and wearing an old chambermaid costume, she is considered a good soul with a good sense of humor.

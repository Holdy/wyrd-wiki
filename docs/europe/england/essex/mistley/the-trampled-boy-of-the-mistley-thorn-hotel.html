The Trampled Boy of The Mistley Thorn Hotel

Summary

The ghost of a young boy who was thrown under a moving cart and trampled haunts The Mistley Thorn Hotel.

Details

Another spirit reported at The Mistley Thorn Hotel is that of a young boy who tragically met his end after being thrown under a moving cart and trampled.

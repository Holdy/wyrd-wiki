Matthew Hopkins at The Mistley Thorn Hotel

Summary

Witchfinder General Matthew Hopkins, who died in 1647, is one of the resident spirits at The Mistley Thorn Hotel.

Details

Matthew Hopkins, better known as the Witchfinder General who died of tuberculosis in 1647, is said to be one of the ghosts haunting The Mistley Thorn Hotel.

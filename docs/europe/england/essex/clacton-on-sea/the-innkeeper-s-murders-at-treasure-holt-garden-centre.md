#title
The Innkeeper's Murders at Treasure Holt Garden Centre
#summary
Smugglers and murderers used Treasure Holt Garden Centre as a hide-out, where an innkeeper allegedly dumped victims' bodies into a well.
#details
Treasure Holt Garden Centre was once used as a hide-out by smugglers and murderers. Legend states that an innkeeper at the time used to murder many people and then dump their bodies through a trapdoor into a well beneath the building.
#location
Treasure Holt Garden Centre, Clacton-on-Sea, Essex, England

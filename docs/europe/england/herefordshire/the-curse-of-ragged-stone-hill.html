The Curse of Ragged Stone Hill

Summary

An embittered monk cast a lasting curse upon Ragged Stone Hill, dooming anyone touched by its shadow to an untimely end. Over the generations, this dread fate became linked to the male heirs of a nearby manor and high-profile figures connected to the estate.

Details

According to local legend, an aggrieved 15th-century monk from Little Malvern Priory was ordered off common land on Raggedstone Hill by a local landowner expanding his estate. Furious at the altercation, the monk pronounced a curse from the peak, declaring that anyone upon whom the shadow of the hill fell would die before their time. Over subsequent centuries, unexpected tragedies, premature deaths among family heirs, and the downfalls of prominent historical figures associated with the nearby Birtsmorton Court—such as Cardinal Thomas Wolsey and the statesman William Huskisson—became superstitiously attributed to the lingering shadow of the curse.

The Vengeful Hound of Warwick Castle

Summary

An elderly woman placed a curse upon Warwick Castle after her trading privileges were revoked, transforming into a ferocious black dog that haunted the grounds until exorcised into a subterranean river chamber.

Details

An elderly woman named Moll Bloxham, rumored to dabble in witchcraft, lived in a cottage near the walls of Warwick Castle and was permitted by the Earl of Warwick to sell surplus dairy produce from the castle kitchens for her own profit. She abused this privilege by shortchanging customers, leading the local townspeople to protest to the Earl, who promptly withdrew her concession. Moll vanished and was never seen alive again, but shortly after her disappearance, a massive, vicious black dog began terrorizing the castle occupants. Believing the creature to be the vengeful spirit of Moll Bloxham, three priests were eventually called in to perform an exorcism, driving the hound to the top of Caesar’s Tower. From there, the beast leapt into the River Avon below, where it became permanently trapped within a chamber built beneath a fletcher dam.

#title
The Ghostly Children of Stocksbridge Bypass

#summary
The Stocksbridge Bypass in West Yorkshire is renowned for its paranormal activities, including sightings of ghostly children and a medieval monk.

#details
In 1988, the construction of the Stocksbridge Bypass began to connect the M1 with the Woodhead Pass and the A616. During this time, security guards reported seeing young children playing in the middle of the construction site. As they approached, the children vanished without a trace.

Over the years, motorists have claimed to see ghostly apparitions appearing in their cars while driving on what is known as "Killer Road" due to its high number of accidents and fatalities. The road has also been associated with sightings of a medieval monk and other unexplained phenomena.

#location
The Stocksbridge Bypass, which connects the M1 with the Woodhead Pass and the A616 in West Yorkshire, is a key location for these ghostly encounters.

    Ripley's skull

    Summary

    A local girl's skull was displayed at Ripley's Odditorium in Blackpool, and several people reported a female ghost standing beside it. Its former owner believed it was cursed and died a week after giving it away.

    Details

    In the 1970s, the skull of a local girl was put on display at Ripley's Odditorium in Blackpool. Several people reported seeing the ghost of a woman beside it. The skull's previous owner was convinced it carried a curse, and died just a week after parting with it.

The Demonic Possession and Healing of Cristina at Vadstena Abbey

Summary

A newlywed peasant woman named Cristina was tormented and physically possessed by a demon that paralyzed her limbs and blinded her until she was cured at Vadstena Abbey.

Details

Cristina, the daughter of a wealthy peasant from Bro in the Linköping diocese, suffered from demonic molestations throughout her youth. Shortly after her wedding, the demonic attacks escalated violently: she fell to the ground stiff, with her limbs rigidly locked together, lost her sight and speech, and was overcome by a horrid stench. When her husband and companions transported her by cart toward Vadstena Abbey in January 1376, the demon pursued them along the journey, even tripping travelers on the frozen ice. Upon arriving at Vadstena Abbey, the demon fiercely resisted her entry, making her body increasingly heavy and attempting to drag her out of the chapel. After clergy gathered to sing hymns and placed Saint Birgitta's head relic and a sacred silver cross upon her, Cristina gradually regained her speech, smell, and sight, ultimately being freed from the possession.

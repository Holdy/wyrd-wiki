The Cursed Stone of Florencecourt

Summary

An ancient standing stone in Florencecourt is bound by a powerful curse that brings ongoing misfortune to anyone who dares to touch or move it.

Details

Marking an ancient druidic site protected by primeval spells, a standing stone in Florencecourt holds a dark reputation among local residents. According to traditional folklore, anyone who foolishly interferes with the stone—whether by touching or attempting to move it—incurs the wrath of the ancient curse, leading to persistent misfortune and bad luck.

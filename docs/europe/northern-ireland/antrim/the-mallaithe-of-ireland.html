The Mallaithe of Ireland

Summary

The Mallaithe are cursed places in Ireland marked by an elder tree, a lonesome corncrake, and thickets of nettles. These locations were avoided due to their association with dark forces and misfortune.

Details

The Mallaithe are cursed places in Ireland that were known for their eerie signs: an elder tree, the sound of a lone corncrake, and thickets of nettles. These places were believed to be inhabited by dark, nameless entities and were avoided by locals. The curses associated with these sites could bring misfortune to anyone who disturbed them, such as cutting down a hawthorn fairy tree or building near an ancient cairn.

One notable example is the old Hellfire Club in County Antrim, built on the site of an ancient cairn that was razed by William Connolly. After its construction, the club became infamous for uncanny visitors and evil doings until it burned down and was abandoned.

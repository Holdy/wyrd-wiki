The Grey Lady of the Dark Hedges

Summary

A spectral figure known as the Grey Lady is said to glide along the shadowy path of the Dark Hedges, vanishing as the lane concludes.

Details

The Dark Hedges, a historic beech tree avenue planted in the 1700s to frame Gracehill House, has long been associated with a ghostly presence. Locals report seeing a woman in grey robes walking the narrow road, her figure dissolving into mist when she reaches the end of the tree tunnel.

The Bloodsucking Chieftain of Slaghtaverty

Summary

A tyrannical 5th-century warlord terrorized his subjects with bloodlust, returning repeatedly from the grave after execution until vanquished by magical means. Buried upside down beneath a heavy stone cairn, his resting place remains marked to this day.

Details

Abhartach was a brutal chieftain who ruled a small kingdom in what is now County Londonderry. Feared by his people for his dark powers and cruelty, he was eventually overthrown and slain by a neighboring chieftain named Cathain. However, death could not hold him; Abhartach rose from his grave demanding a bowl of blood from his terrified subjects. Though Cathain killed him a second time, the tyrant returned once more. Seeking guidance, Cathain learned from a holy figure that Abhartach was an undead creature who could only be permanently subdued by a sword made of yew wood and burial upside down underneath a massive stone. Cathain carried out these instructions, trapping the tyrant beneath the earth at Slaghtaverty.

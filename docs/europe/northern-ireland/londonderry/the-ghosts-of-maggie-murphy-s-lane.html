The Ghosts of Maggie Murphy's Lane

Summary

Maggie Murphy, her lover, and her enraged father are said to haunt Maggie Murphy's Lane following their tragic deaths in the 1880s. Witnesses report spectral figures and chilling phenomena along the road.

Details

According to the Derry Journal, Maggie Murphy and her lover—a farmhand from an unsuitable background—died under mysterious circumstances in the 1880s, alongside Maggie's father. Their ghosts are believed to linger on Maggie Murphy's Lane, which runs from Kilfennan to Nelson Drive. Locals claim to hear wailing and see shadowy figures, prompting many to cross the street rather than walk past the lane. The haunting was documented in Peter McCartney's Parade of Phantoms.

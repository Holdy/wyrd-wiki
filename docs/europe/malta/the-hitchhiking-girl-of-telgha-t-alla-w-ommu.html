The Hitchhiking Girl of Telgha t'Alla w Ommu

Summary

A teenage girl's ghost haunts a steep, isolated Maltese hill road, frantically flagging down passing drivers before mysteriously vanishing or reappearing inside their cars.

Details

Driving along the quiet and virtually uninhabited hill road known as Telgha t'Alla w Ommu, which leads from Salina to Naxxar, motorists have frequently encountered a teenage girl stumbling along the roadside pleading for assistance. Drivers who pull over to help her find that she vanishes into thin air the moment they approach. Meanwhile, those who ignore her and drive past receive a chilling shock when they glance in their rear-view mirrors and discover her sitting silently in the back seat, staring intensely at them. According to local lore, her spirit began haunting the hilltop after she was tragically struck in a hit-and-run accident.

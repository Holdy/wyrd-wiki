#title
The Vengeance of the Skull

#summary
A cursed skull housed in a historic Flintshire hall must remain in its honoured place or else unleash restless torment and disaster upon the household. When a maid ignorantly casts the skull into a horsepond, immediate ghostly terror ensues until the relic is safely retrieved.

#details
At Ffagnalt Hall, a 15th-century manor on Halkyn Mountain, a human skull is kept safely away in a bookcase under strict instruction that it must never be moved or cleaned. According to local lore originating from the dying curse of a poisoned prince, the skull’s continued presence in the house ensures peace, whereas its removal invites supernatural vengeance and haunting. A maid named Anne, annoyed by the dust accumulating around the relic and believing it to be the work of a malicious sprite, threw the skull into a nearby horsepond to hide her deed. That very evening, a blood-curdling shriek awakened the household, and the maid was found terrified in the horsepond clutching the relic and crying out that the dead man's curse had found her. The skull was promptly returned to its proper place, restoring calm to the estate.

#location
Ffagnalt Hall is located at Rhes-y-cae on Halkyn Mountain in Flintshire, near the boundary between the ancient parishes of Halkyn and Cilcain.

The Phantom Paymaster of Halkyn Mountain

Summary

Motorists travelling along the mountain road frequently encountered a mysterious man carrying a bag under his arm who vanished into thin air when offered a lift. Local legend suggests this apparition is the ghost of a 19th-century worker murdered for the mining wages he carried.

Details

During the early 1970s, numerous motorists and commuters travelling along the road connecting Pentre Halkyn and Milwr on Halkyn Mountain reported sightings of a strange figure in the early morning hours. Witnesses described seeing a misty, bluish-grey apparition zigzagging up the roadway, while another commuter named Mr Rees recounted an encounter where he and his companion stopped on a nasty morning to pick up a man wearing a short coat and carrying a bag under his arm, only for him to vanish completely right before them. According to local folklore, the phantom is believed to be the spirit of a 19th-century paymaster who was murdered for the lead miners' wages he was transporting across the mountain.

#title
The Ghostly Figure on Cadhole Road

#summary
In 1972, a couple driving through Denbighshire encountered a ghostly figure on fire on Cadhole Road.

#details
Late one night in 1972, a couple was driving home from Loggerheads to Buckley when they pulled over on Cadhole Road on the outskirts of Gwernaffield. They witnessed a ghostly figure engulfed in flames standing by the roadside. The sight was so terrifying that they reported it to the authorities. The next morning, police found no sign of the woman or any evidence of burning, but they did recover a handbag containing £90, which they returned to its owner.

The locals identified the ghost as Deborah, a "white witch" from the 6th century who had tried to save her village from cholera. According to legend, Deborah led the villagers to higher ground to escape the disease, but when cholera eventually reached them, she was blamed and accused of being a "black witch." The villagers burned down the hospital where she cared for the sick, killing everyone inside, including Deborah.

#location

The Apparitions of the North Promenade

Summary

Hotel guests looking out over the seafront in Aberystwyth witnessed the ghostly figures of a young couple floating above the promenade before vanishing into the sea. The spectral pair were later identified as lovers who had tragically drowned after becoming trapped by the tide.

Details

On July 23rd, 1971, Phil Bishop and his wife were staying at a hotel overlooking Cardigan Bay in Aberystwyth. Unable to sleep, Mrs. Bishop looked out the window onto the North Promenade and spotted a young woman leaning over the sea railings. Moments later, a young man ran toward her, and the couple—floating a foot above the ground—embraced in silence. They then raced hand-in-hand up the promenade, descended onto the beach, and walked straight into the high tide until disappearing completely beneath the water. The following morning, the hotel owner informed the couple that a pair of youths had tragically perished in the 1960s when trapped by the tide near the base of Constitution Hill, perfectly matching the phantom couple's appearance and final path.

    St Teilo's skull

    Summary

    A farmhouse beside St Teilo's holy well at Llandeilo kept the brainpan of a skull that before the Reformation was shown as the saint's own. Patients drank the well water from it until recently and many cures were recorded, though the skull probably belonged to a young woman.

    Details

    At Llandeilo, under the Preseli Hills, is a holy well of St Teilo. In the farmhouse beside it, the tenant, Mr Melchior, kept as hereditary guardian the brainpan of the skull that had been shown and used before the Reformation as the saint's. Patients drank water from the well out of the skull until quite recently, and many cures were recorded. Its genuineness is doubtful: the open sutures show it was a young person's head and the brow ridge suggests a young woman's, while Teilo died at an advanced age.

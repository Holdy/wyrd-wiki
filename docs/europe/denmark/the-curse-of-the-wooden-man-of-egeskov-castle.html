The Curse of the Wooden Man of Egeskov Castle

Summary

Generations of owners have believed that moving a mysterious wooden doll resting in the attic rafters of a historic Danish castle will cause the entire building to sink into its moat on Christmas Eve. To keep the impending doom at bay, the family appeases the figure each year with a traditional bowl of rice pudding.

Details

Hidden among the dusty rafters beneath the spire of Egeskov Castle on the island of Funen sits an ancient, child-sized wooden doll resting on an old pillow. According to longstanding legend, if the doll is ever moved from its designated spot, the magnificent Renaissance castle will plunge straight down into its surrounding moat on Christmas Eve. Fearing the fulfillment of this destructive curse, earlier generations of residents famously refused to stay at the manor during the Christmas holidays. Modern occupants have mitigated the risk by adopting a holiday ritual traditionally reserved for household elves, leaving out a bowl of sweet rice pudding to keep the wooden figure content and the castle safely above water.

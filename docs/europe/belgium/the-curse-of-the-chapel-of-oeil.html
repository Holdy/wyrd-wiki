The Curse of the Chapel of Oeil

Summary

A local man discovers witches conducting rituals inside the isolated Chapel of Oeil, leading to an exorcism that results in a vengeful curse cast upon any dwelling built from the sacred chapel's salvaged stones.

Details

In the hamlet of Oeil, near Tintange in the Belgian Ardennes, an old mill and a small chapel stood by the Sûre river. On full moon nights, local miller's hand Gaspard witnessed sinister lights and heard strange incantations coming from within the chapel, discovering that a coven of local witches (macrales) had been using the holy site to perform dark rituals and cast hexes upon travelers and livestock. Terrified, Gaspard alerted the village clergy. A group of priests converged on the chapel and successfully expelled the witches through a powerful exorcism involving holy water and intense prayer. As they fled in fury, the elder witch pronounced a bitter curse over the sacred ground and its materials, declaring that any structure built using stones taken from the chapel would forever be haunted by restlessness, strange sounds, and misfortune. True to her word, a house later built partly from the chapel's salvaged stones fell permanently under this lingering legacy of bad luck.

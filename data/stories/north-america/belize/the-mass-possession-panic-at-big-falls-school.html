The Mass Possession Panic at Big Falls School

Summary

In May 2007, a wave of violent and inexplicable possession episodes struck students and teachers at a village primary school in southern Belize, forcing its closure and specialized spiritual intervention.

Details

Beginning in early May 2007 at the Big Falls R.C. Primary School in the Toledo District of Belize, 15 students and two adults suffered from sudden, inexplicable trances, severe headaches, and violent outbursts attributed by villagers to demonic possession. The first incident involved a 9-year-old girl who exhibited unnatural physical strength and attempted to drag a classmate toward a nearby river, requiring ten boys to restrain her. As more children and eventually a teacher were afflicted—experiencing screaming, choking sensations, and loss of control—the school was forced to shut down. Ten of the affected children were subsequently flown to a hospital in Rio Dulce, Guatemala, to receive prayer-based healing and medical treatment after local doctors could find no physical explanation for their condition.

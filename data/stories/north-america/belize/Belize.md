#title
The Curse of the Stone Woman

#summary
A ghostly Mayan princess doomed to relive her sacrifice haunts a major temple pyramid, casting an eerie presence over the ancient city after its sanctity was disturbed. Her recurrent appearances span generations following the site's modern rediscovery.

#details
According to local Belizean folklore, the ancient Maya city of Xunantunich is haunted by the spirit of a noble maiden. Legend tells that she was a princess sacrificed on the steps of the grand temple pyramid known as El Castillo to appease the gods. When European explorers—notably archaeologist Thomas Gann—rediscovered the ruins in the late 19th century and disturbed its sacred stones using dynamite, the spectral maiden began manifesting repeatedly across generations. She is seen ascending the stone stairs dressed entirely in white before vanishing directly into a stone wall, often turning back to reveal striking, fire-red glowing eyes that leave witnesses in shock.

#location
El Castillo pyramid at the Xunantunich Archaeological Site located on a ridge above the Mopan River in the Cayo District of western Belize.

#title
The Mass Possession Panic at Big Falls School

#summary
In May 2007, a wave of violent and inexplicable possession episodes struck students and teachers at a village primary school in southern Belize, forcing its closure and specialized spiritual intervention.

#details
Beginning in early May 2007 at the Big Falls R.C. Primary School in the Toledo District of Belize, 15 students and two adults suffered from sudden, inexplicable trances, severe headaches, and violent outbursts attributed by villagers to demonic possession. The first incident involved a 9-year-old girl who exhibited unnatural physical strength and attempted to drag a classmate toward a nearby river, requiring ten boys to restrain her. As more children and eventually a teacher were afflicted—experiencing screaming, choking sensations, and loss of control—the school was forced to shut down. Ten of the affected children were subsequently flown to a hospital in Rio Dulce, Guatemala, to receive prayer-based healing and medical treatment after local doctors could find no physical explanation for their condition.

#location
The events centered around the Big Falls R.C. Primary School located in the village of Big Falls within the Toledo District of Belize.

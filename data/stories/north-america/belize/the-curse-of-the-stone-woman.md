#title
The Curse of the Stone Woman

#summary
A ghostly Mayan princess doomed to relive her sacrifice haunts a major temple pyramid, casting an eerie presence over the ancient city after its sanctity was disturbed. Her recurrent appearances span generations following the site's modern rediscovery.

#details
According to local Belizean folklore, the ancient Maya city of Xunantunich is haunted by the spirit of a noble maiden. Legend tells that she was a princess sacrificed on the steps of the grand temple pyramid known as El Castillo to appease the gods. When European explorers—notably archaeologist Thomas Gann—rediscovered the ruins in the late 19th century and disturbed its sacred stones using dynamite, the spectral maiden began manifesting repeatedly across generations. She is seen ascending the stone stairs dressed entirely in white before vanishing directly into a stone wall, often turning back to reveal striking, fire-red glowing eyes that leave witnesses in shock.

#location
El Castillo pyramid at the Xunantunich Archaeological Site located on a ridge above the Mopan River in the Cayo District of western Belize.

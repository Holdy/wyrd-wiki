#title
Ghosts of Ringwood Manor

#summary
Ringwood Manor, a historic 18th-century estate in New Jersey, is haunted by multiple spirits, including a murdered housemaid and Revolutionary War figures.

#details
Built in the 1700s and expanded in 1807, Ringwood Manor was home to General Robert Erskine, George Washington's Surveyor-General during the Revolutionary War. Local folklore holds that the estate and its grounds are haunted by several ghosts after dark. In the Manor House, visitors and staff have reported hearing footsteps, heavy objects dropping, and soft crying emanating from a second-floor bedroom where a housemaid was allegedly beaten to death. Behind the manor pond, the ghost of General Erskine can be seen at dusk sitting on his grave and gazing across the water. Furthermore, an unmarked grave of French soldiers who fought with Rochambeau lies nearby, and their spirits are said to walk the shores of the pond at night while speaking in soft French voices.

#location
Ringwood Manor is situated within Ringwood State Park in the borough of Ringwood, Passaic County, New Jersey.

Spirits of the Old Idaho State Penitentiary

Summary

Built by the very stone its inmates carved from Table Rock, this historic 19th-century fortress in Boise is said to be haunted by former guards, desperate escapees, and executed criminals.

Details

Opening in 1872 as the Territorial Prison, the Old Idaho State Penitentiary operated for over a century before closing its doors in 1973. Throughout its history, it housed more than 13,000 inmates, witnessed violent riots, and carried out ten executions on its gallows, including high-profile felons. Since its conversion into a museum, visitors and staff have frequently reported heavy feelings of oppression, shadowy figures lurking in the isolation cells known as "The Dungeon" and "Siberia," and disembodied voices whispering from empty cell blocks.

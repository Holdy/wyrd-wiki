#title
The Haunting of the Historic Idanha Hotel

#summary
Constructed in downtown Boise in 1901, the historic Idanha Hotel is plagued by reports of phantom elevator movements, ghostly bells, and an aggressive entity on its upper floors.

#details
Built on the corner of 10th and Main Street in downtown Boise, the Idanha Hotel opened in 1901 as a premier social center before later being converted into apartments. Over the decades, residents, staff, and visitors have reported numerous unexplained phenomena throughout the building. Legend holds that a bellman who was gunned down on the premises continues to ride the elevator up and down without passengers, while a female spirit allegedly murdered and buried in the basement lingers in the lower levels. Additionally, occupants of the upper floors have frequently reported heavy negative presences, objects moving on their own, and strange disturbances, cementing the building's reputation as one of Idaho's most enduring historic legends.

#location
The Idanha Hotel is located at the corner of 10th and Main Street in downtown Boise, Idaho.

Shadows Within the Old Idaho Penitentiary

Summary

Operating for over a century in Boise, the historic Old Idaho Penitentiary is widely reported to be haunted by the spirits of former inmates and executed prisoners.

Details

Established in 1870 and operating as a territorial and state prison until 1973, the Old Idaho Penitentiary at the base of the Boise Foothills witnessed numerous violent crimes, riots, escapes, and ten executions. Visitors and paranormal investigators frequently report eerie experiences, particularly in "Siberia" (the solitary confinement cells) and the Gallows Room inside the Maximum Security cell house—where Raymond Snowden was hanged in 1957. People walking through the decaying cell blocks have reported hearing disembodied voices, feeling sudden physical touches, and sensing an overwhelming feeling of heaviness and dread.

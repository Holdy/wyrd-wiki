#title
Shadows Within the Old Idaho Penitentiary

#summary
Operating for over a century in Boise, the historic Old Idaho Penitentiary is widely reported to be haunted by the spirits of former inmates and executed prisoners.

#details
Established in 1870 and operating as a territorial and state prison until 1973, the Old Idaho Penitentiary at the base of the Boise Foothills witnessed numerous violent crimes, riots, escapes, and ten executions. Visitors and paranormal investigators frequently report eerie experiences, particularly in "Siberia" (the solitary confinement cells) and the Gallows Room inside the Maximum Security cell house—where Raymond Snowden was hanged in 1957. People walking through the decaying cell blocks have reported hearing disembodied voices, feeling sudden physical touches, and sensing an overwhelming feeling of heaviness and dread.

#location
The Old Idaho Penitentiary located at 2445 Old Penitentiary Road in Boise, Idaho.

#title
Spirits of the Old Idaho State Penitentiary

#summary
Built by the very stone its inmates carved from Table Rock, this historic 19th-century fortress in Boise is said to be haunted by former guards, desperate escapees, and executed criminals.

#details
Opening in 1872 as the Territorial Prison, the Old Idaho State Penitentiary operated for over a century before closing its doors in 1973. Throughout its history, it housed more than 13,000 inmates, witnessed violent riots, and carried out ten executions on its gallows, including high-profile felons. Since its conversion into a museum, visitors and staff have frequently reported heavy feelings of oppression, shadowy figures lurking in the isolation cells known as "The Dungeon" and "Siberia," and disembodied voices whispering from empty cell blocks.

#location
The Old Idaho State Penitentiary is located at the base of Table Rock at 2441 Old Penitentiary Road in Boise, Idaho.

#title
The Cursed Egyptian Statuettes of Idaho Falls

#summary
Two ancient Egyptian bronze statuettes housed at a museum brought decades of financial ruin, accidents, and persistent bad luck to everyone who possessed them. Even after arriving in Idaho, the relics continued to disrupt lives, notably causing a visitor's nearly finalized home loan to suddenly collapse.

#details
In the aftermath of Tutankhamun's tomb discovery, an American investor received two ancient bronze Egyptian statuettes as a parting gift, marking the beginning of a multi-generational curse. The original owner lost his vast fortune, endured multiple failed marriages, and died in debt, while his grandson—a decorated Olympic champion turned businessman—subsequently suffered severe athletic injuries and ruined business ventures. In 2012, the grandson donated the statuettes to the Museum of Idaho in Idaho Falls to be rid of the hex. Although housed away in museum archives, the artifacts' baleful reputation persists locally, as demonstrated when a visiting media employee's pending home loan mysteriously fell through shortly after he was shown the relics.

#location
The Museum of Idaho is located at 200 North Eastern Avenue in Idaho Falls, Idaho.

#title
The Frozen Legend of Princess Edahow in the Shoshone Ice Caves

#summary
A natural subterranean ice tube in southern Idaho is said to be haunted by the whispering spirit of a Shoshone princess buried within its ancient ice walls.

#details
Discovered by accident in the 1880s by a young boy searching for a lost goat, the Shoshone Ice Caves are a 1,000-foot-long lava tube maintaining freezing temperatures year-round. According to local legend, centuries ago a beloved Shoshone princess named Edahow died, and her tribe buried her remains inside the sacred ice of the cave so she would rest forever. Visitors and staff exploring the subterranean walkways have reported hearing unexplained voices whispering from the dark recesses, shadowy figures moving behind walls of ice, and heavy footsteps echoing when no one else is around.

#location
The Shoshone Ice Caves are located at 1561 State Highway 75 near Shoshone, Idaho.

#title
The Ghost of Ridenbaugh Hall

#summary
Built in 1902 as a girls' dormitory on the University of Idaho campus, Ridenbaugh Hall is said to be haunted by a female student who tragically took her own life.

#details
Originally constructed in 1902 as a women's dormitory on the University of Idaho campus in Moscow, Ridenbaugh Hall now houses music practice rooms and art galleries. Legend maintains that room 225 is haunted by the spirit of a young woman who allegedly hung herself there. Over the decades, students and staff have reported unexplained temperature drops, phantom music and singing when the building is entirely empty, and shadowy figures roaming the halls. In 1992, renowned paranormal investigators Ed and Lorraine Warren visited the building during a campus tour, with Lorraine reporting the distinct essence of a young female entity dressed in 1930s fashion with her hair pulled back in a bun.

#location
Ridenbaugh Hall on the University of Idaho campus in Moscow, Idaho.

#title
The Haunting of the Historic Idanha Hotel

#summary
Constructed in downtown Boise in 1901, the historic Idanha Hotel is plagued by reports of phantom elevator movements, ghostly bells, and an aggressive entity on its upper floors.

#details
Built on the corner of 10th and Main Street in downtown Boise, the Idanha Hotel opened in 1901 as a premier social center before later being converted into apartments. Over the decades, residents, staff, and visitors have reported numerous unexplained phenomena throughout the building. Legend holds that a bellman who was gunned down on the premises continues to ride the elevator up and down without passengers, while a female spirit allegedly murdered and buried in the basement lingers in the lower levels. Additionally, occupants of the upper floors have frequently reported heavy negative presences, objects moving on their own, and strange disturbances, cementing the building's reputation as one of Idaho's most enduring historic legends.

#location
The Idanha Hotel is located at the corner of 10th and Main Street in downtown Boise, Idaho.

#title
The Haunting of the Idaho Hotel

#summary
Ghostly legends and disembodied screams haunt this historic 19th-century hotel in the remote Owyhee Mountain ghost town of Silver City.

#details
Established during the gold and silver rushes of the 1860s, the Idaho Hotel in Silver City has borne witness to frontier violence, including the fatal 1868 shoot-out between J. Marion Moore and Samuel Lockhart right outside its front doors. Decades later, guests and staff have reported numerous eerie paranormal phenomena throughout the creaking wooden structure. Most famously, a spectral presence known as "Screaming Alice"—said to be a woman who tragically fell to her death from the third floor—is frequently heard screaming in the dead of night, while disembodied footsteps and unexplained cold drafts echo through the long-closed corridors.

#location
The Idaho Hotel stands on Main Street in the historic ghost town of Silver City in the Owyhee Mountains of southwestern Idaho.

#title
The Legend of Princess Edahow in the Shoshone Ice Caves

#summary
Visitors and staff at the Shoshone Ice Caves in southern Idaho have long reported hearing mysterious voices and heavy footsteps echoing through the subterranean lava tube, which local legend attributes to Princess Edahow, whose remains were supposedly buried within the cave's ancient ice.

#details
Formed thousands of years ago within a volcanic lava field in southern Idaho, the Shoshone Ice Caves maintain a freezing year-round temperature that preserves solid ice floors deep underground. According to local folklore, centuries before the caves were rediscovered in the 1880s by a young boy searching for a lost goat, a revered Shoshone princess named Edahow died and was laid to rest within the cave's massive ice formation. Legend holds that her spirit remains trapped in her icy coffin, whispering to visitors and occasionally manifesting as shadow figures behind the ice walls in hopes of being set free.

#location
The Shoshone Ice Caves are located off State Highway 75 near Shoshone, Idaho.

The Phantom Jogger of Canyon Hill

#summary
A legless female apparition haunts the perimeter of an Idaho cemetery, appearing after dark to jog alongside vehicles and tap on car windows.

#details
Local lore in Caldwell, Idaho, tells of a woman who met a tragic end after being struck by a vehicle while jogging near Canyon Hill Cemetery. Her restless spirit now traverses the grounds and roadway as a legless specter. Drivers who park between two specific trees facing the cemetery at night frequently report sudden drops in temperature, an oppressive sense of surveillance, and the chilling experience of the phantom runner tapping directly on their car windows before fading into the darkness.

#title
The Resilient Spirits of the Idaho Hotel

#summary
Nestled in the historic ghost town of Silver City, the 19th-century Idaho Hotel is said to host lingering spirits from the Owyhee County mining boom.

#details
Originally established in 1863 in the mining camp of Ruby City, the hotel's three-story wing was physically dismantled, loaded onto sleds pulled by oxen, and hauled across the snowy Owyhee Mountains in 1866 to its new home in Silver City. After operating through decades of local booms, brawls, and celebrations, the hotel closed in 1942 before being lovingly restored starting in 1972. Guests and visitors staying in the historic building have reported uncanny phenomena, including the sound of children playing in empty hallways, phantom footsteps creaking across old floorboards, and the persistent presence of 19th-century travelers who never checked out.

#location
The Idaho Hotel located in the historic ghost town of Silver City in Owyhee County, Idaho.

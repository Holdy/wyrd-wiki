#title
The Cursed Egyptian Statuettes of Idaho Falls

#summary
Two ancient Egyptian bronze statuettes housed at a museum brought decades of financial ruin, accidents, and persistent bad luck to everyone who possessed them. Even after arriving in Idaho, the relics continued to disrupt lives, notably causing a visitor's nearly finalized home loan to suddenly collapse.

#details
In the aftermath of Tutankhamun's tomb discovery, an American investor received two ancient bronze Egyptian statuettes as a parting gift, marking the beginning of a multi-generational curse. The original owner lost his vast fortune, endured multiple failed marriages, and died in debt, while his grandson—a decorated Olympic champion turned businessman—subsequently suffered severe athletic injuries and ruined business ventures. In 2012, the grandson donated the statuettes to the Museum of Idaho in Idaho Falls to be rid of the hex. Although housed away in museum archives, the artifacts' baleful reputation persists locally, as demonstrated when a visiting media employee's pending home loan mysteriously fell through shortly after he was shown the relics.

#location
The Museum of Idaho is located at 200 North Eastern Avenue in Idaho Falls, Idaho.

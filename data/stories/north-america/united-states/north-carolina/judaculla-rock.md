#title
Judaculla Rock

#summary
A soapstone boulder covered in about 1,500 prehistoric carvings that nobody has deciphered. Cherokee legend says the giant Tsul'kalu (Judaculla) left the marks, often told as a seven-fingered handprint, when he leapt onto the rock. Cherokee gatherings were held here into the 1880s, and a TV show once floated a disputed Ogham reading.

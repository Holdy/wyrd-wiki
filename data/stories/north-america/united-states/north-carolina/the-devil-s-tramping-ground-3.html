The Devil's Tramping Ground

Summary

In the pine forests of Chatham County lies a perfectly round, barren circle where vegetation refuses to grow and the Devil is said to pace each night, tossing out any objects left within its perimeter by dawn.

Details

For generations, locals near Harper's Crossroads have whispered about a mysterious forty-foot circle where no grass, weeds, or trees will ever sprout. According to local folklore, the barren earth is scorched by the cloven hooves of the Devil himself, who spends his nights pacing around the perimeter plotting the damnation of human souls. Dogs whimper and refuse to enter the clearing, and men who attempted overnight vigils allegedly fled or lost their sanity from whatever terror they witnessed. Though modern visitors often leave behind litter rather than encountering demons, the legend remains a deeply embedded staple of North Carolina folklore.

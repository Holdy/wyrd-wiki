#title
Indian Path through Swannanoa Gap

#summary
State marker N32 says the gap was 'used by Indians and pioneers in crossing Blue Ridge'. Local tradition traces the path west past Biltmore toward Canton, with a stretch on Sand Hill Road once worn shoulder-deep.

#location
Swannanoa Gap, Buncombe/McDowell line

#proposed-coordinates
35.621, -82.273

#reference
https://readtheplaque.com/plaque/swannanoa-gap

#title
Swannanoa Tunnel

#summary
An 1,800 ft railway tunnel finished in 1879, mostly by imprisoned Black convict labourers; the marker says 125 died, NCpedia at least 300. Their hammer song 'Swannanoa Tunnel' survives.

#location
Ridgecrest, Buncombe County

#proposed-coordinates
35.621, -82.29

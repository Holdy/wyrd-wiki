#title
Brown Mountain Lights

#summary
Lights seen over the ridge since at least 1833, usually white and sometimes red or blue, rising and fading on clear evenings. No explanation satisfies everyone.

#location
Brown Mountain, Burke/Caldwell line (viewed from Lost Cove Overlook, BRP MP 310, and Wiseman's View)

#proposed-coordinates
35.905, -81.79

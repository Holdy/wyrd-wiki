#title
Hermit of Gingercake Mountain

#summary
A hermit called Watson lived at the foot of the mountain and gave it its name; he died in 1816 and was written up by the traveller Charles Lanman in 1848.

#location
Gingercake Mountain, Linville Gorge, Burke County

Chimney Rock spectral battle (1811) and shining crowd (1806)

Summary

In 1806 the Reaves family watched a crowd of white-robed figures rise and gather on top of Chimney Rock. In 1811 an elderly couple saw armies on winged horses fighting in the sky and heard steel on steel.

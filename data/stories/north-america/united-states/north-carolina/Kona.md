#title
Charlie Silver's three graves

#summary
Frankie Silver killed and dismembered her husband in 1831. His remains were found at different times, so three stones mark his graves. She was hanged in 1833, and a ballad legend says she sang her own song on the scaffold.

#location
Kona Baptist Church cemetery, Mitchell County

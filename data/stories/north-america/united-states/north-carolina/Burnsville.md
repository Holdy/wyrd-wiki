#title
Elisha Mitchell's summit grave

#summary
The professor died in 1857 proving this was the highest peak in the east, and was dug up in Asheville and reburied on the summit. His obelisk blew down in 1915; the cairn that replaced it has a plaque that misspells 'University'.

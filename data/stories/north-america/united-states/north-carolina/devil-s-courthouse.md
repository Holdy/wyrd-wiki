#title
Devil's Courthouse

#summary
A bare rock summit named for the Devil, where Cherokee lore puts the cave of the giant Judaculla. There is an unconfirmed tale of executions on the rock.

#location
Blue Ridge Parkway MP 422.4, Transylvania County

#title
Indian Path through Swannanoa Gap

#summary
State marker N32 says the gap was 'used by Indians and pioneers in crossing Blue Ridge'. Local tradition traces the path west past Biltmore toward Canton, with a stretch on Sand Hill Road once worn shoulder-deep.

#location
Swannanoa Gap, Buncombe/McDowell line

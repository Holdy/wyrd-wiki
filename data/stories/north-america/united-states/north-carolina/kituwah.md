#title
Kituwah

#summary
The Cherokee 'mother town', where a sacred fire burned in townhouses on a platform mound built about 1000 CE. The British burned the town in 1761 and the Eastern Band bought the land back in 1996, keeping it undeveloped as sacred ground.

#location
Tuckasegee River near Bryson City, Swain County

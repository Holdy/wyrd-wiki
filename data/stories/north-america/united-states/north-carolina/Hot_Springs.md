#title
Paint Rock pictographs

#summary
Red and yellow lines painted on a cliff, thought to be about 5,000 years old. A 1799 visitor also saw red figures of people and animals. These are painted (pictographs), not carved.

#location
Paint Rock, French Broad River near Hot Springs, Madison County (NC-TN line)

#proposed-coordinates
35.92, -82.89

#reference
https://www.dncr.nc.gov/blog/2017/09/22/paint-rock-cherokee-pictograph-madison-county

#title
Broughton Hospital (Western Carolina Insane Asylum)

#summary
A Kirkbride asylum opened in 1883 that held over 3,500 patients and has a cemetery of 1,583 burials. It is still in use, and sits right on the 50-mile line.

#location
Morganton, Burke County

#title
Brown Mountain Lights

#summary
Lights seen over the ridge since at least 1833, usually white and sometimes red or blue, rising and fading on clear evenings. No explanation satisfies everyone.

#title
Joara and Fort San Juan (Berry Site)

#summary
The largest Mississippian town in North Carolina, visited by de Soto in 1540. In 1567 the Spanish built Fort San Juan here, the first European settlement in NC; within about 18 months the locals killed the garrison and burned it. Archaeologists found the burned fort in 2013.

#location
NW of Morganton, Burke County

#proposed-coordinates
35.8242, -81.7336

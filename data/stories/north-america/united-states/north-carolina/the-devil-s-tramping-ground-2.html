The Devil's Tramping Ground

Summary

A mysterious, perfectly round forty-foot barren circle in the pine woods of Chatham County is said to be the spot where Satan paces every night. Any objects placed within the ring overnight are invariably found thrown outside by morning, and no vegetation will grow on the path.

Details

Located in the remote pine forests of western Chatham County, the Devil's Tramping Ground is a foot-wide bare path forming a forty-foot circle where normal plant life refuses to grow and wiry grass resists all attempts at transplantation. According to regional folklore, Satan frequents this desolate spot on his nightly walks, pacing the circle as he ponders evil deeds. Generations of curious visitors have tested the legend by placing logs, camping gear, and heavy obstacles inside the circle at dusk, only to return at dawn and find every item tossed completely outside the ring. Alternative legends attribute the sacred or cursed barren ground to ancient Native American tribal gatherings or the burial site of a fallen chief.

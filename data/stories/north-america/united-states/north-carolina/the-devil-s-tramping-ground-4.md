#title
The Devil's Tramping Ground

#summary
A perfectly circular patch of ground in the Chatham County woods remains entirely barren of vegetation and plant life, allegedly worn bare by Satan pacing in circles at night.

#details
Located in the western woods of Chatham County, the Devil's Tramping Ground is a 40-foot diameter circle where no trees, weeds, or normal vegetation will grow, and any objects placed within the circle overnight are invariably found cleared away by morning. Local legends maintain that the Devil paces the perimeter of this path each night while plotting evil deeds. While various folk theories attribute the barren circle to ancient Native American ceremonial dances or a fallen tribal chief's burial site, soil studies have noted high salt content and sterile soil properties, though the enduring mystery continues to captivate visitors and folklorists.

#location
A 40-foot barren circle located in the woods approximately ten miles from Siler City in Chatham County, North Carolina.

#title
Little Cataloochee

#summary
The Ola community, abandoned in the 1920s-30s for the park. A church and graveyard, the Dan Cook cabin and the 1864 Hannah cabin survive along the trail.

#location
Little Cataloochee, Great Smoky Mountains NP, Haywood County

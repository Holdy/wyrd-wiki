#title
The Restless Spirits of Historic Fort Reno

#summary
Established in 1875 on the Oklahoma frontier, Historic Fort Reno is widely reported to be haunted by soldiers, prisoners of war, and a sorrowful "Lady in White." Visitors and paranormal investigators frequently encounter disembodied voices, ghostly faces at windows, and phantom cavalry sounds across the grounds.

#details
Fort Reno served as a vital military post through the Indian Wars, the Land Run of 1889, and as a prisoner of war camp during World War II. Over the decades, the heavy toll of disease, isolation, and military conflict left a lingering spiritual residue across the post. Among the most enduring legends is the "Lady in White," a sorrowful apparition seen wandering near the officers' quarters and chapel, often accompanied by the scent of lavender. Other recurring phenomena include a mysterious face pressed against the upper windows of the former visitor center and the phantom sounds of galloping horses and shouted commands echoing across the parade grounds. 

#location
Historic Fort Reno is located at 7107 W. Cheyenne Street just west of El Reno, Oklahoma.

The Phantom Maid of the Skirvin Hotel

Summary

Built in 1910, Oklahoma City's historic Skirvin Hotel is reputed to be haunted by a Prohibition-era chambermaid named Effie who reportedly jumped from a top-floor window. Guests, staff, and visiting professional sports teams frequently report phantom crying, mysterious door movements, and spectral encounters.

Details

Opened in 1911 by oilman W.B. Skirvin, the luxury hotel became a prominent fixture of Oklahoma City. During the Prohibition era, legend states that Skirvin had an affair with a hotel maid who subsequently became pregnant. To protect his reputation, Skirvin had her locked away on the tenth floor of the hotel. Even after giving birth, the woman remained imprisoned. Distraught and mentally unhinged, she took her infant child and threw herself out of the window. In the decades since, the hotel has gained widespread notoriety—particularly among visiting NBA players and guests—for unexplained paranormal phenomena. Reports include phantom crying of a baby, doors opening and closing independently, objects moving without assistance, and encounters with a spectral presence.

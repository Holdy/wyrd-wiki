The Apparitions of Buffalo Ridge Road

Summary

Drivers traveling along the isolated northern dirt stretch of Buffalo Ridge Road at night have reported encountering a tragic female apparition and mysterious running silhouettes. Local folklore attributes these hauntings to a dark family tragedy that occurred at the site.

Details

According to local legend, the northern unpaved end of Buffalo Ridge Road is haunted following a tragic sequence of events where a man took his life, and his grief-stricken wife subsequently killed their three children before ending her own life. Motorists navigating the dark, bumpy road after dark frequently experience unsettling paranormal phenomena. Witnesses have reported seeing the apparition of a woman with dark hair wearing a dress, as well as bizarre silhouettes of people running on all fours. Others have noted eerie white mists drifting through the trees, ghostly footsteps, and low growling sounds coming from the surrounding woods.

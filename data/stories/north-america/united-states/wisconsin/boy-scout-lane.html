Boy Scout Lane

Summary

Boy Scout Lane is an isolated dead-end road near Stevens Point, Wisconsin, that has inspired decades of urban legends about a doomed troop of Boy Scouts.

Details

Stretching about 2,500 feet between Cemetery Road and Little Chicago Road in the Town of Linwood near Stevens Point, Boy Scout Lane is famous for chilling folklore. Countless versions of the legend claim that a troop of Boy Scouts met a tragic end there in the 1950s or 1960s—whether murdered by their Scoutmaster, killed in a forest fire, trapped in a bus accident, or simply vanishing without a trace. Despite historical records showing no such tragedy ever occurred, generations of visitors have reported eerie phenomena along the road, including glowing lantern lights moving through the woods after dark, ghostly apparitions of scouts, mysterious child-sized handprints appearing on vehicles, and an overwhelming sense of dread.

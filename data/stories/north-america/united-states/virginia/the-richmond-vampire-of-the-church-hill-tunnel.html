The Richmond Vampire of the Church Hill Tunnel

Summary

Following a catastrophic tunnel collapse in 1925, witnesses reported seeing a fanged, decomposed creature emerge from the rubble and take refuge inside a mausoleum at Hollywood Cemetery.

Details

The legend of the Richmond Vampire is rooted in the tragic Church Hill Tunnel collapse in Richmond, Virginia, on October 2, 1925, where a crew working to clear an unstable abandoned tunnel was buried under falling bricks. Amidst the chaos and rescue efforts, survivors and witnesses claimed that a tall, partially decomposed man with blood dripping from his mouth and sharp fangs emerged from the collapsed tunnel. Calmly ignoring the disaster, the creature fled toward the nearby Hollywood Cemetery and entered the locked tomb of W.W. Pool. Shocked workers who followed found the heavy crypt door locked from the inside, or reportedly witnessed the casket lid closing on its own. Local lore connects the mysterious crypt—which lacks a birth or death date for Pool—to this legendary vampire, who is said to still lurk within the cemetery's historic grounds.

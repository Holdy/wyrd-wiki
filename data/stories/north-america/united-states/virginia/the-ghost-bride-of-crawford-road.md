#title
The Ghost Bride of Crawford Road

#summary
Drivers traveling along Crawford Road frequently report encountering the apparition of a bride hanging from or standing atop the Historical Tour Road overpass. According to legend, the spirit is that of a reluctant bride who fled her wedding and took her own life at the bridge.

#details
Crawford Road in Yorktown, Virginia, is notorious for a chilling legend involving a ghost bride. As the story goes, a young woman who was forced into an unwanted marriage fled to the roadway and hanged herself from the Historical Tour Road overpass. Ever since, motorists passing beneath the bridge have reported unsettling phenomena, including car batteries mysteriously dying. Drivers who look in their rearview mirrors often see the ghostly figure of the bride swinging back and forth, and some have even heard a terrifying thud on the roof of their vehicle as if she had fallen onto it.

#location
The Historical Tour Road overpass bridging across Crawford Road in Yorktown, Virginia.

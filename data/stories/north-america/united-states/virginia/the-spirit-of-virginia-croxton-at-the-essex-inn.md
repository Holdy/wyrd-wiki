#title
The Spirit of Virginia Croxton at the Essex Inn

#summary
An elderly resident who lived eighty years in a 19th-century mansion continues to share a guest room with overnight visitors long after her death.

#details
The Essex Inn in Tappahannock, Virginia, built in 1850, is famously haunted by Virginia Scott Croxton, a woman who lived in the mansion for all of her eighty years before passing away in 1939 in what is now Elisabeth's Room. Guests staying in the room have repeatedly reported feeling an invisible presence slide under the bedsheets and lie beside them, mimicking the motion of a spouse. Innkeepers and housekeepers also report rocking chairs moving by themselves, tools rearranging overnight, and piano keys playing unassisted in the parlor.

#location
The Essex Inn is located at 203 Water Lane in Tappahannock, Virginia.

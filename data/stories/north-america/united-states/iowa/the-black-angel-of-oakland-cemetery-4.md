#title
The Black Angel of Oakland Cemetery

#summary
A daunting bronze funerary statue in an Iowa City cemetery is said to turn black and curse anyone who touches or kisses it.

#details
Commissioned in 1913 by Teresa Dolezal Feldevert to honor her late son and husband, the nine-foot-tall bronze angel statue in Oakland Cemetery oxidized over the years from bronze to a dark greenish-black. Local legend transformed the monument into a cursed figure, claiming that kissing or touching the statue will bring about instant death, a terminal illness, or dying within seven years, while others attribute its dark color to Teresa's supposed dark sins or witchcraft.

#location
The Black Angel statue stands in Oakland Cemetery in Iowa City, Iowa.

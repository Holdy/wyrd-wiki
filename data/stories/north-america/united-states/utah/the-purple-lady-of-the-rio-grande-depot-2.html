The Purple Lady of the Rio Grande Depot

Summary

After a tragic accident on the tracks outside a historic train station in downtown Salt Lake City, a spectral woman draped in purple is said to haunt the building's halls and ladies' restroom.

Details

Built in 1910, the Rio Grande Depot in Salt Lake City has long been associated with the enduring legend of "The Purple Lady." According to local lore, after a heated argument with her fiancé, a young woman threw her engagement ring onto the train tracks, dashed after it, and was struck and killed by an oncoming train. Over the decades, employees, visitors, and tour guides have reported paranormal phenomena within the building, including mysterious cold spots, lights and water faucets turning on by themselves, and an eerie, unexplained female scream captured on audio recordings in the ladies' restroom.

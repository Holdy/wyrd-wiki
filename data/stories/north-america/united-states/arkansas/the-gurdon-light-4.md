#title
The Gurdon Light

#summary
A mysterious bobbing lantern light has been seen floating along an old railroad track near Gurdon, with local legend attributing the phenomenon to the ghost of a railroad foreman murdered in 1931.

#details
Since the 1930s, witnesses near Gurdon in Clark County have reported seeing a strange yellow, white, or red light swaying 1 to 3 feet above the ground along the old railroad tracks. Local folklore connects the eerie phenomenon to the December 1931 murder of a railway section foreman who was beaten to death with a spike maul and shovel by a fellow worker, with the light representing his ghost carrying a lantern.

#location
The Gurdon Light appears along the railroad tracks about four miles north of Gurdon in Clark County, Arkansas.

#title
The Spectral Masons and Quack Doctors of the Crescent Hotel

#summary
Built in 1886 as a luxury mountain resort and later operated as a fraudulent cancer hospital, this historic Arkansas hotel is haunted by a fallen stonemason, a phantom nurse pushing a gurney, and the ghost of its former quack owner.

#details
The 1886 Crescent Hotel in Eureka Springs was constructed by Irish stonemasons, one of whom—a red-haired man named Michael—reportedly fell to his death near what is now Room 218 and continues to haunt the room with flickering lights and loud noises. Decades later, con man Norman Baker turned the hotel into a fraudulent cancer hospital during the 1930s, and guests and staff now report seeing his ghost in the basement, alongside a ghostly nurse pushing a gurney down the third-floor hallway at night and a patient named Theodora in Room 419.

#location
The 1886 Crescent Hotel & Spa is located atop West Mountain overlooking Eureka Springs in Carroll County, Arkansas.

#title
The Gurdon Light

#summary
A mysterious glowing orb has haunted a stretch of railroad tracks outside Gurdon since the 1930s, with locals attributing the apparition to a murdered railway foreman's lantern.

#details
Beginning in the 1930s, residents and visitors near the small town of Gurdon have reported sightings of an unexplained, floating light moving silently above the railroad tracks in the wooded area outside town. While skeptics have suggested explanations ranging from car headlights on nearby Interstate 30 to piezoelectric discharges from quartz crystals, local folklore ties the phenomenon to a violent real-world event. In December 1931, a railway foreman named William McClain was beaten to death with a spike maul by an employee during a dispute over work hours. Soon after the murder, the mysterious light began appearing along the tracks, leading generations of locals to believe it is the ghostly lantern of McClain—or a decapitated worker—still searching the dark woods.

#location
The railroad tracks passing through the wooded area just outside of Gurdon in Clark County, Arkansas.

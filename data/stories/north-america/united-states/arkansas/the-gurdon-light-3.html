The Gurdon Light

Summary

A mysterious floating orb of light has been sighted above the railroad tracks near Gurdon, Arkansas since the 1930s, with local folklore attributing it to the lantern of a murdered railroad foreman.

Details

Since the early 1930s, residents and visitors near Gurdon, Arkansas, have reported sightings of an unexplained, glowing orb floating above the railroad tracks. The legend surrounding the light intensified following the December 1931 murder of William McClain, a railroad foreman who was beaten to death by an employee during a dispute over work hours. According to local folklore, the floating light is the ghostly lantern carried by McClain as he wanders the tracks. Over the decades, the mystery gained widespread renown, eventually drawing national television coverage when NBC’s Unsolved Mysteries featured the phenomenon in December 1994.

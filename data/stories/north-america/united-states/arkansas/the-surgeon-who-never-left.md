#title
The Surgeon Who Never Left

#summary
Staff members at a former rural hospital building in Baxter County have reported spontaneous lights, phantom cigar smoke, and the lingering presence of a beloved longtime surgeon.

#details
Dr. John Guenthner operated a hospital in Gasville, Arkansas, starting in the 1930s and 1940s, serving as a pillar of the local medical community until his retirement. After the building later housed the Baxter County Heritage Museum, curators and staff reported supernatural occurrences, including lights turning on by themselves and the distinct scent and wisps of cigar smoke in the hallway, attributed to the spirit of Dr. Guenthner.

#location
The former hospital building is located in Gasville, Baxter County, Arkansas.

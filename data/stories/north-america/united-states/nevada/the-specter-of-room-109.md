#title
The Specter of Room 109

#summary
A pregnant woman named Elizabeth was allegedly locked in a room of the Goldfield Hotel by a wealthy mining magnate, where she died in despair and now paces the historic halls. Her tragic apparition and ghostly cries continue to haunt the abandoned building.

#details
Built in 1908 as a pinnacle of luxury in the Nevada desert, the Goldfield Hotel became the centerpiece of the booming mining town of Goldfield. According to local folklore, the hotel's most famous ghost is Elizabeth, a young woman said to have been the mistress of multimillionaire George Wingfield. When she became pregnant and threatened public scandal, Wingfield allegedly lured her into Room 109 and chained her to a radiator, leaving her with minimal food and water until she gave birth. After a harrowing ordeal, Elizabeth reportedly died, and her newborn child was thrown into a nearby mining shaft. Today, visitors and paranormal investigators report that Room 109 is intensely cold and resists photographic equipment, while Elizabeth's sad apparition in a white gown paces the hallways calling for her lost child. Other paranormal phenomena reported in the hotel include phantom cigar smoke from Wingfield, mischievous child spirits by the staircase, and an aggressive entity known as "The Stabber" in the Gold Room.

#location
The Goldfield Hotel is located on U.S. Route 95 in the historic ghost town of Goldfield, Esmeralda County, Nevada.

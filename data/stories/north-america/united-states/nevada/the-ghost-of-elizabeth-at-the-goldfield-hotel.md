#title
The Ghost of Elizabeth at the Goldfield Hotel

#summary
In the early twentieth century, a wealthy mining magnate allegedly imprisoned his pregnant mistress in a hotel room where she died, and her spirit and cries for help still haunt the building.

#details
During the height of the gold mining boom in Nevada, the luxurious Goldfield Hotel opened in 1908 and was later acquired by mining magnate George Wingfield. According to local legend, Wingfield took a young woman named Elizabeth as his mistress. When she became pregnant and threatened his social standing, he purportedly lured her to Room 109 of the hotel and chained her to a radiator throughout her pregnancy. She was kept isolated until her child was born, after which both she and her newborn baby vanished under tragic circumstances. Today, visitors and paranormal investigators report hearing the cries of a baby, feeling an intense chill in Room 109, and seeing the apparition of Elizabeth wandering the hallways in a white gown searching for her lost child.

#location
The Goldfield Hotel is located at the intersection of 4th Avenue and Crook Avenue in Goldfield, Nevada.

#title
The Curse of the Angry Mermaid

#summary
An enraged mermaid places an ancient curse on a tribe after being unjustly banished from their village. The hex brings generations of conflict, violent storms, and unending misfortune to the region.

#details
A Paiute warrior fell in love with a mermaid and brought her back to his village to marry her, but the tribe demanded he immediately return the strange creature to the deep waters. Enraged by her sudden banishment, the mermaid placed a curse upon the people. This ancient hex is said to have fueled the bloodshed of the Pyramid Lake War and continues to manifest today through sudden turbulence, eerie water baby legends, mysterious drownings, and lingering bad luck for those connected to the lake.

#location
Pyramid Lake is situated in southeastern Washoe County within the Truckee River Basin in Nevada, roughly 40 miles northeast of Reno.

#title
The Ghost of Elizabeth at the Goldfield Hotel

#summary
In the early twentieth century, a wealthy mining magnate allegedly imprisoned his pregnant mistress in a hotel room where she died, and her spirit and cries for help still haunt the building.

#details
During the height of the gold mining boom in Nevada, the luxurious Goldfield Hotel opened in 1908 and was later acquired by mining magnate George Wingfield. According to local legend, Wingfield took a young woman named Elizabeth as his mistress. When she became pregnant and threatened his social standing, he purportedly lured her to Room 109 of the hotel and chained her to a radiator throughout her pregnancy. She was kept isolated until her child was born, after which both she and her newborn baby vanished under tragic circumstances. Today, visitors and paranormal investigators report hearing the cries of a baby, feeling an intense chill in Room 109, and seeing the apparition of Elizabeth wandering the hallways in a white gown searching for her lost child.

#location
The Goldfield Hotel is located at the intersection of 4th Avenue and Crook Avenue in Goldfield, Nevada.

#title
The Hauntingly Impeccable Spirits of Mackay Mansion

#summary
Built during the Comstock Lode mining boom, the historic Mackay Mansion is haunted by multiple restless spirits, including a tidiness-obsessed former maid named Harriet and the apparition of a young girl.

#details
Constructed in 1859 in Virginia City, Nevada, the Mackay Mansion served as the headquarters and home for mining barons like John Mackay. Today, the grand Victorian estate is famous for its active paranormal presence. Among its most notable spectral residents is Harriet, a former maid whose devotion to cleanliness is so enduring that her ghostly presence is said to intensify whenever a room is left in disorder. Other reported spirits include giggling children on the third floor, shadowy figures, and the apparition of John Mackay himself. Notably, actor Johnny Depp reported waking up during a 1995 filming stay to see the ghostly figure of a young girl in white standing at the foot of his bed.

#location
The Mackay Mansion is located at 291 S. C Street in Virginia City, Nevada.

#title
The Lady in Red

#summary
A glamorous woman known as the Lady in Red was murdered on the fifth floor of the Mizpah Hotel in the 1920s by a jealous lover or husband. Her spirit continues to roam the historic corridors, occasionally leaving pearls on guests' pillows.

#details
Opened in 1907 during Tonopah's silver mining boom, the Mizpah Hotel is widely celebrated as one of America's most haunted hotels. Its most infamous resident spirit is the Lady in Red, who operated as a charismatic prostitute or courtesan in the hotel during the 1920s. According to legend, a wealthy patron discovered she had numerous other clients and, in a fit of jealous rage, stabbed and killed her on the fifth floor between rooms 502 and 504. Another variation of the tale claims her husband discovered her infidelity after missing a train and beat her to death in the hotel room. To this day, guests staying on the fifth floor report seeing a spectral woman in a red dress wandering the hallways and the elevator shaft. Astonished visitors have occasionally discovered unexplained pearls left behind on their pillows or nightstands after reporting encounters with her.

#location
The Mizpah Hotel is located at 100 N. Main Street in Tonopah, Nye County, Nevada.

#title
The Lady in Red of the Mizpah Hotel

#summary
The ghost of a woman known as the "Lady in Red," who was murdered on the fifth floor of the historic Mizpah Hotel by a jealous lover, is said to haunt the building's hallways and elevator.

#details
Opened in 1907 in the thriving silver mining boomtown of Tonopah, Nevada, the Mizpah Hotel quickly established itself as one of the most luxurious establishments in the state. According to local lore, a high-class woman often referred to as "Rose" or the Lady in Red lived and conducted her business out of a palatial suite on the hotel's fifth floor during the 1920s. Her life came to a tragic end when an ex-lover or jealous patron strangled and stabbed her in a fit of rage during a violent scuffle. Ever since, her spirit has reportedly remained tied to the hotel. Guests and staff have recounted numerous supernatural encounters over the decades, including hearing a gentle voice whispering in visitors' ears inside the hotel's elevator, finding mysterious pearls placed under pillows—supposedly shedding from her broken necklace—and experiencing her presence around rooms 502 and 504.

#location
The historic Mizpah Hotel, located at 100 North Main Street in Tonopah, Nevada.

#title
The Millionaires' Club Haunting

#summary
Once an elite social sanctuary for Comstock Lode millionaires, the historic Washoe Club in Virginia City now harbors restless spirits, including a mysterious Lady in Blue and a thieving old prospector. Its dark history includes storing winter corpses in its back crypt and tragic nineteenth-century deaths.

#details
Founded in 1875 on B Street and later relocated to C Street after a devastating fire, the Washoe Club originally served as an opulent, exclusive retreat for mining barons, intellectuals, and political dignitaries during the height of Nevada's silver bonanza. Following the decline of the Comstock mines, the club closed in 1897, but its upper floors remained largely untouched for over a century. Because the frozen winter ground in Virginia City prevented gravediggers from burying the dead, the building's back crypt was famously used as a holding area for corpses, cementing its grim reputation. Today, visitors and paranormal investigators frequent the saloon and museum, reporting chilling encounters with the "Lady in Blue"—who manifests at the top of the club's famous unsupported spiral staircase—alongside a phantom prospector who steals unattended drinks, swinging doors, and mysteriously tipping barstools.

#location
The Washoe Club Haunted Museum and Saloon is located at 112 N. C Street in Virginia City, Storey County, Nevada.

#title
The Specter of Room 109

#summary
A pregnant woman named Elizabeth was allegedly locked in a room of the Goldfield Hotel by a wealthy mining magnate, where she died in despair and now paces the historic halls. Her tragic apparition and ghostly cries continue to haunt the abandoned building.

#details
Built in 1908 as a pinnacle of luxury in the Nevada desert, the Goldfield Hotel became the centerpiece of the booming mining town of Goldfield. According to local folklore, the hotel's most famous ghost is Elizabeth, a young woman said to have been the mistress of multimillionaire George Wingfield. When she became pregnant and threatened public scandal, Wingfield allegedly lured her into Room 109 and chained her to a radiator, leaving her with minimal food and water until she gave birth. After a harrowing ordeal, Elizabeth reportedly died, and her newborn child was thrown into a nearby mining shaft. Today, visitors and paranormal investigators report that Room 109 is intensely cold and resists photographic equipment, while Elizabeth's sad apparition in a white gown paces the hallways calling for her lost child. Other paranormal phenomena reported in the hotel include phantom cigar smoke from Wingfield, mischievous child spirits by the staircase, and an aggressive entity known as "The Stabber" in the Gold Room.

#location
The Goldfield Hotel is located on U.S. Route 95 in the historic ghost town of Goldfield, Esmeralda County, Nevada.

#title
The Lady in Red of the Mizpah Hotel

#summary
The ghost of a woman known as the "Lady in Red," who was murdered on the fifth floor of the historic Mizpah Hotel by a jealous lover, is said to haunt the building's hallways and elevator.

#details
Opened in 1907 in the thriving silver mining boomtown of Tonopah, Nevada, the Mizpah Hotel quickly established itself as one of the most luxurious establishments in the state. According to local lore, a high-class woman often referred to as "Rose" or the Lady in Red lived and conducted her business out of a palatial suite on the hotel's fifth floor during the 1920s. Her life came to a tragic end when an ex-lover or jealous patron strangled and stabbed her in a fit of rage during a violent scuffle. Ever since, her spirit has reportedly remained tied to the hotel. Guests and staff have recounted numerous supernatural encounters over the decades, including hearing a gentle voice whispering in visitors' ears inside the hotel's elevator, finding mysterious pearls placed under pillows—supposedly shedding from her broken necklace—and experiencing her presence around rooms 502 and 504.

#location
The historic Mizpah Hotel, located at 100 North Main Street in Tonopah, Nevada.

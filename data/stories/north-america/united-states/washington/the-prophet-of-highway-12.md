#title
The Prophet of Highway 12

#summary
Following the 1980 eruption of Mount St. Helens, drivers along Highway 12 reported picking up a mysterious female hitchhiker who warned of an impending second catastrophe before abruptly vanishing from moving vehicles.

#details
In the wake of the devastating May 1980 eruption of Mount St. Helens, motorists traveling along Highway 12 about 80 miles north of the volcano reported picking up a beautiful woman with haunting eyes. She prophesied a second massive volcanic eruption scheduled between October 12 and 14 that would devastate the surrounding region. Before bewildered drivers could process her words or check on her, she vanished into thin air from the passenger seat or back seat while vehicles were traveling at highway speeds. Although subsequent seismic activity in October was milder and slightly delayed, multiple independent accounts of the phantom woman circulated among local police departments.

#location
This paranormal encounter takes place along Highway 12, located roughly 80 miles north of Mount St. Helens near Castle Rock, Washington.

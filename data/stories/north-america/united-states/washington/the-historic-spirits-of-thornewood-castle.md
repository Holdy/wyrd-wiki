#title
The Historic Spirits of Thornewood Castle

#summary
Built in 1911 using 400-year-old English bricks, Lakewood's Thornewood Castle is haunted by its original wealthy owner, his wife, and a child said to have drowned in the nearby lake.

#details
Commissioned by businessman Chester Thorne and completed in 1911, Thornewood Castle is a 27,000-square-foot English Tudor and Gothic mansion built with genuine antique materials imported from England. After Chester Thorne's death in his second-floor bedroom in 1927 and his wife Anna's passing in 1954, their presences reportedly lingered on the estate. Guests and staff have reported light bulbs unscrewing themselves in Chester's former room, Anna's reflection appearing in an antique mirror in the bridal suite, and the phantom sighting of a small child standing near the edge of American Lake where a grandchild of a former owner tragically drowned.

#location
Thornewood Castle is located at 8601 Gravelly Lake Drive SW in Lakewood, Washington.

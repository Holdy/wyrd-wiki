The Ghost Boy of Dead Man's Curve

Summary

Drivers who leave a coin on a specific bridge along Clinton Road at midnight may have it returned by the spirit of a young boy who drowned nearby. In more chilling accounts, the phantom attempts to push unsuspecting visitors into the water.

Details

According to local folklore popularized in New Jersey, a young boy tragically lost his life after falling or drowning in Clinton Brook near a treacherous stretch known as Dead Man's Curve on Clinton Road. Legend maintains that his spirit continues to haunt the bridge. If a traveler places a coin—such as a quarter—on the middle of the road at midnight, the coin will be mysteriously returned. However, encountering the spectral boy can be perilous, as some witnesses claim the ghost will attempt to push onlookers over the side of the bridge into the rushing waters below.

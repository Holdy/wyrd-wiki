The Phantom of Aldrich Mansion

Summary

A grand estate overlooking Narragansett Bay is said to be haunted by the spirit of a young woman who perpetually wanders toward a forbidden balcony.

Details

Commissioned by U.S. Senator Nelson W. Aldrich in 1896 and completed in 1912, the sprawling Aldrich Mansion in Warwick, Rhode Island, is steeped in Gilded Age grandeur and local folklore. Employees and visitors frequently report seeing the apparition of a woman in her late twenties or thirties roaming the interior halls. Witnesses note that she always seems to be walking purposefully toward the doors opening onto the front balcony—the very spot where local legend claims one of the senator's daughters tragically committed suicide by jumping, despite a lack of historical records confirming the event.

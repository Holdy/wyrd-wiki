The Devil's Footprints of North Kingstown

Summary

Satan chased a local woman along a granite ledge in Rhode Island before leaping into Narragansett Bay, leaving his cloven hoofprints burned into the stone. The impressions remain visible on a rock face near Wickford to this day.

Details

According to colonial-era legend in North Kingstown, Rhode Island, the Devil pursued a woman who had fled through the area, seizing her by the arm as she attempted to escape near Wickford. When she cried out in resistance, her attacker revealed himself as Satan, stamping his feet fiercely onto the granite bedrock. He then grabbed her by the waist and leaped into Narragansett Bay, transforming into a serpent as he vanished into the water. In the wake of his departure, deep impressions resembling a cloven hoof and a human foot with a distinct great toe were left permanently burned into the stone ledge.

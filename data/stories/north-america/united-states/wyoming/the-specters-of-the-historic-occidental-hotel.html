The Specters of the Historic Occidental Hotel

Summary

Built along the Bozeman Trail in Buffalo, Wyoming, the Historic Occidental Hotel is haunted by multiple spirits, including a young cholera victim named Emily and mysterious presences that slide into guests' beds.

Details

Operating since 1880, the Occidental Hotel in Buffalo, Wyoming, has accumulated numerous ghost stories over the decades. The most famous resident spirit is Emily, the young daughter of a former bordello operator who tragically died of cholera in the early 1900s. Staff and guests frequently report hearing a child's laughter, bouncing balls, or a little girl playing hide-and-seek behind doors, and children visiting the hotel still leave stuffed animals and handwritten notes for her. Other eerie phenomena include items shifting on their own, glasses and wine bottles breaking or popping corks without human intervention, and guests in suites reporting an invisible presence sliding into bed between them.

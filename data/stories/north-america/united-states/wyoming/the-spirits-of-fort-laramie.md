#title
The Spirits of Fort Laramie

#summary
Park rangers and visitors at Fort Laramie in southeastern Wyoming report uncanny encounters with spectral entities, including mysterious fragrances, moving objects, and legendary phantoms.

#details
Fort Laramie, established as a major fur trading post and military garrison on the Oregon Trail in Goshen County, Wyoming, has witnessed over a century and a half of history—and lingering supernatural energy. Staff members and visitors have reported numerous ghostly phenomena across the historic grounds, such as coins balancing themselves on open cash boxes in empty rooms, footsteps marching across second-story floors, and sudden, potent floral scents filling mid-19th-century officers' quarters. Among the most enduring local legends is the "Lady in Green," a young woman who allegedly disappeared on horseback in the 1840s and is said to reappear near the fort periodically.

#location
Fort Laramie National Historic Site is situated at the confluence of the Laramie and North Platte rivers in Goshen County, southeastern Wyoming.

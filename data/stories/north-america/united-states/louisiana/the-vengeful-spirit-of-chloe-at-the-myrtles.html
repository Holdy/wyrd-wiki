The Vengeful Spirit of Chloe at The Myrtles

Summary

An enslaved woman seeking revenge on her master mistakenly poisoned his wife and children with oleander leaves, binding her restless spirit to the historic estate where her ghostly apparitions and handprints are still reported.

Details

According to local legend, an enslaved woman named Chloe, who served as a mistress to judge Clark Woodruff and wore a green turban to hide an ear cut off for eavesdropping, baked a poisonous cake containing oleander juice to win back favor in the household. Instead of her intended target, Woodruff's wife and daughters consumed the cake and died, leading to Chloe's execution by other slaves. Visitors and staff have since reported sightings of a woman in a green turban, unexplained cold spots, and handprints or faces appearing in an antique mirror left uncovered after a death.

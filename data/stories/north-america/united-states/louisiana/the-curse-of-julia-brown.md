#title
The Curse of Julia Brown

#summary
According to local legend, a Louisiana woman named Julia Brown predicted her own death and cursed the small swamp town of Frenier, ensuring that a devastating 1915 hurricane would wipe out the entire settlement.

#details
In the small farming community of Frenier, surrounded by the Manchac Swamp in Louisiana, a local resident named Julia Brown frequently sat on her porch singing and playing guitar. Over time, local folklore transformed her into a voodoo priestess who grew embittered by ungrateful neighbors and warned them with an ominous song: "One day I'm going to die and take the whole town with me." On September 29, 1915, Julia passed away of old age, and as villagers gathered for her funeral, a violent Category 4 hurricane struck the region. The ensuing storm surge and powerful winds utterly destroyed Frenier and the neighboring settlement of Ruddock, leaving behind mass graves and cementing the legend that Julia's final curse had doomed the entire community.

#location
Frenier Cemetery is located in the wetlands of St. John the Baptist Parish near LaPlace, Louisiana, situated between Lakes Pontchartrain and Maurepas.

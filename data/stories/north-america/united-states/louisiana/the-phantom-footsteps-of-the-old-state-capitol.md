#title
The Phantom Footsteps of the Old State Capitol

#summary
Staff and security officers at the historic Gothic-style capitol building frequently encounter phantom footsteps, slamming doors, and shadowy apparitions of former political figures.

#details
Guards patrolling the historic Old State Capitol in Baton Rouge late at night have reported running into unseen barriers, hearing heavy footsteps cross balconies above the House of Representatives, and witnessing doors slam shut in empty hallways. Popular local lore attributes these ghostly occurrences to Senator Pierre Couvillian, who allegedly suffered a fatal heart attack during a heated legislative debate, or to political prisoners once held in the building's basement.

#location
Louisiana's Old State Capitol is situated at 100 North Boulevard overlooking the Mississippi River in Baton Rouge, Louisiana.

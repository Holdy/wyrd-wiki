#title
The Ghostly Gambler of La Fonda on the Plaza

#summary
Standing on a historic Santa Fe site that has hosted inns since 1607, the La Fonda Hotel is haunted by the spirit of a desperate salesman who leaped down a well after losing his company's money in a card game.

#details
La Fonda sits at the historic terminus of the Santa Fe Trail, a location that has housed lodging and gambling dens since the early 1600s, witnessing numerous brawls, court sessions, and hangings. Decades ago, a traveling salesman who lost his entire company payroll in a game of chance became so distraught that he threw himself down a deep well located right outside the gambling hall. Today, the hotel's La Plazuela dining room sits directly over that former well. Both guests and employees have reported seeing a ghostly apparition walk to the center of the dining room before vanishing into the floor, while others have encountered mysterious figures walking third-floor hallways near the old stairwells.

#location
La Fonda Hotel is located at 100 E. San Francisco Street in Santa Fe, New Mexico.

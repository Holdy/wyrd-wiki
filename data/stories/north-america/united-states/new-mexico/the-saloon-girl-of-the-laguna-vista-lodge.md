#title
The Saloon Girl of the Laguna Vista Lodge

#summary
Built in 1898 in Eagle Nest using stolen railroad ties, the Laguna Vista Saloon is haunted by the spirit of a stranded honeymooner-turned-saloon-girl who endlessly searches for her lost husband.

#details
Originally constructed as the El Monte Hotel, the Laguna Vista building in Eagle Nest served as a bustling hub for travelers and gamblers in the early 20th century. According to local folklore, a young woman staying at the hotel during her honeymoon was left destitute when her husband went hunting in the mountains and never returned, forcing her to work as a saloon girl to survive. Her restless spirit allegedly remains in the building, manifesting as a phantom piano that plays by itself, dining chairs that repeatedly slide back to the instrument, and kitchen anomalies where equipment operates without power and kitchen staff experience flying kitchenware and disembodied warnings.

#location
The Laguna Vista Lodge is located in Eagle Nest, New Mexico.

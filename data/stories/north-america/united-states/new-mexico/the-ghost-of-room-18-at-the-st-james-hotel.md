#title
The Ghost of Room 18 at the St. James Hotel

#summary
Built in 1872 by former presidential chef Henri Lambert, the St. James Hotel in Cimarron witnessed dozens of murders during the Wild West era, and its locked Room 18 is said to be haunted by a card player who was murdered after winning the hotel in a poker game.

#details
The St. James Hotel was established by Henri Lambert, who previously served as a chef to Abraham Lincoln. During Cimarron's lawless boomtown days, the hotel and its saloon were the backdrop for at least 26 murders, leaving bullet holes still visible in the pressed-tin ceiling of the dining room. Among its most notorious legends is Room 18, which is kept locked to protect guests from the malevolent spirit of Thomas James Wright. Wright was shot in the back and killed just outside the room after winning the rights to the hotel in a poker game. Visitors and staff have reported harrowing encounters in the room, including being pushed, seeing floating balls of colored light, and experiencing phantom poker games and cold spots throughout the hallways.

#location
The St. James Hotel is located at 617 S. Collison Avenue in Cimarron, New Mexico.

#title
The Curse of the Lost Dutchman's Mine

#summary
Generations of prospectors and explorers seeking a legendary hidden gold mine in the rugged desert mountains have met with mysterious deaths and disappearances, cementing a belief in an ancient curse.

#details
According to legend, Spanish conquistadors first explored the sacred Apache mountain range in search of gold and were warned by the indigenous people that their Thunder God would punish trespassers. After numerous men vanished or were found decapitated, the Spaniards fled. Later, Mexican miners, the German prospector Jacob Waltz, and dozens of subsequent treasure hunters met with similar violent ends, mysterious vanishings, and fatal accidents while hunting for the fabulously wealthy lost mine near Weaver's Needle.

#location
Weaver's Needle is a prominent rock pinnacle located within the Superstition Mountains east of Apache Junction, Arizona.

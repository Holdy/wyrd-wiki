The Phantom Lady of the San Carlos Hotel

Summary

Decades after a guest tragically jumped from the roof of the newly opened Phoenix hotel, visitors continue to be awakened by the silent apparition of an elegant woman in an evening gown. Standing at the foot of their beds, she calmly stares at them before floating effortlessly through closed doors.

Details

Constructed in 1928 on the site of Phoenix's first school, the San Carlos Hotel became the site of tragedy just five weeks after opening when a distressed guest named Leone Jensen jumped to her death from the roof. Ever since, guests staying in rooms across the hotel have reported being awakened by a strange feeling in the middle of the night to see a well-dressed woman wearing a flowing evening gown, hat, gloves, and scarf standing at the foot of their bed. She does not cause fear, but rather a calming sensation, staring silently for several seconds before floating out of the room without opening or closing the door.

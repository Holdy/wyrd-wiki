Julia Lowell of the Copper Queen Hotel

Summary

Guests staying in Room 315 of Bisbee's historic Copper Queen Hotel frequently report feeling the phantom presence and whispered pranks of a former resident known as Julia Lowell.

Details

Completed in 1902 by the Phelps Dodge Mining Company, the Copper Queen Hotel in Bisbee is Arizona's oldest continuously operating hotel. Local folklore maintains that several spirits roam its halls, the most famous being Julia Lowell, a woman who reportedly worked as a prostitute at the hotel and took her own life there. According to recurring accounts from guests—particularly men staying in Room 315—Julia makes her presence known late at night by whispering in guests' ears, playfully tugging on bedsheets, or brushing against their feet.

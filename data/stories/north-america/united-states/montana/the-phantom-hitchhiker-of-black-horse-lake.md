#title
The Phantom Hitchhiker of Black Horse Lake

#summary
Motorists driving along a lonely stretch of highway near Black Horse Lake frequently experience a terrifying phantom hitchhiker who appears to hurl himself directly onto their car before vanishing without a trace. When startled drivers stop to investigate, they find no body, no damage, and no sign of anyone in the area.

#details
Travelers driving along US Highway 87 in Cascade County between Great Falls and Fort Benton, particularly at night, often spot a Native American man dressed in denim standing by the shoulder near the seasonal Black Horse Lake. As vehicles approach, the figure suddenly launches himself into the path of the car, rolling over the hood and striking the windshield with a loud, violent thud. Believing they have struck a pedestrian, drivers immediately pull over to check for injuries, but they discover no physical evidence—neither blood, nor a body, nor even a single scratch or dent on their vehicle. Local legend suggests the spirit is trapped in an eternal loop reliving a fatal hit-and-run accident from decades past.

#location
The haunting takes place along a stretch of US Highway 87 in Cascade County, Montana, situated between Great Falls and Fort Benton near Black Horse Lake.

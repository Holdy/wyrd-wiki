#title
The Phantom of the Kelly Saloon

#summary
In the deserted mining town of Garnet, Montana, visitors and caretakers frequently experience paranormal phenomena attributed to a spectral woman who haunts the historic Kelly Saloon.

#details
During the late 19th-century gold rush, Garnet flourished as a bustling mountain camp before declining and becoming a ghost town by the 1940s. Among its preserved wooden structures, the Kelly Saloon stands out as the center of local ghost lore. Visitors and preservationists have long reported hearing the phantom notes of a piano playing in the empty room late at night, accompanied by the sounds of laughter and clinking glasses. Others have noted a distinct scent of lilac perfume drifting through the upper lodgings, along with shadowy figures glimpsed in period dress. Similar legends persist across the settlement, including sightings of a melancholy woman in the window of the F.A. Dahl House and disembodied infant cries echoing near the old cabins.

#location
The Kelly Saloon is located in the historic ghost town of Garnet within the Garnet Mountain Range of western Montana.

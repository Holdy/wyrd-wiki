#title
The Flathead Lake Witch's Curse

#summary
A reclusive woman accused of witchcraft in the 1800s placed a dark curse on the region surrounding her secluded cabin near Flathead Lake before vanishing. Local folklore holds that her malevolent magic lingers, causing unexplained accidents, fires, and mysterious fogs over the water.

#details
During the 1800s, a mysterious woman who lived alone in a secluded cabin near the shores of Flathead Lake in Montana practiced herbalism, brewing potions from local plants. After a series of unexplained tragedies struck the surrounding community, including mysterious fires and unusual accidents, superstitious locals blamed the reclusive woman. When she abruptly disappeared without a trace, rumors quickly spread that she had cursed the area before leaving. Ever since, inhabitants have associated the strange, lingering fogs and ongoing misfortunes of the region with her dark magic.

#location
A secluded cabin site near the shores of Flathead Lake, Montana.

#title
The Girl in the Blue Dress at Hotel Meade

#summary
The former courthouse turned hotel in the ghost town of Bannack is haunted by the apparition of a teenage girl who drowned nearby in 1916.

#details
Originally constructed in 1875 as the Beaverhead County Courthouse before being converted into a luxury hotel in 1891, the Hotel Meade in Bannack became the center of haunting legends following a local tragedy. In August 1916, sixteen-year-old Dorothy Dunn tragically drowned in a dredge pond along Grasshopper Creek. Shortly after her death, her best friend—who had been with her at the time—reported seeing her spirit wearing a long blue dress inside the hotel. Over the years, visitors, children, and tourists have continued to report sighting the young girl looking out the second-story windows, experiencing sudden cold spots, and encountering doors that mysteriously swing open on their own.

#location
The Hotel Meade is located within the historic ghost town of Bannack, Montana, situated along Grasshopper Creek.

#title
The Phantom Hitchhiker of Black Horse Lake

#summary
Motorists driving along a lonely stretch of highway near Black Horse Lake frequently experience a terrifying phantom hitchhiker who appears to hurl himself directly onto their car before vanishing without a trace. When startled drivers stop to investigate, they find no body, no damage, and no sign of anyone in the area.

#details
Travelers driving along US Highway 87 in Cascade County between Great Falls and Fort Benton, particularly at night, often spot a Native American man dressed in denim standing by the shoulder near the seasonal Black Horse Lake. As vehicles approach, the figure suddenly launches himself into the path of the car, rolling over the hood and striking the windshield with a loud, violent thud. Believing they have struck a pedestrian, drivers immediately pull over to check for injuries, but they discover no physical evidence—neither blood, nor a body, nor even a single scratch or dent on their vehicle. Local legend suggests the spirit is trapped in an eternal loop reliving a fatal hit-and-run accident from decades past.

#location
The haunting takes place along a stretch of US Highway 87 in Cascade County, Montana, situated between Great Falls and Fort Benton near Black Horse Lake.

#title
The Phantom of the Kelly Saloon

#summary
In the deserted mining town of Garnet, Montana, visitors and caretakers frequently experience paranormal phenomena attributed to a spectral woman who haunts the historic Kelly Saloon.

#details
During the late 19th-century gold rush, Garnet flourished as a bustling mountain camp before declining and becoming a ghost town by the 1940s. Among its preserved wooden structures, the Kelly Saloon stands out as the center of local ghost lore. Visitors and preservationists have long reported hearing the phantom notes of a piano playing in the empty room late at night, accompanied by the sounds of laughter and clinking glasses. Others have noted a distinct scent of lilac perfume drifting through the upper lodgings, along with shadowy figures glimpsed in period dress. Similar legends persist across the settlement, including sightings of a melancholy woman in the window of the F.A. Dahl House and disembodied infant cries echoing near the old cabins.

#location
The Kelly Saloon is located in the historic ghost town of Garnet within the Garnet Mountain Range of western Montana.

#title
The Phantom Teenager of Hotel Meade

#summary
Visitors and children at the historic Hotel Meade in the abandoned mining town of Bannack frequently report sightings of a spectral teenage girl wearing a blue dress who drowned in a nearby creek long ago.

#details
Established during the 1862 gold rush, Bannack eventually became a deserted ghost town, with its former 1875 courthouse transformed into the Hotel Meade in 1890. Over the decades, visitors have reported cold spots and eerie cries of children inside the building, but the most enduring apparition is that of Dorothy Dunn, a teenager who drowned in a dredge pond along Grasshopper Creek. Witnesses and children have seen her ghost moving through the second-floor hallways or peering out of an upstairs window in her long blue dress, sometimes attempting to mouth silent words to visitors.

#location
The Hotel Meade is located within the historic ghost town of Bannack State Park, situated southwest of Dillon in Beaverhead County, Montana.

#title
The Rocking Chair of Room 349

#summary
Guests staying in Room 349 at Chico Hot Springs Resort frequently report uncanny phenomena and a rocking chair that moves on its own, attributed to the resort's former widowed owner.

#details
Originally established as a luxury resort in 1910, Chico Hot Springs operated temporarily as a hospital before returning to hospitality. In her later years, the widowed owner Percie Knowles spent her final days residing in Room 349, finding comfort sitting by the window in her rocking chair to admire the mountain view. Decades later, guests and staff report that Percie's spirit still occupies the room, where her rocking chair mysteriously moves by itself and swivels back toward the view when turned away.

#location
Chico Hot Springs Resort is located at 163 Chico Road in Pray, Park County, Montana.

#title
The Specter of Hotel Meade

#summary
Visitors and children at the historic Hotel Meade in Bannack frequently report sightings of a teenage girl wearing a blue dress and hearing disembodied sounds. Local legend attributes these encounters to Dorothy Dunn, a young girl who drowned in a nearby creek during the town's gold-mining era.

#details
Constructed initially as a courthouse in 1875 before being converted into a hotel in 1890, the Hotel Meade in the former gold-mining settlement of Bannack stands as the town's most haunted structure. Over the decades, visitors have reported experiencing sudden cold spots, the sounds of crying children, and full-appartition sightings of a teenage girl. According to local lore, this spirit is Dorothy Dunn, a young resident who drowned in a dredge pond along Grasshopper Creek. Witnesses, including young children visiting the site, have reported seeing her peering from the second-story windows or walking the hallways in a long blue dress, her mouth moving silently as if attempting to speak. Additionally, tourists have experienced unexplained paranormal activity inside the building, such as heavy doors unlatching and swinging open on windless days.

#location
The Hotel Meade is located on the main street within Bannack State Park along Grasshopper Creek in Beaverhead County, southwestern Montana.

#title
The Staircase Rider of the Grand Union Hotel

#summary
The historic Grand Union Hotel in Fort Benton is said to be haunted by a cowboy who was shot after riding his horse up the grand staircase. Guests and staff also report phantom horse hooves, mysterious blue orbs, and misplaced items.

#details
Built in 1882, the Grand Union Hotel in Fort Benton stands as Montana's oldest operating hotel. According to local lore, one of its most notorious spirits is a drunken cowboy who accepted a dare to ride his horse up the hotel's prominent main staircase, ending in his fatal shooting by the bar manager. Visitors and workers have frequently reported hearing the phantom sound of horse hooves echoing up the stairs. Additional reported phenomena include glowing blue orbs dancing in second-floor guest rooms, flashlights rolling across tables on their own, and beds found mussed up in vacant rooms.

#location
The Grand Union Hotel is located in Fort Benton, Montana.

#title
The Waiting Madam of the Dumas Brothel

#summary
The ghost of a former madam who died under mysterious circumstances is said to still roam the upper floor of the historic Dumas Brothel in Butte, carrying a packed suitcase.

#details
Operating in Uptown Butte along Mercury Street from 1890 until 1982, the Dumas Brothel was a prominent fixture of the city's red-light district. Between 1950 and 1955, a woman named Elinor Knott served as its madam. Legend states that on a cold February night in 1955, Elinor packed her suitcase, intending to leave the brothel with her lover, who never arrived; the next morning, she was found dead in her room. Since then, staff and visitors have reported encountering a spectral woman carrying a suitcase as she walks past the upstairs rooms and descends the stairs into the empty building.

#location
The Dumas Brothel is situated at 407 E Mercury Street in Uptown Butte, Montana.

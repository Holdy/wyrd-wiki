#title
The Staircase Rider of the Grand Union Hotel

#summary
The historic Grand Union Hotel in Fort Benton is said to be haunted by a cowboy who was shot after riding his horse up the grand staircase. Guests and staff also report phantom horse hooves, mysterious blue orbs, and misplaced items.

#details
Built in 1882, the Grand Union Hotel in Fort Benton stands as Montana's oldest operating hotel. According to local lore, one of its most notorious spirits is a drunken cowboy who accepted a dare to ride his horse up the hotel's prominent main staircase, ending in his fatal shooting by the bar manager. Visitors and workers have frequently reported hearing the phantom sound of horse hooves echoing up the stairs. Additional reported phenomena include glowing blue orbs dancing in second-floor guest rooms, flashlights rolling across tables on their own, and beds found mussed up in vacant rooms.

#location
The Grand Union Hotel is located in Fort Benton, Montana.

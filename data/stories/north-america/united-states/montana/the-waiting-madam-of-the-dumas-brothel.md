#title
The Waiting Madam of the Dumas Brothel

#summary
The ghost of a former madam who died under mysterious circumstances is said to still roam the upper floor of the historic Dumas Brothel in Butte, carrying a packed suitcase.

#details
Operating in Uptown Butte along Mercury Street from 1890 until 1982, the Dumas Brothel was a prominent fixture of the city's red-light district. Between 1950 and 1955, a woman named Elinor Knott served as its madam. Legend states that on a cold February night in 1955, Elinor packed her suitcase, intending to leave the brothel with her lover, who never arrived; the next morning, she was found dead in her room. Since then, staff and visitors have reported encountering a spectral woman carrying a suitcase as she walks past the upstairs rooms and descends the stairs into the empty building.

#location
The Dumas Brothel is situated at 407 E Mercury Street in Uptown Butte, Montana.

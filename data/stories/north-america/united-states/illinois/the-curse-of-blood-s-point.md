#title
The Curse of Blood's Point

#summary
Arthur Blood, the first white settler of Flora Township, is believed to have brought a persistent curse to the rural road, bridges, and cemetery bearing his name. This dark legacy manifests through phantom vehicles, glowing-eyed hounds, and ghostly apparitions.

#details
Blood's Point Road and its surrounding bridges and cemetery in Boone County, Illinois, are shrouded in dark folklore. According to local lore, Arthur Blood brought a lingering curse to the area when he first settled it. Over the decades, travelers and visitors have reported numerous eerie phenomena along the route, including phantom cars, a spectral dog with glowing red eyes, and the echoes of tragic accidents, such as a historic van crash involving children. The cemetery at the end of the road is likewise reputed to experience unexplained orbs, electrical malfunctions, and the disembodied laughter of children.

#location
Blood's Point Road and its historic bridges are located in Cherry Valley, Boone County, Illinois.

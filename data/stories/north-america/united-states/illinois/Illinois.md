#title
Resurrection Mary

#summary
A young woman in a white ball gown is frequently picked up as a hitchhiker along Archer Avenue, only to mysteriously vanish when reaching the gates of Resurrection Cemetery.

#details
Beginning in the early 1930s, motorists driving along Archer Avenue in the southwest suburbs of Chicago reported encountering a lonely, beautiful young woman with blonde hair and a white dress. According to the accounts, drivers would pick her up—often after meeting her at a local dance hall—and offer her a ride home. As they drove along Archer Avenue toward Justice, Illinois, the woman would quietly guide the driver. However, as the vehicle approached the gates of Resurrection Cemetery, she would vanish into thin air from the vehicle, leaving behind only the haunting memory of the encounter. Over the decades, numerous drivers and local residents have shared similar accounts, cementing her status as Chicago's most famous vanishing hitchhiker.

#location
Resurrection Cemetery is located at 7200 S. Archer Road in Justice, Illinois.

#title
The Curse of Blood's Point

#summary
Arthur Blood, the first white settler of Flora Township, is believed to have brought a persistent curse to the rural road, bridges, and cemetery bearing his name. This dark legacy manifests through phantom vehicles, glowing-eyed hounds, and ghostly apparitions.

#details
Blood's Point Road and its surrounding bridges and cemetery in Boone County, Illinois, are shrouded in dark folklore. According to local lore, Arthur Blood brought a lingering curse to the area when he first settled it. Over the decades, travelers and visitors have reported numerous eerie phenomena along the route, including phantom cars, a spectral dog with glowing red eyes, and the echoes of tragic accidents, such as a historic van crash involving children. The cemetery at the end of the road is likewise reputed to experience unexplained orbs, electrical malfunctions, and the disembodied laughter of children.

#location
Blood's Point Road and its historic bridges are located in Cherry Valley, Boone County, Illinois.

#title
The Madonna of Bachelor's Grove

#summary
A spectral woman in a flowing white gown is frequently reported wandering among the gravestones or holding an infant at an abandoned historic cemetery in Midlothian, Illinois.

#details
Established in 1844, Bachelor's Grove Cemetery is widely regarded as one of the most haunted burial grounds in the United States. Among its numerous reported apparitions is the Madonna of Bachelor's Grove—also known as the "White Lady"—who was famously photographed in 1979 appearing as a figure in a white robe holding a baby. Subsequent sightings and infrared photographs over the years have captured her sitting upon headstones or wandering the desolate grounds with a melancholic expression. In addition to the White Lady, visitors have reported encountering a phantom Victorian farmhouse that vanishes when approached, unexplained floating orbs, and spectral animals.

#location
Bachelor's Grove Cemetery is situated within the Rubio Woods Forest Preserve off the Midlothian Turnpike near Midlothian, Illinois.

#title
The Phantoms of Cuba Road

#summary
Travelers driving along a winding rural highway in Barrington encounter various eerie apparitions, including a lantern-carrying woman and a cigar-smoking gangster appearing in rearview mirrors.

#details
Cuba Road, a notoriously dark and twisting route nestled near Barrington, Illinois, is famed for several persistent spectral encounters. Drivers traversing the roadway have reported seeing an old woman wandering the roadside with a lantern, attempting to flag down passing cars before vanishing into thin air. Others have looked into their rearview mirrors while turning onto the road to find a cigar-chomping Prohibition-era mobster sitting silently in the vehicle. In addition to these human spirits, a phantom couple is occasionally seen walking arm-in-arm along the deserted stretches of the highway, adding to the desolate and supernatural atmosphere surrounding the road and the nearby White Cemetery.

#location
Cuba Road is a winding rural route located in Barrington, Illinois, passing adjacent to White Cemetery near Old Barrington Road.

#title
The Phantoms of the Rialto Square Theatre

#summary
Known as the "Jewel of Joliet," this opulent 1926 movie palace is said to be haunted by a glamorous actress and other spectral figures who refuse to leave the stage. Patrons and staff frequently report icy drafts, unexplained footsteps, and ghostly apparitions lingering in the historic auditorium.

#details
Opened on May 24, 1926, the Rialto Square Theatre in Joliet was built as an ornate vaudeville and movie palace reflecting European Renaissance and Baroque architecture. Although saved from demolition in the late 1970s and fully restored, the grand building retained more than just its architectural splendor. Staff, patrons, and workmen have frequently reported seeing a nameless, pretty young woman in her twenties—believed to be a former actress—floating through the theater. Additional reports describe a pair of spirits in the balcony who allegedly fell to their deaths, along with sudden icy coldness, strange noises, and invisible poking fingers.

#location
The Rialto Square Theatre is located at 102 North Chicago Street in Joliet, Illinois.

#title
The Spirits of McPike Mansion

#summary
Built in 1869 by prominent resident Henry Guest McPike, this grand Victorian estate in Alton became famously haunted by roughly fifteen paranormal entities after falling into abandonment. Visitors and owners frequently experience inexplicable sounds, lights, and spectral figures throughout the decaying property.

#details
The McPike Mansion was constructed in Alton, Illinois, in 1869 by horticulturist and entrepreneur Henry Guest McPike. After McPike's death in 1910 and subsequent abandonment, the building deteriorated into disrepair. In 1994, Sharyn and George Luedke purchased the property to restore it, but quickly encountered persistent paranormal activity. Owners and guests regularly report strange orbs of light, loud inexplicable bangs, mists, and human-like silhouettes appearing in windows. Today, the owners conduct historical and paranormal tours of the grounds and cellar to share these mysterious encounters with the public.

#location
The McPike Mansion is situated on its historic estate property in Alton, Illinois.

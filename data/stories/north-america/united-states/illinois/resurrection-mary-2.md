#title
Resurrection Mary

#summary
A mysterious young woman in a white ball gown dances at Chicagoland ballrooms before requesting a ride down Archer Avenue and vanishing near Resurrection Cemetery.

#details
For nearly a century, motorists and dance hall patrons in the Chicago area have shared encounters with Resurrection Mary, a quintessential vanishing hitchhiker. Described as a fashionable blonde in her early twenties wearing a white dress and matching accessories, she typically materializes at local dance venues. After spending the evening dancing, she asks for a ride home. As her driver transports her along Archer Avenue toward Justice, Illinois, she suddenly vanishes right outside the gates of Resurrection Cemetery, leaving behind only her ghostly legacy.

#location
Resurrection Cemetery is located on Archer Avenue in Justice, Illinois, about a 30-minute drive southwest of Chicago.

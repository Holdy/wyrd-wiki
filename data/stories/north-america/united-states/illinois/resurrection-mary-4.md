#title
Resurrection Mary

#summary
A young, fashionable blonde woman in a white ball gown is said to haunt a stretch of Archer Avenue in Chicagoland after hitching a ride from a dance hall and vanishing near a cemetery. Documented since the 1930s, this famous vanishing hitchhiker legend remains one of Illinois's most enduring folklore tales.

#details
According to local lore dating back to the 1930s, a mysterious young woman known as "Resurrection Mary" frequently appears as a lonely guest at dance halls along Chicagoland. After dancing the night away, she asks for a ride home and guides her driver up Archer Avenue. When the vehicle nears Resurrection Cemetery in Justice, Illinois, Mary abruptly vanishes from the backseat without a trace. Countless researchers and motorists have reported encountering her along the roadside, and local establishments even reserve a drink in her honor.

#location
Resurrection Cemetery is located at 7200 S. Archer Road in the village of Justice, Illinois.

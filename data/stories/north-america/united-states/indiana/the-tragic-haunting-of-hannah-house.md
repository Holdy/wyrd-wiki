#title
The Tragic Haunting of Hannah House

#summary
Built in 1858 by Alexander Hannah, this historic Indianapolis home is legendarily haunted by the spirits of fleeing slaves who perished in a cellar fire while hidden on the Underground Railroad.

#details
Alexander Hannah served as a prominent civic leader and purportedly operated a sanctuary for escaping slaves on the Underground Railroad. According to local folklore, a group of slaves sheltering in the cellar accidentally knocked over an oil lamp, causing a fatal fire. Unable to escape, several occupants died from smoke inhalation and burns, and were hastily buried in the cellar dirt. Decades later, visitors and paranormal investigators reported intense paranormal phenomena, including disembodied whispers, foul smells, scratching noises in the cellar, and the ghostly presence of an infant in the upper bedrooms.

#location
The Hannah House is located at 3801 Madison Avenue in Indianapolis, Indiana.

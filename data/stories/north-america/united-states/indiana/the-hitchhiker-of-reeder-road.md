#title
The Hitchhiker of Reeder Road

#summary
A phantom hitchhiker named Elizabeth Wilson is said to suddenly vanish from the backseats of cars as motorists drive past Ross Cemetery.

#details
For generations, drivers traveling along Reeder Road in Northwest Indiana have shared chilling accounts of picking up a female hitchhiker. According to local lore, the apparition—identified as Elizabeth Wilson—silently enters a vehicle before abruptly disappearing into thin air as the car passes Ross Cemetery. While the fears surrounding the desolate stretch of road evolved over decades, the vanishing hitchhiker remains an enduring fixture of local folklore.

#location
The key location is Reeder Road where it passes Ross Cemetery in Northwest Indiana.

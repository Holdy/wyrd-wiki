The Curse of Seven Gables Road

Summary

An aggrieved witch cursed a rural Michigan road and its property, paving the way for a horrific family massacre and deadly supernatural encounters for anyone who trespasses past its rusted gate.

Details

According to local folklore, a malicious witch once lived in a seven-gabled home situated at the dead end of Seven Gables Road in Dansville, Michigan, where she placed a dark hex upon the property and its rustic boundary fence. Legend dictates that anyone who dared to jump over the gate and heard a chilling scream would be struck dead by the curse. Decades later, a new family moved into the home, whereupon the father tragically went insane, murdered his family, hung their bodies from the gables, burned the house to the ground, and took his own life. To this day, thrill-seekers and visitors wandering the overgrown state game area trails report hearing phantom screams echoing through the trees, unexplained heavy breathing, and oppressive supernatural dread near the rusted gate ruins.

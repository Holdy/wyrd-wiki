The Paulding Light

Summary

A mysterious glowing orb appears nearly every night in a remote forest valley in Michigan's Upper Peninsula, inspiring various local legends and ghost stories.

Details

Since the 1960s, visitors and locals have gathered along a rural stretch of road outside of Paulding, Michigan, to witness an unexplained luminous phenomenon. The light typically appears in the valley after dark, bouncing, hovering, and shifting colors between white, red, and green above the tree line and power lines. Popular local folklore offers several explanations for the mysterious light, the most famous being the ghost of a railroad brakeman who was killed while trying to stop a train crash on tracks that once ran through the valley. Other legends attribute the light to the spirit of a slain mail courier or a Native American dancer. Although subsequent scientific studies and student investigations have proposed that the lights are merely distant automobile headlights refracting off US Highway 45, the supernatural legends and nightly viewings endure.

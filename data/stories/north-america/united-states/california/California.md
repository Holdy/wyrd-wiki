#title
The Beautiful Stranger of Hotel del Coronado

#summary
In 1892, a young woman checked into a luxurious California resort under an alias, took her own life five days later, and has allegedly haunted the premises ever since as the hotel's resident ghost.

#details
In November 1892, a despondent 24-year-old woman using the false name Lottie Anderson Bernard arrived at the Hotel del Coronado. Spending five lonely days awaiting a companion who never arrived, she purchased a handgun in town and was subsequently found dead from a gunshot wound on an exterior wooden staircase leading down to the beach. Ever since her tragic death, guests and staff have reported numerous supernatural occurrences, particularly in her former guestroom (now Room 3327), where visitors experience flickering lights, inexplicable drafts, self-operating televisions, and phantom footsteps, cementing her local legend as the "Beautiful Stranger."

#location
The Hotel del Coronado is located at 1500 Orange Avenue in Coronado, California.

#details
On Thanksgiving Day in 1892, a 24-year-old woman registered at the Hotel del Coronado under the name Lottie Bernard, claiming she was waiting for her brother. Over the next five days, she appeared sorrowful and sickly, eventually purchasing a handgun in nearby San Diego. She was found dead on an exterior staircase on November 28 from a gunshot wound to the head, and investigators later identified her as Kate Morgan, an estranged wife who had been deserted by a male companion en route. Since her death, guests and staff have reported frequent paranormal phenomena in her third-floor guestroom—including flickering lights, self-activating televisions, unexplained breezes, and moving objects—as well as sightings of her apparition around the property.

#title
The Curse of Bodie

#summary
Visitors who remove artifacts, rocks, or glass fragments from a historic mining ghost town are plagued by relentless bad luck and personal tragedy until the stolen items are mailed back to the park rangers. Hundreds of remorseful tourists have returned their pilfered souvenirs, creating a massive archive of confession letters and returned objects maintained by park staff.

#details
Bodie, California, established during a late 19th-century gold rush, stands today as one of the West's best-preserved ghost towns. According to local folklore, the spirits of Bodie fiercely guard the settlement and enforce the "Bodie Curse" upon anyone who dares to take a keepsake. Numerous visitors over the decades have pocketed old square nails, shards of glass, or weathered wood, only to experience immediate, inexplicable catastrophes—ranging from sudden accidents and severe medical emergencies to bizarre domestic injuries and structural breakages. Desperate to lift the dark cloud of misfortune, these unfortunate tourists eventually package up their stolen trinkets and mail them back to the Bodie State Historic Park rangers, who maintain a growing archive of confession letters and returned artifacts.

#location
Bodie State Historic Park is located off Highway 270, approximately seven miles south of Bridgeport in Mono County, California.

#title
The Curse of Griffith Park

#summary
Cheated out of her rightful inheritance by political deceit, a grief-stricken young woman cursed her family's ranch to bring endless ruin, blight, and ghostly terror to all future owners. 

#details
In 1863, Don Antonio Feliz fell gravely ill and was manipulated on his deathbed into deeding his vast Rancho Los Feliz estate away from his beloved niece, Petranilla. Upon discovering the betrayal immediately after her uncle's death, Petranilla confronted the new owner and placed a sweeping curse upon the land and its usurpers, wishing upon them financial ruin, violent deaths, blighted fields, and devastating floods before dropping dead herself. True to her dying words, successive owners faced unrelenting misfortunes—from murdered lawyers and dying livestock to withered oaks and destructive floods—culminating in Colonel Griffith J. Griffith donating the blighted land to the city. Even after becoming a public park, the ghosts of Antonio and Petranilla allegedly haunted the grounds, famously appearing to crash and scatter a celebratory banquet at the historic Feliz Adobe.

#location
The historic Feliz Adobe, serving as the Griffith Park headquarters, is located within Griffith Park in Los Angeles, California.

#title
The Dark History and Hauntings of Preston Castle

#summary
This imposing 19th-century reform school in Northern California witnessed brutal violence and tragic deaths, including the unsolved murder of its head housekeeper, leaving the massive Romanesque building haunted by lingering spirits.

#details
Opened in 1894 in Ione, California, the Preston School of Industry was designed as a progressive juvenile reform facility. Behind its grand Romanesque Revival exterior, however, the institution faced overcrowding, harsh conditions, and dark tragedies over the decades. Most notably, head housekeeper Anna Corbin was brutally murdered inside the building in 1950 in a crime that remains unsolved, and several young wards met untimely ends from illness or accidents. Today, paranormal investigators and visitors report disembodied voices, eerie sensations, and sightings of Anna Corbin roaming the decaying halls.

#location
Preston Castle is located at 915 Ione Sutter Road in Ione, California.

#title
The Restless Spirits and Curse of Bodie

#summary
The historic gold-mining ghost town of Bodie in California's Eastern Sierra is said to be haunted by restless spirits and bound by a curse that punishes anyone who steals its artifacts.

#details
Following a wild gold rush starting in the late 1850s, Bodie grew into a notoriously lawless camp before declining into an abandoned ghost town by the mid-20th century. Local legends recount numerous ghostly apparitions lingering among the preserved buildings, such as a heavy-set Chinese maid who haunts the J.S. Cain House by sitting on sleeping visitors, and an old woman knitting in a rocking chair at the Gregory House. Furthermore, visitors who attempt to steal artifacts from the town report suffering intense, relentless bad luck until the items are mailed back to the park rangers.

#location
Bodie State Historic Park is located off Highway 270 in Mono County, California, east of the Sierra Nevada range.

#title
The Restless Spirits of the Whaley House

#summary
Built on the site of a former execution ground in San Diego, this historic home became a site of repeated family tragedy and lingering supernatural presences.

#details
Commissioned in 1857 by Thomas Whaley in Old Town San Diego, the Greek Revival house was constructed on land that had previously served as the town gallows, where a notorious thief known as "Yankee Jim" was hanged. The Whaley family endured numerous hardships and tragic deaths while living in the home, which also briefly functioned as a general store and county courthouse. Today, visitors and docents report numerous ghostly encounters, ranging from heavy footsteps and disembodied voices to the phantom apparition of Yankee Jim and members of the Whaley family.

#location
The Whaley House Museum is located at 2476 San Diego Avenue in San Diego, California.

#title
The White Witch of Niles Canyon

#summary
Drivers navigating a winding mountain highway frequently report encounters with a spectral woman in white who vanishes without a trace or intervenes to prevent deadly accidents.

#details
For decades, motorists driving along Niles Canyon Road have reported encountering a ghostly young woman dressed in white along the dark, winding asphalt. Local folklore depicts her as a phantom hitchhiker or wandering spirit who appears suddenly in the roadway or in the back seats of passing cars, only to vanish into thin air as the vehicle approaches bridges or dangerous curves. In one notable encounter, a late-night driver speeding along the ravine felt an invisible hand grasp his shoulder and force him to brake just before a treacherous turn, saving him from a catastrophic crash.

#location
Niles Canyon Road is a seven-mile stretch of State Route 84 running alongside Alameda Creek between Fremont and Sunol, California.

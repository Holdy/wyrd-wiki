The Phantom in Blue at the Moss Beach Distillery

Summary

A tragic affair between a married woman and a piano player in the 1920s ended in murder along the rugged coast, leaving her spirit to eternally haunt the seaside establishment.

Details

Decades ago, a sophisticated piano player working at a popular coastal establishment began an illicit romance with a naive, married young woman who always dressed in blue. The secret rendezvous came to a violent end when the woman was killed while walking on the beach below the restaurant with her lover, who survived the assault. Ever since, diners and staff have reported unexplained occurrences such as mysterious phone calls, levitating checkbooks, doors locking from the inside, and occasional sightings of a sorrowful female apparition wandering the premises.

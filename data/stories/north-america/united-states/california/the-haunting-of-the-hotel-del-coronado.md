#title
The Haunting of the Hotel del Coronado

#summary
In November 1892, a young woman checked into the historic Hotel del Coronado under an alias and died under mysterious circumstances, with her spirit purportedly remaining on the property ever since.

#details
A young woman arrived alone at the seaside resort in Coronado in late November 1892, checking in under a false name while anxiously waiting for someone to arrive. After five days of waiting, she was found dead from a gunshot wound in her room, and newspapers dubbed her the "Beautiful Stranger" before she was finally identified as Kate Morgan. Ever since, guests and staff have reported numerous paranormal phenomena around the hotel, including phantom sightings of a woman dressed in black, flickering lights, and sudden chills.

#location
Hotel del Coronado is located at 1500 Orange Avenue in Coronado, California.

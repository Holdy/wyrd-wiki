#title
The Tragic Search of Alice Flagg

#summary
A heartbroken young woman denied marriage to her true love died of a fever after her engagement ring was thrown away, and her ghost now wanders her former estate and churchyard searching for the token.

#details
In 1849, Alice Flagg lived with her family at The Hermitage in Murrells Inlet, South Carolina. She fell deeply in love with John Braddock, a common lumberman, much to the fury of her tyrannical brother and mother who demanded she marry within the aristocracy. When Alice was sent away to boarding school in Charleston, she secretly kept an engagement ring on a ribbon around her neck. Upon falling gravely ill and returning home, her brother discovered the ring, tore it from her neck in a rage, and threw it into a creek. Alice died shortly thereafter in profound grief, and ever since, her spirit has been seen roaming the grounds and All Saints' churchyard in a white dress, endlessly searching for her lost ring.

#location
All Saints’ Waccamaw Episcopal Church graveyard is located off Kings River Road near Pawleys Island in Georgetown County, South Carolina.

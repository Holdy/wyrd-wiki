#title
The Ghost of Zoe

#summary
Grieving the loss of her sister, spinster Zoe St. Amand haunts her former Queen Street home, now operating as a historic Charleston restaurant.

#details
Zoe St. Amand and her sister Elizabeth lived together as spinsters for over sixty years in a Victorian-era house on Queen Street in Charleston. After Elizabeth passed away following a brief illness, Zoe struggled deeply with the loss and was eventually moved to a nursing home, where she died in 1954. Decades later, when the building was renovated and opened as Poogan's Porch restaurant, Zoe's spirit reportedly made her displeasure known. Staff and patrons have frequently encountered her apparition—peering around corners, walking through dining rooms, stepping through the front door like a customer, and occasionally startling or pushing guests.

#location
Poogan's Porch restaurant at 72 Queen Street in Charleston, South Carolina.

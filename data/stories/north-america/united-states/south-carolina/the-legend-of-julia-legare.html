The Legend of Julia Legare

Summary

Entombed alive after falling into a death-like coma in the mid-19th century, young Julia Legare's spirit is said to repeatedly unlatch and unhinge her family mausoleum door.

Details

In the mid-1800s, Julia Legare fell severely ill while visiting family on Edisto Island and slipped into a coma. Believing her to be dead, the local physician declared her deceased, and she was hurriedly buried in the family mausoleum at the Edisto Island Presbyterian Church. Fifteen years later, when the tomb was opened for another family member, Julia's remains were found crumpled at the base of the door, revealing the horrifying truth that she had woken up from her coma inside the tomb. Ever since, the heavy marble door of the mausoleum has continually swung open, resisting chains, locks, and even heavy machinery, leading locals to believe Julia's ghost guards the tomb to ensure no door is ever placed on it again.

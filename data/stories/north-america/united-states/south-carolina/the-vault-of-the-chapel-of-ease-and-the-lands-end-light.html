The Vault of the Chapel of Ease and the Lands End Light

Summary

Following an 1861 wartime desecration of an elaborate family mausoleum on St. Helena Island, unexplainable phenomena and a mysterious phantom lantern light have haunted the local roads and churchyard.

Details

Built around 1740, the Chapel of Ease on St. Helena Island was abandoned when Union troops invaded in November 1861. A decade earlier, Edgar and Eliza Fripp had been interred in an elaborate stone mausoleum in the church graveyard. During the occupation, soldiers broke into the vault in search of treasure, ruining the door, which workers subsequently sealed with bricks. Bizarrely, the next morning the bricks were found neatly stacked outside the vault, a cycle that repeated despite multiple attempts to seal it, convincing workers supernatural forces were at work. Nearby along Lands End Road, drivers have long reported encountering the "Lands End Light," a solitary floating lantern light that approaches vehicles before abruptly vanishing into thin air.

#title
Jocassee valley under Lake Jocassee

#summary
Flooded by Duke Power in 1973. Attakulla Lodge lies about 300 ft down, along with a girls' camp, a bridge and Mount Carmel Cemetery, whose headstones divers can still read.

#location
Oconee/Pickens counties, SC

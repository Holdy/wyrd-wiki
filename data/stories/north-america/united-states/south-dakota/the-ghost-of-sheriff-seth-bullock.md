#title
The Ghost of Sheriff Seth Bullock

#summary
Guests and staff at a historic Deadwood hotel frequently report eerie encounters with its original builder and first sheriff, who still oversees the property from beyond the grave.

#details
Built in 1895 by Deadwood's first sheriff, Seth Bullock, and his partner Sol Star after a devastating fire destroyed their original hardware store, the Bullock Hotel is widely considered one of South Dakota's most haunted landmarks. Although Bullock passed away in 1919, numerous accounts from employees and visitors suggest he never truly left his post. Staff members have observed that unexplained paranormal activity—such as flying dishes, flickering lights, and activating showers—notably increases whenever workers stand idle, whistle, or hum. Guests have reported feeling unseen taps on their shoulders, hearing male voices call their names in empty hallways, and spotting the tall silhouette of the old lawman wandering the restaurant, basement, and upper-floor corridors.

#location
The Bullock Hotel is located at 633 Main Street in Deadwood, South Dakota.

The Haunted Woodland of Sica Hollow

Summary

Native American folklore warns against a deep, dark woodland canyon where ancient bloodshed and an evil wanderer left behind unquiet spirits, strange lights, and red waters.

Details

Sica Hollow State Park, whose name derives from the Dakota Sioux word Sica meaning "evil" or "bad," is steeped in dark legends dating back long before white settlement. According to tribal oral tradition, an evil stranger named Hand lured young braves away from traditional peaceful ways into committing violent acts and bloody battles within the ravine. The bloodshed stained the local stream red—giving the stream its historic name—before divine intervention from Thunderer destroyed Hand and cleansed the valley. To this day, locals and visitors report hearing strange unexplainable voices, seeing mysterious lights flashing in the creek bottoms, and encountering an eerie, unsettling atmosphere throughout the canyon.

The Ghostly Sheriff of the Bullock Hotel

Summary

Built in 1896 by Deadwood's first sheriff, the historic Bullock Hotel is said to be haunted by Seth Bullock, whose restless spirit polices the halls and startles guests with paranormal phenomena.

Details

After a devastating fire destroyed his hardware store in 1894, former Deadwood sheriff Seth Bullock and his business partner Sol Star constructed Deadwood's first luxury hotel, opening the grand sandstone building in 1896. Following Bullock's death in 1919, numerous guests and staff members reported unexplainable occurrences throughout the property. Witnesses have described seeing the tall, shadowy silhouette of Bullock patrolling the basement, restaurant, and hallways. Other reported phenomena include plates and glasses flying off tables, appliances turning on by themselves, phantom footsteps, and guests being tapped on the shoulder or hearing their names called in empty rooms.

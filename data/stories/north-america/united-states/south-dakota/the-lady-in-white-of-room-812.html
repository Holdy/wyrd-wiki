The Lady in White of Room 812

Summary

The gothic Hotel Alex Johnson in Rapid City is haunted by several spectral residents, most notably a tragic bride known as the Lady in White who haunts the eighth floor.

Details

Opened in 1928 and named after its founder Alex Carlton Johnson, the Hotel Alex Johnson has accumulated decades of documented paranormal reports compiled in a front-desk journal titled The Haunting. Among its ghostly inhabitants—which include the spirit of Johnson himself and a phantom young girl—the most renowned is the Lady in White. Local lore and guest accounts state that a young woman committed suicide by jumping from the window of room 812 on her wedding night in the 1970s. Visitors staying in room 812 frequently report waking up to find the window mysteriously opened, dresser contents thrown about, and an overwhelming sense of dread. Additionally, night staff and guests report hearing disembodied footsteps in high heels echoing through empty hallways and feeling unseen figures sit on the edge of their beds at night.

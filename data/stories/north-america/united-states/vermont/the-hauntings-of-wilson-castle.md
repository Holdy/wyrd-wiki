#title
The Hauntings of Wilson Castle

#summary
Built in 1885 in Proctor, Vermont, Wilson Castle is widely reputed to be haunted by the spirits of its former residents and visitors who encounter disembodied voices, cold spots, and phantom footsteps throughout its thirty-two rooms.

#details
Constructed in 1885 by Dr. John Johnson and later purchased in 1939 by radio engineer Herbert Lee Wilson, Wilson Castle is a sprawling 32-room estate combining Dutch neo-renaissance, Scottish baronial, and Queen Anne architectural styles. Over the decades, residents, tourists, and professional paranormal investigation teams have reported frequent eerie occurrences within the manor. Classic signs of haunting include cold drafts in the tower, objects moving independently in the basement, phantom footsteps on the grand staircase, and spectral apparitions roaming the halls. Local folklore and whispers also suggest hidden passageways and mysterious burials beneath the castle's stone floors, cementing its reputation as one of Vermont's premier haunted landmarks.

#location
Wilson Castle is located at 297 West Proctor Road in Proctor, Vermont.

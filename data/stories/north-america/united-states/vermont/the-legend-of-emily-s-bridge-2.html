The Legend of Emily's Bridge

Summary

After her lover failed to meet her to elope, a heartbroken young woman tragically ended her life at a historic covered bridge in Stowe. Today, her vengeful spirit haunts the roadway, leaving mysterious scratches on cars and startling visitors with eerie noises.

Details

Built in 1844, the Gold Brook Covered Bridge in Stowe, Vermont, is famously haunted by a spirit known as Emily. According to local lore, Emily arranged to meet her lover at the bridge to elope, but he never showed up. Overcome with grief and despair, she hanged herself from the rafters. Since then, motorists and visitors crossing the bridge have reported numerous paranormal phenomena, including disembodied screams, phantom lights, and inexplicable scratch marks appearing on their vehicles and bodies.

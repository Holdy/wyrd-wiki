#title
The Apparition at the Norwich Inn

#summary
The historic Norwich Inn, dating back to 1797, is said to be haunted by the spirit of a former innkeeper who ran a bootleg liquor operation during Prohibition and now wanders the upper floors and dining room in a black formal gown.

#details
Established in 1797 on land owned by Colonel Jasper Murdock, the Norwich Inn in Norwich, Vermont, carries a rich history that includes entertaining President James Monroe in 1817. In 1920, the property was purchased by Charles and Mary "Ma" Walker. According to local legend, after her husband's death, Ma Walker continued operating the tavern by selling bootleg liquor from the basement throughout Prohibition until the mid-1930s. Following her passing, her spirit has reportedly been seen gliding along the upper floors and wandering the parlor or dining room dressed in a black formal gown, before disappearing into the library. Guests staying in room 20 and other areas have also reported unexplained phenomena such as self-flushing toilets, faucets turning on and off by themselves, and rocking chairs moving without anyone sitting in them.

#location
The Norwich Inn located at the corner of Main Street and Beaver Meadow Road in Norwich, Vermont.

#title
The Curse of the Deer Park Tavern

#summary
After taking an unfortunate tumble into the mud outside St. Patrick's Inn, author Edgar Allan Poe allegedly laid a curse upon the historic building in Newark.

#details
In the mid-19th century, renowned author Edgar Allan Poe arrived by carriage on a rainy night at St. Patrick's Inn in Newark. As he stepped down from the vehicle, he tripped and fell face-first into the mud outside the entrance. Furious over the mishap, Poe reportedly cursed the establishment, declaring that anyone who entered its doors would be compelled to return. Although the original log structure burned down in 1851 and was subsequently replaced by a brick hotel that eventually became the Deer Park Tavern, local legend maintains that Poe's hex endures alongside the spirits of travelers, surveyors, and guests from centuries past.

#location
Deer Park Tavern located at the corner of New London Road and West Main Street in Newark, Delaware.

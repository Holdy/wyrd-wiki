The Phantoms of Fort Delaware

Summary

Built in the 19th century and used as a brutal Civil War prison camp, Fort Delaware on Pea Patch Island is widely reported to be haunted by the spirits of Confederate prisoners and a mysterious "blue man."

Details

Serving as a coastal defense fortress and later a massive prison camp housing nearly 13,000 Confederate prisoners at once—with thousands dying under harsh conditions—Fort Delaware on Pea Patch Island has accumulated decades of ghost lore. Paranormal investigators and visitors have reported disembodied footsteps, doors opening and closing on their own, chilling whispers, and the apparition of a "blue man" captured in photographs within the historic brick barracks.

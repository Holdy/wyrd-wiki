#title
The Spirits of Pea Patch Island

#summary
Fort Delaware, a former 19th-century military fortification and Civil War prison camp on Pea Patch Island, is widely reputed to be haunted by the restless spirits of prisoners and guards.

#details
Constructed in the 19th century to guard the ports of Wilmington and Philadelphia, Fort Delaware famously served as a grim prisoner-of-war camp for Confederate soldiers during the American Civil War. Due to harsh overcrowding and poor conditions, thousands of men suffered and perished on the grounds. Over the decades following its abandonment and conversion into a state park, visitors and paranormal investigators have reported frequent supernatural phenomena. Encounters include phantom footsteps echoing through empty stone corridors, self-opening doors, shadowy figures, and sightings of a mysterious "blue man" spectral apparition captured in doorways and photographs.

#location
Fort Delaware State Park on Pea Patch Island in the Delaware River.

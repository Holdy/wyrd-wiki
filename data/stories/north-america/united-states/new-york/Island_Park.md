#title
Weeping icon of Island Park

#summary
A cheap print of the Sorrowful Mother wept in a Long Island home in March 1960, drawing thousands of visitors before it was moved to the cathedral.

#location
Catsounis family home, Island Park, New York (icon now at St Paul's Greek Orthodox Cathedral, Hempstead), USA

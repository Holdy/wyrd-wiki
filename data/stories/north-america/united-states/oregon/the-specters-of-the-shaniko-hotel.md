#title
The Specters of the Shaniko Hotel

#summary
The historic Shaniko Hotel in Oregon's high desert ghost town is said to be haunted by a playful phantom child and lingering guests from its boomtown past.

#details
Established around 1900 during Shaniko's rise as a major wool-shipping hub, the red-brick hotel survived various booms, busts, and long-term closures before reopening under the care of local volunteer firefighters. Over the years, staff and guests have reported numerous spectral encounters, including the phantom scent of perfume in Room 14 attributed to a woman named Nettie, bathroom doors mysteriously holding shut, and a ghostly young girl named Amelia whose blue rubber ball appears to move on its own across the hallways.

#location
The Shaniko Hotel is located near the intersection of 4th Street and E Street in Shaniko, Oregon.

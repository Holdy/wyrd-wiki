#title
The Haunting of Wolf Creek Inn

#summary
The historic Wolf Creek Inn in Southern Oregon is widely rumored to be haunted by friendly spirits that move furniture, slam doors, and play phantom music.

#details
Built in 1883 as a stagecoach stop along the Applegate Trail, Wolf Creek Inn has hosted numerous historical figures and travelers over the decades. Visitors and staff frequently report unexplained paranormal activity throughout the 140-year-old building, including disembodied voices, piano music playing in empty rooms, chairs moving on their own, and baking pans flying across the kitchen. Room eight is noted by paranormal investigators as a particular hot spot for ghostly energy, though the overall atmosphere is viewed as mischievous rather than malicious.

#location
Wolf Creek Inn is located at 100 Front Street in Wolf Creek, Oregon.

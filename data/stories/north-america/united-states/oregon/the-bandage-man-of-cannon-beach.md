#title
The Bandage Man of Cannon Beach

#summary
A terrifying, blood-soaked apparition known as the Bandage Man haunts lonely stretches of coastal highway near Cannon Beach. He terrorizes motorists by climbing into truck beds and violently pounding on vehicle roofs.

#details
The legend of the Bandage Man centers around a phantom wrapped from head to toe in dirty, bloody gauze, accompanied by the overwhelming stench of rotting and burnt flesh. According to local folklore, he frequently targets drivers and teenagers traveling along Highway 101 near Cannon Beach. Witnesses have reported hearing a heavy thump on their tailgates or truck beds, followed by the entity climbing aboard, shaking the vehicle, and violently pounding against the roof or windows. When the motorists speed away in a panic and inspect their vehicles upon reaching town, they find no trace of the attacker except for a lingering foul odor and scraps of dirty gauze.

#location
Highway 101 between the Highway 26 junction and the northern entrance to Cannon Beach, Oregon.

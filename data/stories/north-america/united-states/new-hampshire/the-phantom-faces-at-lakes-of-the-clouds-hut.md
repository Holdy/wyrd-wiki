#title
The Phantom Faces at Lakes of the Clouds Hut

#summary
An Appalachian Mountain Club crew member sent to open the snow-bound Lakes of the Clouds Hut on Mount Washington was discovered cowering under a sink and clutching an axe after encountering grotesque apparitions peering through securely boarded-up windows.

#details
In the spring, an AMC crew member named George was sent up to the Lakes of the Clouds Hut on Mount Washington with a two-way radio to assess winter damage and prepare the cabin for the upcoming season. Because of the brutal winter winds, the hut's windows were tightly boarded up from the outside. When George failed to respond to evening radio check-ins, a crew hiked up the mountain the following morning to check on him. Finding the cabin doors unlocked and his gear left on the dining room floor, they searched the dark interior until they heard a whimper coming from beneath the kitchen sink. There they found George huddled in absolute terror, clutching an axe in his white knuckles and repeatedly begging them to get him off the mountain. After recovering in a hospital weeks later, George explained that shortly after arriving, he felt an unseen presence approach him from behind. When he spun around, he saw a distorted, grotesque face pressed against the dining room windowpane—despite the heavy wooden boards covering it from the outside. As he watched in horror, the exact same face appeared across every window in the room, seeming to melt right through the barriers into the cabin before he blacked out from fear.

#location
Lakes of the Clouds Hut, located near the summit of Mount Washington in New Hampshire.

#title
The Ghost of Crawford Notch

#summary
Betrayed and abandoned by her fiancé during the harsh winter of 1778, a young servant named Nancy Barton froze to death in the White Mountains while trying to track him down. Her restless spirit is said to still wander the rugged landscape near Crawford Notch.

#details
In the late 1770s, a young woman named Nancy Barton was employed as a servant in the household of Colonel Joseph Whipple in Jefferson, New Hampshire. Betrayed by her fiancé—a hired man who deserted her and reportedly stole her savings—Nancy set out on foot to pursue him southward through the unforgiving wilderness of the White Mountains. As she pressed into Crawford Notch, she was caught in a brutal winter storm. Searchers eventually discovered her frozen body near a stream, which was subsequently named Nancy’s Brook, not far from where the Notchland Inn now stands in Hart's Location. She was buried nearby in what is known as Nancy's Grave. According to local legend, Nancy never completed her pursuit in spirit; travelers, hikers, and drivers passing through Crawford Notch frequently report hearing disembodied weeping, feeling footsteps following them in the woods, or catching a glimpse of a young woman in eighteenth-century attire wandering the roadside.

#location
Nancy's Grave and Nancy's Brook are situated in Crawford Notch near the Notchland Inn in Hart's Location, New Hampshire.

#title
The Haunting of the Chase House

#summary
A historic 18th-century home and former orphanage in Portsmouth, New Hampshire, is said to be haunted by the restless spirit of a young child.

#details
Built in 1762 by merchant Stephen Chase, the stately yellow home later operated as the Chase Home for Children orphanage in the late 19th and early 20th centuries. In August 1893, a severe diphtheria outbreak struck the children living there, resulting in the tragic death of a young orphan girl. Over the years, local ghost lore expanded to claim that a young girl took her life in an upstairs room, leading to decades of paranormal reports, strange glowing orbs, and sightings of a ghostly child peering out from the windows.

#location
The historic Chase House is located at 1 Middle Street (at the corner of Court and Washington Streets) in Portsmouth, New Hampshire.

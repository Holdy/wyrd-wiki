#title
The Haunting of Seven Sisters Road

#summary
Restless spirits of seven sisters murdered by their brother haunt a winding country road where motorists experience inexplicable car trouble and ghostly encounters.

#details
According to local legend, a young man living on a farm southeast of Nebraska City grew enraged after an argument with his family. While his parents were away, he lured or forced his seven sisters to the top of surrounding hills and hanged them from trees one by one. Years later, after the road was built and the hanging trees were removed, the path became known as Seven Sisters Road. Today, drivers traveling along this stretch report car engines stalling, headlights mysteriously dimming, windows rolling up and down on their own, and the sounds of terrified women screaming for help, along with sightings of shadowy figures and red glowing eyes in the darkness.

#location
Seven Sisters Road, also known as L Street, is located approximately five miles southeast of Nebraska City in Otoe County, Nebraska.

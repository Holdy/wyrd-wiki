#title
The Haunting of the Captain Bailey House

#summary
Visitors to this historic seven-gabled home in Brownville report that doors refuse to stay shut due to the lingering presence of a Civil War captain and his wife.

#details
The Captain Bailey House was originally built near the Missouri River by Civil War officer Captain Benson M. Bailey. When the river began shifting its course and endangering the structure in 1877, the entire brick building was dismantled and rebuilt brick-by-brick at its current downtown location. According to local lore, Captain Bailey and his wife Rebecca were poisoned by a jealous neighbor. Today, locals and visitors report enduring supernatural phenomena in the house, most notably interior doors that mysteriously refuse to remain closed.

#location
The Captain Bailey House Museum is located at 404 Main Street in Brownville, Nebraska.

The Sallie House Haunting

Summary

A young family renting a house in Atchison in the 1990s reported violent paranormal attacks and poltergeist activity allegedly tied to the spirit of a young girl who died during a botched medical procedure.

Details

In the early 1990s, tenants renting a house at 508 North 2nd Street experienced unexplained phenomena, including toys scattered about, appliances turning on and off by themselves, and spontaneous small fires. Things escalated when the male tenant began suffering from physical attacks, including mysterious red welts and deep scratches appearing on his body. Psychics consulted later suggested the house was haunted by both a mischievous young girl named Sallie and a malevolent adult spirit.

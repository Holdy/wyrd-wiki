#title
The Specters of the Fargo Theatre

#summary
Opened in downtown Fargo in 1926, the historic Fargo Theatre is said to be haunted by a lady in white, phantom projection noises, and shadowy basement figures.

#details
Designed in the Art Deco style and opened on March 15, 1926, the Fargo Theatre served as a silent film palace and community hub before being restored in the late 20th century. Over the years, patrons, staff, and paranormal investigators have documented eerie phenomena throughout the building. The most famous spirit is the "Lady in White," a spectral figure reportedly seen lingering near the balcony or upper rows of the auditorium, accompanied by faint humming or sudden chills. In the projection booth, staff have reported knocking sounds, flickering lights, and the ghostly sound of film reels spinning when no equipment is running. Additionally, the maze-like basement is notorious for oppressive atmospheres, shadowy darting figures, and disembodied voices telling visitors to be quiet.

#location
The Fargo Theatre is located on Broadway Street in downtown Fargo, North Dakota.

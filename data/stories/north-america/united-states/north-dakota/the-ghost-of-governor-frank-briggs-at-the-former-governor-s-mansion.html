The Ghost of Governor Frank Briggs at the Former Governor's Mansion

Summary

Staff and visitors at the historic Victorian mansion have reported unexplained footsteps, moving curtains, and self-operating doors in the master bedroom where Governor Frank Briggs passed away from tuberculosis in 1898.

Details

Built in 1884, the Victorian mansion served as the official residence for North Dakota governors until 1960 before becoming a museum managed by the State Historical Society. In 1898, Governor Frank Briggs died in the master bedroom from tuberculosis before completing his term or achieving his political goals. Local folklore and museum accounts suggest that his unfulfilled ambitions tethered him to the home. Staff and visitors have repeatedly reported paranormal activity concentrated around his former death room, including mysterious footsteps, curtains swaying without a draft, and doors opening and closing on their own accord.

#title
The Legend of White Lady's Lane

#summary
The ghost of a young woman named Anna Story wanders a rural North Dakota lane in a white nightgown every Halloween night. Murdered by a rejected suitor, her restless spirit searches eternally for peace.

#details
Long ago near Leyden and Walhalla, North Dakota, a young woman named Anna Story lived with her family. A peddler named Sam Kalil sought to marry Anna, but after a dispute over a prior bargain, Kalil forced his way into their home and shot Anna dead in her nightclothes. Though Kalil was eventually apprehended and sentenced to life imprisonment, Anna's spirit refused to rest. Today, her ghost—known as the White Lady—is said to haunt a misty rural stretch of road known as White Lady's Lane near Eddie's Bridge, wandering in her flowing white nightgown every Halloween night in search of the peace she never found in life.

#location
The hauntings occur along White Lady's Lane near Eddie's Bridge in Walhalla, North Dakota.

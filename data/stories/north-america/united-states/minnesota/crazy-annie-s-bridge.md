#title
Crazy Annie's Bridge

#summary
Local legend claims that a World War I widow drowned her three children in the creek beneath a bridge in Henderson before taking her own life, leaving her grieving and vengeful spirit to haunt the roadway.

#details
According to local folklore in Henderson, Minnesota, a woman named Annie lived in a small home near a creek with her husband and three children during World War I. Following the loss of her husband, overwhelming grief reportedly drove Annie to madness; she drowned her three children in the creek beneath the local bridge and hanged herself from a nearby tree, earning the enduring moniker "Crazy Annie." Ever since, locals and visitors traveling the gravel road toward the bridge have reported chilling paranormal encounters. Witnesses have heard disembodied anguished cries, unexplainable footsteps, and radio static, while others have experienced their car batteries suddenly draining, doors mysteriously locking, or ghostly, bodiless faces and handprints emerging from the darkness.

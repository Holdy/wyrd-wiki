#title
The Haunting of the Palmer House Hotel

#summary
The historic Palmer House Hotel in Sauk Centre is said to be haunted by spirit children and restless former employees who interact with guests.

#details
Investigators and guests visiting the historic Palmer House Hotel in Sauk Centre have long reported paranormal activity, ranging from mysterious footsteps and objects moving on their own to flashlights turning on and off in response to questions in the basement. Some report feeling the sensation of ghostly hands pulling at their legs while they sleep in their rooms.

#location
The Palmer House Hotel is situated at 500 Sinclair Lewis Avenue in Sauk Centre, Minnesota.

#title
The Grieving Mother of Timber Lake Road

#summary
A distraught mother whose children were murdered wanders a dark Minnesota country road searching for their killer. Phantom children and eerie wails are also frequently reported at her nearby gravesite.

#details
According to local folklore, a woman once returned to her home near Timber Lake Road to find that her children had been brutally murdered. Overcome by grief and devastation, she hanged herself. Since that tragic day, her ghostly figure has haunted the roadway, endlessly searching for the person responsible for her children's deaths. Drivers passing through the area have also reported unsettling phenomena near the nearby Colfax Cemetery, including the apparition of ghostly children playing among the headstones, howling dogs, and the sound of a woman wailing in the night.

#location
Timber Lake Road is located near New London, Minnesota, passing right by the Colfax Cemetery.

#title
The Whispers of the Wabasha Street Caves

#summary
Carved into sandstone cliffs along the Mississippi River, the historic Wabasha Street Caves in St. Paul are rumored to be haunted by Prohibition-era gangsters and a mysterious lady in white.

#details
Originally mined in the 1840s and later transformed into a 1930s speakeasy called Castle Royal frequented by underworld figures, the caves are steeped in dark legends, including an unrecorded mob-related triple murder during a card game. Visitors and tour guides frequently report hearing disembodied voices, seeing shadowy figures in vintage suits, and spotting a "Lady in White" near the old dance floor who vanishes into thin air.

#location
The Wabasha Street Caves are located at 215 Wabasha Street South in St. Paul, Minnesota.

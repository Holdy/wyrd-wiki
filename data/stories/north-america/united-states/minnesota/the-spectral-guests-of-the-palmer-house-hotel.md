#title
The Spectral Guests of the Palmer House Hotel

#summary
Built on the ashes of a burned hotel in Sauk Centre, this historic inn is famously haunted by a boy bouncing a ball, a tall man standing over beds, and even the spirit of local Nobel laureate Sinclair Lewis.

#details
Originally opened in 1901 by Ralph and Christena Palmer after the town's original hotel burned down, the Palmer House Hotel in Sauk Centre quickly became a bustling local landmark and even employed a young Sinclair Lewis before he won the Nobel Prize. Over the decades, guests and staff have reported numerous paranormal occurrences, including a young boy frequently seen bouncing a ball in the halls, a former prostitute, and a towering man appearing at bedside. Rooms 11 and 17 are particularly notorious among visitors and paranormal investigators for unexplainable footsteps, moving objects, and eerie presences.

#location
The Palmer House Hotel is located at 500 Sinclair Lewis Avenue in Sauk Centre, Minnesota.

#title
The Pittee Sisters of the St. Augustine Lighthouse

#summary
Three young construction foreman's daughters tragically drowned in 1873 when their railway cart tipped into the water, and their playful spirits are said to still haunt the lighthouse and keepers' house.

#details
During the construction of the new St. Augustine Lighthouse tower in July 1873, the young daughters of superintendent Hezekiah Pittee—along with another local girl—frequently rode a supply railway cart down to the water as a makeshift rollercoaster. On July 10, 1873, with the stopping block removed, the cart flipped into the water, trapping and drowning three of the girls. In the decades since, both visitors and staff have reported numerous ghostly phenomena attributed to the children, including mysterious giggling in the empty tower, shoelaces tied together on the stairs, and electrical spikes on EMF meters in the charred basement of the keepers' house.

#location
The St. Augustine Lighthouse and Maritime Museum is located at 81 Lighthouse Avenue in St. Augustine, Florida.

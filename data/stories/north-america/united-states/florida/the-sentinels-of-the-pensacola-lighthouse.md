#title
The Sentinels of the Pensacola Lighthouse

#summary
Standing tall since 1859 on the Pensacola Naval Air Station, this historic lighthouse survived Civil War bombardment and severe storms, and is widely rumored to be haunted by a runaway slave and a former murder victim.

#details
Authorized by Congress and completed in 1858, the Pensacola Lighthouse weathered the trials of the Civil War—including being targeted by Union artillery cannon fire from Fort Pickens in 1861—as well as hurricanes, lightning strikes, and earthquakes. Beyond its rich military and maritime history, local folklore maintains that the lighthouse is haunted by restless phantoms, most notably a runaway slave who sought refuge in the tower and the spirit of an earlier murder victim whose tragic demise left an enduring mark on the structure.

#location
The Pensacola Lighthouse is located on the Pensacola Naval Air Station in Pensacola, Florida.

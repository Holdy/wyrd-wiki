The Ghosts of the I-4 Dead Zone

Summary

Unexplained apparitions, ghostly hitchhikers, and disembodied voices haunt a stretch of Interstate 4 where a 19th-century yellow fever cemetery was paved over. Motorists traversing this highway frequently experience terrifying paranormal phenomena and tragic accidents.

Details

In the late 19th century, a group of German immigrants established St. Joseph’s Catholic Colony along the southern shore of Lake Monroe. In 1887, a devastating yellow fever outbreak struck the settlement, claiming multiple lives and leaving the deceased buried in the surrounding woods without proper last rites. When Interstate 4 was constructed years later, this burial ground was bulldozed and paved over without relocating the graves. Ever since, drivers crossing the I-4 bridge experience bizarre electronic interference, floating orbs of light, and apparitions of men and women standing by the roadside or hitchhiking, only to vanish into thin air.

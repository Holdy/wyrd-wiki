The Spirits of the St. Augustine Lighthouse

Summary

Built in the 1870s on Anastasia Island, this historic brick lighthouse and its keeper's house are famously haunted by the children who tragically drowned in a construction accident and a former keeper who fell to his death.

Details

Construction of the current 165-foot tower began in 1871 and was completed in 1874. During the building process on July 10, 1873, a rail cart carrying materials rolled into the water, resulting in the drowning deaths of two of the site supervisor's daughters, Mary and Eliza Pittee, along with an unnamed companion. Ever since, visitors and staff have reported hearing children's laughter, small footsteps, and doors moving on their own in the keeper's house. Furthermore, stories link shadows and staircase phenomena to Joseph Andreu, a head keeper who fell to his death from an earlier tower while painting it in 1859.

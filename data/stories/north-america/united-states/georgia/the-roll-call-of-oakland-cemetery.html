The Roll Call of Oakland Cemetery

Summary

Atlanta's historic Oakland Cemetery is haunted by the restless echoes of Civil War soldiers and phantom roll calls echoing through its Victorian grounds.

Details

Chartered in 1850 and sprawling across nearly 50 acres, Oakland Cemetery is Atlanta's oldest public burial ground and holds the graves of thousands of unknown Confederate and Union soldiers. Visitors exploring the Confederate section after dark have experienced bone-chilling drops in temperature, phantom drumming, and eerie roll calls where disembodied voices call out lists of names, with unseen entities responding "Here!" Others have reported terrifying visions of ghostly soldiers hanging from ancient oak branches or stone monuments coming to life in the mist.

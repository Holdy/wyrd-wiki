#title
The Spirits of The Marshall House

#summary
Savannah’s oldest operating hotel, which served as a Civil War field hospital, is haunted by the lingering spirits of amputee soldiers, playful ghost children, and its original owner, Mary Marshall.

#details
Built in 1851 by Mary Magdalene Leaver Marshall, the Marshall House on East Broughton Street was occupied by the Union Army under General William Tecumseh Sherman and used as a field hospital during the Civil War. Visitors and staff have reported numerous paranormal occurrences over the decades, including phantom sounds of a typewriter from the room where author Joel Chandler Harris once stayed, occasional whiffs of rotting flesh in rooms tied to the hospital era, phantom children playing and bouncing balls in the hallways, and sightings of an amputee soldier walking the halls while holding his missing arm.

#location
The Marshall House is located at 123 E Broughton St, Savannah, GA 31401.

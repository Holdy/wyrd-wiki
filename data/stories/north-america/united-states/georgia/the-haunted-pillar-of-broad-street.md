#title
The Haunted Pillar of Broad Street

#summary
A stone pillar standing in downtown Augusta is said to be cursed by a traveling preacher, bringing destruction and death to anyone who dares to move or damage it.

#details
The pillar is the sole surviving remnant of the old city market that straddled Broad Street from 1830 until a winter tornado destroyed the building in February 1878. According to local folklore, a traveling preacher was denied permission to evangelize at the market and placed a curse upon it, prophesying that a great storm would destroy the market except for a single pillar, and that anyone who attempted to move or destroy that remaining pillar would be struck dead. Over the decades, the pillar has been struck by automobiles and toppled by cotton bales, yet the legend persists as a cornerstone of Augusta folklore.

#location
The Haunted Pillar stands on the southeast corner of Fifth and Broad Streets in Augusta, Georgia.

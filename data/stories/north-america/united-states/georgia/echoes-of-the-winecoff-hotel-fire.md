#title
Echoes of the Winecoff Hotel Fire

#summary
Decades after the deadliest hotel fire in U.S. history, current occupants and passersby in downtown Atlanta report hearing phantom screams, smelling smoke, and seeing trapped faces in the windows.

#details
Opened in 1913 as the Winecoff Hotel at 176 Peachtree Street NW, the building was advertised as completely fireproof. However, on December 7, 1946, a catastrophic fire swept through the structure, claiming more than 100 lives. Although the building has since been renovated and renamed the Ellis Hotel, the tragedy continues to echo through time. Current guests and staff report that fire alarms routinely trigger at 2:48 a.m.—the exact moment the blaze began—while visitors outside occasionally spot terror-stricken faces pressed against the upper-story windows crying for help.

#location
The Ellis Hotel (formerly the Winecoff Hotel) is located at 176 Peachtree Street NW in Atlanta, Georgia.

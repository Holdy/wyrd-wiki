The Apparition of Room 24 at Concord's Colonial Inn

Summary

Room 24 of Concord's Colonial Inn—a historic building that once served as a makeshift Revolutionary War hospital—has been famously haunted by a shadowy gray apparition since 1966.

Details

During the American Revolution, parts of the historic structure that now make up Concord's Colonial Inn were used as a medical facility where wounded soldiers were treated and many perished. The building's paranormal reputation gained widespread attention in 1966 when honeymooners M.P. and Judith Fellenz checked into Room 24 on the second floor. In the dead of night, Mrs. Fellenz awoke to find a grayish, shadowy figure standing beside the bed before it drifted toward the fireplace and vanished. In the decades following this first documented sighting, guests and staff have reported numerous other hauntings, including phantom soldiers, children's footsteps in the hallways, and restless spirits moving through the historic inn.

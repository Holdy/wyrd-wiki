The Ghosts of the Barnstable House

Summary

Relocated to Cape Cod in the 18th century, the historic Barnstable House is reputed to harbor up to eleven spirits, including a young girl who drowned in the basement well and a former sea captain.

Details

Originally built in Scituate in 1713 and reassembled on Main Street in Barnstable in 1716, the Barnstable House has a long history intertwined with local folklore. Over the centuries, occupants and visitors have reported encounters with up to eleven different ghosts haunting the premises. Among the most prominent tales is that of a young child named Lucy, who according to legend drowned in a well located in the cellar. Other frequent apparitions include a woman in white and a former sea captain who once resided in the home. Paranormal occurrences documented at the site include full-bodied manifestations, phantom footsteps, unexplained knocking, and sudden, intense cold spots.

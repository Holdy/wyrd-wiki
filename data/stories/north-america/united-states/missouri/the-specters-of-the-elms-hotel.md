#title
The Specters of the Elms Hotel

#summary
Built in the late 19th century in Excelsior Springs, Missouri, the historic Elms Hotel is said to be haunted by a Prohibition-era gambler in the basement pool, a diligent 1920s maid on the third floor, and a distressed woman searching for her child.

#details
Opened initially in 1888 and rebuilt after devastating fires in 1898 and 1910, the Elms Hotel in Excelsior Springs became a luxurious mineral resort and a favored hideaway for gangsters such as Al Capone during Prohibition. Over the decades, staff and guests have reported persistent supernatural occurrences throughout the limestone building. In the basement lap pool—once part of a Prohibition-era speakeasy where illegal gambling and drinking took place—visitors report cold spots and the presence of a male spirit believed to be a gambler killed during a mob altercation. On the third floor, a benevolent spirit resembling a 1920s maid is frequently seen observing cleaning staff, while another spirit, a grieving woman searching for her child, startles guests by moving objects and roaming the halls.

#location
The Elms Hotel is located at 401 Regent Street in Excelsior Springs, Missouri.

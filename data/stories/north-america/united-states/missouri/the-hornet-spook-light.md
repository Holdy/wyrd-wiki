#title
The Hornet Spook Light

#summary
A mysterious, glowing orange orb has been witnessed traveling along a rural gravel road on the Missouri-Oklahoma border for well over a century, defying scientific explanation.

#details
For more than a hundred years, an anomalous glowing sphere known as the Hornet Spook Light has been reported along a four-mile stretch of gravel road—historically called Devil's Promenade—near Hornet, Missouri, and Quapaw, Oklahoma. Described variously as an orange ball of fire ranging from the size of a baseball to a basketball, the orb dances, spins, rises above treetops, and travels at high speeds before fading away. Local legends attribute the light to the spirits of a young Quapaw couple who plunged to their deaths to escape pursuing warriors, a grieving miner searching for his family with a lantern, or a decapitated Osage chief seeking his missing head. Although numerous scientific investigations, including studies by the Army Corps of Engineers, have attempted to explain the phenomenon through natural gas, will-o'-the-wisps, or shifting tectonic electrical charges, none have provided a conclusive answer.

#location
The four-mile gravel road known as Devil's Promenade, located southwest of Joplin, Missouri, near the hamlet of Hornet.

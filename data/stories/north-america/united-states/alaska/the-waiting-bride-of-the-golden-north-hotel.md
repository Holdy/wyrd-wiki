#title
The Waiting Bride of the Golden North Hotel

#summary
During the Klondike Gold Rush, a young woman named Mary reportedly locked herself away on the top floor of a Skagway hotel waiting for her fiancé who never returned, becoming a spectral fixture of the building.

#details
Built in 1898 during the height of the Klondike Gold Rush, the Golden North Hotel in Skagway became the setting for one of Alaska's most famous ghost tales. Local legend recounts that a prospector known as "Klondike Ike" arrived at the hotel with his fiancée, Mary. The next morning, Ike set off into the surrounding mountains to stake a gold claim and never came back. Heartbroken, Mary refused to leave her third-floor room, where she eventually passed away. Patrons and staff over the decades have claimed to see her shadowy apparition peering out of the third-floor windows or wandering the hotel's corridors, perpetually searching for her lost fiancé.

#location
The Golden North Hotel located at 3rd Avenue and Broadway in Skagway, Alaska.

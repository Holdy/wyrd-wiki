#title
The Phantoms of McCarthy Road

#summary
Travelers and workers along the McCarthy Road near Chitina frequently report encountering ghostly figures, phantom voices, and mysterious tombstones materializing along the roadside.

#details
After the shutdown of the historic Kennecott copper mines and the Copper River & Northwestern Railway, the surrounding Alaskan wilderness became steeped in local folklore. Motorists driving along the McCarthy Road—which closely follows the abandoned railway grade near Chitina—have repeatedly encountered paranormal phenomena. Travelers and construction crews have reported seeing phantom tombstones appear by the roadside only to vanish into thin air, alongside disembodied voices and the spectral figures of long-dead miners and children haunting the remote corridor.

#location
The hauntings occur along the McCarthy Road near Chitina, Alaska, specifically where the dirt route parallels the abandoned Copper River & Northwestern Railway grade.

#title
Lydia of the Red Onion Saloon

#summary
A preserved 1898 gold rush bordello in Skagway is said to be haunted by the spirit of a former sex worker named Lydia, who wanders the upstairs crib rooms leaving the scent of fresh lilacs.

#details
Built in 1898 during the Klondike Gold Rush, the Red Onion Saloon operated as a high-end dance hall and bordello featuring ten crib rooms upstairs where miners paid for the company of women selected via dolls displayed at the bar. According to local lore and employee accounts, the ghost of Lydia—a former prostitute who allegedly suffered from syphilis and died by suicide—haunts the second floor. Staff and visitors report sudden cold spots, white blurs, mysterious photographs, and the distinct fragrance of freshly cut lilacs as Lydia wanders the historic rooms.

#location
The Red Onion Saloon is located at 205 Broadway St in Skagway, Alaska.

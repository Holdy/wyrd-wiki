#title
The Spectral Madam of the Red Onion Saloon

#summary
A historic 1898 brothel and saloon in Skagway is said to be haunted by the spirit of a former madam who still tends to the building and its rooms.

#details
Opening its doors in 1898 to serve miners during the gold rush, the Red Onion Saloon in Skagway operated both as a saloon on the ground floor and a ten-room brothel upstairs, where madams used miniature dolls to indicate whether the rooms were occupied. Over the years, staff and visitors have reported numerous paranormal phenomena on the upper floor, most notably the ghost of a former resident named Lydia. Witnesses have described hearing heavy footsteps on the stairs, smelling strong waves of vintage perfume accompanied by sudden drops in temperature, and seeing a full apparition running down the hallway into the former madam's room. Witnesses also claim the spirit occasionally waters the indoor plants and maintains a watchful presence over the historical museum.

#location
The Red Onion Saloon at 201 Broadway in Skagway, Alaska.

#title
The Phantoms of the Old Talbott Tavern
#summary
A historic 18th-century inn in Bardstown is famously haunted by a robed figure resembling the notorious outlaw Jesse James and a mysterious lady in white.
#details
Operating since the late 18th century, the Old Talbott Tavern in Bardstown has accumulated numerous legends of supernatural activity, including unexplained moving objects, floating orbs, and phantom footsteps. Among its most frequent apparitions is a tall man in a long coat who has been identified by staff and visitors as the outlaw Jesse James, who reportedly stayed at the tavern while visiting local family. Another legendary specter is a sorrowful "lady in white" with long brown wavy hair who has been seen hovering over sleeping guests before floating out the window, as well as walking through the tavern's Audubon Dining Room.
#location
The Old Talbott Tavern is located at 107 West Stephen Foster Avenue in Bardstown, Kentucky.

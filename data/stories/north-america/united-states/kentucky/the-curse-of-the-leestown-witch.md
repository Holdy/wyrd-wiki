#title
The Curse of the Leestown Witch

#summary
In 1913, terror gripped the historic settlement of Leestown after a young mother's sudden death led to the discovery of hidden, chicken-feather-stuffed stockings and fears of a local witch's curse.

#details
In August 1913, Lillie Harp passed away in the working-class pioneer settlement of Leestown near Frankfort following a brief illness. A year earlier, her newborn infant had died mysteriously without medical explanation. Following Lillie's death, her mother, Margaret Coovert, unstitched wedding pillows she had previously gifted her daughter and discovered stockings packed tightly with chicken feathers hidden inside. Believing this was a sinister witchcraft item used to cast a fatal spell or create a "death crown," the family consulted about boiling the stockings to inflict pain upon the anonymous witch and planned to visit a clairvoyant in Louisville. Although the family later burned the stockings and attempted to publicly downplay the superstition, the chilling legend of the Leestown Witch left a lasting mark on local folklore.

#location
The historic pioneer settlement of Leestown, located in northern Frankfort, Kentucky, near the Buffalo Trace Distillery campus.

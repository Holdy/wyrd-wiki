#title
The Legend of Gore Orphanage

#summary
According to local folklore, a massive fire once engulfed an orphanage on Gore Orphanage Road in Vermilion, Ohio, killing dozens of children whose ghostly screams can still be heard echoing through the ruins. In reality, the legend conflates a historic surveyor's gore, a real nineteenth-century mansion fire, and distant highway traffic sounds.

#details
For over a century, travelers and teenagers visiting Gore Orphanage Road in Vermilion, Ohio, have shared terrifying accounts of glowing lights, ghostly apparitions, and the wailing cries of children. The legend claims that in the late 1800s or early 1900s, a horrific fire broke out at an orphanage, trapping children behind blocked doors and burning them alive. Over time, local folklore fused this myth with the history of the Swift Mansion (Swift's Hollow), the actual Light of Hope Orphanage operated by Reverend Johann Sprunger (which closed in 1916), and the devastating 1908 Collinwood school fire. Although historians have verified that no orphanage fire ever occurred on Gore Road—and that the eerie "cries" heard near the ruins are actually the high-pitched hum of truck traffic carrying across the distant Ohio Turnpike bridges—the legend of Gore Orphanage remains one of Ohio's most enduring and popular local ghost stories.

#location
The ruins of the Swift Mansion and the infamous Gore Orphanage Road are located just south of Vermilion, Ohio, near Sperry Road and the Vermilion River in Lorain County.

#title
The Spirited Guests of the Historic Buxton Inn

#summary
Established in 1812, Granville's Buxton Inn is said to be spiritually active, hosting the benevolent ghosts of former owners, a phantom cigar-smoking major, and a spectral cat.

#details
Operating continuously since the early nineteenth century as a tavern, stagecoach stop, and safe haven on the Underground Railroad, the Historic Buxton Inn in Granville has accumulated a rich legacy of paranormal accounts. Current inn management describes the building as "spiritually active" rather than haunted, noting that former owners and caretakers returned after death to watch over the property. Notable resident spirits include Ethel "Bonnie" Houston (known as the "Lady in Blue" and accompanied by the scent of gardenias), Major Buxton (signaled by the smell of cigar smoke), and a ghostly cat that reportedly jumps onto beds to cuddle guests staying in Room 9.

#location
The Historic Buxton Inn is located at 313 E. Broadway in Granville, Ohio.

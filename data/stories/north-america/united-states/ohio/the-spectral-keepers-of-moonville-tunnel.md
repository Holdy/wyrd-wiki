#title
The Spectral Keepers of Moonville Tunnel

#summary
Deep within the forests of Vinton County, the historic Moonville Tunnel is said to be haunted by former miners, railroad workers, and mysterious figures like the Engineer and the Lavender Lady.

#details
Built in 1856 as part of the Marietta and Cincinnati Railroad, the Moonville Tunnel in Zaleski State Forest saw numerous fatal accidents over more than a century of train operations before closing in 1987. Local folklore and legends quickly grew around the dark, isolated brick tunnel, with visitors and locals reporting sightings of a ghostly engineer holding a swinging lantern, the lingering scent of lavender associated with a female specter, and menacing encounters with a spirit known as "The Bully."

#location
Moonville Tunnel is located along the former rail trail off Hope-Moonville Road in Vinton County, Ohio.

The Ghost of Cling Clang at the Old Stone Store

Summary

An eccentric 19th-century clock repairman known as Cling Clang is said to haunt the historic Old Stone Store in Sullivan Harbor where he once lived and died.

Details

John Cling, nicknamed "Cling Clang" for the loud ringing noise his tin pots and metal stilts made as he leaped across fences and ditches, arrived in Sullivan, Maine from England. He made a living repairing watches and clocks for people along the coast from Bar Harbor to Calais, dressing in discarded salt bags and carrying his tools in a salt bag. During the winter months, Cling Clang lived in an overturned boat situated behind the old stone store in Sullivan Harbor. One cold February, he was found frozen to death underneath his boat. Decades later, after the granite building had served various commercial purposes and eventually became an antique shop and living quarters, the owners and visitors reported recurring supernatural phenomena, including unexplainable ringing doorbells, missing portraits, phantom footsteps, and the gray apparition of a man heading down the stairs.

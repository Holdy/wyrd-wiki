The Phantom Bride of Black's Woods Road

Summary

Motorists traveling along a winding stretch of highway at night encounter the spectral figure of a young woman named Catherine, who haunts the roadside seeking help. Those who ignore her face terrifying paranormal disruptions and fatal accidents.

Details

According to local folklore in Down East Maine, a young woman named Catherine perished in a tragic vehicle accident on Route 182—also known as Black's Woods Road—near Fox Pond and Catherine Mountain. Depending on the version of the legend, she died alongside her companion after their vehicle crashed, leaving her either severely disfigured or decapitated. Ever since, her restless ghost has haunted the heavily forested road, appearing as a woman in a white or blue dress standing near the tree line or flagging down passing vehicles for a ride. Motorists who stop often find that she vanishes upon entering the car or discover her headless form in the backseat, while those who refuse to stop or aid her frequently suffer vehicle stalls, electromagnetic anomalies, or fatal car crashes.

#title
The Caretaker of Fort Knox

#summary
Visitors and staff at a historic coastal fortification frequently report strange noises, shadowy figures, and physical touches attributed to a dedicated nineteenth-century ordnance sergeant. Even in death, the spirit appears to maintain his eternal watch over the grounds.

#details
Built in the mid-19th century to protect the Penobscot River Valley, Fort Knox in Prospect, Maine, never actually saw combat during its military history. Despite its peaceful operational record, modern visitors, park staff, and paranormal investigators report numerous uncanny encounters within its granite chambers and tunnels. Witnesses describe hearing unexplained footsteps, heavy breathing, disembodied voices, and laughter, while others report being touched by unseen hands. One of the most prominent apparitions is a figure dressed in a duster coat, widely believed to be Ordnance Sergeant Leopold Hegyi—who served as the fort's sole resident caretaker between 1887 and 1900—still patrolling the grounds and ensuring the post remains secure.

#location
Fort Knox is located at 740 Fort Knox Road in Prospect, Maine, overlooking the Penobscot River.

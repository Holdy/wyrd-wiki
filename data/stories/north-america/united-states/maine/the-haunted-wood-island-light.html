The Haunted Wood Island Light

Summary

Following a fatal 1896 dispute where a tenant shot his landlord before taking his own life inside his dwelling, the ghosts of both men have reportedly haunted the isolated island lighthouse. Visitors and residents have experienced eerie phenomena on the grounds ever since.

Details

Wood Island Light in Saco Bay became the site of a tragedy in 1896 when a violent confrontation erupted between tenant Howard Hobbs and landlord Frederick Milliken. The dispute ended in Milliken's death by gunfire. After speaking with the lighthouse keeper, Thomas H. Orcutt, who urged him to surrender, Hobbs retreated to his dwelling on the island and took his own life. Ever since, local lore and visitor accounts maintain that the ghosts of both the murdered landlord and the troubled tenant haunt the island station.

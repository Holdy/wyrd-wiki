The Ghost Lights of Silver Cliff Cemetery

Summary

Visitors to a historic cemetery in Custer County report witnessing mysterious, dancing orbs of light that float among the weathered headstones and follow people through the grounds. These phantom lights have been documented since the late 19th century, long before electricity was introduced to the area.

Details

Since the early days of the mining boom in Silver Cliff and Westcliffe, locals and visitors have shared accounts of strange, glowing orbs appearing within the historic Silver Cliff Cemetery after dark. Eyewitnesses describe small, colorful lights—ranging from the size of a fingernail to several inches across—darting between headstones, floating lazily in the grass, and occasionally gathering around a central white marble cross or a larger, campfire-like glow. Observers have reported that the lights actively follow individuals across the cemetery grounds and remain unresponsive to headlights, weather conditions, or nearby town lights, sustaining a legend of ghostly phenomena that predates the arrival of electricity in the region.

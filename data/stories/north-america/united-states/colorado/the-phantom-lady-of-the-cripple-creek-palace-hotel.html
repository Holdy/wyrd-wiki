The Phantom Lady of the Cripple Creek Palace Hotel

Summary

The historic Palace Hotel in Cripple Creek is said to be haunted by an apparition in a white nightgown who mystically lights candles and turns on lights in empty rooms.

Details

Dating back to the gold rush era of 1893, the Palace Hotel (or Palace Block) in Cripple Creek has long been associated with local ghost lore. For decades, owners, family members, and visitors have reported sightings of an ethereal woman with dark hair, dressed in a long white nightgown with a lace-neck collar. Known locally by the name of Kitty Chambers (though historians note the true spirit may be a former manager named Mary Hedges), the phantom is famous for inexplicable paranormal activity. Over the years, witnesses have reported lights turning on in vacant rooms, bedsheets turning down by an unseen hand, and mysterious lit candles appearing in specific patterns on dining room and bar tables when no one else is in the building.

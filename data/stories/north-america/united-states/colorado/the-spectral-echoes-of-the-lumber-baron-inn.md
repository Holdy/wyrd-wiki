#title
The Spectral Echoes of the Lumber Baron Inn

#summary
Built in 1890 by Scottish immigrant John Mouat, this historic Denver mansion became the site of a tragic 1970 double murder that local lore links to persistent paranormal activity.

#details
Originally constructed as a grand Queen Anne-style Victorian mansion showcasing various rare woods, the building fell into decay and served as a tenement by 1970. During that year, a 17-year-old runaway girl living there was raped and strangled, and her 18-year-old friend was shot after discovering the crime. Although the murders remain unsolved, guests and paranormal investigators have since reported sightings of a young woman's apparition, phantom footsteps, and electronic voice phenomena echoing through the restored halls.

#location
The Lumber Baron Inn is located at 2555 W. 37th Ave. in Denver, Colorado.

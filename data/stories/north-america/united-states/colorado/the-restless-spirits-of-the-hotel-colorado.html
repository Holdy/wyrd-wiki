The Restless Spirits of the Hotel Colorado

Summary

Established in 1893 in Glenwood Springs, the Hotel Colorado served as a naval hospital during World War II and is now reputed to be haunted by a wartime nurse and other historic phantoms.

Details

Styled after European Italian resorts, the grand hotel hosted presidents and notable figures before functioning as a naval hospital in WWII, with its basement serving as a temporary morgue. Among its most well-known hauntings is "Bobbie," a nurse reportedly killed by a jealous officer during the war, whose presence is signaled by the scent of vintage gardenia perfume. Other reported phenomena include the spirit of a woman in tower suite room 661 who repeatedly closes windows against drafts, and a ghostly presence smelling of cigar smoke lingering in the hallways.

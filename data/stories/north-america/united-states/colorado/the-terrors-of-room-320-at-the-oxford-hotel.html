The Terrors of Room 320 at the Oxford Hotel

Summary

Opened in 1891 near Union Station in downtown Denver, the historic Oxford Hotel is famously haunted by the violent spirit of a woman murdered in Room 320.

Details

The Oxford Hotel was designed as Denver's premier luxury Victorian hotel, but behind its elegant façade lies a dark history. According to hotel lore, a woman was strangled in Room 320 in the late 1890s by a jealous lover. Today, male guests staying in Room 320 frequently report physical assaults, including scratches and feeling as though they are being strangled, as well as seeing an enraged female apparition in the room's mirrors. Additionally, a bearded apparition has been sighted staring at women in the hotel's restrooms.

#title
Juana Pancha’s Hill

#summary
After being executed by warlocks for her thievery, the witch Juana Pancha placed an eternal curse upon her cave, ensuring that anyone who enters becomes hopelessly lost forever. The curse can only be broken during a Holy Friday if a brave soul enters and sleeps alone inside the cave.

#details
Juana Pancha was a beautiful and powerful witch named Juana Francisca Callejas who lived in a cave at the top of Conchagua Hill, three kilometers from town, and used her abilities to steal money and treasures. She would often fly like the wind or transform into a domestic animal to travel as far as a palace in Antigua Guatemala to commit her thefts. One night during her travels, a group of warlocks caught her and sentenced her to be burned. In revenge, Juana Pancha placed a powerful curse upon her cave. Ever since, anyone who enters her cave never returns, becoming increasingly lost the harder they try to escape. According to prophecy, the curse will finally be broken on a Holy Friday if someone dares to enter the cave and sleep alone.

#location
Conchagua Hill, located three kilometers from the town in the Department of La Unión, El Salvador.

#title
La Descarnada of the Highway

#summary
A beautiful female hitchhiker flags down lone motorists on a desolate stretch of highway at night before peeling away her skin to reveal a living skeleton.

#details
Late at night along the highway between Santa Ana and Chalchuapa, a striking and elegant woman flags down passing cars to request a ride. When a lone driver stops and agrees to take her, she gets into the vehicle and begins to heavily seduce him. As the man reciprocates her advances, a rotting stench fills the air and her flesh begins to peel away layer by layer under his touch. By the time the horrified driver realizes what is happening, she has transformed entirely into a fleshless skeleton, an encounter that typically results in madness or fatal terror for the victim.

#location
The ghost is encountered along the highway connecting the cities of Santa Ana and Chalchuapa in El Salvador.

#title
The Exorcism of the Young Student at Santo Tomás Church

#summary
Father Raymundo Brizuela performed a grueling exorcism at Santo Tomás Church on a 21-year-old university student from Chalatenango who was taken over by an entity that altered her voice and required multiple priests and men to restrain her.

#details
Father Raymundo Brizuela, an exorcist priest appointed by the Archdiocese of San Salvador at Santo Tomás Church, handled a severe case of demonic possession involving a 21-year-old pious university student originally from Nueva Concepción, Chalatenango. The young woman was possessed by an entity that radically altered her voice, causing her to speak like a four-year-old child for days. The exorcism process required three sessions, culminating in a violent confrontation during the third session where four priests were needed to perform the rite, and eight men struggled to physically restrain her as the entity demonstrated extraordinary strength and fury.

#location
The Santo Tomás Church is located in the municipality of Santo Tomás within the San Salvador Department of El Salvador.

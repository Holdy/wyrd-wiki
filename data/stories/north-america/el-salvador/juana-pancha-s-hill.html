Juana Pancha’s Hill

Summary

After being executed by warlocks for her thievery, the witch Juana Pancha placed an eternal curse upon her cave, ensuring that anyone who enters becomes hopelessly lost forever. The curse can only be broken during a Holy Friday if a brave soul enters and sleeps alone inside the cave.

Details

Juana Pancha was a beautiful and powerful witch named Juana Francisca Callejas who lived in a cave at the top of Conchagua Hill, three kilometers from town, and used her abilities to steal money and treasures. She would often fly like the wind or transform into a domestic animal to travel as far as a palace in Antigua Guatemala to commit her thefts. One night during her travels, a group of warlocks caught her and sentenced her to be burned. In revenge, Juana Pancha placed a powerful curse upon her cave. Ever since, anyone who enters her cave never returns, becoming increasingly lost the harder they try to escape. According to prophecy, the curse will finally be broken on a Holy Friday if someone dares to enter the cave and sleep alone.

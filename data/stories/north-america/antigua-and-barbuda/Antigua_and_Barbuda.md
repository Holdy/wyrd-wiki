#title
A Grain of Salt

#summary
A weary resort worker walking home from a late-night bus ride along a dark Antiguan dirt road is hunted by a skeletal jumbie. He escapes its clutches only by dropping his groceries, causing a carton of salt to burst and compel the entity to count the scattered grains.

#details
After finishing a long shift at the St. James Club Resort and taking the late-night bus from St. John's, Donovan gets off at his usual stop marked by an old tamarind tree and a weathered bus bench. As he walks the unpaved road toward his home, he hears stones stirring behind him and senses an ominous, chilling presence. Out of the corner of his eye, he spots a terrifying, tall, thin skeletal creature with protruding bones tracking him through the darkness like prey. Terrified and running for his life, Donovan reaches a crossroads near his yard, where he stumbles and drops his heavy shopping bags. A carton of salt bursts open upon the road, scattering white crystals everywhere. The supernatural pursuer abruptly halts, drops to its knees, and is compelled by folklore compulsion to begin counting the individual grains of salt, allowing Donovan to safely bolt inside his house, slam the door, and barricade it.

#location
The crossroads featuring a lamppost near the unpaved approach to Donovan's home in Antigua, located shortly after stepping off the bus near an old tamarind tree.

#title
Darla and the Jack-O-Lantern

#summary
A young market vendor walking home late at night through Swetes Village encounters a mysterious, blinding spirit known as the Jack-O-Lantern and is inexplicably taken over, later found at the top of the local sugar mill tower.

#details
Darla, a young girl from Swetes Village in Antigua, traveled to the St. John’s Market every Saturday to sell fruits and vegetables. After staying late in St. John's to get her hair pressed for church, she was forced to make the long, dark journey back home alone. Despite warnings from her friends about the legendary Jack-O-Lantern—a terrifying Antiguan spirit that takes the form of a bright, blinding light—she walked the dusty road after nightfall. Suddenly, a brilliant light approached her, radiating intense beams from different parts of its body. As the entity drew close, a strange, paralyzing feeling came over her, freezing her in fear and silencing her voice. The following morning, Darla awoke in her bed to find her family and a priest sprinkling holy water, learning that villagers who heard her screams had discovered her mysteriously transported to the top of the sugar mill tower.

#location
The top of the sugar mill tower in Swetes Village, Antigua.

#upstream-source
Friends of the Antigua Public Library 2010 Short Story Competition by Terrikia Benjamin.

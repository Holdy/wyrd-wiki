#title
The White Witch of Rose Hall

#summary
A cruel mistress places a malicious curse upon the family of a rebellious slave, prompting her ultimate downfall and leaving her spirit bound to the grounds. 

#details
At the historic Rose Hall plantation, the infamous mistress Annie Palmer ruled with cruelty, practicing dark arts and terrorizing the enslaved people who worked the estate. When a powerful enslaved obeah man named Takoo opposed her tyranny, Annie retaliated by placing a malicious curse upon his family or granddaughter. Seeking justice and an end to the torment, Takoo and the other enslaved workers confronted Annie in her bedchamber, where they defeated her using physical force combined with traditional spiritual practices. Her spirit was subsequently said to remain bound to the estate, forever haunting the Great House and its surrounding hills.

#location
Rose Hall Great House is located along the northern coast of Jamaica, just east of Montego Bay in St. James parish.

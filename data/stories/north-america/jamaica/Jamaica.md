#title
The Awakening at Oberlin High

#summary
During a morning devotion led by a teacher warning that God was displeased, multiple students at a Jamaican high school suddenly collapsed, spoke in strange voices, and exhibited signs of demonic possession that required fervent spiritual deliverance.

#details
At Oberlin High School in St. Andrew, Jamaica, a morning school devotion took a dramatic turn when a teacher announced she had a message from God warning that He was not pleased with the institution. As the teacher finished praying and students responded, several attendees—predominantly girls—began screaming, speaking in unnatural and weird voices, and dropping unconscious. Witnesses described the chaotic scene as resembling a demonic invasion where students were overpowered by unseen entities, acting wildly and unable to control their physical bodies. Local evangelists and church members were subsequently brought in to perform deliverance rituals, using prayer, water, and olive oil to cast out the spirits that had supposedly taken residence within the afflicted students.

#location
Oberlin High School is located in the rural hill district of St. Andrew, near Lawrence Tavern in Jamaica.

#title
The Haunting of Skull Point Church

#summary
The restless spirit of a brutally executed slave and other duppies haunt an old abandoned church and the surrounding roadways of Skull Point. Passing motorists frequently warn travelers of spirits suddenly darting out from the ruins.

#details
Long ago in Mile Gully, a Christian slave named James Knight from the Lyndhurst Estate was beheaded and his skull mounted on a pole as a warning. Denied a proper Christian burial, his violent demise caused his restless spirit to roam the community alongside other malevolent duppies. When a journalist later investigated the crumbling ruins of an old local church rumored to be haunted, a passing motorist shouted a warning about duppies running out onto the road. Upon entering the silent ruins, the reporter heard eerie, maniacal squeals echoing from within, cementing the location's terrifying ghostly reputation.

#location
The ruins of an old abandoned church situated at Skull Point in the district of Mile Gully, Jamaica.

#title
The White Witch of Rose Hall

#summary
A cruel mistress places a malicious curse upon the family of a rebellious slave, prompting her ultimate downfall and leaving her spirit bound to the grounds. 

#details
At the historic Rose Hall plantation, the infamous mistress Annie Palmer ruled with cruelty, practicing dark arts and terrorizing the enslaved people who worked the estate. When a powerful enslaved obeah man named Takoo opposed her tyranny, Annie retaliated by placing a malicious curse upon his family or granddaughter. Seeking justice and an end to the torment, Takoo and the other enslaved workers confronted Annie in her bedchamber, where they defeated her using physical force combined with traditional spiritual practices. Her spirit was subsequently said to remain bound to the estate, forever haunting the Great House and its surrounding hills.

#location
Rose Hall Great House is located along the northern coast of Jamaica, just east of Montego Bay in St. James parish.

#upstream-source
Herbert G. de Lisser, *The White Witch of Rose Hall*

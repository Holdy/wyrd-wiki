#title
The Enchanted Maiden of Salto del Pilón

#summary
An enigmatic water maiden guards a sacred waterfall and deep pool in Panama, enchanting treasure-seekers and testing their greed before luring them into the abyss. Those who fail her tests or succumb to her waters vanish into the legend forever.

#details
Along the course of the Río Perales as it flows from the Canajagua heights toward Perales de Guararé, the rushing waters form a deep, round pool known as Salto del Pilón. Local tradition warns of an ancient supernatural curse and guardianship attached to the gorge. According to legend, a beautiful maiden with a long braid and a golden comb haunts the waterfall, luring Spanish explorers who sought gold in the region. When the Spanish explorer Don Julián del Río ignored indigenous warnings and ventured to the rockface, the hypnotic maiden appeared before him and asked whether he loved her or her golden comb more. When he answered that he loved her, she noted that he had spared himself from immediate doom, but he ultimately plunged into the turbulent, deep waters and vanished alongside her forever. To this day, locals regard the pool with awe and fear, avoiding swimming in its mysterious depths.

#location
Salto del Pilón is located on the Río Perales in Perales, Guararé District, Los Santos Province, Panama.

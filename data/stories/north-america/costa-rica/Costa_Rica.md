#title
The Exorcism of the Valverde Residence

#summary
A nine-year-old girl and her family in Escazú suffered from a severe demonic possession and torment orchestrated by a powerful curse cast upon her father.

#details
When investigators from Paranormal Investigation CR and author Sussy Carballo visited the Valverde family residence in Escazú, they encountered a terrifying situation. A nine-year-old girl was found to be possessed by a demonic entity, exhibiting radical personality changes, unnatural voice shifts, and wearing no less than ten rosaries and crucifixes around her neck. Medical and psychological examinations found no medical explanation for her condition. Investigation revealed that the demonic possession was the result of a potent curse and witchcraft directed at the father by a former romantic partner, which ultimately impacted the mother and sisters as well.

#location
The key location is the Valverde family residence located in the canton of Escazú, Costa Rica.

#upstream-source
“The Huntress. Real Cases and Evidence of Paranormal Investigation CR” by Sussy Carballo.

#title
The Phantom Bride

#summary
A heartbroken woman left stranded at the altar tragically ended her life on the railway tracks, and her grieving spirit now haunts the historic train station platform.

#details
Following a devastating betrayal on her wedding day, a young bride walked to the railway in profound despair and threw herself in front of an oncoming train. Since her tragic death, her ghost has been seen wandering the platforms of the old train station at the stroke of midnight. Dressed in her wedding gown, her sorrowful figure paces the tracks while her mournful weeping echoes through the empty station.

#location
The spirit haunts the platform of the historic old train station situated in downtown San José, Costa Rica.

#title
The Unfinished Church and the Headless Priest of Cartago

#summary
A blood-soaked fratricide at a sacred altar triggered a lasting curse, ensuring that every attempt to complete the monumental temple would be violently interrupted by earthquakes.

#details
According to local oral tradition, the construction of the grand parish church in Cartago was cursed after a tragic crime of passion and fratricide unfolded within its walls. A priest discovered that his brother was going to marry his beloved, and in a blind fit of jealous rage, the priest attacked and killed his sibling right on the altar steps during a service. Ever since that sacrilegious murder, a heavy curse settled over the site. Generations of townspeople have whispered that any time an effort was made to finally complete the grand building, a powerful earthquake would strike to tear down the walls anew, leaving the structure perpetually unfinished. Today, the restless ghost of the headless priest is said to wander the stone arches by night.

#location
The Ruins of the Parish of Santiago Apóstol (Las Ruinas de Cartago) located at Calle 2 and Avenida 4 in downtown Cartago, Costa Rica.

The Exorcism of the Valverde Residence

Summary

A nine-year-old girl and her family in Escazú suffered from a severe demonic possession and torment orchestrated by a powerful curse cast upon her father.

Details

When investigators from Paranormal Investigation CR and author Sussy Carballo visited the Valverde family residence in Escazú, they encountered a terrifying situation. A nine-year-old girl was found to be possessed by a demonic entity, exhibiting radical personality changes, unnatural voice shifts, and wearing no less than ten rosaries and crucifixes around her neck. Medical and psychological examinations found no medical explanation for her condition. Investigation revealed that the demonic possession was the result of a potent curse and witchcraft directed at the father by a former romantic partner, which ultimately impacted the mother and sisters as well.

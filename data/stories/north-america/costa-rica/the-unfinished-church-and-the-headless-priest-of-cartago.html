The Unfinished Church and the Headless Priest of Cartago

Summary

A blood-soaked fratricide at a sacred altar triggered a lasting curse, ensuring that every attempt to complete the monumental temple would be violently interrupted by earthquakes.

Details

According to local oral tradition, the construction of the grand parish church in Cartago was cursed after a tragic crime of passion and fratricide unfolded within its walls. A priest discovered that his brother was going to marry his beloved, and in a blind fit of jealous rage, the priest attacked and killed his sibling right on the altar steps during a service. Ever since that sacrilegious murder, a heavy curse settled over the site. Generations of townspeople have whispered that any time an effort was made to finally complete the grand building, a powerful earthquake would strike to tear down the walls anew, leaving the structure perpetually unfinished. Today, the restless ghost of the headless priest is said to wander the stone arches by night.

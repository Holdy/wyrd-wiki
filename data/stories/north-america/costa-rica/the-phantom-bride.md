#title
The Phantom Bride

#summary
A heartbroken woman left stranded at the altar tragically ended her life on the railway tracks, and her grieving spirit now haunts the historic train station platform.

#details
Following a devastating betrayal on her wedding day, a young bride walked to the railway in profound despair and threw herself in front of an oncoming train. Since her tragic death, her ghost has been seen wandering the platforms of the old train station at the stroke of midnight. Dressed in her wedding gown, her sorrowful figure paces the tracks while her mournful weeping echoes through the empty station.

#location
The spirit haunts the platform of the historic old train station situated in downtown San José, Costa Rica.

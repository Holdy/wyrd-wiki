#title
The Lady of the Night on Pasos Street

#summary
On full moon nights, a mysterious female specter appears along Pasos Street to bring both good fortune and warnings of danger to those who encounter her.

#details
In the historic colonial city of Antigua, Guatemala, the legend of the Lady of the Night is closely tied to Pasos Street. Locals recount that during full moon nights, a mysterious ghostly woman materializes on the thoroughfare. Her sudden appearance is interpreted by residents as an omen that brings good luck to witnesses while simultaneously cautioning them of imminent dangers in the area.

#location
The key location is Calle de los Pasos (Pasos Street) situated within the historic colonial city of Antigua, Guatemala.

#title
The Flight of the Soukouyant

#summary
Villagers in Dominica tell of a powerful local woman who made a pact with the devil, transforming into a nocturnal fireball before meeting her demise at the village church. Residents claimed she flew across the ocean and even stole the Queen's dress in England.

#details
Growing up in the far-flung villages of Dominica during the mid-20th century, children and adults shared terrifying tales of soukouyants and lougarous—villagers who had allegedly surrendered their souls to the devil in exchange for dark, superhuman powers. Among the most legendary accounts was that of a prominent soukouyant from La Plaine who was said to routinely fly across the skies to England in a single night, famously stealing the Queen's dress from her palace. Villagers swore they saw her wearing the stolen garment at Sunday church. Eventually, her nocturnal flights came to an end when local residents anticipated her return, lying in wait near the village church with a special concoction that blasted her from the sky. The next morning, she was discovered lifeless in the church yard.

#location
The church yard in the village of La Plaine on the eastern coast of Dominica, where the fallen creature was discovered.

#title
The Haunting of Layou Road

#summary
A motorist stopped driving at night after repeatedly encountering the apparition of an elderly woman attempting to cross the highway along the Layou estate. Local lore connects the haunting to the historic plantation and burial grounds in the area.

#details
A 45-year-old man from St. Joseph was forced to alter his routine, abandoning his nighttime driving habits because he kept encountering the ghost of an old woman trying to cross the road in the Layou Valley area. While some locals speculated that the apparition might be a lost soul or a relative from the past, others noted that the stretch of road runs adjacent to the historical Layou plantation, where old graves are located. The phantom has reportedly been witnessed by multiple people in the area, appearing silently near the roadside after dark.

#location
The section of road passing through the Layou estate near the Layou River in Dominica.

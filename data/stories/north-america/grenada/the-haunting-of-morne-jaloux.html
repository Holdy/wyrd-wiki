    The Haunting of Morne Jaloux

    Summary

    Visitors to the ruined Morne Jaloux Estate Great House in Grenada encountered a glowing white apparition and violent poltergeist activity after driving down its overgrown dirt track at dusk.

    Details

    In January 1941, a group of friends took an evening drive to investigate the notorious, abandoned Morne Jaloux Estate Great House in Grenada. After navigating an overgrown dirt track to reach the property during daylight, they returned after dark and parked their car facing the crumbling building. As they sat in the vehicle with the headlights illuminating the entrance, a transparent, glowing white man-sized figure suddenly appeared, moving rapidly back and forth in front of the house and its steps. Simultaneously, deafening industrial noises, sounds of bouncing objects, and flying stones and wood assailed their car, nearly trapping and terrorizing the occupants. Local accounts later attributed the terrifying haunting to the vengeful spirit of the estate's brutal former French owner and the victims of his historical cruelty.

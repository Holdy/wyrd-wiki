#title
The Apparition of Maria

#summary
A heartbroken young woman who died after falling from a hill while rushing to warn her lover of her father's plot now haunts the highway as a phantom runner. Her apparition is frequently seen darting across the tarmac at night.

#details
In 1909, a young Spanish woman named Maria fell in love with an Indian labourer on the Forres Park Sugar Estate, much to the fury of her father. After learning that her father and his trusted workers plotted to kill her lover, a desperate Maria fled her home to warn him. While crossing the path, she was bitten by a poisonous snake and, weakened by the venom, fell to her death from the adjacent hill. Overcome with grief, her father erected a memorial statue of the Virgin Mary on the estate's highest hill. To this day, Maria's spirit is said to haunt the area, appearing as an apparition running frantically across the highway at night to reach her doomed lover.

#location
The haunting takes place along the Solomon Hochoy Highway near the Claxton Bay turnoff and the former Forres Park Sugar Estate in Trinidad and Tobago.

#title
The Curse of the Caura Water Dam

#summary
When the village and church of Caura were forcibly demolished to build a major water reservoir, a displaced Catholic priest placed a curse upon the project. Consequently, the dam project collapsed amidst scandal and corruption, leaving the valley transformed into a recreational river site.

#details
In the mid-20th century, the historic rural settlement of Caura in northern Trinidad was acquired by authorities, and its vibrant village and church were entirely demolished to make way for a massive water dam intended to supply North Trinidad. Outraged and heartbroken by the destruction of the community and its sacred spaces, the village's Catholic priest cast a curse upon the ambitious construction project. Plagued by escalating costs, political scandal, and administrative corruption, the dam project was abruptly abandoned just a year into construction. Today, the cursed site has evolved into the popular Caura River Valley, where visitors lime along the riverbanks while the remnants of the failed dam project remain a lingering local legend.

#location
The Caura River Valley and former dam site are located in the Northern Range of Trinidad, north of El Dorado and Tacarigua.

#title
The Possession Outbreak at Moruga Composite School

#summary
In 2010, multiple students at a secondary school in southern Trinidad experienced sudden, synchronized seizures and trance-like episodes that locals attributed to demonic possession. The dramatic flare-up resulted in school closures and drew visiting exorcists before being analyzed as a case of mass sociogenic illness.

#details
In 2010, an unprecedented series of events unfolded at the Moruga Composite School in rural southern Trinidad, where several young female students abruptly began manifesting violent seizures, nausea, fainting spells, and altered, trance-like behavior. As the condition rapidly spread among the student body, causing widespread panic and forcing the school to shut down, religious observers and local residents insisted the teenagers were victims of demonic possession and spiritual attack. Pastors and self-proclaimed exorcists rushed to the school compound armed with holy water and rosaries to perform deliverances. Medical and sociological analyses later categorized the incident as mass hysteria or psychosomatic possession rooted in cultural frameworks of spiritual warfare, capturing national attention.

#location
Moruga Composite School is situated in the rural southern coastal region of Trinidad and Tobago.

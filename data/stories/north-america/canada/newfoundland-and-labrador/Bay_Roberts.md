#title
The Apparition of Aunt Jane Churchill's Hill

#summary
A man walking home along the front road in Bay Roberts late at night encountered what he initially suspected was a demonic apparition, only to discover it was the wandering figure of a local resident.

#details
As recounted by Wilbur Sparkes, his grandfather was walking home from a wake at Mosdell's Lane down the front road in Bay Roberts around two o'clock in the morning under a bright full moon. When he reached the top of Aunt Jane Churchill's hill, he saw a strange white figure standing on the bank looking out at the water. Believing it might be a phantom or test of faith, he approached and offered a greeting. Receiving no answer, he realized the figure was actually Aunt Jane Churchill standing outside in her nightdress and white shawl.

#location
Aunt Jane Churchill's hill along the front road in Bay Roberts, Newfoundland and Labrador.

#title
The Ghostly Funeral Procession of Mosdell's Lane

#summary
Outport residents recount local folklore of a silent, ghostly funeral procession that marches past Mosdell's Lane and the local cemetery at four o'clock in the morning.

#details
According to local oral history collected in Bay Roberts, men walking home late at night or early in the morning near Mosdell's Lane—located just below the local church—would occasionally encounter a chilling sight. Witnesses, including family members of local residents like George Jones, passed down accounts of a spectral funeral procession complete with a horse-drawn hearse, a coffin, and bone-weary mourners dressed entirely in black silently making their eerie pre-dawn march.

#location
Mosdell's Lane and the nearby church cemetery in Bay Roberts, Newfoundland and Labrador.

#title
The Woman in White at White Rock

#summary
Residents report sightings of a mysterious woman in white who haunts the cliffside and road near White Rock in the east end of Bay Roberts.

#details
Passed down through generations of outport folklore in Bay Roberts, the area around White Rock on the Back Road is said to be haunted by a spectral woman. According to accounts shared by local residents such as Kyle Mercer and Wilbur Sparkes, late-night travelers have witnessed a phantom woman in white floating down and over the cliff, crossing the road into the marsh, or leaving behind unexplained traces.

#location
White Rock on the Back Road in the east end of Bay Roberts, Newfoundland and Labrador.

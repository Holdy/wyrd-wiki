#title
The Ghostly Funeral Procession of Mosdell's Lane

#summary
Outport residents recount local folklore of a silent, ghostly funeral procession that marches past Mosdell's Lane and the local cemetery at four o'clock in the morning.

#details
According to local oral history collected in Bay Roberts, men walking home late at night or early in the morning near Mosdell's Lane—located just below the local church—would occasionally encounter a chilling sight. Witnesses, including family members of local residents like George Jones, passed down accounts of a spectral funeral procession complete with a horse-drawn hearse, a coffin, and bone-weary mourners dressed entirely in black silently making their eerie pre-dawn march.

#location
Mosdell's Lane and the nearby church cemetery in Bay Roberts, Newfoundland and Labrador.

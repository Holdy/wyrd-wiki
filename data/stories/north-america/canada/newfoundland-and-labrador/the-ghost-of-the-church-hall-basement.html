The Ghost of the Church Hall Basement

Summary

A worker returning to retrieve his forgotten lunch can in a church hall basement encountered a transparent apparition of an elderly man.

Details

Two workers were performing repairs in the basement of the church hall in Bryant's Cove. After working for several hours, one worker said goodbye and left for the day. Shortly after, realizing he had forgotten his lunch can, he returned to the building. Upon entering the dim, empty basement, he saw his partner standing with his back to him. Thinking his partner had also forgotten something, he called out his name. When the figure turned around, it was not his partner, but an old, wrinkled man with long wispy hair through whom the worker could clearly see the back wall. Terrified by the encounter, the man refused to return to work for weeks, and though he eventually returned out of humility to face the teasing of his coworkers, he never spoke of the paranormal again.

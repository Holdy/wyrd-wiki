The Woman in White at White Rock

Summary

Residents report sightings of a mysterious woman in white who haunts the cliffside and road near White Rock in the east end of Bay Roberts.

Details

Passed down through generations of outport folklore in Bay Roberts, the area around White Rock on the Back Road is said to be haunted by a spectral woman. According to accounts shared by local residents such as Kyle Mercer and Wilbur Sparkes, late-night travelers have witnessed a phantom woman in white floating down and over the cliff, crossing the road into the marsh, or leaving behind unexplained traces.

#title
Jacob and the Corpse Candle

#summary
A father in St. Lawrence witnesses a glowing, discarnate flame drift from his home to the local cemetery, foreshadowing the tragic illness and death of his young son.

#details
In the coastal town of St. Lawrence on the Burin Peninsula, a grieving widower named Tom McGrath was struggling to raise his twelve-year-old son, Jacob, following the death of Tom's own father. One early morning, Tom heard mysterious footsteps in his late father's locked bedroom upstairs. Investigating, he discovered no one inside, but moments later watched in astonishment as a steady, golden flame with no wick or candle hovered down the stairs, passed through the front door, and drifted across the path toward the village churchyard cemetery before fading away. Days later, Jacob fell violently ill with a high fever. As the boy lay dying, his fever broke just long enough for him to whisper that his grandfather was there to light the way, right as the room filled with a warm golden glow before he passed away.

#location
The events take place at a saltbox house situated near the churchyard and cemetery in St. Lawrence on the Burin Peninsula, Newfoundland.

#title
The Phantom Viking Longship of Fortune Bay

#summary
Fishermen and coastal residents near the Burin Peninsula reported eerie encounters with a phantom Viking longship and a ghostly crew carrying their vessel over the hills.

#details
In Fortune Bay, adjacent to the Burin Peninsula, a group of local fishermen sailing into Bay de L'Eau encountered a ghostly longship coming out of the bay. The phantom vessel was manned by crew members wearing fur pelts and speaking an unintelligible language before it vanished into the fog. Years earlier in the nearby fishing village of Harbour Mille, a resident named Aunt Sarah looked out her second-storey bedroom window at night to observe men bringing a flat barge ashore from the water, hoisting the heavy craft onto their shoulders, and portaging it on foot over the saddle-like hills separating the communities.

#location
The sightings occurred in the waters and coastal hills between Harbour Mille and Bay de L'Eau in Fortune Bay, near the Burin Peninsula, Newfoundland.

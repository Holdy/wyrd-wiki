The Apparition at the Englee Fishing Stage

Summary

A local fisherman in the coastal community of Englee encountered a mysterious phantom figure lingering near the settlement's waterfront fishing premises late at night.

Details

Collected as part of local oral tradition in Englee, White Bay, this account describes a fisherman who went down to the local fishing stage after dark and witnessed a luminous, silent apparition standing near the salt bins. The figure vanished into thin air when approached, leaving behind an eerie chill and becoming a widely discussed local haunting among residents.

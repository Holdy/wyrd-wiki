#title
The Specter of Drake's Cove

#summary
Residents of Drake's Cove have passed down traditional accounts of unexplained apparitions and mysterious coastal disturbances along the shoreline.

#details
Preserved through oral history and local memorates collected in Marystown, traditional stories from Drake's Cove detail uncanny encounters with restless spirits and unearthly lights near the water's edge. These legends reflect the atmospheric isolation and historic hardships of families living along the rugged coastline of the Burin Peninsula.

#location
Drake's Cove is a coastal inlet situated within the Marystown area on the Burin Peninsula of Newfoundland and Labrador.

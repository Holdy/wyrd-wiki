#title
The Phantom Ship of Bonne Bay

#summary
Decades after a vessel sank in Bonne Bay harbour, local residents witnessed the ghostly, illuminated ship sail toward the shore before mysteriously vanishing.

#details
Many years after a large ship sank in the natural harbour of Bonne Bay, local residents were startled one evening by strange noises coming from the water. Upon looking out, they saw the sunken ship heading toward the shore, appearing exactly as it had on the day it went down. The vessel was brilliantly illuminated by lights, yet no crew members were visible on deck. It drifted to within a mile of the shoreline before completely disappearing into the night.

#location
The natural harbour of Bonne Bay in Newfoundland and Labrador.

#title
The Premature Burial at Woody Point

#summary
During the funeral service of a teenage girl from Woody Point who supposedly died of epilepsy, horrifying sounds from inside her coffin revealed she had been buried alive.

#details
In the village of Woody Point, a 16-year-old girl passed away following a series of severe epileptic convulsions and was laid to rest. Shortly after she was buried, mourners and undertakers gathered for the funeral service heard a distinct, muffled knocking coming from deep within the coffin. Panic-stricken, the undertakers frantically shoveled away the earth and forced the casket open. Inside, they discovered the young girl turned face down with her eyes wide open in sheer terror, indicating she had tragically regained consciousness after burial.

#location
The village graveyard in Woody Point, Newfoundland and Labrador.

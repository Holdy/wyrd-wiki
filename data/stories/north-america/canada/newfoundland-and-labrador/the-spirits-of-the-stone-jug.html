The Spirits of the Stone Jug

Summary

A historic 19th-century stone building in Carbonear is said to be haunted by the ghost of an indentured servant and the echoes of a deadly duel.

Details

Rebuilt in 1860 following a devastating fire that swept through the area, the Stone Jug in Carbonear is steeped in dark local lore. Local accounts and historical records associated with the landmark claim that a deadly duel took place inside the building in its early years. Additionally, legend and local tradition maintain that an indentured servant tragically hung herself in the stairwell during the 19th century. Today, staff and visitors report persistent paranormal activity within the thick stone walls and stairwells of the historic structure.

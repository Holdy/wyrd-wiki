#title
Mortier Bay's Phantom Vessels

#summary
Fishermen in Mortier Bay have long reported sightings of spectral sailing ships drifting silently through unnatural weather conditions before vanishing into the mist.

#details
According to local folklore gathered from long-time residents of Marystown, phantom square-riggers occasionally materialize in the waters of Mortier Bay. Witnesses have historically described seeing these ghost ships navigating against the wind or appearing during dead calms, viewed by coastal communities as lingering echoes or omens tied to the treacherous maritime history of Placentia Bay.

#location
Mortier Bay is a large natural coastal inlet located in Marystown on the Burin Peninsula of Newfoundland and Labrador.

#title
The Specter of Drake's Cove

#summary
Residents of Drake's Cove have passed down traditional accounts of unexplained apparitions and mysterious coastal disturbances along the shoreline.

#details
Preserved through oral history and local memorates collected in Marystown, traditional stories from Drake's Cove detail uncanny encounters with restless spirits and unearthly lights near the water's edge. These legends reflect the atmospheric isolation and historic hardships of families living along the rugged coastline of the Burin Peninsula.

#location
Drake's Cove is a coastal inlet situated within the Marystown area on the Burin Peninsula of Newfoundland and Labrador.

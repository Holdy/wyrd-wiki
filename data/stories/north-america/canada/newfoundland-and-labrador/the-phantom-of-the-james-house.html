The Phantom of the James House

Summary

The historic James House on Riverhead Road in Brigus is said to be haunted by a family spirit that occasionally manifests on the upper floor.

Details

Originally constructed in the early 1800s and later relocated on Riverhead Road in Brigus, the James House was passed down through generations of the James family until recent decades. Following modern renovations by new owners, occupants experienced unexplained paranormal activity on the second floor of the dwelling. According to local lore, the entity haunting the timber-framed home is the spirit of a former James family member who continues to linger within the historic property.

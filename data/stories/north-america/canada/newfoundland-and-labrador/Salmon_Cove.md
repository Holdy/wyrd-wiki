#title
The Blood-Stained Wharf of Salmon Cove

#summary
Following the brutal 1833 murder of planter John Snow at his Salmon Cove fishing stage, the site became steeped in dark local folklore and ghostly legends.

#details
On August 31, 1833, John Snow, a prominent planter in Salmon Cove, mysteriously vanished after a stormy domestic dispute. Local authorities launched an intense investigation that was grimly confirmed when pools of dried blood were discovered on John Snow's fishing stage along the Salmon Cove shore. His servant Arthur Spring and cousin Tobias Mandeville were arrested and confessed that Snow had been murdered and his body thrown into the Atlantic at the instigation of his wife, Catherine Snow. Catherine was later convicted and executed in St. John's as the last woman hanged in Newfoundland, though she maintained her innocence until her death. In the years following the murder, locals whispered of eerie noises, phantom sightings, and supernatural unease lingering around the Salmon Cove wharf where the blood was first found.

#location
John Snow's historic fishing wharf along the shore of Salmon Cove, Newfoundland and Labrador.

#title
The Woman in White of Parsons' Hill

#summary
Local boys stealing crab apples at night in Salmon Cove were terrified when they encountered a glowing figure in white near the church graveyard, which turned out to be a neighbor returning from a meeting.

#details
In the late summer or early fall during the mid-20th century, a group of boys in Salmon Cove were out late picking crab apples. When one of the younger boys became frightened of running into the legendary local specter known as the "Woman in White," the older boys walked him home. As they passed the village church and graveyard and headed up Parsons' Hill, they saw a vivid, white-clad figure standing at the top of the hill. Panicked by the sight, they pelted the apparition with rocks before fleeing in terror. The next morning, the boys learned that the ghost was actually Aunt Gladys Parsons walking home from her Loyal Orange Benevolent Association (LOBA) meeting wearing her white dress.

#location
Parsons' Hill, located just past the church and graveyard in Salmon Cove, Newfoundland and Labrador.

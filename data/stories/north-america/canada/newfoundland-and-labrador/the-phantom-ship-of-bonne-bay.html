The Phantom Ship of Bonne Bay

Summary

Decades after a vessel sank in Bonne Bay harbour, local residents witnessed the ghostly, illuminated ship sail toward the shore before mysteriously vanishing.

Details

Many years after a large ship sank in the natural harbour of Bonne Bay, local residents were startled one evening by strange noises coming from the water. Upon looking out, they saw the sunken ship heading toward the shore, appearing exactly as it had on the day it went down. The vessel was brilliantly illuminated by lights, yet no crew members were visible on deck. It drifted to within a mile of the shoreline before completely disappearing into the night.

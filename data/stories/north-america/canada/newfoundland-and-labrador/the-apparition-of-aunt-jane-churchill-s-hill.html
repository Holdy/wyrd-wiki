The Apparition of Aunt Jane Churchill's Hill

Summary

A man walking home along the front road in Bay Roberts late at night encountered what he initially suspected was a demonic apparition, only to discover it was the wandering figure of a local resident.

Details

As recounted by Wilbur Sparkes, his grandfather was walking home from a wake at Mosdell's Lane down the front road in Bay Roberts around two o'clock in the morning under a bright full moon. When he reached the top of Aunt Jane Churchill's hill, he saw a strange white figure standing on the bank looking out at the water. Believing it might be a phantom or test of faith, he approached and offered a greeting. Receiving no answer, he realized the figure was actually Aunt Jane Churchill standing outside in her nightdress and white shawl.

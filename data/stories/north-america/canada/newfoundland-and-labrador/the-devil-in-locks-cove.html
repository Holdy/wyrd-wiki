    The Devil in Locks Cove

    Summary

    A crew of fishermen sleeping in an old fishing stage in Hare Bay awoke to find their heavily lashed door violently forced open when an unseen force snapped a thick trawl line. Terrified by the supernatural strength, the men fled the cove and never returned.

    Details

    Years ago, a crew returning from the fishing grounds settled down for the night in an old fishing stage located in Locks Cove at the bottom of Hare Bay, Newfoundland. They secured the door tightly, tying it shut with heavy trawl line used for hauling fish, and went to sleep on a calm, clear night. At 3:00 AM, they were jolted awake by loud pounding at the door, which suddenly swung open. Upon inspecting the door, the fishermen discovered that the robust trawl line had been snapped clean like a thread, despite absolute calm and windless weather outside. Shaken and believing that whatever force broke the line was immensely powerful and malevolent, the men quickly gathered their belongings, hopped into their boat, and left the area permanently.

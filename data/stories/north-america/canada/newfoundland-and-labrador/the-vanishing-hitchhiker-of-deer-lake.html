The Vanishing Hitchhiker of Deer Lake

Summary

Drivers traveling along the highway between Deer Lake and Corner Brook frequently report encountering a mysterious woman in white who vanishes into thin air or suddenly appears inside their vehicle.

Details

Documented as a staple of local folklore and examined by folklorists studying Newfoundland legends, the tale of the Deer Lake vanishing hitchhiker has been shared by motorists for decades. According to local accounts, drivers journeying at night along the route connecting Deer Lake to Corner Brook encounter a rain-soaked woman wearing white with her thumb extended. Those who slow down to offer assistance watch in astonishment as she completely disappears before reaching the car. Even more unsettling are reports from drivers who pass her by; checking their rearview mirror to find the road empty, they suddenly discover the hitchhiker sitting silently in the passenger seat beside them.

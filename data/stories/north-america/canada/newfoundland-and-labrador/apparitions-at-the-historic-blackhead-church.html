Apparitions at the Historic Blackhead Church

Summary

Parishioners and visitors walking the coastal path near Newfoundland's first Methodist church have reported unexplained lights and ghostly figures.

Details

Established in 1769 as the site of the very first Methodist church in Newfoundland, the historic settlement of Blackhead holds centuries of deep-rooted maritime and spiritual history. Over generations, locals walking along the old paths and near the historic cemetery after dark have reported sighting pale, indistinct figures wandering among the 18th-century headstones and unexplained glowing lights hovering over the rocky cliffsides facing Conception Bay.

#title
The Fairy Ring of Lewin's Cove

#summary
While returning from the woods in Lewin's Cove, a local man became mysteriously trapped in an invisible circular barrier, walking around and around in the exact same tracks until his companion's voice broke the spell.

#details
As recorded in traditional Newfoundland folklore, two men were walking back together from the woods when one of them lagged behind. Noticing his companion was no longer beside him, the first man turned around, called out, and eventually spotted his friend walking in continuous circles nearby. When he approached, his companion explained that he felt as though he was trapped inside an invisible ring from which he could not escape, finding himself looping back to the exact same starting point no matter which direction he walked. He noted that while he had often heard tales of people getting caught in fairy rings, this was his first personal encounter with the phenomenon. The moment his companion called out to him and broke his disorientation, he was able to step right out of the ring and leave the woods safely.

#location
The forested trails surrounding Lewin's Cove on the Burin Peninsula of Newfoundland and Labrador.

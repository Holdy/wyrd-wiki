#title
The Ghost At Big Nan's

#summary
While visiting her late great-grandmother's abandoned house in Cook's Harbour during Come Home Year, a young girl and her cousin heard a distressed baby crying upstairs while looking at old photo albums. The eerie disembodied screams forced them to flee the home immediately.

#details
During Come Home Year in the tight-knit fishing community of Cook's Harbour, Newfoundland, a young girl named Krystyn and her cousin Mitch needed to use the restroom while visiting family. Because her grandparents' house was crowded with guests, they were sent next door to the vacant home of Krystyn's late great-grandmother, affectionately known as "Big Nan." Upon entering the dark, sinking house, they found the kitchen table covered in leftover personal belongings, including a tiny green photo album filled with old black-and-white photographs. As they looked at a picture of a woman holding a baby in a rocking chair, both children distinctly heard the terrifying sound of a baby screaming and crying from the empty upper floor. Panicked and terrified, they bolted from the house without even closing the front door.

#location
The abandoned house of Big Nan, situated next door to her family's home in Cook's Harbour on the Great Northern Peninsula of Newfoundland and Labrador.

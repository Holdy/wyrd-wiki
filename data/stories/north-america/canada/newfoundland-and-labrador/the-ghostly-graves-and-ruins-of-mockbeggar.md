#title
The Ghostly Graves and Ruins of Mockbeggar

#summary
Construction work and historical digging in the Mockbeggar neighbourhood unearthed centuries-old coffins and a buried boat, giving rise to persistent local legends of haunted houses and spectral singing.

#details
For over a century, construction and canal excavation in the Mockbeggar neighbourhood of Bonavista repeatedly disturbed mysterious, well-preserved coffins containing bodies dressed in ancient attire, alongside the timbers of an old wooden boat. Early 20th-century reports detailed how locals discovered remains outfitted in clothing resembling Puritan or old French wear buried deep in the acidic bogs. Oral traditions and folklore soon took root, with residents recounting stories of houses built directly atop unmarked graves—including one tale where a home's support post sank directly onto a hidden coffin. Local lore maintains that Mockbeggar is intensely haunted, with neighbours claiming that on quiet nights, eerie foreign singing and ghostly spectral phenomena can still be experienced near the old canal.

#location
The canal area connecting O'Dea's Pond to the ocean in the Mockbeggar neighbourhood of Bonavista, Newfoundland and Labrador.

#title
The Ghost of the Church Hall Basement

#summary
A worker returning to retrieve his forgotten lunch can in a church hall basement encountered a transparent apparition of an elderly man.

#details
Two workers were performing repairs in the basement of the church hall in Bryant's Cove. After working for several hours, one worker said goodbye and left for the day. Shortly after, realizing he had forgotten his lunch can, he returned to the building. Upon entering the dim, empty basement, he saw his partner standing with his back to him. Thinking his partner had also forgotten something, he called out his name. When the figure turned around, it was not his partner, but an old, wrinkled man with long wispy hair through whom the worker could clearly see the back wall. Terrified by the encounter, the man refused to return to work for weeks, and though he eventually returned out of humility to face the teasing of his coworkers, he never spoke of the paranormal again.

#location
The basement of St. Andrew's Church Hall located in Bryant's Cove, Newfoundland and Labrador.

#upstream-source
Folk Belief & Legends of Bay Roberts & Area, edited by Lisa Wilson, foreword by Dale Jarvis (Heritage Foundation of Newfoundland and Labrador and Bay Roberts Cultural Foundation Inc., 2014).

#title
The Phantom of the Century-Old School

#summary
Locals report that a century-old school in Bryant's Cove built over a burial ground exhibits supernatural phenomena, including self-activating interior lights and apparitions appearing inside closets and windows.

#details
According to local accounts, an old school building in Bryant's Cove that has stood for over a century is said to be built on an indigenous burial ground. Residents and passersby have noted that on certain nights, the interior lights of the building turn on by themselves even when entirely empty. Years ago, while a student was inside the school, he ventured down to the basement and opened a closet door, only to find a strange man standing inside, terrifying him so badly that he never returned to the school. In another incident, a group of people standing outside the building at night looked through a window and spotted a figure staring down at them from inside.

#location
The century-old former school building situated in Bryant's Cove, Newfoundland and Labrador.

Folk Belief & Legends of Bay Roberts & Area, edited by Lisa Wilson, foreword by Dale Jarvis (Heritage Foundation of Newfoundland and Labrador and Bay Roberts Cultural Foundation Inc., 2014).

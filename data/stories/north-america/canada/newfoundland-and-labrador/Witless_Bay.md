#title
Ghostly Animals and Death Tokens of Witless Bay

#summary
Oral history collections from Witless Bay record local accounts of supernatural death tokens and ghostly animals, including talking black dogs and phantom cows seen throughout the community.

#details
Documented through folklore fieldwork by Memorial University of Newfoundland, long-time residents of Witless Bay shared traditional beliefs and personal accounts regarding the paranormal. These stories include eerie premonitory death tokens—supernatural signs or omens witnessed prior to the passing of a family member or pet—as well as encounters with ghostly animals, specifically talking black dogs and phantom cows roaming the rural paths and woods of Witless Bay.

#location
The community of Witless Bay, Southern Shore, Newfoundland and Labrador.

#title
The Haunting of Hell Hill Pond

#summary
Motorists and travelers passing along the Southern Shore highway near Hell Hill Pond frequently report encountering ghostly apparitions, mysterious figures on the road, and unexplained disturbances tied to a tragic 1932 drowning.

#details
On August 7, 1932, a group of young women from Witless Bay and St. Joseph's went swimming at Hell Hill Pond. Tragically, three of the young women—Veronica Daley, Esther Mullowney, and Mary Norris—stepped into deep water and drowned. Decades later, a white wooden cross remains maintained on the embankment by local residents, and local folklore holds that the area is haunted. Drivers and locals report eerie sightings such as a mysterious man in a long black coat walking behind cars, silent figures standing by the roadside, car radios malfunctioning, and sudden unexplained noises or projectiles near the pond and LaManche Road.

#location
Hell Hill Pond and LaManche Road (Route 10), just outside Witless Bay, Newfoundland and Labrador.

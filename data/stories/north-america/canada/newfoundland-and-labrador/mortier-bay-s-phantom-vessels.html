Mortier Bay's Phantom Vessels

Summary

Fishermen in Mortier Bay have long reported sightings of spectral sailing ships drifting silently through unnatural weather conditions before vanishing into the mist.

Details

According to local folklore gathered from long-time residents of Marystown, phantom square-riggers occasionally materialize in the waters of Mortier Bay. Witnesses have historically described seeing these ghost ships navigating against the wind or appearing during dead calms, viewed by coastal communities as lingering echoes or omens tied to the treacherous maritime history of Placentia Bay.

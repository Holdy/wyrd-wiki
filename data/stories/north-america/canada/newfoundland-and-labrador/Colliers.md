#title
The Drowned Grandson's Warning

#summary
A grandmother in Colliers was spared from a fatal lightning strike when the apparition of her drowned grandson suddenly appeared at her doorway to stop her.

#details
During a severe thunderstorm in Colliers, a woman rushed to close her home's front door and metal screen door, which had blown wide open. Just as she reached out her hand, a young, dripping-wet boy appeared on the front steps, raised his hand, and sternly warned her, "NO!" She instantly recognized him as her seven-year-old grandson, Gary, who had drowned years earlier. Frozen in place by shock and hesitation, she avoided grabbing the metal handle just as a powerful bolt of lightning struck the screen door, sparking wildly across the entryway and sparing her life.

#location
A residential house in Colliers, Newfoundland.

#title
The Weeping Woman of Cemetery Hill

#summary
A grieving woman who dabbled in witch bottles haunts the cemetery hill in Colliers, where her supernatural touch is said to have killed a young groom.

#details
After losing her husband tragically, a young woman in Colliers turned away from her faith and allegedly experimented with witch bottles to seek retribution. Consumed by sorrow, she was frequently seen dressed in black, weeping near the large boulder in front of the community's cemetery. One evening, as a newly married couple drove a sleigh across cemetery hill, the grieving woman leapt aboard and touched the young groom. Her icy touch instantly killed him, condemning her spirit to haunt the hilltop and mourn for eternity.

#location
The cemetery and adjacent hill in Colliers, Newfoundland.

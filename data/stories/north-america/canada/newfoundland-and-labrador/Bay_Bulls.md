#title
The Fiery Phantom of Bay Bulls Road

#summary
A long-vacant house on the Bay Bulls Road gained a terrifying reputation for nightly apparitions and a violent manifestation of glowing fire that rushed through the building. Local fishermen who sought shelter in the empty structure during a rainstorm were shaken to their core by the aggressive fire phantom.

#details
For decades, a particular house situated along the Bay Bulls Road was widely avoided by travelers due to its strong ghostly reputation. The home featured a locked upper room that was said to be haunted nightly by an ancestor of the family. According to local lore, at around dawn, a half-distinct spectral figure would emerge from the locked room, traverse the hallway, and pass directly through the front door before fading into the morning light without ever inflicting harm. 

The haunting reputation of the house was cemented further when a group of trouting fishermen, caught in a heavy downpour near a nearby pond, reluctantly decided to seek shelter inside the vacant building. Around midnight, the men were met with a terrifying supernatural display: a red-hot, glowing, aggressive figure of fire hurled itself against the house, passed entirely through it, and out the rear. The manifestation was accompanied by a volcanic gust of wind that shook the old building like a reed in the wind. When the brief, violent phenomenon subsided, the men inspected the house and found no burn marks or physical damage whatsoever to prove the fiery entity had ever been there.

#location
The events took place in an old house and nearby pond located along the Bay Bulls Road in Newfoundland.

#title
The Coffin House Post of Mockbeggar

#summary
In the Mockbeggar neighbourhood of Bonavista, a house was built over unmarked graves where a wooden support post eventually sank into the mud and rested directly on top of a coffin.

#details
Collected from Newfoundland elder Wilson Hayward, local legend holds that human caskets were once found scattered or shallowly buried across the ground in the Mockbeggar area of Bonavista. According to the account, one particular house in the neighborhood was constructed in such a way that its support post slowly sank down through the mud over time until it struck and rested squarely upon an underground coffin. This chilling encounter with the dead aligns with Mockbeggar's long-standing reputation as a haunted locality, reinforced by decades of accidental discoveries of well-preserved 17th-century remains and unrecorded burials during construction work.

#location
The Mockbeggar neighbourhood in Bonavista, Newfoundland and Labrador.

#title
The Phantom Party at Bradley House

#summary
A resident at the historic Bradley House in Bonavista heard phantom visitors talking, singing, and hosting a lively party complete with slamming doors.

#details
In the historic Mockbeggar neighbourhood of Bonavista, the Bradley House—part of the Mockbeggar Plantation Provincial Historic Site—has long been associated with local ghost stories and strange occurrences. A local resident recounted hearing phantom visitors moving about the rooms, engaging in conversation, singing, and slamming doors as if throwing a late-night party. After the nocturnal celebration eventually quieted down, the resident was able to get some rest. Folklorists and local historians note that these paranormal experiences are often tied to the area's eerie history, including the unearthing of numerous unmarked, ancient coffins during nearby canal excavations.

#location
Bradley House at the Mockbeggar Plantation Provincial Historic Site in Bonavista, Newfoundland and Labrador.

#title
The Horse Who Saw a Ghost

#summary
On a moonlit night in March, a horse driven by young Emile Benoit repeatedly backed up and refused to pass a specific spot along a path between Three Rock Cove and Black Duck Brook on the Port au Port Peninsula, mirroring the experience of the local mailman whose horse also bolted from the unseen apparition.

#details
In March, young Emile Benoit hitched up his one-horse sleigh to visit his brother Ben in Three Rock Cove on the Port au Port Peninsula. After having supper there, he drove to visit his sister Yvonne whose house stood atop a rounded hill about a mile away. Later that evening, as Emile drove back along the path, his prized horse suddenly stopped. Despite Emile's commands, the horse began backing up along the path, refusing to go forward. Even after climbing up to look between the horse's ears, Emile could see nothing. Growing frustrated, he struck the horse with a whip, causing it to rear up, spin the sleigh around, and bolt all the way back to his sister's house in terror. When Emile asked his brother-in-law to accompany him, he refused and revealed that the local mailman's horse had experienced the exact same fright at the exact same spot earlier that night, forcing the mailman to seek shelter. The next morning, when Emile traveled the same path in broad daylight with a layer of frost on the ground, the horse passed the spot without even a twitch of its ears, and no tracks of any kind marred the frosty path. 

#location
A path between Three Rock Cove and Black Duck Brook on the Port au Port Peninsula, Newfoundland and Labrador.

#title
Apparitions at the Historic Blackhead Church

#summary
Parishioners and visitors walking the coastal path near Newfoundland's first Methodist church have reported unexplained lights and ghostly figures.

#details
Established in 1769 as the site of the very first Methodist church in Newfoundland, the historic settlement of Blackhead holds centuries of deep-rooted maritime and spiritual history. Over generations, locals walking along the old paths and near the historic cemetery after dark have reported sighting pale, indistinct figures wandering among the 18th-century headstones and unexplained glowing lights hovering over the rocky cliffsides facing Conception Bay.

#location
The historic Blackhead Methodist church and cemetery grounds in Blackhead, Newfoundland and Labrador.

#title
The Caribou of Adam's Cove

#summary
A bed-ridden man suffered under a witch's curse for eight years until the witch, transformed into a caribou, dramatically crashed through his house.

#details
In the community of Adam's Cove, a resident named John Murray was famously bed-ridden for eight years due to what locals recognized as a powerful witch's spell. The strange ordeal came to an abrupt end one day when the witch, taking the physical shape of a wild caribou, charged through the village and ran directly through Murray's house. The destructive passage broke the supernatural bond of the curse, releasing Murray from his long confinement.

#location
John Murray's former house located within the coastal community of Adam's Cove along the Baccalieu Trail in Newfoundland and Labrador.

#title
The Phantoms of The Hollies

#summary
Travellers passing through the wooded hills of Adam's Cove frequently report hearing disembodied voices and calling whispers known locally as The Hollies.

#details
Deep in the wooded hills and trails of Adam's Cove, an acoustic phenomenon and fairy lore tradition known as "The Hollies" has long unnerved locals. People walking through the area report hearing clear, distinct voices calling out their names or whispering unintelligible warnings from the trees where no one is standing. Believed in local Newfoundland folklore to be the lingering echoes of fairy mischief or restless spirits of the wilderness, these phantom sounds continue to caution travellers wandering the trails after dusk.

#location
The wooded hills and paths known as The Hollies in Adam's Cove, Newfoundland and Labrador.

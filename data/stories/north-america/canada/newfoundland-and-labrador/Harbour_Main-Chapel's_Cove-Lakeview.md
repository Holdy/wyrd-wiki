#title
The Desecration of the Chapel's Cove Cemetery

#summary
The historic 19th-century cemetery in the center of Chapel's Cove was bulldozed in 1959 to make way for a new parish hall, unearthing human remains that local children collected in wooden boxes.

#details
In the center of Chapel's Cove stood an old community cemetery where burials had ceased around 1900. In 1959 and 1960, following an announcement by Father Howard, plans were made to demolish the old parish hall and clear the cemetery grounds to build a new hall on the same footprint. A bulldozer brought in from Holyrood began excavating and leveling the site, cutting across family plots including infant graves. As the heavy machinery pushed forward, skeletal remains and wooden coffin boards were unearthed and crushed. Local school children watched in awe and gathered visible bones into a wooden container near the site, with remaining bones later moved to St. Peter and Paul Cemetery.

#location
The old cemetery site located in the center of Chapel's Cove, Newfoundland and Labrador.

#title
The Specter of Second Pond

#summary
Two boys walking home late at night near Second Pond in Lakeview in 1960 encountered a silent figure carrying a rifle who glided through the water without disturbing the surface before vanishing.

#details
Around midnight in the summer of 1960, Patrick Whelan and his friend Harold were walking home from Avondale to Chapel's Cove. Under a bright full moon shining above Hickey's Mountain, they noticed a man moving steadily along the edge of Second Pond in Lakeview. The figure was positioned outside the bushes in the deep, muddy pond, carrying what looked like a rifle supported on his shoulder and wearing a salt-and-pepper cap. Despite shouting and throwing crushed stone at the figure, he did not respond. When the boys ran ahead and looked back fifty feet later, the figure had completely vanished, leaving the water entirely undisturbed.

#location
Second Pond located alongside the road in Lakeview, Newfoundland and Labrador.

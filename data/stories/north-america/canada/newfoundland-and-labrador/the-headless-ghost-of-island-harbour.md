#title
The Headless Ghost of Island Harbour

#summary
A local resident traveling along a coastal road on Fogo Island encounters a well-dressed gentleman in a black suit who is entirely missing his head. 

#details
According to local folklore documented in community history, a man named Lynch from Island Harbour was walking along the road from Payne's Harbour to Butt's Point when he met a stranger dressed in a black suit. Lynch spoke to the man, but received no reply because the figure possessed no head. On a separate occasion, a woman walking home on the island noticed a man walking ahead of her; thinking it was her brother, she called out to him, only to discover that he, too, was completely headless.

#location
The road between Payne's Harbour and Butt's Point in Island Harbour, Fogo Island, Newfoundland and Labrador.

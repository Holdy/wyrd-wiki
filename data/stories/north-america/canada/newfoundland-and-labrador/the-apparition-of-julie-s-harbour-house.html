The Apparition of Julie's Harbour House

Summary

Card-playing locals in the last remaining house of a remote Newfoundland harbour witnessed an apparition floating through the walls, prompting them to tow the building away forever.

Details

In the resettled coastal community of Julie’s Harbour near Triton, Newfoundland, a small group of men including Skipper Mike’s father stayed up late playing cards inside the area's final remaining home. Amidst their game, they heard an unusual rustling sound and looked up to see an apparition drifting directly through the solid walls of the house. Terrified by the encounter, they never returned to dwell there, instead choosing to tow the entire house across the water to Triton.

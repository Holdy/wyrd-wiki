    Paddy Kelly and the Christmas Cat

    Summary

    On Christmas Eve in 1726, a traveller walking home to Keels encountered a mysterious shapeshifting black cat that stalked him along the snowy trail and grew larger with every step.

    Details

    In 1726, on Christmas Eve, a man named Paddy Kelly set out late at night walking from King's Cove toward his home in Keels along a narrow, snow-covered road. As he traversed the lonely trail past the St. Croix bend, he heard footsteps following him and caught sight of a cat with glowing red eyes. As the creature trailed him through the spruce trees, it inexplicably grew in size—expanding from a stray house cat into a dog, a wolf, and eventually as large as a foal. When the monstrous beast blocked the path and lunged at him, Paddy fought back with his dogberry walking stick. With every blow he landed, the creature shrank in size until it collapsed into the snow as a small, lifeless clump. The following morning, Paddy and his neighbours returned to inspect the bend in the road, finding only a strange, steaming black residue left behind where the terrifying encounter took place.

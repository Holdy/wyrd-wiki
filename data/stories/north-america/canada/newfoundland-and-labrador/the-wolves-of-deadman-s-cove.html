    The Wolves of Deadman's Cove

    Summary

    Wicked sailors who died in a shipwreck off the coast of Harbour Breton returned as phantom wolves that haunted the shore until a virtuous woman's purity caused them to drop dead.

    Details

    According to local folklore collected by students at King Academy in Harbour Breton from an account recorded around 1975, a ship carrying a deeply wicked and sinful crew wrecked upon the rocky shore of Deadman's Cove. Following the disaster, the evil spirits of the drowned sailors haunted the local coast, taking on the terrifying form of savage wolves—or possessing the bodies of real wolves—to attack and kill unwary travelers. One day, a local woman renowned for her moral purity walked down to the beach and was confronted by the pack of monstrous wolves. As the beasts lunged to attack her, they all abruptly fell dead in her tracks, illustrating that ultimate goodness triumphs over wickedness.

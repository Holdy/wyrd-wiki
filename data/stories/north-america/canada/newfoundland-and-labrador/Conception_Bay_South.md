#title
The Phantom Bride of the C.B.S. Train Tracks

#summary
A ghostly bride-to-be is said to wander the old railway tracks in Conception Bay South, doomed to pace the line after being left waiting at the altar.

#details
Local folklore in Conception Bay South speaks of an apparition seen walking along the old railway bed—now part of the Newfoundland T’Railway trail system. According to traditional accounts, the phantom is believed to be the spirit of a heartbroken bride-to-be who was abandoned on her wedding day. Unable to move past her grief, her spectral form continues to pace the lonely tracks in her wedding attire, appearing to startled late-night walkers against the backdrop of the rugged coastal landscape.

#location
The abandoned railway tracks (T'Railway) passing through Conception Bay South, Newfoundland and Labrador.

#title
The Pirate Guardian of Kelly's Island

#summary
Kelly’s Island in southeastern Conception Bay is said to be haunted by the ghost of a murdered pirate guarding his buried treasure.

#details
According to local legend in the Conception Bay South and Kelligrews area, Kelly’s Island—situated just off the coast in southeastern Conception Bay—was once the domain of a pirate named Captain Kelly, who allegedly sailed alongside Peter Easton. As the story goes, Kelly was murdered by his own crewmates, who buried his body atop a massive hidden cache of stolen treasure. Ever since, the pirate’s restless spirit has remained on the island to serve as a ghostly guardian. Campers and visitors to the uninhabited island over the decades have reported eerie encounters, unexplained noises, and shadowy figures pacing the shorelines after dark.

#location
Kelly's Island, located in southeastern Conception Bay just off the coast of Kelligrews / Conception Bay South, Newfoundland and Labrador.

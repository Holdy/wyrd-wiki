Man in the Meadow

Summary

A young girl and her aunt encounter a shadowy, faceless man while walking across a meadow on Fogo Island, only for him to vanish into thin air. Weeks later, another local traveler gets mysteriously disoriented in the exact same field amidst pristine snow while retracing the phantom's footsteps.

Details

In the early twentieth century, a girl and her aunt were walking along a path through Banks Meadow—a field located below Brimstone Head on Fogo Island—when they encountered a man wearing a short coat with his collar turned up against the cold. When the girl greeted him, he offered no reply and stepped back. After passing him, the girl looked back several times, only to find him standing motionless before he suddenly vanished. Later that winter, the same girl attempted to walk home across the meadow in the crisp snow, but found herself inexplicably unable to find the path or exit the field, despite seeing house lights and hearing barking dogs. When her cousin later walked back up with her, they discovered the snow was trampled into fresh footprints alongside her own, matching tales from locals who recalled that a grave had once existed on the meadow.

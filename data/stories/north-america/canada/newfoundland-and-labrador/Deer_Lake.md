#title
The Ghost of Sam at the Main Dam

#summary
Construction workers and visitors at the historic Main Dam near Deer Lake report encountering the spirit of Sam, a laborer who tragically fell into the wet concrete during the dam's construction in the 1920s.

#details
Built between 1922 and 1925 as part of the massive hydroelectric project supplying power to the Corner Brook pulp and paper mill, the Main Dam holds back millions of cubic metres of water in Grand Lake. According to local folklore, a worker named Sam lost his footing and fell into the freshly poured concrete forms during construction. Unable to be rescued before the heavy mix set, he became permanently entombed within the structure. Ever since, maintenance workers and visitors to the dam have reported unsettling paranormal phenomena and sightings of Sam's ghost haunting the concrete corridors and spillway.

#location
The Main Dam structure retaining Grand Lake, located off the Newfoundland T'Railway between Deer Lake and Howley, Newfoundland and Labrador.

#title
The Vanishing Hitchhiker of Deer Lake

#summary
Drivers traveling along the highway between Deer Lake and Corner Brook frequently report encountering a mysterious woman in white who vanishes into thin air or suddenly appears inside their vehicle.

#details
Documented as a staple of local folklore and examined by folklorists studying Newfoundland legends, the tale of the Deer Lake vanishing hitchhiker has been shared by motorists for decades. According to local accounts, drivers journeying at night along the route connecting Deer Lake to Corner Brook encounter a rain-soaked woman wearing white with her thumb extended. Those who slow down to offer assistance watch in astonishment as she completely disappears before reaching the car. Even more unsettling are reports from drivers who pass her by; checking their rearview mirror to find the road empty, they suddenly discover the hitchhiker sitting silently in the passenger seat beside them.

#location
The highway stretch connecting Deer Lake to Corner Brook, Newfoundland and Labrador.

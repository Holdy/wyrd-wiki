#title
Spirits and Apparitions of the Northern Peninsula

#summary
Local residents Nellie and Fenton Rumbolt passed down traditional accounts of restless ghosts and frightening apparitions haunting the coastal settlements near Port au Choix.

#details
Documented through interviews with local informants Nellie and Fenton Rumbolt in the Port au Choix area, this collection of regional folklore preserves chilling accounts of supernatural beings and wandering ghosts. The stories recount frightening figures and unexplained apparitions appearing along the lonely paths and shores of New Ferrole, reflecting the deep-seated oral tradition of Newfoundland's Great Northern Peninsula.

#location
New Ferrole is a coastal community located on the Great Northern Peninsula immediately adjacent to Port au Choix, Newfoundland and Labrador.

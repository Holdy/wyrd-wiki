#title
The White Woman of Arnold's Cove

#summary
On November 23rd every year, the ghost of a heartbroken bride wanders a cliff overlooking the ocean in Arnold's Cove, mourning the loss of her fiancé who drowned at sea on their wedding day.

#details
In the late 1800s, a young couple in the fishing community of Arnold's Cove scheduled their wedding for November 23rd. The groom-to-be went out fishing in August, promising to return in time for the ceremony. Months passed without word, but on the morning of the wedding, as the bride-to-be adjusted her veil, she spotted his ship sailing into the harbour. Her father was sent to greet him at the wharf, only to return with the tragic news that the vessel had been caught in a storm and the groom had drowned. Distraught and refusing to accept his death, the bride ran from her house in her wedding dress across the beach and through the woods to a cliff overlooking the ocean, where she flung herself off to her death. Local folklore maintains that her spirit returns to the cliff every November 23rd, wandering in her wedding attire and wailing for her lost love.

#location
A cliff overlooking the ocean and harbour in the community of Arnold's Cove, Placentia Bay, Newfoundland and Labrador.

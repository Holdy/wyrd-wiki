#title
The Drowned Sailors of Lark Harbour

#summary
During severe storms off the coast of Lark Harbour, the ghostly, desperate cries of a French crew who perished in a shipwreck can be heard echoing over the water.

#details
According to local Newfoundland folklore documented by historian Robert Russell, a French sailing vessel was caught in a violent tempest and driven onto a cluster of jagged sea rocks in the Bay of Islands near Lark Harbour, sinking with all hands lost. Ever since, locals report that whenever a heavy storm blows up along the coast, disembodied voices and cries for help from the drowned sailors ring out across the waves.

#location
The rocky coastline of Lark Harbour in the Bay of Islands, Newfoundland and Labrador.

#title
The Guarding Spirit of Shellbird Island

#summary
Shellbird Island in the Bay of Islands is haunted by the restless ghost of a murdered pirate buried alongside a hidden chest of stolen treasure.

#details
Regional legend holds that during a flight from French warships, pirates buried chests of plunder on Shellbird Island in the Bay of Islands. To ensure the loot remained undisturbed, a pirate first mate executed a companion and interred his corpse with the gold so his spirit would serve as an eternal guardian. Treasure hunters and locals have long maintained that the murdered pirate's ghost still haunts the island to protect the remaining hidden treasure.

#location
Shellbird Island in the Bay of Islands, Newfoundland and Labrador.

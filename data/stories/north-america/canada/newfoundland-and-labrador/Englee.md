#title
The Apparition at the Englee Fishing Stage

#summary
A local fisherman in the coastal community of Englee encountered a mysterious phantom figure lingering near the settlement's waterfront fishing premises late at night.

#details
Collected as part of local oral tradition in Englee, White Bay, this account describes a fisherman who went down to the local fishing stage after dark and witnessed a luminous, silent apparition standing near the salt bins. The figure vanished into thin air when approached, leaving behind an eerie chill and becoming a widely discussed local haunting among residents.

#location
The historic fishing stage along the waterfront of Englee, White Bay, Newfoundland and Labrador.

#proposed-coordinates
50.77236365, -56.07155755

#upstream-source
Collected by Bobby Fillier in *Two local ghost stories from Englee, White Bay, Newfoundland and Labrador* (Memorial University of Newfoundland Folklore and Language Archive, Accession No. 70-012E, 1970).

#title
The Black Heart Book and the Haunting in Englee

#summary
Residents of a house in Englee experienced unexplained disturbances and terrifying nocturnal noises linked to local legends of a forbidden book of dark arts.

#details
According to local folklore recorded in Englee, a specific residence became the site of persistent hauntings, mysterious footsteps, and scratching sounds after a resident allegedly interacted with the legendary "Black Heart Book"—a supernatural volume associated with witchcraft in the White Bay region. The disturbances frightened local inhabitants until traditional folklore remedies and protective measures were invoked.

#location
A residential house situated within the coastal settlement of Englee, White Bay, Newfoundland and Labrador.

#proposed-coordinates
50.77236365, -56.07155755

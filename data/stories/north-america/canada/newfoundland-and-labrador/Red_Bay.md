#title
The Headless Pirate of Red Bay

#summary
Local folklore tells of the spectral apparition of a headless pirate said to haunt the historic coastal waters and shores of Red Bay.

#details
According to regional oral tradition and ghost lore collected on the Labrador coast, the spirit of a decapitated pirate wanders the remote and historic harbor area of Red Bay. Long before it gained renown for its 16th-century Basque whaling history, the deep, cold waters and isolated coves birthed legends of buccaneers meeting violent ends, with the headless pirate remaining as a restless phantom keeping watch over the coastline.

#location
The historic harbor and shores of Red Bay, Labrador.

#title
The Phantom Voices of Red Bay Harbor

#summary
Unexplained disembodied voices and spectral sounds have long been reported echoing across the waters and shoreline of Red Bay.

#details
Folkloric accounts from the Labrador coast recount eerie, disembodied human voices heard echoing over the cold waters and rocky terrain of Red Bay when no living persons are near. Fishermen and local residents over generations have reported hearing mysterious shouting, crying, or conversational murmurs carried on the wind across the harbor, attributed by locals to the lingering spirits of past mariners and whalers lost to the treacherous northern seas.

#title
The Haunted House of Ship Island

#summary
A mysterious light was frequently sighted in the window of an abandoned house on Ship Island where a murder had taken place over a century prior.

#details
In local folklore from the historic outport of Greenspond, a particular house on nearby Ship Island carried a sinister reputation. Following a violent murder that took place within its walls more than a hundred years earlier, locals reported that a ghostly light could regularly be seen shining from the window at night, striking fear into residents who crossed near the island.

#location
An abandoned house on Ship Island, just off Greenspond in Bonavista Bay, Newfoundland and Labrador.

#title
The Phantom Splash of the Ferry Wharf

#summary
Residents crossing the wharf connecting Greenspond to Ship Island at night reported hearing the chilling spectral sound of a man plunging into the water.

#details
According to local oral accounts recorded from Greenspond resident George Meadus, townsfolk once lived with an everyday fear of ghosts and the supernatural. On certain nights, anyone walking across the large ferry wharf linking Greenspond to Ship Island would hear the distinct, terrifying sound of a man falling overboard—a spectral replaying of an accidental drowning that had happened years prior.

#location
The ferry wharf connecting Greenspond to Ship Island in Bonavista Bay, Newfoundland and Labrador.

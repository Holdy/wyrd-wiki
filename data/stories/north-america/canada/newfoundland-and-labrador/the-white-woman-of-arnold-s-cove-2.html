The White Woman of Arnold's Cove

Summary

A heartbroken bride who flung herself off a cliff upon learning of her fiancé's drowning at sea is said to haunt the cliffs of Arnold's Cove every year on November 23rd.

Details

In the late 1800s, two young lovers in Arnold's Cove scheduled their wedding for November 23rd. The groom, a fisherman, set sail in August with a promise to return in time for the ceremony. When November arrived with no word from him, the bride steadfastly maintained he would return. On their wedding morning, as she spotted his ship entering the harbour, her father returned with devastating news: the vessel had been caught in a storm and the groom had drowned. Overcome with grief, the bride ran to a cliff overlooking the ocean and plunged to her death. Local folklore maintains that every year on the anniversary of her death, her ghost wanders the cliffs in her wedding dress and veil, weeping and wailing for her lost love.

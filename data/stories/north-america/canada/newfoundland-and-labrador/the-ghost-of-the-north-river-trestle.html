The Ghost of the North River Trestle

Summary

An abandoned railway bridge in North River is said to be haunted by the spirit of a girl who died there, posing a deadly threat to anyone who crosses after dark.

Details

Local lore surrounding the old railway trestle crossing in North River warns visitors and locals alike to stay away after nightfall. According to regional folklore, a young girl lost her life on the structure, and her restless ghost now haunts the bridge. Locals recount that she actively tries to attack and harm anyone brave or foolish enough to trespass on the trestle during the night.

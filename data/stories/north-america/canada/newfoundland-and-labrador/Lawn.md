#title
The Headless Phantom of the Spring Well

#summary
A restless headless spirit is said to haunt the historic Spring Well in Lawn, perpetually searching for its lost head.

#details
Local folklore in the community of Lawn on the Burin Peninsula tells of a gruesome apparition known to haunt a specific watering hole known as the Spring Well. According to traditional accounts recorded by Newfoundland author Robert Parsons, a headless phantom returns to the spring year after year, doomed to wander the area in an endless quest to find its missing head. 

#location
The Spring Well in Lawn, Burin Peninsula, Newfoundland and Labrador.

#title
The Mystery Light of Middle Island

#summary
In the autumn of 1889, a brilliant, unexplained revolving light appeared nightly off Middle Island near Lawn, puzzling locals and worrying mariners.

#details
Beginning in the first week of October 1889, residents of Lawn on the Burin Peninsula witnessed a strange, glowing phenomenon just off the coast near Middle Island and Jersey Cove. The light appeared every night as a powerful revolving flash with intervals of ten to fifteen minutes, visible from up to twenty miles away. Shifting in color between blinding white and reddish hues before vanishing abruptly, the mysterious light caused genuine alarm among local sea captains who feared it would be mistaken for a navigation beacon and lead vessels to disaster. Despite intense local discussion and coverage in the *Evening Mercury* in November 1889, the true origin of the light was never discovered, later earning comparisons to modern UFOs and traditional weather light folklore.

#location
Middle Island, just off the coast of Lawn, Newfoundland and Labrador.

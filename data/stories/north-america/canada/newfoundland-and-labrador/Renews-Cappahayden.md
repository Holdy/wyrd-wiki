#title
The Clandestine Masses of Mass Rock and Midnight Hill

#summary
A historic hillside and rock formation in Renews where Catholics secretly gathered to worship during the enforcement of Penal Laws, protected by lookouts stationed on a commanding vantage point.

#details
Dating back to the early 18th century, when British colonial authorities strictly enforced Penal Laws against Catholics in Newfoundland, Mass Rock in Renews served as the clandestine site for secret religious gatherings. Disguised priests and local settlers held masses and prayers hidden away from English overseers, while lookouts stood watch at the crest of nearby Midnight Hill—a high vantage point offering a commanding view of the settlement. In 1927 and 1928, a grotto of Our Lady of Lourdes was constructed at the foot of the hill. Local tradition holds that water running into the grotto, mixed with water from Lourdes, France, possesses curative powers, cementing the site's enduring spiritual and legendary significance.

#location
Midnight Hill and Mass Rock are centrally located in the community of Renews, Renews-Cappahayden, Newfoundland and Labrador.

#title
The Devil's Rock of Renews

#summary
A rock outcrop at a community crossroads in Renews where the Devil was imprisoned by a priest, leaving behind his hoof prints and a slowly widening crack that heralds his eventual escape.

#details
According to local oral tradition in Renews, a priest walking past the crossroads encountered the Devil and condemned him into the stone outcrop for a time. As the Devil was being imprisoned, he stamped his hoof prints permanently into the rock. Legend maintains that a crack running through the rock widens infinitesimally each year, and when it finally splits the rock apart, the Devil will break free once more. Despite its fearful name and demonic lore, the rock historically served as a popular romantic rendezvous spot for courting couples in the community.

#location
Devil's Rock is located at the crossroads of the access roads leading into the community of Renews, Renews-Cappahayden, Newfoundland and Labrador.

#title
Billy Philpott and Spirit's Hole

#summary
After taking an Eskimo woman's skull as a makeshift dipper, a fisherman was haunted by a phantom figure during his travels near King's Cove before perishing in a blizzard near a cliffside cave known as Spirit's Hole.

#details
In the nineteenth century, fisherman Billy Philpott of Tickle Cove discovered the decayed skull of an Eskimo woman while fishing in Labrador and kept it to use as a water dipper in his boat. Soon after, he reported seeing a mysterious phantom figure flitting across his boat and later beckoning to him from a hole in the cliff during his frequent trips between King's Cove, Keels, and Tickle Cove. One spring day, despite warnings of an impending severe storm, Billy walked from King's Cove back toward Tickle Cove carrying provisions. A massive blizzard struck, and searchers later found his package tied to a tree and his body face down in the snow. On the night of his death, villagers reported seeing a red ball of fire ascending into the sky with two ghostlike figures clinging to it. The cliffside cave where his apparition was first sighted became known as "Billy Philpott's Hole" or "Spirit's Hole."

#location
Billy Philpott's Hole (Spirit's Hole), a cave in the coastal cliffs along the route between King's Cove and Tickle Cove, Bonavista Bay, Newfoundland and Labrador, Canada.

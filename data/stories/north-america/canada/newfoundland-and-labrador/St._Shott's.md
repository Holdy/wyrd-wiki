#title
The Phantom Vessel of St. Shott's

#summary
Fishermen and coastal residents of St. Shott's have long reported sightings of a spectral ghost ship emerging from the fog and mist off the treacherous southern Avalon coast.

#details
The waters off St. Shott's on the southern tip of the Avalon Peninsula have earned a fearsome reputation as part of the "Graveyard of the Atlantic," claiming numerous vessels over the centuries due to severe weather, fog, and rocky shoals. Within local maritime folklore, this history of tragedy gave rise to persistent accounts of a phantom vessel. According to local lore, on stormy or misty nights, a mysterious ghost ship appears sailing just off the coast of St. Shott's before vanishing without a trace. Believed by locals to be a harbinger of oncoming gales or a spectral reminder of the many souls lost to the sea, the sighting of the phantom ship remains an enduring piece of Newfoundland maritime legend.

#location
The coastal waters just off the community of St. Shott's on the southern Avalon Peninsula of Newfoundland.

#title
The Spirits of the Cape Pine Barrens

#summary
Locals growing up in St. Shott's and traveling the rugged barrens toward Cape Pine frequently spoke of encountering the lingering spirits of those lost in the area's numerous shipwrecks.

#details
In the remote coastal community of St. Shott's and the neighboring historic headland of Cape Pine, older residents historically spoke very casually of encounters with ghosts and spirits. Rather than tales meant purely to frighten, these accounts reflected the thin veil between the living and the dead in a region devastated by maritime disasters in the 19th and early 20th centuries. Travelers along the rugged gravel roads and coastal cliffs between St. Shott's and Cape Pine Lighthouse—built in 1851 to stem the tide of shipwrecks—frequently noted strange mechanical failures, sudden unexplained incidents, and a heavy sense of unseen presences, traditionally interpreted as poor souls in need of a prayer.

#location
The coastal road and barrens between St. Shott's and Cape Pine Lighthouse on the southern tip of the Avalon Peninsula, Newfoundland.

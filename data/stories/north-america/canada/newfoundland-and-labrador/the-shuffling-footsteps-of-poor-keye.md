#title
The Shuffling Footsteps of Poor Keye

#summary
During a stormy night in a Bay de Verde home, children and adults heard the familiar, uneven footsteps of a hospitalized neighbor coming down the hallway. The next morning, a telegram arrived confirming that he had passed away at that exact hour.

#details
In the 1920s in the coastal community of Bay de Verde, a family and visiting neighbors sat talking late into the night while children listened quietly from a daybed. The conversation turned to Poor Keye, a local neighbor who was currently receiving medical treatment in a hospital in St. John's, known affectionately for his distinct "step-shuffle-thump" walk due to an injured leg. Suddenly, the front door crashed open and unmistakable shuffling footsteps were heard marching down the hall. When the mother took a lamp to check the hallway, no one was there, and a subsequent crash was heard in the back kitchen. The following morning, news arrived via telegram that Poor Keye had died at the exact time the spectral footsteps echoed through the house, marking a classic Newfoundland forerunner.

#location
A historic family home in the coastal fishing community of Bay de Verde on the Avalon Peninsula of Newfoundland and Labrador.

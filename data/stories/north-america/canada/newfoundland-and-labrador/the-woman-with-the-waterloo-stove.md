#title
The Woman with the Waterloo Stove

#summary
Travelers along a stretch of road in White Bay have reported encountering a phantom woman dressed in white who carries a heavy cast-iron stove on her back. This bizarre apparition is said to haunt Faulkner's Flat near Hampden.

#details
Stories of Hampden's famous phantom have been in circulation since at least the early 1970s. The majority of these ghostly encounters take place along a section of road near the site of an old camp known as Faulkner's Flat, just outside of Hampden. Witnesses have repeatedly reported seeing the figure of an elderly woman walking along the roadside dressed entirely in white. Most remarkably, she is seen carrying an old cast-iron Waterloo stove on her back. According to local lore, the apparition is either a woman who was killed by a Waterloo stove in a vehicular accident on that road or old Mrs. Faulkner herself, the historical namesake of the flat.

#location
Faulkner's Flat is a section of road located just outside of Hampden in White Bay, Newfoundland and Labrador.

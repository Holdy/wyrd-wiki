#title
The Phantom of the James House

#summary
The historic James House on Riverhead Road in Brigus is said to be haunted by a family spirit that occasionally manifests on the upper floor.

#details
Originally constructed in the early 1800s and later relocated on Riverhead Road in Brigus, the James House was passed down through generations of the James family until recent decades. Following modern renovations by new owners, occupants experienced unexplained paranormal activity on the second floor of the dwelling. According to local lore, the entity haunting the timber-framed home is the spirit of a former James family member who continues to linger within the historic property.

#location
The James House located on Riverhead Road in Brigus, Newfoundland and Labrador.

#title
The Seaman's Pilot of the SS Kyle

#summary
Following a severe winter storm in 1967, the historic steamship SS Kyle broke its moorings and drifted safely to the Riverhead in Harbour Grace, an uncanny voyage that local mariners attributed to a guiding seaman's ghost.

#details
Built in Newcastle-on-Tyne in 1913, the SS Kyle served as Newfoundland's first scheduled ferryboat, an icebreaker, and later as a sealing vessel. After sustaining heavy damage from an iceberg in 1965, the ship was laid up in Harbour Grace. On February 4, 1967, a violent winter storm tore the vessel from its moorings. While most drifting vessels were dashed to pieces against rocky shores, the Kyle traveled upright across the dark waters and settled neatly on a bed of mussels at the Riverhead in Harbour Grace. Local fishermen and mariners maintained that it was not the ship's destiny to sink to a deep underwater grave, and that the vessel was guided to its permanent resting place by a seaman's ghost so its illustrious history would never be forgotten.

#location
The Riverhead area of Harbour Grace, Newfoundland and Labrador, where the beached hull of the SS Kyle rests.

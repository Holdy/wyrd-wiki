The Devil's Stairway of Cape Broyle Head

Summary

A precipitous cliff formation on the Southern Shore is tied to local legends of a quick-tempered sea captain and supernatural encounters along the treacherous headlands.

Details

Overlooking the rough waters of the Southern Shore near Cape Broyle Head, a sheer cliff known locally as the "Devil's Stairway" (or Devil's Staircase) has long been the subject of eerie local folklore. Recorded in mid-20th-century oral history collections such as The Barrelman broadcasts, the legend tells of a coasting captain who experienced a bizarre and harrowing supernatural encounter along the cliffside. The treacherous terrain and dizzying drops of Cape Broyle Head inspired generations of stories regarding uncanny events and otherworldly forces haunting the jagged coastline.

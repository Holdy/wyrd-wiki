#title
The Cheap Ghost of Barn Hill

#summary
Children in Baie Verte were traditionally warned to stay away from the town's old Roman Catholic cemetery because it was haunted by an old man buried with $150 who guarded his money from beyond the grave.

#details
In the town of Baie Verte, a local urban legend tells of a miserly ghost haunting the community's burial grounds. Generations of children were warned by their elders to stay out of the old Roman Catholic cemetery because the ground was haunted by an old man who had been buried there years earlier with $150 in cash in his pocket. According to the tale, the spirit lingered in the cemetery specifically to protect and watch over his money from anyone who might dare disturb it.

#location
The old Roman Catholic cemetery is located on Barn Hill in Baie Verte, Newfoundland and Labrador.

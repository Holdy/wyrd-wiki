#title
The Romaines Spooklight

#summary
A mysterious glowing orb is said to materialize near the tree line of a farmer's field in Romaines, just outside Kippens.

#details
Local folklore on the west coast of Newfoundland tells of an unexplained nocturnal phenomenon in the community of Romaines, located immediately adjacent to Kippens. According to regional accounts passed down among residents, if motorists park their vehicles and wait near the tree line of a farmer's field in Romaines, a spectral ball of light will appear from the darkness. The luminous orb reportedly drifts silently along the edge of the woods before vanishing into the night.

#location
A farmer's field tree line in Romaines, just outside Kippens, Newfoundland and Labrador.

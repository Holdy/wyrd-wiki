#title
The Phantom Viking Longship of Fortune Bay

#summary
Fishermen and coastal residents near the Burin Peninsula reported eerie encounters with a phantom Viking longship and a ghostly crew carrying their vessel over the hills.

#details
In Fortune Bay, adjacent to the Burin Peninsula, a group of local fishermen sailing into Bay de L'Eau encountered a ghostly longship coming out of the bay. The phantom vessel was manned by crew members wearing fur pelts and speaking an unintelligible language before it vanished into the fog. Years earlier in the nearby fishing village of Harbour Mille, a resident named Aunt Sarah looked out her second-storey bedroom window at night to observe men bringing a flat barge ashore from the water, hoisting the heavy craft onto their shoulders, and portaging it on foot over the saddle-like hills separating the communities.

#location
The sightings occurred in the waters and coastal hills between Harbour Mille and Bay de L'Eau in Fortune Bay, near the Burin Peninsula, Newfoundland.

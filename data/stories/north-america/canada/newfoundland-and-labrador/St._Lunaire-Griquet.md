#title
The Ghost of Marguerite on Quirpon Island

#summary
Staff and guests at the historic Quirpon Island lighthouse near St. Lunaire-Griquet have long reported sightings of the ghost of 16th-century French noblewoman Marguerite de La Rocque, who was famously marooned on the island.

#details
In 1542, French noblewoman Marguerite de La Rocque was accompanying her uncle on an expedition to North America when she became romantically entangled with a sailor on the ship. Enraged by the affair, her uncle abandoned her, her lover, her handmaid, and her newborn baby on the forbidding "Isle of Demons"—historically identified as Quirpon Island, located just off the coast of St. Lunaire-Griquet. Her companions and child all perished within months, leaving Marguerite to survive entirely alone in the harsh wilderness for nearly two years before being rescued by passing fishermen. Centuries later, her harrowing ordeal has passed into local folklore, and keepers and visitors at the Quirpon Lighthouse Inn have reported seeing the phantom figure of Marguerite wandering the island's windswept tablelands.

#location
Quirpon Island, located just off the northernmost tip of St. Lunaire-Griquet, Newfoundland and Labrador.

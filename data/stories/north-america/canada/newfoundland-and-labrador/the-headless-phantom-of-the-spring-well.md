#title
The Headless Phantom of the Spring Well

#summary
A restless headless spirit is said to haunt the historic Spring Well in Lawn, perpetually searching for its lost head.

#details
Local folklore in the community of Lawn on the Burin Peninsula tells of a gruesome apparition known to haunt a specific watering hole known as the Spring Well. According to traditional accounts recorded by Newfoundland author Robert Parsons, a headless phantom returns to the spring year after year, doomed to wander the area in an endless quest to find its missing head. 

#location
The Spring Well in Lawn, Burin Peninsula, Newfoundland and Labrador.

#title
My Great Grandmother Attacked by Fairies

#summary
A young girl helping harvest potatoes in Upper Island Cove was struck by an unseen fairy attack at a bonfire, resulting in severe physical trauma.

#details
When Grandmother Mercer was a little girl, she was harvesting potatoes in a garden in Upper Island Cove with her grandparents. In the late evening after supper, she roasted potatoes on a stick over an open fire. Suddenly, she felt an immense, agonizing pain in her jaw. Her grandparents took her home with a towel wrapped over her face, from which a strange fluid drained. The following day, she was taken to St. John's General Hospital where her jawbone had to be removed, and she remained hospitalized for 13 months, with a doctor from Harbour Grace attributing the mysterious affliction to fairies.

#location
A garden in Upper Island Cove, Newfoundland and Labrador.

#title
The Drowned Friend's Beacon

#summary
A fisherman lost at sea returns as a spectral light and ghostly apparition to guide his former benefactor away from dangerous coastal rocks in Upper Island Cove.

#details
One winter, a fisherman in Upper Island Cove found himself without food and facing starvation, but his good friend shared half of his only barrel of flour. The following spring, the man who received the flour drowned when his ship struck a jagged rock off the coast of Island Cove. About a week later, his benefactor was out fishing in the same area when a thick fog rolled in, leaving him lost and disoriented. Suddenly, a large light flashed near the fatal rocks, and the ghostly figure of his drowned friend appeared, warning him with a ghostly voice, "The rocks, the rocks," thereby saving his life.

#location
Off the rocky coast and waters of Upper Island Cove, Newfoundland and Labrador.

#title
The Fairies of the Steep Valley

#summary
Children who wandered into a steep valley in Upper Island Cove vanished without a trace, believed to have been taken by the fairies.

#details
Residents of Upper Island Cove historically forbade children from walking into the steep valley leading from the barren land because, many years ago, children walked down into the gorge and were never seen again. Elders maintained that the children were taken by the fairies and still live with them down in the valley.

#location
The steep valley leading from the barren lands in Upper Island Cove, Newfoundland and Labrador.

#title
The Phantom Sailors of Upper Island Cove

#summary
On misty nights, the ghostly crews of a shipwrecked fishing vessel wander through the valleys of Upper Island Cove searching for their lost companions.

#details
On the shores of Upper Island Cove, there is a massive rock formation where, in the early 1800s, a fishing vessel struck the rocks and everyone on board perished. Local legend maintains that on misty, foggy nights, the ghostly sailors walk through the valleys and along the shores of Upper Island Cove looking for their fellow crew members.

#location
The massive rock formation and coastal valleys of Upper Island Cove, Newfoundland and Labrador.

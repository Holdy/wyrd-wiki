#title
Minnie Keefe's Miracle

#summary
A two-year-old toddler vanishes into the wilderness for eight days before being miraculously found alive, sparking local legends of divine intervention and fairy lore.

#details
In August 1902, two-year-old Minnie Keefe disappeared from her home near Thistle's Mill in Colinet. After a frantic, week-long search by over a hundred townspeople scouring the riverbanks, dense woods, and surrounding wilderness, she was discovered alive eight days later along a seldom-traveled road near the sand pits. Having survived over a week with no food, water, or shelter, her baffling ordeal inspired widespread wonder, with residents whispering about divine protection and traditional fairy lore.

#location
The sand pits road and surrounding wilderness area outside Colinet, Newfoundland and Labrador.

#title
Paddy Mahony's Gold

#summary
A hermit searching for buried treasure on a lonely island encounters a terrifying supernatural guardian.

#details
In days gone by, a man named Paddy Mahony lived in a solitary hut at Horse's Head on Little Colinet Island. When it was mysteriously revealed to him that a great treasure was buried nearby, Paddy eagerly set out with a shovel to unearth the gold. However, upon digging deep into the earth and reaching the bottom of his pit, he discovered that the hidden wealth was fiercely protected by a supernatural guardian, cutting his quest for riches short.

#location
Horse's Head on Little Colinet Island in St. Mary's Bay, Newfoundland and Labrador.

#upstream-source
Dale Gilbert Jarvis, *Haunted Shores: True Ghost Stories of Newfoundland and Labrador* (Flanker Press).

#reference

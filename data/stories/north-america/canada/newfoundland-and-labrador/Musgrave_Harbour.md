#title
Supernatural Lore of the Musgrave Harbour Eastern Shore

#summary
Traditional coastal accounts passed down through generations detail ghostly apparitions, witchcraft, and supernatural encounters along the eastern shore of Musgrave Harbour.

#details
Recorded in local oral history and folklore archives, residents along the eastern shore of Musgrave Harbour preserved numerous traditional accounts dealing with the supernatural, ghosts, and local witchcraft. These legends describe eerie post-mortem visitations, phantom lights, and mysterious occurrences experienced by fishermen and families returning home late at night along the rocky shores and coastal paths of Notre Dame Bay.

#location
The eastern shore settlements and coastal paths of Musgrave Harbour, Notre Dame Bay, Newfoundland and Labrador.

49.42624145, -53.97044295

Folktales form the eastern shore, Musgrave Habour, Newfoundland collected by Betty Gail Mouland (MUNFLA Accession 89-164).

#title
The Phantom of Shalloway Brook

#summary
A local traveler walking near Musgrave Harbour encounters a terrifying one-legged specter materializing out of a sudden, localized whirlwind.

#details
According to coastal folklore collected along the northeast coast of Newfoundland, a local man walking near Shalloway Brook—situated between Deadman's Bay and Musgrave Harbour—experienced a bizarre and terrifying phenomenon. On a completely still day with no other drafts of wind, he was suddenly engulfed by a powerful whirlwind. Out of the swirling vortex materialized a ghostly figure: a terrifying man with one wooden leg, a great wound in his head, wearing a three-cornered hat, and clad in a uniform adorned with gleaming brass buttons down the front while gripping a cutlass in his right hand.

#location
Shalloway Brook, located between Deadman's Bay and Musgrave Harbour, Newfoundland and Labrador.

#title
The Haunted Cries of Flat Bay Brook
#summary
Campers spending the night near the old burial ground at Flat Bay Brook were terrorized by disembodied weeping, mumbling voices, and a violently shaking tent.
#details
A group of friends who went camping near Flat Bay Brook—known locally for its old burial ground—experienced a night of supernatural terror. In the middle of the night, they were awakened by eerie, disembodied cries and a woman's mumbling voice echoing through the dark. Moments later, their tent began to shake violently from an invisible force outside. Terrified by the relentless disturbance, the campers packed up their gear at first light and immediately fled the area.
#location
Flat Bay Brook near St. George's, Newfoundland and Labrador.

#title
Spirits and Apparitions of the Northern Peninsula

#summary
Local residents Nellie and Fenton Rumbolt passed down traditional accounts of restless ghosts and frightening apparitions haunting the coastal settlements near Port au Choix.

#details
Documented through interviews with local informants Nellie and Fenton Rumbolt in the Port au Choix area, this collection of regional folklore preserves chilling accounts of supernatural beings and wandering ghosts. The stories recount frightening figures and unexplained apparitions appearing along the lonely paths and shores of New Ferrole, reflecting the deep-seated oral tradition of Newfoundland's Great Northern Peninsula.

#location
New Ferrole is a coastal community located on the Great Northern Peninsula immediately adjacent to Port au Choix, Newfoundland and Labrador.

#title
The Haunting Reminiscences of Point Riche Lighthouse

#summary
Margaret "Aunt Meg" Breton preserved the harrowing oral histories of shipwrecks, isolation, and eerie supernatural encounters at the remote Point Riche Lighthouse in Port au Choix.

#details
Living for decades as the lighthouse keeper's wife at the isolated Point Riche Lighthouse on the coast of Port au Choix, Margaret Breton ("Aunt Meg") experienced and recounted numerous local legends. Her personal narratives, documented in archival folklore collections, detail the grim realities of coastal shipwrecks along the jagged limestone shores, alongside uncanny traditional accounts of phantom occurrences and spirits witnessed by those tending the light.

#location
The Point Riche Lighthouse is situated on the Point Riche peninsula on the western coast of Port au Choix, Newfoundland and Labrador.

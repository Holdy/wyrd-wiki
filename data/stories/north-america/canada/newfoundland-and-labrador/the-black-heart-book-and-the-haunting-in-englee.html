The Black Heart Book and the Haunting in Englee

Summary

Residents of a house in Englee experienced unexplained disturbances and terrifying nocturnal noises linked to local legends of a forbidden book of dark arts.

Details

According to local folklore recorded in Englee, a specific residence became the site of persistent hauntings, mysterious footsteps, and scratching sounds after a resident allegedly interacted with the legendary "Black Heart Book"—a supernatural volume associated with witchcraft in the White Bay region. The disturbances frightened local inhabitants until traditional folklore remedies and protective measures were invoked.

The Mystery Lady of Long Point Lighthouse

Summary

A light keeper working near the top of the tower fell twelve meters toward the brick floor, only to be caught mid-air by a phantom woman dressed all in white before she vanished into thin air. The miraculous rescue was passed down as legend among generations of lighthouse keepers.

Details

In the early 1900s, a keeper at the Long Point Lighthouse was working near the top of the tower to untangle the weights and cables of the clockwork system. While positioned high above the floor, he lost his footing and plunged twelve meters straight down toward the unyielding brick floor below, with no handholds or safety supports to arrest his fall. Just before impact, he was miraculously caught in the arms of a woman dressed entirely in white. When he recovered and turned to thank her, she vanished completely into thin air, leaving no explanation of who she was or how she could have broken a fatal fall.

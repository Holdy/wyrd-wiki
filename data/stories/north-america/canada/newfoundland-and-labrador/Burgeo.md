#title
The Guardian Spirit of Connoire Bay

#summary
In the late 1960s, a man named James was tormented by an invisible, roaring spirit at his coastal cabin, which ultimately drove him to abandon the property. Days after the cabin was relocated inland, a massive storm struck the area, burying the original site under eight to ten feet of rock and gravel.

#details
In the late 1960s, James purchased a cabin situated on a point of land in West Barachois at Connoire Bay, just west of Burgeo, Newfoundland. Shortly after moving in, he was plagued by terrifying nighttime noises that sounded like an animal scratching at the exterior and tearing at the roof shingles. Despite staying up all night with a loaded gun and setting mud traps outside to capture tracks, James never saw any creature, and no footprints were ever left behind. The invisible entity tormented him for three consecutive nights until he abandoned the cabin and sold it. The new owners subsequently moved the structure further inland. Shortly after the move, a severe storm struck Connoire Bay, hurling massive boulders onto the point and burying the cabin's original site under several feet of rock and gravel, indicating that the haunting served as a life-saving warning.

#location
The cabin and point of land are located in West Barachois at Connoire Bay, just west of Burgeo on the southwest coast of Newfoundland, Canada.

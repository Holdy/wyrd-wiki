#title
The Cloven Hooves at the Spaniard's Bay Dance

#summary
A young woman at a community dance in Spaniard's Bay who carelessly wished to dance with the devil found herself waltzing with a stranger who possessed cloven hooves.

#details
As recounted in local oral tradition, a young woman attending a dance in Spaniard's Bay was having little success finding partners. Frustrated, she remarked aloud that she would dance with the devil himself if he were present. Shortly afterward, an unfamiliar man approached and asked her to waltz. During the dance, she glanced down and was horrified to discover that her partner had cloven hooves instead of human feet.

#location
A community dance hall in Spaniard's Bay, Newfoundland and Labrador.

#title
The Lady in Blue of Spaniard's Bay

#summary
Berry-pickers near Seymours Road encountered an elderly woman dressed in blue who vanished mysteriously over a ridge, a location long known as a haunted fairy path.

#details
Near Seymours Road in Spaniard's Bay, an old stone track known locally as "The Fairy Path" cuts through fields, with portions lost to modern home construction. Around the year 2000, local resident Sheena Butler and her friends were out picking partridge berries near the path when they spotted an older woman with greying hair wearing an old blue sundress, who was also picking berries some distance away. When they called out to ask if the berries were good up ahead, the woman ignored them, struggled up the hill with a hunched back, and disappeared over the ridge. Upon climbing the hill to investigate, the group found nobody there. Other visitors to the path have reported candles being mysteriously extinguished by unseen breezes, followed by rustling in the bushes and eerie laughter.

#location
Seymours Road in Spaniard's Bay, along the historic stone walkway known as the Fairy Path.

#title
The White Lights of Cranes Road

#summary
Travelers along Cranes Road during winter occasionally witness a mysterious white light representing a family that tragically perished in the snow.

#details
According to local folklore collected in Spaniard's Bay, a family traveling toward Island Cove during the winter months tragically perished in the freezing weather. Over the years, locals driving or walking along Cranes Road report seeing their lingering spirits manifested as a strange, ghostly white light moving through the winter landscape.

#location
Cranes Road in Spaniard's Bay, Newfoundland and Labrador.

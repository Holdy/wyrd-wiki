#title
Jacob and the Corpse Candle

#summary
A grieving father in St. Lawrence witnesses a mysterious floating flame drift from his home to the local cemetery, foretelling the tragic illness and passing of his young son.

#details
In St. Lawrence, Tom McGrath was mourning the loss of his father when he began hearing unexplained footsteps coming from his late father's abandoned upstairs room. One morning, he watched in astonishment as a floating, candle-like golden flame drifted down the stairs, out the door, and along the path toward the local churchyard and cemetery before fading away. Shortly afterward, his twelve-year-old son Jacob fell ill with a sudden, fatal fever, whispering as he passed away that his grandfather had come to guide him. The account draws upon traditional Newfoundland folklore regarding "corpse candles," spectral lights believed to act as omens of death and spirit guides for the dying.

#location
A saltbox house near the churchyard and cemetery in St. Lawrence, Newfoundland and Labrador.

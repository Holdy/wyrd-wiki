#title
The Boy of Dunn Place

#summary
A large mansion on a residential side street gained a local reputation for being haunted by the spirit of a young boy who tragically died during a childhood game.

#details
Growing up in Grand Falls, local residents and children often whispered about a spooky, large home located downtown off Goodyear Avenue on Dunn Place. According to local lore, a young boy living in the house had been playing superhero while wearing a towel or scarf as a cape. While sliding down the staircase banister, the fabric tragically caught, resulting in a fatal accident. In the years that followed, neighbors and children believed the grand old house was haunted by the ghost of the young boy, establishing a persistent urban legend in the community.

#location
The historic house situated on Dunn Place, just off Goodyear Avenue in Grand Falls-Windsor, Newfoundland and Labrador.

#title
The Snow-White Soldier

#summary
While driving past a local cemetery late at night, a young boy spotted the spectral apparition of a First World War soldier in a pristine white uniform walking near the graveyard's central crucifix.

#details
When Terry was approximately ten or twelve years old, he was riding in the backseat of his family's car as they left his uncle's house in Grand Falls to return to Bishop's Falls. As they drove past the town's old graveyard, Terry looked out the window and saw a figure walking beside the giant crucifix in the middle of the cemetery. The figure appeared entirely snow-white, wearing a First World War helmet and carrying a rifle tucked under his arm with the barrel pointed downward. Keeping his head low as if staring at the ground, the apparition walked steadily rather than floating or misting away. The eerie sighting left a lifelong impression on Terry, who avoided looking toward the graveyard after dark for years to come.

#location
The old cemetery located along the road connecting Grand Falls and Bishop's Falls in Grand Falls-Windsor, Newfoundland and Labrador.

Ghostly Animals and Death Tokens of Witless Bay

Summary

Oral history collections from Witless Bay record local accounts of supernatural death tokens and ghostly animals, including talking black dogs and phantom cows seen throughout the community.

Details

Documented through folklore fieldwork by Memorial University of Newfoundland, long-time residents of Witless Bay shared traditional beliefs and personal accounts regarding the paranormal. These stories include eerie premonitory death tokens—supernatural signs or omens witnessed prior to the passing of a family member or pet—as well as encounters with ghostly animals, specifically talking black dogs and phantom cows roaming the rural paths and woods of Witless Bay.

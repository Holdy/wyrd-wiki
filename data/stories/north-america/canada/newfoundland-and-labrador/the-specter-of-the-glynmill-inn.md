#title
The Specter of the Glynmill Inn

#summary
The historic Tudor-style Glynmill Inn in Corner Brook is reputed to be haunted by the phantom of a young girl who wanders the property and surrounding stream trails.

#details
Constructed during the early development of Corner Brook's paper mill community, the Glynmill Inn stands as one of the city's most recognizable architectural landmarks. Over the decades, staff and visitors have shared accounts of inexplicable cold spots, phantom footsteps echoing down carpeted hallways, and fleeting sightings of a young girl lingering near the gardens and the adjacent Corner Brook Stream Trail.

#location
The Glynmill Inn at 1 Cobb Lane in Corner Brook, Newfoundland and Labrador.

#title
The Ghostly Crew of Isle aux Morts

#summary
A phantom boat manned by a ghostly crew is said to haunt the rough waters off Isle aux Morts, forever rowing out into the fog to rescue drowning mariners.

#details
Along the dangerous, fog-shrouded southwest coast of Newfoundland, generations of outport fishers have passed down tales of the ghostly crew of Isle aux Morts. According to local legend, the apparition of a small wooden punt rowed by silent, spectral figures appears in the heavy offshore mist near the scene of historic shipwrecks. Outport residents over the decades have claimed to encounter this eerie crew while navigating the local waters, noting their icy presence and silent dedication as they eternally patrol the treacherous breakers.

#location
The coastal waters and harbour approaches of Isle aux Morts, Newfoundland and Labrador.

#title
The Haunting of Deadman's Island

#summary
Ghostly manifestations and unexplained phenomena have long been reported around the historic homesteads and rocky shores of Deadman's Island, linked to the turbulent maritime history and the Harvey family of Isle aux Morts.

#details
Local folklore on the southwest coast of Newfoundland preserves eerie accounts tied to Deadman's Island and the historic settlement sites associated with the Harvey family. Long after the dramatic shipwrecks of vessels like the *Despatch* and the *Rankin*, residents and visitors reported strange nocturnal sounds, shadowy figures moving near the old island dwellings, and an unearthly chill lingering around the historic settlement area. Oral tradition attributes these disturbances to the restless spirits of shipwreck victims who perished in the treacherous waters and the lingering phantom echoes of the island's dramatic maritime past.

#location
Deadman's Island, located just offshore in Isle aux Morts Harbour, Newfoundland and Labrador.

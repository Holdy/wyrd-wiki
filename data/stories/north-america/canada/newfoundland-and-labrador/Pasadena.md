#title
The Roadside Specter of the Humber Valley Highway

#summary
Drivers traveling at night along the highway between Deer Lake and Corner Brook near Pasadena have reported encountering a mysterious hitchhiker in a white dress who vanishes or reappears inside their car.

#details
Folklorists and local tradition document a persistent roadside legend along the highway connecting Deer Lake and Corner Brook, passing through the Humber Valley near Pasadena. Motorists driving late at night have reported seeing a lone woman dressed in white standing by the roadside seeking a ride. Drivers who slow down and pull over find that the woman has completely vanished into thin air. Conversely, those who drive past without stopping will look in their rearview mirror only to discover the ghostly woman suddenly sitting in the passenger-side seat beside them.

#location
The section of the highway between Deer Lake and Corner Brook passing near Pasadena in the Humber Valley, Newfoundland and Labrador.

#title
The Webber of West Haven Camp

#summary
Campers and locals near Pasadena share terrifying tales of The Webber, a feral, webbed-handed forest-dweller who haunts the wooded trails and summer camps of the area.

#details
According to local folklore and contemporary legend on the west coast of Newfoundland, The Webber is a hair-covered, man-like creature with webbed hands and feet who roams the woods and barrens. In versions tied specifically to Pasadena and the nearby West Haven Camp, the legend speaks of a feral outcast or survivor of a wilderness accident who grew up isolated in the local bush. Generations of children and campers at West Haven Camp famously recounted stories and warned each other about the "Webber Trail" section of the camp woods where the creature was said to lurk and stalk anyone wandering after dark.

#location
The trail network of West Haven Camp, located just outside Pasadena in the Humber Valley region of Newfoundland.

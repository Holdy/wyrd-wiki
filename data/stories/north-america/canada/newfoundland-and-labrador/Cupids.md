#title
The Ghost Light of the Cupids Cairn

#summary
A mysterious will-o'-the-wisp was historically seen traveling from Fergus Island across the bay to circle the historic stone cairn known as the "American Man" in Cupids.

#details
In local Newfoundland folklore, residents of Cupids and the surrounding Conception Bay area passed down accounts of an eerie atmospheric ghost light, or will-o'-the-wisp, that haunted the local landscape. According to oral history highlighted by folklorist Dale Jarvis, this luminous sphere of light would regularly detach from Fergus Island, cross the water to the mainland, follow an old track along the bay, and journey directly to the historic stone cairn in Cupids known locally as the "American Man." After hovering around the landmark cairn, the spectral light would make its return journey back across the water to Fergus Island.

#location
The historic stone cairn ("American Man") located in Cupids, Newfoundland and Labrador.

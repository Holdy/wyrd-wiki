#title
Ms. Tilley's Nightly Return

#summary
A house in Clarke's Beach where a former resident passed away is reportedly haunted by her restless spirit, which routinely opens doors and rearranges furniture.

#details
An elderly woman named Ms. Tilley passed away inside a home in Clarke's Beach. One night, while family members were babysitting and watching a movie in the living room, they heard the back door open and chairs scraping across the kitchen floor. Assuming it was a relative returning home, they checked the kitchen, but found no one there. Shortly afterward, they heard heavy footsteps echoing down the hallway and the bathroom door slamming shut on its own. When recounting the eerie events to their grandmother the next morning, they were told it was the ghost of Ms. Tilley, who routinely visits the house every single night.

#location
A specific residence in Clarke's Beach where Ms. Tilley previously lived and passed away.

#title
The Midsummer Echoes of Epaves Bay

#summary
On the night of June 23rd each year, mysterious sounds of foreign speech and invisible longboats beaching themselves echo along the grassy shores of L'Anse aux Meadows. Local tradition holds that the restless spirits of ancient Norse explorers return to retrace their historic landings.

#details
Local oral tradition maintains that the historical legacy of North America's earliest European settlement leaves an auditory trace every summer. Specifically around June 23rd, residents and visitors near the coastal waters of L'Anse aux Meadows have reported hearing the distinct sound of wooden hulls scraping against gravel, oars striking water, and men conversing in an ancient, unintelligible tongue. When investigators or locals look toward the water or approach the shoreline, no boats or travelers are visible, suggesting an ethereal reenactment of the Norse arrival from a thousand years ago.

#location
This annual haunting is reported along the coastal terrace and Epaves Bay shoreline surrounding the L'Anse aux Meadows National Historic Site on the northern tip of Newfoundland.

#title
The Phantom Longship of L'Anse aux Meadows

#summary
On select summer nights along the shores of L'Anse aux Meadows, witnesses have reported encountering a spectral Viking longship complete with fur-clad warriors and battle horns. This ghostly vessel has appeared to stranded fishermen and nighttime trespassers alike before vanishing into mist.

#details
According to local folklore rooted in the historic Norse settlement site, a phantom Viking longboat periodically haunts the waters off L'Anse aux Meadows. In one prominent account, a lone fisherman delayed his return ashore when his boat's motor inexplicably died as bad weather rolled in. Hearing the sound of paddles, he looked out to see a longship with red and white striped sails and armed men brandishing swords bearing down on him, only for a battle horn to sound and the vessel to vanish into thin air, instantly allowing his engine to restart. In another occurrence, two young men breaking into an outbuilding to steal whiskey looked out toward the bay and witnessed the same menacing longship, complete with shouting warriors shaking their sabres, before it dissolved into a cloud of mist.

#location
The haunting takes place along the coastal waters and shores adjacent to the L'Anse aux Meadows National Historic Site at the northernmost tip of Newfoundland's Great Northern Peninsula.

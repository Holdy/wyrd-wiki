#title
The Phantom Officer of Point-au-Gaul

#summary
Two hunters from Point-au-Gaul encountered a ghostlike naval officer in a distinguished uniform who walked past them without a word and boarded a phantom boat that vanished into the sea.

#details
According to an account collected in P. J. Kinsella's 1919 folklore compilation *Some Superstitions and Traditions of Newfoundland*, two men left Point-au-Gaul one morning to shoot wild gulls. Traveling several miles from home, they found themselves at a distance from their village by nightfall and lay down to rest on a fine evening. They were soon disturbed by the approaching footsteps of a stranger wearing the distinguished uniform of a ship's officer. Although the two men saluted him, the officer completely ignored them, passing by haughtily. He continued down to the sea shore where a boat was waiting for him, but no sooner had he embarked than both the boat and the officer vanished instantly as if swallowed by the sea.

#location
Point-au-Gaul on the Burin Peninsula, Newfoundland and Labrador, Canada.

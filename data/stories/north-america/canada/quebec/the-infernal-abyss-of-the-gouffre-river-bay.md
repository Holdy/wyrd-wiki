#title
The Infernal Abyss of the Gouffre River Bay

#summary
Early European explorers and local folklore shrouded the turbulent waters at the mouth of the Gouffre River in myth, describing the treacherous bay as a hellish abyss.

#details
When early explorers navigated the waters surrounding Baie-Saint-Paul, they observed intense swirling currents, deep riverbeds, and numerous jagged rocks clustered at the mouth of the Gouffre River. These hazardous geographic features deeply unsettled travelers and fired the local imagination. Over time, regional folklore enveloped the bay in eerie legends, with inhabitants and visitors referring to the churning waters as an "abîme infernal" (hellish abyss). The dramatic topography, flanked by steep cliffs and colliding currents, cemented the bay's reputation as a mystical and menacing boundary in the collective consciousness of early Charlevoix settlers.

#location
The mouth of the Gouffre River where it empties into the bay in Baie-Saint-Paul, Quebec.

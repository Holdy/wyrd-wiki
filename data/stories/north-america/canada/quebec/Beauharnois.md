#title
The Dark Entity of Rivière-La Guerre

#summary
The ruins of a nineteenth-century Scottish settlement flooded by the Beauharnois Canal are plagued by eerie whispers and a heavy presence known as Gordon.

#details
Founded in 1820 and largely deserted by 1850 after the construction of the Beauharnois Canal flooded the lowlands, the abandoned village of Rivière-La Guerre features crumbling church ruins, an old cemetery, and a decaying presbytery. Local folklore and subsequent investigations by paranormal teams recount unsettling encounters, including shadowy silhouettes in windows, mysterious cries, and a suffocating drop in air pressure in the presbytery basement where an investigator clearly heard the name "Gordon" spoken in response to questioning—a name later verified by a tombstone in the abandoned cemetery.

#location
The ruins of the abandoned village of Rivière-La Guerre near Saint-Anicet and Beauharnois, Quebec.

#title
The Phantoms of Au Vieux Brasserie

#summary
Staff and patrons at a historic Beauharnois brasserie report encountering the spirit of a former regular and playful children haunting the building's mezzanine and basement.

#details
After purchasing Au Vieux — Brasserie Festive in Beauharnois in January 2019, owner Marc-André Rioux and his staff experienced numerous inexplicable events, including customers spotting a child running across the mezzanine and visitors catching the distinct scent of Frank, a regular patron who tragically took his own life. Paranormal investigator Patrick Sabourin, who spent multiple nights investigating the location, captured audio responses regarding children's toys like a donkey plush, observed mysterious dangling legs appearing under strobe lighting, and noted an oppressively heavy atmosphere in the cellar that made even seasoned investigators uneasy.

#location
Au Vieux — Brasserie Festive located in Beauharnois, Quebec.

#title
The Spirits of Saint George's Church

#summary
The former Saint George's Church building in Baie-Comeau, now transformed into the Glacier Garden, is renowned for occult activity where paranormal entities manifest during the predawn hours.

#details
Investigator Donald Dufour identified the building housing the Glacier Garden—formerly Saint George's Church—as a major hotspot for supernatural activity in Baie-Comeau. According to local observations and paranormal tracking, spirit entities frequently interact with the environment inside the former house of worship, with supernatural activity peaking remarkably around three o'clock in the morning.

#location
The historic former Saint George's Church building (now the Glacier Garden) located in Baie-Comeau, Quebec.

#title
The Vacant House of Ragueneau

#summary
A residential property in Ragueneau has remained unsold and uninhabited for a decade after occupants fled from an oppressive, unexplained presence.

#details
Local paranormal investigator Donald Dufour examined accounts surrounding a notorious house located in Ragueneau, just outside Baie-Comeau. The dwelling has sat on the real estate market for ten years because prospective residents refuse to live there, plagued by persistent negative energies and unexplained paranormal phenomena that have baffled locals.

#location
A residential house situated in the municipality of Ragueneau, near Baie-Comeau, Quebec.

#title
The Apparition of the Arctic Winter Games Arena

#summary
The Arctic Winter Games Arena in Iqaluit is plagued by unexplained voices, loud banging noises, and a towering shadowy figure lingering near the ice rink.

#details
The multi-purpose Arctic Winter Games Arena has developed a reputation for paranormal activity among locals and visitors. Witnesses working or visiting the facility have reported hearing disembodied voices echoing through the empty halls and sudden, loud banging noises reverberating off the structure. The most striking phenomenon reported is a tall, shadowy apparition that stands silently on the far side of the ice rink, watching anyone who happens to be inside.

#location
The Arctic Winter Games Arena is located in Iqaluit, Nunavut.

#title
The Phantom Manager of the Hudson's Bay Company Buildings

#summary
The historical warehouses and main offices of the Hudson's Bay Company in Iqaluit are haunted by the apparition of a former manager who assists staff and roams the rooftops at night.

#details
Local lore and staff accounts maintain that the historic Hudson's Bay Company branch complex in Iqaluit is haunted by one of its former managers. Unlike many malevolent spirits, this phantom manager is known to be benevolent, occasionally assisting employees with their tasks. Late at night, witnesses have seen him walking the interior corridors, and on multiple occasions, people have spotted his shadowy figure standing watch from the roof of the building.

#location
The Hudson's Bay Company Buildings are located at 3601 Bill Mackenzie in Iqaluit, Nunavut.

#title
The Ghost of Lake Minnedosa

#summary
Campers at Lake Minnedosa shared tales around the campfire of a young female swimmer who drowned in the lake, unaware that a fellow camper taking a nighttime stroll in a white peignoir would be mistaken for the apparition.

#details
While camping at Lake Minnedosa in Manitoba with a group of friends, campers gathered around the campfire discussing local lore—specifically, the ghostly apparition of a young female swimmer who had tragically drowned in the lake. Later that night, a camper's wife woke up in the dark and walked across the park to use the campground washrooms wearing a white peignoir set with a pure white silk matching coat. The next morning, the campground was abuzz with sightings of a ghost in flowing white robes wandering through the park at precisely that hour, realizing only later that the phantom was actually the camper's wife on her midnight stroll.

#location
Lake Minnedosa campground in Minnedosa, Manitoba.

#title
The Haunted Wartime House of Minnedosa

#summary
A family residing in a cramped wartime house in Minnedosa experienced relentless nocturnal terrors, including phantom soldiers sliding across curtains, disembodied apparitions, and poltergeist activity.

#details
Growing up in a small wartime house in Minnedosa, Manitoba, residents endured persistent and frightening paranormal phenomena. Bedtime frequently brought terrifying encounters, such as phantom soldiers wearing contour hats sliding across window curtains, a gray decapitated head appearing on the old television screen, and a mysterious wind-tunnel roar echoing in a solitary bedroom. Additional activity included doors unlatching on their own, echoing laughter from above, and phantom paper-thin frogs manifesting on the rug while a translucent child hefted a ghostly brick.

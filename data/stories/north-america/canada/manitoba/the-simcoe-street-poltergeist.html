The Simcoe Street Poltergeist

Summary

A woman sleeping in a Carberry home found herself pinned to her bed by invisible hands, an eerie occurrence later linked to a tragic, undiscovered death in the house.

Details

In 1994, a resident living on Simcoe Street in Carberry woke up in the middle of the night to use the washroom. When attempting to get out of her waterbed, she discovered she was entirely immobilized and felt two heavy hands pressing down firmly on her chest. She called out to her husband sleeping beside her, who attempted to pull her up, but he found that pulling harder felt as though he was going to tear her arms out of their sockets. After two or three agonizing minutes, the chest pressure vanished. Sometime later, the residents learned that an elderly man had previously passed away in the house and remained undiscovered for two months, prompting them to move shortly thereafter.

#title
The Little People of the School Field

#summary
A teenager walking home late at night through a foggy path in The Pas encountered three small, crawling humanoid figures wearing tattered clothing and pointed headwear. The mysterious entities stopped, stood upright to stare at him, and then waddled away into the dense bush.

#details
In October 2008, a fifteen-year-old resident of The Pas First Nation Community was walking home from a store around 11:30 at night. His route took him through a dark bush trail and out into a school field covered in a thick layer of mist. Approximately 30 feet away, three small figures emerged from the fog. They appeared to be very small humans wearing tattered clothes and pointed hats, moving on all fours by pulling themselves along with long arms. Paralyzed by fear, the teenager watched as the creatures suddenly stopped, stood upright, and stared directly at him before kneeling back down and waddling into a dense thicket of trees adjacent to a residential street. When he recounted the eerie encounter to his family, his father recognized the description as matching traditional Native American folklore regarding "little people," malevolent spirits that are rarely seen in the area.

#location
The school field and adjacent bush trails located within The Pas First Nation Community in The Pas, Manitoba.

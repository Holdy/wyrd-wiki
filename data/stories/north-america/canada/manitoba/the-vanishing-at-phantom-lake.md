#title
The Vanishing at Phantom Lake

#summary
Named after a Hudson's Bay Company worker who vanished without a trace, Phantom Lake is said to be haunted by the lingering spirit of the lost employee.

#details
Located just south of Flin Flon on the Manitoba-Saskatchewan border, Phantom Lake has long held a reputation rooted in tragedy and mystery. According to local folklore, an employee of the Hudson's Bay Company disappeared into the surrounding wilderness long ago, leaving behind no clues as to his fate. Because he vanished so completely, locals began referring to him as a phantom, ultimately bestowing the name upon the lake itself. Over the decades, visitors and residents visiting the remote shores have reported feeling watched and catching glimpses of a solitary figure wandering the tree line before dissolving into mist.

#location

#title
The Sorrowful Spirit of Bakers Narrows

#summary
Bakers Narrows is haunted by the apparition of a grief-stricken woman who tragically ended her life along the water's rocky banks.

#details
Situated north of Flin Flon amid the rugged and scenic waterways of northern Manitoba, Bakers Narrows is known not only for its natural beauty but also for a dark local legend. Oral tradition maintains that a deeply heartbroken woman, overwhelmed by grief and despair, took her own life at the water's edge. In the years following, campers and travelers navigating the narrows have reported hearing inexplicable crying or sighs carried on the wind, along with fleeting sightings of a sorrowful female figure standing near the rocky shoreline at dusk.

#location
Bakers Narrows, located just north of Flin Flon along the regional highway and lake system in northern Manitoba.

#title
The Vanishing at Phantom Lake

#summary
Named after a Hudson's Bay Company worker who vanished without a trace, Phantom Lake is said to be haunted by the lingering spirit of the lost employee.

#details
Located just south of Flin Flon on the Manitoba-Saskatchewan border, Phantom Lake has long held a reputation rooted in tragedy and mystery. According to local folklore, an employee of the Hudson's Bay Company disappeared into the surrounding wilderness long ago, leaving behind no clues as to his fate. Because he vanished so completely, locals began referring to him as a phantom, ultimately bestowing the name upon the lake itself. Over the decades, visitors and residents visiting the remote shores have reported feeling watched and catching glimpses of a solitary figure wandering the tree line before dissolving into mist.

#location
Phantom Lake, situated just south of Flin Flon on the Manitoba-Saskatchewan border.

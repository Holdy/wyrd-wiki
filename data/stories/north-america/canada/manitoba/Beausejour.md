#title
The Ghost Bride of Milner Ridge

#summary
A ghostly woman in a wedding gown haunts Milner Ridge Road along PR 214, occasionally hitching rides from motorists on their way toward Beausejour before vanishing without a trace.

#details
Local legends around the Beausejour and Lac du Bonnet area tell of a phantom bride who wanders Milner Ridge Road (PR 214) at night. According to an account from a motorist driving through the dark, windy highway toward Beausejour, they stopped to assist a lone woman walking in a white wedding gown. The silent woman climbed into the back seat with her hair obscuring her face, refusing to answer any questions. Intending to drive her to the RCMP detachment in Beausejour for safety, the driver checked the rearview mirror only to find the back seat completely empty. Folklore suggests that a bride and her fiancé died tragically on their wedding day in the region long ago, and her restless spirit continues to roam the road searching for her partner.

#location
Milner Ridge Road (PR 214) between Lac du Bonnet and Beausejour, Manitoba.

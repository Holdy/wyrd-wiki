The Restless Souls of the Vaughan Street Jail

Summary

The decommissioned Vaughan Street Jail, where thirteen prisoners were hanged, is said to be haunted by the troubled souls of those who suffered within its grim walls.

Details

Standing at the corner of Memorial Boulevard and York Avenue, the historic Vaughan Street Jail operated in the late 19th and early 20th centuries under harsh and miserable conditions, holding serious criminals alongside the mentally ill and destitute. Thirteen individuals met their end here via execution by gallows, and countless others died of illness and despair. Today, despite the building being largely vacant except for provincial offices, the original prison cells remain preserved in the basement, where visitors and workers report an oppressive, supernatural atmosphere and lingering paranormal activity.

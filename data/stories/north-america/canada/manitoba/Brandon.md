#title
Hauntings of the Brandon Asylum

#summary
Opened in 1891 as an asylum for the mentally ill, the sprawling Brandon Mental Health Centre complex on the north hill is famously associated with reports of shadow figures, disembodied screams, and flickering lights.

#details
Originally established in 1891 on the north hill overlooking Brandon as the Brandon Asylum for the Insane (later renamed the Brandon Hospital for Mental Diseases and Brandon Mental Health Centre), the institution housed thousands of patients over more than a century before closing in 1998. Over the decades and continuing into its vacant years, staff, visitors, and urban explorers have reported chilling paranormal encounters across its historic buildings, including the Valleyview Receiving Unit and the old nurses' residence. Witnesses frequently report seeing shadow figures, hearing unexplained whispers and banging, and catching phantom screams echoing through the abandoned wards.

#location
The former Brandon Mental Health Centre grounds on the north hill along Highland Road and First Street in Brandon, Manitoba.

#title
The Specters of Daly House

#summary
Built in 1882 for Brandon's first mayor, the historic Daly House Museum is reputed to be haunted by a pacing former resident and the spirits of children playing on the stairs.

#details
Constructed in 1882 for Thomas Mayne Daly, who served as Brandon's first mayor, Daly House has accumulated numerous ghostly legends over the decades. Paranormal investigators and visitors have reported feeling the presence of a worried man with a high forehead and mustache pacing in the den, as well as hearing the sounds of young boys running up and down the stairs. During investigations by local paranormal groups like Shadows of Manitoba and television production teams, observers have noted anomalous phenomena such as floating blue orbs in the guest room and unexplained sounds throughout the Victorian-era mansion.

#location
Daly House Museum, located at 122 18th Street in Brandon, Manitoba.

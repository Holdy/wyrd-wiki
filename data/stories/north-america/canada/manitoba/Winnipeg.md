#title
The Midnight Piano of the Marlborough Hotel

#summary
Decades after a resident pianist passed away and her suite was converted into meeting rooms, guests and staff continue to hear phantom piano music playing in the middle of the night.

#details
The Marlborough Hotel on Smith Street, which opened in 1914, has a long-standing legend regarding a former resident who lived on the top floor. She owned a piano and regularly entertained guests and hotel staff with private concerts. Long after her death and the subsequent renovation of her suite into hotel meeting rooms—removing all pianos from the floor—staff and visitors began hearing disembodied piano melodies echoing through the area during the dead of night.

#location
The Marlborough Hotel is located on Smith Street in downtown Winnipeg, Manitoba.

#title
The Phantom of Room 202 at the Fort Garry Hotel

#summary
Guests staying in room 202 of the historic Fort Garry Hotel frequently report paranormal encounters, famously attributed to a woman who died there after her husband's tragic death.

#details
Opened in 1913, the luxury Fort Garry Hotel in downtown Winnipeg is widely regarded as one of the city's most haunted buildings. The most infamous legend centers on Room 202, where a woman allegedly took her own life following the tragic death of her husband in a traffic accident. Over the decades, guests and staff have reported chilling phenomena in and around the room, including disembodied footsteps, strange cold spots, shadowy figures in period clothing wandering the hallways and ballrooms, and weeping sounds echoing in the dead of night.

#location
The Fort Garry Hotel is located at 222 Broadway in Winnipeg, Manitoba.

#title
The Restless Souls of the Vaughan Street Jail

#summary
The decommissioned Vaughan Street Jail, where thirteen prisoners were hanged, is said to be haunted by the troubled souls of those who suffered within its grim walls.

#details
Standing at the corner of Memorial Boulevard and York Avenue, the historic Vaughan Street Jail operated in the late 19th and early 20th centuries under harsh and miserable conditions, holding serious criminals alongside the mentally ill and destitute. Thirteen individuals met their end here via execution by gallows, and countless others died of illness and despair. Today, despite the building being largely vacant except for provincial offices, the original prison cells remain preserved in the basement, where visitors and workers report an oppressive, supernatural atmosphere and lingering paranormal activity.

#location
The Vaughan Street Jail is located at the corner of Memorial Boulevard and York Avenue in Winnipeg, Manitoba.

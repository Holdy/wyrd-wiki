    The Ghost of the Dauphin Bakery

    Summary

    While studying after school in an antiquated bakery in Dauphin, Manitoba, a young girl frequently heard the disembodied footsteps of a friendly spirit known to the staff as the bakery ghost.

    Details

    Growing up on the outskirts of Dauphin, Manitoba, author Chenise Puchailo would walk to a small, cozy local bakery after school to complete her homework before catching a ride home. The building was antiquated, featuring a dusting of flour on every surface, creaking original hardwood floors, and a narrow hallway leading to a break room. One evening while reading alone, she heard the resounding clatter of heavy footsteps pacing across the hardwood floor in the main part of the bakery. Expecting her mother, who was closing up shop, Chenise went to investigate and discovered no one where the sound had originated. When she mentioned the eerie footsteps to her mother, she was told it was the local "bakery ghost"—a benevolent spirit traditionally heard pacing the shop in the early evening hours when the building was quiet.

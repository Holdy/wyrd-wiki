#title
The Phantom Organ and Spectral Watchers of St. Andrew's on the Red

#summary
At Western Canada's oldest stone church near Selkirk, visitors encounter floating red eyes, ghostly music, and a spectral figure in white.

#details
Completed in 1849 along the Red River just outside Selkirk, St. Andrew's on the Red is steeped in eerie local folklore. A popular local dare challenges visitors to walk backwards around the stone church three times, with legend warning that anyone who completes it will vanish entirely. Nighttime visitors and locals have reported numerous paranormal phenomena on the church grounds, including disembodied red eyes floating through the historic cemetery, the faint sound of an organ playing from an empty building, rattling church gates, and a mysterious woman cloaked in white drifting among the gravestones.

#location
St. Andrew's on the Red River is located on River Road in St. Andrews, just outside Selkirk, Manitoba.

#title
The Spectral Crew of the S.S. Keenora

#summary
Historic steamships at Selkirk's Marine Museum are plagued by moving decorations, self-turning logbooks, and spectral music.

#details
The Marine Museum of Manitoba, situated along Eveline Street in Selkirk, houses several historic vessels with rich maritime histories that have become magnets for paranormal activity. Among them is the S.S. Keenora, an 1897 passenger and freight steamship. Staff, volunteers, and investigators have frequently reported bizarre occurrences aboard the ship, such as lights flickering or turning off by themselves, Halloween decorations shifting on their own, and ship log pages flipping without human touch. Visitors also report hearing piano music playing from upstairs when no one is there, while artifacts associated with the sunken freighter *Suzanne-E* generate intense phantom chills.

#location
The Marine Museum of Manitoba is located at 444 Eveline Street in Selkirk, Manitoba.

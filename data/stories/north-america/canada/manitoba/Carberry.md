#title
The Haunted Legacy of Lyons House

#summary
Built in 1896 and abandoned since 1964, the decaying Lyons House near Carberry is steeped in a chilling, haunted atmosphere.

#details
Located just south of Carberry, the Lyons House is a two-storey red-brick veneer mansion constructed in 1896 for businessman and Member of the Legislative Assembly Robert Fern Lyons. After decades of fluctuating occupancy by farmers and local families, the property was ultimately abandoned in 1964. Over the years, as the structure fell into profound disrepair with rotting floorboards, collapsed basement sections, and overgrown trees encroaching on the property, visitors and urban explorers have frequently reported a distinct, haunting presence lingering within its empty halls and ruined rooms.

#location
The historic Lyons House, located approximately one kilometer south of Carberry, Manitoba.

#title
The Simcoe Street Poltergeist

#summary
A woman sleeping in a Carberry home found herself pinned to her bed by invisible hands, an eerie occurrence later linked to a tragic, undiscovered death in the house.

#details
In 1994, a resident living on Simcoe Street in Carberry woke up in the middle of the night to use the washroom. When attempting to get out of her waterbed, she discovered she was entirely immobilized and felt two heavy hands pressing down firmly on her chest. She called out to her husband sleeping beside her, who attempted to pull her up, but he found that pulling harder felt as though he was going to tear her arms out of their sockets. After two or three agonizing minutes, the chest pressure vanished. Sometime later, the residents learned that an elderly man had previously passed away in the house and remained undiscovered for two months, prompting them to move shortly thereafter.

#location
A residential house on Simcoe Street in Carberry, Manitoba.

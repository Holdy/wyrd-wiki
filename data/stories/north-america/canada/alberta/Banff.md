#title
Sam the Bellman's Posthumous Shift

#summary
A beloved head bellman who worked at the Banff Springs Hotel in the 1960s and 70s swore to return after death, and his helpful ghost is frequently encountered assisting guests and operating elevators.

#details
Sam McAuley served as the head bellman at the Fairmont Banff Springs Hotel during the 1960s and 1970s before passing away in 1975. Having loved his work and the hotel deeply, Sam reportedly vowed to return to his post post-mortem. Over the decades, staff and guests have reported encounters with an elderly Scottish bellman in an antiquated uniform matching Sam's description. Sightings include him unlocking doors for confused guests, haunting his former mezzanine office (now converted into a guest room), and causing elevator doors to open and close mysteriously on various upper floors.

#location
The Fairmont Banff Springs Hotel is located at 405 Spray Avenue in Banff, Alberta.

#title
The Ghost Bride of the Banff Springs Hotel

#summary
A young bride tragically died on her wedding day when her gown caught fire on a candle while descending a marble staircase, and her spectral figure continues to haunt the hotel's ballroom and stairs.

#details
According to local legend dating back to the late 1920s, a young woman was descending a marble staircase to join her groom for their wedding banquet in the Cascade Ballroom at the Banff Springs Hotel. As she walked down, her wedding dress brushed against one of the decorative candles, catching fire. In the ensuing panic, she tripped over her gown, fell down the flight of stairs, broke her neck, and died. Ever since, guests and staff have reported seeing a veiled figure in a wedding gown moving along the staircase or dancing alone in the ballroom, pining for the first dance she never had.

#location
The Fairmont Banff Springs Hotel is located at 405 Spray Avenue in Banff, Alberta.

#title
The Mystery of Room 873

#summary
An entire family was allegedly murdered in a guest room on the eighth floor of the Banff Springs Hotel, leading to violent nightly shrieks and a permanently sealed-off hallway.

#details
On the eighth floor of the Banff Springs Hotel lies the infamous "Missing Room" 873. According to hotel lore, a guest staying with his wife and young daughter snapped and murdered his family before taking his own life. Subsequent guests reported being startled awake in the dead of night by violent shrieks and screams, while cleaning staff continually found bloody fingerprints on the bathroom mirror that defied washing. Because of the persistent disturbances, hotel management eventually sealed off the doorway, leaving a blank stretch of wall in the hallway where the room once stood. Despite this, inexplicable phenomena and ghostly activity continue to be reported in the immediate vicinity.

#location
The Fairmont Banff Springs Hotel is located at 405 Spray Avenue in Banff, Alberta.

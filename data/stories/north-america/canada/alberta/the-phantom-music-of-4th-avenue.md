#title
The Phantom Music of 4th Avenue

#summary
The building formerly known as Mel's Music in High River was plagued by strange noises, phantom footsteps, and slamming doors long after closing time.

#details
Operating out of a building on 4th Avenue SW that previously served as a dry cleaner and Dudley's Pub, musician Mel Wilson and his staff experienced numerous inexplicable events over the years. Mel frequently heard the sound of running water in corners of the store where no water pipes existed, and both he and his employees heard phantom cowboy boot footsteps echoing from the upstairs apartment when no one was there. On multiple occasions, doors slammed shut on their own and shadowy figures were seen walking up the staircase into the private residence.

#location
The building is located at 117 4th Ave S.W. in High River, Alberta.

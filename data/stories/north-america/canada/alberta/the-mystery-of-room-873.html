The Mystery of Room 873

Summary

An entire family was allegedly murdered in a guest room on the eighth floor of the Banff Springs Hotel, leading to violent nightly shrieks and a permanently sealed-off hallway.

Details

On the eighth floor of the Banff Springs Hotel lies the infamous "Missing Room" 873. According to hotel lore, a guest staying with his wife and young daughter snapped and murdered his family before taking his own life. Subsequent guests reported being startled awake in the dead of night by violent shrieks and screams, while cleaning staff continually found bloody fingerprints on the bathroom mirror that defied washing. Because of the persistent disturbances, hotel management eventually sealed off the doorway, leaving a blank stretch of wall in the hallway where the room once stood. Despite this, inexplicable phenomena and ghostly activity continue to be reported in the immediate vicinity.

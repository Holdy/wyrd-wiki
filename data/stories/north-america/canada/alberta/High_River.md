#title
Phantoms of Highwood Cemetery

#summary
Visitors to Highwood Cemetery have frequently captured inexplicable white lights and glowing orbs hovering over headstones in photographs.

#details
Serving as the final resting place for generations of High River residents, Highwood Cemetery has long been a focal point for ghostly encounters and paranormal interest. Annual haunted history tours and visitors wandering the grounds have consistently reported an eerie atmosphere, with inexplicable white lights and luminous orbs manifesting above people's heads in photographs taken among the graves.

#location
Highwood Cemetery is located in High River, Alberta.

#title
The Phantom Music of 4th Avenue

#summary
The building formerly known as Mel's Music in High River was plagued by strange noises, phantom footsteps, and slamming doors long after closing time.

#details
Operating out of a building on 4th Avenue SW that previously served as a dry cleaner and Dudley's Pub, musician Mel Wilson and his staff experienced numerous inexplicable events over the years. Mel frequently heard the sound of running water in corners of the store where no water pipes existed, and both he and his employees heard phantom cowboy boot footsteps echoing from the upstairs apartment when no one was there. On multiple occasions, doors slammed shut on their own and shadowy figures were seen walking up the staircase into the private residence.

#location
The building is located at 117 4th Ave S.W. in High River, Alberta.

#title
The Resident Spirits of the Highwood Station

#summary
Staff at the historic Museum of the Highwood railway station frequently encounter mysterious phenomena, including self-locking doors, disembodied footsteps, and lights turning on by themselves.

#details
Housed in an old Canadian Pacific Railway station where the bodies of victims of train accidents were once temporarily stored on the north end, the museum has long been considered a paranormal hotspot. Employees have reported doors locking and unlocking on their own, inanimate objects shifting overnight, and shadows walking past hallways. Paranormal investigators detecting abnormal energy fields have noted the presence of lingering spirits within the historic building, affectionately referenced by some staff as a resident ghost named George.

#location
The Museum of the Highwood is located at 406 1st Street SW in High River, Alberta.

#title
The Tragedy of Macleod Trail

#summary
In 1912, High River witnessed its first bloody domestic murder on Macleod Trail, an event preserved in local dark history and folklore.

#details
Following weeks of intense domestic disputes and disagreements regarding property and estate rights, Robina Bertrand sought police assistance against her husband, Caliste Bertrand. In broad daylight on Macleod Trail just outside her home, an enraged Caliste shot Robina point-blank in the face, resulting in High River's first recorded murder. Local history and haunting tours continue to recount the sordid event as part of the town's darker historical lore.

#location
Macleod Trail runs through the town of High River, Alberta, where the historic incident took place.

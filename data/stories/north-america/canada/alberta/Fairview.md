#title
The Grey Lady of Dunvegan Bridge

#summary
In November 1960, two men driving across the newly completed Dunvegan Bridge encountered a hooded female apparition walking through a snowstorm who vanished, leaving behind no footprints.

#details
In November 1960, shortly after the completion of the Dunvegan suspension bridge south of Fairview, Alberta, two men driving through the snowy valley encountered a woman leaning into the swirling snow with her head bent beneath a hooded cloak, her bare feet exposed to the cold. When they pulled over and one man walked back following their fresh tire tracks to check on her, the bridge was completely empty except for his own footprints. Subsequent visits by the witnesses and their friends led to another sighting higher up a hill where she appeared to be standing knee-high in the snow picking berries. Over time, witnesses variously described her as wearing a cloak or a nun's habit, cementing her legend as the Grey Lady of Dunvegan.

#location
Dunvegan Suspension Bridge crossing the Peace River on Highway 2, approximately 20 minutes south of Fairview, Alberta.

#title
The Priest of the Rectory

#summary
On the opening day of toric rectory at Dunvegan, a visitor experienced a vivid retrocognitive vision of a bearded priest writing at a desk, a spectral presence still reported by staff and visitors today.

#details
Following the public opening of the historic rectory at Historic Dunvegan Provincial Park south of Fairview, Alberta, a visitor picked up a pen from a desk in the bishop's bedroom on the second floor and experienced a vivid vision of the room as it once appeared in the 19th century, complete with a bearded priest seated at the desk writing a letter. Locals and visitors frequently identify this apparition as Alfred Campbell Garrioch, who served as the mission's priest until 1891. Over the years, visitors and staff have reported seeing unexplained lights in the second-floor windows, catching the silhouette of a priest, and feeling an intense sensation of being watched throughout the historic park.

#location
The historic rectory at Historic Dunvegan Provincial Park, located on the north bank of the Peace River approximately 20 minutes south of Fairview, Alberta.

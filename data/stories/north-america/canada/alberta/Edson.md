#title
The Green Walls and the Soldier's Photograph

#summary
A newly moved-in resident of an Edson home experienced inexplicable paranormal phenomena, including heavy breathing, the sudden appearance of an antique military photograph, and a mysterious shadow figure outside her window. The chilling encounters culminated when the witness and her sister spotted a tall shadow man standing before a room whose walls had mysteriously changed color while the house was empty.

#details
While sleeping in her newly acquired room in an Edson house, a resident named Emily was awakened by heavy breathing and a feeling of suffocation. The closet door mysteriously swung open, and the room filled with the scent of gunpowder and cigar smoke before she passed out. Waking up at 3:00 AM, she discovered an old framed photograph on the kitchen counter depicting a 19-year-old soldier in an army uniform named Raymond Roy, dated 1896–1915—an object neither she nor her mother had ever seen before. The following Monday morning, while riding the school bus, Emily and her sister looked back at their house and saw a tall shadow man wearing a hat standing outside the window, even though the room's walls had inexplicably appeared green, the curtains were wide open, and the lights were on, despite the house having been left dark and white-walled earlier that day.

#location
A residential house in Edson, Alberta, Canada.

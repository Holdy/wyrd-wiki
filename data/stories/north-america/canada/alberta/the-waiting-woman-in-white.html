The Waiting Woman in White

Summary

Visitors and staff at the historic Okotoks railway station have reported eerie encounters with the spirit of a woman in a white dress waiting for a train. Local history notes that in 1922, the body of a woman who died aboard a train was brought into this exact station before being transferred to her family.

Details

The historic building housing the Okotoks Art Gallery—formerly the Canadian Pacific Railway Train Station in downtown Okotoks—has long been associated with paranormal activity. Staff and volunteers working alone in the building have reported unexplained sounds, objects being moved, and the lingering presence of a spirit. Historians conducting local ghost tours recount that in 1922, a woman passed away while riding a train, and her body was temporarily placed in a casket inside the station house before shipment to Blairmore. Many believe this event, coupled with frequent sightings of a figure in a long white dress waiting on the platform, cemented the station's reputation as one of the town's most haunted locations.

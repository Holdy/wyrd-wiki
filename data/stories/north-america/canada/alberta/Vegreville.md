#title
The Spectral Wagon Master

#summary
A ghostly wagon master driving a silent team of horses is frequently sighted riding through the pioneer settlement grounds at night. Local folklore suggests this phantom figure roams the area searching for workers to aid him in his eternal duties.

#details
The Ukrainian Cultural Heritage Village preserves dozens of historic structures representing early pioneer life in east central Alberta. Among the many reported paranormal occurrences across the open-air museum, visitors and staff have described encountering a spectral wagon master accompanied by silent horses traversing the village paths. Additional accounts across the museum grounds include disembodied whispers, phantom cries from empty cradles, and translucent figures wandering near the historic buildings after dark.

#location
The Ukrainian Cultural Heritage Village is situated along Highway 16, approximately 50 kilometers east of Edmonton in Lamont County, Alberta.

#title
The Unseen Mother of Hawreliak House

#summary
Visitors and security staff at a restored pioneer home frequently encounter unexplained footsteps, moving objects, and ghostly deterrents against profanity. The spirit of Vaselina Hawreliak is said to still guard her former residence from disrespect and intrusion.

#details
Vaselina Hawreliak lived a strict, moral life raising her nine children before passing away in 1967. After her home was relocated and restored at the Ukrainian Cultural Heritage Village, night guards and visitors began reporting bizarre paranormal phenomena, including phantom candle lights in windows, mysterious noises, and spontaneously broken items. During a nocturnal investigation, a visitor exploring the upper floor encountered heavy footsteps, locked doors, and a sudden slamming door when improper language and intrusion disturbed the lingering presence of the house's former matriarch.

#location
The Hawreliak House is located within the grounds of the Ukrainian Cultural Heritage Village along Highway 16 in Lamont County, Alberta.

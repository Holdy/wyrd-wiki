#title
The Spectral Children and the Priest's Pipe

#summary
Alongside reported poltergeist phenomena, the historic Castor hospital is famously haunted by the lingering scent of a priest's pipe tobacco and the ghostly presence of children playing in the corridors.

#details
Within the sprawling brick structure of the 1911 Our Lady of the Rosary Hospital in Castor, staff have long reported sensory ghosts and playful spirits. In areas once frequented by the resident parish priest, individuals repeatedly smell the distinct aroma of pipe tobacco lingering in the air. Furthermore, witnesses have reported seeing a spectral little boy and girl playing in the hallways, often seen scurrying underneath beds. Other baffling phenomena reported in the building include a ghostly piano playing by itself in empty rooms, cementing the hospital's reputation as a hotbed for the paranormal.

#location
The haunting occurs at Our Lady of the Rosary Hospital, located at 5402 47 St in Castor, Alberta.

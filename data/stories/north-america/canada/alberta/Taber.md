#title
Strange Music on the Taber Prairie

#summary
Local folklore recounts how a tragic homesteading couple's spirits continue to haunt the prairie, where ghostly music and phantom figures are witnessed at summer dawn.

#details
Collected by local Taber author Maxine Sutherland, regional legend tells of John and Mary Howes, pioneers whose lives were shattered on the harsh southern Alberta prairie when their child died during a relentless windstorm. Overcome by grief and the madness of the unrelenting wind, the homestead became the site of a profound tragedy. Ever since, locals have reported hearing strange, beautiful music drifting across the prairie at summer dawn, accompanied by spectral figures walking from the wheat field toward a window framed by morning glories.

#location
A rural prairie homestead section in Taber, Alberta, Canada.

#title
The Haunting of the Taber Hospital

#summary
Following its closure, the abandoned Taber Hospital and its adjacent nurses' residence became the site of spectral lights, phantom sounds, and a lingering spirit named Maynard who reportedly followed staff to the new facility.

#details
When the original medical facility in Taber closed its doors, urban explorers and former staff began reporting eerie paranormal activity. Nurses staying in the adjacent residence frequently watched an unexplained light turn on and off in the empty attic. Staff also reported a lingering presence they named "Maynard," who supposedly transferred with them when the hospital moved to its new location. Additionally, long-term care residents famously spoke of visits from a spectral figure on nights when a patient would unexpectedly pass away.

#title
The Ghost of 2-25

#summary
Plant operators working night shifts at a remote oilfield battery site in the Giltedge river valley between Vermilion and Wainwright report blood-curdling screams, mysteriously opened locked doors, and displaced tools.

#details
In the Giltedge oilfield situated between Vermilion and Wainwright, the 2-25 battery site—located down in a river valley—is reputed to be haunted. According to local lore, a homestead existed there in the 1930s where a homesteader suffered a mental breakdown and murdered his entire family, including his wife and three daughters. Plant operators conducting middle-of-the-night call-outs at the remote facility have reported hearing a terrifying, close-up woman's scream, finding heavy doors mysteriously flung wide open despite no wind in the valley, and discovering tools like socket sets inexplicably rearranged on the ground outside their cases within minutes. These harrowing encounters became so notorious that several workers refused to perform maintenance calls at the site alone.

#location
The 2-25 satellite battery site in the Giltedge oilfield river valley, located between Vermilion and Wainwright, Alberta.

#title
The Cessna Crane Crash Site Anomaly

#summary
Visitors to the remote site of a 1950 plane crash north of Whitecourt have reported sudden electronic failures and total disorientation.

#details
On October 14, 1950, a Cessna Crane aircraft crashed north of Whitecourt, resulting in five fatalities. Years later, when researchers visited the remote crash site on the anniversary of the tragedy, they experienced uncanny technological failures—such as all electronic device batteries dying simultaneously—alongside sudden, inexplicable disorientation while navigating the area.

#location
The 1950 Cessna Crane plane crash site located north of Whitecourt, Alberta.

#title
The Phantom of Centennial Park

#summary
The ghost of a teenage girl is said to haunt Centennial Park every year on October 13, leaving investigators with unexplained encounters of whistling and glowing eyes.

#details
Local urban legend maintains that the spirit of a teenage girl who was assaulted in Centennial Park during the 1970s returns to the grounds annually on October 13. Ghost investigators exploring the park on this anniversary have reported strange paranormal phenomena, including picking up mysterious whistling sounds on parabolic microphones and encountering unsettling glowing eyes in the dark.

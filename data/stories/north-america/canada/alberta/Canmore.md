#title
Lisa of the Recreation Centre

#summary
The Canmore Recreation Centre is haunted by the ghost of a young girl named Lisa who drowned in the swampy wetlands that once covered the site.

#details
Local legend and ghost tour accounts in Canmore tell of Lisa, the ghost of a little girl with long dark hair. She is said to have drowned in the swampy marshland long before the modern recreation centre and hockey arena were constructed over the wetlands. Today, staff and visitors frequently report seeing her apparition roaming the hallways and nearby streams, making her one of the town's most famous resident spirits.

#location
The Canmore Recreation Centre is situated in Canmore, Alberta.

#title
The Man in the Brown Tweed Coat

#summary
Visitors and staff at the historic Canmore Opera House report encounters with a phantom man wearing a brown tweed coat, particularly those who sit in a specific row.

#details
According to local folklore and ghost tour lore in Canmore, the Canmore Opera House is haunted by the spirit of an unidentified man dressed in a brown tweed coat. Local legend holds that anyone who sits in the third seat of the third row inside the building is likely to experience strange and unsettling paranormal phenomena.

#location
The Canmore Opera House is located in Canmore, Alberta.

#title
The Lady in the White Dress of East Chestermere Drive

#summary
A former armed forces diver haunted by recovering a drowned woman from Chestermere Lake connected with a new resident who was told her home was haunted by a faceless lady in white.

#details
In a recollection shared with the Chestermere Historical Foundation, a resident who purchased a home on East Chestermere Drive in 2002 recounted a bizarre encounter in Toronto. While returning a rental car, an elderly former armed forces diver struck up a conversation upon learning she was moving to Chestermere. He vividly described being called to Chestermere Lake in the early 1950s to recover a young woman who had drowned, recalling how terrifying it was when he became entangled in her flowing white dress underwater—a memory that haunted him for decades. Later, two different visitors independently told the homeowner that her basement was inhabited by a friendly entity: a faceless lady wearing a long white dress.

#location
A residence on East Chestermere Drive in Chestermere, Alberta.

#title
The Tragic Flame of the Wainwright Hotel

#summary
Witnesses have reported hearing menacing male voices and seeing an unexplained white figure in the windows of the historic Wainwright Hotel, tied to a fatal 1914 fire.

#details
Long before the original wooden structure was destroyed in the 1929 fire and rebuilt in concrete, the Wainwright Hotel was the site of a horrific tragedy in 1914. An 18-year-old female employee was cleaning her skirt with a tub of gasoline in a hotel room when oil lantern fumes ignited, engulfing her in flames. Despite medical help, she succumbed to her injuries the following morning. Decades later, after the hotel had closed and stood completely empty without plumbing, electricity, or interior walls, locals and investigators reported eerie paranormal activity. Laundry workers in the basement previously reported hearing a menacing man's voice telling them to leave and feeling strongly unwelcome. Others reported loud, unexplained banging echoing down empty hallways from end to end, as well as a solitary white figure spotted staring out from the top-floor windows of the abandoned building.

#location
The historic Wainwright Hotel building in Wainwright, Alberta.

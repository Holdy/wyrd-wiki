#title
The Phantoms of the Old Brooks Hospital

#summary
Operating as a hospital during the 1940s before becoming a cultural hub, the historic building in Brooks is reportedly haunted by multiple lingering spirits, including a dedicated former janitor, a story-telling elderly woman, and a young boy playing in the basement.

#details
Long before it served as the Community Cultural Centre, the building located at 327 3rd Street West in Brooks operated as a local hospital during the 1940s. Because numerous patients passed away within its walls during that era, the structure developed a reputation for heavy spiritual activity and unexplained phenomena. Over the years, staff, visitors, and children have reported distinct encounters with several persistent phantoms. One of the most prominent spirits is an elderly janitor—believed to be a former worker who suffered a fatal heart attack on the job—who staff and visitors occasionally spot continuing his cleaning rounds after hours. In addition, children frequenting the center have reported seeing and hearing an elderly woman who sits and reads stories to them, alongside a nine-year-old boy wearing a backpack who has been witnessed playing with toys in the basement daycare area.

#location
The Community Cultural Centre is located at 327 3rd Street West in Brooks, Alberta.

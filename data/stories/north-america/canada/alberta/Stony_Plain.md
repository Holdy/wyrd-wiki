#title
The Apparition of the Red Brick School

#summary
Staff and visitors at the historic 1925 Red Brick School in Stony Plain have frequently reported encountering a ghostly woman in a 1950s print dress, alongside unexplained poltergeist activity and phantom shoves.

#details
Built in 1925 and later saved from demolition to become the anchor of the town's heritage site, the Red Brick School has accumulated numerous firsthand accounts of paranormal activity. Staff working late at night often experience a sudden, overwhelming feeling that they have overstayed their welcome and must leave immediately. Museum managers have reported seeing out of the corner of their eyes an apparition of a woman dressed in a 1950s-era print dress standing near office doorways. During evening tours and exhibition setups, paranormal phenomena have escalated: computers and audio equipment have started running on their own, unexplainable knocking echoes through empty rooms, and staff members have reported feeling sudden, physical shoves while on ladders.

#location
The Red Brick School at the Multicultural Heritage Centre, 5411 51st Street, Stony Plain, Alberta.

#title
The Spirits of Oppertshauser House

#summary
Visitors and staff touring the historic Oppertshauser House in Stony Plain have reported inexplicable basement noises and lingering presences tied to the tragic early history of the property.

#details
Situated on the heritage grounds in Stony Plain, the Oppertshauser House is one of the area's prominent historic structures. Local lore and tour accounts associate the building with tragic chapters from the region's pioneering days, including sickness and quarantine periods. During evening ghost tours, visitors and guides have repeatedly heard unexplained knocking, heavy footsteps, and mysterious sounds echoing from the cellar and basement levels when the building is otherwise empty. Researchers and paranormal investigators note that the house remains an active energetic hotspot with frequent auditory anomalies.

#location
Oppertshauser House at the Multicultural Heritage Centre grounds, 5411 51st Street, Stony Plain, Alberta.

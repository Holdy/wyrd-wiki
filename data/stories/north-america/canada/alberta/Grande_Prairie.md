#title
The Caretaker and Holding Cell of the Old Courthouse

#summary
Staff and visitors at the Centre for Creative Arts in Grande Prairie have experienced doors slamming on their own and the phantom footsteps of a former courthouse caretaker.

#details
Housed in Grande Prairie's former courthouse building, the Centre for Creative Arts features a basement holding cell where unexplainable events occur, including heavy doors slamming shut repeatedly for no reason. During renovations about 15 years ago, a board meeting held in the old holding cell was interrupted by loud banging and cupboard doors flying open upstairs, causing panicked board members to flee outside. Additionally, instructors and students working in the pottery studio—located in the former caretaker's space—frequently report the feeling of being watched and phantom footsteps following them when no one else is around, matching descriptions of a caretaker who passed away before the original courthouse opened.

#location
The Centre for Creative Arts is located at 9839 100 Avenue in Grande Prairie, Alberta.

#title
The Lady in White at Al's News Basement

#summary
Employees working in the basement of Al's News in downtown Grande Prairie have reported seeing a spectral woman dressed in a long white gown.

#details
Housed in one of the oldest buildings in downtown Grande Prairie, Al's News has long been associated with paranormal activity in its basement. Multiple past employees have witnessed the figure of a lady wearing a long white dress lurking downstairs. Paranormal investigators have set up equipment in the basement, and staff frequently experience unexplained occurrences such as items falling off shelves and floorboards creaking when the store is empty.

#location
Al's News is located in the historic downtown core of Grande Prairie, Alberta.

#title
The Phantom Vault Door of the Old High School

#summary
Staff at the Art Gallery of Grande Prairie have reported the sound of children laughing in the halls and a heavy vault door mysteriously swinging open and slamming shut on security cameras.

#details
Operating out of the space that once encompassed the 1929 Grande Prairie High School building (now part of the Montrose Cultural Centre), staff members have long recounted supernatural occurrences. Past staff reported hearing children running and laughing through the halls, sometimes becoming so frightened they closed the gallery early. More recently, staff members watching security cameras witnessed the heavy door of the permanent collection vault unlatch, swing slowly open, and then slam shut and latch by itself with no one in the vicinity.

#location
The Art Gallery of Grande Prairie is situated within the Montrose Cultural Centre at 9839 101 Avenue in Grande Prairie, Alberta.

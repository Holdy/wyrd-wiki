The Ghost of Park Place on Ross Street

Summary

The century-old Park Place building in downtown Red Deer is reportedly haunted by a silent female apparition in vintage dress who vanishes into the basement hallway.

Details

Originally constructed over a century ago as the Alexandra Hotel along Ross Street in downtown Red Deer—and later converted for commercial business use—the historic building has long been associated with a lingering apparition. Over the years, various business owners and occupants have reported experiencing a sudden, localized drop in temperature followed by the sight of a woman dressed in Victorian or Edwardian attire. She is seen walking silently across the hallway toward the basement before completely vanishing from sight. The figure never speaks and only appears fleetingly to those working in the building.

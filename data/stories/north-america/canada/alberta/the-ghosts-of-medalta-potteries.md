#title
The Ghosts of Medalta Potteries

#summary
Employees at the historic Medalta Potteries in Medicine Hat frequently experience unexplained phenomena, including moving objects, phantom footsteps, and unseen figures.

#details
Nestled in Medicine Hat's Historic Clay District, the century-old Medalta Potteries is rumored to be haunted by the spirits of former employees who spent their lives crafting pottery there. Current staff members report hearing disembodied voices, squeaky footsteps while alone in the building, and laughter when no one is present. Workers have also described tools mysteriously vanishing and reappearing moments later, feeling taps on their shoulders in empty rooms, and spotting dark figures moving across roped-off museum exhibit areas. Rather than feeling frightened, employees believe these lingering souls have simply returned to a place that was an essential part of their lives.

#location
Medalta Potteries, located at 713 Medalta Ave SE in the Historic Clay District of Medicine Hat, Alberta.

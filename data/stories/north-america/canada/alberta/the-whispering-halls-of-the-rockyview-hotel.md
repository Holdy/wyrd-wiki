#title
The Whispering Halls of the Rockyview Hotel

#summary
Staff members at a historic downtown Cochrane hotel have reported hearing disembodied voices call their names in empty corridors, backed by paranormal investigations recording unexplained electromagnetic fields and phantom voices.

#details
Situated in the heart of downtown Cochrane, the historic Rockyview Hotel has long been associated with local legends of lingering spirits, including a "Ghost Bride" who supposedly fell down the grand staircase in the 1920s and a bellman named Sam. In 2018, a kitchen worker named Caesar Escobar heard a woman's voice clearly calling his name while working in the hotel kitchen. Assuming a coworker was trying to get his attention, he checked the hallway only to find himself completely alone. Subsequent visits by professional medium Bonnie Milner and Ghost Hunt Alberta recorded anomalous electromagnetic fields (EMF) and captured phantom voices on a spirit box, confirming the presence of unseen entities within the building.

#location
The Rockyview Hotel is located in downtown Cochrane, Alberta.

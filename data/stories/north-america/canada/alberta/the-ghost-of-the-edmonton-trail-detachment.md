#title
The Ghost of the Edmonton Trail Detachment

#summary
Staff and police officers stationed at the former RCMP building on Edmonton Trail have reported eerie paranormal activity, including phantom panic alarms, rattling cell doors in empty holding cells, and unexplained electrical anomalies.

#details
Occupied by the Airdrie RCMP until April 2012, the 1980s-era building gained a local reputation for lingering supernatural energy. RCMP Sergeant Dennis Esayenko recalled an incident just before midnight when the panic alarm in the cellblock triggered. When officers rushed inside, they heard a cell door cage violently rattling, yet every cell was completely empty. Years later, when the Boys and Girls Club of Airdrie used the building for a seasonal haunted attraction, Executive Director Denisa Sanness and her team experienced further strange occurrences. Taps turned on by themselves, lights toggled back on immediately after being turned off, and automated props activated even when completely unplugged.

#location
The former RCMP building located on Edmonton Trail in Airdrie, Alberta.

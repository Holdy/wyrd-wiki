#title
The Haunting of the Stone House

#summary
Staff members at the historic Stone House in Sylvan Lake have reported unexplained phenomena, with local lore attributing the hauntings to the spirit of a young girl who drowned in the lake.

#details
Built in 1912 near the shores of Sylvan Lake, the Stone House is one of the town's notable early historic structures, constructed during the area's early settlement period. Over the decades, the building has transitioned from a private residence to various commercial uses, including operating as a spa. During the time it functioned as a spa, employees experienced strange, inexplicable occurrences within its thick stone walls, dirt basement, and narrow interior stairways. Local tradition attributes these eerie, unexplained disturbances to the restless spirit of a little girl who tragically drowned in Sylvan Lake years ago.

#location
The Stone House is located on 46th Street near Lakeshore Drive in Sylvan Lake, Alberta.

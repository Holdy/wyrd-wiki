#title
The Basement Haunting of the Tofield Hotel

#summary
Built in 1912 as the Royal Alexandra Hotel, this historic brick building in Tofield is rumored to be haunted, with staff and visitors noting the basement as a primary hot spot for supernatural activity.

#details
Originally constructed in 1912 under the name the Royal Alexandra Hotel, the Tofield Hotel stands as one of the oldest and most enduring structures in the town of Tofield, Alberta. Over the decades, numerous guests, staff members, and visitors have shared accounts of unexplained phenomena within its walls. In particular, the building's dark basement is frequently cited by locals and travelers as the center of eerie noises, cold drafts, and strange paranormal happenings.

#location
The Tofield Hotel is situated at the corner of 50th Street and 50th Avenue in the town of Tofield, Alberta.

#title
The Phantom Cradle of Hawreliak House

#summary
The historic Hawreliak House at the Ukrainian Cultural Heritage Village near Tofield is said to be haunted by the spirit of its former occupant, who reportedly paces the floors and rocks a crying baby in a cradle at night.

#details
The Hawreliak House originally belonged to Vaselina Hawreliak, a dedicated mother who cared for her nine children before passing away in 1967. The house was later relocated and restored at the Ukrainian Cultural Heritage Village near Tofield, Alberta. Security guards and museum staff working after hours have reported chilling encounters, including phantom footsteps on the top floor, moving chairs, candlelight flickering in empty windows, and the ghostly apparition of a woman rocking a crying infant in a wooden cradle during the dead of night.

#location
The Hawreliak House is located within the Ukrainian Cultural Heritage Village situated along Highway 16 in Lamont County, just east of Tofield, Alberta.

#title
The Spectral Wagon Master of Tofield

#summary
A ghostly wagon master driving a silent team of horses is said to roam through the historic grounds near Tofield, searching for living workers to join him on his phantom journey.

#details
According to regional folklore and local ghost lore, a phantom wagon master frequently appears riding a silent, spectral team of horses through the Ukrainian Cultural Heritage Village area near Tofield, Alberta. Local legends recount that he wanders the grounds after dark, looking for unsuspecting individuals or lingering workers to accompany him on his mysterious wagon ride into the night.

#location
The Ukrainian Cultural Heritage Village is located along Highway 16 in Lamont County, east of Edmonton and near Tofield, Alberta.

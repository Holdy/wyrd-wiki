#title
The Ghost of 2-25

#summary
Oilfield workers at an isolated river valley battery near Wainwright reported eerie encounters, including blood-curdling screams and mysteriously rearranged tools, rooted in a dark 1930s homestead legend.

#details
In the Giltedge river valley field situated between Vermilion and Wainwright, a remote satellite oilfield facility known as the 2-25 battery became infamous among workers for unexplained paranormal occurrences. Local lore claimed that in the 1930s, a homesteader in the valley suffered a mental breakdown and murdered his entire family. Decades later, operators working the night shift reported chilling experiences. One worker on a solo call at 1:00 AM heard a close, blood-curdling woman's scream right in his ear, prompting him to flee the site immediately. Other workers frequently returned to find locked heavy doors wide open despite no wind in the valley. In another instance, a technician walked to his truck for a socket set, stepped inside a building for two minutes, and walked back out to find the entire socket set neatly arranged on the ground while its plastic case remained untouched on the tailgate. The phenomena were so unsettling that several employees refused to perform call-outs at the battery alone.

#location
The 2-25 satellite battery located in the Giltedge river valley situated between Vermilion and Wainwright, Alberta.

#title
The Phantom of the Honeypot Boarding House

#summary
A historic Wainwright building that survived the Great Fire of 1929 is said to be haunted by a mischievous spirit named Charlie, alongside darker legends of a murdered carpenter.

#details
Originally built shortly after Wainwright's catastrophic "Great Fire" of 1929—which started from an ash can and razed most of the town—the property on 2nd Avenue served as a boarding house during the 1930s and 1940s. Over the decades, the building accumulated numerous dark legends, including rumors of an upstairs brothel, a basement moonshine still, illegal gambling that led to a shooting, and a local carpenter who was allegedly murdered and dumped into a well. Staff and visitors operating the historic building over the years reported encounters with a playful and mischievous ghost whom they nicknamed "Charlie," alongside inexplicable cold spots and sudden noises that could not be accounted for by the bustling restaurant and pub that eventually took over the space.

#location
The Honeypot Eatery & Pub building located at 823 2nd Ave, Wainwright, Alberta.

#title
The Tragic Flame of the Wainwright Hotel

#summary
Witnesses have reported hearing menacing male voices and seeing an unexplained white figure in the windows of the historic Wainwright Hotel, tied to a fatal 1914 fire.

#details
Long before the original wooden structure was destroyed in the 1929 fire and rebuilt in concrete, the Wainwright Hotel was the site of a horrific tragedy in 1914. An 18-year-old female employee was cleaning her skirt with a tub of gasoline in a hotel room when oil lantern fumes ignited, engulfing her in flames. Despite medical help, she succumbed to her injuries the following morning. Decades later, after the hotel had closed and stood completely empty without plumbing, electricity, or interior walls, locals and investigators reported eerie paranormal activity. Laundry workers in the basement previously reported hearing a menacing man's voice telling them to leave and feeling strongly unwelcome. Others reported loud, unexplained banging echoing down empty hallways from end to end, as well as a solitary white figure spotted staring out from the top-floor windows of the abandoned building.

#location
The historic Wainwright Hotel building in Wainwright, Alberta.

The Unmarked Crosses of the Gaol Cemetery

Summary

The historic Fort Saskatchewan gaol cemetery, containing the unmarked graves of unclaimed prisoners and executed criminals, is said to be haunted by restless souls.

Details

Originally located south of the North Saskatchewan River bridge before being moved in 1957, the Fort Saskatchewan Gaol Cemetery is tucked behind a residential neighborhood near the edge of the Sherridon subdivision. It holds nineteen unmarked crosses representing prisoners and felons who died or were executed at the old jail and went unclaimed by families. Local folklore and paranormal investigations recount that the spirits of these executed inmates still linger around their final, unmarked resting place. Visitors have reported uneasy feelings, strange atmospheric anomalies, and ghostly voices near the edge of the woods and houses bordering the cemetery.

#title
The Schoolhouse Footprints of Chestermere

#summary
Three friends investigating a rumoured haunted schoolhouse on private land in Chestermere encountered mysterious, lone small footprints appearing in fresh snow and phantom footsteps approaching their room.

#details
According to an eyewitness account set in Chestermere, Alberta, three friends decided to visit an old schoolhouse on private land that was rumoured to have burned down years prior, trapping a young child inside. On a cold winter night with fresh snow and no prior footprints, two of the friends entered the roofless schoolhouse while the third waded through a snow-drifted field. While inside, they heard steady crunching footsteps coming down the hallway toward their room. Fearing the landowner, they hid, but the footsteps stopped right at the doorway. Upon checking, they discovered fresh size 3 or 4 child-sized shoeprints in the snow that started in the middle of the hallway and terminated abruptly at the doorway, with no corresponding footprints entering or leaving the area.

#location
An old schoolhouse site located on private land in Chestermere, Alberta.

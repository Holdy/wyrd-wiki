#title
The Phantom Nun of the Old Castor Hospital

#summary
Staff and visitors at the historic 1911 hospital in Castor report chilling encounters, including the apparition of a nun on the stairs, unexplained cold spots, and poltergeist activity on the untouched third floor.

#details
Built in 1911 by the French-speaking Daughters of Wisdom, Our Lady of the Rosary Hospital served as a regional medical hub, convent, and boarding school. The third floor, where the nuns once slept in small wooden cubicles, has remained largely untouched over the decades. Today, staff and visitors frequently report eerie occurrences: motion sensors triggering with no one present, clocks mysteriously turning backward, phantom names being called down hallways, items vanishing from lower floors only to reappear on the third floor, and the unsettling sensation of bedsheets being violently yanked away from readers at night. Most notably, witnesses have reported seeing the spectral apparition of a nun walking the stairwells.

#location
The haunting takes place at Our Lady of the Rosary Hospital, located at 5402 47 St in Castor, Alberta.

#title
The Spectral Children and the Priest's Pipe

#summary
Alongside reported poltergeist phenomena, the historic Castor hospital is famously haunted by the lingering scent of a priest's pipe tobacco and the ghostly presence of children playing in the corridors.

#details
Within the sprawling brick structure of the 1911 Our Lady of the Rosary Hospital in Castor, staff have long reported sensory ghosts and playful spirits. In areas once frequented by the resident parish priest, individuals repeatedly smell the distinct aroma of pipe tobacco lingering in the air. Furthermore, witnesses have reported seeing a spectral little boy and girl playing in the hallways, often seen scurrying underneath beds. Other baffling phenomena reported in the building include a ghostly piano playing by itself in empty rooms, cementing the hospital's reputation as a hotbed for the paranormal.

#location
The haunting occurs at Our Lady of the Rosary Hospital, located at 5402 47 St in Castor, Alberta.

#title
The Specters of the Alberta Hospital for the Insane

#summary
The historic asylum in Ponoka, plagued by a dark history of harsh psychiatric treatments and forced sterilizations, is widely reputed by locals to be haunted by the troubled spirits of past patients.

#details
Opened in 1912 on the rural outskirts of Ponoka as Alberta's first mental health institution, the hospital housed hundreds of patients under difficult and often grim conditions. Over the decades, generations of local residents and former staff members have passed down chilling accounts of paranormal activity on the grounds, attributing eerie noises, disembodied cries, and unexplained apparitions to the profound suffering and tragic experiences of those who lived and died within its walls.

#location
The historic grounds of the former Alberta Hospital for the Insane (now the Centennial Centre for Mental Health & Brain Injury) located on the southeast outskirts of Ponoka, Alberta.

#title
The Underground Secrets of Downtown Ponoka

#summary
Local folklore in downtown Ponoka speaks of a clandestine network of underground tunnels connecting historic buildings where bootleggers and locals once moved unseen.

#details
For generations, old-timers in Ponoka shared tales of a secret subterranean network running beneath downtown landmarks such as Morrison's Hardware Store, the Royal Hotel, and the Leland Hotel. Dating back to the prohibition era or earlier, these tunnels were purportedly used to transport illicit homemade liquor or simply allow patrons and residents to travel between establishments out of sight during harsh weather or police patrols. Over time, these forgotten passageways became central to local legend, shrouded in mystery and whispered stories of hidden history beneath the town's streets.

#location
Beneath the historic downtown locations of Morrison's Hardware Store, the Royal Hotel, and the Leland Hotel in Ponoka, Alberta.

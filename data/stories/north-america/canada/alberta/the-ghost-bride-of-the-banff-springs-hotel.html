The Ghost Bride of the Banff Springs Hotel

Summary

A young bride tragically died on her wedding day when her gown caught fire on a candle while descending a marble staircase, and her spectral figure continues to haunt the hotel's ballroom and stairs.

Details

According to local legend dating back to the late 1920s, a young woman was descending a marble staircase to join her groom for their wedding banquet in the Cascade Ballroom at the Banff Springs Hotel. As she walked down, her wedding dress brushed against one of the decorative candles, catching fire. In the ensuing panic, she tripped over her gown, fell down the flight of stairs, broke her neck, and died. Ever since, guests and staff have reported seeing a veiled figure in a wedding gown moving along the staircase or dancing alone in the ballroom, pining for the first dance she never had.

    The Phantom Settler of Victoria Settlement

    Summary

    The riverbanks near the historic Victoria Settlement near Smoky Lake are said to be haunted by a lonely settler who vanished during a harsh winter, leaving behind phantom boot prints in the frost that lead to nowhere.

    Details

    Established in the 1860s as a Hudson's Bay Company fur trading post along the North Saskatchewan River, Victoria Settlement is now a preserved provincial historic site. According to local legend, the surrounding riverbanks are haunted by a solitary settler who disappeared without a trace during a brutal winter storm and was never found. On chilly days, visitors and locals have reported seeing mysterious boot prints suddenly appear in the frost, tracing paths across the wintry ground that abruptly end in the snow.

#title
The Legend of Kinosoo

#summary
A giant lake monster known as Kinosoo, or "The Big Fish," is said to inhabit the deep waters of Cold Lake, originating from local Cree and Chipewyan folklore.

#details
Passed down through generations of local Cree and Chipewyan oral tradition, the legend tells of a massive aquatic creature lurking in the depths of Cold Lake. According to the tale, a young Cree man set out in his canoe across the water to visit a woman he hoped to marry, only to be attacked by a colossal fish with a gaping mouth that bit his canoe in half and dragged him beneath the surface while his lover watched from the shore. Over the decades, settlers and residents have reported finding gigantic ancient bones—such as those allegedly discovered at Birch Point—and attributed unexplained disappearances and disturbances on the lake to the monster, a legacy that lives on through Cold Lake's Kinosoo Beach.

#location
Cold Lake, Alberta, specifically inhabiting the waters and shores around French Bay and Birch Point.

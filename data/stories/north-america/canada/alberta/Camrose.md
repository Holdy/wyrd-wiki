#title
The Legend of Driedmeat Hill and Stoney Creek

#summary
Overlooking the waters of Driedmeat Lake and Stoney Creek, Driedmeat Hill is steeped in indigenous folklore and local legend regarding ancient conflict and blood-stained stones.

#details
Known historically as *Kahkewak* in Cree, Driedmeat Hill and the surrounding Battle River and Stoney Creek valley served as vital Indigenous and Métis gathering, food-preparation, and lookout points. According to local historical accounts and folklore, the rocks along Stoney Creek bear the memory of bloody encounters from antiquity, giving rise to the haunting name "Driedmeat" for both the prominent hill and the adjacent lake. Generations of locals and travelers have passed down tales of eerie whispers carried on the prairie wind and an oppressive, sorrowful atmosphere lingering around the historic ridge where ancient overland trails converge.

#location
Driedmeat Hill rises near Driedmeat Lake and Stoney Creek, just northeast of Camrose, Alberta.

#title
The Spirits of the Bailey Theatre

#summary
Operating on Main Street since 1911, the historic Bailey Theatre is reputed to be haunted by unseen entities, most notably a spectral pianist who plays in the empty building.

#details
Originally opened as the David Theatre in February 1911 by rancher Camille David and later renamed after being purchased by Stan Bailey in 1919, the Bailey Theatre stands as Western Canada's oldest operating theatre. Throughout its history of hosting vaudeville acts, silent films, community gatherings, and eventual periods of dormancy prior to its grand restoration in 2011, staff, visitors, and investigators have reported lingering paranormal activity. Local radio broadcasters and ghost tour guides have documented chilling experiences while spending nights alone inside the darkened auditorium, including phantom piano music playing from an empty stage, disembodied footsteps echoing across the wooden floorboards, and cold spots shifting inexplicably through the historic building.

#location
The Bailey Theatre is located at 5041 50 Street on Main Street in Camrose, Alberta.

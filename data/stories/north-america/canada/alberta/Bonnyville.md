#title
The Haunting of the Bonnyville Legion Hall

#summary
Staff and visitors at the Bonnyville Legion Hall experience unexplained paranormal phenomena, including self-activating water faucets, flying objects, and phantom footsteps originating from a historic church section moved to the building in the 1940s.

#details
According to Legion manager Jamie Beaupre and staff members such as Lorna, the building is plagued by active and unsettling occurrences. Water faucets in the older section frequently turn on by themselves overnight. Staff have reported hearing loud hammering and crashing noises in the kitchen, yet upon investigation, nothing is out of place or disturbed. Panic bars on the doors also engage automatically when no one is near. 

Lorna experienced persistent, unexplained tapping noises while preparing food in the kitchen, and on another occasion, while checking the restrooms after closing, her cigarettes violently flew out of her pocket and slid across the floor of the men's bathroom. Witnesses have also glimpsed shadow figures and heard phantom footsteps heading toward the building's historic church structure, which was originally relocated from Duclos in the 1940s.

#location
The Bonnyville Legion Hall, located in the town of Bonnyville, Alberta.

#title
The Legend of the Fort Kent Wendigo

#summary
Local folklore in the rural area surrounding Bonnyville tells of a tall, dark, glowing-eyed creature that haunts the local woods, terrifying residents and ranchers with eerie wails and aggressive encounters.

#details
Dating back decades in local oral tradition within the Bonnyville region, the legend of the Wendigo in Fort Kent describes a terrifying cryptid haunting the woods. Area ranchers and locals have reported hearing unearthly moans, howls, and wails echoing from the dense forest at night. 

One prominent local account tells of a rancher who armed himself with a rifle to protect his horses from an attacker outside his home, only to be confronted by a massive, dark, shambling shape with glowing red eyes. When the rancher fired a shot at the entity, it unconcernedly retreated back into the timberline. Other versions of the regional lore tie the creature's presence to local tragedies and isolation in rural northern Alberta.

#location
The forested rural areas surrounding the hamlet of Fort Kent in the Municipal District of Bonnyville, Alberta.

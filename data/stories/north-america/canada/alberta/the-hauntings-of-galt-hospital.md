#title
The Hauntings of Galt Hospital

#summary
The historic Galt Museum and Archives in Lethbridge, formerly Galt Hospital, is plagued by reports of a patient who tragically died following an elevator accident as well as phantom children's laughter and sightings.

#details
Built in 1891 as the Galt Hospital, the building has accumulated numerous ghost stories over the decades. One of the most famous spirits is George Bailey, a farmer who died in 1933 after his gurney got caught in the hospital elevator shaft, dropping him to the basement. Since then, staff and visitors have reported the sound of shuffling slipper feet, floating blue lights, and an old elevator running on its own. In addition, the upper level—formerly the children's nursery—is associated with the quiet chattering and laughter of children, with witnesses reporting phantom children or a young girl waving goodbye from upper windows after hours.

#location
The Galt Museum and Archives (formerly Galt Hospital) located at 501 1st Street South in Lethbridge, Alberta.

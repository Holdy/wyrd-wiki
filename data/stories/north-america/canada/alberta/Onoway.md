#title
The Resident Spirit of the Old Brick Schoolhouse

#summary
Paranormal investigators and visitors at the Onoway Museum report unexplained phenomena, including phantom shoveling noises in the old coal room and anomalous EMF spikes linked to local folklore.

#details
The Onoway Museum, housed in the historic Old Brick Schoolhouse built in 1921, has long been rumored to be haunted. During investigations by paranormal researchers, anomalous activity was documented in the building's loft and basement coal room. Investigators captured disembodied audio resembling a shovel scraping across the floor in the furnace room—mirroring local folklore about a mischievous schoolboy from decades past who avoided the cold outdoor latrines by hiding in the coal room—along with sudden electromagnetic field fluctuations and shadowy mists.

#location
The Onoway Museum, housed in the historic Old Brick Schoolhouse located in Onoway, Alberta.

#title
The Serpent of Lac Ste. Anne

#summary
Indigenous oral traditions and local folklore describe a giant serpent living in Lac Ste. Anne near Onoway whose movements stirred dangerous, churning waters.

#details
Long before European settlement, the waters of Lac Ste. Anne—known as Wakamne by the Nakota Sioux and Manitou Sakhahigan by the Cree—were regarded as deeply sacred. According to enduring legends, a massive water serpent inhabited the depths of the lake. Oral histories recount that when the creature shifted or moved through the water, it generated violent, treacherous currents capable of capsizing canoes, prompting young people to peer anxiously into the clear depths to catch a glimpse of the spirit beast.

#location
Lac Ste. Anne, located a few kilometers outside the townsite of Onoway, Alberta.

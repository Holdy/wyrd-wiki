The Cessna Crane Crash Site Anomaly

Summary

Visitors to the remote site of a 1950 plane crash north of Whitecourt have reported sudden electronic failures and total disorientation.

Details

On October 14, 1950, a Cessna Crane aircraft crashed north of Whitecourt, resulting in five fatalities. Years later, when researchers visited the remote crash site on the anniversary of the tragedy, they experienced uncanny technological failures—such as all electronic device batteries dying simultaneously—alongside sudden, inexplicable disorientation while navigating the area.

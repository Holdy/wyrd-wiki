#title
The Specter of the Historic Airdrie House

#summary
Originally built in Airdrie in 1906, this historic pioneer home is associated with inexplicable footsteps and an oppressive atmosphere that unnerves visitors and staff.

#details
Constructed by pioneer Samuel John Bushfield in Airdrie in 1906 (and later relocated to Heritage Park), the modest Airdrie House developed a reputation for intense paranormal activity despite lacking a well-documented personal tragedy in its history. A visiting clairvoyant once sensed an immediate, heavy presence upon approaching the structure. Years later, park staff led an investigation where several witnesses heard distinct, heavy footsteps walking across the unoccupied upper floor at midnight. Additionally, staff frequently report that holiday decorations refuse to stay upright and items mysteriously relocate between visits.

#location
The historic Airdrie House, originally built in Airdrie, Alberta in 1906.

#title
The Restless Spirits of the Fort Saskatchewan Police Post

#summary
Staff and visitors at the historic North West Mounted Police post and museum grounds report disembodied footsteps, strange lights, and the ghostly apparition of Alberta's last executed female prisoner.

#details
Established in 1875 as the second fort constructed by the North West Mounted Police, the post later became a provincial jail where numerous inmates faced execution before capital punishment was abolished in Canada. Today, the preserved grounds and heritage buildings—including the Carpenter's Shop and Doctor Henry House—are widely reputed to be haunted. Visitors and curators have reported experiencing unexplained phenomena such as lights turning on and off by themselves, objects moving mysteriously, and chilling disembodied voices and whispers. Among the most prominent legends is the sighting of a spectral female figure identified as Florence Lassandro, the infamous "Mob Princess" who was hanged at the jail in 1923, as well as the phantom apparition of a small girl wandering the site.

#location
The Fort Heritage Precinct, situated along the North Saskatchewan River in Fort Saskatchewan, Alberta.

#title
The Unmarked Crosses of the Gaol Cemetery

#summary
The historic Fort Saskatchewan gaol cemetery, containing the unmarked graves of unclaimed prisoners and executed criminals, is said to be haunted by restless souls.

#details
Originally located south of the North Saskatchewan River bridge before being moved in 1957, the Fort Saskatchewan Gaol Cemetery is tucked behind a residential neighborhood near the edge of the Sherridon subdivision. It holds nineteen unmarked crosses representing prisoners and felons who died or were executed at the old jail and went unclaimed by families. Local folklore and paranormal investigations recount that the spirits of these executed inmates still linger around their final, unmarked resting place. Visitors have reported uneasy feelings, strange atmospheric anomalies, and ghostly voices near the edge of the woods and houses bordering the cemetery.

#location
The Fort Saskatchewan Gaol Cemetery, located on the edge of the Sherridon residential subdivision in Fort Saskatchewan, Alberta.

#title
The Haunting of McKay Avenue School

#summary
Edmonton's oldest brick schoolhouse is said to be haunted by the spirit of a construction worker who fell to his death, alongside mischievous child apparitions.

#details
Originally built between 1904 and 1905, the McKay Avenue School building—now operating as the Edmonton Public Schools Archive and Museum—has long been the subject of local ghost lore. Staff and visitors have reported numerous unexplained occurrences, including objects moving on their own, water taps turning on unexpectedly, blinds being ripped down from windows, and chairs strewn about empty rooms. According to local legend, the building is haunted by a construction worker who tragically fell to his death from the roof in 1912. Additionally, visiting psychics and visitors have claimed to spot ghostly children running along the exterior fire-escapes.

#location
McKay Avenue School is located at 10704 97 Street in Edmonton, Alberta.

#title
The Specters of Fairmont Hotel Macdonald

#summary
Edmonton's historic Fairmont Hotel Macdonald is said to be haunted by several ghostly figures, including a workhorse that died during construction in 1914 and a 1913 riverboat sailor.

#details
Guests staying on the eighth floor of the Fairmont Hotel Macdonald frequently report hearing the heavy pounding of a horse's hooves echoing through the hallways, a phenomenon attributed to a workhorse that collapsed and died of exhaustion while workers were pouring the hotel's foundation in 1914. Beyond the spectral horse, guests in the executive suites have encountered the apparition of a 1913 riverboat sailor sitting quietly in a wing chair and smoking a pipe. Staff members have also experienced unexplainable events, such as phone calls from vacant sixth-floor rooms, deadbolted doors locking themselves from the inside of empty rooms, and vintage 1950s music playing from radios that are turned completely off.

#location
Fairmont Hotel Macdonald is located at 10065 100 Street in Edmonton, Alberta.

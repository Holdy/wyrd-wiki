#title
The Wendigo at Bald Hill

#summary
During a winter trapping camp in 1899 at Bald Hill, a trapper seized by terrifying windigo psychosis and threats of cannibalism was executed by his peers.

#details
In March 1899, at a winter trapping camp at Bald Hill on the Little Smoky River southwest of Lesser Slave Lake, a trapper named Louis Moostoos fell violently ill and began exhibiting signs of windigo possession, threatening to kill and eat his family. Following extraordinary disturbances inside his cabin, fellow camp members struck him down with tomahawks, staked his body to the earth, and performed traditional rituals to neutralize the malevolent spirit. The North-West Mounted Police subsequently investigated the incident and brought the participants to trial.

#location
The events occurred at Bald Hill along the Little Smoky River, located southwest of Lesser Slave Lake in Alberta, Canada.

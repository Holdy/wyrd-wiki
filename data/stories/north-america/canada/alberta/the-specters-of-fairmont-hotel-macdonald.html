The Specters of Fairmont Hotel Macdonald

Summary

Edmonton's historic Fairmont Hotel Macdonald is said to be haunted by several ghostly figures, including a workhorse that died during construction in 1914 and a 1913 riverboat sailor.

Details

Guests staying on the eighth floor of the Fairmont Hotel Macdonald frequently report hearing the heavy pounding of a horse's hooves echoing through the hallways, a phenomenon attributed to a workhorse that collapsed and died of exhaustion while workers were pouring the hotel's foundation in 1914. Beyond the spectral horse, guests in the executive suites have encountered the apparition of a 1913 riverboat sailor sitting quietly in a wing chair and smoking a pipe. Staff members have also experienced unexplainable events, such as phone calls from vacant sixth-floor rooms, deadbolted doors locking themselves from the inside of empty rooms, and vintage 1950s music playing from radios that are turned completely off.

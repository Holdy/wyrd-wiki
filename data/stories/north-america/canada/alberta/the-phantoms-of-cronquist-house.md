#title
The Phantoms of Cronquist House

#summary
The historic Edwardian-style Cronquist House overlooking Bower Ponds is reported to be haunted by a woman seen at an upper window, phantom footsteps, and moving household objects.

#details
Originally built in 1911 by Emmanuel Cronquist in West Park, the historic eight-bedroom Victorian farmhouse was moved across the frozen Red Deer River in the 1970s to its current location overlooking Bower Ponds to save it from demolition. Many locals believe that moving the building disturbed the spirits within. Over the years, staff and visitors have reported numerous unexplained occurrences, including tea sets and saucers sliding across tables, doors slamming shut when no one is near, and the sound of heavy footsteps echoing on upper floors when the house is entirely empty. In one notable incident, former executive director Elizabeth Plumtree witnessed the double doors leading into the kitchen swinging rapidly back and forth as if someone had just run through them, though security alarms had not triggered and no one was found inside.

#location
Cronquist House at Bower Ponds in Red Deer, Alberta.

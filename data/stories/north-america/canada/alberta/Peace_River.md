#title
The Grey Lady of Dunvegan Bridge

#summary
Motorists crossing the suspension bridge over the Peace River during a snowstorm encountered a hooded woman with bare feet who vanished without leaving footprints, later appearing on a nearby hillside.

#details
In November 1960, shortly after the completion of the suspension bridge across the Peace River at Dunvegan, two men driving through a heavy snowstorm spotted a woman leaning into the weather wearing a hooded cloak but with bare feet. When they pulled over and walked back to assist her, the bridge was completely empty, with no footprints in the snow other than the man's own. Subsequent sightings by locals placed her further up the valley hill, and due to her cloak resembling a habit, she became known as the Grey Lady of Dunvegan.

#location
The Dunvegan Suspension Bridge spanning the Peace River in northwestern Alberta.

#title
The Priest of Fort Dunvegan Rectory

#summary
Visitors and staff touring the historic rectory building along the Peace River have reported feeling watched, seeing unexplained lights, and witnessing the apparition of a bearded priest writing at a desk.

#details
Following the opening of the historic buildings at Fort Dunvegan Provincial Park along the Peace River, visitors and staff reported paranormal phenomena centered around the old rectory. On the building's opening day, a visitor picking up a pen in the second-floor bishop's bedroom experienced a vivid vision of a bearded priest writing a letter at the desk. Locals associate the manifestation with Alfred Campbell Garrioch, a priest who served at the mission until 1891, alongside ongoing reports of glowing second-floor windows and an oppressive sense of being watched.

#location
The historic rectory at Historic Dunvegan Provincial Park situated along the Peace River in Alberta.

The Phantoms of Gaoler's Mews

Summary

Cobblestoned Gaoler's Mews in Gastown—once the site of Vancouver's first jail and public hangings—is haunted by a woman in black and other restless spirits.

Details

Located in the heart of Gastown, Gaoler's Mews stands on ground that once held Vancouver's mid-19th-century jail and a hangman's scaffold where over forty public executions took place. Historic buildings around the cobblestone courtyard, such as the former Irish Heather pub and adjacent spaces, have long been plagued by paranormal activity. Witnesses have reported a woman dressed in black gliding down the cobblestone passageway toward Blood Alley before vanishing into thin air, believed to be the mourning widow of a condemned prisoner. Other reported phenomena include tools mysteriously relocated by unseen hands, kitchen staff hearing their names called in empty rooms, chairs in the second-story loft violently overturned overnight, and phantom footsteps echoing down wooden stairs.

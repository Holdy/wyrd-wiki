Voices in the Beairsto School Gymnasium

Summary

A back room connected to the gymnasium at Beairsto School in Vernon is said to be haunted by unseen spirits, with strange, unexplainable voices echoing late at night.

Details

Beairsto School holds a prominent place in local supernatural lore due to recurring reports from night staff and security regarding a specific back room off its gymnasium. Long after students and teachers have departed for the day, people in the building have reported hearing unexplained whispers and disembodied voices coming from the empty room, fueling local legends about spirits lingering in the school.

#title
The Haunting of Canada's Oldest Operating Movie Theatre

#summary
Operating continuously since 1913, the Patricia Theatre in Powell River's Townsite is haunted by a strict former dentist and active poltergeist phenomena.

#details
Housed in a Spanish revival building designed by architect Henry Holdsby Simmonds, the Patricia Theatre has accumulated numerous ghostly legends, especially following major renovations in the late 1990s and early 2000s. Workers experienced severe paranormal activity, including self-opening curtains, moving supply carts, inexplicable cold spots, and tools mysteriously shifting. A contractor named Dan Kressel was pushed down a flight of concrete stairs by an unseen force and quit on the spot. The building's most famous spirit is Dr. Samuel Paul Marlatt, a stern dentist who practiced on the upper floor from 1928 to 1956 and is frequently seen as a man in a white medical smock. Conversely, long-time owner Myron McCleod is also reported to peacefully appear sitting at an old-fashioned desk before fading away.

#location
The Patricia Theatre is located at 5848 Ash Avenue in the historic Townsite of Powell River, British Columbia.

#title
The Phantoms of the Old Courthouse Inn

#summary
Housed in Powell River's former 1939 government building and jail, the Old Courthouse Inn is haunted by several entities, including a prank-playing child ghost and voices on hotel phones.

#details
Built in 1939 as a provincial building that housed the town's police station, jail, clerical offices, and courtroom, this Tudor-style building was later converted into a historic inn. Guests and paranormal investigators have documented various haunting phenomena, particularly across rooms 1, 3, 6, and 9. In Room 1, a pint-sized phantom boy nicknamed "Leo" hides belongings and pulls hair. In Room 3—the former police station—guests report sudden temperature drops, feelings of being watched, and an unnerving incident where a guest heard a loud male voice snap "shut up" through her phone's earpiece while speaking to a friend. Room 6 features self-opening doors and shadowy mirror apparitions, while restaurant staff frequently encounter the scent of rosewater and moving objects.

#location
The Old Courthouse Inn is located in the Historic Powell River Townsite, British Columbia.

#title
The Spirits of the Rodmay Heritage Hotel

#summary
Built in 1911 in Powell River's historic Townsite, the Rodmay Heritage Hotel is haunted by multiple resident spirits, including its original matron and a phantom Chinese cook.

#details
Originally opened as the Powell River Hotel to serve workers and travelers, the historic building is known as one of the most haunted locations on the coast. Among its most prominent spirits is Mrs. McKinney, the original matron of the hotel, who is frequently seen in the Rainbow Room bar area. Another resident entity is Charlie, a former Chinese cook who reportedly died in the basement during a poker game and now haunts the lower floor restaurant. Employees and visitors have reported Charlie commenting on workers' singing voices through headphones, alongside unexplained movements of tools, stools, and lightbulbs. Other spectral figures reported in the hotel include a young German girl holding a teddy bear on the second floor, a woman in white, and a dapper man named Frank.

#location
The Rodmay Heritage Hotel is located in the historic Townsite area of Powell River, British Columbia.

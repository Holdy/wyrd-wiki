#title
The Phantom Piano and Unseen Footsteps at the White Rock Players Club

#summary
The historic Oceana Parc Playhouse, home to the White Rock Players Club, is frequently visited by mysterious footsteps, self-playing pianos, and disembodied voices.

#details
For decades, the White Rock Players Club and its home at the Oceana Parc Playhouse have been the epicenter of unexplained paranormal phenomena. Longtime members and technical workers have repeatedly reported hearing heavy footsteps pacing the dressing rooms upstairs when everyone else is gathered downstairs on the stage. Other baffling occurrences include lights in storage and costume areas mysteriously shutting off while someone is working inside, and random piano notes echoing through the building. On multiple occasions, crew members working late on stage have heard a piano playing out in the lobby, only to investigate and find both pianos securely covered with their keyboards closed. Voices have also been heard speaking from empty doorways, asking workers if they are ready to talk.

#location
The Oceana Parc Playhouse (home of the White Rock Players Club) is located in White Rock, British Columbia.

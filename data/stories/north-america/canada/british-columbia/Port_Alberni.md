#title
The Lady Who Walks on Water

#summary
An 80-year-old local legend tells of a ghostly woman in old-fashioned clothing who wanders the banks and glides across the surface of the Somass River at Paper Mill Dam Park, searching for her lost baby.

#details
Dating back at least to the late 1930s or early 1940s, residents of Port Alberni have reported encounters with a mysterious female apparition at Paper Mill Dam Park along the Somass River. Witnesses describe an attractive woman wearing old-fashioned clothing who is seen walking along the riverbank or gliding directly on top of the water, sometimes emerging with completely dry hair before vanishing. The spirit is known to approach people with a heartbreaking question: "Have you seen my baby?" In addition to sightings of the woman, visitors to the park have reported uncanny shadow figures and floating ghost-light orbs along the river system.

#location
Paper Mill Dam Park along the Somass River in Port Alberni, British Columbia.

#title
The Mysterious Weiner House Explosion

#summary
Homesteader Nils Weiner mysteriously vanished in 1944 when a massive nighttime explosion completely obliterated his Sproat Lake cabin, leaving behind a legacy of local mystery and unrecovered remains.

#details
Nils Weiner and his wife Anna arrived in the Alberni Valley around 1913, homesteading near Sproat Lake Landing. Following Anna's sudden and tragic death at age 42, Nils became an isolated and troubled figure. In the early hours of September 23, 1944, residents around the lake were jolted awake by a tremendous explosion that rattled windows for miles. The Weiner house was completely obliterated, and all outbuildings burned to the ground. Despite the devastation, Nils's body was never found, fueling decades of enduring local speculation over whether the event was a tragic accident, foul play, or an intentional disappearance.

#location
The former Weiner homestead site near Sproat Lake Landing in Port Alberni, British Columbia.

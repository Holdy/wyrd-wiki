The Gentleman of Room 335

Summary

Guests and staff at a historic downtown hotel frequently report encountering benevolent caretaker spirits, including a bowler-hatted gentleman and an auditory warning in the basement.

Details

Built in 1898, the Hume Hotel has long been associated with paranormal reports. A guest once attempted to check out immediately after checking in because she encountered a well-dressed gentleman wearing a bowler hat sitting on the edge of her bed who politely tipped his hat and smiled at her, unaware he was a spirit. During subsequent paranormal investigations by local researchers in the basement near Ward Creek, recording equipment captured an otherworldly voice whispering a clear warning: "Careful."

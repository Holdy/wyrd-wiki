#title
The Apparition at Trethewey House

#summary
Built in 1920 by industrialist J.O. Trethewey, this heritage house at Mill Lake is said to be haunted by a woman who passed away in one of its upper guest bedrooms.

#details
Situated on the east side of Mill Lake Park, the grand Craftsman-style Trethewey House was constructed for Abbotsford Lumber Company proprietor J.O. Trethewey. Though meticulously restored as a heritage museum showcasing early regional history, staff and visitors have long reported uncanny paranormal activity within its walls. According to local lore, a woman died in a guest bedroom upstairs long ago and continues to linger in the home. Witnesses have reported seeing an apparition staring out upper-level windows, lights turning on spontaneously when the building is empty, doors opening by themselves, and visitors feeling an invisible presence watching them in empty rooms.

#location
Trethewey House, located at 2313 Ware Street near Mill Lake in Abbotsford, British Columbia.

#title
The Haunting of Keeping Road

#summary
Keeping Road in Abbotsford is widely regarded as one of Canada's most haunted rural stretches, notorious for legends of a hanging apparition, phantom demon dogs, and inexplicable vehicle disturbances.

#details
Located near the base of Sumas Mountain, Keeping Road has accumulated a dark and prolific local lore over the years. Numerous motorists and ghost hunters have reported eerie phenomena along the deserted stretch after dark. Among the most enduring legends are a spectral little girl who emerges from the darkness to remove keys from the hoods of parked cars, and a phantom man hanging from a bank of trees who appears when drivers flash their headlights. Other terrifying encounters include sightings of a woman in white walking the road, a giant "demon dog" with glowing green eyes that chases vehicles, and mysterious audio distortions, vehicle malfunctions, and doors opening by themselves.

#location
Keeping Road, located near Sumas Mountain in Abbotsford, British Columbia.

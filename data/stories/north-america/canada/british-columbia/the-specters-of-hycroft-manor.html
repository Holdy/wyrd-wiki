The Specters of Hycroft Manor

Summary

Built in 1911, the stately Hycroft Manor in Shaughnessy is reportedly haunted by seven distinct ghosts, including a World War I general and mischievous veteran spirits.

Details

Hycroft Manor was completed in 1911 for General Alexander Duncan McRae and his wife Blaunche, who hosted lavish high-society gatherings there. In 1942, the couple donated the mansion to the Canadian government to serve as a convalescent hospital for war veterans. Today, the 30-room estate is renowned as one of Vancouver's most haunted sites, home to at least seven resident spirits. Among them are the apparition of General McRae in a WWI officer's uniform, a nurse from the convalescent hospital era, and three mischievous soldier spirits dubbed "The Pranksters" who manipulate doors and lights. Staff and investigators have also reported a "Crying Man" whose loud sobbing echoes from a lower-floor room, alongside unexplained phenomena such as floating black energy orbs.

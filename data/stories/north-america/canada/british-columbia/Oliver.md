#title
The Phantom Princess of McIntyre Bluff

#summary
The towering rock formation of McIntyre Bluff near Oliver is said to be visited and watched over by the spirit of a First Nations princess who died in the region long ago.

#details
Rising dramatically above the South Okanagan valley, McIntyre Bluff carries longstanding regional folklore regarding a tragic historical figure. Hikers and locals passing through the area have reported sensing a heavy, sorrowful presence and unexplained phenomena along the cliffside trails, tying the haunting to ancient oral history.

#location
McIntyre Bluff is a prominent limestone cliffside landmark located just north of Oliver, British Columbia.

#title
The Specters of Fairview Ghost Town

#summary
The historic gold-mining boomtown of Fairview, located just west of Oliver, is said to be haunted by the restless spirits of miners and pioneers who perished during its rough-and-tumble era.

#details
Established in the 1890s during the South Okanagan gold rush, Fairview grew into a bustling settlement before fading into an abandoned ghost town by the 1920s. Its brief history was marked by sudden fires, gold heists, and violent deaths. Today, nighttime tours through the old townsite and the historic Fairview Cemetery regularly report strange phenomena, inexplicable shadows, and the lingering presence of the town's former residents.

#location
Fairview Cemetery and the historic townsite are situated just west of the modern-day town of Oliver, British Columbia, off Rockcliffe Road.

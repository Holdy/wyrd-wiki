#title
The Apparition of the Ingram Block

#summary
The historic Ingram Block in downtown Fernie is haunted by the restless spirit of William Ingram, a prominent local businessman who was murdered there in 1939.

#details
William Ingram was a well-known businessman who was attacked and struck on the back of the head with an iron pipe on the night of November 21, 1939, dying of his injuries five days later. Decades later, when the top floor of the Ingram building was used for artist studios and offices, tenants frequently experienced unexplained phenomena. Artists reported hearing a key turn in the downstairs lock followed by heavy, slow footsteps climbing the stairs, even when convex mirrors installed to check the stairwell showed no one was there. Others reported the strong, lingering scent of cigar smoke and shadowy figures moving through the building.

#location
The historic Ingram Block building located in the 500 block between 1st and 2nd Avenue in downtown Fernie, British Columbia.

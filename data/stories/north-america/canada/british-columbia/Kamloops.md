#title
Echoes of the Tranquille Sanatorium

#summary
Built in 1907 as a tuberculosis hospital, this sprawling facility is widely considered one of Canada's most haunted locations due to lingering disembodied moans and ghostly apparitions.

#details
Originally established as a treatment center for tuberculosis sufferers before later housing individuals with mental disabilities, the Tranquille Sanatorium near Kamloops witnessed countless deaths over its decades of operation. Witnesses and visitors to the historic grounds have reported intense paranormal activity, including mysterious orbs drifting through empty hallways, eerie disembodied groans, and shadowy figures wandering the property. The subterranean tunnels beneath the complex—historically used to transport supplies and bodies away from the view of recovering patients—are particularly noted for carrying the heavy residual energy of the past.

#location
The historic Tranquille Sanatorium grounds are located off Tranquille Road near Kamloops, British Columbia.

#title
The Phantom of the Plaza Hotel

#summary
Former co-owner and guests of a historic downtown Kamloops hotel have frequently reported encountering the apparition of a mysterious woman and experiencing inexplicable mechanical mishaps.

#details
Operating since the early 20th century, the Plaza Hotel in downtown Kamloops has earned a lasting reputation for paranormal activity. Former co-owner Tina Lange recounted multiple late-night experiences where she caught glimpses out of the corner of her eye of a woman walking around corners and ascending the grand wooden stairwells, only to find no one there. Other eerie occurrences in the heritage building included heavy metal doors closing entirely on their own and coffee maker baskets violently ejecting themselves without human touch. Guests have also specifically chosen to stay at the hotel due to its widespread reputation on lists of purported haunted sites.

#location
The Plaza Hotel is located at the intersection of Victoria Street and Fourth Avenue in downtown Kamloops, British Columbia.

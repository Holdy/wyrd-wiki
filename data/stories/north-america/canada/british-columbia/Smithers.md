#title
The Phantoms of the Grand Trunk Pacific Railway Station

#summary
Staff and visitors at a historic railway station in Smithers have reported unexplained poltergeist activity, ghostly apparitions in vintage railway uniforms, and lights turning on in abandoned upper-floor rooms.

#details
Built in 1919 by the Grand Trunk Pacific Railway, the historic railway station at 3815 Railway Avenue has long been reputed to be haunted. Fawn Engen, owner of the Trackside Cantina restaurant operating out of the building, and various staff members have experienced numerous paranormal occurrences over the years. Staff frequently encounter a mischievous poltergeist entity believed to be a former cheerful employee named Kevin, who is credited with making kitchen clocks fall off walls and tossing paper towels. Other workers have reported being hugged in restrooms by unseen presences and witnessing glassware violently flying off shelves. Additionally, witnesses have spotted lights shining in the completely vacant and locked upper-floor rooms where railway workers once lodged, and one employee caught the reflection of a man wearing an old-school grey train uniform and hat in a window before turning to find nobody there.

#location
The historic Smithers Railway Station is located at 3815 Railway Avenue in Smithers, British Columbia.

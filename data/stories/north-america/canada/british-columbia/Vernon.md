#title
The Lady of O'Keefe Mansion

#summary
Staff and paranormal investigators at the historic O'Keefe Ranch north of Vernon have reported feeling a lingering female presence, seeing a mysterious woman watch from an upstairs window, and capturing disembodied screams in the attic.

#details
Founded in 1867, the Historic O'Keefe Ranch is regarded as one of the most haunted historic sites in the region. Staff members working alone in the stately O'Keefe Mansion frequently report a strong sense of a woman's presence watching them. Observers outside have occasionally spotted a lady peering out from an upstairs window. Furthermore, paranormal investigation groups utilizing audio equipment in the mansion's attic have recorded unexplainable disembodied voices and a woman's scream echoing through the empty space.

#location
The Historic O'Keefe Ranch is situated at 9385 Highway 97 North just north of Vernon, British Columbia.

#title
The Phantom Projectionist of Vernon Towne Cinema

#summary
Moviegoers and staff at the historic Vernon Towne Cinema frequently experience paranormal phenomena, including phantom hair-pulling, disembodied voices, and sightings of a former projectionist known as Old Lloyd.

#details
Patrons of the vintage Vernon Towne Cinema in Vernon have long shared accounts of unexplained occurrences within the auditorium. Visitors have reported startling sensations such as having their hair pulled while seated in the dark, while staff members working after hours have heard mysterious voices echoing through empty halls. The most famous spirit associated with the theater is believed to be the ghost of a deceased projectionist dubbed "Old Lloyd," whose spectral form is said to wander the aisles.

#location
The Vernon Towne Cinema is located at 2910 30 Avenue in Vernon, British Columbia.

#title
Voices in the Beairsto School Gymnasium

#summary
A back room connected to the gymnasium at Beairsto School in Vernon is said to be haunted by unseen spirits, with strange, unexplainable voices echoing late at night.

#details
Beairsto School holds a prominent place in local supernatural lore due to recurring reports from night staff and security regarding a specific back room off its gymnasium. Long after students and teachers have departed for the day, people in the building have reported hearing unexplained whispers and disembodied voices coming from the empty room, fueling local legends about spirits lingering in the school.

#location
Beairsto School is located at 3302 27 Avenue in Vernon, British Columbia.

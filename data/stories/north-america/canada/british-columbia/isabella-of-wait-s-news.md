#title
Isabella of Wait's News

#summary
Employees at a downtown Nelson newsstand routinely experience unexplained phenomena attributed to the ghost of a young librarian named Isabella.

#details
Since around 2010, the owner and staff of Wait's News have kept a logbook documenting strange occurrences in the shop. They attribute the activity to Isabella, believed to be the spirit of a 23-year-old librarian who once worked in the area. Staff members have reported items moving on their own, such as a half-full case of soda appearing neatly in the middle of the bathroom floor without any preceding noise, as well as recurring sightings of Isabella near the stairs and a gentleman in a top hat standing near the back freezers.

#location
Wait's News is located on Baker Street in downtown Nelson, British Columbia.

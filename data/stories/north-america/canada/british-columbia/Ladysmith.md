#title
Ghost Tales of the Black Nugget Museum

#summary
The former Miners Hotel in Ladysmith, later known as the Black Nugget Museum, is said to be haunted by its original 19th-century hoteliers and victims of a historic poker shooting. Visitors and owners have reported encounters with a man in a handlebar mustache, a lady in white, and objects moving on their own.

#details
Originally constructed as the Miners Hotel in Wellington in 1881, the building was transported to Ladysmith in 1900 by hoteliers Lodwick and Sarah Jones, who renamed it the Jones Hotel. The building's gambling room was the site of a deadly shooting after a gambler was caught cheating at poker, and later sparked a town-wide gold rush panic when prospectors mistook gold trimming on the walls for real gold. After Lodwick and Sarah lived into their 80s and passed away in the building, subsequent owners and mediums reported intense paranormal activity. Accounts include sightings of a man with a handlebar mustache in the attic window, a lady in white walking the parlour, decorative elephants mysteriously rotating overnight, and a grand piano playing notes by itself.

#location
The Black Nugget Museum at 12 Gatacre Street in Ladysmith, British Columbia.

#title
The Headless Woman of Mount Sicker Road

#summary
A spectral apparition of a headless woman is said to haunt the old Lenora town site along Mount Sicker Road near Ladysmith. According to local folklore, she wanders the roadway searching for her head after being murdered and dismembered by a jealous husband during the early 1900s mining boom.

#details
For decades, travellers driving along Mount Sicker Road north of Ladysmith near the historic Lenora town site have reported seeing the apparition of a woman in old-fashioned clothing walking alongside or gliding across the road. Local legend claims that in the early 1900s, a miner discovered his wife's infidelity, murdered her, and chopped her body into pieces across the mountain. Although historical newspaper archives from the era show no record of such a murder, the eerie legend persists, with witnesses frequently reporting sightings of the headless figure or partial apparitions missing their lower halves.

#location
Mount Sicker Road, north of Ladysmith, British Columbia, near the historic Lenora town site.

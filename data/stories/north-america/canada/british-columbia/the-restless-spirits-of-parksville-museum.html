    The Restless Spirits of Parksville Museum

    Summary

    Staff and visitors at the historic Parksville Museum grounds have frequently reported unexplained occurrences, including objects mysteriously shifting positions and lights turning on and off by themselves.

    Details

    The Parksville Museum, also known as Craig Heritage Park, preserves several historic buildings dating back to the late 19th and early 20th centuries—such as the Knox Heritage Church, the McMillan House, and old pioneer cabins. According to museum management and those sensitive to the paranormal, the grounds are home to distinct supernatural activity. Visitors and staff have recounted experiences where historical artifacts move on their own, lights flicker or operate independently of power sources, and an unmistakable, sentient presence is felt throughout the property.

The Haunting of Gibsons Pioneer Cemetery

Summary

Visitors and locals at the historic Gibsons Pioneer Cemetery have reported eerie encounters, including unexplained orbs, a ghostly photograph of a skeletal face, disembodied screams in the woods, and flying stones.

Details

Nestled at the base of Mount Elphinstone in an isolated mountainside forest, the Gibsons Pioneer Cemetery—often called "Boot Hill"—is the final resting place of the town's early founding families. Over the years, numerous paranormal accounts have emerged from the neglected graveyard. A teenager taking photographs of the cemetery later discovered unusual light orbs on his computer, followed shortly by a phantom knock at his door when no one was outside. On another occasion, a woman and her daughters visiting the cemetery at night felt they were being followed down the dark trail; a quick photo snapped before reaching the trailhead revealed orbs and a shape resembling a skeletal face. Additionally, visitors have reported hearing distant, disembodied screams echoing through the surrounding trees, and an eyewitness personally experienced a pebble flying horizontally out of open foliage to strike her ankle while walking down the path with complete silence blanketing the cemetery grounds.

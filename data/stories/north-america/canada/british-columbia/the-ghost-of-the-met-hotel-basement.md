#title
The Ghost of the Met Hotel Basement

#summary
The historic Met Hotel on Columbia Street, one of the few survivors of New Westminster's 1898 Great Fire, is haunted by a former caretaker named George and female entities recorded via electronic voice phenomena.

#details
Housed in the historic Burr Block, The Met Hotel survived the Great Fire of New Westminster in 1898. Paranormal investigators and staff have noted intense activity, particularly in the basement where a former caretaker named George lived and died. Staff have reported George floating through hallways, walking through walls, and playing pranks like invisible hands clapping loudly in front of visitors' faces. Audio recordings (EVPs) captured during investigations have also recorded a female entity identifying herself as Mary.

#location
The Met Hotel is located at 411 Columbia Street in New Westminster, British Columbia.

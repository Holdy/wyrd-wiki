#title
The Spirit of Canoe Cove

#summary
The historic Stonehouse Pub is said to be haunted by the ghost of a young boy who died tragically on the property in 1940.

#details
Built in 1935 as an English manor house by British immigrant Hugh Rodd, the stone building at Canoe Cove became the site of a tragedy in 1940 when the owner's eight-year-old son was found hanging in an upstairs room. According to local lore, the boy never left. Visitors and staff have reported inexplicable phenomena over the decades, including lights turning on and off by themselves, objects being moved or thrown, and young children claiming to play with an unseen red-headed playmate upstairs who matches the exact description of the long-deceased boy.

#location
The Stonehouse Pub is located at 11800 Canoe Cove Road in North Saanich, British Columbia.

#title
Something Spooky at the George Little House

#summary
Staff and paranormal investigators at the historic George Little House in Terrace experience unexplained heavy presences, moving rocking chairs, and electronic voice phenomena associated with the home's original owners.

#details
Built in 1914 and later relocated to Kalum Street, the George Little House—former residence of city founder George Little and his wife Clara—has long been reputed to harbor restless spirits. House manager Debbie Letawski and other staff reported an oppressive, draining heaviness in the upstairs office rooms, as well as rocking chairs moving on their own. During an investigation by regional paranormal teams, investigators captured photographic anomalies, including a shadowed figure in an empty room, and recorded electronic voice phenomena (EVPs) using a spirit box that specifically answered with the name "Clara," believed to be Clara Little, who passed away in the home.

#location
The George Little House is located on Kalum Street in Terrace, British Columbia.

#title
The Spirits of Terraceview Lodge

#summary
A health care aide working night shifts at Terraceview Lodge encounters phantom footsteps, unexplained electrical disruptions, and uncanny resident descriptions of former patients.

#details
While working night shifts at the Terraceview Lodge long-term care facility in Terrace, British Columbia, health care aide Kendra Rae encountered several unexplainable events. In 2018, while alone in a locked dementia unit, she repeatedly heard shuffling footsteps and saw the shadowy figure of an elder in a nightgown walking into an empty, locked kitchen. In 2019, a resident who had never met a former patient accurately described the man's physical appearance—including his amputated leg—claiming he was stuck in a chair in the room. Later that year, during another night shift, Rae's fan continuously turned itself on and off for an hour, only to stop precisely at the time a long-term resident passed away on the unit.

#location
Terraceview Lodge is located in Terrace, British Columbia.

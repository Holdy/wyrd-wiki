#title
The Haunting of the Qualicum College Inn

#summary
A historic Tudor-style former boys' college is plagued by full-body apparitions, disembodied crying on the upper floors, and self-playing pianos. 

#details
Originally established as a boys' college in 1937 before later operating as the Qualicum Heritage Inn, the sprawling Tudor-style building has accumulated decades of ghost lore. Staff and guests have reported full-body apparitions of a man in an old-fashioned military uniform, a young boy, and a woman. The fourth floor is particularly notorious for disembodied voices, weeping, moaning, and chanting. In room 453 (later renumbered 459), guests and staff experienced furniture moving on its own, beds appearing slept in when locked away, and blankets being pulled off sleeping visitors. Additionally, staff members have witnessed a piano playing by itself in an empty room and heard phantom footsteps running down the stairs.

#location
The former Qualicum Heritage Inn (Qualicum College) is located on Memorial Avenue in Qualicum Beach, British Columbia.

#title
The Phantom Puppy and General Money's Mansion

#summary
Visitors and staff at a historic 1914 seaside estate in Qualicum Beach frequently encounter paranormal phenomena, including the ghostly apparition of a small dog and unexplained activity in the game room. 

#details
Built in 1914 by General Money, the Crown Mansion has long been reputed to be one of Qualicum Beach's most actively haunted properties. Hotel staff and visitors have documented numerous unexplained occurrences, particularly in the dining room and the games room. Most notably, people report hearing and seeing the phantom puppy of General Money, whose likeness appears alongside him in a historic portrait hanging in the lobby. Witnesses frequently report scampering paws, phantom footsteps, and anomalous activity such as hanging decorative crystals swaying independently of drafts or temperature shifts.

#location
Crown Mansion is located at 293 Crescent Road East in Qualicum Beach, British Columbia.

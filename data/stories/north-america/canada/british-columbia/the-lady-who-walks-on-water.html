The Lady Who Walks on Water

Summary

An 80-year-old local legend tells of a ghostly woman in old-fashioned clothing who wanders the banks and glides across the surface of the Somass River at Paper Mill Dam Park, searching for her lost baby.

Details

Dating back at least to the late 1930s or early 1940s, residents of Port Alberni have reported encounters with a mysterious female apparition at Paper Mill Dam Park along the Somass River. Witnesses describe an attractive woman wearing old-fashioned clothing who is seen walking along the riverbank or gliding directly on top of the water, sometimes emerging with completely dry hair before vanishing. The spirit is known to approach people with a heartbreaking question: "Have you seen my baby?" In addition to sightings of the woman, visitors to the park have reported uncanny shadow figures and floating ghost-light orbs along the river system.

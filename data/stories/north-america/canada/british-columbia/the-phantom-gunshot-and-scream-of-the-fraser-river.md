#title
The Phantom Gunshot and Scream of the Fraser River

#summary
Local folklore and police reports claim that the waterfront of Fort Langley echoes with a tragic 19th-century gunshot and a heartbroken woman's scream.

#details
During the 1800s, a First Nations man named Louis Satakarata dit Rabaska worked at the Hudson's Bay Company post in Fort Langley. After marrying a local woman against his father's wishes, a severe family confrontation erupted. Distraught, Louis crossed the water to McMillan Island and took his own life with a musket shot. When a retrieval party brought his body back, his wife let out a harrowing, grief-stricken scream. According to local oral tradition and modern accounts, the tragic events continue to reverberate across the river: local residents and RCMP officers occasionally receive middle-of-the-night calls reporting a mysterious gunshot echoing across the water from McMillan Island, immediately followed by the sound of a woman screaming from within the historic fort grounds.

#location
The Fort Langley waterfront and McMillan Island sit along the Fraser River in Langley, British Columbia.

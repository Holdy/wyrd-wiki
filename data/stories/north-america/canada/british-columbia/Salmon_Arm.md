#title
The Apparition in Room 117

#summary
A hotel guest in Salmon Arm experienced a dark female entity emerging from the bathroom of Room 117, a room later kept vacant due to recurring paranormal reports.

#details
While staying overnight at the Best Western Salmon Arm Inn, a guest named Donna was sitting awake while her spouse slept. Suddenly, a dark female apparition emerged from the bathroom and passed directly through her. After waking her spouse in a panic, they reported the incident to the front desk and learned that a woman named Bernadette had previously hanged herself in the bathroom. Due to ongoing reports from guests and staff, Room 117 was permanently removed from rental circulation and converted into a storage room, where maintenance workers still announce themselves before entering out of respect for the resident spirit.

#location
Room 117 is located inside the Best Western Salmon Arm Inn in Salmon Arm, British Columbia.

#title
The Haunting of the Carlin House (The Trickle Inn)

#summary
The historic Carlin House along the Trans-Canada Highway is said to be haunted by a family grandmother and other lingering paranormal energies.

#details
Built in stages starting in the late 19th century and purchased by James Carlin in 1904, the grand white and green house served as a family home and later as an inn. Throughout its history as a residence and a boarding house, owners, visitors, and professional mediums reported feeling distinct paranormal presences. In particular, mediums identified one of the persistent entities as a grandmother from the original Carlin family who once inhabited the sprawling home.

#location
The historic Carlin House (The Trickle Inn) is located at 5290 Trans-Canada Highway in Tappen, British Columbia.

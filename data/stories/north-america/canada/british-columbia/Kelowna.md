#title
The Child Spirit of Leon Avenue

#summary
During the 1918 Spanish flu outbreak in Kelowna, members of the local Chinese community reported being stalked after dark by an evil spirit in the guise of a shoeless white boy.

#details
In November 1918, as Kelowna's segregated Chinatown on Leon Avenue was gripped by a deadly wave of the Spanish flu, residents experienced a chilling visitation recorded in local newspapers of the era. According to historical accounts unearthed by local historian Bob Hayes from the *Kelowna Record*, community members reported seeing a timid, barefoot young white boy around six years old lurking near outbuildings and courts after dark. It was believed that anyone who laid eyes upon the ghostly child was afflicted with the flu and subsequently perished, marking a tragic and eerie chapter in Kelowna's lost Chinatown history.

#location
Leon Avenue (between Abbot Street and Water Street), Kelowna, British Columbia

#title
The Phantom Carriage of Guisachan House

#summary
Visitors to a historic 1891 Kelowna estate have reported hearing the phantom sound of horse hooves clip-clopping down the gravel driveway and stopping as if to drop off invisible passengers.

#details
Built in 1891, Guisachan House served as the summer residence for Lord and Lady Aberdeen on a sprawling 480-acre ranch. Over the years, visitors and staff at the historic estate have frequently heard the distinct sound of horse hooves trotting down the gravel driveway, coming to a sudden halt—as though a carriage is unloading guests—before falling silent. Today, the building operates as a landmark restaurant and park in Kelowna.

1060 Cameron Ave., Kelowna, British Columbia

#title
The Spectral Apparitions of DeHart House

#summary
The historic 1914 DeHart House in Kelowna is said to be haunted by a phantom man in a grey suit and the spirit of a young soldier appearing on a bedroom bed.

#details
Constructed in 1914 on Bowes Street by the DeHart family and later owned by Premier W.A.C. Bennett, DeHart House (also known as Brookside Manor) has accumulated numerous ghost stories over the decades. Subsequent residents and family members reported seeing an elderly man dressed in a grey suit wandering the halls. Additionally, during the Second World War, a maid witnessed a young man sitting on a bed in the house right around the exact time that one of the DeHart sons was killed in battle overseas.

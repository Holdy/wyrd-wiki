#title
The Phantom Child and Apparitions of Beban House

#summary
Built in the 1930s by a prominent timber baron family, Beban House is frequently reported to be haunted by a young Chinese boy and the apparition of a woman.

#details
Constructed in 1930 by logging magnate Frank Beban along Bowen Road, the rustic log-sided mansion has accumulated numerous ghost stories over the decades as it transitioned through daycare, office, and municipal uses. Over the years, multiple children attending daycare in the building independently drew pictures of a young Chinese boy wearing a white gown with a long braid who played with a red ball. Additionally, visitors, staff, and police officers using community office space have reported inexplicable sounds such as faucets turning on, locked doors opening, and the shadowy apparition of a woman appearing in upper windows.

#location
Beban House is located at 2290 Bowen Road in Nanaimo, British Columbia.

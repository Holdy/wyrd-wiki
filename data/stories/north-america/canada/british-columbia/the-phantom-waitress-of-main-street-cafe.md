#title
The Phantom Waitress of Main Street Cafe

#summary
Staff at Main Street Cafe experienced years of supernatural occurrences, including phantom footsteps, rogue security alarms, and self-moving chairs. A medium later concluded the spirit was a former waitress who felt overly attached to serving customers.

#details
Located on the corner of Third Street and Mackenzie Avenue, Main Street Cafe is a popular local eatery known for cozy brunches. However, for a period in its history, the staff dealt with repeated supernatural encounters. Unexplained phenomena included hearing footsteps when completely alone in the building, rogue security alarms going off without cause, and chairs shifting across the floor by themselves. Eventually, a medium was brought in to investigate, concluding that the spirit was a thin woman who believed she was the one destined to serve customers and felt jealous of the living waitstaff.

#location
Main Street Cafe, located at the corner of Third Street and Mackenzie Avenue in Revelstoke, British Columbia.

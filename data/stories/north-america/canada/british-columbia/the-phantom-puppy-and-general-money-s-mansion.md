#title
The Phantom Puppy and General Money's Mansion

#summary
Visitors and staff at a historic 1914 seaside estate in Qualicum Beach frequently encounter paranormal phenomena, including the ghostly apparition of a small dog and unexplained activity in the game room. 

#details
Built in 1914 by General Money, the Crown Mansion has long been reputed to be one of Qualicum Beach's most actively haunted properties. Hotel staff and visitors have documented numerous unexplained occurrences, particularly in the dining room and the games room. Most notably, people report hearing and seeing the phantom puppy of General Money, whose likeness appears alongside him in a historic portrait hanging in the lobby. Witnesses frequently report scampering paws, phantom footsteps, and anomalous activity such as hanging decorative crystals swaying independently of drafts or temperature shifts.

#location
Crown Mansion is located at 293 Crescent Road East in Qualicum Beach, British Columbia.

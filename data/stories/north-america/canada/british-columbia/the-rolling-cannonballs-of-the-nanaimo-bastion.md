#title
The Rolling Cannonballs of the Nanaimo Bastion

#summary
Nanaimo's iconic coastal landmark is reportedly haunted by former caretakers and phantom noises, most notably heavy cannonballs rolling across the wooden floorboards.

#details
Built in 1853 by the Hudson’s Bay Company as a fur-trade outpost and defense fortification, the octagonal wooden Bastion on Nanaimo's waterfront carries a long legacy of ghostly lore. Visitors and staff have repeatedly reported hearing heavy, mysterious thuds and the distinct sound of cannonballs rolling across the upper wooden floorboards when nobody else is upstairs. Paranormal investigators and historians attribute the activity to former 20th-century caretaker Joseph Muir and other lingering spirits watching over the historic landmark.

#location
The Nanaimo Bastion is located at 98 Front Street along the waterfront in downtown Nanaimo, British Columbia.

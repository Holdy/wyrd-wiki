#title
The Haunting of the Gilbert House

#summary
A heritage house built in 1925 in North Vancouver became the site of unexplained phantom footsteps, childlike spirit entities, and terrifying apparitions of an angry old man and a woman in black.

#details
When a family with two young boys moved into the historic Craftsman-style Gilbert House on Osborne Road in North Vancouver, they expected a peaceful renovation project. Instead, they quickly realized they were sharing their home with unseen residents. The young sons complained of being kept awake at night by a phantom boy and girl, and the mother was startled on multiple occasions by running footsteps in an empty hallway and finding her toddler son outside in the driveway despite locked doors. Previous occupants and visitors reported even more vivid encounters, including a family friend who bolted from the house in sheer terror after seeing the apparition of an angry old man on the staircase and a young woman dressed in black in the living room.

#location
The historic Gilbert House on Osborne Road in the Upper Lonsdale neighbourhood of North Vancouver, British Columbia.

#title
The Phantoms of Presentation House Theatre

#summary
Built in 1903 and later serving as a school, municipal hall, and police station, Presentation House Theatre in North Vancouver is plagued by decades of inexplicable ghostly phenomena, including phantom footsteps across locked upper floors and an entity nicknamed "Frank."

#details
Originally constructed as a schoolhouse in 1903 before transitioning into a municipal hall and police station, Presentation House Theatre on Chesterfield Avenue has a long history of municipal and supernatural activity. Over the decades, artists, staff members, and production crews working late into the night have frequently reported unexplained occurrences, such as doors slamming shut in empty rooms, lights flickering without cause, and the distinct sound of heavy footsteps pacing across the locked third-floor roof and attic when no one else is in the building. Longtime caretaker Cecil Halsey spent years monitoring the building's supernatural occurrences and even gave the theatre's primary resident spirit a name—Frank.

#location
Presentation House Theatre, located at 333 Chesterfield Avenue in North Vancouver, British Columbia.

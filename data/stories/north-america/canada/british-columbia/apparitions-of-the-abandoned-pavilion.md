#title
Apparitions of the Abandoned Pavilion

#summary
Staff and security personnel patrolling the aging corridors of a sprawling historic psychiatric institution have reported chilling encounters with shadow figures, disembodied footsteps, and phantom lights.

#details
Opened in 1913 on a 1,000-acre tract of land, Riverview Hospital served as a major mental health treatment facility for nearly a century. With many of its historic structures—such as the long-abandoned West Lawn Pavilion—sitting vacant, the grounds have generated extensive local folklore. Former janitors, night-shift security guards, and film crews working late hours have repeatedly documented unsettling paranormal activity. Witnesses have described seeing dark shadow figures darting through desolate hallways, hearing heavy footsteps echoing up empty stairwells, and experiencing sudden, inexplicable temperature drops in rooms long disconnected from power.

#location
The historic West Lawn Pavilion is located on the grounds of Riverview Hospital at 2601 Lougheed Highway in Coquitlam, British Columbia.

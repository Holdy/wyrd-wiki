#title
The Demolished House on Ardmore Drive

#summary
A once-grand residence on Ardmore Drive gained a reputation for intense paranormal activity before it was ultimately demolished because no one could be persuaded to live there.

#details
Local folklore and ghost-lore chroniclers on the Saanich Peninsula recount the tale of a particular residential property on Ardmore Drive that was plagued by persistent, inexplicable disturbances. The haunting was so pervasive and unsettling that successive occupants fled, and prospective buyers refused to move in, leading local authorities and owners to eventually tear the building down entirely.

#location
The former site of the haunted house is located along Ardmore Drive in North Saanich, British Columbia.

#title
The Phantom Tractor of Stelly’s Cross Road

#summary
Motorists passing along Stelly’s Cross Road at night have reported seeing a ghostly farmer operating a phantom tractor to tend his fields in the dead of night.

#details
Local legend on the Saanich Peninsula tells of a supernatural agricultural apparition that appears in the fields near Stelly’s Cross Road. Witnesses report seeing a glowing or shadowy farmer diligently working his land aboard a vintage tractor during the darkest hours of the night, only for the tractor and operator to vanish completely the moment someone looks away.

#location
The agricultural fields are situated along Stelly’s Cross Road in Central Saanich, British Columbia.

#title
The Spirit of Canoe Cove

#summary
The historic Stonehouse Pub is said to be haunted by the ghost of a young boy who died tragically on the property in 1940.

#details
Built in 1935 as an English manor house by British immigrant Hugh Rodd, the stone building at Canoe Cove became the site of a tragedy in 1940 when the owner's eight-year-old son was found hanging in an upstairs room. According to local lore, the boy never left. Visitors and staff have reported inexplicable phenomena over the decades, including lights turning on and off by themselves, objects being moved or thrown, and young children claiming to play with an unseen red-headed playmate upstairs who matches the exact description of the long-deceased boy.

#location
The Stonehouse Pub is located at 11800 Canoe Cove Road in North Saanich, British Columbia.

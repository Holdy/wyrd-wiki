#title
Poltergeist Activity in a Gibsons Restaurant

#summary
A teenage server working at a restaurant in Gibsons experienced unexplained phenomena, including stove burners turning on by themselves, a moving beer bottle, and a knife flying off a magnetic wall strip.

#details
Around twenty years ago, a 16-year-old high school student named Brenda took a serving job at a well-known restaurant in Gibsons. While closing up late one night and working entirely alone in the kitchen, she was startled when three burners on the large gas stove spontaneously turned on. During another late-night closing shift, she witnessed an empty beer bottle slide across the counter by itself before crashing to the floor. The most alarming incident occurred when a knife detached from the magnetic strip on the kitchen wall and flew directly at her, forcing her to dive out of the way. These unsettling poltergeist-like occurrences all took place within a span of two months while she was alone in the kitchen, after which the activity ceased entirely.

#location
A restaurant located in Gibsons, British Columbia.

#title
The Apparition Behind a Gibsons Home

#summary
A teenager looking out his bedroom window at 5:00 a.m. in Gibsons witnessed a tall, mist-like apparition shaped like a woman with a veil walking slowly across the backyard.

#details
Late one night in 2005, a 14-year-old resident named Mike was staying up late watching television in his upstairs bedroom in Gibsons. Around 5:00 a.m., he peered through the window blinds to check the dawn light and spotted a lone figure moving across his family's backyard patio. Described as a translucent white mist in the shape of a person—resembling a woman with a veil over her head and hands raised upward as if in prayer—the entity moved very slowly from right to left beneath his window toward the kitchen deck before vanishing out of sight. Shaken by the encounter, he woke his father, who dismissed it as a dream, but Mike remained certain of what he witnessed, noting that a neighbor had recently passed away undiscovered in their home nearby around the same time.

#location
A residential property with a backyard patio in Gibsons, British Columbia.

#title
The Haunting of Gibsons Pioneer Cemetery

#summary
Visitors and locals at the historic Gibsons Pioneer Cemetery have reported eerie encounters, including unexplained orbs, a ghostly photograph of a skeletal face, disembodied screams in the woods, and flying stones.

#details
Nestled at the base of Mount Elphinstone in an isolated mountainside forest, the Gibsons Pioneer Cemetery—often called "Boot Hill"—is the final resting place of the town's early founding families. Over the years, numerous paranormal accounts have emerged from the neglected graveyard. A teenager taking photographs of the cemetery later discovered unusual light orbs on his computer, followed shortly by a phantom knock at his door when no one was outside. On another occasion, a woman and her daughters visiting the cemetery at night felt they were being followed down the dark trail; a quick photo snapped before reaching the trailhead revealed orbs and a shape resembling a skeletal face. Additionally, visitors have reported hearing distant, disembodied screams echoing through the surrounding trees, and an eyewitness personally experienced a pebble flying horizontally out of open foliage to strike her ankle while walking down the path with complete silence blanketing the cemetery grounds.

#location
Gibsons Pioneer Cemetery (Mount Elphinstone Pioneer Cemetery) located in the mountainside forest at the base of Mount Elphinstone in Gibsons, British Columbia.

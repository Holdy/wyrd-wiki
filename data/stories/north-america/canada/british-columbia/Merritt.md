#title
The Ghosts of the Coldwater Hotel

#summary
The historic Coldwater Hotel in Merritt, famous for its golden dome and ties to train robber Billy Miner, is said to be haunted by unseen presences and former guests.

#details
Constructed in 1910, the Coldwater Hotel is one of Merritt's most prominent historical landmarks, instantly recognizable by its flag-topped gold dome. Beyond its history of hosting famous figures like the legendary train robber Billy Miner—who famously hid stolen securities in the dome—the hotel carries a long-standing reputation for haunting encounters. In the late 1960s, a transient man was found dead in one of the rooms, adding to the building's somber history. Guests and visitors exploring the hotel after midnight have reported hearing disembodied growls ordering them to leave, feeling unexplainable chills, and witnessing ghostly apparitions. 

#location
The Coldwater Hotel is located on Quilchena Avenue in downtown Merritt, British Columbia.

#title
The Phantom Chimes of the Baillie House

#summary
Staff and visitors at the historic Baillie House in Merritt frequently report unexplained footsteps, sightings of a mysterious woman, and a doorbell that rings out the Westminster Chimes on its own.

#details
The Baillie House is a heritage property in Merritt that currently serves as a visitor information centre. Over the decades, it has gained a strong local reputation for paranormal activity. Visitors and staff have reported hearing distinct footsteps pacing the upstairs corridors, seeing a ghostly woman moving up and down the staircase, and feeling an overwhelming sense of an unseen presence. One of the most frequently recurring phenomena is the building's doorbell ringing when no one is outside—specifically chiming the Westminster Chimes rather than its standard programmed tone. The site has been the subject of multiple overnight investigations by paranormal groups, such as BC Spirits and the Interior Paranormal Society, which have recorded unusual audio anomalies and electronic voice phenomena (EVPs).

#location
The Baillie House is located at 1951 Garcia Street in Merritt, British Columbia.

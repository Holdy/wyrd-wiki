#title
The Lady in Red of Hotel Vancouver

#summary
An elegantly dressed phantom known as the Lady in Red haunts the 14th floor and hallways of the historic Fairmont Hotel Vancouver.

#details
First opened in 1939, the Fairmont Hotel Vancouver is said to be haunted by a benevolent, stylish apparition often identified as the ghost of Jennie Pearl Cox, a Vancouver socialite who frequented the hotel's ballroom dances in the early 1940s before dying in a car accident in 1944. The spirit is frequently spotted on the 14th floor and in the ground floor lobby near guest elevator doors. Guests and staff have reported seeing her glide through disused, locked elevator doors that lead to dummy elevator shafts. In one instance, a Japanese family staying on the 14th floor called the front desk to ask if their room had been double-booked after seeing her inside, while a bellman once watched her follow guests into room 1403 before vanishing entirely.

#location
Fairmont Hotel Vancouver is located at 900 West Georgia Street in downtown Vancouver, British Columbia.

#title
The Phantoms of Gaoler's Mews

#summary
Cobblestoned Gaoler's Mews in Gastown—once the site of Vancouver's first jail and public hangings—is haunted by a woman in black and other restless spirits.

#details
Located in the heart of Gastown, Gaoler's Mews stands on ground that once held Vancouver's mid-19th-century jail and a hangman's scaffold where over forty public executions took place. Historic buildings around the cobblestone courtyard, such as the former Irish Heather pub and adjacent spaces, have long been plagued by paranormal activity. Witnesses have reported a woman dressed in black gliding down the cobblestone passageway toward Blood Alley before vanishing into thin air, believed to be the mourning widow of a condemned prisoner. Other reported phenomena include tools mysteriously relocated by unseen hands, kitchen staff hearing their names called in empty rooms, chairs in the second-story loft violently overturned overnight, and phantom footsteps echoing down wooden stairs.

#location
Gaoler's Mews is located at 12 Water Street in the Gastown neighbourhood of Vancouver, British Columbia.

#title
The Specters of Hycroft Manor

#summary
Built in 1911, the stately Hycroft Manor in Shaughnessy is reportedly haunted by seven distinct ghosts, including a World War I general and mischievous veteran spirits.

#details
Hycroft Manor was completed in 1911 for General Alexander Duncan McRae and his wife Blaunche, who hosted lavish high-society gatherings there. In 1942, the couple donated the mansion to the Canadian government to serve as a convalescent hospital for war veterans. Today, the 30-room estate is renowned as one of Vancouver's most haunted sites, home to at least seven resident spirits. Among them are the apparition of General McRae in a WWI officer's uniform, a nurse from the convalescent hospital era, and three mischievous soldier spirits dubbed "The Pranksters" who manipulate doors and lights. Staff and investigators have also reported a "Crying Man" whose loud sobbing echoes from a lower-floor room, alongside unexplained phenomena such as floating black energy orbs.

#location
Hycroft Manor is located at 1489 McRae Avenue in the Shaughnessy neighbourhood of Vancouver, British Columbia.

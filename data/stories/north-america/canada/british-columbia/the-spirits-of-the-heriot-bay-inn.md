#title
The Spirits of the Heriot Bay Inn

#summary
A historic coastal inn is reportedly haunted by multiple specters, including a white-haired woman who knits in the lounge and a bearded fisherman who wanders the upper hallways.

#details
Built over a century ago and carrying a colorful history of early pioneers, financial scandals, and rebuilding after a devastating fire, the historic Heriot Bay Inn has long been associated with paranormal activity. Staff and guests over the decades have frequently reported eerie encounters with two prominent spirits. One is a white-haired woman seen quietly knitting in the lounge, gazing out windows, or rearranging furniture in guest rooms. The other is a large, bearded man dressed in a rain slicker and fisherman's hat who appears dripping wet inside the pub and hallways before suddenly vanishing when approached.

#location
The Heriot Bay Inn located on Heriot Bay Road on Quadra Island, British Columbia, just across the strait from Campbell River.

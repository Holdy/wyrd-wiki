#title
The Ghosts of the Mount Baker Hotel

#summary
Opened on Halloween night in 1923, the Mount Baker Hotel in downtown Cranbrook is haunted by phantom giggles, moving objects, and the apparitions of two young girls associated with its original proprietor.

#details
The Mount Baker Hotel opened its doors on October 31, 1923, and over the decades, staff and guests have reported chilling paranormal encounters throughout its halls. Most infamous are the spirits of two young girls allegedly linked to the hotel's original proprietor, Theodore "Teddy" Clauson, whose old photograph used to hang in the restaurant lounge. Employees closing up for the night regularly hear the faint giggles of children in empty rooms, find water taps turning on by themselves, and watch objects move independently. A former employee even reported seeing a spectral girl in a pink dress walk directly through a closed door, while guests frequently experience unexplained footsteps, shadows, and soft rhythmic breathing in empty corridors.

#location
The Mount Baker Hotel is located at 1009 Baker Street in Cranbrook, British Columbia.

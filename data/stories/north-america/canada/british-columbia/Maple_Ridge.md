#title
The Kanaka Hat Man

#summary
Hikers and paranormal investigators along a local wooded trail have reported sightings of a phantom figure dressed in a top hat and period attire, accompanied by disembodied whispers.

#details
Local ghost hunters exploring the wooded paths along Kanaka Creek received accounts from residents about a mysterious specter known as the "Kanaka Hat Man." Witnesses report seeing a man clad in old-fashioned clothing and a top hat walking through the middle of the woods where no one should be. When investigators visited the trail with local witnesses, audio equipment captured a clear, chilling whisper saying "I see you" in an empty stretch of the forest.

#location
The Kanaka Creek Trail is located within Kanaka Creek Regional Park in Maple Ridge, British Columbia.

#title
The Spirit of Mary Charlton at the Billy Miner Pub

#summary
The historic building housing a local pub is said to be haunted by the spirit of Mary Charlton, who once ran businesses in the area alongside her husband.

#details
Local historical research into the Billy Miner Pub revealed that the property is associated with the ghost of Mary Charlton. Rather than acting as a malevolent entity, local folklore and paranormal researchers suggest that Mary’s spirit remains tethered to the location simply because she deeply loved the place and her life there during the pioneering days of the community.

#location

#title
The Victorian Haunting of Haney House

#summary
Built in 1883, the historic Haney House has become the center of local ghost lore, with visitors and paranormal investigators reporting unexplained energies and ghostly encounters.

#details
The Victorian-era Haney House, which was inhabited by the Haney family for decades and witnessed multiple family members pass away within its walls, is frequently investigated by professional paranormal teams. Organizers and investigators note that the house retains an eerie, authentic atmosphere filled with original family artifacts, where people frequently sense unseen presences, cold spots, and unexplained anomalies after dark.

#location
Haney House is located at 11612 224th Street in Maple Ridge, British Columbia.

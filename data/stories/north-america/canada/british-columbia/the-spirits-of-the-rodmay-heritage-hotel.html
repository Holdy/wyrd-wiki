The Spirits of the Rodmay Heritage Hotel

Summary

Built in 1911 in Powell River's historic Townsite, the Rodmay Heritage Hotel is haunted by multiple resident spirits, including its original matron and a phantom Chinese cook.

Details

Originally opened as the Powell River Hotel to serve workers and travelers, the historic building is known as one of the most haunted locations on the coast. Among its most prominent spirits is Mrs. McKinney, the original matron of the hotel, who is frequently seen in the Rainbow Room bar area. Another resident entity is Charlie, a former Chinese cook who reportedly died in the basement during a poker game and now haunts the lower floor restaurant. Employees and visitors have reported Charlie commenting on workers' singing voices through headphones, alongside unexplained movements of tools, stools, and lightbulbs. Other spectral figures reported in the hotel include a young German girl holding a teddy bear on the second floor, a woman in white, and a dapper man named Frank.

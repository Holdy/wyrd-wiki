#title
Apparitions of the Abandoned Pavilion

#summary
Staff and security personnel patrolling the aging corridors of a sprawling historic psychiatric institution have reported chilling encounters with shadow figures, disembodied footsteps, and phantom lights.

#details
Opened in 1913 on a 1,000-acre tract of land, Riverview Hospital served as a major mental health treatment facility for nearly a century. With many of its historic structures—such as the long-abandoned West Lawn Pavilion—sitting vacant, the grounds have generated extensive local folklore. Former janitors, night-shift security guards, and film crews working late hours have repeatedly documented unsettling paranormal activity. Witnesses have described seeing dark shadow figures darting through desolate hallways, hearing heavy footsteps echoing up empty stairwells, and experiencing sudden, inexplicable temperature drops in rooms long disconnected from power.

#location
The historic West Lawn Pavilion is located on the grounds of Riverview Hospital at 2601 Lougheed Highway in Coquitlam, British Columbia.

#title
The Whispers and Shadows of Minnekhada Lodge

#summary
Caretakers and visitors exploring a historic 1930s timber lodge nestled in the woods have reported encountering shadowy figures on the stairs, disembodied humming, and unexplained whiffs of roses.

#details
Built in the 1930s by business mogul and future B.C. Lieutenant Governor Eric Hamber, Minnekhada Lodge stands in a remote park area with a history that includes a devastating 1929 farm fire. Over the decades, staff, caretakers, and paranormal investigators have reported lingering phenomena within its timber walls. A caretaker's relative once fled in terror after spotting a dark shadow figure standing atop the staircase when the building was entirely locked and empty. Later ghost-hunting groups conducting evening investigations reported hearing a woman softly humming a few bars of a tune, alongside sudden, inexplicable scents of roses filling rooms devoid of any flowers.

#location
Minnekhada Lodge is situated at the northern end of Minnekhada Regional Park in Coquitlam, British Columbia.

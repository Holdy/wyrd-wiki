#title
The Haunting of the Revelstoke Courthouse Square

#summary
Staff working late shifts at the Revelstoke Courthouse frequently report hearing unexplained voices, crying, closing doors, and rattling chains. The surrounding land is believed to be haunted due to its history as the site of one of Revelstoke's earliest hospitals.

#details
Built between 1911 and 1913, the Revelstoke Courthouse is a majestic heritage landmark, but both the building and the surrounding square are noted as one of the town's most active haunted locations. Staff working late at night have repeatedly reported hearing eerie voices, crying, doors slamming shut, and the sound of rattling chains when no one else is present. Local folklore links some of this activity to the history of Revelstoke's first hospital, which opened in 1897 and stood nearby between the courthouse and Douglas Street before being replaced in 1902. Subsequent homes and properties built on and around the old hospital grounds have also reported frequent sightings of various apparitions.

#location
The Revelstoke Courthouse, situated between Douglas Street and Courthouse Square in Revelstoke, British Columbia.

#title
The Phantom Waitress of Main Street Cafe

#summary
Staff at Main Street Cafe experienced years of supernatural occurrences, including phantom footsteps, rogue security alarms, and self-moving chairs. A medium later concluded the spirit was a former waitress who felt overly attached to serving customers.

#details
Located on the corner of Third Street and Mackenzie Avenue, Main Street Cafe is a popular local eatery known for cozy brunches. However, for a period in its history, the staff dealt with repeated supernatural encounters. Unexplained phenomena included hearing footsteps when completely alone in the building, rogue security alarms going off without cause, and chairs shifting across the floor by themselves. Eventually, a medium was brought in to investigate, concluding that the spirit was a thin woman who believed she was the one destined to serve customers and felt jealous of the living waitstaff.

#location
Main Street Cafe, located at the corner of Third Street and Mackenzie Avenue in Revelstoke, British Columbia.

#title
The Restless Spirits Above Bocci's

#summary
A former grocery clerk renting the large apartment above Bocci's Groceteria experienced escalating paranormal activity, ranging from phantom dinner parties and footsteps to a sudden blue flash in every window.

#details
From 2000 to 2006, Brennan Storr worked at Bocci's Groceteria at the corner of Fourth Street and Victoria Road, renting the sprawling five-bedroom apartment above it from 2002 to 2004. Over time, a creeping unease took hold, leading him to lock his bedroom door every night. He began hearing sounds like clinking wine glasses, silverware, and a grand dinner party taking place in empty rooms, as well as phantom footsteps coming from the store below. Later tenants reported identical feelings of paranoia and mysterious occurrences. Years later, while standing across the street observing the building during renovations, Storr witnessed a bright blue flash of light erupt simultaneously from every upstairs window.

#location
The historic building formerly housing Bocci's Groceteria at the corner of Fourth Street and Victoria Road in Revelstoke, British Columbia.

#title
The Specter of the Big Eddy Farmhouse

#summary
A young boy living in a farmhouse in the Big Eddy area of Revelstoke repeatedly witnessed an elderly man in a black hat unhooking a pasture gate in the dead of night. The ghostly figure vanished into the morning light whenever approached.

#details
Growing up in a two-storey clapboard farmhouse in the rural Big Eddy community on the west bank of the Columbia River, a young boy named Turk Wilson and his family continually struggled with a cow that kept escaping into their yard. For weeks, a pasture gate chain was inexplicably unfastened every night. One summer night around 3:00 AM, alerted by the growling of his collies, Wilson looked out toward the gate and saw an elderly man wearing a black hat standing there and moving the chain. When Wilson gathered his nerve and stepped off the porch, the figure disappeared into the gathering dawn light, the chain lay on the ground, and the cows were nowhere in sight. The apparition was never seen again, and the gate troubles ceased completely.

#location
The historic farmhouse site in the Big Eddy community along Highway 23 on the west bank of the Columbia River in Revelstoke, British Columbia.

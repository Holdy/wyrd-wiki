#title
The Hanging Judge of Bastion Square

#summary
The historic courthouse in Bastion Square is said to be haunted by Sir Matthew Baillie Begbie, whose slender figure glides down the main staircase.

#details
Completed in 1889, the old courthouse in downtown Victoria served as a center for the judicial system during the colonial era. The building is considered one of the city's most haunted locations, with witnesses reporting a slender apparition—believed to be Victoria's infamous "Hanging Judge"—moving silently through the halls near the preserved original courtroom and prisoner's dock.

#location
The old courthouse building is situated in Bastion Square, extending from Government Street to the waterfront in Victoria, British Columbia.

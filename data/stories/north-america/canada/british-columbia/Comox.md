#title
Dancing Mary

#summary
An apparition known as "Dancing Mary," believed to be the spirit of a murdered woman, haunts Comox Hill and Comox Road as a shimmering blue orb and glowing mist.

#details
Local legend tells of a woman who was murdered by her husband, her spirit remaining tied to the area where she dances within a glowing blue mist. Over the decades, travelers and locals have reported harrowing encounters with the specter. During World War I in 1914 and later through World War II, cyclists and soldiers traversing Comox Hill and Comox Road reported riding directly through the shimmering blue light, experiencing an intense, bone-chilling cold that left a lasting impression.

#location
Comox Hill along Comox Road in Comox, British Columbia.

#title
Ian of The Manor

#summary
A playful and noisy spirit named Ian haunts "The Manor," a historic Comox residence built in 1938.

#details
Originally built in 1938 by Sir Ernest Petter and dubbed "The Fort," the historic estate served as a wartime haven for evacuated English children before transitioning into an extended care facility and college administrative offices. Throughout its varied history, staff and visitors have reported hearing unexplained moans, heavy footsteps, and slamming doors. During a college staff party, occupants used a Ouija board to contact the unseen presence, who identified himself as Ian. Today, an empty room within the building is designated as "Ian's Room" in honor of the harmless yet mischievous resident ghost.

#location
"The Manor" (formerly known as "The Fort") located in Comox, British Columbia.

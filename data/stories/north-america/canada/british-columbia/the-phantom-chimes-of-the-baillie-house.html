The Phantom Chimes of the Baillie House

Summary

Staff and visitors at the historic Baillie House in Merritt frequently report unexplained footsteps, sightings of a mysterious woman, and a doorbell that rings out the Westminster Chimes on its own.

Details

The Baillie House is a heritage property in Merritt that currently serves as a visitor information centre. Over the decades, it has gained a strong local reputation for paranormal activity. Visitors and staff have reported hearing distinct footsteps pacing the upstairs corridors, seeing a ghostly woman moving up and down the staircase, and feeling an overwhelming sense of an unseen presence. One of the most frequently recurring phenomena is the building's doorbell ringing when no one is outside—specifically chiming the Westminster Chimes rather than its standard programmed tone. The site has been the subject of multiple overnight investigations by paranormal groups, such as BC Spirits and the Interior Paranormal Society, which have recorded unusual audio anomalies and electronic voice phenomena (EVPs).

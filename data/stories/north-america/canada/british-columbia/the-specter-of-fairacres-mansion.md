#title
The Specter of Fairacres Mansion

#summary
The historic Fairacres Mansion in Deer Lake Park is said to be haunted by its former mistress, Grace Ceperley, and other restless spirits from its turbulent past.

#details
Built in 1911 by Henry and Grace Ceperley, Fairacres Mansion eventually became home to the Burnaby Art Gallery after decades of hosting private tenants, a tuberculosis ward, Benedictine monks, and a notorious mid-century cult. Over the years, staff and visitors have reported numerous paranormal occurrences. The ghost of Grace Ceperley has been seen floating up the staircases and walking the galleries in flowing colored dresses while trailing the scent of perfume. Other phenomena include mysterious footsteps, windows opening and closing on the top floor, a basement poltergeist that throws tools, and sightings of a kneeling monk.

#location
Fairacres Mansion (now the Burnaby Art Gallery) is located at 6344 Deer Lake Ave in Burnaby, British Columbia.

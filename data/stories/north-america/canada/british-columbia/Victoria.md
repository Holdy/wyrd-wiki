#title
The Hanging Judge of Bastion Square

#summary
The historic courthouse in Bastion Square is said to be haunted by Sir Matthew Baillie Begbie, whose slender figure glides down the main staircase.

#details
Completed in 1889, the old courthouse in downtown Victoria served as a center for the judicial system during the colonial era. The building is considered one of the city's most haunted locations, with witnesses reporting a slender apparition—believed to be Victoria's infamous "Hanging Judge"—moving silently through the halls near the preserved original courtroom and prisoner's dock.

#location
The old courthouse building is situated in Bastion Square, extending from Government Street to the waterfront in Victoria, British Columbia.

#title
The Phantoms of Craigdarroch Castle

#summary
Built by a wealthy coal baron in the late 19th century, Craigdarroch Castle is reported to be haunted by a woman in white, running children, and phantom piano music.

#details
Visitors and staff at the historic Scottish baronial mansion have frequently reported paranormal experiences, including the apparition of a woman standing alone in the ballroom, the echoing sounds of crying children, and mysterious piano melodies playing in rooms without any instruments.

#location
Craigdarroch Castle is located just off Fort Street on Joan Crescent in Victoria, British Columbia.

#title
The Wandering Nuns of St. Ann's Academy

#summary
St. Ann's Academy is reported to be haunted by the spirits of nuns wandering the convent grounds and the ghost of famous Canadian painter Emily Carr.

#details
Operating as a 19th-century Catholic school and convent before closing in 1974, the historic site has since generated numerous ghost stories after the early graves on the property were relocated. Staff and visitors have reported seeing phantom nuns roaming the gardens and halls, as well as sightings of renowned artist Emily Carr.

#location
St. Ann's Academy is located at 835 Humboldt Street in Victoria, British Columbia.

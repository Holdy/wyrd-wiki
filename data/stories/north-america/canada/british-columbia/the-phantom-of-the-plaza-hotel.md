#title
The Phantom of the Plaza Hotel

#summary
Former co-owner and guests of a historic downtown Kamloops hotel have frequently reported encountering the apparition of a mysterious woman and experiencing inexplicable mechanical mishaps.

#details
Operating since the early 20th century, the Plaza Hotel in downtown Kamloops has earned a lasting reputation for paranormal activity. Former co-owner Tina Lange recounted multiple late-night experiences where she caught glimpses out of the corner of her eye of a woman walking around corners and ascending the grand wooden stairwells, only to find no one there. Other eerie occurrences in the heritage building included heavy metal doors closing entirely on their own and coffee maker baskets violently ejecting themselves without human touch. Guests have also specifically chosen to stay at the hotel due to its widespread reputation on lists of purported haunted sites.

#location
The Plaza Hotel is located at the intersection of Victoria Street and Fourth Avenue in downtown Kamloops, British Columbia.

#title
Poltergeist Activity in a Gibsons Restaurant

#summary
A teenage server working at a restaurant in Gibsons experienced unexplained phenomena, including stove burners turning on by themselves, a moving beer bottle, and a knife flying off a magnetic wall strip.

#details
Around twenty years ago, a 16-year-old high school student named Brenda took a serving job at a well-known restaurant in Gibsons. While closing up late one night and working entirely alone in the kitchen, she was startled when three burners on the large gas stove spontaneously turned on. During another late-night closing shift, she witnessed an empty beer bottle slide across the counter by itself before crashing to the floor. The most alarming incident occurred when a knife detached from the magnetic strip on the kitchen wall and flew directly at her, forcing her to dive out of the way. These unsettling poltergeist-like occurrences all took place within a span of two months while she was alone in the kitchen, after which the activity ceased entirely.

#location
A restaurant located in Gibsons, British Columbia.

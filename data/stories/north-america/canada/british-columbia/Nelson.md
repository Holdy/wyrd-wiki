#title
Isabella of Wait's News

#summary
Employees at a downtown Nelson newsstand routinely experience unexplained phenomena attributed to the ghost of a young librarian named Isabella.

#details
Since around 2010, the owner and staff of Wait's News have kept a logbook documenting strange occurrences in the shop. They attribute the activity to Isabella, believed to be the spirit of a 23-year-old librarian who once worked in the area. Staff members have reported items moving on their own, such as a half-full case of soda appearing neatly in the middle of the bathroom floor without any preceding noise, as well as recurring sightings of Isabella near the stairs and a gentleman in a top hat standing near the back freezers.

#location
Wait's News is located on Baker Street in downtown Nelson, British Columbia.

#title
The Gentleman of Room 335

#summary
Guests and staff at a historic downtown hotel frequently report encountering benevolent caretaker spirits, including a bowler-hatted gentleman and an auditory warning in the basement.

#details
Built in 1898, the Hume Hotel has long been associated with paranormal reports. A guest once attempted to check out immediately after checking in because she encountered a well-dressed gentleman wearing a bowler hat sitting on the edge of her bed who politely tipped his hat and smiled at her, unaware he was a spirit. During subsequent paranormal investigations by local researchers in the basement near Ward Creek, recording equipment captured an otherworldly voice whispering a clear warning: "Careful."

#location
The Hume Hotel is located at 422 Vernon Street in Nelson, British Columbia.

The Phantom Projectionist of Vernon Towne Cinema

Summary

Moviegoers and staff at the historic Vernon Towne Cinema frequently experience paranormal phenomena, including phantom hair-pulling, disembodied voices, and sightings of a former projectionist known as Old Lloyd.

Details

Patrons of the vintage Vernon Towne Cinema in Vernon have long shared accounts of unexplained occurrences within the auditorium. Visitors have reported startling sensations such as having their hair pulled while seated in the dark, while staff members working after hours have heard mysterious voices echoing through empty halls. The most famous spirit associated with the theater is believed to be the ghost of a deceased projectionist dubbed "Old Lloyd," whose spectral form is said to wander the aisles.

#title
The Ghosts of Cranbrook City Hall

#summary
Cranbrook City Hall's basement, which operated as a municipal jail for over fifty years and witnessed multiple tragic deaths, is haunted by inexplicable office noises and phantom figures of children and Victorian-dressed women.

#details
Cranbrook City Hall, particularly its windowless basement that housed the municipal jail from 1911 onwards, is widely regarded as a hotbed of paranormal activity. Staff working late in the building frequently report hearing mysterious office-type hubbub, low murmurs, and thuds when no one else is around. On occasion, employees catching glimpses into hallways have spotted a young girl darting around a corner or a lady in a Victorian outfit floating down the corridors. Historians attribute these lingering presences to the half-century of despair experienced by the criminals, addicts, and the condemned who passed through the jail cells, as well as at least four recorded deaths in the basement—including suicides by hanging in the 1950s.

#location
Cranbrook City Hall is located at 40 Cranbrook Street North in Cranbrook, British Columbia.

#title
The Ghosts of the Mount Baker Hotel

#summary
Opened on Halloween night in 1923, the Mount Baker Hotel in downtown Cranbrook is haunted by phantom giggles, moving objects, and the apparitions of two young girls associated with its original proprietor.

#details
The Mount Baker Hotel opened its doors on October 31, 1923, and over the decades, staff and guests have reported chilling paranormal encounters throughout its halls. Most infamous are the spirits of two young girls allegedly linked to the hotel's original proprietor, Theodore "Teddy" Clauson, whose old photograph used to hang in the restaurant lounge. Employees closing up for the night regularly hear the faint giggles of children in empty rooms, find water taps turning on by themselves, and watch objects move independently. A former employee even reported seeing a spectral girl in a pink dress walk directly through a closed door, while guests frequently experience unexplained footsteps, shadows, and soft rhythmic breathing in empty corridors.

#location
The Mount Baker Hotel is located at 1009 Baker Street in Cranbrook, British Columbia.

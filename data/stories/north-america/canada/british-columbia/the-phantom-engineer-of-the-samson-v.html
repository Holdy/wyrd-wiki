The Phantom Engineer of the Samson V

Summary

The historic sternwheeler Samson V moored in New Westminster is haunted by Frank Creeden, a chief engineer who suffered a fatal heart attack in the engine room in 1942.

Details

The Samson V is a historic snag boat that served on the Fraser River removing logs and obstacles. In October 2022, paranormal investigator Greg Mansfield witnessed a grey, transparent shadow figure moving swiftly through the vessel's galley, appearing clearly as a man wearing a thick sweater and cropped hair. This apparition is widely believed to be Frank Creeden, the ship’s chief engineer who died of a heart attack in the engine room in 1942. Staff and visitors over the decades have repeatedly reported the full-bodied apparition of the sailor brushing past them before vanishing into thin air.

#title
The Blue Lady of Panorama Ridge

#summary
Several children walking toward a local school on a snowy evening encountered a glowing, floating woman made of various shades of blue.

#details
On a snowy January twilight, four children running down a sidewalk toward Colebrook Elementary School encountered a strange apparition referred to as the "blue lady." Hovering a few feet off the ground near a neighborhood intersection, the entity possessed long hair, a flowing nightgown reaching her ankles, and an oily, soap-bubble appearance surrounded by a distinct blue halo. Making muffled noises, she drifted around the children before vanishing instantly. Moments later, a vehicle skidded out of the nearby school parking lot down the icy hill, leading the children and their families to believe the mysterious specter had intervened to slow them down and prevent them from being struck by the car.

#location
Near the intersection of 55th Avenue and 125A Street by Joe Brown Park in the Panorama Ridge neighborhood of Surrey, British Columbia.

#title
The Haunting of Harvie Road

#summary
A split-level home built near an old mill pond in Port Kells is plagued by multiple aggressive and silent apparitions, including an indigenous woman and a specter dropping from the ceiling.

#details
Constructed in 1954 near a former shingle mill pond and historical Katzie campsite in the Port Kells neighborhood, a residential house became the site of numerous unsettling paranormal encounters across successive families. Residents and neighbors frequently reported the apparition of a young indigenous woman standing with folded arms at the foot of a bedroom bed facing the pond, remaining solid until approached. Other inhabitants witnessed a white, smoky figure drifting across the upstairs landing, and a phantom man leaning against a bathroom door smoking a cigarette—leaving fresh ashes behind in the bathtub. Most terrifyingly, a young girl staying in the guest room experienced a hostile visitation from a terrified woman in a purple dress who screamed as she fell or dropped down from the ceiling onto the bed in a freezing cold room.

#location
A house built in 1954 along Harvie Road in the Port Kells neighborhood of northeastern Surrey, British Columbia.

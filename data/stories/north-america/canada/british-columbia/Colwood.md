#title
The Ghost of Annabelle at Hatley Castle

#summary
A young parlor maid named Annabelle is said to haunt Hatley Castle, where she weeps in the halls and plunges from an upper-floor window after a devastating betrayal.

#details
During the years Hatley Castle functioned as a training academy, students and staff occasionally spotted a young woman wandering the corridors before mysteriously vanishing through doors or windows. On other occasions, she was heard weeping in a specific room before drifting out toward the sea, while a nearby window would open and close on its own. Legend identifies this spirit as Annabelle, a young parlor maid who had won the affections of Laura Dunsmuir. When Annabelle planned to marry, Laura generously offered to pay for the wedding, but the groom abandoned her at the altar after Annabelle discovered he was already married with children. Devastated by the betrayal, she returned to Hatley Castle and leaped to her death from one of the upper windows.

#location
Hatley Castle at Royal Roads University, located at 2005 Sooke Road in Colwood, British Columbia.

#title
The Haunting Presence of Laura Dunsmuir

#summary
Cadets and visitors at Hatley Castle frequently report encounters with the ghost of Laura Dunsmuir, who wanders the halls and pulls at the bedding of those sleeping in her former home.

#details
Built between 1908 and 1909 for coal baron and politician James Dunsmuir and his wife Laura, Hatley Castle later served as Royal Roads Military College. After the Department of National Defense acquired the property, military cadets living in the castle dormitories began experiencing eerie phenomena. Students studying late at night reported sudden drops in temperature akin to freezing cobwebs, accompanied by the apparition of a stern elderly woman standing by their beds or tugging at their blankets. One cadet even awoke to find the female spirit physically gripping his legs before vanishing into thin air. Many believe this spirit is Laura Dunsmuir, still keeping a restless watch over the estate.

#title
The Travelling Nanny of Hatley Castle

#summary
A family touring Hatley Castle inadvertently attracted the spirit of a historic nanny who followed them home, broadcasting phantom cries over an unplugged baby monitor.

#details
In 2013, a family visited Hatley Castle for a tour, experiencing an entirely normal and uneventful afternoon. However, the night after returning to their home, the parents were abruptly awakened by the sound of a baby crying. Upon checking their daughter's room, they found her sleeping soundly. The crying occurred a second time, prompting the mother to trace the sound directly to a baby monitor in their bedroom—to their horror, the monitor was completely unplugged and contained no batteries. Following additional unexplained incidents such as falling boxes at the mother's workplace, the family consulted a psychic who revealed that the ghost of a former castle nanny had attached herself to them and followed them home from Colwood before eventually moving on.

#title
The Phantoms of Gordon Street

#summary
A three-story nursing home in Sussex was plagued by violent poltergeist activity, including doors that slammed shut by themselves and an unplugged alarm clock that shrieked without power.

#details
While staying with his grandparents who operated a nursing home in a large three-story house, a young boy named Carter experienced intense paranormal disturbances that frequently chased him out of rooms in the middle of the night. During one terrifying night in an upstairs suite, he and his brother watched as the bedroom door swung open and slammed shut on its own while an alarm clock began making strange screeching noises and glowing. When they unplugged it, the clock continued to function with its display lit and screeching loudly. Upon opening the back panel, they discovered it contained no batteries and had no power source whatsoever, prompting the door to swing open again and sending the brothers fleeing to sleep outside in a motor home.

#location
The three-story former nursing home located on the corner of Gordon Street and Church Avenue in Sussex, New Brunswick.

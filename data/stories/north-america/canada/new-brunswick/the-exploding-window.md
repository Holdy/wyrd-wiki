#title
The Exploding Window

#summary
A young boy who tragically fell to his death from a second-floor window on Old Shediac Road is said to haunt the home as his window mysteriously shatters outward every year on the anniversary of his death.

#details
A family living in a house along the Old Shediac Road between Moncton and Shediac had a young son with a playroom on the second floor. The boy often knocked on the fixed, non-opening window to catch the attention of people outside in the front yard. One day, while knocking for attention, he lost his footing, crashed through the glass, and fell to his death on the lawn below. Following the tragedy, the family repeatedly replaced the window rather than removing it, as every year on the exact day and time of his fatal fall, the window shatters outward on its own.

#location
Along the Old Shediac Road between Moncton and Shediac, New Brunswick.

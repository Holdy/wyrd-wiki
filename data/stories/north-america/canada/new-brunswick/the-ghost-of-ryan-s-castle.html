    The Ghost of Ryan's Castle

    Summary

    Built by an eccentric millionaire dentist in the late 19th century, Ryan's Castle overlooking Darling's Lake in Hampton became the center of enduring local legends about restless spirits guarding a hidden fortune.

    Details

    Dr. John James Ryan was an extraordinarily wealthy and eccentric dentist who catered to European royalty in Paris before returning to New Brunswick to build a lavish summer mansion known as "Ryan's Castle" overlooking Darling's Lake in Hampton. The estate was modeled after a French princess's home and featured opulent imported furnishings, stained glass, a private tower, and grounds altered to fit his exact whims. Local folklore tied the family to the paranormal, noting that Dr. Ryan had grown up with a ghostly mother who caused doors to slam and objects to fly. After Dr. Ryan died in 1934 following years of declining fortune, locals whispered that he, too, never left the property. Generations of residents maintained that his ghost—and his mother's—remained behind to guard a rumored hidden fortune of diamonds buried somewhere on the estate grounds, long after the grand mansion mysteriously burned down in 1963.

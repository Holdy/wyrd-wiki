The Phantom Fire and the Pirate of Hartland Island

Summary

Residents near Hartland frequently witnessed a terrifying phantom fire engulfing a barn on the river island below the town, where a sunken-cheeked pirate spirit guarded hidden treasure. Whenever onlookers approached to investigate, they were paralyzed by the cold touch of the apparition.

Details

According to a historical newspaper clipping from the Hartland Advertiser preserved in historian William Ganong’s collection, locals were regularly startled by the sight of a massive barn fully ablaze on an island in the Saint John River just below Hartland. Yet, every time daylight broke, the structure stood completely undamaged. Traditional accounts explained that the spectral fire signaled that pirates were on the island counting their hidden gold. Anyone who ventured close to investigate found themselves struck with utter terror and paralyzed when confronted by a ragged, sunken-cheeked pirate who laid a shrunken, freezing hand upon them.

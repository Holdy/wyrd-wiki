#title
The Ghost of the Grand-Anse Schoolhouse

#summary
A family residing in a converted old school building near Grand-Anse experienced repeated encounters with a protective ghostly little girl.

#details
When a family lived in the old school building near the Dugas Office in Grand-Anse, they discovered the property was shared by a supernatural presence. A young ghostly little girl frequently appeared and disappeared around the house. Rather than being malicious, she appeared to watch over the children and the home. On one occasion, after the narrator's brother glued paper to a heater to create a toy village, the phantom girl woke the narrator up at dusk to ensure the dangerous paper was removed before it could catch fire. She also shocked the children's mother when she unexpectedly materialized right in front of her.

#location
The old school building near the Dugas Office in Grand-Anse, Rivière-du-Nord, New Brunswick.

#title
The Phantom Ship of Baie des Chaleurs

#summary
A mysterious burning ship frequently haunts the waters off the shores of Grand Anse in Baie des Chaleurs, often preceding severe maritime storms and tragedies.

#details
For generations, coastal residents and mariners along Baie des Chaleurs have reported sightings of a terrifying burning phantom ship. In 1875 and again five years later, a massive wall of fire resembling a blazing vessel appeared directly off the shores of Grand Anse, burning on the water for over two hours. According to local folklore, the apparition is tied to various historical tragedies, including a 16th-century Portuguese ship attacked by the British, the 1838 wreck of the British vessel Lady Colbourne, or a cursed pirate ship. Witnesses have described seeing sailors and figures running across the burning deck. Over the decades, sightings of the fiery phantom have frequently coincided with or preceded violent storms and fatal boating accidents in the bay.

#location
The coastal waters off the shores of Grand Anse in Baie des Chaleurs, Rivière-du-Nord, New Brunswick.

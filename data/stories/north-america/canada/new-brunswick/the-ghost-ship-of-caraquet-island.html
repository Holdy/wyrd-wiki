The Ghost Ship of Caraquet Island

Summary

A burning French refugee ship attacked by pirates during the 1757 deportation era repeatedly reappears off Caraquet Island as a spectral apparition accompanied by cries and moans.

Details

According to local tradition recounted in Caraquet, during the autumn raids of 1757 along the Baie des Chaleurs, pirates seized a vessel carrying Acadian refugees and burned it in the open sea off the coast of Caraquet Island. Survivors who made it to shore were terrified by the ghostly sight of the burning ship accompanied by echoing screams and groans. Locals note that the phantom vessel returns periodically, with documented sightings including an early morning appearance recorded near the Oyster Eco-Museum in Caraquet.

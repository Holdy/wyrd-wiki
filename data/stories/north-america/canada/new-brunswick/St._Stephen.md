#title
The Phantoms of the Todd Mansion

#summary
The historic Second Empire Victorian mansion on Union Street in St. Stephen is a local landmark long whispered to be haunted by phantom footsteps and past residents.

#details
Constructed between 1890 and 1895 for the prominent Todd family, the Todd Mansion is an elaborate Second Empire style residence featuring a mansard roof, intricate wrought-iron cresting, and a central tower. Over the decades, as the estate passed through subsequent owners and managers—such as H.N. Turner—local folklore and community accounts began to link the stately home to supernatural occurrences. Visitors and residents over the years have reported hearing inexplicable footsteps echoing across empty upper floors, experiencing sudden pockets of freezing air in closed rooms, and catching fleeting glimpses of shadow figures near the mansion's distinctive tower.

#location
Todd Mansion is located at 45 Union Street in St. Stephen, New Brunswick.

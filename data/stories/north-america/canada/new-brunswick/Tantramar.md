#title
The Haunting of the Dorchester Jail Double Hanging

#summary
The historic Dorchester Jail is plagued by phantom noises, shadow figures, and electrical anomalies linked to the tragic 1936 double hanging of the Bannister brothers.

#details
Built in 1875, the Dorchester Jail served as a prison until 1998 before being converted into a bed and breakfast. On September 23, 1936, brothers Arthur (17) and Daniel (20) Bannister—manipulated by their mother into a botched kidnapping—were hanged together on a single rope in an execution that took 21 minutes. Their bodies were buried in unmarked graves on the property. Current owners and paranormal investigators have reported frequent hauntings on the prison's "Dark Side" beneath death row, including shadowy figures peering from cells, phantom doors slamming, invisible touches, and electrical surges occurring precisely at the time of the brothers' execution.

#location
The Dorchester Jail located at 498 Main Street in Dorchester, New Brunswick.

#title
The Mourning Mother of Anchorage House

#summary
Anchorage House is haunted by the phantom footsteps of a heartbroken mother eternally waiting for her two sons who were killed in World War II.

#details
Before becoming part of Mount Allison University's campus offices, Anchorage House was home to Captain Ronald Vivian Bennett and his family. His first wife, Elva Bennett, frequently watched her young sons, Edwin and Henry, race up and down the stairs. Tragically, both sons were killed in action just days apart during the Normandy campaign in August 1944. Devastated by the news, Elva died months later. Today, staff and visitors in Anchorage House report hearing distinct, phantom footsteps running up and down the stairs where she once watched her boys play.

#location
Anchorage House at Mount Allison University in Sackville, New Brunswick.

#title
The Phantom Voice and Push of Hart Hall

#summary
The ghost of voice professor Ethel Peake is said to haunt Hart Hall, manifesting through strange musical sounds, moving objects, and a terrifying encounter where a student was pushed down the stairs.

#details
Built in 1910, Hart Hall at Mount Allison University is said to be haunted by former voice professor Ethel Peake, who passed away in 1954. Legend and accounts note that during her funeral, an unexplained voice sang two octaves above the organ. Over the decades, students and faculty have reported doors closing on their own, objects shifting, and phantom footsteps. In 2012, a student exploring Hart Hall at night reported feeling an invisible force push him backward down a flight of stairs, resulting in a broken leg and concussion.

#location
Hart Hall at Mount Allison University on the campus in Sackville, New Brunswick.

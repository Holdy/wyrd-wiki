#title
The Phantom Fireship of Heron Island

#summary
A burning, three-masted ghostly vessel frequently appears on Chaleur Bay near Heron Island, accompanied by chilling paranormal encounters on the shore. Witnesses have reported seeing the phantom ship and even encountering legless apparition sailors near farmhouses on the island.

#details
According to local folklore, the legend stems from a fateful expedition in 1501 when a Portuguese captain landed on Heron Island in Chaleur Bay. When he returned on a second voyage to capture local Mi'kmaq people for the slave trade, he was killed by the locals. The following year, his brother arrived seeking vengeance, but his ship was attacked and caught fire, with the crew plunging into the waters and swearing to haunt the bay for a thousand years. Ever since, residents and early settlers—most notably the Pettigrew family on Heron Island—have reported sightings of a flaming three-masted ship gliding across the north side of the island during full moons. In 1878, Mrs. Pettigrew famously reported being approached at dusk by the ghostly figure of a legless sailor at the corner of her farmhouse asking for help with his burns.

#location
Heron Island is located in Chaleur Bay, approximately 4 kilometers off the coast of New Mills within the municipality of Heron Bay, New Brunswick.

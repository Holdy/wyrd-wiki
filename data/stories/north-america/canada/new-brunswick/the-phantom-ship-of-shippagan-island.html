    The Phantom Ship of Shippagan Island

    Summary

    A spectral, flaming vessel known as the John Craig Light is frequently sighted patrolling the dark waters off Shippagan Island, acting as an eerie warning of approaching storms.

    Details

    For over two centuries, coastal residents and fishermen around Shippagan Island and Chaleur Bay have reported witnessing a phantom full-rigged ship engulfed in supernatural red and orange flames. Local tradition links this fiery apparition to the John Craig, a barque that wrecked off the shores of Shippagan Island near the turn of the 19th century, resulting in the drowning of all crew members save for a lone cabin boy who perished upon reaching land. Known regionally as le feu du mauvais temps or the John Craig Light, the blazing ghost ship is said to materialize across all seasons, moving at impossible speeds and vanishing into glowing embers just above the waterline before major storms or disasters strike the coast.

#title
Science East Haunted by County Jail Past

#summary
Staff and visitors at Science East in Fredericton report lingering paranormal activity and eerie phenomena inside the 170-year-old granite building that previously served as the York County Jail.

#details
Operating as an interactive science centre since 1998 inside the former York County Jail, employees have frequently reported unexplained spooky occurrences connected to the building's dark penal history. Historically, crowds would gather outside the courtyard walls to witness executions, and prisoners attempted desperate escapes—evidenced by hacksaw blades and tools later discovered hidden behind cell sinks and window frames during renovations. Today, staff and visitors continue to report ghostly sightings and strange phenomena within the historic granite structure.

#location
Science East is located at 575 Queen Street in Fredericton, New Brunswick.

#title
The Residence Ghost

#summary
A friendly spectral woman believed to be original owner Mae Boyce haunts a historic Victorian mansion on Waterloo Row, where she has historically tidied rooms, tucked in university students, and directed residents to a hidden kitchen safe.

#details
Built in 1913 by Mae and Walter Boyce, Boyce House was later converted into a student residence for the University of New Brunswick in the 1960s. Over the decades, multiple student residents and staff experienced encounters with a benevolent grey lady who roamed the halls, played the piano to empty rooms, and occasionally cleaned apartments or tucked sleeping students into bed. In one famous early encounter, a residence Don commanded the ghost, who directed him to uncover a hidden safe behind wall paneling in the kitchen. Though the safe was ultimately empty, the gentle haunting became a celebrated piece of local folklore.

#location
Boyce House is located at 467 Waterloo Row in Fredericton, New Brunswick.

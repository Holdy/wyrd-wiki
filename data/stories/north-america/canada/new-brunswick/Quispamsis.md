#title
Grandma Ghost

#summary
In a residential home in Quispamsis, a family and their dog experienced a lingering spiritual visitation from their late grandmother, Theresa, who had passed away a few years prior.

#details
In the late 1970s, a family living in Quispamsis shared their home with 72-year-old Theresa, a beloved grandmother of Lebanese descent known for her warm personality and cooking. After Theresa suffered her third heart attack and passed away in May 1979, her daughter and granddaughter experienced a distinct paranormal visitation in 1982. While going about their normal Saturday routine, their family dog suddenly became agitated and started barking frantically in the living room. The dog then bolted down the hallway into the spare bedroom that Theresa had occupied when she was alive. Jumping onto the bed, the dog stared and barked intently at the wall directly over the headboard, much like the famous scene in the horror film *Poltergeist*. After barking for roughly ten seconds, the dog stopped, looked at the bewildered mother and daughter, and calmly walked out of the room as if nothing had happened. Recognizing the familiar energy, the family took comfort in the belief that Theresa's spirit had simply returned to check in on them.

#location
A residential house in Quispamsis, New Brunswick.

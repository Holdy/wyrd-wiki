#title
The Dungarvon Whooper

#summary
A young lumber camp cook was murdered for his money along a remote river, and his restless ghost haunted the forest with terrifying screams until a priest laid the spirit to rest.

#details
In the 19th century at a logging camp along the Dungarvon River, a young Irish cook named Ryan was murdered by his boss for the savings he carried in his money belt. After hastily burying the cook in the frozen forest, the remaining lumberjacks were kept awake all night by unearthly, piercing whoops and screams echoing through the trees, forcing the terrified crew to abandon the camp. The haunting continued for years until a Catholic priest visited the gravesite to offer prayers and perform an exorcism, finally bringing peace to the troubled woods.

#location
A former lumber camp site and spring along the Dungarvon River in the Miramichi Highlands of central New Brunswick.

#title
The Headless Nun of French Fort Cove

#summary
An 18th-century French nun was brutally decapitated while traveling through the woods, and her headless ghost has haunted the area ever since, eternally searching for her missing head.

#details
Dating back to the mid-18th century during the colonial conflicts in the region, the legend centers on Sister Marie Inconnue, a French nun who served local settlements. According to lore, she was beheaded—either by sailors attempting to force her to reveal the location of hidden treasure or by a deranged trapper wandering the forest. Her head was never recovered, and her spectral, headless form has been reported roaming the wooded trails of the cove for over two centuries, striking fear into travelers and locals.

#location
French Fort Cove in Nordin, now part of the City of Miramichi, New Brunswick.

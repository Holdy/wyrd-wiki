#title
The Spectre of Keenan Bridge

#summary
Travelers and local residents in the Johnville and Bath region have long reported strange paranormal phenomena and a lingering spirit haunting the Keenan Bridge.

#details
In the rural communities surrounding Johnville and Bath within the Saint John River Valley, local folklore preserves eerie accounts of a haunting centered around the Keenan Bridge. Documented through decades of regional oral history collection in Carleton County, the legend describes chilling encounters and an unexplained presence that travelers and locals experienced while crossing or lingering near the bridge after dark.

#location
The Keenan Bridge in the Johnville and Bath area of Carleton North, New Brunswick.

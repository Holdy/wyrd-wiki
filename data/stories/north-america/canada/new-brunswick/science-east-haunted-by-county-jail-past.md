#title
Science East Haunted by County Jail Past

#summary
Staff and visitors at Science East in Fredericton report lingering paranormal activity and eerie phenomena inside the 170-year-old granite building that previously served as the York County Jail.

#details
Operating as an interactive science centre since 1998 inside the former York County Jail, employees have frequently reported unexplained spooky occurrences connected to the building's dark penal history. Historically, crowds would gather outside the courtyard walls to witness executions, and prisoners attempted desperate escapes—evidenced by hacksaw blades and tools later discovered hidden behind cell sinks and window frames during renovations. Today, staff and visitors continue to report ghostly sightings and strange phenomena within the historic granite structure.

#location
Science East is located at 575 Queen Street in Fredericton, New Brunswick.

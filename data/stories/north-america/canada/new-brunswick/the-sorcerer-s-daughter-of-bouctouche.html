The Sorcerer's Daughter of Bouctouche

Summary

Marguerite "Old Dollar," born in Bouctouche before settling in Prince Edward Island, carried a terrifying family legacy of witchcraft inherited from her sorcerer father who allegedly bargained with the devil.

Details

Marguerite Dollar was originally from Bouctouche, New Brunswick, before her family relocated to Tignish in the 1830s. In Bouctouche, her father was famously reputed to be a sorcerer, with local legends claiming he had bartered the souls of seven descendants to the devil in exchange for magical powers. Marguerite—known locally as "La Vieille Dollar" or Old Dollar—inherited this dark reputation, with villagers whispering of her ability to shapeshift into animals and cast hexes. One prominent local legend tells of a curse she allegedly placed on a neighbor's baby after her daughter's marriage proposal was rejected by the boy's family. When the baby fell violently ill, the family tried to break the curse by burning the infant's shirt in the oven; shortly afterward, Old Dollar died suddenly in her home, her skin covered in mysterious blisters.

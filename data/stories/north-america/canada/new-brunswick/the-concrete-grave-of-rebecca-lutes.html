The Concrete Grave of Rebecca Lutes

Summary

A roadside burial site on Gorge Road holds the grave of a 19th-century teenager whose resting place became the center of dark folklore about a condemned witch.

Details

Located along Gorge Road near an old quarry and cement factory, a small patch of concrete covers the historic Lutes family plot. According to local legend, the site holds the grave of a young woman named Rebecca who was accused of witchcraft and hanged in the 1800s, with concrete poured over her remains to prevent her from returning from the dead. Although local historians have clarified that Rebecca Lutes was a real teenager who likely died of tuberculosis and whose family capped the gravesite in concrete simply to protect it from being accidentally plowed over, motorists and ghost hunters continue to report phantom lights, unexplained car trouble, and an eerie presence along the roadside on moonless nights.

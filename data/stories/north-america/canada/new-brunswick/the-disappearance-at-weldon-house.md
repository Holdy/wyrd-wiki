#title
The Disappearance at Weldon House

#summary
A Moncton bar owner vanished without a trace after staying at Shediac's finest hotel, inspiring a chilling local legend and cautionary folklore song.

#details
On a dark and stormy night, a Moncton bar owner named Timothy McCarthy traveled to the Weldon House, renowned as the finest hotel in Shediac, and was never seen alive again. When a lone reporter attempted to investigate his disappearance, he encountered a web of evasive locals and conflicting stories, with suspicion ultimately centering around an enigmatic 17-year-old Acadian named Annie Parker. The ominous mystery became immortalized in a dark local folk song warning travelers to avoid the Weldon House lest they meet McCarthy's grim fate.

#location
The historic Weldon House site on Weldon Street in Shediac, New Brunswick.

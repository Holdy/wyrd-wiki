#title
The Crypt of James Putnam in the Loyalist Burial Ground

#summary
The Loyalist Burial Ground in Saint John contains the historic crypt of James Putnam, encircled by iron bars to deter grave robbers, where visitors claim to feel his lingering spirit.

#details
Established after the arrival of United Empire Loyalists in 1783, the Loyalist Burial Ground holds generations of early settlers. Deep within the grounds lies the crypt of Chief Justice James Putnam, a descendant of the family linked to the Salem Witch Trials, who died in 1789. His crypt is surrounded by a cast-iron fence originally intended to keep grave robbers at bay and form a physical barrier between the living and the dead. Today, many visitors report feeling an uneasy presence and the lingering spirit of James Putnam within the cemetery.

The Phantom Firefighter of the Capitol Theatre

Summary

The historic Capitol Theatre on Main Street is haunted by the ghost of a volunteer firefighter who was crushed to death during a 1924 blaze.

Details

In 1924, a volunteer firefighter named Alexander "Sandy" Lindsay tragically lost his life when the main stage collapsed on him while he was battling a fire at the theatre. Decades later, his ghostly apparition is frequently reported sitting quietly in the empty auditorium, patiently waiting for a performance to begin. Patrons and staff also report sudden drops in temperature and cold drafts sweeping through the historic building whenever his restless spirit is near.

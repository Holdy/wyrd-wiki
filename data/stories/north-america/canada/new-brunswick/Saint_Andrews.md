#title
The Ghost of the Condemned Cell

#summary
The historic Charlotte County Gaol in St. Andrews is haunted by the spirit of Tom Hutchings, an executed inmate whose terrifying presence causes visitors to experience a choking sensation.

#details
In 1944, Royal Air Force sergeant Tom Hutchings was convicted of murder and became the last person to be hanged at the Charlotte County Gaol in St. Andrews. Because the gallows were improperly built, his execution was a prolonged and agonizing ordeal. Ever since, his small, dark stone cell has been a hotspot for paranormal activity. During an investigation by paranormal researchers, visitors inside the cell reported feeling an intense, deathly cold wrap tightly around their throats in simulation of hanging, accompanied by unexplained electromagnetic spikes. Tour guides note that this choking sensation is frequently experienced by visitors who dare enter the cell.

#location
The Charlotte County Gaol is located on Frederick Street in St. Andrews, New Brunswick.

#title
The Phantoms of the Algonquin Resort

#summary
The historic Algonquin Resort in St. Andrews is said to be haunted by multiple specters, including a weeping jilted bride in Room 473 and a helpful phantom bellhop.

#details
Opened over a century ago, the Algonquin Resort in St. Andrews has accumulated a rich lore of paranormal activity. Guests and staff have frequently encountered a phantom elderly bellhop who cheerfully guides visitors to their rooms before vanishing into thin air before a tip can be offered. In Room 473, designated as "The Bride's Room," visitors often hear the sorrowful sobbing of a ghost bride allegedly left standing at the altar. Additional spectral inhabitants include an older female staff member who rearranges dining room tables at night, a former night watchman heard climbing the back staircase while clanging his keys, and a playful child whose laughter echoes through the halls. Mysterious lights have also been spotted in the resort's long-abandoned tower.

#location
The Algonquin Resort is located at 184 Adolphus Street in St. Andrews, New Brunswick.

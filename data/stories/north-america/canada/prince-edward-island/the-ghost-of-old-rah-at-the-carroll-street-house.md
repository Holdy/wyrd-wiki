#title
The Ghost of Old Rah at the Carroll Street House

#summary
An elderly Scottish resident who died in his driveway near Alberton became a local legend when his apparition was allegedly sighted in the upstairs bedroom of his former home.

#details
In the town of Alberton, Prince Edward Island, an elderly Scottish resident known by the nickname "Old Rah"—so called because of his frequent greeting, "It's very raw the day"—lived in an old house situated near the corner of Main and Carroll Streets. After he dropped dead in his driveway, the property eventually changed hands and was purchased by a new family. Local lore and tradition soon maintained that Old Rah's ghost haunted the upper floor, where his spectral figure was reportedly witnessed in an upstairs bedroom. Although some skeptics suggested the sighting was merely a whitened chimney flue, the family departed shortly after, cementing the tale in local memory and giving rise to decades of local ghost stories.

#location
The historic Gordon-Dunn-Holm House is located at the intersection of Main Street and Carroll Street in Alberton, Prince Edward Island.

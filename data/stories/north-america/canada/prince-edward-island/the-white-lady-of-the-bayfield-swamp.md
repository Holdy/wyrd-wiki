#title
The White Lady of the Bayfield Swamp

#summary
Travelers along the road connecting Bayfield and Rock Barra frequently report encountering a silent phantom woman dressed in a white gown who vanishes without a trace.

#details
Local folklore tells of a spectral woman known as the "Swamp Lady" who haunts the wooded marshland where Bayfield and Rock Barra meet. Early accounts include local resident Alec "Little Johnny" MacDonald, who looked beside him on his horse and buggy to find a mysterious woman silently accompanying him before she abruptly vanished. Decades later, another local named Donald MacGregor walked past the swamp on a summer evening and saw a ghostly woman in a long white dress standing motionless, watching him. Overwhelmed by an eerie feeling, he fled to a neighbor's home, where he learned of the legend of the Swamp Lady.

#location
The road and adjacent marshland where Bayfield and Rock Barra meet in Three Rivers, Prince Edward Island.

    Charlie of the Summerside Aerospace Plant

    Summary

    Employees working late shifts at a manufacturing facility in Summerside frequently encounter a mischievous, phantom presence known as "Charlie," who leaves behind echoing footsteps and unexplained mechanical anomalies.

    Details

    Operating first as a Kohltech window manufacturing plant and later as the Tube Fab Aerospace facility in Summerside, Prince Edward Island, the industrial building gained an eerie reputation among its staff. Workers staying past regular hours—particularly during late-night and early-morning shifts—regularly reported hearing heavy footsteps echoing through dark, empty warehouses where no one else was present. Several employees described feeling an invisible presence following closely behind them, while others experienced bizarre equipment malfunctions, such as a paint printer suddenly surging with excessive spray until directly asked to stop. At least one worker caught a visual glimpse of the apparition: a man dressed in gray denim pants and a plain button-down shirt. Affectionately named "Charlie" by the staff, the resident spirit is widely regarded as harmless and playful rather than frightening.

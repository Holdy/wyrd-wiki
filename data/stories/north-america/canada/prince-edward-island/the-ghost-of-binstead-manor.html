    The Ghost of Binstead Manor

    Summary

    In 1856, a wealthy Englishwoman living in a grand mansion near Charlottetown investigated a series of ghostly apparitions and uncovered the tragic history of a former servant.

    Details

    In 1856, Georgina Pennée and her husband moved into Binstead Manor, the largest mansion on the outskirts of Charlottetown, Prince Edward Island. Shortly after settling in, Georgina and her visitors began hearing rumbling noises, heavy dragging sounds, and weeping throughout the house. One night in the guest room, a guest witnessed a young, anxious woman holding a baby and stirring the ashes by the fireplace. Georgina later encountered the same glowing apparition face-to-face as it vanished into a wall. Fascinated rather than frightened, Georgina investigated the house's past and learned it previously belonged to a local businessman whose female servants had given birth out of wedlock, with one young woman and her baby mysteriously disappearing. Years later, Georgina realized that their quiet, long-standing farm worker, Harry—whose last name she had never thought to ask—was the surviving child, and the haunting spirit was his mother who had died giving birth to him.

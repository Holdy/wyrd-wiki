#title
The Cursed Treasure of West Point Dunes

#summary
Generations of treasure seekers have attempted to unearth hidden riches in the dunes behind the West Point Lighthouse, only to be frightened away by a ghostly ship and an enduring curse.

#details
Local legend holds that immense riches are buried in the dunes near West Point, rumored to be plunder from one of General Wolfe’s pay ships or the pirate Captain Kidd. According to folklore, a man named John Campbell of Enmore dreamt of the treasure three nights in a row and led a crew to dig near a half-moon-shaped cove behind marked pine trees. Just as their shovels struck wood, the air filled with the sound of a ship raising anchor, and a fully rigged phantom vessel appeared offshore while sand poured back into the pit. Subsequent attempts by local treasure hunters over the years were similarly plagued by misfortune, accidents, and sudden inexplicable frights that forced them to abandon their tools, leaving the legendary treasure forever unrecovered.

#location
The sand dunes and coastal woods surrounding the West Point Lighthouse near O'Leary on Prince Edward Island.

#title
The Haunting of the West Point Lighthouse

#summary
The historic West Point Lighthouse near O'Leary is said to be haunted by its first keeper, William "Willie" MacDonald, whose spectral presence and mischievous care for the beacon continue to manifest in unexplained light activity.

#details
Built as the first square-tapered wooden lighthouse on Prince Edward Island, the West Point Lighthouse operated under William Anderson MacDonald from 1876 until 1925. Affectionately nicknamed "Lighthouse Willie," his spirit is widely believed by staff, visitors, and volunteers to remain on the property. Over the decades, individuals staying overnight have experienced chilling paranormal occurrences, such as upstairs lights turning themselves back on moments after being switched off, and kitchen lights activating by themselves despite breakers and switches being completely powered down. These uncanny events are attributed to the old keepers still watching over and tending to the lighthouse.

#location
The West Point Lighthouse located at 364 Cedar Dunes Park Road near O'Leary on Prince Edward Island's western coast.

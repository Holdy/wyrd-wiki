#title
The Crying Ghost of Goblin Hollow

#summary
Following the tragic 1859 murder of Annie Beaton, a wooded ravine along the Montague River became haunted by an eerie silence and the sounds of a woman wailing.

#details
On May 12, 1859, 41-year-old Annie Beaton left a neighbor's home to walk the short mile back to her farm along Queens Road. She cut through a ravine near the Montague River, where she was brutally assaulted and murdered. Following her tragic death, the site was renamed Goblin Hollow. Ever since, visitors entering the hollow report experiencing an unnerving, sudden silence accompanied by the haunting sounds of a woman crying, screaming, and wailing in the dark.

#location
Goblin Hollow is located along the Montague River near Queens Road in Montague, Three Rivers, Prince Edward Island.

#title
The Phantom Resident of King's Playhouse

#summary
King's Playhouse in Georgetown is haunted by a friendly 19th-century spirit known as Captain George, who continues to attend performances from a permanently reserved seat.

#details
Built in the 19th century, King's Playhouse is one of Canada's oldest operating theatres. For decades, staff, performers, and visitors have reported unexplained phenomena inside the building, including technical and lighting malfunctions, doors opening on their own, heavy props shifting without physical contact, and phantom footsteps. The resident spirit is identified as Captain George, a former local sea captain. To keep him content, theatre staff ensure a seat is permanently reserved for him during every performance. Paranormal investigators and television crews exploring the theatre have captured anomalous audio and physical movement.

#location
King's Playhouse is located at 65 Grafton Street in Georgetown, Three Rivers, Prince Edward Island.

#title
The White Lady of the Bayfield Swamp

#summary
Travelers along the road connecting Bayfield and Rock Barra frequently report encountering a silent phantom woman dressed in a white gown who vanishes without a trace.

#details
Local folklore tells of a spectral woman known as the "Swamp Lady" who haunts the wooded marshland where Bayfield and Rock Barra meet. Early accounts include local resident Alec "Little Johnny" MacDonald, who looked beside him on his horse and buggy to find a mysterious woman silently accompanying him before she abruptly vanished. Decades later, another local named Donald MacGregor walked past the swamp on a summer evening and saw a ghostly woman in a long white dress standing motionless, watching him. Overwhelmed by an eerie feeling, he fled to a neighbor's home, where he learned of the legend of the Swamp Lady.

#location
The road and adjacent marshland where Bayfield and Rock Barra meet in Three Rivers, Prince Edward Island.

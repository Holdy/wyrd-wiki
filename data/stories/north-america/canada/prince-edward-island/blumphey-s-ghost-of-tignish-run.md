#title
Blumphey's Ghost of Tignish Run

#summary
Three fishermen walking along a sandy road by MacLeod's Pond are joined in silence by a mysterious fourth man who suddenly vanishes, later identified as the restless spirit of a lonely Irishman buried in a washed-away grave.

#details
One Sunday in late May, three fishermen were walking along the sandy road following the shores of MacLeod's Pond (now known as the Little Tignish River) in Tignish Run to meet friends at Myrick's bunkhouse. As they walked, a silent stranger joined their group, bringing an oppressive, freezing silence before abruptly vanishing into thin air. When the shaken fishermen arrived at the bunkhouse with pale faces, another fisherman immediately recognized the encounter, declaring it to be "Blumphey." Blumphey was an isolated Irish immigrant who had taken his own life years prior; because of contemporary burial laws regarding suicide, he was laid to rest in an unblessed shallow grave by the pond. When the grave washed away in a storm, his wandering spirit began haunting travellers along that exact path.

#location
The sandy road along the shores of MacLeod's Pond (Little Tignish River) at Tignish Run in Tignish, Prince Edward Island.

#upstream-source
Collected from Island folklore and published in *Ghost Stories and Legends of Prince Edward Island*.

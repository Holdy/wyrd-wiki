The Phantom Resident of King's Playhouse

Summary

King's Playhouse in Georgetown is haunted by a friendly 19th-century spirit known as Captain George, who continues to attend performances from a permanently reserved seat.

Details

Built in the 19th century, King's Playhouse is one of Canada's oldest operating theatres. For decades, staff, performers, and visitors have reported unexplained phenomena inside the building, including technical and lighting malfunctions, doors opening on their own, heavy props shifting without physical contact, and phantom footsteps. The resident spirit is identified as Captain George, a former local sea captain. To keep him content, theatre staff ensure a seat is permanently reserved for him during every performance. Paranormal investigators and television crews exploring the theatre have captured anomalous audio and physical movement.

    The Millpond Ghost of Lennox

    Summary

    According to local legend, the ghost of a drowned millhand haunts the old millpond of Innisfil's vanished hamlet of Lennox, where his tragic death cursed the local dam and caused repeated structural failures.

    Details

    In the mid-19th century, pioneer William Pitt Lennox established a thriving water-powered mill complex along Innisfil Creek, giving rise to a small sawmill and gristmill community known as Lennox (or Lennox Mills) just west of present-day Churchill. Tragedy struck the settlement when a labourer accidentally fell into the millpond and drowned, forcing the pond to be drained so his body could be recovered for burial. Following the harrowing incident, locals and family members noted that the dam mysteriously failed time and again, draining the pond and crippling operations. Over time, as the hamlet faded into a ghost town, persistent folklore claimed that the restless spirit of the drowned millhand haunted the area. Witnesses reported seeing a silent, floating spectral figure drifting through the overgrown ruins without disturbing a single leaf, while others heard inexplicable splashes and desperate cries for help echoing across the empty waters.

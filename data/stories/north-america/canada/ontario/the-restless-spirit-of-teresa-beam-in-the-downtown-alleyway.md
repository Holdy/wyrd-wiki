#title
The Restless Spirit of Teresa Beam in the Downtown Alleyway

#summary
A downtown Kingston alleyway is haunted by Teresa Beam, a pregnant woman murdered in 1868 whose restless spirit pleads with passersby to help locate her unburied remains.

#details
In 1868, Teresa Beam was murdered in Kingston by her husband, John Napier, while she was pregnant. Because her body was concealed nearby, her remains never received a proper Christian burial. Decades later, businesses operating in the buildings alongside the pathway between Princess Street and King Street East began experiencing inexplicable pounding on doors and scraping sounds along the walls. Occupants who utilized Ouija boards in attempts to understand the phenomena received messages from a spirit identifying as Teresa, asking for her bones to be found and laid to rest. Numerous witnesses and pedestrians walking through the alley have reported seeing a short woman dressed in black with a veiled face, or experiencing sudden physical paralysis and choking sensations while passing through the corridor.

#location
The historic alleyway is situated between Princess Street and King Street East in downtown Kingston, Ontario.

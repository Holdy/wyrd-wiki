#title
Captain Kingsley's Haunted Bed

#summary
Following a painful death in the officers' quarters, Captain Kingsley's restless spirit is said to haunt his old bed, leaving behind displaced sheets and inexplicable sounds.

#details
Captain Kingsley spent his final months bedridden in the officers' quarters of Old Fort Erie, enduring severe fevers, hallucinations, and mercury poisoning. After he passed away in that very bed, museum staff and visitors reported eerie phenomena. People have witnessed his apparition lying in the bed, found bedsheets strewn about in disarray, heard phantom footsteps pacing the room, and watched windows and doors shift open or closed without anyone touching them.

#location
Old Fort Erie is located at 350 Lakeshore Road in Fort Erie, Ontario.

#title
The Faceless and Handless Soldiers of the Battlefield

#summary
Two American soldiers killed during the British invasion of the War of 1812 are said to perpetually roam the battlegrounds of Old Fort Erie at night, one missing his head and the other missing his hands.

#details
Dating back over a century, a chilling local legend at Old Fort Erie describes the spectral remains of two American soldiers who met gruesome ends during the bloody battles of the War of 1812. Over the decades, night-time visitors and staff patrolling the historic grounds have reported encountering these two phantom soldiers wandering across the battlefield. True to their tragic fates, one spectral figure appears entirely headless, while the other walks without hands.

#location
The grounds of Old Fort Erie are situated at 350 Lakeshore Road in Fort Erie, Ontario.

#title
The Restless Spirits of Bertie Hall

#summary
A historic Greek Revival mansion that once served as a safe house on the Underground Railroad is plagued by phantom footsteps, moving furniture, and eerie poltergeist activity.

#details
Built in 1832 along the Niagara River, Bertie Hall functioned as a refuge for freedom-seekers escaping slavery via the Underground Railroad, later operating as a doll museum. Psychics, ghost hunters, and former occupants have reported intense paranormal activity throughout the grand manor. Witnesses have documented unexplained cold spots, heavy furniture shifting across rooms on its own, phantom footsteps echoing on the grand staircase, and eerie phenomena emanating from its hidden basement spaces.

#location
Bertie Hall is located at 657 Niagara Boulevard and Phipps Street in Fort Erie, Ontario.

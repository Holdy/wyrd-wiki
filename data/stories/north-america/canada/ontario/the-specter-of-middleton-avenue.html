The Specter of Middleton Avenue

Summary

In 1928, a house on Middleton Avenue became a local sensation when residents and onlookers reported phantom intruders, flying stones, and unassisted music.

Details

In February and March 1928, the home of Mr. and Mrs. Jos. Bellefeuille at 66 Middleton Avenue in Timmins became the center of public attention following reports of bizarre paranormal phenomena. Mr. Bellefeuille returned home one evening to find an apparition descending the stairs holding a revolver, prompting his wife to faint. After locking the house and fleeing to a neighbor's, police investigated but found no one inside. Over subsequent days, heavy stones were thrown around, mysterious bells rang, and a broken gramophone allegedly played records on its own without spring power. Hundreds of curious townspeople gathered outside the property as local rumors of a haunting spread throughout the community.

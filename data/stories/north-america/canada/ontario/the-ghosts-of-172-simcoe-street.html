The Ghosts of 172 Simcoe Street

Summary

The historic building at 172 Simcoe Street in Peterborough is said to be haunted by a former bar owner named Abe who accepts beer offerings and a tragic female guest from its days as the King George Hotel.

Details

Located at 172 Simcoe Street, a site that has housed various drinking establishments and the historic King George Hotel, paranormal accounts trace back to both a former owner and a 1910 guest. Abe, a former bar owner who was struck and killed by a taxi outside the building after going to get coffee, allegedly haunts the unfinished basement. Staff have reported unexplained thumps, televisions turning on and off, tampered cash registers, and complete power shutoffs. Remarkably, staff claim that leaving an open can of Labatt’s 50 pale ale in the basement appeases Abe, stopping the poltergeist activity while the can is subsequently found empty. Additionally, dating back to its time as the King George Hotel in 1910, a female guest who died by suicide is reported to haunt the upper rooms, with guests describing sightings of a woman with long straggly hair wearing a green dress watching them sleep or appearing in the shower.

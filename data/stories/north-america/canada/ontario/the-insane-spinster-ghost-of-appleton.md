#title
The Insane Spinster Ghost of Appleton

#summary
A historic 1830s home in Appleton is haunted by a spinster driven mad by a brood of children, whose cold presence and footsteps still linger.

#details
Legend tells of a house built in the 1830s in the village of Appleton for an affluent couple who invited the husband's spinster sister to live with them. After her brother died, leaving twelve children behind, the couple insisted the children call the spinster "Mummy dear." The overwhelming chaos slowly drove her insane, leading to an early death. Decades later, a family who purchased the home—complete with its seven-grave family cemetery—reported a frigid spiritual presence, the swish of skirts through hollyhocks, and angry whispering as the phantom spinster haunts the halls.

#location
A historic house in the village of Appleton, Mississippi Mills, Ontario.

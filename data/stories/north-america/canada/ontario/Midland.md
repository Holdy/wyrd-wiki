#title
The Haunted Honeymoon House

#summary
A custom log house built in the 1850s as a wedding present remained vacant for decades after a bitter family feud ended the engagement, and is now said to be haunted by the ghost of Amelia Jeffery.

#details
In the 1850s, a young Irish immigrant named Samuel Frazer fell in love with Amelia Jeffery in Midland, Ontario. Eager to surprise his bride-to-be, Samuel spent the winter constructing a custom log house to serve as their marital home. However, just before the wedding, a heated argument erupted between Samuel and the Jeffery family over his decision to have an Anglican minister perform the ceremony rather than a Presbyterian one. Offended, Samuel abruptly broke off the engagement, never speaking to Amelia again. Both lived into their late nineties unmarried and were eventually buried in separate cemeteries. The log house remained unoccupied for a long time, but after being relocated and restored, locals and visitors reported that the ghost of Amelia Jeffery still haunts the halls of the building, which now operates as the Heritage Animal Hospital.

#location
The Heritage Animal Hospital at the corner of King Street and Galloway Boulevard in Midland, Ontario.

#title
The Legend of Kitchikewana

#summary
The giant Huron god Kitchikewana, heartbroken after rejection, flew into a rage that carved out the five bays of Georgian Bay and scattered the 30,000 islands.

#details
According to local Huron folklore, Georgian Bay was once protected by Kitchikewana, a colossal giant taller than the C.N. Tower with a famously volatile temper. Hoping to soothe his anger, the other gods encouraged him to woo a young woman named Wanakita. When Kitchikewana asked for her hand, Wanakita refused because she was already in love with a warrior from her own tribe. Devastated and enraged, Kitchikewana dug his massive hands deep into the earth and threw dirt across the region in a blind fury. His five massive finger gouges formed Midland Bay, Penetang Bay, Hog Bay, Sturgeon Bay, and Matchedash Bay, while the scattered earth created the 30,000 islands. Exhausted and heartbroken, Kitchikewana lay down and fell asleep forever, his massive stone form now known as Giant's Tomb Island in Georgian Bay.

#location
Giant's Tomb Island in Georgian Bay, near Midland, Ontario.

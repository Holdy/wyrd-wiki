#title
The Phantoms of Robinson House

#summary
Staff and visitors at Robinson House in Oshawa frequently reported chilling paranormal encounters, including shadowy figures passing directly through people and heavy, unhappy presences lingering in the rooms.

#details
Visitor hosts and staff working at Robinson House, one of the historic buildings managed by the Oshawa Museum, documented numerous unexplainable phenomena over the years. One staff member cataloging items in the attic felt an eerie presence before a black, shadowy shape moved directly through her body, leaving her feeling nauseated. Other museum workers experienced heavy wooden doors slamming shut by themselves with solid thumps when no one was nearby, while visitors sensitive to the paranormal reported sensing a heavy, deeply unhappy presence lingering within the historic walls.

#location
Robinson House at the Oshawa Museum in Oshawa, Ontario.

#title
The Watcher in the Historic Oshawa Home

#summary
A family moving into a turn-of-the-century house near Parkwood Estate in Oshawa found themselves stalked by an unseen presence that moved their belongings, plunged rooms into freezing temperatures, and slammed doors.

#details
After moving from Boston into a large turn-of-the-century home not far from Colonel Sam McLaughlin’s historic Parkwood Estate in Oshawa, a homeowner experienced a series of unsettling paranormal events while renovating alone. His watch repeatedly vanished from his nightstand, only to turn up later in distant rooms he had never entered. He also encountered sudden, localized temperature drops, doors that slammed shut by themselves in empty hallways with freezing doorknobs, and the distinct, chilling sensation of being watched by someone gauging his reactions.

#location
A turn-of-the-century residential house located near Parkwood Estate in Oshawa, Ontario.

#title
The Haunting of the Lawrence House

#summary
Built in 1892 by a local lumber baron, this grand Victorian mansion is said to be haunted by the spirit of his widow, Elizabeth Lawrence, whose presence is marked by a mysteriously operating elevator and ghostly footsteps.

#details
Constructed in 1892 by lumber tycoon William Lawrence, the imposing Victorian mansion at the corner of Christina and Wellington streets became a fixture of local folklore after Elizabeth Lawrence lived there for decades following her husband's death in 1922. After she passed away in 1940, the house remained mothballed and untouched for nearly forty years before being converted into a community arts center. Since its restoration, staff members, visitors, and paranormal investigators have frequently reported anomalous phenomena, including the building's elevator moving between floors by itself without passengers, muffled voices echoing from the turret room, and heavy footsteps pacing overhead.

#location
The Lawrence House is located at the corner of Christina Street and Wellington Street in Sarnia, Ontario.

#title
The Phantom of the Imperial Theatre

#summary
Staff and paranormal investigators at this historic downtown theatre have frequently reported unexplained encounters, including a phantom figure in a tuxedo on the catwalks and disembodied whispers in the dark.

#details
Opened in the 1930s, the historic Imperial Theatre in downtown Sarnia has long been rumored to harbor unseen theatrical spirits. During paranormal investigations and late-night lock-ins, crew members and staff have witnessed a mysterious figure dressed in a tuxedo walking through the locked lobby and pacing the stage catwalks high above the auditorium. Others have reported hearing the eerie rattling of chains, heavy footsteps in empty restricted areas, and tactile sensations such as an unseen presence tapping someone on the shoulder and whispering "psst" directly into their ear when no one else is around.

#location
The Imperial Theatre is located at 127 Christina Street North in Sarnia, Ontario.

#title
The Ghost of Queen Street

#summary
In August 1939, unexplained violent pounding and phantom lights on an attic door at a Queen Street home in Picton baffled residents, neighbors, and police.

#details
In August 1939, 86-year-old Mrs. James Ackerman, along with her niece and nephew, were awakened at midnight by violent pounding on the attic door of her home on Queen Street in Picton. Despite the nephew nailing the door shut and sticking a pair of scissors into the hinges, the loud thumping intensified and the heavy door burst open. Local police officers, including Constable John Armstrong, investigated the home and witnessed the inexplicable phenomena firsthand, leaving the mystery officially unresolved.

#location
The home of Mrs. James Ackerman on Queen Street in Picton, Prince Edward County, Ontario.

#title
The Shadows of Picton Historic Courthouse

#summary
The decommissioned jail within Picton’s historic courthouse—site of an 1884 double-hanging—is said to be haunted by a Victorian man and disembodied sobbing.

#details
Situated at 44 Union Street in Picton, the historic courthouse and its now-decommissioned jail carry a dark history stemming from the 1884 double-hanging of Joseph Thomset and George Lowder, two local men widely believed to have been wrongfully convicted. Visitors and ghost tour participants have reported paranormal activity in the old jail, including seeing a spectral figure in Victorian clothing pacing near the gallows and hearing chilling, disembodied weeping echoing through the halls.

#location
Picton Historic Courthouse, 44 Union Street, Picton, Ontario.

#title
The Spirits of Macaulay House

#summary
Built in 1830 for Prince Edward County’s first Anglican reverend, Macaulay House in Picton is reported to be haunted by gentle spectral figures, including a little girl in a white dress.

#details
Located at 23 Church Street in Picton, Macaulay House was constructed in 1830 for Reverend William Macaulay. Over the years, staff and visitors have reported numerous gentle paranormal occurrences within the historic home. Most notably, a phantom little girl wearing a white dress has been frequently sighted haunting the dining room, adding to the lore of the heritage property.

#location
Macaulay House, 23 Church Street, Picton, Ontario.

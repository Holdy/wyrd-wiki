#title
The Green Lady and Rattling Doors of Victoria Hall

#summary
Cobourg's historic Victoria Hall is said to be haunted by the restless spirit of a desperate prisoner trapped against locked doors and a mysterious woman in green velvet.

#details
Completed in 1860, Victoria Hall is an architectural landmark in downtown Cobourg. Local legend holds that sometime in the 1870s, a condemned prisoner bolted from the courtroom in a final escape attempt, only to find the front doors locked tight. On quiet nights, those doors are still said to rattle violently from his unseen attempts to escape. In addition, since the building's major restoration in the early 1980s, actors, staff, and visitors have reported encountering the "Green Lady"—an apparition garbed in a green velvet dress who wanders the backstage areas and corridors of the concert hall.

#location
Victoria Hall is located at 55 King Street West in Cobourg, Ontario.

#title
The Trio of Spirits at MacKechnie House

#summary
Built in 1843, the historic MacKechnie House in Cobourg is home to three distinct spirits, including a phantom bagpiper and a grandmotherly ghost who tucks guests into bed.

#details
Constructed in the mid-19th century, MacKechnie House has long held a reputation as one of Cobourg's most haunted residences. According to local lore, the home is inhabited by a trio of harmless ghosts. The first is a bagpipe player whose music is frequently heard echoing through the building even though he is rarely sighted. The second is an elderly woman residing in the Rose Room, where she is said to affectionately tuck sleeping guests into bed at night and temporarily rearrange room items. The third is a little girl named Elizabeth, who reportedly died of pneumonia on the property and spends her afterlife playing hide-and-seek and watching visitors from the staircase.

#location
MacKechnie House is located at 173 Tremaine Street in Cobourg, Ontario.

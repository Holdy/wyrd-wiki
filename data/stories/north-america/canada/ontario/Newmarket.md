#title
The Grey Lady of Pickering College

#summary
A mysterious nurse in grey has haunted the halls and windows of Pickering College for nearly a century.

#details
Legend holds that a nurse who worked at Pickering College years ago never truly left the school grounds, earning her reputation as the "Grey Lady." Sightings of her spectral form roaming the halls and peering out windows have been reported across generations. Most famously, a staff and student group photograph taken outside Rogers House in the 1920s captured the ghostly visage of a woman looking out from a first-floor window. Paranormal researchers note that the sightings have remained consistent over decades, tying the school's long history of ghostly encounters to its early staff.

#location
Pickering College campus on Bayview Avenue in Newmarket, Ontario.

#title
The Orange Blossom Caretaker of Old Town Hall

#summary
The second-floor auditorium of Newmarket's historic town hall carries the lingering scent of oranges, attributed to the spirit of a former caretaker who died tragically.

#details
Arthur O'Brien served as a caretaker at the Old Town Hall on Botsford Street, where he had a distinct habit of leaving bowls of orange peel or freshness agents around the auditorium to keep the air pleasant. Following his tragic death in an accident on Davis Drive, staff and visitors have continuously reported the unexplainable, sweet aroma of orange blossoms drifting through the upper auditorium despite multiple renovations and air system upgrades. Additionally, paranormal investigators and staff report banging and moaning coming from the basement area that once housed the town's jail cells.

#location
Old Town Hall, located on Botsford Street in downtown Newmarket, Ontario.

#title
The Spirits of the Grey Goat Inn

#summary
Main Street's historic Grey Goat Inn is home to two distinct spirits: a benign woman dressed in black on the second floor and a menacing presence in the basement.

#details
Housed in a heritage building that once served as a private residence and later a funeral parlor in its basement, the Grey Goat Inn on Main Street is reputed to be haunted by two different entities. On the second floor, staff and patrons frequently encounter "Georgette," a benign spirit in a long black dress who hangs around an office and resembles a woman from an old portrait once displayed in the home. In sharp contrast, the basement—utilizing the footprint of the former funeral home—harbors a negative and unsettling presence, with staff reporting invisible fingers grasping at their ankles as they walk the stairs.

#location
The Grey Goat Inn on Main Street in downtown Newmarket, Ontario.

#title
The Cold Staircase of the Old Stone House

#summary
Visitors and staff at the historic Ermatinger Old Stone House have reported experiencing unexplainable cold spots and phantom noises tied to a tragic historical accident.

#details
According to local lore, a small child fell down the steep wooden stairs of the historic home and later died from the injuries. Years later, staff and visitors frequently report sudden, unnatural drops in temperature at the base of the staircase. Additionally, staff working in the summer kitchen have heard doors squeaking open and shut when no one else is inside the building.

#location
The Ermatinger-Clergue National Historic Site is located at 699 Queen Street East in Sault Ste. Marie, Ontario.

#title
The Legless Soldier of Fort St. Joseph

#summary
A British soldier who perished from severe frostbite after deserting his post in the 1800s is said to still patrol the grounds of Fort St. Joseph without legs.

#details
In the early 19th century, two British soldiers attempted to desert Fort St. Joseph during a brutal winter by walking across a frozen lake. They succumbed to hypothermia and were rescued by First Nations traders, but one died and the survivor lost his limbs to the cold. Years later, staff closing the historic site observed a phantom soldier walking toward the flagpole who noticeably lacked legs below the thigh.

#location
Fort St. Joseph National Historic Site is located on the southern tip of St. Joseph Island in Ontario, southeast of Sault Ste. Marie.

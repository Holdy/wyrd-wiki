#title
The Spirits of the Baker Street Burial Ground

#summary
Downtown Guelph's Baker Street parking lot, which served as a public burial ground in the 19th century, is frequently associated with sightings of a spectral woman in white.

#details
Before it was paved over and utilized as a municipal parking lot, the triangular block bounded by Baker Street was Guelph's first non-denominational public cemetery from 1827 to 1853. Although many bodies were eventually relocated, archaeological digs and construction over the decades have repeatedly uncovered human skeletal remains. Local legend and paranormal researchers maintain that the displaced souls never truly left the grounds, with numerous witnesses reporting encounters with a phantom woman in a white dress wandering the asphalt after dark.

#location
The Baker Street parking lot is located at the Baker District site in downtown Guelph, Ontario.

#title
Ethel Steele of Lighthouse Theatre

#summary
The historic theatre in Port Dover is reportedly haunted by Ethel Steele, a devoted local homemaker and director whose spirit manifests as unexplained activity and whom actors tap for good luck.

#details
Housed in Port Dover’s historic town hall building, the Lighthouse Theatre is widely believed to be haunted by Ethel Steele (1881–1979), a pillar of the local community and theatre scene who directed numerous productions during her lifetime. Today, a portrait of Mrs. Steele hangs in the second-floor lobby. Actors and performers maintain a tradition of tapping her portrait for good luck before going on stage. Staff members and paranormal investigators have reported mysterious occurrences inside the building, including computers turning on by themselves, pens flying off desks, footsteps heard in empty halls, and the gentle clinking of glasses.

#location
Lighthouse Theatre, located at 247 Main Street in Port Dover, Norfolk County, Ontario.

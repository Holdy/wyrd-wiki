#title
The Pewtress House Haunting

#summary
Former tenants of the historic Pewtress House in Milton experienced paranormal activity before the building was relocated.

#details
According to Milton Historical Society President John Challinor, residents who rented the historic Pewtress House—previously situated at the corner of Tremaine Road and Main Street—reported various haunting experiences. The house was eventually deconstructed to make way for a park and relocated to King Street to serve as a private heritage residence, leaving locals to wonder if the alleged spirit made the move across town.

#location
The Pewtress House, formerly located at the intersection of Tremaine Road and Main Street and relocated to King Street in Milton, Ontario.

#title
The Ghost and Gold of Murdoff's Tavern

#summary
Operated along the Napanee River during the 19th century, this tavern became the center of a local legend regarding buried gold and an innkeeper's restless spirit pacing the fields in search of his lost fortune.

#details
Established on the Daly Farm along Deseronto Road during the mid-19th-century timber and barley trade booms, Murdoff's Tavern served boat travelers and river drivers along the Napanee River. Fearing civil unrest and Fenian Raids during the mid-1800s, innkeeper Murdoff reportedly hoarded and buried his gold coins on the property for safekeeping. Following his death, generations of locals and farmhands—including farm boy Harry Oliver, whose grandmother previously worked as a servant at the tavern—recalled searching the soil for the hidden treasure. Over time, local folklore maintained that Murdoff's ghost never left, joining the treasure hunt by restlessly pacing back and forth across the farm fields at night.

#location
Murdoff's Tavern was located on the Daly Farm along Deseronto Road on the Napanee River in Greater Napanee, Ontario.

#title
The Spirits of Old Hay Bay Church

#summary
Built in 1792 as Canada's oldest surviving Methodist building, this historic church served as a makeshift morgue after a tragic 1819 boating accident and has since become famous for reported hauntings.

#details
Situated on the shores of Hay Bay, the Old Hay Bay Church was constructed by United Empire Loyalist settlers in 1792, serving variously as a place of worship, a courtroom, and a community meeting hall. In 1819, tragedy struck when a small boat carrying churchgoers across Hay Bay took on water and capsized, drowning eight youths and two adults while family and friends watched helplessly from the shore. The church building was pressed into service as a temporary morgue for the victims. In the years since, visitors, historians, and staff have frequently reported paranormal experiences, with numerous spirits allegedly sighted on the church grounds.

#location
The Old Hay Bay Church is located at 2355 Hay Bay Road in Greater Napanee, Ontario.

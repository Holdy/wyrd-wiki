#title
The Second-Floor Footsteps at Country Heritage Park

#summary
Unexplained footsteps echo across the upper floor of the historic Ford garage at Country Heritage Park, where 19th-century garments are stored.

#details
Local oral history recounts eerie experiences inside the Ford garage located on the grounds of Country Heritage Park in Milton. The second floor of the building was utilized as storage for clothing dating back to the 1800s, and visitors have frequently reported hearing distinct, disembodied footsteps pacing across the floorboards, believed by locals to be the former owners of the historic clothing walking among their belongings.

#location
The Ford garage building located within Country Heritage Park at 856 Steeles Avenue West in Milton, Ontario.

#title
George the Ghost of The Grove Hotel

#summary
A mischievous spirit known as George is said to haunt a historic hotel on Main Street in Kingsville, frequently playing with lights and opening doors.

#details
Ask anyone in Kingsville about local folklore, and they will likely mention George, an unidentified spirit said to haunt one of the town's historic buildings. Theories on who George was range from a local shopkeeper to a weary mariner who never left. Today, guests and staff at The Grove Hotel report unexplained phenomena such as flickering lights, creaking floors, and sudden cold chills, cementing George's reputation as a semi-mischievous resident ghost watching over the historic property.

#location
The Grove Hotel is located on Main Street in Kingsville, Ontario.

#title
The Spirits of the Waggott House and General Store

#summary
Historic buildings relocated to the Canadian Transportation Museum & Heritage Village in Kingsville are allegedly haunted by former residents and a vengeful doll.

#details
The Canadian Transportation Museum & Heritage Village preserves several historical buildings relocated from around the region, including the 1869 Waggott House originally built in Kingsville and an 1840s general store from Olinda. Staff and visitors have reported numerous supernatural encounters, including feeling cold hands on their necks and being physically blocked from removing books by the lingering spirit of Mrs. Waggott. Additionally, upstairs in the general store, an antique doll reacts negatively to insults—manifesting as rolling skates that nearly trip volunteers or jammed locks that trap unwary tour guides inside.

#location
The Canadian Transportation Museum & Heritage Village is located at 6153 North Talbot Road in Kingsville, Ontario.

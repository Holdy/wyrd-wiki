#title
The Haunted Aviation Pilot of Wilmot House

#summary
The former home of decorated military pilot and mayor Charles Eardley Wilmot is said to be haunted by his restless spirit following his tragic decapitation accident.

#details
The Wilmot House was the residence of S/L Charles Eardley Wilmot, A.F.C., a skilled World War military pilot and former mayor of Belleville. On October 15, 1941, Wilmot died a violent and tragic death when he was struck by an airplane propeller on a tarmac. Local folklore maintains that his restless spirit continues to roam his former residence on Dundas Street West.

#location
Wilmot House is located at 293 Dundas Street West in Belleville, Ontario.

#title
The Phantoms of Glanmore Mansion

#summary
Built in 1883, Glanmore National Historic Site is haunted by the apparition of a British redcoat soldier, a self-playing piano, and the ghostly presence of original owner Hattie Phillips.

#details
Glanmore House, a striking Second Empire style Victorian mansion built in 1883 by John and Hattie Phillips, has long been associated with paranormal activity in Belleville. Family members and visitors have reported several distinct encounters over the decades, including a British redcoat soldier standing at attention and smiling by a bedside, the drawing room piano playing autonomously, heavy doors swinging open without wind, and sightings of a tiny woman in a grey silk dress with keys at her waist identified as original owner Hattie Phillips.

#location
Glanmore National Historic Site is located at 257 Bridge Street East in Belleville, Ontario.

#title
The Spirits of Pinnacle Playhouse

#summary
Housed in a historic downtown building, the Pinnacle Playhouse is said to be haunted by a resident spirit named "Ophelia" and a disembodied male laugh echoing through the washrooms.

#details
Since 1969, the Belleville Theatre Guild has operated out of the former D.L. Storey Centre, now known as Pinnacle Playhouse. Members and guests have reported numerous supernatural phenomena, nicknaming their resident spirit "Ophelia" after Shakespeare's character. Reported occurrences include hearing voices within the walls, glowing green eyes peering from air vents, phantom lights and voices backstage, a mysterious lady in black spotted in the control booth, and a terrifying disembodied male laugh in the female washroom attributed to a former janitor.

#location
Pinnacle Playhouse is located on Pinnacle Street in downtown Belleville, Ontario.

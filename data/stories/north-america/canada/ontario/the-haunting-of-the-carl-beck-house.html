The Haunting of the Carl Beck House

Summary

Visitors and overnight guests at the historic 19th-century Carl Beck House in Penetanguishene report phantom footsteps, flickering lights, and the lingering spirit of the builder's disinherited daughter.

Details

Built in 1866 by wealthy lumber magnate Charles Beck, the grand Italianate mansion in Penetanguishene has long been reputed as one of Ontario's most haunted buildings. Over the years, residents, paranormal investigators, and overnight guests have reported unexplained phenomena, including objects moving on their own, phantom whispers, and blankets being tugged in the night. Local legend attributes much of the paranormal activity to former family members who died in the home, as well as Charles's eldest daughter, Mary. According to local lore, Mary haunts the halls in protest after being left with only one dollar in her father's multi-million dollar will following a family dispute over her marriage. Apparitions of Victorian-era women have also been witnessed strolling along adjacent Church Street outside the mansion.

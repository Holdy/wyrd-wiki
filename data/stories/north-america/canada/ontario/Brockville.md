#title
The Phantoms and Poltergeists of Fulford Place

#summary
Built at the turn of the 20th century by a wealthy patent-medicine tycoon, the grand Edwardian mansion of Fulford Place in Brockville is renowned for ghostly footsteps, moving objects, and spirit encounters.

#details
Fulford Place was constructed between 1899 and 1901 by George Taylor Fulford, who amassed a massive fortune from his patent medicine, "Pink Pills for Pale People." Following the family's occupancy and the mansion's eventual transition into a historic museum maintained by the Ontario Heritage Trust, staff, volunteers, and visitors began reporting persistent paranormal activity. Caretakers over the decades noted mysterious balls of light, books violently flying off library shelves, and sudden drops in temperature. Modern ghost-hunting groups and paranormal investigators visiting the estate have recorded disembodied voices, spirit names, and cold sensations using specialized equipment like spirit boxes and the Estes method, cementing the mansion's reputation as one of eastern Ontario's most active haunted sites.

#location
Fulford Place, 287 King Street East, Brockville, Ontario.

#title
The Unearthed Bones of Victoria Park

#summary
Historic walking tours in Brockville highlight the unsettling past of Victoria Park and the First Presbyterian Church, where human remains and skulls were once unearthed and scattered by children in earlier decades.

#details
Although Victoria Park is now a peaceful green space and public park where children play, local historical lore and museum-led walking tours remind residents of its grim origins as an old burial ground. Standing before the First Presbyterian Church across from the Brockville Courthouse, guides recount dark historical accounts of bones being unearthed during early town development. According to local historical accounts shared on the town's haunted histories tours, children in past eras occasionally discovered human remains and skulls protruding from the earth or displaced near the old cemetery boundaries, leaving enduring legends of restless spirits haunting the older corridors of Brockville.

#location
Victoria Park and the First Presbyterian Church near the Brockville Courthouse, Brockville, Ontario.

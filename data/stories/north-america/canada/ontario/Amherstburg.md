#title
The Hauntings of Park House Museum

#summary
Built in the 1770s and later moved to Amherstburg, the historic Park House Museum is frequently visited by ghostly apparitions, disembodied footsteps, and inexplicable sounds.

#details
Constructed originally in the 1770s and floated down the Detroit River to Amherstburg in 1798, the Park House Museum has accumulated numerous ghostly tales over its two-century history. Staff and visitors have reported numerous paranormal encounters throughout the building. In the parlour, witnesses have seen a man in a jacket—referred to as "Mr. Park"—walk by and vanish into thin air. In the upstairs bedroom, a visitor once spent time chatting with a woman wearing a black dress before realizing she was alone in the building, and the historic rope bed frequently appears as though someone has been resting in it. Other reported phenomena include disembodied footsteps on the stairs, music boxes playing on their own, glasses clinking in empty rooms, and phantom voices telling people to get out.

#location
The Park House Museum is located at 214 Dalhousie Street in Amherstburg, Ontario.

#title
The Spirits of Gordon House

#summary
Constructed in 1798 as Amherstburg's oldest standing home, Gordon House is said to be haunted by restless spirits that react whenever alterations are made to the property.

#details
Built in 1798 and having served variously as a trading post, infirmary, and home to a War of 1812 general, Gordon House now serves as the Amherstburg tourism office. Staff members have documented multiple paranormal occurrences over the years, particularly when changes or renovations are made to the building. The manager reported hearing loud crashes of glass every morning when she was the sole person working inside. In the former birthing room, visitors have reported hearing the sounds of babies crying. Other reported activity includes a mysterious rushing wind on the stairwell and an office worker witnessing a heavy desk lift off the floor and drop back down.

#location
Gordon House is located on Dalhousie Street in Amherstburg, Ontario.

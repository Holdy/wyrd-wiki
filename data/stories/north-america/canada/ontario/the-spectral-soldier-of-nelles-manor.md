#title
The Spectral Soldier of Nelles Manor

#summary
Built in 1798 by United Empire Loyalist Colonel Robert Nelles, Nelles Manor is said to be haunted by various spirits from its long history, most notably a British soldier in a red coat.

#details
Nelles Manor is a historic house in Grimsby, Ontario, constructed between 1788 and 1798 by Colonel Robert Nelles and occupied during the War of 1812. Over the centuries, residents, visitors, and tour guides have reported various inexplicable paranormal occurrences and unexplained noises within the home. Among the most frequent accounts is the sighting of a ghostly soldier wearing a historical red coat roaming the heritage property. The manor regularly hosts annual ghost tours and paranormal events to explore its centuries-old spectral history.

#location
Nelles Manor Museum is located at 126 Main St. W., Grimsby, ON.

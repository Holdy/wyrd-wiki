#title
The Phantom Actress of the Academy Theatre

#summary
The historic Academy Theatre in Lindsay is said to be haunted by a young woman named Mary who tragically fell to her death down the stairs, along with other spectral residents and unexplained phenomena.

#details
Opened in the late 19th century and later renovated, the Academy Theatre in Lindsay has long been a fixture of local folklore for its ghostly inhabitants. Staff and paranormal investigators have frequently reported encounters with "Mary" (sometimes captured on audio recordings responding as Martha), a young woman said to have tripped and broken her neck on the stairs near the costume room. Visitors and actors have also sensed the presence of Dennis, a former maintenance worker with a limp, experienced sudden cold spots, heard phantom footsteps, and noticed seat number 13 mysteriously folding down on its own.

#location
The Flato Academy Theatre is located at 2 Lindsay Street South in Lindsay, Ontario.

#title
The Spirits of Kawartha Settlers' Village

#summary
Kawartha Settlers' Village in Bobcaygeon comprises historic relocated buildings—such as the Duggan, Kimble, and Wray houses—where visitors and investigators have reported disembodied voices, shifting furnishings, and oppressive heaviness linked to past pioneer hardships.

#details
Established to preserve the pioneer history of the region, Kawartha Settlers' Village features over twenty historic structures collected from across the Kawartha Lakes area. Within buildings like the 1850s Duggan House and the Kimble House, visitors and paranormal investigators have experienced sudden sensations of suffocation, unexplained floor vibrations, and shifting shadows. Electronic Voice Phenomena (EVP) captured across the village have recorded whispered voices and replies from unseen entities, particularly inside the old General Store, which bears the lingering atmospheric weight of historic local tragedies and pioneers' struggles.

#location
Kawartha Settlers' Village is located at 85 E Turkey Street in Bobcaygeon, Ontario.

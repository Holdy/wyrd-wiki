The Disappearing Groom of Cherry Hill

Summary

Following his wedding celebration at a historic Mississauga home, George Silverthorn stepped outside to answer a visitor at the door and vanished without a trace. Ever since, locals report that whenever his disappearance is discussed, the front door opens and closes on its own.

Details

Cherry Hill House, built by the Silverthorn family on a 200-acre cherry orchard at 680 Silver Creek Boulevard, carries a chilling legend surrounding the youngest son, George. After seeking his fortune in the California gold rush, George returned home to marry. During the wedding reception, a knock came at the door; George stepped outside to speak with an unknown visitor and was never seen again. Alongside this tragic disappearance, staff and patrons have reported ghostly phenomena, including the phantom sightings of a small girl in upstairs windows, dimming lights, cold spots, objects moving on their own, and mirrors shifting on the walls.

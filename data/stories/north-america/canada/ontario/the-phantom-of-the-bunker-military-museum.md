#title
The Phantom of the Bunker Military Museum

#summary
Housed in a landmark 1910 silver exchange and luxury hotel, the Bunker Military Museum in Cobalt is stalked by a silent, dark phantom that mimics the mannequin exhibits.

#details
Marg Harrison, the museum board president who was skeptical of the paranormal for nearly seven decades, experienced multiple sightings of a menacing, dark figure standing motionless among 65 uniformed mannequin soldiers on display in the "parade ground" room. Since the building was converted into a military museum in 2012, staff and visitors have reported numerous chilling encounters and unexplained presences within the historic boomtown structure.

#location
The Bunker Military Museum is located in the historic Royal Exchange building in Cobalt, Ontario.

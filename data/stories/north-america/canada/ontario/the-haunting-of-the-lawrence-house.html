The Haunting of the Lawrence House

Summary

Built in 1892 by a local lumber baron, this grand Victorian mansion is said to be haunted by the spirit of his widow, Elizabeth Lawrence, whose presence is marked by a mysteriously operating elevator and ghostly footsteps.

Details

Constructed in 1892 by lumber tycoon William Lawrence, the imposing Victorian mansion at the corner of Christina and Wellington streets became a fixture of local folklore after Elizabeth Lawrence lived there for decades following her husband's death in 1922. After she passed away in 1940, the house remained mothballed and untouched for nearly forty years before being converted into a community arts center. Since its restoration, staff members, visitors, and paranormal investigators have frequently reported anomalous phenomena, including the building's elevator moving between floors by itself without passengers, muffled voices echoing from the turret room, and heavy footsteps pacing overhead.

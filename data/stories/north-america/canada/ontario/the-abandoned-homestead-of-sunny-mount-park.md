#title
The Abandoned Homestead of Sunny Mount Park

#summary
The vacant mid-19th-century pioneer farmhouse at Sunny Mount Park has earned a lasting reputation among local residents as a haunted landmark.

#details
Nestled within Sunny Mount Park, the historic heritage home originally known as Sunny Mount Farm was built around 1856 by pioneer resident Austin Willmott. Over the decades, as various commercial redevelopment plans stalled and the property remained vacant and weathered, the eerie atmosphere of the abandoned house prompted generations of Miltonians to dub it one of the town's most prominent haunted locations.

#location
The historic Sunny Mount Farm house located in Sunny Mount Park at 155 Ruhl Drive in Milton, Ontario.

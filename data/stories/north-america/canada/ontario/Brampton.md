#title
Spirits of the Peel County Jail

#summary
Staff working inside the historic Peel County Jail building have reported hearing unexplained footsteps and seeing shadowy figures lurking in the corners of empty rooms.

#details
The Peel Art Gallery Museum and Archives (PAMA) complex in downtown Brampton incorporates the historical Peel County Courthouse and jail. Dating back to the 19th century—with several executions having taken place on the grounds—the building has developed a reputation for paranormal activity. Museum staff and night-shift workers have frequently reported hearing mysterious knocks, echoing footsteps in empty corridors, and catching shadowy figures darting at the edge of their vision.

#location
Peel Art Gallery Museum and Archives is located at 9 Wellington Street East in downtown Brampton, Ontario.

#title
The Apparition of the Bovaird House Nursery

#summary
Visitors and investigators at a restored 19th-century farmhouse have reported seeing a misty female figure standing near an antique child's crib and hearing the cries of an infant.

#details
Historic Bovaird House, a preserved 19th-century farmhouse in Brampton, features an upstairs nursery containing an antique child's crib. According to local folklore, a ghostly, misty female figure has been spotted standing vigil beside the crib, accompanied by the faint, unexplained sounds of an infant crying. The apparition is widely believed by local residents and paranormal investigators to be the spirit of a mother watching over her child.

#location
Historic Bovaird House is located at 563 Bovaird Drive East in Brampton, Ontario.

#title
The Restless Manager of Shoppers World

#summary
Following a tragedy in the office of a former shopping centre movie theatre, overnight staff frequently experienced doors opening on their own and objects moving mysteriously.

#details
Local tradition maintains that a movie theatre once situated on the Shoppers World property in Brampton became haunted after its manager reportedly died by suicide in the administrative office. When retail businesses later occupied the footprint of the former theatre, employees working overnight shifts reported unsettling phenomena, including heavy doors swinging open unassisted, furniture shifting positions, and an oppressive, watchful presence in the back rooms.

#location
The former movie theatre site at Shoppers World is located at the intersection of Steeles Avenue and Hurontario Street in Brampton, Ontario.

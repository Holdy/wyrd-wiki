#title
Echoes at the Woodland Cultural Centre

#summary
The former residential school grounds in Brantford are marked by reports of translucent figures crossing the property followed by distant, muffled voices.

#details
Operating today as a cultural center, the building was formerly a residential school carrying a tragic history of mistreatment and abuse. Local residents and visitors have frequently reported seeing translucent human figures crossing the grounds, which quickly vanish only to be followed by the sound of distant, muffled voices echoing across the property.

#location
The Woodland Cultural Centre is located at 184 Mohawk Street in Brantford, Ontario.

#title
The Haunting of the Pauline Johnson House

#summary
The historic childhood home of renowned Canadian author Pauline Johnson is said to be haunted by aggressive unseen forces and a vanishing apparition.

#details
Built in 1856, the house where poet Pauline Johnson spent her childhood has long been a site of strange occurrences investigated by the Brantford Paranormal Society. During their visits, an investigator was physically kicked down a flight of stairs by an unseen force. Paranormal teams also documented unexplainable foul smells and a persistent apparition near the top of the stairs that repeatedly vanished into the attic whenever approached.

#location
The Pauline Johnson House is located on Mohawk Street in Brantford, Ontario.

#title
The Specter of the Sanderson Centre

#summary
A historic downtown theatre experiences violent poltergeist activity and the recurring apparition of a former manager.

#details
Opened in 1919, the Sanderson Centre remained free of paranormal claims until 2008, when a lone stagehand working in the lighting booth heard a sudden table crash next door and witnessed a vacant chair hurl across the room. Subsequent investigations by the Brantford Paranormal Society recorded further anomalies, most notably when an investigator spotted an elderly man sitting in a chair who vanished upon approach. The theatre manager later identified the apparition as the former manager who regularly sat in that exact spot with his wife to watch performances.

#location
The Sanderson Centre is located at 88 Dalhousie Street in downtown Brantford, Ontario.

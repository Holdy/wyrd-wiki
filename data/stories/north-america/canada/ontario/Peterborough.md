#title
The Ghosts of 172 Simcoe Street

#summary
The historic building at 172 Simcoe Street in Peterborough is said to be haunted by a former bar owner named Abe who accepts beer offerings and a tragic female guest from its days as the King George Hotel.

#details
Located at 172 Simcoe Street, a site that has housed various drinking establishments and the historic King George Hotel, paranormal accounts trace back to both a former owner and a 1910 guest. Abe, a former bar owner who was struck and killed by a taxi outside the building after going to get coffee, allegedly haunts the unfinished basement. Staff have reported unexplained thumps, televisions turning on and off, tampered cash registers, and complete power shutoffs. Remarkably, staff claim that leaving an open can of Labatt’s 50 pale ale in the basement appeases Abe, stopping the poltergeist activity while the can is subsequently found empty. Additionally, dating back to its time as the King George Hotel in 1910, a female guest who died by suicide is reported to haunt the upper rooms, with guests describing sightings of a woman with long straggly hair wearing a green dress watching them sleep or appearing in the shower.

#location
172 Simcoe Street in downtown Peterborough, Ontario.

#title
The Haunting of the Peterborough Lift Lock

#summary
The historic Peterborough Lift Lock on the Trent-Severn Waterway is associated with the restless spirits of workers, including an unrecovered construction worker from 1903 and a former employee named Arthur.

#details
The Peterborough Lift Lock, once the world’s tallest hydraulic boat lift, has accumulated several tragic legends and ghost stories over more than a century of operation. According to local lore, during the lift's construction in 1903, a worker fell to his death and his body could not be retrieved, leading workers to reportedly build around him within the structure. Another prominent spirit associated with the site is Arthur, a former worker who passed away from a heart attack; employees today customarily greet him by calling out "Hi, Art!" upon arriving for work. Visitors and paranormal investigators have also reported unexplained phenomena around the stairwells, such as phantom footsteps echoing on the concrete steps when no living person is present.

#location
The Peterborough Lift Lock on the Trent-Severn Waterway in Peterborough, Ontario.

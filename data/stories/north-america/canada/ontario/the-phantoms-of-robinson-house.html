The Phantoms of Robinson House

Summary

Staff and visitors at Robinson House in Oshawa frequently reported chilling paranormal encounters, including shadowy figures passing directly through people and heavy, unhappy presences lingering in the rooms.

Details

Visitor hosts and staff working at Robinson House, one of the historic buildings managed by the Oshawa Museum, documented numerous unexplainable phenomena over the years. One staff member cataloging items in the attic felt an eerie presence before a black, shadowy shape moved directly through her body, leaving her feeling nauseated. Other museum workers experienced heavy wooden doors slamming shut by themselves with solid thumps when no one was nearby, while visitors sensitive to the paranormal reported sensing a heavy, deeply unhappy presence lingering within the historic walls.

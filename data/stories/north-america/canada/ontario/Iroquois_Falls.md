#title
The Ghosts of Hotel Iroquois

#summary
Children growing up in the 1960s at the Hotel Iroquois experienced terrifying encounters with spectral phenomena, bone-chilling sounds, and eerie spirits lurking in the building's basement and ice rooms.

#details
During the 1960s, Kim Price and her sister Alexa Wollan lived at the Hotel Iroquois in Iroquois Falls while their father managed the property for the Abitibi Paper Company. The children frequently had to brave the hotel's terrifying boiler room, where the furnace banged mysteriously amidst deep darkness, as well as the creepy, creaking abandoned ice room. Price maintained a firm conviction that spirits and ghosts haunted the hotel's dark basement before the structure was eventually demolished in 1977.

#location
The former site of Hotel Iroquois, situated right outside the gate of the Abitibi paper mill in Iroquois Falls, Ontario.

#title
The Legend of the Doomed War Party

#summary
An Iroquois war party was lured to their watery doom over a roaring northern waterfall by a captive Cree guide who tricked them into navigating the deadly rapids.

#details
According to traditional Indigenous folklore, an Iroquois war party attacked a Cree camp along the river, taking plunder and sparing one woman to serve as their guide. When the war party approached the precipice of the massive waterfall, the clever guide told them they could shoot the rapids if the canoes were lightened, stepping ashore herself to take the portage path. Realizing too late that the current was impossibly swift and the drop deadly, the trapped Iroquois headed their canoes over the falls while singing their war songs, plunging to their deaths on the rocks below.

#location
Iroquois Falls on the Abitibi River in northeastern Ontario.

#upstream-source
Katharine Berry Judson, *Myths and Legends of British North America*.

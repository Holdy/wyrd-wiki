#title
The Phantom Organ and Feasts of Sharon Temple

#summary
Visitors and staff at the historic Sharon Temple have reported hearing phantom music, ghostly singing, and the sounds of a large feast being prepared in empty buildings.

#details
Built in 1832 by the Children of Peace in the village of Sharon, the Sharon Temple is an open-air museum and National Historic Site. Over the years, visitors and paranormal investigators have reported hearing inexplicable sounds echoing through the historic structure. Accounts include hearing faint organ or piano music and ethereal singing coming from above when the building is entirely locked and vacant, as well as auditory phenomena resembling a large feast or gathering being prepared.

#location
The Sharon Temple is located at 18974 Leslie Street in East Gwillimbury, Ontario.

#title
The Restless Rooms of the Newmarket Inn

#summary
Guests staying at the Newmarket Inn have reported phantom activity, including beds shaking autonomously and apparitions appearing in rooms.

#details
Located along Yonge Street in East Gwillimbury, the Newmarket Inn has accumulated a reputation for paranormal occurrences reported by travelers and guests. Visitors have described disturbing experiences such as beds shaking violently as if someone is trapped underneath, phantom figures appearing in room corners, and mysterious sounds of running water or disembodied voices echoing through the halls.

#location
The Newmarket Inn is located at 18667 Yonge Street in East Gwillimbury, Ontario.

#upstream-source
None.

#reference
https://www.hauntedplaces.org/item/newmarket-inn/

#title
The Self-Swinging Swings of Anchor Park

#summary
Local legend and visitor accounts surrounding Anchor Park tell of a swing set that moves on its own and shadowy figures following visitors after dark.

#details
Situated in the community of Holland Landing within East Gwillimbury, Anchor Park has long been the subject of local ghost lore. According to local folklore and visitor reports, a middle swing on the playground set is frequently observed moving independently with no wind or human presence. Witnesses have also reported hearing faint screams, encountering car engine failures near the park at midnight, and spotting strange shadowy figures or a white apparition near the grounds.

#location
Anchor Park is located at the intersection of Doane Road and Yonge Street in Holland Landing, East Gwillimbury, Ontario.

None.

#title
The Phantom of Sadler's Pond

#summary
A young woman named Andrea, who hanged herself after discovering her boyfriend's infidelity, is said to haunt the waters and wooded trails of Sadler's Pond at night.

#details
Local legend and firsthand accounts maintain that Sadler's Pond in Belle River is haunted by the spirit of a young woman named Andrea. According to folklore, Andrea hanged herself from a tree near the water after learning of her boyfriend's unfaithful actions. Visitors walking the trail after dark have reported encountering a transparent apparition wearing a dress with a rope tied around her neck, sometimes standing silently or screaming her partner's name. Other witnesses have described experiencing sudden, unnatural internal coldness, malfunctioning camera equipment, floating orbs, and sightings of an eerie, glowing orange face in the surrounding trees.

#location
Sadler's Pond, located off Keown Street near Belle River in the Town of Lakeshore, Ontario.

#title
The Restless Soldiers of Drummond Hill Cemetery

#summary
Serving as the burial ground for casualties of the 1814 Battle of Lundy's Lane, Drummond Hill Cemetery is frequently reported to be haunted by phantom soldiers marching among the graves.

#details
Drummond Hill Cemetery holds immense historical significance as the site of the Battle of Lundy’s Lane during the War of 1812, one of the deadliest military engagements in Canadian history. Because numerous soldiers who died in combat were buried directly on the grounds, visitors, local residents, and ghost tour guides have long reported paranormal activity. Among the most frequent accounts are sightings of ghostly British soldiers wearing Royal Scots uniforms or red coats, seen limping across the headstones or marching silently at dusk before vanishing into thin air. Witnesses have also reported shadowy figures, glowing orbs, and distant cries echoing through the night.

#location
Drummond Hill Cemetery is located at 611 Lundy's Lane in Niagara Falls, Ontario.

#title
The Screaming Tunnel

#summary
A limestone drainage tunnel on the outskirts of Niagara Falls is haunted by the ghost of a young girl who died in a fire, and striking a match inside supposedly summons her terrifying scream.

#details
Built in the early 20th century as a limestone drainage culvert beneath a railway line, the Screaming Tunnel has become one of the region's most famous ghost legends. According to local folklore preserved in community archives, a young girl fleeing a burning barn with her clothes and hair ablaze stumbled into the tunnel and perished from her burns. Another variation of the legend claims she was driven into the tunnel and set on fire by an enraged father. Tradition dictates that if a person enters the passage at midnight and strikes a wooden match, an unseen force will extinguish it amidst a blood-curdling shriek. Alternatively, oral history suggests the name originated from a local woman who frequently screamed in the tunnel during domestic disputes.

#location
The Screaming Tunnel is located just off Warner Road on the northern outskirts of Niagara Falls, Ontario.

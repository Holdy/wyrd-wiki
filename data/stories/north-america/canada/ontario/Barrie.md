#title
The Carnegie’s Quiet Guard

#summary
A heritage library building in downtown Barrie is rumored to be haunted by an elderly ghost who continues to watch over the space.

#details
Constructed in 1915 with funding from the Andrew Carnegie Foundation, the original Beaux Arts-style library building on Mulcaster Street has long served as a cultural landmark in Barrie. Although the public library has since relocated, the heritage building—now part of the MacLaren Art Centre—is whispered to be haunted by a dedicated former staff member or patron. Visitors and staff have reported inexplicable cold spots and objects moving on their own, attributed to an old soul so devoted to the institution that they never truly left.

#location
The MacLaren Art Centre (formerly the Carnegie Public Library) is located at 37 Mulcaster Street in Barrie, Ontario.

#title
The Unfinished Justice of Elizabeth Meyer

#summary
The ghost of a woman found dead outside a 19th-century hotel is said to haunt the building seeking justice for her mysterious demise.

#details
In January 1872, a woman named Elizabeth Meyer was discovered lifeless at the front entrance of the Simcoe Hotel in Barrie on a bitterly cold night. Although a coroner's inquest concluded her death was due to exposure and intemperance, contemporary newspaper accounts noted that her body bore bruises and injuries consistent with physical blows. Decades later, staff working in the historic building have reported feeling a chilling female presence in the stone basement and near the entrance—believed to be Elizabeth's restless spirit lingering to reveal the true circumstances of her death.

#location
The Simcoe Hotel was located at the corner of Bayfield Street and Dunlop Street East in Barrie, Ontario.

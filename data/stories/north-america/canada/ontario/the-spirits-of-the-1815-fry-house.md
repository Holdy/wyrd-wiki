#title
The Spirits of the 1815 Fry House

#summary
Built by early pioneers along Twenty Mile Creek, the historic 1815 Fry House in Jordan Village is said to retain the lingering presences and unexplained spectral echoes of its original inhabitants.

#details
Constructed in 1815, the Fry House stands as one of the oldest surviving structures in Jordan Village, reflecting the rugged early settlement era of the Niagara Benchlands. Local folklore and heritage accounts passed down through generations suggest that the deep historical roots of the house have left an imprint on the property. Visitors and local historians participating in heritage tours have reported heavy, unexplained footsteps echoing through the timber-framed rooms and sudden atmospheric chill inside the historic home, widely attributed to the lingering spirits of the pioneer Fry family who once farmed and lived along the creek.

#location
The 1815 Fry House is located on Main Street within the historic community of Jordan Village in the Town of Lincoln, Ontario.

    The Stairwell Spectre of Grand Valley

    Summary

    Following his tragic murder in 1915, the ghost of Harry Strutt is said to haunt a farmhouse on the 4th Concession, where witnesses report the ghostly re-enactment of his fatal shooting on the stairs.

    Details

    On November 2, 1915, a violent dispute erupted on a farm located on the 4th Concession of East Luther, just outside Grand Valley. Thomas Duckworth shot his brother-in-law, Harry Strutt, in the heart with a .44-calibre rifle during an argument over furniture. Fatally wounded, Strutt was assisted by his sister to the top of the stairwell, where he collapsed and tumbled downward toward the front entrance. According to local folklore and historical archives curated by the Museum of Dufferin, Strutt's restless spirit remained tethered to the home, where witnesses have reported eerie phenomena and spectral re-enactments of his collapse on the stairs.

#title
Spirits of the Penetanguishene Centennial Museum

#summary
Staff and visitors at the historic Penetanguishene Centennial Museum frequently hear disembodied footsteps, mysterious store bells ringing, and phantom voices echoing through the 1875 building.

#details
Originally constructed in 1875 by lumber magnate Charles Beck as a general store and lumber office, the building now serves as the Penetanguishene Centennial Museum. Staff, volunteers, and paranormal investigators have reported countless supernatural encounters, including doors opening and closing on their own, the distinct chime of an entrance bell that no longer exists, and apparitions of figures dressed in 19th-century attire. Psychics and mediums who have investigated the museum sensed multiple spirits, including Charles Beck himself acting as a spiritual gatekeeper, a World War I soldier, and a troubled former resident. Despite the eerie activity, visitors and staff note that the lingering presences are entirely friendly.

#location
The Penetanguishene Centennial Museum is located in Penetanguishene, Ontario.

#title
The Haunting of the Carl Beck House

#summary
Visitors and overnight guests at the historic 19th-century Carl Beck House in Penetanguishene report phantom footsteps, flickering lights, and the lingering spirit of the builder's disinherited daughter.

#details
Built in 1866 by wealthy lumber magnate Charles Beck, the grand Italianate mansion in Penetanguishene has long been reputed as one of Ontario's most haunted buildings. Over the years, residents, paranormal investigators, and overnight guests have reported unexplained phenomena, including objects moving on their own, phantom whispers, and blankets being tugged in the night. Local legend attributes much of the paranormal activity to former family members who died in the home, as well as Charles's eldest daughter, Mary. According to local lore, Mary haunts the halls in protest after being left with only one dollar in her father's multi-million dollar will following a family dispute over her marriage. Apparitions of Victorian-era women have also been witnessed strolling along adjacent Church Street outside the mansion.

#location
The Carl Beck House is a historic mansion located on Church Street in Penetanguishene, Ontario.

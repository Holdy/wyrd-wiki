#title
The Eternal Gratitude of Whitehern Mansion

#summary
Staff members at Whitehern Mansion in downtown Hamilton have reported feeling watched and encountering disembodied legs, believed to be the grateful spirits of the historic McQuesten family.

#details
Whitehern Mansion, tucked away behind Hamilton City Hall, was the grand home of the influential McQuesten family starting in 1852. Following the financial ruin and tragic suicide of Isaac McQuesten in his study in 1888, his family's legacy was saved and the property was ultimately preserved as a museum. Over the years, workers and museum staff maintaining the historic estate have reported unexplainable phenomena. In one notable account, a worker named Jennifer turned around while arranging velvet ropes near an antique seat and saw a pair of disembodied legs standing behind her before they vanished, leaving behind an overwhelming sense of calm and protective gratitude from the spirits who still care for the home.

#location
Whitehern Historic House and Garden is located at 41 Jackson Street West in downtown Hamilton, Ontario, directly behind Hamilton City Hall.

#title
The Woman in Black of the Hamilton Custom House

#summary
The historic Hamilton Custom House is said to be haunted by a grieving spirit who arrived on an immigrant ship and tragically died before she could reunite with her loved one.

#details
Located near Bayfront Park, the Hamilton Custom House is a striking 19th-century landmark that harbors one of the city's most famous ghost legends. According to local lore, a young woman arrived in Hamilton on an immigrant ship and passed away shortly after stepping onto the docks. Her body was reportedly buried on the grounds where the Custom House was later built. Ever since, stories have persisted of a "Woman in Black" roaming the building and grounds, endlessly waiting for her lost partner to arrive.

#location
The Hamilton Custom House is located at 51 Stuart Street East near Bayfront Park in Hamilton, Ontario.

#title
The Spirits of Macaulay House

#summary
Built in 1830 for Prince Edward County’s first Anglican reverend, Macaulay House in Picton is reported to be haunted by gentle spectral figures, including a little girl in a white dress.

#details
Located at 23 Church Street in Picton, Macaulay House was constructed in 1830 for Reverend William Macaulay. Over the years, staff and visitors have reported numerous gentle paranormal occurrences within the historic home. Most notably, a phantom little girl wearing a white dress has been frequently sighted haunting the dining room, adding to the lore of the heritage property.

#location
Macaulay House, 23 Church Street, Picton, Ontario.

#title
The Legend and Haunting of White Otter Castle

#summary
Built by an eccentric hermit driven by a childhood curse, the remote three-story log castle on White Otter Lake north of Atikokan is said to be haunted by its creator after he drowned in 1918.

#details
James Alexander "Jimmy" McOuat spent years single-handedly constructing an elaborate three-story log castle with a turret on the shores of remote White Otter Lake, north of Atikokan, driven by a childhood prophecy that he would "die in a shack." Though he built the magnificent structure hoping to win the hand of a noblewoman, he tragically drowned while fishing on the lake in 1918. Today, visitors and locals report that McOuat's restless spirit still lingers around his lonely wilderness castle.

#location
White Otter Castle on White Otter Lake, north of Atikokan, Ontario.

#title
The Crying Boy of Gananoque Town Hall

#summary
Children playing in the park near Gananoque's historic town hall have reported hearing and seeing the weeping apparition of a young man named Percy Ireland.

#details
Percy Ireland, whose family roots run deep in the McDonald family of Gananoque, spent his youth visiting his grandparents' grand brick home. After passing away in British Columbia and being transported back east by train, his restless spirit drifted back to his childhood home—which now serves as the Gananoque Town Hall. Haunting the northwest corner of the building where he once hid from relatives as a boy, Percy's profound sorrow is so powerful that children playing in the park have heard him crying and occasionally caught sight of his spectral form.

#location
The northwest corner of the Gananoque Town Hall located at 30 King Street East, Gananoque, Ontario.

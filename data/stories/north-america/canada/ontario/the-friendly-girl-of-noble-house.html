The Friendly Girl of Noble House

Summary

A young ghostly girl haunts the historic Noble House at the Georgina Village Museum, greeting visitors with a soft hello and turning on electric candles.

Details

Located on the grounds of the Georgina Village Museum, the historic Noble House is widely recognized by staff and paranormal investigators as a hub of local ghostly activity. Among the reports is the recurring presence of a little girl who haunts the building. Described as entirely friendly, she is frequently seen playing inside the rooms. She is known to turn on electric candles by herself and has occasionally surprised visitors exploring the house by whispering a faint, welcoming "hello."

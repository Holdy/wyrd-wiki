The Spirits of Orangeville Town Hall

Summary

Orangeville's historic Town Hall is said to be haunted by lingering spirits, including a spectral woman who haunts the former Opera House and entities linked to coroner inquests held in the building's 19th-century morgue.

Details

Built in 1875 at 87 Broadway, Orangeville’s Town Hall has long served as a community hub, but its past includes darker functions, such as coroner inquests and examinations of suspicious or unlawful deaths in the late 1800s and early 1900s, with the meat locker of the market doubling as a temporary morgue. Mediums visiting the historic building have reported the presence of lingering spirits seeking justice or closure. Furthermore, the second-floor assembly hall—transformed into the Orangeville Opera House around 1876—is reportedly haunted by the figure of a woman descending a set of stairs before vanishing into thin air, most frequently seen near the back seats where the original stage stood. These investigations and hauntings were prominently documented by the paranormal research group The Searcher Group.

#title
The Specter of the Right of Way Headframe

#summary
The historic headframe of the Right of Way Mine in Cobalt is said to be haunted by violent pounding noises and phantom mists, recalling the tragic deaths of miners who perished deep underground.

#details
During tours of the towering Right of Way Mine headframe—which dives 164 meters into the bedrock beneath Cobalt Lake—tour guide Dan Larocque experienced terrifying unexplained pounding on the lower floor and windows while completely alone on the third level. Photographic evidence from the site frequently reveals thick unexplainable mists and floating orbs. The mine claimed the lives of up to 27 men during its operation, many of whose bodies were never recovered from the stygian darkness.

#location
The Right of Way Headframe stands at the north end of Cobalt Lake in Cobalt, Ontario.

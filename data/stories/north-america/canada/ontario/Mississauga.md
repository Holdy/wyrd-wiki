#title
The Disappearing Groom of Cherry Hill

#summary
Following his wedding celebration at a historic Mississauga home, George Silverthorn stepped outside to answer a visitor at the door and vanished without a trace. Ever since, locals report that whenever his disappearance is discussed, the front door opens and closes on its own.

#details
Cherry Hill House, built by the Silverthorn family on a 200-acre cherry orchard at 680 Silver Creek Boulevard, carries a chilling legend surrounding the youngest son, George. After seeking his fortune in the California gold rush, George returned home to marry. During the wedding reception, a knock came at the door; George stepped outside to speak with an unknown visitor and was never seen again. Alongside this tragic disappearance, staff and patrons have reported ghostly phenomena, including the phantom sightings of a small girl in upstairs windows, dimming lights, cold spots, objects moving on their own, and mirrors shifting on the walls.

#location
Cherry Hill House is located at 680 Silver Creek Boulevard in Mississauga, Ontario.

#title
The Eternal Celebration at Benares House

#summary
Visitors standing outside the historic Benares estate have reported hearing the unmistakable sounds of a lively party echoing from an empty parlor. Preserved with the original family's possessions, the house is believed to exist in a timeless state where past spirits continue their celebrations.

#details
Benares Historic House, a Georgian-style estate built in 1857 on Clarkson Road North, remained home to four generations of the Harris family before becoming a museum. Because the home is filled with the original family's authentic possessions and furnishings, investigators from paranormal research groups note that the atmosphere feels uniquely suspended in time. Visitors and passersby have recounted standing outside the front door and hearing distinct sounds of a bustling social gathering and party echoing from the parlor inside, even when the building is entirely unoccupied.

#location
Benares Historic House is located at 1507 Clarkson Road North in Mississauga, Ontario.

#title
The Polite Poltergeist of Franklin House

#summary
A benevolent spirit named Jessie is said to haunt a historic Streetsville hotel, occasionally turning lights on and off or setting up pool tables. Seances conducted on the property revealed her presence, cementing the building's reputation for paranormal activity.

#details
Located in the historic heart of Streetsville, the Franklin House has long been associated with ghostly lore. According to paranormal researchers and local accounts documented by author Terry Boyle, a gentle female spirit named Jessie lingers within the building. Patrons and staff have experienced her benevolent poltergeist abilities, which include operating light switches, letting a dog out into the night, and arranging beer bottles on the pool table. While her exact origins remain mysterious, her playful and harmless manifestations have made her a well-known fixture of local folklore.

#location
Franklin House is situated in Streetsville, Mississauga, Ontario.

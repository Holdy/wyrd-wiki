The Apparition of the Bovaird House Nursery

Summary

Visitors and investigators at a restored 19th-century farmhouse have reported seeing a misty female figure standing near an antique child's crib and hearing the cries of an infant.

Details

Historic Bovaird House, a preserved 19th-century farmhouse in Brampton, features an upstairs nursery containing an antique child's crib. According to local folklore, a ghostly, misty female figure has been spotted standing vigil beside the crib, accompanied by the faint, unexplained sounds of an infant crying. The apparition is widely believed by local residents and paranormal investigators to be the spirit of a mother watching over her child.

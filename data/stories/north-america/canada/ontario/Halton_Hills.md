#title
The Guardian of Acton Town Hall

#summary
According to local legend, the ghost of a former caretaker named Jimmy still watches over Acton Town Hall after remaining spiritually tied to the historic building he once saved from a fire.

#details
Acton Town Hall, an 1883 Italianate heritage building, is said to be haunted by its former caretaker, Jimmy. According to the legend, Jimmy woke up one night smelling smoke from a pot-bellied stove in the auditorium and heroically formed a one-man bucket brigade to put out the blaze while ringing the bell tower to alert the town. To this day, staff and visitors believe his spirit remains in the building to protect the historic hall and ensure its safety.

#location
Acton Town Hall is located at the southeast corner of Bower and Willow streets in Acton, Halton Hills, Ontario.

#title
The Haunting of Cedarvale Park

#summary
Local paranormal investigators have explored historic buildings within Georgetown's Cedarvale Park following reports of eerie activity and unexplainable encounters from park staff.

#details
Cedarvale Park in Georgetown has become a focal point for local ghost lore, prompting paranormal investigators to examine its historic structures. Park staff and visitors have reported unsettling experiences inside one of the park's older buildings, which local accounts associate with uncanny phenomena, such as unexplained sounds and the perceived presence of a mischievous spirit.

#location
Cedarvale Park is situated in Georgetown within the Town of Halton Hills, Ontario.

#title
The Spirit of Devereaux House

#summary
Paranormal investigators and psychic mediums have reported encounters with a lingering spirit named "Anna" at the historic 1860s Devereaux farmhouse.

#details
Built in the 1860s, the Devereaux House has long been the subject of local ghost stories and investigations by groups like The Searcher Group and spiritual advisors from the InSpirit Centre. During blind tests and investigations, visiting medium students repeatedly sensed a presence named Anna—matching historical records of a former resident—and team members experienced unexplained phenomena such as phantom physical touches and disembodied voices.

#location
Devereaux House is located at 11494 Trafalgar Road in Halton Hills, Ontario.

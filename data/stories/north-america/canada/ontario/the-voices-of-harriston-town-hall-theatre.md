#title
The Voices of Harriston Town Hall Theatre

#summary
Long-standing rumors of a haunting at the historic Harriston Town Hall Theatre were corroborated by paranormal investigators who recorded disembodied singing and electronic voice phenomena.

#details
For years, municipal employees and local residents working alone in the Harriston Town Hall Theatre during the early morning hours reported hearing heavy, unexplainable footsteps echoing through the empty building. During a community storytelling event exploring local folklore, former employee Gary MacDougall shared his own unsettling experiences of hearing footsteps while alone. To investigate these claims, the Wellington County Paranormal Investigators set up infrared cameras and audio equipment for an overnight stakeout. During their investigation, they captured electronic voice phenomena (EVPs), including a distinct female voice asking, "Do you want to help me?", as well as faint, disembodied singing echoing from the balcony.

#location
The Harriston Town Hall Theatre is located on Elora Street South in Harriston, Ontario.

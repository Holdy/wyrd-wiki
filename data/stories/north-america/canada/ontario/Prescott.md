#title
The Phantom Sentinels of Fort Wellington

#summary
Staff and visitors at Fort Wellington have repeatedly documented phantom soldiers, inexplicable footsteps triggering security alarms, and spectral apparitions inside the historic blockhouse.

#details
Fort Wellington in Prescott, Ontario, has never faced military attack, but its grounds and three-story blockhouse are famously haunted by restless spirits. In one widely recounted account, a re-enactor practicing his fife on the second floor was approached by a soldier from the War of 1812 who introduced himself as Terrence Anderson and expressed approval of the music. In another chilling incident during the winter, motion sensor alarms in the blockhouse repeatedly triggered, prompting a team of investigators to search the building. They heard heavy footsteps pacing the third-floor catwalk and entering the core of the blockhouse without any visible person present. Other staff members over the decades have reported doors slamming violently by themselves, cats hissing at empty corners, and mysterious figures walking through locked gates into the dark courtyard.

#location
Fort Wellington National Historic Site is located at 370 Vankoughnet Street in Prescott, Ontario.

#title
The Restless Spirits of the Blue Church

#summary
The historic Blue Church and its surrounding burying ground near Prescott are reputed to be haunted by early religious pioneers and the spirit of a murdered parish clerk.

#details
Located just west of Prescott along Ontario Route 2, the Blue Church rests on hallowed ground established in 1790. It serves as the final resting place of Barbara Heck, widely known as the founder of Methodism in Upper Canada, who was buried there in 1804. Generations of locals and visitors walking through the antique cemetery have reported eerie phenomena, including sudden cold spots, whispered voices, and shadowy figures pacing among the headstones. Among the enduring legends of the churchyard is the ghost of George Stelz, a former parish clerk whose brutal murder in 1897 is said to tether his restless spirit to the sacred grounds, where people occasionally report catching sight of an unsettled figure lingering near the old graves.

#location
The Blue Church is located at 1512 Ontario Route 2, approximately 4 km west of Prescott, Ontario.

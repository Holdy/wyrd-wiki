The Phantom Sentinels of Fort Wellington

Summary

Staff and visitors at Fort Wellington have repeatedly documented phantom soldiers, inexplicable footsteps triggering security alarms, and spectral apparitions inside the historic blockhouse.

Details

Fort Wellington in Prescott, Ontario, has never faced military attack, but its grounds and three-story blockhouse are famously haunted by restless spirits. In one widely recounted account, a re-enactor practicing his fife on the second floor was approached by a soldier from the War of 1812 who introduced himself as Terrence Anderson and expressed approval of the music. In another chilling incident during the winter, motion sensor alarms in the blockhouse repeatedly triggered, prompting a team of investigators to search the building. They heard heavy footsteps pacing the third-floor catwalk and entering the core of the blockhouse without any visible person present. Other staff members over the decades have reported doors slamming violently by themselves, cats hissing at empty corners, and mysterious figures walking through locked gates into the dark courtyard.

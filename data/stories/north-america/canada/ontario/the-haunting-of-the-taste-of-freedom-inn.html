The Haunting of the Taste of Freedom Inn

Summary

Guests and staff at the Taste of Freedom Inn in Tottenham have reported supernatural encounters, including rattling door handles and phantom figures wandering the halls.

Details

Built in 1883 as a bank, the Taste of Freedom Inn in Tottenham retains its original vaulted ceilings and bank vault. Paranormal investigations and guest reports reveal that the historic building is far from empty. Witnesses have reported seeing an apparition wearing a red smoking jacket or housecoat walking the main floor and adjoining hallways. Additionally, visitors and investigators frequently hear door handles rattling violently during the night in upstairs bedrooms—a phenomenon attributed to an old banker's habit of checking locks to secure the building against intruders.

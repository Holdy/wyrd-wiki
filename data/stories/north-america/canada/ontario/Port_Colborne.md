#title
The Curse and Hauntings of Hopkins Tomb

#summary
Samuel Hopkins' grand stone mausoleum in Oakwood Cemetery is said to be haunted by the wealthy businessman, his faithful dog, and burdened by a curse that strikes anyone who disturbs the tomb.

#details
Built for wealthy Port Colborne businessman Samuel Hopkins, who died in 1899, the imposing stone mausoleum in Oakwood Cemetery has long been the center of eerie town folklore. Locals and paranormal investigators report hearing strange sounds from within the crypt, witnessing the apparition of Hopkins walking the cemetery grounds, and spotting his loyal dog guarding the stone steps or howling on nights of the full moon. Over the decades, thrill-seekers and vandals who broke into the vault reportedly suffered bizarre and tragic fates—such as severe accidents resulting in lost limbs—adding weight to tales of a lingering curse that can supposedly only be broken by walking around the tomb seven times and knocking on the door.

#location
Hopkins Tomb is situated within Oakwood Cemetery on Lakeshore Road West, just west of Quarry Road in Port Colborne, Ontario.

#title
The Spectral Residents of Roselawn Centre

#summary
Built in 1860, the historic Roselawn Centre in Port Colborne is reported to be haunted by spectral figures wandering its staircases and peering from its upper windows.

#details
Dating back to the 1860s, the grand Second Empire-style Roselawn Centre stands as a prominent historic landmark on Fielden Avenue. Former employees, visitors, and paranormal investigators have consistently recounted chilling encounters with unseen entities and spectral figures. Apparitions are said to manifest on the building's staircases and gaze out from the lonely window at the very top of the three-storey structure, cementing its reputation as one of the region's most active haunted locations.

#location
The Roselawn Centre is located on Fielden Avenue in Port Colborne, Ontario.

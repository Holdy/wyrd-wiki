#title
The Creature of Spruce Road

#summary
While walking along a deserted lakeside road in late summer, a local resident encountered a small, dark entity with a human face and glowing eyes that exuded a paralyzing primal terror. The apparition vanished silently into the woods when caught in the headlights of an oncoming car.

#details
In late August 2007, an individual walking without lights near Spruce Beach and Spruce Road in Elliot Lake experienced a sudden, eerie silence where all natural forest noises ceased. Shortly after, a small, pitch-black creature measuring between three-quarters of a foot and a foot and a half tall stepped onto the roadside. While its body resembled a mix between a cat and a dog without a tail, it possessed an almost perfect human face and glowing eyes. The entity walked toward the witness from about sixty to ninety feet away, instilling an overwhelming, deep primal fear and an icy chill. Just as the witness felt utterly defenseless, a car's headlights swept across the road, causing the creature to dart silently into the surrounding woods.

#location
The encounter took place along Spruce Road near Spruce Beach in Elliot Lake, Ontario.

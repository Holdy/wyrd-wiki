#title
The Headless Spectre of the Oxford County Courthouse

#summary
Following his botched 1862 execution where he was accidentally decapitated, the ghost of Thomas Cook is said to haunt the halls and cells of the Woodstock courthouse and gaol.

#details
Thomas Cook, a blind labourer from Innerkip, was convicted of murdering his wife and sentenced to hang at the Oxford County Gaol in Woodstock on December 16, 1862. Because his heavy weight was not properly calculated for the drop, the fall severed his head from his body, making it the most infamous execution in the county's history. According to local legend, his body was subsequently exhumed from a secret grave near Finkle and Dundas Street where medical students had buried it after acquiring it illicitly. Over the decades, prisoners and renovation workers alike reported terrifying encounters with a restless phantom—believed to be Cook searching for his missing head—cowering in the jail cells and roaming the upper floors of the courthouse.

#location
The Oxford County Courthouse and former Gaol located at Hunter Street and Finkle Street in Woodstock, Ontario.

#title
The Phantom Drunkard of Zorra Township

#summary
For twenty years after breaking his neck in a drunken wagon accident, the ghost of Robert Gourley haunted a rural side road near Woodstock, lamenting his thirst in Gaelic.

#details
Robert Gourley and his brothers were notorious for hard drinking, though they made a weekly habit of driving eight kilometers to attend church services in nearby Embro. One Sunday afternoon in the late summer, after finishing church, the brothers began drinking heavily on their return journey. When the wagon hit a bump on a dusty Zorra Township path, Robert was thrown from his perch and slammed headfirst into a tree root, breaking his neck. He was found the next day still clutching his broken whisky bottle. For two decades, travellers along that wooded side road reported encountering a stout, shadowy figure with glowing red eyes who groaned and called out for "deoch"—the Gaelic word for drink—until a church elder finally confronted the spirit and heard its last confession.

#location
A rural side road in Zorra Township near Embro, just outside Woodstock, Ontario.

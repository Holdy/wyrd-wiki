#title
The Haunting of Texas Road

#summary
Texas Road is renowned for local legends involving a phantom Jeep, swinging lanterns, and restless spirits haunting its bridges and rural roadside.

#details
Spanning parts of Essex County including Lakeshore, Texas Road has earned a reputation as one of the region's most actively haunted routes. Local folklore attributes the eerie phenomena to several tragic events, including an 1881 murder where a man's body was discovered in a roadside ditch. Drivers frequently report seeing a lantern swinging wildly in the dark, phantom horses, and a headless horseman. A particularly chilling legend surrounds a bridge on the road where a driver crashed his vehicle, losing his girlfriend in the creek; distraught, he took his own life, and motorists today report being chased by a ghostly single-headlight Jeep that vanishes into thin air. Additionally, a blood-stained woman in white is said to wander the roadside and the adjacent cemetery areas.

#location
Texas Road bridge and adjacent areas in Lakeshore, Ontario.

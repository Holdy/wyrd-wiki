#title
The Unearthed Bones of Victoria Park

#summary
Historic walking tours in Brockville highlight the unsettling past of Victoria Park and the First Presbyterian Church, where human remains and skulls were once unearthed and scattered by children in earlier decades.

#details
Although Victoria Park is now a peaceful green space and public park where children play, local historical lore and museum-led walking tours remind residents of its grim origins as an old burial ground. Standing before the First Presbyterian Church across from the Brockville Courthouse, guides recount dark historical accounts of bones being unearthed during early town development. According to local historical accounts shared on the town's haunted histories tours, children in past eras occasionally discovered human remains and skulls protruding from the earth or displaced near the old cemetery boundaries, leaving enduring legends of restless spirits haunting the older corridors of Brockville.

#location
Victoria Park and the First Presbyterian Church near the Brockville Courthouse, Brockville, Ontario.

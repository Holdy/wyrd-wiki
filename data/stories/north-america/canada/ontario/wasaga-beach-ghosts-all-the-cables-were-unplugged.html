    Wasaga Beach Ghosts: All The Cables Were Unplugged

    Summary

    A resident experiencing eerie electronic disturbances and apparitions at her home learns that a man had committed suicide earlier that same day by jumping off the nearby blue bridge at Beach 1.

    Details

    On a cold, rainy summer night in Wasaga Beach, a resident named Amanda was home alone watching a movie when her television suddenly turned off by itself. Hearing a knock at the window and finding nothing outside, she attempted to resume her movie only to discover that every cable connected to the television had been completely unplugged. After reconnecting them and tuning into the evening news, another knock sounded at the window, and a painting crashed down from the wall above the television. Turning around, she saw a bright flash and the apparition of a soaking wet man in a black hoodie standing on her back porch. When she rushed to call 911, her fully charged phone instantly died, and her phone charger cord in the kitchen was mysteriously broken. Terrified, she fled to her neighbour's house. When she described the man she had seen, her neighbour consulted a local newspaper and revealed that a man matching that exact description had died earlier that day after jumping from the blue bridge at Beach 1.

The Phantoms of Amherstburg's Historic Houses

Summary

Staff and visitors at two historic Amherstburg buildings frequently encounter ghostly apparitions, unexplained piano music, and disembodied voices from the 19th century.

Details

Park House Museum and Gordon House in Amherstburg are steeped in paranormal activity spanning over two centuries. At the Park House Museum, visitors and curators have reported encounters with the spirit of Mrs. Park on the second floor, where doors open and close by themselves, cradles rock unassisted, and a phantom woman in a black dress has been mistaken for a living staff member while giving tours. Nearby at Gordon House—which served as a home in the early 1800s and later a maternity house—witnesses have heard phantom piano music in parlors without pianos, crying babies in the former birthing room, fully dressed soldiers from the War of 1812 walking the halls, and even an office desk hovering in mid-air before gently settling back down.

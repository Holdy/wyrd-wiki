#title
The Ghost of the Old Squire at Ruthven Mansion

#summary
Following the death of prominent local founder David Thompson in 1851, residents blamed the mysterious midnight collapse of the Cayuga Courthouse stone façade on his departing spirit. Today, his ghost is said to still haunt the historic Ruthven Mansion grounds.

#details
David Thompson served as a messenger under General Isaac Brock during the War of 1812 before establishing the Grand River Navigation Company and founding the Town of Indiana in Haldimand County. In 1845, he constructed the grand Ruthven Mansion near Cayuga, where he lived and ran his business until his death at age 58 in 1851. On the exact night of his death, a tremendous crash shook nearby Cayuga: the heavy stone front façade of the newly built Cayuga Courthouse mysteriously collapsed to the ground. A local reporter noted the identical timing and playfully suggested that Thompson's spirit had knocked it down as a final dramatic exit, giving birth to the enduring legend of "The Ould Squire's Ghost."

#location
Ruthven Park National Historic Site, located along Highway 54 in Cayuga, Haldimand County, Ontario.

#title
The Haunting of the Old Town Hall and Jail

#summary
Built in 1857 and featuring basement jail cells, the historic Caledonia Old Town Hall is said to be haunted by the spirit of beloved local constable William Stotts, who lived and worked in the building for decades.

#details
Constructed in 1857, the two-story brick town hall served as the heart of municipal governance and law enforcement in Caledonia, housing stone jail cells in its basement until 1945. For nearly twenty years, from 1937 to 1955, constable William Stotts and his wife lived in an apartment on the building's main floor. Stotts was widely known in the community as a gentle, dependable protector who handled policing, traffic direction, and animal control with fairness and dedication until his death in 1959. Local paranormal investigators and visitors report that the historic hall remains active with ghostly phenomena, with many believing that Constable Stotts continues to watch over his former home and workplace.

#location
The Old Town Hall in Caledonia, Haldimand County, Ontario.

#title
Shadows of Rolph Street and the Station Arts Centre

#summary
Historic public and cultural buildings in downtown Tillsonburg, including Rolph Street Public School and the Station Arts Centre, are legendary locations where educators and residents have reported eerie paranormal encounters.

#details
During historical research and local ghost walk initiatives across Tillsonburg, teachers and staff from institutions like Rolph Street Public School have recounted unsettling, unexplained encounters within the aging school halls. Nearby cultural landmarks downtown, such as the Station Arts Centre on Bridge Street West, have similarly been tied to local ghost lore, whispers of secret societies, and unexplained nighttime occurrences that anchor the town's guided Eerie Encounters tours.

#location
The Station Arts Centre is located at 41 Bridge Street West in Tillsonburg, Ontario.

Research compiled by the Downtown Tillsonburg BIA and published in the Tillsonburg Post.

#title
The Victorian Echoes of Annandale House

#summary
Built by Tillsonburg's first mayor in the late 19th century, Annandale House is a magnificent Queen Anne-style Victorian mansion that serves as a central fixture of local ghost lore and historical tours.

#details
Constructed between 1880 and 1883 by E.D. Tillson, the town's first mayor, Annandale House was designed after a Victorian pattern book and embellished with intricate hand-painted ceilings inspired by the Aesthetic Movement. Following the passing of the Tillson family and its eventual rescue by local preservationists in 1981, the sprawling estate became a museum and National Historic Site. Over the years, locals and visitors exploring its richly preserved parlors and corridors have passed down tales of lingering Victorian presences, making the mansion a permanent fixture in the community's annual Halloween folklore and autumn ghost events.

#location
Annandale House is situated at 30 Tillson Avenue, at the corner of Hyman Street in the Town of Tillsonburg, Ontario.

#title
The Haunting of the Bell Mansion

#summary
The historic Bell Mansion in Sudbury is said to be haunted by its original resident, Katherine Bell, whose ghostly footsteps and rustling skirts still echo through the halls.

#details
Built by prominent lumberman William Bell, the grand mansion at 251 John Street was home to his wife, Katherine Bell, until her death in January 1954. After the building was rebuilt following a fire and later became the Art Gallery of Sudbury, staff and visitors began reporting persistent paranormal occurrences. Witnesses have reported hearing the distinct sound of high heels clicking on the wooden floors, the rustling of heavy skirts, and even ghostly horse-drawn carriages or neighing outside on the grounds.

#location
The Bell Mansion is located at 251 John Street in Sudbury, Ontario.

#title
The Tragedy and Ghosts of Cedar Street

#summary
The former Castonguay family home—now operating as the HourGlass Restaurant—is reportedly haunted by the couple who were tragically murdered there in 1957.

#details
In 1957, respected community members Gerard and Jeanne D'arc Castonguay were murdered in their home by a tenant renting a room from them while their children managed to escape. The residence, located at 183 Cedar Street, later became the HourGlass Restaurant. Over the years, patrons and staff have reported various ghostly encounters, including sightings of a woman in white near the staircase where the tragedy occurred and a figure resembling Gerard standing near the top of the stairs.

#location
The HourGlass Restaurant (formerly the Castonguay House) is located at 183 Cedar Street in Sudbury, Ontario.

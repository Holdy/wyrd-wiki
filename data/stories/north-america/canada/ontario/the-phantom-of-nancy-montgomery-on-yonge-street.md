#title
The Phantom of Nancy Montgomery on Yonge Street

#summary
Drivers along Yonge Street have long reported seeing a translucent female apparition darting in front of their vehicles near the 1843 historic murder site of housekeeper Nancy Montgomery.

#details
In 1843, Thomas Kinnear and his housekeeper Nancy Montgomery were murdered in their Richmond Hill residence by employees James McDermott and Grace Marks—a notorious historical crime that inspired Margaret Atwood's novel *Alias Grace*. The Kinnear farm once stood across from the Loyal True Blue and Orange Home on Yonge Street, north of Elgin Mills Road. For decades, motorists driving along this stretch of Yonge Street have reported seeing a whitish, translucent vision of a woman darting across the road, with some drivers stopping under the harrowing impression that they had struck a pedestrian, only to find nothing there. Local lore attributes these ghostly encounters to the lingering spirit of Nancy Montgomery.

#location
Yonge Street, just north of Elgin Mills Road in Richmond Hill, Ontario.

#title
The Phantom Seamstress of Joseph Schneider House

#summary
The oldest standing building in Kitchener, the Joseph Schneider House, is reportedly haunted by a phantom seamstress who continues working in the upper rooms and attic.

#details
Constructed in 1840, the Joseph Schneider House is a historic landmark and museum that preserves the early pioneer heritage of the area. Beyond its historical artifacts, the building is famous for accounts of a phantom seamstress. Visitors and staff have reported seeing an apparition working away in the upstairs bedroom and attic windows. Even when no figure is directly visible, those stepping onto the property describe a sharp drop in atmosphere and a constant, heavy feeling of being watched from the small attic panes.

#location
Joseph Schneider House at 466 Queen St S, Kitchener, Ontario.

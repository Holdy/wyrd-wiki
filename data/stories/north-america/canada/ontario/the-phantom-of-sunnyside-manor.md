#title
The Phantom of Sunnyside Manor

#summary
The historic Heintzman House in Thornhill-Markham is said to be haunted by figures from its 19th-century past, including a former coachman and the property's original owner, Colonel George Cruickshank.

#details
Built originally as an adobe brick cottage in the early 19th century and later expanded into a grand mansion known as Sunnyside Farm by Colonel George Cruickshank in 1817, the Heintzman House has witnessed centuries of local history. Over the decades after it transitioned from a private estate and farm into a community venue, visitors, staff, and local psychics have reported paranormal phenomena on the grounds. Eerie presences and shadowy sightings within the manor are frequently attributed to a former coachman who once worked on the sprawling property, as well as the spirit of Colonel Cruickshank himself, who is said to still watch over his historic home.

#location
The Heintzman House is located at 135 Bay Thorn Drive in Markham, Ontario.

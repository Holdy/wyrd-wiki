#title
Spirits of the Penetanguishene Centennial Museum

#summary
Staff and visitors at the historic Penetanguishene Centennial Museum frequently hear disembodied footsteps, mysterious store bells ringing, and phantom voices echoing through the 1875 building.

#details
Originally constructed in 1875 by lumber magnate Charles Beck as a general store and lumber office, the building now serves as the Penetanguishene Centennial Museum. Staff, volunteers, and paranormal investigators have reported countless supernatural encounters, including doors opening and closing on their own, the distinct chime of an entrance bell that no longer exists, and apparitions of figures dressed in 19th-century attire. Psychics and mediums who have investigated the museum sensed multiple spirits, including Charles Beck himself acting as a spiritual gatekeeper, a World War I soldier, and a troubled former resident. Despite the eerie activity, visitors and staff note that the lingering presences are entirely friendly.

#location
The Penetanguishene Centennial Museum is located in Penetanguishene, Ontario.

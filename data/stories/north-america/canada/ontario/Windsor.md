#title
The Curse of Peche Island

#summary
Following a vengeful widow's curse in the 19th century, Peche Island in the Detroit River has been plagued by a relentless chain of sudden deaths, mysterious fires, and failed developments.

#details
In the 1830s, family members of Hiram Walker acquired land on Peche Island in the Detroit River, displacing widow Rosalie Laforet from her homestead. As she was forced out, Rosalie issued a chilling curse declaring that no one would ever successfully use or develop the island. True to her warning, a series of tragedies struck: Hiram Walker's son Willis died suddenly at age 28, and Hiram himself passed away soon after following a stroke. Later, when the Windsor Detroit Ferry Co. purchased the property to build a resort, the company's president died mysteriously that exact year, and their grand summer mansion burned to the ground in 1929. Subsequent attempts at housing developments collapsed from unstable soil, and ski resort plans ended in sudden bankruptcy, leaving Peche Island permanently wild and untouched.

#location
Peche Island is situated in the Detroit River off the coast of Windsor, Ontario.

#title
The Dragging Ghost of Mackenzie Hall

#summary
Staff and visitors at Windsor's historic Mackenzie Hall have long reported a mysterious "Dragging Ghost" that scrapes heavy ladders across the floor and leaves unexplained paranormal traces.

#details
Built between 1855 and 1856 by Alexander Mackenzie (who later became Canada's second Prime Minister), Mackenzie Hall served as the Essex County courthouse, jail, and site of executions until 1963. Beyond its grim history of public and shielded hangings, staff frequently experience unexplained phenomena. A custodian working alone at night reported hearing a heavy ladder scrape across the floor three distinct times, only to find the room empty. Other accounts include a young boy witnessing a floating apparition staring intently at the portrait of Judge Stewart (nicknamed the "bloody judge"), unexplained handprints appearing on windows, and electronic voice recorders capturing mysterious moans.

#location
Mackenzie Hall is located at 3277 Sandwich Street in Windsor, Ontario.

#title
The Guardian Spirits of the Capitol Theatre

#summary
Performers and late-night visitors to downtown Windsor's historic Capitol Theatre report eerie encounters with a phantom man in a top hat and protective theatrical spirits.

#details
The Capitol Theatre has long been rumored to be haunted by unseen forces. Local lore holds that the spirits of old circus performers and seamstresses roam the backstage and corridors, devoted to safeguarding the landmark against harm. Anyone alone on the main stage at night is warned against letting their gaze wander to the balcony, lest they spot the phantom man in a top hat wandering the upper levels. While these entities are generally benevolent—especially in forgotten spaces like the abandoned projection room—their watchful presence creates an eerie, supernatural atmosphere.

#location
The Capitol Theatre is located at 121 University Avenue West in Windsor, Ontario.

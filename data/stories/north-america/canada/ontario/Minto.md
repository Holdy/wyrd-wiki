#title
The Phantom Lantern of the Minto Pond

#summary
Local folklore in Minto Township tells of a family who tragically drowned when their horse and buggy fell through pond ice, leaving a ghostly lantern light to haunt the frozen waters.

#details
According to longstanding local legend in the community, a family perished during the winter when their horse and buggy broke through the ice of a rural pond popular with local youth. Ever since the tragedy, residents claimed that the flickering light of the family's lantern could be seen moving across the frozen surface on winter nights. Years later, local teenagers including future mayor George Bridge attempted to stage a practical joke by securing a flashlight in a nearby tree to mimic the ghostly lantern and scare visitors. However, the prank took an eerie turn when the flashlight appeared to detach from the tree branch and float freely up and down in mid-air, remaining defyingly unexplained to this day.

#location
The pond is located in Minto Township, Wellington County, Ontario.

#title
The Voices of Harriston Town Hall Theatre

#summary
Long-standing rumors of a haunting at the historic Harriston Town Hall Theatre were corroborated by paranormal investigators who recorded disembodied singing and electronic voice phenomena.

#details
For years, municipal employees and local residents working alone in the Harriston Town Hall Theatre during the early morning hours reported hearing heavy, unexplainable footsteps echoing through the empty building. During a community storytelling event exploring local folklore, former employee Gary MacDougall shared his own unsettling experiences of hearing footsteps while alone. To investigate these claims, the Wellington County Paranormal Investigators set up infrared cameras and audio equipment for an overnight stakeout. During their investigation, they captured electronic voice phenomena (EVPs), including a distinct female voice asking, "Do you want to help me?", as well as faint, disembodied singing echoing from the balcony.

#location
The Harriston Town Hall Theatre is located on Elora Street South in Harriston, Ontario.

#title
The Spirits of Pickering Museum Village

#summary
The 19 heritage buildings of Pickering Museum Village are home to restless pioneer spirits, including a voice that orders guards to leave and a child ghost peeking from windows.

#details
Pickering Museum Village is the largest living history museum in Pickering, featuring 19 restored pioneer and heritage buildings relocated from across the region. Staff, security guards, and visitors have reported numerous ghostly encounters across the site. In one historic home, a security guard was startled when a disembodied man's voice commanded him to "get out." In another building, witnesses have spotted the apparition of a young child peering out from a second-story window. Multiple paranormal investigations and ghost walks have documented these recurring manifestations, tying them to the displaced spirits of early settlers and bygone eras.

#location
Pickering Museum Village, located on the northern edge of Greenwood in Pickering, Ontario.

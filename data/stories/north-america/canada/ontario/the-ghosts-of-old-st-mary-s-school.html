The Ghosts of Old St. Mary's School

Summary

For sixteen years prior to its demolition, the old St. Mary's School in Fort Frances was reported to be haunted by mysterious footsteps, doors locking themselves, and phantom noises heard by night custodians.

Details

Erected in 1912, St. Mary's School in Fort Frances harbored a long-standing reputation for eerie nighttime occurrences. Night-shift caretakers Orielle DeGagne and Jerry Bedard regularly experienced inexplicable phenomena during their shifts, such as radios suddenly dying, heavy doors locking themselves overnight, and heavy, measured footsteps echoing through completely empty and locked upper hallways. Over the decades, generations of students and teachers embellished the tales, linking creaking floors and bats slipping through the old belfry to supernatural activity, with some students even creating makeshift "miraculous medals" as protection against the school's resident spirit.

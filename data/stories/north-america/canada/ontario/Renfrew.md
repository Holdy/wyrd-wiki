#title
The Phantom Lantern of Buck Hill Road

#summary
Travelers who drive up a lonely dirt road near Round Lake in Renfrew County and perform a nighttime ritual often report seeing a mysterious glowing lantern light and hearing ghostly cries.

#details
Local legend holds that during the Depression, a logging family lived near the base of Buck Hill. When their young daughter chased the family dog into a severe snowstorm and vanished, her grieving father searched for her endlessly with a lantern until his death. To this day, motorists visiting Buck Hill Road at night who flash their headlights three times and call out into the darkness report seeing a baseball-sized orb of amber, green, or white light swaying among the trees, mimicking someone walking with a lantern. Visitors have also reported mysterious handprints appearing on their car windows and hearing disembodied screams echoing through the forest.

#location
Buck Hill Road, located near Round Lake Centre in the Township of Killaloe-Hagarty-Richards, Renfrew County, Ontario.

#title
The Unseen Residents of the Pioneer Log House

#summary
Staff and paranormal investigators at the Pembroke museum have repeatedly encountered moving objects, disembodied footsteps, and mysterious noises within its historic pioneer structures.

#details
Located in Pembroke within Renfrew County, the Champlain Trail Museum and Pioneer Village features historical buildings reflecting the early settlement era. Multiple paranormal investigations conducted by the Canadian Haunting and Paranormal Society have documented persistent eerie occurrences. Staff and visitors have reported hearing distinct footsteps walking across the upper floor of the pioneer log home when the building is empty, watching toys shift position on their own in the children's exhibit room, and hearing dishes clinking in the dining area.

#location
Champlain Trail Museum and Pioneer Village, located at 1032 Pembroke Street East in Pembroke, Renfrew County, Ontario.

#title
The Whispers and Footsteps of the McDougall Mill Museum

#summary
Visitors and paranormal investigators at the historic stone mill in Renfrew have frequently reported unexplained footsteps on the upper floor, the sound of children playing, and the apparition of a mysterious woman.

#details
Built in the 19th century along the Bonnechere River in Renfrew, Ontario, the McDougall Mill Museum preserves local pioneer history and heritage. Over the years, caretakers, visitors, and members of paranormal research groups have documented various eerie phenomena inside the building. People touring the museum have reported hearing heavy footsteps echoing on the empty wooden floorboards upstairs, as well as the faint, playful sounds of children when no children are present. During formal investigations by groups like the Canadian Haunting and Paranormal Society, investigators have glimpsed the shadowy figure of a woman and heard sudden, unexplained loud bangs near their recording equipment.

#location
McDougall Mill Museum, located in O'Brien Park along the Bonnechere River in Renfrew, Ontario.

#title
The Phantom of the Whitby Courthouse Theatre

#summary
Built in the 1850s, the historic Whitby Centennial Building is haunted by the spirit of a man named Jack who fell from the courtroom balcony, as well as a phantom figure that transforms into a fiery-eyed black dog.

#details
Constructed between 1852 and 1854 as the Ontario County Courthouse on Centre Street in Whitby, the landmark now operates as the Whitby Courthouse Theatre. According to local lore and paranormal investigations, the building carries a dark history. Legend states that a man named Jack was pacing the upper courtroom balcony awaiting a verdict for a relative when the ruling was announced; startled or rushing forward, he fell over the short ledge to his death. Visitors and investigators have reported connecting with Jack, alongside other spectral phenomena such as doors slamming shut on their own, lights toggling mysteriously, and sightings of a tall man in a hat outside who allegedly transforms into a large black dog with glowing eyes.

#location
The Whitby Centennial Building (Courthouse Theatre) is located on Centre Street in downtown Whitby, Ontario.

#title
The Unsolved Murder and Haunting of the Whitby Junction Station

#summary
The historic Whitby Junction railway station, now home to the Station Gallery, is haunted by the spirit of a telegraph operator murdered in 1914 and his grieving father.

#details
Originally built in 1903 at the foot of Byron Street in Whitby, the railway station served as a stop on the Grand Trunk Railway before being relocated. On December 11, 1914, 21-year-old night telegraph operator William "Billy" Stone Jr. was shot in the heart inside the station in an unsolved murder. Grief-stricken and under suspicion during the subsequent inquest, Billy's father, William Stone Sr., died by suicide on the nearby train tracks six months later. Today, paranormal investigators and visitors to the Station Gallery have captured electronic voice phenomena and unexplained sensor activations, reporting spectral encounters with both Billy and his father within the old wooden framework of the historic station.

#location
The Station Gallery (formerly the Whitby Junction railway station) is located near Henry Street in Whitby, Ontario.

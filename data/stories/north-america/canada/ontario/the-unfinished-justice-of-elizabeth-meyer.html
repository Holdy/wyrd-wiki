The Unfinished Justice of Elizabeth Meyer

Summary

The ghost of a woman found dead outside a 19th-century hotel is said to haunt the building seeking justice for her mysterious demise.

Details

In January 1872, a woman named Elizabeth Meyer was discovered lifeless at the front entrance of the Simcoe Hotel in Barrie on a bitterly cold night. Although a coroner's inquest concluded her death was due to exposure and intemperance, contemporary newspaper accounts noted that her body bore bruises and injuries consistent with physical blows. Decades later, staff working in the historic building have reported feeling a chilling female presence in the stone basement and near the entrance—believed to be Elizabeth's restless spirit lingering to reveal the true circumstances of her death.

#title
Applause in the Empty Roxy Theatre

#summary
The historic Roxy Theatre in Owen Sound is said to be haunted by unseen audience members and entities that leave cold spots and clapping sounds in an empty auditorium.

#details
Operating for over a century in Owen Sound, the Roxy Theatre is a cornerstone of local arts and entertainment, but it also harbors a spooky reputation. When the stage lights go down and the crowds leave, staff and actors have reported sudden cold spots in the wings, the eerie feeling of being watched while alone, and faint, disembodied applause echoing from the empty auditorium. Explorers wandering into the old prop rooms and backstage stairwells have also reported startling physical sensations and sudden frights that defy practical explanation.

#location
The Roxy Theatre is located at 251 9th Street East in Owen Sound, Ontario.

#title
The Apparition of Highland Manor

#summary
Visitors and guests staying at the historic Highland Manor in Owen Sound have reported smelling pine disinfectant, hearing disembodied baby cries, and seeing the shadow of a nurse in a rocking chair.

#details
Built in 1872 by Mr. Ross, Highland Manor on West Hill operated as a private maternity hospital between 1919 and 1940, where over 2,000 births took place. Decades later, guests staying in the Ross Room reported sudden, overpowering scents of old-fashioned pine cleaner at midnight, accompanied by the chilling sound of a crying baby that could not be heard in the hallway. Later in the night, a guest woke up to see the silhouette of a woman wearing a nurse or nun's head covering sitting quietly in a corner rocking chair before vanishing into thin air.

#location
Highland Manor is located on West Hill overlooking Owen Sound, Ontario.

#title
The Haunting of the Billy Bishop Museum

#summary
Staff and visitors at the childhood home of Canada's renowned WWI flying ace report paranormal occurrences ranging from phantom footsteps to moving objects.

#details
Housed in the historic Victorian boyhood home of World War I aviation hero Billy Bishop in Owen Sound, the museum has gained a reputation for uncanny ghostly encounters. Staff members and volunteers have frequently reported hearing unexplained bumps and bangs, cupboards opening on their own, and phantom footsteps echoing through the rooms. During séances held within the house, participants noted sudden drops in temperature and heavy, cold air filling the rooms, cementing its status as a place where the past refuses to rest.

#location
The Billy Bishop Museum is located at 948 3rd Avenue West in Owen Sound, Ontario.

#title
The Restless Spirits of the Old Grey County Courthouse and Jail

#summary
Staff, artists, and visitors at the historic Owen Sound jail and courthouse have long reported sudden chills, unexplained noises, and shadowy figures linked to its dark frontier justice history.

#details
The imposing limestone Grey County Courthouse and Jail in Owen Sound was constructed to mete out swift frontier justice, most notably witnessing the 1884 execution of Cook Teets, a blind man convicted of poisoning his wife who maintained his innocence until a troubled execution. Over the years, artists renting studio space in the old courthouse and staff working in the facility have reported inexplicable phenomena, including windows spontaneously blowing open in winter, objects mysteriously manipulated, and towering ceilings marred by unexplained marks, all accompanied by a lingering, oppressive sense of being watched in the dark corridors.

#location
The Old Grey County Courthouse and Jail is situated in downtown Owen Sound, Ontario.

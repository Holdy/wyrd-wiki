#title
The Phantoms of the Orillia Opera House

#summary
Staff and visitors at the historic Orillia Opera House have reported spectral music, ghostly applause, and sightings of a female apparition known as "Doris."

#details
Opened in 1895 and rebuilt after a devastating fire in 1915, the Orillia Opera House has served as a cultural hub, municipal office, and local jail over its long history. Over the decades, actors, staff, and paranormal investigators have experienced various unexplainable phenomena within its walls. Accounts include phantom applause echoing through empty auditoriums, spectral piano playing originating from the balcony, a fire hose mysteriously unravelling on its own, and sightings of "Doris"—believed to be the spirit of a former manager or actress dressed in period attire who roams the balcony and dressing rooms.

#location
The Orillia Opera House is located at 20 Mississaga Street West in downtown Orillia, Ontario.

#title
The Specter of St. Columbkille's Church

#summary
Generations of parishioners have whispered tales of a mysterious figure in a black hat, spectral organ music, and creaking vaults at St. Columbkille's Church.

#details
Founded in 1855 by Irish and Scottish Catholic immigrants in the hamlet of Uptergrove just outside Orillia, the current Gothic brick structure was erected in 1905. Almost since its construction, local folklore has maintained that the church is haunted. Witnesses have reported shadowy figures appearing suddenly in the choir area—specifically a man wearing a black hat—as well as mysterious candlelight flickering in windows during violent storms and eerie music drifting from the organ loft. Local legend suggests the spirit belongs to a former priest who died before he could complete his pledged number of sermons and has returned to finish his sacred duty.

#location
St. Columbkille's Church is located in the hamlet of Uptergrove, just northeast of Orillia, Ontario.

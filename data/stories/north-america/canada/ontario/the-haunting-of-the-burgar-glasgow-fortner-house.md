#title
The Haunting of the Burgar-Glasgow-Fortner House

#summary
Originally constructed in 1859, this historic Welland mansion—where several children tragically succumbed to scarlet fever in the 19th century—is haunted by residual energy, unexplained physical noises, and ghostly voices.

#details
Built in 1859 by George H. Burgar, the large three-story home in Welland was later occupied by medical practitioners including Dr. William E. Burgar and Dr. Sinclair H. Glasgow. The property carries a sorrowful history; George and Jemina Burgar lost five of their eight children at young ages, with three passing away in 1871 during a scarlet fever outbreak. Modern paranormal investigators visiting the restored estate have documented extensive unexplained phenomena. Investigators have captured distinct electronic voice phenomena (EVPs), recorded old-fashioned music playing in empty rooms where no music sources existed, and witnessed objects like a children's playpen crashing to the floor on its own. Ghost-box and Ovilus sessions have repeatedly yielded names of the historical family members and responses regarding the children who once lived and died in the house.

#location
The Burgar/Glasgow/Fortner House in Welland, Ontario.

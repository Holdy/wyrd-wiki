#title
The Legless Soldier of Fort St. Joseph

#summary
A British soldier who perished from severe frostbite after deserting his post in the 1800s is said to still patrol the grounds of Fort St. Joseph without legs.

#details
In the early 19th century, two British soldiers attempted to desert Fort St. Joseph during a brutal winter by walking across a frozen lake. They succumbed to hypothermia and were rescued by First Nations traders, but one died and the survivor lost his limbs to the cold. Years later, staff closing the historic site observed a phantom soldier walking toward the flagpole who noticeably lacked legs below the thigh.

#location
Fort St. Joseph National Historic Site is located on the southern tip of St. Joseph Island in Ontario, southeast of Sault Ste. Marie.

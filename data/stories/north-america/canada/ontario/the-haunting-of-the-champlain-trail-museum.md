#title
The Haunting of the Champlain Trail Museum

#summary
Staff and paranormal investigators at the Champlain Trail Museum and Pioneer Village have frequently reported unexplained upstairs footsteps, moving children's toys, and phantom noises inside its historic pioneer log home.

#details
The Champlain Trail Museum and Pioneer Village in Pembroke has long been a focal point for local ghost lore and paranormal investigations. Over the years, staff members and visiting investigators from organizations like the Canadian Haunting and Paranormal Society have documented numerous unexplainable phenomena. Visitors and staff have reported hearing heavy footsteps echoing in the upper levels of the pioneer log home when no one else is upstairs, watching toys shift mysteriously in the children's room, and hearing dishes clatter in the dining area. 

#location
The Champlain Trail Museum and Pioneer Village located in Pembroke, Ontario.

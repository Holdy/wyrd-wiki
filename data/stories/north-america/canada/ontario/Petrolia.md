#title
The Grey Lady of Petrolia Line

#summary
A grand Victorian house in Petrolia is said to be haunted by a benevolent spirit known as the Grey Lady, alongside a restless musical presence and an oppressive energy in the basement.

#details
Local folklore and paranormal accounts recount that a prominent Victorian house along Petrolia Line is home to several lingering spirits. Most notable is the "Grey Lady," an apparition frequently spotted by visitors and residents at the top of the staircase. Witnesses have also reported hearing piano music played by an unseen pianist in the home's music room, while investigators have documented a heavy, forceful presence lurking in the basement.

#location
A grand Victorian house located along Petrolia Line in Petrolia, Ontario.

#title
The Specter of Sunnyside Mansion

#summary
Sunnyside Mansion, the historic 22-room estate built by oil baron John Henry Fairbank, carries a long-standing local reputation as a classic haunted house defined by its imposing Victorian architecture and decaying grandeur.

#details
Constructed in 1890 by pioneer oilman John Henry Fairbank, Sunnyside Mansion is a massive 22-room estate that has long captured the imagination of Petrolia residents. Over decades of changing fortunes and gradual disrepair, the mansion acquired a strong reputation in local legend as a haunted landmark, characterized by its weathering woodwork and imposing presence. Its classic spooky ambiance eventually attracted filmmakers to use it as a haunting movie set, cementing its status as the town's most legendary historical structure.

#location
Sunnyside Mansion (The Fairbank House), located on Petrolia Line between Oil Street and Barretts Lane in Petrolia, Ontario.

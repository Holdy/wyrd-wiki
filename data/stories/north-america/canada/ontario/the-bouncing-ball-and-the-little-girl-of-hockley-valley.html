    The Bouncing Ball and the Little Girl of Hockley Valley

    Summary

    Three sisters exploring an abandoned bed and breakfast in Hockley Valley encountered unexplainable noises and a bouncing ball, later discovering the photographic image of a young girl inside.

    Details

    Growing up near the sheltered valleys of Hockley Valley in Mono, Ontario, three sisters were long fascinated by a dilapidated, weathered abandoned house that had once operated as a bed and breakfast. Driven by curiosity, they decided to sneak into the house late one night with flashlights and cameras. After stepping inside and taking photos in the large kitchen pantry, they were startled by a shrill scream and unseen footsteps echoing through the halls. As they fled toward the front door, a small rubber ball bounced down the stairs right behind them. Days later, when they had their film developed, the photograph clearly showed a small, curly-haired girl staring back at them. Terrified yet captivated, the sisters dedicated themselves to uncovering the spirit's story and became lifelong paranormal investigators.

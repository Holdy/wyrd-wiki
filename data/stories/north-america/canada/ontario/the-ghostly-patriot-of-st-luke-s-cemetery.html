The Ghostly Patriot of St. Luke's Cemetery

Summary

Every year on the anniversary of his death, the spirit of Mohawk leader and Burlington founder Joseph Brant is said to manifest as a fully-formed apparition visiting his daughter's final resting place.

Details

Joseph Brant, a prominent leader of the Mohawk people and namesake for much of Burlington, died in 1807. Although his remains were later relocated to the Mohawk Chapel in Brantford, local legend and eyewitness accounts maintain that on November 24th right around the witching hour, Brant appears at St. Luke's Cemetery. Standing six feet tall in full traditional Indigenous dress while holding a rifle and a sword, he reportedly lets out a loud battle cry before fading away among the gravestones. Visitors to the 192-year-old cemetery have also captured mysterious light orbs and witnessed dark shadows moving among the headstones.

#title
The Headless Ghost of the Avon River

#summary
Following a grisly 19th-century grave robbery and botched disposal, a headless apparition has been witnessed wandering the banks of the Avon River in Stratford.

#details
In 1876, after a prisoner named Henry Derry died in the Stratford Jail, his body was stolen from its resting place by a medical student for anatomical study. Panicked by the crime, the student attempted to sink the corpse in the Avon River, where a headless body was later discovered floating near "The Wooden Bridge." Although Derry was eventually reburied in Avondale Cemetery and a skull was unearthed near the bridge nearly a century later in 1980, generations of locals have continued to report sighting a headless figure walking along the river's shoreline.

#location
The William Hutt Bridge (formerly known as The Wooden Bridge) carries Waterloo Street across the Avon River in Stratford, Ontario.

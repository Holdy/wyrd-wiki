#title
The Ghost of Captain Colin Swayze

#summary
The resident ghost of a Canadian militia officer killed during the War of 1812 haunts a historic inn in Niagara-on-the-Lake as long as the British flag flies overhead.

#details
During the American invasion in May 1813, Captain Colin Swayze delayed his retreat to rendezvous with his sweetheart. Surprised by American soldiers searching the inn, he hid in a wine barrel in the cellar, where he received a fatal wound from a bayonet. His ghost is said to wander the halls of the inn at night, and modern security cameras have even captured figures resembling an 1812 militia officer walking through the premises. Local lore maintains that his spirit will remain harmless as long as the British flag continues to fly over the doorway.

#location
The Olde Angel Inn at 224 Regent Street in Niagara-on-the-Lake, Ontario.

#title
The Slain War Bride of Room 207

#summary
A British war bride waiting for her soldier husband during the War of 1812 was tragically killed by an American soldier, and her spirit is said to haunt Room 207 of a historic hotel.

#details
During the War of 1812, a woman named Molly McGuire stood at her window waiting for her husband, a British soldier, to return. An American soldier patrolling outside mistook her silhouette in the window for an enemy combatant and thrust his bayonet through her, killing her instantly. Legend holds that her spirit never left, and guests staying in Room 207 have frequently reported unexplained occurrences, including mysterious footsteps, lights turning on by themselves, and an eerie lingering presence.

#location
The Prince of Wales Hotel at 6 Picton Street in Niagara-on-the-Lake, Ontario.

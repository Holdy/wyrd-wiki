#title
Sax's Friendly Ghost

#summary
Uncle Eddie, a former resident who passed away in 1969, haunts his family's restaurant in Keswick with harmless pranks, disembodied footsteps, and moving objects.

#details
Sax's Fish and Chips Restaurant (later operating as the Church St. Diner) at 207 Church St. in Keswick has been a local landmark operated by the Saxton family since the 1950s, with the original building dating back to 1906. The most notable spirit haunting the premises is Uncle Eddie, the brother-in-law of the owner's mother, who lived in the building for the final ten years of his life until his death in 1969. In his later years, Eddie spent much of his time smoking at a small table and helping with chores. On the night he died, six-year-old Carolynne woke up and found his room bathed in daylight while Eddie sat in his chair, telling her not to worry before he passed away in his sleep. Ever since, staff and visitors have frequently encountered his presence: cleaning personnel have seen him slouched in his chair smoking, toilets flush and doors slam by themselves, kitchen burners and ovens turn on unexpectedly, and personal items like keys and toilet paper teleport or fly across rooms.

#location
The Church St. Diner (formerly Sax's Fish and Chips) at 207 Church St., Keswick, Ontario.

#title
The Friendly Girl of Noble House

#summary
A young ghostly girl haunts the historic Noble House at the Georgina Village Museum, greeting visitors with a soft hello and turning on electric candles.

#details
Located on the grounds of the Georgina Village Museum, the historic Noble House is widely recognized by staff and paranormal investigators as a hub of local ghostly activity. Among the reports is the recurring presence of a little girl who haunts the building. Described as entirely friendly, she is frequently seen playing inside the rooms. She is known to turn on electric candles by herself and has occasionally surprised visitors exploring the house by whispering a faint, welcoming "hello."

#location
The Noble House at the Georgina Village Museum, 26557 Civic Centre Road, Keswick, Ontario.

#title
The Jilted Lady of Room 217

#summary
A heartbroken woman who was abandoned by her lover in the early 20th century continues to haunt Room 217 at the historic Briars Resort in Jackson's Point.

#details
Originally built as a homestead in 1840 by Captain William Bourchier, The Briars Resort and Spa in Jackson's Point is renowned for its rich history and active paranormal phenomena. Most famously, Room 217 and the manor's watchtower are said to be occupied by the ghost of a woman who was jilted by her fiancé during a stay in the early 1900s. Guests and staff have reported numerous unsettling occurrences in and around the room, including handprints appearing out of nowhere on freshly made beds, heavy curtains billowing even when there is no breeze, and the spectral apparition of a woman in a white dress with long dark hair who vanishes into thin air while leaving behind the distinct, sweet fragrance of rosewater.

#location
The Briars Resort and Spa at 55 Hedge Road, Jackson's Point, Ontario.

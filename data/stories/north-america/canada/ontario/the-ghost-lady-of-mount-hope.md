#title
The Ghost Lady of Mount Hope

#summary
A tragic female hitchhiker is said to haunt the highway corner near Mount Hope outside Fort Frances, appearing in the dark to wave at passing motorists and ask for rides before mysteriously vanishing.

#details
Local folklore in the Fort Frances region has long circulated the legend of the "Ghost Lady of Mount Hope," a woman who reportedly died in an accident near the roadway below the old Air Force camp. For decades, drivers traversing the area after dark have reported seeing her standing by the roadside, attempting to hitch a ride or waving at passing cars. Over the years, various retellings of the legend have warned travelers against picking her up, noting that she tends to vanish without a trace, cementing her status as one of the Rainy River District's most persistent ghost stories.

#location
The highway corner near Mount Hope below the old Air Force camp near Fort Frances, Ontario.

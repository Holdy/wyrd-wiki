#title
The Phantom of the Gravenhurst Opera House

#summary
The historic Gravenhurst Opera House is said to be haunted by Ben, a former lighting technician who tragically fell to his death from the catwalk.

#details
Opened in 1901 to serve as both a town hall and an opera house, the Gravenhurst Opera House has stood as a cultural cornerstone of the community for over a century. Over the decades, local lore has linked the historic building to a resident phantom named Ben. According to the legend, Ben was a dedicated lighting operator who either accidentally slipped or fell from the overhead catwalk above the stage. Since his untimely death, staff and visitors have reported inexplicable cold spots, sudden chills, doors slamming shut unprompted, and fleeting sightings of a translucent figure roaming the backstage area.

#location
The Gravenhurst Opera House located at 290 Muskoka Road South in Gravenhurst, Ontario.

#title
The Restless Spirits of Hill House

#summary
Hill House within Muskoka Heritage Place is believed to be haunted by its 19th-century builder, Robert Norton Hill, and his wife, Caroline.

#details
As part of the recreated pioneer village at Muskoka Heritage Place, the two-storey frame Hill House carries a rich and storied history. It was originally built by Robert Norton Hill, a minister who moved to the region in 1867 and established the local post office before passing away in 1895. Local historian and author Andrew Hind identified Hill House as the most spiritually active building in the pioneer village during historical ghost tours. Visitors and staff have reported encountering the enduring presence of both Robert and Caroline Hill, who continue to watch over the historic home they built in the 19th century.

#location
Hill House at Muskoka Heritage Place located at 88 Brunel Road in Gravenhurst, Ontario.

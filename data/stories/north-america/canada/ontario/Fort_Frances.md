#title
The Ghost Lady of Mount Hope

#summary
A tragic female hitchhiker is said to haunt the highway corner near Mount Hope outside Fort Frances, appearing in the dark to wave at passing motorists and ask for rides before mysteriously vanishing.

#details
Local folklore in the Fort Frances region has long circulated the legend of the "Ghost Lady of Mount Hope," a woman who reportedly died in an accident near the roadway below the old Air Force camp. For decades, drivers traversing the area after dark have reported seeing her standing by the roadside, attempting to hitch a ride or waving at passing cars. Over the years, various retellings of the legend have warned travelers against picking her up, noting that she tends to vanish without a trace, cementing her status as one of the Rainy River District's most persistent ghost stories.

#location
The highway corner near Mount Hope below the old Air Force camp near Fort Frances, Ontario.

#title
The Ghosts of Old St. Mary's School

#summary
For sixteen years prior to its demolition, the old St. Mary's School in Fort Frances was reported to be haunted by mysterious footsteps, doors locking themselves, and phantom noises heard by night custodians.

#details
Erected in 1912, St. Mary's School in Fort Frances harbored a long-standing reputation for eerie nighttime occurrences. Night-shift caretakers Orielle DeGagne and Jerry Bedard regularly experienced inexplicable phenomena during their shifts, such as radios suddenly dying, heavy doors locking themselves overnight, and heavy, measured footsteps echoing through completely empty and locked upper hallways. Over the decades, generations of students and teachers embellished the tales, linking creaking floors and bats slipping through the old belfry to supernatural activity, with some students even creating makeshift "miraculous medals" as protection against the school's resident spirit.

#location
St. Mary's School in Fort Frances, Ontario.

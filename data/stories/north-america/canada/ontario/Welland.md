#title
The Ghost of Birdie at Rose Manor

#summary
Birdie Rose, the daughter of a prominent local merchant who died in 1956 after being forbidden from marrying her true love, continues to gently haunt her historic family home in Welland.

#details
Built in the historic heart of Welland, Rose Manor and its adjacent family properties were home to Birdie Rose for much of her life. According to local lore, Birdie fell deeply in love with a man from Buffalo, but her father, prominent merchant Hugh Alexander Rose, strongly disapproved of the relationship and forbade her from marrying him. Because archaic laws of the era prevented unmarried women from holding property ownership independently, her brother oversaw the home where she resided until her passing in 1956—with her final act reportedly being the reading and burning of letters from her lost beau. Today, current owners and guests frequently experience her gentle presence. Rather than hostile activity, Birdie is known to open doors and drawers, make pots of coffee, pace softly upstairs at night, and playfully return items that had previously gone missing.

#location
Rose Manor Bed & Breakfast located on Frazer Street in Welland, Ontario.

#title
The Haunting of the Burgar-Glasgow-Fortner House

#summary
Originally constructed in 1859, this historic Welland mansion—where several children tragically succumbed to scarlet fever in the 19th century—is haunted by residual energy, unexplained physical noises, and ghostly voices.

#details
Built in 1859 by George H. Burgar, the large three-story home in Welland was later occupied by medical practitioners including Dr. William E. Burgar and Dr. Sinclair H. Glasgow. The property carries a sorrowful history; George and Jemina Burgar lost five of their eight children at young ages, with three passing away in 1871 during a scarlet fever outbreak. Modern paranormal investigators visiting the restored estate have documented extensive unexplained phenomena. Investigators have captured distinct electronic voice phenomena (EVPs), recorded old-fashioned music playing in empty rooms where no music sources existed, and witnessed objects like a children's playpen crashing to the floor on its own. Ghost-box and Ovilus sessions have repeatedly yielded names of the historical family members and responses regarding the children who once lived and died in the house.

#location
The Burgar/Glasgow/Fortner House in Welland, Ontario.

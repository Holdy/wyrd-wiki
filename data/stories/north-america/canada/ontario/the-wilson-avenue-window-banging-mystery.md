#title
The Wilson Avenue Window Banging Mystery

#summary
In 1940, local police were repeatedly called to a Wilson Avenue home to investigate mysterious, relentless window banging that continued despite police surveillance.

#details
Over a three-week period in December 1940, the residence at 73 Wilson Avenue in Timmins was plagued by persistent, unexplained noises that brought police to the scene multiple times a day. Nightly between 8:30 p.m. and 2:30 a.m., a sharp, three-stroke banging sound—resembling a fist striking glass—echoed against a front window. Even when police officers staked out the interior and exterior of the house in pitch darkness, the loud raps continued while no one was near the window. Though the mysterious occurrences sparked rumors of a haunting, investigators eventually discovered that a 15-year-old boy in the home was flicking the loose frame with his toe from bed.

#location
The house is located at 73 Wilson Avenue in Timmins, Ontario.

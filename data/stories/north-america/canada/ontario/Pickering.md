#title
The Hauntings of Salem United Church Cemetery

#summary
The historic Salem United Church Cemetery is haunted by the residual presence of its 19th-century caretaker, William Brown, alongside unidentifiable barking and ghostly EVPs.

#details
Erected in 1849 and closed in 1890, the Salem United Church and its surrounding graveyard on Salem Road preserve generations of early Pickering history dating back to 1831. Over the decades, visitors and paranormal investigators have frequently reported sightings of a man wandering the grounds accompanied by the faint, unidentifiable sound of a dog barking—believed to be the original 1831 caretaker, William Brown, and his hound. Inside the church building, investigators have captured electronic voice phenomena (EVPs) and floating anomalies, which are locally attributed to a stubborn parishioner who reportedly continued sitting in his pew to worship long after the congregation dissolved.

#location
Salem United Church Cemetery, located on Salem Road just south of Highway 7 in Pickering, Ontario.

#title
The Spirits of Pickering Museum Village

#summary
The 19 heritage buildings of Pickering Museum Village are home to restless pioneer spirits, including a voice that orders guards to leave and a child ghost peeking from windows.

#details
Pickering Museum Village is the largest living history museum in Pickering, featuring 19 restored pioneer and heritage buildings relocated from across the region. Staff, security guards, and visitors have reported numerous ghostly encounters across the site. In one historic home, a security guard was startled when a disembodied man's voice commanded him to "get out." In another building, witnesses have spotted the apparition of a young child peering out from a second-story window. Multiple paranormal investigations and ghost walks have documented these recurring manifestations, tying them to the displaced spirits of early settlers and bygone eras.

#location
Pickering Museum Village, located on the northern edge of Greenwood in Pickering, Ontario.

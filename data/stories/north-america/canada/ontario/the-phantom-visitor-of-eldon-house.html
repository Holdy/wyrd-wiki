The Phantom Visitor of Eldon House

Summary

During a ball in May 1841, Sarah Harris and guests witnessed her beau, British Lieutenant Wenman Wynniatt, walk into the parlor moments before his drowned body was recovered from the Thames River.

Details

In the spring of 1841, Sarah Harris, daughter of district treasurer John Harris residing at Eldon House in London, Ontario, struck up a courtship with 26-year-old Lieutenant Wenman Wynniatt of the 83rd Regiment. Sarah invited him to attend a formal dance at Eldon House on the evening of May 14, 1841. That afternoon, Wynniatt set out for a horseback ride near the northern branch of the Thames River and was never seen alive again. At 10:15 PM, while Sarah was in the ballroom speaking with guests, she and several others clearly saw Wynniatt walk into the room, look steadily at her, and pass into the dining room. Puzzled by his seeming rudeness in not greeting her, Sarah followed him into the dining room, but found no one there. Witnesses noted that he looked strangely pale and his clothes were wet. The following morning, his horse was returned riderless, and on Sunday, his lifeless body was discovered in the Thames River covered in sand. His pocket watch had stopped at exactly 10:15 PM, perfectly matching the moment his apparition had appeared inside the house.

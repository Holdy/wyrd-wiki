#title
The Daughters of Matheson House

#summary
Built in the early 1800s, the historic Perth Museum—formerly the Matheson family home—is said to be haunted by the lingering spirits of the Senator's daughters who famously tended its lush gardens.

#details
Originally constructed as an opulent limestone residence for distinguished Canadian Senator Roderick Matheson, the grand manor on Gore Street East was occupied by the family for nearly a century. Of the Senator's seven daughters, six never married and spent much of their lives passionately tending the property's lush rear gardens filled with apple trees and rosebushes. After the last family member passed away in 1929, the building transitioned through various uses, including the Birkacre Tea Room and a restaurant named Vanity Fair that famously hosted spiritualists, clairvoyants, and tarot card readers like Madame Cornel and Madame Kildare during the late 1930s and 1940s. Over the decades, staff, visitors, and volunteers have reported paranormal occurrences, including unexplained shadows, shifting temperatures, disappearing objects, and ghostly figures resembling the Matheson daughters strolling through the moonlit gardens at dusk.

#location
Perth Museum (historically known as Matheson House) at 11 Gore Street East, Perth, Ontario.

#title
The Phantom of Victoria Hall

#summary
Originally built in 1858 as an opulent residence and later converted into a hospital, Victoria Hall on Drummond Street is haunted by the spectral presence of a frail, white-haired woman and associated with an early cursed family legacy.

#details
Judge John Glass Malloch constructed a magnificent 17-room stone mansion named Victoria Hall in Perth in 1858. During its construction, a bitter dispute arose between the judge and a tardy building supplier over unpaid materials, leading the frustrated supplier to curse the judge and his family for as long as they lived in the home. Following premature deaths among his family members and the judge's own passing in 1873, the vacant mansion gained a spooky reputation as the "Haunted House of Perth" due to locals reporting figures wandering its halls at night. In 1923, the building was converted into the Great War Memorial Hospital. Decades later, during the 1970s, evening kitchen and support staff regularly encountered the apparition of a small, slender woman with snow-white hair and a faded blue robe standing outside what nurses later confirmed had been empty utility storage space for years.

#location
The former Great War Memorial Hospital (historically Victoria Hall) on Drummond Street in Perth, Ontario.

#title
The Abandoned Homestead of Sunny Mount Park

#summary
The vacant mid-19th-century pioneer farmhouse at Sunny Mount Park has earned a lasting reputation among local residents as a haunted landmark.

#details
Nestled within Sunny Mount Park, the historic heritage home originally known as Sunny Mount Farm was built around 1856 by pioneer resident Austin Willmott. Over the decades, as various commercial redevelopment plans stalled and the property remained vacant and weathered, the eerie atmosphere of the abandoned house prompted generations of Miltonians to dub it one of the town's most prominent haunted locations.

#location
The historic Sunny Mount Farm house located in Sunny Mount Park at 155 Ruhl Drive in Milton, Ontario.

#title
The Pewtress House Haunting

#summary
Former tenants of the historic Pewtress House in Milton experienced paranormal activity before the building was relocated.

#details
According to Milton Historical Society President John Challinor, residents who rented the historic Pewtress House—previously situated at the corner of Tremaine Road and Main Street—reported various haunting experiences. The house was eventually deconstructed to make way for a park and relocated to King Street to serve as a private heritage residence, leaving locals to wonder if the alleged spirit made the move across town.

#location
The Pewtress House, formerly located at the intersection of Tremaine Road and Main Street and relocated to King Street in Milton, Ontario.

#title
The Second-Floor Footsteps at Country Heritage Park

#summary
Unexplained footsteps echo across the upper floor of the historic Ford garage at Country Heritage Park, where 19th-century garments are stored.

#details
Local oral history recounts eerie experiences inside the Ford garage located on the grounds of Country Heritage Park in Milton. The second floor of the building was utilized as storage for clothing dating back to the 1800s, and visitors have frequently reported hearing distinct, disembodied footsteps pacing across the floorboards, believed by locals to be the former owners of the historic clothing walking among their belongings.

#location
The Ford garage building located within Country Heritage Park at 856 Steeles Avenue West in Milton, Ontario.

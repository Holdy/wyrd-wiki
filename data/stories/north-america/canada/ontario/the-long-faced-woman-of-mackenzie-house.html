The Long-Faced Woman of Mackenzie House

Summary

Caretakers and visitors at a historic downtown Toronto home have reported terrifying encounters with a malicious apparition known as the "Long-Faced Woman." The spirit famously manifested directly above a bedroom headboard, staring down at a sleeping resident with a twisted smile before vanishing into thin air.

Details

In the 1950s, after a caretaker couple named Mr. and Mrs. Edmund moved into Mackenzie House at 82 Bond Street, they quickly established the building's reputation for intense paranormal activity. Late one night, Mrs. Edmunds awoke to a creaking sound behind her bed and looked up to see a terrifying apparition—a woman with a remarkably long face peering down at her from the impossibly narrow space behind the headboard, wearing a horrifying smile. When Mrs. Edmunds screamed, her husband woke up, but the entity had already vanished. The same entity was subsequently witnessed by other occupants and visiting grandchildren during sleepovers.

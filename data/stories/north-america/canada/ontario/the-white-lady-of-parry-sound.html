The White Lady of Parry Sound

Summary

An 1890 lakeside vacation cottage south of Parry Sound is allegedly haunted by a phantom woman in an old-fashioned white dress and wide-brimmed hat who quietly watches over the property.

Details

Built in 1890 on the eastern shores of Georgian Bay just south of Parry Sound, a historic Victorian vacation cottage has developed a reputation for being haunted by a spectral woman clad in a white dress and wide-brimmed hat. In the summer of 1980, a young schoolteacher reported seeing the figure wander into the woods behind the cabin, and years later, a visiting engineer spotted her pale face peering through a window. Although the owner of the cabin does not know who the spirit was in life, she maintains the distinct impression that the phantom's presence is benign and protective.

    The Ghost of the Dodge Heir

    Summary

    Following the tragic and accidental death of millionaire automotive heir Daniel Dodge on his honeymoon in 1938, locals have reported strange noises and sightings of his ghost lingering at his former estate.

    Details

    In August 1938, 21-year-old Daniel Dodge—son of John Dodge and heir to the Dodge motor company fortune—married local telephone operator Annie Laurine MacDonald and brought her to his newly established 600-acre summer estate on Maple Point, near Gore Bay and Kagawong on Manitoulin Island. Shortly after their arrival, a tragic accident occurred when workmen were disposing of or handling dynamite used for clearing land. A stick of dynamite exploded near Daniel, severely injuring him. In a desperate attempt to rush him to medical help across the choppy waters of the North Channel, Daniel fell overboard from a speedboat and drowned. Though his body was eventually recovered and his death ruled an accidental drowning, the dramatic tragedy left a deep mark on the region. Decades later, despite the estate changing hands and being temporarily run as a bed-and-breakfast, local residents and visitors continue to report unexplained noises and paranormal sightings of Daniel Dodge's spirit haunting the property.

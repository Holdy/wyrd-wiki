#title
The Blue Ghost Tunnel

#summary
An abandoned railway tunnel running beneath the old Welland Canal in Thorold is notoriously haunted by a ghostly fog and tragic echoes from its railway past.

#details
Built in the 1870s as part of the railway infrastructure, the Merritton Tunnel—widely known as the Blue Ghost Tunnel—was abandoned over a century ago after a new bridge route was established. Spanning over 500 feet through stone and timber beneath the canal bed, the damp, subterranean corridor earned its eerie moniker from visitors who reported seeing a strange, glowing blue mist or fog drifting through the freezing interior. Local legend attributes the haunting to fatal railway accidents and workers who lost their lives during the operational years of the line.

#location
The Blue Ghost Tunnel is located along the abandoned railway right-of-way near Seaway Haulage Road in Thorold, Ontario.

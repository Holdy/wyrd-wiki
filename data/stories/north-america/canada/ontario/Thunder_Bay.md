#title
Spirits and Specters of Trowbridge Falls
#summary
A popular municipal campground and park in Thunder Bay is rumored to be haunted by a woman in white, aggressive phantom trailrunners, and an entity known as the undertaker.
#details
Trowbridge Falls, a city-run park and campground located off Copenhagen Road on Thunder Bay's north side, is widely considered one of the area's most active paranormal hotspots. Campers and visitors have reported numerous unexplainable phenomena, including growling sounds in the forest, phantom children playing in the trees, and a mysterious woman in white drifting across the field near the parking lot. Joggers on the hiking trails have reported being shoved by invisible entities, while motorists parked in the lot at night have encountered an entity dubbed the "undertaker," who peers directly into car windows and bangs on vehicle doors.
#location
Trowbridge Falls park and campground is located off Copenhagen Road in Thunder Bay, Ontario.

#title
The Ghost of Room 203 at the Prince Arthur Hotel
#summary
The historic waterfront hotel in Thunder Bay is said to be haunted by a cigar-smoking spirit named Harvey, whose presence in Room 203 dictates the maintenance of the rooms around him.
#details
Opened in 1911 on the Thunder Bay waterfront, the Prince Arthur Hotel is legendary for its upscale history hosting dignitaries and celebrities. However, it is also famous for its resident ghost, Harvey. Said to be a former long-time resident or owner, Harvey haunts the second floor—particularly Room 203—leaving behind the distinct unexplainable scent of cigar smoke. Hotel lore states that when staff once removed photographs of him from the walls, the room mysteriously flooded, only stopping once the pictures were returned to their place. Other sightings include a specter in the darkened basement hallways wearing an outdated maitre'd uniform.
#location
The Prince Arthur Hotel is located at 29 Cumberland Street North in Thunder Bay, Ontario.

#title
The Haunted House on Archibald Street South
#summary
A historic 1905 home in Thunder Bay plagued its residents with unexplainable cold spots, terrifying nightmares, and a chilling discovery built directly into the guest room wall.
#details
Built in 1905 on Archibald Street South and once belonging to a former mayor of Fort William, a grand historic house was purchased in 1986 by a resident named Rose. The southeast back guest bedroom was perpetually freezing, and visitors who slept there experienced terrifying dreams of a dark, demonic figure. While pets persistently stared into the bedroom closet and walls, human residents heard constant phantom footsteps and furniture dragging upstairs. A local medium warned them against keeping children in the house, a warning tragically underscored before they moved out. Upon investigating the guest room's walk-in closet, the owner made the most chilling discovery: an old Ouija board built directly into the back wall.
#location
A historic house on Archibald Street South in Thunder Bay, Ontario.

#title
The Lingering Patrons of Paulucci’s Wayland
#summary
Staff closing up a historic Westfort bar in Thunder Bay frequently experience unseen presences, phantom pool games, and objects moving on their own.
#details
Operating on Gore Street since 1937, Paulucci's Wayland Bar & Grill (originally known simply as The Wayland) is a staple of the Westfort neighborhood with a history of supernatural activity. Bartenders closing the establishment alone have reported intense feelings of being watched, accompanied by phantom sounds of pool being played, change dropping into tip jars, doors opening and closing, and echoing footsteps. Glassware, furniture, and toilet seats frequently move without human intervention, and staff note that a can of Budweiser beer is regularly knocked off the shelf by an invisible force.
#location
Paulucci's Wayland Bar & Grill is located on Gore Street in Thunder Bay, Ontario.

#title
The Spirits of Pinnacle Playhouse

#summary
Housed in a historic downtown building, the Pinnacle Playhouse is said to be haunted by a resident spirit named "Ophelia" and a disembodied male laugh echoing through the washrooms.

#details
Since 1969, the Belleville Theatre Guild has operated out of the former D.L. Storey Centre, now known as Pinnacle Playhouse. Members and guests have reported numerous supernatural phenomena, nicknaming their resident spirit "Ophelia" after Shakespeare's character. Reported occurrences include hearing voices within the walls, glowing green eyes peering from air vents, phantom lights and voices backstage, a mysterious lady in black spotted in the control booth, and a terrifying disembodied male laugh in the female washroom attributed to a former janitor.

#location
Pinnacle Playhouse is located on Pinnacle Street in downtown Belleville, Ontario.

#title
The Headless Ghost of the Avon River

#summary
Following a grisly 19th-century grave robbery and botched disposal, a headless apparition has been witnessed wandering the banks of the Avon River in Stratford.

#details
In 1876, after a prisoner named Henry Derry died in the Stratford Jail, his body was stolen from its resting place by a medical student for anatomical study. Panicked by the crime, the student attempted to sink the corpse in the Avon River, where a headless body was later discovered floating near "The Wooden Bridge." Although Derry was eventually reburied in Avondale Cemetery and a skull was unearthed near the bridge nearly a century later in 1980, generations of locals have continued to report sighting a headless figure walking along the river's shoreline.

#location
The William Hutt Bridge (formerly known as The Wooden Bridge) carries Waterloo Street across the Avon River in Stratford, Ontario.

#title
The Man in the Balcony

#summary
Staff and patrons at the historic Avon Theatre have repeatedly reported seeing a glowing, solitary figure seated in the empty balcony who vanishes when approached.

#details
Opened in 1901 as a vaudeville house called Theatre Albert before becoming the Avon Theatre in the 1940s, the building is said to harbor lingering spirits from its theatrical past. Witnesses have observed a pale face glowing in the darkness of the empty upper balcony, offering a blank stare before slowly dissolving into nothingness when staff walk up to investigate. 

#location
The Avon Theatre is located at 99 Downie Street in downtown Stratford, Ontario.

#title
The Phantom Violinist of the Queen's Inn

#summary
Guests and management at the historic Queen's Inn have heard mysterious violin music playing from empty, locked guest rooms on the upper floor.

#details
Following a rebuild in 1905 after the original 1858 structure was destroyed by fire, the Queen's Inn became the site of unexplained auditory phenomena concentrated on the second floor. On one occasion, a guest phoned the front desk to complain about loud violin music coming from the adjacent room; while the innkeeper could clearly hear the music over the phone line, checking the hallway revealed complete silence, and the neighboring room was found entirely empty.

#location
The Queen's Inn is located at 161 Ontario Street in Stratford, Ontario.

#title
The Restless Soldiers of Drummond Hill Cemetery

#summary
Serving as the burial ground for casualties of the 1814 Battle of Lundy's Lane, Drummond Hill Cemetery is frequently reported to be haunted by phantom soldiers marching among the graves.

#details
Drummond Hill Cemetery holds immense historical significance as the site of the Battle of Lundy’s Lane during the War of 1812, one of the deadliest military engagements in Canadian history. Because numerous soldiers who died in combat were buried directly on the grounds, visitors, local residents, and ghost tour guides have long reported paranormal activity. Among the most frequent accounts are sightings of ghostly British soldiers wearing Royal Scots uniforms or red coats, seen limping across the headstones or marching silently at dusk before vanishing into thin air. Witnesses have also reported shadowy figures, glowing orbs, and distant cries echoing through the night.

#location
Drummond Hill Cemetery is located at 611 Lundy's Lane in Niagara Falls, Ontario.

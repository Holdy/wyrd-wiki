#title
The Ghost Lights of Buck Hill Road

#summary
Motorists who visit Buck Hill Road at night and perform a specific ritual often report seeing mysterious, swaying lanterns or balls of light associated with a heartbroken father searching for his lost daughter.

#details
According to local legend in the Ottawa Valley, during the early 20th century, a logger returned home during a severe winter storm, warning his family not to venture outside while he stepped out briefly for firewood. When he returned, he discovered his family dog had bolted into the storm and his young daughter had run out after it. Despite a frantic night-long search with a lantern, he never found her, eventually spending the rest of his life in grief. Ever since, visitors driving down the road near Round Lake at night who flash their headlights three times and call out "Daddy!" into the darkness report seeing a baseball-sized amber, green, or white light swaying back and forth in the trees, mimicking a lantern being carried by someone searching the woods.

#location
Buck Hill Road near Round Lake, located west of Pembroke, Ontario.

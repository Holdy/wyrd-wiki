#title
The Ghost of Selina's Memorial on Kirby Road

#summary
Following a fatal 1993 car crash, the roadside memorial of a teenager named Selina on Kirby Road became the site of persistent ghost sightings where her spirit reportedly taps on visitors' car windows.

#details
On November 1, 1993, a tragic car accident occurred at the intersection of Kirby Road and Pine Valley Drive when a minivan carrying teenagers lost control and plunged into a ditch. Fifteen-year-old Selina Degasperis was ejected from the vehicle and tragically killed. In the aftermath, a roadside tombstone memorial was erected at the site. Over time, local folklore and urban legends enveloped the spot. Visitors and ghost hunters who park near the memorial in the dead of night have reported seeing a young girl sitting under nearby trees, while others claim that if you sit in silence, Selina's spectral presence will manifest and gently tap on your car windows.

#location
The intersection of Kirby Road and Pine Valley Drive in Vaughan, Ontario.

#title
The Phantom One-Headlight Truck of Kirby Road

#summary
Nighttime visitors traveling along Kirby Road frequently report encounters with a menacing phantom semi-truck equipped with only a single working headlight that vanishes into thin air.

#details
Kirby Road in Vaughan has long held a reputation as one of the most mysterious and eerie rural stretches in the region, drawing numerous curiosity-seekers and paranormal investigators. Among the most famous vehicular legends associated with the road is that of a ghost semi-truck featuring just one operational headlight. According to accounts gathered by locals and visitors, the truck suddenly materializes near the roadway's underpass to intimidate trespassers, sometimes appearing to drive directly through parked vehicles before disappearing completely without a trace.

#location
The underpass on Kirby Road in Vaughan, Ontario.

#title
The Haunting of the Old Town Hall and Jail

#summary
Built in 1857 and featuring basement jail cells, the historic Caledonia Old Town Hall is said to be haunted by the spirit of beloved local constable William Stotts, who lived and worked in the building for decades.

#details
Constructed in 1857, the two-story brick town hall served as the heart of municipal governance and law enforcement in Caledonia, housing stone jail cells in its basement until 1945. For nearly twenty years, from 1937 to 1955, constable William Stotts and his wife lived in an apartment on the building's main floor. Stotts was widely known in the community as a gentle, dependable protector who handled policing, traffic direction, and animal control with fairness and dedication until his death in 1959. Local paranormal investigators and visitors report that the historic hall remains active with ghostly phenomena, with many believing that Constable Stotts continues to watch over his former home and workplace.

#location
The Old Town Hall in Caledonia, Haldimand County, Ontario.

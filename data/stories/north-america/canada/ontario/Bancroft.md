#title
The Forgotten Graves of Umfraville Ghost Town

#summary
The overgrown pioneer cemetery of the abandoned 19th-century logging and farming village of Umfraville is said to be haunted by the spirits of early Irish settlers and infants who perished in the harsh wilderness.

#details
Umfraville was once a bustling Irish settlement established along the Old Hastings Road north of Bancroft in the 1860s, boasting hundreds of residents, mills, and churches before economic decline turned it into a ghost town. Today, the village has been entirely reclaimed by the forest, save for stone foundations, cedar fences, and its remote pioneer cemetery located along Umfraville Road. Hidden deep in dense bush, the cemetery contains weathered headstones—including that of Bridgeta Cavanaugh—and numerous unmarked graves of infants and pioneers who succumbed to harsh frontier conditions. Visitors exploring the ruins and the overgrown graveyard have reported an oppressive, eerie atmosphere and unexplained ghostly phenomena tied to the forgotten hardships of the early settlers.

#location
Umfraville Road, north of Ormsby in the Bancroft area, Ontario.

#title
The Hidden Spirits of Old L'Amable Road

#summary
Deep along the historic Old L'Amable Road near Bancroft lies a hidden, overgrown pioneer graveyard where visitors have experienced chilling supernatural encounters.

#details
L'Amable was a thriving lumber and farming community established in the mid-19th century along the colonization roads near Bancroft. While much of the old settlement has faded or been replaced by modern cottages, remnants of the 1800s remain along Old L'Amable Road. Tucked away in the surrounding woods is the hidden and heavily overgrown St. George's Cemetery. Descendants of early settlers and local explorers who have ventured into the graveyard report a profoundly unsettling, creepy atmosphere, sudden drops in temperature, and the lingering sense of unseen watchers among the ancient headstones.

#location
St. George's Cemetery off Old L'Amable Road, L'Amable (near Bancroft), Ontario.

#title
The Lantern Lights of Buck Hill

#summary
According to local legend, a grieving logger who lost his daughter on Buck Hill during the Depression went mad searching for her, and his lantern now manifests as wandering ghost lights.

#details
Buck Hill, located in the region near Bancroft and Round Lake Centre, carries a grim legend dating back to the Great Depression. A young girl chased after the family's pet dog into the woods and vanished into the night. Her father, a local logger, spent relentless days and nights searching the rugged terrain for her until grief ultimately drove him insane. Locals and travelers along Buck Hill Road have reported seeing strange, baseball-sized balls of amber, green, and white light drifting across the hillside, sometimes flaring up dramatically. These mysterious orbs are said to be the flickering lantern carried by the heartbroken logger who continues his endless search for his lost child.

#location
Buck Hill Road, near Round Lake Centre / Bancroft, Ontario.

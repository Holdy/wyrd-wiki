#title
The Guardian Grandfather on the Hope Chest

#summary
A young girl taking an afternoon nap in a Kirkland Lake apartment awoke to see her deceased grandfather sitting calmly across the room on a hope chest.

#details
When Erin was around five years old, her family moved into a larger apartment in Kirkland Lake, giving her a bedroom of her own. During afternoon naps, she would lie in bed and look across the room at a wooden hope chest. One afternoon, she woke up to find a man with bright blue eyes and blonde hair sitting on the chest, watching over her with a warm smile. Feeling completely safe and unafraid, she drifted back to sleep. Later that day, when she described the man to her mother, her mother showed her a photograph of her own father—Erin's grandfather—who had tragically died in a car crash four years before Erin's mother was born.

#location
An apartment located within the town of Kirkland Lake, Ontario.

#title
The Phantom Smoker of Kirkland Lake

#summary
A residential home in Kirkland Lake became the site of poltergeist activity and phantom cigarette smoke, believed to be haunted by the previous owner who died following a fatal fall down the stairs.

#details
Tanya and her family experienced a series of unexplainable disturbances in their Kirkland Lake home. A cookie sheet was thrown across the kitchen, a broom was hurled down the basement stairs, and ceiling fan lights inexplicably flickered and burned out. Later, a baby bottle vanished without a trace while being prepared. Neighbours informed Tanya that the previous owner, an elderly man, had tragically died after breaking his neck in a fall down the house's stairs. To this day, the strong scent of cigarette smoke occasionally drifts through the kitchen and living room at night, echoing the habits of the former resident.

#location
A residential house located within the town of Kirkland Lake, Ontario.

The Ghost of Birdie at Rose Manor

Summary

Birdie Rose, the daughter of a prominent local merchant who died in 1956 after being forbidden from marrying her true love, continues to gently haunt her historic family home in Welland.

Details

Built in the historic heart of Welland, Rose Manor and its adjacent family properties were home to Birdie Rose for much of her life. According to local lore, Birdie fell deeply in love with a man from Buffalo, but her father, prominent merchant Hugh Alexander Rose, strongly disapproved of the relationship and forbade her from marrying him. Because archaic laws of the era prevented unmarried women from holding property ownership independently, her brother oversaw the home where she resided until her passing in 1956—with her final act reportedly being the reading and burning of letters from her lost beau. Today, current owners and guests frequently experience her gentle presence. Rather than hostile activity, Birdie is known to open doors and drawers, make pots of coffee, pace softly upstairs at night, and playfully return items that had previously gone missing.

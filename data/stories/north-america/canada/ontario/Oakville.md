#title
The Ghostly Smoke of Market Square

#summary
Visitors to the historic Market Square intersection in Oakville have reported smelling sudden, unexplained burning odours tied to the site's fiery past.

#details
Long before modern commerce filled the area, the intersection of Navy Street and William Street in downtown Oakville was home to the town's original municipal town hall and jailhouse—both of which were tragically destroyed in massive fires during the 1800s. Today, tourists and passersby lingering at Market Square have reported picking up a distinct, heavy scent of burning smoke hanging in the air when no physical fire source is present, believed by locals to be a phantom echo of the historic blaze that leveled the town buildings.

#location
Market Square is located at the intersection of Navy Street and William Street in downtown Oakville, Ontario.

#title
The Phantoms of Erchless Estate

#summary
Home to Oakville's founding family, the historic Erchless Estate is said to be haunted by nearly a dozen spirits, including the matriarch Rebecca Chisholm.

#details
Erchless Estate, built by the Chisholm family beginning in the 1830s, served as the homestead for Oakville's founder, William Chisholm. According to local lore and historical society accounts, at least eight or nine distinct ghosts haunt the grounds and the main house (now the Oakville Museum). Among them is Rebecca Chisholm, the wife of William, who reportedly dropped dead near the property's gate. Visitors and staff have frequently reported seeing her apparition picking flowers by the garden gate or wandering the second-storey hallways. Other reported phantoms include the spirit of butler and Underground Railroad traveler Christopher Columbus Lee, as well as unexplained phenomena like lights flashing from locked rooms and unassisted movement of items. Additionally, the estate's rebuilt Coach House is said to echo the sounds of the fire that destroyed its predecessor.

#location
Erchless Estate is located at the corner of Navy Street and King Street in downtown Oakville, Ontario.

#title
The Restless Breckons of Spruce Lane Farmhouse

#summary
The Victorian-era Spruce Lane Farmhouse at Bronte Creek Provincial Park is plagued by unexplained footsteps, self-locking doors, and phantom activity.

#details
Built in the Victorian era and once home to the Breckon family, Spruce Lane Farmhouse inside Bronte Creek Provincial Park retains its original nineteenth-century styling and furnishings. Over the years, staff, visitors, and paranormal investigators have reported numerous uncanny occurrences within the home. The ghost of the Breckon family is said to still inhabit the rooms, frequently locking and unlocking the smoking room door on its own, pacing the hallways with heavy footsteps, and occasionally showing displeasure by rearranging household items. The farmhouse's lingering paranormal activity has even attracted television crews from programs such as *Ghost Trackers* and surveys by regional paranormal societies.

#location
Spruce Lane Farmhouse is situated within Bronte Creek Provincial Park in western Oakville, Ontario.

#title
The Legend of Maria Baldwin's Cursed Grave

#summary
At one of St. Thomas's oldest churchyards, the gravestone of a young mother turned pitch black, sparking enduring local legends that she was a witch whose tomb caught fire on Halloween.

#details
Located in the historic cemetery of the Old St. Thomas Church in St. Thomas, Ontario, the grave of Maria Baldwin has inspired eerie local folklore for decades. Maria tragically died in 1863 at the age of 22 from complications during childbirth, and her infant child passed away nine months later to be buried beside her. Beginning in the 1970s, her gravestone inexplicably turned a stark, pitch black. This discoloration prompted widespread local rumors that Maria was a witch, that her tomb would spontaneously catch fire on Halloween night, and that anyone who dared cross the four corner posts and touch her stone would be cursed. In truth, historians explained that the stone darkened because it was made of a highly porous material that reacted over time to acid rain and soot from nearby railways.

#location
The cemetery at the Old St. Thomas Church, located near the ravine in St. Thomas, Ontario.

#title
The Resident Ghosts of the Princess Avenue Playhouse

#summary
Performers and visitors at a historic St. Thomas theatre have reported numerous paranormal phenomena, including the weeping ghost of a young girl named Maisie and a mischievous spirit named Mort who returns lost items when cussed out.

#details
Originally built as a church before becoming the Princess Avenue Playhouse (home of the Elgin Theatre Guild), the building at 40 Princess Avenue in St. Thomas is famously haunted by multiple spirits. Among them is a 5- or 6-year-old blonde girl named Maisie, whose crying for help has startled cast members into conducting thorough late-night searches of the building. Another famous entity is a prankster spirit known as Mort (identified by mediums as John), who frequently hides crew members' personal possessions during rehearsals and shows. Crew members discovered that if they stand center stage and loudly hurl profanities at Mort, their missing items promptly reappear where they were left. Other reported apparitions include an unhappy tower ghost of a former church secretary, a shadowy male figure sitting quietly in the balcony, and a mysterious "Philanthropist" who leaves the box office float consistently over by a single nickel every night.

#location
The Princess Avenue Playhouse, located at 40 Princess Avenue, St. Thomas, Ontario.

#title
The Haunting of the Taste of Freedom Inn

#summary
Guests and staff at the Taste of Freedom Inn in Tottenham have reported supernatural encounters, including rattling door handles and phantom figures wandering the halls.

#details
Built in 1883 as a bank, the Taste of Freedom Inn in Tottenham retains its original vaulted ceilings and bank vault. Paranormal investigations and guest reports reveal that the historic building is far from empty. Witnesses have reported seeing an apparition wearing a red smoking jacket or housecoat walking the main floor and adjoining hallways. Additionally, visitors and investigators frequently hear door handles rattling violently during the night in upstairs bedrooms—a phenomenon attributed to an old banker's habit of checking locks to secure the building against intruders.

#location
The Taste of Freedom Inn is located at 64 Queen Street South in Tottenham, Ontario.

#title
The Spirits of the Museum on the Boyne

#summary
The historic buildings of the Museum on the Boyne in Alliston are plagued by paranormal phenomena, including phantom footsteps, disembodied voices, and lingering historical spirits.

#details
Located in Alliston, the Museum on the Boyne features a collection of historic buildings, including the 1851 MacDonald Log Cabin, an 1858 English barn, and a 1915 agricultural fair building. Staff and paranormal investigators have recorded numerous strange occurrences here, such as loud stomping across floors, mysterious cold spots, and display cases opening or rearranging themselves on their own. Among the resident spirits are Andrew, an older gentleman who frequents the attic and balcony, a ghost cat wandering the main floor, and the "Lovely Ladies," who appear every June in high heels before vanishing. In the MacDonald Log Cabin, visitors have reported eerie electronic voice phenomena (EVPs) telling people to get out, intense cold spots, and the oppressive presence of Angus, a spirit said to dislike women with long dark hair.

#location
The Museum on the Boyne is located at 250 Dawson Street in Alliston, Ontario.

#title
The Watchful Caretaker of Beeton Town Hall

#summary
Beeton Town Hall is said to be watched over by the spirit of a former caretaker, with mysterious shuffling footsteps and shadowed figures appearing in the upper windows after dark.

#details
Constructed in the 1880s, Beeton Town Hall has weathered fires, renovations, and decades of community gatherings. According to local lore and eyewitnesses passing by after dusk, the building is never truly empty. Faint shuffling footsteps echo from inside empty corridors, and movement or shadowy figures can occasionally be seen pacing near the upper windows. Local tradition holds that one of the building's dedicated early caretakers, who spent his life maintaining the hall, continues his watch long after passing away.

#location
Beeton Town Hall is located at 38 Main Street West in Beeton, Ontario.

The Ghost of Phoebe Watson at Homer Watson House

Summary

Staff and visitors at a historic Kitchener art gallery frequently encounter the spirit of Phoebe Watson, the artist's sister who passed away in 1947.

Details

Homer Watson, a renowned Canadian landscape artist, famously hosted spiritualist seances in his home after his wife's death. Following Homer's passing, his sister Phoebe continued living in the house until her death in 1947, after which her apparition began appearing throughout the property. Visitors and employees have reported seeing an elderly woman in full Victorian dress with her hair in a bun assisting guests or walking down the hallways. Staff members have also experienced being tapped on the shoulder, having research papers mysteriously left out on the floor, and being locked outside when the front door locked itself. Additionally, employees have heard the sounds of children playing hide-and-seek in the basement after hours.

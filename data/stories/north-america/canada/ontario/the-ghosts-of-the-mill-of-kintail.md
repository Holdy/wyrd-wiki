#title
The Ghosts of the Mill of Kintail

#summary
The historic Mill of Kintail is said to be haunted by sculptor Robert Tait McKenzie and his wife Ethel, whose spirits react to building renovations and guard their former summer home.

#details
In 1932, prominent Canadian doctor and artist Dr. Robert Tait McKenzie converted a long-abandoned 1830s grist mill along the Indian River into a summer home for himself and his wife, Ethel. Following Dr. McKenzie's death in 1938, visitors and local artists working in the mill have frequently reported paranormal phenomena. The spirit of Ethel McKenzie is said to become agitated when her former bedroom and washroom are disturbed by renovations, while Dr. McKenzie's ghost reportedly roams the halls at night to guard his beloved domain.

#location
The Mill of Kintail is located along the Indian River in Ramsay Township, Mississippi Mills, Ontario.

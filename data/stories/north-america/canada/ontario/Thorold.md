#title
The Blue Ghost Tunnel

#summary
An abandoned railway tunnel running beneath the old Welland Canal in Thorold is notoriously haunted by a ghostly fog and tragic echoes from its railway past.

#details
Built in the 1870s as part of the railway infrastructure, the Merritton Tunnel—widely known as the Blue Ghost Tunnel—was abandoned over a century ago after a new bridge route was established. Spanning over 500 feet through stone and timber beneath the canal bed, the damp, subterranean corridor earned its eerie moniker from visitors who reported seeing a strange, glowing blue mist or fog drifting through the freezing interior. Local legend attributes the haunting to fatal railway accidents and workers who lost their lives during the operational years of the line.

#location
The Blue Ghost Tunnel is located along the abandoned railway right-of-way near Seaway Haulage Road in Thorold, Ontario.

#title
The Spirits of Keefer Mansion

#summary
Built in the late 19th century and later serving as a hospital and maternity ward, Thorold's historic Keefer Mansion is reportedly haunted by the lingering spirits of former patients, crying infants, and shadowy figures.

#details
Constructed in 1885 by the Keefer family, this grand heritage mansion in Thorold remained in their possession only briefly before financial ruin forced its sale. Over the decades, the building underwent dramatic changes in purpose, serving at various times as a hospital, a maternity ward, and a chronic care facility. In the years since, visitors, staff, and paranormal investigators have reported hearing the distressed cries of unseen babies, witnessing phantom children playing in the upper halls, and experiencing heavy doors slamming shut on their own in the dead of night.

#location
Keefer Mansion is located at 18 Wright Street in Thorold, Ontario.

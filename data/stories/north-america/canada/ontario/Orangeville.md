#title
The Haunting of the Mill Street Library

#summary
Staff at the Orangeville Public Library's Mill Street branch have experienced doors opening on their own and books pulled from shelves at night, phenomena attributed to the site's turbulent history of fires and early hotel buildings.

#details
Standing in the heart of downtown Orangeville at 1 Mill Street, the library's current structure incorporates historical buildings, including the 1908 Carnegie Library and an old bank. Before these structures were built, the location was home to Bell's Hotel (built in 1860) and later the Gordon House Hotel, both of which were destroyed by devastating fires in 1874 and 1903, respectively. Following renovations in the 1980s, staff reported strange nighttime activity: books mysteriously removed from shelves and left scattered on the floor, and doors swinging open as if someone entered the room when no one was there. A paranormal investigation in 2009 confirmed the presence of multiple spirits, believed by some to be former hotel patrons who perished in the fires or bank employees from decades past.

#location
The Mill Street branch of the Orangeville Public Library is located at 1 Mill Street in Orangeville, Ontario.

#title
The Spectral Residents of Greystones Restaurant

#summary
Greystones Restaurant at 63 Broadway in Orangeville is reportedly haunted by up to eight ghosts, including a former inn worker who died under mysterious circumstances and a scotch-loving spirit named Carl.

#details
Housed in one of the oldest stone buildings in Orangeville, Greystones Restaurant & Lounge—originally established as Graham's Tavern in the mid-1800s—is said to be haunted by as many as eight different spirits. Among the most well-known legends is a female spirit who worked alongside innkeeper Thomas Clegg around 1879. Rumoured to have been romantically involved with Clegg before her body was discovered on the property following a suspicious death, her lingering presence is joined by the presumed spirit of Clegg’s wife, Ann. Another prominent ghost, known as Carl, is said to haunt a second-storey table and is reportedly happiest when an offering of scotch is left for him. Staff over the years have continually reported unexplained noises, moving shadows, and eerie encounters throughout the historic building.

#location
Greystones Restaurant & Lounge is located at 63 Broadway in Orangeville, Ontario.

#title
The Spirits of Orangeville Town Hall

#summary
Orangeville's historic Town Hall is said to be haunted by lingering spirits, including a spectral woman who haunts the former Opera House and entities linked to coroner inquests held in the building's 19th-century morgue.

#details
Built in 1875 at 87 Broadway, Orangeville’s Town Hall has long served as a community hub, but its past includes darker functions, such as coroner inquests and examinations of suspicious or unlawful deaths in the late 1800s and early 1900s, with the meat locker of the market doubling as a temporary morgue. Mediums visiting the historic building have reported the presence of lingering spirits seeking justice or closure. Furthermore, the second-floor assembly hall—transformed into the Orangeville Opera House around 1876—is reportedly haunted by the figure of a woman descending a set of stairs before vanishing into thin air, most frequently seen near the back seats where the original stage stood. These investigations and hauntings were prominently documented by the paranormal research group The Searcher Group.

#location
Orangeville Town Hall is located at 87 Broadway in Orangeville, Ontario.

#title
The Slain War Bride of Room 207

#summary
A British war bride waiting for her soldier husband during the War of 1812 was tragically killed by an American soldier, and her spirit is said to haunt Room 207 of a historic hotel.

#details
During the War of 1812, a woman named Molly McGuire stood at her window waiting for her husband, a British soldier, to return. An American soldier patrolling outside mistook her silhouette in the window for an enemy combatant and thrust his bayonet through her, killing her instantly. Legend holds that her spirit never left, and guests staying in Room 207 have frequently reported unexplained occurrences, including mysterious footsteps, lights turning on by themselves, and an eerie lingering presence.

#location
The Prince of Wales Hotel at 6 Picton Street in Niagara-on-the-Lake, Ontario.

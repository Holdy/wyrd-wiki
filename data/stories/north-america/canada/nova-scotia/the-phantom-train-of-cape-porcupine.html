    The Phantom Train of Cape Porcupine

    Summary

    Years before the railway was ever built, two women walking along the highway near Cape Porcupine on the Mulgrave side of the Strait of Canso were terrified by a roaring phantom train that hurtled past them and crashed through a local fish house. Long afterward, when the railway tracks were officially surveyed and laid out, they passed directly through that exact fish house.

    Details

    In the years before railway lines were extended across the region, two elderly women were walking along the highway skirting the cliffs above the Strait of Canso on the Mulgrave side near Cape Porcupine. Suddenly, the quiet evening was broken by a terrifying rushing noise and a frantic clatter. An enormous, spectral black object with a single blazing headlight roared past them at breakneck speed and plunged directly through a fish house standing near the road, vanishing into thin air. Shaken and terrified, the women fled to the nearest home. Years later, one of the women heard the sound of a real train engine on the mainland and immediately recognized it as the exact mechanical roar she had witnessed years prior. When the railway was eventually surveyed and constructed through the area, the tracks were laid straight through the site of the old fish house.

#title
The Vengeful Spectre of North Mountain

#summary
Following his execution for a brutal murder at his farm, William Robinson vowed to return from the grave, and witnesses later reported seeing his armed ghost wandering the local roads.

#details
In 1904, a horrific crime shocked the rural community on Nova Scotia's North Mountain near Berwick. Theresa McAuley Robinson, a well-regarded local writer and farmer, was found brutally murdered and dismembered within the ashes of her burned-down farmhouse. Suspicion immediately fell upon her second husband, William Robinson, who was subsequently tried, found guilty, and sentenced to hang. Before his execution, William fiercely maintained his innocence and vowed that no grave could hold him, promising to return to seek vengeance on the community. According to historical newspaper reports in *The Acadian*, eyewitnesses soon began spotting William's ghost wandering the local area carrying a firearm and searching for those he believed had wronged him before mysteriously vanishing into thin air.

#location
North Mountain, near Berwick and Burlington, Nova Scotia.

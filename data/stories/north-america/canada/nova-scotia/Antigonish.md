#title
The Blue Nun and the Red Priest of Gilmora Hall

#summary
Gilmora Hall at St. Francis Xavier University is said to be haunted by the tragic spirits of a nun and a priest who took their own lives after an illicit romance in the late nineteenth century.

#details
According to longstanding campus lore dating back to the late 1800s, a young nun working at Mount St. Bernard College entered into a forbidden affair with a Catholic priest. Overwhelmed by guilt after becoming pregnant, the young nun took her own life, prompting the heartbroken priest to follow suit. Ever since, students and staff residing in Gilmora Hall have reported paranormal disturbances including doors slamming on their own, objects moving unassisted, mysterious footsteps echoing through the corridors at night, and the drifting apparition of a woman in a glowing blue habit.

#location
Gilmora Hall on the campus of St. Francis Xavier University in Antigonish, Nova Scotia.

#title
The Fire Spook of Caledonia Mills

#summary
In the winter of 1922, an isolated farmhouse in Caledonia Mills was plagued by dozens of inexplicable spontaneous fires, culminating in thirty-eight blazes in a single night that forced the family to flee.

#details
Beginning in January 1922 at the remote rural farm of Alexander and Janet MacDonald in Caledonia Mills near Antigonish, strange phenomena began occurring around their teenage adopted daughter, Mary Ellen MacDonald. Household items, walls, and ceilings spontaneously burst into pale blue flames, even when soaked in water or covered in snowmelt. On one stormy night, the family and visiting neighbors battled and extinguished thirty-eight separate fires in rapid succession. The bizarre outbreak attracted massive North American media attention, drawing investigations from veteran Maritime detective Peachie Carroll and Boston psychic researcher Walter Prince, who ultimately concluded that Mary Ellen was acting as a poltergeist medium. Fearing for their lives, the MacDonalds abandoned the homestead permanently, and the mysterious fires never returned.

#location
The site of the former Alexander MacDonald farm located in the rural community of Caledonia Mills, approximately 25 kilometres south of Antigonish, Nova Scotia.

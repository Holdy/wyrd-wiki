#title
The Beach-Strolling Maiden of Pictou Island

#summary
Island residents have long shared chilling accounts of a ghostly young woman in a glowing white dress wandering the beaches and winding pathways of Pictou Island. She is said to be the spirit of a heartbroken maiden endlessly searching for her sailor lover, whose ship perished in a fierce Northumberland Strait tempest.

#details
According to island folklore, a radiant local maiden fell deeply in love with a passing sailor who vowed to return to her. Shortly after setting sail on a fishing expedition, his ship encountered a violent tempest near Pictou Island and sank with all hands lost. Devastated by grief, the maiden spent her days and nights roaming the island's desolate shores and pathways under the moonlight, always searching for a ship that would never return. Over time, her sorrow transformed her into an eternal specter. To this day, when the moon is high and the night is still, witnesses report seeing her ethereal figure in a glowing white dress haunting the island's beaches.

#location
The beaches and pathways of Pictou Island, located in the Northumberland Strait off Pictou, Nova Scotia.

#title
The Ghost in the Chimney at the Old Pictou Post Office

#summary
Locals and visitors have long reported an eerie presence and the feeling of being watched at the historic 1895 post office in downtown Pictou. Legend holds that the ghost is that of the town's stern first postmaster, whose desk was positioned to look out through a unique window embedded within the building's massive chimney.

#details
Built in 1895, the old post office at the corner of George and Water Streets in downtown Pictou features a rare and peculiar architectural feature: a massive chimney containing an embedded window, unparalleled anywhere else in North America. Over the years, tenants and visitors reported feeling watched and sensing a male spectral presence lurking behind the chimney. According to local lore, this spirit belongs to Pictou's first postmaster—a humorless, grim individual who kept his desk positioned to command a view through the chimney window. Trapped in an eternal loop, his ghost continues to cast disapproving glares at anyone interrupting his work across time.

#location
The old 1895 post office located at the corner of George and Water Streets in downtown Pictou, Nova Scotia.

#title
The Ghost Ship of Northumberland Strait

#summary
For over two centuries, sailors and fishermen traversing the Northumberland Strait near Pictou have reported sightings of a mysterious phantom vessel. Often appearing engulfed in an unearthly fiery glow, the ghostly ship features a doomed crew fruitlessly battling eternal flames.

#details
Dating back to the late 1700s, legends of the Ghost Ship describe a mysterious vessel sailing through the Northumberland Strait. Early accounts told of a ghostly captain searching for a lost love, while later 19th-century legends spoke of a mutinous crew murdered by their captain, condemned to roam the seas. One of the most striking features associated with the legend is its appearance on fire: eyewitnesses describe an incandescent, flickering inferno illuminating the night sky, with ghostly crew members desperately manipulating ropes and sails in a vain attempt to extinguish the flames. Whether seen as a weather warning or a harbinger of death, the burning phantom ship remains one of the Maritimes' most enduring maritime legends.

#location
The Northumberland Strait waters surrounding Pictou, Nova Scotia.

    The Legend of Myrtle the Shadowless Ghost

    Summary

    Originating from an old photograph of a young girl missing her shadow, the legend of Myrtle developed into local folklore regarding paranormal activity at a historic community hall in Clark's Harbour.

    Details

    The folklore centers around an old photograph displayed at the local heritage center depicting a young girl who casts no shadow. What began as local lore and a lighthearted joke eventually transformed into a persistent haunting legend. Visitors and staff inside the historic building have reported unexplainable loud noises, heavy footsteps, and unexplained bumps echoing through the halls when the building is empty. Paranormal investigators and film crews have since visited the site to document the eerie occurrences tied to Myrtle's purported presence.

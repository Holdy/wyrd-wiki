#title
The Hidden Hauntings of O'Dell House

#summary
Built around 1869 as a stagecoach inn and tavern along the Annapolis Royal waterfront, the O'Dell House Museum is known for recurring paranormal activity and after-hours ghost investigations.

#details
The O'Dell House Museum stands on the Annapolis Royal waterfront as a testament to the town's 19th-century commercial and travel history, originally operating as a busy stagecoach inn and tavern. In local folklore and among heritage staff, the historic building is frequently associated with unexplained phenomena, leading to regular after-hours paranormal investigations conducted by groups such as Paranormal Phenomena Research & Investigation (PPRI). Visitors and investigators inside the circa-1869 structure have reported strange atmospheric shifts, unexplained noises, and shadowy apparitions moving through the historic rooms.

#location
O'Dell House Museum, located on the waterfront along St. George Street in Annapolis Royal, Nova Scotia.

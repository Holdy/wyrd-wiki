#title
The Bible Hill Poltergeist

#summary
In the winter of 1941, a suburban home on Farnham Road in Bible Hill experienced relentless poltergeist activity, featuring self-moving furniture, levitating kitchenware, and phantom footsteps. The phenomena drew massive crowds and media attention before being investigated by local journalists and photographers.

#details
Beginning in December 1941 inside a house on Farnham Road occupied by Mrs. Lucy Langille, Mrs. Rachel Hull, and their children, a series of bizarre and inexplicable events terrified the household. Closet doors opened to reveal empty shoes walking across the floor, stove lids jittered, clocks and cutlery leaped through the air, and a fireplace poker was hurled at a visiting neighbor. As word spread, curious onlookers flooded Bible Hill, prompting intervention and traffic control by the Royal Canadian Mounted Police. Newspapers and photographers eventually visited the home, capturing photographs during an incident where a flat iron on the stove rattled and moved, leading to intense public debate over whether supernatural forces or young family members were responsible.

#location
The key location of the haunting is a residential house situated on Farnham Road in Bible Hill, a suburban community directly across the Salmon River from Truro, Nova Scotia.

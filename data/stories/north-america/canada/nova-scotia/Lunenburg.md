#title
The Phantom Janitor of Lunenburg Academy

#summary
Perched atop Gallows Hill next to a centuries-old cemetery, the 19th-century Lunenburg Academy is famously haunted by a beloved former school custodian whose face was captured in spirit photographs decades after his death.

#details
Dominating the Lunenburg skyline from its hilltop perch, the sprawling Victorian-era Lunenburg Academy has long been a focal point for local ghost lore. Generations of students whispered tales of a heavy, oppressive atmosphere in the basement—locally nicknamed "The Dungeon"—while paranormal investigators later captured the clear apparition of a face peering from one of the school's windows. When circulated among town residents, locals immediately recognized the face from a 1960 school yearbook as Sidney Kernickle, a long-serving former school janitor. Directly across the street sits Hillcrest Cemetery, established in 1761, where local legend also tells of the restless spirit of a 14-year-old girl named Sophia, buried behind a wrought-iron fence after reportedly dying of a broken heart.

#location
Lunenburg Academy, located at the top of Gallows Hill in Lunenburg, Nova Scotia.

#title
The Spectres of King Street

#summary
Built in 1831 to commemorate a royal coronation, the historic Georgian mansion at 15 King Street in Lunenburg has served multiple roles over the centuries while accumulating numerous reports of ghostly apparitions and unexplained phenomena.

#details
Originally constructed by attorney Charles Bolman to mark the 1831 coronation of King William IV, the striking building at 15 King Street features a regionally specific architectural feature known as a "Lunenburg bump"—an alcove where local legend claims fishermen's wives once watched the ocean for their husbands' returning boats. Over nearly two centuries, the property has operated as a private home, a doctor's office, a rooming house, a nursing home, a mayor's residence, and eventually a boutique hotel known as the Mariner King Inn. Throughout its long history, staff members and occupants have frequently reported paranormal encounters, including sudden flashes of light, doors slamming shut on their own, and melancholic spectral figures gazing out from the upper windows.

#location
15 King Street, Lunenburg, Nova Scotia.

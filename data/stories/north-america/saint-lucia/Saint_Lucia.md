#title
The Haunting and Possession in Bisee

#summary
A Guyanese family living in Saint Lucia was forced to abandon their home after an aggressive evil spirit possessed their teenage son. Religious leaders later stated that the entity had been maliciously planted on the property.

#details
After purchasing land and beginning construction on a three-bedroom house in the Bisee community of Castries, a family from Guyana encountered a strange, eel-like creature while digging for a septic tank. The father poured gasoline on the creature and burned its legs; that same evening, he experienced a matching burning sensation on his own legs. Soon after moving into the unfinished house, intense supernatural phenomena began, including scratching, tapping, disembodied whispers, and doors moving on their own. The terror culminated when their 16-year-old son was violently possessed in the middle of the night, screaming that a "jumbie" was coming for him as his neck twisted unnaturally. The entity then attempted to possess the father before the terrified family fled the property entirely.

#location
An unfinished three-bedroom house located in the residential hills of Bisee, Castries, Saint Lucia.

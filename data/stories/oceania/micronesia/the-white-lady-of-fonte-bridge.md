#title
The White Lady of Fonté Bridge

#summary
A grieving spirit of a woman mistreated by her husband haunts a village bridge in Guam, appearing at dusk to travelers passing by. Her tragic death in the nearby river led to generations of sightings by villagers and churchgoers.

#details
In the late 1600s, a Spanish officer married a prominent Chamorro woman and settled in the village of Maina. Over time, the officer grew abusive and demanding, forcing his wife to make daily treks to fetch fresh water from the nearby Fonté River. One stormy night, during a violent downpour, he ordered her to fetch water again. While walking in the dark, she slipped into the turbulent, swollen river and was swept away to her death, her body never found. Stricken with remorse, her husband died lonely shortly afterward. Since then, a sorrowful apparition known as the White Lady—dressed in a flowing white gown with long silvery hair and teary eyes—has been frequently seen near the river bridge in Maina at dusk before fading away as the moon rises.

#location
The bridge crossing the Fonté River in the village of Maina on the island of Guam.

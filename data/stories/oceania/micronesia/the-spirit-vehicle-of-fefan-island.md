#title
The Spirit Vehicle of Fefan Island

#summary
A young girl in Chuuk State is suddenly seized and possessed by the spirits of her deceased relatives during a family gathering, exhibiting preternatural strength and speaking in their exact voices to resolve family disputes.

#details
Documented in ethnographic records of Micronesian folklore and real-world accounts collected by researchers Francis X. Hezel and Jay D. Dobbin, the event occurred within a family home in the islands of Chuuk. A young woman suddenly fell back from her seat and lost consciousness after complaining of a severe headache. Upon awakening, she began screaming and thrashing violently. When male relatives attempted to intervene, she displayed astonishing, preternatural physical strength that easily overpowered grown men. Adopting the exact timbre, voice characteristics, facial expressions, and mannerisms of her deceased mother and aunt, the possessing entity harangued the household regarding unresolved family tensions and a bitter land dispute. After hours of upheaval, the spirits departed, leaving the girl exhausted and with total amnesia of the possession episode, while the family promptly resolved their disputes.

#location
A residential family house in the village on Fefan Island within the Chuuk Lagoon, Federated States of Micronesia.

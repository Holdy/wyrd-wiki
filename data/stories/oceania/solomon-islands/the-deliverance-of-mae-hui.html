The Deliverance of Mae-hui

Summary

During a 1936 revival in the Solomon Islands, an elderly witchdoctor named Mae-hui was violently possessed by demonic spirits within his hut, until missionaries intervened and commanded the entities to leave in the name of Jesus.

Details

During the 1936 Christian revival in South Malaita, an elderly and long-resistant witchdoctor named Mae-hui began experiencing profound conviction and sought to turn away from his past practices of sorcery and murder. However, the malevolent entities that had controlled him for decades refused to surrender their hold without a fierce struggle. One day, villagers rushed to fetch missionaries, shouting that Mae-hui was shaking terribly and had been overtaken by an evil spirit. Inside his hut, the missionaries found Mae-hui crying out in eerie tones, using supernatural strength to violently shake a rafter with one hand while brandishing an adze in a threatening manner with the other. Stepping forward, the missionaries placed a hand on his shoulder and commanded the possessing demon to leave in the name of Jesus. Mae-hui immediately collapsed in a comatose state as the spirit departed, regaining consciousness shortly afterward completely freed from the entity's control.

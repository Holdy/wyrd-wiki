#title
The Deliverance of Mae-hui

#summary
During a 1936 revival in the Solomon Islands, an elderly witchdoctor named Mae-hui was violently possessed by demonic spirits within his hut, until missionaries intervened and commanded the entities to leave in the name of Jesus.

#details
During the 1936 Christian revival in South Malaita, an elderly and long-resistant witchdoctor named Mae-hui began experiencing profound conviction and sought to turn away from his past practices of sorcery and murder. However, the malevolent entities that had controlled him for decades refused to surrender their hold without a fierce struggle. One day, villagers rushed to fetch missionaries, shouting that Mae-hui was shaking terribly and had been overtaken by an evil spirit. Inside his hut, the missionaries found Mae-hui crying out in eerie tones, using supernatural strength to violently shake a rafter with one hand while brandishing an adze in a threatening manner with the other. Stepping forward, the missionaries placed a hand on his shoulder and commanded the possessing demon to leave in the name of Jesus. Mae-hui immediately collapsed in a comatose state as the spirit departed, regaining consciousness shortly afterward completely freed from the entity's control.

#location
Mae-hui's house in the village of Menehelisi on South Malaita Island in the Solomon Islands.

#title
The Wave Curse of Teonimenu

#summary
After his wife eloped with another man to the island of Teonimenu, a betrayed husband purchased a devastating wave curse that unleashed successive waves until the entire island was submerged. Survivors scattered across neighboring islands, passing down the catastrophic tale through generations.

#details
When a woman named Sauwete'au eloped from Ali'ite Island with another man named Kaliita'alu and settled on the island of Teonimenu, her aggrieved husband Roraimenu vowed vengeance. He travelled and used shell money to purchase a powerful wave curse consisting of eight destructive waves. Returning to Ali'ite Island, Roraimenu climbed a high hill in the interior to watch as the magical waves battered Teonimenu one by one. The eighth and final wave completely overwhelmed and sank the island into the sea, drowning its inhabitants. While most perished, a few survivors managed to float on debris and driftwood, eventually washing ashore on nearby islands such as Ulawa, Malaita, and Makira, where oral traditions continue to preserve the memory of the disaster.

#location
The submerged island of Teonimenu (now known as Lark Shoal) is located at coordinates 9°59'36"S 161°59'10"E in the Pacific Ocean within the Solomon Islands.

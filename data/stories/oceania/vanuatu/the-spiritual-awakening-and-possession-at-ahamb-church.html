    The Spiritual Awakening and Possession at Ahamb Church

    Summary

    During a 2014 religious revival on the small island of Ahamb in Vanuatu, local children suddenly fell into trances and were possessed by spiritual entities, leading to intense spiritual warfare against hidden sorcery. Guided by these visions, villagers uncovered buried sorcery stones and animal remains outside their community hall.

    Details

    In early 2014, a charismatic Christian revival swept through the Presbyterian island of Ahamb, just off the south coast of Malekula Island in Vanuatu. During worship services at the Ahamb Presbyterian Community Church, local children and youth unexpectedly fell to the ground unconscious—a state known locally as being "slain in the spirit" ("slen")—as they were taken over by spiritual forces. In this possessed or tranced state, the young visionaries received direct messages and visions from the Holy Spirit, exposing hidden evil forces and sorcery (_posen_) within the community. The tension climaxed when the children experienced sudden trances near the Ahamb community hall, pointing out an active sorcery stone carved with a human face buried in the grass. Led by the children's spiritual guidance, villagers dug up the stone and subsequently conducted nighttime prayer missions across the island, where children shook, shouted, and cried as they battled invisible entities, culminating in the discovery of ritual items such as a buried cat skeleton used in dark magic.

#title
The Wainuiomata Mākutu Lifting

#summary
During a family ritual to lift a suspected curse or possession, a young woman tragically drowned while her relatives attempted to flush out perceived malevolent entities. The improvised exorcism became a landmark real-world account of spirit possession belief in New Zealand history.

#details
In October 2007, 22-year-old Janet Moses began exhibiting erratic behavior following personal hardships and stress. Convinced that she was afflicted by a malignant curse or possessed by dark spirits, members of her extended family gathered at a residential flat in Wainuiomata to perform an improvised mākutu lifting (exorcism). Over the course of the ritual, family members forcibly poured large amounts of water into her mouth and eyes and physically attempted to expel the entities they saw in her. Tragically, the ordeal resulted in Janet's death by drowning on October 12, 2007, leading to a landmark police investigation and manslaughter convictions for several relatives who genuinely believed they were saving her life.

#location
The events took place at a residential flat in the Wellington suburb of Wainuiomata, New Zealand.

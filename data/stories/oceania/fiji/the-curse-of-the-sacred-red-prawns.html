The Curse of the Sacred Red Prawns

Summary

A scorned chief's daughter caused a rejected suitor and his delicacy of red prawns to be cast into a pool on the island of Vatulele, establishing a strict taboo. Anyone who dares to touch or remove the sacred red prawns from their pool is cursed with a disastrous shipwreck.

Details

Long ago on the island of Vatulele, a chief's daughter named Yalewan-ni-Cagi-Bula—the Maiden-of-the-Fair-Wind—was renowned for her beauty but possessed a harsh and scornful heart. When a young mainland chief traveled across the sea to court her, he brought a prized gift of rare red prawns cooked in coconut milk. Rejecting both the suitor and his offering, the haughty maiden ordered her attendants to cast him over the cliffside. The young chief fell into the sea below, and his gift of prawns plunged into a secluded rocky pool, where they miraculously came back to life and turned bright scarlet. From that day onward, a strict sacred taboo (tabu) was placed upon the pool, declaring that the red prawns must never be disturbed or harmed. Anyone foolish enough to steal or interfere with them faces a delayed and dreaded curse: a violent shipwreck on the surrounding seas.

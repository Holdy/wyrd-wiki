#title
The Fatal Exorcism of Joan Vollmer

#summary
In January 1993, a Victorian pig farmer and a group of fundamentalist acquaintances subjected his wife to a brutal four-day exorcism to expel ten demons, resulting in her tragic death.

#details
In January 1993, pig farmer Ralph Vollmer became convinced that his 49-year-old wife Joan was possessed by evil spirits after her personality shifted and she began acting erratically, speaking in unusual voices and behaving like animals. Believing her body housed ten distinct demons—including entities named "Princess Joan" and "Legion"—Ralph and a circle of local acquaintances, including neighbour Leanne Reichenbach and spiritual advisor Leah Clugston, decided to perform an exorcism at their remote rural property. Over four days in extreme summer heat, Joan was bound with stockings to a chair, denied food and water, beaten across the face, and subjected to intense pressure on her neck and abdomen to squeeze the entities from her body. The violent ordeal fractured her thyroid cartilage and triggered a fatal heart attack. Following her death, the group left her body in the sweltering heat, praying and waiting for days under the firm belief that she would miraculously resurrect from the dead, before a minister and local authorities intervened.

#location
The remote farmhouse located in the rural township of Antwerp in the Wimmera district of Victoria, Australia.

#title
The Legend of Devil's Pool

#summary
A grieving Aboriginal woman named Oolana threw herself into the waters of far north Queensland, casting a lethal curse that lures young men to their deaths. Her restless spirit continues to guard the pool, pulling swimmers into its deadly undercurrents.

#details
According to local Indigenous lore, a young Yindinji woman named Oolana was newly married to an elder named Waroonoo when she fell in love with a handsome man named Dyga from another tribe. When the lovers were discovered by tribal elders, Oolana broke free from her captors and cast herself into the waters of the Babinda Boulders, calling out for Dyga to follow her. As Dyga plunged in, her anguished cries transformed the tranquil pools into a violent, churning torrent. Since then, a dark curse has gripped the waters. Oolana's spirit is said to forever patrol the depths, luring unsuspecting swimmers—predominantly young men—into the deadly underwater chutes and fierce currents known locally as the "Washing Machine."

#location
Devil's Pool at the Babinda Boulders is located within the rainforest of far north Queensland, approximately 60 kilometres south of Cairns.

#title
The Pilliga Princess

#summary
An eccentric woman who famously wandered the Newell Highway in New South Wales was tragically struck and killed by a truck in 1993. Since her death, motorists and truck drivers have frequently reported seeing her ghostly apparition pushing her shopping trolley along the highway after dark.

#details
For many years, an eccentric woman known as the "Pilliga Princess" was a well-known figure along the Newell Highway between Narrabri and Coonabarabran in New South Wales, frequently hitchhiking and pushing her shopping trolley. On a dark night in 1993, her life came to a tragic end when she was struck and killed by a truck while wandering across the road. Ever since that fateful night, drivers traveling along the highway continue to encounter her spectral form, faithfully pushing her shopping trolley through the dark Australian bushland.

#location
The Newell Highway between the towns of Narrabri and Coonabarabran in New South Wales.

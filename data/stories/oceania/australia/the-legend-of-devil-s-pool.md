#title
The Legend of Devil's Pool

#summary
A grieving Aboriginal woman named Oolana threw herself into the waters of far north Queensland, casting a lethal curse that lures young men to their deaths. Her restless spirit continues to guard the pool, pulling swimmers into its deadly undercurrents.

#details
According to local Indigenous lore, a young Yindinji woman named Oolana was newly married to an elder named Waroonoo when she fell in love with a handsome man named Dyga from another tribe. When the lovers were discovered by tribal elders, Oolana broke free from her captors and cast herself into the waters of the Babinda Boulders, calling out for Dyga to follow her. As Dyga plunged in, her anguished cries transformed the tranquil pools into a violent, churning torrent. Since then, a dark curse has gripped the waters. Oolana's spirit is said to forever patrol the depths, luring unsuspecting swimmers—predominantly young men—into the deadly underwater chutes and fierce currents known locally as the "Washing Machine."

#location
Devil's Pool at the Babinda Boulders is located within the rainforest of far north Queensland, approximately 60 kilometres south of Cairns.

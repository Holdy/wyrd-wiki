#title
The Mass Possession at Atauloma Girls School

#summary
Eighteen students at a Samoan girls school suffered a violent mass spirit possession after trespassing in a sacred water hole near ancient star pyramids. The powerful entity hurled the girls off a bus while local pastors and families attempted in vain to intervene.

#details
Following a field trip to ancient star pyramids where students swam naked in a restricted water hole, eighteen girls from the Atauloma Girls School in Afao fell victim to a severe mass possession attributed to an ancient spirit named Tuiatua. All eighteen students began speaking simultaneously, overwhelmed by a powerful, unseen force that resisted the prayers of local pastors and families trying to restrain them. Witnesses noted that the terrifying event coincided with a large twenty-foot statue of the Virgin Mary mysteriously crashing down onto the road outside a nearby church.

#location
The events took place at the historic Atauloma Girls School located in the village of Afao on Tutuila Island, American Samoa.

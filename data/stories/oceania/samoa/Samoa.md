#title
The Legend of the House of Rock

#summary
When a labor competition between village men and women to thatch a chief's house resulted in the men's abysmal failure, the infuriated chief cursed the structure, turning it permanently into solid stone. Today, this volcanic cave formation stands as a celebrated cultural landmark and the origin of a famous Samoan proverb.

#details
In ancient Samoa, a chief named Tautunu commanded the people of Falealupo to build a grand communal house, with the men and women tasked with thatching opposite halves of the roof. While the women worked diligently and completed their side ahead of schedule, the men lagged far behind in their performance. Enraged by the men's abysmal failure and incompetence, Chief Tautunu placed a powerful curse upon the building, transforming the entire structure—along with its poles and thatched roof—into permanent rock. Known as the *Fale o le Ma'a* (House of Rock), this lava tube cave became a permanent monument and inspired the traditional Samoan proverb honoring the determination and reliability of women.

#location
The House of Rock (*Fale o le Ma'a*) is situated along a winding path amidst volcanic rock in the village of Falealupo on the island of Savai'i in Samoa.

#title
The Mass Possession at Atauloma Girls School

#summary
Eighteen students at a Samoan girls school suffered a violent mass spirit possession after trespassing in a sacred water hole near ancient star pyramids. The powerful entity hurled the girls off a bus while local pastors and families attempted in vain to intervene.

#details
Following a field trip to ancient star pyramids where students swam naked in a restricted water hole, eighteen girls from the Atauloma Girls School in Afao fell victim to a severe mass possession attributed to an ancient spirit named Tuiatua. All eighteen students began speaking simultaneously, overwhelmed by a powerful, unseen force that resisted the prayers of local pastors and families trying to restrain them. Witnesses noted that the terrifying event coincided with a large twenty-foot statue of the Virgin Mary mysteriously crashing down onto the road outside a nearby church.

#location
The events took place at the historic Atauloma Girls School located in the village of Afao on Tutuila Island, American Samoa.

#title
The Midnight Passengers of Savai'i

#summary
A police officer driving his pickup truck through a deserted rainforest road in Samoa picks up two elderly women dressed in Sunday best, only to discover they have mysteriously vanished from the passenger seat upon reaching the edge of town.

#details
In the 1960s or 1970s, police officer Lipine was driving his battered two-seater pickup truck alone at night through a dark rainforest road on the island of Savai'i. Rounding a bend, he was startled to see two large, older women dressed in matching Sunday best walking along the empty road. Lipine stopped and offered them a ride, and the women climbed into the crowded bench seat, filling the cabin with their loud chatter and laughter. However, when Lipine reached the outskirts of town and turned to let them out, the passenger seat was entirely empty.

#location
The rainforest road on the southern edge of the island of Savai’i, Samoa.

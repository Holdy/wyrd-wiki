#title
The Children of Houma Road

#summary
A driver frequently buys fruit from two young roadside vendors before learning they are the ghosts of siblings killed years earlier. When he attempts to offer them a ride, the children sprint and vanish into the brush.

#details
While driving along an isolated road in Tonga, a man regularly stopped over the course of several days to buy small fruits and peppers from a young boy carrying a machete and his little sister. Sometime later, while driving alone at night, he spotted the two children walking along the roadside and pulled over to offer them a ride home, calling out the boy's name, Mafi. Instead of answering, the children turned and bolted into the high roadside grass at supernatural speed before completely disappearing. Upon recounting the eerie encounter to locals, he discovered that Mafi and his sister, from the village of Houma, had been killed years prior by a drunk driver.

#location
A roadside stretch near the village of Houma on Tongatapu island in Tonga.

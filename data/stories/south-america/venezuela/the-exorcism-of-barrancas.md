#title
The Exorcism of Barrancas

#summary
A malicious demonic entity took complete possession of a local person in Venezuela, triggering a desperate nocturnal exorcism inside the church sacristy.

#details
Documented within the traditional urban legends of the Táchira region in Venezuela, the account of the Exorcism of Barrancas tells of a violent spiritual possession that terrified the local community. A resident was overtaken by a dark, hostile force that granted them unnatural strength and caused them to utter terrifying, defiant declarations rejecting sacred prayers. Faced with the escalating threat, the local religious leadership ordered an immediate exorcism. The afflicted individual was tightly bound with ropes soaked in holy water onto a heavy table within the church sacristy, where priests conducted an intense spiritual battle throughout the night to cast out the entity.

#location
The events took place in the sacristy of the church located in the Barrancas sector of San Cristóbal, Táchira State, Venezuela.

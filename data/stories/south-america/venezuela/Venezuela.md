#title
The Curse of the Güigüe Clock

#summary
A magnificent four-faced clock, cursed by a dying dictator in Venezuela, brings mysterious and untimely deaths to every watchmaker who dares to repair its stopped hands. Over generations, anyone who successfully restarts the mechanism meets a fatal end precisely aligned with the time the clock displays.

#details
Originally commissioned in the 19th century by President Antonio Guzmán Blanco for his coffee haciendas, the elaborate four-faced clock was later moved to the estate of the dictator General Juan Vicente Gómez. As Gómez's health failed and he faced losing his power and possessions, he placed a dark curse upon the clock so that its hands would stop forever, halting at 11:45 p.m.—the exact moment of his death. Over the decades, the clock was relocated to Plaza Bolívar and Plaza Ávila in front of the parish house in the city of Güigüe. Whenever a bold watchmaker or technician managed to repair the mechanism and get the clock running again, they met a sudden, unexplained death shortly afterward, with their time of passing uncannily matching the hour displayed on the clock face. From early local technicians like Salvatore Consoli and Andrés Mijares to later skeptics and even an international watchmaker named Crónidas Piaget, all succumbed to the relentless curse.

#location
The cursed clock is located in Plaza Bolívar / Plaza Ávila in front of the parish house in the city of Güigüe, Carlos Arvelo Municipality, Carabobo State, Venezuela.

#title
The Exorcism of Barrancas

#summary
A malicious demonic entity took complete possession of a local person in Venezuela, triggering a desperate nocturnal exorcism inside the church sacristy.

#details
Documented within the traditional urban legends of the Táchira region in Venezuela, the account of the Exorcism of Barrancas tells of a violent spiritual possession that terrified the local community. A resident was overtaken by a dark, hostile force that granted them unnatural strength and caused them to utter terrifying, defiant declarations rejecting sacred prayers. Faced with the escalating threat, the local religious leadership ordered an immediate exorcism. The afflicted individual was tightly bound with ropes soaked in holy water onto a heavy table within the church sacristy, where priests conducted an intense spiritual battle throughout the night to cast out the entity.

#location
The events took place in the sacristy of the church located in the Barrancas sector of San Cristóbal, Táchira State, Venezuela.

#title
The Seaside Bride

#summary
A spectral bride haunts a mountain highway, waiting on the roadside or manifesting in the back seat of lonely vehicles to cause fatal accidents.

#details
Drivers traveling at night along the old Caracas-La Guaira road are warned never to pick up lone female hitchhikers. Those who ignore the warning may suddenly feel an unseen gaze from the back seat, only to discover a terrifying apparition if they look in the rearview mirror, causing them to crash. Others have spotted her walking slowly along the roadside dressed in a bridal gown, featuring a gruesome twisted torso where her face looks backward while her feet face forward, re-enacting the tragic agony of her wedding day death.

#location
The legend takes place along the old Caracas-La Guaira road connecting Caracas to the coastal state of La Guaira in Venezuela.

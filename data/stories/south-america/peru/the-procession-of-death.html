The Procession of Death

Summary

A gossiping widow witnessing a midnight funeral procession of skeletal figures through the streets of Cajamarca receives a terrifying premonition of her own imminent demise.

Details

In colonial Cajamarca around 1620, an inquisitive and gossipy widow named Doña Jordana looked out her window late at night to observe a solemn funeral procession moving through the dark streets. The figures were not living citizens, but terrifying skeletons covered in white shrouds who carried burning bones like candles and chanted funeral psalms. As the spectral parade passed, one of the skeletal figures stepped forward, warned Doña Jordana to repent for her gossiping tongue, and handed her a burning bone from the afterlife, prophesying that she would join the procession within two days. True to the warning, Doña Jordana was found dead two days later with the burning bone beside her.

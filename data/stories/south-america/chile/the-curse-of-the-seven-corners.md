#title
The Curse of the Seven Corners

#summary
After being rejected and mocked by the townspeople, a wandering preacher climbed the hills above Taltal and cast a generational curse decreeing that its corners would be consumed by fire one by one. True to his prophecy, successive parts of the city have burned down over the generations as the fires slowly march toward the final, doomed intersection.

#details
In 1932, a self-proclaimed ascetic and itinerant preacher known as the "Cristo de Elqui" arrived in the coastal desert town of Taltal. Rather than welcoming him, the local residents met his fiery sermons with mockery, pelting him with stones and driving him out in disgrace. Enraged by the town's hostility, the preacher climbed the slopes of the surrounding hills, turned to look down at the grid of streets, and hurled a bitter curse upon the municipality. He prophesied that Taltal would burn corner by corner, block by block, over future generations, and that the city would not find peace until the very last corner met its fiery fate. Over the decades, local history has recorded a series of devastating, seemingly inexplicable fires that have systematically destroyed designated corners of the town's grid exactly as foretold, leaving the populace living in dread of the final, unfulfilled section.

#location
The hills overlooking the coastal city of Taltal in the Antofagasta Region of northern Chile, specifically descending into the historic urban grid of the town.

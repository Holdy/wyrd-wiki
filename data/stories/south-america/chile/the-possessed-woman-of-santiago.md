#title
The Possessed Woman of Santiago

#summary
In 1857, a young orphan girl residing in a Santiago hospice suffered violent seizures, superhuman strength, and terrifying contorsions that culminated in the first officially documented exorcism in Chile.

#details
Carmen Marín, an orphan girl living under the care of the Sisters of Charity at their hospice in Santiago, began experiencing terrifying nighttime attacks at the age of twelve. During these episodes, she suffered from severe convulsions, unnatural bodily arches, voice distortions, and superhuman strength while hurling curses at divine figures. While contemporary physicians debated whether her symptoms stemmed from severe hysteria or psychological trauma, attending priests noted that her violent fits could only be subdued by reading aloud from the Gospel of Saint John. The overwhelming manifestations prompted the Archbishopric of Santiago to order a formal exorcism, marking the first documented case of its kind in Chilean history before the entity reportedly departed with a dark prophecy.

#location
The events took place at the Hospicio de las Hermanas de la Caridad located on Calle de la Maestranza in Santiago, Chile.

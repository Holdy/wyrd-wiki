#title
The Curse of the Seven Corners

#summary
After being rejected and mocked by the townspeople, a wandering preacher climbed the hills above Taltal and cast a generational curse decreeing that its corners would be consumed by fire one by one. True to his prophecy, successive parts of the city have burned down over the generations as the fires slowly march toward the final, doomed intersection.

#details
In 1932, a self-proclaimed ascetic and itinerant preacher known as the "Cristo de Elqui" arrived in the coastal desert town of Taltal. Rather than welcoming him, the local residents met his fiery sermons with mockery, pelting him with stones and driving him out in disgrace. Enraged by the town's hostility, the preacher climbed the slopes of the surrounding hills, turned to look down at the grid of streets, and hurled a bitter curse upon the municipality. He prophesied that Taltal would burn corner by corner, block by block, over future generations, and that the city would not find peace until the very last corner met its fiery fate. Over the decades, local history has recorded a series of devastating, seemingly inexplicable fires that have systematically destroyed designated corners of the town's grid exactly as foretold, leaving the populace living in dread of the final, unfulfilled section.

#location
The hills overlooking the coastal city of Taltal in the Antofagasta Region of northern Chile, specifically descending into the historic urban grid of the town.

#title
The Possessed Woman of Santiago

#summary
In 1857, a young orphan girl residing in a Santiago hospice suffered violent seizures, superhuman strength, and terrifying contorsions that culminated in the first officially documented exorcism in Chile.

#details
Carmen Marín, an orphan girl living under the care of the Sisters of Charity at their hospice in Santiago, began experiencing terrifying nighttime attacks at the age of twelve. During these episodes, she suffered from severe convulsions, unnatural bodily arches, voice distortions, and superhuman strength while hurling curses at divine figures. While contemporary physicians debated whether her symptoms stemmed from severe hysteria or psychological trauma, attending priests noted that her violent fits could only be subdued by reading aloud from the Gospel of Saint John. The overwhelming manifestations prompted the Archbishopric of Santiago to order a formal exorcism, marking the first documented case of its kind in Chilean history before the entity reportedly departed with a dark prophecy.

#location
The events took place at the Hospicio de las Hermanas de la Caridad located on Calle de la Maestranza in Santiago, Chile.

#title
The Widow of the Route

#summary
A mysterious woman dressed in black frequently appears on lonely stretches of the highway at night, vanishing whenever drivers stop to offer assistance. Her spectral presence is traditionally associated with tragic accidents occurring further ahead along the route.

#details
Along the desolate expanses of Chile's northern highway, lone travelers and truck drivers navigating the night often encounter a solitary woman dressed entirely in mourning black standing by the asphalt. When kind-hearted motorists pull over to help or offer her a ride, she disappears without a trace into the desert air. Lore among regional drivers links these encounters to fatal vehicle accidents waiting miles further down the road.

#location
The ghost is sighted along the remote Quillagua–Pozo Almonte stretch of Route 5 North traversing the Atacama Desert in Chile.

#title
The Widow of the Route

#summary
A mysterious woman dressed in black frequently appears on lonely stretches of the highway at night, vanishing whenever drivers stop to offer assistance. Her spectral presence is traditionally associated with tragic accidents occurring further ahead along the route.

#details
Along the desolate expanses of Chile's northern highway, lone travelers and truck drivers navigating the night often encounter a solitary woman dressed entirely in mourning black standing by the asphalt. When kind-hearted motorists pull over to help or offer her a ride, she disappears without a trace into the desert air. Lore among regional drivers links these encounters to fatal vehicle accidents waiting miles further down the road.

#location
The ghost is sighted along the remote Quillagua–Pozo Almonte stretch of Route 5 North traversing the Atacama Desert in Chile.

#title
The Bride of Puerto Colombia

#summary
Motorists traveling along the old road between Barranquilla and Puerto Colombia have long reported encountering a spectral bride who hitches rides or manifests inside vehicles.

#details
Since the 1970s, taxi drivers and bus operators traveling the lonely stretches of the old road connecting Barranquilla and Puerto Colombia have shared chilling accounts of a phantom bride. Most frequently encountered near a notorious stretch known as the "Curva del Diablo" (Devil's Curve), the entity appears on the roadside asking for a ride or materializes inside passing vehicles. Witnesses—including a bus driver named Hugo Rangel—have described the apparition as a terrifying, skeletal woman covered in dirt wearing tattered wedding garments. Those who pick her up or find her suddenly seated in the back report that she leaves behind damp seats, the heavy stench of rotting flowers, or unearthly silence before disappearing into thin air.

#location
The "Curva del Diablo" on the old road between Barranquilla and Puerto Colombia in the Atlántico Department of Colombia.

#title
The Mass Possession of Nóvita

#summary
Twenty-two schoolgirls in Colombia suffered violent seizures, hallucinations, and apparent demonic possession after playing the "Charlie Charlie" pencil game. Rushed to a local medical center foaming at the mouth, the victims described terrifying visions of a menacing man dressed in black.

#details
In July 2016, during a school day in the Chocó region of Colombia, a group of 22 schoolgirls aged 12 to 15 began experiencing simultaneous, violent episodes of bizarre behavior, seizures, and fainting. They were rushed by teachers to the municipal health center in the town of Nóvita. Medical staff reported that the children arrived screaming, mumbling incoherently, hallucinating, and foaming at the mouth. After conducting tests to rule out disease, poisoning, or drug use, doctors found no physical or toxicological causes for their conditions, and their vital signs eventually normalized. One of the affected girls described a horrifying vision she experienced while possessed, claiming a "man dressed in black" warned them about a sheet with red letters. Local officials and spiritual leaders, including a pastor who prayed against witchcraft, intervened to calm the community and address the panic.

#location
The municipal health center in the town of Nóvita, located in the Chocó Department of Colombia.

#title
The Phantoms of Castillo La Alboraya

#summary
Passing down generations through the local community, a sinister curse and haunting atmosphere cling to an abandoned colonial fortress in Barranquilla where dark deeds once took place. Locals tell of ghostly apparitions and a phantom black stallion whose midnight gallop terrorizes those who stay too late near the grounds.

#details
Standing within the bounds of the La Alboraya neighborhood in Barranquilla, a crumbling historical mansion known as Castillo La Alboraya has long been a source of dread and fascination. Local legends trace its dark reputation back to a despotic owner named Borrás, who was rumored to have made a pact with the devil and constructed subterranean tunnels to imprison and enslave victims. Generations of residents have grown up with warnings about the haunted property, passing down tales of a terrifying rider on a blood-smeared black stallion whose hooves can be heard galloping through the halls in the dead of night. Night guards and brave visitors have reported spine-chilling encounters, including a woman dressed in white wandering the ruined corridors and blood-curdling screams echoing from the forgotten courtyards.

#location
Castillo La Alboraya is located within the grounds of the Castillo de la Alboraya District Institute in the La Alboraya neighborhood of Barranquilla, Colombia (near Calle 41B and Carrera 8A).

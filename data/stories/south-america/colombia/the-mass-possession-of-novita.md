#title
The Mass Possession of Nóvita

#summary
Twenty-two schoolgirls in Colombia suffered violent seizures, hallucinations, and apparent demonic possession after playing the "Charlie Charlie" pencil game. Rushed to a local medical center foaming at the mouth, the victims described terrifying visions of a menacing man dressed in black.

#details
In July 2016, during a school day in the Chocó region of Colombia, a group of 22 schoolgirls aged 12 to 15 began experiencing simultaneous, violent episodes of bizarre behavior, seizures, and fainting. They were rushed by teachers to the municipal health center in the town of Nóvita. Medical staff reported that the children arrived screaming, mumbling incoherently, hallucinating, and foaming at the mouth. After conducting tests to rule out disease, poisoning, or drug use, doctors found no physical or toxicological causes for their conditions, and their vital signs eventually normalized. One of the affected girls described a horrifying vision she experienced while possessed, claiming a "man dressed in black" warned them about a sheet with red letters. Local officials and spiritual leaders, including a pastor who prayed against witchcraft, intervened to calm the community and address the panic.

#location
The municipal health center in the town of Nóvita, located in the Chocó Department of Colombia.

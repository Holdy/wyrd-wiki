#title
The Bride of Puerto Colombia

#summary
Motorists traveling along the old road between Barranquilla and Puerto Colombia have long reported encountering a spectral bride who hitches rides or manifests inside vehicles.

#details
Since the 1970s, taxi drivers and bus operators traveling the lonely stretches of the old road connecting Barranquilla and Puerto Colombia have shared chilling accounts of a phantom bride. Most frequently encountered near a notorious stretch known as the "Curva del Diablo" (Devil's Curve), the entity appears on the roadside asking for a ride or materializes inside passing vehicles. Witnesses—including a bus driver named Hugo Rangel—have described the apparition as a terrifying, skeletal woman covered in dirt wearing tattered wedding garments. Those who pick her up or find her suddenly seated in the back report that she leaves behind damp seats, the heavy stench of rotting flowers, or unearthly silence before disappearing into thin air.

#location
The "Curva del Diablo" on the old road between Barranquilla and Puerto Colombia in the Atlántico Department of Colombia.

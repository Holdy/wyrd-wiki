#title
The Exorcism of Mik in Limón Indanza

#summary
A young Shuar girl in Ecuador became afflicted by a violent affliction that prompted a Catholic priest to perform an exorcism at the local parish office. Following the ritual, her pet rabbit mysteriously died on a bus, leaving locals to speculate whether the entity had inhabited the animal.

#details
Father Silverio was called to examine a twelve-year-old Shuar girl named Mik living in Limón Indanza, who suffered from inexplicable behavioral outbursts, nervous tics, and explosive fits of vulgar obscenities whenever holy water or blessings were applied. Brought to the parish office for further intervention, Mik suddenly bolted, racing up the steep slopes surrounding the mission before being restrained by neighbors and brought back. Father Silverio placed his stole over her head and firmly recited an exorcism, commanding the evil spirit to leave three times, after which the girl became completely calm. Shortly afterward, while traveling to Loja with her mother, Mik's pet rabbit died on the bus, prompting community whispers that the devil had possessed the animal.

#location
The events took place at the parish mission office and surrounding hills in the town of Limón Indanza within the Morona Santiago province of Ecuador.

#upstream-source
*Limón Indanza: Notes from My Land Compilation of Travelers' Stories, Interviews, Editorials and Writings from before its foundation in 1914* by Nardo Iñiguez Marín (incorporating the notes of Father Silverio Equisoain).

#title
The Incomplete Basilica Curse

#summary
A persistent urban legend claims that if the massive Basilica del Voto Nacional in Quito is ever fully finished, it will trigger the end of the world. Because of this ominous, delayed curse, construction is kept permanently incomplete.

#details
Dominating the historic skyline of Quito, the Basílica del Voto Nacional is the largest neo-Gothic basilica in the Americas. According to widespread local folklore, the structure carries a fearful supernatural penalty linked to its completion. It is said that the day the final stone is laid and the building is declared fully finished will mark the end of the world. To avert this apocalyptic doom, construction has been intentionally prolonged and left incomplete through perpetual minor repairs and unfinalized details, ensuring the fateful milestone is never reached.

#location
The Basílica del Voto Nacional is located in the historic center of Quito, Ecuador, bordered by Calle Carchi and Calle Venezuela.

#title
The Specters of El Ángel Highway

#summary
While parked on a road near El Ángel in Carchi, a woman and her companions witnessed three robed figures carrying scythes and a trident float past them towards the local cemetery before vanishing into the dawn mist.

#details
In 2021 during the COVID-19 pandemic, Sintia Robles was traveling by car with family and friends from Carchi to Pichincha. At around 2:30 in the morning, she stopped the vehicle near the city of El Ángel to adjust the air conditioning. While waiting, she noticed three figures approaching down the road. As they drew closer, she realized they were dressed in dark robes resembling Holy Week penitents. The two figures on the outer edges carried scythes, while the middle figure carried a trident. Chillingly, the specters were not walking on the ground but floating, and as they passed, a thick mist enveloped them. All five occupants of the vehicle shared the terrifying vision as the entities drifted toward the El Ángel cemetery before disappearing completely into the dawn fog.

#location
The sighting occurred on the road adjacent to the El Ángel cemetery in the Espejo Canton of Carchi Province, Ecuador.

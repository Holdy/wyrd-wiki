The Exorcism of Mik in Limón Indanza

Summary

A young Shuar girl in Ecuador became afflicted by a violent affliction that prompted a Catholic priest to perform an exorcism at the local parish office. Following the ritual, her pet rabbit mysteriously died on a bus, leaving locals to speculate whether the entity had inhabited the animal.

Details

Father Silverio was called to examine a twelve-year-old Shuar girl named Mik living in Limón Indanza, who suffered from inexplicable behavioral outbursts, nervous tics, and explosive fits of vulgar obscenities whenever holy water or blessings were applied. Brought to the parish office for further intervention, Mik suddenly bolted, racing up the steep slopes surrounding the mission before being restrained by neighbors and brought back. Father Silverio placed his stole over her head and firmly recited an exorcism, commanding the evil spirit to leave three times, after which the girl became completely calm. Shortly afterward, while traveling to Loja with her mother, Mik's pet rabbit died on the bus, prompting community whispers that the devil had possessed the animal.

#title
The Serpent of the Matriz

#summary
An abandoned infant cursed to become a giant serpent slumbers beneath a historic church, causing the building to gradually sink as it strains against the holy statue pinning it down.

#details
In a historic coastal town, a desperate mother carrying a baby born of a forbidden destiny crossed the square on a cloudy afternoon and left her crying child at the foot of the high altar inside the main church. Imbued with a dark curse and rumored to be kin to the devil, the abandoned infant did not grow as a normal child; instead, it transformed into a gigantic serpent. Its head became trapped directly beneath the feet of the statue of Our Lady, while its immense, winding tail stretched all the way toward the local bridge. Driven by a bitter resentment despite the holy site's shelter, the creature periodically stirs, shifting its weight and causing the church to slowly sink into the earth as the front and rear levels shift out of balance. Local lore warns that on certain nights, the phantom cries of the desperate mother and a wailing newborn can still be heard echoing across the square, a reminder of the restless beast straining against its divine restraint.

#location
The Igreja Matriz de Nossa Senhora dos Remédios (Main Church of Our Lady of Remedies) located in the historic center of Paraty, Rio de Janeiro state, Brazil.

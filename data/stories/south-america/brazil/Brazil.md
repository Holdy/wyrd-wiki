#title
The Ghost Bride of Estrada da Rhodia

#summary
A tragic bride killed in a 1970s truck accident haunts a highway between Campinas and Paulínia, leaving terrifying physical traces on the vehicles of unsuspecting motorists.

#details
In the 1970s, a bride traveling in a Ford Corcel along the Rhodia Road between Campinas and Paulínia was struck and killed by a truck, her wedding dress stained with blood. For decades afterward, motorists have reported sightings of a levitating woman in a wedding dress on the highway during the early hours of the morning. In one chilling encounter, a driver stopped to help the figure, who communicated telepathically and asked for a ride. The next day, the driver discovered two cauterized handprints burned into his car's hood, which a body shop worker confirmed were unlike any burn marks he had ever seen.

#location
The Rhodia Road (Estrada da Rhodia), which connects Campinas to Paulínia via the Barão Geraldo district in the state of São Paulo, Brazil.

#title
The Guarulhos Demonic Siege

#summary
A young mother and her family in Brazil became the center of a terrifying multi-year demonic haunting involving aggressive spirit entities that slashed furniture, sparked fires, and forced family members into trances. Researchers from the Brazilian Institute of Psychobiophysical Research extensively documented the harrowing events as an apparent supernatural siege.

#details
Beginning in April 1973 in Guarulhos, São Paulo, 21-year-old Noêmia and her extended family experienced bizarre and violent phenomena in their home. Furniture, mattresses, and folded blankets were repeatedly slashed with deep parallel cuts resembling claw marks, while mysterious stones rained down onto their roof. Spontaneous fires broke out across the house, and family members—including Noêmia, her father-in-law Pedro, and her young children—were periodically overtaken by violent trances, spasms, and visions of a red-furred beast with long black claws and a fiery visage. Investigators from the Brazilian Institute of Psychobiophysical Research (IBPP), led by Hernani Guimarães Andrade, studied the case for over a decade, ruling out natural causes and noting that religious scriptures and prayers specifically provoked the entities. Believing they had been cursed through witchcraft involving *umuluns* or *Exú* spirits summoned by a spurned ex-lover, the family endured years of torment until Noêmia finally stood her ground against the entity during a terrifying nocturnal confrontation.

#location
The events took place at a family property located in the Jardim Rosa de Franca neighborhood of Guarulhos, São Paulo, Brazil.

#title
The Serpent of the Matriz

#summary
An abandoned infant cursed to become a giant serpent slumbers beneath a historic church, causing the building to gradually sink as it strains against the holy statue pinning it down.

#details
In a historic coastal town, a desperate mother carrying a baby born of a forbidden destiny crossed the square on a cloudy afternoon and left her crying child at the foot of the high altar inside the main church. Imbued with a dark curse and rumored to be kin to the devil, the abandoned infant did not grow as a normal child; instead, it transformed into a gigantic serpent. Its head became trapped directly beneath the feet of the statue of Our Lady, while its immense, winding tail stretched all the way toward the local bridge. Driven by a bitter resentment despite the holy site's shelter, the creature periodically stirs, shifting its weight and causing the church to slowly sink into the earth as the front and rear levels shift out of balance. Local lore warns that on certain nights, the phantom cries of the desperate mother and a wailing newborn can still be heard echoing across the square, a reminder of the restless beast straining against its divine restraint.

#location
The Igreja Matriz de Nossa Senhora dos Remédios (Main Church of Our Lady of Remedies) located in the historic center of Paraty, Rio de Janeiro state, Brazil.

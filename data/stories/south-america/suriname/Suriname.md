#title
The Curse of Mariënburg

#summary
Following a violent labor insurrection and bloodshed at a colonial sugar estate, a lingering curse plagues the plantation with decades of mysterious illnesses, fatal accidents, and tragic deaths among its residents and directors.

#details
In 1902, the massive Mariënburg sugar plantation in Suriname became the site of severe labor unrest, culminating in a violent insurrection where contract workers clashed with authorities, leaving several dead. Following this bloodshed, a dark curse settled over the estate, manifesting as a series of unexplained illnesses, tragic suicides, and bizarre fatal accidents. Over the ensuing decades, successive directors and residents met untimely ends—ranging from being hacked to death or struck by estate trains to sudden, inexplicable illnesses claiming children and staff alike—until the lingering dread forced remaining families to flee the cursed grounds permanently.

#location
The historic Mariënburg sugar plantation and former factory site located in the Commewijne District of Suriname.

#title
The Possessions at Tanto Colin School

#summary
Multiple students at a Surinamese high school were struck by sudden demonic possessions, experiencing violent trances, strange speech, and exhaustion. The outbreak prompted government intervention, psychiatric investigations, and cleansing rituals by religious leaders.

#details
In 2006, authorities and educators in Suriname confronted a wave of alleged demonic possessions at the Tanto Colin School, located approximately 150 kilometers west of Paramaribo. Around seven students across various classrooms suddenly fell victim to uncontrollable trances, screaming, speaking in incomprehensible languages, and exhibiting erratic, violent behavior before collapsing from exhaustion. Several affected children had to be hospitalized. Education Minister Edwin Wolfe dispatched teams of psychiatrists and religious leaders to investigate the phenomenon and offer prayers, while local Hindu priests performed purification rites on the grounds. Despite these efforts and worried parents keeping children home, students continued to suffer sudden trances and possessions.

#location
The Tanto Colin School, situated 150 kilometers west of Paramaribo in Suriname.

#title
The Vanishing Passengers of the Cemetery Road

#summary
A taxi driver in Suriname who picked up two women near a motion-picture house discovered they had vanished from his vehicle as he drove past the cemetery.

#details
Late one night at eleven o'clock, a taxi driver in Paramaribo was parked near a motion-picture house when two young women, dressed in pink as if for church, hired his cab. They agreed on a fare of one guilder fifty cents and directed him down a road that led past the local cemetery. However, upon passing the cemetery, the driver turned around to ask for their exact directions, only to find the back seat completely empty. The terrifying encounter left the driver severely shaken and sick with fever for months afterward.

#location
The road leading past the cemetery in Paramaribo, Suriname.

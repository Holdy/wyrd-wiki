#title
The Cursed Village of Chokhercamiri

#summary
When the villagers of Chokhercamiri discovered a young girl was secretly consorting with the Devil, they burned him to death, triggering a permanent curse that froze the village in eternal night and doomed anyone who enters it to turn to stone.

#details
In times long past, the vibrant and prosperous village of Chokhercamiri was home to a family whose only daughter, Bernita, was kept locked away in a room due to her extraordinary beauty. Despite her locked door, every night a handsome young man would mysteriously materialize beside her bed to court her, vanishing before the first rooster crowed. When her father and the villagers eventually discovered her nocturnal visitor and realized he was none other than the Devil himself, a fierce rage gripped the community. They captured him and burned him to death. In that instant, divine or infernal retribution struck: the sun abandoned the valley forever, leaving Chokhercamiri trapped in perpetual, freezing darkness. To this day, the village remains an eerie landscape of ruins where anyone who dares to trespass is petrified into a stone statue, sharing the doom of the cursed settlement.

#location
The ruined settlement of Chokhercamiri is located in the mountainous region of Bolivia, historically associated with Andean folklore collections from the La Paz Department.

#title
The Phantoms of the Death Road

#summary
The restless spirits of forced laborers and victims of tragic accidents haunt the precipitous cliffs of the North Yungas Road, appearing as ghostly apparitions and whispering in the night.

#details
Constructed in the 1930s by Paraguayan prisoners of war following the Chaco War, the North Yungas Road—infamously known as the Death Road—earned a dark reputation as hundreds of travelers and workers lost their lives over the decades. According to local folklore, the prisoners cursed the treacherous 64-kilometer mountain pass before being cast over its sheer cliffs. Today, motorists and travelers navigating the narrow, unpaved route after dark frequently report hearing disembodied whispers and spotting human figures lingering along the misty abysses, believed to be trapped souls lingering in limbo.

#location
The North Yungas Road is a 64-kilometer mountain route connecting the city of La Paz to the Yungas region in Bolivia.

#title
The Possession of José in San Pedro Cave

#summary
In February 1991, a young boy named José became possessed by a malevolent entity after exploring a forbidden cave on Cerro San Pedro in Oruro, Bolivia, leading to a harrowing, multi-hour exorcism.

#details
In February 1991, a robust and cheerful boy named José, living in the San Pedro neighborhood of Oruro, Bolivia, decided to explore a natural fissure inside a cave located on top of Cerro San Pedro, a site locally associated with dark rituals. Within three months of his visit, José's appearance and behavior drastically deteriorated; he became emaciated, deeply aggressive, and began shouting in strange, unrecognizable languages while emitting deep, guttural laughs. Recognizing the signs of severe possession, local pastors intervened. Accompanied by his uncle and witnessed by journalist Humberto Cabezas from the newspaper *La Patria*, a grueling exorcism session lasting over twenty hours was conducted. During the ordeal, the entity inside the boy resisted fiercely, proclaiming that the child and his family belonged to it. Through continuous prayer, singing, and scripture readings, the entity was eventually cast out, allowing José to make a full recovery.

#location
The key location is the cave situated on the summit of Cerro San Pedro in the San Pedro district of Oruro, Bolivia.

#title
The Phantoms of the Death Road

#summary
The restless spirits of forced laborers and victims of tragic accidents haunt the precipitous cliffs of the North Yungas Road, appearing as ghostly apparitions and whispering in the night.

#details
Constructed in the 1930s by Paraguayan prisoners of war following the Chaco War, the North Yungas Road—infamously known as the Death Road—earned a dark reputation as hundreds of travelers and workers lost their lives over the decades. According to local folklore, the prisoners cursed the treacherous 64-kilometer mountain pass before being cast over its sheer cliffs. Today, motorists and travelers navigating the narrow, unpaved route after dark frequently report hearing disembodied whispers and spotting human figures lingering along the misty abysses, believed to be trapped souls lingering in limbo.

#location
The North Yungas Road is a 64-kilometer mountain route connecting the city of La Paz to the Yungas region in Bolivia.

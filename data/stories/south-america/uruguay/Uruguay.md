#title
The Cursed Castle of President Idiarte Borda

#summary
Commissioned by Uruguay's only assassinated president who died before ever crossing its threshold, a grand Montevideo mansion became plagued by tragic deaths, mysterious apparitions, and a terrifying wedding night sighting that left the building perpetually doomed.

#details
Juan Bautista Idiarte Borda, the President of Uruguay from 1894 to 1897, ordered the construction of a magnificent European-style mansion in the Colón neighborhood of Montevideo as a luxurious tribute. However, before the building could be completed, the president was assassinated in downtown Montevideo during Independence Day celebrations. Though he never stepped foot inside, his widow moved in and soon reported hearing him wandering the upper floors and the tower. Following her death, the estate fell into decay, punctuated by dark rumors of clandestine detentions during the military dictatorship and a final, ill-fated wedding reception where a bride fled the bathroom in terror after claiming to see a blood-soaked man staring at her. The eerie occurrences and tragic history cemented the property's reputation as a cursed castle, halting all events and leaving the architectural landmark wrapped in persistent gloom.

#location
Castillo Idiarte Borda is located at Avenida Lezica 5912 in the Colón neighborhood of Montevideo, Uruguay.

#title
The Exorcism of the Witchcraft Initiate in Malvín

#summary
An 18-year-old girl involved in witchcraft sessions was violently taken over by a malevolent entity during a religious intervention, letting out an infernal howl and lunging savagely at the priest with a fully distorted face.

#details
Father Julio César Elizaga, a prominent Uruguayan priest and demonologist known as the exorcist of Malvín, documented numerous harrowing encounters with the occult during his decades of ministry. Among his most striking cases was that of an 18-year-old girl who had been actively participating in witchcraft rituals. When brought in for spiritual intervention, the young woman manifested clear signs of demonic possession. Mid-ceremony, her appearance twisted dramatically, and she emitted an unearthly, spine-chilling howl that terrified everyone present. She then lunged aggressively at Father Elizaga, hurling vicious curses while completely overtaken by a hostile and alien force.

#location
The Parroquia de Belén (Bethlehem Parish), located in the Malvín neighborhood of Montevideo, Uruguay.

#upstream-source
*Memorias de un Cura* by Julio César Elizaga (also documented in *El exorcista de Malvín* by Luis Alberto Brenner).

#title
The Wanderer

#summary
Drivers on the access road to the CALNU sugar mill in Bella Unión frequently pick up a dapper hitchhiker in an old-fashioned suit who mysteriously vanishes upon reaching their destination.

#details
Near the city of Bella Unión in the department of Artigas, a dangerous curve on the access road to the CALNU sugar mill became the site of a fatal accident involving an investor arriving from Montevideo. Ever since, motorists traveling that road at night occasionally encounter a polite gentleman wearing a vintage suit and hat, carrying a suitcase and hitchhiking. When a driver stops to offer him a ride, the passenger speaks of his work as an investor, but once the vehicle arrives at the mill, he utters a cold thank you and vanishes from the car like magic.

#location
The access road to the former CALNU sugar mill on the outskirts of Bella Unión in the department of Artigas, Uruguay.

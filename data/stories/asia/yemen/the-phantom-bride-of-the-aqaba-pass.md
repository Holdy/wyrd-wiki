#title
The Phantom Bride of the Aqaba Pass

#summary
A mysterious and beautiful woman is frequently sighted walking alone at night along the rugged Aqaba mountain road, with some motorists claiming she possessed the legs of a donkey or attempted to lure drivers toward a hidden world beneath the bay.

#details
Carved through the mountains to link the historical Crater district with the port town of Al-Mualla in Aden, the narrow Aqaba road became infamous for terrifying nocturnal encounters. Eyewitnesses over generations described seeing a woman of unearthly beauty walking silently along the roadside under the cover of darkness. While many motorists caught only fleeting glimpses of her—some reporting the horrific detail that she possessed the legs of a donkey—at least one driver claimed to have actually picked her up in his vehicle. According to his account, she fell in love with him and directed him to follow her toward her family's home in a mythical city located beneath the waters of Haqqat Bay.

#location
The Aqaba road is a mountain pass linking the Crater district with Al-Mualla in the city of Aden, Yemen.

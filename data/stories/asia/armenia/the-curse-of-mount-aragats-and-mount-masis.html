    The Curse of Mount Aragats and Mount Masis

    Summary

    An ancient sibling rivalry between two majestic peaks resulted in an enduring mutual curse that separated them forever and locked their slopes into perpetual grief and desolation.

    Details

    Long ago, the mighty mountain peaks of Aragats and Masis were sister mountains living in harmony, but they eventually began to quarrel over who was taller, grander, and more beautiful. A mountain elder named Maruta tried in vain to reconcile the sisters. Frustrated by their endless bickering, Maruta laid a severe curse upon them, decreeing that they would be separated from one another and never be able to meet again. In response, Mount Masis cursed Aragats, wishing that her sister's grief would never cease and her tears would flow forever. Aragats retaliated with her own curse, wishing that Masis would dry up from sorrow, remain entirely deserted, and never receive the blessing of human footsteps or ritual sacrifices. The ancient curse came to pass: Masis became a desolate peak devoid of life or pilgrimage, while Aragats remained bound to perpetual weeping, her endless tears pooling into high-altitude lakes and feeding the rushing springs along her slopes.

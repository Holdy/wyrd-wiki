The Lost School Boy

Summary

A ghostly schoolboy haunts a winding mountain highway after dark, hailing passing motorists to ask if they have seen his mother.

Details

A young boy and his mother were driving along the highway when their vehicle was struck in a violent hit-and-run collision. His mother was thrown through the windshield, and the boy suffered fatal cuts to his throat from broken glass while still wearing his school uniform. His restless spirit now lingers by the roadside at night, signaling passing cars and asking drivers whether they have seen his mother.

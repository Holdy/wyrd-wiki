#title
The Curse of Mahsuri

#summary
Falsely accused of infidelity and wrongfully executed, a young woman used her dying breath to curse the island of Langkawi with seven generations of hardship and misfortune.

#details
In the late 18th century on Langkawi Island, a maiden named Mahsuri lived while her warrior husband, Wan Darus, went off to war. During his absence, she befriended a wandering minstrel. Jealous whispers and slander led Wan Darus to accuse Mahsuri of infidelity upon his return, a charge she vehemently denied. Bound to a tree and subjected to multiple execution attempts, ordinary weapons refused to puncture her skin until she instructed them to use her father’s ceremonial dagger. When she was stabbed, white blood flowed from her wound, proving her absolute innocence too late. With her dying breath, Mahsuri placed a powerful curse upon the island, dooming Langkawi to seven generations of war, crop failure, and suffering.

#location
Kota Mahsuri is located in Mukim Ulu Melaka, Langkawi Island, Kedah, Malaysia.

#title
The Lost School Boy

#summary
A ghostly schoolboy haunts a winding mountain highway after dark, hailing passing motorists to ask if they have seen his mother.

#details
A young boy and his mother were driving along the highway when their vehicle was struck in a violent hit-and-run collision. His mother was thrown through the windshield, and the boy suffered fatal cuts to his throat from broken glass while still wearing his school uniform. His restless spirit now lingers by the roadside at night, signaling passing cars and asking drivers whether they have seen his mother.

#location
The haunting occurs along the Karak Highway, a major expressway connecting Kuala Lumpur to Karak in Malaysia.

#title
The Possession of Mr Gopal

#summary
During a 2015 paranormal investigation at an unfinished colonial mansion in Malaysia, a visitor was suddenly taken over by the spirit of a disgruntled historical worker. The entity spoke through the participant in English, recounting his unfulfilled promises and deep resentment.

#details
In 2015, the management of Kellie's Castle in Batu Gajah organized a special program to investigate the mansion's notorious paranormal activity. During the event, one of the male participants unexpectedly entered a trance state and was temporarily possessed by an entity. Speaking fluent English, the man introduced himself as "Mr Gopal," a South Indian washerman who had worked in the mansion's laundry area during its construction in the early 20th century. The spirit expressed bitterness and sorrow over how the estate's owner, Scottish planter William Kellie Smith, failed to honor a promise to send him back to his wife in India before he died.

#location
Kellie's Castle is an unfinished 20th-century mansion situated along Jalan Kellie Smith in Batu Gajah, Perak, Malaysia.

The Possession of Mr Gopal

Summary

During a 2015 paranormal investigation at an unfinished colonial mansion in Malaysia, a visitor was suddenly taken over by the spirit of a disgruntled historical worker. The entity spoke through the participant in English, recounting his unfulfilled promises and deep resentment.

Details

In 2015, the management of Kellie's Castle in Batu Gajah organized a special program to investigate the mansion's notorious paranormal activity. During the event, one of the male participants unexpectedly entered a trance state and was temporarily possessed by an entity. Speaking fluent English, the man introduced himself as "Mr Gopal," a South Indian washerman who had worked in the mansion's laundry area during its construction in the early 20th century. The spirit expressed bitterness and sorrow over how the estate's owner, Scottish planter William Kellie Smith, failed to honor a promise to send him back to his wife in India before he died.

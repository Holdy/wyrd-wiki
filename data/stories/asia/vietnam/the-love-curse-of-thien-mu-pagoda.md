#title
The Love Curse of Thien Mu Pagoda

#summary
Unrequited sorrow from a historical tragedy birthed a supernatural curse claiming that unmarried couples who visit the ancient pagoda together will inevitably suffer a separation. 

#details
Centuries ago, a young couple deeply in love faced insurmountable opposition from their families due to differing social classes. Overcome by despair, they journeyed to the Perfume River near Ha Khe Hill to make a final pact, but only the young man perished while his lover survived and was forced into another marriage. Imbued with the lingering grief and resentment of this broken romance, a curse settled upon the site, dictating that any unmarried couple entering the sacred grounds will face relationship discord and eventual breakup.

#location
Thien Mu Pagoda is situated on Ha Khe Hill, overlooking the Perfume River approximately five kilometers west of the center of Hue, Vietnam.

#title
The Curse of the Sanzhi UFO Houses

#summary
An ambitious futuristic coastal resort in Taiwan became plagued by rumors of a dragon curse and fatal accidents after construction mysteriously collapsed and left the pod-shaped buildings abandoned. The eerie ruins eventually gained global notoriety as a haunted, cursed ghost city before being demolished.

#details
Beginning around 1978, developers in Taiwan set out to build an innovative holiday resort featuring futuristic, spaceship-shaped pod houses on the grey coastal flats of Sanzhi, near Tamsui. Designed on concrete pillars to offer seaside views, the complex aimed to attract city dwellers looking for a sci-fi getaway. However, financial ruin, economic shocks, and soaring costs caused construction to grind to a halt around 1980, leaving the eccentric capsules unfinished and abandoned. As the salt air and typhoons weathered the uncompleted structures, dark folklore took root around the site. Rumors spread that the project was doomed by the curse of a stone dragon statue that had been moved or destroyed during initial land clearance, disturbing the local feng shui. Other macabre tales claimed that a series of fatal accidents and deaths among construction workers had cursed the ground, trapping vengeful spirits within the hollow pods. The site became widely known as one of Asia's most famous cursed ruins until it was finally demolished between 2008 and 2010.

#location
The Sanzhi UFO Houses were located along the coastal flats in Sanzhi District, New Taipei City, Taiwan.

#title
The Ghost Sweeper of Xinhai Tunnel

#summary
Drivers passing through Taipei's Xinhai Tunnel late at night frequently encounter a ghostly old woman sweeping the roadway who abruptly vanishes when approached.

#details
Built directly upon a former hillside cemetery, the Xinhai Tunnel in Taipei is notorious for nocturnal apparitions. Late-night drivers have reported encountering an old woman calmly sweeping the roadway in the middle of the tunnel. Despite motorists honking their horns for her to move, she continues sweeping without acknowledging them until vehicles come within a few feet, at which moment she vanishes into thin air.

#location
The Xinhai Tunnel is located on Xinhai Road in Taipei, Taiwan, connecting central Taipei to the Muzha area of Wenshan District.

#title
The Wu Family Possession Tragedy

#summary
A family of six in Kaohsiung became convinced they were possessed by various deities and demons, leading to bizarre rituals and tragic consequences.

#details
In early 2005, members of the Wu family living in Gushan District, Kaohsiung City, fell into a state of shared delusion and extreme belief in spirit possession after the youngest daughter claimed to be possessed by the Third Prince of Nezha. Soon, other family members also claimed possession by various figures such as the Jade Emperor, Queen Mother, and Guanyin Bodhisattva, while believing that everyone else in the house was inhabited by evil spirits. In an attempt to exorcise the supposed demons, the family subjected one another to severe beatings, burning with incense, and force-feeding each other human feces. The eldest daughter, Wu Fengjuan, was targeted as the primary source of evil, imprisoned, and ultimately died of starvation and abuse. Afterward, the surviving family members dumped her body near a hospital before police intervened and uncovered the horrific scene inside their home.

#location
The Wu family's residential home located in the Gushan District of Kaohsiung City, Taiwan.

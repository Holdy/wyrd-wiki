The Wu Family Possession Tragedy

Summary

A family of six in Kaohsiung became convinced they were possessed by various deities and demons, leading to bizarre rituals and tragic consequences.

Details

In early 2005, members of the Wu family living in Gushan District, Kaohsiung City, fell into a state of shared delusion and extreme belief in spirit possession after the youngest daughter claimed to be possessed by the Third Prince of Nezha. Soon, other family members also claimed possession by various figures such as the Jade Emperor, Queen Mother, and Guanyin Bodhisattva, while believing that everyone else in the house was inhabited by evil spirits. In an attempt to exorcise the supposed demons, the family subjected one another to severe beatings, burning with incense, and force-feeding each other human feces. The eldest daughter, Wu Fengjuan, was targeted as the primary source of evil, imprisoned, and ultimately died of starvation and abuse. Afterward, the surviving family members dumped her body near a hospital before police intervened and uncovered the horrific scene inside their home.

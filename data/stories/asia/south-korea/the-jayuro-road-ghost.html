The Jayuro Road Ghost

Summary

A late-night driver along a foggy highway encounters a hitchhiking young woman wearing what appears to be sunglasses, only to discover upon closer inspection that her face features empty, hollow sockets instead of eyes. In another variation, a driver picks up the hitchhiker and unknowingly transports her to a cemetery before she vanishes.

Details

Late at night along the foggy Jayu-ro highway connecting Seoul to Paju in South Korea, motorists frequently report seeing a young woman standing alone on the roadside or median. From a distance, she appears to be a stranded pedestrian or hitchhiker wearing a dark coat and large black sunglasses. However, as drivers approach or pull closer to investigate, the terrifying realization sets in that the dark shapes are not sunglasses at all, but empty, lightless black voids where her eyes should be. In alternative retellings, a driver stops to give the hitchhiker a ride, only to discover midway or upon arrival that her intended destination is a cemetery where she abruptly disappears.

#title
The 40-Day Ordeal at the Samarkhel Shrine

#summary
Families across Afghanistan bring afflicted relatives to a remote shrine graveyard believing they are possessed by evil spirits or jinns. The patients are chained to trees for forty days under harsh conditions in hopes of a supernatural cure.

#details
At the Mia Ali Sahib shrine located within a dusty graveyard in Samarkhel village, individuals suffering from severe psychological distress—frequently attributed by locals to spirit or jinn possession—undergo a grueling forty-day confinement. Bound by heavy iron chains to trees and surrounded by simple headstones, the afflicted are fed only dry bread, salt, pepper, and water. Managed by descendants of the local saint buried at the shrine, families pay a daily fee in the belief that the saint's supernatural powers will exorcise the malevolent entities and cure the patients. Despite modern medical interventions and the abolition of official institutional shackle practices in the country, hundreds of people continue to be brought to this remote site annually to endure the ritual restraint.

#location
The Mia Ali Sahib shrine is located in the graveyard of Samarkhel village in Afghanistan's eastern Nangarhar province.

#title
The Whispering Grave of Sarkai Banda

#summary
An abandoned village in the shadow of the Spin Ghar mountains remains haunted by the shallow grave of a corrupt cleric whose corpse demands blood from anyone who approaches or mocks his resting place. Locals believe that the cleric practiced forbidden rites, ensuring that his tomb would continue to claim victims generations later.

#details
Deep in the rugged terrain of eastern Afghanistan near the Spin Ghar mountains lies the deserted village of Sarkai Banda. According to local folklore, the village was once terrorized by Mullah Rahim, a stern and cruel cleric who hid dark, occult practices beneath a facade of piety. After a series of unexplainable deaths among the villagers, Mullah Rahim mysteriously died and was hastily buried at the edge of the village cemetery. Following his death, strange whispers, backwards-recited prayers, and terrifying apparitions began emanating from his shallow grave, driving residents mad and causing children to sleepwalk toward the site. The entire village was eventually abandoned out of fear. Years later, a skeptical student filming a documentary mocked the tomb by standing upon it, only to disappear overnight while leaving behind bloody scratch marks leading directly back into the restless earth of the grave.

#location
The abandoned village of Sarkai Banda and its cemetery lie nestled in the shadow of the Spin Ghar mountains in eastern Afghanistan.

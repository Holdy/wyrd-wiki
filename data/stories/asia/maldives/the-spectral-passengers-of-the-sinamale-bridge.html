The Spectral Passengers of the Sinamale' Bridge

Summary

Taxi drivers and motorists crossing a major maritime bridge in the Maldives late at night have reported unsettling encounters with spectral entities and unseen hitchhikers. These modern ghostly legends intertwine state-of-the-art infrastructure with traditional island folklore.

Details

After the construction of the Sinamale' Bridge connecting Malé to the surrounding islands, drivers traversing the multi-kilometer asphalt span after dark began experiencing bizarre supernatural phenomena. Taxi drivers and moped riders frequently reported feeling invisible or phantom passengers hitchhiking on the backs of their vehicles, while others witnessed shadowy figures appearing unexpectedly along the roadway. These haunting accounts reflect how traditional Maldivian beliefs surrounding spirits and jinn adapted to modern engineering and transit routes.

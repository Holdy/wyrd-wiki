    The Terror of Ghoul Alley

    Summary

    In the historic neighborhood of Barahat Al Jufairi, a narrow alleyway became feared and avoided after being claimed by a malicious, cannibalistic ghoul that hunted anyone venturing out after dark. Residents strictly avoided the passageway at night to escape its predatory grasp.

    Details

    Long ago in the old quarters of Doha, a specific narrow alley within the fereej (neighborhood) of Barahat Al Jufairi earned a dark and sinister reputation. According to local Qatari folklore, a demonic ghoul took up residence within the confined passageway, lying in wait for unsuspecting late-night travelers. The creature was believed to possess terrifying supernatural strength and a hunger for human flesh, effectively casting a permanent pall of dread over the street. So severe was the folklore surrounding this stretch that locals and elders strictly forbade anyone from passing through after sunset, transforming the thoroughfare into a ghostly, abandoned hazard of the night.

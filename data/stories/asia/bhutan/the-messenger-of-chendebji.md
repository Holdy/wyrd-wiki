#title
The Messenger of Chendebji

#summary
Travelers navigating the dark highway near the Chendebji Chorten on the Trongsa road frequently report eerie paranormal encounters tied to the legendary royal messenger who died there.

#details
Along the old east-west highway near the white chorten at Chendebji, motorists resting at night have long reported chilling, unexplained encounters on the shadowy mountain bends. Local legend attributes these hauntings to the tragic fate of Garp Lungi Khorlo, Bhutan's famous historical royal runner who could move as fast as the wind, before he was allegedly overcome by a powerful female demon in this very area.

#location
The highway bends immediately surrounding the Chendebji Chorten on the Trongsa road in Bhutan.

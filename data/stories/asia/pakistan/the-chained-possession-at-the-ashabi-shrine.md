#title
The Chained Possession at the Ashabi Shrine

#summary
An 18-year-old Pakistani youth experiencing violent fits attributed to jinn possession was brought by his family to a Sufi shrine, where he was chained by the ankle to a wall. His relatives sought spiritual exorcism after conventional medical remedies and hospital treatments failed to cure his condition.

#details
When eighteen-year-old Muhammed began suffering from severe, violent fits, his family turned away from modern medical treatments amid local stigma and the scarcity of mental health support. Convinced that his affliction was caused by a malicious jinn—a supernatural spirit born of fire—his mother, Jamila Bibi, transported him to the Abdullah Shah Ashabi shrine. To prevent him from injuring himself or others during his violent episodes, Muhammed was restrained by his ankle to a wall inside the shrine complex. There, he sat alongside others enduring prolonged spiritual confinement near the tomb of the Sufi saint in hopes of achieving an exorcism and finding deliverance from the entity.

#location
The Abdullah Shah Ashabi shrine located in the historic town of Thatta in Sindh province, Pakistan.

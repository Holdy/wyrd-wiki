#title
The Trance of the Mamluk House

#summary
A young boy in the Old City of Jerusalem encountered faceless, floating white entities that placed him in a trance and later took his infant brother away. Years later, renovating his ancient family home revealed a subterranean cave containing three mysterious ancient tombs.

#details
Growing up in a historic Mamluk-era house in the Old City of Jerusalem, a resident recalled a chilling supernatural encounter from his childhood. When he was around eight years old, he opened his front door to go outside and came face-to-face with three ethereal white figures wearing white capes. They were entirely faceless, handless, and floating above the ground. Though initially petrified, the entities cast him into a trance that instantly stripped away his fear. On another fateful occasion, he witnessed one of these strange beings taking his six-month-old baby brother out of the house; tragically, the infant passed away the very next day. Decades later, during the renovation of the ancient family home, workers uncovered a hidden subterranean cave beneath the building that housed three ancient tombs, finally shedding light on the otherworldly presence that had haunted the property.

#location
A historic Mamluk-era residential building situated within the Old City of Jerusalem, featuring a subterranean rock-cut cave containing three ancient tombs discovered beneath its foundations.

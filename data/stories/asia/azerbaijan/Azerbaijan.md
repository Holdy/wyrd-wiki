#title
The Stone Bride of Todan

#summary
A distracted young mother was instantly transformed into stone along with her infant when her enraged father cursed her for ignoring the child's cries. The petrified figure remains a permanent fixture of the village landscape as a testament to the absolute power of a father's curse.

#details
In the village of Todan, a young mother was busy washing clothes while her baby cried incessantly nearby. Distracted by her chores, she failed to comfort or attend to the infant. Her father happened to walk past her house at that exact moment and, overcome with frustration at her neglect, uttered a powerful curse: "May you turn into a stone! Why don't you comfort your baby?" Immediately, the words manifested into reality, transforming both the daughter and the baby in her arms into stone. Though the father was instantly filled with remorse, the transformation was irreversible, leaving behind a silent stone monument that inspired the traditional Azerbaijani saying about the potency of a parent's breath and wrath.

#location
Todan Village in the Goranboy region of Azerbaijan.

#title
The Deserted Village of Kuldhara

#summary
Faced with the tyrannical demands of a local minister, the Paliwal Brahmins abandoned their prosperous settlement overnight, laying a powerful curse that no one would ever be able to successfully resettle or inhabit the land again.

#details
For centuries, the desert village of Kuldhara and its surrounding settlements in Rajasthan were thriving communities inhabited by the Paliwal Brahmins, known for their agricultural and trading success. However, the peace of the village was shattered when the tyrannical diwan (minister) of the region, Salim Singh, cast his eyes upon the beautiful daughter of the village chieftain and demanded her hand in marriage under threat of heavy punitive taxes. Rather than submit to his oppression, the elders of Kuldhara and neighboring communities held a council during the night. Before packing up their belongings and vanishing into the darkness by morning, they placed a binding curse upon the entire settlement, decreeing that anyone who attempted to re-occupy the homes or lands would face ruin and paranormal torment, leaving the village permanently frozen in time and uninhabited.

#location
The abandoned village of Kuldhara is located approximately 18 kilometers southwest of Jaisalmer city in the Thar Desert of Rajasthan, India.

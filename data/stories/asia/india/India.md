#title
The Deserted Village of Kuldhara

#summary
Faced with the tyrannical demands of a local minister, the Paliwal Brahmins abandoned their prosperous settlement overnight, laying a powerful curse that no one would ever be able to successfully resettle or inhabit the land again.

#details
For centuries, the desert village of Kuldhara and its surrounding settlements in Rajasthan were thriving communities inhabited by the Paliwal Brahmins, known for their agricultural and trading success. However, the peace of the village was shattered when the tyrannical diwan (minister) of the region, Salim Singh, cast his eyes upon the beautiful daughter of the village chieftain and demanded her hand in marriage under threat of heavy punitive taxes. Rather than submit to his oppression, the elders of Kuldhara and neighboring communities held a council during the night. Before packing up their belongings and vanishing into the darkness by morning, they placed a binding curse upon the entire settlement, decreeing that anyone who attempted to re-occupy the homes or lands would face ruin and paranormal torment, leaving the village permanently frozen in time and uninhabited.

#location
The abandoned village of Kuldhara is located approximately 18 kilometers southwest of Jaisalmer city in the Thar Desert of Rajasthan, India.

#title
The Exorcism Temple of Chougan

#summary
Every Monday in the tribal village of Chougan, Madhya Pradesh, individuals believed to be possessed by malevolent spirits and demons are brought to a local temple to undergo exorcism rituals. Violent or aggressive subjects are secured within the temple's open-air enclosures while hereditary priests use sacred ash, tongs, and feather brooms to drive out the entities.

#details
Every Monday, the remote tribal village of Chougan near Mandla, Madhya Pradesh, draws people from across Central India who believe themselves or their loved ones to be possessed by malevolent spirits, demons, or unresolved torments. At the village's mystical temple complex—anchored by towering iron ladders representing Goddess Kali and open-air enclosures fitted with heavy iron chains—hereditary Gond priests perform ancient rituals of liberation. Violent or aggressive individuals suffering from suspected possession are securely chained for safety. The fourth-generation priest, Ramesh Parthi, officiates these sessions by brushing the afflicted with metal tongs and feather brooms, applying sacred ash from an eternal flame, and invoking divine powers to cleanse and free them from the entities taking them over.

#location
The exorcism rituals take place at the tribal temple situated in Chougan village, approximately 40 kilometers from Mandla town in Madhya Pradesh.

#title
The Phantom Couple of Khunia More

#summary
A young driver in West Bengal encounters a stranded tribal couple hitching a ride at a forest junction, only to realize they vanished into thin air and were actually victims of a past murder.

#details
While driving through the Chapramari Wildlife Sanctuary at night, a novice driver named Rajib stopped at Khunia More after spotting a young man and woman hailing his car for a lift. After driving a short distance past them, he second-guessed his decision and turned his vehicle around within seconds to offer assistance. When his headlights illuminated the spot, the couple had completely vanished with nowhere to hide in the dense surrounding forest and no other vehicles on the road. Upon returning to base in Siliguri, a senior colleague explained that the apparitions were a young tribal couple who had eloped years earlier and were brutally murdered by passing truckers.

#location
The event took place at Khunia More, an intersection located on NH17 within the Chapramari Wildlife Sanctuary in West Bengal, India.

The Phantom Couple of Khunia More

Summary

A young driver in West Bengal encounters a stranded tribal couple hitching a ride at a forest junction, only to realize they vanished into thin air and were actually victims of a past murder.

Details

While driving through the Chapramari Wildlife Sanctuary at night, a novice driver named Rajib stopped at Khunia More after spotting a young man and woman hailing his car for a lift. After driving a short distance past them, he second-guessed his decision and turned his vehicle around within seconds to offer assistance. When his headlights illuminated the spot, the couple had completely vanished with nowhere to hide in the dense surrounding forest and no other vehicles on the road. Upon returning to base in Siliguri, a senior colleague explained that the apparitions were a young tribal couple who had eloped years earlier and were brutally murdered by passing truckers.

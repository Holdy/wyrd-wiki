#title
The Curse of the Abrash House

#summary
Built upon the grounds of an ancient cemetery, the infamous Abrash House in Damascus carries a severe curse that brings tragic fates, mysterious disappearances, and untimely deaths to anyone who dares to reside within its walls.

#details
Constructed in the Jisr Al-Abyad neighborhood of Damascus, the grand stone mansion known as the Abrash House was allegedly built over an old burial site, burdening the property with a dark curse. According to local folklore, this curse manifests in a series of terrible misfortunes, starting during its construction when numerous workers reportedly fell to their deaths under inexplicable circumstances. In the decades that followed, families and tenants who occupied the building met with horrifying ends—most notably a family in the 1940s who vanished entirely overnight, leaving behind their belongings and an unsettling diary entry. Later temporary residents suffered equally grim fates, including tragic suicides, sudden unexplained illnesses, and missing children. Although descendants of the owning family and local neighbors dismiss the tales as rumors born from years of abandonment and neighborhood gossip, the enduring legend of the cursed house remains a prominent fixture of Damascene folklore.

#location
The Abrash House is located in the Jisr Al-Abyad neighborhood of Damascus, Syria.

#title
The Curse of Al Qasimi Palace

#summary
After royal artisans were cheated out of their wages, they cast a vengeful black magic curse on a multi-million-dollar desert mansion, driving out its owners and trapping the palace in eternal silence. Ever since, the abandoned halls have echoed with chilling screams and phantom children peering from the windows.

#details
Al Qasimi Palace, a sprawling 35-room mansion in Ras Al Khaimah constructed at a staggering cost of 500 million dirhams, was intended to be a magnificent symbol of luxury and power. However, shortly after completion, the royal family abruptly fled the estate and vowed never to return. According to local folklore, the Moroccan artisans and interior designers hired to decorate the palace were never paid properly for their painstaking labor. In retaliation, they used black magic to curse the mansion. Ever since, visitors and locals report supernatural occurrences within its walls, including doors slamming shut on their own, floorboards creaking without cause, harrowing screams echoing through the dark, and spectral children gazing out of the upper windows at night.

#location
Al Qasimi Palace is situated in the desert region of Ras Al Khaimah, United Arab Emirates.

#title
The Specter of Jumeirah

#summary
Occupants of a residential villa in Dubai experienced terrifying nocturnal visitations by a malevolent entity that pinned them down, shook furniture, and locked doors.

#details
Deep in the heart of Jumeirah 1 in Dubai, a seven-bedroom residential villa became the center of relentless paranormal activity experienced by a group of occupants over the course of a year. The disturbances frequently manifested at night. One resident, Lora, woke up at 3:00 AM to an intense pressure crushing her entire body, as though an invisible entity was lying directly on top of her. Unable to move anything except her fingers and toes, she witnessed a dark shadow cast over the curtains above her. A few nights later, groaning sounds emanated from her roommate's bed, where a similar dark shadow was reflected on the wall. Other flatmates reported terrifying incidents, including a bed shaking incessantly during quiet hours while nothing else moved, a secretary being locked inside a bathroom by unseen forces for two hours, and socks inexplicably peeling off feet repeatedly throughout the night. Local folklore attributes these phenomena to malicious spirits or jinns inhabiting the older properties built upon historical areas of the district.

#location
The key location is a seven-bedroom residential villa situated in the district of Jumeirah 1 in Dubai, United Arab Emirates.

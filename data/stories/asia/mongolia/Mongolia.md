#title
The Curse of Burkhan Khaldun and the Lost Tomb of Genghis Khan

#summary
According to Mongolian legend, a formidable curse guards the sacred mountain of Burkhan Khaldun and the hidden tomb of Genghis Khan, threatening catastrophe to anyone who dares disturb the resting place. This strict spiritual taboo and legendary curse have kept the imperial grave undiscovered for over eight centuries.

#details
Following the death of Genghis Khan in 1227, his funeral procession and burial site within the Khentii Mountains of northeastern Mongolia were subjected to extreme measures of secrecy, including the execution of witnesses and the diversion of rivers over the grave. Over the centuries, a powerful legend and curse developed around the *Ikh Khorig* (the Great Taboo) surrounding Burkhan Khaldun. It is believed that disturbing the sacred earth will awaken the wrath of the mountain spirits and strip away the spiritual protection safeguarding Mongolia, resulting in national ruin. Modern explorers and archaeological expeditions attempting to locate the tomb have frequently encountered relentless harsh weather, logistical failures, and mysterious misfortunes attributed to this enduring curse.

#location
The sacred mountain of Burkhan Khaldun and the *Ikh Khorig* (Great Taboo) are located within the rugged Khentii Mountains of northeastern Mongolia.

#upstream-source
Anonymous (13th Century / Translation by Urgunge Onon), *The Secret History of the Mongols*.

#title
The Shaman's Ancestral Possession

#summary
While visiting nomadic reindeer herders in the northern Mongolian tundra, travelers witnessed a shaman become possessed by the spirit of an ancestor who revealed startling, secret details about a traveler's deceased relative.

#details
An adventurous traveler and his companions journeyed deep into the northern Mongolian tundra to visit the Dukha reindeer herders. After interviewing a local shaman named Halzan, they returned to his teepee that evening to observe a traditional ritual. Amid intense drumming, chanting, and offerings of vodka and cigarettes, Halzan's body was taken over by the spirit of an ancestral entity. Operating as a vessel for the dead ancestor, the possessed shaman singled out visitors and demonstrated uncanny, specific knowledge of family secrets—including accurately describing the burial clothing and hidden tattoo of a traveler's recently deceased great-grandmother—before the spirit finally departed.

#location
The ritual and spirit possession took place inside a Dukha shaman's teepee located in the northern Mongolian tundra near Tsagaannuur in Khövsgöl Province.

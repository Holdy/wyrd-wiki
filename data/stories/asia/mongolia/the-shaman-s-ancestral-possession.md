#title
The Shaman's Ancestral Possession

#summary
While visiting nomadic reindeer herders in the northern Mongolian tundra, travelers witnessed a shaman become possessed by the spirit of an ancestor who revealed startling, secret details about a traveler's deceased relative.

#details
An adventurous traveler and his companions journeyed deep into the northern Mongolian tundra to visit the Dukha reindeer herders. After interviewing a local shaman named Halzan, they returned to his teepee that evening to observe a traditional ritual. Amid intense drumming, chanting, and offerings of vodka and cigarettes, Halzan's body was taken over by the spirit of an ancestral entity. Operating as a vessel for the dead ancestor, the possessed shaman singled out visitors and demonstrated uncanny, specific knowledge of family secrets—including accurately describing the burial clothing and hidden tattoo of a traveler's recently deceased great-grandmother—before the spirit finally departed.

#location
The ritual and spirit possession took place inside a Dukha shaman's teepee located in the northern Mongolian tundra near Tsagaannuur in Khövsgöl Province.

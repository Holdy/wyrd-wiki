The Possession at the Sabath of Tyre

Summary

A local man was violently attacked and possessed by a female jinni after encountering her at a historic stone archway in Tyre. He suffered eight days of torment before a visiting spiritual healer successfully exorcised the entity.

Details

Hassan Shour, known as Abu Khalil, approached the Sabath—a crumbling historic stone archway located in the old quarters of Tyre—when he was suddenly blocked by a female jinni. She demanded that he carry her to the sea. When Hassan refused and attempted to push her away, she attacked him and took complete control of his body. For eight days, Hassan remained possessed and trapped in severe torment until a spiritual healer from the village of Jouya traveled to assist him, performing rituals that successfully freed him from the spirit's grasp.

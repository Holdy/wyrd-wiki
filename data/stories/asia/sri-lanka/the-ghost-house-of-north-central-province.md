#title
The Ghost House of North Central Province

#summary
A Sri Lankan woman suffering from debilitating physical pain and severe demonic oppression found complete deliverance after visiting a village church, transforming her notorious home from a shunned "Ghost House" into a place of worship.

#details
In a rural area of North Central Province, Sri Lanka, a day labourer named Dinesh and his wife Chandra lived in a small red-brick house surrounded by palm trees. Following the tragic death of their second daughter, Chandra began experiencing inexplicable physical weakness, heart palpitations, and terrifying nightmarish visions. Her condition deteriorated to the point where she would become rigid and unresponsive for hours, prompting neighbors and relatives to abandon the family and dub their dwelling the "Ghost House." After consulting 35 Hindu priests and swamis without finding relief, Dinesh and Chandra followed their relatives' advice and visited a church on July 23, 2021. While church members prayed over her, Chandra felt a violent internal struggle before losing consciousness and experiencing a vision of Jesus defeating the demons that choked her. Upon regaining consciousness, she realized she was entirely freed from her affliction. The family embraced Christianity, moved back into their formerly shunned home, and eventually built a chapel onto the house for a growing local congregation.

#location
A small red-brick house situated along a dusty rural road in the North Central Province of Sri Lanka, locally known as the "Ghost House."

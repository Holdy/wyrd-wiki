#title
The Curses of Devol Deviyo at Seenigama

#summary
Devotees travel to a secluded offshore island shrine in Seenigama to invoke divine curses and retribution against adversaries by grinding chili peppers on stone slabs. Rooted in the folklore of the deity Devol Deviyo, this centuries-old practice serves as a ritualized channel for revenge and justice.

#details
According to Sri Lankan folklore, Devol Deviyo was an exiled prince who survived a shipwreck and fiery trials off the southern coast, eventually establishing his dominion over justice and retribution. At the Seenigama Devalaya, situated on a tiny offshore island behind a protective stone wall, individuals seeking redress for stolen property, broken contracts, land disputes, or betrayal perform ritual curses. Guided by a priest, the supplicant grinds red chili, black pepper, and mustard on a stone slab while reciting imprecations calling for illness, ruin, or exile upon their wrongdoer in the name of the deity and his demonic servant, Diwi-yakka.

#location
Seenigama Devalaya is located on a small island just off the southwestern coast of Sri Lanka, near Hikkaduwa.

#title
The Ghost House of North Central Province

#summary
A Sri Lankan woman suffering from debilitating physical pain and severe demonic oppression found complete deliverance after visiting a village church, transforming her notorious home from a shunned "Ghost House" into a place of worship.

#details
In a rural area of North Central Province, Sri Lanka, a day labourer named Dinesh and his wife Chandra lived in a small red-brick house surrounded by palm trees. Following the tragic death of their second daughter, Chandra began experiencing inexplicable physical weakness, heart palpitations, and terrifying nightmarish visions. Her condition deteriorated to the point where she would become rigid and unresponsive for hours, prompting neighbors and relatives to abandon the family and dub their dwelling the "Ghost House." After consulting 35 Hindu priests and swamis without finding relief, Dinesh and Chandra followed their relatives' advice and visited a church on July 23, 2021. While church members prayed over her, Chandra felt a violent internal struggle before losing consciousness and experiencing a vision of Jesus defeating the demons that choked her. Upon regaining consciousness, she realized she was entirely freed from her affliction. The family embraced Christianity, moved back into their formerly shunned home, and eventually built a chapel onto the house for a growing local congregation.

#location
A small red-brick house situated along a dusty rural road in the North Central Province of Sri Lanka, locally known as the "Ghost House."

#title
The Phantom Hitchhiker of Anderson Road

#summary
Late-night motorists traveling along Anderson Road frequently report picking up a lone young woman, only for her to mysteriously vanish from the back seat moments later.

#details
Motorists driving along Anderson Road between Kalutara and Colombo after dark have long recounted chilling encounters with a phantom hitchhiker. According to local urban legends, drivers who stop to offer a ride to a young woman standing alone by Xiang roadside watch as she gets into the vehicle. However, within a short distance, she completely disappears from the back seat, leaving startled motorists with an inexplicable and eerie experience.

#location
Anderson Road, situated along the route between Kalutara and Colombo in Sri Lanka.

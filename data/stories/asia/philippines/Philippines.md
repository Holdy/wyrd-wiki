#title
The Curse of the Manila Film Center

#summary
Rushed construction during the Marcos regime caused a catastrophic collapse at a grand cinema project, resulting in workers being allegedly entombed in quick-drying cement. This tragedy gave rise to persistent paranormal hauntings and a lingering dark curse over the building.

#details
In November 1981, construction workers labored around the clock under extreme pressure to finish the Manila Film Center in time for an international film festival spearheaded by First Lady Imelda Marcos. During this breakneck, round-the-clock schedule, a major structural collapse occurred, plunging workers deep into the rubble. According to enduring urban legends and eyewitness testimonies, many victims were left buried within the quick-drying cement that was rapidly poured to meet the impossible deadline. Ever since, the structure has been plagued by restless spirits, with visitors and guards reporting disembodied cries for help echoing from within the concrete walls, ghostly apparitions, and tragic misfortunes befalling those connected to the site.

#location
The Manila Film Center is situated within the Cultural Center of the Philippines Complex along Roxas Boulevard in Pasay City, Metro Manila, Philippines.

#title
The Demonic Possession of Clarita Villanueva

#summary
In May 1953, an incarcerated 17-year-old girl named Clarita Villanueva was allegedly attacked and possessed by invisible demonic entities inside her prison cell. Amid intense medical and media scrutiny, American evangelist Lester Sumrall performed a multi-day deliverance prayer session that culminated in the spirits' departure.

#details
In May 1953, Clarita Villanueva was arrested for vagrancy and incarcerated at the Old Bilibid Prison (now the Manila City Jail) in the Philippines. Shortly after her arrival, she began screaming in terror and convulsing, claiming she was being attacked and bitten by invisible entities. The case quickly drew immense local and international media attention. Witnesses, medical professionals, and even Manila Mayor Arsenio Lacson reported seeing human bite marks and scratches materialize on her body while she was restrained. Despite psychiatric evaluations concluding she was mentally sane, her violent trances and bizarre behaviors continued. American pastor Lester Sumrall intervened, conducting a three-day prayer and deliverance session which resulted in the entities fleeing and Clarita eventually achieving full recovery and release.

#location
The primary location of the possession and exorcism is the Manila City Jail (formerly the Old Bilibid Prison), situated along P. Casal Street in the district of Quiapo, Manila, Philippines.

#upstream-source
Sumrall, Lester (1995). *Alien Entities. A look behind the door to the spirit realm*. Whitaker House.

#title
The White Lady of Balete Drive

#summary
A compassionate motorist picks up a mysterious woman in white on a darkened street, only to discover she has vanished from the backseat and died years prior.

#details
Driving alone at night, a motorist encounters a beautiful woman dressed in a flowing white dress standing on the roadside, asking for a ride home. Feeling compassionate, the driver picks her up and heads toward her destination. However, upon arriving, the driver checks the backseat and finds that she has completely vanished. When visiting the address she provided, the current occupants reveal that the woman actually perished in a tragic accident there long before.

#location
The haunting takes place along Balete Drive located in Quezon City, Metro Manila, Philippines.

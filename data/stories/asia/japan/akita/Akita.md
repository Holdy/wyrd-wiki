#title
Our Lady of Akita

#summary
A wooden statue at a small convent wept 101 times between 1975 and 1981, and a TV crew filmed it in 1978. It is linked to messages reported by Sister Agnes Sasagawa and is the best-known approved weeping statue.

#location
Convent of the Handmaids of the Holy Eucharist, Yuzawadai, Soegawa, Akita, Japan

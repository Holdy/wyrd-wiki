The Possessed Woman and the Silver Fox

Summary

A mischievous silver fox living near Tower Mountain in China possesses a neighboring village woman and refuses to leave until confronted by a local farmer armed with his ox-whip. The defiant spirit is finally subdued when the farmer cracks his whip and commands the entity to flee.

Details

In the region southwest of Jiaozhou Bay in China, Tower Mountain was known for an old temple dedicated to the Old Mother and a nearby cave inhabited by talking silver foxes. One such fox frequently tricked travelers by speaking to them in human voices and wearing disguises made of leaves and plants. After a local farmer named Wang struck the fox with his ox-whip during one of its pranks, the fox vowed vengeance and subsequently possessed a woman in a neighboring village. The woman's family attempted various exorcisms, including hanging a Taoist priest's picture and threatening to call upon the God of War, but the fox boasted that it feared only Wang from the Eastern village. Summoned to help, Wang arrived with his ox-whip, shouted at the hidden entity, and snapped his whip, causing the possessed woman to be freed as the fox hissed and fled out the window.

#title
The Curse of the Bahla Jinn

#summary
Bahla Fort is bound by a persistent curse where any attempts to renovate or rebuild its ancient mud-brick structures mysteriously collapse overnight due to a haunting jinn. 

#details
According to Omani folklore, the medieval Bahla Fort is plagued by a dark curse tied to its supernatural origins and mystical history. Believed by locals to be haunted or initially conjured by powerful jinns and sorcery, a malevolent spirit guards the citadel. For generations, whenever builders, craftsmen, or government authorities attempt to restore, repair, or add to the decaying fortifications, the curse manifests itself. Newly completed walls and restored rooms mysteriously crumble or collapse into ruin overnight, frustrating human efforts to tame the ancient stronghold.

#location
Bahla Fort is a prominent historic fortification located in the heart of the oasis town of Bahla in the Ad Dakhiliyah Governorate of Oman.

#title
The Exorcism of Bahla

#summary
A possessed American woman travels to Oman to undergo a harrowing jinn exorcism at the home of a traditional healer.

#details
Plagued by an unyielding spirit possession that persisted despite prior exorcisms in the United Arab Emirates, an American woman traveled from Boston to Muscat, Oman. Guided by local contacts, she journeyed across the Hajar Mountain Range to the ancient oasis town of Bahla to meet Moalim Salim, a respected Islamic exorcist. Upon arriving at his traditional residence during a severe desert storm, the healer performed a rigorous ritual involving Quranic recitation, frankincense, and water. As the exorcism commenced, supernatural phenomena—including racing shadows, sudden electrical failures, and unearthly screams—signaled the presence of a powerful entity known as Al Malak Al Aswad (The Black Prince of the Jinn). Through intense prayers and spiritual intervention, the healer successfully cast out the entity, leaving the woman temporarily freed from the possession.

#location
Moalim Salim's residence in the oasis town of Bahla, Oman.

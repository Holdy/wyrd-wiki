#title
The Curse of the Sleeping Vishnu

#summary
Following a prophetic vision in the seventeenth century, King Pratap Malla was warned that any ruling monarch of Nepal visiting the Budhanilkantha Temple would meet an untimely death. Fearing this royal curse, generations of Nepalese kings avoided entering the sacred site.

#details
In the seventeenth century, King Pratap Malla experienced a terrifying prophetic dream and vision warning him that a divine curse had been placed upon the royal lineage. According to the vision, if any reigning King of Nepal were to set foot inside or visit the sacred Budhanilkantha Temple—home to the monumental reclining statue of Lord Vishnu floating in a pool—he would suffer a sudden and premature death. Gripped by this supernatural decree, Pratap Malla and all subsequent monarchs of Nepal strictly avoided visiting the temple grounds, sending representatives or worshiping at alternative shrines instead to evade the fatal hex.

#location
The Budhanilkantha Temple is located at the northern end of the Kathmandu Valley, situated below Shivapuri Hill along Golfutar Main Road in Budhanilkantha, Nepal.

#title
The Possession of the Mask

#summary
During the annual Indra Jatra festival, a human dancer donning a sacred wooden mask is overcome and possessed by the spirit of the ancient demon Lakhe. 

#details
According to traditional Newar folklore in Nepal, a flesh-eating demon named Lakhe once haunted the settlement of Majipa before making a pact with the king to renounce his carnivorous habits and protect the local children in exchange for a place among the community. In the living tradition of the Lakhe dance, a chosen performer wears a terrifying red mask known as the *khawpa*. It is believed that the spirit of the demon resides within the mask itself; the moment the dancer puts it on, the entity takes total possession of his body, driving him into a frenzied, powerful dance among the crowds and deities without any rehearsal or choreography.

#location
Kathmandu Durbar Square is a historic public plaza located in the center of Kathmandu, Nepal.

#title
The Woman in Red on the Thimi Highway

#summary
Two motorcyclists riding home late at night encounter a towering, pale-skinned woman in a red sari standing motionless in the middle of the highway. As they attempt to steer past her, her arms unnaturally stretch out toward them before she vanishes into thin air.

#details
Late one night, a driver from Bhaktapur and his friend were returning home from a party on separate motorcycles. Riding ahead on a dark, empty stretch of the highway around Thimi, the lead driver noticed what looked like a tall red pillar standing directly in the middle of the road. Upon drawing closer, they realized the pillar was actually an exceptionally tall woman wearing a red sari with golden embroidery, featuring loose black hair, an expressionless face, and impossibly white skin. As the motorcyclists tried to ride around her on either side, the woman extended her arms and made a beckoning gesture. Her hands began stretching unnaturally toward them at terrifying speed. Narrowly avoiding her grasping hands by inches, the two men sped away without looking back, leaving the road completely empty behind them. Both men fell ill from the shock and trauma of the encounter, which has also been witnessed by other nighttime travelers passing through the area.

#location
The encounter takes place on the highway passing through Thimi (Madhyapur Thimi) in the Bhaktapur District of the Kathmandu Valley, Nepal.

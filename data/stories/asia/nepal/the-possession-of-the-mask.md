#title
The Possession of the Mask

#summary
During the annual Indra Jatra festival, a human dancer donning a sacred wooden mask is overcome and possessed by the spirit of the ancient demon Lakhe. 

#details
According to traditional Newar folklore in Nepal, a flesh-eating demon named Lakhe once haunted the settlement of Majipa before making a pact with the king to renounce his carnivorous habits and protect the local children in exchange for a place among the community. In the living tradition of the Lakhe dance, a chosen performer wears a terrifying red mask known as the *khawpa*. It is believed that the spirit of the demon resides within the mask itself; the moment the dancer puts it on, the entity takes total possession of his body, driving him into a frenzied, powerful dance among the crowds and deities without any rehearsal or choreography.

#location
Kathmandu Durbar Square is a historic public plaza located in the center of Kathmandu, Nepal.

The Phantom Passengers of the D-100 Highway

Summary

Motorists traveling along the D-100 highway at night near the town of Kargali Hanbaba report seeing phantom passengers fleeing across the road who mysteriously vanish upon touching the pavement. This eerie urban legend stems from a tragic 1965 chemical accident on that exact stretch of roadway.

Details

According to local folklore, drivers passing through the village of Kargali Hanbaba on the D-100 highway after midnight occasionally witness a terrifying spectral scene. People watch as a group of desperate passengers rush out of vehicles and attempt to cross the highway, only for their bodies to seemingly melt into the concrete the moment they touch the pavement. This chilling urban legend is rooted in a real, devastating historical disaster that occurred on August 11, 1965, when a passenger bus collided with a tanker truck carrying nitric acid, causing a catastrophic chemical spill and explosion in the drainage ditch where victims sought refuge. Today, travelers sharing the legend warn others that the cursed stretch of asphalt can swallow people whole.

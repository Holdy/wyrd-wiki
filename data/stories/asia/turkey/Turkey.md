#title
The Phantom Passengers of the D-100 Highway

#summary
Motorists traveling along the D-100 highway at night near the town of Kargali Hanbaba report seeing phantom passengers fleeing across the road who mysteriously vanish upon touching the pavement. This eerie urban legend stems from a tragic 1965 chemical accident on that exact stretch of roadway.

#details
According to local folklore, drivers passing through the village of Kargali Hanbaba on the D-100 highway after midnight occasionally witness a terrifying spectral scene. People watch as a group of desperate passengers rush out of vehicles and attempt to cross the highway, only for their bodies to seemingly melt into the concrete the moment they touch the pavement. This chilling urban legend is rooted in a real, devastating historical disaster that occurred on August 11, 1965, when a passenger bus collided with a tanker truck carrying nitric acid, causing a catastrophic chemical spill and explosion in the drainage ditch where victims sought refuge. Today, travelers sharing the legend warn others that the cursed stretch of asphalt can swallow people whole.

#location
The key location is the D-100 highway where it passes through the town of Kargali Hanbaba, located between Izmit and Bolu in Turkey.

#title
The Prophecy of the Maiden's Tower

#summary
An anxious ruler constructed a fortress in the middle of the Bosphorus to protect his daughter from a deadly prophecy, but a venomous snake hidden within a basket of fruit ultimately fulfilled the curse.

#details
According to longstanding Istanbul folklore, a Byzantine ruler was devastated when an oracle prophesied that his beloved daughter was cursed to die from a venomous snakebite before her eighteenth birthday. Desperate to outwit fate, the king ordered a fortified tower to be built upon a rocky islet in the middle of the Bosphorus strait, believing the surrounding waters would keep all snakes at bay. The princess grew up in total isolation inside the stone tower, safely cut off from the mainland. However, on her eighteenth birthday, a celebratory basket of fruit sent from the city was delivered to her quarters. A deadly serpent had coiled itself unseen within the fruit, striking and killing the princess just as the oracle had foretold.

#location
Maiden's Tower (Kız Kulesi) is situated on a small rocky islet in the Bosphorus strait just off the coast of Üsküdar in Istanbul, Turkey.

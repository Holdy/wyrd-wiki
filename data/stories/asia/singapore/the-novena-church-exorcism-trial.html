The Novena Church Exorcism Trial

Summary

A woman brought to a Singapore church by her family under the belief that she was possessed by evil spirits subsequently sued the priests and helpers who performed an exorcism rite upon her. Her claims were ultimately dismissed following a high-profile High Court trial.

Details

On August 10, 2004, Amutha Valli Krishnan was brought to church by family members who believed she was possessed by malevolent spirits. Witnesses reported that she crept on the ground like a snake and marched into a private room where Redemptorist priests Simon Tan and Jacob Ong, along with six lay helpers, prayed over her and performed an exorcism rite. Valli later filed a civil lawsuit in the High Court seeking damages, claiming she was forcibly restrained, assaulted, and subjected to a traumatic exorcism without her consent. Following a 32-day trial spanning across 2007 and 2008, Justice Lee Seiu Kin dismissed the lawsuit in February 2009, ruling in favor of the priests and accepting the defense's consistent accounts of the possession and prayer session.

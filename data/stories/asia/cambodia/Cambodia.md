#title
The Curse of Suryavarman

#summary
An ancient Khmer monarch inscribed a powerful stone curse condemning anyone who would betray his royal lineage to unending ruin and foreign conquest. Centuries later, after a melon farmer usurped the throne, the long-delayed curse began to manifest across generations through national turmoil and repeated calamities.

#details
Long before the decline of the great Khmer Empire, King Suryavarman I united the realm and left behind a solemn stone inscription. He warned that anyone who dared to betray him or his royal descendants would face unending ruin, slaughter, and the loss of all prosperity. Centuries later, a melon farmer named Taeng Whan led a rebellion, overthrew the last royal descendant of the lineage, and seized the throne. This usurpation triggered the ancient curse, which is traditionally believed to have doomed the kingdom to generations of foreign invasion, civil conflict, and continuous national tragedy.

#location
Angkor Wat temple complex in Siem Reap, Cambodia, where the ancient inscriptions and historical events of the Khmer Empire are centered.

#title
The House the Ghost Bought

#summary
A newly built house in Kampong Chhnang province became legendary after a spirit allegedly purchased it with a fortune in gold, later drawing afflicted villagers seeking cures from psychological torment. When a local man suffered severe psychological affliction and wandered the streets naked, his family prayed at the spectral dwelling, leading to his sudden and complete recovery.

#details
In 1993, a family built a wooden house beside National Road 5 in the Baribor district of Kampong Chhnang province, Cambodia. According to local legend, a ghost appeared to the occupants in a dream and offered $3,000 in gold to purchase the home, leaving the gold on their doorstep by morning while moving their belongings into the surrounding fields. The abandoned stilt house became widely known as "the house the ghost bought" and transformed into a revered local shrine. Over the years, travelers and relatives began visiting the site to make offerings and pray for miraculous interventions. Among them was Phnom Penh resident Chhe Phallin, whose father had fallen into severe mental turmoil—losing his mind and wandering naked through the streets at night. Desperate for a cure, Phallin traveled to the house, burned incense, and begged the spirits for help. Two days later, her father returned home completely normal and able to resume daily life. Today, the mysteriously dust-free house stands as a sacred landmark where believers continue to offer supplications and seek relief from spiritual afflictions.

#location
The abandoned stilt house is located beside National Road 5 in the Baribor district of Kampong Chhnang province, Cambodia.

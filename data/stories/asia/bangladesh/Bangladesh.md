#title
The Dragon's Vengeance of Boga Lake

#summary
According to local tribal legend, Boga Lake was formed overnight through a catastrophic curse after villagers killed a sacred dwelling dragon. The resulting transformation completely swallowed the settlement and permanently altered the landscape.

#details
Centuries ago, the indigenous inhabitants of a hill settlement in the region encountered a gigantic creature—often described in local folklore as a dragon or a divine serpent—that lived quietly deep within the mountain hollow. Instead of respecting its presence, greedy villagers hunted, killed, and consumed the creature. The very next day, a devastating natural cataclysm struck as retribution for the sacrilege: the earth collapsed, a tremendous roar echoed through the peaks, and the entire village vanished into a massive crater. Water immediately flooded the basin, transforming the cursed site into a deep, mysterious blue lake that remains active and revered to this day.

#location
Boga Lake is situated atop the hills approximately 60 kilometers from Bandarban town in the Bandarban Hill District of southeastern Bangladesh.

#title
The White Lady of Airport Road

#summary
Drivers traveling past midnight frequently encounter a phantom woman in a white sari who suddenly materializes in the middle of the highway and rushes toward oncoming vehicles before vanishing.

#details
Late-night motorists traversing the stretch of Dhaka Airport Road between the Biman Office and Jasimuddin Avenue have repeatedly reported harrowing encounters with a mysterious woman dressed in a white sari. As vehicles approach, the spectral figure suddenly steps out into the middle of the road and barrels toward cars at breakneck speed, forcing drivers to slam on their brakes to avoid a collision. Moments after the panicked stop, the woman vanishes entirely into thin air.

#location
The events take place along the stretch of Dhaka Airport Road situated between the Biman Office and Jasimuddin Avenue in Dhaka, Bangladesh.

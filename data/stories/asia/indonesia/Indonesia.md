#title
The Curse of Tanah Lot

#summary
An abandoned princess cursed a sacred sea temple to doom any unmarried couple who visits it to relationship failure within six months. Originally created as a moral warning against pre-marital indiscretions near holy grounds, the legend persists as a cautionary tale for modern travelers.

#details
According to ancient lore, a prince and a princess belonging to the high Brahman caste traveled from neighbouring Java to visit Bali, eventually spending time at the iconic Tanah Lot Temple. Overcome by the romantic sunset along the black sand coast, the couple crossed traditional boundaries and shared an intimate bond. Soon afterward, however, the prince abandoned the princess and vanished without marrying her. Consumed by heartbreak and humiliation, the enraged princess cast a powerful curse over the sacred site, declaring that any unmarried couple visiting the temple together would see their relationship fall apart within six months. Local tradition later suggested the legend served as a cultural guardrail to prevent young, unwed lovers from disrespecting sacred grounds with premarital affairs.

#location
Tanah Lot Temple is located on a large offshore rock formation in the Tabanan Regency, approximately 20 kilometers northwest of Denpasar, Bali, Indonesia.

#title
The Library Exorcism at a Banjarmasin High School

#summary
Four female high school students were simultaneously possessed by an angered tree-dwelling spirit after campus branches were pruned. Staff members successfully performed Islamic exorcism rites to drive out the entities and restore the students.

#details
Following an extended flag ceremony at a public high school in Banjarmasin, South Kalimantan, four female students suddenly suffered a violent mass trance and were pinned to the floor of the school library while thrashing and screaming in inhuman voices. As a tense crowd of teachers and students gathered, the school's resident exorcist—an electrical engineering teacher—along with the school's religion teacher, knelt beside the afflicted girls. Pressing their hands onto the tops of the students' heads and reciting passages from the Qur'an, they confronted the entity, which communicated through a student that it was furious because its tree on school grounds had recently been trimmed. After the prayers and commanding exorcism rites were completed, the spirits departed, leaving the students completely limp, waking with no memory of the possession before returning to class.

#location
The library of a public high school in Banjarmasin, South Kalimantan, Indonesia.

#title
The Sweet Maiden of Ancol Bridge

#summary
Motorists driving across Ancol Bridge in North Jakarta frequently report encountering the apparition of a murdered maiden suddenly crossing the road in front of their vehicles. According to local urban legend, her restless spirit haunts the area following her tragic death and the disposal of her body in the river below.

#details
Decades ago, a young woman was reportedly kidnapped and murdered by a bandit, who threw her body into the Ancol River. Since around the 1950s, drivers navigating the bridge along Jalan RE Martadinata at night have experienced sudden close calls caused by a mysterious woman abruptly stepping into traffic before vanishing. Although skeptics attribute these encounters to driver fatigue, the enduring legend of "Si Manis Jembatan Ancol" has become one of Jakarta's most famous urban legends, inspiring subsequent books, films, and television series.

#location
The story takes place at Ancol Bridge along Jalan RE Martadinata where it crosses the Ancol River in North Jakarta, Indonesia.

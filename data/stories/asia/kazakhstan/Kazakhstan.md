#title
The Phantom Hitchhiker of the Kapchagay Highway

#summary
Motorists traveling along the Kapchagay highway late at night frequently encounter a ghostly young woman hitchhiking who asks drivers to call her parents using an outdated Soviet-era phone number.

#details
Drivers heading north of Almaty on the Kapchagay highway past midnight have reported seeing a figure in white standing by the roadside thumbing for a ride. According to local lore passed down through generations, motorists who stop to pick up the female hitchhiker find that she asks them to call her parents, providing an old six-digit Soviet phone number. When people dial the number, grieving relatives on the other end inform them that their daughter died years prior. In alternative accounts of the haunting, the phantom passenger begins to flirt with the driver until he glances down to discover she has hooves instead of feet.

#location
The Kapchagay highway extending north from Almaty toward the city of Konayev in Kazakhstan.

#title
The Stone Bride of Kelinshektau

#summary
A wealthy merchant's daughter named Aisulu discovered a single silver bowl in her golden wedding dowry and furiously sent a message of complaint back to her father, prompting him to instantly curse her and the entire caravan into stone. To this day, the majestic rock formations in the mountains stand as petrified monuments to the ill-fated bride and her procession.

#details
In the ancient days, a young woman named Aisulu, celebrated for her beauty, lived with her wealthy father in the mountains. Though she initially resisted marriage, she eventually agreed when her father promised her a lavish dowry composed entirely of gold. A grand wedding caravan set off down the mountain slopes toward her bridegroom's village. Along the way, Aisulu inspected the dowry items and was infuriated to find a single bowl made of silver rather than gold. She immediately sent a messenger back to her father to voice her indignant complaint. Blinded by sudden anger at her ingratitude, her father pronounced a curse upon her. Right at that moment, Aisulu—still adorned in her traditional bridal gown and tall peaked headdress—along with her entire caravan and all the golden treasures, were instantly petrified into stone formations, giving the mountain range the name Kelinshektau, meaning "the stone bride."

#location
The Kelinshektau mountain range on the eastern side of the Karatau mountains in the Suzak region of Turkestan, Kazakhstan.

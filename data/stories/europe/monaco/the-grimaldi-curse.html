    The Grimaldi Curse

    Summary

    According to legend, a medieval sorceress cursed Prince Rainier I and all his descendants so that no member of the House of Grimaldi would ever find true happiness in marriage. For centuries, this dynastic hex has allegedly plagued the ruling family of Monaco with failed unions, affairs, and romantic tragedies.

    Details

    In the late 13th or early 14th century, Prince Rainier I—the foundational figure of the Grimaldi dynasty—captured and held captive a young Flemish woman who rejected his advances, or alternatively condemned a local witch to be burned at the stake. In retaliation, the woman invoked a powerful curse upon Rainier and his lineage, declaring that no Grimaldi would ever experience lasting happiness or harmony in marriage. Over the centuries, this generational doom has seemingly manifested in a long line of marital turbulence, scandalous divorces, and fatal accidents among the royal family, ranging from historical scandals to the tragic death of Princess Grace and the troubled romances of her descendants.

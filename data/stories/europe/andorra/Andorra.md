#title
The Possessed Wolf of Auvinyà

#summary
After a village leader kills a monstrous wolf terrorizing an Andorran valley, he becomes gravely ill and possessed by the vengeful spirit of a dead warlord that had inhabited the beast.

#details
In ancient times, the southern border pass of Andorra was guarded by a mysterious figure known as the White Lady. Years later, a ruthless warlord who had long terrorized the region vanished upstream along the Valira river. Shortly after his disappearance, a ferocious, unnatural wolf began slaughtering livestock across the area. A prominent local leader organized a hunt, cornering and killing the beast, which uttered strange, unintelligible words with its dying breath. Days later, the leader fell gravely ill, suffering from terrifying nighttime deliriums until the White Lady appeared to him. She revealed that the body of the wolf had been taken over and animated by the soul of the missing warlord. Though the harrowing affliction ultimately claimed the leader's life, he died knowing his sacrifice had freed the valleys from the entity.

#location
The events center around the waterfall of Auvinyà (Cascada d'Auvinyà) in the parish of Sant Julià de Lòria, Andorra.

#title
The White Lady of Auvinyà

#summary
The ghost of a medieval noblewoman appears along the mountain paths and border passes of Auvinyà to protect Andorra from foreign tyrants. She famously lured a despotic Bishop of Urgell into the woods, leading to his mysterious disappearance.

#details
Centuries ago, the border passes and mountain tracks between Andorra and Alt Urgell were guarded by a noble family whose last surviving member eventually passed away. Upon her death, her spirit became the "Dama Blanca" (White Lady), a protective phantom dedicated to watching over Andorran independence. When a tyrannical Bishop of Urgell repeatedly threatened local liberties and imposed heavy taxes, the White Lady manifested as a stunningly beautiful woman to the bishop as he journeyed along the mountain road toward the Principality near Auvinyà. She lured him deep into the dense surrounding forests, leaving him so terrified by morning that he fled back to La Seu d'Urgell and swore never to return. Years later, when the bishop broke his oath and returned to subjugate Andorra once more, he vanished without a trace along the path, never to be seen again. To this day, travellers and locals report sightings of the White Lady wandering the wooded tracks and tracks around Auvinyà, ever watchful to bar the way of anyone seeking to oppress the Andorran people.

#location
The forested tracks and border passes surrounding the village of Auvinyà in the parish of Sant Julià de Lòria, Andorra.

#title
The Witch of Fener

#summary
Following a catastrophic landslide that destroyed the hamlet of Fener, a woman who returned for her hidden money miraculously outpaced the disaster to arrive safely ahead of her fleeing neighbors, earning a lingering reputation as a witch. Her uncanny survival became a dark local legend passed down through generations in the valley.

#details
In 1865, a violent storm lashed the Pyrenees, triggering a massive landslide above the small Andorran hamlet of Fener. Alerted by a sharp-eyed servant who noticed the earth cracking, the villagers abandoned their homes just in time, watching in horror as the mountain slope gave way and utterly obliterated their settlement. Amidst the chaos, one woman realized she had left behind her bag of gold and coins inside her house and rushed back toward the collapsing ruins, leading everyone to believe she had perished beneath the tons of rock and mud. Yet, when the bewildered survivors finally made their way down to the nearby parish of Les Escaldes, they were astounded to find the woman already waiting there safely, entirely unharmed and clutching her heavy bag of money. Because no mortal could have outrun or survived the catastrophic slide on foot, her supernatural escape cemented her lasting legacy in local folklore as a witch protected by dark forces.

#location
The hamlet of Fener lies in the valley near Les Escaldes in the Principality of Andorra.

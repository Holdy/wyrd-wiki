The Card Players of Strassen

Summary

Four card players in Strassen who wagered their souls on who would stop playing first find themselves trapped in a grueling game while a demonic black dog lurks beneath their table.

Details

On a Sunday in the village of Strassen, four men gathered to play cards and made a reckless pact, declaring that whoever stopped playing first would be claimed by the Devil. Scarcely had they begun when a large black dog appeared under their table, acting as the embodiment of the Devil waiting to claim his victim. Driven by sheer terror, the men played continuously through day and night, their eyes wild and staring like men possessed. Finally, the parish priest heard of their plight, entered the house, sat down at the table, and after a few rounds intentionally threw down his cards to quit first. At that exact moment, the demonic dog let out a terrifying howl and smashed violently through the window, shattering the glass as it vanished into the night.

#title
The Veiled Spirits of Lenningen Forest

#summary
Two veiled spectral figures silently accompany nocturnal hikers along a forest path above Lenningen before vanishing at the tree line. These apparitions are the restless ghosts of a distraught maiden and her unfaithful lover who both tragically perished.

#details
In the area near Canach and Lenningen, a young man carried on a passionate romance before betraying his first love for another woman. Upon discovering his cruel infidelity and overhearing him speak disdainfully of her, the heartbroken young woman threw herself into a forest stream, where her lifeless body was found the next morning. Stricken by horror and guilt over his former lover's suicide, the young man soon fell ill and passed away, shortly followed by his grieving new partner. Ever since, the two betrayed lovers haunt the forest path together as veiled spirits, silently guiding nocturnal hikers from the bridge to the edge of the woods before disappearing into thin air.

#location
The haunting takes place along the forest path and woods situated above the village of Lenningen in eastern Luxembourg.

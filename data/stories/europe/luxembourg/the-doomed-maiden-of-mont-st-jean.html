The Doomed Maiden of Mont St. Jean

Summary

Elisabeth von Honulstein, a noblewoman who lived in quiet seclusion until her death, is fated by an ancient curse to roam Mont St. Jean as a phantom and transform into a fire-spitting snake guarding a treasure shrine until a brave man redeems her.

Details

Elisabeth von Honulstein was the last descendant of the noble family that once owned Dudelange Castle. Following an unhappy arranged marriage in Reims, she fled back to her ancestral home and lived a life of secluded piety until she passed away and was buried on top of Mont St. Jean. Heartbroken by her sudden death, the local villagers began whispering that she had been cursed to inhabit the hill in eternal limbo. According to legend, every seven years she wanders the slopes in white clothes, and she can also transform into a fire-spitting snake guarding a treasure-filled shrine. To break the curse, win the treasure, and redeem her in marriage, a courageous young man must retrieve a key directly from the snake's mouth using his own mouth.

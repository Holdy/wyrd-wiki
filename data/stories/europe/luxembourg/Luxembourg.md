#title
The Card Players of Strassen

#summary
Four card players in Strassen who wagered their souls on who would stop playing first find themselves trapped in a grueling game while a demonic black dog lurks beneath their table.

#details
On a Sunday in the village of Strassen, four men gathered to play cards and made a reckless pact, declaring that whoever stopped playing first would be claimed by the Devil. Scarcely had they begun when a large black dog appeared under their table, acting as the embodiment of the Devil waiting to claim his victim. Driven by sheer terror, the men played continuously through day and night, their eyes wild and staring like men possessed. Finally, the parish priest heard of their plight, entered the house, sat down at the table, and after a few rounds intentionally threw down his cards to quit first. At that exact moment, the demonic dog let out a terrifying howl and smashed violently through the window, shattering the glass as it vanished into the night.

#location
The events took place inside the Lorang house (Maison Lorangs) located in Strassen, Luxembourg.

#title
The Doomed Maiden of Mont St. Jean

#summary
Elisabeth von Honulstein, a noblewoman who lived in quiet seclusion until her death, is fated by an ancient curse to roam Mont St. Jean as a phantom and transform into a fire-spitting snake guarding a treasure shrine until a brave man redeems her.

#details
Elisabeth von Honulstein was the last descendant of the noble family that once owned Dudelange Castle. Following an unhappy arranged marriage in Reims, she fled back to her ancestral home and lived a life of secluded piety until she passed away and was buried on top of Mont St. Jean. Heartbroken by her sudden death, the local villagers began whispering that she had been cursed to inhabit the hill in eternal limbo. According to legend, every seven years she wanders the slopes in white clothes, and she can also transform into a fire-spitting snake guarding a treasure-filled shrine. To break the curse, win the treasure, and redeem her in marriage, a courageous young man must retrieve a key directly from the snake's mouth using his own mouth.

#location
Mont St. Jean (Gehaansbierg) is a 417-meter-high hill in Dudelange, Luxembourg, marked by the ruins of Dudelange Castle and St. John's Chapel at its summit.

#title
The Veiled Spirits of Lenningen Forest

#summary
Two veiled spectral figures silently accompany nocturnal hikers along a forest path above Lenningen before vanishing at the tree line. These apparitions are the restless ghosts of a distraught maiden and her unfaithful lover who both tragically perished.

#details
In the area near Canach and Lenningen, a young man carried on a passionate romance before betraying his first love for another woman. Upon discovering his cruel infidelity and overhearing him speak disdainfully of her, the heartbroken young woman threw herself into a forest stream, where her lifeless body was found the next morning. Stricken by horror and guilt over his former lover's suicide, the young man soon fell ill and passed away, shortly followed by his grieving new partner. Ever since, the two betrayed lovers haunt the forest path together as veiled spirits, silently guiding nocturnal hikers from the bridge to the edge of the woods before disappearing into thin air.

#location
The haunting takes place along the forest path and woods situated above the village of Lenningen in eastern Luxembourg.

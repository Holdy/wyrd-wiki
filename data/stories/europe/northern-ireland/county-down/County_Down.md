#title
The Blue Lady of Tollymore

#summary
The Blue Lady is a ghostly female specter who wanders the tree-lined avenues of Tollymore Forest Park at night.

#details
Following the post-war destruction of Tollymore House, the historic seat of the Roden family, a female spirit known as the Blue Lady began roaming the surrounding grounds. On dark nights, she has been frequently sighted walking along the tree-lined avenues and park paths where the estate once stood.

#location
The tree-lined avenue within Tollymore Forest Park near Bryansford in County Down.

#title
The Cursed Stone of Dundrum

#summary
An ancient standing stone in Dundrum carries a persistent curse that brings misfortune and disaster upon anyone who dares to touch, move, or disturb it. Believed to mark a protected druidic site, the stone's malignant aura punishes trespassers and vandals alike.

#details
Local folklore surrounding the standing stone in Dundrum warns that the monolith is bound by powerful ancient spells. According to tradition, the stone guards an old sacred site or ritual ground. Anyone foolish enough to tamper with the stone, move its position, or disrespect the surrounding earth is said to court swift retribution, suffering severe misfortune, ill health, or sudden tragedy. Generations of locals have thus left the stone strictly undisturbed to avoid provoking the wrath of the unseen forces guarding it.

#location
The cursed standing stone is located in the village of Dundrum, County Down.

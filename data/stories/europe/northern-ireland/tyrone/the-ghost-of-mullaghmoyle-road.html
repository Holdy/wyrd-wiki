The Ghost of Mullaghmoyle Road

Summary

A mysterious white lady has been spotted on the Mullaghmoyle Road near Coalisland, County Tyrone, causing a stir and attracting crowds.

Details

Over the holiday period, reports emerged of a ghostly figure appearing on the Mullaghmoyle Road outside Coalisland in County Tyrone. The apparition was described as an old woman dressed in white, often seen running across the road near three telegraph poles. This sighting led to a significant influx of people, with up to 60 cars lining up along the road in hopes of catching a glimpse of the ghost.

The Cursed Butter Stone of Knockmany

Summary

When giant Oonagh's hurried toss of a massive butter miscaun misses its target across the Tyrone valley, she places a fierce curse upon it, transforming the dairy lump into stone. Anyone who attempts to move or injure the boulder is doomed to suffer evil misfortune.

Details

In local folklore popularized by author William Carleton, the giant Fin M'Coul and his clever wife Oonagh lived atop Knockmany Hill in County Tyrone. When the terrifying giant Cucullin threatened to attack Fin, Oonagh's sister Granua asked across the valley for a huge miscaun of butter to help feed her guests. Distracted by the impending duel, Oonagh hurled the massive tub of butter across the Clogher Valley without her protective charm, causing it to fall short into the bog near Augher. Infuriated by the mistake, Oonagh cursed the butter, turning it into a grey stone bearing the permanent imprint of her fingers and decreeing that evil would befall the first person to ever move or damage it.

The Phantom Pedestrian of Brookeborough

Summary

A motorcyclist nearly struck a woman in 1950s attire who crossed a narrow road near Brookeborough before walking through an open gate into a field. When he investigated the gate, he discovered an empty, steep slope with no trace of her.

Details

While riding a motorcycle on a narrow road above Brookeborough in broad daylight, a man narrowly avoided hitting a woman wearing 1950s-style clothing as she walked across the asphalt and passed through an open gate into a field. Intending to reprimand her for the dangerous maneuver, he stopped, turned his motorcycle around, and rode back to the gate. Upon reaching it, he found an open field rising steeply toward a thick hedge with absolutely no sign of the woman. Overcome by a sudden chilling dread and goosebumps, he made a hasty retreat.

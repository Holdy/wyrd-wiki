    The Curse of Myrrha

    Summary

    Angered by a perceived insult, the goddess Aphrodite inflicted a tragic curse of unnatural desire upon Princess Myrrha of Cyprus, driving her to illicit transgression against her father, King Kinyras. Overcome with grief and pursued in rage, Myrrha was ultimately transformed by the gods into a myrrh tree, from whose trunk the youthful deity Adonis was born.

    Details

    According to ancient Cypriot myth, Princess Myrrha incurred the wrath of Aphrodite after failing to properly honor the goddess. As punishment, Aphrodite cursed Myrrha with an inescapable and unnatural desire for her own father, King Kinyras. Tormented by this divine affliction and desperate to keep her shame hidden, Myrrha acted under the cover of darkness. When King Kinyras eventually discovered the horrific truth of her deception, he flew into a rage and attempted to kill her. Fleeing in terror across the landscape of Cyprus, Myrrha prayed to the gods for deliverance from her unbearable existence. Taking pity upon her plight, the gods intervened and transformed her into a myrrh tree. Trapped within the bark, she shed resin tears while her trunk eventually split to give birth to Adonis, intertwining her tragic curse with themes of seasonal renewal, sorrow, and rebirth.

#title
The Benedictine Curse of Lokrum Island

#summary
Fleeing their historic sanctuary after being forcibly expelled, Benedictine monks cast a chilling procession-based curse upon anyone who should ever claim the island for personal ownership. True to the warning, a string of tragic and unnatural deaths befell subsequent owners and the Habsburg family.

#details
In the late 18th and early 19th centuries, foreign powers seized control of Dubrovnik and ordered the dissolution of the ancient Benedictine monastery on the island of Lokrum. On the night before their forced departure, the monks gathered under the cover of darkness, pulling their hoods down and turning their candles upside down. In total silence, they performed a solemn, continuous nocturnal procession around the island perimeter, dripping wax along the path as they chanted prayers. With every circuit, they pronounced a dire curse upon anyone who would ever acquire the island for private ownership, declaring that disaster would follow them to the grave. The curse soon gained notoriety when local nobles who bought parts of the island suffered sudden ruins, financial wipeouts, and violent ends. The dark reputation cemented itself further when Archduke Maximilian of Austria and members of the Habsburg imperial family purchased the island, subsequently meeting legendary fates that included execution, assassination, and tragic suicide. To this day, local superstition holds that no one can peacefully own or spend the night on the island without inviting profound misfortune.

#location
Lokrum Island, located in the Adriatic Sea just a short distance off the coast of Dubrovnik, Croatia.

#title
The Cursed Stretch of the A3 Highway

#summary
A notorious segment of the A3 motorway in Croatia is plagued by ghostly phenomena and a devastating number of fatal crashes stemming from disturbed graves. Drivers navigating this road at night frequently experience eerie hallucinations and supernatural encounters.

#details
The section of the A3 motorway running between Staro Petrovo Selo and Nova Gradiška in Croatia has gained a dark reputation as one of the most haunted stretches of highway in Europe. According to local folklore, the road's curse originated when construction workers used stone and materials from old tombstones and graves during its paving, combined with youths mocking the resting places at the time. This desecration is believed to have angered dark forces and trapped restless spirits, resulting in a staggering frequency of unexplained accidents, hallucinations among drivers, and ghostly apparitions. Over the years, the deadly stretch has claimed dozens of lives, including several prominent figures who perished in eerily similar circumstances just meters apart on the exact same section of the road.

#location
The haunted stretch of the A3 motorway is located in Croatia between the towns of Staro Petrovo Selo and Nova Gradiška.

#title
The Vampire of Kringa

#summary
Following his death in 1656, a villager's corpse was animated by a malevolent entity that terrorized his Istrian hometown for sixteen years. Night after night, the undead entity haunted his widow and knocked on doors, foretelling the deaths of local residents until courageous villagers finally decapitated him.

#details
Jure Grando Alilović was a stonemason living in the small village of Kringa, Croatia, who passed away from illness in 1656. According to local folklore and historical accounts, his body was possessed by a *štrigon*—a warlock or demonic entity—causing him to rise from his grave every night. For sixteen years, the entity roamed the village streets, knocking on doors to mark residents for impending death, and visiting his terrified widow, Ivana, in her bedroom. Desperate to end the haunting, the village priest and local townspeople tracked his corpse to the graveyard, where they found his body miraculously preserved with a smile. After failed attempts to pierce his heart with a hawthorn stick, villager Stipan Milašić used a saw to sever his head, causing the corpse to scream and bleed, which finally brought peace to the region.

#location
The graveyard and churchyard located behind the main church in the village of Kringa on the Istrian peninsula in Croatia.

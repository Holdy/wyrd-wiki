    Oliver Plunkett's head

    Summary

    Oliver Plunkett's severed head is often compared with the screaming skull of Father Ambrose Barlow, but it never screamed. Instead, it is said to have communicated with the living through miracles.

    Details

    Historian Frances Larson has compared the story of Oliver Plunkett's head with that of Father Ambrose Barlow, the martyred priest whose skull is kept at Wardley Hall. Unlike Barlow's skull, Plunkett's decapitated head is not said to have screamed. Instead, it was believed to communicate with the living through miracles.

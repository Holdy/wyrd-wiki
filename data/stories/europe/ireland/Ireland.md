#title
The Bent Spectre of Old Tramore Road

#summary
Drivers along the Old Tramore Road frequently report encountering the spectral apparition of an elderly man with a hunched posture who unexpectedly steps directly into the path of oncoming vehicles before vanishing. This eerie figure has made the stretch near the N25 one of the most widely discussed haunted locations in the region.

#details
Motorists travelling along the Old Tramore Road in County Waterford have long shared harrowing accounts of a ghostly encounter after dark. Witnesses describe seeing an old man with a noticeably bent posture suddenly step out from the roadside into the path of their cars. Panicked drivers who slam on their brakes to avoid a collision discover that the figure has completely vanished into thin air, leaving no trace behind.

#location
The Old Tramore Road near its intersection with the N25 in County Waterford, Ireland.

#title
The Cooneen Poltergeist and Demonic Infestation

#summary
Following the death of the family patriarch, a remote rural farmhouse in County Fermanagh was subjected to violent poltergeist disturbances and alleged demonic infestation. Terrified by flying objects, disembodied voices, and spectral attacks, the family was ultimately driven to flee across the Atlantic.

#details
After the tragic death of Michael Murphy in 1907, his widow Bridget and their children residing at their homestead in Cornarooslan experienced intense and unexplained phenomena. Rapping sounds echoed against the walls, heavy furniture moved on its own, and household objects hurled violently across rooms. Local lore attributed the awakening of these malevolent forces to the eldest son, James, who had allegedly experimented with an occult text titled *The Legions of Doom* in an attempt to summon a spirit, resulting in an entity taking hold of the household. Despite two formal exorcisms performed by Father Eugene Coyle, the haunting persisted with relentless intensity. The family was eventually ostracized by superstitious neighbors and forced to emigrate to America in 1913, where witnesses claimed the entity followed them aboard their ship and into their new lives.

#location
The key location is the abandoned Murphy farmhouse, commonly known as the Cooneen Ghost House, situated in the townland of Cornarooslan near Brookeborough in County Fermanagh.

#title
The Curse of Macha

#summary
After being forced to race the king's horses while heavily pregnant, the otherworldly woman Macha cursed the warriors of Ulster to suffer crippling pains in their time of greatest need for nine generations.

#details
Crunden, a farmer from Ulster, lived happily with his mysterious and swift wife, Macha, after she appeared in his home and cared for his children. When King Connor boasted at a feast that no creature in Ireland was faster than his new chariot-horses, Crunden drunkenly boasted that his wife could outrun them. The king ordered Crunden seized under threat of death unless Macha proved the claim. Despite being heavily pregnant and pleading with both the king and the assembled Red Branch warriors of Ulster for help, Macha was forced to run the race. She outpaced the king's horses and won, but gave birth to dead twins at the finish line. Before vanishing, she cursed the warriors of Ulster, declaring that whenever they needed their strength most, it would fail them and they would suffer the agonizing pains of a woman in labor for nine days and nine nights, spanning across nine generations.

#location
The royal site of Emain Macha (Navan Fort), located near Armagh City in County Armagh, Northern Ireland.

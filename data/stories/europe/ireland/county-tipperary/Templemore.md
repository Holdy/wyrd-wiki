#title
Templemore bleeding statues

#summary
During the War of Independence, all the statues in a teenage farm labourer's cottage were said to bleed, and crowds came by the tens of thousands. Michael Collins reportedly smashed one rigged statue, but it is unclear if it was Walsh's.

#location
James Walsh's cottage, Curraheen, near Templemore, County Tipperary, Ireland

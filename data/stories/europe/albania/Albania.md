#title
The Mother's Vengeance Upon the Peaks

#summary
A grieving mother fleeing the Ottomans laid a terminal curse of perpetual fire and drought upon the northern highlands when her children died of thirst. To this day, the landscape suffers seasonal wildfires and recurring blights in accordance with her dying decree.

#details
Fleeing the destruction of her home by Ottoman soldiers, a widowed mother climbed the rugged northern highlands of Albania carrying her two young children. Trapped in the scorching heat of July without water or shade, her children's lips cracked and they grew delirious from dehydration. Passing dry springs like *Gurrat e Guarda* and finding no relief, the children collapsed in agony and died before her eyes. Overwhelmed by grief and helpless despair, the mother cast a powerful curse upon the range: that the mountains should forever lack water, burn with perpetual fires, and be darkened by storms. Her curse took root in the geography, creating the Accursed Mountains (*Bjeshkët e Namuna*), where annual wildfires and sudden lightning strikes continue to devastate the forests during the anniversary of her tragic journey. Local shepherds still honor a carved rock formation resembling the mother and children by pouring water over it to appease the enduring curse.

#location
The Accursed Mountains (Albanian Alps) rise across northern Albania, bordering Montenegro and Kosovo.

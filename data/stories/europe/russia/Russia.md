#title
The Phantom of the Fortress Palace

#summary
Emperor Paul I built a heavily fortified St. Petersburg residence only to be assassinated within its walls shortly after completion, fulfilling ominous prophecies and leaving his restless ghost to haunt the halls. The castle's architecture and tragic history became permanently bound to a curse of untimely death.

#details
Commissioned by Emperor Paul I as a secure refuge from potential coups, the Mikhailovsky Castle was completed in 1801. From the start, the project was shrouded in dark omens and folklore, including stories of encounters with spectral figures and a chilling prophecy regarding the number of letters in the inscription above the main gate—matching the exact age at which the Tsar would meet his violent end. Just months after moving into the fortress, Paul was betrayed and strangled by conspirators in his own bedchamber. Ever since, locals and guards have reported sightings of the Emperor's glowing silhouette wandering the corridors and holding a candle in the second-floor windows, eternally trapped within the walls of the palace he built to protect himself.

#location
Mikhailovsky Castle (St. Michael's Castle) is located at 2 Sadovaya Street in St. Petersburg, Russia.

#title
The Phantom Signal of Sofia Perovskaya

#summary
The ghostly apparition of the 19th-century revolutionary Sofia Perovskaya haunts a St. Petersburg bridge on the anniversary of Tsar Alexander II's assassination. Anyone unfortunate enough to witness her waving her white handkerchief is doomed to fall into the canal and perish.

#details
Sofia Perovskaya was a key member of the Narodnaya Volya revolutionary group that orchestrated the 1881 assassination of Emperor Alexander II, during which she signaled the attackers by waving a white handkerchief. Following her subsequent trial and execution by hanging, local folklore emerged that her spirit returns annually on the night of March 1st. She is said to appear on the bridge near the Church of the Savior on the Blood, performing her fatal signal once more, which lures unlucky witnesses to their watery deaths in the canal below.

#location
The bridge adjacent to the Church of the Savior on the Blood spanning the Griboyedov Canal in St. Petersburg, Russia.

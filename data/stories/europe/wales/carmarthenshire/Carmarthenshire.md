#title
Merlin's Oak

#summary
A curse on Carmarthen town was linked to an oak tree that stood at the junction of Oak Lane and Priory Street.

#details
In Arthurian lore, the Welsh town of Carmarthen is said to be the birthplace of Merlin Ambrosius, King Arthur's wizardly sidekick. The town has some associations with him, including a pedunculate oak tree known as Merlin's Oak. Allegedly planted in 1659 or 1660 by a local schoolmaster to celebrate the Restoration of Charles II, Merlin's Oak stood on the corner of Oak Lane and Priory Street.

Local tradition held that "when Merlin's Oak shall tumble down, then so will fall Carmarthen Town." It was also said that Merlin himself once prophesized that the tree's downfall would cause the town to drown. Some even claimed that a pointed notch in the oak tree was a manifestation of the wizard's face.

In the early 19th century, Merlin's Oak was poisoned by a local man who objected to people holding meetings beneath it, and its trunk was shored up within iron railings and concrete. Later on, someone set fire to the tree and it was removed from Carmarthen in 1978, also due to its need for a roadway. Curiously, the town suffered its worst floods for many years afterward... but survived, somehow.

#location
The oak stood at the junction of Oak Lane and Priory Street in Carmarthen

#title
The Phantom Men of Carmarthen

#summary
A truck driver encounters three ghostly men on the A40 road between Carmarthen and Llangathen, one of whom vanishes under his vehicle.

#details
In 1981, a heavy goods vehicle driver was traveling along the A40 between Carmarthen and Llangathen when he noticed a pickup truck parked by the side of the road. Three men were standing nearby, seemingly in conversation. As he approached, one of the men was pushed onto the road and fell under the cab of his truck. The driver expected to hear a bump or a cry but heard nothing. When he looked in his rearview mirror, both the truck and the men had vanished.

#location
The A40 road between Carmarthen and Llangathen is one of the key locations of this story.

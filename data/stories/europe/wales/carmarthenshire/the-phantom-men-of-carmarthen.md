#title
The Phantom Men of Carmarthen

#summary
A truck driver encounters three ghostly men on the A40 road between Carmarthen and Llangathen, one of whom vanishes under his vehicle.

#details
In 1981, a heavy goods vehicle driver was traveling along the A40 between Carmarthen and Llangathen when he noticed a pickup truck parked by the side of the road. Three men were standing nearby, seemingly in conversation. As he approached, one of the men was pushed onto the road and fell under the cab of his truck. The driver expected to hear a bump or a cry but heard nothing. When he looked in his rearview mirror, both the truck and the men had vanished.

#location
The A40 road between Carmarthen and Llangathen is one of the key locations of this story.

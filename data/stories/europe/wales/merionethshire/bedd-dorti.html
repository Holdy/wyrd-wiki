    Bedd Dorti

    Summary

    A 17th-century woman executed for witchcraft was buried beneath a cairn on a mountain pass, where a lingering curse dictates that anyone passing without adding a stone will die within a year.

    Details

    In the seventeenth century, a woman named Dorti who was widely feared as a local witch—frequently seen with a black cat perched on her shoulder—incurred the wrath of her neighbours. Seized by the local people, she was sealed inside a wooden cask and hurled from the cliffs above Llyn Tecwyn Uchaf. The spot where her barrel came to rest became her grave, marked by a heap of stones known as Bedd Dorti on the old road between Llandecwyn and Maentwrog. Dorti's malice survived her execution, manifesting as a persistent curse upon the route: travellers must observe the custom of throwing a stone onto her cairn when passing by, as failing to pay this tribute guarantees death within the year.

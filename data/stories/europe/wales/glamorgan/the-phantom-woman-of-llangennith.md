#title
The Phantom Woman of Llangennith

#summary
A phantom woman dressed in white is frequently seen on the main street of Llangennith, Gower.

#details
In Llangennith, a small village in the Gower Peninsula, South Wales, locals and visitors alike have reported sightings of a spectral figure—a woman dressed entirely in white. This ghostly apparition has been spotted numerous times on the main street, adding to the eerie atmosphere of this otherwise picturesque location.

#location
The main street of Llangennith is where the phantom woman is frequently seen.

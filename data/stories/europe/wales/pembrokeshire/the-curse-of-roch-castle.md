#title
The Curse of Roch Castle

#summary
A local witch cursed the Norman knight Adam de la Roche to die from an adder bite within a year, a prophecy that ultimately came true on the very final day of the curse when a hibernating snake was inadvertently brought into his secure tower room.

#details
Adam de la Roche, a powerful Norman ruler, built Roch Castle high upon a rocky crag overlooking St Brides Bay to protect himself from his enemies. However, after he deeply offended a local woman known to be a witch, she laid a curse upon him, prophesying that he would die from the bite of an adder within a year. Terrified by the prediction, Adam spent the rest of the year in seclusion, sealing himself inside the topmost room of the castle tower while his servants brought him everything he needed. As the year drew to a close and only one day remained, Adam mocked the warning, believing he had outsmarted the curse. That freezing evening, he ordered a servant to bring up a bundle of logs from the wood store to build a warm fire. Unbeknownst to them, an adder had been hibernating inside the wood and awoke from its slumber in the heat of the hearth, fatally biting Adam in his bed before the year could end.

#location
Roch Castle stands high on a rocky crag in the village of Roch, overlooking St Brides Bay in Pembrokeshire, Wales.

#title
The Devil in a Snuffbox

#summary
The mischievous ghost of a dishonest tanner terrorized local riders until a clever vicar exorcised the spirit, shrank it into a bluebottle, and trapped it inside a sealed snuffbox buried beneath a bog.

#details
Following his accidental death, a widely disliked tanner named Charles Lewis—infamous in life for using deceitful weights—returned as a malignant ghost. After sunset, he haunted the deserted lanes of Disserth, leaping up behind horseback riders to frighten them while proclaiming himself a devil. 

When local clergy attempted to banish the spirit to St Cewydd’s Church, the ghost blew out the candles of the guest ministers, but the vicar, Rev Jones, kept his candle hidden safely in his boot. Armed with relentless Bible reading, the vicar forced the ghost to shrink down until it transformed into a bluebottle fly. Rev Jones trapped the fly inside a goose quill, sealed it within a snuffbox, and buried it deep in a local bog beneath an iron stake to keep the curse from returning.

#location
St Cewydd’s Church is located in Disserth, near Newbridge-on-Wye in Powys (formerly Radnorshire).

#title
The Ghostly Figure on the A470

#summary
A driver and passenger reported seeing a ghostly figure with an oval, featureless head by the side of the road near Llyswen.

#details
In January 2008, a driver and their passenger were traveling north on the A470 near Llyswen when they spotted what appeared to be an older gentleman standing by the side of the road. The figure was described as being around six feet tall, wearing a long grey coat. However, the head was oval in shape with no features and gave off a faint glow. Immediately after passing the entity, the driver felt hot and shivery, and this sensation lasted for about a minute.

#location
The A470 near Llyswen is one of the key locations mentioned in the story.

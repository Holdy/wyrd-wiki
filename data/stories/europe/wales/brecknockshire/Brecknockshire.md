#title
The Curse of Craig-y-Nos

#summary
When Captain Rice Davies Powell built his grand country estate in Brecknockshire, unpaid debts sparked a devastating family curse. Over successive generations, the hex brought financial ruin, disease, and untimely deaths to all who inhabited the house.

#details
In 1842, Captain Rice Davies Powell commissioned the construction of a magnificent Gothic country home along the River Tawe in Brecknockshire. However, due to excessive construction costs and mounting debts, legend states that an aggrieved creditor placed a severe curse upon the captain and his descendants. The hex manifested immediately: in 1851, the captain's youngest son succumbed to cholera, followed shortly by his youngest daughter and his wife, before the captain himself died in 1862. The curse persisted when his eldest son died in a hunting accident on the Isle of Wight in 1864, and his surviving daughter's husband also suffered an early, unexpected death shortly after moving into the estate.

#location
Craig-y-Nos Castle stands beside the River Tawe in the upper Swansea Valley within the Brecon Beacons National Park in Powys (historically Brecknockshire), Wales.

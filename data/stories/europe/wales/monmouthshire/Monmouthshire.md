#title
The Curse of St Gwynllyw's Church

#summary
Before his death, the converted king St Gwynllyw placed a protective curse on his sanctuary to punish any who dared desecrate or steal from it. When pirates later plundered the holy site, his vengeful spirit manifested to reclaim the sacred relics amidst a violent storm.

#details
St Gwynllyw, a former warrior king who repented and founded a church on a hill following a divine vision of a white ox, established a mystical safeguard before his passing. He placed a curse upon the holy site to deter thieves and despoilers. Around a year after his death, a band of pirates broke in and stole precious treasures from the church. As they attempted to escape down the Bristol Channel, Gwynllyw's spirit appeared to take back the sacred objects, whereupon their ship was struck by a fierce storm.

#location
St Woolos Cathedral (St Gwynllyw's Church) is located on Stow Hill in Newport, South Wales.

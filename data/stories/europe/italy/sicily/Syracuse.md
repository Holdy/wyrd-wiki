#title
Madonna delle Lacrime (Our Lady of Tears)

#summary
A mass-produced plaster plaque of Mary above a couple's bed wept for four days in August-September 1953. Church chemists said the liquid matched human tears. The plaque is now in a huge modern sanctuary built for it.

#location
Sanctuary of the Madonna delle Lacrime, Syracuse, Sicily (statue first wept in a house on Via degli Orti di San Giorgio), Italy

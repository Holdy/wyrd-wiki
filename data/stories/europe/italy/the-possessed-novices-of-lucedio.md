#title
The Possessed Novices of Lucedio

#summary
During a medieval gathering of witches, the Devil possessed the young novices of a nearby convent and used them to corrupt the monks of the Principato di Lucedio into a century of dark rituals.

#details
According to local Italian folklore, the Prince of Darkness materialized during a witch's sabbath at the Darola cemetery near Vercelli. Eager to take over the peaceful Cistercian abbey of Santa Maria di Lucedio, the entity possessed the young novices of a convent in Trino Vercellese. These possessed novices infiltrated the monastery and seduced the monks, plunging the sacred foundation into a century of perdition, torture, and black magic. The demonic reign only ended centuries later, and legend maintains that the demonic presence was ultimately captured and sealed in a secret crypt beneath the abbey church, guarded eternally by four pious monks whose mummified bodies remain seated on chairs inside.

#location
The Principato di Lucedio is located in the Piedmont region of northern Italy, situated in the countryside north of Vercelli.

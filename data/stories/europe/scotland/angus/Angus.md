#title
The Ghost of Jock's Road

#summary
Jock's Road in Angus is periodically haunted by an anonymous figure dressed in winter clothes.

#details
Jock's Road in Angus has a reputation for being haunted. In 1993, a mysterious figure dressed in winter clothing was seen on the road. The identity and purpose of this apparition remain unknown, adding to the eerie atmosphere of the area.

#location
Jock's Road can be found on a map as it is located near Arbroath in Angus.

    The Stolen Hearth-Stone of Auchquhorthies

    Summary

    A poor man who took a stone from the ancient circle at Auchquhorthies to use as his fireplace hearth found himself nightly tormented by a terrible noise and din until he carried the stone back.

    Details

    A local man living near Auchquhorthies removed one of the stones from the prehistoric recumbent stone circle to use as a hearth-stone inside his home. However, he soon discovered that breaking the taboo against disturbing the ancient site brought immediate retribution. Every single night, a relentless and terrifying noise and din tormented him and his household, giving them no rest. Believing the haunting to be a direct consequence of his sacrilege, the frightened man finally returned the stone to its rightful place at the stone circle, whereupon the nightly disturbances ceased.

#title
Forvie's Curse

#summary
Three sisters cursed their village after being cast adrift in a leaky boat, leading to its eventual burial by sand.

#details
Near Rockend, the remnants of a medieval settlement can be found, including the ruins of Forvie’s 12th-century kirk. According to local legend, the village met its end due to a curse placed by three sisters who were denied their inheritance and cast adrift in a leaky boat. In their rage, they cursed the land, saying, “Let nocht bee funde in Furvye’s glebes/Bot thystl, bente and sande.” A storm ensued for nine days and nights, burying the village under sand.

#location
The ruins of Forvie's 12th-century kirk can be found near Rockend, Aberdeenshire.

The Veiled Lady of Nun's Walk

Summary

Along the historic lane of Nun's Walk in St Andrews, the ghostly figure of a woman known as the Veiled Lady is said to eternally wander in sorrowful penance. Her spectral procession remains a haunting fixture of the ancient pathway after dark.

Details

In the ancient coastal town of St Andrews in Fife, hidden pathways and medieval lanes carry centuries of dark folklore. Among them is Nun's Walk, a secluded lane where a sorrowful apparition known as the Veiled Lady wanders. According to local tradition, her ghostly form moves silently along the path, her countenance covered by a ghastly veil she crafted herself as an eternal penance. Alongside other eerie local legends tied to the town's historic passages, her haunting presence continues to stir the leaves and chill the air of the old Fife lane.

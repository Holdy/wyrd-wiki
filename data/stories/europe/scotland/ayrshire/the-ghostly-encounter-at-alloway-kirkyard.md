#title
The Ghostly Encounter at Alloway Kirkyard

#summary
Two American tourists fled after encountering a dark mist that created eerie sounds near the ruined kirk.

#details
Alloway Kirkyard is reputed to be haunted by several spirits. Two American tourists had a particularly unsettling experience when they encountered a dark mist that drifted towards the ruined kirk. The mist produced an eerie sound like a barrel being rolled along the road, causing the tourists to flee the area. Other reported ghosts include a woman who committed suicide, a tramp found dead on the site, and a murdered child.

#location
Alloway Kirkyard is located in Alloway, South Ayrshire, Scotland.

#title
The Harperrig Reservoir 'Missing Time' Encounter

#summary
Two men reported experiencing missing time after encountering strange lights or UFOs at night near Harperrig Reservoir.

#details
In 1992, two men had an unusual experience while driving on the A70 road near Harperrig Reservoir in West Lothian. They encountered strange lights or UFOs and subsequently experienced puzzling memory gaps, often referred to as 'missing time.' This incident highlights one of the many bizarre and unexplained phenomena that can occur on our roads.

#location
The A70 road near Harperrig Reservoir is a key location for this story.

#title
The Phantom Hound of Hound Point

#summary
Following the disappearance of a crusader lord in the Holy Land, his faithful dog's haunting howls and spectral companion portend a curse of doom for any future lords of Barnbougle.

#details
When Sir Roger de Mowbray left his ancestral home in West Lothian to join the Crusades, his devoted hound chased after him, eventually meeting its end searching for him in foreign lands. The legend recounts that the faithful dog's spirit returned to the headland where they last parted ways, known thereafter as Hound Point. On dark winter nights, the ghostly howls of the hound can still be heard sweeping across the water. On rare and ominous occasions, the spectral beast is accompanied by the phantom figure of a Saracen soldier—a dire premonition that spells certain death for the Lords of Barnbougle.

#location
Hound Point is a headland extending into the Firth of Forth on the Dalmeny Estate near South Queensferry in West Lothian.

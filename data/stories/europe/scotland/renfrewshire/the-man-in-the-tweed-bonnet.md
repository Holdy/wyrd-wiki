#title
The Man in the Tweed Bonnet

#summary
A man in a tweed bonnet with an eerily featureless face is seen standing by the road on the A75, vanishing as quickly as he appears.

#details
In 1998, witnesses reported seeing a spectral figure of a man wearing a tweed bonnet. The man's face was eerily featureless and he stood by the side of the road before vanishing into thin air. This ghostly apparition has been described as a gamekeeper from a bygone era.

#location
The A75 is the location where this ghostly figure was seen.

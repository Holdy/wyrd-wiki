#title
The White Lady of Overtoun

#summary
Overtoun Bridge is haunted by the White Lady, the apparition of the grieving widow of former estate owner John White.

#details
The stone bridge situated on the approach road to Overtoun House in West Dunbartonshire is reputed to be haunted by the White Lady of Overtoun. Believed to be the spirit of the grieving widow of the estate's former owner, John White, the ghostly figure is said to wander the bridge and surrounding woodland paths. In addition to sightings of the apparition, the location has gained widespread notoriety for unexplained phenomena and local legends.

#location
Overtoun Bridge is located on the estate road leading to Overtoun House just north of Dumbarton in West Dunbartonshire.

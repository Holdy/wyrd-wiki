#title
The Curse of the Aspen Tree

#summary
The aspen tree in Banffshire is believed to be cursed, causing its leaves to tremble constantly.

#details
In parts of Banffshire, the aspen tree is known as "quackin' aish," or quaking ash. This name comes from a belief that the tree is cursed because it was used to make the cross on which Jesus Christ was crucified. As a result of this curse, the leaves of the aspen tree are said to be in constant motion.

#location
The story refers to parts of Banffshire where the aspen tree can be found.

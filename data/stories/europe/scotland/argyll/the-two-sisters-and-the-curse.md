#title
The Two Sisters and the Curse

#summary
A tale of two sisters, one cursed for revealing her sister's secret fairy lover, leading to a tragic chain of events.

#details
Rebecca Brown recounts an encounter with a mysterious woman on Mull who was singing a mournful tune. The woman was later identified as Lovely Mairearad, the elder sister of Limpet Ailsa. The story reveals that Lovely Mairearad had been cursed by her fairy lover after Limpet revealed their secret to the villagers. As a result, Lovely Mairearad became a wanderer, singing a curse on her sister and any descendants.

Years later, Torquil, Limpet's son, was challenged by a mysterious reaper known as the Maiden of the Cairn. Despite his efforts, he could not keep up with her, and eventually fell dead in the field after she uttered a final curse. The story ends with a reminder that fairies should not be messed with, but it is ultimately human actions that bring about curses.

#location
The key location of this story is Lochbuie, Mull where the two sisters lived.

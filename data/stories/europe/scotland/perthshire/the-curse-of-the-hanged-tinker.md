#title
The Curse of the Hanged Tinker

#summary
A condemned tinker pronounced a devastating prophecy against the Spalding Barons of Ashintully Castle, warning that their family line would soon come to a permanent end. True to his dying words, the curse was fulfilled shortly afterward when the bloodline failed.

#details
Ashintully Castle in Perthshire was built as a fortified tower house in 1583 by the Spalding family, the Feudal Barons of Ashintully. Known for their severe rule, one of the Spalding Barons caught a tinker trespassing on the castle's demesne and summarily ordered him to be hanged. As he stood facing execution near an avenue of tall trees on the estate, the desperate and condemned tinker placed a fierce curse upon his captors. He prophesied that the Spalding family line would soon die out completely and disappear from the lands. This ominous prediction came to pass shortly thereafter, bringing an end to the family's reign at the castle, and the spectral tinker is said to still haunt the tree-lined spot where he met his end.

#location
Ashintully Castle, situated near the village of Kirkmichael, north of Blairgowrie in Perthshire.

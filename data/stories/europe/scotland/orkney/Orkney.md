#title
The Ghostly Woman of Newhouse

#summary
A woman dressed in black with a large hat is seen by two girls and later by their cousins on the same stretch of road near Newhouse, Dounby.

#details
Johina Leith, nee Kirkness, recalled an eerie encounter when she was about nine years old. She and her sister Mary were playing near their family home in Newhouse, Dounby. Suddenly, they saw a woman dressed in black with a large hat adorned with flowers. The woman crossed the road into a field and vanished as abruptly as she had appeared. Years later, Johina's cousins, Charlotte Clouston and Maggie Kirkness, had a similar experience on the same stretch of road. Maggie realized she had forgotten her gloves and ran back to the house. When she came out, she saw a figure ahead and called out, but there was no reply, and the figure vanished. Decades later, this story was shared with a school friend of Johina's daughter Stephanie, whose grandfather had a similar encounter while driving along the same road.

#location
The stretch of road near Newhouse, Dounby, where the ghostly woman was seen.

#title
The Spitting Witch and the Curses of Rendall

#summary
An elderly Orkney woman accused of witchcraft inflicts madness upon a doubting farmer and a laird's horse before facing her execution with a final prophetic promise. 

#details
Living at the isolated cottage of Rigga on the shores of Wass Wick in Rendall, Jonet Rendall cultivated a reputation as a cunning woman who consulted on personal troubles to earn her keep. When a local farmer named Andrew accused her of stealing the profit of his cow's milk, Jonet warned he would regret it, resulting in Andrew falling violently mad three days later until Jonet arrived to cure him with a triple spit over her shoulder. Later, when the wealthy laird Gilbert Sandie refused her silver, his prize horse was struck with madness ("wood"). Eventually arrested and brought to trial in 1629, Jonet met her fate at Gallow Ha in Kirkwall with serene calm, leaving the laird with a parting prophecy that her little friend from beyond would soon visit him too.

#location
Rigga was an old cottage situated on the shore at the western end of the Bay of Wass Wick in the parish of Rendall, Orkney.

#title
The Curse of Lady Margaret Sinclair

#summary
Following the devastating loss of Caithness clansmen at the Battle of Flodden, Lady Margaret Sinclair became bound by an enduring curse of eternal grief. Her unyielding sorrow transformed her into a spirit guard, haunting the castle ruins and mist-laden peatbogs of the far north.

#details
In 1513, three hundred men from Caithness marched south under the banner of William Sinclair, 2nd Earl of Caithness, to participate in the fateful Battle of Flodden, where King James IV and most of the Scottish nobility were slain. Only a single soldier boy returned to tell the tale. Left behind at Castle Sinclair-Girnigoe, the Earl's wife, Lady Margaret Sinclair, was consumed by overwhelming sorrow. Over the centuries, her tragic grief hardened into an enduring legend and curse, binding her spirit as an eternal guard to the crumbling fortress and surrounding myres to lament the ravages of human conflict and environmental destruction.

#location
Castle Sinclair-Girnigoe is located on the rugged North Sea coastline just north of Wick in Caithness, Scotland.

#title
The Green Lady of Castle Mey

#summary
The story tells of a young woman who fell in love with an unsuitable partner. Her father forbade the relationship and she was confined to the top floors of the castle, where she eventually died.

#details
The tale begins with a young woman named Elizabeth Sinclair, daughter of the 5th Earl of Caithness, who fell deeply in love with a local man deemed unsuitable by her father. Despite her feelings, her father forbade their relationship and confined her to the upper floors of Castle Mey. The circumstances surrounding her death are unclear; some say she died from heartbreak or an accident, while others suggest suicide.

Her spirit is said to still wander the castle, waiting for her lost love. Visitors have reported feeling a chilling presence, witnessing doors slamming shut, and lights turning off unexpectedly. This haunting presence has become a part of Castle Mey's lore, drawing those interested in the paranormal to explore its eerie halls.

#location
Castle Mey is located near Thurso in Caithness.

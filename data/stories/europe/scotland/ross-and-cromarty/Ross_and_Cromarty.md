#title
The Ghost of Hugh Ross

#summary
The ghost of a wealthy landowner is said to haunt his former room, throwing legal papers around in anger.

#details
Hugh Ross was a wealthy man who had made his fortune through the use of slaves in the West Indies. He purchased many estates in his native county but was not popular due to his wealth and progressive nature. Before his death, he was engaged in a bitter lawsuit and died amid his legal papers. After his death, no one would venture near his room for a time because bundles of legal papers were being tossed about the room by an invisible hand. Many credible witnesses declared that the papers were thrown about as if by an angry man, furious at having mislaid something. It was later discovered that an important deed on which Ross relied could not be found.

#location
The former room of Hugh Ross in Cromarty House

#title
The Lady of Balconie

#summary
The wicked lady of Balconie Castle was condemned by a chained subterranean curse to an iron chair in a dark ravine for a period of ten years. Her disappearance and eventual doom are tied to the deep geological chasm of the Auldgrande river.

#details
About two centuries ago, the proprietor of Balconie Castle in Ross and Cromarty was married to a reserved lady who spent much of her time taking solitary walks along the banks of the Auldgrande. One evening, she dragged her terrified maid down to the precipitous edge of the deep river chasm, where a dark-looking green man appeared and offered to act as a willing substitute guide. Before disappearing into the gorge with the stranger, the lady flung her bunch of household keys up the bank, where they struck a huge granitic boulder and sank into it as if it were melted wax, leaving an impression still visible to visitors. Ten years later, a bold Highlander named Donald tracked a stolen fish along a hidden path down into a cavern within the chasm. There he found the lady chained by the ankle to a corroded iron chair fixed to the solid rock, guarded by two giant dogs. Though she gave him bread to distract the beasts and let him escape, she declared she could never leave. No one has seen her since, and her fate remains bound to the gloomy gorge.

#location
The deep chasm and banks of the River Auldgrande in Kiltearn parish, Ross and Cromarty, Scotland.

#title
The Curse of the Bukumiri

#summary
When villagers boiled their lake to kill a troublesome dragon, a mysterious rider emerged from the water and cursed the Bukumiri tribe to destroy one another. In time, the horrific curse fulfilled itself, leaving the tribe completely extinct and surrounded only by barren stones.

#details
In ancient times, the nomadic Bukumiri tribe inhabited the shores of a high mountain lake in the Kučka region. A malicious dragon living within the lake tormented the locals, prompting them to take action. They gathered wood on a rocky cliff above the water, set it ablaze until the stone glowed white-hot, and pushed it into the lake, causing the water to boil violently. Startled and enraged, a mysterious man mounted on a white horse leaped out of the boiling depths and placed a curse upon the Bukumiri, dooming them to fight and mutually exterminate one another. Slowly and inevitably, the curse took effect as the tribe turned on itself in bitter strife until every last member perished, leaving behind only desolate stones—known as kiljans—marking the site of their extinction.

#location
Bukumir Lake (Bukumirsko Jezero) is situated at the foot of the Kučka Mountains, roughly fifty kilometers north of Podgorica, Montenegro.

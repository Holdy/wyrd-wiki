The Wounded Soul of Ceklin

Summary

When a Ceklin zduhać was mortally wounded during a nocturnal spirit battle in the clouds, his brother traveled to a cliff beneath Mount Rumija to intervene in a supernatural duel between spirit bulls and save his life.

Details

In 19th-century Montenegrin folklore, zduhaći were men whose souls could leave their bodies during sleep to ride the winds and defend their region against hostile weather spirits and rival zduhaći. A zduhać belonging to the Ceklin clan in southern Montenegro was beaten so severely in a spiritual clash that he lay dying in his home, refusing all ordinary remedies. He informed his brother that his soul was losing a battle in the skies and instructed him to walk to a stair-like cliff beneath Mount Rumija. Armed with a silver-sheathed knife, the brother traveled to the desolate cliff at night. As a ferocious, roaring storm cloud swept in from the mountain, three bulls tumbled out of the sky onto the earth—two dark and hostile bulls attacking a weaker, light-haired bull representing the dying zduhać. Following the exact instructions, the brother stabbed and defeated the two hostile bulls with his knife, allowing the light-haired bull to prevail. Immediately, the storm vanished into pristine silence, and when the brother hurried back home, he found the zduhać sitting by the hearth, completely healed.

#title
The Curse of the Bukumiri

#summary
When villagers boiled their lake to kill a troublesome dragon, a mysterious rider emerged from the water and cursed the Bukumiri tribe to destroy one another. In time, the horrific curse fulfilled itself, leaving the tribe completely extinct and surrounded only by barren stones.

#details
In ancient times, the nomadic Bukumiri tribe inhabited the shores of a high mountain lake in the Kučka region. A malicious dragon living within the lake tormented the locals, prompting them to take action. They gathered wood on a rocky cliff above the water, set it ablaze until the stone glowed white-hot, and pushed it into the lake, causing the water to boil violently. Startled and enraged, a mysterious man mounted on a white horse leaped out of the boiling depths and placed a curse upon the Bukumiri, dooming them to fight and mutually exterminate one another. Slowly and inevitably, the curse took effect as the tribe turned on itself in bitter strife until every last member perished, leaving behind only desolate stones—known as kiljans—marking the site of their extinction.

#location
Bukumir Lake (Bukumirsko Jezero) is situated at the foot of the Kučka Mountains, roughly fifty kilometers north of Podgorica, Montenegro.

#title
The Wounded Soul of Ceklin

#summary
When a Ceklin zduhać was mortally wounded during a nocturnal spirit battle in the clouds, his brother traveled to a cliff beneath Mount Rumija to intervene in a supernatural duel between spirit bulls and save his life.

#details
In 19th-century Montenegrin folklore, zduhaći were men whose souls could leave their bodies during sleep to ride the winds and defend their region against hostile weather spirits and rival zduhaći. A zduhać belonging to the Ceklin clan in southern Montenegro was beaten so severely in a spiritual clash that he lay dying in his home, refusing all ordinary remedies. He informed his brother that his soul was losing a battle in the skies and instructed him to walk to a stair-like cliff beneath Mount Rumija. Armed with a silver-sheathed knife, the brother traveled to the desolate cliff at night. As a ferocious, roaring storm cloud swept in from the mountain, three bulls tumbled out of the sky onto the earth—two dark and hostile bulls attacking a weaker, light-haired bull representing the dying zduhać. Following the exact instructions, the brother stabbed and defeated the two hostile bulls with his knife, allowing the light-haired bull to prevail. Immediately, the storm vanished into pristine silence, and when the brother hurried back home, he found the zduhać sitting by the hearth, completely healed.

#location
The key location is the stair-like cliff situated beneath Mount Rumija in southern Montenegro.

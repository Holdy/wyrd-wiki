#title
The Tragic Burning of Arminda de Jesus

#summary
In February 1933, superstitious villagers in the hamlet of Oliveira, Soalhães, accused a 32-year-old woman named Arminda de Jesus of being possessed by the devil and burned her alive to purge the perceived demons.

#details
In the winter of 1933, within the remote hamlet of Oliveira in the parish of Soalhães, Portugal, a horrific tragedy rooted in religious superstition and mass hysteria took place. A 32-year-old egg vendor named Arminda de Jesus was targeted by local villagers who became convinced that she was possessed by the devil. Swept up in fervent belief and manipulated by fanaticism, four men dragged Arminda between two stone houses in the hamlet and threw her into a massive bonfire, believing they were casting out evil spirits. The brutal murder shocked the nation, later inspiring Bernardo Santareno’s famous 1959 play *O Crime da Aldeia Velha* and remaining deeply embedded in local folklore as the ultimate dark legend of the region.

#location
The events took place in the hamlet of Oliveira, within the civil parish of Soalhães in the municipality of Marco de Canaveses, Portugal.

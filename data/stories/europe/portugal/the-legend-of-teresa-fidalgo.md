#title
The Legend of Teresa Fidalgo

#summary
A young female hitchhiker picked up by motorists on a remote mountain road warns of her fatal accident before vanishing into thin air.

#details
Motorists driving along the dark, forested roads of the Sintra mountains in Portugal frequently encounter a pale young woman standing by the roadside seeking a ride. After getting into the vehicle, she behaves with growing anxiety and unease. As they approach a specific curve ahead, she points forward and reveals that she died there in a tragic car crash years prior, before screaming in terror and disappearing from the backseat.

#location
The events take place along Estrada do Vale dos Anjos in Colares, situated within the Sintra mountain range in Portugal.

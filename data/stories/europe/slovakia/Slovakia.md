#title
The Seven Cursed Monks of the Dunajec River

#summary
Seven monks who broke their ascetic vows by gathering forbidden herbs were petrified into stone pillars along the banks of the Dunajec River. They are bound by an eternal curse that can only be lifted if an impossible number of virgins cross the bridge before them.

#details
In the Pieniny Mountains of northern Slovakia, seven monks from the Red Cloister ventured into the forbidden forest to collect plants. According to local folklore, their transgression against strict monastic rules resulted in divine punishment, transforming them into stone figures frozen along the course of the Dunajec River. The curse locks them in perpetual petrifaction unless 11,000 virgins cross the bridge standing in front of them, an impossible condition ensuring their eternal confinement.

#location
The Red Cloister (Červený Kláštor) is a historic monastery complex located in the St. Anton Valley along the Dunajec River in northern Slovakia, near the village of Lechnica.

#title
The Curse of the Moved Stećak Tombstone

#summary
Local villagers near Jajce suffered a devastating loss of their livestock after incorporating ancient stećak tombstones into their barns. The misfortune only ceased when the displaced stones were returned to their original necropolis.

#details
In the settlement of Polipci near Jajce, a severe curse befell the local residents when they disregarded traditional taboos surrounding medieval monolithic tombstones, known as *stećci*. Seeking building materials for their farm structures, some locals moved and incorporated these sacred tombstones directly into the walls of their barns. Immediately following this desecration, a delayed and relentless plague struck their livestock, causing cattle to sicken and die one by one. Convinced that the calamity was retribution for disturbing the ancient graves, the villagers finally reversed their actions and hauled the tombstones back to the necropolis, whereupon the mortality among their animals ceased.

#location
The stećak necropolis near Polipci, on the outskirts of Jajce in central Bosnia and Herzegovina.

#title
The Fatal Kiss of Boughton Green

#summary
An unmarried person who accepts a kiss from a phantom stranger outside a ruined church on Christmas Eve is doomed to die precisely one month later. When a young farmer ignored the warning and accepted a rendezvous, he perished on the exact day predicted.

#details
According to local folklore attached to a ruined church, a tragic young bride took her own life next to her newlywed husband’s grave in the churchyard. Over time, her spirit became a deadly portent: any unmarried person passing the church on Christmas Eve would be approached by an attractive apparition offering a kiss. Accepting this embrace bound the victim to a fatal deadline—they would die within the month. On December 24, 1875, a young farmer named William Parker walked past the site, encountered a striking red-haired woman sitting on the graveyard wall, and agreed to meet her there in exactly one month after exchanging a kiss. Despite his family's horror when he recounted the tale, Parker died on January 24, 1876, fulfilling the grim curse.

#location
The ruins of St. John's Church stand just north of Northampton, mid-way between Boughton and Moulton.

#title
The Phantom Monk of Barford Bridge

#summary
The ghost of a monk is said to haunt a road near Barford Bridge, Northamptonshire, often appearing in the passenger seat of cars or stepping out onto the road, causing accidents and frightening drivers.

#details
The phantom monk is believed to have once served at a chapel in the now-deserted village of Barford, which was near the Cistercian Abbey at Pipewell. Drivers on the single-track road from the dual carriageway to Geddington have reported seeing the ghost sitting in their empty passenger seat, only for him to vanish further along the road. The monk has also been known to step out onto the road, causing accidents and leading drivers to stop, thinking they have hit someone, but finding no body.

#location
The key location is Barford Bridge on the A6003 between Kettering and Corby in Northamptonshire.

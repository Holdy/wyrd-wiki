#title
Browsholme Hall skull

#summary
A skull in a cabinet at Browsholme Hall is thought to belong to a martyr of the 1536 Pilgrimage of Grace. Legend says dire things will happen if it ever leaves the house.

#details
Browsholme Hall, a Grade I listed house dating from 1507, keeps a skull in a cabinet. It is thought to be that of a martyr of the Pilgrimage of Grace, the 1536 rising against Henry VIII's break with the Catholic Church, and a 2010 Lancashire Life article said it belonged to a lady who was one of the martyrs. Legend says dire things will happen if the head ever leaves Browsholme. No attempt to move it is recorded, and the current head of the household, Robert Parker, dismisses the ghostly talk.

#location
Browsholme Hall, Forest of Bowland, Lancashire, England

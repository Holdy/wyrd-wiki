Phantom Miners of Platt Lane

Summary

Drivers on Platt Lane near Westhoughton report encountering ghostly miners hauling carts and glowing eyes in the hedges, linked to the 1910 Pretoria Pit disaster that claimed 344 lives.

Details

Platt Lane, a quiet rural road near Westhoughton in Lancashire, has been tied to the tragic Pretoria Pit mining disaster of 1910, where 344 miners lost their lives in an explosion. Locals and drivers describe eerie encounters at night: shadowy figures of miners hauling carts through the hedges, glowing eyes peering from roadside thickets, and an overwhelming sense of being accompanied by unseen workers. These sightings, reportedly dating back over a century, have cemented Platt Lane as one of the UK's most haunted roads, with some residents avoiding the stretch after dark due to the persistent feeling of being watched by the spirits of those who perished.

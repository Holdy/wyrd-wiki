#title
Turton Tower skulls

#summary
Two skulls are stored at Turton Tower, and when they were kept elsewhere two ghostly men were said to appear and fight. The tower is also said to have poltergeist activity, a woman in mourning on the staircase and a phantom coach outside.

#details
Turton Tower keeps a pair of skulls, said to be the ones brought from Timberbottom Farm. When the skulls were stored somewhere else, two phantom men were reported appearing and fighting each other. The tower has other ghosts as well: poltergeist activity, a woman dressed in mourning on the staircase and a phantom coach outside.

#location
Turton Tower, Chapeltown, Lancashire, England

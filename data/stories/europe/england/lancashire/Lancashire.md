#title
Phantom Miners of Platt Lane

#summary
Drivers on Platt Lane near Westhoughton report encountering ghostly miners hauling carts and glowing eyes in the hedges, linked to the 1910 Pretoria Pit disaster that claimed 344 lives.

#details
Platt Lane, a quiet rural road near Westhoughton in Lancashire, has been tied to the tragic Pretoria Pit mining disaster of 1910, where 344 miners lost their lives in an explosion. Locals and drivers describe eerie encounters at night: shadowy figures of miners hauling carts through the hedges, glowing eyes peering from roadside thickets, and an overwhelming sense of being accompanied by unseen workers. These sightings, reportedly dating back over a century, have cemented Platt Lane as one of the UK's most haunted roads, with some residents avoiding the stretch after dark due to the persistent feeling of being watched by the spirits of those who perished.

#location
Platt Lane near Westhoughton, Lancashire, stretching between the village and the site of the Pretoria Pit disaster.

#title
The Idiot's Curse

#summary
An imprisoned heir starved to death by his ambitious brother placed a generational curse upon the Barcroft family, dooming their name to perish and their estate to be lost.

#details
The Barcrofts were a long-standing and respected Lancashire family who owned Barcroft Hall. According to tradition, one of the heirs to the estate was an imbecile or idiot. Seeking to usurp the property, his younger brother chained the unfortunate heir inside the hall's ample cellars and left him to starve to death while secretly reporting him dead. During a lucid interval before his death, the captive prisoner carved rude scribblings onto the cellar walls and pronounced a dire curse upon the Barcroft family: that their family name would perish forever and the ancestral property would pass away from them. True to the prophecy, the direct male line ended with Thomas Barcroft in 1688 without male issue, and the historic estate eventually passed out of the family's hands entirely.

#location
Barcroft Hall is situated approximately one mile from Towneley in Lancashire.

#title
The Idiot's Curse

#summary
An imprisoned heir starved to death by his ambitious brother placed a generational curse upon the Barcroft family, dooming their name to perish and their estate to be lost.

#details
The Barcrofts were a long-standing and respected Lancashire family who owned Barcroft Hall. According to tradition, one of the heirs to the estate was an imbecile or idiot. Seeking to usurp the property, his younger brother chained the unfortunate heir inside the hall's ample cellars and left him to starve to death while secretly reporting him dead. During a lucid interval before his death, the captive prisoner carved rude scribblings onto the cellar walls and pronounced a dire curse upon the Barcroft family: that their family name would perish forever and the ancestral property would pass away from them. True to the prophecy, the direct male line ended with Thomas Barcroft in 1688 without male issue, and the historic estate eventually passed out of the family's hands entirely.

#location
Barcroft Hall is situated approximately one mile from Towneley in Lancashire.

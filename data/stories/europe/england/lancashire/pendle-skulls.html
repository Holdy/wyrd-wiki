    Pendle skulls

    Summary

    In 1612 Anne Chattox, said to lead a group of Lancashire witches, was accused of digging up three skulls from a Catholic cemetery for use in demonic rituals. The skulls were reportedly found at her home and were said to howl once disturbed, and she was hanged for the crime.

    Details

    Anne Chattox was described as the leader of a group of witches in Lancashire. At her trial in 1612 she was accused of taking three skulls from a Catholic cemetery to use in demonic rituals. Three skulls were reportedly found in her home, and they were said to howl after being disturbed. She was hanged for the crime.

#title
The Knight of Smithdown Lane

#summary
A man encountered a spectral knight in armor on Smithdown Lane during a dense fog in 1987, later realizing he had witnessed a timeslip to medieval times.

#details
On Christmas Eve 1987, Patrick walked along Smithdown Lane in foggy Edge Hill, heading home to his mother's house. As he entered a thick fog, he saw a knight in armor on horseback demanding his identity. Terrified, Patrick fled and recited the Lord's Prayer, but the knight's hooves faded as he encountered a local named Johnno, who heard the approaching horses. The incident occurred on Smithdown Lane, Liverpool's oldest road, which historically connected to Toxteth Forest and the manor of Esmedune.

#location
Smithdown Lane, Edge Hill, Liverpool (specifically near the junction with Lodge Lane)

#title
The Curse of the Mexborough Ragger

#summary
Following the tragic death of a young rag-collector in a South Yorkshire railway tunnel, her grieving grandmother placed a vengeful curse upon anyone who invokes her name. Anyone foolish enough to summon her phantom faces the wrath of this dark spell.

#details
Known locally as the Mexborough Ragger, a young girl who collected rags and carried water for quarrymen once took a shortcut through Cadeby Tunnel. Distracted by the deafening sound of blasting from nearby quarries, she failed to hear an oncoming train. Though she scrambled toward a safety archway in the tunnel wall, she paused to retrieve her dropped rags and was struck and killed. Her body went undiscovered for a period, during which time her grieving grandmother went into Mexborough marketplace and issued a public curse, declaring that any person who dared call out her granddaughter's name would face a severe judgment. Local legend warns that if anyone sits in the tunnel's archway and speaks her name three times, her spirit will appear, subjecting the visitor to her grandmother's enduring curse.

#location
Cadeby Tunnel, situated in The Crags and Don Gorge area between Mexborough and Conisbrough in South Yorkshire.

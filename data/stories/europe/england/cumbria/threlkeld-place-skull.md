#title
Threlkeld Place skull

#summary
Nobody knows whose skull was kept at Threlkeld Place, but it kept returning to the house however often the owners tried to get rid of it. In the end they bricked it up behind a wall.

#details
The history of the Threlkeld Place skull is unknown. What is remembered is that the owners repeatedly tried to dispose of it, and each time it found its way back to the property. Defeated, they bricked it up behind a wall.

#location
Threlkeld Place, Threlkeld, Cumbria, England

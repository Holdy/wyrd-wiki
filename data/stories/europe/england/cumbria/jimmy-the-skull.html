    Jimmy the skull

    Summary

    A skull nicknamed Jimmy is kept in a cupboard at the General Burgoyne pub in Great Urswick, and touching it is considered bad luck. The cupboard sometimes opens by itself, and in 2016 a bar manager saw a faceless girl in a white nightgown pass through a closed door.

    Details

    The General Burgoyne in Great Urswick keeps a skull known as "Jimmy" in a cupboard that has a habit of opening on its own, and touching the skull is said to bring bad luck. The pub has another ghost too. In 2016 a bar manager saw a faceless girl in a white nightgown walk through a closed door, and she is thought to be Livvy, a girl who drowned in Urswick Tarn.

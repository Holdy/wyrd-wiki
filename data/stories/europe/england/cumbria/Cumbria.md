#title
The Ghostly Horse of Wasdale

#summary
The ghost story involves a pony that bolted with a coffin, leading to the disappearance of both the pony and the body. Months later, another pony pulling a different coffin also bolted at the same spot, resulting in the discovery of the first missing coffin but not the second.

#details
In Wasdale, Cumbria, there is a well-known ghost story associated with the coffin path. The church in Wasdale did not have a license to bury the dead, so bodies were transported by pony and cart over the mountains to St. Catherine’s Church in Eskdale. One day, a pony carrying a young man's body bolted, taking the cart and coffin with it. Despite an extensive search, neither the pony nor the body was found. A few months later, the young man's mother died and was also taken along the same coffin path. Tragically, her pony bolted at the exact same location. A pony and coffin were eventually found, but they belonged to the son who had gone missing months earlier. The son was buried, but the mother’s body remained lost. It is said that a ghostly horse, still pulling the missing coffin, is sometimes spotted galloping across the moor.

#location
The story takes place on the coffin path from Wasdale Head to Eskdale via Burnmoor in Cumbria.

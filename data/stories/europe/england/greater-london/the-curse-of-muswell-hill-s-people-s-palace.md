#title
The Curse of Muswell Hill's People's Palace

#summary
Alexandra Palace has long been plagued by a persistent curse resulting in catastrophic fires, financial ruin, and ongoing misfortune. According to local folklore, a witch placed a hex on the site and an ancient folk saying dictated that nothing built atop the hill could ever flourish.

#details
Opened in 1873 as north London's answer to the Crystal Palace, Alexandra Palace was intended as a grand public center for recreation, entertainment, and education. However, disaster struck just 16 days after its grand opening when a massive fire gutted the building, killing three people and leaving only the outer walls standing. Although rebuilt, the venue struggled continuously with financial losses and setbacks, echoing an old local adage that "nothing on the top of Muswell Hill could ever flourish." In July 1980, a second catastrophic fire destroyed the Great Hall, Banqueting Suite, and theatre dressing rooms, further cementing the venue's reputation for being cursed among locals and performers alike.

#location
Alexandra Palace is located at the top of Muswell Hill in north London, between Muswell Hill and Wood Green within the London Borough of Haringey.

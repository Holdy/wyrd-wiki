#title
The Cursed Beam of Barn Hall

#summary
When a squire attempted to build Barn Hall in a woodland claimed by Satan, the Devil threw a heavy timber across the site and cursed it, vowing that anyone entering the cellar would suffer a severe wound. The legend is tied to a local parish church featuring a mysterious stone effigy of a knight clutching his heart.

#details
According to local folklore, a squire decided to construct Barn Hall within what was known as Devils Wood at Tolleshunt Knights, but every night the trenches dug for the foundations were mysteriously filled back in. A sentry was posted to guard the site at night, and when Satan appeared and demanded the ground, a violent confrontation ensued. The Devil hurled a massive timber across the land, declaring that Barn Hall must be built wherever it landed, and placed a curse upon the beam so that anyone who dared to enter the cellar would receive a nasty wound. The Devil also vowed to claim the sentry's soul, leading to the man being buried within the church wall to keep him safe from Satan's grasp, while a stone effigy of a knight holding his heart remains inside All Saints Church to this day.

#location
All Saints Church and Barn Hall are located in the village of Tolleshunt Knights in Essex.

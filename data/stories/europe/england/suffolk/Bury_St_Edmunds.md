#title
William Corder's skull

#summary
The skull of William Corder, the Red Barn murderer, was kept at the prison in Bury St Edmunds and was believed to be evil. Strange sounds and figures were reported around it until it was removed and buried in a country graveyard.

#details
William Corder was hanged for the notorious Red Barn Murder, and his skull was afterwards kept at the prison in Bury St Edmunds, probably the old gaol. It was believed to be evil, and strange noises and figures were reported in its presence. Eventually the skull was removed and buried in an unnamed country graveyard nearby.

#location
Old Bury Gaol, Bury St Edmunds, Suffolk, England

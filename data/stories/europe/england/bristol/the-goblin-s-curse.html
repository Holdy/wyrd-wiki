The Goblin's Curse

Summary

When a fearless Bristol captain defies local warnings to explore a subterranean cavern, he and his companion are cursed by a malevolent goblin who foretells their deaths. Within a year, both men perish exactly as the apparition predicted.

Details

Captain Sturmey, a bold sailor from Bristol, scoffed at the warnings of townsfolk regarding the dreaded Goblin of Pen Park Hole. Accompanied by a miner named Dick, he descended deep into the dark, winding abyss of the cave until they reached an underground lake. There, perched upon a stone, sat a hideous goblin. Mocking the men, the goblin used a fishing rod to pull a shroud out of the water for the captain and a coffin for Dick before vanishing behind a stone door. True to the curse, both explorers met their fatal ends within a single year.

#title
The Phantom Highwayman

#summary
The story tells of a phantom highwayman who is said to return to a lane in Warwickshire where he was hanged for his crimes. A nearby cottage is also reputed to be haunted by a headless woman.

#details
Legend has it that a phantom highwayman returns to a creepy lane in Warwickshire, the very spot where he was hanged for his crimes hundreds of years ago. The lane in question is located near Barton-on-the-Heath and leads towards Kitebrook Preparatory School. A frail weeping willow tree now overhangs the sharp bend on this unnamed road, marking the site of the hanging.

Nearby, a 'haunted cottage' is said to be occupied by a headless woman who roams around a garden pond by night. Despite efforts to locate the cottage, it remains well-hidden and elusive. The area is also home to the 12th Century Church of St Lawrence, adding to the eerie atmosphere.

#location
The weeping willow tree on the sharp bend of an unnamed road out of Barton-on-the-Heath in Warwickshire marks the spot where the phantom highwayman was hanged.

#title
The Vengeful Hound of Warwick Castle

#summary
An elderly woman placed a curse upon Warwick Castle after her trading privileges were revoked, transforming into a ferocious black dog that haunted the grounds until exorcised into a subterranean river chamber.

#details
An elderly woman named Moll Bloxham, rumored to dabble in witchcraft, lived in a cottage near the walls of Warwick Castle and was permitted by the Earl of Warwick to sell surplus dairy produce from the castle kitchens for her own profit. She abused this privilege by shortchanging customers, leading the local townspeople to protest to the Earl, who promptly withdrew her concession. Moll vanished and was never seen alive again, but shortly after her disappearance, a massive, vicious black dog began terrorizing the castle occupants. Believing the creature to be the vengeful spirit of Moll Bloxham, three priests were eventually called in to perform an exorcism, driving the hound to the top of Caesar’s Tower. From there, the beast leapt into the River Avon below, where it became permanently trapped within a chamber built beneath a fletcher dam.

#location
Caesar's Tower at Warwick Castle, overlooking the River Avon in Warwickshire.

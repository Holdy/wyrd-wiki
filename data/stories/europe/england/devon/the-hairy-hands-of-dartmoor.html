The Hairy Hands of Dartmoor

Summary

The story tells of ghostly hairy hands that appear on a stretch of road near Postbridge and Princetown on Dartmoor, Devon. These hands are said to grab at vehicles and try to force them off the road.

Details

The legend of the Hairy Hands of Dartmoor involves a pair of large, hairy, calloused hands that seemingly appear out of nowhere and attempt to wrestle control of vehicles from their drivers. The first reported incident occurred in June 1921 when a worker at Dartmoor Prison lost control of his motorcycle and crashed, resulting in his death. His children, who were riding in the sidecar, recalled their father shouting at them to get off the bike and struggling with the controls.

Another motorcyclist later reported a similar experience, with his pillion passenger clearly seeing a large hairy hand grasp the handlebars and forcibly upset the bike. The tale also extends to campers in the area, with one young couple reporting a hairy hand crawling up their caravan window, exuding an intent to harm.

The area around Postbridge and Princetown has become notorious for accidents, with many survivors attributing their experiences to the ghostly hands. Despite the legend, locals often dismiss it as tourists driving too fast on narrow roads.

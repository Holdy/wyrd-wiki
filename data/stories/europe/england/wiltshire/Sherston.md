#title
Pinkney Park skull

#summary
A woman's skull kept at the house at Pinkney Park is believed to belong to a young girl who was killed by her own sister. Her ghost is said to roam carrying her head under her arm.

#details
At Pinkney Park near Sherston, a woman's skull is kept in the house. It is believed to belong to a young girl who was murdered by her sister, and whose ghost is seen carrying her head beneath her arm. The park has another grim landmark too: the "Elm-Ash" tree, said to have grown from the grave of a suicide that was pinned down with two stakes.

#location
Pinkney Park, Sherston, Wiltshire, England

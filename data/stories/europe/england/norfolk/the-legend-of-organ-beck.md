#title
The Legend of Organ Beck

#summary
A mysterious water-filled quarry pit in Beeston Regis is said to conceal a church pump-organ in its depths. Local belief holds that the pool is entirely bottomless, connecting directly to Satan's lair.

#details
Organ Beck is a deep pool situated on the site of a coastal caravan park in Beeston Regis, occupying an old brickworks quarry pit near the cliffs. According to local folklore, the pond takes its peculiar name from the local church's original pump-organ, which allegedly came to rest somewhere in its murky water. More sinister tales suggest that the pit has no bed at all, forming a bottomless abyss that leads straight down to Satan's lair.

#location
Organ Beck is a water-filled quarry pit located within the coastal caravan park at Beeston Regis in North Norfolk.

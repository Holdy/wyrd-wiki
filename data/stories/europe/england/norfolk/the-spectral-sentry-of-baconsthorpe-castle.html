The Spectral Sentry of Baconsthorpe Castle

Summary

Visitors to the medieval ruins of Baconsthorpe Castle are frequently startled by stones splashing into the moat, thrown from above by a harmless ghostly sentry still keeping watch.

Details

Built by the opportunistic and wealthy Heydon family starting in 1450, Baconsthorpe Castle eventually fell into ruin and partial demolition by the mid-17th century due to family insolvency. Centuries later, visitors exploring the shattered remains and tranquil waters of the moat often hear the distinct sound of stones breaking the surface. When they look up to find the source, they catch sight of a spectral medieval soldier standing atop the castle walls, casually throwing stones into the water as if passing the time on eternal guard duty. Though startling, the phantom sentry has never been reported as threatening, continuing his lonely watch over the historic estate.

#title
The Black Hound of Cooke's Lane

#summary
A member of the Thurton darts team cycling home late at night was terrified when a large, snarling black dog sprang from a roadside pond on the Thurton-Bergh Apton road and vanished into a wall.

#details
In November 1944 or 1945, around midnight, a man cycling home from an away darts match as part of the Thurton George & Dragon team was riding along the Thurton to Bergh Apton road, known as Cooke's Lane. As he approached a pond opposite Washingford House, a massive black dog—about the size of a large Labrador with a smooth back, shaggy under-body hair, and bared, snarling teeth—suddenly leaped out from beside the water. It lunged at him crossbar-high, growling fiercely. Terrified and expecting to be knocked off his bicycle and bitten, the cyclist was astounded when the beast abruptly vanished right through a stone wall on the right-hand side of the road. Shaken to his core, he pedalled home at top speed.

#location
The encounter took place on Cooke's Lane (the Thurton to Bergh Apton road), specifically by the pond opposite Washingford House.

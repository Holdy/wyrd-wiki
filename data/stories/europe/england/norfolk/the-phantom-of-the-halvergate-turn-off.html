The Phantom of the Halvergate Turn-Off

Summary

A driver traveling along the Acle Straight near Halvergate experienced sheer terror when a middle-aged man stepped directly into the path of their car, only for the vehicle to pass right through the spectral figure.

Details

While driving past the Halvergate turn-off on the Acle Straight, a motorist watched in horror as a middle-aged man suddenly walked out into the middle of the road from the right-hand side. With no time to brake or swerve, the driver braced for impact as the figure turned to look directly at them, but the car passed straight through the phantom without resistance.

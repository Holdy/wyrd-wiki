#title
The Tap Room Specter

#summary
The tap room of a Dersingham public house is haunted by the spirit of a young boy who was brutally beaten to death there. His lingering presence is believed to be responsible for various supernatural disturbances in the area.

#details
Many years ago, a young boy met a tragic end when he was beaten to death inside the tap room of The Feathers in Dersingham. Over time, the echoes of this dark event remained, and the boy's ghost is thought to haunt the establishment, with locals attributing subsequent paranormal outbreaks and uncanny phenomena in the vicinity to his restless spirit.

#location
The Feathers is a public house located in the village of Dersingham, Norfolk.

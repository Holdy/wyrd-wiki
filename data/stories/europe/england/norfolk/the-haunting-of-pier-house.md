#title
The Haunting of Pier House

#summary
Residents of a historic quayside home in Gorleston-on-Sea experienced terrifying ghostly activity, ranging from unexplained noises and physical touches to a malevolent entity targeting the homeowner in her dreams. Paranormal investigators were called in to cleanse the property and free the resident from a dark spiritual attachment.

#details
Daniel and his fiancé Claire Meakins sought help from paranormal investigators Barri Ghai, Jayne Harris, and Ian Lawman after being plagued by persistent whispers, strange smells, growling, and heavy footsteps at their home, Pier House on Quay Road. Historically associated with 20th-century harbour master Captain Sutton, the house harbored a heavy, oppressive atmosphere that deterred visiting family members. During the investigation, the team captured unexplained noises and physical interactions where clothing was tugged, while clairvoyant Ian Lawman worked to sever a dangerous, suffocating dark entity that had been attaching itself to Claire during her sleep.

#location
Pier House is situated on Quay Road in Gorleston-on-Sea, Norfolk.

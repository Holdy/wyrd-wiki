#title
Old Tom

#summary
A young child visiting a dying parson in Wiveton encounters Old Tom, a scarred veteran of the Irish Wars who is already a ghost.

#details
Set in Wiveton during the seventeenth century, a young child is brought along by their mother to sit with the dying Parson Poynter at the flint-built parsonage. While waiting in the kitchen, the child interacts with Old Tom, a grievously scarred and disabled veteran of the Irish Wars who is universally feared and avoided by the villagers. Later, during a rowdy disturbance by local men targeting the parsonage, Old Tom slips away and is pursued, though the parson reassures the child that Old Tom can no longer be harmed by the living because he is already a spirit who has crossed over.

#location
The old parsonage house situated on the hill along the low road leading from Wiveton.

#title
The Pedlar of Swaffham

#summary
A poor Norfolk pedlar travels to London Bridge after experiencing a prophetic dream, only to be told by a local shopkeeper that a massive treasure is buried beneath an oak tree in his own orchard back in Swaffham. Upon returning home, he uncovers the hidden gold and uses his newfound wealth to magnificently restore the town's parish church.

#details
Tradition maintains that in former times, a poor pedlar named John Chapman lived in Swaffham, Norfolk. He experienced a recurring dream telling him that if he went to London Bridge and stood there, he would hear joyful news. Though initially skeptical, he eventually made the journey to London and stood on the bridge for several days. A local shopkeeper eventually approached him, asking what business he had standing about aimlessly. When the pedlar recounted his dream, the shopkeeper laughed and told him he was foolish to heed such things, mentioning that he himself had dreamed of traveling to Swaffham, where a great treasure lay buried beneath an oak tree in an orchard behind a pedlar's house. Recognizing that the description matched his own home, the pedlar hurried back to Swaffham, dug under the oak tree in his orchard, and unearthed a prodigious treasure. With the immense wealth he discovered, he funded the restoration of the dilapidated parish church of St Peter and St Paul at his own expense. To this day, his stone statue—complete with his pack and dog—is preserved within the church.

#location
St Peter and St Paul's Church is located in the center of Swaffham, Norfolk (PE37 7LJ).

#upstream-source

#title
The Phantoms of The George Hotel

#summary
Ghost hunters investigating a historic Norfolk coaching inn discover multiple active spirits, including the infamous Green Lady and an unexpected mischievous presence in the dry store room. Guests and staff report chilling encounters ranging from unnerving room disturbances to physical contact.

#details
During an overnight paranormal investigation at The George Hotel in Swaffham, investigators explored the 16th-century coaching inn using scientific equipment and spiritual mediums. While investigating the hotel's dry store room—formerly linked to an old bakery—mediums encountered an assertive energy that interacted with their hair and clothing. Later, staff confirmed that a young waiter had experienced an unseen force attempting to undo his trousers in the exact same room. When shown an old sepia photograph of former bakery staff, the mediums identified the spirit. Elsewhere in the hotel, the elusive Green Lady manifested to a guest in Room 204, another guest was pinned to their bed in Room 206, and spirits behind the bar frequently sent glasses flying off the shelves.

#location
The George Hotel is a historic 16th-century coaching inn situated on the central market place and crossroads in the town of Swaffham, Norfolk.

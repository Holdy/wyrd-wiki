#title
Old Tom

#summary
A disfigured veteran of the Irish Wars named Old Tom, who lived in Thursford Wood, would visit the local parsonage on autumn nights before his spirit became a haunting presence in the village. Years later, his ghostly figure and tapping crutch are said to still roam Thursford Wood and the surrounding low roads.

#details
Set in Thursford during the reign of King James I, a narrator recalls visiting the parsonage where Parson Poynter lay dying. On stormy nights, Old Tom—a crippled and severely scarred veteran of the Irish Wars who lived in Thursford Wood—would take shelter by the kitchen fire alongside the staff and a young child. Tom haunted the house with tragic memories of losing his captain during an ambush in Ireland. Although local men hostile to the parson chased Tom during a riot, Parson Poynter reassured the child that Tom was beyond harm. Following the deaths of the parson and Tom, locals reported hearing the spectral tapping of Tom's crutch along the low road and seeing him wander Thursford Wood.

#location
Thursford Wood is an ancient woodland located in the parish of Thursford in North Norfolk, England.

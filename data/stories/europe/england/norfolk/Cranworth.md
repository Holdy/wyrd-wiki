#title
The Headless Donkey of Cranworth

#summary
A terrifying apparition of a towering, headless donkey and phantom coaches pulled by invisible horses haunt the rural parish of Cranworth.

#details
Late at night in the parish of Cranworth, a farmer's wife was startled by approaching footsteps in the dark and turned to find a monstrous donkey standing on its hind legs, prancing and towering above her without a head. Alongside this bizarre entity, locals have reported chilling encounters with phantom coaches driven by headless or invisible horses traversing local routes like Shipdam Road.

#location
Shipdam Road runs through the civil parish of Cranworth in the Breckland district of Norfolk, England.

The Folklore of East Anglia by Enid Porter (citing the Eastern Counties Magazine, 1900)

#title
The White Figure of Cranworth Ash

#summary
A mysterious tall white figure was long reported to emerge from an old ash tree in Cranworth, walking along the road toward Letton Hall before vanishing at midnight.

#details
According to local tradition recorded in the late nineteenth century, a phantom white specter was said to haunt Cranworth. The ghostly figure would materialize from a long-vanished ash tree, pace up and down the road leading toward Letton Hall, and finally disappear into thin air exactly at midnight.

#location
The road leading from Cranworth toward Letton Hall in Norfolk.

#upstream-source
W. B. Gerish: 'Norfolk Folklore Notes' (unpublished, 1890-93), p.26.

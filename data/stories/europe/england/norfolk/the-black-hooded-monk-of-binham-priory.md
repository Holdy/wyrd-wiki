#title
The Black-Hooded Monk of Binham Priory

#summary
During evening services and near midnight around the ancient ruins, witnesses have repeatedly reported seeing the phantom figure of a black-hooded Benedictine monk walking along a high interior ledge of Binham Parish Church and lingering near the historic Gaol Gate.

#details
Local accounts in Binham tell of eerie paranormal encounters centered around Binham Priory and its parish church, which serves as the ancient nave of the former monastery. During an evening service, a churchgoer witnessed a spectral figure resembling a Benedictine monk wearing a black cowl. The phantom slowly walked along a high stone ledge several feet above the floor, ascended a set of spiral steps, and vanished. A similar sighting was reported by another parishioner who watched a dark, monk-like figure repeatedly appear and disappear upon the ledge during a sermon. Beyond the church interior, villagers have also reported seeing the apparition of a Benedictine monk near the Gaol Gate at the entrance to the priory grounds, with local warnings advising people to stay away from the gateway at midnight.

#location
Binham Priory and Parish Church is situated on Priory Lane in the village of Binham, Norfolk, England.

The Glowing Dome Over Cromer Road

Summary

On a late August night in 1971, multiple witnesses watched a silent, glowing orange craft hover over the fields near Hainford. Investigators later discovered mysterious flattened circles of reed grass and grey dust at the landing site.

Details

On the evening of August 27, 1971, a motorist named Mr. Arthur was driving home to New Hainford along the Cromer New Road. Around 9 p.m. near Norwich Airport, he spotted a glowing orange dome suspended motionless in the sky. Stopping his vehicle, he watched as the object drifted closer, its orange light giving way to five red flashing lights around its base. For fifteen minutes, the silent craft maneuvered above the marshy ground before disappearing behind trees. Upon arriving home, he discovered his mother had independently witnessed a similar glowing orange object rise from the trees at the bottom of their garden at the exact same time. The sighting was investigated by the Borderline Science Investigation Group, who found unexplained flattened circles in the marsh grass and a coating of grey dust on surrounding bushes. A second independent witness in North Earlham confirmed seeing the same descending orange object along a bearing pointing directly to Hainford.

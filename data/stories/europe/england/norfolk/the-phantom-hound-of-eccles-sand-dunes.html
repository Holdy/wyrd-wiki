The Phantom Hound of Eccles Sand Dunes

Summary

A spectral black hound with fiery eyes haunts the midnight dunes near Lessingham, bringing fatal doom to anyone who meets its gaze.

Details

At the stroke of midnight, a terrifying phantom black dog is said to roam the Eccles sand dunes. The apparition is distinguished by its eerie yellow eyes which feature glowing red centres. Local folklore holds a grim warning for anyone who crosses its path: those who look directly into the hound's burning eyes are destined to die.

The Ghosts of Wayland Wood

Summary

The spirits of two young orphaned children, abandoned in a dark Norfolk forest by their treacherous uncle seeking an inheritance, are said to wander the trees wailing in the dark.

Details

An orphaned brother and sister were placed in the care of their uncle at Griston Hall near Watton. Eager to seize their father's fortune, the villainous uncle lured the children deep into the murky expanse of Wayland Wood during the bitter winter and left them to die. As the children succumbed to the cold, a robin famously covered their frozen bodies with leaves. Local folklore maintains that their little ghosts continue to wander the woodland, heard wailing in the darkness. The tragic legend inspired the famous tale of "Babes in the Wood," and is commemorated on the Watton town sign, which depicts the two children beneath an oak tree.

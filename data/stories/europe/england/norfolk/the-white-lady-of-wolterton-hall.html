    The White Lady of Wolterton Hall

    Summary

    A spectral white lady is said to haunt Wolterton Hall to warn the Walpole family of impending tragedy or death. Additionally, a phantom hearse and the spirit of Lady Walpole are reported to roam the estate.

    Details

    Wolterton Hall is haunted by a white lady believed to be a member of the Scamler family, who owned the land before the Walpoles built the mansion. Her apparition traditionally appears to herald a calamity or death among the Walpole family. Accounts also describe a phantom hearse, complete with horses and attendants, which was traditionally driven three times around the ruined church in Wolterton Park before burials in the family vault at Wickmere. Furthermore, the ghost of Lady Walpole (née Lombard) is said to haunt the drawing room, restlessly searching for her family members after a full-length portrait of her was cut into pieces and distributed.

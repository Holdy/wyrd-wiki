The Phantom Hound of RAF Oulton

Summary

During World War Two, an airman walking to his military station in Oulton encountered a silent, spectral black dog standing in the moonlight near Blickling Hall before it vanished into thin air.

Details

In the early years of the Second World War, an airman was walking from Aylsham toward his station at RAF Oulton in Norfolk. As he passed by the moonlit grounds of Blickling Hall, he glanced across the parkland and noticed a massive black dog observing him from a short distance away. The beast's mouth was wide open as though howling or roaring, yet no sound emanated from it. After watching him for a few terrifying seconds and filling him with an overwhelming sense of dread, the spectral hound vanished completely into thin air.

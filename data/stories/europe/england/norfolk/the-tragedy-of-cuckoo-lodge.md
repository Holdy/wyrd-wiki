#title
The Tragedy of Cuckoo Lodge

#summary
Cuckoo Lodge at Gallows Hill in Wells-next-the-Sea is haunted by the tragic spirit of a young girl who drowned in its deep garden well near a historic execution crossroads.

#details
Built in the mid-1800s, Cuckoo Lodge is a charming cottage located at Gallows Hill just outside Wells-next-the-Sea, accessible via a footpath running along the edge of the local cemetery. According to local lore, the property was once home to a family touched by tragedy when a young girl fell into their garden well—reputedly one of the deepest in Norfolk—and drowned in the dark waters below. Her apparition is said to still haunt the cottage. Just outside the grounds, the quiet country crossroads once served as a gibbet site where condemned criminals were displayed, lending an even darker historical atmosphere to the area.

#location
Cuckoo Lodge is located at Gallows Hill on the outskirts of Wells-next-the-Sea, reachable via a footpath running along the edge of the town's cemetery.

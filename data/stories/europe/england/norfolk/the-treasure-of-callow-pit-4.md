#title
The Treasure of Callow Pit

#summary
Two adventurous men attempted to haul a chest of gold from the murky depths of Callow Pit in Southwood, only to lose the treasure in a struggle against a demonic black hand after boastfully defying the Devil.

#details
According to local tradition in Southwood, Norfolk, an iron chest filled with gold lay hidden at the bottom of Callow Pit, a deep, gloomy pool located on the parish boundary between Southwood and Moulton. During a period of unusually low water, two local men resolved to recover the treasure. They built a platform of ladders across the pit, reached the center, and hooked a staff through the ring on the chest's lid, successfully drawing it to the surface and placing it upon their shoulders. However, as one of the men triumphantly exclaimed that they had secured the prize and that the devil himself could not take it from them, a cloud of foul sulphurous steam engulfed the pit. A black hand emerged from the dark water to seize the chest, sparking a violent tug-of-war between the men and the unseen entity. The chest eventually broke apart under the strain, and the gold sank back into the water forever, leaving the men with nothing but the iron ring from the lid. 

#location
Callow Pit is situated on the boundary between the parishes of Southwood and Moulton in Norfolk, England.

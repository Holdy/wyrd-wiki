#title
The Secret Tunnel of Little Fransham Old Hall

#summary
Little Fransham Old Hall is said to be connected to the nearby main road by a concealed underground tunnel, allegedly linked to a royal visit by Queen Elizabeth I. This narrow subterranean passage has long fueled local legends of secret escapes and covert movements.

#details
Little Fransham Old Hall is a 16th-century farmhouse in Norfolk that local tradition connects with a visit by Queen Elizabeth I. Alongside this royal association, a persistent legend surrounds a subterranean tunnel—described as being just large enough for a man to crawl through—that supposedly stretches from the house out toward the A47 road about 150 meters away. Although archaeological and historical records suggest the feature is likely a medieval or post-medieval drainage system, generations of locals have maintained tales of a hidden passage associated with the queen's stay.

#location
Little Fransham Old Hall is a 16th-century farmhouse situated at Ordnance Survey grid reference TF902118 in the parish of Little Fransham, Norfolk.

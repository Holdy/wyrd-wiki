The Mistletoe Bride of Terrington St Clement

Summary

During a wedding day game of hide-and-seek, a young bride tragically locked herself inside an antique chest and suffocated, with local legend claiming her ghost returns to St Clement's Church every year to join in the Christmas carol singing.

Details

The legend tells of a lavish wedding celebration where the young bride decided to hide in a heavy wooden chest or cassone as part of a game. Unbeknownst to her, a spring-lock mechanism on the lid slammed shut, trapping her inside the dark container. When she failed to reappear, her disappearance was initially blamed on cold feet or an elopement. Decades later, her skeletal remains were discovered inside the chest wearing her decayed wedding gown. In Terrington St Clement, local tradition maintains that her spirit never left the parish church, and once a year on the Sunday before Christmas, her spectral figure emerges from her resting place to join the congregation in singing carols.

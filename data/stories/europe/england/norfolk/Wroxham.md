#title
Sir Thomas Boleyn’s Penance Across Wroxham Bridge

#summary
Every year on the anniversary of his daughter's execution, the ghost of Sir Thomas Boleyn drives a carriage across twelve Norfolk bridges, including the one at Wroxham. Doomed for eternity, the phantom coach is pulled by four headless horses as it completes its cursed journey.

#details
According to local Norfolk folklore, Sir Thomas Boleyn—father of the ill-fated Tudor queen Anne Boleyn—is condemned to perform an annual penance following his daughter's execution. Each year on May 19th, his restless spirit must drive a ghostly coach pulled by four headless horses across twelve distinct bridges in Norfolk. This nocturnal journey of torment takes him from Blickling Hall toward Wroxham, traversing ancient crossings at Aylsham, Burgh, Buxton, Coltishall, Meyton, Belaugh, Oxnead, Braydeston, and Wroxham Bridge itself. Those unfortunate enough to cross paths with the spectral carriage on its midnight voyage risk witnessing the terrifying apparition as it thunders across the river.

#location
Wroxham Bridge spans the River Bure on the A1151 road in the Norfolk village of Wroxham.

#title
The Care Home Apparition

#summary
An assistant manager staying in an upstairs flat of a century-old care home in Wroxham witnessed a grey mist transform into a human figure at midnight. The building is also said to be haunted by the spirit of a World War II Italian prisoner of war and other unexplained phenomena.

#details
An assistant manager at a Wroxham care home—housed in a building over a hundred years old that was formerly a private residence, hotel, and stable block—was reading in her bedroom at 1 AM when she noticed a motionless grey mist. As she watched in fear, the mist coalesced into the shape of a person and took a step forward, prompting her to flee the room and alert the night staff. Subsequent discussions with colleagues revealed a history of paranormal occurrences in the building, including the sounds of crying children, phantom footsteps, and the rumored ghost of a young Italian prisoner of war who hanged himself in the former stables during wartime.

#location
The care home is located in Wroxham, Norfolk, situated within a historic property featuring a converted stable block flat.

#title
The Black Dog of Catfield Hall

#summary
A spectral black hound guides a group of trespassing women safely across treacherous marshes to help them evade a gamekeeper.

#details
Around 1842, several women were out on the marshes near Catfield Hall gathering rushes when they suddenly heard the gamekeeper approaching. As they panicked over being caught trespassing, a large black dog materialized among them, whimpering and darting back and forth. Realizing the phantom animal was attempting to guide them, the women followed it across the most dangerous parts of the marsh to a secure footpath that led onto the main road. Once they reached safety and looked around, the mysterious hound had completely vanished.

#location
The marshes situated near Catfield Hall (grid reference TG378213) in the parish of Catfield, Norfolk, UK.

#title
The Rebels' Excavations of Marsham Pits

#summary
According to 19th-century local folklore, the ancient depressions known as Marsham Pits were dug by rebel forces during the Peasants' Revolt of 1381.

#details
In local tradition recorded by folklorists, the ancient earthworks and pits located in Marsham—which bear similarities to early medieval iron workings found elsewhere in Norfolk—were attributed to the participants of the 1381 Norfolk peasants' uprising. Rather than being ordinary industrial excavations, local legend held that the pits were created by the rebels during their tumultuous uprising in the region.

#location
Marsham Pits, situated within the parish of Marsham, Norfolk.

#upstream-source

#title
The Upright Corpse of Narborough Church

#summary
Local legend holds that the 17th-century judge Sir Clement Spelman was buried upright inside an 8-foot-tall pedestal beneath his monument in All Saints' Church to prevent people from walking over his grave.

#details
Sir Clement Spelman, the Recorder of Nottingham who died in 1670 at the age of 72, left behind a peculiar local legend at All Saints' Church in Narborough. According to the tradition, because he did not want anyone walking over his final resting place, his corpse was interred in an upright position inside a massive 8-foot-tall stone pedestal that supported his memorial statue on the north side of the chancel. His upright remains stayed in this position for nearly two centuries until Victorian restorers intervened in 1865, removing him for a conventional burial elsewhere in the churchyard and lowering the pedestal to a standard height.

#location
All Saints' Church, situated on Main Road in the village of Narborough, Norfolk (PE32 1TE).

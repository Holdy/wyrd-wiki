#title
The Specter of Shrieking Woman's Grove

#summary
The ghost of a distressed woman is said to haunt a historic woodland on the parish boundary of Brooke, where her terrifying cries echo through the trees. Local tradition and historical records link the haunting to a forgotten tragedy and a fearful crime committed in the area.

#details
East of the village of Brooke, stretching along the parish boundary toward Bergh Apton, lies a wooded area historically known as Shrieking Woman's Grove. Although half of the ancient wood has been lost to time, the surviving trees and public footpath retain a chilling reputation. According to local lore and regional folklore collections, the phantom of a woman has been spotted between the trees, frequently accompanied by piercing screams and wails. While heritage databases and local histories suggest the name may originate from an ancient crime or historical iron-working site similar to the Shrieking Pits of northern Norfolk, the true origin of the restless spirit remains shrouded in mystery.

#location
Shrieking Woman's Grove is situated in a wooded area just east of Brooke village along the parish boundary with Bergh Apton (centred around OS grid reference TM304992).

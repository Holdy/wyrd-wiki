#title
King John's Hole

#summary
A dank fenland pool known as King John's Hole is traditionally believed to conceal the legendary royal treasure lost when the king's baggage train was overwhelmed by the tide in 1216.

#details
According to local folklore, a treacherous pool on the southern side of the King's Lynn to Long Sutton Road marks the hiding place of King John's royal treasure and baggage train. In October 1216, as the king and his retinue attempted to cross the Wash, they were overtaken by the fast-rising tide, swallowing carts laden with gold, silver, and crown jewels. Locals long maintained that portions of this lost medieval hoard settled in or near this specific pool within Walpole St. Andrew.

#location
King John's Hole, located on the southern side of the King's Lynn to Long Sutton Road in Walpole St. Andrew.

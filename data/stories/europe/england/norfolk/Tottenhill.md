#title
Thieves' Bridge and the Stolen Bells

#summary
Thieves who stole the church bells from Tottenhill hid them under a stream crossing on the parish boundary, leading to their capture and execution and giving the site its enduring name.

#details
According to local tradition, men once stole the bells from the church in Tottenhill. Because the heavy metal bells were too cumbersome to carry away all at once, the robbers concealed them beneath a small bridge over a stream for safe-keeping. The stolen bells were eventually discovered, and the thieves were apprehended and hanged for their crime. Ever since, the spot has been known as Thieves' Bridge, a name that also came to be shared by a nearby road, wood, and farm. Historically, records note that one of the church's bells was indeed stolen in 1642, leaving it with only a single bell remaining and lending a kernel of truth to the legend.

#location
Thieves' Bridge is located on Watlington Road on the parish boundary between Watlington and Tottenhill in Norfolk (grid reference TF636103).

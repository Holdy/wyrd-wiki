#title
The Ghost of Horstead Mill Pond

#summary
A ghostly figure bearing the drowned body of a servant girl is said to haunt the old mill pond in Horstead after being abandoned by her lover.

#details
Horstead Mill, which dated back to the 18th century before burning down in 1963, features a mill pond at grid reference TG267193. According to local tradition, the pond is occasionally haunted by a spectral figure carrying the body of a servant girl who took her own life by drowning after her lover deserted her.

#location
The mill pond located at the former site of Horstead Mill in Horstead (grid reference TG267193).

#title
The Phantom Hound of Coltishall Bridge

#summary
Two RAF officers driving a Mini near Coltishall Bridge in Horstead encountered an enormous spectral black dog with fiery red eyes that vanished into thin air.

#details
Late one night between 1960 and 1962, two RAF officers were driving a Mini back from Norwich to RAF Coltishall. As they crossed Coltishall Bridge and turned left into the High Street in Horstead, they were forced into a sudden stop when an enormous black dog crossed the road directly in front of them. The creature, built like a Labrador but standing as tall as the roof of their Mini (approximately 53 inches), slowly turned its head to glare at them with piercing, fiery red eyes before continuing onto the cobbles and vanishing entirely.

#location
Coltishall Bridge and the adjacent High Street in Horstead, Norfolk.

#title
The Phantom of Horstead Mill Pond

#summary
A heartbroken servant girl who drowned herself after being abandoned by her lover is said to haunt the waters, appearing with a spectral apparition of her own lifeless body in her arms. Witnesses occasionally report seeing her near the site of the old mill.

#details
The tragic haunting takes place around the mill pond in Horstead, near the site where an eighteenth-century mill once stood before burning down in 1963. According to local folklore, a young servant girl took her own life by drowning in the pond after her lover cruelly abandoned her. Since then, her ghostly form has been spotted lingering by the water's edge, seemingly cradling the drowned body of the girl in her arms.

#location
The key location is the Horstead Mill pond, situated at grid reference TG267193 along the River Bure.

#title
The Prophecy of the Three-Thumbed Miller

#summary
Local folklore places an ancient prophecy at a great tree on Heggatt Heath in Horstead, where a miller with three thumbs is destined to hold three kings' horses during a catastrophic battle.

#details
In his early 20th-century travels around Norfolk, writer Christopher Marlowe recorded a local legend concerning a great tree in the centre of Heggatt Heath in Horstead (around grid reference TG277184). Tradition states that a miller possessing three thumbs will hold three kings' horses during the course of a great battle in which nearly the entire county's population will perish. Similar apocalyptic prophecies involving a three-thumbed miller have also appeared in historical collections and the writings attributed to Mother Shipton and Robert Nixon, mysteriously attaching themselves to this Horstead common.

#location
The site of the great tree in the centre of Heggatt Heath in Horstead (around grid reference TG277184).

#upstream-source
Christopher Marlowe: 'People & Places in Marshland' (Cecil Palmer, 1927), p.237.

#title
The Spectral Wolf of Southery

#summary
A monstrous wolf-hound descended from a pack brought to guard medieval monks haunts the fens and ruins of St. Mary's Church. Anyone who hears its terrifying howl on Southery Feast Day is doomed to die within a year.

#details
During the 14th century, monks building St. Mary's Church in Southery were guarded by fierce wolfhounds sent by the Baron of Northwold. When provisions ran out, the hounds turned on the monks and eventually on each other until only a single massive female remained, which was later adopted and tamed by a local fenman. The hound subsequently gave birth to a monstrous, ox-sized offspring that provided game for the villagers. When the church was finally completed and the Bishop of Elmham arrived with his men, a soldier panicked and attacked the beast. The creature killed the soldier before being riddled with arrows and fleeing mortally wounded into the marshes. According to local legend, the phantom wolf returns to the ruins of St. Mary's Church every year on Southery Feast Day (May 29th), and hearing its deathly howl foretells doom within twelve months.

#location
The ruined St. Mary's Church is located in the village of Southery in West Norfolk, near the border with Cambridgeshire.

Walter Barrett, *Tales from the Fens* (1963)

#summary
A monstrous, donkey-sized spectral wolf haunts the ruined church of St Mary in Southery on its annual feast day. Hearing the phantom beast's terrifying howl is said to bring certain death within a year.

#details
In the 14th century, monks building St Mary's church were protected by wolfhounds that eventually turned feral and terrorised the village before cannibalising each other. A single surviving female gave birth to an unearthly, ox-sized hybrid pup that faithfully served the villagers until it was attacked and mortally wounded by the bishop's guards. Ever since, on May 29 (Southery Feast Day), the ghost of this colossal black wolf returns to the ruins of St Mary's church, where anyone who crosses its path or hears its blood-curdling howls is doomed to die within twelve months.

#title
The Spectral Wolf of Southery

#summary
A monstrous wolf-hound sent to guard monks in the Fens turned feral, leaving behind an unworldly offspring whose spectral ghost returns every year to howl near the village church on May 29th.

#details
In the 14th century, when monks from Ely were building a church in Southery and facing lawless attacks, the Baron of Northwold sent a pack of wolf-hounds to protect them. When food ran scarce, the starving hounds turned on each other until only a massive female remained, who was later rescued and tamed by a local Fenman. After returning from a mysterious journey pregnant, she gave birth to a monstrous pup half-dog and half-wolf as big as an ox. When the church was finally completed, one of the Bishop's soldiers tried to attack the creature, only for the beast to devour him before being fatally shot with arrows. Ever since, the ghostly hound's phantom returns every year on Southery Feast Day (May 29th) to howl near the church ruins, foretelling death within a year to anyone who hears it.

#location
The ruins of St. Mary's Church in the village of Southery, Norfolk.

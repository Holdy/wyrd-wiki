#title
The Phantom Hound of the Links

#summary
A spectral dog has been reported haunting the fairways of the Old Hunstanton golf course. Local folklore and regional surveys explicitly distinguish this apparition from the infamous Black Shuck.

#details
Visitors and players navigating the links at Old Hunstanton have periodically encountered a ghostly canine appearing across the course. While East Anglian folklore is dominated by tales of the demonic Black Shuck, local paranormal investigators and collectors have documented this phantom hound as an independent local presence rather than the legendary coastal fiend.

#location
The sightings occur across the grounds of the Old Hunstanton Golf Club situated in Old Hunstanton, Norfolk.

#title
The Faithful Labrador Ghost

#summary
A spectral black hound belonging to a former landlord continues to roam the historic halls of a village inn out of eternal canine loyalty.

#details
The Fox Inn, a long-standing landmark in Garboldisham, is haunted by the phantom of a black Labrador. Believed to be the devoted companion of a former landlord, the spirit of the dog refuses to leave the premises, frequently appearing as it roams the rooms and corridors of the inn to embody a timeless devotion that outlived its mortal life.

#location
The Fox Inn is situated within the village of Garboldisham in the county of Norfolk, England.

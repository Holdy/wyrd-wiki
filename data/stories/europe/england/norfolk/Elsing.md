#title
The Fading Bell of Elsing Hall

#summary
A subterranean passage linking Elsing Hall to St. Mary's Church is shrouded in mystery after a hound sent to explore it vanished, leaving only a phantom ringing bell that slowly faded into silence.

#details
According to local folklore, a secret subterranean passage measuring nearly a mile long connects the 15th-century Elsing Hall to St. Mary's church. Years ago, before the tunnel entrance was permanently sealed, an exploratory dog wearing a small bell around its neck was sent into the dark passage. As the hound ventured deeper underground, the ringing of its bell gradually grew fainter until it fell completely silent, and the dog was never seen again. The entrance was subsequently closed up, and the exact location of the tunnel has remained lost ever since.

#location
The subterranean passage connects Elsing Hall (TG039160) to St. Mary's Church (TG051165) in the village of Elsing, Norfolk.

#title
The Lost Tunnel of Elsing Hall

#summary
According to local legend, a hidden subterranean passage connects Elsing Hall to St. Mary's church. A dog sent to explore the tunnel vanished into the darkness, its collar bell fading into eternal silence.

#details
Set in the Norfolk village of Elsing, local tradition maintains that a secret underground passage measuring nearly a mile long once linked the 15th-century moated manor house of Elsing Hall to St. Mary's church. Years ago, when an opening to the tunnel was uncovered, a dog with a small bell tied around its neck was sent inside to investigate the depths. As the animal ventured further underground, the chiming of the bell slowly grew fainter until it stopped altogether, and the dog was never seen again. Following the disappearance, the entrance to the passageway was sealed shut and its true location remains lost to this day.

#location
Elsing Hall is a 15th-century moated manor house located in the village of Elsing, Norfolk (grid reference TG039160).

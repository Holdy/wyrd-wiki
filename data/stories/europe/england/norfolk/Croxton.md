#title
The Devil's Nightcap of Croxton

#summary
On damp autumn evenings, a mysterious circlet of mist known as the Devil's Nightcap forms over the Devil's Punch Bowl in Croxton. Local folklore attributes this haunting atmospheric phenomenon to the devil's presence in the landscape.

#details
In south-west Norfolk, traditional folklore holds that the devil was once very busy shaping and roaming the countryside. Near the village of Croxton lies the Devil's Punch Bowl, a striking circular depression in the Breckland terrain. On damp autumn evenings, a distinct circlet of mist frequently appears hovering above this hollow, earning the local moniker the Devil's Nightcap as a testament to the area's enduring devil legends.

#location
The Devil's Punch Bowl is a circular depression located just north of the village of Croxton in Norfolk.

#title
The Hearse from the Pit

#summary
A gamekeeper murdered by rabbit poachers on Croxton Heath returns as a phantom funeral hearse that rises from a sandpit at midnight.

#details
Long ago on Croxton Heath, a gamekeeper was set upon and killed by rabbit poachers, who loaded his body onto their cart to dispose of it. Finding a sandpit on the wild heath, they began to unload him, only for the gamekeeper—who was not quite dead—to sit up and swear he would haunt them forever. Startled by this warning, the poachers finished him off and buried him in the pit. Ever since, local lore maintains that a phantom hearse, complete with a coffin and pallbearers, rises from the sandpit at midnight, travels slowly down the Thetford road, and vanishes through a nearby field gate.

#location
A sandpit on Croxton Heath near the Thetford road in Croxton, Norfolk.

#title
The Phantom Midnight Funeral

#summary
Poachers who murdered a gamekeeper on Croxton Heath are cursed to be haunted by his ghostly funeral procession every night. Rising from a sandpit at midnight, a phantom hearse with a coffin and pallbearers travels down the Thetford road before vanishing through a field gate.

#details
Long ago on the wild expanse of Croxton Heath, poachers caught by a gamekeeper murdered him to protect their illicit rabbit-hunting operation. As they prepared to bury him in a sandpit, the gamekeeper—who was not quite dead—spoke up and swore to haunt them for eternity. True to his dying threat, a ghostly hearse accompanied by a coffin and pallbearers emerges from the sandpit every midnight, moving slowly down the Thetford road before disappearing through a nearby field gate.

#location
A sandpit adjacent to the Thetford road on Croxton Heath in the parish of Croxton, Norfolk.

#title
The Mad Axeman of Brumstead

#summary
A fictional woodchopper who murdered his family in a remote cottage became a terrifying local legend after being deliberately planted in school essays.

#details
As a classroom experiment to test how legends are born, a Norfolk schoolteacher had his students invent a local ghost. A pupil named John created the tale of a deranged woodchopper who slaughtered his wife and children in a ruined cottage before being hanged for his crimes. His restless spirit supposedly haunted the derelict building, wailing for his lost family. The teacher actively propagated the fiction among other classes and homework assignments. By the following year, the concocted phantom had taken on a life of its own, with local villagers firmly believing in the Mad Axeman and warning relatives to avoid the haunted cottage after dark.

#location
The ghost haunts a half-derelict ruined cottage situated up a road near the school in the village of Brumstead, Norfolk.

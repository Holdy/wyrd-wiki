#title
The Witch's Wicked Love Potion

#summary
To secure a wealthy bachelor's hand in marriage for her daughter, an ambitious mother enlisted a local witch to brew a potent love potion, triggering a horrifying supernatural reckoning on the wedding night.

#details
In 1741, Lady Carew of Potter Heigham was determined that her daughter, Evelyn, should marry the astoundingly wealthy Sir Godfrey Haslitt of Bastwick. When Sir Godfrey showed no interest in Evelyn, Lady Carew turned to a local witch who brewed a powerful love potion. Refusing monetary payment, the sorceress demanded instead that if the potion worked, Lady Carew must grant her whatever she subsequently asked for. 

The charmed liquid was secretly slipped into Sir Godfrey's drink during a hunting party in King's Lynn. Instantly, Sir Godfrey fell madly in love, and he and Evelyn were married on May 31, 1741, in Norwich before returning to Bastwick for the wedding reception. However, as the clock struck midnight, the doors flew open to reveal a grinning skeleton. The spectral figure seized the bride in its boney arms, flung her into a carriage, and galloped off toward Potter Heigham. The wedding party gave chase, but as the coach reached Potter Heigham Bridge, it burst into flames and plunged into the river, claiming Lady Evelyn as the dreadful payment demanded by the witch.

#location
Sir Godfrey Haslitt's estate was located in the village of Bastwick within the civil parish of Repps with Bastwick in Norfolk, England.

#title
The Phantoms of the Wartime Airfield

#summary
The historic structures of the former RAF base extending into Helhoughton parish are frequently reported to be haunted by the residual echoes and spectral manifestations of wartime personnel. Visitors and investigators have encountered unexplained sounds, shadowy figures, and eerie phenomena lingering around the old control tower and command bunkers.

#details
Opened in 1939 and operating through World War II and the Cold War before closing in 1994, West Raynham Airfield—which extends across the parish of Helhoughton—has long been associated with ghostly encounters. Staff, paranormal investigators, and local residents have documented various uncanny occurrences across the sprawling military site. Footsteps echo through empty control rooms, disembodied voices chatter in derelict bunkers, and shadowy airmen or wartime personnel are glimpsed moving through the former command headquarters and watch offices. The heavy atmosphere of the base preserves the memory of its turbulent past, making it one of Norfolk's most notable haunted military installations.

#location
The key location is the historic control tower and former military airfield buildings situated within the parish of Helhoughton, Norfolk (Grid Reference TF 8482 2470).

#title
The Faines

#summary
Calf-sized spectral creatures with glowing saucer eyes and giant silent black dogs haunt the roads and playing fields of Hethersett. Witnesses have reported these phantom hounds padding alongside cyclists before vanishing into thin air.

#details
Historical records and modern accounts document uncanny canine apparitions in Hethersett. In the early 20th century, antiquary Walter Rye noted calf-sized creatures with saucer-like glowing eyes frequenting Mill Road and Gravel Pit Lane, with one witness describing an entity that shed light like a bicycle lamp. Decades later, a local worker cycling along Henstead Road was shadowed by a silent, huge rough-coated black dog that vanished at a bend. Another witness recounting an experience at the Hethersett Memorial Playing Field described a similar towering hound that paced alongside him before disappearing completely behind a brick toilet block despite clear visibility.

#location
Hethersett Memorial Playing Field in Hethersett, South Norfolk, can be located via Recreation Road or Canns Lane.

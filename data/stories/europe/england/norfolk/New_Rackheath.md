#title
The Prophecy of Stone Hill

#summary
According to regional folklore and prophecies attributed to Mother Shipton, Stone Hill in Rackheath is destined to be the site of a cataclysmic battle where nearly everyone will perish. During this dreadful conflict, a three-thumbed miller will hold the horses of three competing kings.

#details
For generations, local folklore in Norfolk has preserved a grim prophecy concerning a future apocalyptic war. Recorded in early antiquarian notes and publications such as *Norfolk Archaeology*, the legend states that a massive and bloody battle will take place at Stone Hill in Rackheath. The slaughter during this fight is said to be so horrific that nearly every man in the county will be killed, and the blood will pool and clot so deeply that ravens will carry pieces of it away. Central to this terrifying vision is a mysterious miller born with three thumbs, whose fateful role in the conflict will be to hold the horses of three kings while they fight for dominion over the realm.

#location
Stone Hill (TG277143 area) in the parish of Rackheath, Norfolk, England.

#title
The Curse of Langley Cross

#summary
A cursed stone cross brought disaster to a noble estate when it was moved from its original site near the abbey. True to the prophecy, Langley Hall was struck by fire shortly after the relocation.

#details
Langley Cross carried a traditional curse stating that if it were ever removed from its original site near the abbey, Langley Hall would be gutted by fire. In 1801, Lady Beauchamp disregarded the folklore and had the cross moved into the park grounds to mark a parish meeting-place. True to the warning, a destructive fire broke out near the estate around the same time, burning down the predecessor of Abbey Farm and causing a turret of the Hall to catch fire.

#location
Langley Cross stood near the abbey precincts at the corner of a wood known as The Thicks in Langley, Norfolk.

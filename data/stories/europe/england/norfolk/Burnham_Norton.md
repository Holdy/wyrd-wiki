#title
The Wishing Wells of Burnham Norton

#summary
According to local tradition, the monks of Burnham Norton Friary walked to nearby holy wells where drinking the cool water was said to make wishes come true.

#details
Founded in 1241, the Carmelite friary at Burnham Norton was home to friars who regularly walked out to nearby springs. These wells, known locally as "wishing wells" and marked on the 1887 Ordnance Survey map as "Our Lady's Well," are situated close to a small stream just north of the friary ruins. Local folklore maintained that drinking the cool water from these wells possessed the magical property of making wishes come true.

#location
Our Lady's Well is located near a small stream just north of the Carmelite Friary ruins in Burnham Norton, Norfolk (TF838428).

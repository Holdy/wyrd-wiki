#title
The Ghost Dog of the Railway Crossing

#summary
A phantom hound killed by a passing steam train is said to reappear at midnight on the anniversary of its death near the old railway crossing. Local residents were long warned to fear crossing the tracks after dark because of the lingering spirit.

#details
Passed down through family memory, local lore tells of a ghost dog that haunts the old Midland and Great Northern railway crossing in Hempton. The canine met its end after being struck by a steam train while returning to "The Grove," an old house near the railway station. On the anniversary of its death, the spectral hound returns at midnight, leaving a lasting dread in anyone traveling across the line at night.

#location
The haunting takes place at the old Midland and Great Northern railway crossing (grid reference TF917292) located next to the railway station in Hempton, Norfolk.

#title
The Phantom Hound of the Hempton Railway Crossing

#summary
A spectral dog reportedly haunts a former railway crossing at midnight on the anniversary of its death after being struck by a steam train.

#details
Passed down through family memory from Nellie Best, who moved to the area in 1930, an old legend tells of a ghost dog that appears at midnight each year at the former railway crossing. The phantom hound was believed to have been killed by a passing steam train while making its way home to the Grove, a rambling old house situated next to the local railway station. Although the exact date of the tragedy was lost to time, the chilling tale lingered in the minds of local children traversing the tracks after dark.

#location
The events take place at the former Midland and Great Northern Joint Railway crossing (grid reference TF917292) and the adjacent site of the old railway station and the Grove in Hempton, Norfolk.

#title
The Ghost of Queen Isabella at Castle Rising Castle

#summary
The upper rooms of the medieval Castle Rising Castle are said to be haunted by Queen Isabella, whose final years were marked by violent mental decline. Visitors and local villagers have reported hearing her hysterical laughter and chilling shrieks echoing from the empty top floors during the early morning hours.

#details
According to local legend, Queen Isabella—infamously known as the "She-Wolf of France" for her role in the murder of her husband, Edward II—spent her later years residing at Castle Rising. Although historical records indicate she lived in regal comfort, popular tradition maintains that her mind deteriorated into violent dementia towards the end of her life. To this day, visitors touring the castle have been startled by the sound of hysterical cackling emanating from the top floor. Furthermore, residents in the surrounding village are occasionally disturbed in the dead of night by ghostly shrieks and maniacal laughter echoing across the countryside from the ancient keep.

#location
Castle Rising Castle is a 12th-century medieval fortress situated in the village of Castle Rising, just north of King's Lynn in Norfolk, England.

#title
The Madness of the She-Wolf

#summary
The upper rooms of Castle Rising are haunted by the crazed spirit of Queen Isabella, who is said to pace the corridors and fill the night air with manic laughter and shrieks. Visitors and locals have reported hearing her terrifying outbursts and the rustle of her dress in the historic keep where she spent her final years.

#details
Following the execution of her lover Roger Mortimer and the assumption of power by her son Edward III, Queen Isabella lived at Castle Rising for decades until her death in 1358. Legend holds that her final years were plagued by violent dementia and mental instability. To this day, ghostly phenomena plague the 12th-century keep, particularly the upper floors and the White Room. Locals and visitors have reported hearing bloodcurdling shrieks, hysterical cackling echoing across the surrounding countryside in the early morning hours, and the phantom sound of a dress brushing against the floors.

#location
Castle Rising Castle is located off Lynn Road (PE31 6AH) in the village of Castle Rising, just northeast of King's Lynn in Norfolk.

#title
The Nailed Effigies of Castle Rising

#summary
In 1962, parishioners in Castle Rising discovered human effigies and a thorn-studded sheep's heart nailed to their church door in an act of malicious folk magic. This mysterious event later became the subject of a BBC documentary archive investigating ritualistic magic in Norfolk.

#details
In 1962, the quiet Norfolk village of Castle Rising was disturbed by a grim discovery when parishioners found human effigies and a thorn-studded sheep's heart nailed directly to their church door. Regarded by historians as a tangible example of real-world *maleficium*—harmful folk magic or attempted witchcraft—this physical act mirrored ancient traditions of cursing and magical attacks. The unsettling occurrence caught national attention when broadcaster Chris Brasher investigated it for a 1964 BBC *Tonight* documentary, discussing the symbols and rituals found across Norfolk churches with occult commentator F.R. Buckley. Historian Malcolm Gaskill later highlighted this incident in academic writings as a rare, historical instance of aggressive magic and witchcraft attempts in modern Britain.

#location
The door of St Lawrence Church in the village of Castle Rising, Norfolk.

#upstream-source
Malcolm Gaskill, *Witchcraft: A Very Short Introduction* (Oxford University Press, 2010); BBC *Tonight* broadcast (1964).

#title
The Phantom Wolf of the She-Wolf

#summary
Queen Isabella, renowned as the She-Wolf of France, haunts the upper chambers of Castle Rising accompanied by the spectral apparition of her pet wolf. Paranormal investigators and visitors have captured photographic evidence of the phantom canine alongside reports of ghostly howls and rustling medieval garments.

#details
Following decades spent residing at Castle Rising in Norfolk, Queen Isabella's spirit is said to remain tied to the fortress where she lived out her later years. During a night-time paranormal investigation in the castle's White Room, researchers captured a full-spectrum photograph depicting the shadowy figure of a medieval queen accompanied by a distinct dog or wolf-shaped entity at her feet. Witnesses have also reported hearing the swishing sound of heavy dresses sweeping across stone staircases and phantom animal howls echoing through the ancient stone keep in the dead of night.

#location
The White Room on the upper floor of Castle Rising Castle in the village of Castle Rising, Norfolk.

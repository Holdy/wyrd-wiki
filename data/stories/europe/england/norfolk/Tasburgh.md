#title
Old Shocks on the Lane to Flordon

#summary
Locals became convinced they had witnessed a spectral phantom known as "Old Shocks" haunting the pathway between Tasburgh and Flordon railway station. The apparition was regarded with superstitious dread by those traveling the rural lane.

#details
Residents living near Tasburgh and Flordon maintained a firm belief in the existence of a ghostly black hound, locally referred to as "Old Shocks." Sightings of the spectral creature were reported along the lane leading from Tasburgh toward the old Flordon railway station, striking fear into travelers navigating the rural route after dark.

#location
The sighting occurred along the lane running between Tasburgh and the former Flordon railway station in Norfolk.

#title
The Apparition of Amy Robsart

#summary
The tragic Elizabethan noblewoman Amy Robsart is said to haunt the grounds of Rainthorpe Hall every September. On the anniversary of her death, her apparition appears in the gardens alongside a gentleman.

#details
Amy Robsart, whose mysterious death at the foot of a staircase in 1560 sparked historic scandal, is said to return to Rainthorpe Hall due to her family's close ties to the estate. According to local lore, every year on the evening of September 8th, her ghostly figure is spotted meeting a mysterious gentleman in the pleasure gardens of the Elizabethan manor.

#location
Rainthorpe Hall is a historic Elizabethan manor house located near Tasburgh in Norfolk, positioned on the west bank of the River Tas.

#title
The Spectral Apparition of Rainthorpe Hall

#summary
On the anniversary of her mysterious Elizabethan death, the tragic noblewoman Amy Robsart is said to return as a specter, appearing alongside a gentleman in the gardens of Rainthorpe Hall.

#details
Amy Robsart, the ill-fated wife of Robert Dudley (the Earl of Leicester) whose suspicious death in 1560 at Cumnor Place has baffled historians for centuries, is woven into local Norfolk folklore. According to tradition, after her former residences were demolished, her wandering spirit travelled back to Rainthorpe Hall in Tasburgh. Every year on the anniversary of her passing, the ghostly figure of Amy is said to materialize in the historic hall's gardens, sitting quietly alongside a mysterious gentleman companion—sparking ongoing local debate over whether the spirit beside her is her husband or her half-brother.

#location
Rainthorpe Hall, situated in the parish of Tasburgh, south of Norwich in Norfolk, England.

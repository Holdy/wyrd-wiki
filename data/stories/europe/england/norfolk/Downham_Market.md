#title
The Fugitive King at the Swan

#summary
Following his defeat in the English Civil War, King Charles I sought secret refuge in Downham Market disguised as a clergyman while hiding from Parliamentary pursuers.

#details
In late April and early May of 1646, as the English Civil War drew to a desperate close following his defeat at the Battle of Naseby and his escape from the besieged city of Oxford, King Charles I fled northward across the Fens. Traveling in disguise with his iconic long, curly hair cut short and using the alias "Harry", the fugitive monarch arrived in Downham Market. On May Day in 1646, he sought sanctuary and hid inside an inn that once stood on the site of the modern Swan Inn, desperately attempting to evade capture by Oliver Cromwell's Parliamentary forces before continuing his journey toward the Scottish army camp.

#location
The site of the Swan Inn located within the market town of Downham Market, Norfolk.

#title
The Spectre of The Crown

#summary
The historic Crown pub in Downham Market is haunted by the spirit of a young woman named Emily, whose footsteps and fleeting apparition are frequently encountered by staff in the 15th-century rooms above the old bar.

#details
The ancient Crown pub in Downham Market, featuring parts dating back to the 1400s, is said to be haunted by a young woman named Emily. According to the landlord, Emily was a young lady who was murdered in one of the bedrooms situated directly above the old bar during the 18th century. Staff and the landlord have frequently heard Emily moving about upstairs when the building is empty. The landlord has also caught brief, hazy glimpses of her on the upper landing and in the restaurant area as he clears up after service, with her apparition vanishing almost instantly in less than a second.

#location
The Crown pub is located in the town of Downham Market, Norfolk.

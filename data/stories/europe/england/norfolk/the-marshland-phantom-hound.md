#title
The Marshland Phantom Hound

#summary
While keeping vigil in the lonely marshes near Wells-next-the-Sea, a writer was stalked and nearly pounced upon by a terrifying, giant black spectral hound with glowing eyes.

#details
Intrigued by local legends of the Shuck Dog haunting the coastal salt-lands and marshes between Wells and Sheringham, author Christopher Marlowe resolved to spend the night waiting in the marsh near Stiffkey. Settling into a hollow beside a pool near the high-tide mark under a half-moon, he heard a chilling howl and spotted an indefinable shadow approaching. As the creature drew closer, he clearly made out a massive black dog with ferocious, glowing eyes sniffing the ground like a hunter. Realizing he was the quarry, Marlowe fled in absolute terror back toward his lodging lane, feeling the beast's scorching breath on the back of his neck before narrowly escaping inside as the hound's heavy body slammed against the door.

#location
The coastal salt marshes and lane situated approximately 800 yards from the A149 near Stiffkey, between Wells-next-the-Sea and Sheringham in Norfolk.

#upstream-source
Christopher Marlowe: 'People and Places in Marshland' (Palmer, 1927).

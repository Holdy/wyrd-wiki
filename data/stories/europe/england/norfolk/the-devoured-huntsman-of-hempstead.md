#title
The Devoured Huntsman of Hempstead

#summary
A ruthless huntsman suffered a gruesome fate when he was attacked and devoured by his own pack of hounds. This macabre event became a haunting legend deeply rooted in the folklore of Hempstead.

#details
Local tradition in Hempstead preserves the dark tale of a huntsman whose cruelty caught up with him in the most horrific manner possible, resulting in his hounds turning on him and devouring him alive. Over time, this terrifying narrative became intertwined with regional legends of eccentric and doomed local figures, such as those associated with the Brograve family and 'Owd Sir Barney'.

#location
The legend is centered around the village of Hempstead in North Norfolk, located near Holt.

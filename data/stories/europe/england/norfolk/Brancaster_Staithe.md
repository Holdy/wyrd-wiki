#title
The Haunting of the Ship Hotel

#summary
The historic Ship Hotel is said to be haunted by the spectral presence of the young Lord Nelson's nanny, who continues to watch over the establishment. Guests and visitors to the coastal inn have long whispered of her phantom presence lingering in the old rooms.

#details
Located in the coastal parish near Brancaster Staithe, the Ship Hotel has served travelers and locals for centuries. Among its tales of the past is the persistent local legend that the inn is haunted by the ghost of the nursemaid who cared for Horatio Nelson during his youth in nearby Burnham Thorpe. Though the famous naval hero grew up a few miles away, local folklore maintains that his devoted nanny's spirit found a lasting home at the coastal hotel, where her ghostly footsteps and gentle presence are occasionally reported by staff and overnight guests.

#location
The Ship Hotel is situated along the main coast road in the village of Brancaster, just west of Brancaster Staithe on the north Norfolk coast.

#title
The Battle Slopes of Bloods Dale

#summary
Local legend maintains that the valley of Bloods Dale in Drayton was the site of a ferocious historical battle between the Saxons and the Danes, where the slaughter was so great that the earth became stained with their blood. Subsequent archaeological discoveries of ancient cremations and human skeletons in the vicinity have lent enduring weight to the grim tale.

#details
Nestled between the A1067 and the road connecting Drayton to Hellesdon, near the historic ruins of Drayton Lodge, lies a sloping depression known locally as Bloods Dale. According to regional folklore, these slopes witnessed a desperate and bloody clash between invading Danes and defending Saxons. The tradition of wartime slaughter passed down through generations was seemingly corroborated by physical discoveries in the 19th century, when an early Saxon cremation cemetery was unearthed in the valley, followed later by the discovery of thirteen skeletons during nearby railway construction. To this day, the hollow way stands as a quiet monument to the rumored ancient carnage.

#location
Bloods Dale is a sloping valley located in Drayton, Norfolk, situated between the A1067 and the road to Hellesdon near Drayton Lodge (TG185130).

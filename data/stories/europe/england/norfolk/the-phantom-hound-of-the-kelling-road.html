The Phantom Hound of the Kelling Road

Summary

While walking along the moonlight road between Salthouse and Kelling, a traveler heard rattling chains and encountered the monstrous phantom dog Black Shuck, which glided straight through a closed gate.

Details

Sometime in the early 1930s, a man pushing his bicycle along the road leading from Salthouse toward Kelling on a clear night illuminated by a full moon heard the chilling sound of rattling iron chains drawing closer behind him. Believing the noise might be a horse that had strayed off the nearby marshes, he stepped to the side of the road to let it pass. Instead, a massive, shaggy black dog—the legendary Black Shuck—trotted past him and passed effortlessly through a closed five-barred gate opposite. When the startled witness immediately checked the gate, he found it still firmly closed. When he recounted the strange encounter to locals in Kelling the following day, residents unremarkably told him it was only Old Shuck, who frequently roamed those roads.

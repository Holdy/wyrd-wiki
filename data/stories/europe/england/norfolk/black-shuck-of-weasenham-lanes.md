#title
Black Shuck of Weasenham Lanes

#summary
While staying at her late great aunt's cottage in Weasenham, a recovering librarian is pursued by the spectral black hound Black Shuck along the misty autumn lanes near Weasenham Woods.

#details
While visiting her late Great Aunt Iris's cottage in Weasenham to sort her affairs and recover from a prolonged illness, the narrator takes evening walks through the chilly, mist-shrouded lanes near Weasenham Woods. During these walks, she has terrifying personal encounters with Black Shuck, a monstrous black hound with glowing red eyes stalking the lanes. The chilling experiences culminate in a frantic chase where the phantom dog pursues the narrator into the path of an oncoming motorist.

#location
The lanes surrounding Weasenham Woods, within the parish of Weasenham in Norfolk, England, serve as the haunting grounds for Black Shuck.

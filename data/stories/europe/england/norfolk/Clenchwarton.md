#title
The Secret Tunnel of Clenchwarton

#summary
A local legend tells of a hidden underground passage that once connected the moated Margaretta House to St. Margaret's Church in Clenchwarton.

#details
The original moated Margaretta House, located at grid reference TF596200, was destroyed by a fire long ago. In the wake of its destruction, local tradition maintained that a secret underground tunnel ran from the manor house all the way to St. Margaret's Church, situated approximately 500 meters away. Though no physical trace of the passage has ever been uncovered, the tale persists as a piece of local lore concerning hidden subterranean connections between historic village landmarks.

#location
St. Margaret's Church and the former site of Margaretta House (TF596200) in Clenchwarton, Norfolk.

#title
The Spirit of Margaretta House

#summary
Margaretta House in Clenchwarton is believed to be haunted by the ghost of a woman named Margaret who died in the building and whose grave remains on the property.

#details
The original moated Margaretta House burned down long ago, but a subsequent house built on the site inherited eerie legends. Local folklore states that a woman named Margaret lived and died in the house, and her grave is situated outside the front door at the rear of the property, where her spirit is said to linger. Furthermore, tradition claims an underground secret tunnel once linked the moated estate to St. Margaret's Church approximately 500 meters away.

#location
Margaretta House is situated at a moated historic site (grid reference TF596200) in Clenchwarton, Norfolk.

#title
The Phantom Hound of RAF Oulton

#summary
During World War Two, an airman walking to his military station in Oulton encountered a silent, spectral black dog standing in the moonlight near Blickling Hall before it vanished into thin air.

#details
In the early years of the Second World War, an airman was walking from Aylsham toward his station at RAF Oulton in Norfolk. As he passed by the moonlit grounds of Blickling Hall, he glanced across the parkland and noticed a massive black dog observing him from a short distance away. The beast's mouth was wide open as though howling or roaring, yet no sound emanated from it. After watching him for a few terrifying seconds and filling him with an overwhelming sense of dread, the spectral hound vanished completely into thin air.

#location
The grounds of Blickling Hall adjacent to the former RAF Oulton site in Oulton, Norfolk.

#title
The Soldiers of Cromwell's Rest

#summary
Local legend whispers that soldiers who fought under Oliver Cromwell during the English Civil War rest in unmarked graves within a village churchyard. Their presence and the specific reasons for their burial remain shrouded in mystery.

#details
According to local tradition, several of Oliver Cromwell's men are interred within the churchyard of St. Peter and St. Paul in the Norfolk village of Oulton. While the exact historical circumstances of their deaths and presence in the area have faded from written records, oral tradition maintains that these parliamentary soldiers were laid to rest on the hallowed grounds. To this day, the exact placement of their graves within the churchyard remains completely unknown.

#location
The graves are said to be located within the churchyard of St. Peter and St. Paul in Oulton, Norfolk (grid reference TG136285).

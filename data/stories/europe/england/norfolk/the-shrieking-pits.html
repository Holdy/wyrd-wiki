The Shrieking Pits

Summary

A spectral woman in white haunts the ancient iron-working hollows between Aylmerton and West Runton Heath, weeping and wailing as she searches every pit for her murdered baby. According to local legend, her husband killed both their child and her, leaving her spirit to wander the depressions in the ground in eternal despair.

Details

Between the 9th and 12th centuries, extensive iron-working activity across the wooded slopes between Aylmerton and West Runton Heath left behind numerous circular depressions and hollows in the earth. Over time, these pits became the center of a tragic local legend. It is said that a man, convinced that his infant child was not his own, murdered the baby and buried it in one of the pits near St John's Church before returning home to kill his wife as well. Ever since, the ghostly figure of the woman has been witnessed wandering through the hollows on dark nights. Clad in white, she peers desperately into each pit, shrieking in anguish when she discovers each one empty, trapped forever in her hopeless search for her lost child.

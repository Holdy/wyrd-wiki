#title
The Phantom Hound of the Holt Road

#summary
While driving to visit family, a motorist and his wife encountered a massive, calf-like spectral hound crossing the road directly in front of their car.

#details
A man and his wife were driving to his mother's home in Holt when a strange entity, described as looking like a dog or a calf resembling the legendary Black Shuck, suddenly crossed the road in front of their vehicle. The apparition appeared near the Tithe Barn on the borders of Letheringsett and Holt, just before reaching the old Holt Rectory.

#location
The encounter took place near the Tithe Barn on the borders of Letheringsett and Holt, just before the old Holt Rectory in Norfolk.

The Demonic Hound of Old Scarfe

Summary

A depraved 13th-century baron was transformed by the Devil into a fiery-eyed, chained hellhound that haunts Southtown Road and the vaults of a local inn. To this day, the spectral beast terrorizes benighted travelers with sorrowful moans and clanking chains.

Details

Baron Rudolph Scarfe was a wicked and depraved German man notorious in the 13th century for his evil deeds who sought asylum in England, settling at Burgh Castle and terrorizing the people of Great Yarmouth. When Scarfe was eventually killed, the Devil transformed him into a black hellhound with glowing red eyes and heavy chains, sending him back to earth. Although an attempt was made to exorcise the spectre—dooming the evil spirit to remain in a vault under the Duke's Head Inn until the river should cease to run under Yarmouth bridge—Old Scarfe continues to rush up and down Southtown Road on dark winter nights, making sorrowful moanings and dragging a clanking chain behind him.

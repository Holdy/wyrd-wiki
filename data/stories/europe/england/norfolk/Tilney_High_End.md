#title
The Legend of Tom Hickathrift

#summary
Born in the Norfolk Marshland, the prodigious young giant Tom Hickathrift used his immense strength to defeat local beasts and demons before leaving his mark upon the landscape and churches of Tilney. Legend holds that his final resting place and legendary exploits are immortalized by ancient stone monuments and grave markers in the village.

#details
Tom Hickathrift was born in the Norfolk Marshland as the son of a poor laborer. Even by the age of ten, he grew to an enormous height and possessed superhuman strength, though he was initially known to be quite lazy. Eventually, Tom became the champion of the Marshland, performing legendary feats such as defeating a fierce ogre or beast on the Smeeth and hurling heavy stones or balls vast distances across the fens. According to local tradition, after kicking a stone that landed in Tilney, he declared it would mark his final resting place. For centuries, villagers pointed to an ancient stone coffin and medieval lid featuring carved wheel-and-axle motifs in the churchyard at Tilney All Saints as the true tomb of the Norfolk giant.

#location
All Saints' Church in Tilney All Saints (grid reference TF 5685 1795), located in the West Norfolk marshland region near King's Lynn.

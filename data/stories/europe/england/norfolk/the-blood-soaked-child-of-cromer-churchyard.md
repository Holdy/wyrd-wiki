#title
The Blood-Soaked Child of Cromer Churchyard

#summary
While clearing debris in the ruined churchyard of St. Peter and St. Paul, a caretaker was slashed across the throat by the terrifying apparition of a murdered child wearing blood-soaked white clothes. The spectral figure vanished instantly, leaving behind a chilling sighing sound in the air.

#details
During the period when the chancel of the church of St. Peter and St. Paul in Cromer lay in ruins, a caretaker was working on-site to clear away debris and overgrowth. Without warning, he was confronted by a horrifying apparition of a murdered child dressed in white garments that appeared entirely soaked in blood. The ghostly figure inflicted a mysterious and violent gash across the caretaker's throat before disappearing in a flash, leaving the worker shaken as a heavy sigh echoed in his ears.

#location
The churchyard of St. Peter and St. Paul (Cromer Parish Church) located on Church Street in Cromer, Norfolk.

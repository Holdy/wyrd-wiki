#title
The Secret Tunnels of Magazine Cottage

#summary
Local tradition holds that a subterranean passage links Sedgeford's parish church to Magazine Cottage, while a second secret tunnel supposedly runs from the cottage all the way to the coast at Heacham.

#details
Local legend tells of two secret underground passages connected to Magazine Cottage, a 17th-century building originally erected as a gunpowder store during the English Civil War. One tunnel is said to run eastward across the parish to St Mary's Church, while another subterranean passage allegedly leads westward from the cottage several miles across the countryside to the sea at Heacham.

#location
St Mary's Church is situated in the village of Sedgeford, Norfolk (TF707354), and Magazine Cottage is located at TF722369.

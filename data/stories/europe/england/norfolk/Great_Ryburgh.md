#title
The Spirits of the Coaching Inn

#summary
The historic Blue Boar Inn is haunted by friendly phantom guests who have never quite checked out, accompanied by local legends of secret underground passages linking the building to the parish church across the road.

#details
Established in 1685 as a coaching inn during the reign of King Charles II, The Blue Boar Inn has witnessed centuries of travellers, horse-drawn transit, and village life along the River Wensum. Over the generations, local folklore has preserved tales of secret tunnels once connecting the inn's ancient cellars directly to St Andrew's Church situated opposite. Alongside these subterranean legends, tradition maintains that friendly resident spirits continue to inhabit the low-beamed rooms and gather near the massive inglenook fireplace.

#location
The Blue Boar Inn is a historic building situated on The Street in Great Ryburgh, directly opposite St Andrew's Church.

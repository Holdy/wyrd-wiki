#title
The Cannon of Pepper Hill

#summary
A round barrow in Weeting parish received its name from the local tradition that Oliver Cromwell positioned his cannon upon it to bombard the nearby medieval manor.

#details
Near the medieval church and the ruins of Weeting Castle in Norfolk, a round barrow known as Pepper Hill (or Pepper High Hill) stands about a mile away. Local folklore attributes the barrow's name to the belief that during the English Civil War, Oliver Cromwell stationed his cannon atop the mound so that he could "pepper" the castle with artillery fire.

#location
Pepper Hill is a round barrow located in the parish of Weeting, Norfolk, at grid reference TL787882.

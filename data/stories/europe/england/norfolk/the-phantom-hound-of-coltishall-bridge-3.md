#title
The Phantom Hound of Coltishall Bridge

#summary
A massive, silent black hound known as Black Shuck—often considered a manifestation of the devil—terrorises the bridge and high street of Coltishall as an ominous spectral presence. Two RAF officers famously encountered the phantom beast before it mysteriously vanished into thin air on the village cobbles.

#details
For centuries, the Norfolk village of Coltishall has been haunted by legends of Black Shuck, a terrifying phantom hound viewed by locals as either the devil's dog or the devil himself. Described as a silent beast as large as a calf with blazing red eyes, Shuck is frequently sighted prowling Coltishall Bridge at the upper reaches of the River Bure. In addition to serving as a foreboding omen of death, the creature startled two Royal Air Force officers when it appeared directly on the cobbles of Coltishall High Street before completely vanishing into thin air.

#location
Coltishall Bridge spans the River Bure in the Norfolk village of Coltishall, UK.

#title
The Ghost of Gressenhall Workhouse

#summary
Staff members at a former workhouse experienced inexplicable phenomena, including lights turning themselves on, heavy footsteps, and objects moving overnight in the upper-floor Spong Room.

#details
When the Norfolk Archaeological Unit moved into the east wing of the former Gressenhall Workhouse (known as Union House) in the 1970s, workers in the first-floor Spong Room experienced persistent strange occurrences. Lights left turned off at night were frequently found on in the mornings, stacks of empty finds boxes were moved across the room, and windows would fly open by themselves. Former staff from when the building operated as an old people's home also recounted a local tradition of a "Green Lady" haunting the room. Additional unsettling incidents included a wall clock being mysteriously displaced into a sink full of water, heavy footsteps knocking over boxes in the corridors late at night, and an archaeologist being closely followed by unseen footsteps along the building's driveway.

#location
The Spong Room is located on the first floor of Union House at the former Gressenhall Farm and Workhouse in Gressenhall, Norfolk.

#title
The Ghost of Gressenhall Workhouse

#summary
Staff and visitors at Gressenhall Workhouse experienced unexplained paranormal phenomena in the Spong Room, including lights turning on by themselves, moving boxes, flying windows, and phantom footsteps.

#details
When the Norfolk Archaeological Unit occupied the east wing of Gressenhall Workhouse in the 1970s, persistent paranormal activity was reported in the first-floor Spong Room. Lights left turned off at night were regularly found illuminated in the mornings, heavy boxes were mysteriously rearranged, and windows flew open on their own. Later, staff members reported hearing heavy footsteps and crashing boxes in the corridors at night, while an archaeologist walking down the building's drive after dark was persistently followed by unseen footsteps until she sought refuge at a nearby house.

#location
The Spong Room is situated on the first floor near the north-east stairs in the east wing of Gressenhall Farm and Workhouse in Norfolk.

#title
The Epitaph of William Salter

#summary
William Salter, a Yarmouth stage-coachman killed in a coaching accident on the steep hill in Haddiscoe in 1776, is commemorated by a celebrated rhyming epitaph built into the churchyard wall of St Mary's Church.

#details
William Salter worked as a stage-coachman driving the Yarmouth stage during the eighteenth century. On October 9, 1776, at the age of 59, his life came to a tragic end when his coach overturned on the steep hill running against St Mary's Church in the Norfolk village of Haddiscoe. He was laid to rest in the village churchyard, and a stone wall monument was set into the churchyard wall bearing a famous rhyming epitaph praising his honesty, dependability, and care for his horses, concluding with the poetic wish that he might drive straight up to heaven like Elijah. Over the centuries, the memorial has become a celebrated local landmark and piece of regional folklore, widely recorded in historical chronicles of the coaching age.

#location
The monument is set into the churchyard wall of St Mary's Church on Church Lane in Haddiscoe, Norfolk.

#title
The Ghostly Figure of Haddiscoe Dam

#summary
A driver and his colleagues captured video footage of a mysterious, dark-clad figure walking along the treacherous and unpaved Haddiscoe Dam late at night. Local residents later confirmed that the accident-prone road has a long-standing reputation for being haunted.

#details
While driving home from work late at night along the twisty and unpaved Haddiscoe Dam, 26-year-old Aden Hudson and his two colleagues were entirely unaware of any pedestrians on the road. However, upon reviewing video footage of the foggy drive home, they discovered a chilling, shadowy figure dressed completely in black strolling along the roadside. Given the dangerous nature of the road and the absence of any pavements, seeing someone walking there at that hour was unheard of. Upon sharing the footage online, local residents informed him that the accident-prone route is widely regarded as a haunted road where ghostly apparitions are frequently reported.

#location
Haddiscoe Dam is a specific road located in Haddiscoe, Norfolk, UK.

#title
The Black-Hooded Monk of Binham Priory

#summary
During evening services and near midnight around the ancient ruins, witnesses have repeatedly reported seeing the phantom figure of a black-hooded Benedictine monk walking along a high interior ledge of Binham Parish Church and lingering near the historic Gaol Gate.

#details
Local accounts in Binham tell of eerie paranormal encounters centered around Binham Priory and its parish church, which serves as the ancient nave of the former monastery. During an evening service, a churchgoer witnessed a spectral figure resembling a Benedictine monk wearing a black cowl. The phantom slowly walked along a high stone ledge several feet above the floor, ascended a set of spiral steps, and vanished. A similar sighting was reported by another parishioner who watched a dark, monk-like figure repeatedly appear and disappear upon the ledge during a sermon. Beyond the church interior, villagers have also reported seeing the apparition of a Benedictine monk near the Gaol Gate at the entrance to the priory grounds, with local warnings advising people to stay away from the gateway at midnight.

#location
Binham Priory and Parish Church is situated on Priory Lane in the village of Binham, Norfolk, England.

#title
The Black-Hooded Monk of Binham Priory

#summary
A spectral Benedictine monk is frequently sighted walking along a high ledge inside the parish church and haunting the ruins of the medieval priory. Local folklore links the phantom to subterranean tunnels and the tragic imprisonment of a former prior.

#details
For generations, inhabitants and visitors of Binham have reported encounters with a mysterious figure wearing a black cowl and habit. Witnesses inside the parish church—which serves as the surviving nave of the ancient Binham Priory—have watched the phantom walk slowly along an elevated ledge near the church door before ascending spiral steps and vanishing into thin air. Other villagers have reported seeing the cowled specter near the priory's Gaol Gate or emerging from subterranean tunnels said to connect the priory to the shrine at Walsingham. Local folklore and historical accounts suggest the haunting may be linked to Alexander de Langley, a former prior who was imprisoned and punished in chains at Binham after losing his sanity.

#location
Binham Priory and its parish church are located in the heart of the village of Binham in North Norfolk.

#title
The Fiddler of Fiddler's Hill

#summary
A daring musician who ventured into a legendary underground tunnel connecting Blakeney Guildhall to Binham Priory vanished without a trace when his music abruptly ceased beneath a roadside mound. Locals whispered that the Devil himself had snatched him away, forever naming the site Fiddler's Hill.

#details
Local folklore long whispered of a mysterious subterranean tunnel running from Blakeney Guildhall all the way to Binham Priory, rumored to be haunted by a spectral, black-robed monk. To solve the mystery of the tunnels, a brave fiddler volunteered to walk underground while playing his instrument so townsfolk could track his progress above ground. However, when his music suddenly stopped underneath a Neolithic burial mound at a crossroads, searchers found no trace of him. Rumors quickly spread that the Devil had taken him, and the mound was subsequently named Fiddler's Hill in memory of his uncanny disappearance.

#location
Fiddler's Hill is an archaeological mound located at a crossroads on the North Norfolk coast between Blakeney and Binham Priory.

#title
The Haunting of Bumbler's Farm

#summary
At the close of the 19th century, Bumbler's Farm in Shelfanger became plagued by violent poltergeist activity and the apparition of a spectral woman after a cursed candlestick was disturbed from a farm pond.

#details
At the close of the 19th century, Bumbler's Farm in Shelfanger became the center of eerie paranormal events that terrified local residents, with doors slamming shut, a kitchen towel roller spinning without cause, and ornaments crashing to the floor. According to family lore later recounted by local farmer Thomas Clifton Porcher, the disturbances were linked to a former resident, Dinah Freeman, who tragically died in 1814 after plunging into a deep farm pond during a fever. Village superstition warned that a heavy candlestick taken into the water with her must never be removed from the mud. When the pond was cleaned and the candlestick was pulled out, immediate chaos erupted in the farmhouse with objects hurled about and a menacing female spirit appearing until the candlestick was hastily returned to the water.

#location
Bumbler's Farm was located in the rural village of Shelfanger, near the town of Diss in Norfolk.

#title
The Phantom Dog of Kelling

#summary
While pushing his bicycle along a moonlit road toward Kelling, a local man encountered a gigantic, shaggy black dog that walked silently past him and vanished through a closed gate. When he recounted the event to villagers the next day, they identified the beast as Old Shuck.

#details
On a clear night under a full moon, a local resident (recorded in various accounts as Leslie Goodwin or Tom Stirling/Starling) was pushing his bicycle along the road leading from Salthouse toward Kelling. He heard the chilling sound of rattling chains growing closer, initially believing it to be a stray horse from the marshes. Stepping aside to let it pass, he watched in astonishment as a massive, shaggy black dog padded silently by. The spectral hound proceeded to walk straight through a closed five-barred gate by the roadside. When the witness investigated immediately afterward, he found the gate still firmly shut. The following day, residents in Kelling confirmed that the apparition was Old Shuck, a phantom hound known to frequently roam the local roads.

#location
The encounter took place on the road running between Salthouse and Kelling in Norfolk, near a roadside gate.

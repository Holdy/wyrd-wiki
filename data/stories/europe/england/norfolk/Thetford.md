#title
Chunk Harvey's Grave

#summary
A notorious pirate was long believed to have been executed and staked at a remote crossroads, though historical records proved the site belonged to a local carpenter who died in 1786.

#details
Local tradition maintained that a pirate named Chunk Harvey had been betrayed by an ex-comrade, sentenced to death, and buried at the exact spot where the old road from Thetford to Euston crossed the ancient Icknield Way. According to the legend, Harvey was struck through the heart with a wooden stake, and a lone pine tree grew upon the spot from that very stake. In reality, parish and newspaper records from September 1786 identified the inhumation as that of Thomas Harvey, a local carpenter who hanged himself following a domestic dispute with his wife.

#location
The crossroads where the old road from Thetford to Euston crossed the Icknield Way, situated just east of Mill Road in Thetford.

#title
The Spectral Hound of Warren Lodge

#summary
A glowing-eyed spectral hound prowls the grounds and ruins of a 15th-century gamekeeper's lodge near Thetford, filling the surrounding woodland with eerie howls.

#details
Built in the 15th century as a gamekeeper's lodge to guard the woodland near Thetford, Warren Lodge has accumulated centuries of dark folklore, linked to violent former inhabitants and tragic deaths. Alongside apparitions like the Grey Lady and unexplained children's laughter, the ruins and grounds are famously haunted by a spectral hound with glowing eyes. This phantom beast prowls the estate at night, sending spine-chilling howls echoing through the trees to terrify visitors and paranormal investigators alike.

#location
Warren Lodge is a ruined 15th-century building situated in Thetford Forest, just outside Thetford in Norfolk.

#title
The Tunnel-Bound Haunting of Station Road

#summary
During childhood, two sisters living in a sinking semi-detached house in Thetford experienced moving objects, paranormal sounds, and sightings of a cone-headed figure and ghostly soldiers.

#details
Growing up in a small, white-fronted house on Station Road in Thetford, two young sisters experienced a sharp increase in paranormal activity as the rear of their aging home slowly subsided into subterranean tunnels running beneath the town. The children frequently heard unexplainable bumps in the night, found their toys mysteriously displaced, and had their pets spooked. One sister was regularly visited by a quiet, small man with a cone-shaped head who sat at the foot of her bed when she was frightened. Household items constantly went missing—such as hairbrushes discovered inside lunchboxes—culminating in an unsettling encounter by their father, who witnessed a troop of phantom soldiers marching straight through the house with their legs visibly sunken into the ground.

#location
The key location is the semi-detached house on Station Road in Thetford, Norfolk.

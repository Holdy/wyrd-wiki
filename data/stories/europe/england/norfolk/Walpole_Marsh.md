#title
King John's Lost Treasure at Walpole Marsh

#summary
In October 1216, King John's baggage train carrying royal jewels and valuables was swallowed by the rising tides and treacherous mudflats of the Wash near Walpole Marsh.

#details
According to centuries-old legend and historical accounts, on October 12, 1216, King John attempted to cross a shortcut across the tidal salt marshes of the Wash between Lincolnshire and Norfolk. While the King successfully crossed west of Lynn, his baggage train, servants, and soldiers carrying his accumulated wealth—including gold and silver plate and crown jewels—underestimated the speed of the incoming tide. The rapidly rising waters and quicksands engulfed the entire train, plunging the royal treasure into the shifting marshland where it has remained lost ever since.

#location
Walpole Marsh, situated in the Fenlands between Walpole Cross Keys and Sutton Bridge in Norfolk, England.

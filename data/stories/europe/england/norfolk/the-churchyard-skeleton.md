#title
The Churchyard Skeleton

#summary
A man who traded his soul to the devil to fund the construction of a village church died before the debt could be collected, leaving a skeletal spirit to haunt the consecrated grounds on the anniversary of his death.

#details
According to local folklore, a struggling man struck a bargain with the devil in exchange for immense wealth, using a portion of his ill-gotten fortune to build a chapel. When the devil returned years later to collect payment—holding the parchment bearing the man's signature—he discovered he was too late, as the man had died just hours earlier and was buried within the churchyard. Protected by the sacred ground, the man remained safely out of the devil's reach, but his spectral skeleton is said to haunt the churchyard every year on the anniversary of his death, clutching the parchment and attempting to reclaim his lost soul.

#location
The events of the story are set at St. Mary's Church in the village of Burgh St Peter, Norfolk (grid reference TM493938).

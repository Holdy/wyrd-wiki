#title
The Phantom Footmen of Barford

#summary
In the early hours of September 1, 2013, a motorist driving along Watton Road between Barford and Kimberley narrowly avoided three silent figures dressed in Georgian-era clothing standing in the middle of the road. Subsequent inquiries confirmed that no costume events or re-enactments were taking place in the area that night.

#details
At approximately 1:15 AM on September 1, 2013, a driver travelling along the quiet stretch of Watton Road between Barford and Kimberley had to slam on the brakes and swerve to avoid hitting three figures blocking the road. One of the apparitions was wrapped in a long, flowing red cloak, accompanied by two others dressed in the immaculate livery of eighteenth-century footmen. The figures appeared completely oblivious to the oncoming car, displaying no reaction or movement as the vehicle steered around them. Shaken by the encounter, the driver contacted nearby Kimberley Hall the next day to inquire if a fancy-dress ball or historical re-enactment had been held, but was informed that no such events were scheduled.

#location
The encounter occurred on Watton Road on the rural stretch between the villages of Barford and Kimberley in Norfolk.

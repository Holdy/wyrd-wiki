#title
The Ostrich Pub's Secret Tunnel

#summary
The cellar of the Ostrich pub in Castle Acre is said to conceal a bricked-up secret tunnel leading directly to the ruins of the medieval Cluniac priory.

#details
Local tradition maintains that beneath the historic Ostrich pub in Castle Acre, a subterranean passage exists that once allowed movement unseen to the nearby Castle Acre Priory. Although the building incorporates 18th and 19th-century architecture around an earlier core, locals have long pointed to a bricked-up entrance in the cellar as the starting point of this underground route.

#location
The cellar of the Ostrich pub is located at map reference TF81661514 in the village of Castle Acre, Norfolk.

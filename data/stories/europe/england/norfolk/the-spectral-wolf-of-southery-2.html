The Spectral Wolf of Southery

Summary

A monstrous, donkey-sized spectral wolf haunts the ruined church of St Mary in Southery on its annual feast day. Hearing the phantom beast's terrifying howl is said to bring certain death within a year.

Details

In the 14th century, monks building St Mary's church were protected by wolfhounds that eventually turned feral and terrorised the village before cannibalising each other. A single surviving female gave birth to an unearthly, ox-sized hybrid pup that faithfully served the villagers until it was attacked and mortally wounded by the bishop's guards. Ever since, on May 29 (Southery Feast Day), the ghost of this colossal black wolf returns to the ruins of St Mary's church, where anyone who crosses its path or hears its blood-curdling howls is doomed to die within twelve months.

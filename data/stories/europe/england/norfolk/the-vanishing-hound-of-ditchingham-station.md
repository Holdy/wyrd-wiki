#title
The Vanishing Hound of Ditchingham Station

#summary
While walking toward Ditchingham station in the autumn of 1938, a traveler encountered a massive, shaggy black dog that vanished into thin air right as it reached him. Local patrons at a nearby pub later identified the phantom as Black Shuck, the infamous devil dog.

#details
In the early autumn of 1938, a man from Aldeburgh was walking home from Bungay along the road towards Ditchingham station. He noticed a large, shaggy black dog approaching from the opposite direction on his side of the road. Stepping into the center of the road to allow the creature to pass, the traveler was startled when the animal drew level with him and instantly vanished into thin air. Upon recounting the bizarre encounter at a local pub, he was informed by residents that Black Shuck—the spectral devil dog—frequented the neighborhood and had been witnessed by many others.

#location
The encounter occurred on the road leading toward Ditchingham station in the village of Ditchingham, Norfolk, UK.

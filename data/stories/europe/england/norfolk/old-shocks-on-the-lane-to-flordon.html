Old Shocks on the Lane to Flordon

Summary

Locals became convinced they had witnessed a spectral phantom known as "Old Shocks" haunting the pathway between Tasburgh and Flordon railway station. The apparition was regarded with superstitious dread by those traveling the rural lane.

Details

Residents living near Tasburgh and Flordon maintained a firm belief in the existence of a ghostly black hound, locally referred to as "Old Shocks." Sightings of the spectral creature were reported along the lane leading from Tasburgh toward the old Flordon railway station, striking fear into travelers navigating the rural route after dark.

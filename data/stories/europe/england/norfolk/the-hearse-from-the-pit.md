#title
The Hearse from the Pit

#summary
A gamekeeper murdered by rabbit poachers on Croxton Heath returns as a phantom funeral hearse that rises from a sandpit at midnight.

#details
Long ago on Croxton Heath, a gamekeeper was set upon and killed by rabbit poachers, who loaded his body onto their cart to dispose of it. Finding a sandpit on the wild heath, they began to unload him, only for the gamekeeper—who was not quite dead—to sit up and swear he would haunt them forever. Startled by this warning, the poachers finished him off and buried him in the pit. Ever since, local lore maintains that a phantom hearse, complete with a coffin and pallbearers, rises from the sandpit at midnight, travels slowly down the Thetford road, and vanishes through a nearby field gate.

#location
A sandpit on Croxton Heath near the Thetford road in Croxton, Norfolk.

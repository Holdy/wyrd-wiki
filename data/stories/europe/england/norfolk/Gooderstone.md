#title
The Turning Milestone of the Gooderstone Road

#summary
A roadside milestone between Gooderstone and Cockley Cley was feared by local children because it was believed to physically rotate whenever church bells chimed in nearby Swaffham.

#details
Generations of children growing up near Gooderstone were taught to fear a specific milestone standing alongside the rural road leading towards Cockley Cley. According to local folklore, the stone marker possessed an eerie, animated life of its own, supposedly turning completely around on its base whenever the church bells in Swaffham rang out across the countryside.

#location
A roadside milestone located along the road connecting Gooderstone and Cockley Cley in southwest Norfolk.

#upstream-source
Letter in the 'East Anglian Magazine', Vol.8, No.8 (April 1949), p.427.

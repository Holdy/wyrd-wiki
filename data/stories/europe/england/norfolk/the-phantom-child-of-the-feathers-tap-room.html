The Phantom Child of The Feathers Tap Room

Summary

The historic Feathers inn in Dersingham is reputed to be haunted by a young boy who was allegedly beaten to death in the tap room centuries ago, leaving phantom children to occasionally startle guests.

Details

Situated on Manor Road in the Norfolk village of Dersingham, The Feathers is an ancient inn with deep roots in local history, later incorporated into the nearby Sandringham estate. According to local lore and historical traditions gathered from residents and visitors, the tap room of the inn was the scene of a tragic crime many years ago when a young boy was beaten to death. Over the generations, proprietors and patrons have reported various spectral phenomena attributed to this dark history, including phantom children said to reach out and grab at the ankles of drinkers, as well as unaccountable cold spots and eerie disturbances echoing through the old building.

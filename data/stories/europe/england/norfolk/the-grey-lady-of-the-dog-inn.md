#title
The Grey Lady of the Dog Inn

#summary
A spectral grey lady frequently haunted a young child's bedroom at the Dog Inn in Hingham, sitting at the foot of the bed while the family dog refused to enter the room.

#details
During the late 1920s when her family ran the Dog Inn in Hingham, a young girl experienced nightly visits from a ghostly figure known as the Grey Lady. The apparition would walk into the child's bedroom and sit quietly on the end of the bed. Adding to the eerie atmosphere, the family dog steadfastly refused to cross the threshold into the room. Local tradition connected the haunting to the tragic death of a serving girl at the inn years prior.

#location
The Dog Inn was a historic public house located in the Market Place adjacent to the churchyard in the Norfolk village of Hingham.

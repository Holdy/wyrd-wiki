The Prophecy of the Three-Thumbed Miller

Summary

Local folklore places an ancient prophecy at a great tree on Heggatt Heath in Horstead, where a miller with three thumbs is destined to hold three kings' horses during a catastrophic battle.

Details

In his early 20th-century travels around Norfolk, writer Christopher Marlowe recorded a local legend concerning a great tree in the centre of Heggatt Heath in Horstead (around grid reference TG277184). Tradition states that a miller possessing three thumbs will hold three kings' horses during the course of a great battle in which nearly the entire county's population will perish. Similar apocalyptic prophecies involving a three-thumbed miller have also appeared in historical collections and the writings attributed to Mother Shipton and Robert Nixon, mysteriously attaching themselves to this Horstead common.

#title
The Shrieking Pits of Aylmerton

#summary
A weeping, white-clad phantom of a woman wanders among ancient woodland pits near Aylmerton, endlessly wailing in search of her murdered baby.

#details
In the wooded slopes close to Gresham Cross in Aylmerton, five shallow pits—originally medieval iron ore workings—are known locally as the Shrieking Pits. According to local folklore, these depressions are haunted by the spectral figure of a tall, willowy woman dressed in white. The legend tells that her jealous husband murdered their infant child, convinced the baby was not his, and buried the infant in a pit west of Aylmerton church before turning on and killing his wife. Ever since, her ghost wanders between the pits, wringing her hands and uttering piercing, heartbreaking cries as she searches eternally for her lost child.

#location
Five shallow medieval iron ore pits located in the wooded slopes near Gresham Cross in Aylmerton, Norfolk.

#title
The Weeping Woman of the Shrieking Pits

#summary
A tall phantom woman in white haunts the medieval iron-ore pits of Aylmerton, wailing and searching for her murdered child.

#details
Local folklore tells of a grieving mother whose baby was murdered by her jealous husband, who was convinced the infant was not his. After burying the child in a pit west of Aylmerton, the husband then murdered his wife as well. Ever since, a tall ghostly figure clothed in white has been seen wandering around the shallow medieval pits near Gresham Cross, wringing her hands and shrieking as she searches the depressions in the ground for her lost baby.

#location
The Shrieking Pits are located in the fields just north and west of Gresham Cross (TG181388) in Aylmerton.

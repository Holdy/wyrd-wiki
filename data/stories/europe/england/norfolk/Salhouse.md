#title
The Midnight Mass of Salhouse Pool

#summary
Following the Norman Conquest, the banks of Salhouse Pool became the site of a magnificent spectral midnight Mass that is periodically witnessed by visitors and locals.

#details
After William the Conqueror granted the Manor of Salhouse to his standard-bearer, Raoul de Chassagne, the local peasants were gripped by fear of the new Norman nobleman. Upon learning of their terror, the Count announced that he meant them no harm and invited them to attend a grand open-air Mass held at midnight by the torchlit banks of Salhouse Pool. This breathtaking service proved so memorable that the spectral re-enactment of the torchlight Mass has reportedly been seen by various witnesses down through the centuries, including the principal of an Oxford college in 1903.

#location
Salhouse Pool is a small lagoon located beside Salhouse Broad in the village and civil parish of Salhouse, Norfolk.

#title
The One-Eyed Hound of Salhouse

#summary
A gigantic spectral hound sporting a single blazing eye in the middle of its forehead terrorized the Norfolk countryside around Salhouse. Local youths bravely ventured out at night armed only with sticks to hunt the terrifying beast, to the horror of villagers who expected them to meet certain death.

#details
In the 19th century, accounts emerged of a terrifying manifestation of Black Shuck haunting the area around the Norfolk village of Salhouse. Unlike typical accounts of the spectral dog, this monstrous hound was described as having a single blazing eye right in the middle of its forehead, and it prowled the countryside across a twelve-mile radius. The terror inspired by the beast was so profound that when a local man's sons took up sticks at night to hunt the phantom, the villagers were convinced they were walking straight to their doom.

#location
The parish and village of Salhouse in Norfolk, England.

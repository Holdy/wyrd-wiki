#title
The Apparition of St Olave's Priory

#summary
Ghost hunters investigating long-standing reports of a ghostly monk at a ruined medieval priory captured footage of an inexplicable white figure lurking in a doorway. Upon reviewing their video evidence, the investigators confirmed the apparition could not be debunked as light glare or reflection.

#details
Members of the Ghosted UK investigation team visited the 14th-century brick-vaulted refectory undercroft at St Olave's Priory to verify recurring sightings of a phantom monk. While traversing the historic ruins, the investigators unknowingly filmed a pale, spectral figure standing in a doorway behind them. Upon sifting through the footage later, they discovered the white apparition appeared to shift slightly as team members walked past, defying conventional explanations such as light reflection or optical glare.

#location
The sighting took place within the 14th-century brick-vaulted refectory undercroft at St Olave's Priory, situated off Beccles Road in the village of St Olaves, Norfolk.

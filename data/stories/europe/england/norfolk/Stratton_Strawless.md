#title
The Burial of the Fiddler

#summary
A musician and his cart are said to lie interred beneath a tree-shrouded earthwork known as Fiddler's Hill.

#details
Local legend maintains that a fiddler and his cart are buried beneath Fiddler's Hill, a mysterious tree-covered earthwork on the parish boundary. Whether regarded as an ancient burial mound, a boundary bank, or former archery butts, the site carries the dark folklore of the musician and his transport being buried together.

#location
Fiddler's Hill is located at grid reference TG22262005 on the Stratton Strawless side of the boundary with Hainford.

#title
The Fiddler of Fiddler's Hill

#summary
According to local legend, an unfortunate fiddler and his cart are buried beneath the ancient earthwork situated on the border of Stratton Strawless and Hainford. 

#details
Local tradition maintains that a travelling musician, along with his cart, met his end and was buried beneath the mound now known as Fiddler's Hill. Documented as "Fidler's Hill" on the 1837 Hainford tithe map, the site is a tree-shrouded earthwork that has long been regarded by locals as a mysterious burial mound, boundary marker, or ancient feature tied to the tale of the buried fiddler.

#location
Fiddler's Hill is a tree-covered earthwork located at Ordnance Survey map reference TG22262005, situated on the Stratton Strawless side of the parish boundary with Hainford.

#title
The White Lady of St Mary's Church

#summary
A ghostly white lady haunts St Mary's Church in Worstead, having reportedly frightened a skeptic to death on Christmas Eve and later appearing clearly in a 1975 tourist photograph. While some traditions warn that seeing her foretells doom, visitors have also experienced her presence as a comforting and healing influence.

#details
According to local legend, St Mary's Church in Worstead is haunted by a benevolent yet chilling spirit known as the White Lady. On Christmas Eve in 1830, a man staying in the village resolved to test the legend by climbing up into the church belfry. His friends later discovered him gibbering with fright, gasping that he had seen the apparition before immediately dropping dead. Decades later, in August 1975, a tourist named Diane Berthelot was photographed sitting in the church pews. When the film was developed, a ghostly figure in a bonnet and old-fashioned clothing appeared seated directly behind her, though Diane recalled feeling only a warm tingling sensation and believes the spirit possesses healing powers.

#location
St Mary's Church stands in the heart of the village of Worstead in North Norfolk, England.

#title
The White Lady of Worstead Church

#summary
A mysterious spectral figure known as the White Lady is said to haunt Worstead's parish church, appearing on Christmas Eve and acting as a healing presence to those suffering from illness.

#details
According to local tradition, the White Lady haunts St Mary's Church in the Norfolk village of Worstead. An 1830 account tells of a man who climbed into the church belfry on Christmas Eve boasting that he would kiss the ghost, only to be found huddled and terrified moments later before whispering that he had seen her and dying of fright. Decades later, in August 1975, a visitor named Diane Berthelot visited the church to escape the summer heat and sat praying for recovery from an ongoing infection. When her husband later developed photographs taken inside the church, the image revealed a translucent woman in old-fashioned light-colored clothing and a bonnet sitting directly on the wooden bench right behind Diane. The local vicar subsequently explained to her that the White Lady was reputed to be a healing spirit who appeared during times of sickness.

#location
St Mary's Church in the village of Worstead, North Norfolk, England.

#title
The Mistletoe Bride of Terrington St Clement

#summary
During a wedding day game of hide-and-seek, a young bride tragically locked herself inside an antique chest and suffocated, with local legend claiming her ghost returns to St Clement's Church every year to join in the Christmas carol singing.

#details
The legend tells of a lavish wedding celebration where the young bride decided to hide in a heavy wooden chest or cassone as part of a game. Unbeknownst to her, a spring-lock mechanism on the lid slammed shut, trapping her inside the dark container. When she failed to reappear, her disappearance was initially blamed on cold feet or an elopement. Decades later, her skeletal remains were discovered inside the chest wearing her decayed wedding gown. In Terrington St Clement, local tradition maintains that her spirit never left the parish church, and once a year on the Sunday before Christmas, her spectral figure emerges from her resting place to join the congregation in singing carols.

#location
St Clement's Church, Church Street, Terrington St Clement, King's Lynn, Norfolk, PE34 4HX.

#title
The Phantom Companion of Beacon Hill Farm

#summary
During the pre-First World War era, a woman walking near Beacon Hill Farm House encountered a spectral black dog that vanished into thin air upon being touched. When she reached down to pat the creature, her hand passed straight through its body.

#details
In the years leading up to the First World War, a woman was walking home near Beacon Hill Farm House in Terrington St. Clement when she noticed a black dog walking quietly by her side. Mistaking the phantom hound for a living animal, she bent down to pet it, only to discover that her hand passed completely through the apparition.

#location
The encounter occurred near Beacon Hill Farm House (grid reference TF547214) in Terrington St. Clement, Norfolk.

#title
The Phantom Hound of Beacon Hill Farm

#summary
Walking near a farmhouse in Terrington St Clement prior to the First World War, a local woman encountered a spectral black hound that proved to be entirely incorporeal. When she attempted to stroke the dog walking beside her, her hand passed straight through its form.

#details
In the years leading up to the First World War, a woman was walking home near Beacon Hill Farm House in Terrington St Clement when she became aware of a mysterious companion. A silent black dog was pacing quietly right beside her. Mistaking the apparition for a real, living animal, she bent down to pat its head, only to be startled when her hand met no physical resistance and passed straight through the spectral creature into thin air.

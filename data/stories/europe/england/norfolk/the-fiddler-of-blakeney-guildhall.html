The Fiddler of Blakeney Guildhall

Summary

A daring fiddler and his dog who ventured into a secret tunnel beneath Blakeney Guildhall to map its route vanished without a trace when his music abruptly stopped. Only his terrified dog emerged, leaving behind a haunted hill and ghostly strains of violin music on dark nights.

Details

According to local tradition, a network of underground smugglers' tunnels once connected Blakeney Guildhall to Binham Priory. Reluctant to explore the dark, subterranean depths themselves, the villagers offered a fee to a local fiddler to walk the tunnel while playing his violin so his underground progress could be tracked from above. Accompanied by his little dog and holding a lantern on a rod, the fiddler descended into the hole beneath the Guildhall. As he walked underground playing lively tunes, crowds of villagers and officials followed his music along the route. Suddenly, the music stopped completely and never resumed. Hours later, while waiting at the other end near the priory, only the fiddler's traumatized dog emerged, shivering and whining, while the fiddler himself was never seen again. A mound of earth known as Fiddler's Hill was raised to mark his disappearance, and on dark nights, locals still report hearing a solitary violin playing across the fields between Blakeney and Binham.

#title
The Spectral Hound of Beeston Heath

#summary
A terrifying headless spectral dog with flaming saucer eyes rises from the sea at Beeston to prowl along Shock's Lane toward Overstrand churchyard every evening at twilight.

#details
Old Shuck, a monstrous black dog described as either headless with glowing saucer eyes or wearing a white handkerchief tied over the place where its head should be, emerges from the sea near Beeston Heath. Accompanied by the scent of brimstone and scorched earth, the phantom hound races along the coastal path known as Shock's Lane, maintaining its eerie patrol before passing under the stone wall of Overstrand churchyard at twilight as a portent of coastal storms.

#location
Beeston Bump is a prominent glacial hill rising on the North Norfolk coast in Beeston Regis.

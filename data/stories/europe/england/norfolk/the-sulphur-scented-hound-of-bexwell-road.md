#title
The Sulphur-Scented Hound of Bexwell Road

#summary
In 1870, the Bexwell parish clerk encountered a mysterious black hound on the A1122 road that vanished into a hedge amidst rattling chains and a pungent scent of sulphur.

#details
Around 1870, the parish clerk of Bexwell was traveling along the A1122 road between Downham Market and Bexwell when he had a terrifying encounter with a large black dog. The creature abruptly vanished through a hedge, leaving behind a loud rattling of heavy chains and an overwhelming smell of sulphur. Although the Rector of Bexwell later suggested the apparition was simply his own great black dog that had broken free from its chain, he remained completely at a loss to explain the supernatural stench of brimstone that accompanied its disappearance.

#location
The encounter took place along the A1122 road situated between Downham Market and the village of Bexwell in Norfolk.

#title
The Smugglers' Secret Tunnel

#summary
Placing an ear to the ground at Cross Green is said to reveal the phantom singing of smugglers echoing from a hidden underground passage.

#details
According to local tradition, a secret tunnel once used by smugglers runs for several miles from the former White Horse inn at Cross Green northwards to the coast at Cley. Generations of villagers have claimed that if you press your ear against the earth near the old inn, you can hear the ghostly voices and singing of the smugglers reverberating beneath the surface.

#location
Cross Green in Edgefield, located on the corner of Briston Road and Ramsgate Street at the site of the former White Horse inn (TG090342).

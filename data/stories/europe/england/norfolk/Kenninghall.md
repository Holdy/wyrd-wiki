#title
The Palace of Candleyards

#summary
Local legend maintains that the ancient Iceni queen Boudicca established her royal palace at the moated site of Candleyards in Kenninghall.

#details
Long before the village of Kenninghall became associated with Anglo-Saxon royalty and the later Tudor palace of the Dukes of Norfolk, local tradition linked the parish to the legendary Celtic warrior queen, Boudicca (Boadicea). According to regional folklore, the queen of the Iceni tribe maintained a primary royal residence or palace at a moated site known as Candleyards, situated just west of where Kenninghall Place was later built. While modern archaeology identifies the earthworks as a medieval moated enclosure, generations of local tradition held that the deep ring of water and earthen ramparts protected the ancient stronghold of Britain's most famous rebel queen.

#location
The moated site of Candleyards, located just west of Kenninghall Place (grid reference TM065855) in Kenninghall, Norfolk.

#title
The Ghostly Tannoy Voice of Tibenham Airbase

#summary
An abandoned World War II airfield in Norfolk is haunted by phantom sounds, including an echoing tannoy voice barking orders and the rumble of a warming bomber engine. Witnesses have reported hearing these spectral broadcasts long after the base's operational years.

#details
Deep in the Norfolk countryside, the abandoned Second World War airbase in Tibenham remains a site where the past lingers heavily. Visitors and witnesses have reported hearing a strange, echoing voice broadcasted over a non-existent tannoy system, barking military orders to crews who lost their lives decades ago. This spectral transmission is occasionally accompanied by the distinct auditory backdrop of a bomber engine warming up before takeoff, serving as a chilling reminder of the airfield's wartime history.

#location
The haunting takes place at the former Second World War airbase located in Tibenham, Norfolk.

#title
The Phantoms of RAF Tibenham

#summary
The former World War II airfield in Tibenham is widely reputed to be haunted by the spirits of American airmen and bomber crews who perished during missions over Europe. Visitors and paranormal investigators have reported encountering spectral figures and hearing eerie echoes of wartime operations across the historic grounds.

#details
Established during the Second World War as Station 114, RAF Tibenham served as a vital base for the 445th Bomb Group of the United States Army Air Forces, a unit that notably included actor and pilot Jimmy Stewart. The base suffered severe casualties during harrowing bombing raids over Europe, leaving a permanent historical scar on the rural landscape. Long after the airfield was decommissioned and its control tower demolished, locals and paranormal historians continued to report intense supernatural phenomena. Those visiting the windswept site have described feeling a heavy, frantic energy, hearing phantom voices barking orders, and witnessing the ghostly apparitions of airmen still bound to their wartime duties.

#location
The former RAF Tibenham airfield and control tower site located in Tibenham, South Norfolk.

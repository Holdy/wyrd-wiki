#title
The Bride of Bastwick

#summary
On her wedding night in 1741, a bride whose marriage was secured by witchcraft is snatched away by a skeleton-driven coach that plunges into flames off Potter Heigham Bridge.

#details
Lady Carew sought the help of a witch to secure a wealthy bachelor, Sir Godfrey Haslitt of Bastwick, as a husband for her daughter, Lady Evelyn. The witch provided a potent love potion, demanding only one undisclosed wish in return. After a lavish wedding, the couple returned home, but at the stroke of midnight, a horrifying skeleton appeared at the hall door, grabbed the new bride, and dragged her into a carriage pulled by four black horses. As the wedding party gave desperate chase, the coach hurtled toward Potter Heigham Bridge, where it burst into flames and crashed into the waters below, fulfilling the dark and fatal price exacted by the witch.

#location
Potter Heigham Bridge crosses the River Thurne in Potter Heigham, Norfolk, near the historic parish of Woodbastwick (Bastwick).

#title
The Phantom Coach of Bastwick

#summary
On her wedding night, Lady Evelyn is abducted by a skeletal figure in a midnight coach that ultimately plunges into the river at Potter Heigham Bridge.

#details
In the 18th century, Lady Carew sought a witch's help to arrange a marriage between her daughter, Evelyn, and the wealthy bachelor Sir Godfrey Haslitt of Bastwick. The magical potion succeeded, and Evelyn married Sir Godfrey on May 31, 1741, following a wedding in Norwich. During the celebratory reception back at Bastwick, the midnight festivities were shattered when the doors burst open to reveal a skeleton. The spectral figure kidnapped the new bride, whisking her away in a coach drawn by four black horses toward Potter Heigham. As the frantic wedding party gave chase, the coach reached Potter Heigham Bridge, burst into flames, and plunged into the water with Lady Evelyn trapped inside—fulfilling the dark price demanded by the witch. On the anniversary of May 31st, witnesses can still hear the hoofbeats and screeching wheels before a fiery phantom coach hurtles over the bridge into the water.

#location
Potter Heigham Bridge is a historic bridge carrying the A149 across the River Thurne in Norfolk.

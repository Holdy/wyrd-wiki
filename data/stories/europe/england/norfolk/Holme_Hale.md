#title
The Midnight Coach of Holme Hale Dale

#summary
A spectral carriage pulled by headless horses and driven by a headless coachman relives a fatal runaway accident at midnight. On certain nights, the phantom coach hurtles down Holme Hale Dale and overturns with an unearthly scream.

#details
Many years ago, a horse-drawn carriage set out from Scants Corner Farm traveling toward Watton. Upon reaching Top Cross, the reins snapped, causing the horses to bolt wildly down Holme Hale Dale. At the bottom of the dale, the carriage overturned, breaking the driver's neck. Ever since, at the stroke of midnight on certain nights of the year, witnesses can see the phantom coach re-enact the disaster, driven by a headless coachman behind two headless horses, culminating in a terrifying, unearthly scream as the vehicle overturns.

#location
Holme Hale Dale, running between Scants Corner Farm and Top Cross toward Watton in Norfolk.

#title
The Phantom Coach of Holme Hale Dale

#summary
A spectral carriage driven by a headless coachman and drawn by headless horses relives a fatal midnight crash along Holme Hale Dale. On certain nights, the phantom vehicle careens down the slope from Top Cross toward Watton, ending with a terrifying unearthly scream.

#details
Many years ago, a carriage drawn by a pair of horses started out from Scants Corner Farm to travel toward Watton. Upon reaching Top Cross, the reins suddenly broke, causing the frightened horses to race faster and faster down the dale. At the bottom, the carriage overturned, violently breaking the driver's neck in the accident. Ever since, on certain nights of the year as the clock strikes midnight, the ghostly carriage is seen once again, drawn by two headless horses and driven by a headless coachman who utters an unearthly scream as the vehicle flips over.

#location
Holme Hale Dale and Top Cross in the village of Holme Hale, Norfolk, along the route towards Watton.

Alec Hunt's account of life in Holme Hale throughout the ages.

#title
The Phantom Dog of Buxton Lamas Church

#summary
A gentleman encountering a large black shaggy dog outside a Norfolk church in 1930 attempts to pet the beast, only for it to vanish into thin air just as his brother passes away in hospital.

#details
In 1930, at the ancient Buxton Lamas church, a man encountered a large, black shaggy dog that initially aroused no suspicion. When he reached out to pat the creature, it abruptly vanished before his eyes. Deeply perplexed by the sudden disappearance, he was later troubled to receive word that his brother had died in the hospital at the exact moment he witnessed the phantom hound.

#location
Buxton Lamas church located in the parish of Buxton with Lammas in Norfolk.

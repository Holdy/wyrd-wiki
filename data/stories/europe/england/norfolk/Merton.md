#title
The Legend of the Merton Stone

#summary
According to local legend, if the gigantic glacial erratic known as the Merton Stone is ever removed, a catastrophic flood will cover the world, while a past attempt to move it is said to have sparked an era of wild debauchery in the village.

#details
The Merton Stone is a massive boulder of Neocomian sandstone resting in a marl pit near the western boundary of Merton parish with Threxton, just off Peddar's Way near Capp's Bush. According to local folklore, if the stone were ever moved or disturbed, all the waters would rise and cover the earth. The 5th Lord Walsingham once gathered local residents with ropes and plenty of beer in an unsuccessful attempt to haul the heavy boulder away. Although the stone refused to budge, the failed feat was said to have been followed by an outbreak of local erotic debauchery, leading generations of villagers to refer to children born thereafter as having arrived at "the time of the old stone." A later attempt in the mid-20th century to move the boulder using a heavy mechanical rotary plough, known as a Gyrotiller, similarly ended in failure.

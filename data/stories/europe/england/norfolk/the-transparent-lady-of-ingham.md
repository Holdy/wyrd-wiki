#title
The Transparent Lady of Ingham

#summary
A transparent ghostly figure haunts the old gravel pit behind Holy Trinity Church in Ingham, where nineteenth-century church renovations also uncovered nineteen skeletons guarding a secret underground passage.

#details
At Holy Trinity Church in Ingham, a 13th-century building with a history rooted in an ancient priory, local lore tells of both architectural secrets and spectral encounters. During Victorian restoration work when the church altar was dismantled, nineteen human skeletons were uncovered piled beneath it, serving as a grim guard to a subterranean passage said to link the priory to a nearby manor house half a mile away. In addition to these skeleton sentries, a transparent lady is reported to haunt the north side of the churchyard, specifically sighted by those with acute vision near the large gravel pit behind the church.

#location
The former gravel pit and churchyard on the north side of Holy Trinity Church in Ingham, Norfolk.

# title
Peter the wild boy

# summary
In 1725 a boy of about twelve, walking on all fours, unable to speak and living on leaves, bark and raw food, was found in the woods near Hamelin in Hanover. King George I brought him to England as a curiosity, and he became the talk of London society before being sent to live on a farm in Hertfordshire, from which he wandered off in 1751 and was arrested in Norwich as a suspected spy.

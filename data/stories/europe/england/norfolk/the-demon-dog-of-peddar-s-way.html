The Demon Dog of Peddar's Way

Summary

The legendary East Anglian hell-hound Black Shuck is said to haunt the ancient trackway of Peddar's Way as it passes through the countryside surrounding Great Bircham. This spectral demon dog is famously mirrored in the stone carvings of St Mary's Church within the village.

Details

While exploring the rural landscapes of Great Bircham, traveler and writer Christopher Somerville visited the historic St Mary's Church, home to a malevolent-looking medieval stone carving above its chancel arch depicting a creature with pointed ears and blank eyes. This stone beast is closely tied to local folklore surrounding Black Shuck, the iconic East Anglian hell-hound that prowls the ancient Peddar's Way trackway passing near the village. While traversing the grassy path where the demon dog is said to roam, the author noted the lingering presence of the legendary beast in the local imagination and village architecture.

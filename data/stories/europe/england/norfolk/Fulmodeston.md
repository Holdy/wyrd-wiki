#title
Fulmodeston's Unwelcoming Mist

#summary
In August 2005, a group of walkers exploring the ruined St Mary's Church in Fulmodeston were confronted by a sulphurous white mist, phantom voices, and a ghostly figure walking on non-existent upper floors.

#details
In August 2005, a group of walkers stumbled upon the atmospheric, ivy-covered ruins of St Mary's Church in Fulmodeston, which had been abandoned in the 1880s. As they navigated the brambles to explore the site, they were suddenly met by an unnatural white mist and an overpowering smell of sulphur. Terrified by the hostile atmosphere, the group attempted to beat a hasty exit, during which they heard disembodied voices and witnessed a spectral figure walking upon a church floor that had long since crumbled away.

#location
The ruined St Mary's Church is located in the quiet village of Fulmodeston in Norfolk, England.

#title
The Demon Hound of Saint Mary's

#summary
The abandoned ruins of an eleventh-century church in Fulmodeston are haunted by Black Shuck, a terrifying demon hound said to roam near the site. Local lore also speaks of an unburied coffin beneath the rubble whose occupant wanders the derelict grounds.

#details
Standing at the end of a dirt track five miles east of Fakenham, the ivy-wreathed ruins of Saint Mary's Church in Fulmodeston have been abandoned since 1882. Over the centuries, the hollowed-out medieval husk has gathered chilling local legends, including reports of Black Shuck—the legendary demon hound of East Anglia—being sighted near the site. Alongside sightings of the spectral black dog, tales persist of an unburied coffin remaining beneath the rubble, with its restless occupant said to haunt the decaying nave and chancel.

#location
The ruins of Saint Mary's Church are located at the end of a dirt track in the village of Fulmodeston, Norfolk, UK.

#title
The Mystery Mist and Spectral Figure of St Mary's Church

#summary
While exploring the abandoned ruins of St Mary's Church, a group of walkers encountered an eerie white mist, an overpowering stench of sulphur, and a ghostly figure walking on a long-vanished floor. Unexplained voices were also heard echoing through the overgrown site.

#details
In August 2005, a group of walkers exploring the Norfolk countryside happened upon the atmospheric, ivy-covered ruins of St Mary's Church in Fulmodeston, which had been abandoned since the 1880s. Intrigued by the forgotten structure, they picked their way through the surrounding brambles to explore the remains. Almost immediately, the atmosphere turned hostile as a strange white mist rolled in, accompanied by a heavy and overpowering smell of sulphur. As the startled group attempted to make a hasty retreat, disembodied voices echoed around them, and they caught sight of a spectral figure calmly walking across a stone floor that had crumbled away long ago.

#location
The ruined remains of St Mary's Church are situated amidst vegetation and trees in the rural village of Fulmodeston, Norfolk, UK.

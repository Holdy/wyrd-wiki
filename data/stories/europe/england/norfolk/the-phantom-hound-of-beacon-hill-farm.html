The Phantom Hound of Beacon Hill Farm

Summary

Walking near a farmhouse in Terrington St Clement prior to the First World War, a local woman encountered a spectral black hound that proved to be entirely incorporeal. When she attempted to stroke the dog walking beside her, her hand passed straight through its form.

Details

In the years leading up to the First World War, a woman was walking home near Beacon Hill Farm House in Terrington St Clement when she became aware of a mysterious companion. A silent black dog was pacing quietly right beside her. Mistaking the apparition for a real, living animal, she bent down to pat its head, only to be startled when her hand met no physical resistance and passed straight through the spectral creature into thin air.

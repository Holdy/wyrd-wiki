    The Red-Faced Ghoul of Rollesby Hall

    Summary

    Two spectral figures, including a blood-soaked man known as "Old Red Face," are said to haunt the site of the long-demolished Rollesby Hall at midnight on the second Monday of every month.

    Details

    Rollesby Hall, a historic building dating back to the mid-16th century that was eventually demolished in the 1950s, was long reputed to be haunted by two distinct ghosts. Among them is a chilling apparition of a man whose face is covered in blood—dubbed "Old Red Face"—who is accompanied by the weeping ghost of a woman wringing her hands in despair. According to local lore, their appearances at midnight on the second Monday of each month commemorate a dark tragedy or murder at the estate, leaving behind an eerie mystery as to whether the blood on the spirit's face belonged to the killer or the victim.

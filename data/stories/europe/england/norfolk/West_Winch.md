#title
Hollow Hills of West Winch

#summary
Local tradition and folklore record the presence of mysterious "hollow hills" in a field north-east of St. Mary's Church in West Winch, historically associated with ancient burial mounds or fairy dwellings.

#details
According to an account given by a local ploughman, mysterious "hollow hills" once existed in a field situated just north-east of St. Mary's church in West Winch, a plot recorded as Church Close on the 1841 tithe map (grid reference TF63301594). While historians have raised the possibility of an underground structure, in regional folklore the term "hollow hills" is traditionally tied to ancient barrows and the subterranean abodes of fairy folk. No visible trace of these earthen mounds remains today.

#location
A field located just north-east of St. Mary's Church (TF63301594) in West Winch, Norfolk.

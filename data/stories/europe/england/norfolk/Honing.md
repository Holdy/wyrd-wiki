#title
The Ghost Donkey of Honing

#summary
A phantom white donkey that breathes billows of sulphuric smoke from its nostrils is said to haunt the road leading to the parish church in Honing.

#details
In the quiet North Norfolk village of Honing, local legend speaks of an unusual and unsettling apparition that startles travelers after dark. A ghostly white donkey is reported to roam the lonely road leading towards St Peter and St Paul's Church. According to regional folklore accounts, witnesses have encountered the spectral beast standing silently or wandering the lane, exhaling thick, choking clouds of smoke and fire from its nostrils into the night air.

#location
St Peter and St Paul's Church and its approach road in the village of Honing, Norfolk.

#upstream-source
Weird Norfolk by Stacia Briggs and Siofra Connor (originally published in the Eastern Daily Press).

#title
The Ghost Donkey of Honing

#summary
A translucent white donkey exhaling sulphurous smoke haunts the grounds of St Peter and St Paul church in Honing. The spectral animal terrifies onlookers by galloping at high speed and phasing straight through solid masonry.

#details
A witness near the sacred church of St Peter and St Paul in Honing was stunned to encounter a phantom white donkey. The creature, with sulphurous smoke pouring from its flared nostrils, galloped past at a frantic pace. To the witness's utter amazement, the beast was entirely transparent, allowing them to see right through its body to the hedge beyond before it shockingly passed straight through a church wall.

#location
The key location is St Peter and St Paul church in the village of Honing, Norfolk.

#title
The Black-Hooded Monk of Binham Priory

#summary
A spectral Benedictine monk is frequently sighted walking along a high ledge inside the parish church and haunting the ruins of the medieval priory. Local folklore links the phantom to subterranean tunnels and the tragic imprisonment of a former prior.

#details
For generations, inhabitants and visitors of Binham have reported encounters with a mysterious figure wearing a black cowl and habit. Witnesses inside the parish church—which serves as the surviving nave of the ancient Binham Priory—have watched the phantom walk slowly along an elevated ledge near the church door before ascending spiral steps and vanishing into thin air. Other villagers have reported seeing the cowled specter near the priory's Gaol Gate or emerging from subterranean tunnels said to connect the priory to the shrine at Walsingham. Local folklore and historical accounts suggest the haunting may be linked to Alexander de Langley, a former prior who was imprisoned and punished in chains at Binham after losing his sanity.

#location
Binham Priory and its parish church are located in the heart of the village of Binham in North Norfolk.

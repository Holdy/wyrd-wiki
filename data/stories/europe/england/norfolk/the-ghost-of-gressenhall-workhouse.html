The Ghost of Gressenhall Workhouse

Summary

Staff members at a former workhouse experienced inexplicable phenomena, including lights turning themselves on, heavy footsteps, and objects moving overnight in the upper-floor Spong Room.

Details

When the Norfolk Archaeological Unit moved into the east wing of the former Gressenhall Workhouse (known as Union House) in the 1970s, workers in the first-floor Spong Room experienced persistent strange occurrences. Lights left turned off at night were frequently found on in the mornings, stacks of empty finds boxes were moved across the room, and windows would fly open by themselves. Former staff from when the building operated as an old people's home also recounted a local tradition of a "Green Lady" haunting the room. Additional unsettling incidents included a wall clock being mysteriously displaced into a sink full of water, heavy footsteps knocking over boxes in the corridors late at night, and an archaeologist being closely followed by unseen footsteps along the building's driveway.

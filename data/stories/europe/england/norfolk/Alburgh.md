#title
The Phantom Organ of St. Mary's

#summary
Shadowy figures roam the churchyard of St. Mary's in Alburgh during twilight, while ghostly organ music and disembodied voices emanate from the empty building. These lingering manifestations have made the historic church a focal point of local ghost lore.

#details
The historic St. Mary's Church in the Norfolk parish of Alburgh is reputed to be a site of frequent paranormal activity. Residents and visitors have reported seeing eerie, shadowy figures lurking near the churchyard boundaries as twilight descends. Furthermore, witnesses have heard unearthly organ music and disembodied voices echoing from inside the church when the building is known to be completely locked and empty.

#location
St. Mary's Church is located in the village of Alburgh, situated in south Norfolk, UK.

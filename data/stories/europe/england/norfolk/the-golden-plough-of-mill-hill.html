    The Golden Plough of Mill Hill

    Summary

    The ancient round barrow known as Mill Hill in Belton is said to conceal subterranean riches, specifically a pair of golden gates or a golden plough.

    Details

    According to regional folklore recorded by archaeologists, local tradition maintains that the prehistoric round barrow of Mill Hill hides remarkable treasure beneath its earthen mound. Generations of villagers passed down the tale that deep within the ancient burial mound lies either a pair of magnificent golden gates or a precious golden plough, imbued with the mystery and allure common to ancient earthworks across East Anglia.

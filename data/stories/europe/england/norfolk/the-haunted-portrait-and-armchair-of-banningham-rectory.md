#title
The Haunted Portrait and Armchair of Banningham Rectory

#summary
Antiquarian Bryan Hall acquired a haunted portrait of Henrietta Nelson and a spectral George III armchair, both of which brought paranormal manifestations to his home.

#details
Norfolk antiquarian Bryan Hall collected numerous curiosities throughout his life, including two items accompanied by spirits. One was a portrait of Henrietta Nelson, a woman who died after falling down the stairs at Yaxley Hall; after her mausoleum was destroyed, her ghost began haunting wherever her portrait was kept, leading to sightings of her walking in the grounds of Banningham Rectory. The other item was a George III armchair from Newstead Abbey that was haunted by a flame-haired woman who would appear if the chair was placed near a fireplace, causing Hall to keep it safely distanced.

#location
Banningham Rectory is situated in the village of Banningham, located in North Norfolk, England.

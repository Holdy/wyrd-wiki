#title
The Christmas Eve Apparition at Temple Bar

#summary
A late-night winter traveler walking home to Earsham witnessed a large black hound emerge from a private estate, cross the road, and vanish directly through a rectory wall without leaving any footprints in the snow.

#details
Late one night shortly before Christmas, a local man was walking the final mile from Bungay back to Earsham under a clear, moonlit sky with thick snow covering the ground. As he approached the row of cottages known as Temple Bar, he was suddenly overcome by a chilling sensation and a prickling of his skin. He watched as a large black dog emerged through the fence of a property called 'The Elms', crossed the road directly in front of him, and vanished straight through the wall of the opposite Rectory. Shocked by the apparition, he inspected the snow and discovered a complete absence of paw prints or any other marks, prompting him to flee in terror to his relative's home in the village.

#location
The incident took place near the Temple Bar cottages in Earsham, situated opposite 'The Elms' and the village rectory.

#title
The Gentle Spirits of Earsham Hall

#summary
Earsham Hall is haunted by benevolent phantoms whose presence is marked by mysterious faces peering through upstairs windows, echoing footsteps, and dancing orbs on the grand staircase.

#details
Visitors and occupants of Earsham Hall frequently encounter benign paranormal activity throughout the historic building. Unexplained phenomena include spectral faces looking out from upper-floor windows, heavy footsteps echoing through empty corridors, and tools mysteriously shifting between workshops. Additionally, chilling drafts and dancing orbs of light are routinely observed along the grand, creaky staircase, though these gentle ghostly manifestations are entirely non-malevolent.

#location
Earsham Hall is a historic Georgian country house located in the village of Earsham within the county of Norfolk, near the border with Suffolk.

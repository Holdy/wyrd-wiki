#title
The Legend of Tom Hickathrift

#summary
Born in Emneth as the son of a poor labourer, Tom Hickathrift grew to enormous size and strength, eventually becoming the legendary giant of the Norfolk Marshland. Armed with an axle-tree and a cart-wheel, he famously defended the common land of the Smeeth against invaders and slew a terrifying ogre.

#details
According to local folklore across the Norfolk Marshland, Tom Hickathrift was born in Emneth "in the reign before William the Conqueror." As a youth, he possessed monstrous strength, standing eight feet tall by the age of ten with hands like shoulders of mutton. When a fierce dispute arose over the common grazing rights of the Smeeth—shared by the Seven Towns of Marshland, including Emneth—or when a menacing ogre terrorised travellers between King's Lynn and Wisbech, Tom rose to the occasion. Using an axle-tree as a sword and a cart-wheel as a shield, he single-handedly routed his adversaries and defeated the marshland ogre in brutal combat. He claimed the giant's cave and hidden treasure, built a fine home, and spent the rest of his legendary life performing astonishing feats of strength across East Anglia before his eventual burial at Tilney All Saints.

#location
The historical site of Hickathrift's Field and the former Giant's Grave mound is located near Smeeth Road in the parish of Emneth, Norfolk.

#title
The Lonely Cottage Poltergeist

#summary
During the 1960s, residents of the Lonely Cottage in Emneth endured a prolonged and intense poltergeist haunting. The phenomenon manifested through doors opening on their own, appliances turning on mysteriously, and ornaments crashing to the floor.

#details
In the 1960s, the Lonely Cottage in Emneth, Norfolk, became the center of a severe poltergeist haunting that lasted for several years. The occupants were subjected to frightening and unexplainable events, including doors and windows opening and shutting independently, household appliances mysteriously activating, and decorative objects shifting around before shattering on the floor. Although specific witness names and dates are lost to time, the inhabitants were left genuinely terrified by the relentless paranormal activity.

#location
The haunting took place at the Lonely Cottage situated within the village of Emneth, Norfolk.

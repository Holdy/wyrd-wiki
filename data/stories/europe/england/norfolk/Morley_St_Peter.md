#title
The Televised Ghost of Morley Old Hall

#summary
During a 1964 televised ghost hunt at Morley Old Hall, dozens of viewers across Britain reported seeing the spectral figure of a hooded monk lurking behind the investigators. Earlier local traditions also maintained that the 16th-century moated manor house was haunted by a tragic female phantom.

#details
In August 1964, Anglia Television broadcast a documentary titled *The Unknown*, featuring renowned parapsychologist and skeptic Tony Cornell demonstrating a standard spontaneous case investigation at Morley Old Hall in Morley Saint Peter. Although Cornell concluded during his visit that there was no evidence of paranormal activity at the 16th-century moated manor, the broadcast proved otherwise to viewers. Immediately after the transmission, viewers contacted the station reporting a distinct apparition—a cowled, hooded monk with a gaunt face and raised hands—standing between Cornell and the interviewer. When the segment was re-broadcast, 27 viewers wrote in describing the figure in chilling detail, with some identifying it with local lore surrounding Alexander de Langley, a former prior who suffered mental decline. Alongside the spectral monk, older accounts associated the hall with the ghost of a beautiful woman reportedly seen looking out across the gardens from an upper window.

#location
Morley Old Hall is a 16th-century moated manor house situated in the village of Morley Saint Peter, approximately twelve miles southwest of Norwich in South Norfolk, England (52°32′40″N 1°01′49″E).

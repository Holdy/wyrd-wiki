    The Cursed Cross of Langley

    Summary

    Langley Cross was bound by a traditional curse warning that its removal from near the abbey would result in Langley Hall being destroyed by fire, a prophecy that partially fulfilled itself in 1801.

    Details

    According to local Norfolk legend, Langley Cross (dating back to the 15th century or earlier) was protected by a curse declaring that if the stone cross were ever moved from its original site near Langley Abbey, Langley Hall would be gutted by fire. In 1801, Lady Beauchamp relocated the cross into the park to mark the meeting place of Langley, Chedgrave, Thurton, and Carleton St. Peter parishes at the corner of a wood called The Thicks. Around that same time, a single turret of Langley Hall caught fire, though fortunately it was swiftly extinguished before the entire estate could be consumed.

#title
The Ancestral Banquet of Waxham Hall

#summary
On New Year's Eve, an eccentric Norfolk baronet hosted a ghostly banquet for the shades of his ancestors in his ancestral home. As midnight struck, the phantom guests vanished, leaving him alone in the freezing hall.

#details
Sir Berney Brograve, a famously eccentric landowner residing at Waxham Hall, decided to host a macabre gathering on New Year's Eve. He laid six places at his dining table and filled glasses for the ghostly shades of his ancestors who had died violent deaths. Throughout the evening, toasts were solemnly drunk to the departed spirits. Exactly as the clock struck midnight, the phantoms vanished into thin air, leaving Sir Berney alone in the cold and desolate hall.

#location
Waxham Hall is a historic manor house located in the village of Waxham, Norfolk, situated near the east coast north-east of Stalham.

#title
The Ghosts and Banquet of Waxham Hall

#summary
Waxham Hall is reputed to be haunted by the restless spirits of the Brograve family, whose 18th-century owner famously hosted a midnight New Year's Eve banquet for the ghosts of his ancestors.

#details
According to local tradition, the historic Waxham Hall in Norfolk is haunted by the unshriven spirits of the Brograve family, whose male descendants frequently met violent or tragic ends. In the late 18th century, the eccentric landowner Sir Berney Brograve—known locally as 'Owd Sir Barney'—became the center of dark folklore. Legend recounts that on New Year's Eve, Sir Berney prepared a grand banquet within the hall, laying covers and filling glasses for six ghostly ancestor guests. He solemnly drank toasts to each of the spectral figures until midnight struck and they vanished. Beyond the ghostly dinner, the hall became notorious for its haunted attic chamber, purported bloodstains, and sightings of Sir Berney himself driving a phantom coach along the local causeway.

#location
Waxham Hall is a historic manorial building located in the village of Waxham on the north-east coast of Norfolk (Grid reference TG439262).

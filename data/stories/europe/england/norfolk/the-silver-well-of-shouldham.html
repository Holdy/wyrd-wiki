    The Silver Well of Shouldham

    Summary

    Workers clearing a legendary spring in Shouldham unearthed a trove of silver treasure, only for it to be lost again during a struggle with the devil. According to alternative local lore, the well's shimmering waters hide abbey wealth concealed during the Dissolution of the Monasteries.

    Details

    Located in the small woodland known as High Plantation on the south slope of the Nar valley in Shouldham, the Silver Well was long celebrated for its sparkling waters. Local tradition ascribes this distinct silver sheen and name to hidden riches. One version of the legend states that during the Dissolution of the Monasteries, precious treasure from a nearby abbey was hidden deep within the well. Another popular account recounts that workmen cleaning and repairing the well unexpectedly brought up a container or box of silverware from the bottom. Just as they raised the treasure to the surface, the devil intervened and fought them for it, causing the workmen to flee in terror and accidentally drop the silver back into the depths, where it remains unrecovered.

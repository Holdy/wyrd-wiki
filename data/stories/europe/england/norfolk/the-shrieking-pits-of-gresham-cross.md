#title
The Shrieking Pits of Gresham Cross

#summary
A spectral mother dressed in white haunts the ancient woodland pits near Gresham Cross, weeping and wailing as she searches endlessly for her murdered baby.

#details
According to local legend, a jealous husband became convinced that his infant child was not his own. He brutally murdered the baby and buried it in a pit west of the church, before returning home to kill his wife as well. Ever since, her ghostly figure has been seen haunting the shallow pits in the wooded slopes near Gresham Cross, pacing between the depressions in the ground and crying out in grief for her lost child.

#location
The wooded slopes and shallow pits located near Gresham Cross in Norfolk.

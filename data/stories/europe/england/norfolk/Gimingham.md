#title
The Secret Smugglers' Tunnel of Gimingham Hall

#summary
Local folklore tells of a subterranean passage running from Gimingham Hall all the way to the sea, historically used by smugglers. Another legendary tunnel is also said to connect the village directly to Bromholm Priory in Bacton.

#details
According to local legend, a hidden underground tunnel stretches for a mile and a half from Gimingham Hall to the coast, constructed and used for smuggling operations to move illicit goods inland undetected. In addition to this coastal escape route, traditional accounts speak of a second subterranean passage linking Gimingham to Bromholm Priory in neighbouring Bacton.

#location
Gimingham Hall is situated in the village of Gimingham, Norfolk (specifically at grid reference TG279365).

#title
The Tragic Fall of Agnes at The Ffolkes

#summary
The historic Norfolk inn is haunted by the sorrowful spirit of a nanny who plummeted to her death from the attic, as well as the lingering presence of a former prison guard.

#details
The Ffolkes in Hillington has long been renowned for paranormal activity, most famously centered around a nurse or nanny named Agnes. During the nineteenth century, Agnes met a violent and untimely demise when she fell from her attic room and was impaled on spikes below. Staff and visitors have since reported chilling phenomena throughout the property, such as the second back room remaining persistently cold regardless of outside weather and unsettling sightings of a large black shape filling doorways. Historically, the building also served as a temporary holding post for prisoners being transported to Norwich, giving rise to persistent folklore that the attic spaces are haunted by the lingering presence of a prison guard.

#location
The story takes place at The Ffolkes hotel and public house situated on Lynn Road in the village of Hillington, Norfolk (PE31 6BJ).

The Weeping Woman of the Shrieking Pits

Summary

A tall phantom woman in white haunts the medieval iron-ore pits of Aylmerton, wailing and searching for her murdered child.

Details

Local folklore tells of a grieving mother whose baby was murdered by her jealous husband, who was convinced the infant was not his. After burying the child in a pit west of Aylmerton, the husband then murdered his wife as well. Ever since, a tall ghostly figure clothed in white has been seen wandering around the shallow medieval pits near Gresham Cross, wringing her hands and shrieking as she searches the depressions in the ground for her lost baby.

#title
The Church Door Pin and the Midnight Apparition

#summary
Local children once believed that driving a pin into the wooden door of All Saints Church and running around the building three times would summon a phantom. This eerie rite was passed down as a reliable method to encounter the supernatural.

#details
During the 1940s, a peculiar and chilling superstition persisted among the youngsters of Hilgay. It was widely whispered that anyone brave enough to take a pin, drive it into the heavy wooden door of All Saints Church, and then sprint around the entire building three consecutive times would cause a ghost to appear.

#location
All Saints Church is located in the village of Hilgay, Norfolk, positioned just east of the River Wissey and south of Downham Market.

#title
The Pin and the Church Ghost

#summary
In 1940s Hilgay, children believed that sticking a pin into the door of All Saints church and running around the building three times would summon a ghost.

During the 1940s, a persistent local superstition among children in the Norfolk village of Hilgay centered around a ritualistic challenge at All Saints church. It was widely believed that if an individual pushed a pin into the wooden church door and then ran completely around the building three times, a ghostly apparition would appear.

    The Hostile Church and the Mysterious Residue

    Summary

    While visiting a picturesque Norfolk village church in 1971, a paranormal investigator and her companion were overwhelmed by an intense feeling of evil hostility. Upon fleeing to their car, they discovered the vehicle inexplicably covered in strange, sticky green droplets.

    Details

    In the autumn of 1971, writer and paranormal investigator Joan Forman and her friend Mary took a countryside drive into the Breckland area, arriving at a picturesque village featuring a neat green and a medieval church. Upon entering the building, they noticed the interior was cool and damp, but the atmosphere rapidly chilled into a tangible oppression. As they walked toward the chancel, Forman was struck by an overwhelming sensation of active and malevolent hostility that became almost insupportable. Overcome by terror, both women hurried out of the church and back to their car. To their astonishment, they found the vehicle's bonnet and windscreen spattered with strange, sticky green droplets, despite there being no overhanging trees or other vehicles nearby—a phenomenon Mary attributed to witchcraft.

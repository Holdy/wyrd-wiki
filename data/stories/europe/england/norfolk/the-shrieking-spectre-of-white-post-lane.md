#title
The Shrieking Spectre of White Post Lane

#summary
A neurotic female apparition repeatedly crosses White Post Lane in Hanworth while uttering three bloodcurdling shrieks.

#details
Hannah Whitepost is a restless spirit said to haunt White Post Lane in the village of Hanworth, near common land locally known as 'Hannah's Common'. Described as a permanently hysterical and erratic figure, she startles passers-by by crossing the road three times in the exact same direction. Each time she crosses, her eerie appearance is accompanied by three terrifying, bloodcurdling shrieks echoing through the night.

#location
White Post Lane and the adjacent common land known as Hannah's Common in the village of Hanworth, Norfolk.

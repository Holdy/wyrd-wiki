#title
The Ghost Light of St Edmund's Church

#summary
On dark, moonless nights, a mysterious phantom light is said to shine within the tower of St Edmund’s Church in Thurne. This glowing apparition serves as an echo from the past when signals were sent across the misty marshes.

#details
Local tradition in the Norfolk village of Thurne maintains that on very dark, moonless nights, a spectral light flickers inexplicably within the tower of St Edmund the King Martyr church. Folklore suggests this phantom illumination lingers as an echo from centuries past, when the church tower served as an important landmark and signaling beacon for travellers navigating the treacherous, fog-laden waterways and marshes of the surrounding Broads.

#location
St Edmund's Church stands in the village of Thurne, Norfolk, near the banks of the River Thurne in the Broads.

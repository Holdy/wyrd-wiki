#title
The Hound of Anchor Street

#summary
Two teenagers exploring a woodland stream off Anchor Street in Tunstead encountered a massive, crimson-eyed spectral hound. Fearing for their lives, they fled as the terrifying creature vanished back into the trees.

#details
In the summer of 2005, two teenage boys were exploring the woods near their home village of Tunstead, North Norfolk, specifically near a stream off Anchor Street. As they walked past an abandoned car used as a landmark, they heard a loud crashing sound in the surrounding trees. Spinning around, they saw an enormous black dog—standing roughly 4 feet 5 inches at the shoulder, resembling a giant boar hound with pure black fur and unnatural dark crimson eyes—dash into the clearing. Terrified, the pair bolted and cleared a six-foot-wide stream in a single leap. When they looked back, the beast had stopped at the water's edge before disappearing into the woods.

#location
The encounter occurred in the woodland area adjacent to Anchor Street in the village of Tunstead, North Norfolk.

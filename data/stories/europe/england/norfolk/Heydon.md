#title
Cromwell's Oak

#summary
Local tradition maintains that Oliver Cromwell was once chased across the parkland of Heydon Hall by a bull and forced to take refuge in an ancient tree.

#details
According to local legend, during a visit to Heydon Hall—then the home of his lawyer, Erasmus Earle—Oliver Cromwell was pursued across the estate's parkland by an aggressive bull. Fearing for his safety, Cromwell scrambled up an ancient oak tree to escape the animal. The tree where he sought refuge has since been known as Cromwell's Oak.

#location
Cromwell's Oak stands in the parkland surrounding Heydon Hall in the village of Heydon, Norfolk.

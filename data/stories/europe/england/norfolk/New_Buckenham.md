#title
The Subterranean Passages of New Buckenham Castle

#summary
Local legend claims that a network of subterranean tunnels connects the medieval fortress of New Buckenham Castle to distant historic estates and neighboring strongholds. These hidden passages allegedly span miles across the Norfolk countryside toward Boyland Hall, Kenninghall Place, and Old Buckenham Castle.

#details
According to local folklore surrounding the ruins of the Norman stronghold at New Buckenham Castle, three secret underground tunnels extend outward from the fortress across the surrounding landscape. The first subterranean passageway is said to run eastward over several miles to the demolished Elizabethan mansion of Boyland Hall in Morningthorpe. A second tunnel purportedly heads south-west toward Kenninghall Place, while a third leads north toward the remnants of the priory and prior stronghold at Old Buckenham Castle, located roughly a mile and a half away.

#location
The remains of New Buckenham Castle are situated at grid reference TG084903 in the parish of New Buckenham, Norfolk.

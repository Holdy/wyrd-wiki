The Faines of Hethersett

Summary

Calf-sized phantom beasts with glowing saucer eyes and silent, massive phantom dogs have long been reported haunting the lanes and playing fields of Hethersett.

Details

For generations, local folklore in Hethersett has spoken of strange phantom creatures known as the Faines. In his 1920 work Recreations of a Norfolk Antiquary, antiquarian Walter Rye recorded accounts of these calf-sized beasts with abnormally large, glowing saucer eyes frequenting specific parts of the village, including Mill Road and Gravel Pit Lane. Later twentieth-century accounts expanded on these sightings when local residents reported being shadowed by enormous, silent, rough-coated phantom dogs while walking across the Hethersett Memorial Playing Field or cycling along Henstead Road, with the spectral hounds pacing alongside witnesses before vanishing completely into thin air.

#title
The Thorpe Marriott Poltergeist

#summary
A modern house on a quiet cul-de-sac in Thorpe Marriott was terrorized by a malevolent poltergeist. The spectral activity was linked by a medium to a hostile deceased relative named George.

#details
In 2004, a couple living in a 1980s three-bedroom house in Thorpe Marriott began experiencing alarming paranormal events shortly after visiting a clairvoyant who revealed the recent death of an aggressive uncle named George. The couple was plagued by a sickening smell of rotting organic matter in their bedroom that resisted redecoration, along with persistent tapping and scratching noises, duvets being pulled from their bed, and doors blocked by unseen forces. Paranormal investigators and a television film crew later visited the property, capturing anomalies such as darting white lights, unexplained temperature drops, and shadowy figures on the stairs. Even when the couple stayed at a relative's home elsewhere, the supernatural disturbances followed them, leaving them desperate for an end to the haunting.

#location
A residential house situated on a quiet cul-de-sac within the Thorpe Marriott housing estate in Norfolk, UK.

#title
The Secret Tunnel of Norton Subcourse Church

#summary
Local folklore maintains that a small door in the north wall of the village church conceals a subterranean passage leading to a former priory site on Low Road. In addition, tradition recalls that early marshland priests used the church's ancient tower as a place of refuge during times of danger.

#details
In the Norfolk village of Norton Subcourse, the historic parish church features a small door set into its north wall. Although the structure actually conceals a stone staircase heading upwards within the building, local legend long maintained that it was the entrance to a secret underground tunnel. According to local belief, this hidden passage stretched all the way from the church to an old farmhouse on Low Road, which was traditionally thought to have been the location of a medieval priory. Further compounding the parish's legendary history, another local account tells of early priests settling in the surrounding marshlands who would climb into the church's Saxon-era round tower for safety whenever danger threatened.

#location
St. Margaret's Parish Church is located off Church Road in the village of Norton Subcourse, Norfolk (Grid Reference TM 407987).

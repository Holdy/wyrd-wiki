The Mistletoe Bride of Brockdish Hall

Summary

During a midwinter wedding celebration at Brockdish Hall, a young bride playing hide-and-seek climbed into a heavy oak chest that accidentally locked shut, sealing her inside until her skeleton was discovered years later.

Details

On her midwinter wedding day, the newlywed bride at Brockdish Hall celebrated with family and friends. Seeking a brief moment alone with her groom after the wedding feast, she playfully challenged him to a game of hide-and-seek. Clutching a bough of mistletoe so she could invite a kiss when he found her, she slipped away and hid inside a heavy, antique oak chest in a back room. Unfortunately, the heavy lid slammed down and locked with a spring mechanism, trapping her inside in total darkness. Despite desperate searches by her new husband and the guests that night and throughout the following week, she vanished without a trace. Around a hundred years later, when estate workers cleared out the storage room, the trunk was opened to reveal her skeleton still wearing her wedding dress and clutching the withered bough of mistletoe.

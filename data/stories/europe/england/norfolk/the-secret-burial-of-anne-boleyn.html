    The Secret Burial of Anne Boleyn

    Summary

    According to local legend, after her execution at the Tower of London, Queen Anne Boleyn's body was secretly exhumed and transported to her family's ancestral church in Norfolk. Her restless spirit is said to return and walk the nave on the anniversary of her death.

    Details

    Following her execution in 1536 at the Tower of London, where official records state she was buried beneath the floor of the Chapel Royal of St Peter ad Vincula, a persistent local legend arose regarding Queen Anne Boleyn's true final resting place. According to tradition, trusted friends and supporters secretly exhumed her body under the cover of night, carrying it out of London to the Norfolk village of Salle. There, her remains were laid to rest beneath a plain black stone in the 15th-century Church of St Peter and St Paul, a magnificent building erected by her paternal great-grandfather, Geoffrey Boleyn. Alongside this clandestine burial, locals have long whispered that Anne's restless phantom haunts the church, reappearing to walk its aisles on the anniversary of her execution.

The Vicar Apparition of East Rudham

Summary

On Boxing Day in 1908, multiple witnesses in East Rudham reported seeing their vicar walking the church grounds, unaware he was recovering from a train crash 1,700 miles away in Algeria.

Details

On December 26, 1908, word reached East Rudham that Reverend Dr. Astley and his wife had been severely injured in a train derailment while traveling to Biskra, Algeria. While Dr. Astley was recovering in bed from a head injury far overseas, parishioners and staff at East Rudham experienced a baffling phenomenon. The vicar's housekeeper, Mrs. Hartley, spotted him sitting at his usual outdoor reading desk on the vicarage lawn. When she called upon Reverend Brock to witness the sight, he too observed the figure in clerical garb. Furthermore, the maid, Florence Breese, reported seeing the vicar on the church grounds earlier that same day. Although journalists investigated and debated whether it was an instance of a stress-induced apparition or psychic double, Dr. Astley himself was amused to confirm he was confined to his bed in North Africa when the sightings occurred.

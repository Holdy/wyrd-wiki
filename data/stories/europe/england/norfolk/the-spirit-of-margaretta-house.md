#title
The Spirit of Margaretta House

#summary
Margaretta House in Clenchwarton is believed to be haunted by the ghost of a woman named Margaret who died in the building and whose grave remains on the property.

#details
The original moated Margaretta House burned down long ago, but a subsequent house built on the site inherited eerie legends. Local folklore states that a woman named Margaret lived and died in the house, and her grave is situated outside the front door at the rear of the property, where her spirit is said to linger. Furthermore, tradition claims an underground secret tunnel once linked the moated estate to St. Margaret's Church approximately 500 meters away.

#location
Margaretta House is situated at a moated historic site (grid reference TF596200) in Clenchwarton, Norfolk.

    The Phantom Hound of Hemsby Lane

    Summary

    While walking home from the amusement arcades along an unlit lane in Hemsby, a family encountered a massive dark hound with glowing red eyes that suddenly vanished into thin air.

    Details

    In the summer of 1996 at around midnight, a couple and their teenage daughter were walking home from the amusement arcades down an unlit lane leading towards a caravan park in Hemsby. In a nearby garden, they spotted a huge, dark dog with glowing red eyes letting out a fierce snarl unlike any ordinary animal. Deeply frightened, they hurried on, but when the father looked back after covering a short distance, the creature had completely vanished. Although they knew nothing of Black Shuck folklore prior to that night, they later realized their sighting matched the legend, and they claimed to have experienced continuous bad luck ever since.

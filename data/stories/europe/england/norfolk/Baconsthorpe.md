#title
The Spectral Sentry of Baconsthorpe Castle

#summary
Visitors to the medieval ruins of Baconsthorpe Castle are frequently startled by stones splashing into the moat, thrown from above by a harmless ghostly sentry still keeping watch.

#details
Built by the opportunistic and wealthy Heydon family starting in 1450, Baconsthorpe Castle eventually fell into ruin and partial demolition by the mid-17th century due to family insolvency. Centuries later, visitors exploring the shattered remains and tranquil waters of the moat often hear the distinct sound of stones breaking the surface. When they look up to find the source, they catch sight of a spectral medieval soldier standing atop the castle walls, casually throwing stones into the water as if passing the time on eternal guard duty. Though startling, the phantom sentry has never been reported as threatening, continuing his lonely watch over the historic estate.

#location
The ruins and moat of Baconsthorpe Castle, situated just off the Holt Road in the village of Baconsthorpe, Norfolk.

#title
The Spectral Sentry of Baconsthorpe Castle

#summary
Visitors to the ruined 15th-century manor house frequently hear mysterious splashes in the moat and spot a ghostly medieval soldier standing on the walls, harmlessly tossing stones into the water.

#details
Baconsthorpe Castle, a fortified manor house built by the Heydon family in the 15th century, now stands in peaceful ruins surrounded by meadows and farmland. Visitors walking near the moat are often startled by the sound of stones breaking the surface of the water. Upon looking up, they see a spectral sentry standing atop the castle walls, methodically lobbing stones into the lake below as if passing the time on guard duty. Despite being startled by the eerie splashing, witnesses report that the ghostly soldier feels entirely non-threatening and continues his solitary vigil undisturbed.

#location
Baconsthorpe Castle is a ruined 15th-century fortified manor house located just off the Holt Road in the civil parish of Baconsthorpe, Norfolk, UK.

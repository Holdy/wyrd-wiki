The Chain of Cley Hill

Summary

Walking home from Cley along the coastal road late one summer night, a local woman was stalked up Cley Hill by the phantom sound of dragging chains belonging to the legendary Black Shuck.

Details

In the summer around 1968, a woman was walking home from Cley toward Blakeney along the A149 road around midnight. As she reached the bottom of Cley Hill, she heard the distinct, light dragging and tinkling sound of a chain following closely behind her in the dark. Though she could see nothing, the unseen presence trailed her all the way up the hill to the streetlit crossroads at the top, before veering down Back Lane. Recognizing the classic manifestations of East Anglia's spectral black dog, Black Shuck, she fled in terror down the High Street.

#title
The Phantom Carriage of Ingoldisthorpe Manor

#summary
In the mid-1950s, local teenagers kept vigil outside the long-demolished Ingoldisthorpe Manor and reportedly witnessed a ghostly carriage carrying a former resident journeying down the road toward Mount Amelia Hall.

#details
Ingoldisthorpe Manor, which stood opposite the top end of Hill Road on St. Thomas's Lane before being destroyed by fire, was the center of local legend regarding a spectral carriage. Around 1955 or 1956, local teenagers heard tales that a phantom carriage would leave the manor gates on a specific date to visit Mount Amelia Hall. Intrigued, they hid in a farmyard opposite the gates to test the story. To their astonishment, they watched as a ghostly carriage rolled out of the property and headed down the road toward Mount Amelia, an account the witnesses maintained well into their later years. According to tradition, the carriage carried the spirit of former occupant Mrs. Tylden on a visit to the Misses Davey at Mount Amelia.

#location
The site of the former Ingoldisthorpe Manor, located opposite the top end of Hill Road on St. Thomas's Lane (now Manor Close) in Ingoldisthorpe, Norfolk.

#title
The Phantom Carriage of Ingoldisthorpe Manor

#summary
A spectral carriage carrying a former resident has been witnessed departing the long-lost Ingoldisthorpe Manor and heading towards Mount Amelia Hall. Local teenagers who staked out the route in the 1950s famously swore they saw the ghostly vehicle pass by.

#details
Ingoldisthorpe Manor, which once stood opposite the top end of Hill Road on St Thomas's Lane, became the center of a local legend concerning a phantom carriage. According to accounts passed down by residents, on a specific date, a spectral carriage carrying former occupant Mrs Tylden would leave the manor gates and travel down the road towards Mount Amelia Hall to visit the Misses Davey. Around 1955/6, a group of local teenagers—curious about the local lore—hid in a farmyard opposite the manor gates and witnessed the ghostly carriage making its journey. Additionally, locals have reported seeing Mrs Tylden's phantom walk through a historic green gate connecting the nearby church to the former manor grounds.

#location
The former site of Ingoldisthorpe Manor is situated opposite the top of Hill Road on St Thomas's Lane in Ingoldisthorpe, now known as Manor Close.

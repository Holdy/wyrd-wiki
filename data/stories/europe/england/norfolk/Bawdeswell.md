#title
The Portal to St Martin's Land

#summary
A mysterious deep hole found in a field beside the road connecting Bawdeswell and Swanton Morley was whispered to be a hidden portal to the twilight realm of the Green Children. Believed by locals to be an entrance to Fairyland, the unexplained shaft was eventually filled in.

#details
Local tradition and historical folklore accounts record that a strange hole of unguessed depth once existed in a field beside the thoroughfare between Bawdeswell and Swanton Morley. Surrounded by coarse clumps of grass and bracken, the pit was neither an old well nor a drain, nor was it used by burrowing animals. Whispers quickly spread among locals that the aperture was a subterranean entrance to St Martin’s Land—the twilight realm famously associated with the legendary green-skinned children who appeared in Suffolk during the 12th century. To put an end to the mystery and any perceived supernatural danger, the eerie portal was ultimately filled in by residents.

#location
The key location is situated in a field beside the road connecting Bawdeswell and Swanton Morley in Norfolk.

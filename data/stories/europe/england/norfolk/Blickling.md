#title
The Spectral Return of the Accused Queen at Blickling Hall

#summary
Born at Blickling Hall, Anne Boleyn was executed after being accused of witchcraft and treason, and her headless ghost is said to return to her birthplace every year on the anniversary of her death.

#details
Anne Boleyn, the second wife of King Henry VIII, was born at Blickling Hall in Norfolk. When Henry grew desperate for a male heir and sought to discard her, charges of treason, adultery, incest, and witchcraft were levied against her to ensure her downfall. In Tudor England, witchcraft was a capital crime often invoked against powerful or unpopular figures. Following her execution by beheading on May 19, 1536, local folklore maintained that her spirit could not rest. On the anniversary of her death every year, Anne's headless apparition returns to Blickling Hall. Legend describes her arriving in a carriage driven by a headless coachman, holding her severed, blood-stained head in her lap, before gliding through the darkened rooms of the hall until dawn.

#location
Blickling Hall, situated near Aylsham in Norfolk, UK.

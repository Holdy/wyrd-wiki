#title
The Ghost Light of St Edmund's Church

#summary
On dark, moonless nights, a mysterious phantom light is said to shine within the tower of St Edmund’s Church in Thurne. This glowing apparition serves as an echo from the past when signals were sent across the misty marshes.

#details
Local tradition in the Norfolk village of Thurne maintains that on very dark, moonless nights, a spectral light flickers inexplicably within the tower of St Edmund the King Martyr church. Folklore suggests this phantom illumination lingers as an echo from centuries past, when the church tower served as an important landmark and signaling beacon for travellers navigating the treacherous, fog-laden waterways and marshes of the surrounding Broads.

#location
St Edmund's Church stands in the village of Thurne, Norfolk, near the banks of the River Thurne in the Broads.

#title
The Ghost of Thurne Mouth

#summary
Following a violent murder driven by romantic jealousy and family betrayal, the restless ghost of a local bailiff wanders the marshes near the mouth of the River Thurne.

#details
In the 1830s, a bailiff living at Sunnyside near the mouth of the River Thurne forbade his daughter Phyllis from marrying a hardworking ploughman named William, pushing her instead toward an unsuitable suitor named Bert Isaacs. After Bert's mismanagement and jealousy caused deep conflict, a violent altercation broke out, resulting in the bailiff's murder and Bert drowning himself in the River Thurne. Following these tragic events, the phantom of the murdered bailiff has been reported periodically wandering the fields near Thurne Mouth, eternally searching for his killer.

#location
The fields and riverbank surrounding the mouth of the River Thurne where it meets the River Bure in Thurne, Norfolk.

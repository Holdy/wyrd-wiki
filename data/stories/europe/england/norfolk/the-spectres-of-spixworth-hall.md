#title
The Spectres of Spixworth Hall

#summary
While staying overnight at the Elizabethan Spixworth Hall, young Arthur Longe was visited in strict succession by a glowing apparition of an old watchman, a phantom woman holding a candle, and a terrifying midnight shriek.

#details
As a child, Arthur Longe stayed at Spixworth Hall and went to sleep in a panelled bedroom. Woken during the night, he saw a bright light and an old watchman with a lantern, knee breeches, and a white beard standing in the doorway before disappearing. After locking the door and falling back asleep, he was woken a second time by a light shining on his face, revealing a woman standing at the foot of his bed holding a candle; she also vanished instantly. Finally, a third awakening brought a dreadful shriek echoing through the room. These apparitions formed part of a haunting family lore that included a phantom hearse driving up to the hall at midnight.

#location
The events took place at Spixworth Hall, a historic Elizabethan mansion that stood in the village of Spixworth, Norfolk.

#title
The Witch of East Somerton

#summary
A suspected witch with a wooden leg was buried alive in the nave of St Mary's Church in East Somerton, where an oak tree grew from her leg and her restless spirit continues to haunt the ruins.

#details
During the height of the English witch trials, villagers in East Somerton accused a woman of witchcraft and buried her alive in the nave of St Mary's Church to prevent her soul from joining Satan. According to local legend, because she had a wooden leg, a mighty oak tree sprouted from it and grew through the center of the church. On cold, dark nights, her restless spirit is said to drift across the skeletal ruins of the 13th-century church, and visitors who circle her tree three times while whispering her name may provoke her ghost.

#location
The ruined St Mary's Church located in the woodlands of East Somerton, Norfolk.

#title
The Haunting of Winston Hall

#summary
Winston Hall is renowned for its ghostly auditory phenomena, where witnesses report hearing a rustling silk dress, heavy nighttime footsteps, and a disembodied voice.

#details
Dating back to the reign of Queen Elizabeth I and historically linked to local traditions involving Richard Cromwell, the ancient farmhouse known as Winston Hall is the site of persistent and eerie sounds. Although the spirits are never visually sighted, residents and visitors frequently hear the distinct, stiff rustling of a silk dress moving along a corridor at the back of the house. Additionally, heavy footsteps pace about during the night, and a phantom voice calls out "are you there" from an old exterior door, leaving no one behind when investigated.

#location
Winston Hall is an old historic farmhouse situated in the village of Gillingham, Norfolk.

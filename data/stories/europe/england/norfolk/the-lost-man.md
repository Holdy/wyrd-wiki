#title
The Lost Man

#summary
A retired soldier on an early morning fishing trip becomes hopelessly disoriented in a dense marshland fog and is attacked by a terrifying, clawed entity on Meadow Lane.

#details
In November 1921, retired soldier Jonathan Barkley ventured out early for a fishing trip along the marshes near Blakeney and Cley. Trapped by an unnatural, choking fog, he found himself unable to navigate the winding waterways until he spotted a glowing lantern carried by someone wading through water far too deep to touch. Terrified, he fled the marsh and reached solid ground, stumbling onto Meadow Lane in Cley next the Sea. There, a jittery, black-veiled figure holding a lantern approached him and slashed his side with a clawed hand before Barkley lost consciousness and was later discovered outside The George pub.

#location
Meadow Lane in Cley next the Sea, Norfolk, where the protagonist was attacked by the mysterious lantern-bearing entity.

#title
The Devil's Footstep at Fossditch

#summary
The ancient linear earthwork known as the Fossditch is said in local legend to have been gouged out by the Devil dragging his foot across the landscape.

#details
The miles-long linear earthwork dividing the parish of Weeting from Hockwold, known as the Fossditch or Fendyke, is the subject of old Breckland folklore. According to the tradition, the deep trench was created when the Devil dragged his foot along the ground, and a clod of soil that he scraped off his boot subsequently fell to form the earthworks at Thetford Castle.

#location
The Fossditch is an early Saxon linear earthwork running along the western boundary of Weeting parish in Norfolk, around grid reference TL771953.

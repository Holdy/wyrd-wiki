#title
The Secret Tunnel and Golden Gates of Caistor Camp

#summary
Local legend maintains that a subterranean tunnel links the ancient Roman town of Caistor St Edmund to a neighboring church, accompanied by priceless golden gates buried deep underground. According to tradition, this mysterious passage begins at the old water-gate of the Roman earthworks.

#details
For generations, local lore surrounding the ancient Roman site of Venta Icenorum at Caistor St Edmund has spoken of hidden subterranean wonders. Tradition records the existence of a secret tunnel running from the Roman camp across the landscape to Arminghall church. Alternative accounts specify that this underground passage originates at the water-gate situated in the northwest corner of the Roman town's defensive earthworks. Compounding the mystery, local stories insist that priceless golden or brass gates are buried deep beneath the soil near the ancient encampment.

#location
The secret tunnel and buried gates are said to be located at the site of Venta Icenorum (Caistor Roman Town / Caistor Camp) in Caistor St Edmund, Norfolk.

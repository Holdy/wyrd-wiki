#title
The Phantom Patron of The Crown Inn

#summary
The 15th-century Crown Inn in Pulham Market is said to be haunted by the spirit of a former regular who still returns to his favourite corner and table.

#details
Standing proudly on the village green in Pulham Market next to the parish church, The Crown Inn is a Grade II listed building whose oldest parts date back to the 15th century, originally constructed from timber-framed buildings that reportedly housed workers building the adjacent church. According to local tradition, no building of such antiquity is without its resident spirit. Tales from several sources indicate that the ghost of a gentleman from the past continues to frequent the historic pub. Staff and visitors have reported sensing his presence in a specific corner of the establishment, accompanied by the distinct aroma of his favourite smoke whenever he visits his preferred table.

#location
The Crown Inn, situated on The Green in the village of Pulham Market, Norfolk.

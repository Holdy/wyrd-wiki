#title
Murder in the Well

#summary
Mary Bone's Well, situated between Coxford and Broomsthorpe, is said to be named after a local woman who was drowned in its waters by a priest from the neighboring priory.

#details
Marked on Ordnance Survey maps near the remains of the Augustinian St. Mary's Priory, Mary Bone's Well carries a grim local legend. According to tradition, the ancient well derives its eerie name from a woman named Mary Bone, who met a violent end when she was drowned in the well's depths by a corrupt priest from the nearby priory.

#location
Mary Bone's Well is located a little south-east of the remains of St. Mary's Priory, between Coxford and Broomsthorpe at grid reference TF847287.

#title
The Phantom of Mary Bone's Well

#summary
A woman was tragically drowned in an ancient well by a corrupt priest near the historic priory, leaving her ghostly spirit bound to the waters.

#details
Deep in the quiet fields of West Norfolk near the River Tat, Mary Bone's Well marks the site of a dark historical tragedy. According to local folklore, a woman associated with the nearby Augustinian St. Mary's Priory was murdered when a local priest drowned her in the well. The priory itself was historically plagued by scandal and lax discipline among its clergy, fostering an environment where such dark deeds could occur. To this day, the phantom of a White Lady is said to haunt the well, forever linked to the violent crime that tainted the priory's primary water supply.

#location
Mary Bone's Well is situated just south-east of the remains of St. Mary's Priory, in the countryside between Coxford and Broomsthorpe in Norfolk.

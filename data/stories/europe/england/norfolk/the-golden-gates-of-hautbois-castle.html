The Golden Gates of Hautbois Castle

Summary

During a period of medieval unrest, the owner of Hautbois Castle cast the estate's golden gates into a nearby bottomless pond for safekeeping, where they are said to remain hidden to this day.

Details

In the marshes beside the River Bure within the parish of Coltishall lie the sparse earthwork remains of Hautbois Castle, originally built in 1312. Local tradition relates that when trouble threatened the manor, the owner had the castle's solid golden gates removed and dropped into a nearby pond until the danger passed. Because the pool was reputed to be bottomless, the precious gates could never be retrieved and continue to rest in the depths of what is now known as Golden Gates Pond.

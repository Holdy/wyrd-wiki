#title
The Friendly Hound of Swanton Morley

#summary
In November 1939, a young soldier cycling back to his mid-Norfolk airfield on a dark night was unexpectedly accompanied along a narrow lane by a silent, panting black dog. The spectral hound trotted beside him with an aura of friendliness before vanishing into the darkness when the moonlight faded.

#details
During the winter of 1939, a young Territorial soldier was cycling back late at night to his unit guarding a mid-Norfolk airfield after visiting his parents. Passing All Saints' Church in Swanton Morley, he took a wrong fork onto a narrow green lane. As the moonlight broke through, he realized a large black dog was running alongside him. The hound had its tongue lolling and its flanks heaving as it panted silently, yet it radiated an unexpected sense of deep friendliness. When sudden clouds blotted out the moon and he emerged onto the main tarmac road to Dereham, his canine companion had completely vanished.

#location
The encounter took place along a narrow green lane running east-west between the former B1147 and Woodgate Lane near Swanton Morley church (around grid reference TG015164).

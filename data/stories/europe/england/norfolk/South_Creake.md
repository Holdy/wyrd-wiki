#title
The Blood-Soaked Slopes of Bloodgate Hill

#summary
Local folklore attributes the gruesome name of Bloodgate Hill to a vicious battle fought between ancient inhabitants and invaders, where so much blood was shed that it streamed down the road. According to tradition, this fierce conflict shaped both the grim name and the legacy of the route leading from the hilltop fort.

#details
Overlooking the village of South Creake, the ancient earthwork fort on Bloodgate Hill has long been associated with tales of brutal warfare. Local tradition and the village sign depict a fierce clash between early Britons or Saxons and invading Danes. According to the legend, the combat was so ferocious and the slaughter so immense that the blood of the wounded and dying flowed freely down the hill, giving the thoroughfare and the hill its grim moniker—Bloodgate.

#location
Bloodgate Hill is situated just southwest of the village of South Creake in North Norfolk, marking the site of the ancient Iron Age hillfort.

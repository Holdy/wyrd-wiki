#title
The Devil's Ditch and Hangour Hill

#summary
According to local legend, the ancient linear earthwork known as the Bichamditch was dug by the Devil, who created the nearby round barrow of Hangour Hill by scraping the leftover earth from his spade.

#details
In the parish of Beachamwell, Norfolk, a long prehistoric or early Saxon linear earthwork known as the Bichamditch—frequently referred to locally as the Devil's Ditch—cuts across the countryside. According to enduring folk tradition, the ditch was excavated by the Devil himself using supernatural powers. When he finished digging the massive trench, he cleaned his spade by scraping the excess soil onto a single spot, thereby forming the prominent round barrow known as Hangour Hill. Local lore further maintains that hidden deep beneath the earth of the mound lies a pair of mysterious silver gates.

#location
Hangour Hill is a round barrow located at grid reference TF752087 in the parish of Beachamwell, Norfolk.

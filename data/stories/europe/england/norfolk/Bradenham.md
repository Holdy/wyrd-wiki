#title
The Screaming Gibbet

#summary
A weary farmer riding across a desolate heath on a stormy night has a terrifying encounter with a hanging gibbet when an unearthly scream sounds and a skeletal bone drops from the cage.

#details
Late one stormy night, a tired farmer was riding his exhausted horse across Bradenham Heath when a sudden squall broke and the moon illuminated the local gibbet. As the horse came to a halt directly beneath the hanging iron cage containing the remains of convicted murderer Stephen Watson, an unearthly scream pierced the air. Simultaneously, a heavy skeletal leg bone broke loose from the cage and plummeted past the rider's boot into the darkness.

#location
The events take place on Bradenham Heath (also known as Bradenham Common) located near Swaffham in Norfolk.

The Churchyard Phantom of Buxton

Summary

A woman walking past the Buxton parish church encountered a spectral black dog moments before a tragic family loss occurred miles away.

Details

During the 1970s, a local woman living in Buxton, Norfolk, was walking past the village church when the clock struck four in the afternoon. Suddenly, a large black dog materialized by her side. Feeling no fear, she reached out to stroke the animal and greeted it, but the creature vanished into thin air almost instantly. Four days later, she received news that her brother had passed away in Liverpool at the exact minute the phantom hound had appeared beside her at the church.

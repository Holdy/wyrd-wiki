#title
The Halvergate Shuck Encounter

#summary
During a severe November storm in 2000, a motorist driving across the marshes near Acle witnessed a gigantic, wolf-like spectral hound with burning red eyes pacing his vehicle. Another similar midnight encounter on the Acle Straight was later recounted by student researchers interviewing local witnesses.

#details
In November 2000, motorist Nigel Thorpe was driving home from Great Yarmouth during a heavy storm around 5 pm. As he passed the Vauxhall Caravan site and headed onto the open road across the marshes towards Acle, he spotted an enormous dog—resembling a giant wolfhound with glowing red eyes—running alongside his car in the fields. Rather than feeling frightened, he felt elated recognizing it as Black Shuck. Thorpe later learned of another encounter from student researchers in Norwich, where a musician driving a gear van down the Acle Straight at 2 am encountered Shuck sitting in the road before the creature trotted alongside the vehicle.

#location
The sighting took place along the Acle Straight (the A47 road running across Halvergate Marshes between Great Yarmouth and Acle).

#title
The Phantom Pedestrian of the Acle Straight

#summary
A motorist driving along the Acle Straight near the Halvergate turn-off watched in horror as a middle-aged man stepped directly into the path of their car, only for the vehicle to pass right through the phantom figure.

#details
Stretching nine miles across reclaimed marshland between Acle and Great Yarmouth, the Acle Straight has long carried a reputation as one of Norfolk's most haunted highways. While driving along the road toward Acle one evening, a motorist approached the curve near the Halvergate turn-off and the former Stracey Arms. Suddenly, a middle-aged man emerged from the right-hand side of the road and stepped directly into the path of the oncoming vehicle. With no time to brake or swerve, the driver braced for impact, but as the figure turned to look at them, the car passed completely through his body without resistance.

#location
The sighting took place on the Acle Straight (A47) near the Halvergate turn-off and the former Stracey Arms between Acle and Great Yarmouth, Norfolk.

#title
The Spectres of the Acle Straight

#summary
Motorists travelling along the Acle Straight have reported harrowing encounters with a phantom pedestrian near the Halvergate turn-off who vanishes as vehicles pass right through him.

#details
A driver heading towards Acle watched in horror as a middle-aged man stepped out from the right side of the road into the path of their car near the Halvergate turn-off. With no time to brake, the vehicle passed straight through the figure, who turned and looked directly at the driver before disappearing into thin air. Other motorists across this nine-mile stretch have similarly reported unexplained apparitions, phantom horse-drawn carts, and sudden, unprompted impulses to slam on their brakes.

#location
The Acle Straight (A47) near the Halvergate turn-off and the former Stracey Arms between Acle and Great Yarmouth.

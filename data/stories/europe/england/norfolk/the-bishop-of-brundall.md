#title
The Bishop of Brundall

#summary
A ghostly bishop is said to glide down the River Yare past Brundall on a barge rowed by white-robed figures, bestowing blessings twice a year. Witnessing the apparition is believed to bring a year of good health and fortune.

#details
Twice a year, on June 24th and September 18th, a phantom barge carrying the "Bishop of Brundall" appears on the River Yare as it flows past Brundall. The bishop, dressed in a long white gown, is accompanied by twenty-eight rowers similarly robed in white. As the ghostly vessel glides along the waterway, the bishop bestows a ghostly blessing upon those on the riverbanks. Local folklore holds that anyone fortunate enough to witness the phantom barge will receive good health and good fortune for the coming year.

#location
The stretch of the River Yare flowing past the village of Brundall in Norfolk.

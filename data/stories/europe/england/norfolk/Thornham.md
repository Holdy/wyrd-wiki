#title
The Haunting of Thornham Staithe

#summary
Unexplained deaths and terrifying hauntings grip a Norfolk coastal village, leading the local vicar and two paranormal investigators to uncover a dark supernatural threat. When the village historian is pushed to the brink of madness and a former Deliverance Minister must be summoned, the community faces perilous consequences.

#details
In the coastal village of Thornham, a series of seemingly unrelated hauntings begins to plague the residents, initially attracting little attention until the first mysterious death occurs. As more unwitnessed deaths follow, villagers are reluctant to speak out or believe in ghosts. Following a publican's psychic supper that terrifies a medium, the local vicar calls in two paranormal investigators. Events quickly escalate: villagers face direct threats, a mysterious stranger plays a dangerous hand, the doctor's wife seemingly returns from the dead, and the village historian is driven to the edge of sanity while trying to connect the hauntings. Realising the gravity of the situation, the vicar decides to take matters into his own hands and summons an old priest who once served as the diocese's Deliverance Minister to confront the malevolent force.

#location
Thornham Staithe is situated on the north Norfolk coast within the village of Thornham, adjacent to the local salt marshes and historic harbour.

#title
The Smugglers' Trap at Thornham Lifeboat Inn

#summary
On New Year's Eve in 1782, local smugglers lured excise officers and dragoon guards into a violent ambush at a coastal cottage in Thornham that is now known as The Lifeboat Inn.

#details
During the late 18th century, the coastal village of Thornham and its harbour were notorious havens for contraband and illegal trade. On December 31, 1782, a large band of approximately eighty smugglers, organized by local smuggling leader Thomas Franklyn, learned that excise authorities intended to search local storehouses. Operating out of a 16th-century cottage and outbuildings owned by William Southgate—a building that later became The Lifeboat Inn on Ship Lane—the smugglers laid a cunning trap for the authorities. When Excise Superintendent Robert Bliss led a small force of dragoon guards to search the property, they were violently ambushed by Franklyn's militia. The ensuing skirmish resulted in a bloody battle that left numerous combatants wounded and incapacitated, firmly cementing Thornham's legacy in local folklore as one of the most lawless and fiercely defended smuggler strongholds on the Norfolk coast.

#location

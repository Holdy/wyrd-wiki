#title
The Halvergate Shuck

#summary
In November 2000, a motorist driving past the Halvergate Marshes during a severe storm encountered the legendary phantom Black Dog, a gigantic wolfhound with burning red eyes that kept pace alongside his vehicle.

#details
In November 2000, a driver heading home from Great Yarmouth during a heavy storm noticed the road was deserted as he approached the open stretches near Halvergate. Looking across the marshland, he spotted a colossal hound resembling a wolfhound with intense red eyes. Recognizing the apparition as the legendary Black Shuck, the motorist watched as the phantom beast ran parallel to his car, keeping exact pace across the field for about half a mile before turning back into the marshes. Subsequent local accounts, including one from a musician driving a gear van along the Acle Straight near Halvergate at two in the morning, described similar encounters with the spectral dog falling in beside vehicles in the dead of night.

#location
Halvergate Marshes, situated alongside the Acle Straight in Norfolk, UK.

#title
The Halvergate Shuck Encounter

#summary
In November 2000, a motorist driving past the marshes toward Halvergate during a storm was accompanied alongside his vehicle by a massive wolfhound-like spectral dog with burning red eyes.

#details
While driving home from Great Yarmouth during a severe storm in November 2000, a motorist passed the Vauxhall Caravan site and headed onto the open road crossing the marshes toward Halvergate. Glancing to his right, he was astonished to see an enormous, wolfhound-sized hound with glowing red eyes running alongside his car. Recognizing the legendary apparition, he felt exhilarated rather than afraid as the beast kept pace for about half a mile before turning back across the marshes. Years later, another driver encountered the same spectral hound on the nearby Acle Straight at two in the morning, where it fell in beside his van and accompanied him along the road.

#location
The open road crossing the marshes between the Vauxhall Caravan site and Halvergate in Norfolk.

#title
The Phantom of the Halvergate Turn-Off

#summary
A driver traveling along the Acle Straight near Halvergate experienced sheer terror when a middle-aged man stepped directly into the path of their car, only for the vehicle to pass right through the spectral figure.

#details
While driving past the Halvergate turn-off on the Acle Straight, a motorist watched in horror as a middle-aged man suddenly walked out into the middle of the road from the right-hand side. With no time to brake or swerve, the driver braced for impact as the figure turned to look directly at them, but the car passed straight through the phantom without resistance.

#location
The junction where the Halvergate branch road meets the Acle Straight (A47) near Halvergate, Norfolk.

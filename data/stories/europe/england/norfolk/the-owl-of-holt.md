#title
The Owl of Holt

#summary
According to local folklore, the people of Holt once attempted to trap an owl overnight in a walled spring and later in a church waterspout, an infamous blunder commemorated to this day on the town sign.

#details
In the town of Holt in Norfolk, a longstanding piece of local folklore recounts the amusing antics of the townsfolk. On the western edge of Spout Common, a walled spring once served as the town pound. According to legend, a local man attempted to pen a captured owl inside the walled enclosure overnight. A variant of the tale tells that the men of Holt also tried to drown an owl by trapping it within the waterspout of the parish church tower, believing it would perish in the next downpour, only for the bird to easily fly out of the top. In lighthearted remembrance of these traditional noodle tales, an owl features proudly on the Holt town sign.

#location
The spring is located on the western edge of Spout Common (TG075387) in Holt, Norfolk.

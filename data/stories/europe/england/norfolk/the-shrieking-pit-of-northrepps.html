The Shrieking Pit of Northrepps

Summary

On the anniversary of her tragic death, the ghost of a heartbroken young woman named Esmeralda is said to haunt the pits near Hungry Hill, endlessly wailing for help.

Details

Local folklore tells of a 17-year-old village girl named Esmeralda who fell deeply in love with a wealthy farmer. After the local vicar discovered their secret affair and ordered them to part ways, the farmer abandoned her and returned to his wife. Overcome with grief and depression, Esmeralda wandered out one frosty night and threw herself into one of the ancient pits near Hungry Hill in Northrepps. Instantly regretting her desperate act, she cried out for help before drowning in the murky water. According to legend, her haunting shrieks and cries can still be heard echoing from the water every year on February 24th, the anniversary of her death.

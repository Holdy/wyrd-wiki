#title
The Haunting of the Ffolkes Arms

#summary
The historic 300-year-old coaching inn in Hillington is famously haunted by the spirit of Agnes, a young nanny who plunged to her death from an attic bedroom. Her restless apparition and chilling paranormal phenomena continue to be reported by guests and staff.

#details
Standing on Lynn Road in Hillington, the Ffolkes Arms is a venerable 300-year-old coaching inn steeped in local history and folklore. According to traditional accounts handed down through generations of staff and travelers, the inn is haunted by the ghost of a young nursemaid or nanny named Agnes. In the 19th century, Agnes tragically fell or leapt from her quarters in the attic rooms, reportedly becoming impaled upon spikes below. Ever since, her spectral presence has supposedly lingered within the historic building. Visitors and staff have reported encountering eerie anomalies, including chilling drops in temperature in the back rooms regardless of the weather, and fleeting sightings of a heavy, shadowy black shape filling doorways.

#location
The Ffolkes (formerly the Ffolkes Arms) is a historic coaching inn situated on Lynn Road in the village of Hillington, Norfolk.

#title
The Tragic Fall of Agnes at The Ffolkes

#summary
The historic Norfolk inn is haunted by the sorrowful spirit of a nanny who plummeted to her death from the attic, as well as the lingering presence of a former prison guard.

#details
The Ffolkes in Hillington has long been renowned for paranormal activity, most famously centered around a nurse or nanny named Agnes. During the nineteenth century, Agnes met a violent and untimely demise when she fell from her attic room and was impaled on spikes below. Staff and visitors have since reported chilling phenomena throughout the property, such as the second back room remaining persistently cold regardless of outside weather and unsettling sightings of a large black shape filling doorways. Historically, the building also served as a temporary holding post for prisoners being transported to Norwich, giving rise to persistent folklore that the attic spaces are haunted by the lingering presence of a prison guard.

#location
The story takes place at The Ffolkes hotel and public house situated on Lynn Road in the village of Hillington, Norfolk (PE31 6BJ).

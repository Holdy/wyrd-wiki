#title
The Ghostly Shuck Dog of Clip Street Lane

#summary
A grandmother witnessed a ghostly Shuck Dog just outside her bungalow on a green lane in Bale. The spectral hound's appearance was preserved as a vivid family memory.

#details
Passed down through family lore, a grandmother's encounter with a spectral 'Shuck Dog' occurred just a few yards from the gate of her newly built bungalow on a green lane in Bale. Although the primary witnesses have since passed away, the childhood recollection of this phantom hound remained a deeply real and enduring part of the family's oral tradition.

#location
The ghostly hound was encountered just outside a bungalow located on Clip Street Lane in Bale, Norfolk.

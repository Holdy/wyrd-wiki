The Woman in Red at Hardley Crossroads

Summary

At a lonely crossroads in Hardley, the apparition of an old woman dressed in red appears at midnight to sit upon a historic stone marker. The site's supernatural reputation dates back centuries, earning it the historic map designation of 'Haunted Gap'.

Details

Near Hardley, at the junction where three roads and a dirt track meet about half a mile south-west of the village church, stands a small medieval cross-stone. According to local folklore first documented in the late 19th century, a spectral old woman clad in red materialises at midnight on certain nights to sit silently upon the stone. The area's eerie reputation predates formal collection, as Bryant's 1826 map of Norfolk explicitly named the spot 'Haunted Gap'.

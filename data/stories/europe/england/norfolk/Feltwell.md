#title
Mary Barley of Short Beck

#summary
Mary Barley, who passed away in Feltwell in 1875, is frequently reported appearing at night dressed in dark clothing and a shawl near the forks of Short Beck.

#details
Mary Barley lived in a house in Short Beck, Feltwell, and died in 1875 at the age of 67. Following her death, she was reportedly seen at various times walking at night in the Borough—the triangular patch of land situated between the left and right forks in Short Beck. Local tradition suggests her nocturnal wanderings might be tied to a hidden or buried hoard of money that she was unable to leave behind.

#location
The Borough, a triangular patch of land between the left and right forks of Short Beck in Feltwell, Norfolk.

#upstream-source
An old Feltwell pamphlet by the Rev. Daubeney from 1954 (citing an account from the Thetford and Watton Times).

https://www.feltwell.net/feltwell2/times/ghost_stories.htm

#title
The Apparition of Mary Barley

#summary
An elderly woman dressed in dark clothing and a shawl is occasionally seen wandering at night in Short Beck. She is believed to be the spirit of Mary Barley, who passed away in Feltwell in 1875.

#details
Mary Barley died in Feltwell in 1875 at the age of 67. Since her passing, she has been reported appearing at night in the Borough—the triangular patch of land situated between the left and right forks in Short Beck—near the house where she once lived. Local folklore suggests she continues to walk the area because of a hidden or buried hoard of money she cannot leave behind.

#location
The Borough is the triangular patch of land situated between the left and right forks in Short Beck in Feltwell, Norfolk.

#upstream-source
An account given in the *Thetford and Watton Times*, as cited in a 1954 pamphlet by Rev. Daubeney.

#reference
https://www.feltwell.net/feltwell2/times/ghost_stories.htm

#title
The Hound of Poppylot Road

#summary
A cyclist traveling along Poppylot Road at midnight in the 1930s encountered a massive black hound with glowing green eyes that was dragging a heavy rattling chain.

#details
In the 1930s, a witness was cycling along Poppylot Road between Feltwell and Southery at approximately midnight on a clear, moonlit night. Two green eyes suddenly appeared in the beam of their front bicycle light, revealing a large black dog running directly towards them in the center of the road. The oversized creature was dragging a heavy, rattling chain behind it, but maintained its course and completely ignored the cyclist as they passed by.

#location
Poppylot Road situated between Feltwell and Southery in Norfolk.

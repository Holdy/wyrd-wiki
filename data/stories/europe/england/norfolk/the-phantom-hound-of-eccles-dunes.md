#title
The Phantom Hound of Eccles Dunes

#summary
A spectral black dog with glowing yellow and red-centered eyes haunts the coastal dunes at midnight, bringing certain death to anyone who looks into its gaze. 

#details
At the stroke of midnight, a terrifying phantom hound is said to roam the sand dunes of Eccles within the coastal parish of Lessingham. Described as a large black dog, its most striking and lethal feature is its glowing yellow eyes, each marked with a piercing red center. According to local lore, anyone unfortunate enough to cross paths with the spectral beast and gaze directly into its burning eyes is doomed to die shortly after.

#location
The sand dunes of Eccles within the coastal parish of Lessingham in northeast Norfolk.

#title
The Witch of Loddon

#summary
A nineteenth-century wise woman known as Mother Chergrave made her living in Loddon by selling graveyard charms and telling fortunes, ultimately attempting to pass her box of familiars on to her daughter. Before her death, she instructed her daughter to let the imps bite her breasts to inherit her full magical powers, but the daughter instead burned the unopened box.

#details
In Victorian times, Mother Chergrave (often pronounced 'Chegriff') operated in the Norfolk village of Loddon as a service magician and cunning woman. She earned a living by reading tealeaves and selling charms, including a verse charm that promised to reveal a woman's future husband if performed over a graveyard stone at midnight, at the grim cost of losing the final year of one's natural life. Mother Chergrave possessed bat-winged imps the size of small rats, which she kept in a box. When she was dying, she urged her sole daughter, Mara, to take the box, let the imps bite her breasts to become a greater witch than her mother, and vowed that she must never marry. However, the moment her mother passed away, Mara threw the unopened box straight into the fireplace and subsequently married.

#location
Holy Trinity Church in Loddon, Norfolk.

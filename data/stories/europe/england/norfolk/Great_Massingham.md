#title
The Black Shuck of Peddar's Way

#summary
A young scout on a night hike along Peddar's Way was suddenly attacked by a massive, phantom black hound that leaped clean over him before vanishing into thin air. Exactly one year later to the day, he survived a near-fatal pedestrian traffic accident.

#details
In October 1977, an 18-year-old participating in a night hike with a Venture Scout unit was walking southward along Peddar's Way in Great Massingham. While straggling slightly behind the rest of the group about a quarter of a mile past a landmark known as Shepherd's Bush, he spotted what he initially took to be a sheepdog running swiftly toward him down a slight rise in the road. Before he could make sense of it, the creature lunged directly at him. He put up his arms and stumbled to his knees bracing for impact, but the beast passed straight over his head without resistance. Turning around, he found the country road completely empty. Unfamiliar with local folklore at the time, he later learned from his family about the legend of Black Shuck—and the chilling superstition that seeing the phantom hound foretells tragedy before the year is out. True to the legend, exactly one year later on October 23rd, 1978, he was struck by a vehicle as a pedestrian and spent months recovering from severe injuries.

#location
The encounter took place on Peddar's Way, approximately a quarter of a mile south of Shepherd's Bush in Great Massingham (around grid reference TF797197).

#title
The Phantom Hound of Peddar's Way

#summary
An 18-year-old scout on a night hike along Peddar's Way was suddenly confronted by a massive black spectral hound that leaped toward him before mysteriously vanishing. Exactly one year later, the witness suffered a near-fatal accident, fulfilling the local omen associated with sighting the beast.

#details
On Sunday, 23 October 1977, an 18-year-old member of the 14th Wootton Venture Scout Unit was participating in a night hike heading south along Peddar's Way in Great Massingham. At around 5:30 in the morning, while walking about a quarter of a mile past a landmark known as Shepherd's Bush, he spotted what he initially took to be a sheepdog running rapidly toward him down a slight rise in the road. As the distance closed, the creature leaped directly at him. Described as a huge black dog comparable in size to a St. Bernard, it featured a shaggy black coat, a wide mouth with prominent teeth, and wide eyes. The scout braced for impact and stumbled to his knees, but the anticipated physical thrust never materialized, and when he looked up, the animal had completely vanished. Later that day, his mother told him about the legend of Black Shuck and the folklore that seeing the apparition portends death within the year. True to the superstition, exactly one year later on 23 October 1978, the witness was struck as a pedestrian in a severe road traffic accident that left him in critical condition.

#location
The encounter occurred on Peddar's Way in Great Massingham, approximately a quarter of a mile past the location known as Shepherd's Bush.

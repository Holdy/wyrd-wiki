#title
The Screaming Cockler of Blacknock Sandbank

#summary
A headstrong 19th-century cockler named Nancy refused to leave the mudflats before filling her sack, becoming trapped by a sudden dense fog and rising tide. She drowned while screaming curses at the elements, and her vengeful spirit now haunts the Stiffkey saltmarshes.

#details
In the 19th century, cockling on the tidal flats of Stiffkey was harsh and perilous work. A headstrong young woman named Nancy stayed out on the mudflats longer than the other villagers, determined to fully fill her sack despite worsening weather. As a thick coastal fog—known locally as a roke—rolled in and the tide rapidly rose, Nancy became hopelessly disorientated. Though villagers heard her frantic cries, nobody could navigate through the dense mist in time to save her. Her drowned body was discovered the following morning on Blacknock sandbank with her cockle sack still tied to her wrist. Ever since, her ghostly apparition is said to haunt the marshes on foggy nights, her terrifying screams and curses echoing across the coast.

#location
Blacknock sandbank is a tidal mudflat situated within the saltmarshes directly north of Stiffkey village in Norfolk.

#title
The Screaming Cockler of Stiffkey

#summary
On foggy nights across the treacherous saltmarshes of Stiffkey, the ghost of a drowned cock-gatherer named Nancy haunts the mudflats, her angry screams warning travelers of the lethal tides.

#details
In the 18th century, collecting cockles from the saltmarshes was a grueling and hazardous daily race against the fast-moving North Norfolk tides. One evening, after the other women had headed back to the safety of Stiffkey village, a young cockler named Nancy lingered behind because her sack was only half full. As thick fog rolled in, she became completely disoriented and trapped by the incoming water. Although fishermen heard her desperate cries, the tide was too low to launch large boats, and rescuers in smaller craft arrived too late. Nancy drowned, and her body was discovered the next day clutching her harvesting knife with a fierce glare. Today, her spirit is said to haunt Blacknock sandbank, where her terrifying screams still echo across the marshes.

#location
The saltmarshes and Blacknock sandbank located off the coast of the village of Stiffkey in North Norfolk.

#title
The Shadow in the Marsh

#summary
While spending the night on the Stiffkey saltmarshes to investigate local legends, an author is stalked and pursued back to his lodging by a terrifying spectral hound.

#details
Author Christopher Marlowe, lodging in a cottage off the A149 in Stiffkey, resolved to witness the legendary Shuck Dog firsthand. He ventured out onto the marshes at night, settling into a hollow near a tidal pool. By the light of the half-moon, a monstrous black dog with a massive body and glowing, ferocious eyes emerged, howling menacingly as it hunted its prey. Terrified, Marlowe fled across the marsh toward his cottage with the beast hot on his heels. Just as he reached the door, he felt the scorching breath of the hound before tumbling inside as the creature slammed into the heavy timber with a deafening thud.

#location
A cottage situated along a lane approximately 800 yards from the A149 road in Stiffkey, alongside the adjacent coastal saltmarshes.

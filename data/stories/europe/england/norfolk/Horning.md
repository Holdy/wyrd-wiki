#title
The Walking Statues of Horning Church

#summary
The stone statues of the Four Evangelists perched atop St. Benedict's church tower were once rumored by locals to descend one by one in successive years before returning to their perches.

#details
At St. Benedict's church in Horning, four stone statues representing the Four Evangelists adorn the tower. Although the church tower itself dates back to the 14th century, these effigies were actually added during the 1880s. Within a few decades of their installation, a curious local folk belief emerged claiming that the statues were animate, descending from the roof one by one in successive years to walk about before eventually returning to their stone pedestals.

#location
St. Benedict's Church stands in the Broadland village of Horning, Norfolk (grid reference TG355166).

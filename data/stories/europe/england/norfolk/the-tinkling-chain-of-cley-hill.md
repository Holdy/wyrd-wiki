#title
The Tinkling Chain of Cley Hill

#summary
Walking home along the A149 from Cley to Blakeney at midnight, a woman is stalked up a hill by an invisible presence dragging a chain. She hears the unseen entity pass down Back Lane before fleeing down the High Street.

#details
During the summer of around 1968, a woman was walking home from Cley to Blakeney along the A149 road at approximately midnight. As she reached the bottom of Cley Hill (also referred to as Howe Hill), she heard the light dragging and tinkling of a chain behind her. Although she could see nothing in the dark, whatever was making the noise closely followed her all the way up the slope. Upon reaching the street-lit crossroads at the top, she stopped and waited to see what would pass, yet still spotted nothing. She then heard the entity turn and move away down Back Lane. Recognizing the local folklore that Black Shuck frequented this lane before heading across the marsh banks, she panicked and fled all the way home down the High Street.

#location
The encounter occurred along the A149 road ascending Cley Hill towards the crossroads and Back Lane in Blakeney.

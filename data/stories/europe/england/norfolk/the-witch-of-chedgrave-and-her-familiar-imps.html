The Witch of Chedgrave and her Familiar Imps

Summary

In 19th-century Chedgrave, a notorious wise woman known as Mother Chergrave practiced magic alongside her bat-winged imps until her dramatic death, after which her adopted daughter cast the familiars into the fire.

Details

In the 19th century, Mother Chergrave operated as a renowned wise woman in the Chedgrave and Loddon area, openly practicing magic, palmistry, and creating herbal remedies. After one of her long absences, she brought back a young girl named Mary, whom she claimed was bound to Satan. Mary was rescued and raised by a kindly neighbor, Mrs Crab, while carrying protective charms. When Mother Chergrave lay on her deathbed, she commanded Mary to take her box of bat-winged imps and embrace witchcraft rather than marry. As the old woman died shrieking, villagers crowded around the room clamoring to see the legendary familiars. Hearing the music of a fiddle played by a local pedlar known as "Lucky Chance" outside, Mary placed the sealed box of imps directly onto the fire, destroying them before she and Chance married and left the village.

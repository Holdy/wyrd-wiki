#title
The Nailed Effigies of Castle Rising

#summary
In 1962, parishioners in Castle Rising discovered human effigies and a thorn-studded sheep's heart nailed to their church door in an act of malicious folk magic. This mysterious event later became the subject of a BBC documentary archive investigating ritualistic magic in Norfolk.

#details
In 1962, the quiet Norfolk village of Castle Rising was disturbed by a grim discovery when parishioners found human effigies and a thorn-studded sheep's heart nailed directly to their church door. Regarded by historians as a tangible example of real-world *maleficium*—harmful folk magic or attempted witchcraft—this physical act mirrored ancient traditions of cursing and magical attacks. The unsettling occurrence caught national attention when broadcaster Chris Brasher investigated it for a 1964 BBC *Tonight* documentary, discussing the symbols and rituals found across Norfolk churches with occult commentator F.R. Buckley. Historian Malcolm Gaskill later highlighted this incident in academic writings as a rare, historical instance of aggressive magic and witchcraft attempts in modern Britain.

#location
The door of St Lawrence Church in the village of Castle Rising, Norfolk.

#title
Pigg's Grave

#summary
Marked by a crossroads where three Norfolk parishes meet, Pigg's Grave is steeped in local legend concerning a hanged highwayman or a suicide burial.

#details
At the isolated crossroads on Gallowhill Lane where the boundaries of Briningham, Melton Constable, and Swanton Novers converge lies Pigg's Grave. Local tradition holds that a man named Pigg—variously remembered as a notorious highwayman who was hanged at the site or, alternatively, as a traveler murdered by a robber—was buried at the junction. Antiquarian and historical records note that the ominous burial site also aligns with the traditional practice of interring suicides at crossroads.

#location
Pigg's Grave is situated at the crossroads on Gallowhill Lane where the parishes of Briningham, Melton Constable, and Swanton Novers meet in north Norfolk (Grid Reference TG 0279 3312).

#title
Pigg's Grave

#summary
A notorious highwayman named Pigg was executed and interred at the isolated crossroads where the parish boundaries of Briningham, Melton Constable, and Swanton Novers converge.

#details
According to local folklore and historical records, a highwayman named Pigg met his end at the gallows and was buried at the crossroads where the Aylsham to Fakenham road intersects with the Holt to East Dereham road. While some versions of the tale name Pigg as the criminal who was hanged there, alternative traditions claim he was a victim whose attacker was executed nearby at Gallowhill Lane, or that the site marks an ancient crossroads burial place for a suicide. The landmark, known as Pigg's Grave, continues to be a notable piece of dark local history where Briningham meets its neighboring parishes.

#location
Pigg's Grave is situated at the road junction where the Aylsham/Fakenham road meets the Holt/East Dereham road on the parish boundary of Briningham, Norfolk.

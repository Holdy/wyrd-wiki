#title
The Phantom of Dinah's Lane

#summary
Local folklore recalls the tragic fate of Dinah Bunting, a woman who was hanged from a tree for theft and whose name endures along a quiet lane in Spooner Row. Her lingering spirit is said to haunt the route where she met her execution.

#details
According to traditional folklore preserved by the Wymondham Heritage Society, Dinah Bunting was accused of stealing and condemned to death. As punishment, she was hanged from a tree in the area that is now known as Dinah's Lane in Spooner Row. Over generations, locals have passed down tales of her execution and reports of her ghostly presence lingering among the trees along the lane.

#location
Dinah's Lane is a specific rural track located within the village of Spooner Row in Norfolk.

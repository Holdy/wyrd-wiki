Bodger's Gap

Summary

According to village folklore, a hedgerow at Cross Green in Edgefield remains barren of plant life where a murderer named Bodger allegedly fled after a bloody rampage.

Details

In the village of Edgefield, local legend tells of a violent incident where a young man named Mr. Bodger used an axe to kill his father and a neighbour named Mr. Ford who tried to intervene. According to folklore recorded by local schoolchildren during the 1986 Domesday Project, Bodger fled through a specific gap in a hedge at Cross Green. Because of the immense amount of blood spilled during his escape, it is said that nothing has ever been able to grow at that spot since. In reality, the tale stems from a genuine double murder committed in June 1907 by labourer William Jacobs, who attacked his neighbour and killed his elderly father before fleeing through a hedge, eventually becoming garbled into local legend.

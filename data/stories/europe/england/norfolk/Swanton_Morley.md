#title
The Devil at Swanton Morley

#summary
According to local folklore, the Devil can be summoned at All Saints Church in Swanton Morley by running around the building at midnight and whistling through the crypt grille or keyhole.

#details
Recorded by folklorist Leslie Valentine Grinsell after speaking with villager Charles Lewton-Braine, the legend dictates that anyone wishing to see the Devil must run around All Saints Church at the stroke of midnight. Once the circumambulation is complete, the person must whistle through the keyhole of the door or through the grille peering into the crypt beneath the chancel, at which point Satan will appear.

#location
All Saints Church is located in the village of Swanton Morley in Norfolk, UK.

#title
The Friendly Hound of Swanton Morley

#summary
In November 1939, a young soldier cycling back to his mid-Norfolk airfield on a dark night was unexpectedly accompanied along a narrow lane by a silent, panting black dog. The spectral hound trotted beside him with an aura of friendliness before vanishing into the darkness when the moonlight faded.

#details
During the winter of 1939, a young Territorial soldier was cycling back late at night to his unit guarding a mid-Norfolk airfield after visiting his parents. Passing All Saints' Church in Swanton Morley, he took a wrong fork onto a narrow green lane. As the moonlight broke through, he realized a large black dog was running alongside him. The hound had its tongue lolling and its flanks heaving as it panted silently, yet it radiated an unexpected sense of deep friendliness. When sudden clouds blotted out the moon and he emerged onto the main tarmac road to Dereham, his canine companion had completely vanished.

#location
The encounter took place along a narrow green lane running east-west between the former B1147 and Woodgate Lane near Swanton Morley church (around grid reference TG015164).

#title
The Phantom Hound of Swanton Morley

#summary
In the winter of 1939, a young soldier cycling past All Saints Church in Swanton Morley was unexpectedly accompanied by a large, silent black dog along a country lane.

#details
During a dark night in late November 1939, a young territorial soldier was cycling back to his military unit after visiting his parents. Passing All Saints Church in Swanton Morley on his way towards Dereham, he became disoriented at a road fork and turned down a narrow green lane. When the moonlight broke through the clouds, he noticed a large black dog running right beside him. The hound's tongue was lolling out and its flanks heaved as it panted, yet it made absolute silence and emanated a surprising sense of companionship. As heavy clouds blocked the moonlight once more, the soldier emerged onto the tarmac road to Dereham, and his phantom companion had completely vanished.

#location
The encounter took place along a green lane branching off near All Saints Church in Swanton Morley on the route towards Dereham.

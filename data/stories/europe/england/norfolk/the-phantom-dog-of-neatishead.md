#title
The Phantom Dog of Neatishead

#summary
A local resident walking home after dark in Neatishead encountered a spectral black dog that proved to be entirely incorporeal when kicked.

#details
A man named Finch from Neatishead was walking along a village road after dark when he spotted a dog he believed belonged to Dick Allard. Having been snapped and snarled at by the animal on previous occasions, Finch decided not to give way and resolved to stand his ground. As the dog came straight down the middle of the road toward him, Finch lashed out and kicked at it. To his astonishment, his foot passed right through the creature as if it were a sheet of paper with such force that he nearly lost his balance and fell backward.

#location
A country road within the small village of Neatishead, Norfolk.

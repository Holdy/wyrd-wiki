#title
The Giant's Effigy and the Devil's Ball

#summary
According to local tradition, a weathered stone carving on the exterior wall of St Peter's Church depicts the legendary Norfolk giant Tom Hickathrift, who famously clashed with the Devil at the sacred site. 

#details
Embedded in the exterior corner between the north aisle and the chancel of St Peter's Church in Walpole St Peter is a weathered stone figure approximately 20 inches high. Although architectural historians classify the carving as a medieval caryatid, enduring local folklore maintains that it is an effigy of Tom Hickathrift, the celebrated Fenland giant. Legend relates that Hickathrift threw a stone from the Marshland Smeeth that struck the church wall, or alternatively, that the Devil hurled a ball at the giant that left a mark on the masonry. Local tradition further asserts that Hickathrift and the Devil tested their strength in the churchyard through a wrestling match or a game of football.

#location
St Peter's Church stands in the village of Walpole St Peter in Norfolk (grid reference TF502168).

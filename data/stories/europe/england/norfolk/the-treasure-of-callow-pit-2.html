    The Treasure of Callow Pit

    Summary

    Two men attempting to haul a golden chest from the depths of Callow Pit watched in horror as the heavy coffer slipped back into the dark water, forever protected by supernatural forces.

    Details

    Near Freethorpe, where the boundaries of Southwood and Moulton meet, lies Callow Pit—a deep, tree-shrouded pool at a lonely crossroads long notorious in local folklore. According to local tradition, an iron chest filled with gold rests engulfed at the bottom of the murky water. Determined to claim the fortune, two adventurous men resolved to recover the coffer themselves. They laid a ladder across the pool, climbed out to the middle, and probed the gloomy depths with long wooden poles until they struck a solid, heavy object bearing a metal ring-like handle.

    Excitedly, they managed to hook the ring with their sticks and began heaving the massive treasure chest toward the surface. As the coffer neared the top, one of the men reached down to grasp the handle. At that exact moment, the ring snapped in his grip—or, as some accounts warn, a ghostly black hand emerged from the dark water to reclaim the stolen gold—and the heavy chest plummeted back down into the cavernous depths, disappearing forever. To this day, local legend maintains that the surrounding area is patrolled by a headless horseman who ensures the treasure remains undisturbed.

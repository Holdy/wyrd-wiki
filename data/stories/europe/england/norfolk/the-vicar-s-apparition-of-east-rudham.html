The Vicar's Apparition of East Rudham

Summary

On Boxing Day in 1908, the acting vicar and housekeeper of St. Mary's Church in East Rudham witnessed a spectral double of the parish vicar sitting outside the vicarage window, shortly before news arrived of his tragic death abroad.

Details

In December 1908, Reverend R. Brock was serving as the Acting Vicar of St. Mary's Church in East Rudham while the regular vicar, Reverend Dr. Astley, was travelling in Algeria. On the afternoon of Boxing Day (December 26), the vicarage housekeeper rushed to fetch Reverend Brock to the study window, pointing outside to an alcove by the garden wall. There, both of them clearly saw a full apparition of Dr. Astley seated at a desk reading, complete with his distinctive clerical collar and gold watch chain across his waistcoat. Knowing the vicar was thousands of miles away in North Africa, they watched in astonishment as the manifestation lingered before eventually fading away. Sometime later, word reached the village that Dr. Astley and his wife had died in a railway accident in Algeria around the exact date and time of the ghostly visitation.

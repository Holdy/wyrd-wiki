#title
The Weeping White Ghost Horse

#summary
Following a tragic drowning in the freezing waters of the River Yare, a phantom white horse with tear-filled eyes has been reported roaming the village of Trowse. The mournful apparition is linked to a mother who slipped beneath the ice while her young son watched helplessly from the riverbank.

#details
During a bitter winter, a young local woman tragically fell through the fragile ice on the River Yare and drowned in the freezing water while her young son watched in horror from the bank. In the wake of this devastating accident, villagers began reporting eerie encounters with a spectral white horse traversing the village lanes. Known as a ghost horse that sheds tears, the phantom animal is said to endlessly mourn the tragedy that occurred on the river.

#location
The village of Trowse and the adjacent banks of the River Yare in Norfolk, England.

#title
The Weeping White Horse of Trowse

#summary
A phantom white horse that sheds bitter tears haunts the village of Trowse, representing the spirit of a mother who drowned in the icy river while her young son watched. The gentle apparition is especially drawn to fair-haired boys, seeking the child she lost.

#details
According to local folklore, a tragedy unfolded one winter when a woman slipped beneath the freezing surface of the River Yare and drowned as her young son watched helplessly from the bank. Since her death, villagers have reported sightings of a spectral white horse wandering the lanes around the village of Trowse. This benevolent spirit is said to be particularly fond of fair-haired boys, gently nudging their faces with its nose in hopes of being stroked, all the while shedding bitter tears as it continues its endless search for her lost son.

#location
The village of Trowse and the adjacent banks of the River Yare in Norfolk, located just south-east of Norwich.

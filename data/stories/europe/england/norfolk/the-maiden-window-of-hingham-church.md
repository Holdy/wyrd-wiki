#title
The Maiden Window of Hingham Church

#summary
Local tradition maintains that the Trinity chapel window within the parish church was funded and glazed entirely through the contributions of the unmarried young women of Hingham.

#details
According to local historical record and tradition, the Trinity chapel within the grand church of St. Andrew in Hingham was established and adorned through the collective efforts of the town's maidens. The medieval window in the chapel was glazed at their sole expense, accompanied by an ancient inscription reading "Thys Windowe ys ye Mayden cost of Nengham" (This window is the maidens' cost of Hingham), commemorating their devotion and financial sacrifice to the parish church.

#location
The Trinity chapel window is located within St. Andrew's Church in the market town of Hingham, Norfolk.

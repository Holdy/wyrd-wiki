The Barrows of Soldier's Hill

Summary

Garboldisham Heath features ancient round barrows, including Soldier's Hill, which local folklore identifies as the burial place of the warrior queen Boudicca and soldiers fallen in ancient battles. Nearby lies the earthwork known as Devil's Ditch.

Details

Garboldisham Heath is covered with prehistoric earthworks dating back to the Bronze Age, alongside Roman and Saxon remains. Among these are several round barrows traditionally said to be the graves of soldiers "killed in the wars." The most prominent of these, an isolated mound known as Soldier's Hill, has long been associated in local legend with the final resting place of the Iceni queen Boudicca. Just nearby is the two-mile-long Dark Age earthwork of Devil's Ditch, where folklore recounts that combatants once took shelter and fought during ancient conflicts.

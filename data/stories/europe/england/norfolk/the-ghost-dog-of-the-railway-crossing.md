#title
The Ghost Dog of the Railway Crossing

#summary
A phantom hound killed by a passing steam train is said to reappear at midnight on the anniversary of its death near the old railway crossing. Local residents were long warned to fear crossing the tracks after dark because of the lingering spirit.

#details
Passed down through family memory, local lore tells of a ghost dog that haunts the old Midland and Great Northern railway crossing in Hempton. The canine met its end after being struck by a steam train while returning to "The Grove," an old house near the railway station. On the anniversary of its death, the spectral hound returns at midnight, leaving a lasting dread in anyone traveling across the line at night.

#location
The haunting takes place at the old Midland and Great Northern railway crossing (grid reference TF917292) located next to the railway station in Hempton, Norfolk.

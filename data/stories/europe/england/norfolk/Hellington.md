#title
The Glowing Warriors

#summary
Ghostly lights flicker across Hellington Common at dusk, believed by locals to be the restless spirits of ancient warriors from historic battles.

#details
For generations, residents of Hellington have reported mysterious, flame-like lights darting across Hellington Common at dusk. These spectral glimmers are traditionally linked to bloody ancient battles fought between Vikings and Saxons, whose blood soaked the marshy ground, or even as the eerie presence of East Anglia's martyr-king, St. Edmund. While modern science attributes the phenomenon to marsh gases, local folklore portrays them as aggressive marsh sprites or the eternal vigil of fallen combatants roaming the valley.

#location
Hellington Common, situated in the small hamlet of Hellington approximately six miles south-east of Norwich in South Norfolk.

#title
The Glowing Warriors of Hellington

#summary
Ghostly lights dance across Hellington Common at dusk, believed by locals to be Will o' the Wisps or the lingering spirits of ancient warriors from a bloody battle between Vikings and Saxons.

#details
For generations, residents of the quiet Norfolk hamlet of Hellington have shared tales of eerie, glowing lights flickering over Hellington Common at dusk. These mysterious Will o’ the Wisps are said to be tied to a violent, centuries-old clash between invading Vikings and Saxons, whose blood soaked into the marshy ground. Some local lore even suggests that Hellington was the true site of St. Edmund's final stand against the Danes. While modern science explains the glowing phenomenon as marsh gas resulting from the decay of organic matter, traditional folklore warns that these are dangerous Lantern Men or the restless spirits of fallen warriors keeping eternal watch over the valley.

#location
Hellington Common in the parish of Hellington, South Norfolk, situated approximately six miles south-east of Norwich.

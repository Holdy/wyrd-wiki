#title
The Haunting of Winston Hall

#summary
Winston Hall is renowned for its ghostly auditory phenomena, where witnesses report hearing a rustling silk dress, heavy nighttime footsteps, and a disembodied voice.

#details
Dating back to the reign of Queen Elizabeth I and historically linked to local traditions involving Richard Cromwell, the ancient farmhouse known as Winston Hall is the site of persistent and eerie sounds. Although the spirits are never visually sighted, residents and visitors frequently hear the distinct, stiff rustling of a silk dress moving along a corridor at the back of the house. Additionally, heavy footsteps pace about during the night, and a phantom voice calls out "are you there" from an old exterior door, leaving no one behind when investigated.

#location
Winston Hall is an old historic farmhouse situated in the village of Gillingham, Norfolk.

#title
The Phantom Hound of All Saints

#summary
In 1913, a late-night cyclist narrowly avoided crashing into a spectral dog near a ruined church tower in Gillingham before the creature vanished into the graveyard.

#details
One night in 1913, a cyclist was pedalling quickly along a road in Gillingham when a ghostly dog suddenly materialized directly in front of him near the ruined tower of All Saints Church (grid reference TM411923). Expecting to be thrown from his bicycle by the impact, the cyclist braced for a collision. At the last possible second, however, the spectral hound darted away and fled into the adjacent, still-used graveyard.

#location
The encounter occurred near the ruined tower of All Saints Church (grid reference TM411923) in the parish of Gillingham, Norfolk.

#title
The Whispers of Winston Hall

#summary
Winston Hall in Gillingham is reputed to be haunted by unseen spirits whose rustling silk garments, heavy footsteps, and disembodied calls echo through the ancient farmhouse at night.

#details
Dating back to the reign of Queen Elizabeth I, the historic farmhouse of Winston Hall has long been associated with local legend and tradition, including the tale that Richard Cromwell once resided there in retirement. Although the spirits residing within its walls are never seen, they are frequently heard by those inside. Occupants and visitors have reported hearing the distinct rustling sound of a stiff silk dress sweeping along the corridor at the back of the house. At night, heavy footsteps pace the rooms, and a phantom voice calls out "are you there?" from a little-used exterior door, though opening it reveals no one standing outside.

#location
Winston Hall is a historic farmhouse situated near Winston Rectory in the village of Gillingham, Norfolk.

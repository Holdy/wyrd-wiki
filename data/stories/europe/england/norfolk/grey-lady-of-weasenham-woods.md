#title
Grey Lady of Weasenham Woods

#summary
While staying at her late great aunt's cottage in Weasenham, a recovering librarian experiences a terrifying encounter with the grey lady of Weasenham Woods during her autumn evening walks.

#details
While visiting her late Great Aunt Iris's cottage in Weasenham to sort her affairs and recover from a prolonged illness, the narrator takes evening walks through the chilly, mist-shrouded lanes near Weasenham Woods. During these walks, the narrator recalls local folklore about the grey lady searching for her lost fiancé and glimpses a spectral woman carrying a lantern among the trees. The encounter leaves the narrator shaken, and a passing motorist later stops, breathlessly asking if she has seen a phantom woman with a lantern near the woods.

#location
Weasenham Woods, situated within the parish of Weasenham in Norfolk, England, serve as the haunting grounds for the grey lady.

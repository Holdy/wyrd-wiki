#title
The Coastguard's Phantom Hound

#summary
In April 1972, a coastguard on duty early in the morning witnessed a large black hound suddenly appear and vanish on the beach north of the Gorleston rescue headquarters.

#details
On April 19th, 1972, at 4:45 AM, coastguard officer Graham Grant was on night duty at the Gorleston rescue headquarters. He spotted a large black hound-type dog running and stopping on the beach about a quarter of a mile north of the lookout, as if searching for someone. After watching it for a couple of minutes, the creature vanished entirely into thin air right before his eyes on a stretch of beach that had been recently flattened by bulldozers, leaving nowhere for an animal to hide. Unfamiliar with local folklore at the time, Grant later learned from a colleague that he had encountered the legendary Black Shuck.

#location
The beach approximately a quarter of a mile north of the former Gorleston coastguard rescue headquarters in Gorleston-on-Sea.

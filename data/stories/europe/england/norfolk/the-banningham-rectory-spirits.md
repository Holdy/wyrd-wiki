#title
The Banningham Rectory Spirits

#summary
Banningham Rectory was once home to an antiquarian who housed both a spectral George III armchair that summoned a flame-haired ghost when placed near a fireplace and a haunted portrait of a woman who tragically died at Yaxley Hall.

#details
For decades, antiquarian Bryan Hall lived at Banningham Rectory alongside two remarkable and uncanny items. The first was a George III armchair acquired decades earlier by a Nottinghamshire rector's wife after she witnessed a ghostly flame-haired woman sitting in it. True to legend, whenever the chair was placed close to a fireplace, the spectral woman would appear, and those standing near it reported feeling an unexplainable chill even on hot days. The second item was an oil portrait of Henrietta Nelson, a former resident of Yaxley Hall who tragically fell down the stairs in 1816 and whose spirit reportedly haunted her portrait and the grounds of wherever the painting resided—including the gardens of Banningham Rectory. Both items remained in Mr Hall's care until a massive auction held on the property prior to his passing in 2004.

#location
Banningham Rectory in Banningham, Norfolk.

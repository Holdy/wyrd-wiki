#title
The Duelling Knights of Blofield

#summary
The rural path between Blofield church and St Michael's at Braydeston is haunted by Sir Thomas Boleyn and Sir Thomas Paston, who reenact a fiery duel with burning swords each year while Anne Boleyn looks on.

#details
According to local Norfolk legend, the ghosts of Sir Thomas Boleyn—father of Anne Boleyn—and Sir Thomas Paston engage in a spectral battle once a year along the rural pathway leading from Blofield church to St Michael's Church at Braydeston. The two Tudor knights clash with fiery, burning swords in reenactment of their long-standing historical feud. Adding to the eerie spectacle, the ghost of Lady Anne Boleyn is said to watch the duel from a ghostly carriage drawn by headless horses.

#location
The rural path leading from Blofield church (St Andrew and St Peter) in Blofield to St Michael's Church at Braydeston in Norfolk.

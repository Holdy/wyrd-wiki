#title
The Phantom White Dog of Little Snoring Road

#summary
In the years leading up to the Second World War, a spectral white dog was frequently seen racing across the road connecting Great and Little Snoring, famously passing straight through an unsuspecting motorist's vehicle.

#details
Before the outbreak of World War II, travelers along the country road linking Great Snoring and Little Snoring in Norfolk reported encountering a ghostly white hound. The preternatural beast would suddenly appear, darting furiously across the tarmac. On one notable occasion, an eyewitness driving his car along the route watched in disbelief as the spectral animal crossed his path, and his vehicle drove directly through the phantom dog as if it were made of mist before it vanished into the night.

#location
The road linking the Norfolk villages of Great and Little Snoring.

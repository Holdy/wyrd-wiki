#title
The Walking Statues of Horning Church

#summary
The stone figures of the Four Evangelists atop the church tower mysteriously descend one by one in successive years before returning to their perches.

#details
Although the tower of St. Benedict's Church in Horning dates back to the 14th century, the stone effigies representing the Four Evangelists were only added in the 1880s. Within a few decades of their installation, a curious local legend emerged claiming that these statues come down from the roof one by one each year and subsequently return.

#location
St. Benedict's Church is located in the Norfolk Broads village of Horning (grid reference TG355166).

#title
The Ghost of Boyland Hall

#summary
The phantom of Oliver Cromwell was long rumored to haunt the staircase of Boyland Hall near Morningthorpe, supposedly returning to the historic estate due to an alleged past romance with a former resident.

#details
Boyland Hall, a grand Elizabethan country house situated to the north of Morningthorpe in Norfolk, was steeped in local legend long before its eventual demolition in 1947. Among the most persistent local accounts was the tale of a ghostly figure observed pacing the hall's grand staircase. This apparition was traditionally identified as Oliver Cromwell, the Lord Protector. According to regional folklore, Cromwell's spirit haunted the property because he had once engaged in an extra-marital affair with a former occupier of the hall. Although historical records emphasize Cromwell's deep devotion to his wife, generations of local residents maintained the legend of his repentant ghost lingering within the lost rooms of Boyland.

#location
The site of Boyland Hall, located to the north of the village of Morningthorpe in Norfolk, England.

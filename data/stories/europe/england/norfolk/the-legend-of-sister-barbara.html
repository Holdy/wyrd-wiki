    The Legend of Sister Barbara

    Summary

    An ambitious nun named Sister Barbara conspired with highwaymen on Vinegar Hill to rob wealthy merchants before nursing them back to health to collect generous rewards, ultimately meeting a grim fate that left her spirit to haunt the area.

    Details

    In Marham, an ambitious nun known as Sister Barbara craved the riches and comforts of life. To fund her desires, she devised a cunning and wicked scheme along Vinegar Hill, a narrow, rutted road bordered by high hedges that served as a main route between Swaffham and King's Lynn. She hired rough men of ill repute to waylay and render unconscious any wealthy merchants travelling through. Once the victims were robbed, Sister Barbara and her fellow nuns would conveniently "discover" them, bringing them back to the abbey to nurse them tenderly back to health. Overcome with gratitude, the victims and their families showered the abbey with lavish gifts and money, making the institution prosperous.

    However, suspicious monks from Pentney Abbey across the fen kept watch and eventually caught Sister Barbara paying off the robbers. For her crimes, she was condemned to be bricked up alive behind a wall. Ever since, her glowing ghostly figure has been reported gliding along Vinegar Hill and passing mysteriously through walls near the old path on dark November nights.

The Mystery Mist and Spectral Figure of St Mary's Church

Summary

While exploring the abandoned ruins of St Mary's Church, a group of walkers encountered an eerie white mist, an overpowering stench of sulphur, and a ghostly figure walking on a long-vanished floor. Unexplained voices were also heard echoing through the overgrown site.

Details

In August 2005, a group of walkers exploring the Norfolk countryside happened upon the atmospheric, ivy-covered ruins of St Mary's Church in Fulmodeston, which had been abandoned since the 1880s. Intrigued by the forgotten structure, they picked their way through the surrounding brambles to explore the remains. Almost immediately, the atmosphere turned hostile as a strange white mist rolled in, accompanied by a heavy and overpowering smell of sulphur. As the startled group attempted to make a hasty retreat, disembodied voices echoed around them, and they caught sight of a spectral figure calmly walking across a stone floor that had crumbled away long ago.

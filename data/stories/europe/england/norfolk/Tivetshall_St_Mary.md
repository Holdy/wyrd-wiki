#title
The Haunting of the Tivetshall Murder House

#summary
New homeowners in Tivetshall St Mary experienced an oppressive atmosphere and inexplicable paranormal activity linked to a dark history of murder in the property. Paranormal investigators were called in to uncover and rid the home of these haunting presences.

#details
After moving into what appeared to be a normal countryside home in Tivetshall St Mary, Norfolk, a couple began feeling deeply unwelcome and unsettled in their own dwelling. Plagued by disturbing phenomena and a heavy, oppressive presence, they learned the property was historically connected to a violent murder. Seeking help, they enlisted a team of paranormal investigators—including Chris Fleming, Barri Ghai, and Sandy Lakdar—to investigate the disturbances, confront the restless energies, and attempt to restore peace to the house.

#location
A residential property located within the village of Tivetshall St Mary, Norfolk.

#title
The Phantom Dog of St Mary's

#summary
An old man and his spectral black dog haunt the rural paths around the ruined church, causing herds of cattle to part in startled silence as they pass.

#details
In the isolated landscape surrounding the ruined 14th-century St Mary's Church in Tivetshall St Mary, local folklore tells of ghostly apparitions. The most celebrated of these spectral encounters features an old man walking a phantom black dog, whose eerie passage across the fields causes cattle to part in startled terror.

#title
The Phantom Tower and Ruined Church of Tivetshall St Mary

#summary
According to local folklore, the medieval tower of St Mary's Church was felled by the sonic boom of a low-flying jet in 1947, leaving behind an atmospheric ruin that is said to be haunted by various local spirits.

#details
St Mary's Church in Tivetshall St Mary is a largely abandoned and roofless medieval church situated in an isolated location away from the main village. While the building had already fallen into decay by the early 20th century, local legend maintains that its dramatic final blow came in 1947 when a low-flying jet plane broke the sound barrier overhead, sending a fatal tremor through the 14th-century stone tower and causing it to collapse into the nave. Today, the ivy-clad ruins are enveloped in local folklore, with stories persisting that multiple spectres and spirits roam the lonely churchyard and remaining stone walls.

#location
The ruins of St Mary's Church are located off a narrow country lane in Tivetshall St Mary, South Norfolk, at grid reference TM 1662 8580.

The Halvergate Shuck Encounter

Summary

During a severe November storm in 2000, a motorist driving across the marshes near Acle witnessed a gigantic, wolf-like spectral hound with burning red eyes pacing his vehicle. Another similar midnight encounter on the Acle Straight was later recounted by student researchers interviewing local witnesses.

Details

In November 2000, motorist Nigel Thorpe was driving home from Great Yarmouth during a heavy storm around 5 pm. As he passed the Vauxhall Caravan site and headed onto the open road across the marshes towards Acle, he spotted an enormous dog—resembling a giant wolfhound with glowing red eyes—running alongside his car in the fields. Rather than feeling frightened, he felt elated recognizing it as Black Shuck. Thorpe later learned of another encounter from student researchers in Norwich, where a musician driving a gear van down the Acle Straight at 2 am encountered Shuck sitting in the road before the creature trotted alongside the vehicle.

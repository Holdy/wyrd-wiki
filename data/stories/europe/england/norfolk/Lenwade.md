#title
Owd Rugman

#summary
During the 1890s, a spectral devil dog known as Owd Rugman haunted the dark lanes and byways of Lenwade, Lyng, and Attlebridge. The terrifying hell-hound prowled through the shadows at night, striking fear into local villagers.

#details
In the final decade of the nineteenth century, residents of the rural Norfolk communities of Lenwade, Lyng, and Attlebridge lived in dread of a local spectral hound. Known as Owd Rugman, this fierce devil dog was said to pad silently through the darkness along the lanes and byways connecting the villages. On dark nights, the phantom creature would stalk the shadows, terrorizing anyone unfortunate enough to cross its path.

#location
The rural lanes and byways connecting Lenwade, Lyng, and Attlebridge in Norfolk, England.

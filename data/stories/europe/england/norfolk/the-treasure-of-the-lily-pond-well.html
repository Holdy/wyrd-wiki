The Treasure of the Lily Pond Well

Summary

Local folklore tells of an ancient well situated in a field south of Hoe Hall where a heavy treasure chest was discovered, with its iron handle purportedly repurposed as the door handle of the local parish church. Ploughing in the area has also historically uncovered buried flint foundations, echoing the site's long-standing reputation for antiquity.

Details

According to local tradition recorded in the parish of Hoe, a field south of Hoe Hall—near a feature known as the Lily Pond—harbours the site of an old well associated with a remarkable find. Legend relates that a treasure chest was once recovered from the well's depths, and in a motif echoed in other Norfolk folklore traditions, the iron handle of the chest was removed and attached to the door of the parish church. Beyond the legend, informants and archaeological records note that farmers ploughing the field have struck ancient flint building foundations, lending tangible weight to the oral history attached to the land.

#title
The Phantom Pedestrian of the Acle Straight

#summary
A motorist driving along the Acle Straight near the Halvergate turn-off watched in horror as a middle-aged man stepped directly into the path of their car, only for the vehicle to pass right through the phantom figure.

#details
Stretching nine miles across reclaimed marshland between Acle and Great Yarmouth, the Acle Straight has long carried a reputation as one of Norfolk's most haunted highways. While driving along the road toward Acle one evening, a motorist approached the curve near the Halvergate turn-off and the former Stracey Arms. Suddenly, a middle-aged man emerged from the right-hand side of the road and stepped directly into the path of the oncoming vehicle. With no time to brake or swerve, the driver braced for impact, but as the figure turned to look at them, the car passed completely through his body without resistance.

#location
The sighting took place on the Acle Straight (A47) near the Halvergate turn-off and the former Stracey Arms between Acle and Great Yarmouth, Norfolk.

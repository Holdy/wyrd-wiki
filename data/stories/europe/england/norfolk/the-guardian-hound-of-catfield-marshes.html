    The Guardian Hound of Catfield Marshes

    Summary

    While illegally gathering rushes on the marshes near Catfield Hall, a group of women fleeing a gamekeeper were guided safely home by a mysterious black dog.

    Details

    Several women were trespassing at night to gather rushes on the marshes near Catfield Hall when they heard the gamekeeper approaching. Suddenly, a large black dog appeared among them, darting back and forth and whimpering. Realizing that the hound wanted them to follow, they let it lead them across the most treacherous parts of the marsh, onto a footpath, and out onto the main road. When they finally looked around for their guide, the dog had vanished into thin air.

The Bexwell Shuck and the Rector's Hound

Summary

Around 1870, the Bexwell parish clerk encountered a terrifying black dog vanishing through a hedge with a loud rattling of chains and a smell of sulphur along the road to Downham Market. Although the local rector suggested the beast was simply his own escaped hound, he was left entirely puzzled by the supernatural stench.

Details

In approximately 1870, the parish clerk of Bexwell was travelling along the A1122 road between Downham Market and Bexwell when he had a frightful encounter with a spectral black dog. The phantom hound abruptly vanished through a hedge accompanied by a heavy rattling of chains and an overwhelming scent of sulphur. When the uncanny event came to light, the Rector of Bexwell claimed that the apparition was actually his own large black dog, which had recently broken its chain. Yet, the rector remained completely unable to explain where the sulfurous odor had come from.

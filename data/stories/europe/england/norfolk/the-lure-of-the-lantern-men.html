The Lure of the Lantern Men

Summary

After leaving a Norfolk pub on a dark night to retrieve a forgotten package, a wherryman was led astray across the marsh by mysterious ghostly lights to his untimely death. His grave can still be found in Thurlton.

Details

In August 1809, a wherryman named Joseph Bexfield moored his cargo boat at Thurlton Staithe on the River Yare and walked across the marshes to the White Horse Inn for a drink. Settling down with a hot rum, he suddenly remembered he had left a package behind on his boat that his wife had asked him to purchase in Norwich. Despite warnings from fellow boatmen that the terrifying "lantern men" were out in the dark, Joseph insisted on retracing his steps across the lonely marshland. The following day, his body was discovered floating downstream, having fallen victim to the deadly lights.

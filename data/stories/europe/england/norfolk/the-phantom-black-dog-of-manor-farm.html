The Phantom Black Dog of Manor Farm

Summary

A young farm boy encountered a monstrous, phantom black dog that vanished through a closed gate in a meadow at Manor Farm in Aslacton.

Details

During the early twentieth century, twelve-year-old C. Skillings was staying with his grandparents at Manor Farm in Aslacton. While approaching the top field with an empty wagon, his horse suddenly shied in terror. Skillings looked up to see an extremely large, rough-coated black dog with a massive head and foaming, open jaws sprinting across the meadow. To his utter shock, the spectral beast passed straight through a closed gate and vanished entirely. When he recounted the terrifying experience to his grandfather, he was warned that it was an omen of bad luck and impending family death—a prophecy that seemed to come true when his other grandparents died soon after, leaving him permanently spooked and unwilling to ever cross that particular field alone again.

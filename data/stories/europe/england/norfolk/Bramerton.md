#title
The Ghostly Lights of the Viking Landing

#summary
Local folklore recounts that Bramerton served as the historic landing site for Viking raiders in 866 AD, whose violent incursions across the valley are said to be echoed by phantom lights flickering at dusk.

#details
According to regional legend, Viking longships navigated the waterway and arrived at Bramerton in 866 AD, with their crews gathering at Woods End Common before pushing inland toward Norwich. Generations of residents have linked the marshy lowlands between Bramerton and Hellington with tales of ancient bloodshed and restless spirits. As evening falls, eerie 'Will o' the Wisp' lights are reported dancing across the common, traditionally believed to be the spectral vigil of fallen warriors illuminating the historic invasion route.

#location
Woods End Common and St Peter's Church in the parish of Bramerton, Norfolk.

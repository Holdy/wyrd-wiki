#title
The Veiled Lady in the Mirror

#summary
In the early 1900s, while resting in his room at Sandringham House, Prince Christopher of Greece and Denmark saw the reflection of a mysterious veiled woman in a vanity mirror who later matched a portrait at Houghton Hall.

#details
During a visit to his aunt, Queen Alexandra, at Sandringham House in the early 20th century, Prince Christopher of Greece and Denmark was resting in his room located in the modern wing of the estate. While his valet prepared his clothes, Prince Christopher glanced at the vanity mirror and saw the reflection of a young, beautiful woman with curly brown hair, a dimpled chin, and a small black veil obscuring the top half of her face, staring at him with a deeply painful expression. When he turned around, the room was empty, and his valet—who walked right past the mirror—had seen and heard nothing. A few days later, during a royal excursion to neighboring Houghton Hall, his relatives brought him before a gallery portrait. To his astonishment, it was the exact same woman in the exact same dress, holding the veil. Local tradition identified the apparition as Lady Dorothy Walpole, a restless historical spirit said to appear to those connected to royalty.

#location
Sandringham House is a royal country house situated on the 20,000-acre Sandringham Estate in West Norfolk, England.

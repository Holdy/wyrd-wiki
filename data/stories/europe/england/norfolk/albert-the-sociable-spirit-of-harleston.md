#title
Albert the Sociable Spirit of Harleston

#summary
A six-year-old girl living in a historic house in Harleston formed a close, playful friendship with a spectral old man named Albert in 1983. Despite phantom footsteps and doors opening on their own, the family's ghostly visitor remained entirely benevolent until a medium helped him move on.

#details
In June 1983, six-year-old Gemma Wiles and her family living in a centuries-old house close to the centre of Harleston experienced a series of gentle paranormal events. Gemma was frequently heard talking, giggling, and playing with an unseen companion she called Albert, an old man who entertained her with harmless pranks. Other family members also encountered unexplained phenomena, such as footsteps going down the stairs and the kitchen door opening by itself when no one was there. Although entirely unafraid, the family eventually called in a medium who communicated with Albert and guided him to his final resting place, after which Gemma felt bereft for years.

#location
The events took place in a centuries-old residential house situated close to the town centre of Harleston, Norfolk.

#title
The Stolen Tower of West Walton

#summary
The detached bell tower of St. Mary's Church is said to have been left behind after the Devil or a local giant attempted to steal the entire structure.

#details
St. Mary's Church features a striking 13th-century bell tower standing completely detached from the main nave on open arches. According to local legend, the Devil resolved to steal the church tower and enlisted a group of human helpers to carry it off. However, the labourers found the tower too cumbersome, failing to navigate the narrow gates or walls, and ultimately dropped it where it now stands. An alternative tradition claims that the legendary Fenland giant Tom Hickathrift lifted the tower as part of a wager, only to drop it because it proved far too heavy.

#location
St. Mary's Church is situated on Church End in West Walton, Norfolk (grid reference TF471133).

#title
The Night-Watchman of Spixworth Hall

#summary
Spixworth Hall was famously haunted by a series of spectral visitors, including a bearded night-watchman carrying a lantern who roamed the corridors and a shrieking phantom in the panelled bedroom. Another whimsical tale from the estate recalls a pet monkey that secretly spent its nights riding the stable horses until they were soaked in sweat.

#details
For centuries, Spixworth Hall in Norfolk—home to the Longe family from 1693 until the mid-20th century—accumulated a rich repository of ghostly lore and unusual anecdotes. Among the most famous apparitions reported by residents and visitors was the old night-watchman, a bearded figure carrying a lantern who would appear in a strict sequence, only to vanish instantly when spotted. Guests also experienced unsettling encounters in the hall's panelled bedroom, where a shrieking female wraith would manifest. In addition to these supernatural phenomena, family memoirs record lighter domestic legends, such as the time a pet monkey kept in the stable hay loft was caught climbing down a rope each night to ride the horses around the stables for hours, leaving them exhausted by morning.

#location
Spixworth Hall was formerly located in Spixworth Park, north of Norwich in Norfolk, England.

#upstream-source
Arthur Longe, *The Old Night-Watchman - The Ghost of Spixworth Hall: A Norfolk Ghost Story and other Anecdotes* (Ipswich: W. S. Cowell, 1950).

#title
The Spectres of Spixworth Hall

#summary
While staying overnight at the Elizabethan Spixworth Hall, young Arthur Longe was visited in strict succession by a glowing apparition of an old watchman, a phantom woman holding a candle, and a terrifying midnight shriek.

#details
As a child, Arthur Longe stayed at Spixworth Hall and went to sleep in a panelled bedroom. Woken during the night, he saw a bright light and an old watchman with a lantern, knee breeches, and a white beard standing in the doorway before disappearing. After locking the door and falling back asleep, he was woken a second time by a light shining on his face, revealing a woman standing at the foot of his bed holding a candle; she also vanished instantly. Finally, a third awakening brought a dreadful shriek echoing through the room. These apparitions formed part of a haunting family lore that included a phantom hearse driving up to the hall at midnight.

#location
The events took place at Spixworth Hall, a historic Elizabethan mansion that stood in the village of Spixworth, Norfolk.

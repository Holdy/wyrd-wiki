#title
The Suicide's Mound at Cross's Grave

#summary
At the crossroads where Harpley and West Rudham meet, a local man who took his own life was buried, leading passing road-menders to traditionally tend his mound.

#details
According to local tradition, the crossroads where the parishes of Harpley and West Rudham meet—known as Cross's Grave—derives its name from a local man named Cross who committed suicide and was buried directly at the intersection. For generations, road-menders working in the area were said to occasionally add soil to the small mound that formed over his grave to ensure that Cross would not be forgotten. Despite this grim folklore and its popular name, medieval documents reveal that the spot was historically referred to as 'Adys Grave'.

#location
The crossroads known as Cross's Grave where the parishes of Harpley and West Rudham meet at grid reference TF803253.

#title
The Spectral Hound of Felbrigg Woods

#summary
Black Shuck, the legendary devil dog with glowing red eyes, is said to roam the ancient woods and grounds surrounding Felbrigg House as a fatal omen. Encountering the spectral hound in this North Norfolk estate forewarns of death before the year is out.

#details
Local folklore surrounding the historic Felbrigg estate tells of the haunting presence of Black Shuck, the spectral devil dog of East Anglia. Roaming the deep, ancient beech and oak woods planted centuries ago to shelter the grand house from North Sea gales, the phantom hound is described as a terrifying apparition with glowing red eyes. According to tradition, catching sight of this monstrous hell-hound is not merely a startling experience, but an ominous prophecy guaranteeing that the witness will meet their death before the year comes to an end.

#location
Felbrigg Hall and its surrounding historic parkland and woods in North Norfolk.

#title
Itteringham Mill Ghost

#summary
An ancient miller's ghost haunts his former watermill on the River Bure after it is converted into a modern residence, confronting the renovator about the changes made to the machinery.

#details
At midnight, a visitor wandering through Itteringham Mill climbs up to the old grinding floor where he encounters the apparition of the mill's former miller—a short, stocky, bearded spirit covered in dust. The ghost looks around with an angry frown at the ruined and dismantled milling machinery, silently demanding to know what has been done to his workplace. The visitor unrepentantly explains his grand renovation plans, detailing how the old breastwheel and grinding floors are being transformed into a comfortable home featuring an oaken staircase, dining rooms, cosy ingle-nooks, bedrooms, and a modern bathroom. Upon hearing the explanation, the ghost's blue eyes brighten in understanding, but as the visitor attempts to shake his hand, the spectral miller abruptly vanishes into thin air.

#location
Itteringham Mill is located beside the River Bure within the Norfolk parish of Itteringham.

#title
The Miller's Ghost of Itteringham

#summary
While exploring the abandoned interior of a local watermill, a man encounters the apparition of the former miller standing upon the old grinding floor. He proceeds to explain to the silent spirit how the historic machinery is being removed to convert the building into a modern home.

#details
In 1938, seventy-one-year-old Ernest Edward Smith wandered alone by moonlight into Itteringham Mill on the River Bure. Climbing through the south door and up the ladder to the grinding floor, he found himself face-to-face with the ghost of an old, bearded miller standing where the mill post once stood. The phantom looked upon the wreckage and disarray of the broken gear with an accusatory frown, questioning why his workspace had been destroyed. Unfazed, the visitor addressed the spirit, explaining his grand plan to transform the old watermill into a comfortable residence with a dining hall, bedrooms, and heated conduits where the breastwheel once turned. After listening attentively with bright eyes, the ancient miller vanished as quickly as he had appeared, leaving the builder alone with the ceaseless murmur of the river.

#location
Itteringham Mill is located on the River Bure within the village of Itteringham in North Norfolk.

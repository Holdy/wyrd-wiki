#title
The Secret Tunnel of St. Andrew's

#summary
A local legend in the Norfolk village of Congham claims that a secret underground passage connects the tower of St. Andrew's Church to the nearby Old Rectory. Despite the two buildings standing only a short distance apart, villagers maintain that the flooded tunnel remains hidden beneath the earth.

#details
In the village of Congham, folklore tells of a secret tunnel running between St. Andrew's Church and the Old Rectory. Although the two buildings are separated by a mere forty meters—rendering a subterranean passage seemingly redundant—local tradition insists on its existence. According to recurring rumors, the tunnel originates specifically inside the church tower and is currently filled to the brim with water.

#location
St. Andrew's Church and the Old Rectory stand roughly forty meters apart in the village of Congham, Norfolk.

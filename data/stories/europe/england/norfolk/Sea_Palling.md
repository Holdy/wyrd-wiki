#title
The Devil's Mill and Sir Berney's Bargain

#summary
Sir Berney Brograve built a drainage mill on his Norfolk estate in 1771, sparking local legends that he had struck a pact with the Devil. According to folklore, the Devil attempted to destroy the mill on a stormy night, leaving the brick tower permanently tilted to the west.

#details
In the late 18th century, landowner Sir Berney Brograve constructed a drainage windpump on the low-lying Brograve level within the parish of Sea Palling to protect his property from relentless flooding and tidal erosion. Because of his ambitious attempts to tame the marshlands, superstitious locals whispered that he had made a dark pact and sold his soul to the Devil. According to the legend, on a dark and stormy night, Brograve sought refuge inside the mill while fleeing the wrath of Satan. The Devil pursued him and battered furiously against the heavy door with his hooves. By morning, Brograve found the door covered in deep hoof prints and discovered that the entire brick structure had been shoved off its foundations, leaving it with a permanent, ominous lean toward the west where the Devil struck. To this day, locals maintain that the ghost of Sir Berney wanders the lonely marshlands around the dilapidated mill on stormy nights.

#location
Brograve Mill stands on the Brograve level in the parish of Sea Palling, Norfolk (Ordnance Survey grid reference TG448235).

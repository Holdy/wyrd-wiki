#title
The Spirit Photographs of St Mary's Church

#summary
Dating back to the 14th century, St Mary's Church in Stalham became famous when unexplained spirit photographs were captured inside its ancient walls.

#details
Constructed around 1400 on the foundations of an earlier building, St Mary's Church in Stalham stands as a prominent historic landmark. The medieval church gained paranormal renown when mysterious spirit photographs were taken on the premises, capturing inexplicable manifestations within the sacred grounds.

#location
St Mary's Church is located in the town of Stalham in Norfolk, England.

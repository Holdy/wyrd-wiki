#title
The Phantom Coach of Bastwick

#summary
Following a bargain struck with a witch for a love potion, a newlywed bride is abducted from her wedding reception in Bastwick by the devil in a skeleton-driven coach. The fiery carriage ultimately plunges into the water at Potter Heigham bridge.

#details
In 1741, Lady Carew sought the help of a local witch to ensure her daughter, Lady Evelyn, could win the affections of the wealthy bachelor Sir Godfrey Haslitt of Bastwick. In exchange for the potent love potion, Lady Carew swore that whatever the witch later requested would be granted. The potion worked, and Sir Godfrey and Evelyn were married on May 31, 1741. However, when the wedding party returned to Bastwick for the evening celebration, a skeleton-driven coach thundered up to the hall doors at midnight. The devil leapt out, seized the bride, and whisked her away toward Potter Heigham. As the coach reached Potter Heigham bridge, it burst into flames and plunged into the river with Lady Evelyn still inside, fulfilling the witch's grim price. It is said that on every anniversary of May 31st, the sound of hoofbeats and screeching wheels can be heard before a fiery phantom coach appears over the bridge and plunges into the water.

#location
Potter Heigham Bridge is an ancient stone bridge crossing the River Thurne in Norfolk where the phantom coach famously plunges into the water.

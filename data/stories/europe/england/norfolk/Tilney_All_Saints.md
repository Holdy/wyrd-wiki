#title
The Haunting of Ivy Farm

#summary
Occupants of a historic farmhouse in Tilney All Saints have reported eerie phenomena and unsettling presences within its walls. Subsequent residents investigated the property's reputation for unexplainable activity.

#details
Ivy Farm in Tilney All Saints became the focus of paranormal inquiry when researchers examined accounts of strange occurrences reported by residents such as Judith Maxey. Although earlier occupants like the relatives of Jean David recalled a peaceful time at the farm prior to the Maxeys taking ownership, the property's inclusion in regional ghost lore highlights ongoing traditions and personal testimonies of anomalous events experienced at the historic farmhouse.

#location
Ivy Farm is situated in the rural village of Tilney All Saints, located in the borough of King's Lynn and West Norfolk, England.

#title
The Legend of Tom Hickathrift

#summary
An unusually strong and tall fenland boy named Tom Hickathrift used a broken beer cart axle to defeat the fierce Ogre of Smeeth, and he is traditionally said to be buried in the local churchyard.

#details
In West Norfolk folklore, a remarkably tall and strong youth named Tom Hickathrift was sent to work carting beer across the fens by his exasperated mother. When his delivery route took him through the treacherous Marshland Fen known as The Smeeth—which was terrorized by a fierce, man-eating ogre—Tom refused to take the long way around. Encountering the beast in the thick fog, Tom overturned his cart, snapped off the wooden wheel axle to use as a cudgel, and struck off the monster's head with a single mighty blow before returning home a hero. According to local legend, Tom's grave is marked by a long stone slab within the churchyard of All Saints' Church in Tilney All Saints.

#location
All Saints' Church is located in the village of Tilney All Saints in West Norfolk (grid reference TF 5685 1795).

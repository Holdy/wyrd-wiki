#title
The Churchyard Phantom of Buxton

#summary
A woman walking past the Buxton parish church encountered a spectral black dog moments before a tragic family loss occurred miles away.

#details
During the 1970s, a local woman living in Buxton, Norfolk, was walking past the village church when the clock struck four in the afternoon. Suddenly, a large black dog materialized by her side. Feeling no fear, she reached out to stroke the animal and greeted it, but the creature vanished into thin air almost instantly. Four days later, she received news that her brother had passed away in Liverpool at the exact minute the phantom hound had appeared beside her at the church.

#location
St Andrew's Church in Buxton, Norfolk.

#title
The Phantom Hound of Buxton Churchyard

#summary
A spectral black dog appeared to a local woman in the Buxton churchyard as a terrifying omen coinciding with her brother's sudden death.

#details
While visiting the churchyard in the Norfolk village of Buxton, a woman was confronted by a phantom black hound—the demonic apparition known across the region as Black Shuck. Although the spectral beast inflicted no physical harm upon her, the eerie encounter filled her with dread. It was subsequently discovered that the exact moment the hell-hound manifested to her in the churchyard coincided precisely with the sudden death of her brother.

#location
The churchyard of St Andrew's Church in the village of Buxton, Norfolk.

#title
The Spectral Dog of Buxton Church

#summary
A young woman walking near Buxton church encountered a phantom black dog that vanished instantly, moments before her brother died in Liverpool. Years later, the village schoolmaster recounted tales of Black Shuck sightings in Buxton.

#details
While walking home toward Lamas with a female friend after parting near Buxton church, a young woman watched the church clock strike 4:00 pm. Instantly, a large black dog appeared at her side. Feeling no fear, she reached down to pat the animal and said hello, but before she could finish her sentence, the dog vanished into thin air. Four days later, she received a letter informing her that her brother had passed away in Liverpool at the exact date and time of the sighting. In the 1920s, a local schoolmaster recounted to his pupils that Black Shuck had been seen in Buxton.

#location
The apparition appeared right beside Buxton church as the witness began her walk toward Lamas.

#title
The Witch Across the River of Buxton Lammas

#summary
A mill worker in Buxton Lammas sought urgent counsel after a local woman reputed to be a witch threatened him with a curse across the river.

#details
In the early 20th-century folkloric record of Norfolk, an account was documented regarding a worker employed at the historic mill in Buxton-with-Lammas. The man found himself in great distress after a local woman, widely suspected by villagers of practicing witchcraft and residing just across the river, threatened to place a dark spell upon him. Fearing the potency of her curses and the misfortune she might bring to his life and work, the panicked employee sought out advice and protection to ward off the threatened malediction. 

#location
Buxton Mill situated by the River Bure in the village of Buxton-with-Lammas, Norfolk.

#upstream-source
Mark R. Taylor, "Norfolk Folklore", published in *Folklore*, Vol. 40, No. 2 (1929).

#title
The Gentleman of Foulsham Hall

#summary
A milk collector arriving early at Foulsham Hall encountered a white-haired gentleman in a dressing gown walking the grounds. This sighting sparked enduring local legends of a ghostly resident or a secret recluse linked to a subterranean tunnel.

#details
According to local tradition recorded in Thomas Quarles' historical accounts, Foulsham Hall is haunted by a mysterious white-haired gentleman. A poor local woman who routinely collected milk from the hall each morning arrived unusually early one day and spotted the figure wandering the grounds in a dressing gown. Speculation long surrounded whether the apparition was supernatural or Major-General Philip Skippon living in hidden seclusion following the Restoration. Adding to the mystery, local lore speaks of a rumored underground passage connecting the hall to the village church a quarter of a mile away.

#location
Foulsham Hall is located in the historic village of Foulsham in North Norfolk, England, situated approximately a quarter of a mile from the local parish church.

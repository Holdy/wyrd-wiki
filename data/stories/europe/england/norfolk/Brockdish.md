#title
The Mistletoe Bride

#summary
During a Christmas wedding celebration at Brockdish Hall, a young bride playfully hides in an old oak chest during a game of hide-and-seek, accidentally trapping herself inside. Years later, her skeletal remains are discovered in her wedding dress, and the tragic tale became a famous English ghost legend.

#details
The legend tells of a festive winter wedding at Brockdish Hall where the young bride challenged her new husband and guests to a game of hide-and-seek. Looking for a clever and secluded hiding spot, she slipped away to a dusty attic and climbed inside an ancient oak chest. Tragically, the heavy lid closed and locked shut with a spring mechanism, sealing her fate in a living tomb. Despite extensive searches by her distraught groom and family over the following days, weeks, and years, she could not be found. Long after grief had aged the household, the old chest was finally opened, revealing her skeleton still wearing her bridal wreath and gown.

#location
Brockdish Hall in the village of Brockdish, Norfolk, England.

#title
The Mistletoe Bride of Brockdish Hall

#summary
During a midwinter wedding celebration at Brockdish Hall, a young bride playing hide-and-seek climbed into a heavy oak chest that accidentally locked shut, sealing her inside until her skeleton was discovered years later.

#details
On her midwinter wedding day, the newlywed bride at Brockdish Hall celebrated with family and friends. Seeking a brief moment alone with her groom after the wedding feast, she playfully challenged him to a game of hide-and-seek. Clutching a bough of mistletoe so she could invite a kiss when he found her, she slipped away and hid inside a heavy, antique oak chest in a back room. Unfortunately, the heavy lid slammed down and locked with a spring mechanism, trapping her inside in total darkness. Despite desperate searches by her new husband and the guests that night and throughout the following week, she vanished without a trace. Around a hundred years later, when estate workers cleared out the storage room, the trunk was opened to reveal her skeleton still wearing her wedding dress and clutching the withered bough of mistletoe.

#location
Brockdish Hall is located in the parish of Brockdish, near Diss, in Norfolk, England.

#upstream-source
Hugh Lupton, *Norfolk Folk Tales*, published by The History Press.

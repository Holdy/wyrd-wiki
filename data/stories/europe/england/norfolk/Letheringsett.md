#title
The Ghost of Lord Cozens-Hardy

#summary
Patrons visiting a Norfolk village pub were once so unnerved by the spectral presence of a former judge that they refused to walk home alone at night.

#details
Inspired by the local atmosphere of Letheringsett, Poet Laureate John Betjeman captured a local legend in verse concerning the haunting of the village by the spirit of Lord Cozens-Hardy. Regulars drinking at The King's Head found themselves terrified of encountering the ghostly peer along the darkened country lanes, leaving them too frightened to return home unescorted after dark.

#location
The King's Head is a public house situated in the village of Letheringsett in North Norfolk.

#title
The Phantom Hound of the Tithe Barn

#summary
While driving to visit family late at night, a couple encountered a massive, calf-like phantom hound crossing the road near the Tithe Barn.

#details
A man and his wife were driving by car to visit his mother's home in Holt when they had a startling encounter on the borders of Letheringsett and Holt. As they drove past the old Holt Rectory near the Tithe Barn, a mysterious spectral creature described as looking like a large dog or a small calf—recognized as the legendary Shuck—suddenly stepped out and crossed the road directly in front of their vehicle.

#location
The sighting occurred near the Tithe Barn on the border between Letheringsett and Holt, situated before the old Holt Rectory.

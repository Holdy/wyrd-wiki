#title
The Black Shuck of Cooke's Lane

#summary
A cyclist riding home late at night encountered a ferocious, snarling black hound that suddenly lunged from a roadside pond before vanishing directly into a solid wall.

#details
Returning home late at night after a darts match in the mid-1940s, a cyclist was travelling along the Thurton to Bergh Apton road, known as Cooke's Lane. As he approached the section near the pond opposite Washingford House around midnight, a large black dog resembling a heavy Labrador with shaggy fur and bared, snarling teeth sprang out from the roadside. Expecting to be knocked from his bicycle and bitten, the terrified cyclist watched in disbelief as the phantom hound lunged toward him at crossbar height before vanishing straight through a solid wall.

#location
The encounter took place along Cooke's Lane (the road between Thurton and Bergh Apton), specifically by the pond opposite Washingford House.

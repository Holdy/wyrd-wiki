#title
The Phantom Hound of the Salthouse Road

#summary
While bicycling along the road between Salthouse and Kelling on a moonlit night, a local man encountered the terrifying phantom dog known as Black Shuck as it passed straight through a closed gate.

#details
On a clear night lit by a full moon, a resident named Tom Stirling was pushing his bicycle along the road leading from Salthouse toward Kelling. Hearing the ominous, distinct sound of rattling chains growing closer behind him, he initially assumed a horse had strayed from the nearby marshes and stepped aside to let it pass. Instead, a massive, shaggy black dog—the legendary hellhound Black Shuck—trotted past him and vanished straight through a closed five-barred gate on the opposite side of the road. When Stirling walked over to investigate, he found the gate still firmly locked and closed. The following day, residents in Kelling confirmed to him that the apparition was none other than Old Shuck, a spectral beast frequently seen roaming the North Norfolk roads.

#location
The sighting took place along the stretch of road connecting Salthouse and Kelling in North Norfolk.

#upstream-source
James Wentworth Day: 'Black Shuck', in East Anglian Magazine, Vol. 21, No. 11 (Sept. 1962), p. 640; also cited in G. McEwan: 'Mystery Animals of Britain and Ireland' (Hale, 1986).

#title
The Phantom Hound of the Salthouse Road

#summary
While pushing his bicycle along the road between Salthouse and Kelling on a moonlit night, a man encountered a massive, chain-rattling black dog that vanished directly through a closed gate. When he inspected the gate afterward, he found it securely latched and untouched.

#details
On a bright moonlit night, a local man was pushing his bicycle along the road leading from Salthouse toward Kelling in North Norfolk. Hearing the distinct, chilling sound of rattling chains drawing closer from behind, he initially believed a horse had strayed from the nearby marshes and stepped aside to let it pass. Instead, a gigantic, shaggy black hound strode past him and walked straight through a closed five-barred gate on the opposite side of the road. When the bewildered cyclist went to investigate, he found the gate firmly closed and locked, with no way the beast could have passed through physically. The following day, residents in Kelling told him he had crossed paths with Old Shuck, a spectral hound that frequently roamed those coastal roads.

#location
The coastal road running between Salthouse and Kelling in North Norfolk, UK.

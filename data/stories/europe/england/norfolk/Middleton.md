#title
The Horseman's Mysterious Box

#summary
In the Norfolk village of Middleton, a farm horseman steadfastly refused to take his team out to work unless he carried a small box whose contents remained a strict secret from everyone, including his wife.

#details
Local folklore collected from West Norfolk recounts the curious behavior of a farm worker in Middleton. Whenever the horseman prepared to take his horses out for the day's labor in the fields, he insisted on carrying a small box with him at all times. Despite intense curiosity from his fellow villagers and even his own wife, he guarded the box fiercely and never revealed what was hidden inside, sparking local speculation about secret charms or talismans used to command and protect his working animals.

#location
The agricultural parish and village of Middleton, situated near King's Lynn in West Norfolk.

#upstream-source

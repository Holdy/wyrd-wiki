#title
The Phantom Hound of Coltishall Bridge

#summary
Multiple residents and travellers have reported encountering a massive, silent black dog with glowing eyes lurking around Coltishall Bridge after dark. Over the decades, terrified witnesses have watched the monstrous phantom hound materialize, cross their path, and vanish into thin air.

#details
For generations, local folklore has linked the terrifying phantom hound Black Shuck with Coltishall Bridge over the River Bure. Throughout the 20th century, several specific encounters were recorded at this location. In the 1930s, a middle-aged couple stopping to light a cigarette watched a noiseless dog as big as a calf pass within a foot of them. In the early 1950s, a young couple walking from Coltishall Island encountered a pony-sized black dog that looked at them before vanishing as it walked. Later in the early 1960s, two RAF officers driving back to RAF Coltishall were forced to brake sharply when a massive, labrador-like hound roughly 53 inches tall crossed the road toward Coltishall High Street before abruptly disappearing.

#location
Coltishall Bridge crosses the upper reaches of the River Bure in the village of Coltishall, Norfolk.

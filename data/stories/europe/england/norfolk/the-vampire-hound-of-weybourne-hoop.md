#title
The Vampire Hound of Weybourne Hoop

#summary
A gigantic black hound haunts the coastal salt-lands originating from Weybourne Hoop, leaping upon benighted wayfarers to tear their throats before vanishing in smoke.

#details
Local marshland folklore describes a colossal black beast, much larger than any normal dog, that stalks the lonely coastal salt-lands. Beginning its prowl from Weybourne Hoop, the spectral hound targets anyone unfortunate enough to be caught out in the district after dark. Leaping upon its unsuspecting quarry from behind, the creature viciously mauls their throats like a vampire before mysteriously dissolving into thin air in a wisp of smoke.

#location
Weybourne Hoop is a coastal salt-marsh and indentation located along the North Norfolk shoreline near the village of Weybourne.

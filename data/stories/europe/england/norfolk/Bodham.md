#title
The Round House

#summary
Builders attempting to construct a traditional building in Bodham found their work repeatedly torn down each night by Old Nick. In response, they redesigned the structure as a circular building, which completely confused the Devil and forced him to abandon his attacks.

#details
During the construction of a building in Bodham, the Devil—known locally as Old Nick—grew angry and tore down the rising walls night after night. Frustrated by the constant destruction of their daily labour, the builders decided to outwit their tormentor by altering the architectural design. Instead of standard straight walls and sharp corners, they constructed the building in a circular form. Thoroughly disoriented and confused by the absence of corners, Old Nick was completely thwarted by the unique shape, ultimately abandoning his campaign and leaving the area.

#location
The Round House is a distinctive circular cottage located in the village of Bodham in North Norfolk, positioned between Holt and Cromer.

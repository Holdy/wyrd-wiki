#title
The Chained Hound of Bexwell

#summary
Around 1870, the Bexwell parish clerk encountered a mysterious black dog that vanished through a hedge with the sound of rattling chains and a strong smell of sulphur. Although the local rector later claimed it was his own escaped pet, he could not explain the demonic odor.

#details
In approximately 1870, the parish clerk of Bexwell encountered a spectral black dog while walking along the A1122 road. The creature abruptly vanished through a hedge, accompanied by a loud rattling of chains and an overwhelming scent of sulphur. When the incident was discussed, the Rector of Bexwell suggested the apparition was actually his own large black dog that had broken its chain, though he remained completely baffled by the unexplained smell of sulphur.

#location
The encounter took place along the A1122 road between Downham Market and Bexwell in Norfolk.

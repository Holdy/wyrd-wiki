#title
The Secret Tunnel of Church Close

#summary
A persistent local legend in Pulham St Mary maintains that a hidden subterranean passage connects the historic Middle Rectory to the medieval Guild Chapel.

#details
In the Norfolk village of Pulham St Mary, local tradition and rumor have long spoken of a secret underground tunnel linking two of the parish's most notable historic buildings. The passage is said to run from the 16th-century Old Rectory—specifically the section known as the Middle Rectory located in Church Close—westward toward the Guild Chapel, which was built in 1401 and now operates as the Pennoyer Centre. Although subsequent property alterations and divisions of the old rectory have uncovered no physical evidence of such a passage, the tale remains a well-known local legend concerning hidden medieval and ecclesiastical pathways.

#location
The reputed subterranean passage connects the Middle Rectory in Church Close to the 15th-century Guild Chapel (Pennoyer Centre) in Pulham St Mary, Norfolk.

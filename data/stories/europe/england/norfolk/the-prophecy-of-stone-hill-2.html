    The Prophecy of Stone Hill

    Summary

    According to regional folklore and prophecies, Stone Hill in Rackheath is destined to be the site of a cataclysmic battle where a miller with three thumbs will hold the horses of three kings.

    Details

    Local legend and traditional prophecy long foretold that a great and bloody battle would take place on Stone Hill. In this apocalyptic clash, nearly every man in the county would perish, and the bloodshed would be so severe and clotted that ravens would carry away the remains. Central to the legend is the prophecy of a mysterious miller born with three thumbs who is fated to hold the horses of three kings during the progress of the battle.

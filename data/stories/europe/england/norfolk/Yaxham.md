#title
The Gibbet of Cutthroat Drift

#summary
A man named Clifton murdered a wealthy farmer for his money after meeting him at a Yaxham inn, leading to his execution and the public display of his body in chains near Cutthroat Drift. Decades later, his skull was unearthed during ploughing and passed down through local generations before ultimately reaching a museum.

#details
Cutthroat Drift is a straight green track in Yaxham that runs from Dereham Road to Mattishall Road, traditionally named after a young woman whose throat was cut there in the distant past. Near a right-angle bend in the track, a man named Clifton was once gibbeted in chains. According to local history and legend, Clifton encountered a boastful, wealthy farmer in the bar-parlour of an inn on Church Lane. Learning that the farmer was carrying a large amount of cash, Clifton lured him along a path toward the mill on Mattishall Road. He murdered the farmer in a field subsequently known as "Clifton's field" and stole his money. Following his trial and execution outside the county gaol in Norwich, Clifton's body was returned to Yaxham and hung in chains on a gibbet as a grim warning to others. Years later, his skull was unearthed during ploughing, passed among local residents, and eventually deposited in the Norwich Castle Museum.

#location
Cutthroat Drift is a green track located in Yaxham, Norfolk, running between Dereham Road and Mattishall Road.

The Journal (Saturday 15th October 1932) by Peter Poulter.

#title
The Legend of Clifton's Field

#summary
A greedy man murdered a wealthy farmer for his cash along a remote path in Yaxham, resulting in his execution and the public display of his body in chains.

#details
While drinking at the "Great A" inn in Church Lane, a man named Clifton overheard a farmer boasting about a large sum of cash he had recently earned. Feigning friendship, Clifton lured the farmer down a quiet path leading from the church toward the mill on Mattishall Road. Midway along this route, in a field that became known as "Clifton's" field, Clifton murdered the farmer and stole his money. He was subsequently captured and hanged at Norwich, after which his body was brought back to Yaxham to be hung in chains on a gibbet near the crime scene as a gruesome warning to others.

#location
Cutthroat Drift is a straight green track and bridleway connecting Dereham Road to Mattishall Road in Yaxham, Norfolk.

#title
The Clogging Ghosts of Greenhow Hill

#summary
The story revolves around the eerie phenomenon of ghostly clogs being heard on the roads around Stump Cross, near Greenhow Hill in North Yorkshire.

#details
Greenhow Hill is a place where the ordinary and the unexplained seem to coexist. It's here, on the roads around Stump Cross, that locals and travellers alike have reported hearing the unmistakable sound of an invisible pair of clogs making their way through the neighbourhood. The clogs are said to travel along the roads, their rhythmic thud echoing through the otherwise quiet landscape.

#location

#title
The A3 Ghost Crash

#summary
On December 11, 2002, multiple drivers reported witnessing a car crash on the A3 near Guildford. However, when police arrived, they found no evidence of a recent accident until they discovered an old wreckage and a body hidden in a ditch.

#details
On December 11, 2002, Surrey Police received numerous calls reporting a car had lost control and crashed on the A3 near Guildford. Upon arrival, police found no signs of a recent crash but discovered an old maroon Vauxhall Astra wreckage and a body hidden in a ditch further down the road. The body was identified as 21-year-old Christopher Brian Chandler, who had been wanted for robbery since July 16 that year. Some speculated that the reported crash was a ghostly replay of an incident five months earlier.

#location
The A3 near Burpham, Guildford

#title
The Cursed Borrowed Cauldron of Frensham

#summary
A borrower fails to return a cauldron borrowed from the local fairies on a Surrey hill, earning a curse that makes the animated utensil chase him relentlessly until he collapses in a village church.

#details
Local legends recount that fairies living within Stony Jump (one of the hills known as the Devil’s Jumps near Frensham) would lend utensils to humans who politely knocked and whispered their requests into a rock fissure. When a man borrowed a cauldron but failed to return it by the agreed time, the enraged fairies cursed him to be endlessly followed by the vessel as its tripod legs morphed into feet. Tormented by the ceaseless pursuit of the animated cauldron, the man's health broke down entirely until he sought sanctuary in St Mary's Church in Frensham, where he died and left the cauldron permanently behind.

#location
St Mary's Church is located off Church Lane in the village of Frensham, Surrey.

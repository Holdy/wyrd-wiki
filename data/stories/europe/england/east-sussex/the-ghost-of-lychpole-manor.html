The Ghost of Lychpole Manor

Summary

A highwayman's ghost haunts a stretch of road between Lancing and Steyning in East Sussex.

Details

The manor of Leechpool, nestled under Cissbury Ring, is said to have a ghost. The name "Leechpool" might refer to a pool filled with leeches or a gallows-tree. The ghost is believed to be a highwayman who was hanged by the side of an old coach road running between Lancing and Steyning. According to legend, when he walked to the gallows, he vowed never to sleep in his grave, which had been dug in the center of the road. His body repeatedly rose from the grave, causing coaches to rattle and bump over his bones. Eventually, his ghostly counterpart, mounted on a ghostly horse, haunted this Downland trackway.

An old farmer named Parkyn recounted that a coach driver was held up by what he thought was a real highwayman but passed through him when he whipped up his horses. Farm laborers also reported sinister tales of their wagons bumping over something unseen on the road at nightfall.

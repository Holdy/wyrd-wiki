#title
The Witch's Crossroads Hex

#summary
A farmer who attempted to escape a local witch's hex by moving away found his departure thwarted when his moving van mysteriously broke down directly over her crossroads grave. Even in death, the witch's lingering power ensured no target of her wrath could easily flee.

#details
In the village of Bartlow, tradition held that a succession of village witches passed down their magical paraphernalia from generation to generation. Before the First World War, the final incumbent witch was buried at the crossroads, and the raised mound of her grave remained visible in the grass for years. A local farmer, wishing to escape her malice and the hex she had placed upon him, packed up his belongings and arranged to move away from the village. However, as the moving van carrying his furniture reached the crossroads where she was buried, the vehicle inexplicably broke down, halting his escape and cementing the enduring power of her hex.

#location
The crossroads at the junction of Dean Road and Camps Road in Bartlow, Cambridgeshire (grid reference TL584453).

    Whittlesford barrow skull

    Summary

    When the prehistoric barrows at Whittlesford were destroyed for farmland in the 19th century, a farmhand took a human skull from the site. The next night, the rest of the skeleton visited him to demand its head back.

    Details

    In the nineteenth century the prehistoric barrows at Whittlesford were levelled to make way for farmland, and they are no longer there. One account says a farmhand carried off a human skull from the site. The following night he had a visitor: the rest of the skeleton, come to demand the return of its head.

    Balsham talking skulls

    Summary

    Talking skulls were once said to have been found in the churchyard at Balsham. What became of them is not known.

    Details

    A Balsham legend tells of skulls in the village graveyard that could talk. The story is short on detail: it does not say whose they were, and what happened to them afterwards is unknown.

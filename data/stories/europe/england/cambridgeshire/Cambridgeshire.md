#title
The Ghost of Conington Crossing

#summary
Conington Crossing in Cambridgeshire is believed to be haunted by the ghost of a colonel and his dog, who died in a train accident in the 1940s. The crossing has been the site of numerous accidents and paranormal activity.

#details
The tale of Conington Crossing near Peterborough is dubbed by natives as 'Cambridgeshire's crossing of death'. According to legend, the crossing is haunted by the ghost of a colonel and his dog who died after an accident in the 1940s. The Colonel was heading home with his friend Mr Percival, when tragedy struck. The colonel's friend got out of the car to open the gates but while crossing, the 4pm train to London struck his Chrysler and he and his dog died instantly.

Since then, people have reported lots of paranormal activity at the crossing, with most people attributing it to one of the aforementioned calamities. Voices have been heard - sometimes yelling or crying and some people have reported seeing a phantom car trying to use the crossing before disappearing into thin air. According to the site, there have also been reports of the gates opening and closing seemingly by themselves.

#location
The crossing is located on a very narrow road with just the Fens and a few farms around it near Peterborough.

#title
The Witch's Crossroads Hex

#summary
A farmer who attempted to escape a local witch's hex by moving away found his departure thwarted when his moving van mysteriously broke down directly over her crossroads grave. Even in death, the witch's lingering power ensured no target of her wrath could easily flee.

#details
In the village of Bartlow, tradition held that a succession of village witches passed down their magical paraphernalia from generation to generation. Before the First World War, the final incumbent witch was buried at the crossroads, and the raised mound of her grave remained visible in the grass for years. A local farmer, wishing to escape her malice and the hex she had placed upon him, packed up his belongings and arranged to move away from the village. However, as the moving van carrying his furniture reached the crossroads where she was buried, the vehicle inexplicably broke down, halting his escape and cementing the enduring power of her hex.

#location
The crossroads at the junction of Dean Road and Camps Road in Bartlow, Cambridgeshire (grid reference TL584453).

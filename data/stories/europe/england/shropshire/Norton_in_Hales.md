#title
The Devil's Ring and Finger

#summary
Following the mysterious murder of a young girl, two ancient megaliths materialized overnight and became associated with Satan's own jewelry.

#details
Local legend holds that after a young girl was murdered at a remote spot on the edge of the parish, two strange stones mysteriously appeared overnight. Attributed to dark and infernal forces, the tall, vertically grooved monolith became known as Satan's finger, while the nearby flat holed stone was called his ring.

#location
The Devil's Ring and Finger standing stones stand in a field near Forge Lane on the boundary of Norton-in-Hales, Shropshire.

#upstream-source
Report of the N.S. Field Club published in the Newcastle Guardian (22nd August 1908).

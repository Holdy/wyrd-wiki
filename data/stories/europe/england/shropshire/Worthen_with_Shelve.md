#title
The Devil's Apronful at the Stiperstones

#summary
While carrying a load of stones to fill a nearby valley, the Devil rested on a hill ridge, causing his apron strings to snap and scatter the boulders across the summit. On winter nights, he returns to this rocky throne to preside over his followers.

#details
Long ago, the Devil was striding across Britain carrying thousands of stones in his apron, intending to dump them into Hell's Gutter to fill the valley. Growing tired of the long journey, he sat down for a rest upon the highest ridge of the Stiperstones. When he stood back up, the immense weight of the stones snapped his apron strings, sending the boulders tumbling out across the landscape where they remain today. Local folklore also warns that on the longest night of the year, the Devil returns to sit upon this rocky throne—known as the Devil's Chair—to summon his followers and choose their earthly ruler.

#location
The Devil's Chair is a prominent rocky outcrop located at the summit of the Stiperstones ridge in Shropshire.

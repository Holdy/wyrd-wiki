#title
The Origin and Throne of the Devil's Chair

#summary
While carrying stones in his apron across Shropshire to fill Hell's Gutter, the Devil dropped his load when his apron strings snapped, forming the rocky outcrop known as the Devil's Chair where he still gathers with witches and evil spirits.

#details
As Old Scratch was traveling back through the hills of Shropshire after spending time in Ireland, he carried a large quantity of stones in his apron pocket with the intention of filling up a nearby valley called Hell's Gutter. Overcome by the long journey and the heavy strain, his apron strings suddenly snapped, scattering the rocks across the ridge. Infuriated, the Devil left the stones where they fell and adopted the highest rocky outcrop—now known as the Devil's Chair—as his personal throne and vantage point. According to local legend, he returns here on the longest night of the year to preside over a gathering of witches, warlocks, and evil spirits to judge their misdeeds and elect the King of Evil.

#location
The Devil's Chair is the highest rocky outcrop located on the Stiperstones ridge within the parish of Worthen with Shelve, Shropshire.

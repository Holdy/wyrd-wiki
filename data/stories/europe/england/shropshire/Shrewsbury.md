#title
Hell's Bells: The Devil Visits St Alkmund's

#summary
During a violent storm in Shrewsbury in January 1553, Satan scaled the spire of St Alkmund's Church and struck the church bell with such force that his claw marks were melted into the metal.

#details
On a severe stormy night in January 1553, torrential rain and deafening thunder struck the town of Shrewsbury. Under the cover of this tempest, the Devil—referred to locally as 'Owd Scratch'—ascended the spire of St Alkmund's Church, damaging part of the clock along the way. He reached the belfry and violently struck one of the church bells, leaving the scorched imprint of his claws burned into the metal. According to local folklore, the Devil continues to watch over Shrewsbury from the church spire, and any sudden rumbles of thunder signify his displeasure.

#location
St Alkmund's Church is located on St Alkmund's Square in the historic center of Shrewsbury, Shropshire, UK.

#title
The Devil's Chair

#summary
While carrying stones in his apron across Britain, the Devil paused to rest on a Shropshire ridge and accidentally spilled his load, creating a rocky throne where he gathers his followers once a year.

#details
According to local legend, the Devil was travelling across Britain from Ireland carrying a large load of stones in his apron, intending to use them to fill in a nearby valley known as Hell's Gutter. Growing tired, he stopped to rest on the highest ridge of the Stiperstones in Shropshire. When he stood up, his apron strings snapped, causing the heavy stones to tumble out and scatter across the landscape. Leaving them where they fell, the Devil abandoned his task, and it is said that on hot days the rocks still carry the smell of brimstone. The highest and most imposing outcrop became known as the Devil's Chair, where Satan is said to return on the longest night of the year to sit and summon local witches and evil spirits to choose their annual king.

#location
The Devil's Chair is the highest rocky outcrop on the Stiperstones ridge within the parish of Worthen with Shelve in Shropshire.

#title
The Devil's Causeway

#summary
Built overnight by the Devil, the ancient causeway between Acton Burnell and Cardington is haunted at midnight by 'Owd Scratch', who judges and punishes any wretched souls unfortunate enough to cross his path.

#details
According to local folklore, the Devil created a 200 to 300-yard stretch of rude pavement known as the Devil's Causeway in a single night to aid his travels. At midnight, 'Owd Scratch' still wanders this isolated road, taking the form of an unnaturally tall man riding a white horse, sometimes bearing horns and hooves. He stops travellers to judge their souls; while virtuous individuals are allowed to pass unheeded, wretched souls risk facing a brutal reckoning.

#location
The Devil's Causeway is an ancient stretch of road located between Acton Burnell and Cardington in south Shropshire.

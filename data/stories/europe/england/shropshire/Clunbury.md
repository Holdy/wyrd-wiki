#title
The Abandonment of Clunbury Church

#summary
The Devil waged a relentless campaign of terror against the village church in Clunbury, disrupting sermons and creating terrifying nocturnal noises until the congregation fled in fear, leaving the building to become Chapel Farm.

#details
Near the River Clun in Clunbury, the Devil set his sights on the local village church, subjecting the parishioners to a campaign of terror. He actively disrupted church services and spent his nights tormenting the village with loud, ungodly noises. Unable to bear the constant supernatural harassment and fear, the congregation ultimately abandoned the sacred building, which was subsequently converted into a farm known as Chapel Farm.

#location
Chapel Farm is situated near the River Clun in the village of Clunbury, Shropshire.

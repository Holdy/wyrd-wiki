#title
The Devil's Card Game at Jackfield

#summary
A well-dressed stranger visiting a local pub on a winter evening is exposed as the Devil when his cloven hooves are spotted under the gaming table.

#details
On a long winter night leading up to Christmas, a mysterious stranger dressed in dark, fine clothing entered The Boat Inn in The Tuckies, Jackfield. He charmed the barmaids and began mingling with the locals, coaxing them into card games with promises of wealth and gold. Eventually, a gruff local man stepped forward to challenge him. As they played intensely, the frustrated stranger dropped a card on the floor. When the local man glanced under the table, he was horrified to see a pair of cloven hooves. Realizing he was playing cards with Owd Scratch, a violent gust of wind suddenly shook the pub, blowing open the heavy doors and sweeping the laughing Devil out into the night.

#location
The Boat Inn located in The Tuckies area of Jackfield, within The Gorge civil parish, Shropshire.

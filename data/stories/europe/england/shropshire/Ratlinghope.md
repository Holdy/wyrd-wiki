#title
The Devil's Chair

#summary
While carrying stones in his apron to fill a nearby valley, the Devil rested on a rocky ridge and dropped his load when his apron strings snapped. Ever since, he has used the prominent stone throne as a meeting place to hold court with witches and evil spirits on the longest night of the year.

#details
According to Shropshire folklore, the Devil was once traveling across the land carrying a large apron full of stones with the intention of filling in the valley known as Hell's Gutter. Growing tired, he stopped to rest on the highest rocky outcrop of the Stiperstones ridge. When he rose to leave, his apron strings snapped, spilling the massive quartzite boulders across the hillside. The highest and most imposing rock formation became known as the Devil's Chair. Legend holds that on the longest night of the year, the Devil sits upon this stony throne to summon witches, demons, and evil spirits to choose their king for the year.

#location
The top of the Devil's Chair rock formation on the Stiperstones ridge in Ratlinghope, Shropshire.

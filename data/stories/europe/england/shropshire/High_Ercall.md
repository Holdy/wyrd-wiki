#title
The Conjuror and the Evil One of Walton

#summary
Local villagers visiting a wise man's cottage in Walton heard him wrestling with the Devil and rattling chains on winter nights. Thomas Light, a renowned 19th-century conjuror, was also said to battle the Evil One along the dark lanes and byways surrounding High Ercall.

#details
During the late 18th and early 19th centuries, Thomas Light operated as a famous fortune-teller, cunning man, and "wise man" from his half-timbered cottage in the village of Walton, near High Ercall. Clients traveling from afar to consult him for lost goods, weather charms, and spells would frequently hear him engaged in fierce combat behind closed doors. Amidst rattling chains and supernatural disturbances, it was whispered that the conjurer was physically wrestling with the Devil himself. Light was also reputed to encounter and battle the Evil One out in the frosty lanes and byways of High Ercall on cold winter nights.

#location
The half-timbered cottage of Thomas Light in the village of Walton, situated just outside High Ercall in Shropshire, served as the primary site where he was heard wrestling with the Devil.

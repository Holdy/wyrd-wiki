#title
The Devil at The Boat Inn

#summary
A mysterious stranger playing card games with locals in a riverside pub reveals cloven hooves beneath the table before vanishing in a supernatural gale.

#details
On a long winter night around 1896, a stranger dressed in fine, eccentric clothing entered The Boat Inn in Jackfield. After charming the barmaids, he challenged the local men to card games, using his skill and promises of wealth to win their hard-earned wages. Eventually, a gruff local man stepped forward to challenge him. As they played, the frustrated stranger scattered his cards across the floor. When the local man looked under the table to help retrieve them, he recoiled in horror upon seeing a pair of great black hooves. Realizing they were playing cards with the Devil, a violent gust of wind suddenly shook the building, smashing pint glasses and sweeping Owd Scratch out the door amid curses and laughter.

#location
The Boat Inn is located in Jackfield on the southern bank of the River Severn within the Ironbridge Gorge in Telford, Shropshire.

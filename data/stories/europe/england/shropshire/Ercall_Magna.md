#title
The Conjuror of Walton and the Evil One

#summary
In the village of Walton, a notorious 19th-century conjuror frequently terrified clients by loudly wrestling with unseen supernatural forces behind closed doors. The violent scuffles and rattling chains convinced local observers that the wise man was regularly battling the Devil himself.

#details
Thomas Light, widely known as "Tommy Light the Conjuror," ran a thriving practice from his half-timbered cottage in the village of Walton within the parish of Ercall Magna, acting as a fortune-teller, healer of livestock, and finder of stolen property. When clients arrived to seek his mystic advice, Light would retire to a private inner room to consult the stars and commune with his familiar spirits. Waiting customers frequently heard him engaged in fierce physical struggle with a mysterious presence, accompanied by the distinct rattling of heavy chains. These dramatic, spine-chilling sounds left visitors in awe and firmly persuaded locals that the conjurer was locking horns with the Evil One.

#location
The half-timbered cottage in the village of Walton, located within the parish of Ercall Magna in Shropshire.

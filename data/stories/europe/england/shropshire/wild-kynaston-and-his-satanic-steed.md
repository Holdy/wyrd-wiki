#title
Wild Kynaston and his Satanic Steed

#summary
The notorious outlaw Sir Humphrey Kynaston acquired a magnificent black horse named Beelzebub after making a bargain with the devil himself. Riding this supernatural steed, he evaded capture through legendary leaps across Shropshire while operating from his ancestral castle and cave hideouts.

#details
Sir Humphrey Kynaston, the impoverished lord of Myddle Castle, turned to a life of highway robbery after being outlawed in the late fifteenth century. According to local folklore, his remarkable ability to evade the sheriff's men and perform impossible feats was tied to his demonic mount, Beelzebub. Legend states that the devil incarnate—impressed by Kynaston's daring exploits—transformed himself into a swift black horse in exchange for a bargain regarding his soul. With his horse's shoes reversed to confuse pursuers, Kynaston leaped across rivers and cliffs with supernatural ease before ultimately securing a royal pardon.

#location
Myddle Castle stands in ruins just south of Castle Farm in the village of Myddle, Shropshire.

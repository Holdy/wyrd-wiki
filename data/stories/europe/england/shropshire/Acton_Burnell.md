#title
The Devil's Causeway

#summary
Built by the Devil in a single night, the ancient stone road between Acton Burnell and Cardington is haunted by Old Scratch, who appears at midnight on a white horse to judge and punish late-night travelers.

#details
According to Shropshire folklore, a stretch of rude ancient pavement between Acton Burnell and Cardington—once part of an old Roman road—was constructed by the Devil in a single night to aid his nocturnal travels. Known as the Devil's Causeway, this isolated path is said to be haunted by Old Scratch himself. At the stroke of midnight, the Devil appears on the road riding a white horse, towering above mortals with the horns and hooves of a beast. He acts as an arbiter of justice, stopping anyone unfortunate enough to be traveling late. Those who have led righteous lives are allowed to pass unharmed so they can warn others of the peril, but anyone deemed a wretched soul faces a brutal beating from the Devil.

#location
The Devil's Causeway is a historic stretch of ancient road running between Acton Burnell and Cardington in Shropshire.

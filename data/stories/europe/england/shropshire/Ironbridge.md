#title
The Card Game at the Boat Inn

#summary
A mysterious and well-dressed stranger visited a crowded winter pub and challenged patrons to high-stakes card games. His true identity was exposed when a player spotted his cloven hooves, prompting a violent gust of wind to sweep him away.

#details
On a cold winter night before Christmas, a mysterious, finely dressed stranger entered the Boat Inn and began mingling with the patrons. After tempting several locals into gambling away their wages with promises of wealth and desire, a gruff local man sat down to challenge him. As the stranger dropped a playing card and scrambled to retrieve it, the local man glanced under the table and spotted two great cloven hooves. Instantly recognizing his opponent as the Devil, a ferocious gust of wind shook the pub walls and swept the visitor out into the night.

#location
The Boat Inn is situated in The Tuckies area along the banks of the River Severn in Jackfield, Shropshire.

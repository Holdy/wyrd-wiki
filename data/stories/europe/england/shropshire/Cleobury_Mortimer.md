#title
The Devil's Leap and the Crooked Spire

#summary
The Devil, startled by the sudden ringing of church bells, leaped from the top of the parish church spire and warped its structure in his panic. This supernatural escape left behind the uniquely twisted steeple that still defines the town's skyline today.

#details
Long ago, the Devil chose to rest upon the lofty heights of the parish church tower, wrapping his tail around the stonework for support. While he brooded over the sacred building, the church bells suddenly rang out loud and clear. Terrified and infuriated by the booming sound of the bells, the Devil panicked and leaped off the spire in a frantic dash to escape. As he launched himself into the air, his powerful thrashing and awkward exit knocked the spire completely out of shape, leaving it permanently twisted and warped.

#location
St Mary the Virgin Church is situated in the heart of Cleobury Mortimer, a historic market town in south Shropshire, England.

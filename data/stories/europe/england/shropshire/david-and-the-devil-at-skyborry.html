    David and the Devil at Skyborry

    Summary

    A farmworker named David repeatedly outwits the Devil after a chance encounter, eventually defeating him before leaving instructions for a bizarre and cunning burial.

    Details

    David ap-Evan, a farmworker living at Skyborry in Llanfair Waterdine, desperately wanted to attend the annual Llanfair Wakes, but his master ordered him to stay home and scare crows. A mysterious stranger appeared and solved the problem by gathering all the crows into a barn so David could enjoy the fair, though his master soon recognized this as the work of the Devil. Later, the Devil returned to tempt David for his soul. When tasked with dividing a wheat crop, David had the Devil take the roots, and when planting potatoes, the Devil demanded the roots, allowing David to keep the rewarding parts instead. Finally, David challenged the Devil to carry water from Llanfair to Skyborry in an old sieve in exchange for his soul; the Devil failed completely and was defeated. Upon his death, David left instructions in his will to throw his heart onto a dunghill to see whether a raven or dove would claim it, and requested his corpse be taken out through the roof and buried with his head inside St. Mary's Church and his feet in the churchyard.

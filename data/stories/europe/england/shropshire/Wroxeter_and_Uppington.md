#title
The Fiend and the Burnt City of Wroxeter

#summary
Following a giant's death, a diabolical spirit took possession of the ruined ancient city of Wroxeter, guarding its hidden underground treasures until confronted by a courageous knight carrying the holy cross. Later, in the thirteenth century, an enchanter similarly compelled the fiend to reveal buried gold near the village.

#details
According to medieval border folklore, after the giant Geomagog perished, his body was inhabited by a devilish spirit that haunted the charred ruins of the ancient walled city of Uriconium (Wroxeter), leaving it uninhabited and cursed. When the bold knight Payn Peverel lodged within the ruined palaces, a terrifying tempest broke out, and the fiend appeared in the likeness of Geomagog, breathing fire and smoke from his mouth. Protected by the sign of the cross and armed with his sword, Payn attacked the demon until it surrendered, confessing that it guarded vast underground treasures including a golden bull. Centuries later in 1287, another encounter occurred near Wroxeter at Bilebury, where an enchanter forced the fiend to appear before a lad and reveal buried urns, a ship, and a house filled with immense gold.

#location
The ancient Roman city ruins of Uriconium are situated immediately north of the village of Wroxeter in Shropshire, England, near the River Severn.

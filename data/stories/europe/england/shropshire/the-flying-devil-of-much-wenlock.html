    The Flying Devil of Much Wenlock

    Summary

    After being acquitted of murdering his minister's daughter, a rejected quarryman invokes the devil's judgment upon himself and is subsequently killed by a machinery part known as the flying devil.

    Details

    In Much Wenlock, a local quarryman and heavy drinker falls in love with the 17-year-old daughter of a Methodist minister. After courting her and eventually proposing on Wenlock Edge, she angrily rejects him and slams her door. That same night, the young woman is brutally murdered in her bedroom. The quarryman is discovered sleeping on the low wall between the town prison and the church, and muddy boots matching footprints outside the crime scene lead to his arrest. During his trial in the Guildhall, the judge dismisses the case due to a lack of proper forensic evidence. Walking free, the man defiantly states, "If I did this evil deed, may the devil get me." Though he eventually moves away to Kidderminster to work in a carpet factory, he is struck dead three weeks later when a broken component from the factory's loom—known as the "flying devil"—shatters his temple.

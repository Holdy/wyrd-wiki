#title
The Black Bull of Wem

#summary
A group of bored young men in Wem conjure the Devil in the form of a ferocious black bull inside a local pub on New Street. Only the quick thinking and a special hundred-hour candle brought by Reverend Leonard Hotchkiss manage to banish the beast back to the underworld.

#details
Around the start of the 1700s, a group of unruly young men in Wem gathered at their local pub on New Street looking for excitement. Following instructions from a witchcraft book, they drew a chalk circle on the quarry-tiled floor, walked around it three times, and chanted magic words. A blinding flash filled the room as the Devil appeared in the shape of a massive, snorting black bull. When the local clergy tried to intervene with bell, book, and candle, the bull blew out their candles in the dark. Desperate, the youths traveled to find Reverend Leonard Hotchkiss, who arrived armed with an advanced "ever-burn" candle. Hotchkiss circled the beast while intoning counter-charms, defeating the bull's attempts to extinguish the flame and forcing the creature to vanish through a cracked floor.

#location
The main events take place inside a local public house situated on New Street in the town of Wem, Shropshire.

#title
The Midnight Roll Call of Chirbury Churchyard

#summary
Two drunken men who performed a forbidden Halloween ritual at a Shropshire churchyard heard a ghostly roll call of impending local deaths, culminating in the fatal fulfillment of the omen.

#details
On Halloween night in 1788, two intoxicated men sought to discover who was fated to die within the year so they could run up unpaid debts. Following local folklore, they walked counter-clockwise around the church twelve times and waited on the church steps until midnight. As the clock struck twelve, a haunting, rasping voice echoed through the mist, reading aloud a lengthy list of names of parishioners slated to die before the next Halloween—including the brother of one of the men. Although initially dismissed in disbelief by the family, the grim prophecy came true a week later when the brother died, prompting the terrified men never to speak of or repeat the ritual again.

#location
St Michael's Church, located in the village of Chirbury in Shropshire, England.

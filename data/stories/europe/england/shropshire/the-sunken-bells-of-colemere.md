#title
The Sunken Bells of Colemere

#summary
When an attempt was made to haul the old church bells out of Colemere using twenty oxen, a boast invoking the devil caused the chains to snap, sending the bells plunging back into the mere forever.

#details
According to local tradition, the old church at Colemere was pulled down, and its bells were cast into the mere. An attempt was later made to recover them from the water by fastening heavy chains to the bells and hitching twenty oxen to haul them ashore. Just as the bells were successfully brought to the water's edge, a man assisting in the effort boasted to a doubter, "In spite of God and the devil we have done it." At these words, the chains instantly snapped, and the bells rolled back into the water, never to be seen or heard of again.

#location
Colemere lake and the surrounding village near Ellesmere in Shropshire, UK.

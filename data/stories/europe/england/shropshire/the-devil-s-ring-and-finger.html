    The Devil's Ring and Finger

    Summary

    Two ancient standing stones near Market Drayton are said to have been dropped by the Devil to represent his ring and finger following a mysterious tragedy.

    Details

    According to local folklore, two prehistoric megaliths located near Market Drayton on the border of Norton-in-Hales and Mucklestone are directly attributed to Satan. One tall, vertically grooved monolith represents the finger of his Satanic Majesty, while an adjacent broad stone featuring a circular porthole hole represents his ring. 19th-century local tradition and Victorian field club reports recount that the stones arrived mysteriously overnight after a murder occurred at the spot, while antiquarian theories also linked the site to druidic altars and dark pagan rituals.

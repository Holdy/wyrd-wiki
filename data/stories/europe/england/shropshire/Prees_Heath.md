#title
The Rector's Shadow

#summary
After being dismissed by a local rector with a dark warning, a young boy is relentlessly trailed by a sinister shadow that leads to his untimely demise.

#details
In 1915, a group of women from Prees and Whitchurch recounted a chilling account to writer Edmund Vale regarding the rector of Ightfield. The rector had dismissed a garden boy for being cheeky, sending him away down the road. Later that day, the rector encountered the boy again as the youth was searching for work, and sniggered that he would never find another place. Immediately following this interaction, the boy fell strangely ill, shivering continuously and reporting to his doctor that a malevolent, shadowy entity had appeared on the road and refused to leave his side. Despite medical attention, the boy died three days later, remaining haunted by the shadow until his final breath.

#location
The road in the village of Ightfield, Shropshire, where the rector confronted the garden boy and the shadow appeared.

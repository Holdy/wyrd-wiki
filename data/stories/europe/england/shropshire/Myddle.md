#title
Wild Kynaston and the Devil's Steed

#summary
Outlaw knight Sir Humphrey Kynaston evaded the law by riding a magical black horse given to him by the devil himself. Shoeing his steed backwards to confound pursuers, he became a legendary figure known for his supernatural feats.

#details
After inheriting the crumbling ruins of Myddle Castle and running up severe debts, Sir Humphrey Kynaston became an outlawed highwayman. Local folklore relates that he made a bargain with Old Nick or a wizard to acquire a demonic steed named Beelzebub, whose hooves were reversed so that tracking footprints always pointed in the reverse direction. Backed by his Satanic horse, he performed miraculous leaps across rivers and cliffs, evading sheriffs and becoming a legendary folk hero.

#location
Myddle Castle ruins are located in the village of Myddle in Shropshire, England.

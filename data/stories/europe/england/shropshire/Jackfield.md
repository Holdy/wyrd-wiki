#title
The Devil's Card Game at Jackfield

#summary
Just before Christmas, the Devil joined locals for a card game at a village pub, revealing his true identity when a dropped card exposed his cloven hoof. Before anyone could react, a sudden and violent gust of wind swept him away.

#details
Many years ago, shortly before Christmas, a mysterious stranger visited the Boat Inn in Jackfield to join the local patrons in a festive game of cards. As the game progressed into the evening, a playing card accidentally slipped from the table and dropped to the floor. When another player bent down to retrieve it, they caught a horrifying glimpse of the stranger's cloven hoof—revealing him to be the Devil. The moment his true identity was uncovered, a fierce gust of wind burst through the doors and swept the Devil out into the night.

#location
The Boat Inn was a historic public house located in Jackfield, Shropshire, alongside the River Severn.

#title
The Judgment of the Devil's Causeway

#summary
Built overnight by the Devil, the ancient Devil's Causeway near Rushbury is haunted at midnight by 'Owd Scratch', who tests and judges the souls of night-time travelers.

#details
Local folklore holds that a historic stretch of Roman road and stone pavement leading from Rushbury, known as the Devil's Causeway, was constructed by the Devil in a single night to assist his dark travels. At midnight, 'Owd Scratch' still haunts the route in the guise of a towering figure on a white horse, complete with horns and hooves like a goat or cow. He intercepts anyone unfortunate enough to travel the isolated path at that hour, testing their character: righteous individuals are allowed to pass safely to warn others, while wicked souls are beaten mercilessly.

#location
The Devil's Causeway is a historic stretch of road running between Acton Burnell and Cardington that originally connected to Rushbury in Shropshire.

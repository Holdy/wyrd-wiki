#title
The Black Bull of Wem

#summary
A group of rowdy youths in Wem conjure the Devil in the form of a black bull inside a local pub on New Street. When local clergy fail to banish the beast, the courageous Reverend Leonard Hotchkiss successfully drives him away using an everlasting candle.

#details
Around the start of the 18th century, a group of restless young men drinking in a pub on New Street in Wem decided to pass the time by conjuring the Devil using a witchcraft book and a chalk circle. Their spell succeeded, filling the room with smoke and summoning a snarling, large black bull possessed by Beelzebub. Panic ensued as the beast trapped them within the circle and easily extinguished the candles of local vicars with his snorting breath. Desperate, the youths recruited the Reverend Leonard Hotchkiss. Armed with a special hundred-hour everlasting candle and holy scripture, Hotchkiss walked the circle three times, ultimately forcing the devilish bull to vanish in a blinding flash of smoke.

#location
A pub located on New Street in the town of Wem, Shropshire.

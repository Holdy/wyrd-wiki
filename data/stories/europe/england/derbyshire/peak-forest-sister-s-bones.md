#title
Peak Forest sister's bones

#summary
Two sisters at a Peak Forest farmhouse loved the same man, and one killed the other. The dead sister's bones are kept on the farm, and moving them is said to bring noises in the night and death to the cattle.

#details
At an unnamed farmhouse in Peak Forest, two sisters fell in love with the same man, and the rivalry ended with one killing the other. The murdered sister's bones were kept on the property afterwards. If they are moved, the story goes, the farm is filled with noises at night and its cattle begin to die.

#location
Farmhouse, Peak Forest, Derbyshire, England

#title
O'Lafferty's skull

#summary
A skull around a thousand years old was found in the basement of O'Lafferty's pub, formerly The George, in Derby. An entity blamed for switching off the beer barrels and causing general mischief may be linked to it.

#details
In the 2000s, O'Lafferty's on Sadlergate, formerly The George, was said to be home to a mischievous presence that liked to turn off the beer barrels and cause other trouble. The poltergeist has been linked to a skull around a thousand years old that was discovered in the building's basement.

#location
O'Lafferty's (The George), Derby, Derbyshire, England

#title
Flagg Hall skull

#summary
Flagg Hall is said to be home to another of Derbyshire's screaming skulls. If the skull is ever removed, the house is said to echo with cries and moans.

#details
Flagg Hall is described as the home of another Derbyshire screaming skull. Nothing is recorded of whose it was, but the warning is a familiar one: if the skull is taken away, the building echoes with cries and moans.

The Clever Defiance of Piers Shonks

Summary

After outsmarting the Devil's curse through a loophole regarding his final resting place, the legendary dragon-slayer continued to police his parish from beyond the grave, ensuring local thieves were terrified into honesty.

Details

Piers Shonks, the legendary Lord of Pelham, rid the local countryside of a terrifying dragon that had lived beneath the roots of an ancient yew tree. When he defeated the monster, the Devil swore vengeance and declared that upon his death he would claim Shonks's soul regardless of whether he was buried inside or outside a church. However, Shonks outsmarted the Devil by requesting that his tomb be built directly into the north wall of St Mary's Church—positioning himself neither inside nor outside the building. Even after his death, Shonks's spirit remained active to protect his lands; when a local man named Jack O' Pelham attempted to steal a faggot of wood, the ghost of Shonks intercepted him on his way home, frightening the thief so severely that he fainted and vowed never to commit another crime.

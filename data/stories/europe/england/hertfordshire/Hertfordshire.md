#title
The Clever Defiance of Piers Shonks

#summary
After outsmarting the Devil's curse through a loophole regarding his final resting place, the legendary dragon-slayer continued to police his parish from beyond the grave, ensuring local thieves were terrified into honesty.

#details
Piers Shonks, the legendary Lord of Pelham, rid the local countryside of a terrifying dragon that had lived beneath the roots of an ancient yew tree. When he defeated the monster, the Devil swore vengeance and declared that upon his death he would claim Shonks's soul regardless of whether he was buried inside or outside a church. However, Shonks outsmarted the Devil by requesting that his tomb be built directly into the north wall of St Mary's Church—positioning himself neither inside nor outside the building. Even after his death, Shonks's spirit remained active to protect his lands; when a local man named Jack O' Pelham attempted to steal a faggot of wood, the ghost of Shonks intercepted him on his way home, frightening the thief so severely that he fainted and vowed never to commit another crime.

#location
St Mary's Church is located in the small village of Brent Pelham in East Hertfordshire.

#upstream-source
Doris Jones-Baker, Tales of Old Hertfordshire (Countryside Books, 1987)

#title
The Monk of Spooky Lane

#summary
A silent robed monk and a Roman soldier are reportedly seen walking along Spooky Lane near Devil's Bridge, Hertfordshire, with locals attributing the sightings to ancient Roman and medieval-era hauntings.

#details
Local legends describe a robed monk silently walking toward a long-vanished monastery along Spooky Lane, while a Roman soldier is said to stand guard on the hillside near Devil's Bridge. The bridge, built in the 18th century, sits atop a steep incline along a sunken Roman road that once served as a carriage drive to Ashridge House. Villagers report strange red lights, mechanical failures in vehicles that later resolve, and horses acting frightened near the site. The area is steeped in tales of witchcraft, with chalked symbols like pentagrams and 666 appearing on nearby walls until recent years.

#location
Spooky Lane, a sunken Roman road near Devil's Bridge in Hertfordshire, situated on a steep hill with the bridge positioned above the valley.

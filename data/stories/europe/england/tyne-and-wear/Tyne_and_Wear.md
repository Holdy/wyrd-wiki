#title
The Curse of the Lambton Worm

#summary
A young heir who skipped church to fish hooked a monstrous serpent, and when he later slew the beast without following a wise woman's instructions, he cursed nine generations of his family. True to the prophecy, for nearly ten generations the Lambtons met unnatural or unpeaceful ends.

#details
While truant from church on a Sunday morning, young John Lambton went fishing in the River Wear and caught an ugly little worm-like creature, which he disgustedly tossed into a local well before leaving for the Crusades. While he was away, the creature grew into an enormous serpent that terrorised the countryside from its lair at Worm Hill, wrapping itself around the hill and swallowing livestock and children. Upon his return, Lambton consulted a wise woman who told him to wear spiked armour and fight the monster in the middle of the river, but warned him that he must immediately kill the first living thing he saw afterward—otherwise, a curse would fall upon nine generations of the Lambton family so that none would die peacefully in their beds. Lambton successfully slew the beast, but when he emerged from the river, the first living thing he saw was his own excited father. Unable to kill his father, Lambton brought the curse down upon his bloodline, which history recorded as coming true as subsequent generations met violent or unnatural deaths.

#location
Worm Hill is located near Fatfield, Washington, alongside the River Wear within the City of Sunderland, Tyne and Wear.

#title
The Ghost of the A696

#summary
Two radio presenters encounter a ghostly figure dressed in RAF uniform while driving on the A696 near Belsay, Northumberland.

#details
In August 2015, Rob Davies and Chris Felton, radio presenters from Gateshead, had an eerie experience on the A696 near Belsay. Around 11:30 PM, they spotted a figure dressed in RAF uniform standing by the side of the road. The figure appeared to be hitchhiking but vanished when they attempted to turn back to pick him up. The area is known for a World War II air force jet crash that occurred nearby.

#location
The A696 near Belsay, Northumberland

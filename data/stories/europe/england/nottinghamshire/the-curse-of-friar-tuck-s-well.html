    The Curse of Friar Tuck's Well

    Summary

    When the legendary Friar Tuck was driven from his woodland cell by a rival, he placed a lingering curse upon the local springs that causes them to run completely dry for seven-year intervals.

    Details

    Deep within the southern reaches of Sherwood Forest lies Fountain Dale, long associated with the dwelling place of the legendary outlaw and hermit Friar Tuck. According to local lore, when Tuck was ultimately ousted from his sacred sanctuary and hermitage cell by an interloper or rival, he left behind a bitter curse upon the surrounding waters. He decreed that the nearby springs and holy wells would be punished for his displacement, drying up completely and remaining empty for regular seven-year intervals. True to his parting malediction, generations of locals have observed periods when the water ceases to flow from the cascade altogether, leaving the basin dry until the curse lifts.

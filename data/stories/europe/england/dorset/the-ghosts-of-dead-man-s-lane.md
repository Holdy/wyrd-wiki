#title
The Ghosts of Dead Man's Lane

#summary
Dead Man's Lane is a track between Old Mapperton and Netherbury that has been haunted by the spirits of plague victims buried there during the Black Death.

#details
During the Black Death, the people of Mapperton were forced to bury their dead in Netherbury due to an unsuitable cemetery. However, when the residents of Mapperton also succumbed to the plague, the people of Netherbury refused to allow further burials for fear of a plague outbreak. A battle ensued, and eventually, over 80 victims were buried on Dead Man’s Lane, marked by a Posy/Cosy Tree.

#location
Dead Man's Lane is located between Old Mapperton and Netherbury in Dorset.

#title
The Screaming Skull of Bettiscombe Manor

#summary
A human skull housed within an ancient manor is bound by a terrible curse dictating that if it is ever removed, the building will shake to its foundations and the perpetrator will die within the year.

#details
Deep in Dorset folklore, Bettiscombe Manor houses a mysterious human skull that must never leave the premises. According to the legend recorded by folklorists, the skull is bound to the house, and any attempt to bury or remove it triggers violent supernatural disturbances, including the house rocking to its foundations, while anyone daring to move it will meet their death within the year. 

#location
Bettiscombe Manor is located in the small village of Bettiscombe in west Dorset, roughly five miles north-west of Bridport.

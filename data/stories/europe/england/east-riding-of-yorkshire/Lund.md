#title
Lund walled-up skull

#summary
A skull at a manor house in Lund was bricked up in the attic so that nobody could ever take it away. Little else is recorded about it, and it is unknown whether it is still there.

#details
A manor house in Lund once held a skull that was considered too dangerous to be moved. To make sure it never left, it was bricked up in the attic. Almost nothing is recorded of whose skull it was or what legend surrounded it, and it is not known whether it is still there.

#location

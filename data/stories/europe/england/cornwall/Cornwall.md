#title
Mother Ivey's Curse

#summary
A wise woman curses a wealthy family for their greed, leading to a series of misfortunes.

#details
The legend of Mother Ivey’s Curse is set against the backdrop of the cliffs between Harlyn Bay and Mother Ivey’s Bay on Cornwall’s north coast. Mother Ivey was known as a wise woman or white witch who lived near the sea and was respected for her powers. In the 19th century, the Hellyar family, wealthy landowners and fish merchants, operated pilchard cellars near Harlyn. After a large haul of pilchards, they refused to donate any to a starving local family, instead ploughing the surplus into a field. Outraged by their greed, Mother Ivey cursed the land and its owners, vowing that suffering would follow anyone who profited unjustly from the sea.

The curse was soon fulfilled: a fishing boat linked to the cellars was lost at sea, taking several lives. The merchant family’s own son was allegedly thrown from his horse and killed while riding. Over time, the local pilchard trade declined, the fish cellars were abandoned, and the family’s fortunes faded. Locals believe this stretch of coast remains under Mother Ivey’s curse, especially during stormy nights.

#location
Mother Ivey's Bay is a key location in the story and can be found on Cornwall’s north coast between Harlyn Bay and Mother Ivey’s Bay

#title
The Lady of the Lane

#summary
The Lady of the Lane is a ghostly apparition said to haunt the narrow roads between West Looe and Talland Bay in Cornwall. She appears as a woman dressed in white, with a sorrowful expression and no eyes.

#details
The story tells of a man walking down a dark lane near Talland Bay one summer evening. He hears sobbing and sees a woman in white nightclothes standing barefoot in the middle of the lane. As he approaches, he realizes she is floating above the ground. When she turns to face him, he sees that she has no eyes, just empty sockets. Terrified, the man flees, never looking back.

#location
The Lady of the Lane is said to haunt the narrow roads around Tencreek Caravan Park between West Looe and Talland Bay.

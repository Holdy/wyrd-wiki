#title
Looe Island skull

#summary
A skull was kept behind glass in a cupboard in a sitting room on Looe Island, off East and West Looe. No tradition about it could be found, though the island was once a great haunt of smugglers.

#details
Sabine Baring-Gould recorded that on Looe Island, off East and West Looe, a skull was still kept, or had been until a few years earlier, in a cupboard behind glass in the sitting room. He could find no tradition connected with it. The island had once been a great resort of smugglers, until a coastguard station was set up there.

#location
Island House, Looe Island, Cornwall, England

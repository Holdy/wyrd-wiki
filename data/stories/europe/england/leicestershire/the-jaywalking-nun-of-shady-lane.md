#title
The Jaywalking Nun of Shady Lane

#summary
A couple driving through Evington reported encountering a ghostly nun appearing directly in front of their car on Shady Lane, leaving skid marks but no collision.

#details
On a quiet summer evening, a newlywed couple traveling along the B582 route from Earl Shilton to Evington Village encountered a figure dressed as a nun standing on Shady Lane between the small bridge over the brook and the entrance to Shady Lane Arboretum. The couple braked sharply, expecting impact, but the figure disappeared into a hedge without any physical evidence. Witnesses later noted skid marks on the road suggesting other vehicles had also braked suddenly at the same location.

#location
Shady Lane, Evington, Leicestershire, specifically between the small bridge over the brook and the entrance to Shady Lane Arboretum.

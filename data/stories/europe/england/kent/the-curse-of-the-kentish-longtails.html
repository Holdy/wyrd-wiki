The Curse of the Kentish Longtails

Summary

Following an act of desecration against his horse by locals, Thomas Becket placed a generational curse upon the inhabitants of Strood. Ever since, all children born in the town were said to carry a physical appendage as punishment.

Details

While traveling through the town of Strood in Kent, the Archbishop Thomas Becket was subjected to a cruel insult when local inhabitants—or members of the hostile de Broc family—cut off the tail of his horse. Deeply angered by the disrespect shown to him and his mount, Becket pronounced a curse upon the settlement. From that day onward, every subsequent generation of children born in Strood would be cursed to come into the world with a tail, earning the locals the enduring nickname "Kentish Longtails".

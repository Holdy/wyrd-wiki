#title
Pennis Farm nun's skull

#summary
A nun's skull is kept at Pennis Farm near Fawkham Green, and removing it is said to bring a great curse on the whole valley. Her figure is reported floating down Pennis Lane at dusk in autumn.

#details
Pennis Farm keeps the skull of a nun, and local belief holds that taking it away would bring a great curse down on the valley. The nun herself is said to appear in autumn, a floating figure drifting along Pennis Lane as dusk falls.

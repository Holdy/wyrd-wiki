#title
The Chained Oak

#summary
An old beggar curses the Earl of Shrewsbury by declaring that a member of his family will die for every branch that falls from a specific oak tree. To prevent further deaths, the Earl orders the tree's branches to be bound in heavy iron chains.

#details
One night in 1821, the Earl of Shrewsbury was traveling back to Alton Towers in his carriage when an impoverished beggar woman (or old man, depending on the variant) stopped him to beg for alms. The Earl cruelly dismissed the request and told his driver to move on. In retaliation, the beggar placed a dreadful curse upon the noble family, proclaiming that for every branch that fell from a nearby oak tree, a member of the Earl's family would die. That very night, a severe storm struck, a branch broke from the tree, and a member of the Earl's family mysteriously passed away. Terrified that the falling wood would claim more lives, the Earl ordered his servants to bind the remaining limbs of the tree together with heavy iron chains. To this day, the tree stands wrapped in chains in the woods near Alton.

#location
The Chained Oak is located in the woodland of Dimmingsdale, near the village of Alton in Staffordshire.

#title
The Phantom Eyes of Short Street

#summary
Short Street in Longton, Stoke-on-Trent is known for its paranormal activity. People have reported feeling watched and experiencing strange phenomena such as the smell of cigarette smoke and voices.

#details
Short Street, a narrow cobbled road in Longton, Stoke-on-Trent, is infamous for its paranormal activities. The street features three listed workers' cottages and is located near Uttoxeter Road. Visitors have reported various strange occurrences, including the smell of cigarette smoke, unexplained voices, and even the apparition of a cat that vanishes when approached.

One man, Gary, shared his eerie experience on Short Street. He encountered a woman in old-fashioned attire sitting on a doorstep, who disappeared along with the open doorway when he looked back. The street's historical significance adds to its mystique, as it was once home to workers from nearby Enson Works. Despite the lack of direct paranormal experiences by some residents and visitors, Short Street remains a hotspot for ghost hunters and those intrigued by its haunted reputation.

#location
Short Street is located just off Uttoxeter Road in Longton, Stoke-on-Trent.

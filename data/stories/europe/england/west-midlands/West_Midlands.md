#title
The Melting Phone Box Woman of Station Road

## Summary
The story tells of two men waiting to use a phone box on Station Road in the 1970s, only to find that a woman who was using it had mysteriously vanished.

## Details
In the 1970s, two men were waiting to use a phone box on Station Road. They noticed a woman inside and asked her how long she would be. When they checked again, she had completely disappeared, leaving no trace behind. The story gained local folklore status, with some believing it was a young mother who had run to the phone box to call the fire brigade after her house caught fire, resulting in the deaths of her children. Overwhelmed by grief, she committed suicide not long after.

## Location
The key location is Station Road in Erdington, West Midlands.

#title
The Sinking Stone of Little Onn Hall

#summary
A sinister stone at the edge of Little Onn Hall acted as a delayed portent of doom for the Cavendish family, sinking into the soil to predict impending tragedy before a fateful voyage on the Titanic.

#details
Local lore surrounding Little Onn Hall in Staffordshire held that an ominous stone, allegedly displaced by a fleeing cow and a witch, acted as a fortune-telling barometer for the estate's occupants. According to tradition, as long as the stone remained above ground, the family would prosper, but if it sank into the soil, great trouble lay ahead. Just before Tyrell Cavendish and his wife boarded the RMS Titanic for America, the stone had nearly submerged entirely into the earth. True to the ominous warning, Tyrell perished when the ship went down, adding to a legacy of family misfortune linked to the unlucky property.

#location
Little Onn Hall is located near the village of Church Eaton in Staffordshire, West Midlands.

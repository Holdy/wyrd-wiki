#title
The Phantom Headless Soldier of Barkham Road

#summary
The ghost of a headless soldier is said to roam along Barkham Road in Berkshire, adding to the eerie atmosphere of the area.

#details
Barkham Road in Berkshire is known for its spectral encounters. One of the most chilling apparitions is that of a phantom headless soldier who wanders along this road. This ghostly figure is believed to be one of the injured ex-servicemen who purchased plots of land in the area from the Walter family after the Great War. The sightings are often accompanied by an overwhelming sense of terror, making it a haunting experience for those who encounter it.

#location
Barkham Road is located near Rectory Marsh and leads towards the parish church, providing a specific location to explore this ghostly tale on a map.

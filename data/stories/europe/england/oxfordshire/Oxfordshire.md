#title
The Ghost of West Hendred

#summary
Two sisters driving on the A417 near West Hendred encounter a ghostly figure who vanishes before impact.

#details
In late 1964, Margaret Prior and Marcia Colling-Hill were driving at night on the A417 near West Hendred when a figure suddenly darted out in front of their car. They slammed on the brakes but felt no impact. When they looked around, there was no sign of the figure. Both sisters described the apparition as a man wearing an overcoat and cap, leading them to believe it was supernatural. The incident occurred near the site where an elderly man had reportedly been killed three years prior, raising questions about whether they witnessed a replay of that tragic event.

#location
The A417 road near West Hendred, Oxfordshire

#title
The Sealed Doom of Lord Lovell

#summary
Following the defeat of the Yorkists, Lord Lovell retreated into a secret hiding place at his family hall where he was locked away by a faithful servant who subsequently died, leaving him to starve. Decades later, his skeleton was discovered seated at a table alongside his dog, both turning instantly to dust upon exposure to the fresh air.

#details
Francis Lovell, 1st Viscount Lovell, was a staunch supporter of Richard III during the Wars of the Roses. Following the defeat of the Yorkists at the Battle of Stoke Field in 1487, he managed to escape capture and returned in secret to Minster Lovell Hall. Seeking refuge from his enemies, he retreated into a concealed underground room known only to a single, trusted servant. The servant locked him securely inside and brought him food in secret. However, the servant died unexpectedly before he could return or reveal the hiding place, leaving Lord Lovell trapped with no way out. He suffered a grim fate, dying of starvation in his own concealed cell. Long afterwards, during renovations, a hidden vault behind an old chimney was broken open, revealing the tragic remains of the fugitive lord seated at a table with books, pen, and paper, accompanied by the skeleton of his dog at his feet. As soon as the fresh air hit the subterranean chamber, the skeletons crumbled instantly to dust.

#location
Minster Lovell Hall ruins are located beside the River Windrush in the village of Minster Lovell, Oxfordshire.

#title
The Black Ghost of Littledean Hall

#summary
Littledean Hall is haunted by the phantom of an 18th-century enslaved servant boy who is said to seek retribution against his young master. Visitors and residents have long reported poltergeist activity, flickering lights, and sightings of the boy on the landing.

#details
Littledean Hall, reputed to be one of the oldest and most haunted houses in England, preserves a dark and enduring legend surrounding an 18th-century portrait known as *The Red Boy*. The painting depicts young Charles Pyrke alongside his enslaved black manservant, who wears a locked silver collar. According to local folklore and family accounts passed down through generations, the servant took vengeance on Charles after his sister was wronged, resulting in a fatal clash. Ever since, the ghostly figure of the black boy has haunted the upper landing and rooms of the hall. Countless paranormal occurrences have been documented by residents and visitors, including unexplained poltergeist phenomena in the dining room, objects mysteriously thrown into fireplaces, eerie lights flickering beneath bedroom doors, and spectral manifestations tied to the tragic history of the house.

#location
Littledean Hall is situated in the historic village of Littledean within the Forest of Dean district of Gloucestershire, England.

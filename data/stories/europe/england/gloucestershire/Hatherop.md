#title
The Knight's Ride from Hatherop to Oxford

#summary
In the fourteenth century, a knight riding from Hatherop Castle to fetch holy water to save his dying beloved was waylaid by demonic apparitions in the woods, only to return and find she had passed away.

#details
In 1305, a knight named Sir Aldred returned from the Holy Land to the Cotswolds and fell deeply in love with Mira, the beautiful daughter of the house at Hatherop Castle. When Mira fell gravely ill with a mysterious, lingering sickness, Sir Aldred was desperate to save her. A preceptor told him of a miraculous holy well dedicated to St Winifred and St Margaret on the outskirts of Oxford that could cure any disease. Sir Aldred mounted his horse and rode out through the night, passing through the woods of the Hatherop estate towards the old Roman road of Akeman Street. 

As he rode through the darkness, his horse strayed into a tangled wildwood where nightmarish creatures and an enchantress of terrifying beauty tried to tempt and frighten him. Drawing his sword and invoking Christ's name, Sir Aldred fled the fiends and pressed on to Oxford, where he filled a bottle at the holy well. He rode back as fast as he had come, arriving at Hatherop Castle just as night fell. He watched Mira drink the holy water, but it was in vain; she died that very night. Heartbroken and tormented by a dream that the nightmare woman in the woods had poisoned the bottle, Sir Aldred's mind and spirit broke, and he did not long survive her.

#location
Hatherop Castle (now Hatherop School) in the village of Hatherop, Gloucestershire.

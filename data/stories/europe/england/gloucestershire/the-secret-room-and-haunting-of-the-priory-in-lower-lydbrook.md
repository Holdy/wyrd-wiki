#title
The Secret Room and Haunting of The Priory in Lower Lydbrook

#summary
Built in the late 15th or early 16th century as Lidbrook Farm, The Priory in Lower Lydbrook is steeped in local folklore regarding a secret priest hole, underground escape tunnels, and persistent accounts of ghostly hauntings.

#details
Originally constructed around 1500 as Lidbrook Farm and later associated with the prominent Probert family, The Priory is recognized as the oldest surviving building in Lydbrook, Gloucestershire. Located at the corner of Proberts Barn Lane, the late medieval timber-framed structure features close studding, Tudor-arched doorways, and medieval fireplaces. For centuries, local tradition has maintained that the house conceals a secret room—variously interpreted as a Catholic priest hole from penal times or a hiding place for smugglers—along with a network of subterranean tunnels allegedly connecting the farm to Courtfield and the nearby Anchor Inn. Alongside these architectural mysteries, generations of locals have passed down accounts of paranormal phenomena within its ancient timber walls, including unexplained footsteps and spectral sightings.

#location
The Priory is located at the corner of Proberts Barn Lane in Lower Lydbrook, Gloucestershire (OS Grid Reference SO 59523 16685).

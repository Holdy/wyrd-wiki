    The River Crossing Cure for Whooping Cough

    Summary

    Local tradition held that crossing the River Evenlode between Kingham and Bledington possessed the miraculous power to cure whooping cough. This folk belief was closely tied to the rural heritage of the border parish.

    Details

    According to traditional accounts recorded in the early twentieth century, residents of the Cotswolds maintained a curious folk belief regarding the River Evenlode where it forms the boundary between Kingham and Bledington. It was believed that simply crossing the river at this location served as an effective remedy for whooping cough. While medical science would attribute any health benefits to fresh air or the sheer vigor of participating in the village's energetic Morris dancing jigs that naturally exercised the lungs, the local belief persisted as a cherished piece of regional folklore.

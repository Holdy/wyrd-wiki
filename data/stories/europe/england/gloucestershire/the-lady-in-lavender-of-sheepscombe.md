#title
The Lady in Lavender of Sheepscombe

#summary
The historic village of Sheepscombe is haunted by the spectral figure of a woman dressed in lavender or mauve clothing. She is frequently sighted in and around The Butchers Arms pub, a haunting linked to a stern woman from the early 19th century who was allegedly responsible for the closure of several local drinking establishments.

#details
Local tradition holds that Sheepscombe is haunted by a mysterious woman wearing lavender or mauve-colored garments. Her apparition has been reported by locals and visitors alike, particularly in and around The Butchers Arms pub. According to regional lore, her spirit is believed to be that of a formidable woman from the early 19th century who took it upon herself to campaign against and successfully shut down several competing local pubs due to moral objections, leaving an enduring mark on the village that she continues to police from beyond the grave.

#location
The Butchers Arms is a traditional pub located in the heart of the village of Sheepscombe, Gloucestershire.

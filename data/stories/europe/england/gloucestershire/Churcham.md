#title
The Penance of Wulfin le Rue

#summary
According to ancient abbey chronicles, a medieval nobleman murdered six priests along the route between Churcham and Gloucester, resulting in a papal decree that forever tied his lands to perpetual spiritual intercession.

#details
In the early eleventh century, a nobleman named Wulfin le Rue committed a heinous crime by slaying six priests along the way between the village of Churcham and the city of Gloucester. Desperate for salvation and seeking forgiveness for his grave sins, Wulfin journeyed to Rome to petition the Pope for absolution. The Pope granted absolution upon a stringent condition: Wulfin had to ensure that six priests would intercede for his soul in perpetuity. To fulfill this divine mandate, Wulfin surrendered his extensive manors of Churcham and Highnam to the Abbey of Gloucester, stipulating that the monastic community must forever maintain prayers and intercession for his redemption.

#location
The historic parish lands and route between Churcham and Gloucester, Gloucestershire.

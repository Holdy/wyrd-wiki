#title
The Phantom Bird of the 1644 Skirmish Road

#summary
Two travellers driving near Stanton along a road linked to a 1644 Civil War skirmish experienced a sudden, inexplicable sensation of drowning before a white bird struck their windscreen, covering the glass in blood and feathers.

#details
Outside the North Cotswolds village of Stanton, a stretch of road historically tied to a Civil War skirmish in 1644 has become the site of a bizarre local mystery. As two young travellers drove along the route, the passenger was abruptly overcome by a terrifying physical sensation of water rising up her legs, making her feel as though she were drowning. Simultaneously, the driver was gripped by an unreasoning wave of panic. When they brought the vehicle to a halt, a white bird violently crashed into the windscreen, instantly coating the glass with blood and feathers. No other similar incidents have been reported along this stretch of road, leaving the event as a singular and chilling local tale.

#location
A stretch of road just outside Stanton, Gloucestershire, associated with a Civil War skirmish from 1644.

#title
The Blacksmith's Curse at Nether Lypiatt Manor

#summary
A desperate blacksmith condemned for sheep-stealing was promised his freedom if he forged flawless gates for Judge Coxe, but upon realizing betrayal was inevitable, he introduced a deliberate flaw and cursed the household with misfortune with his dying breath. The spectral blacksmith and the lingering curse are said to haunt the estate and afflict those who inherit it.

#details
Judge Charles Coxe, a stern and unforgiving magistrate, arrested a local blacksmith for sheep-stealing—a capital crime at the time. Seeking magnificent wrought-iron gates for his newly built home, Nether Lypiatt Manor, Coxe offered the craftsman a bargain: if he could forge gates of absolute perfection, his life would be spared. The blacksmith labored endlessly, pouring all his skill into the ironwork. However, realizing the judge never intended to pardon him, the blacksmith's patience snapped, and he wove a deliberate mistake into the intricate metalwork. Enraged by the flaw upon inspection, Coxe reneged on his promise and had the blacksmith hanged. With his dying breath, the blacksmith cursed the judge, the house, and all its future occupants with bad luck. True to the legend, the curse's effects were later blamed for financial ruin, family tragedies, and strange accidents among subsequent residents and descendants.

#location
Nether Lypiatt Manor sits on a hill overlooking the Toadsmoor Valley near Stroud, Gloucestershire.

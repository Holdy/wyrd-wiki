The Haunting of the Trouble House Inn

Summary

The historic Trouble House Inn in Cherington has long been plagued by unsettling supernatural phenomena, including rushing winds, clanking chains, and sightings of a mysterious lady in blue.

Details

Situated in the Cherington area, the Trouble House Inn earned its distinctive name from a turbulent history marked by financial ruin and tragedy. From the 1930s onward, the public house experienced severe disturbances where inexplicable gusts of wind whipped through the rooms, accompanied by the chilling sound of clanking chains—events so intense that one landlord and his family fled the premises. In later decades, a phantom woman known as the "Lady in Blue"—described wearing a blue dress and pinafore with long dark hair or a hooded cloak—has been witnessed by staff and visitors, on occasion seen carrying a tray of bottles through the back rooms before vanishing into thin air.

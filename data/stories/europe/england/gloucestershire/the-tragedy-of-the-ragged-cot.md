#title
The Tragedy of the Ragged Cot

#summary
An 18th-century innkeeper who attempted to rob a stagecoach returned home to find his wife and infant son dead at the foot of the stairs, beginning centuries of haunting spectral phenomena at the coaching inn.

#details
In 1760, Bill Clavers, the unruly landlord of the Ragged Cot coaching inn, decided to rob a midnight stagecoach traveling to London. Fortifying his courage with rum, he grabbed two loaded pistols and prepared to leave. When his wife, holding their infant son, desperately tried to dissuade him, Clavers forcefully pushed her aside, causing her and the baby to tumble down the steep wooden stairs. After carrying out his botched robbery, Clavers returned to the inn only to discover the lifeless bodies of his wife and child at the base of the staircase. In a panic, he hid their remains in a trunk. Local law enforcement, tracking his footprints through the snow, arrived at the inn and were confronted by the eerie apparitions of the mother and child before fleeing in terror. Clavers was subsequently convicted and hanged for his crimes, and ever since, guests and staff have reported hearing the cries of a baby and seeing the ghostly figure of a woman in old-fashioned garments roaming the premises.

#location
The Ragged Cot is an historic coaching inn located on Cock Road in Minchinhampton, near Stroud, Gloucestershire.

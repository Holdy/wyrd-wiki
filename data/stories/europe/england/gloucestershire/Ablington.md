#title
The Thief and the Hangman's Stone

#summary
A sheep stealer carrying a stolen animal tied around his neck met a fatal end when he rested his burden on a wayside stone that unexpectedly rolled the sheep over, hanging the thief.

#details
As local legend recounts in the Cotswolds near Ablington and Hampnett, a thief who had stolen a sheep sought to carry it away across his shoulders by fastening it with a rope around his neck. Growing weary during his journey over the wolds, he paused to rest and placed the heavy sheep on top of a prominent boundary stone. While he rested, the sheep suddenly slipped or rolled off the opposite side of the stone, pulling the rope taut and hanging the thief by his own neck.

#location
The Hangman's Stone is situated near Hampnett and Ablington Camp in the Cotswold hills of Gloucestershire.

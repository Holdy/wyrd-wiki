#title
The Murder at Trunkhouse Barn

#summary
In 1727, a brutal murder in Doynton involving a stolen gold ring sparked a local legend and ghost story that endured in village oral tradition for over two centuries.

#details
In 1727, a shocking crime shook the small Gloucestershire parish of Doynton when Hannah Williams, a servant girl working for local farmer Tobias Luton, was murdered and the farmhouse was robbed. The perpetrator, a local man named Roger Bryant, fled the scene but was eventually tracked down and apprehended after being discovered wearing a distinctive gold ring stolen during the robbery. Bryant was committed to Gloucester Gaol, condemned at assizes, and subsequently hanged and gibbeted in August 1727. Over the generations, the tragic and violent event became deeply embedded in local folklore, surviving through village oral tradition and ghost stories tied to Trunkhouse Barn.

#location
Trunkhouse Barn is situated along the rural tracks just north of the village of Doynton in South Gloucestershire.

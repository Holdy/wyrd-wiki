#title
The Haunting of Thornbury Castle

#summary
Thornbury Castle is said to be haunted by several restless spirits from its Tudor past, including a misogynistic poltergeist identified as Jasper Tudor who disrupts staff in the upper chambers. Other spectral sightings include a phantom greyhound and young children appearing in empty rooms during festivities.

#details
Built in the 16th century by Edward Stafford, the 3rd Duke of Buckingham, Thornbury Castle has accumulated a rich tapestry of ghostly lore recounted by staff and guests over the years. Its most famous and active spirit is believed to be Jasper Tudor, the former Duke of Bedford and uncle to Henry VII. Because the first-floor rooms he once frequented are now used as offices predominantly staffed by women, Jasper reportedly expresses his displeasure by knocking over books, activating equipment, and dropping items off shelves. Other eerie phenomena reported across the castle include phantom children seen watching fireworks from empty suites, a ghostly lady in grey walking the battlements, and a phantom greyhound accompanying the spirit of Princess Mary Tudor through the Oriel dining room.

#location
Thornbury Castle is located on Castle Street in the town of Thornbury, South Gloucestershire (BS35 1HH).

#title
The Phantom of Greet Road

#summary
While walking home along Greet Road in November 1949, a local man encountered a tall, dark figure gliding two feet above the ground that vanished into thin air just moments before he learned of a relative's death.

#details
In November 1949, a local resident named Mr. Bassett-Green returned to the area by train after visiting a sick relative. As he walked along Greet Road toward home and approached the hollow near the cemetery, an overwhelming sense of being followed came over him, though no one was visible behind him. Suddenly, a tall, dark figure strode past him down the middle of the road—except the apparition was hovering a full two feet off the ground. Thinking the person was foolishly risking their safety in traffic, Mr. Bassett-Green moved to warn them off the road, whereupon the figure abruptly dissolved into thin air. Shaken by the strange encounter, he hurried home, only to be met immediately with the news that his ailing relative had just passed away.

#location
Greet Road, near the cemetery in Greet, Gloucestershire.

#title
The Haunting of Arle Court House

#summary
Arle Court House is said to be haunted by an unseen presence that knocks on the door for expected visitors and lingers behind residents on the stairs.

#details
Built in the reign of Queen Elizabeth I, Arle Court House has long been regarded as haunted by local tradition and former residents. When visitors are expected at the house, a loud knock frequently echoes on the door, though no one is ever there when it is answered. In the 1960s, a resident named Mrs. Moot, whose husband managed Western Estates, used a first-floor landing room for sewing and frequently sensed an unseen presence standing directly behind her, vanishing whenever she turned around.

#location
Arle Court House is located on Kingsmead Road in the district of Arle, Cheltenham, Gloucestershire.

#title
The Tragedy of Arlington Mill

#summary
A wealthy miller discovering his young wife and eldest son in an illicit affair exacted a cruel punishment that resulted in double tragedy and a permanent spectral haunting.

#details
After moving to Bibury to run the local water mill, a wealthy widower married a young woman named Mary, who had been persuaded into the match by her parents despite her reluctance. While the miller was frequently away on business, Mary and her stepson grew close and fell in love. One winter evening, the miller returned home unexpectedly early and caught the two together. Enraged, he threw his son into the mill pond where he drowned, and locked Mary out in the freezing snow. The next morning, Mary was discovered frozen to death by the waterside, and it is said that her spirit still haunts the mill and surrounding area on cold winter mornings.

#location
Arlington Mill is located beside the River Coln in the village of Bibury, Gloucestershire.

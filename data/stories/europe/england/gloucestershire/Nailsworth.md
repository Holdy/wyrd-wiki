#title
The Apparition of Arnold's Lane

#summary
Arnold's Lane in Nailsworth is said to be haunted by the unclear apparition of a wealthy man who was murdered there in the 1880s.

#details
Local tradition and historical paranormal documentation record that Arnold's Lane in Nailsworth, Gloucestershire, is haunted by the restless spirit of a wealthy individual. According to local accounts, this well-to-do man was murdered in the lane during the 1880s. Ever since, his indistinct, shadowy apparition has been reputedly spotted by passersby lingering in the historic lane where he met his violent end.

#location
Arnold's Lane in Nailsworth, Gloucestershire.

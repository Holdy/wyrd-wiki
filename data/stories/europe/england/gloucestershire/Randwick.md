#title
The Galloping Highwaymen of Randwick Woods

#summary
Randwick Woods and the underground passages connecting Randwick Church to Moor Hall are steeped in local folklore, where the phantom sounds of galloping hooves echo through the trees at night. In addition to highwayman sightings, a photograph of a resident ghost once hung in the village's Vine Tree pub.

#details
According to local tradition and folklore in the Cotswold village of Randwick, the woods above the settlement are haunted by the spirits of historic highwaymen. Tales are frequently told of eerie, disembodied sounds of horses' hooves galloping furiously through the dark woodland when nothing can be seen. The spooky atmosphere is heightened by persistent rumors of hidden underground tunnels and secret passages connecting Randwick Church to Moor Hall. Additionally, local lore recalls that a photograph capturing an apparition was once proudly displayed inside the village's Vine Tree pub, though its current whereabouts remain unknown.

#location
Randwick Woods, stretching between Randwick Church and Moor Hall in the village of Randwick, Gloucestershire.

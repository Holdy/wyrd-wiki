#title
The Grey Lady of Arlington Mill

#summary
A young woman locked out in the snow by her jealous miller husband froze to death outside Arlington Mill, while his eldest son was cast into the millpond below. To this day, ghostly faces appear in the mill windows and her spirit haunts the nearby waterside path.

#details
In the Gloucestershire village of Bibury, local legend tells the tragic tale of a young woman named Mary who was married off for wealth and status to an older miller, a widower with three grown-up sons. While her husband was away on business, Mary fell in love with his eldest son. On a bitter, frigid winter night, the miller returned unexpectedly and discovered the two together. Consumed by a blinding rage, the miller shoved his son from the top of Arlington Mill to his death in the millpond below, and locked Mary outside on the freezing threshold. Mary perished in the snow, freezing to death by the waterside. Local accounts and folklore maintain that ghostly, sorrowful faces can still be seen peering from the windows of Arlington Mill, and Mary—known as Bibury's Grey Lady—haunts the path running alongside Rack Isle by the river Coln.

#location
Arlington Mill stands beside the River Coln in the village of Bibury, Gloucestershire.

#title
The Tragedy of Arlington Mill

#summary
A wealthy miller discovering his young wife and eldest son in an illicit affair exacted a cruel punishment that resulted in double tragedy and a permanent spectral haunting.

#details
After moving to Bibury to run the local water mill, a wealthy widower married a young woman named Mary, who had been persuaded into the match by her parents despite her reluctance. While the miller was frequently away on business, Mary and her stepson grew close and fell in love. One winter evening, the miller returned home unexpectedly early and caught the two together. Enraged, he threw his son into the mill pond where he drowned, and locked Mary out in the freezing snow. The next morning, Mary was discovered frozen to death by the waterside, and it is said that her spirit still haunts the mill and surrounding area on cold winter mornings.

#location
Arlington Mill is located beside the River Coln in the village of Bibury, Gloucestershire.

#title
The Witches of Betty's Grave

#summary
According to local Gloucestershire legend, a rural crossroads near Fairford marks the final resting place of executed witches and restless spirits whose phantoms continue to haunt the area.

#details
Betty's Grave is a quiet rural crossroads situated between the villages of Poulton and Fairford in Gloucestershire. Local tradition offers several competing explanations for the ominous name and haunted reputation of the spot. One prominent legend describes Betty as an accused witch who was executed and buried at the crossroads without Christian rites. According to the tale, after being buried, she repeatedly rose from her grave until the villagers agreed to leave ongoing floral tributes at her resting place. Whenever the practice of laying flowers lapsed, her ghostly shade would wander through the surrounding country lanes and Fairford until the offerings were resumed. Other related local folklore attributes the hauntings to a group of local witches who were buried at the crossroads after their deaths, leaving their spectral figures to plague the junction.

#location
Betty's Grave crossroads on the A417 between Poulton and Fairford, Gloucestershire.

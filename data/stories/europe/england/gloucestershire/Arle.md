#title
The Haunting of Arle Court House

#summary
Arle Court House in Arle is reputed to be haunted by phantom knocks at the door and an unseen presence lingering behind residents on the stairs.

#details
Dating back to the Elizabethan era when it was built by the Lygon family, Arle Court House has long been the subject of local ghost lore. According to accounts recorded by local historians and residents—including Mrs. Moot, who lived in the historic building in the 1960s—the house experiences unexplained phenomena. Whenever visitors are expected, a heavy, distinct knock echoes on the door, though opening it reveals nobody there. Furthermore, those working or walking on the first-floor landing frequently sense an oppressive, invisible "presence" standing directly behind them, vanishing into thin air the moment they turn around.

#location
Arle Court House is situated on Kingsmead Road in the district of Arle, Cheltenham, Gloucestershire.

#upstream-source
Chris Green, *The History of Hesters Way* (Volume 1).

#title
The Haunting of Arle Court House

#summary
Arle Court House is said to be haunted by an unseen presence that knocks on the door for expected visitors and lingers behind residents on the stairs.

#details
Built in the reign of Queen Elizabeth I, Arle Court House has long been regarded as haunted by local tradition and former residents. When visitors are expected at the house, a loud knock frequently echoes on the door, though no one is ever there when it is answered. In the 1960s, a resident named Mrs. Moot, whose husband managed Western Estates, used a first-floor landing room for sewing and frequently sensed an unseen presence standing directly behind her, vanishing whenever she turned around.

#location
Arle Court House is located on Kingsmead Road in the district of Arle, Cheltenham, Gloucestershire.

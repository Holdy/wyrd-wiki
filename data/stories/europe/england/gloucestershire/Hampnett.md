#title
The Thief and the Hangman's Stone

#summary
A sheep rustler met a bizarre and fatal end when his stolen animal struggled and accidentally hanged him over a stone barrier near Hampnett. Local tradition maintains this grim mishap gave the landmark its enduring name.

#details
According to local legend associated with the ancient sarsen stone near Hampnett, a thief once stole a sheep from a local flock. To transport his ill-gotten gains across the fields, he bound the animal and hoisted it over his shoulders as he attempted to cross a field boundary or stile. As he climbed over, the terrified sheep panicked and struggled violently, throwing the thief off balance. The tether tightened around his neck, leaving him suspended and strangling him to death. When morning came, the thief was discovered dead alongside the struggling animal, cementing the stone's grim reputation as the Hangman's Stone.

#location
The Hangman's Stone is located near Hampnett and Oxpens Farm, positioned along the rural trackways and parish boundaries west of Northleach in Gloucestershire.

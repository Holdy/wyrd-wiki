#title
The Hangman of Bull's Cross

#summary
A high Cotswold crossroads near Slad is haunted by a phantom stagecoach and the tragic ghost of an executioner who mistakenly hanged his own son.

#details
High above the village of Slad at Bull's Cross, where a gibbet once stood in former times, local folklore tells of dark supernatural occurrences. According to tradition, an executioner once lived in the secluded dingle of Deadcombe Bottom just below the ridge. One dark night, the hangman unwittingly hanged his own son on the gallows at Bull's Cross. Upon discovering the identity of his victim, the wretched man returned to his cottage and hanged himself from a hook behind his door. Ever since, locals report that a phantom stagecoach rises from the remains of the hangman's house at midnight—especially on New Year's Eve—while the crossroads remain haunted by restless spirits.

#location
Bull's Cross is a hilltop crossroads on the B4070 road located approximately one mile north of Slad in Gloucestershire.

#upstream-source
Recounted by local guide Jim Fern in a walking booklet published by the Ramblers' Association.

#title
The Wandering Woodland of Dixton

#summary
According to local tradition, a massive ancient landslip once carried a portion of Dixton Wood across the county boundary from Gloucestershire into Worcestershire.

#details
Nestled near the foot of the Cotswold escarpment between Alderton Hill and Dixton Hill, the parish of Alderton with Dixton lies extraordinarily close to the historic boundary separating Gloucestershire from Worcestershire. Local tradition and historical records maintain that a significant landslip occurred on the eastern slopes of Woolstone Hill within Dixton Wood, reportedly carrying a portion of the woodland bodily across the county line into Worcestershire. This enduring local legend captures both the precarious geology of the Lias clay slopes and the lively boundary lore of the rural borderlands.

#location
Dixton Wood on the eastern slopes of Woolstone Hill, adjacent to the Alderton and Dixton parish boundary in Gloucestershire.

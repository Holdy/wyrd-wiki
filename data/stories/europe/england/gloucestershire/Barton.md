#title
The Haunting of the New Olympus Theatre

#summary
During an overnight charity ghost hunt at the New Olympus Theatre on Barton Street, a visitor experienced unexplained glowing orbs, sudden temperature shifts, and the distinct sensation of unseen hands touching her hair and shoulders.

#details
Jo Pockett took part in an overnight paranormal investigation at the historic New Olympus Theatre on Barton Street to raise money for charity. Throughout the night, attendees witnessed floating orbs of light and experienced abrupt shifts between hot and cold spots in an otherwise empty room. While saying a protection prayer at the beginning of the session, the witness felt an overwhelming wave of warmth, describing the sensation of someone touching her hair and placing a comforting hand on her shoulders when no other person was nearby.

#location
The New Olympus Theatre on Barton Street in Gloucester, Gloucestershire.

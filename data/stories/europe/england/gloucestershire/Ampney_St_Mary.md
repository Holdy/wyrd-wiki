#title
The Legend of Betty's Grave

#summary
Betty's Grave is a rural Gloucestershire crossroads named after an accused witch who repeatedly rose from her burial site until villagers vowed to leave her regular floral tributes.

#details
Local folklore tells of a mysterious mute woman who arrived in the area near Ampney St Mary and Poulton. Because of her silence and strange behavior, she was accused of witchcraft and hanged by locals, who buried her body at a quiet country crossroads. However, she repeatedly rose from the dead and returned to haunt the area until the terrified villagers swore to regularly lay fresh flowers upon her grave. Generations of residents maintained this tradition of leaving floral offerings at the crossroads signpost—which is marked "Betty's Grave" and first appeared on Ordnance Survey maps in 1830—to keep her restless spirit at bay.

#location
Betty's Grave is a rural crossroads located on a lane near Ampney St Mary and Poulton in Gloucestershire.

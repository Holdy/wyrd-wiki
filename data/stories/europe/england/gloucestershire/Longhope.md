#title
The Treasure of Crockett's Hole

#summary
An underground cavern on the eastern slope of May Hill was long rumored to contain hidden riches and served as a place of refuge during times of religious persecution. Despite numerous expeditions by hopeful treasure hunters over the centuries, including a disbanded soldier in 1665, no gold or treasure has ever been uncovered.

#details
Overlooking the village of Longhope, the prominent summit and slopes of May Hill have accumulated a rich tapestry of local legends. Among the most enduring is the tale of Crockett's Hole, an underground chamber or cavern situated on the east side of the hill near Yartleton Wood. According to regional tradition recorded as early as the turn of the eighteenth century, the concealed chamber was allegedly packed with great wealth and had served as a safe haven during the Protestant persecutions under Queen Mary. 

Intrigued by tales of hidden riches, many individuals attempted to find the secret entrance over the generations. In 1665, a disbanded soldier named Fairfax traveled all the way from London specifically to explore Crockett's Hole and claim its legendary bounty. Like every fortune-seeker who came before and after him, Fairfax found no trace of the treasure. Although the exact location of the cavern remains lost to time and overgrowth, the legend of Crockett's Hole endures as a hallmark of local folklore in Gloucestershire.

#location
Crockett's Hole is situated on the eastern slope of May Hill, near Yartleton Wood in Gloucestershire.

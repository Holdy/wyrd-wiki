#title
The Coaching Ox Bow of Stinchcombe Hill

#summary
Coaches traveling the old London road up Stinchcombe Hill relied on an ingenious side route and teams of oxen to conquer the punishing climb. 

#details
Local tradition and historical accounts handed down from the era of horse-drawn transport describe the grueling ascent of Stinchcombe Hill along the old Berkeley-to-London route. The incline was so steep that draft horses pulling heavy wagons frequently suffered fatal strains, prompting the use of a gentler side route known as the "Ox bow." Along this path, horses were unhitched and led up the easier slope while heavy-duty oxen hauled the coaches straight up the main track. At the summit, passengers used a stone mounting step to re-board the coaches before continuing their journey.

#location
The historic coaching track ascending the top of Stinchcombe Hill from The Street in the parish of Stinchcombe, Gloucestershire.

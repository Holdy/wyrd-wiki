#title
The Ghost of Longlevens

#summary
A young couple driving through Longlevens, Gloucester, hit what they thought was a child but found no body or damage to their car.

#details
In late autumn 1969, a young couple, Mr. Black and Miss White, were driving down the Cheltenham road through Longlevens, a suburb of Gloucester. They were heading to Churchdown where Miss White lived. As they drove past Anderson’s garage (now a BP Garage), a girl appeared in front of their car. Both felt they had driven over her body, but there was no sign of damage to the car or any trace of the child.

The couple were extremely shaken and deeply upset. They eventually married but never resolved what had happened that night. Investigations later revealed that a woman was killed on the exact spot in February 1963, but there is no particular reason to connect the two incidents.

#location
Anderson’s garage (now a BP Garage) on the Cheltenham road through Longlevens, Gloucester

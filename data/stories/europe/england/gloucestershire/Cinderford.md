#title
The Phantom at the Dilke Hospital Bus Stop

#summary
A late-night driver twice encountered a mysterious figure waiting at the roadside bus shelter near the Dilke Hospital, only for the man to vanish completely when the driver pulled over to offer a lift.

#details
A long-serving driver who frequently passed the Dilke Hospital during late-night shifts experienced two unsettling encounters eighteen months apart on the Coleford-Cinderford road. On the first occasion at around half-past midnight, and later at approximately 7:00 PM, he noticed a solitary man standing inside the glass-paneled bus shelter, looking intently in a fixed direction as though waiting for someone. Each time, knowing no bus was due at that hour, the driver pulled in to offer the man a lift into Cinderford. However, upon stopping and checking the surroundings, he found the shelter entirely empty and no one anywhere in sight. Convinced there was something paranormal about the recurring figure, the driver later questioned whether an unrecorded tragedy had once taken place at that exact location.

#location
The bus stop located near the Dilke Hospital on the Coleford-Cinderford road in Cinderford, Gloucestershire.

#title
The Phantom of Dilke Hospital Bus Stop

#summary
A night driver twice encountered a mysterious figure waiting at a roadside bus stop in Cinderford who vanished into thin air whenever he pulled over to offer a lift. Convinced of the supernatural, the driver wondered if a forgotten tragedy had occurred at the spot.

#details
A long-time driver who frequently passed Dilke Hospital during the night experienced two unnerving encounters with a spectral figure waiting by the bus stop on the Dilke side of the road in Cinderford. The first incident took place at half-past midnight when, knowing no buses were operating at that hour, the driver pulled over to offer the lone figure a ride out of kindness. Peering through the glass panels of the shelter, he found nobody inside and nobody in sight along the road. Nearly two years later at around 7:00 PM, the identical figure appeared again, gazing fixedly in the exact same direction as if waiting for someone. Once more, the moment the vehicle drew level, the figure had completely vanished without a trace, leaving the driver haunted by the mystery.

#location
The bus stop located immediately outside Dilke Hospital on the Dilke side of the road in Cinderford, Gloucestershire.

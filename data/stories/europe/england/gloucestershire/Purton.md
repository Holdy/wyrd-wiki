#title
The Fiddlers of Fiddler's Pool

#summary
On stormy nights on the River Severn near Purton, the ghostly music of Welsh musicians who drowned in a treacherous whirlpool can still be heard playing from the depths.

#details
According to local folklore, a party of Welsh fiddlers spent an evening playing and merrymaking in Berkeley. When the night grew dark, they set out in a boat to cross the treacherous River Severn back toward home. However, their vessel was caught in a violent whirlpool known as Fiddler's Pool, situated in the estuarine waters just downriver from Purton. The boat was overcome by the churning maelstrom and sank, plunging all the musicians to their deaths. To this day, locals maintain that on stormy nights, the phantom sounds of their frantic fiddling can still be heard echoing across the water.

#location
Fiddler's Pool in the River Severn, located approximately 300 yards downriver from the old Severn Railway Bridge piers near Purton, Gloucestershire.

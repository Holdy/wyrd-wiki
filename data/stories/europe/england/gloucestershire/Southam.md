#title
The Phantoms of Southam House

#summary
The historic Southam House manor in Gloucestershire is said to be haunted by a phantom shepherd leading his flock through the paddocks and a mysterious female spirit who tugs on the sleeves of visitors.

#details
Built originally in the late 15th century and later passing to the illustrious De la Bere family, Southam House (subsequently known as Hotel De la Bere and Ellenborough Park) in Southam, Gloucestershire, is steeped in history and centuries of local lore. Over the generations, as the sprawling Tudor manor hosted royalty, nobility, and travelers, it accrued accounts of spectral activity within its ancient walls and grounds. Among the most enduring legends associated with the estate are two distinct apparitions: a phantom shepherd who is said to silently lead his sheep through the paddocks behind the manor, and a female ghost whose presence is felt when she mysteriously tugs on the sleeves of unsuspecting individuals.

#location
Southam House (now Ellenborough Park hotel), situated at the foot of Cleeve Hill in the village of Southam, Gloucestershire, UK.

#title
The Phantom Woman of Cainscross Road

#summary
An apparition of a woman has been witnessed crossing Cainscross Road near Tricorn House before mysteriously fading into thin air.

#details
Local accounts in Cainscross tell of a spectral woman who has been seen crossing Cainscross Road in the vicinity of Tricorn House. Drivers and pedestrians have reported observing the mysterious female figure step out into the road, only to vanish completely before reaching the opposite side.

#location
Cainscross Road, near Tricorn House in Cainscross, Gloucestershire.

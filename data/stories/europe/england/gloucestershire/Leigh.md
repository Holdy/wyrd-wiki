#title
The Coombe Hill Sea Serpent

#summary
A local lad named Tom Smith successfully lured and slew a giant sea serpent that had been terrorizing the riverbanks of Coombe Hill by gaining its trust with food and striking it with an axe. In gratitude, he was rewarded with limitless free ale for life at the local tavern.

#details
Long ago, a massive sea monster—suspected to be a knucker dragon—swam up the River Severn and established its lair on the riverbanks at Coombe Hill in Gloucestershire. Initially feeding on sheep and poultry, the creature soon escalated to preying on local children and milkmaids, driving many villagers away in terror. A local young man named Tom Smith devised a daring plan to stop the beast. He began leaving food out for the serpent, gradually earning its trust until he was able to feed it by hand. One day, while offering the creature a large marrowbone, Tom drew his axe and severed its head with a single swift blow. As his reward, he was granted free ale for life at the local village tavern.

#location
The riverbanks along the River Severn at Coombe Hill in the parish of Leigh, Gloucestershire.

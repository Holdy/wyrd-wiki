#title
The Ghost of Tom Long

#summary
The notorious eighteenth-century highwayman Tom Long is said to haunt the Amberley Inn and the crossroads on Minchinhampton Common that bear his name. Visitors and guests have reported hearing inexplicable footsteps and witnessing a spectral horseman roaming the roads where he met his end.

#details
According to local Gloucestershire tradition, Tom Long was a fearsome highwayman who preyed upon travellers and coaching traffic across the Cotswolds. The exact details of his death remain shrouded in conflicting legends: some accounts suggest he died by his own hand to avoid capture, while others maintain he was hunted down and executed at the junction of six roads on Minchinhampton Common. This historic meeting point has since been known as Tom Long's Post. 

Following his violent demise, his ghost allegedly became bound to the landscape. He is said to haunt the Amberley Inn—where local lore suggests he was either taken after capture or returns to search for his lost love, the landlord's daughter—disturbing guests with phantom footsteps and doors slamming in the dead of night. Furthermore, travellers crossing the common have occasionally reported sightings of a spectral figure riding a black horse along the old coaching routes where he once plied his trade.

#location
Tom Long's Post at the junction of six roads on Minchinhampton Common, near Amberley, Gloucestershire.

#title
The Phantom Highwayman of the Amberley Inn

#summary
The notorious 19th-century highwayman Tom Long is said to haunt the Amberley Inn, where guests and staff are frequently disturbed by mysterious footsteps and slamming doors.

#details
Tom Long was a notorious highwayman who operated across the Cotswolds and ultimately met a grim end around Minchinhampton Common. According to local folklore, his restless spirit haunts the Amberley Inn—the establishment where he was either brought upon his capture or where he returns in death to seek his lost love, the landlord's daughter. To this day, visitors and residents report uncanny paranormal activity within the historic pub, including unexplained footsteps echoing through the corridors and doors slamming shut on their own accord.

#location
The Amberley Inn is located in the village of Amberley, near Stroud in Gloucestershire, overlooking Minchinhampton Common.

#title
The Phantom Mother of Swan Pool

#summary
The dark waters of Swan Pool near Newland are haunted by the spectral apparition of a mother cradling her child and an ethereal dog that circles the banks. According to local lore, the haunting stems from a tragic murder committed near the water's edge.

#details
Located on the road leading towards Newland from Redbrook, Swan Pool has long carried a sinister reputation among locals. After dark, witnesses have reported seeing the ghostly figure of a woman emerging from the water while cradling a young child, emitting a mournful cry before dissolving into the mist. Alongside the grieving mother, local legend tells of an eerie dog that emerges from the surrounding woods to patrol the perimeter of the pool before vanishing back into the trees.

#location
Swan Pool is situated on the road between Redbrook and Newland in the Forest of Dean, Gloucestershire.

#title
The Tragedy of the Churchyard Well

#summary
A parish priest summoned to administer last rites mysteriously plunged to his death down a deep village well in the dark. A stone cover was subsequently built over the well to prevent future tragedies in the churchyard.

#details
Late one night, the parish priest of All Saints Church in Bisley was called upon to administer the last rites to a dying parishioner. When he failed to arrive at his destination, concerned villagers launched a search through the dark settlement. His body was eventually discovered at the bottom of the deep well located within the churchyard, having evidently lost his footing in the pitch black. To ensure no one else suffered the same fate, a permanent stone structure and cover were built over the dangerous drop.

#location
All Saints Church is located on Parsons Lane in the village of Bisley, near Stroud, Gloucestershire.

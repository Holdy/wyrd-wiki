#title
The Phantom Knight of Pitham Bottom

#summary
A towering Saxon knight in gleaming armor haunts the eerie hollow of Pitham Bottom between Fairford and Quenington, startling unwary travelers on winter nights.

#details
Pitham Bottom is an atmospheric hollow located between the market town of Fairford and the village of Quenington. According to local lore, a seven-foot-tall Saxon knight roams this stretch on cold winter nights, appearing either standing silently by the roadside or mounted on a large black horse. Over the years, several residents have encountered the spectral figure, including a local boy who bravely put up his fists to challenge the giant, a garage owner who fled in terror, and a doctor whose car headlights illuminated the gleaming metal of the armor, prompting him to speed away in fright. These chilling encounters are said to be corroborated by an 1852 archaeological discovery of exceptionally tall skeletons—exceeding six and a half feet—in a burial mound just yards from the hollow.

#location
Pitham Bottom is a hollow located on the road between Fairford and Quenington in Gloucestershire.

#title
The Threepence Wife Sale at Epney

#summary
In 1877, a man drinking at a local inn in Epney auctioned off his wife to his fellow patrons for the sum of threepence. Although she was taken away by the buyer, she abandoned the agreement and returned home to her husband just two hours later.

#details
On a Monday afternoon in June 1877, a group of men were gathered drinking at the Anchor Inn in Epney. During the session, one of the men offered to sell his wife, who was present and willingly assented to the transaction. Finding a buyer proved somewhat challenging despite her personal attractions, but she was eventually purchased for threepence and led away by the buyer. The arrangement did not last, however, as she failed to adhere to the contract and returned to her lawful husband within two hours.

#location
The Anchor Inn is a historic public house situated on the eastern bank of the River Severn in the Gloucestershire hamlet of Epney.

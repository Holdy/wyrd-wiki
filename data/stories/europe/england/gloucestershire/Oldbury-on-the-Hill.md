#title
Nan Tow and the Witch's Tump

#summary
A wicked local witch named Nan Tow incurred the wrath of a Duke of Beaufort and was buried alive in an upright position. Her dwelling was subsequently sealed beneath one of the largest round barrows in the Cotswolds.

#details
According to local legend, Nan Tow was a powerful and feared witch operating in the area. Having deeply offended one of the Dukes of Beaufort through her wicked deeds, she met a grim punishment ordered by the nobleman. Rather than receiving a standard burial, she was buried alive while standing upright within her own dwelling. Over time, her house and grave became entombed beneath a massive earthen mound measuring nine feet high and around one hundred feet in diameter, which remains a prominent feature beside the road.

#location
Nan Tow's Tump is a Bronze Age round barrow located beside the A46 road in Oldbury-on-the-Hill, Gloucestershire.

#title
The Solitary Grave of Shorncote

#summary
According to local legend, the single grave standing in the waterlogged churchyard of All Saints' Church in Shorncote belongs to a young boy who drowned in the surrounding marshes.

#details
All Saints' Church in Shorncote is a tiny, isolated 800-year-old Norman building nestled in the water-rich landscape south of Cirencester. Due to the high water table and waterlogged soil of the surrounding Thames floodplains and gravel pits, the churchyard proved largely unsuitable for traditional burials throughout its history. Consequently, parishioners were buried elsewhere, leaving the churchyard almost entirely empty save for a single grave. Local legend maintains that this solitary mound marks the resting place of a young boy who tragically drowned in the marshy waters nearby.

#location
All Saints' Churchyard, located in the hamlet of Shorncote, Gloucestershire.

#title
The Drowning at Joyce's Pool

#summary
According to local tradition, a young girl named Joyce drowned in a natural spring-fed pool on the edge of the village sometime before the 19th century, giving the water body its enduring name.

#details
Located near the edge of Didmarton beside the road heading toward Leighterton, Joyce's Pool is a natural pool fed by an underground hillside spring that acts as a seasonal starting point for the Sherston branch of the River Avon. While its historical use as a watering hole for village livestock dates back centuries, local legend maintains that the pool was named in memory of a young girl named Joyce who tragically drowned there prior to 1839.

#location
Joyce's Pool is situated on the edge of Didmarton, Gloucestershire, immediately adjacent to the Leighterton road.

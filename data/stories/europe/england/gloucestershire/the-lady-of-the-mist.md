#title
The Lady of the Mist

#summary
Lady Maud haunts the riverside path at Kempsford, where villagers report seeing her ghostly figure floating above the water in the moonlight. Local tradition ties her haunting both to her grief over a drowned brother and to a tragic medieval murder at the site of the former castle.

#details
In Kempsford, a medieval castle belonging to the Dukes of Lancaster once overlooked the River Thames, leaving behind a raised terrace path known as Lady Maud's Walk in the garden adjoining the village church and Old Vicarage. According to local folklore, the path is haunted by Lady Maud, whom villagers refer to as the "Lady of the Mist" because she appears floating above the river in the pale moonlight, eternally waiting for her young brother Henry, who tragically drowned in the treacherous ford. Another version of the legend attributes the haunting to a dark episode where an enraged nobleman threw his wife from the castle ramparts into the river after being falsely stirred by a jealous knight. The ghost of Lady Maud is said to be so active that during the Second World War, Home Guard patrols would only walk the grounds in pairs.

#location
Lady Maud's Walk is situated along the riverbank in the grounds adjoining St Mary's Church and the Old Vicarage in Kempsford, Gloucestershire.

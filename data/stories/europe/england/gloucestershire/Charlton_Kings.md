#title
The Boy Visitor of Charlton Kings

#summary
On New Year's Day, households in Charlton Kings welcomed a dark-haired "boy visitor" through the back door to ensure good luck for the coming year. Children also performed "bud-welling," singing door-to-door to receive apples and cakes for a bountiful harvest.

#details
Until the mid-20th century, the New Year in Charlton Kings was marked by specific household rituals to secure prosperity. The first person to cross the threshold, known as the "boy visitor," had to be a dark-haired male stranger who entered via the back door—using a ladder and window if necessary—and exited through the front, being rewarded with a silver coin for his trouble. Alongside this first-footing custom, children practiced "bud-welling" on New Year's Day morning, carrying baskets from house to house and singing traditional verses to wish residents well, earning gifts of apples and cakes to ensure a fruitful year ahead.

#location
The historic parish and village of Charlton Kings, located just southeast of Cheltenham in Gloucestershire.

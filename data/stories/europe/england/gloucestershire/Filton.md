#title
The Haunting of Spooky Farm

#summary
Security guards patrolling a historic, Grade I-listed farmhouse preserved within a sprawling military complex have frequently reported ghost sightings and unexplained noises. Over the years, these eerie encounters earned the restored building the local moniker "Spooky Farm."

#details
When the Ministry of Defence acquired land in Filton during the early 1990s to construct a major administrative and procurement headquarters at Abbey Wood, the footprint included Stanley Farm—an old, deteriorating agricultural property. Because the building held Grade I-listed status, it could not be demolished alongside the surrounding fields. Instead, it was meticulously restored by stonemasons and carpenters to be repurposed as a training centre. As night falls and the thousands of personnel leave the site, security guards patrolling the grounds have encountered paranormal phenomena. Multiple guards over the years have reported inexplicable sounds and phantom sightings within the historic structure, cementing its enduring reputation among staff as "Spooky Farm."

#location
Stanley Farm is located in the south-east corner of the Ministry of Defence (MoD) Abbey Wood complex in Filton, South Gloucestershire.

#title
The Ghostly Rider of Hill Barn Farm

#summary
A 19th-century farm labourer was repeatedly pursued by the apparition of his former master along a Cotswold track before meeting the spirit at midnight to receive a secret message concerning hidden money.

#details
Following the death of Charles Marshall, who occupied Snowshill Manor and farmed the surrounding land during the first half of the 19th century, his widow continued managing the property. Sometime before 1858, a labourer named Richard Carter was returning home from work at Hill Barn Farm along a little-used track on a winter evening when he was startled by the apparition of his former master, Charles Marshall, riding a black pony alongside him. Though Carter fled in terror, the ghostly encounter repeated itself several times. On the advice of the local rector, Carter finally gathered his courage and demanded of the phantom what it wanted. The spirit instructed Carter to meet him at midnight in the chaff-house. During this nocturnal rendezvous, Marshall entrusted Carter with a secret message for Mrs. Marshall. Although the exact contents were never publicly disclosed, rumors persisted that the message revealed the whereabouts of hidden funds, enabling the widow to finance new building work at the manor.

#location
Hill Barn Farm and the nearby tracks leading to Snowshill Manor, Gloucestershire.

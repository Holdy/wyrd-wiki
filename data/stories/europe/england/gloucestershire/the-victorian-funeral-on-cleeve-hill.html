The Victorian Funeral on Cleeve Hill

Summary

A teacher driving down Cleeve Hill witnessed a ghostly Victorian funeral procession complete with merry attendants crossing a field before it vanished into thin air. Months of unsettling dreams followed the bizarre encounter near St Peter's Church.

Details

While driving down the slopes of Cleeve Hill, a teacher noticed a funeral procession making its way across a field adjacent to the road rather than using the standard route. The smart cortege was accompanied by men in black attire who appeared unsteady on their feet and were laughing heartily—reflecting Victorian customs where mourners consumed spirits to ward off the dead. Intrigued, the teacher turned her car around to get a better view, but the entire procession had vanished in an impossibly short span of time. Believed to have originated near the overlooking St Peter's Church, the spectral sighting left the driver with lingering, unpleasant dreams of Victorian faces for months afterward.

#title
The Legend of the Devil's Chimney

#summary
Angered by the region's Christian churches, the Devil sat atop Leckhampton Hill and hurled rocks at churchgoers until the stones rebounded, burying him underground beneath a crooked limestone chimney.

#details
According to local legend, Old Nick was deeply provoked by the numerous churches dotting the surrounding landscape. Seeking to disrupt pilgrims on their way to prayer, he took up residence on Leckhampton Hill and pelted them with heavy stones. His wicked endeavor ultimately backfired when the hurled rocks ricocheted back toward him, burying him deep underground and piling up into a jagged pillar to trap him forever. Generations of visitors have since frequented the landmark, leaving coins at the top as insurance against the Devil's wrath.

#location
The Devil's Chimney is a distinctive limestone rock pillar located on the western edge of Leckhampton Hill, just south of Cheltenham off the B4070 road.

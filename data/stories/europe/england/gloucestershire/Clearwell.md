#title
Th' Old Mon of Clearwell Caves

#summary
An enigmatic phantom miner occasionally appears in the subterranean depths of Clearwell Caves to assist workers before vanishing without a trace.

#details
For centuries, iron ore miners working the labyrinthine tunnels of Clearwell Caves spoke of "Th' Old Mon," a spectral miner said to haunt the deep workings. Generations of miners shared accounts of seeing the mysterious figure moving through the shadows. This legend was seemingly reinforced in 1973 when television technicians filming a series in the caves ran short of electrical cable while attempting to rig equipment deep underground. An elderly miner unexpectedly appeared and directed them down an alternative, shorter route that solved their logistical problem. When the crew turned back moments later to express their gratitude, the man had completely vanished into thin air, leaving behind no explanation of how he could have left the dead-end chamber so fast.

#location
Clearwell Caves are located off the B4228 road near the village of Clearwell in the Forest of Dean, Gloucestershire.

#title
The Ghost Miner of Clearwell Caves

#summary
A shadowy, benevolent spirit known as "th'old mon" roams the ancient subterranean iron ore workings of Clearwell Caves, where he has been seen assisting lost visitors and technicians. This ghostly miner remains a fixture of local folklore in the Forest of Dean.

#details
Clearwell Caves in Gloucestershire are a vast network of natural caverns that have been extensively mined for iron ore over millennia. Within these deep, dark tunnels—often referred to as the "old men's workings"—miners and visitors have long whispered of encounters with a spectral figure known as "th'old mon." 

In 1973, while film technicians were underground rigging equipment and running electrical cables for a television production, they found themselves short of cable to reach their intended filming location. Suddenly, an old miner appeared and directed them through an alternative, shorter route. When the technicians turned back to thank him moments later, he had completely vanished without a trace. Over the decades, visitors walking through the labyrinthine depths have frequently reported feeling watched, and local lore holds that the phantom miner still guides lost souls and children to safety out of the cavernous maze.

#location
Clearwell Caves are situated underground off The Rocks in the village of Clearwell near Coleford, Gloucestershire.

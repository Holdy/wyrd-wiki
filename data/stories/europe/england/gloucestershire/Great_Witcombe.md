#title
The Shepherd and the Devil of Birdlip Hill

#summary
A quick-witted shepherd traveling up Birdlip Hill tricked the Devil into drinking foul sheep medicine instead of cider, causing the fiend to leap into the Witcombe reservoirs in agony.

#details
A shepherd walking back from Gloucester to Birdlip with medicine for a sick sheep knew that the Devil was waiting in the woods to catch travellers. To gain courage, he finished his flagon of cider and refilled the vessel with the noxious sheep medicine. As he passed Rowland Badger’s Knap by Black Dog Lane, the Devil jumped out and declared he would take the shepherd to hell. Feigning submission, the shepherd offered the fiend some spiced cider as a parting gesture. The Devil eagerly snatched the flagon and downed it in one draught, but the burning, foul-tasting medicine scorched his throat. Coughing and spluttering, the Devil leaped straight into the nearby Witcombe reservoirs to quench the agony, leaving the shepherd free to escape up the hill.

#location
Rowland Badger’s Knap and Black Dog Lane along Birdlip Hill within the parish of Great Witcombe, Gloucestershire.

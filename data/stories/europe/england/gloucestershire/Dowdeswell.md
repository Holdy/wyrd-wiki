#title
The Legend of the Dowdeswell Pike

#summary
A monstrous pike that evaded anglers for years in a private reservoir became a celebrated local legend after being found in 1896.

#details
In the late 19th century, the Dowdeswell Reservoir near Cheltenham was strictly managed as a private fishing reserve, inaccessible to the general public. Over time, tales grew among frustrated poachers and anglers about an elusive, gigantic fish dwelling in the deep waters that routinely snapped lines and escaped capture. On May 11, 1896, the legend materialized when an enormous pike measuring 4 feet 6 inches in length was found in the reservoir. The remarkable specimen was preserved and entered local lore, becoming an enduring symbol of Gloucestershire folklore.

#location
Dowdeswell Reservoir, situated on the western edge of the parish of Dowdeswell near Cheltenham, Gloucestershire.

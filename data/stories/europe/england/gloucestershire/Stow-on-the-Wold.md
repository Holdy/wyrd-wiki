#title
The Christmas Snow Foresters and the Talking Kitten

#summary
On Christmas Eve, a group of travellers lost in a blizzard near Idbury while heading toward Stow-on-the-Wold were harassed by ghostly snow foresters until a mysterious white kitten guided them to safety through the sound of church bells.

#details
On Christmas Eve in the 1860s, a family of travellers journeying from Burford in covered caravans attempted to reach Stow-on-the-Wold before nightfall. Caught in a severe blizzard, they lost their way in the woodland near Idbury. Their caravans were soon surrounded by malicious spirits known as "Snow Foresters," who whined, shrieked, and scratched at the glass. During the chaos, a small white kitten managed to slip inside through a slightly opened door. Remembering the old belief that animals could speak in rhyme on Christmas Eve, a boy in the caravan addressed the kitten, which responded with directions to safety: they were to follow the tweeting of birds and the ringing bells of the church in Stow. Following the birds and the sound of the bells of St Edward's Church, the travellers safely reached a farm on the outskirts of Stow-on-the-Wold, where shelter was provided until morning, by which time the white kitten had vanished.

#location
St Edward's Church in Stow-on-the-Wold, Gloucestershire, whose ringing bells guided the lost travellers out of the blizzard.

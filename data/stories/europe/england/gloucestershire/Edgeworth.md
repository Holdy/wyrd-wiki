#title
The Edgeworth Miracle

#summary
A young herdsman from Edgeworth who contracted severe leprosy after sleeping outdoors while tending his master's horses was miraculously cured after invoking St. Thomas Becket.

#details
Richard Sunieve, a herdsman serving a local knight in the village of Edgeworth, contracted leprosy after frequently sleeping out of doors while guarding his master's livestock. Over the course of eight years, the disease spread relentlessly across his entire body until he was covered in painful ulcers and forced into exile from the community, sustained only by his devoted mother who left food for him at the end of a long stick. Hearing of the fame of the martyr St. Thomas Becket, Richard wept because he was too weak to travel to Canterbury, but he earnestly invoked the saint and resolved to make the journey. Miraculously finding the strength to travel, he arrived at Canterbury Cathedral, kissed the sepulchre, and tasted the holy water from the crypt well. Instantly, the severe swellings vanished, and his diseased skin was restored to health, allowing him to return home to Edgeworth completely cured.

#location
St Mary’s Church in the village of Edgeworth, Gloucestershire, holds a medieval stained glass fragment and local heritage associations commemorating the miracle.

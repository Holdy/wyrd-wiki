#title
The Ghost of Shipton's Grave

#summary
An old coaching track passing through Barton End leads to Shipton's Grave at a remote crossroads, where the spirit of a locally executed man is said to haunt travelers.

#details
In earlier centuries, stagecoaches traveling between Bristol and Minchinhampton would turn off the A46 at Tiltups End to follow the old track passing Barton End toward Shipton's Grave, before descending through Hazel Wood toward the Weighbridge Inn. The crossroads and grave are named after a local man who was executed for stealing sheep to provide for his starving family. Over generations, locals and travelers navigating this isolated track high above Nailsworth have reported eerie encounters and sightings associated with the ghost of Shipton at the crossroads.

#location
The crossroads of Tetbury Lane near Barton End, high above Nailsworth in Gloucestershire.

#title
The Legend of Shipton's Grave

#summary
Shipton's Grave is a lonely roadside burial site at a crossroads near Barton End, named after a tragic local man executed for stealing sheep to feed his starving family. His memorial at the intersection has long remained a haunting landmark along the ancient Cotswold ridgeway tracks.

#details
Local legend and regional history record the tale behind Shipton's Grave, situated at the crossroads near Upper Barton End and Tetbury Lane. According to accounts passed down through generations, the grave marks the final resting place of a local man who was caught and executed for stealing sheep in order to provide food for his starving family during desperate times. Because executed criminals were traditionally barred from burial in consecrated churchyards, he was interred directly at the remote roadside crossroads. Stagecoaches and travelers navigating the old pack-horse tracks between Tiltups End, Barton End, and Chavenage frequently passed the lonely gravesite, cementing its solemn place in local folklore.

#location
Shipton's Grave is located at the crossroads of Tetbury Lane near Upper Barton End, just south of Nailsworth in Gloucestershire.

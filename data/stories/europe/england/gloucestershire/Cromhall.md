#title
The Monks' Hidden Passage of Cromhall

#summary
Local tradition maintains that a secret underground tunnel connects St. Andrew's Churchyard to Abbotside, constructed so that inhabitants could evade Tudor authorities and conceal church wealth.

#details
Generations of villagers have passed down the tale of a subterranean passage running beneath the fields between St. Andrew's Church and the historic grange at Abbotside. According to the legend, during the turbulent era of Henry VIII and the dissolution of religious houses, monks or stewards used the tunnel to escape royal agents and spirit away hidden stashes of gold and valuables. While historical records indicate that Abbotside was an abbey farm rather than a monastic house, physical memories of old cellars and foundations on the hillside have kept the legend alive.

#location
St. Andrew's Church is located on Rectory Lane in the village of Cromhall, South Gloucestershire.

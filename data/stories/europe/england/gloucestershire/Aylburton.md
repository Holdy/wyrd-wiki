#title
The Devil's Lap of Stones

#summary
Local legend tells of ancient stone cairns near Aylburton where the Devil dropped his heavy load of rocks after being startled by a crowing rooster.

#details
Deep in the woods near Camp Hill and Aylburton, there are three prehistoric round barrows or cairns known traditionally as the Devil's Lap of Stones. According to local folklore, these massive piles of moss-covered stones were created when the Devil stayed out too late one morning and was suddenly surprised by the crowing of a cock, causing him to drop his enormous lapful of stones right where he stood.

#location
The prehistoric cairns known as the Devil's Lap of Stones in Barbadoes Wood near Camp Hill and Aylburton, Gloucestershire.

#title
The Ghost of Dame Creswyke at the Manor House Hotel

#summary
In the historic market town of Moreton-in-Marsh, the 16th-century Manor House Hotel is said to be haunted by the spirit of Dame Creswyke, who was murdered within the building in the late 17th century.

#details
Dating back to the 16th century, the Manor House Hotel on the High Street in Moreton-in-Marsh has a long history and was once owned for over a century by the Creswykes, a wealthy family of local landowners. According to local legend, toward the end of the 17th century, Dame Creswyke met a gruesome end when she was murdered inside the house. Her restless spirit allegedly continued to linger in the building across the centuries, with witnesses reporting various paranormal encounters. The most notable modern sighting occurred in 1987, when her ghostly apparition distinctly materialized right outside the front door of the hotel.

#location
The Manor House Hotel is situated on the High Street in the market town of Moreton-in-Marsh, Gloucestershire.

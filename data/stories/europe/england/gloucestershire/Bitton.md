#title
The Grey Lady of St Mary's

#summary
St Mary's Church and the nearby Grange in Bitton are reputed to be haunted by the apparition of a Grey Lady.

#details
For generations, locals and churchgoers have passed down accounts of a mysterious Grey Lady haunting the historic parish church of St Mary's and the adjacent Grange (the former rectory). Witnesses over the years—including a churchwarden's grandmother in the early 20th century and residents during the Second World War—have reported seeing the spectral figure moving near the choir vestry before drifting off towards the Grange. Though sightings have become rare in recent decades, the legend remains a noted piece of local lore attached to the ancient buildings.

#location
St Mary's Church and the Grange, Church Lane, Bitton, South Gloucestershire, UK.

#title
The Grey Lady of St Mary's Church

#summary
St Mary's Church and the nearby Grange in Bitton are reputed to be haunted by a spectral Grey Lady who materializes near the choir vestry and vanishes toward the historic rectory.

#details
The village church of St Mary in Bitton, Gloucestershire, alongside the neighbouring house known as the Grange (historically the prebendal manor house and rectory), has long been associated with the apparition of a Grey Lady. According to parish records, the phantom figure appears near the choir vestry within the church before drifting off toward the Grange. Documented encounters include a sighting in the early 1900s by the grandmother of a churchwarden, as well as further sightings reported within the Grange during the Second World War.

#location
St Mary's Church is located on Church Road in the village of Bitton, South Gloucestershire.

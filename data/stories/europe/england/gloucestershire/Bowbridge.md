#title
The Bewitched Boatwoman of Bowbridge

#summary
The ghostly reflection of a woman rowing a boat is said to appear on the surface of the canal at Bowbridge even when the water is entirely still. Local legend holds that she is the spirit of a boatwoman doomed to endlessly search the waters for her lost love.

#details
Along the stretch of the canal passing through Bowbridge on the Stroudwater Navigation, locals and travellers have long spoken of a strange and eerie phenomenon known as the Bewitched Boatwoman. On calm, quiet evenings when the water's surface is completely unruffled and still, the clear reflection of a woman rowing a boat will suddenly materialize upon the water. Those who have witnessed the apparition describe how the phantom reflection mirrors the rhythmic motion of oars cutting through unseen currents, despite no physical boat or person being present on the surface. Folklore maintains that the apparition is the restless spirit of a boatwoman who lost her beloved to the perilous waters of the canal, and her phantom reflection continues its eternal search across the mirror-like surface of the waterway.

#location
The canal passing through Bowbridge, on the outskirts of Stroud, Gloucestershire.

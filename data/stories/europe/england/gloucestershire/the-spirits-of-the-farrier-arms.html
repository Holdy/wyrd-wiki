    The Spirits of the Farrier Arms

    Summary

    The historic Farrier Arms in Todenham is said to be haunted by the apparitions of two small white dogs and a monk. Witnesses have reported seeing the phantom dogs ascending the staircase before vanishing, as well as a monk standing at the foot of a bed in one of the rooms.

    Details

    For generations, the Farrier Arms—an 18th-century building situated in the village of Todenham—has been associated with paranormal activity and local ghostly lore. Two distinct spectral phenomena have been reported by multiple visitors and residents over the years. The first involves a pair of small white dogs that are frequently seen climbing the pub's staircase, only to mysteriously vanish midway up. The second apparition is that of a monk who manifests at the foot of the bed in a specific upstairs room, appearing with consistent and matching descriptions across different accounts.

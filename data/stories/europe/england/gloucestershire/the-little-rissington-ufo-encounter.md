#title
The Little Rissington UFO Encounter

#summary
In October 1952, two military pilots flying out of RAF Little Rissington encountered three fast-moving, saucer-shaped anomalies in the skies above Gloucestershire.

#details
On the afternoon of 21 October 1952, Flight Lieutenant Michael Swiney and Royal Navy Lieutenant David Crofts took off on a training flight from RAF Little Rissington aboard a Gloster Meteor T.7 aircraft. As they climbed through a layer of cloud at 12,000 feet, they spotted three perfectly circular, white saucer-shaped objects positioned at approximately 35,000 feet. Flt Lt Swiney immediately radioed ground control at RAF Sopley and aborted the training mission. While observing the anomalies, the pilots watched as the objects crossed their starboard flight path and vanished in a fraction of a second. Radar stations in Gloucestershire and Wiltshire subsequently tracked the objects moving at extreme speeds, prompting RAF Fighter Command to scramble interceptor jets to the area.

#location
RAF Little Rissington is an airfield located just east of the village of Little Rissington in the Cotswold District of Gloucestershire.

#title
The Witch of Berkeley

#summary
An aging woman who practiced dark arts foresaw her death, and despite her children's desperate attempts to protect her corpse in a chained sarcophagus inside the church, demons shattered her tomb and dragged her away on a barbed stallion.

#details
In the town of Berkeley, a wealthy woman long accustomed to wickedness and the practice of augury was startled one day when her pet jackdaw uttered a chilling omen. Moments later, a messenger arrived with the tragic news that her son and his entire family had died. Knowing her life had reached its end and fearing eternal damnation, she summoned her remaining children—a monk and a nun—to her bedside. Confessing her pact with dark forces, she begged them to try to mitigate her punishment by sewing her corpse into the hide of a deer, placing it inside a stone sarcophagus sealed with lead and iron, binding the tomb with three heavy iron chains, and having clerics sing fifty psalms and say daily masses for three nights prior to burial.

Her children followed her instructions with great care, but her lifelong wickedness proved too strong for their prayers. On the first two nights, demons breached the church and snapped the outer chains. On the third night, the monastery shook violently as a colossal demon shattered the heavy entrance door to pieces, broke the final iron chain as if it were cord, and ripped the coffin lid away. Dragging the woman out into the night, the demons strapped her onto a monstrous black stallion whose back was covered in sharp iron barbs, vanishing into the darkness as her shrieks of terror echoed for miles.

#location
St Mary's Church stands in the historic town of Berkeley, Gloucestershire.

#title
The Witch of Berkeley

#summary
When a notoriously wicked woman in Berkeley died after a lifetime of practicing dark arts, her children tried in vain to protect her corpse from demonic forces using deer skin, heavy iron chains, and church vigils, only for a horde of demons to smash open her tomb and drag her away on a barbed horse.

#details
In 11th-century Berkeley, a wealthy woman known for her indulgence in witchcraft and debauchery received a dreadful omen when her pet jackdaw spoke with human speech, warning her that her time was short. Shortly thereafter, word arrived that her son and his entire family had perished. Knowing her own death was near, she summoned her surviving children—a monk and a nun—and confessed her alliance with dark powers. Terrified that demons would seize her body and soul, she begged her children to protect her corpse by sewing it into the hide of a deer, sealing it within a stone sarcophagus bound by three massive iron chains, and having priests chant psalms over her for three nights before burial. Despite their diligent efforts, demonic forces violently battered the church doors on the first two nights. On the third night, a towering, terrifying demon shattered the heavy doors, snapped the iron chains as if they were flax, and dragged the woman from her coffin out of the church, placing her upon a fierce black horse bristling with iron barbs before vanishing into the night amid her desperate cries for mercy.

#location
St Mary's Church in the town of Berkeley, Gloucestershire, is the historic site where the woman's body was entombed and ultimately snatched away by demons.

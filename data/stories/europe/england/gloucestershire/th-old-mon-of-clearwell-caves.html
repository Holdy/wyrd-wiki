Th' Old Mon of Clearwell Caves

Summary

An enigmatic phantom miner occasionally appears in the subterranean depths of Clearwell Caves to assist workers before vanishing without a trace.

Details

For centuries, iron ore miners working the labyrinthine tunnels of Clearwell Caves spoke of "Th' Old Mon," a spectral miner said to haunt the deep workings. Generations of miners shared accounts of seeing the mysterious figure moving through the shadows. This legend was seemingly reinforced in 1973 when television technicians filming a series in the caves ran short of electrical cable while attempting to rig equipment deep underground. An elderly miner unexpectedly appeared and directed them down an alternative, shorter route that solved their logistical problem. When the crew turned back moments later to express their gratitude, the man had completely vanished into thin air, leaving behind no explanation of how he could have left the dead-end chamber so fast.

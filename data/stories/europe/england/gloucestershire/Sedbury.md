#title
The Devil's Pulpit

#summary
Perched high on a limestone outcrop overlooking the Wye Valley, the Devil used a rocky vantage point to whisper tempting and deceitful words to the monks of Tintern Abbey.

#details
According to local folklore, the Devil chose a prominent limestone pillar high above the River Wye—now known as the Devil's Pulpit—to torment the monks of Tintern Abbey. Positioned directly opposite the abbey's skeletal arches, he would broadcast honeyed, mischievous words designed to tempt the holy men into abandoning their vows and spiritual discipline. The dramatic rock formation, left behind as an outlier during the ancient construction of Offa's Dyke, became permanently associated with this malevolent spirit, retaining a haunting atmosphere of ancient myth and lingering unease.

#location
The Devil's Pulpit is a limestone rock outcrop perched high above the River Wye in Tidenham parish near Sedbury, Gloucestershire.

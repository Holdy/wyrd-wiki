#title
The Doctor's Blizzard Ride

#summary
Faced with parental disapproval, the pioneer of vaccination risked his life riding through a fierce winter blizzard to reach his beloved in the Cotswolds. The harrowing journey finally melted her family's resolve, allowing their romance to triumph.

#details
As the local doctor in nearby Berkeley, Edward Jenner fell deeply in love with Catharine Kingscote. However, her parents strongly opposed the match and forbade the courtship. Determined to see her, Jenner braved a severe blizzard, riding on horseback up through the steep Cotswold landscape to the village. The perilous journey nearly cost him his life in the freezing snow and biting winds. Moved by his harrowing ordeal and steadfast devotion, her parents finally relented, and Edward and Catharine were married in 1788 at Kingscote Church.

#location
Kingscote Church is situated within the historic parish of Kingscote and Bagpath in the Cotswold district of Gloucestershire.

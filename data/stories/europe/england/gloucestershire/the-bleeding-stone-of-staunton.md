#title
The Bleeding Stone of Staunton

#summary
An ancient standing stone positioned near the Gloucestershire village of Staunton is said to miraculously bleed if pricked with a pin at the stroke of midnight.

#details
Standing between seven and eight feet tall, the Staunton Longstone is a prehistoric unhewn monolith located right beside the A4136 road near Staunton in the Forest of Dean. Local folklore surrounding the megalith, which dates back centuries and was recorded in late 19th-century archaeological journals, attributes a macabre and mysterious life to the stone. According to tradition, the massive rock is not entirely inanimate; if a person pricks the surface of the standing stone with a pin at precisely midnight, actual blood will ooze from the stone. 

#location
The Staunton Longstone stands immediately off the north side of the A4136 road, approximately one mile from the village of Staunton in the Forest of Dean, Gloucestershire.

    The Monastic Phantoms of Newark Park

    Summary

    Local legend maintains that Newark Park in Ozleworth is haunted by the restless spirits of medieval monks whose abbey stones were repurposed to build the 16th-century hunting lodge. Visitors and staff have long reported disembodied voices, phantom footsteps, and sightings of hooded figures roaming the historic estate.

    Details

    When Sir Nicholas Poyntz constructed Newark Park as a Tudor hunting lodge in 1550 on the edge of the Ozleworth valley, he utilized stone salvaged from the recently dissolved Kingswood Abbey. According to local folklore, the displacement of these sacred building materials brought the restless spirits of the abbey's monks along with them. Over the centuries, residents and staff—including long-serving estate managers—have experienced frequent paranormal phenomena, hearing disembodied chants and footsteps, and occasionally spotting small, robed phantom figures on the upper floors and grounds of the house.

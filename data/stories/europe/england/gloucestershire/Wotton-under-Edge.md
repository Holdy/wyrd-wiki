#title
The Hauntings of the Ancient Ram Inn

#summary
The Ancient Ram Inn in Wotton-under-Edge is renowned as one of England's most haunted houses, steeped in centuries of grim legends, ritual sites, and spectral encounters.

#details
Dating back to the 12th century, the Ancient Ram Inn in Wotton-under-Edge has long been associated with dark local folklore, including claims of pagan burial sites, child sacrifice, and highwaymen hideouts. Over the centuries, visitors and residents have reported terrifying supernatural encounters throughout the building. The ground-floor "Men's Kitchen," built over an ancient pagan burial ground, is frequently disturbed by the sound of a crying baby. On the first floor, the notorious Bishop's Room is said to be haunted by a ghostly cavalier, phantom monks, and malevolent entities such as an incubus or succubus, with visitors experiencing extreme oppression, bitter cold, and physical assaults by unseen forces. In the attic above, heavy footsteps and dragging sounds are heard, echoing the tragic legend of an innkeeper's daughter murdered there in the 16th century.

#location
The Ancient Ram Inn is located on Church Street in Wotton-under-Edge, Gloucestershire.

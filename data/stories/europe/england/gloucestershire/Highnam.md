#title
The Legend of the Dog at Over

#summary
When two robbers viciously attacked a remote farmhouse, they left behind a mysterious black dog that led the grieving farmer straight to their hideout at a local inn, resulting in the criminals' capture and execution.

#details
Around a century before Ploddy House farm went on the market in 1962, two men broke into the property demanding money. When the farmer’s wife and maidservant refused, the intruders attacked both women with knives. Returning home to find the devastating scene, the farmer discovered a black dog left behind by the attackers. Setting the animal free, he mounted his horse and tracked the dog across the countryside until it led him to the inn at Over, which subsequently became known as the Dog Inn. There, he found the two culprits, who were later tried and hanged in Gloucester.

#location
The Dog Inn was located at Over within the parish of Highnam in Gloucestershire.

#title
The Grey Lady of Slowwe House

#summary
During renovations in the 1970s, residents of Slowwe House repeatedly encountered the spectral apparition of a woman in a grey dress and bonnet. The phantom was witnessed moving across the courtyard, wandering the upper landing, and standing silently in the guest bedroom.

#details
Slowwe House on Silver Street in Arlingham is an ancient property with a history stretching back to the Domesday Book, having once served as a hunting lodge for Berkeley Castle. In the 1970s, the Lacey family moved into the rear wing of the tumbledown building to undertake extensive renovations. Soon after beginning work, strange phenomena occurred: lights were repeatedly switched on after being turned off, windows opened and closed on their own, and hung pictures continually fell off the walls. Mrs Lacey eventually witnessed a ghostly figure of a woman wearing a long grey dress and a grey bonnet walking across the courtyard before vanishing into thin air. The entity, dubbed the Grey Lady, was subsequently seen by family members and guests in the kitchen, along the upstairs landing, and standing motionless in the guest bedroom.

#location
Slowwe House is located on Silver Street in the village of Arlingham, Gloucestershire.

#title
The Phantom Funeral at Court House

#summary
In May 1757, members of the Yate family residing at Court House witnessed a spectral funeral procession making its way up the avenue. Exactly one year later, the last male heir of the family passed away.

#details
On May 24, 1757, residents of Court House in Arlingham belonging to the Yate family observed a dark and eerie premonition. A ghostly funeral procession was seen making its way up the avenue toward the house. Exactly one year to the day after this phantom manifestation, the last male heir of the Yate family died, leading to the ancient estate passing out of the family's hands.

#location
Court House is situated in the village of Arlingham, Gloucestershire.

#title
The Vicar's Housekeeper at St Mary the Virgin Church

#summary
While visiting St Mary the Virgin Church in June 1902 to inspect church carvings, a visitor encountered an elderly woman sitting in a front pew who vanished into thin air. Local inquiries revealed the apparition to be the ghost of Mrs Budge, a former vicar's housekeeper.

#details
In June 1902, Alice Godfrey entered St Mary the Virgin Church in Arlingham to examine some architectural carvings. Inside, she noticed an elderly woman sitting on the front pew on the left-hand side wearing a black gown and a white lace cap. As Alice approached to ask directions to the carvings, the woman looked up, smiled, and completely vanished. Shaken, Alice fled the church and spoke with a gardener working in the graveyard, who informed her that she had seen "Old Mrs Budge," the deceased former housekeeper of the vicar.

#location
St Mary the Virgin Church is located in the parish of Arlingham, Gloucestershire.

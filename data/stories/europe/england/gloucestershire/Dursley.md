#title
The Phantom Inn of Downham Hill

#summary
A traveller caught in a snowstorm north of Dursley spent the night at a warm, mysterious inn, only to return the next day and discover nothing remained except his footprints and two guineas in the snow.

#details
During a heavy snowstorm in the wolds north of Dursley, a traveller was delighted to spot the bright lights of an inn along the road. An old groom attended to his horse, and a servant in bright green livery showed him to a warm, comfortable bedroom where he was well fed and cared for. Waking before dawn and finding no one about, he left two guineas on the table and rode off towards Stroud. When he mentioned his stay at the splendid inn to his friends in Stroud, they insisted no such building existed. Together, they rode back to investigate and found only his horse's tracks in the snow alongside two gleaming guineas lying on the ground. A knowledgeable friend explained that the phantom fairy inn only appeared on stormy nights, never accepted lasting payment, and vanished before cockcrow.

#location
Downham Hill, situated north of Dursley and southwest of Uley Bury in Gloucestershire.

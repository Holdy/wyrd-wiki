#title
The Wandering Stones of Churchdown Hill

#summary
When parishioners attempted to build a church on a convenient lowland site, nocturnal forces repeatedly transported all the construction materials to the steep summit of the hill. Interpreting this as a divine mandate, the builders abandoned the original plan and erected St. Bartholomew's Church on the heights.

#details
Long ago, when plans were made to establish the parish church in Churchdown, the local community selected an easily accessible and convenient plot of land in the village below. However, every morning the builders returned to work only to find that all the stone, timber, and tools gathered during the previous day had vanished. Upon investigation, it was discovered that every single piece of building material had been mysteriously carried up the steep, tortuous slopes to the very top of Churchdown Hill overnight. After this nocturnal relocation happened repeatedly, the townspeople realized it was a supernatural intimation that the church was not meant to be built in the village. Yielding to the divine will, they hauled the materials no further and constructed St. Bartholomew's Church on the summit of the hill.

#location
St. Bartholomew's Church is located on the summit of Churchdown Hill (also known as Chosen Hill) in Churchdown, Gloucestershire.

#title
The Wandering Stones of Churchdown Hill

#summary
According to local legend, the ancient church of St Bartholomew was originally intended to be built on a lower, more accessible site, but unseen forces persistently moved the construction materials to the summit of Chosen Hill overnight.

#details
Perched high on the steep summit of Chosen Hill (also known as Churchdown Hill) in Gloucestershire stands the ancient church of St Bartholomew, dating back to the 12th century. The difficult, tortuous ascent to the top has long puzzled visitors who wonder why such a remote and elevated location was chosen for a parish church. Local folklore explains this through a miraculous foundation legend: when construction of the church first began, builders selected a much more convenient and accessible spot of ground at the base of the hill. However, every night, the building materials placed during the day were mysteriously carried away and deposited at the very top of the hill. Interpreting this nightly removal as a clear supernatural intimation of divine will, the builders abandoned the lower site and erected St Bartholomew's Church on the summit.

#location
St Bartholomew's Church on the summit of Chosen Hill (Churchdown Hill) in Churchdown, Gloucestershire.

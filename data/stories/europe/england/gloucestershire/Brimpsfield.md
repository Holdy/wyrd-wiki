#title
The Phantom People of Buckholt Wood

#summary
Following the 19th-century excavation of ancient burial mounds in Brimpsfield, villagers in the surrounding woods began reporting terrifying encounters with tattooed, leather-clad prehistoric figures who could no longer rest in peace.

#details
In July 1880, archaeologist G.B. Witts investigated Buck Barrow in Brimpsfield and stumbled upon a hidden long barrow nearby in Buckholt Wood, unearthing the skeletal remains of an adolescent and numerous other ancient individuals. Soon after the graves were disturbed, unsettling rumours spread across the parishes of Birdlip and Cranham. Strange figures were glimpsed lingering in the trees, culminating in a chilling encounter when a young woman walking from Birdlip to Cranham passed West Tump. She came face-to-face with beings unlike anyone she had ever seen: they wore long leather cloaks, their faces were heavily tattooed, and the men carried long spears tipped with leaf-shaped stone. These phantom prehistoric people were subsequently sighted multiple times, with local folk blaming the archaeologists for raising the dead and trapping their spirits in the woods.

#location
West Tump long barrow in Buckholt Wood, Brimpsfield, Gloucestershire.

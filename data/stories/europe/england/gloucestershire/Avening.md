#title
The Distressed Woman of the Avening Road

#summary
A motorist driving late at night near Minchinhampton Golf Club encountered a distressed woman attempting to flag him down on the road between Avening and Minchinhampton, only for her to vanish into thin air as he stopped to help.

#details
Local folklore long held that the road between Avening and Minchinhampton was haunted by the earthbound spirit of someone who had died by suicide. In November 2013, this local legend gained chilling credibility when a motorist experienced a strange encounter along that very stretch. Driving home from Malmesbury at around 1:00 AM on a clear, cold winter night under ambient moonlight, the driver approached the area near Minchinhampton Golf Club. Suddenly, he spotted a slim, middle-aged woman wearing only a dress—completely unsuited for the freezing weather—standing by the roadside and trying desperately to flag him down while looking profoundly distressed. Reacting immediately, he pulled his car over and rolled down his window to ask if she needed assistance. The moment he stopped, the woman vanished completely, leaving behind an empty road and a profound impression of the supernatural.

#location
The road between Avening and Minchinhampton, specifically near the Minchinhampton Golf Club in Gloucestershire.

#title
The Strolling Stones of Avening

#summary
The ancient Tinglestone standing atop its Neolithic long barrow in Avening parish is said to come alive at midnight, uprooting itself and strolling around the field under the cover of darkness. Local tradition also attributes peculiar magical qualities to the stones, warning of their restless nocturnal movements.

#details
Deep within the rural parish of Avening, Gloucestershire, stands the Tinglestone—a solitary oolitic limestone menhir crowning a ruined Neolithic long barrow. According to local folklore passed down through generations, this ancient monument does not remain static. Like several legendary megaliths in the surrounding region, the Tinglestone is said to break free from the earth at the stroke of midnight, uprooting itself and strolling around the field. In addition to its nocturnal wanderings, the site is tied to enduring local legends that connect the mysterious stone to the ancient landscape and its superstitious past.

#location
The Tinglestone stands atop a long barrow located within the Gatcombe Park estate grounds in the parish of Avening, Gloucestershire.

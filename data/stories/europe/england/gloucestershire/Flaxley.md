#title
The Lost Fiddler of the Flaxley Abbey Tunnel

#summary
A local fiddler entered a subterranean passage running between Old Grange and Flaxley Abbey to test the route while playing music for his friends above, but vanished without a trace when his tune abruptly stopped near Fiddler's Copse.

#details
Local tradition in the Forest of Dean speaks of a secret tunnel that allegedly connected a historic house known as Old Grange to Flaxley Abbey, approximately a mile away. While portions of this subterranean structure genuinely existed and served as a daring haunt for local children—who attributed its origins variously to medieval monks or Royalists—it became the setting for a chilling disappearance. According to the legend, a local fiddler decided to walk the length of the underground passage while playing his fiddle, allowing his friends to follow his progress from above ground by listening to the music filtering up through the earth. As they walked near a small wood between Flaxley and Littledean, the music suddenly stopped mid-tune. The fiddler never emerged from the tunnel and was never seen again, leaving his name immortalized in the woodland known ever since as Fiddler's Copse.

#location
The tunnel route running between Old Grange and Flaxley Abbey, passing near Fiddler's Copse between Flaxley and Littledean in Gloucestershire.

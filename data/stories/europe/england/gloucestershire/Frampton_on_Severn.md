#title
The Phantom of The Perry Way

#summary
An apparition of an old, tall bearded man has repeatedly been seen on The Perry Way in Frampton-on-Severn since the late 1960s after first appearing inside a local family home.

#details
Beginning over forty years ago, an old, tall bearded man with short dark hair and grey patches first appeared inside a family home on The Perry Way in Frampton-on-Severn, shouting at children and demanding that they leave "his house." Since that initial encounter, numerous people have witnessed a man matching the exact same description on the road directly outside the property. Witnesses note that he appears completely legitimate and corporeal before suddenly vanishing into thin air.

#location
The Perry Way (B4071) in Frampton-on-Severn, Gloucestershire.

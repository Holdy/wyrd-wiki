#title
The Shepherd and the Devil of Dog Lane

#summary
Walking up Dog Lane toward Birdlip after visiting Little Witcombe, a shepherd encounters the Devil and tricks him into drinking potent sheep medicine mixed with ale.

#details
A shepherd had walked to Little Witcombe to purchase medicine for a sick sheep from the landlady, who was skilled in the still room. As he carried the medicine and a flask of ale up Dog Lane toward Birdlip, a shadowy, horned figure emerged from the gloom. Realizing he had met the Devil, the quick-thinking shepherd poured a dose of the harsh sheep medicine into his ale flask and handed it over when the creature demanded a drink. The Devil took a massive draught, but the bitter medicine struck the back of his throat so fiercely that he let out a roar heard as far away as Cheltenham. Taking advantage of the distraction, the shepherd fled up the hill and escaped safely.

#location
Dog Lane, the route connecting Little Witcombe and Birdlip in Gloucestershire, UK.

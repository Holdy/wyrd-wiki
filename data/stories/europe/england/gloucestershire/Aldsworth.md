#title
The Phantom Coach of Lodge Park

#summary
A spectral coach and team of horses driven by Sir John Dutton are said to careen through Lodge Park and Larkethill Wood in Aldsworth. The apparition is linked to the 16th-century estate's history and its former owner, nicknamed "Crump."

#details
Local legend in Aldsworth, Gloucestershire, tells of a phantom coach and ethereal horses that sporadically appear near Lodge Park and Larkethill Wood. The apparition is attributed to Sir John Dutton, a historical figure who built Lodge Park in the 16th century as a hunting lodge and was nicknamed "Crump" on account of his hunched back. Witnesses have reported seeing the spectral carriage careen erratically through the park gates and along the woodland edges. Alongside the phantom coach, local accounts also mention sightings of a ghostly white stag roaming the historic parkland.

#location
Lodge Park and Larkethill Wood are situated in Aldsworth, Gloucestershire, UK.

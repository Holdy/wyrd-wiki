#title
The Wandering Stones of Churchdown Hill

#summary
According to local legend, the ancient church of St Bartholomew was originally intended to be built on a lower, more accessible site, but unseen forces persistently moved the construction materials to the summit of Chosen Hill overnight.

#details
Perched high on the steep summit of Chosen Hill (also known as Churchdown Hill) in Gloucestershire stands the ancient church of St Bartholomew, dating back to the 12th century. The difficult, tortuous ascent to the top has long puzzled visitors who wonder why such a remote and elevated location was chosen for a parish church. Local folklore explains this through a miraculous foundation legend: when construction of the church first began, builders selected a much more convenient and accessible spot of ground at the base of the hill. However, every night, the building materials placed during the day were mysteriously carried away and deposited at the very top of the hill. Interpreting this nightly removal as a clear supernatural intimation of divine will, the builders abandoned the lower site and erected St Bartholomew's Church on the summit.

#location
St Bartholomew's Church on the summit of Chosen Hill (Churchdown Hill) in Churchdown, Gloucestershire.

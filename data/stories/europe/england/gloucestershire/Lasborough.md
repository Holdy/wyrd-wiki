#title
The Fatal Flame of Lasborough Park

#summary
While relaxing in the library of Lasborough Park after dinner, a widow's cap caught fire from a nearby candle, engulfing her dress in flames. Despite efforts to save her, she succumbed to her catastrophic burns the following day.

#details
In November 1833, Mrs. Caroline Walrond, the widow of the late Joseph Lyons Walrond, was residing at Lasborough Park. Following a sumptuous evening dinner, she was sitting quietly in the house's library. A candle flickered nearby, and tragically, the flame caught the edge of her lace cap. In a panicked attempt to beat out the ignition, she fanned the flames, causing her dress to rapidly catch fire and turn into a blazing ball of fire. Though the fire was extinguished, her body was severely charred, and she passed away from her injuries the next day.

#location
The library inside Lasborough Park (Lasborough House), a historic country house located in the parish of Westonbirt with Lasborough, near Tetbury, Gloucestershire.

#title
The Haunting and Exorcism at All Saints' Church

#summary
In the late 1970s, All Saints' Church in Down Ampney was plagued by unexplained phenomena, including self-slamming doors, flying objects, and a heavy spiritual chill that prompted an official exorcism.

#details
During the late 1970s, the rector of All Saints' Church in Down Ampney, Major the Reverend Robert Nesham, experienced an overwhelming "spiritual chill" whenever he entered the church after dark, finding he could only walk inside while reciting the Lord's Prayer. Other locals and workmen reported similar unease; a long-serving churchwarden refused to enter after nightfall, and workmen repairing the building heard the vestry door slam shut on its own alongside phantom footsteps. On another occasion, two visitors kneeling in prayer on the church floor were nearly struck by a vase that suddenly flew off the rood screen. Seeking answers, a diocesan exorcist and a Christian medium investigated the church, ultimately identifying two lingering spirits: the ghost of a member of the historic Hungerford family who had once been prominent in the area, and the spirit of an alleged witch. Both entities were subsequently cleared from the building.

#location
All Saints' Church in the village of Down Ampney, Gloucestershire.

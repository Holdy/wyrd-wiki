#title
The Phantom Horsewoman of Stoke Park

#summary
Seventeen-year-old Lady Elizabeth Somerset, who tragically died after falling from her horse in 1760, is said to haunt the grounds of Stoke Park and the Dower House. Visitors and conservation workers frequently report hearing the phantom sound of galloping hooves echoing through the estate's woodlands where no horses have ridden for decades.

#details
In 1760, Lady Elizabeth Somerset, the 17-year-old daughter of the 4th Duke of Beaufort, met a tragic end when she suffered a fatal riding accident on the Stoke Park estate and broke her neck. In the centuries since, her restless spirit has reportedly lingered across the parklands and around the prominent yellow Dower House that overlooks the M32 motorway. Walkers and estate workers have recounted hearing the vivid, startling sound of thumping hooves and galloping horses charging directly behind them in the wooded areas, despite horses having been absent from the grounds for generations. A memorial obelisk still stands upon the hill where the tragic accident took place.

#location
The Dower House and Stoke Park estate are located on Purdown overlooking the M32 motorway on the border of Stoke Gifford and Bristol.

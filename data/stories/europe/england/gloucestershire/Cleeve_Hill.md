#title
The Phantom Cortege of Cleeve Hill

#summary
While driving down from Cleeve Hill, a motorist witnessed a peculiar Victorian funeral procession traversing a field before it abruptly and impossibly vanished.

#details
A teacher driving down from Cleeve Hill in Gloucestershire noticed an unusual funeral procession making its way across a field adjacent to the road rather than using the proper thoroughfare. The cortege was accompanied by men dressed in black who appeared tipsy and were laughing loudly. Intrigued by the strange sight, she turned her car around to get a closer look, but by the time she returned, the entire procession had completely disappeared in an impossibly brief span of time. Local folklore notes that Victorian funeral parties sometimes took unconventional routes across fields to prevent restless spirits from finding their way back home, and mourners occasionally imbibed heavily, suggesting she had encountered a spectral time-slip overlooked by nearby St Peter's Church.

#location
The road descending from Cleeve Hill near Cheltenham, Gloucestershire, passing near St Peter's Church.

#upstream-source
White, D. Haunted Cheltenham, History Press, 2010.

#reference
https://motivationtopublication.com/2013/06/07/holy-but-haunted/

#title
The Victorian Funeral on Cleeve Hill

#summary
A teacher driving down Cleeve Hill witnessed a ghostly Victorian funeral procession complete with merry attendants crossing a field before it vanished into thin air. Months of unsettling dreams followed the bizarre encounter near St Peter's Church.

#details
While driving down the slopes of Cleeve Hill, a teacher noticed a funeral procession making its way across a field adjacent to the road rather than using the standard route. The smart cortege was accompanied by men in black attire who appeared unsteady on their feet and were laughing heartily—reflecting Victorian customs where mourners consumed spirits to ward off the dead. Intrigued, the teacher turned her car around to get a better view, but the entire procession had vanished in an impossibly short span of time. Believed to have originated near the overlooking St Peter's Church, the spectral sighting left the driver with lingering, unpleasant dreams of Victorian faces for months afterward.

#location
The haunting occurred on the slopes of Cleeve Hill, near St Peter's Church overlooking Cheltenham in Gloucestershire.

#upstream-source
Peter Ackroyd, *The English Ghost* (Vintage, 2011) and David White, *Haunted Cheltenham* (History Press, 2010).

https://motivationtopublication.com/2013/06/07/holy-but-haunted/

#title
The Cheltenham Road Phantom Child

#summary
While driving through Longlevens in late autumn 1969, a young couple experienced a terrifying phantom collision with a child that vanished without a trace, leaving their car entirely undamaged.

#details
In late autumn 1969, at approximately 11:30 PM, a young courting couple were driving from Oxstalls in Gloucester toward Churchdown along Cheltenham Road. As they passed Anderson's garage in the Longlevens suburb, a child of roughly twelve years old suddenly appeared directly in front of their windscreen. The passenger screamed as the driver slammed on the brakes, and both distinctively felt the vehicle run over a body. Shaken and panicked, the driver got out of the car to find the child and call an ambulance, but discovered no victim on the road. Inspection of the front of the car revealed zero dents, scratches, or broken lights, and subsequent searches of the area turned up no trace of the child.

#location
Cheltenham Road, near the former site of Anderson's Garage (now the BP Garage) in Longlevens, Gloucester, Gloucestershire.

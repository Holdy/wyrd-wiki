#title
The Vicar's Housekeeper at St Mary the Virgin Church

#summary
While visiting St Mary the Virgin Church in June 1902 to inspect church carvings, a visitor encountered an elderly woman sitting in a front pew who vanished into thin air. Local inquiries revealed the apparition to be the ghost of Mrs Budge, a former vicar's housekeeper.

#details
In June 1902, Alice Godfrey entered St Mary the Virgin Church in Arlingham to examine some architectural carvings. Inside, she noticed an elderly woman sitting on the front pew on the left-hand side wearing a black gown and a white lace cap. As Alice approached to ask directions to the carvings, the woman looked up, smiled, and completely vanished. Shaken, Alice fled the church and spoke with a gardener working in the graveyard, who informed her that she had seen "Old Mrs Budge," the deceased former housekeeper of the vicar.

#location
St Mary the Virgin Church is located in the parish of Arlingham, Gloucestershire.

#title
The Blood-Sealed Ring of Boxwell Court

#summary
During the English Civil War, a member of the Huntley family of Boxwell Court uncovered an ambush plotted by three Roundheads, turned the tables to slay them all, and had a drop of each enemy's blood sealed into three small holes drilled into a gold ring.

#details
At Boxwell Court, an ancient and secluded country estate in Gloucestershire where the Huntley family has resided for centuries, a chilling local legend dates back to the turbulent years of the English Civil War. Three Parliamentary soldiers, known as Roundheads, laid a treacherous ambush intending to capture and kill the local Huntley squire. Forewarned of the plot, the squire preempted their trap, ambushed the three soldiers, and slew them all. To commemorate the bloody victory, he commissioned a gold ring featuring three tiny drilled holes, into each of which was sealed a drop of blood taken from the three slain enemies. This macabre heirloom and the tale behind it have been preserved by the Huntleys at Boxwell Court across the generations.

#location
Boxwell Court is a historic country house located near the village of Leighterton in South Gloucestershire, approximately 3 miles east of Wotton-under-Edge.

#title
The Phantom Lady of Boxwell Court

#summary
During the English Civil War, a pregnant woman at Boxwell Court died of shock when armed Roundhead soldiers raided the manor searching for fugitive Royalists, leaving her sorrowful spirit to wander the halls ever since.

#details
In the tumultuous years of the English Civil War, Boxwell Court served as a sanctuary for royalist supporters, including the hunted Cavalier Mathew Huntley and his relatives. During one of the aggressive house-to-house raids conducted by Parliamentarian soldiers searching for fugitives, the heavily pregnant wife of Sir John Wynyard was seized with overwhelming terror at the sudden intrusion of armed men. The extreme shock threw her into a premature confinement, resulting in the tragic deaths of both mother and newborn infant. In the centuries following this dark event, local tradition and verse record that the spectral form of the lady—often commemorated as Lady Constance—glides silently through the chambers, hall, and staircase of Boxwell Court at night, emerging from the adjacent chancel tomb to bless the sleeping household and cradle her child before fading away at dawn.

#location
Boxwell Court is a historic manor house situated in the secluded village of Boxwell, near Leighterton in the South Gloucestershire Cotswolds, standing directly adjacent to St. Mary's Church.

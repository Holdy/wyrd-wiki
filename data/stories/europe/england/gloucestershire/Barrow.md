#title
The Moving Stones of Nympsfield Long Barrow

#summary
Local folklore surrounding the ancient Neolithic burial mound in Gloucestershire warns of moving stones and terrifying nocturnal screams that plague anyone who disturbs the sacred site.

#details
Perched high on the Cotswold escarpment in Gloucestershire, the Neolithic Nympsfield Long Barrow is steeped in centuries of local legend and ghostly accounts. According to traditional folklore, the massive stone slabs that form the ancient tomb are capable of mysterious movement, reportedly rolling down the hillside into the valley and back up again on certain nights. The most chilling account involves a local resident who removed a stone slab from the barrow to use as building material in his own wall. Night after night, he was tormented by blood-curdling, unearthly screams echoing around the stone until, terrified out of his wits, he towed the slab back and re-erected it on the mound. Nearby barrows along the same ridge share similarly eerie reputations, including sightings of a phantom Roman soldier standing in the mist and a sword-wielding figure haunting the ancient burial sites.

#location
Nympsfield Long Barrow, situated on the ridge of the Cotswold escarpment close to the village of Nympsfield in Gloucestershire.

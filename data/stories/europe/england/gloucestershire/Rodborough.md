#title
The Murder and Haunting of Spillman's Court

#summary
While a family attended Sunday church services, their cook was brutally murdered inside their manor home, leaving behind a lingering supernatural presence that birthed generations of local ghost stories. 

#details
According to local historical accounts, the household at Spillman's Court left their cook behind to prepare meals while they attended church services on a Sunday morning. Upon returning home, they discovered the cook had been barbarously murdered, her body left lying on the hearth before the kitchen fire. This dark tragedy left an indelible mark on the community and became the origin of enduring local legends claiming that the manor house was haunted by restless spirits.

#location
Spillman's Court was situated between Lower and Middle Spillmans, bordered by Rodborough Hill in the parish of Rodborough, Gloucestershire.

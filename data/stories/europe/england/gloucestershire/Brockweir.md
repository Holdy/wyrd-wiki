#title
The Mermaids of Brockweir Quay

#summary
At the historic tidal quay of Brockweir on the River Wye, elusive mermaids are said to haunt the murky waters during the quiet hours of night and early morning when the bustling trade and lawlessness of the old port have faded.

#details
In times past, Brockweir was a busy, bustling, and somewhat lawless trading port—the highest tidal quay up the River Wye from the Bristol Channel—where wide-sailed trows and barges transferred wood, coal, iron, and goods between Bristol and the Forest of Dean, alongside a local river ferry crossing to Wales. Amidst the chaos of commerce and river traffic, daytime was far too busy for anything out of the ordinary, but as night fell and dawn approached, the atmosphere shifted entirely. During these quiet hours, local folklore maintains that mermaids emerge to inhabit the tidal waters of the quay. While the port is much quieter today, the legend endures, suggesting that visitors to the waterside might still catch a glimpse of these mysterious river dwellers.

#location
Brockweir tidal quay on the eastern bank of the River Wye in Gloucestershire.

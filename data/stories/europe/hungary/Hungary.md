#title
The Cursed Maiden of Máré Castle

#summary
A faithless wife's dying curse dooms her daughter to haunt a medieval fortress in the shape of a monster every 77 years until an impossible trial is completed.

#details
According to Hungarian folk tradition, Máré Castle was once inhabited by the knight Máré and his beautiful young wife, while his brother Miklós lived in a neighboring stronghold. When Knight Máré was called away to war for many years, his wife grew lonely and began an affair with his brother, eventually giving birth to a daughter. 

When Máré finally returned and discovered the betrayal, he furiously besieged and destroyed both castles, resulting in the deaths of his wife and brother. Before she perished, the faithless mother uttered a bitter curse upon her young daughter. Doomed by this malediction, the daughter was cast into the depths of the mountain. Every 77 years, on Whit Sunday morning, she breaks free from her subterranean prison in a monstrous form to bathe in the nearby stream and comb her long hair. The curse can only be broken—and the maiden freed—if a wandering hunter encounters her during this brief window and bravely kisses a bear, a snake, and a toad.

#location
Máré Castle (Máré-vár) is perched on a steep wooded peak in the Mecsek Mountains, approximately 5 kilometers from Magyaregregy in Baranya County, Hungary.

#title
The Demonic Possession of Csíkszentgyörgy

#summary
In 1726, a visiting woman became demonically possessed and disrupted a small village, leading to intense and scandalous exorcisms performed by the local parish priest. Her dramatic trances, prophecies, and supernatural afflictions gripped the entire community.

#details
During the winter of 1726–1727, a non-local woman arrived in the village of Csíkszentgyörgy and began exhibiting terrifying signs of demonic possession. Her violent convulsions, unearthly strength, and prophetic utterances alarmed the local inhabitants, prompting the village parson to intervene. Driven by an intense obsession with the case, the priest conducted prolonged exorcisms inside the village church and parsonage using forbidden European ritual manuals. The possessed woman's ecstatic trances and claims regarding the afterlife captivated not only the religious leader but also the guards and villagers keeping watch over her. The situation eventually drew the scrutiny of high church authorities due to the scandalous atmosphere and moral transgressions surrounding the protracted exorcisms.

#location
The events took place at the historic village church and parsonage in Csíkszentgyörgy (Ciucsângeorgiu), located in Harghita County within the historical region of Transylvania.

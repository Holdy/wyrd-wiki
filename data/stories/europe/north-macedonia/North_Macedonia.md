#title
The Exorcism at Bigorski Monastery

#summary
A young woman possessed by an unclean spirit after adopting a secular lifestyle found deliverance when she traveled to Bigorski Monastery to seek help from Saint John the Baptist. During the recitation of exorcism prayers before the monastery's wonder-working icon, the tormenting entity was successfully expelled.

#details
In the spring of 2006, a young woman named E.H., originally from Skopje, North Macedonia, traveled to the St. John Bigorski Monastery seeking relief from a severe demonic possession. While working in Western Europe, she had lived a lifestyle contrary to Orthodox tradition, which she believed allowed an unclean spirit to enter her. The possessing entity frequently attacked her during business meetings, forcing her to utter obscene and blasphemous remarks that ruined her reputation and made her life a living hell. Upon arriving at the monastery, the guest master recognized the severity of her condition and brought her before the monastery's miracle-working icon of Saint John the Baptist. The elder then recited St. Basil’s traditional prayers of exorcism while holding the Holy Cross. As the prayers were read, the woman's face nightmarishly transformed, horrible screams erupted from her, and violent winds blew through the area. Following the rite, she regained consciousness, made a sacramental confession, and received Holy Communion, permanently freeing her from the evil spirit.

#location
The events took place at the historic Saint John Bigorski Monastery (St. John the Baptist Monastery), located near the Radika River in western North Macedonia.

#title
The Petrified Wedding of Kuklica

#summary
A jilted lover's bitter curse petrified an entire wedding party into stone pillars the moment the newlyweds kissed.

#details
A young man in the village of Kuklica promised his love to a local girl, but instead chose to marry another. Heartbroken and enraged, the rejected girl tracked down the celebration on the wedding day. Approaching the groom under the pretense of a final farewell kiss, she uttered a devastating curse that instantly petrified the entire wedding assembly. The moment the bride and groom kissed, the curse took effect, freezing the bride, groom, matchmakers, and all wedding guests into towering stone pillars that still stand today.

#location
The legend is centered at the Kuklica Stone Dolls natural monument, located 8 kilometers northwest of the town of Kratovo in North Macedonia.

#title
The Ghostly Bride of Blue Bell Hill

#summary
A young woman killed in a tragic car crash on the eve of her wedding is said to haunt a Kentish hill, frequently appearing as a phantom hitchhiker who vanishes without a trace.

#details
Linked to a real-life tragedy on November 19, 1965, when bride-to-be Suzanne Browne and her friends died following a car crash, Blue Bell Hill on the A229 became famous for spectral sightings. Over the years, numerous motorists have reported picking up a cold, shivering young woman in a white dress who requests a lift. Upon reaching her destination, the passenger mysteriously vanishes from the car entirely, leaving behind bewildered drivers and unverified addresses.

#location
Blue Bell Hill is located on the A229 between Maidstone and Chatham in Kent.

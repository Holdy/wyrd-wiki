#title
The Possession at Spanjaardstraat

#summary
During a 19th-century spiritualist investigation at a historic house in Bruges, a visiting medium was violently possessed by the earthbound entity of a 15th-century monk. The possessing spirit re-enacted a heinous murder he had committed centuries prior within the building's walls.

#details
In the late 15th century, a convent stood along the Spanjaardstraat in Bruges, connected by subterranean passages to a nearby Augustinian monastery. An Italian monk fell desperately in love with a young nun named Hortense Dupont. When she steadfastly refused to break her religious vows, the enraged monk stalked her through the convent and stabbed her to death, concealing her body in the tunnels below. Over the centuries, the building became widely known as the *Spookhuis* (Ghost House), haunted by the spectral apparitions of both the murdered nun and the grim monk. In 1890, an English couple living in the house hosted the famous spiritualist medium William Eglinton and novelist Florence Marryat for a series of séances. Upon stepping inside the house, Eglinton was suddenly seized by a demonic and tormenting entity. With a diabolical expression, he ran about shouting and mumbling strange words before collapsing. Upon regaining consciousness, he spoke in an altered voice recounting the centuries-old murder, confessing the crime through the medium's lips to help the tormented soul find release.

#location
The key location is the historic house situated at Spanjaardstraat 17 in Bruges, Belgium, located on the corner next to the Augustinian Bridge.

#title
The Phantom Nun of Kloosterstraat

#summary
A medieval nun punished for a forbidden romance haunts a historic city street with phantom footsteps and shadowy apparitions. Witnesses along the thoroughfare report hearing her lingering steps and catching glimpses of her spirit near the former monastery site.

#details
In medieval Antwerp, a young nun broke her sacred vows by entering into a romance with a soldier. When their forbidden affair was uncovered, she was severely punished by being walled up alive within the monastery walls. Centuries later, locals and workers along Kloosterstraat continue to experience her lingering presence, frequently hearing her phantom footsteps echoing through the night and occasionally sighting her shadowy form wandering near where the old monastery once stood.

#location
The haunting occurs along Kloosterstraat, a well-known street located in the heart of Antwerp, Belgium.

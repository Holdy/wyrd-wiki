#title
The Century-Burn Curse of Gräfsnäs Castle

#summary
A mistreated servant reportedly cursed a newly built Swedish noble estate to burn down three times at exact century-long intervals. True to the prophecy, devastating fires consumed the castle in 1634, 1734, and 1834.

#details
During the mid-16th century, Count Sten Eriksson Leijonhufvud established Gräfsnäs Castle beside Lake Anten in Västergötland. Following his death, his widow Ebba Lilliehöök governed the estate harshly, ruling workers and servants with an iron fist. Pushed to the breaking point by this tyranny, a female laborer placed a hex upon the fortress, prophesying that it would suffer three catastrophic fires exactly one hundred years apart and ultimately fall into ruin without peace or prosperity. Decades later, the prophecy manifested with uncanny precision: the castle burned down in 1634, again in 1734, and for the third time in 1834 on the very day the property was purchased with a massive shipment of brandy. The final blaze and the subsequent explosive demolition of its slate roof in 1880 sealed its fate as an open ruin, which is now preserved as a cultural reserve.

#location
Gräfsnäs Castle Ruin is located on a peninsula at the northern tip of Lake Anten near Sollebrunn in Alingsås Municipality, Västra Götaland, Sweden.

#title
The Demonic Possession and Healing of Cristina at Vadstena Abbey

#summary
A newlywed peasant woman named Cristina was tormented and physically possessed by a demon that paralyzed her limbs and blinded her until she was cured at Vadstena Abbey.

#details
Cristina, the daughter of a wealthy peasant from Bro in the Linköping diocese, suffered from demonic molestations throughout her youth. Shortly after her wedding, the demonic attacks escalated violently: she fell to the ground stiff, with her limbs rigidly locked together, lost her sight and speech, and was overcome by a horrid stench. When her husband and companions transported her by cart toward Vadstena Abbey in January 1376, the demon pursued them along the journey, even tripping travelers on the frozen ice. Upon arriving at Vadstena Abbey, the demon fiercely resisted her entry, making her body increasingly heavy and attempting to drag her out of the chapel. After clergy gathered to sing hymns and placed Saint Birgitta's head relic and a sacred silver cross upon her, Cristina gradually regained her speech, smell, and sight, ultimately being freed from the possession.

#location
Vadstena Abbey is located in the historic town of Vadstena on the eastern shore of Lake Vättern in Östergötland County, Sweden.

#title
The Preacher and the Ghost

#summary
A pious Swedish preacher's nightly walk home from church is intercepted by a servant dressed as a ghost in a prank that turns fatal. When the preacher demands to know the apparition's identity, the mock ghost sinks permanently into the earth.

#details
Around three hundred years ago in the Swedish village of Veckholm, a devout preacher named Melchior habitually visited the local church every evening to pray at the altar. Displeased by his nightly absences, his wife persuaded their servant, Lorenz, to put on a white sheet and intercept Melchior along his path home to frighten him. When Lorenz appeared and blocked the preacher's way, Melchior calmly asked his identity twice. Receiving no answer, Lorenz suddenly began sinking into the ground and pleading for forgiveness for his sacrilege. Melchior declared it was too late to save him, and the servant sank completely beneath the earth.

#location
The encounters took place along the path leading from Veckholm Church in Uppsala County, Sweden.

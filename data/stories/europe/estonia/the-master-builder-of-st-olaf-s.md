#title
The Master Builder of St. Olaf's

#summary
A mysterious master builder agreed to construct a towering church for the townspeople in exchange for an impossible reward, only to meet a fatal end when his secret name was discovered. Ever since, the ill-fated spire has been repeatedly plagued by devastating fires and lightning strikes through the centuries.

#details
The citizens of medieval Tallinn desired a magnificent church to draw merchants and sailors, but they lacked the funds to build it. A mysterious stranger appeared and offered to construct the grand building on the condition that if the townspeople could not guess his name before its completion, they would pay him ten barrels of gold. As construction neared its end, the people grew desperate until a spy overheard a distant lullaby sung by the builder's wife, revealing his name to be Olaf. When Olaf placed the final cross atop the soaring spire, a townsman shouted out his name, causing the startled builder to lose his balance and plummet to his death. Legend states that a toad and a snake crawled from his mouth upon impact, sealing a dark legacy upon the building that manifests through recurrent lightning strikes and destructive fires.

#location
St. Olaf's Church (Oleviste kirik) is located at Lai 50, in the historic Old Town of Tallinn, Estonia.

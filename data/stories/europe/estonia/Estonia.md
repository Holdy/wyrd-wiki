#title
The Demonic Fall of St. Olaf's Builder

#summary
During the construction of St. Olaf's Church in st, a mysterious master builder plunged to his death from the spire, after which a snake and a toad crawled from his mouth as proof of his diabolical possession.

#details
In the 16th century, the city of Tallinn sought to build a massive spire on St. Olaf's Church, but the project became plagued by tragedy as seven successive master builders fell to their deaths, leading locals to believe the site was cursed. Eventually, a mysterious stranger named Olav stepped forward and promised to complete the towering structure for a thousand pieces of gold. Olav successfully finished the perilous work, but as he hammered the final nail into the spire and prepared to attach the golden rooster, he slipped and plummeted to the ground. When his body struck the earth before the horrified crowd, a snake and a toad crawled out of his mouth, revealing that he had been possessed and driven by dark, diabolical forces.

#location
St. Olaf's Church, located at Lai 50 in Tallinn, Estonia.

#title
The Master Builder of St. Olaf's

#summary
A mysterious master builder agreed to construct a towering church for the townspeople in exchange for an impossible reward, only to meet a fatal end when his secret name was discovered. Ever since, the ill-fated spire has been repeatedly plagued by devastating fires and lightning strikes through the centuries.

#details
The citizens of medieval Tallinn desired a magnificent church to draw merchants and sailors, but they lacked the funds to build it. A mysterious stranger appeared and offered to construct the grand building on the condition that if the townspeople could not guess his name before its completion, they would pay him ten barrels of gold. As construction neared its end, the people grew desperate until a spy overheard a distant lullaby sung by the builder's wife, revealing his name to be Olaf. When Olaf placed the final cross atop the soaring spire, a townsman shouted out his name, causing the startled builder to lose his balance and plummet to his death. Legend states that a toad and a snake crawled from his mouth upon impact, sealing a dark legacy upon the building that manifests through recurrent lightning strikes and destructive fires.

#location
St. Olaf's Church (Oleviste kirik) is located at Lai 50, in the historic Old Town of Tallinn, Estonia.

#title
The Monk of Short-Leg Gate Tower

#summary
A violent phantom monk, believed to be a 13th-century executioner's apprentice, haunts a medieval gate tower and street in Tallinn. He frequently pulls pranks, hides items, and physically interacts with the living.

#details
In 1233, a young novice named Justinius—who had previously worked as an executioner's apprentice—was killed before he could repent for his past sins. Ever since, his dark, sometimes bloodied apparition has been witnessed roaming the medieval lane of Lühike jalg and the Short-Leg Gate Tower. Known for his mischievous and aggressive behavior, the spirit has been reported breaking items, hiding objects, and even pulling toes or kicking the ribs of unsuspecting residents and visitors in the area.

#location
The story takes place at the Short-Leg Gate Tower on Lühike jalg street in the Old Town of Tallinn, Estonia.

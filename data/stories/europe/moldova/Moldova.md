#title
The Mass Exorcisms of Saharna Monastery

#summary
During weekly midnight services at a historic monastery in rural Moldova, dozens of young people and villagers gather to undergo intense exorcisms to purge themselves of alleged demonic possession.

#details
Every Thursday night, hundreds of pilgrims and locals travel to the remote Saharna Monastery in northern Moldova to participate in mass exorcisms led by Orthodox priests. As midnight approaches and the clergy loudly call out the names of the afflicted, members of the congregation—predominantly young people—begin screaming, writhing, and convulsing as they react violently to the prayers. Witnesses and participants recount how the afflicted throw themselves into trances, requiring family members or partners to restrain them, splash them with holy water, and force them to consume sanctified silt in efforts to cast out the tormenting entities.

#location
The Saharna Monastery is situated in the village of Saharna within the Rezina District of northern Moldova, overlooking the Dniester River.

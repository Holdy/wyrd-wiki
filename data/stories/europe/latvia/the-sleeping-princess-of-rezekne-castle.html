    The Sleeping Princess of Rēzekne Castle

    Summary

    Princess Rozālija sits in a deep enchanted slumber upon her chest of gold deep beneath the castle ruins, awaiting a brave youth every nine years to break her ancient curse. Because none have yet succeeded, she remains bound to the underground vault until the cycle can finally be undone.

    Details

    Long ago, the ruler of the Latgalian lands divided his realm among his three daughters. The eldest, Princess Rozālija—affectionately known as Rose—received the fortress at Rēzekne as her inheritance. She was famed for her breathtaking beauty, and fresh roses constantly adorned her hair and attire. Following the destruction of the fortress and the passage of centuries, a persistent legend took root: Princess Rose did not truly perish. Instead, she sits asleep in the subterranean vaults beneath the castle hill, resting upon a magnificent chest filled with gold. Her magical slumber is strictly guarded by two fierce bloodhounds—one secured to her right by a golden chain, and the other to her left by a silver chain. Bound by a powerful curse, Princess Rose is only granted a brief window every nine years on Easter morning to emerge from the underground chamber and seek a brave youth capable of breaking the enchantment. Though many adventurous souls have attempted over generations to free her, none have yet succeeded, leaving her to return to her long sleep.

#title
The White Lady of Belchen Tunnel

#summary
Motorists traversing a Swiss mountain tunnel frequently pick up a pale female hitchhiker in white who issues a dire warning before mysteriously vanishing from the backseat.

#details
Beginning in the early 1980s, the Belchen Tunnel on the A2 motorway in Switzerland gained widespread notoriety for spectral encounters. Motorists and travelers reported encountering an old woman dressed entirely in white hitchhiking on the roadside. In September 1983, two women stopped to assist a pale middle-aged woman near the tunnel entrance. After she entered the vehicle's backseat and whispered that something awful was about to happen, she vanished completely into thin air while the car was moving inside the tunnel, leaving authorities baffled when a police search of the vehicle found nothing.

#location
The Belchen Tunnel is located on the A2 motorway through the Jura Mountains on the boundary between the Solothurn and Basel-Landschaft cantons in Switzerland, near Eptingen.

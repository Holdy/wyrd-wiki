#title
Eternally Cursed (The Legend of the Sardona Glacier)

#summary
A mother places a devastating curse upon her cruel son and his arrogant lover for their heartless neglect, burying their magnificent alpine pasture beneath eternal ice and snow.

#details
A wealthy shepherd named Segnes inherited a prosperous alpine pasture on the condition that he provide for his aging mother. However, under the sway of his proud and haughty mistress, Sardona, he abandoned his filial duty and cut off all care for his mother. When the frail, starving woman dragged herself up the mountain to beg her son for a piece of bread, Segnes cruelly offered her a bowl of liquid manure instead, while quickly laying down fine cheeses on the path so his beloved would not soil her shoes. Heartbroken and outraged, the mother cast a dying curse upon them: that they and their land remain together in ice and snow for all eternity. Immediately, torrential rains and unceasing snow buried the entire alp under what is now the Sardona Glacier. To this day, the damned couple is trapped within the ice, forced to emerge from glacier crevasses each year to vainly plead for their mother's forgiveness amidst the roaring avalanches and howling alpine winds.

#location
The Sardona Glacier (Sardonagletscher) is located in the high Glarus Alps within the Tectonic Arena Sardona, situated in eastern Switzerland across the cantons of St. Gallen, Glarus, and Graubünden.

#title
The Exorcism of Fridiburga

#summary
A noblewoman possessed by malevolent demons was freed when an Irish monk performed an exorcism, causing the evil entities to escape from her mouth as black birds. In profound gratitude for her deliverance, she bestowed a large estate upon the saint.

#details
In 7th-century Switzerland, the noblewoman Fridiburga—who was betrothed to King Sigebert III of Austrasia—became severely afflicted by demonic possession. As her torment worsened and conventional remedies failed, the holy Irish missionary Saint Gall intervened. Through his spiritual power and prayers, he successfully exorcised the entities possessing her, causing the demons to fly out of her mouth in the visible form of black birds. Freed from the dark presence, Fridiburga rewarded Saint Gall by granting him a large abode in the Swiss territory of Arbon.

#location
The events are traditionally anchored in the town of Arbon, situated on the southern shore of Lake Constance in the canton of Thurgau, Switzerland.

#title
The White Lady of Belchen Tunnel

#summary
Motorists traversing a Swiss mountain tunnel frequently pick up a pale female hitchhiker in white who issues a dire warning before mysteriously vanishing from the backseat.

#details
Beginning in the early 1980s, the Belchen Tunnel on the A2 motorway in Switzerland gained widespread notoriety for spectral encounters. Motorists and travelers reported encountering an old woman dressed entirely in white hitchhiking on the roadside. In September 1983, two women stopped to assist a pale middle-aged woman near the tunnel entrance. After she entered the vehicle's backseat and whispered that something awful was about to happen, she vanished completely into thin air while the car was moving inside the tunnel, leaving authorities baffled when a police search of the vehicle found nothing.

#location
The Belchen Tunnel is located on the A2 motorway through the Jura Mountains on the boundary between the Solothurn and Basel-Landschaft cantons in Switzerland, near Eptingen.

The Haunting of Małachowo's Road

Summary

The vengeful spirit of a cruel local count haunts motorists traveling through his former domain, frequently causing dangerous car accidents.

Details

Centuries ago, the village of Małachowo was ruled by a tyrannical and exploitative count who tormented his subjects. His life ended abruptly when his carriage sank into deep mud, causing him to drown. Instead of finding peace, his ghost returned to haunt the village roads, startling drivers and causing numerous vehicular accidents. Consequently, the settlement was renamed Małachowo-Złych Miejsc—meaning "Małachowo of Bad Places"—to warn travelers passing through the area.

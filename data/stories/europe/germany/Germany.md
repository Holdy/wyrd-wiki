#title
The Lady in Black of the Munich Residenz

#summary
A veiled spectral woman in black haunts a royal palace across generations, serving as a delayed and unyielding death omen that precedes the passing of members of the House of Wittelsbach.

#details
For centuries, a mysterious specter known as the Lady in Black has glided through the corridors and chambers of the Munich Residenz, the ancient city palace of the Bavarian royal family. According to family lore and historical accounts, whenever this dark-clad figure is sighted walking through the halls or stone staircases, it acts as an ominous curse foretelling the imminent death of a member of the House of Wittelsbach. Over generations, sightings of the spirit have consistently preceded tragedies, from King Maximilian II and King Ludwig I to King Ludwig II and later royal descendants. Despite various theories attempting to identify the woman behind the legend—ranging from grieving electors to historical duchesses—her appearances remain an inescapable, lingering curse that continues to manifest within the palace walls.

#location
The Munich Residenz, located between Odeonsplatz and the English Garden in the heart of Munich, Germany.

#title
The Tragic Possession and Exorcism of Anneliese Michel

#summary
A young German woman suffering from severe seizures and psychiatric distress became convinced she was possessed by multiple demons, leading to dozens of Catholic exorcisms that ultimately resulted in her tragic death from malnutrition.

#details
In her late teens, Anneliese Michel began experiencing severe seizures and was diagnosed with temporal lobe epilepsy and psychosis, undergoing psychiatric treatment that failed to alleviate her worsening symptoms. By her early twenties, she developed an intense aversion to sacred Christian objects, heard voices telling her she was damned, and began exhibiting extreme behaviors such as growling, eating insects, and drinking her own urine. Convinced that she was suffering from demonic possession rather than medical illness, her family and local clergy appealed to the Catholic Church. Following authorization from Bishop Josef Stangl, two Catholic priests performed 67 exorcism rites over a period of ten months in Klingenberg am Main. During the rituals, the priests claimed that six demons—identifying as Lucifer, Cain, Judas Iscariot, Nero, Adolf Hitler, and a corrupt priest named Fleischmann—spoke through her. Refusing medical intervention and food as the grueling rites continued, Michel died of malnutrition and dehydration on July 1, 1976. Her parents and the two priests were subsequently convicted of negligent homicide in a landmark trial that drew international attention.

#location
The family home in Klingenberg am Main, Bavaria, Germany.

#title
The White Lady of Ebersberg Forest

#summary
A ghostly female hitchhiker haunts a sharp bend on a Bavarian forest highway, vanishing from the backseat of helpful motorists or causing fatal accidents for those who refuse to pick her up.

#details
Along State Road 2080 in the vast Ebersberg Forest, drivers traversing the sharp curve near the Hubertus Chapel at midnight frequently report encountering a mysterious woman in white. According to local folklore, the apparition is the spirit of a woman who either froze to death after being refused a ride or was the victim of a hit-and-run accident. Male drivers traveling alone at night are said to find her thumbing for a ride by the roadside; if picked up, she quietly sits in the back seat before vanishing into thin air. Conversely, motorists who refuse to stop are confronted by the ghost suddenly materializing in the passenger seat, where she aggressively grabs the steering wheel to force a disastrous crash.

#location
The Hubertus Chapel is situated on State Road 2080 (Staatsstrasse 2080) between Ebersberg and Schwaberwegen at the junction with Scheibenweg within the Ebersberg Forest in Bavaria, Germany.

#title
The White Lady of Ebersberg Forest

#summary
A ghostly female hitchhiker haunts a sharp bend on a Bavarian forest highway, vanishing from the backseat of helpful motorists or causing fatal accidents for those who refuse to pick her up.

#details
Along State Road 2080 in the vast Ebersberg Forest, drivers traversing the sharp curve near the Hubertus Chapel at midnight frequently report encountering a mysterious woman in white. According to local folklore, the apparition is the spirit of a woman who either froze to death after being refused a ride or was the victim of a hit-and-run accident. Male drivers traveling alone at night are said to find her thumbing for a ride by the roadside; if picked up, she quietly sits in the back seat before vanishing into thin air. Conversely, motorists who refuse to stop are confronted by the ghost suddenly materializing in the passenger seat, where she aggressively grabs the steering wheel to force a disastrous crash.

#location
The Hubertus Chapel is situated on State Road 2080 (Staatsstrasse 2080) between Ebersberg and Schwaberwegen at the junction with Scheibenweg within the Ebersberg Forest in Bavaria, Germany.

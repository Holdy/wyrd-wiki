#title
Our Lady of the Turning Eyes

#summary
During a French siege in the Thirty Years' War, more than 40 witnesses said the statue turned pale, raised its eyes and later shed tears. It was moved to the Münster in 1802.

#location
Holy Cross Münster, Rottweil (wept in the Dominican church, now the Predigerkirche), Germany

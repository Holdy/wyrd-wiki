#title
The Curse of Ämmänhauta

#summary
According to local folklore in Vesilahti, a pact made with an 11th-century witch established a permanent curse upon anyone who passes her forest tomb without tossing a branch upon it. Passing travelers and villagers have faithfully adhered to this ritual across generations to avoid invoking her wrath.

#details
In the 11th-century village of Vesilahti, a local witch made a treaty with the villagers regarding her final resting place. Knowing that her practice of witchcraft would disqualify her from being buried in consecrated church ground, she stipulated that if she passed away while being transported there, she must be buried right where she fell in the forest. She placed a curse upon the site, decreeing that anyone who walks past her tomb without placing a branch or twig upon it will face the power of her curse. For centuries, locals, school children, and travelers have maintained the tradition of tossing branches onto the grave to appease the witch and escape her hex.

#location
Ämmänhauta (The Witch's Tomb) is located in the forest in the municipality of Vesilahti, Finland.

#title
The Martin Croft Devil

#summary
In January 1885, a Finnish croft in Ylöjärvi suffered a violent sixteen-day haunting centered around a young maid, during which objects flew through the air, furniture moved spontaneously, and an invisible entity terrorized the inhabitants. The extraordinary case was subsequently documented in extensive court records and witness testimonies.

#details
On January 12, 1885, strange disturbances broke out at the croft of Efraim Martin in Ylöjärvi, Finland. The phenomena—which included flying knives, doors refusing to stay closed, heavy clay accumulating from nowhere, and locked drawers emptying themselves without the binding ties breaking—appeared to center around the household's 13-year-old maid, Emma Lindroos. As word spread, curious visitors and local authorities flocked to the remote cottage, witnessing objects such as candlesticks and bread poles move autonomously through the air. The haunting culminated in a legal trial where fifteen witnesses testified under oath regarding seventy-eight inexplicable occurrences before the disturbances abruptly ceased on January 27.

#location
Efraim Martin's former croft located in the rural parish and village of Ylöjärvi, near Keijärvi in Finland.

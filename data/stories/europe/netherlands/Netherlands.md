#title
The Curse of Ghost Alley

#summary
After confessing on her deathbed to murdering her sister out of jealousy over a sailor, a woman was cursed by her husband for all eternity. Her tormented spirit now haunts the narrow Amsterdam alley where the crime took place.

#details
In 18th-century Amsterdam, two sisters named Helena and Dina lived with their father, a tanner on the lane known as Spooksteeg. Both sisters fell in love with the same handsome sailor, but when Dina announced her impending marriage to him, a jealous Helena pushed her down the stairs into the tannery cellar, resulting in her death. Helena subsequently married the sailor in Dina's place. Years later, on her deathbed in 1753, a guilt-ridden Helena finally confessed to the murder. Upon hearing the truth, her husband cursed her soul for all eternity, preventing her spirit from ever finding rest. To this day, ghostly sightings and harrowing screams are reported in the narrow alleyway.

#location
Spooksteeg is a specific historic alley located in the Red Light District of Amsterdam, Netherlands.

#title
The Demonic Pact and Fall of Doctor Faust

#summary
The notorious alchemist Doctor Faust traded his soul to the devil in exchange for forbidden knowledge and a demonic familiar, ultimately meeting a gruesome end inside his castle chamber.

#details
In the mid-16th century, the alchemist, physician, and astrologer Doctor Faust grew frustrated with his academic and professional failures and successfully summoned the Prince of Darkness in a forest to forge a fateful pact. In exchange for his soul, Faust demanded unlimited knowledge and worldly pleasures, receiving in return a demonic familiar—a dog capable of shifting into a human servant. After years of dark revelry and excess, including an illicit affair that conjured Helen of Troy, the pact finally expired. The devil returned to claim his due, violently attacking Faust in his bedchamber at Waardenburg Castle, splattering blood and brains across the walls and tearing out his eyes. The entity then dragged Faust's lifeless body to the top of the castle tower and hurled it to the ground below, leaving permanent bloodstains that could never be scoured away.

#location
Waardenburg Castle is situated in the village of Waardenburg in the province of Gelderland, Netherlands.

#title
The Jealous Sister of Spooksteeg

#summary
Consumed by jealousy, a woman murdered her sister to steal her lover and now eternally haunts the narrow Amsterdam alleyway where her crime took place.

#details
In 18th-century Amsterdam, a tanner named Schaap lived with his two daughters, Helena and Dina. When a visiting sailor named Wouter fell in love with the kind-hearted Dina instead of the passionate Helena, jealousy consumed Helena. She pushed Dina down the cellar stairs and ultimately beat her to death, making it look like an accident. Wouter subsequently married Helena, unaware of the truth, until Helena confessed on her deathbed in 1753 and was abandoned in disgust by her husband. Ever since her death, people walking through Spooksteeg—an alleyway just off the Zeedijk—have reported encountering her sorrowful, lingering spirit.

#location
Spooksteeg is a historic alleyway located in the center of Amsterdam, Netherlands, connecting the Zeedijk to the Warmoesstraat.

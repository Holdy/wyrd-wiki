#title
The Cursed Countess of Gradišče

#summary
An arrogant countess was struck by a powerful curse that caused her hilltop castle to vanish into the earth along with her. She remains trapped under the spell, waiting for someone to break her curse by washing their face with clean water.

#details
Long ago, upon the summit of Gradišče above Dobrova, a haughty and wealthy countess lived in a grand castle. Her extreme arrogance and disregard for others ultimately brought down a severe curse upon her domain. In a flash of retribution, the castle completely disappeared from the mountaintop, plunging the countess into a subterranean realm where she was bound by magic. According to local folklore, she occasionally appears to shepherds and travelers, offering great wealth if they wash their face with clean water, which is the only act capable of breaking her spell. Because those who encounter her have refused or failed, the countess remains trapped in her cursed existence to this day.

#location
The story centers on Gradišče, a prominent forested hill and former castle site located just above Dobrova in Slovenia.

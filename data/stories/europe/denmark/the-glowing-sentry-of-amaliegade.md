#title
The Glowing Sentry of Amaliegade

#summary
Witnesses near a historic Copenhagen intersection have reported seeing a luminous, spectral soldier standing watch after dark. The apparition is said to haunt the street corner where old skeletal remains were once unearthed during construction.

#details
In the historic district of Copenhagen near Amaliegade and Sankt Annæ Plads, past excavations for new buildings uncovered human skeletons buried beneath the streets. Following these discoveries, locals and visitors reported strange nocturnal phenomena, including glowing figures and phantom wagons. Most notably, on the street corner where Sankt Annæ Plads meets Amaliegade, people have repeatedly encountered the glowing phantom of a sentry standing guard in the night.

#location
The corner of Sankt Annæ Plads and Amaliegade in Copenhagen, Denmark.

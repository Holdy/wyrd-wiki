#title
The Midnight Hitchhiker of Asmara

#summary
A motorist in Asmara gives a late-night lift to a shivering young woman walking home, only to discover the following morning that he had picked up a ghost and left his jacket on her grave.

#details
Late one dark and windy night in Asmara, a driver noticed a young woman in her early twenties walking along the street in light clothes and smilingly asking for a lift. Feeling sympathetic, he pulled over and let her in. Finding her shivering, he offered her his jacket, and the two chatted casually about the weather as he drove her to her destination. Upon arriving, she thanked him in a whisper and stepped inside her house. The next morning, realizing he had left his jacket behind, the driver returned to the house. There, he met a grieving old woman who asked him to describe the girl. Upon hearing his description, the old woman led him to the girl's grave, where his jacket lay resting on top of the tombstone.

#location
The key location is the graveyard in Asmara containing the young woman's grave where the driver recovered his jacket.

#title
The Spirit of Fodie Cave

#summary
When an abusive stepmother disrespects a mystical entity inside a sacred village cave, her soul is seized and possessed by the supernatural force. Her haunted screams are said to still echo from within the cavern.

#details
According to Kunama folklore passed down through generations in the Eritrean village of Fodie, located about 40 kilometers from Barentu, two young neglected children were secretly nourished and cared for by a mysterious spirit dwelling within a sacred cave. Intrigued by how the children remained healthy despite her neglect, their cruel stepmother followed them to the cavern. Upon entering, she found a lush subterranean sanctuary complete with a spring and gardens. Furious that the entity favored the children, she uttered disrespectful words to the spirit and turned to leave. Instantly, her life force was drained, she collapsed and died, and her soul was taken over by the cave entity. To this day, community members participating in annual rituals near the cave report hearing high-pitched screams emanating from within, believing her possessed soul remains permanently entrapped there.

#location
The sacred cave situated near the village of Fodie, approximately 40 kilometers away from Barentu, Eritrea.

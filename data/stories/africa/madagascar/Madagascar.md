#title
The Awakening at Ankaramalaza

#summary
When a young Malagasy woman was compelled by an invisible force to intervene in a violent spirit possession, her successful exorcism sparked a nationwide revival.

#details
On August 1, 1941, in the rural settlement of Ankaramalaza, Madagascar, a catechist named Petera struggled unsuccessfully to cast out an evil spirit tormenting the daughter of catechist Mosesy Tsirefo. Volahavana—a young woman who would later be known as Nenilava—was tending the cooking fire nearby when an unseen force suddenly shoved her forward. She grabbed the possessed girl and wrestled with her until the entity declared that it was departing because someone stronger had arrived, leaving the girl instantly healed and restored to her senses. This pivotal miracle marked the beginning of Nenilava's legendary ministry of exorcism and spiritual revival across Madagascar.

#location
The events took place in the village of Ankaramalaza, located about twenty kilometers north of Vohipeno in southeastern Madagascar.

#title
The Curse of Queen Ranavalona

#summary
The tyrannical reign and violent purges of Queen Ranavalona I provoked ancestral wrath, casting a spiritual curse over the Merina royal court and kingdom that manifested in political instability and ultimate decline.

#details
When Queen Ranavalona I seized control of the Merina Kingdom, she instituted a brutal reign characterized by political purges, the persecution of Christian converts, and the widespread use of the deadly tangena poison ordeal. According to Malagasy folklore, these violent acts ruptured the sacred stewardship between the monarch and the *razana* (ancestral dead). Wrongfully executed subjects and advisers left behind a spiritual backlash, prophesying that the royal house would never know peace. This accumulated curse manifested as a pattern of misfortune, nightmares within the palace, and the steady collapse of the kingdom's sovereignty until its eventual annexation.

#location
The story is centered on the Royal Hill of Ambohimanga and the Manjakamiadana palace within the Rova of Antananarivo in Madagascar.

#title
The Phantom Children of Route 7

#summary
Ghostly children haunt the bridges along National Route 7 in southern Madagascar, suddenly leaping out of the darkness to cause fatal accidents. These phantom figures appear unexpectedly on the roadway, targeting passing vehicles.

#details
Along the arid southern stretches of National Route 7 in Madagascar, motorists face a chilling hazard not of flesh and blood. Phantom children are frequently reported haunting the road's bridges, materializing abruptly out of the pitch-black night. As drivers approach, these spectral figures leap directly into the path of oncoming cars, attempting to force vehicles careening off the roadway and into the abyss below. Locals and travelers alike treat these treacherous bridge crossings with extreme superstition and fear.

#location
The haunted bridges are situated along National Route 7 (RN7) as it traverses southern Madagascar.

#title
The Healers and Curses of Ukerewe

#summary
In the island communities of Lake Victoria, traditional healers use ritual ceremonies at sacred crossroads and hills to lift potent curses and hexes cast by local sorcerers. Victims of disputes or stolen property seek out these practitioners to cleanse evil spirits and restore spiritual balance.

#details
Across the 28 islands of the Ukerewe archipelago in Tanzania, witchcraft and curses remain deeply embedded in daily life, addressing everything from family disputes and financial ruin to mysterious illnesses. When individuals believe they have been targeted by a witch's hex, modern medicine is often bypassed in favor of local traditional healers, known as *waganga*. A prominent healer named Dina Charles explains that her calling began after she experienced a sudden mental breakdown, retreated to a hilltop for two weeks, and later discovered a sacred Bible beneath a historical cross commemorating 19th-century missionaries. To cure those afflicted by curses, healers perform nocturnal rituals where patients inhale medicinal fumes under a black muslin cloth while practitioners dance and chant. Afterward, the patient is led to a specific crossroads—a traditional site for warding off malevolent forces—where they are ritually washed to cleanse the hex and invoke benevolent ancestral spirits.

#location
The southern shore and surrounding hills of Ukerewe Island in Lake Victoria, Tanzania.

#title
The Jinni Hunter of Stone Town

#summary
A traditional herbalist in Zanzibar investigates and expels malevolent spirits possessing locals and haunting historical ruins. During his work, he successfully extracts a tormenting jinni from a suffering woman by binding it with a Quranic contract.

#details
While traveling through Stone Town in Zanzibar, filmmaker Justin Fornal accompanied local herbalist and jinni hunter Said Suleiman Mohammed to witness how traditional exorcisms are performed. Said recounted how his calling was confirmed one midnight when he rushed to a local house where a woman was screaming in agony from severe cramps. Recognizing the signs of possession, he recited verses from the Quran directly into her eyes until the malevolent jinni wept, apologized, and agreed to sign a binding contract never to return. Later, while exploring the crumbling underground chambers of the Royal Forodhani Baths—a former Persian bathhouse now operating as an elementary school—Said and Fornal encountered a mysterious white cat that vanished instantly in a sealed room, which Said confirmed was another manifestation of a local jinni.

#location
The Royal Forodhani Baths are situated within the historic Stone Town district of Zanzibar City on Unguja Island, Tanzania.

#title
The White Lady of Malindi Street

#summary
While walking along Malindi Street at night, Abbas Darjani encountered a spectral white lady. The terrifying meeting left him profoundly shaken, leading to a severe illness that ultimately caused him to lose his eyesight.

#details
Abbas Darjani encountered a spectral white lady while walking along Malindi Street at night. The sudden appearance of the ghostly figure terrified him so deeply that he fell seriously ill soon after. His condition deteriorated over time, culminating in complete blindness.

#location
The sighting occurred on Malindi Street in Stone Town, Zanzibar, Tanzania.

#upstream-source
*Zanzibar Ghost Stories* by Mohammad A. Amir.

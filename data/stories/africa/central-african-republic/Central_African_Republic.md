#title
The Lake of Sorcerers

#summary
After a grieving mother's son dies during a secret tribal initiation rite in the forest, a supernatural entity helps her exact vengeance by drawing a furrow around the village, causing the earth to open up and swallow the entire community into a deep, black lake. To this day, phantom music and voices rise from its waters, and any who disbelieve its supernatural power face a grim fate.

#details
In the Central African Republic, a mother eagerly awaits the return of her son from an initiation camp in the deep forest where young boys are tested to become men. However, her son dies during the grueling trials. Bound by strict laws of secrecy, the village elders and the boy’s father hide the tragedy from the mother, falsely assuring her that he is healthy and will return soon. When the day of celebration arrives and the other boys return, the mother realizes her son is missing and learns the devastating truth. Distraught and abandoned to her grief, she wanders into the bush, where a mysterious ancient woman offers her vengeance. Armed with a branch from the sacred tree of good and evil, the grieving mother traces a furrow around the entire village at sunset. Instantly, the ground opens with a deafening noise, and the entire village, its inhabitants, houses, and instruments are devoured by boiling waters, creating the bottomless Lake of Sorcerers. Years later, a colonial administrator in the neighboring village of Damara mocks warnings about the lake's phantom sounds; when he investigates at midnight, ghostly music and terrifying waves envelop him, and he vanishes forever.

#location
The Lake of Sorcerers, located near Damara in the Central African Republic.

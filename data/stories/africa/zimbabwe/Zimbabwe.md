#title
Deliverance at Mutemwa Hill

#summary
During a school prayer retreat at Mutemwa Hill in Zimbabwe, a 17-year-old student was possessed by a hostile ancestral entity that was subsequently cast out during a deliverance session.

#details
In October 2011, students and teachers from St. Peter's Secondary School in Mbare traveled to Mutemwa Hill in Mutoko for an overnight prayer retreat to prepare for their final examinations. During the early morning hours, as a spiritual deliverance session took place on the mountain, a 17-year-old girl named Memory became violently possessed by a demonic entity manifesting as the spirit of her deceased grandmother. The entity spoke out in Shona, fiercely resisting the prayers and declaring its intent to sabotage her future, cause academic failure, and perpetuate a generational family pattern of poverty and witchcraft. Following intense spiritual intervention, the entity was cast out, leaving the student freed and restored.

#location
Mutemwa Hill is a prominent prayer mountain located in Mutoko, Zimbabwe.

#title
The Curse of Mount Nyangani

#summary
Mount Nyangani is bound by a powerful curse that causes hikers and visitors who break traditional taboos to vanish without a trace into sudden, disorienting mists. Among its most famous victims were two daughters of a government minister who disappeared in 1981 despite massive multi-week searches.

#details
Standing as Zimbabwe's highest peak in the Eastern Highlands, Mount Nyangani is revered by the local VaManyika people as a sacred, sentient landscape governed by powerful ancestral guardians and water spirits. According to local lore, visitors who violate sacred protocols—such as wearing bright red clothing, making noise, pointing at sacred features, or showing disrespect—trigger a curse that blinds them and casts them into a spiritual dimension where they become permanently lost. Over the decades, numerous hikers, tourists, and officials have gone missing on its slopes. The mountain's chilling reputation was cemented in April 1981 when the two young daughters of former Finance Minister Dr. Tichaendepi Masaya disappeared into a sudden, blinding fog during a holiday hike. Despite extensive rescue operations involving hundreds of soldiers, trackers, and thermal imaging equipment, no footprints, clothing, or trace of the sisters was ever found, solidifying the mountain's legend as a place that swallows the living whole.

#location
Mount Nyangani is located within Nyanga National Park in the Eastern Highlands of Zimbabwe, rising to an elevation of 2,593 meters near the border with Mozambique.

#title
The Vanishing Hitchhiker of Joshua Mqabuko Nkomo Street

#summary
Motorists in Bulawayo who pick up a strikingly beautiful female hitchhiker at a city intersection find that she mysteriously vanishes from their cars before reaching Athlone Cemetery.

#details
Late-night motorists in Bulawayo, Zimbabwe, have reported picking up a beautiful young woman wearing a long dress and a hoodie at the intersection of Joshua Mqabuko Nkomo Street and Connaught Avenue. After requesting a ride toward Campbell Avenue near Athlone Cemetery, the mysterious passenger engages in casual conversation before suddenly vanishing into thin air while the vehicle is still in motion. Local lore suggests the apparition is the restless spirit of a female sex worker who was murdered by a client and buried in the nearby cemetery, returning to haunt and terrify unsuspecting male drivers.

#location
The intersection of Joshua Mqabuko Nkomo Street and Connaught Avenue in Bulawayo, Zimbabwe, serves as the primary spot where motorists encounter the phantom hitchhiker.

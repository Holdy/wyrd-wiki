Deliverance at Mutemwa Hill

Summary

During a school prayer retreat at Mutemwa Hill in Zimbabwe, a 17-year-old student was possessed by a hostile ancestral entity that was subsequently cast out during a deliverance session.

Details

In October 2011, students and teachers from St. Peter's Secondary School in Mbare traveled to Mutemwa Hill in Mutoko for an overnight prayer retreat to prepare for their final examinations. During the early morning hours, as a spiritual deliverance session took place on the mountain, a 17-year-old girl named Memory became violently possessed by a demonic entity manifesting as the spirit of her deceased grandmother. The entity spoke out in Shona, fiercely resisting the prayers and declaring its intent to sabotage her future, cause academic failure, and perpetuate a generational family pattern of poverty and witchcraft. Following intense spiritual intervention, the entity was cast out, leaving the student freed and restored.

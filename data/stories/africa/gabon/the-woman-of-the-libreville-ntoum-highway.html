    The Woman of the Libreville-Ntoum Highway

    Summary

    A motorist driving along the Libreville-Ntoum highway late at night picks up a mysterious female hitchhiker who vanishes into thin air near Okolassi.

    Details

    Around 3:00 AM on the Libreville-Ntoum road, a motorist noticed a woman hitchhiking in the dark and, captivated by her appearance, stopped to pick her up. Identifying herself as Claire, she asked to be taken to Ntoum 6, and the two chatted cheerfully during the journey. However, when the car's radio played a traditional Mungongo song, the woman became visibly distressed and begged the driver to turn it off. Upon reaching the exit of Okolassi, she asked to be let out of the vehicle. When the driver pulled over and she stepped outside, he immediately restarted his car only to discover she had completely vanished. Shaken by the experience, the driver vowed never to pick up female hitchhikers at night ever again.

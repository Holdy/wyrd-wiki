#title
The Beautiful Lady Ghost Who Disappeared on a Moving Bike

#summary
A late-night motorcycle taxi rider in Abuja picks up a mysterious woman at a junction who inexplicably vanishes from the moving bike.

#details
Working late on a cold December night in Abuja, an okada rider named Ibrahim parked near NNPC Junction. A young woman dressed in a long black gown approached him and requested a ride to Wuse Market. As they traveled along the road, her presence felt unnaturally cold. Before reaching their destination, Ibrahim looked back and discovered she had completely vanished from the moving bike, leaving him terrified.

#location
The encounter begins at NNPC Junction in Abuja, Nigeria, and proceeds toward Wuse Market.

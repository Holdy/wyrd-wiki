#title
The Haunting of Wilkinson Road

#summary
Motorists driving along Wilkinson Road at night frequently encounter a sorrowful woman in white named Mama Lungi, who perished in a car crash while searching for her child. Drivers who see her reflection in their rearview mirror are granted safe passage, but those she approaches or knocks upon face misfortune.

#details
According to local legend, Mama Lungi is a restless spirit trapped along Wilkinson Road in Freetown since the 1970s after dying in a tragic car accident while looking for her missing child. At night, motorists traversing the winding road occasionally spot her walking slowly along the roadside in a flowing white gown, her expression filled with sorrow. It is said that drivers who spot her in their rearview mirror receive protection for their journey, but anyone who tries to approach or help her finds she vanishes instantly, and if she knocks on their vehicle's window, great misfortune follows.

#location
The haunting takes place along Wilkinson Road in Freetown, Sierra Leone.

The Spirit Possession of Nana Oparebea

Summary

As a young girl in Larteh, Ghana, Akua Oparebea vanished for days and upon her return revealed she had been possessed and guided away by a powerful shrine spirit.

Details

As a young girl growing up in Ghana, Akua Oparebea experienced mysterious disappearances that baffled her family. On one notable occasion, she vanished for several days, prompting her family to enlist local shrine rituals to find her. When she finally reappeared, Oparebea stated that she had been seized and possessed by the spirit of the Akonnedi Shrine. These periodic possession trances and deep spiritual episodes continued into her adulthood, ultimately leading to her formal initiation and lifelong service as the high priestess and custodian of the shrine.

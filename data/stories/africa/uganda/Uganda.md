#title
The Curse of Death

#summary
When the celestial spirit Walumbe followed his sister down to Earth in disobedience of divine law, he established an eternal curse that brought mortality and disease to humanity. Pursued by a divine hero, Walumbe escaped underground by burrowing into the earth, creating mysterious pits that remain linked to death ever since.

#details
In Baganda mythology, Kintu the first human married Nambi, daughter of the sky god Ggulu. Ggulu warned them never to return to heaven lest Nambi's brother Walumbe—the embodiment of death and sickness—should follow them. When Nambi forgot millet for her hen and returned to the heavens, Walumbe slipped down to Earth with her. Demanding one of Kintu's children as a servant and facing refusal, Walumbe cursed humanity by beginning to take a child's life every day. When the divine hero Kayikuuzi was sent to capture him, Walumbe evaded capture by digging deep into the ground, hiding underground and sealing humanity's mortal fate forever.

#location
The Walumbe Tanda Pits are located in the Mityana District of Uganda, where the spirit is said to have burrowed underground.

#title
The Jinx of the Consumer of Life

#summary
Travelers crossing a historic bridge over the Nile River are gripped by terror, believing the crossing is haunted by the restless spirits of thousands who perished there during past political purges. Locals and bus passengers maintain a silent, fearful reverence until they safely reach the other side.

#details
Constructed in 1963, Karuma Bridge spans the roaring rapids of the River Nile on the highway connecting Masindi and Gulu districts. During the 1970s under Idi Amin's regime, the bridge earned the Luo nickname *Lamyony jo*, meaning "consumer of life," after soldiers turned it into an execution and dumping site for thousands of innocent people, including political prisoners and disabled individuals. Later, Joseph Kony's rebel forces frequently ambushed and burned vehicles crossing the bridge. Today, motorists and bus passengers still experience overwhelming dread when approaching the span, often going completely silent or praying fervently until they have safely crossed, convinced that the souls of the victims and vengeful entities still haunt the gorge.

#location
Karuma Bridge spans the River Nile along the highway connecting Masindi and Gulu districts in Uganda.

#title
The Tragic Christmas Eve Exorcism in Serere

#summary
During a Christmas Eve prayer service intended to drive out evil spirits, a reportedly demon-possessed man kicked a Ugandan pastor, resulting in fatal internal injuries.

#details
On December 24, 2024, a 30-year-old man named Osagani was brought to church by his father, who believed his son was being attacked by demons. During the prayer and deliverance session inside the Agule Pentecostal Assemblies of God (PAG) Church, Osagani suddenly kicked the presiding minister, 52-year-old Pastor John Michael Ekamu. Despite the blow, the pastor continued prayers and stayed overnight at the church. On Christmas morning, Pastor Ekamu collapsed on the pulpit and was later rushed to Serere Health Center IV, where surgery revealed severe trauma to his pancreas. He succumbed to his injuries later that afternoon, while the suspect remained in police custody.

#location
The Agule Pentecostal Assemblies of God Church is located in Ocuko Village, Omagara Parish, Kateta Sub-county, in the Serere District of Uganda.

#title
The Tragic Christmas Eve Exorcism in Serere

#summary
During a Christmas Eve prayer service intended to drive out evil spirits, a reportedly demon-possessed man kicked a Ugandan pastor, resulting in fatal internal injuries.

#details
On December 24, 2024, a 30-year-old man named Osagani was brought to church by his father, who believed his son was being attacked by demons. During the prayer and deliverance session inside the Agule Pentecostal Assemblies of God (PAG) Church, Osagani suddenly kicked the presiding minister, 52-year-old Pastor John Michael Ekamu. Despite the blow, the pastor continued prayers and stayed overnight at the church. On Christmas morning, Pastor Ekamu collapsed on the pulpit and was later rushed to Serere Health Center IV, where surgery revealed severe trauma to his pancreas. He succumbed to his injuries later that afternoon, while the suspect remained in police custody.

#location
The Agule Pentecostal Assemblies of God Church is located in Ocuko Village, Omagara Parish, Kateta Sub-county, in the Serere District of Uganda.

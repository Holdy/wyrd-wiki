#title
The Curse of the Lady of Mali

#summary
A woman cursed by her husband for infidelity was transformed into stone, eternally immortalizing her profile on a prominent cliff face.

#details
High up in the northern region of the Republic of Guinea, an unfaithful woman was subjected to a bitter curse cast by her husband, resulting in her petrifaction. Her striking and permanent silhouette, resembling a woman's face and figure, was carved by natural elements onto the rock face on the eastern flank of the mountain, where it remains to this day as a towering landmark overlooking the surrounding plains.

#location
The eastern slope of Mount Loura, located near the town of Mali in the prefecture of Mali within the Fouta Djallon region of northern Guinea.

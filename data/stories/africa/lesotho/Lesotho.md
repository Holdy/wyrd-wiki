#title
The Awakening at Ha Ratau

#summary
A mute, demon-possessed man in a remote mountain village is delivered from his affliction during an impromptu prayer meeting. His healing sparked a widespread spiritual awakening throughout the community.

#details
In the village of Ha Ratau, situated across the Qeme Plateau in Lesotho, a family requested spiritual help for their adult son who had fallen victim to severe demonic possession and was mostly unable to speak. When Pastor Kali Nthabi arrived to hold a small cottage prayer gathering at their home, the local chief intervened and urged that a larger meeting be opened to the entire village due to widespread oppression by witchcraft and demonic forces. As dozens of villagers gathered in the yard to listen to testimonies and preaching, the possessed man sought prayer alongside the congregants. At the conclusion of the service, he was delivered from the entity and miraculously regained his voice.

#location
A residential home in the village of Ha Ratau, located across the Qeme Plateau in Lesotho.

#title
The Mourning Field Hailstorm

#summary
A strict traditional taboo in Lesotho mandates that no agricultural work may be performed in the fields the day immediately following a burial. Violating this mourning restriction triggers a delayed curse in the form of a devastating hailstorm that destroys the village's crops.

#details
According to traditional Basotho custom, the day immediately following a funeral is a sacred period of mourning and rest during which the community must abstain from all agricultural labor, such as ploughing, hoeing, or reaping. If any individual ignores this taboo and takes tools out to work the fields, a delayed curse is activated. Rather than striking the offender immediately, the transgression invokes a powerful retributive consequence: a fierce hailstorm descends upon the village, punishing the entire community by destroying their crops and livelihood.

#location
In a traditional village or agricultural field across the rural lowlands and foothills of Lesotho.

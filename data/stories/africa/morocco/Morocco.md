#title
The Court of the Jinn King

#summary
Afflicted by invisible spirits and severe spiritual possession, troubled individuals are brought to a sacred mountain shrine in the High Atlas Mountains to face the supreme jinn judge for deliverance. According to Moroccan folklore, those overtaken by jinn must submit to spiritual trials and rituals overseen by hereditary guardians at this ancient sanctuary.

#details
In Moroccan folklore, human beings who fall victim to spiritual possession—often attributed to unseen jinn entering the body—suffer from erratic behavior, trance states, and mysterious ailments. To seek a cure, the afflicted are taken on a pilgrimage to the shrine of Sidi Chamharouch, believed to be the earthly court and resting place of the supreme jinn sultan who lived for centuries and converted to Islam during the time of the Prophet Muhammad. Located near the Berber village of Aroumd, the shrine features a large boulder draped in green and white cloth where rituals, animal sacrifices, and trance dances (*Al-Hadra*) are conducted. Believers maintain that Chamharouch still presides over spiritual trials to compel possessing entities to release their human hosts.

#location
The shrine of Sidi Chamharouch is located at the base of Mount Toubkal near the Berber village of Aroumd in the High Atlas Mountains of Morocco.

#title
The Curse of Qara's Labyrinth

#summary
Constructed by a captive architect under Sultan Moulay Ismail, the sprawling subterranean vaults of Qara Prison became bound by a dark curse of endless corridors and vengeful spirits that swallows those who venture too deep.

#details
Commissioned in the early 18th century in Meknes, Morocco, the massive underground complex was built during the reign of Sultan Moulay Ismail. According to legend, a Portuguese prisoner named Qara designed the vast, doorless labyrinth in exchange for his promise of freedom, after which thousands of captives were dropped through iron-grated roof openings into pitch-black cells. The immense suffering and death within the subterranean vaults left the site cursed by malevolent spirits. Over the decades, adventurers and explorers who attempted to map the labyrinth's endless, twisting corridors vanished without a trace, prompting authorities to permanently seal off many sections with concrete walls.

#location
Qara Prison is located beneath the square in front of Qubbat al-Khayyatin within the imperial district of the Kasbah of Moulay Ismail in Meknes, Morocco.

The Phantoms of the Desert Highway

Summary

Motorists traveling along a desolate desert highway at night frequently encounter phantom figures that vanish upon impact or sprint alongside cars at impossible speeds.

Details

The Wadi El-Natrun Highway in Egypt's Beheira governorate is notorious for deadly accidents and chilling nocturnal encounters. Drivers navigating the dark desert stretch have reported hitting pedestrians who suddenly disappear or whose bodies pass right through the vehicle. Other travelers have witnessed bloodied figures running at vehicle speed alongside their cars on the lonely road.

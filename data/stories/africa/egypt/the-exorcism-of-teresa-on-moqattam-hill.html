The Exorcism of Teresa on Moqattam Hill

Summary

During a public Thursday evening service, a young woman named Teresa was brought before Father Samaan Ibrahim to be freed from multiple demons that had taken possession of her body.

Details

Every Thursday, crowds gather at the cave monastery on Moqattam Hill where Father Samaan Ibrahim performs public exorcisms to cast out malevolent entities possessing the afflicted. During one such gathering, a 22-year-old woman named Teresa was brought forward by her mother while suffering from violent seizures and screams. As Father Samaan pressed a wooden cross to her forehead and recited prayers, Teresa's body convulsed and she spoke in a deep, altered voice, declaring that there was only pain and no Christ. Her mother insisted that the harsh, defiant words were spoken not by her daughter, but by the cursed demons inhabiting her. Through prayers, commands in the name of Jesus, and the application of holy water, the priest confronted the entities and ultimately compelled them to depart from her.

#title
The Curse of the Umuvumu Tree

#summary
Local men who attempted to harvest firewood from a sacred tree in Buhanga Eco Park suffered a fatal curse when their cut wood mystically reassembled itself. The following day, all the responsible men and their families perished.

#details
Deep within the sacred grounds of Buhanga Eco Park, an ancient fig tree known locally as *Umuvumu*—translating to "the curse"—stands as a strict warning against desecration. Long ago, a group of local men entered the sacred royal forest and cut down branches from this tree to use as firewood. However, before they could even gather or burn the wood, a powerful supernatural force caused the cut branches to lift and reassemble themselves back into the living tree. Stricken by terror, the men returned home, only for all of them and their entire families to die mysteriously the very next day. To this day, visitors and locals regard the tree and the surrounding forest with deep reverence and fear.

#location
The Umuvumu tree is located within Buhanga Eco Park in the Northern Province of Rwanda, near Musanze.

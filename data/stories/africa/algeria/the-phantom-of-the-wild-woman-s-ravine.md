#title
The Phantom of the Wild Woman's Ravine

#summary
A grieving 19th-century widow who lost her children continues to haunt an Algerian ravine where her spectral shadow and harrowing screams are still encountered by travelers.

#details
In the 19th century, a young widow was picnicking with her two children in the wooded gorge known as the Ravin de la Femme Sauvage in Algiers. Tragically, she lost her children during the outing, plunging her into a profound and irreversible madness. For years, she wandered the paths and tracks of the ravine, wailing in despair until her lifeless body was finally discovered. Ever since, her tormented spirit has remained bound to the area, with passersby and walkers frequently reporting hearing her blood-curdling screams or catching fleeting glimpses of her shadowy figure moving through the terrain.

#location
Le Ravin de la Femme Sauvage is a specific ravine and roadway passage located between Bir Mourad Raïs and Oued Kniss in Algiers, Algeria.

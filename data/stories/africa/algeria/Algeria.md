#title
The Bath of the Damned

#summary
According to local folklore, the extraordinary mineral cascade of Hammam Maskhoutine was created when a divine curse struck down a forbidden wedding party, freezing the participants and their flowing robes into stone for eternity. 

#details
Local tradition explains that the name Hammam Maskhoutine—meaning "Bath of the Damned"—stems from a legendary wedding celebration that violated sacred taboos. When a man attempted to marry his own sister, the defiance of natural and moral law provoked an immediate wrath from the heavens. As the wedding procession made its way up the picturesque valley, the skies suddenly darkened into a deep gloom, and a catastrophic curse descended upon them. Struck by lightning, the entire wedding party, including the bride and groom, their guests, and their elaborate garments, were instantly frozen in place, turning permanently into stone. Today, the dramatic, multicolored conoid travertine formations and cascading mineral walls of the hot springs are viewed by locals as the everlasting monuments of that cursed procession.

#location
The thermal complex of Hammam Maskhoutine (Hammam Debagh) is situated in the Guelma Province of northeastern Algeria.

#title
The Phantom of the Wild Woman's Ravine

#summary
A grieving 19th-century widow who lost her children continues to haunt an Algerian ravine where her spectral shadow and harrowing screams are still encountered by travelers.

#details
In the 19th century, a young widow was picnicking with her two children in the wooded gorge known as the Ravin de la Femme Sauvage in Algiers. Tragically, she lost her children during the outing, plunging her into a profound and irreversible madness. For years, she wandered the paths and tracks of the ravine, wailing in despair until her lifeless body was finally discovered. Ever since, her tormented spirit has remained bound to the area, with passersby and walkers frequently reporting hearing her blood-curdling screams or catching fleeting glimpses of her shadowy figure moving through the terrain.

#location
Le Ravin de la Femme Sauvage is a specific ravine and roadway passage located between Bir Mourad Raïs and Oued Kniss in Algiers, Algeria.

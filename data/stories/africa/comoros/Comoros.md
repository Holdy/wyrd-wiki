#title
The Curse of Lake Niamawi

#summary
When the inhabitants of the village of Niamawi refused to provide water to a weary traveler, she laid a devastating curse upon the settlement, promising the wrath of a volcanic eruption. True to her word, the town was completely obliterated and submerged beneath a mysterious, bottomless crater lake.

#details
According to Comorian folklore, the northern tip of Grande Comore once held a thriving settlement known as the village of Niamawi. One day, a wandering holy woman or witch arrived in the village and begged the townspeople for water to quench her thirst. The inhabitants selfishly turned her away and refused to offer her even a drop. Enraged by their inhospitable cruelty, the stranger cursed the entire village, warning them that they would face the devastating wrath of a volcanic eruption. Shortly after, the earth violently shook, and the village was consumed by fire, ash, and lava. When the eruption subsided, the ruined settlement had vanished entirely, replaced by a deep, beautiful saltwater crater lake that locals say is bottomless.

#location
Lac Salé (also known as Lac Niamawi), situated in a volcanic crater next to the village of Mitsamiouli on the northern tip of Grande Comore.

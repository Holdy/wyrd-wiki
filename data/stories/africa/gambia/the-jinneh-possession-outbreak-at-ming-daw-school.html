The Jinneh Possession Outbreak at Ming Daw School

Summary

Dozens of teenage students at a Gambian secondary school were suddenly overtaken by malevolent spirits, exhibiting violent convulsions and unnatural superhuman strength.

Details

At Ming Daw Upper Basic School in Farato, around 30 students initially—growing to nearly 100 over subsequent days—suddenly began screaming, running, and collapsing in fits across the school campus. Witnesses and officials reported that the affected girls seemed possessed by evil spirits known locally as jinneh, displaying extraordinary physical strength that made it difficult for multiple adults to restrain them. Education officials and religious leaders, including the State House Imam, attributed the phenomenon to spiritual forces and responded by organizing Quranic recitations, while encouraging students to strengthen morning devotions and alter their attire to ward off further attacks.

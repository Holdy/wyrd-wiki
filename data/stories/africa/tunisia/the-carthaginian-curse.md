#title
The Carthaginian Curse

#summary
Following the destruction of ancient Carthage by the Roman Republic, Queen Dido's spirit cast a powerful curse over the land, declaring that no city would ever truly prosper upon its ruins. To this day, many believe this ancient hex explains why the historic site remains largely undeveloped and hindered from lasting prosperity.

#details
After the fateful fall and devastation of Carthage in 146 B.C., local Tunisian folklore and mythology intertwined around the tragic legacy of Queen Dido. Her enduring spirit laid a permanent hex upon the geography where the great Punic empire once stood, foretelling discord and failure for any civilization attempting to rise from the ashes of the destroyed city. Over the centuries, despite subsequent historical settlements and the establishment of a renowned UNESCO World Heritage archaeological site, the shadow of Dido's curse has persisted as the reason true prosperity has eluded the area.

#location
The archaeological site of Carthage is located in the modern coastal suburb of Carthage, situated northeast of Tunis, Tunisia.

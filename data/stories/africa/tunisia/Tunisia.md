#title
The Carthaginian Curse

#summary
Following the destruction of ancient Carthage by the Roman Republic, Queen Dido's spirit cast a powerful curse over the land, declaring that no city would ever truly prosper upon its ruins. To this day, many believe this ancient hex explains why the historic site remains largely undeveloped and hindered from lasting prosperity.

#details
After the fateful fall and devastation of Carthage in 146 B.C., local Tunisian folklore and mythology intertwined around the tragic legacy of Queen Dido. Her enduring spirit laid a permanent hex upon the geography where the great Punic empire once stood, foretelling discord and failure for any civilization attempting to rise from the ashes of the destroyed city. Over the centuries, despite subsequent historical settlements and the establishment of a renowned UNESCO World Heritage archaeological site, the shadow of Dido's curse has persisted as the reason true prosperity has eluded the area.

#location
The archaeological site of Carthage is located in the modern coastal suburb of Carthage, situated northeast of Tunis, Tunisia.

#title
The Maiden of Hammam Ed-Dahab

#summary
Seeking shelter inside a historic bathhouse in Tunis, a young woman is claimed by a malevolent jinn after her mother's greed causes an enchanted candle to extinguish. Her spirit and a haunting strand of her hair remain trapped within the bathhouse walls.

#details
According to Tunisian folklore passed down in the Old Medina of Tunis, an impoverished widow and her daughter were granted permission to stay overnight inside the historic Hammam Ed-Dahab bathhouse. Late at night near the hot water pool, a fissure opened in the wet floor and a malevolent jinn appeared before them. The entity offered them a deal: they could take as much gold as they desired, provided they finished before the flame of a single candle burned out. While the daughter hurriedly gathered the coins, the mother's insatiable greed drove her to demand more. A sudden gust of wind swept through and extinguished the candle, causing the opening to instantly snap shut and swallowing the daughter into the underground spirit realm. Ever since, local legend holds that a spectral strand of her hair appears to young visitors inside the bathhouse.

#location
The story is centered at Hammam Ed-Dahab, a specific historic Turkish bathhouse building located within the Old Medina of Tunis, Tunisia.

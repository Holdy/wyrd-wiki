The Demonic Hitchhikers of Kaf Ajnoun

Summary

An intrepid traveler became possessed by malevolent spirits after ignoring local warnings and climbing a notoriously haunted mountain in the Libyan desert. The parasitic entities remained hidden within her until they were violently purged during a ritual ceremony years later.

Details

While traveling through the Fezzan region of the Libyan Sahara, journalist Kira Salak became determined to climb Kaf Ajnoun (also known as Idinen or the Mountain of Ghosts), a rugged rock fortress feared by the local Tuareg people as the dwelling place of evil jinn and demons. Dismissing warnings from guides who refused to approach the forbidden summit, she successfully climbed the mountain and returned. However, a year and a half later while participating in a shamanic ceremony, her body began violently contorting as otherworldly screeches erupted from her throat. During the purging process, a vision of the Libyan mountain revealed that demonic entities had attached themselves to her soul during her unauthorized ascent, manifesting as serpentine and monstrous forms that fought for control before being driven out.

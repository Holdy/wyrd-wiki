#title
The Demonic Hitchhikers of Kaf Ajnoun

#summary
An intrepid traveler became possessed by malevolent spirits after ignoring local warnings and climbing a notoriously haunted mountain in the Libyan desert. The parasitic entities remained hidden within her until they were violently purged during a ritual ceremony years later.

#details
While traveling through the Fezzan region of the Libyan Sahara, journalist Kira Salak became determined to climb Kaf Ajnoun (also known as Idinen or the Mountain of Ghosts), a rugged rock fortress feared by the local Tuareg people as the dwelling place of evil jinn and demons. Dismissing warnings from guides who refused to approach the forbidden summit, she successfully climbed the mountain and returned. However, a year and a half later while participating in a shamanic ceremony, her body began violently contorting as otherworldly screeches erupted from her throat. During the purging process, a vision of the Libyan mountain revealed that demonic entities had attached themselves to her soul during her unauthorized ascent, manifesting as serpentine and monstrous forms that fought for control before being driven out.

#location
Kaf Ajnoun, also known as Idinen or the Mountain of Ghosts, is located in the southern region of Libya approximately 25 kilometers north of the town of Ghat.

#title
The Phantom Army of Kaf Ajnoun

#summary
A local resident witnessed an entire ghostly military division marching in formation around the base of Kaf Ajnoun before vanishing completely into the desert night. The apparition is one of many supernatural legends associated with the remote mountain.

#details
In the Fezzan region of southern Libya, the isolated sandstone massif of Kaf Ajnoun—frequently referred to as the Mountain of Ghosts or Idinen—has long held a reputation among local Tuareg nomads as a supernatural domain inhabited by jinn and spirits. Among the various encounters reported near the peak, a local man swore by Allah that he watched an entire phantom army division march in formation around the base of the mountain one night. The ghostly soldiers marched silently along the desert tracks flanking the mountain before disappearing entirely into thin air right before his eyes.

#location
The events take place at the base of Kaf Ajnoun (Idinen), a prominent desert mountain located approximately 25 kilometers north of Ghat in southern Libya.

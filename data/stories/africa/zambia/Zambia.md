#title
The Curse of Kariba Dam

#summary
When the construction of the Kariba Dam forcibly displaced local Tonga people and threatened their sacred river god, a devastating curse brought unprecedented floods and destruction to the project. The wrath of Nyaminyami manifested in catastrophic floods, worker deaths, and lingering threats to the dam's structure.

#details
For centuries, the Tonga people lived peacefully along the banks of the Zambezi River, venerating the river god Nyaminyami, who resided in the Kariba Gorge. In the 1950s, colonial authorities initiated the construction of the massive Kariba Dam, ignoring local warnings and forcibly relocating thousands of villagers. Believing that the dam would permanently separate Nyaminyami from his wife and desecrate his domain, traditional healers and elders invoked a powerful curse against the project. In July 1957, a torrential storm caused a historic flood that breached the temporary dam. The very next rainy season brought an even more catastrophic flood—an event calculated to occur only once every thousand years—which destroyed the coffer dam, access bridge, and parts of the main wall, claiming hundreds of lives. Even after completion, the curse was said to manifest through choking floating weeds and persistent structural threats to the dam walls.

#location
Kariba Dam spans the Zambezi River gorge on the border between Zambia and Zimbabwe.

#title
The Exorcism of Chimtembo

#summary
After experiencing tormenting dreams from her ancestors, a Zambian woman living in Chimtembo village undergoes a traditional exorcism ceremony performed by a renowned n'ganga. The ritual brings the community together through drumming, singing, and dancing to persuade the spirits to cease their haunting.

#details
While living in the village of Chimtembo, a volunteer witnessed a dramatic community exorcism. A local woman had been plagued by disturbing dreams in which her ancestors demanded she brew beer; failure to comply would result in ongoing haunting and torment. To appease the spirits, the villagers gathered in the center of the village, brewing maize beer, playing rhythmic drums, and singing urgently. Soon, a prominent local healer and n'ganga named Mr. Phiri arrived adorned in feathers, beaded necklaces, and ritual skirts. With his distinctive left eye scarred from a childhood accident, Phiri performed a frenzied dance and conducted the ritual alongside the possessed woman, who sat draped in purple. Through the shared music, dance, and spiritual communion, the ancestors were placated, and the woman was freed from her spiritual torment.

#location
The ceremony took place in the center of Chimtembo village in Zambia.

#title
The Mercedes Phantom Hitchhikers

#summary
Three young men who died in a car crash after arriving from the airport haunt Kafue Road, picking up hitchhikers who invariably meet a fatal end.

#details
After losing their lives in a tragic traffic accident while traveling home from the airport upon arriving in Lusaka from the United Kingdom, three young men in a Mercedes became bound to Kafue Road. Local lore holds that they now roam the highway picking up unsuspecting hitchhikers, with everyone they encounter tragically ending up dead.

#location
Kafue Road in Lusaka, Zambia.

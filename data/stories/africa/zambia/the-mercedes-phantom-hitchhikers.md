#title
The Mercedes Phantom Hitchhikers

#summary
Three young men who died in a car crash after arriving from the airport haunt Kafue Road, picking up hitchhikers who invariably meet a fatal end.

#details
After losing their lives in a tragic traffic accident while traveling home from the airport upon arriving in Lusaka from the United Kingdom, three young men in a Mercedes became bound to Kafue Road. Local lore holds that they now roam the highway picking up unsuspecting hitchhikers, with everyone they encounter tragically ending up dead.

#location
Kafue Road in Lusaka, Zambia.

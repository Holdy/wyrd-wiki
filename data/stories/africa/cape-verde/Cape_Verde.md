#title
The Midnight Procession of Cidade Velha

#summary
A spectral procession of robed souls silently patrols the midnight streets of an ancient Cape Verdean town, handing out candles that transform into human bones by morning.

#details
At the stroke of midnight in the historic town of Cidade Velha, a chilling ghostly phenomenon known as the Encamisada takes place. A silent procession of phantom souls, dressed in long white tunics and shrouds, moves methodically through the ancient cobblestone streets while carrying flickering candles and torches. According to island folklore, anyone who witnesses this ghostly procession risks being approached by one of the white-clad figures offering a candle. Those who accept the candle find that by morning it has turned into a human bone, serving as a dark omen that their own time on earth is drawing to a close.

#location
The historic cobblestone streets of Cidade Velha on the southern coast of Santiago Island, Cabo Verde.

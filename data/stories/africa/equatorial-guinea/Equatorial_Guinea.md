#title
The Ancestral Curse of Batete Heights

#summary
In the mountain village of Batete on Bioko Island, a wealthy man named Sochí fell gravely ill and was told by a witch doctor that he was suffering under an ancestral curse because his family had embraced the Christian mission. Facing imminent death, he ultimately renounced the spiritual condemnation on his deathbed to receive Christian baptism.

#details
According to the traditional folklore and beliefs of the indigenous Bubi people of Bioko Island, Equatorial Guinea, severe and lingering illnesses are never viewed as natural events, but rather as the fatal effects of curses, witchcraft, and offended ancestral spirits. When Sochí, the wealthiest resident of the mountain settlement of Batete, fell deathly ill, his family summoned the renowned witch doctor Molubbela. Through ritual divination and whistling, Molubbela declared that Sochí and his household were suffering under a severe spiritual curse inflicted by their ancestors, whose wrath had been provoked because Sochí's siblings had abandoned tribal traditions to join the Catholic mission. The witch doctor insisted that no sacrifice could lift the curse as long as his family remained tied to the church, leaving Sochí doomed. As the illness progressed to its final stages, Sochí recognized the futility of the curse, rejected the spirits and the witch doctor, and asked the missionary priest for baptism to break the spiritual affliction before passing away.

#location
Batete village in the mountain highlands of Bioko Island, Equatorial Guinea.

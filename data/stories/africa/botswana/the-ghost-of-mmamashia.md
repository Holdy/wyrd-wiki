#title
The Ghost of Mmamashia

#summary
A ghostly twelve-year-old boy named Poloko perpetually waits by the A1 road traffic lights in Mmamashia for parents who never arrive. Unaware of his passing, his presence by the highway startles passing motorists before an elderly traveler stops to comfort him.

#details
Every year during his birth week, a ghostly twelve-year-old boy named Poloko waits by the traffic lights on the A1 road in Mmamashia for his parents, who never arrive. Unaware that he is dead, Poloko frequently lingers near the highway hoping to be seen, inadvertently shocking passing drivers who spot him in the dark. While sitting alone on a bench near a local gas station, an elderly woman named Legakwa approaches the weeping boy, gently wipes away his tears, and promises to guide him home safely.

#location
The story takes place at the traffic lights and gas station along the A1 road in Mmamashia, Botswana.

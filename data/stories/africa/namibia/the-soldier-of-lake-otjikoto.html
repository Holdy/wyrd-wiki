    The Soldier of Lake Otjikoto

    Summary

    During World War I, a German soldier was dragged to his death in Lake Otjikoto by ropes attached to discarded military munitions. His restless spirit has since been seen standing on the lake's bank, pointing toward the water.

    Details

    In 1915, as World War I drew to a close in the region, the German Schutztruppe disposed of their ammunition, machine-guns, and field cannons in Lake Otjikoto to prevent them from falling into enemy hands. During this operation, an unfortunate soldier's foot became entangled in a rope, dragging him down into the deep waters where he drowned. In the years following, witnesses reported seeing his restless spirit haunting the bank of the sinkhole, typically described as standing with one arm outstretched and pointing toward the water.
